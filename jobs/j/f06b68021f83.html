Job Title: Mountain Front of House Supervisor

Location: Solitude Mountain Resort

Department: Food & Beverage

Reports To: Director of Mountain Food & Beverage


Job Overview:

The Mountain Front of House Supervisor at Solitude is responsible for overseeing the daily operations of the mountain lodge and restaurant, ensuring a seamless guest experience in a high-paced, casual dining environment. The role focuses on maintaining efficient service during peak hours, ensuring quality food and beverage offerings, and leading a front-of-house team. The supervisor will ensure that all guests, whether grabbing a quick bite between runs or enjoying après-ski drinks, receive exceptional service.


Key Responsibilities:

Team Leadership:

  • Supervise, train, and motivate front-of-house staff including cashiers, servers, bartenders, and hosts.
  • Conduct pre-shift briefings to communicate daily goals, specials, and any service updates.
  • Manage staff schedules using Workday, ensuring optimal coverage during peak times.
  • Track employee attendance, time-off requests and shift swaps in Workday, ensuring all information is accurately recorded and in compliance with HR policies and timelines.
  • Mentor staff on delivering efficient, friendly service, and handling large crowds while maintaining high standards.
  • Attend all mandatory meetings in person.
  • Be in charge of and execute all internal, private & public events that occur in Last Chance location.
  • Make schedules for all FOH staff.
  • Make sure that ski school lunches are served in timely manner on wekends.
  • Order all nessecary food and beverage items for FOH.

Guest Experience:

  • Ensure smooth and speedy service, particularly during peak times.
  • Address guest inquiries, requests, or concerns promptly and professionally.
  • Oversee the seating process to ensure tables are turned quickly and guests are served in a timely manner.

Perks of Working Here:

Work & Play in the Mountains:

  • Embrace the stunning Wasatch Mountains.
  • Connect with fellow mountain enthusiasts.

Benefits:

  • Access to select Alterra Mountain Company Destinations.
  • Free Solitude season pass for you and eligible dependents.
  • Discounts on solitude lift tickets for friends and family.
  • Discounts on food, rentals, retail, and lessons.

Other Perks:

  • Comprehensive medical, dental, vision, and life insurance for eligible employees
  • Pro deals with outdoor brands.
  • Generous 401(k) plan with company match.
  • Free counseling service through Employee Assistance Program.
