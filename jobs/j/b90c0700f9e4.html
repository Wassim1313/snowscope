About the job

Eldora is the backyard winter mountain playground for Greater Denver, Boulder County, and Colorado's northern Front Range. Eldora is located just outside of the charming mountain town of Nederland, Colorado, about 20 miles west of Boulder and less than 50 miles from downtown Denver.

Eldora strives to deliver Colorado’s Front Range the most memorable outdoor adventures to locals and out-of-town visitors. Eldora Mountain Resort employs up to 700 employees at peak season and has 680 acres of lift-accessible terrain for skiing and snowboarding, including a Woodward Mountain Park experience, and a world class Nordic Center.

Position Summary: Eldora Mountain Resort is looking for an IT Systems Support Specialist II. This position provides advanced technical support throughout the resort so our employees can provide our guests with an exceptional day at the mountain. This role serves as an experienced technical resource for end-users, guest-facing systems, and resort operations, and works closely with the IT Director to install, configure, maintain, and improve data, network, voice, point-of-sale, and business-critical systems. The Specialist II is expected to independently troubleshoot complex issues, support project work, assist with system documentation, and help mentor seasonal or less experienced IT team members. This is a full-time year-round job onsite in Colorado. We are not able to sponsor any type of visa for this role at this time.


Essential Functions/Major Responsibilities:

  • Provide advanced troubleshooting support for end-users, guest-facing systems, and business-critical resort applications.
  • Install, configure, maintain, and upgrade computers, servers, network equipment, telephones, printers, scanners, cameras, audio systems, time clocks, RFID systems, and related IT infrastructure.
  • Independently diagnose and resolve software, hardware, system, network, and connectivity issues, escalating when appropriate.
  • Support day-to-day operation, monitoring, and maintenance of backup systems, server environments, and core network infrastructure.
  • Provide infrastructure support including premise wiring, fiber, wireless access points, switches, firewalls, and related network hardware.
  • Recommend and perform corrective actions to improve software, system, network, and operational reliability.
  • Research, evaluate, test, and help implement new software, hardware, tools, and process improvements.
  • Support, install, maintain, and troubleshoot race timing systems for daytime and evening races/events.
  • Assist in maintaining Siriusware point-of-sale, e-commerce, and ticket scanning systems, including issue resolution during high-volume operating periods.
  • Create and maintain basic system documentation, troubleshooting guides, asset information, and internal IT procedures.
  • Provide guidance and support to seasonal IT staff, new team members, or department users on technology procedures and best practices.
  • Support IT projects, system upgrades, seasonal readiness efforts, and department initiatives as assigned by the IT Director.
  • Offer assistance to other departments as needed throughout the season.
  • Leads by example for all company safety policies and procedures, actively contributing to a safe working environment for self, coworkers, and guests.
  • High school or GED diploma required; bachelor’s degree in computer science, MIS, information technology, or a related field preferred.
  • Minimum four to five years of experience in an IT support, systems support, help desk, network support, or related technical role preferred.
  • Must be able to work weekends and holidays as required by resort operations.

Specific Job Skills:

  • Strong experience with Windows-based servers and clients, including SQL, e-commerce websites, file servers, point-of-sale systems, Office 365, Entra, Mac iOS, and current Windows operating systems.
  • Working knowledge of core network hardware and concepts, including firewalls, switches, wireless networks, VLANs, and advanced network troubleshooting.
  • Proficiency troubleshooting printers, scanners, point-of-sale systems, cameras, time clocks, ticketing systems, and other resort technology.
  • Experience with Siriusware, Sales Point Solutions, Cisco\Aruba switching, VMware, Nutanix, Intune or similar enterprise systems preferred.
  • Ability to independently prioritize tasks, manage competing operational needs, and provide support during high-volume guest and employee periods.
  • Ability to document technical processes clearly and communicate technical information to both skilled and non-technical end-users.
  • Exceptional customer service skills with a calm, solutions-oriented approach in fast-paced operational environments.
  • Successful track record communicating with unskilled and skilled end-users, department leaders, vendors, and cross-functional partners.
  • Strong attention to detail with emphasis on accuracy, quality, follow-through, and system reliability.
