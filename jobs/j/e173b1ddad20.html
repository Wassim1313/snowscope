SeasonalPalisades TahoeWe share the spirit of these legendary mountains with the world.


To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential duties.

Job Summary: Maintains the cleanliness of the restrooms located in the dining area by performing general housekeeping duties. Supervises activities of cleaning crew.


Applicants must be at least 18 years of age.

Essential Job Responsibilities/Duties/Tasks include the following: other duties may be assigned:

  • Supervises cleaning crew in accordance with Palisades Tahoe policies and procedures.
  • Conducts safety meetings for crew
  • Maintains cleaning supply inventory and informs manager of shortages
  • Maintain restrooms to the highest degree of cleanliness
  • Wash sinks, pick up trash, clean toilets and stalls
  • Maintain adequate stock of restroom provisions & ordering supplies
  • Monitor trash receptacles and dispose of trash that is in or around dining facilities including decks
  • Sweep and mop concourse tile area
  • Vacuum carpeted areas throughout entire facility
  • Accommodate guests’ requests as needed
  • Heavy labor, snow removal, hauling trash, and furniture moving.
  • Assist with set up & breakdown; set tables and chairs
  • Compliance with chemical handling & usage policies

Competencies and Job Requirements:
Required:

  • Effective communication skills, oral & written
  • Strong sense of customer service & safety awareness
  • Able to understand and follow verbal and written instructions.
  • Have a neat & clean appearance in compliance with the company grooming standards
  • Punctual, able to report at scheduled start time
  • Work well as part of a team.
