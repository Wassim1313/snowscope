About the job

Overview

Join Team Yeti!

Sunday River is looking for a few animated and energetic individuals to help bring the resort mascots, Eddy & Betty the Yetis, to life this winter. As one of these beloved characters, you will be expected to follow specific behavioral standards and practices, maintain character integrity, and keep a planned schedule of appearances. Individuals interested in performing as Betty must be between 5’-5’4”. Individuals interested in performing as Eddy must be between 5’4”-6’ tall.


Responsibilities

  • Perform as Eddy or Betty for guests
  • Attend all shifts/events you are signed up for
  • Maintain personality of character while performing
  • Pose for pictures and give endless amounts of high-five

Qualifications

  • Previous experience preferred but not necessary
  • Must be at least 16 years old

Compensation & Benefits

  • Wage is $16.50 to $18 per hour
  • Team Member Perks include Season Pass, resort discounts, and access to other Boyne Resorts amenities.
  • Eligibility for affordable Team Member housing.
