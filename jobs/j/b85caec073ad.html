About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!


We’re currently hiring Rental Technicians for the upcoming winter season. This role requires helping guests get set up quickly, safely, and confidently with everything they need to enjoy their day on the hill.


Perks:

  • Starting pay: $18/hour
  • Health insurance options for ALL employees
  • Free season pass to Boreal, Soda Springs, and all POWDR resorts
  • Full access to the Bunker—Woodward Tahoe’s indoor training facility with trampolines, foam pits, skateparks, and more
  • Discounts on food, retail, and gear
  • Discounts for friends and family
  • Work in a fun, supportive, mountain-town environment
  • Career advancement opportunities across the Woodward and POWDR network

What You’ll Be Doing:


As a Rental Technician, you’ll help guests gear up for a great day on the mountain. Whether it’s a first-time skier or a returning, your job is to ensure each person leaves the rental shop with properly fitted equipment and a positive, confident attitude.


Daily Responsibilities:


  • Fit guests with ski or snowboard boots, bindings, and equipment based on ability and size
  • Adjust, test, and maintain rental gear to ensure safety and functionality
  • Assist guests with returns, exchanges, and troubleshooting
  • Educate guests on proper use of equipment and basic safety
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and efficient rental area
  • Provide friendly and professional customer service
  • Work closely with other departments like ticketing, ski school, and lift ops
  • Keep a positive attitude, even during peak weekends and holidays

Work Environment & Schedule:


  • Indoor/outdoor environment with high guest traffic
  • Fast-paced setting—especially on weekends and powder days
  • Early morning, weekend, and holiday shifts are expected
  • Must be able to stand, bend, and lift (up to 50 lbs) for extended periods
  • Willingness to learn rental software and safety standards

Who We’re Looking For:

  • No prior rental experience required—we’ll train you!
  • Friendly, approachable, and customer-focused
  • Mechanically inclined or willing to learn basic gear setup
  • Dependable and team-oriented
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, guest-facing environment
  • Bonus if you ski or ride—but not required!
