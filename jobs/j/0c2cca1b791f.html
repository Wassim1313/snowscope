Job Summary:

This is a supervisory position for the housekeeping function, with direct reports. Ensures the efficient, high quality operation of the housekeeping team during assigned schedule.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $22.00/hr - $29.22/hr
  • Employment Type: Year Round
  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: Yes

Job Responsibilities:

  • Responsible for hiring/staffing, scheduling and performance management/evaluations.
  • Also fulfills day-to-day work responsibilities for the organization as needed.
  • There are no supervisory responsibilities associated with this position.

Job Requirements:

  • High school diploma or general education degree (GED);
  • Six months to one year months related experience and/a-or training; or equivalent combination of education and experience.
  • Six months supervisory experience
  • Need a valid California Driver’s License. Certification for any resort equipment as needed for the success of the department.

The expected pay range is $22.00/hr - $29.22/hr. This is the pay range we reasonably expect to pay for this position. Individual compensation decisions are based on a variety of factors.
