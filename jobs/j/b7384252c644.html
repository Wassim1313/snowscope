Position Overview

The Food & Beverage Manager supports the Hotel Manager in the day to day operation of the lodge, with primary responsibility for the Food & Beverage program including front of house service and culinary. This role provides leadership and oversight to ensure the department operates efficiently, delivers exceptional guest experiences and contributes meaningfully to overall financial performance.

The position requires a balance of hands on operational leadership and administrative oversight, including team supervision, problem solving and continuous improvement of service standards.

Along with a year-round hotel, pub and dining room, The Lodge at Arrow Lakes is also host to the CMH Kootenay Heli-Skiing operation during winter months. To learn more about The Lodge at Arrow Lakes, please visit the website: The Lodge at Arrow Lakes

Essential Duties and Responsibilities

  • Collaborate with the management team to achieve Food & Beverage key performance indicators and financial targets.
  • Support the Hotel Manager in providing direction, performance feedback and ongoing coaching to develop and lead the team.
  • Act as a key point of contact for F&B operations, supporting the Hotel Manager in departmental coordination.
  • Ensure F&B service standards are consistently delivered and aligned with brand expectations.
  • Oversee inventory management, ordering processes and cost controls within F&B operations.
  • Support menu development, product costing and overall F&B program execution.
  • Maintain all liquor licences and service compliance with liquor laws.
  • Ensure full compliance with all BC food safety regulations and standards.
  • Maintain a strong working knowledge of all hotel roles to support operational effectiveness and team alignment.
  • Partner with the Hotel Manager to uphold company policies related to Occupational Health & Safety, including WorkSafe BC and WHMIS compliance.
  • Contribute to the development and implementation of sustainability initiatives across operations.
  • Act as Duty Manager in the absence of Hotel Manager.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Post-secondary education in hospitality, travel and tourism or related field preferred.
  • Minimum of 5 years of leadership experience in the Food & Beverage industry.
  • Food Safe Certificate.
  • BC Serving It Right.
