About the job

Our mission is to create the Experience of a Lifetime for our employees, so they can, in turn, create the Experience of a Lifetime for our guests. We own and operate the most renowned destination resorts in the world as well as regional and local ski areas outside major cities, and connect them all through one unrivaled network. We are looking for ambitious leaders, innovators and creators to join our talented team. If you’re ready to pursue your fullest potential, we want to get to know you!

Candidates for year-round positions are reviewed on a rolling basis. Applications will be accepted up to 90 days after the posting date, or until the position is filled (whichever is first).

Job Summary:

Reach Your Peak at Vail Resorts. You're someone who pushes boundaries and challenges the status quo. You're brave, ambitious and passionate in everything you do. And we want you on our team. Pursue your fullest potential and never settle in the quest to deliver extraordinary guest service. Join one of the world's 50 most innovative companies as named by Fast Company, and re-imagine a mountain resort experience with us. Welcome to Vail Resorts. Reach Your Peak.

Our mission is to create the “experience of a lifetime” not only for our external guests, but also for our internal stakeholders. Use empathy, listening skills and product knowledge to deliver an extraordinary customer experience within the Sales Enterprise.

The Catering and Conference Services Manager will plan and service groups and weddings within Breckenridge Resort and Keystone Resort.

Job Responsibilities:

Is this opportunity right for you? We are looking for candidates who can:

  • Maximize food and beverage revenue to the Resorts through strong product knowledge, selling skills and client upselling techniques.
  • Utilize an in-depth knowledge of all areas of the Resort as well as outside vendors used by the department as related to group functions including the capabilities, procedures, policies and pricing. Maintain professional working relationship with food and beverage managers, departments within the Resorts and outside vendors.​
  • Service groups as business levels demand.
  • Work with clients to ensure that the function is professionally planned and have contact with client once in house to ensure any requests or problems are resolved.
  • Monitor collection of pre-payment for all groups prior to their arrival.
  • Keep client aware of time frames for Banquet Event Orders to be completed, signed and returned 45 days prior to arrival for distribution no later than two weeks out.
  • Develop and maintain professionalism with clients, in house departments and vendors, maximizing profitability and guest service.
  • Take responsibility to ensure that group leaders are comfortable with the resort and knowledgeable about our product, personnel and responsibilities.
  • Catering and Conference Services Manager is required to work all major functions as assigned by the Director or Associate Director of Catering and Conference Services, understanding many events will occur in the evening after hours and on weekends.
  • Contribute to the health and strength of a dynamic team culture.
  • Embrace and contribute to an Enterprise-wide culture of leadership.
  • Have two years Conference/Catering services management experience including weddings
  • Have two years hotel/lodging experience
  • Have Delphi.FDC experience
