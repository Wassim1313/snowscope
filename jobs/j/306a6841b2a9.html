Why Work with Us?

  • Join us as an Ellis certified lifeguard at our Split Rock Pools! We provide Ellis certification and training at the resort. We’re seeking enthusiastic and attentive individuals to ensure a safe and enjoyable swim environment for our guests.

  • Adventure Awaits: Explore the stunning Snowshoe Mountain and enjoy all the outdoor activities it has to offer while you work.

  • Make Connections: Meet guests and employees from across the country and around the world and provide them with exceptional hospitality that makes Snowshoe their home away from home.

Job Responsibilities:

  • Maintain professional knowledge by having current lifeguard, CPR, basic life support, and AED certifications and regular in-service training.

  • Provides guests with a safe swimming facility by maintaining your zone of protection, making save, and making safety checks as necessary.

  • Serve guests by checking them into the facility and answering questions

  • Maintains professional skills by swimming on a regular basis and working on skills with other lifeguards.

  • Maintains operations by following defined procedures and guidelines, filling out reports, and acting on the situation if there is an incident.

  • Maintains pool appearance by vacuuming the pool, cleaning pool tiles, and keeping the pool deck clean.

  • Maintains lake appearance by raking the beach, setting up/taking down chairs and umbrellas, cleaning inflatables and boats, and inspecting equipment for safety.

  • Maintains appearance of the facilities by cleaning bathrooms, pool deck, rugs, sauna room, beach area, and washing towels.

  • Maintains work area by picking up trash and shoveling snow.

  • Assist guests with launching boats from the beach and provide related information to guests.

  • Provide daily startup and shutdown of the pool and lake facilities.

  • Enforce established guidelines for guest safety and experience.

  • Maintains appearance of areas by raking beach, mowing grass, tending to the landscaping, and keeping the workplace clean and tidy.

  • Lifeguard, CPR, BLS, and AED certifications (On job training will be provided)

Preferred Experience:

  • Lifeguard Certification

  • First Aid/CPR/AED
