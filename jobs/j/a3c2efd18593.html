About the job

Overview

The Jordan Spa at Sunday River Resort is seeking a Maine Licensed Esthetician to join our team in providing exceptional spa experiences for our resort guests. If you are passionate about skincare, wellness, and helping others look and feel their best, we'd love to hear from you. This part-time position is ideal for someone who enjoys building client relationships and delivering personalized treatments in a relaxing resort environment.


Responsibilities

  • Provide consistent and professional facials, body treatments, and hand & foot treatments in accordance with spa protocols and accepted certification practices.
  • Utilize proper body mechanics to provide services that benefit guests while promoting your own health and wellness.
  • Consult with clients to determine their skincare needs and recommend appropriate treatments.
  • Ensure treatments are completed on schedule so guests arrive on time for their next appointment or activity.
  • Uphold the standards of sanitation and sterilization as directed by law and the spa's policies and procedures, maintaining a clean and organized treatment room and workspace.
  • Work independently, maintain a positive attitude, and contribute to a collaborative, high-quality work environment.
  • Learn product offerings to make knowledgeable recommendations to clients.
  • Assist with guest questions and concerns professionally and courteously.

Qualifications

  • Current Maine Esthetician license required.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work a flexible schedule, including evenings, weekends, and holidays. Weekend availability is required.

Compensation & Benefits

  • Hourly wages range from $15.10 to $19, plus gratuities and commissions, with opportunities for professional growth and advancement.
  • Eligibility for affordable Team Member housing.
  • Full-time seasonal, part-time, and temporary team members receive free skiing and golf, lessons, rentals, and resort discounts on dining, lodging, retail, and spa services.

Work in a peaceful mountain setting while helping guests relax, recharge, and enjoy a memorable spa experience.
