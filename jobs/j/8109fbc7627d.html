About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!


As the Lodge Services Supervisor, you will oversee the daily cleaning and maintenance of lodge facilities, ensuring a clean, safe, and welcoming environment for guests and employees. This role is responsible for helping lead the Lodge Services team, assigning tasks, training employees, and maintaining cleanliness standards.


As a key player at Woodward, you will work with teams across the mountain to ensure our facilities are operating at their best. If you are a hands-on leader who takes pride in your work and enjoys creating a positive team environment, we want to hear from you!


The Perks:


  • Compensation: $19 - $23 per hour.
  • Employee housing options available.
  • Health insurance options for ALL employees.
  • Complimentary access to most POWDR-owned resorts.
  • Full access to our world-class facilities.
  • Friends & family discounts and complimentary passes.
  • Discounts on food, retail, and gear!
  • Career development and growth opportunities.
  • A team of people who share the same passions and embrace each other's unique style.

The Job:


  • Supervise, train, and support the Lodge Services team.
  • Assign daily duties and ensure cleaning standards are met.
  • Oversee cleaning and maintenance of lodges, restrooms, kitchens, dining areas, and common spaces.
  • Perform hands-on cleaning duties as needed, including vacuuming, bussing tables, emptying garbage, and cleaning surfaces.
  • Monitor cleaning supplies and equipment and maintain inventory as needed.
  • Conduct facility inspections and report maintenance or safety concerns.
  • Ensure proper handling and storage of cleaning chemicals and familiarity with SDS information.
  • Ensure employees follow company policies and BRC safety standards.
  • Coordinate staffing needs and daily operations.
  • Provide excellent customer service and assist guests and employees as needed.



What we're looking for:


  • Previous custodial, housekeeping, or facilities experience.
  • Strong communication and organization skills.
  • Ability to train, motivate, and hold team members accountable.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to cleanliness and safety.
  • Ability to work independently and in a fast-paced environment.
  • Dependable, self-motivated, and flexible.
  • Ability to perform physical cleaning duties and work in cold weather conditions.
  • Must be comfortable working around the public and assisting guests.
  • Must be available to work weekends and holidays.
