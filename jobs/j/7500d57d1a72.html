Essential Job Responsibilities/Duties/Tasks include the following; other duties may be assigned:

  • Provides a safe environment for staff and guests 24 hours a day.
  • Patrols the Resort and provide protection to all guests and employees.
  • Always be visible and accessible, watching for criminal activity, violations of the Courtesy Code, and assisting guests.
  • Check doors and windows of buildings to ensure they are tightly closed and locked; notes unlocked doors/windows in the Daily Log; submits information to supervisors and managers.
  • Transports guests and staff as needed.
  • Unlock and/or lock doors as requested by appropriate staff.
  • Identify safety hazards and either remedy the problem or report to Risk/Safety Manager.
  • Responds to alarms and dispatched calls; decides what actions to take based on situation, facts known and position limitations.
  • Maintains comprehensive Daily Log.
  • Interacts with fire department and law enforcement as required and assist all departments when necessary.
  • Complete accurate and thorough incident reports.

Competencies and Job Requirements

Required:

  • Knowledge of basic security and fire inspection procedures.
  • Able to follow all policies and procedures contained in Security Manual.
  • Effective communication skills, oral & written.
  • Strong sense of customer service & safety awareness.
  • Able to understand and follow verbal and written instructions.
  • Have a neat & clean appearance in compliance with the company grooming standards.
  • Punctual, able to report at scheduled start time in all weather conditions.
  • Work well as part of a team.
  • Skill in observing situations and decision making.
  • Able to work with minimum supervision.
  • Effectively communicate with people in potentially stressful situations.
  • High level of interpersonal skills to handle sensitive and confidential situations.

Education and Experience:

Required:

  • 21 years of age
  • High School Diploma or GED
  • Valid Drivers License and clean driving record
  • Ability to pass a criminal background check

Preferred:

  • A valid California Security License issued by the BSIS
  • Basic CPR/AED and first aid training
  • Minimum one year security experience and/or training; or equivalent combination of education and experience
