PURPOSE OF POSITION: Our Level IV Fleet Maintenance Mechanics plays a critical role in the success of our maintenance shop. You will lead as a highly skilled technical mechanic responsible for diagnosing, repairing, and delivering meticulous workmanship. In this role, you will provide advanced technical support and serve as a resource for lower-level technicians. Deer Valley Resort is known for providing the highest standard in the industry as we focus on attention to detail, genuine care, and exceptional service.



RESPONSIBILITIES:


Advanced Diagnostic & Technical Expertise


  • Perform advanced diagnostics and repairs on gas and diesel engines, hydraulics, and electrical systems


  • Monitor proper usage, maintenance, and storage of all fleet equipment (shop tools, vehicles, etc.)


  • Perform overhaul operations on all components of assigned equipment


  • Accurately troubleshoot all assigned vehicle/equipment systems


  • Perform on-equipment fabrication of covers, brackets, and frame members


  • Perform corrosion control and spot repair of body and frame components


Leadership Opportunities


  • Provide mentorship and hands-on training for Level I, II, and III technicians


  • Provide quality control for all Level III mechanics and below


  • Support a culture of continuous improvement by contributing to training programs and materials


  • Lead by example, fostering accountability, craftsmanship, and pride in work


  • Support department operations and decision-making in the absence of leadership


Quality, Precision, & Attention to Detail


  • Ensure all repairs meet the highest standards of safety, reliability, and workmanship


  • Ensure work order accuracy and completion for assigned jobs


  • Maintain complete, accurate, and detailed work orders and service records



  • Monitor proper use, maintenance, and storage of all tools, vehicles, and shop equipment


Operational Excellence & Service Mindset


  • Approach every tsk with a commitment to exceptional service, ensuring vehicles are safe, reliable, and ready to support resort operations


  • Demonstrate genuine care through attention to team support, equipment stewardship, and workplace safety


  • Other duties as assigned


QUALIFICATIONS:


  • 5+ years experience in fleet maintenance, including gas/diesel engines, hydraulics, and electrical systems


  • Thorough knowledge of diagnostic tools and their proper use (i.e. volt ohm meters, scan tools, etc.) required


  • Must be able to lift, carry, and install heavy items


  • Must supply personal tools and tool chest


  • Must have a valid driver’s license and clean driving record


  • Must be able to work a flexible schedule, including nights, weekends, and holidays


DATES OF EMPLOYMENT:


  • Year Round


PAY:


  • $35-44 DOE
