Position Summary:

This is a part time position. Must have a valid Driver’s License and Must know federal and state laws. Transport guests to and from the resort village and parking areas. Responsible for the safe, timely and courteous operation of the transit vehicle(s) assigned to him/her and for daily maintenance and upkeep of the vehicle(s) and equipment in her (his) charge. He or she will be asked to work weekends and holidays and special events.


Essential Functions and Major Responsibilities:

  • Conduct thorough pre-trip inspection of assigned vehicle and report any maintenance issues.
  • Rectify any simple maintenance problems as they appear, i.e., filling fluids, fuel, tire pressure, light bulbs, etc.
  • Maintain a safe, courteous and friendly atmosphere on the bus.
  • Know and keep to transit schedule.
  • Be aware of road conditions and respond appropriately. Will occasionally install chains on buses and vans.

Specific Job Skills:

License/Certifications- Driver’s license with clean driving record

Technical- Comfortable operating larger vehicles, 14 passenger van.

Physical Capabilities- Able to sit for extended periods of time and walk on slippery and uneven terrain.

Additional - Effective clear and legible writing and verbal communication. Must possess excellent guest service skills.


Education/Related Experience:

Minimum education required - High school diploma or equivalent.

Minimum time in related position- 3 years driving experience.
