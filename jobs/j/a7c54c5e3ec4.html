About the job

Overview

Sugarloaf is looking for Snowboard Shop Sales Associates for our coming winter season. A Snowboard Shop Sales Associate is responsible for the provision of quality customer service and thorough product knowledge and will also perform the daily tasks of the retail operation. Our Sales Associates are excited to help each and every guest with their needs.

This full-time winter season role comes with great job perks and an hourly wage of $17-22 depending upon previous experience.

If you enjoy working with people in a fun and supportive location, please apply today!


Responsibilities

  • Give accurate and current information about the merchandise for all brands carried in assigned shop
  • Utilize the tools and training provided by Sugarloaf Resort to develop long-term customer relationships and increase sales
  • Develop strong knowledge of Sugarloaf Resort property as well as the surrounding area in order to be able to answer questions regarding area activities
  • Maintain a positive and cheerful attitude at all times
  • Take advantage of additional in-store and outside training opportunities offered
  • Must be able to work independently and multi-task while greeting all guests promptly as they enter the store and hosting a warm atmosphere at all times

Qualifications

The ideal candidate for this position will:

  • have effective communication skills
  • have experience in customer service and sales
  • be able to effectively work as part of a team
  • pay attention to detail and be accurate with money usage and register use
  • be able to exert a moderate amount of physical activity in order to carry, stock and move inventory
  • have some knowledge or familiarity with equipment sold as well as some experience with Microsoft Office and Point of Sale programs

Sugarloaf Team Member Benefits include:

  • Free lift pass for skiing and/or riding
  • Discounts at resort F&B and retail locations
  • Discounted ski/snowboard lessons and rentals
  • Access to Nordic trails and discounted equipment rentals at our Outdoor Center location
  • Discounted passes at other industry partner resorts
