About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!


The Rental Services Assistant Manager is a key member of the Boreal Ridge Corporation team and plays an integral role in the development and operation of the Rental Services department. This position supports the Rental Services Manager in overseeing the rental shop and demo shop, ensuring efficient operations, exceptional guest experiences, and a high level of team member performance.


We’re looking for a hands-on, team-oriented leader who is passionate about action sports, team development, and delivering an exceptional guest experience. The ideal candidate is organized, innovative, adaptable, and comfortable working in a fast-paced environment while leading by example.


What's in it for you?


  • $22 - $24 per hour.
  • Annual performance bonus plan.
  • Company Matched 401K.
  • Paid time off.
  • This a year-around position working 10-months out of the year.
  • Health, Dental, and Vision plans.
  • A work culture based on fun and progression.
  • Full access to our world-class facilities.
  • Retail discounts on apparel and equipment.
  • Complimentary access to most POWDR-owned resorts.
  • Enough free time to enjoy the Woodward lifestyle!

Essential Duties & Responsibilities:


  • Assist the Rental Services Manager with the overall operation and quality control of the rental and demo shops.
  • Assist with hiring, training, developing, and overseeing Rental Services team members.
  • Lead by example with honesty, integrity, and a commitment to the company’s core values.
  • Ensure established policies, procedures, systems, processes, and service standards are followed.
  • Support the development and implementation of annual revenue center plans and strategies.
  • Contribute to revenue growth through pricing, sales, process improvement, and performance tracking.
  • Assist with managing daily operations for a department that operates 7 days a week during peak seasons.

Basic Requirements:


  • 2-5 years of supervisory experience.
  • Experience supervising 10+ employees preferred.
  • Ability to multitask while maintaining strong attention to detail.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced and potentially high-pressure environment.
  • Ability to motivate, coach, develop, and lead team members.
  • Strong business planning and measurement skills.
  • Microsoft Office and Office 365 proficiency.
  • RTP/Intouch or equivalent rental POS experience is a plus.

Physical Capabilities:


  • This position requires both indoor and outdoor work.
  • May require off-site travel, including travel between Boreal Ridge Corporation locations.
  • Must be able to lift up to 50 lbs. and carry 50 yards.
  • Must be able to visually review written documents and observe coworkers on a regular basis.
