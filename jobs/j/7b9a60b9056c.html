Job Summary:


The Rock Wall Attendant supports daily operations at Stowe Rocks Climbing Gym by creating a safe, welcoming, and organized environment for guests and staff. This role is responsible for monitoring climbing activities, supporting guest check-in and orientation, maintaining cleanliness and equipment readiness, and helping deliver a positive experience for climbers of different ages and skill levels.


Job Specifications:


  • Starting Wage: $20.00/hr
  • Employment Type: Summer Seasonal 2026
  • Shift Type: Part Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No


Job Responsibilities: 


Guest experience:

  • Welcome guests in a friendly and professional manner
  • Provide clear instructions on climbing wall rules, procedures, and expectations
  • Help guests feel comfortable and supported during their visit
  • Answer questions about climbing sessions, equipment, and general facility use
  • Maintain a positive, approachable presence in the gym at all times


Safety and Supervision:

  • Monitor the climbing area closely to help ensure safe participation
  • Enforce safety rules, gym procedures, and staff direction consistently
  • Assist with harness fitting, safety checks, and basic orientation as trained
  • Identify and communicate risks, concerns, or equipment issues promptly
  • Respond appropriately to incidents, concerns, or emergency situations according to procedure

Operations and Facility Support:

  • Help open, operate, and close the climbing gym according to daily procedures
  • Keep the facility clean, organized, and ready for guests
  • Inspect and maintain assigned equipment and report wear or damage
  • Support daily documentation, logs, and operational checklists as required
  • Assist with setup, breakdown, and general flow of climbing programs or sessions


Teamwork and Communication:

  • Work closely with supervisors and teammates to support smooth daily operations
  • Communicate clearly about guest needs, safety issues, and operational updates
  • Adapt to changing guest volume, schedules, and staffing needs
  • Support a team culture that is professional, helpful, and guest-focused



Job Qualifications:

  • Strong communication and customer service skills
  • Ability to remain attentive and calm in a fast-paced environment
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to follow established procedures
  • Comfortable enforcing rules and maintaining safety standards
  • Ability to work as part of a team while also staying self-directed during assigned tasks
  • Willingness to learn climbing gym procedures, safety expectations, and equipment use

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Previous experience in guest service, recreation, climbing, or hospitality
  • Experience working in a safety-focused environment
  • Basic knowledge of climbing gym operations or belay systems, if required for the role
  • Ability to stand and move throughout the shift
  • Ability to lift and carry equipment or supplies within job expectations
  • Ability to work in an active, guest-facing environment
  • Ability to respond quickly to safety concerns or operational needs

Work Environment:


  • Indoor climbing gym environment
  • Evening, weekend, and holiday availability may be required based on business needs
  • Frequent interaction with guests, children, families, and team members
