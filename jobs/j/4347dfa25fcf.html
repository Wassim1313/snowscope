POSITION SUMMARY


The Advanced Groomer operates heavy construction equipment and/or snow grading equipment with attachments and accomplishes assigned job requests with minimal direct supervision. This person understands objectives of job functions, knows capability of equipment and completes assignments in a safe, efficient and acceptable manner. Additionally, the Advanced Groomer is responsible for assisting the Foreman in the training of Journeyman operators. This is a full-time seasonal position, compensated at $25.00 - $29.00/hour, based on experience.

Recruitment for this role will be ongoing through December.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Operating a vehicle is an essential function and will require the applicant to meet the minimum requirements of the Intrawest Drivers Standards Policy
  • Grooming Stratton’s trails consistent with the operations plan for the next day
  • Appropriately responding to weather conditions and how it will affect grooming
  • Communicating with evening supervisor and the rest of the crew throughout the evening to report progress, issues, breakdowns, changes etc.
  • Pushing snow in early season
  • Build and maintain proper grades for lift ramps and mazes
  • Deliver propane to various outbuildings on the mountain
  • Assist the snowmaking department with moving of equipment when needed
  • Building of start bumps for mogul events
  • Training and mentoring of Groomer level I and II operators
  • Freestyle terrain (park) building and maintenance
  • Work in a safe and appropriate manner utilizing all safety equipment provided
  • Maintaining grooming logs on a nightly basis
  • Communicating with Mechanics about any equipment issues or breakdowns
  • Perform nightly checks before operating equipment
  • Responsible for cleaning snow cats on a nightly basis
  • Must be able to work third shift through the night efficiently and safely
  • Required to work weekends and holidays
  • Overtime may be required, but not guaranteed
  • Working knowledge of snow cat maintenance, basic troubleshooting, and the ability to fix/maintain minor issues

QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILL AND ABILITY REQUIREMENTS

  • 5+ years of grooming experience, park building experience and/or winch cat experience
  • Must be at least 18+ years of age
  • Valid Driver's License
  • Skiing or snowboarding experience is a plus
  • Must be able to operate equipment in extreme weather conditions
  • Must be able to work third shift through the night efficiently and safely

PHYSICAL DEMANDS AND WORKING CONDITIONS

This position will be required to work evenings, weekends and holidays.

To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily with or without reasonable accommodations.
