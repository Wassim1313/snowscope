Job Summary:

Responsible for the effective supervision and the daily operation of Breckenridge Resort Fleet Maintenance Shops and Technicians that maintain the snowcats, snowmobiles, buses, heavy equipment and rubber tire vehicles.

Reviews work orders, work progress, schedules, and priorities to ensure efficient and effective utilization of personnel and equipment. Receives requests for emergency and unscheduled work and makes arrangements for prompt services.

Assists with hiring, training and developing new and existing talent. Plans daily work schedules. Communicates and coordinate with other departments on repairs and status of equipment.

Makes recommendations as to the cost effectiveness of repairing, replacing or contracting repairs; performs procurement activities for outside repairs and services. Performs quality control to ensure work is correctly and safely completed; inspects work of employees for conformance to work plans and standard operating procedures. Prepares reports, work orders and documentation as required. Responds to customer requests, status updates and complaints; researches issues and resolves as appropriate.

Job Responsibilities:

  • Coordination of Work – Establish and implement effective administrative programs and procedures. Establishes priorities for the completion of work in accordance with sound time management methodology.
  • Actively reviews, monitors and analyzes maintenance activity, work orders and reports for accuracy, trends in repairs, equipment failures and, parts usage; to determine if corrective action is needed to minimize disruption on usage of equipment
  • Responsible for carrying out effective preventative maintenance and repair programs, equipment safety programs and equipment/machinery utilization and service
  • Assist in the selection of new employees; train, supervise and evaluate assigned employees; recommend disciplinary action or promotion as appropriate; review completed work and provide feedback as necessary
  • Actively supports and develops ongoing skill building for Fleet Maintenance Technicians by providing training for skills enhancement.
  • Develops and maintains cooperative and courteous relationships with employees, managers, representatives from other departments and organizations. Shares knowledge with staff for mutual and departmental benefit
  • Ensure compliance with corporate policies, shop rules, environmental and safety procedures and applicable laws
  • Review spare parts/maintenance supply inventory and coordinate with Parts Specialist regarding the purchasing of materials and supplies for maintenance services
  • Be hands on and lead technicians by example
  • Perform additional duties as assigned
