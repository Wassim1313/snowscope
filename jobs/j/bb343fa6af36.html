Job Summary:

The assistant manager of Kirkwood Rentals will oversee operations of two medium-high volume, seasonal rental store locations. The assistant manager is a key holder and acts as manager-on-duty in absence of other management presence. They are responsible for overseeing all aspects of rental operations and directing the day-to-day routine work of a larger group of associates. Responsible for maintaining business standards and has increased decision-making authority. Also, the Assistant Manager fulfills day-to-day work responsibilities for the organization as needed.
