Position Summary

The Slope Maintenance Manager is responsible for the year-round leadership, planning, and execution of all slope maintenance, snowmaking, terrain park, bike park, and vegetation management operations at Solitude Mountain Resort. This position leads a diverse team of supervisors, operators, builders, and forestry professionals to maintain world-class skiing, snowboarding, biking, and outdoor recreation experiences while ensuring the highest standards of safety, operational efficiency, environmental stewardship, and guest satisfaction.

The Slope Maintenance Manager oversees seasonal staffing, budgeting, capital planning, infrastructure development, equipment utilization, project management, and long-term strategic planning for all mountain surface assets and related operations.


Leadership Responsibilities

  • Lead, mentor, and develop year-round and seasonal employees through training, coaching, performance management, and succession planning.
  • Foster a culture of safety, accountability, innovation, teamwork, and continuous improvement.
  • Manage departmental staffing, workforce planning, scheduling, hiring, onboarding, and employee development.
  • Establish operational standards and ensure compliance with all resort, OSHA, state, federal, and environmental regulations.
  • Coordinate closely with Mountain Operations, Ski Patrol, Snowmaking, Lift Operations, Facilities, Events, and Resort Leadership teams.
  • Develop and manage departmental operating and capital budgets.
  • Monitor labor, equipment, fuel, material, and project costs to ensure fiscal responsibility.

Snowmaking Operations & Infrastructure

The Slope Maintenance Manager provides leadership and strategic oversight for all resort snowmaking operations, infrastructure, and planning.


Snowmaking Responsibilities

  • Lead all seasonal snowmaking operations and personnel.
  • Develop snowmaking production plans to support early-season openings and terrain expansion.
  • Manage a gravity-fed snowmaking system and associated distribution infrastructure.
  • Oversee the operation, maintenance, and improvement of three snowmaking pump houses.
  • Monitor water usage, system efficiency, pressures, flow rates, and production capabilities.
  • Coordinate snowmaking operations with grooming, lift operations, patrol, and mountain operations teams.
  • Develop snowmaking staffing plans, operational procedures, and safety standards.
  • Manage startup, shutdown, maintenance, and winterization of all snowmaking infrastructure.

Technical Knowledge and Infrastructure Management

  • Maintain working knowledge of:
    • Water pressure and hydraulic systems
    • Pumping systems and water distribution networks
    • Snowmaking technology and equipment
    • Electrical systems and controls
    • Air and water delivery systems
    • Industrial mechanical systems
  • Coordinate troubleshooting, repairs, preventative maintenance, and system upgrades.
  • Work collaboratively with Facilities and outside contractors on infrastructure projects and system improvements.

Strategic Snowmaking Planning

  • Lead planning efforts for future snowmaking infrastructure development.
  • Identify opportunities for system expansion, efficiency improvements, and increased production capacity.
  • Assist in the development of capital budgets and project scopes.
  • Manage snowmaking construction and infrastructure improvement projects.
  • Support long-range mountain development and master planning initiatives.

Operational & Project Management Responsibilities

  • Develop annual department goals, operating plans, and strategic initiatives.
  • Lead capital improvement projects from planning through implementation.
  • Manage construction, grading, drainage, trail-building, and infrastructure projects.
  • Oversee fleet utilization and equipment replacement planning.
  • Ensure all projects are completed safely, efficiently, and within budget.
  • Participate in emergency response planning and incident management.
  • Drive continuous improvement efforts that enhance operational efficiency and guest experiences.

Qualifications


Required

  • Minimum five years of progressive leadership experience in mountain operations, slope maintenance, snowmaking, construction, forestry, terrain parks, bike parks, or related fields.
  • Lead by example in demonstrating and reinforcing company values, fostering a positive team culture.
  • Demonstrated experience leading large seasonal and year-round teams.
  • Experience operating and managing heavy equipment and mountain infrastructure projects.
  • Strong understanding of snowmaking systems, water distribution, and pump operations.
  • Knowledge of electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, and industrial systems.
  • Experience managing budgets, capital projects, and operational planning.
  • Valid driver's license with an acceptable driving record.

Preferred

  • Degree in Business, Construction Management, Forestry, Recreation Management, Environmental Science, Engineering, or related field.
  • Resort snowmaking management experience.
  • Terrain park leadership experience.
  • Bike park operations experience.
  • Forestry or sawyer certification experience.
  • Incident Command System (ICS) training.
  • Project management certification or experience.

Working Conditions

This position requires the ability to work in a dynamic mountain environment year-round.

  • Regularly work both day and night shifts, particularly during snowmaking operations and storm events.
  • Work outdoors for extended periods in cold, wet, snowy, windy, dusty, and adverse weather conditions.
  • Work on steep, uneven, and challenging mountain terrain.
  • Operate snowmobiles, ATVs, UTVs, snowcats, heavy equipment, and utility vehicles.
  • Respond to emergencies and operational needs outside normal business hours.
  • Lift and carry up to 50 pounds regularly.
  • Perform field work at elevation and in remote mountain locations.
