About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE: Server

LOCATION: Wildflower, located inside the Iron Blosam at Snowbird

STATUS: Seasonal (Winter Only)

SCHEDULE: Shifts vary, may be morning or late evening, weekends, holidays, depending on business levels. THIS POSITION WILL NOT START UNTIL LATE NOVEMBER OR EARLY DECEMBER.

  • Must be able to work any day of the week including weekends and holidays.
  • EMPLOYEE HOUSING IS NOT AVAILABLE.

SUMMARY

A Snowbird Server is expected to maintain Snowbird’s values by performing all required duties in a friendly and professional manner to guests and team members. A Server's responsibility is attending to diners before, during, and after their meals by helping them place, receive, and pay for their orders. They also ensure the timely delivery of food and beverages by checking on their tables periodically throughout the service time.


WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Greeting guests in a professional and friendly manner
  • Assist with setting table to restaurant standards
  • Present menu and specials to each table 
  • Deliver food
  • Resolve guest concerns and questions 
  • Review receipts and assist guests with transactions
  • Help other restaurant positions as needed
  • Communicate with other servers, restaurant managers, and kitchen personnel 

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

  • High School Diploma or equivalent
  • Great listening, speaking, and presentation skills
  • Multitask is a must
  • Strong organizational skills 
  • Job experience is a plus
  • Food Handler Permit and Alcohol Certification
  • 21 years of age 
  • A positive attitude and ability to work well under pressure

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Must be able to work any day of the week including weekends and holidays.  Schedules vary based on business levels.

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Travel to Snowbird Resort
  • Must be able to lift 25 lbs
  • Must be able to stand for long periods of time
  • Shifts may vary from early morning to late evening
  • Work some weekends and holidays depending on business levels
  • Stay overnight in case of road closures

WHY WORK HERE

  • Free ski pass and comp ticket benefits
  • Discounts at Snowbird eateries, retail shops, mountain school, lodging and daycare for employees
  • 401k
  • Discounts with Powdr partners
  • Free transportation to work: UTA Bus, Rideshare Vans, and Employee Blue Shuttles.
