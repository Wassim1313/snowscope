WHAT WILL I BE DOING?

The purpose of this role is to supervise, lead and support the grounds maintenance team with various property maintenance projects across the resort. The Supervisor, Grounds Maintenance is well versed in the routine grounds maintenance duties as well as the assigned work orders and will supervise tasks and projects to time and budget specifications.

  • Provides support and works alongside direct reports to complete property maintenance and waste management operations
  • Leads various maintenance projects
  • Supports with the onboarding of new hires and administers the performance of direct reports (including corrective action)
  • Completes weekly audits of properties to formulate work projects
  • Supports the scheduling and planning of audits and completion of daily tasks
  • Ensures the safety of team members and the following of safety standards
  • Maintains compliance with industry standards by attending upgrade courses and staying current with emerging trends and best practices.

WHAT DO YOU NEED FROM ME?

  • 3 years Landscape and/or Property Maintenance (including Snow Removal) experience is mandatory.
  • Possess a working knowledge of all staff roles and equipment in the Grounds Department.
  • Experience in light and medium sized equipment operation is required
  • Must show competency with using the basic Microsoft Office suite of products (Word, Excel, MS Outlook) as well as the Internet
  • Valid G Drivers License
  • Reliable transportation

BLUE MOUNTAIN RESORT COMPANY OVERVIEW

Blue Mountain Resort is Ontario’s largest mountain resort. Nestled on the shores of Southern Georgian Bay, on the traditional territory of the Anishinabek Nation: The People of the Three Fires known as Ojibway, Odawa, and Pottawatomie Nations. Only 90 minutes outside Toronto, Blue Mountain has become the four-season outdoor destination of the North. Blue Mountain is owned by Alterra Mountain Company.

ALTERRA COMPANY OVERVIEW

Alterra Mountain Company is a family of iconic year-round mountain destinations, the world’s largest heli-skiing operation, and Ikon Pass - the premier ski and snowboard season pass offering access to more than 50 iconic mountain destinations around the world.

Headquartered in Denver, Colorado and born out of a shared love of the mountains and adventure, Alterra Mountain Company exists to shape the future of mountain adventure.
