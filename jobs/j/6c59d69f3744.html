About the job

Overview

This position is responsible for the appearance of the Snowsports School’s fencing, logos, and other on snow media.

Come work and play at Seattle’s home mountain, The Summit at Snoqualmie. Located an hour from Seattle, at the junction where the Pacific Crest Trail crosses Interstate 90. With summer activities ramping up with spectacular Mountain Biking trails and Scenic Lift Rides, there is lots to do all year long.


Responsibilities

Long Term Thinking

  • Evaluate and identify opportunities for improvement in the set-up of the SnowSports outdoor Create, maintain, and repair fencing, poles, signs, etc., as required.
  • Assist daily operations of the SnowSports School to ensure program efficiency and guest service.

Excellence in Execution

  • Set up and tear down fencing, poles, flags, animation, ski racks, etc. as required for operations

Attitude is Everything

  • Provide superior guest service!

Serve First

  • Assist daily operations of the SnowSports School to ensure program efficiency and guest service.
  • Operate surface lifts as required for SnowSports lessons.
  • Other duties as assigned

Qualifications

Required

  • Must be at least 18 years old
  • Ability to work independently with minimum amount of supervision.

Preferred

  • Ability to prioritize within a lively, and often chaotic, environment.
  • Ability to create meaningful, quality experiences for guests and staff.
  • Ability to adapt to changing situations and to deal with deadline pressure while maintaining a professional presentation.
