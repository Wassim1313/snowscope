Position Summary:

Responsible for removal of snow from decks and walkways of all Schweitzer Mountain Resort facilities. Remove trash and cardboard and deposit in compactor and baler and conduct other maintenance functions.


Essential Functions and Major Responsibilities:

  • Operates a snowblower and shovel to remove snow and ice from decks, walkways.
  • Operate trash compactor and cardboard baler.
  • Must present a neat, clean and safe appearance and deal with guests and owners in a pleasant, service-oriented manner.
  • Utility Worker will perform other maintenance duties as needed or as directed by Facility and Grounds Manager.

Secondary Responsibilities:

  • Must have or be willing to obtain extensive knowledge of Schweitzer Mountain Resort facilities and activities.
  • Will initiate guest contact on a daily basis to ensure their comfort, answer questions and provide general assistance whenever possible.
  • Other duties as assigned.

Specific Job Skills:

License/Certifications- Valid Driver’s license preferred.

Technical- None

Physical Capabilities- Able to stand or walk up to 8 hours a day, lift up to 50lbs regularly and up to 100lbs with assistance. Will be expected to be outside in all weather conditions.

Additional- some mechanical knowhow is preferred, able to minor repairs on snowblowers.

Minimum education required - English reading, writing, communication and basic math.
