Job Summary:

The Stevens Pass Uniform Coordinator handles the delivery, inventory, issuance, and return of employee uniforms as well as assists with housing and Employee Center duties. Provides the best possible internal and external guest service to ensure everyone has an Experience of a Lifetime! The role requires guest service skills and supports the Base Area Operations Team as a whole. The position reports to the Uniform Team Lead.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $20.00/hr - $23.00/hr
  • Employment Type: Winter Seasonal 2026/2027
  • Shift Type: Part Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

  • Accurately receives uniforms from the vendor and handles exchanges of uniform items when needed.
  • Issues employee uniforms and nametags
  • Coordinates with laundry vendor, or completes laundry in-house, to maintain the cleanliness of uniforms.
  • Ensures inventory levels are at acceptable levels within the uniform room.
  • Receives uniforms from employees at the end of the season.
  • Works within the inventory management system (software).
  • Provide our employees with a high level of guest service to make for an enjoyable experience.
  • Maintains the Uniform Room and Storage in a welcoming, clean, organized, and orderly fashion.
  • Greet and assist employees with self-service systems.
  • Ability to multi-task in a fast-paced environment with attention to detail while maintaining a positive attitude.
  • Deliver exceptional guest service to internal and external guests.
  • Perform daily sales and close procedures while following company and departmental policies and procedures.
  • Communicate courteously and respectfully to all internal and external guests.
  • Self-starter who can proactively problem-solve when necessary.
  • Other duties as assigned

Job Requirements:

  • High School Diploma or GED
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including fluency in English (both written and verbal)
  • Able to lift and carry up to 50 lbs - Required.
  • Valid driver’s license with a clean record.
  • Adequate proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite and software systems to effectively complete assigned tasks.
  • Organizational and guest service skills, ability, and desire to support resort employees and guests.
