About the job

Overview

Sugarloaf is looking for a hands-on Building Maintenance Supervisor to help lead the team responsible for keeping our resort and managed properties running smoothly and safely. This role starts at an hourly wage of $28-32 depending upon previous leadership/related field experience and offers a career growth path with our resort.

This is a full-time, year-round position that is eligible for benefits, access to seasonal amenities, and many other great job perks.

If you take pride in a job done right, enjoy leading a team, and want steady, meaningful work in a beautiful setting, this role was built for you.


Responsibilities

  • Lead, train, and support a maintenance crew of 5 to 12 team members, adjusting staffing to seasonal demand
  • Schedule and assign staff and equipment to keep work orders moving and service standards high
  • Operate and maintain departmental equipment safely and correctly
  • Prepare estimates and initiate billable maintenance work
  • Inspect ongoing and completed assignments to confirm quality and safety standards are met
  • Oversee resort and HOA maintenance and capital improvement projects
  • Champion a strong safety culture, including Job Hazard Analyses (JHAs) and clear safety communication
  • Support hiring and training of new team members and help build effective systems and processes
  • Step in for the Property Support Manager when needed
  • Maintain professional, cordial relationships with staff, property owners, and guests

Qualifications

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Previous supervisory experience preferred; hands-on building maintenance or field experience preferred
  • Valid driver's license required
  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Office and comfort with email-based communication
  • Familiarity with OSHA general industry and construction safety standards
  • Sound judgment and the ability to adapt procedures to new or unexpected situations
  • Ability to perform physical work on a regular basis, including frequent lifting and carrying (up to 50+ lbs), climbing ladders, and working outdoors in varying weather conditions

Sugarloaf Team Member Benefits include:

  • Free lift pass for skiing and/or riding
  • Discounts at resort F&B and retail locations
  • Access to mid-week golf rounds at our nationally ranked 18-hole Sugarloaf Golf Course
  • Access to resort adventure activities
  • Discounted passes at other Boyne and partner resorts
  • Eligible for Medical/Dental/Vision/Life Insurance for self and family
  • Year round Team Members are also be eligible to earn paid time off and contribute to a 401K program with company match
