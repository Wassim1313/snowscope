POSITION SUMMARY:

Food and Beverage employees at Arapahoe Basin are committed to creating a day worth repeating for our guests, ensuring “Legendary” service and food quality throughout our Food and Beverage outlets.


The F&B Roving Manager at Arapahoe Basin works as a flexible Front of House Manager to help assist other F&B Managers when they need coverage in their F&B outlet.


This position would act as Manager on Duty when needed for the following F&B locations: Legend's Café and 6th Alley Bar & Grill.


At A-Basin, we look to be thoughtful, creative, and energetic and we are known not only for our A-mazing skiing/ riding but also for our A-mazing Food and Beverage options.


We are like a family here at A-Basin. Apply now and join our Food and Beverage Family!

SUPERVISORY DUTIES:

  • Supervise, train, coach, and discipline F&B staff in accordance with A-Basin standards.
  • Present, on-floor manager, covers outlet manager’s days off
  • Works with F&B managers on scheduling and day-to-day management including processing payroll for any F&B Staff in absence of Manager

ESSENTIAL DUTIES:

  • Interacts positively with customers to ensure they are receiving the best food product/experience and works well with all other F&B staff and other A-Basin staff
  • Responsible for open and close of various F&B outlets that serve breakfast and/ or lunch including but not limited to labor management, securing building, and daily reconciliation of registers
  • Monitors staff for service standards set forth my F&B management team in regards to cashiering, bussing, hosting and serving tables
  • Ensures completion of daily and weekly cleaning checklists
  • Ensures upkeep and maintenance of vacuums, drink machines, reach in coolers and other related appliances
  • Works closely with F&B management team to ensure consistent message to staff
  • Holds staff accountable for operational standards including attendance, grooming, task list, and overall service level
  • Help execute group functions in any of the F&B locations including set up, tear down, serving, and collecting payment for event
  • Help to achieve company wide sustainability goals through proper management of daily bussing duties so that all trash, recycling and compost are sorted into the correct receptacle
  • Fulfill duties of Café Team Members and other F&B staff when necessary.
  • Other duties as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Highschool Diploma or Equivalent
  • 2 years management or supervisory experience
  • 2 years cash handling experience
  • Prior F&B experience – preferred
  • Strong verbal communication skills required for guest interaction
  • Multitasking and strong time management skills
  • Adept at problem-solving, including being able to identify issues and resolve problems in a timely manner
  • Flexibility and adaptability are key in this position
  • Must provide own non-slip footwear
  • Must be available to work weekends and holidays

PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS:

  • Use full range of body motion: bend, twist, kneel, push, pull and lift up to 50 lbs.
  • Stand and walk for 8 hours.
  • Able to walk up and down stairs throughout shift.
  • Withstand high altitude conditions at all times.

To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily- reasonable accommodations may be made to enable qualified individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.

WORKING CONDITIONS:

  • You will work in a table service restaurant environment with most time spent working indoors in a high-altitude environment. Some outside work in a cold and snowy environment is possible on any given day. Most work is performed in a highly trafficked area in view of guests. A very frequent amount of high-volume guest interaction is involved in this role.
