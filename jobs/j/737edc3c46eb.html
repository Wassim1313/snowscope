POSITION SUMMARY

Stratton's Events team is looking for team members to join us this winter! The Events Staff is responsible for the setup, breakdown, cleanup, security and execution of resort events, concerts & festivals. This is an occasional position, compensated at $20.00/hr.


ESSENTIAL DUTIES

Event Setup/Breakdown/Cleanup

  • Moving chairs, tables, etc.
  • Setting up movie screen and sound system for movie night
  • Selling popcorn, candy, soda, etc.
  • Possible ‘runners’ for band needs

Event Security Assistance

  • Set up of fencing and other security measures
  • Monitoring exits to ensure guests are not leaving venue with alcohol or entering without a ticket; we will provide all training for any security needs
  • Additional duties required

QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILL AND ABILITY REQUIREMENTS

  • Actively pursuing a high school diploma, or equivalent
  • Previous event work, a plus
  • Previous guest service experience, a plus
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Self-motivated
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment
  • Ability to follow directions and ask any pertinent questions

PHYSICAL DEMANDS AND WORKING CONDITIONS

This position is required to work evenings, weekends and holidays.


To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily with or without reasonable accommodations.
