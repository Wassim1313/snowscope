Job Summary:
This position will assist the housekeeping management staff to achieve the expected level of guest satisfaction. This position will assist with training and mentoring new staff


Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $20.00/hr - $23.00/hr
  • Employment Type: Year Round
  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: Yes


Job Responsibilities:



  • Coordinate needs of department and help delegate work to housepersons, and housekeepers
  • Tracks progress of all housekeeping staff, works closely with housekeeping managers to ensure housekeepers and inspectors are properly updating the housekeeping system and that rooms are being prioritized as they should.
  • Make sure that all daily work and tracking spreadsheets are updated and complete at the end of the day.
  • Verifies all room statuses are correct in the computer at the end of the day and properly follows up on any inconsistencies.
  • Assist with Lost and found – ensure all items handed in at end of day.
  • Responsible for weekly inventory of office closet.
  • Responsible for the daily washing/drying of the housekeeping rags to ensure sufficient supply each morning.
  • Submit work orders, as needed
  • Assist in the housekeeping office with ordering and other paperwork as necessary
  • Help coordinate housekeeping projects and annual deep cleans
  • Promote a safe, happy and healthy work environment
  • Assist as needed in field operations, may include inspecting, preparing pars and inventories
  • Develop relationships and communicate effectively with the Housekeeping staff, Front Desk, Guest Services and Property Managers
  • Conduct induction for new staff on their first day, ensure all documents are signed and Lift training is commenced
  • Direct and be in charge of department when housekeeping managers are not present
  • Other duties as assigned

Job Requirements:

  • Must be able to communicate in English& Spanish (preferred)
  • Ability to work a flexible schedule including holidays, and weekends
  • Must have a US Driver license and pass a license check.
  • Experience in a fast paced and diverse environment
  • Detail oriented, self-motivating and strong communicator, both written & verbal
  • Must be flexible, adaptable, versatile, and responsive to the needs of the housekeeping responsibilities
  • Proficient in Microsoft office – Excel, Word, Powerpoint, Publisher
