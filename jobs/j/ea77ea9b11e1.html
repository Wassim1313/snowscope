POSITION SUMMARY:


The Rental Shop Supervisor supports the daily operation of the Lincoln Peak Rental Shop by providing leadership to staff, ensuring exceptional guest service, and overseeing rental, demo, tuning, and repair activities. This hands-on role helps maintain efficient operations, equipment readiness, safety standards, and a positive team environment while supporting the department's operational and financial goals. This position works from 11/01/2026-05/07/2027. The pay range is between $1,840-$2,000 every two weeks.



RESPONSIBILITIES:


  • Lead and support daily rental, demo, repair, and tuning operations at the Lincoln Peak Rental Shop.
  • Supervise, train, and support a team of 10-20 employees while promoting a safe, productive, and guest-focused environment.
  • Monitor business levels and staffing needs, making operational adjustments to support service and efficiency goals.
  • Resolve guest and employee concerns in a professional and timely manner.
  • Utilize RTP and other rental management systems to support daily operations and accurate transactions.
  • Maintain the readiness, organization, and safety of rental and demo equipment fleets.
  • Perform and oversee equipment inspections, testing, tuning, repairs, and related service work in accordance with company and industry standards.
  • Support daily shop operations, including opening and closing procedures, facility security, and operational readiness.
  • Ensure compliance with all company safety, risk management, and equipment testing requirements.
  • Perform any role within the rental operation as needed to support business demands and deliver exceptional guest service.

QUALIFICATIONS:


  • Must be at least 18 years of age.
  • High school diploma or equivalent required.
  • Previous supervisory or leadership experience required; experience in a rental, retail, service, or repair environment preferred.
  • Previous ski/snowboard tuning, equipment repair, or hard goods experience preferred.
  • Demonstrated mechanical aptitude and ability to work with hand tools and equipment.
  • Strong guest service, communication, and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment while managing multiple priorities.
  • Familiarity with RTP or similar point-of-sale and rental management systems preferred.
  • Must be a skiing or snowboarding enthusiast with a strong understanding of ski resort operations and equipment.
  • Ability to work weekends, holidays, and other periods of peak business demand.
  • Ability to lift and move equipment and merchandise and perform the physical requirements of the position.
