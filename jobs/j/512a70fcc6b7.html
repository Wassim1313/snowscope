Why Work with Us?
  • Are you detail-oriented and organized? Join our team and become a key part of the behind the scenes operations behind our 18 unique restaurants! Your primary responsibility will be entering invoices accurately and efficiently, ensuring that all data is processed correctly. This position requires a keen eye for detail and a strong work ethic. If you're looking for a role that allows you to dive into the backend of a dynamic food and beverage operation, this is the job for you!
  • Adventure Awaits: Explore the stunning Snowshoe Mountain and enjoy all the outdoor activities it has to offer while you work.
  • Make Connections: Meet guests and employees from across the country and around the world and provide them with exceptional hospitality that makes Snowshoe their home away from home.
Job Responsibilities:
  • Accurately enters all invoices in the inventory system
  • Assists warehouse manager by verifying/editing requisitions and completing to transfers
  • Prioritizes tasks/projects and assignments with limited direction
  • Filing, photocopying, laminating, and printing
  • Enters data weekly into the Daily/Weekly/Monthly spreadsheet
  • Reviews statements and obtains any missing invoices
  • Maintains a safe and clean work environment
  • Supports the resort by performing cross-functional duties as required

Driving:
  • Have a valid Driver’s license and ability to pass the Snowshoe Driving Program

Preferred Experience:
  • Strong computer skills/previous administrative field skills
