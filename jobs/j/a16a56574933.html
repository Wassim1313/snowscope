POSITION SUMMARY:

At the Arapahoe Basin Ski and Ride School, we like to have fun while doing great work and hope to instill a love of snow sports in every guest we meet. Likewise, we want our team to have rewarding careers working alongside people they can trust and respect.

As part of our Indoor Support team, you will work with the Ski & Ride School Management Team to create a safe, clean facility and a positive guest experience in a high-energy environment. This is a no-sliding-necessary role within the Ski and Ride School. In this role, you will be managing indoor operations for our Kids Center: a safe place for our kids lessons to take breaks, have snacks, and warm up.

This role assists with the check-in and late arrival procedures for our Kids Club eight-week program. Comfortability outdoors is necessary, but you will not be required to ski or snowboard. This position is a 15-day commitment for the season, structured to support our Kids Club program. This position has an estimated start date of December 5, 2026.

If you are passionate about kids having a great time outdoors with others in one of the most beautiful places on earth, A-Basin is the place for you!
