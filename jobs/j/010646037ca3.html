About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!

We’re currently hiring Lift Operators for the upcoming winter season. These are the front-line pros who keep our mountain moving and our guests safe. If you love working outdoors, being part of a high-energy team, and helping people have the best day ever, this is your moment.

This is a Full-Time, Part-time, Night Operations or Holiday Week help Seasonal position that runs during the winter operating season, with employee housing available starting at $325/month.

Perks:

  • Starting pay: $18/hour

  • Health insurance options for ALL employees

  • Employee housing available – starting at just $325/month

  • Free season pass to Boreal, Soda Springs, and all POWDR resorts

  • Full access to the Bunker—Woodward Tahoe’s indoor training facility with trampolines, foam pits, skateparks, and more

  • Discounts on food, retail, and gear

  • Discounts for friends and family

  • Paid volunteer opportunities

  • Work in a fun, supportive, mountain-town environment

  • Career advancement opportunities across the Woodward and POWDR network

What You’ll Be Doing:

As a Lift Operator, you’ll be responsible for the safe, friendly, and efficient operation of our ski and snowboard lifts. You’ll be the person guests see first in the morning and last on their way off the hill so your energy sets the tone.

Daily Responsibilities:

  • Greet and assist guests in the lift line and at loading/unloading zones

  • Operate aerial and surface lifts according to safety standards

  • Monitor lift function and report any issues to maintenance staff

  • Help manage guest flow, spacing, and ticket/pass checks

  • Maintain a safe work area by regularly clearing snow and keeping ramps clean

  • Provide clear, friendly communication to guests and co-workers

  • Assist with lift opening/closing procedures

  • Jump in to help out with Mountain Operations including Tubing and Ticket Checking

Work Environment & Schedule:

  • Outdoor mountain environment with exposure to snow, wind, cold, and sun

  • Expect early morning, weekend, and holiday shifts

  • Must be able to stand on your feet in snow boots for extended periods

  • Willingness to work in all weather conditions and lift up to 50 lbs

Who We’re Looking For:

  • No prior experience needed—just bring a positive attitude and love for the outdoors

  • Strong communication and people skills

  • Reliable, punctual, and team-oriented

  • Able to follow instructions and safety protocols

  • Comfortable working at altitude in winter conditions

  • Bonus if you ski or ride—but not required!
