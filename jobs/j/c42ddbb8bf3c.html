Job Summary:


As a Lead Driver, it is your responsibility to execute the safe and efficient transportation of both guests and employees to and from designated pickup and drop-off locations, as well as maintain a friendly and positive attitude every day, with all guests and employees. Join our team in providing our guests an Experience of a Lifetime!


Job Specifications:


  • Starting Wage: $25.45/hr - $33.93/hr


  • Employment Type: Winter Seasonal 2026/2027


  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available


  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age


  • Housing Availability: Yes



Job Responsibilities:


  • Leading your team to accomplish daily goals


  • Adheres to all safe driving regulations and standards


  • Directs day to day work within transportation function


  • Enforces policies, procedures, and safety guidelines


  • Addresses safety issues and notifies senior management


  • May resolve guest issues and concerns


  • Coordinates with dispatch supervisor and operations manager and other departments and outside agencies as needed


  • Thoroughly familiar with all areas of transportation and functions


  • Obey all laws of the highway and drive in a defensive manner



  • Fill out daily reports and notify the manager of any bus difficulties


  • Assist passengers to ensure they are properly seated


  • Handles passenger emergencies and disruptions


  • Safely transport small and large groups of people on and off property to various assigned locations


  • Maintain and clean buses


  • Report maintenance problems


  • Install and remove tire chains as needed


  • Other duties as assigned



Job Requirements:


  • Valid US Driver’s License and clean driving record


  • Able to lift up to 50 lbs


  • Valid US Commercial Driver's License, Class A or B with air brake and passenger endorsement (obtained at own expense) - Required


  • 2 years prior experience in CDL required driving position – Required


  • Current Medical Examination Report (valid for 2 years) - Required


  • High School Diploma


  • Must be 21 years of age or older


  • Pre-employment drug test


  • Current DOT medical certificate
