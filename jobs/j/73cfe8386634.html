About the job

Overview

The Sugarloaf is currently seeking a Massage Therapist to join our fun, energetic team for the coming winter season. Massage Therapists are responsible for providing spa services to clients and will work with Front Desk Attendants to manage scheduling.

Applicants for this role must have a valid Maine massage license with some experience and must be able to work weekends and vacation weeks.

This full-time seasonal role comes with great job perks. Come join our team!

Pay Range is $17.00 to $20.00 hourly plus gratuities


Qualifications

Applicants for this position must have a Maine Massage License and must have some prior experience. Applicants must also have the availability to work a flexible schedule based on guest volume which will include weekends and holidays.

Sugarloaf Team Member Benefits include:

  • Free lift pass for skiing and/or riding
  • Discounts at resort F&B and retail locations
  • Discounted ski/snowboard lessons and rentals
  • Access to Nordic trails and discounted equipment rentals at our Outdoor Center location
  • Discounted passes at other Boyne and partner resorts
