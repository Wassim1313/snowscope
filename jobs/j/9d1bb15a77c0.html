About the job

Copper Mountain Resort is a vacation destination located 75 miles west of Denver, Colorado in the heart of the Rocky Mountains. Three vibrant, pedestrian-friendly village areas host lodging, retail outlets, restaurants, bars, and family activities. During the winter months, Copper's world-class terrain offers skiing and riding for all ages and abilities. Each summer, the mountain transforms into a playground for hikers, mountain bikers, and anyone seeking a genuine Colorado experience. Copper Mountain Resort is also home to Woodward Copper action-sports camps, programs, and training facility.

Job Summary:

Responsible for serving provided products according to standards of service while maintaining efficiency, cleanliness, and an accurate accounting of sales. Specifically, provide guests with fast, efficient food and beverage service while making the guest feel as comfortable and relaxed as possible to ensure overall satisfaction with dining experience.

Colorado Tip Credit Notice:

For non-Banquets roles only: This position is eligible for an employer tip credit subject to Colorado and federal law. Your direct cash wage plus tips must equal Colorado minimum wage for each workweek; if they do not – the company will pay the difference in the pay period covering that workweek. You retain all tips you receive, except for amounts contributed to a lawful tip pool or tip-sharing arrangement; the company does not keep your tips. The tip credit applies only while you qualify as a tipped employee and perform tipped work or qualifying directly supporting work. Copper Mountain will pay the applicable full minimum wage for time that is not eligible for the tip credit. A mandatory service charge is not a tip and will not be counted as tip income toward this tip credit. You must accurately and timely report all tips through Copper’s required payroll process (clocking in/out in the correct position, etc.) Reporting requirements do not transfer ownership of your tips to the company.
