Position Summary

The Lift Maintenance Mechanic, Level 1, is the entry-level tier as a full-time, year-round Lift Mechanic. The candidate will be proficient in all the duties of the Lift Maintenance Assistant, as well as being able to guide, direct, and train the Lift Maintenance Assistant in their fundamental responsibilities. The position also requires demonstrated or verified mechanical and low-voltage control system electrical knowledge, safe industrial work practices, general machine or equipment-related maintenance procedures, and the ability to work with minimal supervision. This position is an opportunity to learn a trade that can lead to a sustainable career.

Essential Functions/Major Responsibilities

  • Be proficient in all the duties and responsibilities of the Lift Maintenance Assistant.
  • Guide Lift Maintenance Assistants in the performance of their duties and responsibilities.
  • Safely direct and complete the preparation of a lift for operational use.
  • Be proficient at operating all lifts using main drive and auxiliary drives.
  • Perform weekly and monthly lift inspections, fundamental preventative maintenance tasks, and basic mechanical repairs.
  • Troubleshoot basic mechanical component discrepancies or failures.
  • Be familiar with lift service and maintenance documentation, safety bulletins, drawings and parts catalogues, and industry-specific codes and requirements.
  • Complete work orders and other maintenance-related documentation.
  • Acquire familiarity with general mechanical concepts and principles as related to hydraulics, lubrication, power transmission, and rotating machinery as well as basic electricity.
  • Develop working knowledge of industry-related tasks such as rigging and lifting, general ropeway maintenance principles and procedures, and industrial safety.
  • Operate various over-the-snow and wheeled vehicles.
  • Assist higher-level Lift Maintenance Mechanics with advanced troubleshooting, repairs, and Adjustments.
  • Report all hazards, injuries, or other concerns immediately.
  • Operate all types of vehicles in a safe manner.

Secondary Function

  • Operate lift for passengers.
  • Provide courtesy transportation for guests as dictated by Ski Patrol.

Education/Related Experience

  • Education: High School Diploma and 18 years of age.
  • Minimum Experience: 2 years of verifiable experience as a Lift Mechanic at another ski area or 1 year of experience as a Lift Maintenance Assistant at Mt. Bachelor.
  • Licenses/Certifications: Current & Valid driver’s license.
  • Communication & Professional Capabilities: Ability to analyze and interpret business periodicals, technical procedures, and government regulations. Ability to write reports, business correspondence, and procedural manuals.
  • Technical: Basic mechanical theories; industrial safety proficiency; fundamental troubleshooting principles.
  • Physical Capabilities: Climb ladders; climb on, over, and around structural elements; work with hand tools and power tools; perform necessary work functions while wearing a full body harness or other personal protective equipment; walk on uneven or irregular surfaces.
  • Additional: Communicate clearly and effectively using radio communications; hazard recognition; environmental training.
