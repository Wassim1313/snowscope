Job Summary:
The PC Technician: Coordinator is an entry level, on-site, IT position that provides day-to-day support, maintenance, and assistance for all IT hardware and software at a Vail Resorts location. The PC Tech supports and completes a variety of tasks, including providing end-user support for computer hardware and peripherals, creating and updating documentation, and uses a work ticket management system to track progress and document solutions. The PC Technician works as part of the IT team to proactively identify problems and facilitate solutions, repair hardware and software in a Windows Client/Server LAN environment, and support multiple point of sale systems in a manner that provides an Experience of a Lifetime for Vail Resorts employees and guests. The PC Tech prioritizes safety and guest service while living the company’s core values to deliver excellent IT support.
