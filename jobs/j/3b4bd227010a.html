About the job

Copper Mountain Resort is a vacation destination located 75 miles west of Denver, Colorado in the heart of the Rocky Mountains. Three vibrant, pedestrian-friendly village areas host lodging, retail outlets, restaurants, bars, and family activities. During the winter months, Copper's world-class terrain offers skiing and riding for all ages and abilities. Each summer, the mountain transforms into a playground for hikers, mountain bikers, and anyone seeking a genuine Colorado experience. Copper Mountain Resort is also home to Woodward Copper action-sports camps, programs, and training facility.

Job Title: Base Operations Supervisor


Job Status: Year Round Full Time


Compensation and Benefits:

  • Compensation: Proposed hiring rate of $2,307.69 to $2,500.00+ biweekly amount, actual pay will be adjusted based on experience.
  • Benefits: This position is eligible to enroll in company subsidized medical, dental, vision, flexible spending, HSA, life/disability options; season pass for employees/dependents and privileges at several other resorts. Several discount programs (i.e. food & beverage, retail/rental, tickets, etc.) also available.

Job Summary:

Provides for the day to day supervision, coordination, administration and organization of the Base operations for the Mountain Operations Division to include scheduling, receipt, security, storage and disbursement of available equipment, manpower and materials in order to provide Employees, Guests, Business Units and strategic partners with reliable, safe and timely service aligned with approved goals and objectives.


Essential Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Supervise and oversee the daily operations of the Base & Equipment crews to include general village/ FF & E maintenance functions and equipment operations to insure that assignments are achieved in a timely, safe and effective manner meeting service level expectations.
  • Manage the recruiting, training, retention, termination, evaluation and motivation of all personnel assigned to insure effective, innovative and professional competent operations that align with corporate values and goals.
  • Assist in formulation and implementation of tactical plans to meet service level expectations to include construction/event/maintenance objectives.
  • Maintain proficient understanding of legal and risk management issues as directed by management to insure appropriate levels of understanding and practice exist within assigned areas of responsibility.
  • ​​Contribute to preparation and compliance with department goals and objectives.
  • Provide for the physical maintenance of assigned base facilities, warehouse buildings, assigned equipment & vehicles.
  • Provide a safe work environment, while complying with all state and federal safety regulations, including OSHA, CDOT and local fire codes.
  • Maintain assigned base facilities in a clean, orderly condition.
  • Administer, coordinate and schedule staff assignments in order to achieve timely, safe, efficient, and cost effective project requirements that meet expectations.
  • Compile and appraise special projects in order to provide safe & timely completion within the scope of project’s needs, available manpower & equipment.
  • Monitor and record all appropriate administrative data to include labor, material and supply expense, equipment availability etc. to insure department and project evaluation.
  • Coordinate appropriate intercompany and contract services invoicing.
  • Assist in the preparation, evaluation & administration of project bids, proposals and contracts provided by vendors/contractors for outside services, materials and equipment to ensure company objectives are met on a timely basis within the scope of approved budgets.
  • Assist in accident investigations involving Base Operations resources to determine cause of accidents and makes recommendations to establish a safe environment for employees, guests and homeowners
  • Assist in the development, administration and allocation of expenses associated with department budgets and assigned projects.
  • Maintain effective inter and intra department communications and relations with Business Units to ensure harmonious and effective operations.
  • Maintain effective communication with outside agencies and associations to ensure company objectives are accomplished to include the Village Company, Metro District, County Road & Bridge, Xcel Energy, Sheriff, State Patrol, Department of Transportation and State Highway Department, etc.
  • Conduct required safety courses and maintain safety attendance records
