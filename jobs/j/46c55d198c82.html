Job Summary:

At Stevens Pass, reliable drinking water is essential to every aspect of resort operations, from guest services and lodging facilities to food and beverage operations, employee housing, and private cabins. The Lead Water Treatment Plant Operator reports to the Primary Water Treatment Plant Operator is the second-in-command, helping ensure safe, compliant, and uninterrupted water service across all resort public water systems.

This position supports the management, operation, maintenance, monitoring, regulatory compliance, and continuous improvement of Stevens Pass's potable water treatment and distribution infrastructure, including two Group A public water systems and one Group B public water system. The Lead Water Treatment Plant Operator provides day-to-day operational leadership, is a technical resource for water system operations, water quality management, preventative maintenance, emergency response, and regulatory reporting, and acts on behalf of the Primary Water Treatment Plant Operator when delegated or during their absence.

The ideal candidate is a self-motivated utility professional with strong mechanical and troubleshooting skills, proven leadership ability, and a commitment to public health and safety. This individual can effectively mentor and lead a small team, make sound operational decisions, and thrive in a dynamic mountain resort environment.

Job Responsibilities:

  • Water System Operations
  • Serve as a certified operator responsible for the safe and reliable operation of all Stevens Pass potable water systems.
  • Operate and monitor water treatment equipment including pumps, motors, valves, filters, reservoirs, storage tanks, chemical feed systems, well infrastructure, and distribution system assets.
  • Monitor and control treatment and distribution systems through SCADA and related monitoring technologies.
  • Conduct routine inspections of water treatment facilities, storage reservoirs, distribution infrastructure, pumping systems, and utility assets.
  • Analyze system performance and implement operational adjustments to optimize treatment efficiency, water quality, and system reliability.
  • Lead troubleshooting and repair efforts involving treatment, pumping, storage, and distribution system equipment.
  • Support and monitor compliance with Washington State Department of Health drinking water regulations and escalate compliance concerns to the Primary WTPO.
  • Collect and analyze water samples and conduct required testing including chlorine residual, pH, turbidity, temperature, and other regulatory parameters.
  • Maintain accurate operational records, water quality logs, maintenance records, and regulatory documentation.
  • Compile operational data and assist with preparation of compliance reporting and other regulatory reports and filings.
  • Coordinate with laboratories, consultants, and regulatory agencies regarding water system operations and compliance matters.
  • Follow Washington Department of Heath Water Quality Monitoring Schedule, and deliver samples for processing.
  • Maintain readiness for inspections, audits, sampling requirements, and regulatory reviews.
  • Lead response efforts during utility failures, severe weather events, water quality issues, and operational emergencies.
  • Partner with departments across the resort to ensure reliable utility service and support the One Resort philosophy.
  • Perform preventative and corrective maintenance on pumps, motors, controls, valves, chemical feed systems, plumbing infrastructure, and treatment equipment.
  • Coordinate and execute preventative maintenance schedules established by the Primary WTPO.
  • Assist in identifying capital repair, replacement, and infrastructure improvement needs.
  • Provide field leadership during maintenance and repair activities.
  • Provide training, guidance, and operational support to Operator-in-Training personnel and entry-level operators.

Job Requirements:

  • High School Diploma or GED equivalent.
  • WA State Water Treatment Plant Operator Level 1 (WTPO 1) certification; must be able to obtain WA State WTPO 2 within 12 months of employment.
  • WA State Water Distribution Manager (WDM 1) certification; must be able to obtain WA State WDM 2 within 12 months of employment.
  • Minimum three (3) years of experience operating or maintaining potable water treatment and distribution systems.
  • Working knowledge of water treatment processes, pumping systems, water storage facilities, distribution systems, and utility infrastructure.
  • Experience utilizing SCADA systems, process controls, telemetry systems, and computerized recordkeeping platforms.
  • Understanding of Washington State drinking water regulations, compliance requirements, and sampling programs.
  • Strong mechanical aptitude and ability to diagnose and troubleshoot operational and equipment issues.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical manuals, schematics, engineering drawings, and operational procedures.
  • Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and commitment to public health and safety.
  • Valid driver's license and ability to pass a Motor Vehicle Records Check.
  • Ability to work independently and make sound operational decisions.
  • Ability to regularly lift up to 50 pounds and occasionally lift up to 75 pounds.
  • Ability to work outdoors year-round in varying weather and mountain conditions.
  • Ability to participate in emergency response and after-hours utility support as required

Preferred Skills & Experience:

  • Previous experience in maintenance, mechanical systems, utilities operations, facility operations, construction trades, or other hands-on technical environments preferred but not required.
  • Experience working with pumps, motors, plumbing systems, mechanical equipment, automated control systems, or SCADA systems preferred.
