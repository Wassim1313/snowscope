About the job

Overview

Sugarloaf Base Services is currently looking for a full-time, year-round maintenance technician to join our team. For this role, we are looking for a reliable team player with a knowledge of general carpentry, painting, and proper cleaning methods. Base Services Techs maintain our Base Lodge facility and the surrounding Food and Beverage outlet areas at our resort. While this role will be maintenance focused, it may require custodial tasks at times.

The ideal candidate for this position would be timely, detail oriented, willing to learn, and have a positive attitude. This position is eligible for shift meals and many other great benefits.

We have one full-time, year-round position and full-time seasonal spot available at this time and will provide on-the-job training. Starting wage is anticipated to be $18-20/hour depending upon experience.

Come join our team!


Responsibilities

  • Perform routine building maintenance and repair tasks throughout the Base Lodge, such as; basic carpentry, painting, staining, replacing light bulbs, and minor drywall repair as needed.
  • Complete scheduled monthly inspections and facility checks in a timely manner.
  • Organize and prioritize daily tasks, managing time across multiple projects.
  • Assist with routine custodial duties, including vacuuming, sweeping, mopping, windows and bathroom cleaning, dusting, and trash removal.
  • Setup and breakdown of conference spaces for concerts, meetings, and other special events.
  • Maintain indoor and outdoor areas, including seasonal preparations for winter operations, maintaining entrances and outdoor areas, assisting with snow and ice removal, sanding and salting as assigned.
  • Help ensure the safety and comfort of guests, and employees in the base lodge.
  • Complete other duties, maintenance projects, and assignments as directed by the manager or supervisor.

Qualifications

The right fit for this role will:

  • be a self-starter
  • be willing to work days, nights, weekends and holidays as needed
  • be able to lift up to 40+ lbs in order to complete tasks
  • have own transportation

Sugarloaf Team Member Benefits include:

  • Free lift pass for skiing/riding and access to resort adventure activities
  • Discounts at resort F&B and retail locations
  • Access to Nordic trails and discounted equipment rentals at our Outdoor Center location and midweek golf rounds at our nationally ranked course
  • Discounted passes at other Boyne and partner resorts
  • Full-time, year-round positions are eligible for medical/dental/vision/life insurance benefits and paid time off
