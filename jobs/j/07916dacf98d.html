Job Summary:

The Assistant Manager, Ski & Ride School (Competition Services & Administration) supports the overall operation of the Ski & Ride School through leadership of staff, oversight of competition services, and management of departmental administrative functions. This position assists in delivering exceptional guest experiences, maintaining operational excellence, supporting employee development, and ensuring safe and efficient program operations. The role coordinates race and competition programming, staffing, scheduling, budgets, payroll support, and operational resources while serving as a key liaison between guests, athletes, coaches, parents, and resort departments. The Assistant Manager works closely with Ski & Ride School leadership and other resort departments to achieve business objectives and support department growth.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $52,000.00 - $67,267.00
  • Employment Type: Winter Seasonal 2026/2027
  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

  • Support the Ski & Ride School Manager in the daily operation of the department, ensuring exceptional guest service, operational efficiency, and adherence to company policies and procedures.
  • Lead, coach, develop, schedule, and support supervisors, instructors, competition coaches, and support staff.
  • Oversee daily operations of competition services, including race programs, training groups, athlete registration, attendance tracking, and event logistics.
  • Coordinate race schedules, training venues, course preparation, equipment needs, and event execution.
  • Assist with recruiting, onboarding, training, and performance management of departmental staff.
  • Resolve guest concerns and complaints using company values and established service recovery practices.
  • Manage administrative functions including payroll support, staffing coordination, record keeping, reporting, equipment inventory, and program documentation.
  • Assist with departmental budgeting, labor management, and expense control to support financial goals.
  • Maintain a strong culture of safety by ensuring compliance with resort policies, risk management practices, and safety standards for guests, athletes, and employees.
  • Collaborate with Ski Patrol, Mountain Operations, Marketing, and other resort departments to support instruction programs, competition services, and departmental initiatives.
