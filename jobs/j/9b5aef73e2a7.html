About the job

Overview

Sugarloaf is looking for a service and task-oriented person to join our IT team as an Information Systems Coordinator (ISC). A Sugarloaf ISC works daily with team members and guests to provide excellent service as they maintain and monitor the computer systems and networks for our resort. This full-time, year-round position is eligible for benefits, paid time off, and other great job perks. We offer a beautiful work environment, excellent quality of life, and a solid team. If you are seeking a lifestyle change and have previous IT experience, we have a place for you. Come join our team!

The primary functions of this role are to:

  • provide desktop PC support to employees and customers
  • maintain voice, data, and TV cabling infrastructure (indoor and outdoor); repair or replace damaged wiring; install and terminate wiring to support renovation, relocation, or new construction
  • provide direct guest support for guest room issues, including but not limited to: TV, phone and internet
  • use vendor support to resolve difficult problems
  • provide users with basic instruction
  • perform and maintain an inventory of tools and equipment; maintain stocks of consumable supplies
  • perform additional tasks as assigned by supervisor

The use of a personal vehicle for travel within the Sugarloaf area is required. Mileage reimbursement will be provided.

On-site work is required with the option of some remote capabilities.

Wages are dependent upon experience and will start between $24-30 per hour with the potential for increase based on performance and experience.


Qualifications

The ideal candidate for this role will:

  • have a minimum of a High School diploma or equivalent.
  • be regularly available for weekend shifts and for evening shifts as needed during scheduled maintenance or in case of emergency.
  • have at least 2 years of related work experience (preferred, but willing train the right person).
  • be A+ Certified Professional (or be able to obtain certification in a short period of time).
  • have advanced knowledge of network cabling, T568A/B wiring standards, and cable count color codes.
  • be proficient with Microsoft Windows and Microsoft Office applications.
  • be able to learn the operation and maintenance of complex PBX telephone systems.
  • have basic knowledge of networking.
  • be able to critically think and use various resources, including vendor support, reference manuals, internet resources, and/or documentation to solve problems.
  • have a current, valid driver’s license and a functioning vehicle with current registration and insurance.
  • be able to ski or snowboard at or above the beginner level to perform on-mountain tasks.

Sugarloaf Team Member Benefits include:

  • Free lift pass for skiing and/or riding
  • Discounts at resort F&B and retail locations
  • Discounted ski/snowboard lessons and rentals
  • Access to Nordic trails and discounted equipment rentals at our Outdoor Center location
  • Discounted passes at other Boyne and partner resorts
  • Eligibility for benefits such as medical/dental/vision/life insurances, paid time off, 401K with company match
