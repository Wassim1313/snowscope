About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!



The Parking Attendant is an essential member of the Boreal team as they greet customers as they arrive in their vehicles. With a friendly welcome you will work as part of a team to provide the orderly, efficient, and smooth arrival and departure of those vehicles in Boreal parking lots according to established parking plan and resort parking procedures. If you are a looking for a part-time position who can contribute to a positive work environment we want to hear from you!


The Perks:


  • Compensation: Hiring rate of $19
  • Health insurance options for ALL employees
  • Complimentary access to most POWDR owned resorts (Skiing, Riding and more!)  
  • Full access to our world-class facilities. Work and play in the same innovative environments as professional and Olympic athletes.
  • Friends & family discounts and complimentary passes. 
  • Discounts on food, retail, and gear!
  • Enough free time to enjoy the Woodward lifestyle.
  • Career development and growth opportunities both here and at our facilities across the globe.
  • A connection with people that share the same passions and embrace each other's unique and individual stye

The Job:


  • Provide a friendly welcoming attitude and behavior as you execute your responsibilities. 
  • Maintain a positive attitude dedicated to overall guest, employee, and management satisfaction.
  • Safety awareness is a top priority at all times. 
  • Affect the first and last impression as well as the experience, safety and satisfaction of every single guest that visits.

 
What we’re looking for:



  • Able to work outdoors in inclement weather
  • Able to work and communicate with fellow staff and the public in a friendly and courteous manner
  • Ability to work positively with fellow staff members
  • Safety awareness at all times, for yourself, other staff, and guest
