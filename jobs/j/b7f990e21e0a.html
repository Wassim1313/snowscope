About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE: Spa Attendant (Female Only)

LOCATION: Cliff Spa, Cliff Lodge

STATUS: Hourly, Part-time, Year-Round

SCHEDULE: 12:30PM - 9PM - Thurs-Monday

  • Female only. This position works in a women's locker room during business hours.
  • This is a janitorial position.
  • Year-round.

SUMMARY

As a Spa Attendant at the Cliff Spa, you will clean and maintain the Cliff Spa to the highest of industry standards with emphasis on sanitation.


WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Clean all areas of the Cliff Spa, providing outstanding cleanliness standards in the following areas; locker rooms, (showers, toilets, floors, etc.), treatment rooms, specialty rooms, (sauna, steam room, etc.), salon, lobbies, offices, pool deck, etc.
  • Work in a women's locker room during business hours in a spa environment.
  • Identify, correct and/or report any maintenance deficiencies.
  • Commit to safe work practices and identifies areas of concern.
  • Maintain a high standard of work quality and productivity.
  • Provide outstanding guest service.
  • Demonstrate competency in problem solving and initiation of activities within job scope and responsibility limitations.
  • Ensure that all equipment and supplies are adequate and in good working order.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

  • High School Diploma or equivalent preferred.
  • This is a female-only position. You will be working during business hours in a women's locker room/showers/sauna/spa environment.
  • 0-6 months of job-specific work experience with demonstrated competence.
  • Must be at least 18 years of age.
  • No certifications needed for this position.
  • Responsible for safe use of chemicals, vacuum, floor machines and other cleaning equipment.
  • Follows safe practices on the job and property and reports any safety hazards or concerns to a supervisor.
  • May be required to assist with minor office duties, i.e. inventory, photocopying, etc.

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Part-time, year-round
  • 12:30PM - 9PM - schedule may vary
  • Schedule will change for Winter

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Must be prepared to stay overnight if necessary on occasional times of road closures.
  • May experience unusual stress from high business volumes, deadlines, continual work with public, winter conditions, snow night requirements and commuting to/from Snowbird.
  • Must be able to lift 35 lbs.
  • Must be able to stand for long periods of time.
  • Must demonstrate ability to bend, kneel, handle, reach, grasp and perform repetitive motions.
  • Work is subject to frequent interruptions making continuity and accuracy difficult, frequent exposure to noise.
  • Other/Additional: Daily work with cleaning chemicals may present some hazards. Must use care and caution with chemicals and require the same from staff.
  • Direct and continuous contact with guests.
  • Direct and continuous contact with co-workers, management, and other departments.
  • Must have the ability to interact at any time with guests and other employees in a pleasant manner and maintain good working relationships with others and all departments at Snowbird.

WHY WORK HERE

  • Season pass and comp ticket benefits
  • Discounts at Snowbird eateries, retail shops, mountain school, lodging and daycare for employees
  • Discounts with POWDR destinations and resorts.
  • Free transportation to work: UTA Bus, Rideshare vans, Canyon Transportation vans, and Employee Shuttles.

Spa-Specific Discounts

  • Complimentary spa access that includes use of pool, hot tub, fitness center, steam room, and sauna
  • Complimentary yoga classes
  • 30% discount on retail items
  • $10 friend pass

Snowbird’s renovated Cliff Spa features 21 treatment rooms, a newly designed yoga studio and an upgraded fitness center, both with mountain views. The Cliff Spa has a rooftop pool and hot tub with sensual surroundings, radiant heated decks and gas-fed fire pits with increased seating capacity. With incredible views and a eucalyptus-infused environment, the solarium is the perfect place to relax. The boutique offers a range of unique Cliff Spa items, high-end yoga apparel and holistic skincare. Located on the 9th and 10th floors of The Cliff Lodge, The Cliff Spa delivers the ultimate spa experience with amenities that match the mountain.
