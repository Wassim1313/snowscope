About the job

Year RoundPalisades TahoeWe share the spirit of these legendary mountains with the world.


To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential duties.

Job Summary: Provide leadership and support for all Contact Center employees. Be able to resolve issues regarding all products and systems. This will require an extensive knowledge of over twelve different systems, numerous products across all lines of business and the ability to conduct sales and service on computers over the phone when business levels demand. Deliver exceptional guest service to the internal and external guest.


Applicants must be 18 years of age.

Essential Job Responsibilities/Duties/Tasks include the following--other duties may be assigned:

  • Provide customers with top quality customer service in a friendly and positive manner
  • Oversee the activities of Contact Center agents. Provide exceptional guest service with a courteous and respectful manner towards all internal and external guests via telephone. Must be able to perform all duties of a Contact Center Agent
  • Understand and develop an in-depth knowledge of the products sold and how to accurately book and modify a reservation. These product categories include, but are not limited to, lodging, season passes, daily tickets, adult and child ski school, and resort activities and events
  • Assist with training for Contact Center staff
  • Provide phone, email and live chat support when business levels demand
  • Assist with daily open/close procedures according to company policy and procedures
  • Contribute and ensure the completion of day-to-day operational tasks, including reporting, attendance and troubleshooting of systems
  • Assist in the resolution of all guest complaints in accordance with company policy and procedures. Handle guest concerns and communicate when needed
  • Assist with developing, implementing, and maintaining standard operating procedures which support overall company policies and goals
  • Communicate in a courteous and respectful manner to all internal and external guests
  • Supply back-up for data reporting to Alterra Mtn. Co. and internal requests. This may include incentive and other employee related reporting
  • Support all corporate visions, goals and objectives
  • Adhere to all departmental and company policies and procedures and participate in all departmental and resort training and development courses

Competencies and Job Requirements:

Required:

  • Ability to communicate effectively—both written and verbal--across all levels of the organization
  • Possess excellent organizational and problem-solving skills with the ability to handle multiple tasks
  • Ability to establish and maintain effective working relationships and interact with others. Teamwork builder
  • Possess a proficient knowledge of computers and systems including MS Office (Word, Excel, Outlook)
  • Have a neat & clean appearance in compliance with the company grooming standards
  • Be punctual, able to report at scheduled start time
