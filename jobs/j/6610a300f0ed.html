ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Provide a safe, healthy, and secure environment with an emphasis on children’s social-emotional, physical, and cognitive needs
  • Perform daily child care duties as assigned
  • Help in other classrooms as needed
  • Ensure compliance with all legal requirements
  • Register in the PDIS and complete all required trainings including 15 hours of continuing education/year. Provide documentation to Director
  • Maintain confidentiality
  • Be prepared to engage children in outdoor activities for long periods of time, sometimes in inclement weather
  • Attend all staff meetings and trainings
  • Work cooperatively on a team with other teaching professionals
  • Provide positive guidance and redirection
  • Implement behavior plans for children with challenging behavior
  • Maintain professional communication with parents and other staff
  • Support children and families with potty training if applicable
  • Attend back-to-school night
  • Compliance with all company policies and procedures as well as any regulatory requirements
  • Attend monthly staff meetings on the first Thursday of each month
  • Other duties as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • Knowledge of child development and willingness to stay up to date with current best practices
  • Must demonstrate an interest in young children and a concern for their proper care and well-being.
  • Flexibility to care for children of all ages birth-5 years
  • Skills and ability to inspire and lead children through a large variety of indoor and outdoor activities
  • Willingness to engage children in outdoor activities for long periods of time, sometimes in inclement weather
  • Ability to maintain professional communication with parents, supervisors, co-teachers, and assistants
  • Ability to work cooperatively with a teaching team to create a safe and cognitively stimulating environment
  • Experience in environmental education or nature-based preschools preferred
  • Routine sanitizing and disinfecting of toys and surfaces
  • Must be able to work in a busy and sometimes stressful atmosphere while maintaining composure and good communication skills
  • Must be flexible and willing to work with children of all ages 0-5 and abilities in inclusive classrooms
  • Must be able to work for extended periods of time standing, walking, kneeling, talking, and sitting on the floor
  • Must be able to register in the PDIS and complete a minimum of 15 clock hours of continuing education each year
  • 2 years of previous work history/education preferred
  • Communicate clearly in English, both verbal / non-verbal, and written
  • Interact and maintain positive relationships with guests, co-workers and management consistent with Steamboat Ski and Resort Company’s Service Excellence standards

Must meet the qualifications for an Assistant Early Childhood Teacher. An Assistant Early Childhood Teacher must meet one of the following qualifications:

1. A minimum of nine months (1,365 hours) of verified experience in the care and supervision of four (4) or more children less than eight (8) years of age who are not related to the individual. Assistant Early Childhood Teachers must be enrolled in and attending the second (2nd) Early Childhood Education course, which will be used as the basis for their qualification for the position of Early Childhood Teacher and Completion of one of the following Early Childhood Education courses with a course grade of “C” or better:

  • (1) Introduction to Early Childhood Professions; or,
  • (2) Early Childhood Guidance Strategies for Children or has been issued a Colorado Pyramid Model Training certificate of completion;

2. Completion of two (2) of the department approved Early Childhood Education courses

3. A current Early Childhood Professional Credential Level I or higher in PDIS

  • Ability to maintain a calm demeanor under stressful situations
