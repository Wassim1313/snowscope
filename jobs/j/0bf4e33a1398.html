POSITION SUMMARY

The Sommelier provides wine expertise, operational leadership, and a commitment to elevated hospitality to enhance service quality of Stratton Mountain Club’s Food & Beverage operations. Through a visible leadership presence and a commitment to refined, attentive service, the Sommelier upholds the highest standards of quality. This is a full-time seasonal position, with a starting wage of $20.00-$22.00/hour + commission.


ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Oversees the wine program for all outlets at the club ensuring consistency in quality, presentation, service, and overall execution
  • Development, implementation, & training of wine program
  • Responsible for all tableside wine service and selling in all F&B outlets
  • Tracks inventory and updates circulating wine list as needed
  • Maintain an active presence on the floor, providing hands-on mentoring, coaching and support to ensure seamless service execution across all outlets
  • Communicates and builds rapport with returning high-end clientele
  • Contribute to a positive, collaborative, and accountable work environment that supports employee engagement and professional development.
  • Grow member participation in the wine program through tastings, featured selections, special events and other engaging experiences.
  • Works in conjunction with the F&B Manager and Executive Chef to ensure Stratton high standards of food quality are being upheld
  • Takes an active part in weekly SMC meetings to ensure accurate forecasting
  • Works with F&B Manager and Executive Chef to identify areas of opportunity for future growth and development
  • Additional duties as assigned

QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILL AND ABILITY REQUIREMENTS

  • 3+ years of experience working closely with wine, building a wine program, and/or selling wine
  • Experience as a leader of with emphasis on coaching and training
  • Exceptional interpersonal skills
  • Self-motivated, resourceful, organized, detail oriented, enthusiastic, outgoing and reliable
  • Microsoft Office and F&B systems experience, a plus
  • Creative problem-solving skills
  • Proven track record in creating and maintaining a courteous, friendly, professional team environment
  • Forward thinking with a willingness to adapt, learn and take on new responsibilities
  • Ability to work weekends and holidays

PHYSICAL DEMANDS AND WORKING CONDITIONS

This position may be required to work evenings, weekends and holidays.
