COLLABORATION |AUTHENTICITY | PURPOSE | EMPOWERMENT

Alterra Mountain Company is a family of iconic year-round mountain destinations, the world’s largest heli-skiing operation, and Ikon Pass - the premier ski and snowboard season pass offering access to more than 50 iconic mountain destinations around the world. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado and born out of a shared love of the mountains and adventure, Alterra Mountain Company exists to shape the future of mountain adventure.

Alterra Experiences division is an operating unit of Alterra Mountain Company. The Experiences division is dedicated to building Alterra’s adventure hospitality operations and maximizing the impact of bringing together the world’s two largest heli-skiing businesses – CMH Heli-Skiing & Summer Adventures (“CMH”) & Mike Wiegele Helicopter Skiing (“MWHS”). The Experiences team structure ensures operational focus, while providing shared services functionality supporting growth of both CMH & MWHS.

CMH - With 60 years of experience, CMH has evolved into the world’s largest Heli-Skiing and Heli-Hiking company, operating 11 lodges in the winter and 3 in the summer throughout British Columbia, Canada. The business’s central office is in Banff, Alberta, Canada.

MWHS - Has 50+ years of experience and operates the first and only heli-ski village resort. Located in Blue River, BC, MWHS features 22 exclusive log chalet accommodations plus village amenities and provides access to more than 1.5 million acres of terrain and over 100 peaks.

POSITION SUMMARY

CMH and MWHS are category-defining brands operating in an ultra-premium, experience-led market. The guests we serve and aspire to reach require a high level of marketing sophistication, cultural fluency, and brand stewardship. Our Performance Marketing Manager is a cross-functional role responsible for CMH’s email marketing program and paid media efforts which are executed through Alterra's media agency. This position will play a pivotal role in bringing MWHS onto the Alterra Marketing Technology stack.


Working within a portfolio-based marketing team, you'll drive acquisition and nurture-based programs for CMH guests and prospects. Through cross-team collaboration with our Sales, Guest Services and Finance teams, you’ll help shape strategic planning, and budgeting decisions for demand generation with the reporting and insight to back them up. This is a hands-on program management role, focused on executing without needing day-to-day oversight, while navigating the realities of a corporate parent company structure, including disconnected data across both new and legacy technology platforms.


ESSENTIAL DUTIES

Performance Marketing

  • Define paid media goals, audiences, and targeting priorities for CMH, and recommend and manage budget allocation, working with the Marketing leadership team and media agency, expanding to MWHS over time.
  • Strong strategic capabilities for navigating the most impactful landing destinations across our digital web properties that considers audience expectations, channel performance, path to conversion, and brand platform goals.
  • Partner with Brand & Content leads, expertly pairing channel needs with seasonal creative strategy and production workflows.
  • Brief and direct the Alterra media agency that executes campaigns across search, social, and display.
  • Review agency performance against goals including pacing, CAC, and targeting.
  • Balance collaboration and accountability with agency partner to ensure you’re meeting marketing team and core business KPIs.

SEO & Organic Search

  • Protect and grow the search rank of all web properties through keyword research, competitor analysis, AI and organic search optimization, and on-page optimization recommendations.

Email, Lifecycle & CDP

  • Build and run automated personalized email and lifecycle flows including welcome, inquiry/abandoned-booking nurture, trip communication, win-back, and repeat-guest/referral.
  • Build audience strategy for the marketing ESP (Insider One) and support the sales organization in their CRM marketing efforts. This requires strong business data literacy.
  • Run A/B tests and monitor deliverability, sender reputation, and list health.
  • Monitor list hygiene, data management, and privacy compliance for guest data in the CDP.
  • Serve as liaison for email and database strategy within the Alterra network.
  • Support the CMH CDP transition to Hightouch and help to build the roadmap to in bringing MWHS onto the same Martech stack over time.

Collaboration & Reporting

  • Build strong partnerships across the business, agency partners, and Alterra shared services teams. A high level of emotional intelligence and diplomacy is required to be successful long-term in this role.
  • Map and refine the end-to-end customer journey across paid, SEO, and email, closing gaps where prospects drop off.
  • Track KPIs (ROI, CPA, CPC, conversion rate, CTR) and build reporting that ties spend and sends to leads for the commercial team.
  • Stay current on emerging AI-driven discovery and ensure the agency is accounting for it.
