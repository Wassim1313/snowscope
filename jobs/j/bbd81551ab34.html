Job Summary:

The Senior Manager, Fleet Maintenance is responsible for leading all fleet maintenance operations across the resort, overseeing a diverse inventory of buses, trucks, rubber-tired vehicles, snowcats, grooming equipment, snowmobiles, UTVs, snow-removal equipment, and specialty mountain equipment. The role is accountable for ensuring fleet reliability, asset preservation, regulatory compliance, preventive maintenance programs, capital planning, and team development. This position directly supervises two Assistant Managers who oversee separate maintenance facilities:

    • Upper Shop: Snowcats, grooming equipment, snowmobiles, UTVs, and specialized mountain vehicles.
    • Lower Shop: Buses, trucks, fleet vehicles, snow-removal equipment, and other rubber-tired equipment.

The Senior Manager partners with Mountain Operations, Transportation, Grooming, Public Safety, and other operational teams to ensure fleet assets are available and performing at the highest levels throughout the year.

Job Responsibilities:

Leadership & Team Development

  • Lead, coach, and develop Assistant Managers, Lead Technicians, Mechanics, and support staff across two maintenance facilities.
  • Foster a culture of safety, accountability, continuous improvement, and operational excellence.
  • Support recruiting, training, succession planning, and performance management initiatives.
  • Establish clear performance expectations and development opportunities for maintenance teams.

Fleet Operations & Maintenance

  • Oversee preventive and corrective maintenance programs for all fleet assets, including buses, trucks, snowcats, grooming equipment, snowmobiles, UTVs, and other specialty vehicles.
  • Ensure fleet readiness to support daily mountain and transportation operations.
  • Monitor equipment utilization, reliability, downtime, and maintenance costs to drive operational efficiency.
  • Establish maintenance standards, inspection protocols, and repair procedures that maximize equipment lifespan and performance.

Multi-Shop Oversight

  • Provide strategic direction and operational oversight for both the Mid-Mountain and Lower Shop maintenance facilities.
  • Coordinate staffing, resources, inventory, and work priorities across locations.
  • Ensure consistency in maintenance practices, documentation, and safety standards between shops.
  • Promote collaboration and communication between teams to support seasonal operational demands.

Safety & Regulatory Compliance

  • Ensure compliance with all applicable federal, state, local, and company safety regulations.
  • Maintain adherence to DOT requirements, vehicle inspection standards, environmental regulations, and manufacturer maintenance recommendations.
  • Lead incident investigations, corrective actions, and continuous safety improvement initiatives.
  • Promote safe work practices and maintain a strong safety culture throughout the department.

Asset Management & Capital Planning

  • Develop and manage long-term fleet replacement strategies and lifecycle planning.
  • Identify capital improvement opportunities and support equipment procurement decisions.
  • Manage fleet maintenance budgets, operating expenses, parts inventories, and vendor contracts.
  • Utilize maintenance management systems and data analytics to improve fleet performance and cost effectiveness.

Vendor & Parts Management

  • Establish and maintain relationships with equipment manufacturers, dealers, and service vendors.
  • Oversee warranty programs, outsourced repairs, and vendor performance.
  • Ensure effective parts inventory management to support operational readiness while controlling costs.
  • Negotiate service agreements and procurement opportunities that deliver value to the
    organization.

Cross-Department Collaboration

  • Partner with Transportation, Grooming, Mountain Operations, and Resort Leadership to align fleet maintenance priorities with operational needs.
  • Support seasonal planning efforts and operational readiness initiatives.
  • Serve as the primary fleet maintenance representative for strategic planning, capital projects, and operational improvement efforts.
  • Ensure equipment availability supports guest experience, employee safety, and business objectives.

Performance & Continuous Improvement

  • Establish key performance indicators (KPIs) for fleet reliability, maintenance compliance, safety, and cost management.
  • Analyze maintenance trends and identify opportunities for process improvements.
  • Drive initiatives that improve equipment uptime, technician productivity, and operational efficiency.
  • Champion innovation, best practices, and technological advancements within fleet maintenance operations.

Job Requirements:

  • 8+ years of progressive leadership experience.
  • Demonstrated experience managing budgets, capital plans, purchasing, inventory, and vendor relationships.
  • Strong change leadership, communication, organizational, and problem-solving skills.

Success Measures
