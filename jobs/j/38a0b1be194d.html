Job Summary:
The Executive Chef is responsible for the overall leadership, management, and execution of all culinary back of house operations at Keystone Lodge & Spa. This position oversees Bighorn Bistro & Bar, Lakeside Cafe, the full-service breakfast operation, and banquet functions. The Executive Chef ensures exceptional guest experiences through culinary excellence, financial stewardship, team development, and operational leadership.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $58,859.00 - $84,084.00 + annual bonus
  • Employment Type: Year Round
  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: Yes

Job Responsibilities:

  • Culinary leadership, menu development, recipe standardization, and quality assurance across all outlets.
  • Direct day-to-day culinary operations for Bighorn Bistro & Bar, Lakeside Cafe, breakfast service, and banquet operations.
  • Partner with Sales and Events teams to execute weddings, conferences, and special events.
  • Manage food cost, labor cost, purchasing, inventory, and annual culinary budgets.
  • Recruit, train, coach, and develop high-performing culinary teams.
  • Ensure compliance with food safety, sanitation, and workplace safety standards.
  • Maintain exceptional guest satisfaction through quality, consistency, and innovation.
  • Ensure compliance with company policy and execution of various initiatives.

Job Requirements:

  • Minimum 5 years of progressive culinary leadership experience.
  • Experience leading multiple restaurant concepts and banquet operations.
  • Strong financial acumen with expertise in food and labor cost management.
  • ServSafe certification or ability to obtain upon hire.
  • Excellent leadership, communication, and organizational skills.
  • Resort, hotel, or destination hospitality experience preferred.

Key Success Metrics

  • Guest satisfaction scores
  • Food and labor cost performance
  • Banquet execution quality
  • Team engagement and retention
  • Health and safety compliance
