POSITION SUMMARY:

Arapahoe Basin Vehicle Maintenance Mechanics perform maintenance on all of Arapahoe Basin’s vehicle fleet which includes snowcats, heavy equipment, snowmobiles, and on-road vehicles.

Join our team and work hands on with state-of-the-art snow cats and equipment to keep A-Basin open from October through June.

Actual pay will vary and may be above or below the range based on various factors, including but not limited to experience, education, training, location, merit system, quantity or quality of production, and responsibilities. The range listed is just one component of Arapahoe Basin’s total compensation package for employees. Other rewards may include many region-specific benefits.

Hourly pay range: $22.75 - $ 30.75/hour


ESSENTIAL DUTIES:

  • Ensure A-Basin’s vehicle fleet is in safe and proper working condition by following all regular maintenance schedules for any specific machine
  • Understand basic machine operation for a variety of vehicle types
  • Perform weekly machine checks for leaks, wear, and other potential damage to vehicles
  • Perform maintenance, diagnostics, and repair with the direct supervision and/or assistance of Vehicle Maintenance Mechanic III
  • Maintain neat, clean, and orderly workshop and surrounding areas
  • Learn and stay up-to-date on A-Basin’s grooming methods and trail network
  • Direct the company’s Hazardous Materials Handling Procedures and Guidelines; educate others on this topic
  • Properly document all completed work orders on a daily basis
  • Maintain and meet all OSHA standards
  • Work closely with other departments as necessary
  • Follow departmental sustainability guidelines
  • Perform other duties as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Previous experience in automotive or heavy equipment repair
  • Previous experience working in a shop or field-service environment
  • Ability to demonstrate fundamental knowledge of mechanical maintenance on various types of machinery
  • Ability to maintain a high level of safety consciousness
  • Strong mechanical problem-solving skills
  • Ability to work with others and in a team environment
  • Must be available weekends and holidays, occasional nights
  • This position requires successful completion of a Motor Vehicle Record background check

EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS

  • High school diploma or equivalent

PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS:

  • Ability to withstand high altitude conditions at all times
  • Ability to use full range of body motion and lift up to 75 lbs.
  • Ability to bend, twist, kneel, pull, and push
  • Ability to stand, kneel, squat, and lay under machines throughout a 10-hour shift
  • Ability to crawl under and/or hoist oneself on top of machinery
  • Ability to visually and audibly inspect machinery for irregularities

To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily- reasonable accommodations may be made to enable qualified individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.


WORKING CONDITIONS:

  • The position works mainly indoors in the Vehicle Maintenance Shop. Some work outside in snowy, icy, and inclement weather is required. Most work time is spent moving around on one’s feet or in other potentially strenuous positions.

Vehicle Mechanic Staging Program

Vehicle Mechanics at A-Basin are eligible for a staging program that starts when hired and continues into employment.


Vehicle Mechanic I: Pay Range: $22.75 - $30.75/hr

See requirements above.


Vehicle Mechanic II: Pay Range: $25.75 – $38.75/hr


Ability to fulfill all Vehicle Mechanic I requirements in addition to the following:

  • Meet all EPA standards for fuel and hazardous waste storage
  • Perform simple diagnostics and repair without any supervision
  • Perform higher level diagnostics and repair with the supervision and assistance of the Vehicle Maintenance Mechanic III
  • Ability to operate required equipment (including all of A-Basin’s vehicle fleet) in a safe and responsible manner
  • Basic understanding of hydraulic and hydrostatic systems and how they work
  • Basic fabrication skills (ex: oxy-acetylene torch, mig welding, stick welding)
  • Diagnostic skills for all heavy machinery that fall under A-Basin’s vehicle fleet
  • Basic parts ordering and computer use
  • Ability to build and replace hydraulic hoses

Vehicle Mechanic III: Pay Range: $27.75 – $41.75/hr

Ability to fulfill all Vehicle Mechanic II requirements in addition to the following:


  • Demonstrate advanced knowledge of mechanical systems on various types of machinery
  • Log machine issues, repairs, and parts usage on computer with Microsoft Excel
  • Advanced fabrication skills (ex: tig/aluminum welding, structural welding, ability to completely fabricate machine parts)
  • Complete knowledge of hydraulic system function; ability to adjust/repair hydraulic pumps
  • Complete diagnostic capability for diesel engines including use of Caterpillar ET and SisWeb
  • Complete knowledge of electrical systems including diagnostics and repair
