Position Summary

Tube hill attendants are responsible for operating the tubing hill. This includes operation of the tube walking paths, managing traffic flow and safety of the tube lanes. This also includes the use and sale of tickets through a point of sale system. Tube attendants will verbally give all safety information to all guests, monitor guests’ safety and ensure they have tickets. Tube attendants will be responsible for daily setup and tear down of the entire tube area. This is an outdoor job in a demanding winter environment. Attendants will use shovels and other grooming tools to prepare and maintain the tube lift areas and lanes and outrun.

Major Responsibilities

  • Opening procedures; setting fences, move tubes, prepare ramps and lanes.
  • Ticket sales and checking.
  • Provide continuous guest service.
  • Maintain rope lines, fencing, and deceleration carpets throughout the day.
  • Maintain a safe environment; ensure rules are being followed.
  • Maintain clean and organized working environments.
  • Effectively communicate while organizing a large crowd. 
  • Assist in snow removal throughout the mountain.
  • Closing Procedures; return equipment to storage and maintain a lane log.
  • Shovel a lot of snow.

Essential Functions

Opening Procedures

  • Set up fences and rope lines for tube area access.
  • Move tubes from overnight storage to daily staging areas.
  • Groom snow as necessary around tube hill.
  • Report any snow problems to lead or supervisor.
  • Prepare walking paths and tube lanes for the day.
  • Perform daily lane checks.

Pass/Ticket Sales and Checking

  • Check all guests to see that they have the proper ticket.
  • Assist guests with the sale of tickets.

Guest Service Responsibilities

  • Greet guests as they arrive.
  • Give guests information on tube hill use.
  • Give information on the location of other services.
  • If you can’t answer a question find someone who can.

Safety Duties

  • Ensure all guests using the tube hill have a ticket.
  • Ensure all Tubing Hill rules are followed.
  • When snow conditions require starting point will be set lower on lanes to reduce speed.
  • Tube hill operators are to watch out for the guests safety.
  • Distribute deceleration mats across lanes in a way that maximizes tubes to slow down.

Department Responsibilities

  • Be at work on your scheduled days on time.
  • Do your share of the work.
  • Be on time for lunch and breaks.
  • Participating in department safety huddles.

Closing Procedures

  • Inform guests of closing time ahead of time.
  • Take down all fences and rope lines.
  • Put tubes and other equipment away.
  • Report any equipment problems to lead or supervisor.
  • Turn in any lost gear to the Concierge.

Secondary Function

  • Assist in snow removal projects around resort.
  • Experienced operators assist in training new operators

Interpersonal Contacts

  • Guest Services, Parking, Lift Operations, Public Safety, Ski Patrol, Concierge, Grooming, & Receiving.

Specific Job Skills

  • Physical Capabilities: Able to remain standing for extended amounts of time in all weather conditions.
  • Additional: Tube Hill Attendants will be expected to shovel an extremely large amount of snow throughout their employment at MTB.

Education/Related Experience

  • Minimum Education: High School or Equivalent Required.
  • Minimum time in related position: Must be able to demonstrate success in safety awareness.
