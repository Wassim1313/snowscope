POSITION SUMMARY

Responsible for maintaining the cleanliness, appearance, and overall condition of resort properties, rental units, common areas, and grounds while performing a variety of maintenance and repair tasks. Technicians may be assigned to Chateau Chamonix, The Ranch, Steamboat Association Management, or Mountain Resorts Core based on operational needs and experience.


Mountain Resorts and Simply Steamboat are now a part of Steamboat Hospitality, Steamboat Resort's new hospitality division. We are proud to manage Steamboat's most popular vacation rental properties and offer uncommon hospitality to guests year-round.


Work location will be assigned based on applicants skillset, professional , and business needs.


ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Maintain the cleanliness, appearance, and overall condition of resort buildings, grounds, common areas, and rental units.
  • Perform snow and ice removal, basic landscaping, sweeping, and trash removal.
  • Clean common areas including lobbies, lounges, restrooms, corridors, elevators, stairways, and locker rooms.
  • Deliver and retrieve guest-requested items as needed.
  • Perform minor repairs, including replacing lightbulbs, CO monitors, smoke detectors, and batteries.
  • Perform basic and advanced maintenance repairs involving electrical, plumbing, audio/visual, pool and spa, painting, and drywall systems.
  • Maintain CPO certification and complete required training.
  • Safely operate power tools following required training.
  • Complete assigned work orders and maintain accurate time and production records.
  • Assist other departments with moving furniture, supplies, and equipment.
  • Communicate maintenance concerns that may impact guest/owner satisfaction or safety.
  • Compliance with all company policies and procedures as well as any regulatory requirements
  • Other duties as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • Previous maintenance experience preferred
  • Ability to read and understand basic instructions, correspondence, and safety information.
  • Ability to communicate effectively with supervisors, coworkers, guests, and other employees.
  • Ability to use sound judgment and solve routine problems.
  • Ability to safely perform basic maintenance and repair tasks.
  • Ability to safely operate power tools after completing required training.
  • Ability to obtain and maintain CPO certification as required.
  • Ability to follow company policies, procedures, and safety regulations.
  • Communicate clearly in English, both verbal / non-verbal, and written
  • Interact and maintain positive relationships with guests, co-workers and management consistent with Steamboat Ski and Resort Company’s Service Excellence standards

PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS

  • Must be minimum 18 years of age
  • Regularly lift and move up to 25 lbs. and frequently lift and move up to 50 lbs.
  • Regularly stand, walk, reach, bend, stoop, kneel, crouch, crawl, and climb or balance.
  • Frequently use hands and fingers to handle tools, equipment, and materials.
  • Work outdoors in varying weather conditions.
  • Work around moving mechanical equipment and moderate noise.
  • Occasionally work at heights and in areas with potential electrical hazards.
  • Must have close vision and the ability to adjust focus.
