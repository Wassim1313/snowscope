About the job

Overview

As a Painter you are responsible for maintaining the cosmetic appearance of all the painted areas of the buildings both internally and externally as well as the grounds of the entire property. As a Boyne Mountain team member, there are so many awesome perks for you to take advantage of, such as: ski passes, golf passes, waterpark passes, and discounts for food, beverages, retail, daycare, and so much more! To learn more about Boyne Mountain Resort, employment perks, and employee testimonials, click here - Boyne Mountain Employment!


Responsibilities

The Painter's typical job functions include:

  • Preparing various surfaces for application of paint, enamels, stain, varnishes or other required mediums using brushes and various equipment
  • Repairing and or replacing damaged drywall
  • Completing work orders and daily projects
  • Ensuring the satisfaction of all guests and owners by marking off work areas, working neatly and using all safety requirements

Qualifications

This position might be for you if:

  • You are at least 18 years old
  • Achieved a high school diploma or general education degree (GED)
  • You have painting job related experience
  • You are able to productively work independently and meet deadlines
