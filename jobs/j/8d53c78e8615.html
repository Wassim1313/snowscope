POSITION SUMMARY

Steamboat Ski & Resort Corp is looking for a Catering & Conference Services Manager. This position will sell, manage and service social groups and meetings, as well as plan and service conference groups at the Hotel and Resort. The Catering & Conference Services Manager is responsible for maximizing revenue opportunities across hotel and Steamboat event venues, maintaining strong client relationships and delivering service excellence.

An attached pdf or word document of your resume is required; cover letter is recommended.


ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Solicit new catering and meeting business opportunities for hotel, including prospecting, sales blitzes and in person meetings with prospective clients. Maintain and develop a strong sales pipeline, tracking leads, forecasting revenue, and identifying new market opportunities
  • Provide superior service to our customers (internal and external) at all times
  • Consistently meet or exceed sales targets as set by the Director of Hotel Sales & Catering, demonstrating a strong commitment to achieving revenue goals. 
  • Respond to written and telephone inquiries for the defined market (social, wedding and meeting only)
  • Prepare proposals for customers detailing general Steamboat information, meeting space, lodging and activities etc. required to secure business
  • Plan all aspects of conference services provided by the Resort. Be the primary contact for all conference client requests to include but not limited to: Meeting, banquet F&B, retail, activities, information technology, lift tickets, and billing (communicate off site excursions, transportation, entertainment and decoration recommendations with local partners)
  • Handle contract negotiations with a focus on securing favorable terms and ensuring mutual satisfaction including detailing deposit amounts, due dates, cancellation clauses and other criteria agreed upon.  Negotiate pricing with the client based on the entire scope of the event needs. Exercise discretion and judgment to determine the best scenarios for the client based on size, budget, length of stay, eligible repeat business, media exposure, community relations and multiple revenue center opportunities.
  • Occasionally represent the Resort at a client event or trade show, ability to speak in front of small or large audiences about Steamboat and the hotel.
  • Keep all Delphi accounts and bookings up to standards developed by Hotel. Utilize system to track leads, prospect accounts, manage client relationships, manage event space and log all relevant client communication.
  • Deal regularly with a variety of outside individuals/organizations, including clients, potential clients and vendors.
  • Determine deposit and payment schedules based on Hotel guidelines. Monitor deposits as scheduled
  • Prepare and maintain financial documents and reports as directed
  • Create and distribute Banquet Event Orders and Conference Resumes as Hotel procedures dictate. Provide detailed descriptions of services, product, pricing, timing, location and layout of each individual event. Occasional need to meet groups upon arrival or execution of event
  • Anticipate the group’s impact on Resort operations both directly and casually affected by the events via the Group Resume and direct communication
  • Monitor actual events as appropriate. Assure that the event is understood and confirmed by the Resort’s Operations management as set forth in the Banquet Event Order and Conference Resume. Report client feedback to Hotel and Resort operations supervisors and managers to evaluate event planning and performance on an ongoing regular basis.
  • Carry out special assignments as requested by the client. Delegate these tasks where standard Hotel operations procedures exist or perform the task personally where no standard exists
  • Charge appropriately as set forth in policy for services rendered. Work with the client, operations managers/directors, director of hotel sales & catering and VP of food & beverage to determine charges for unusual service or product requests or special availability.
  • Actively collaborate with other hotel and resort departments to deliver exceptional service and results for clients.
  • This position may require working weekends and holidays
  • Compliance with all company policies and procedures as well as any regulatory requirements
  • Other duties as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • 1 to 3 years of event or conference services experience, or a combination of education and experience from which comparable knowledge and skills are required
  • Sales, banquet, event, or conference services experience required
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Office with knowledge of Excel
  • Knowledge of Delphi/Salesforce software preferred, but not required
  • Experience in organizing and maintaining moderately complex filing, computerized record systems
  • Good basic administrative and/or organizational skills
  • Broad base knowledge and skills in food service management, including menu planning, cooking/food preparation operations, dining rooms set-up and service, wine service, ordering, staffing, pricing, and budgeting
  • Ensure understanding and compliance with all company policies and procedures as well as any regulatory requirements
  • Be a consistent role model for company’s Service Excellence standards
  • Must have Colorado approved Alcohol Server Training certificate or must attend a Colorado approved alcohol server training educational seminar within 30 days of employment
  • Must have current Food Handler and Allergy training certificate, or must attend Food Handler and Aller-train certification courses for Alterra Mountain company within 14 days of employment
  • Communicate clearly in English, both verbal / non-verbal, and written
  • Interact and maintain positive relationships with guests, co-workers and management consistent with Steamboat Ski and Resort Company’s Service Excellence standards
  • Must have valid driver’s license without limitations or restrictions and meet company’s insurability standards (will be required to complete Motor Vehicle Record)

EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS

  • High school diploma or GED equivalent
  • College Degree preferred
