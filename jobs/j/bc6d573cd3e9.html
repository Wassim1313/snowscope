POSITION SUMMARY

During the first month of employment, from mid-November through mid- to late December, the employee will work as an Employee Housing Attendant, with a primary focus on employee shuttle driving and maintaining the cleanliness of housing facilities. The Human Resources Coordinator role is the primary responsibility of this position and will become the main focus from mid-December through the conclusion of employment in late April.


The Housing Attendant is the primary person in charge of cleanliness and transportation.


The Human Resources Coordinator supports business processes such as onboarding, staffing, and daily operations. They serve as the primary link between employees and the HR team by addressing general employee needs and directing concerns to the appropriate personnel. They also help employees understand their duties and responsibilities, as well as Crystal Mountain’s operating procedures and policies.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES - HR Coordinator

  • Employee pass printing
  • Track & verify eligibility for employee ski perks.
  • Assist with general staff inquiries.
  • Monitors employee services email and distributes emails to appropriate HR team members
  • Plan & assist in the execution of employee events that fulfill the obligations of the J1 visa cultural events
  • Work with transportation in planning & logistics surrounding employee events.
  • Responsible for the cleanliness & overall appearance of the Employee Lounge.
  • Assist with employee Orientation and Onboarding.
  • Ensure that all decisions comply with company policies and regulatory standards.
  • Work with human resources team to advise them of staffing needs and other information necessary for the smooth flow of work
  • Oversee the flow of employee mail and packages
  • Report scheduling issues and job vacancies to HR and impacted department.
  • Assist human resources and/or assigned business unit with daily activities as required.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES - Housing Attendant

  • Conducts pre/post-trip inspections, level 1 basic maintenance (oil, fluid, fuel), and reporting mechanical issues.
  • Follows speeding laws, passenger capacity limits, and all other traffic laws. Mandatory safety training is required.
  • Ensures timely arrivals at collection points, passenger transport, and supervisor communication.
  • Transports employees to transit centers, shopping trips, appointments and activities.
  • Manages passenger behavior, ensuring safe disembarkation with personal belongings, and incident reporting.
  • Oversees sanitizing and stocking kitchen bathrooms, hallways, entryways, and common areas.
  • Gathers and transports trash/refuse to on-site dumpsters and manages compost and recycling collection and disposal.
  • Aids in maintaining common spaces, outdoor areas and reports maintenance requirements to facilities.
  • Covers day-to-day upkeep, handling move-ins/move-outs in the manager's absence, and departmental coordination. Assists management with room inspections and reports policy violations.
  • Serves as a point of contact for resident advisors, policy enforcement and community building.
  • Partners with Employee Experience Coordinator and International Employee Coordinators for events and activities.

EDUCATION, EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENTS, AND QUALIFICATIONS

  • High school diploma/GED.
  • Valid Drivers License and comfortable driving a 15 passenger van
  • Experience as a Crystal Mountain employee highly beneficial.
  • Ability to efficiently manage people and resources.
  • Excellent English communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite of applications.
  • Highly organized and detail oriented.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Strong analytical skills combined with excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Demonstrated ability to adapt and grow as business needs change.
  • Ability to work in a self-directed environment.
  • Experience with J1 or H2B visa preferred.

PHYSICAL DEMANDS AND WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Manual dexterity to operate a computer and other common office equipment on a constant basis.
  • Auditory and visual acuity to operate computers, phones, mobile devices, and basic office equipment on a constant basis.
  • The ability to work for extended periods of time sitting, bending, reaching, and speaking on a constant basis.
  • Talking and hearing occur continuously in the process of communicating with guests, supervisors, and other employees.

To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily with or without reasonable accommodation.

This position description is an overview of the scope of responsibilities for the role described above. With the evolution of the resort, the responsibilities of this position may change as may the dates associated with many of the tasks. During these times of change, this position requires flexibility and willingness to accept new responsibilities and potentially transfer others. It will also be the responsibility of any employee of the resort to be a positive influence on the transformation of the resort and its associated lines of business.
