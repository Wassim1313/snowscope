About the job

SeasonalWork, Play, Get Paid, and Enjoy the Perks!

  • Housing: Affordable on-mountain employee housing available for rent.

  • Work Location: 10 Snowshoe Drive, Snowshoe, WV 26209

  • Pay: $15.75 per hour

  • Start Date: The end of November and beginning of December with positions available throughout the season.

  • Seasonal: Seasonal work involves temporary employment tied to specific times of the year.

  • Schedule: May require working early mornings, evenings, weekends, and holidays

Employee Perks:

  • Medical Benefits – Minimal Essentials Coverage available

  • Free skiing and snowboarding privileges at Alterra resorts + Discounts on Ikon Passes!

  • 401k plan available to any employee over the age of 18

  • Discounted Friends and Family Lift Ticket Vouchers

  • 50% off Snowshoe Quick Service Restaurants and 30% off Snowshoe Full Service Restaurants (excludes alcohol)

  • 40% off Retail Merchandise and 45% off Mountain Essentials (excludes hard goods, 2 hard good sales for employees throughout winter)

  • Pro Deals from some of the industry’s top brands such as North Face, Darn Tough, and many many more!

Why Work with Us?

  • As a Supervisor, you’ll lead our sales team, ensuring excellent customer service and adherence to policies and procedures. Collaborate with a dedicated team to drive outstanding customer satisfaction and create a seamless shopping experience. If you’re passionate about retail leadership and customer service, we want to hear from you!

Job Responsibilities:

  • Provide excellent customer service through greeting, phone handling, and efficient POS transactions.

  • Foster a positive team environment and enforce operational procedures, including opening/closing and daily duties.

  • Communicate effectively with written and verbal instructions, resolve conflicts, and share relevant information.

  • Assist with inventory management, including cycle counts, merchandise handling, and monitoring shrinkage.

  • Support staff with POS transactions, housekeeping, and merchandising.

  • Ensure store quality through restocking and proper product merchandising.

  • Maintain a safe work environment by adhering to safety procedures and completing weekly training.

Education:

  • High School Diploma or GED

Preferred Experience:

  • 6 months in a position involving sales, hospitality, or training of others.

  • 1 year in a retail operation is preferred.

  • Basic computer skills are required to complete necessary daily tasks.

All Information above is subject to change at any time.
