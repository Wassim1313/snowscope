POSITION SUMMARY:

The Winter Park Resort Events department is looking for highly motivated, energetic, and passionate individuals that are looking to assist in the production of live events. This position will assist in day-of logistics of event operations (i.e. event set-up and tear down, vendor load-in/out coordination, serving alcoholic beverages, staffing activation booths, operating POS systems, event check-in booths, ticket booths, stage management, activation/set build, etc.) Individuals interested in this position should be willing to work weekends, holidays, and irregular hours.

Events this position will be supporting are as follows but are not limited to – Brand signature events, festivals, Holiday-themed events, musical performances, movie nights, mascot appearances, and experiential activations.

To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily with or without reasonable accommodations.


ESSENTIAL DUTIES:

General Responsibilities

  • Assist with day of execution of event logistics.
  • Move, set up, and tear down event elements.
  • Give direction to vendors that are participating in the event.
  • Hand out promotional materials, giveaways, etc. at events.
  • Assist in any pre-event promotional needs.
  • Assist in mascot appearances, serve as the mascot at special events, festivals, pop-up events, etc. in an enthusiastic, engaging and responsible manner.
  • Understanding of the event and certain elements, ability to communicate to guests these different elements.
  • Knowledge of the Resort base/facilities, ability to answer guests’ questions on where things are.
  • Knowledge of Winter Park Resorts Brand and Vision, able to communicate this through your work.
  • Other duties as assigned

QUALIFICATIONS:

Required

  • Ability to lift 40+ pounds.
  • Age Requirement: 21+
  • Ability to work evenings and weekends, including holidays.
  • Stand for an extended period of time (3+ hours consecutively).
  • Eligible to work in the United States.

Preferred

  • Previous experience in live events.

EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS:
