Position Summary

To perform the duties assigned, including pre-tripping and post-tripping of vehicles used. Transporting staff and/or guests around the mountain, to and from Bend, to and from Sunriver, and/or to and from La Pine.

Essential Duties & Major Responsibilities

  • Maintain a commercial driver's license and Department of Transportation medical card.

  • Complete driver's vehicle inspection report before operating vehicle.

  • Complete post-trip inspection report at the end of each day’s vehicle use.

  • Report any vehicle deficiencies on the inspection report to the manager or supervisor, and vehicle maintenance shop in a prompt manner.

  • Transport guests and staff from one destination to another in a safe manner at a reasonable time interval.

Job Scope

Your primary function is to ensure that the bus and/or transport vehicle you are operating is in a safe and functional condition. Transportation of guests and staff from Point to point in a safe and timely manner. Understand and follow federal regulations, state regulations, and company policies and procedures.

Secondary Function

  • Exercise customer service skills when applicable to engage guests and make them feel welcome. Also share knowledge of the resort services and future events appropriate for public disclosure.

  • Professionally and politely, inform guests of needed corrective action. Such as having guests move to an appropriate parking space so as not to block bus lanes.

  • In extreme emergencies, drivers may be directed to drop passengers off and support Emergency operations; drivers will never be asked to do anything that is high risk.

Interpersonal Contacts

Guests, fellow employees, & ski instructors.

Specific Job Skills

  • License/Certification: Commercial driver's license, passenger endorsement, current DOT medical card.

  • Technical: An understanding of police terminology and classification of crimes/incidents. Can look up ORS # and understand. Report Writing. Testifying in Court.

  • Physical Capabilities: Must be able to bend, Crouch, and stretch in a manner that allows proper inspection of a commercial vehicle. The ability to climb at least 4 steps and spend periods of time in a seated position behind a steering wheel for up to 4 hours at a time.

  • Additional: First Aid/CPR/AED preferred.

Education/Related Experience

  • Minimum Education: High School Diploma or G.E.D.

  • Minimum time in related position: 1+ year in commercial driving capacity.
