About the job

Year RoundWork, Play, Get Paid, and Enjoy the Perks!

  • Housing: Affordable on-mountain employee housing available for rent.

  • Work Location: 10 Snowshoe Drive, Snowshoe, WV 26209

  • Schedule: May require working early mornings, evenings, weekends, and holidays

Employee Perks:

  • Medical Benefits – Minimal Essentials Coverage available

  • Free skiing and snowboarding privileges at Alterra resorts + Discounts on Ikon Passes!

  • 401k plan available to any employee over the age of 18

  • Discounted Friends and Family Lift Ticket Vouchers

  • 30% off Snowshoe food & beverage locations (excludes alcohol), 30% off soft goods like clothes, 15% off hard goods like skis at Snowshoe owned retail locations.

  • Pro Deals from some of the industry’s top brands such as North Face, Darn Tough, and many many more!

Job Responsibilities:

  • Provides leadership and direction by:

    • Managing standards of quality, service, safety, and profit

    • Developing and implementing goals, policies, and objectives Overseeing all aspects of the operations under his/her direction

    • Assisting in recruiting, interviewing, hiring and training of management and staff

    • Carrying out all administrative functions pertaining to hiring, scheduling, payroll, performance reviews, coaching, counseling and discipline

    • Overseeing managers, supervisors and employees under their direction Attending all meetings as required

    • Conducting training seminars (Tips, food handling, etc.)

  • Ensures guest satisfaction by:

    • Assisting in the menu planning, analysis, and pricing

    • Coordinating additional staff needs for special events and conference center business

    • Inspecting outlet(s) and employees to ensure compliance with health, safety and state regulations Addressing guest feedback concerning outlets(s) service, presentation, quality of product and overall experience

    • Creating and implementing promotions, animation and special events for the outlet(s)

  • Maintains profitability by:

    • Monitoring labor costs, cost of goods sold, and other costs. Contributing to the annual budget process and business plan

  • Contributes to team effort by:

    • Accomplishing related results as needed

Education:

  • High School Diploma or GED

Preferred Experience:

  • 5 years of restaurant/business supervisory or management

All Information above is subject to change at any time.
