About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!


We are excited to welcome our J1 International Students for the upcoming season! This position is only open to students who have already been approved through the Boreal, Woodward, or Soda Springs J1 Program.


If you have not already been selected and approved as a J1 participant with us, please do not apply to this posting.


What You’ll Do


As part of our team, you will help create an unforgettable guest experience by working in seasonal roles across our resorts. Opportunities may include:

  • Guest services
  • Food & beverage
  • Rentals and retail
  • Mountain Operations

Requirements


  • Must already be an approved J1 International Student with Boreal, Woodward, or Soda Springs.
  • Ability to follow program guidelines and complete onboarding steps.
  • Willingness to work in a team environment with a positive attitude.

Important Note


This posting is strictly for pre-approved J1 participants.
If you are not part of our approved program list, your application will not be considered.


You make us Woodward. We are a welcoming community that celebrates individual stories, backgrounds, experiences, and identities where we all have "different" in common. We believe in the strength of representing all voices and having space for everyone. We believe in pushing boundaries and bringing our authentic selves to work while embracing uniqueness, creativity, and different points of view. Even if you don't think you meet all the qualifications listed for a job, we'd love to hear from you.
