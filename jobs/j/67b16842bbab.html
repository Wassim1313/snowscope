About the job

Overview

Sugarloaf is looking for a few safety-oriented people to join the grooming/outdoor operations team at our Outdoor Center location. Outdoor Center Groomers provide the best possible Nordic skiing surface while safely operating the grooming equipment in a variety of outdoor weather conditions. Sugarloaf also operates an NHL sized ice rink, and this role has opportunity to learn how to operate the Olympic Ice surfacing equipment.

The ideal candidate for this role will have a flexible schedule, good judgment, and the ability to operate machines in different snow conditions. Groomers are expected to maintain equipment, perform routine services and have good communication skills. Knowledge of local Nordic trail system and basic grooming routines are preferred but we are willing to train the right fit.

This part-time/full-time seasonal role is anticipated to start at an hourly wage of $17-22 (depending upon experience) and comes with great job perks and resort discounts.


Responsibilities

  • Groom all trails as assigned
  • Safely complete all tasks with no damage to equipment or to areas of work by evaluating performance and from feedback of crew leaders, team, and supervisor
  • Ensure that equipment is fueled and shoveled off at the end of each shift
  • Must be proficient in the use of vehicle attachments like compactor bars, tillers, and blades
  • Operation of a snowmobile and trail drag, as well as a chainsaw will also be a regular work task for this position
  • Maintain the assigned equipment in routine manner
  • Maintain an awareness of surroundings and the safest way to navigate the equipment by evaluating all directions and safety information available
  • Snow removal from around lodge and Ice rink
  • Ability to identify and report snow conditions and/or hazards

Qualifications

  • Must be at least 18 years old and have a valid driver's license
  • Must be able to work with little direct supervision and as part of a team
  • Must have an understanding and willingness to adhere to safety standards
  • Must be able to work nights, holidays, and weekends
  • Grooming experience is preferred

Sugarloaf Team Member Benefits include:

  • Free lift pass for skiing and/or riding
  • Discounts at resort F&B and retail locations
  • Discounted ski/snowboard lessons and rentals
  • Access to Nordic trails and discounted equipment rentals at our Outdoor Center location
  • Discounted passes at other Boyne and partner resorts
