Job Summary:

As a Team Lead of the Keystone Condo Registration Center, you are the face of Keystone Property Management! You are the first point of contact for all guests, and help make their stay a memorable one.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $22.96/hr - $30.61/hr
  • Employment Type: Year Round (11-month)
  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: Yes

Job Responsibilities:

  • Work face-to-face with customers
  • Check-in guests and condo owners, processing necessary paperwork and credit cards
  • Check-out guests and condo owners ensuring posting and collection of payments
  • Answer Call Center and Front Desk Phones in a timely and professional manner
  • Provide guests with information on Keystone Resort and the surrounding area
  • Handle all customer needs
  • Maintain a professional appearance and demeanor at all times
  • Help resolve discrepancies with guest bills
  • Help Condo Owners with their Reservations
  • Drive Guest Courtesy Vehicle
  • Expected to be cross-trained with Lodging Operations
  • Collaborates and works with other Departments including but not limited to: Lodging Operations, Owner Relations, HOA Management, Housekeeping, Maintenance, IT, and Sales
  • Other duties as assigned

Job Requirements:

  • High School education or equivalent - Required
  • Extensive phone and computer skills with data entry experience – Required
  • Basic reading, spelling, and math skills – Required
  • Ability to converse clearly and comfortably with many different types of people in English – Required
  • Ability to work with guests using good social manners, diplomacy, and tact – Required
  • Valid US Driver’s License, Clean driving record and must be able to pass a Vail MVR – Required
  • Ability to work well under pressure – Required
  • Previous hotel/hospitality experience – Preferred

The expected pay range is $22.96/hr - $30.61/hr. This is the pay range we reasonably expect to pay for this position. Individual compensation decisions are based on a variety of factors.
