About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE:                  Restaurant Assistant General Manager

LOCATION:    71 Restaurant, located inside the Cliff Lodge at Snowbird

STATUS:             Full-time, Year-round

SCHEDULE:    Schedules vary based on business needs

  • Must be able to work any day of the week including weekends and holidays

SUMMARY

As an Assistant General Manager you will work along the General Manager, a committed team of cooks, servers and restaurant attendants to provide quality food and beverage services and a dining experience that exceeds guest expectations in order to increase revenues and decrease costs.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Assists Service Manager in providing directional flow of restaurant, plan supervise all work done by dining room employee in order to meet guest expectations.
  • Works with Service Manager to recommend promotions transfer or termination of employees based on performance evaluation in order to improve staff performance, guest services, and business objectives.
  • Ensures all guest needs are met during the meal in order to achieve a positive dining experience for the guest.
  • Responsible for all financial transactions occurring within the operation, in order to assure accuracy and integrity of process.  Maintain daily records including revenues and payroll and completes nightly reports.
  • Ensures standards of appearance and sanitation are maintained daily.  Maintains good safety practices of employees and guests throughout the property.  Follows state policies and regulations for serving alcoholic beverages in a responsible manner.
  • Direct staff activities and address guest issues while maintaining professionalism.

WHAT YOU WILL NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

  • High School Diploma or equivalent required.
  • 2 years of job-specific work experience with demonstrated competence
  • Must be at least 21 years of age.
  • Food Handler Permit, Tips Cert, Sheriff Work Card, 
  • Strong interpersonal skills, including excellent guest service, listening and oral communication skills, also conflict management and dispute resolution skills. 
  • Competency in reading, writing and arithmetic with accuracy in counting, adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing. 

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Must be able to work any day of the week including weekends and holidays.  Schedules vary based on business levels.

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Rotating schedule based on business levels, including early mornings, nights, weekends and holidays.
  • Be prepared to stay overnight, if necessary, on occasional times of road closures.
  • May experience unusual stress from continual work with public, winter conditions, snow night requirements and commuting to/from Snowbird.
  • Be able to lift 35 lbs.
  • Must be able to stand for long periods of time.
  • Must demonstrate ability to bend, kneel, handle, reach, grasp and perform repetitive motions.

WHY WORK HERE

  • Ski pass and comp ticket benefits
  • Discounts at Snowbird eateries, retail shops, mountain school, lodging and daycare for employees
  • Available medical, dental, vision and accident insurance – benefits are available based on position and are subject to change
  • 401k
  • Discounts with Powdr partners
  • Free transportation to work: UTA Bus, Alta/Snowbird shuttle vans and Employee Blue Shuttles
