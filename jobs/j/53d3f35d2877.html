​Why Work with Us?

Are you passionate about winter sports and working with young children? Join us for what promises to be an amazing season! As a Pre-Ski School Attendant, you'll assist the Pre-Ski School Supervisors in daily operations, caring for kids aged 2-4 years old. In this role, you'll help nurture the next generation of skiers and snowboarders, ensuring they have as much fun as their families on the slopes. Create unforgettable experiences with engaging activities, tasty snacks, and plenty of playtime both indoors and out. Be among the first to introduce these little ones to the thrill of the outdoors and make their vacation truly special!

Job Responsibilities:

  • Ensure children’s comfort and happiness by assisting with bathroom breaks, diaper changes, minor accidents, clothing adjustments, and dressing for outdoor weather.

  • Perform housekeeping duties in the pre-ski school area and assist with organizing lunch.

  • Coordinate with Food & Beverage to order and serve daily lunches, ensuring adequate supplies.

  • Manage check-in and check-out procedures, including assisting with paperwork and waivers.

  • Prepare children for the day and escort them to activities.

  • Handle basic RTP functions, such as inventory checks and order closures.

  • Serve as a resort ambassador, offering knowledgeable information about facilities and products.

  • Teach snow sports in a safe, enjoyable environment to ensure memorable experiences.

  • Build rapport with guests and staff by establishing trust and demonstrating care.

  • Adhere to resort practices and policies, participate in training, and maintain professionalism through uniform and grooming standards.

Education:

  • High School Diploma or GED

Preferred Experience:

  • Must be willing & able to learn to ski

  • At least 3 months experience in a related field

  • Knowledge of safety practices, CPR/first aid certification preferred

  • May require lifting and moving up to 50 pounds.
