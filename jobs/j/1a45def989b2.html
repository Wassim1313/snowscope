Job Summary:

The Administrative Specialist is responsible for providing administrative, organizational, and operational support to ensure the efficient day-to-day functioning of the Building Maintenance. This position serves as a central point of contact for employees, visitors, vendors, and management while coordinating office operations, maintaining records, supporting departmental initiatives, and assisting with special projects. The Administrative Specialist plays a key role in maintaining a professional, productive, and well-organized work environment.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $22.87/hr - $28.59/hr


  • Employment Type: Year Round


  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available


  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age


  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

Administrative Support:

  • Provide administrative support to management and department staff Leads.
  • Prepare, format, and distribute correspondence, reports, presentations, and other business documents.
  • Maintain electronic and physical filing systems to ensure accuracy and accessibility of records.
  • Manage calendars, schedule meetings, and coordinate conference room reservations.
  • Assist with meeting preparation, including agendas, meeting materials, and minutes.
  • Handle incoming phone calls, emails, and visitor inquiries in a professional manner.

Office Operations:

  • The Administrative Specialist will serve as the department's administrative and operational coordinator, managing the MaintainX CMMS platform, supporting maintenance planning activities, coordinating the purchase of maintenance parts and supplies, maintaining accurate records, and ensuring the Building Maintenance team has the resources necessary to operate safely and efficiently. This role will be instrumental in improving work order management, inventory control, vendor coordination, and overall department effectiveness.
  • Serve as a liaison between employees, vendors, and management.
  • Coordinate vendor services, deliveries, and office-related contracts.
  • Maintain office equipment and arrange service or repairs when necessary.
  • Support onboarding activities for new employees, including workspace preparation and administrative documentation

Records & Reporting:

  • Maintain confidential personnel, operational, and business records.
  • Assist with data entry, recordkeeping, and database management.
  • Generate routine reports and track key administrative metrics.
  • Ensure documentation complies with company policies and procedures.

Financial and Purchasing Support:

  • Assist with purchase requisitions, purchase orders, and invoice processing through COUPA.
  • Track expenditures and maintain administrative budgets as assigned.
  • Reconcile receipts through COUPA, vendor invoices, and expense reports.
  • Coordinate with accounting and procurement departments as needed

Facility and Project Coordination:

  • Support facility-related administrative activities.
  • Coordinate work orders, service requests, and contractor scheduling.
  • Assist with departmental projects and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Track project milestones, deadlines, and action items.
  • Support company events, training sessions, and employee engagement activities

Customer Service:

  • Provide exceptional customer service to internal and external stakeholders.
  • Respond promptly to requests and resolve administrative issues efficiently.
  • Promote a positive and professional work environment

Job Requirements:

  • High school diploma or GED required; Associate degree preferred.
  • Minimum 3 years of administrative, office management, or business support experience.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Teams).
  • Strong organizational and time-management skills.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to handle confidential information with discretion.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience supporting maintenance, operations, facilities, or industrial environments.
  • Experience with purchasing systems, work order systems, or enterprise software platforms.
  • Knowledge of office administration, records management, and business operations.

Physical Requirements:

  • Ability to sit, stand, and walk for extended periods.
  • Ability to lift and carry office supplies and materials up to 25 pounds.
  • Ability to operate standard office equipment
