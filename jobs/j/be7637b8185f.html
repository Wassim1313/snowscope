About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!

We’re currently hiring Ski and Snowboard Patrollers for the upcoming winter season. If you’re experienced on the mountain, confident in first aid response, and thrive under pressure, this is your opportunity to join one of the most respected teams on the hill.

This is a Full-Time or Part-Time, Seasonal position with employee housing available starting at $325/month.

Perks:

  • Starting pay: $19/hour (with higher pay possible for advanced EMT/OEC certifications or returning patrollers)

  • Health insurance options for ALL employees

  • Employee housing available – starting at just $325/month

  • Free season pass to Boreal, Soda Springs, and all POWDR resorts

  • Full access to the Bunker—Woodward Tahoe’s indoor training facility with trampolines, foam pits, skateparks, and more

  • Discounts on food, retail, and gear

  • Discounts for friends and family

  • Paid volunteer opportunities

  • Work in a fun, supportive, mountain-town environment

  • Career advancement opportunities across the Woodward and POWDR network

What You’ll Be Doing:

As a member of our Patrol team, you'll play a critical role in guest safety, terrain management, and emergency response. You’ll be visible across the mountain, ensuring trails are safe, responding to incidents, and supporting operational excellence.


Daily Responsibilities:

  • Perform morning trail checks and open/close terrain

  • Respond quickly to on-mountain incidents and injuries

  • Provide first aid and coordinate guest transport as needed

  • Assist with toboggan handling and loading patients safely

  • Support avalanche safety, terrain closures, and hazard marking

  • Educate guests on mountain safety and etiquette

  • Maintain and restock medical supplies at patrol stations

  • Serve as a first responder in emergency situations, including lift evacuations

Work Environment & Schedule:

  • Outdoor mountain environment with challenging winter conditions (snow, wind, cold, variable visibility)

  • Early morning start times, weekend and holiday shifts expected

  • Must be comfortable skiing or riding in varied terrain, including off-piste

  • Physically demanding role: lifting, hiking, and toboggan operation required

  • On-call or extended hours possible during weather events or major incidents

Who We’re Looking For:

  • Strong skiing or snowboarding ability in all conditions (required)

  • OEC, EMT, or WEMT (required or in progress)

  • Active CPR certification

  • Calm under pressure and able to work efficiently in emergencies

  • Strong communication and teamwork skills

  • Dependable, punctual, and safety-minded

  • Experience in patrol, guiding, mountain rescue, or similar roles is a plus

  • Willingness to train and grow within the department

  • Required certifications will need to be submitted prior to your first day
