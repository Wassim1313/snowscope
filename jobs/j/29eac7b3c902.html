About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!


If you’re skilled behind the wheel of heavy equipment and love working in the mountains, this is the job for you! As a Snow Removal Equipment Operator at Boreal and Soda Springs, you’ll play a vital role in keeping our resort safe, accessible, and running smoothly through all weather. You’ll be behind the controls of loaders, plows, and other large machinery to remove snow from roads, parking lots, walkways, and key resort areas.


This is a part-time, hands-on, high-impact job for experienced operators who enjoy early mornings, working outdoors, and making a difference.



Perks:

  • Starting pay: $20–$25 / hour (DOE)
  • Health insurance options for ALL employees
  • Free season pass to Boreal, Soda Springs, and all POWDR resorts
  • Full access to the Bunker — Woodward Tahoe’s indoor training facility with trampolines, foam pits, skateparks, and more
  • Discounts on food, retail, and gear
  • Discounts for friends and family
  • Work in a fun, supportive, mountain-town environment
  • Career advancement opportunities across the Woodward and POWDR network



What You’ll Be Doing:

  • Operating front-end loaders, plow trucks, skid steers, and other snow removal equipment
  • Clearing snow from access roads, parking lots, building entrances, walkways, and resort infrastructure
  • Maintaining equipment through regular checks, fueling, and reporting maintenance needs
  • Working early mornings, nights, weekends, and during active storms
  • Following all safety procedures while operating equipment in high-traffic or guest-facing areas
  • Supporting other departments with equipment needs as necessary (e.g., hauling, clearing space for deliveries, etc.)
  • Collaborating with the facilities team to prioritize areas based on operational needs



Qualifications:

  • Previous experience operating heavy machinery in winter or snow removal environments
  • Valid driver’s license required; CDL preferred but not required
  • Able to work outdoors for extended periods in cold, snowy, and icy conditions
  • Comfortable working overnight or early morning shifts, weekends, and holidays
  • Strong situational awareness and safety-first mindset
  • Reliable, punctual, and capable of working independently or with a team
