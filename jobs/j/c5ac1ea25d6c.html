Job Summary:
Join a great team! We have openings at The Arrabelle, Vail Resorts’ premier property located at the base of Vail Mountain. Here you will maintain assets inside and outside of guest rooms, owner condos and common areas. Ensure all areas are safe and in working order.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $22.00/hr - $27.70/hr
  • Employment Type: Year Round
  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

  • Perform electrical, mechanical and general construction, repair and maintenance work on existing facilities and equipment. Conduct daily checks of the Building Automation System (JCI) and building mechanicals.
  • Perform routine maintenance on the property’s physical structure, mechanical, electrical and HVAC systems, and other related equipment that contributes to the safe and effective operation of the facility.
  • Perform daily rounds to check boilers, pools, common areas, sprinkler systems, the fire pump room and fire panels.
  • Visually inspect completed work for quality and report deficiencies.
  • Comply with company policy on vehicle operation.
  • Attend to work orders, guest requests, housekeeping requests, front desk requests and room calls.
  • Assist with required inspections and certifications for property equipment as directed.
  • Maintain all facilities and operating systems in safe and efficient working order, including automatic doors, exit doorways and required snow and garbage removal. Work will require lifting 20 pounds or more.
  • Complete assigned tasks in a safe, timely manner.
  • Work with outside contractors to provide necessary improvements or maintenance as directed.
  • Carry ladders up and down stairs with required tools, which may exceed 20 pounds.
  • Work may require lifting arms above the head and shoulders to perform ceiling repairs, including addressing small leaks, electrical shorts and motor replacements.
  • Maintain records of all work activities and supporting materials.
  • Provide written reports to other departments as needed.
  • Safely dispose of all waste materials.
  • Maintain tools, equipment and vehicles, and keep work areas safe, secure and organized.
  • Work with other departments when directed.
  • Keep track of supplies and complete requisitions for needed supplies.
  • Maintain vehicle maintenance records and schedules.
  • Maintain all pools and hot tubs according to applicable safety and health standards.
  • Order parts and items necessary for repairs to the building and units, following established purchasing procedures.
  • Document engineering work in forms that can be directly applied to the billing program.
  • Assist with preventive maintenance in common areas, back-of-house areas and guest rooms.
  • Other duties as assigned.

Job Requirements:

  • High school diploma.
  • 3–4 years of experience at a large resort property or equivalent experience in construction, cruise ships or other similar industries.
  • Basic plumbing skills and knowledge of electrical and mechanical systems.
  • Valid Colorado driver’s license. Relevant accreditation is an advantage.
  • Ability to complete assigned work independently and effectively

The expected pay range is $22.00/hr - $27.70/hr. This is the pay range we reasonably expect to pay for this position. Individual compensation decisions are based on a variety of factors.
