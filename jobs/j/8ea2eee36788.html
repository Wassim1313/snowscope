Job Summary:

The Barista performs the same general functions as an Entry-Level, Server, but operates specifically within a coffeehouse setting during peak operational periods. In addition to taking orders and serving food and beverages, this role is responsible for operating specialized equipment to prepare a wide variety of coffee, tea, and related beverages. As a role within a franchise coffeehouse, the Barista is required to learn and abide by all Marriott franchise policies, operational procedures, and brand standards.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $12.14/hr - $16.04/hr
    • If hired into a tipped position, guaranteed to make a minimum of $20/hour, inclusive of tips
  • Employment Type: Year Round (10-month)
  • Shift Type: Full Time and Part Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 16 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

  • Beverage Preparation: Operate specialized espresso machines, grinders, and brewing equipment to prepare and serve high-quality coffee, tea, and custom related beverages.
  • Food Service: Prepare and serve simple food items, including breakfast items, salads, sandwiches, wraps, and other basic menu options.
  • Customer Service: Greet customers, take precise orders, answer menu questions, and deliver exceptional service in a fast-paced environment.
  • Franchise Compliance: Learn, maintain, and abide by established franchise policies, recipe standards, and service guidelines (where applicable).
  • Daily Operations: Maintain a clean, organized, and stocked workstation while following health, safety, and sanitation protocols.

Job Requirements:

  • Minimum 1 year experience as a server in a coffeehouse setting.
  • High School Diploma or equivalent preferred.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organizational and multitasking abilities.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional under pressure.
  • Team-oriented mindset and willingness to assist others.
  • Physical stamina to stand and walk for extended periods.
  • Basic computer and math skills; familiarity with POS systems preferred.
  • Prior customer service or restaurant experience is a plus.
  • Ability to work flexible schedules, including nights, weekends, and holidays.
  • Ability to lift 50+lbs.
