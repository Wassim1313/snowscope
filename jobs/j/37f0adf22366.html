About the job

Overview

Work with Guests in lift lines to ensure a smooth, efficient and enjoyable lift line experience by making sure the lift line is flowing, people are grouping together and chairs are full. The position will need to interact in a fun and pleasant manner with the guests to make sure they are moving and grouping together with other guests.


Responsibilities

  • Exhibits excellent guest interactions by being courteous, efficient, being a strong communicator, being reliable, patient and friendly.
  • Ensure a smooth lift line. Making sure the Guests are grouping together to maximize lift capacity and if they are not, working with them to make sure they do in a very pleasant manner.Provide guidance to guests to access lift load area in a pleasant, orderly, fair fashion.
  • Travel safely between lifts, be apprised of daily conditions, safety concerns, events and any developments to operate efficiently.
  • Maintains and enhances a guest focused culture in the lift lines and outside of that area as well.
  • Work with the Lift Operations group to make sure the overall lift experience is exceptional.
  • Continually look for better and more efficient ways for the lift line experience to improve and be exceptional.
  • Require at least 100 hours, 20 of which are designated high priority such as Christmas vacation mid-week days, Martin Luther King Monday, and February vacation mid-week days. Regular working days are Saturdays, Sundays and vacation periods noted above starting @ December 14 and ending @ March 30th.

RESPONSIBILITIES TO SAFETY:

  1. Protect the safety of self, co-workers, and Loon Mountain Resort guests at all times.
  2. Report any potentially harmful equipment or situations to the immediate supervisor without delay.
  3. Report safety-related accidents and incidents at once to immediate supervisor.
  4. Follow all company and department safety policies and procedures.
  5. Operate equipment in a safe manner that will not lead to injury of yourself or others.
