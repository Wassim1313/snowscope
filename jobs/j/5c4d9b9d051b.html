About the job

SeasonalPalisades TahoeWe share the spirit of these legendary mountains with the world.


To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential duties.

Job Summary: A Snowcat Operator will prepare and maintain a Terrain Park snow surface that is safe and functional for skiers and snowboarders during all snow and weather conditions. Builds and maintains terrain features in the freestyle parks and other locations where applicable. Depending on level, a snowcat operator may also fulfill the hailing and dozing needs of other mountain departments, assist in directing grooming activities, and assist with special projects.

Applicants must be at least 18 years of age.

Essential Job Responsibilities/Duties/Tasks includes the following: other duties may be assigned:

Slope grooming machine operation:

  • Ability to properly check out/in a snowcat.

  • Demonstration safe operation and distances near obstacles and personnel.

  • Operate all machine functions such as blade, tiller, and other grooming implements.

  • Ability to park a snowcat safely and properly.

  • Minimize downtime and prevent machine damage through preventative maintenance, cleanliness, and up-keep of machine.

  • Strong communication with the vehicle maintenance shops regarding needs.

  • Perform light to medium manual labor such as changing implements, installing features and vehicle check-outs and miscellaneous other chores.

Safe slope operation:

  • Able to clean up a turn around on the slope surface with a focus on skier safety.

  • Navigate and properly operate grooming machine on beginner terrain and travel routes.

  • Navigate and properly operate on select intermediate terrain and travel routes.

  • Ability to follow and groom in a pack as directed.

  • Operate machine safely with minimal supervision.

Final Product

  • Technical ability to maintain the slope with the proper flow and direction.

  • Groom seamlessly from one pass to the next.

  • Can groom a smooth finish down fall line with no blade dives or tiller hops.

  • Must be able to recognize when repairs to slope are necessary

  • Able to perform proper and safe tie-ins with other grooming.

  • Prepare and maintain Terrain Park features and a skiing/snowboard surface that is safe and functional for skiers and snowboarders of all skill levels using Grooming vehicles.

Competencies and Job Requirements:

  • Maintain snow surfaces to meet high standards with necessary supervision.

  • Excellent communication skills, oral & written.

  • Strong sense of customer service & safety awareness.

  • Able to understand and follow verbal and written instructions.

  • Punctual, able to report at scheduled start time.

  • Work well as part of a team.

  • Required to ski or snowboard in variable conditions while carrying equipment
