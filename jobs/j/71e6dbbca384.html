About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!

We’re currently hiring Food & Beverage Crew Members to work in our lodges and pop-up outlets. This team is essential to keeping guests fueled and happy.

This is a Part Time Seasonal position that runs during the winter operating season.

Perks:

  • Starting pay: $18/hour plus tips

  • Free season pass to Boreal, Soda Springs, and all POWDR resorts

  • Full access to the Bunker—Woodward Tahoe’s indoor training facility with trampolines, foam pits, skateparks, and more

  • Discounts on food, retail, and gear

  • Discounts for friends and family

  • Work in a fun, supportive, mountain-town environment

  • Career advancement opportunities across the Woodward and POWDR network

What You’ll Be Doing:

As a Food & Beverage Crew Member, you’ll be serving up good vibes and great food to hungry riders and their families. Whether you're working a register, prepping meals, or delivering hot cocoa to a stoked kiddo, you’re helping create the full mountain experience.

Daily Responsibilities:

  • Provide friendly, fast, and accurate service to all guests

  • Operate POS systems and handle cash/credit transactions

  • Assist with food prep, stocking, restocking, and maintaining clean stations

  • Ensure health and safety standards are followed at all times

  • Bussing, dishwashing, and basic cleaning duties

  • Communicate clearly with team members and supervisors

  • Keep energy high during busy weekends and powder days

Work Environment & Schedule:

  • Indoor environment with frequent peak traffic during meal times

  • Weekend and holiday shifts are expected

  • Morning, mid-day, and evening shifts available depending on role

  • Must be able to stand, walk, and lift up to 40 lbs for extended periods

  • Kitchen roles may require working around heat, cold, and sharp tools

Who We’re Looking For:

  • Minimum 5 Years Experience Bartending

  • Valid RBS Number

  • Valid Serve Safe California Training or able to aquire one within 30 days of start date

  • Positive attitute

  • Knowledgable in cocktails, wine, and beer
