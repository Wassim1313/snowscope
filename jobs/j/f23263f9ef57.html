​Why Work with Us?

We’re looking for a highly organized and enthusiastic individual to support our Employee Housing operations. In this role, you will provide vital administrative, secretarial, and office services, ensuring smooth and efficient operations.

If you’re ready to contribute to delivering exceptional mountain experiences, apply now and become a key player in our team!


Job Responsibilities:

Customer Service

  • Delivers exceptional service to employee-tenants and hiring managers, ensuring adherence to departmental policies and procedures.

Administrative

  • Maintains and updates the Employee Housing Daily Activity Report.
  • Drafts and manages office documents; operates office equipment; screens calls; and handles messages.
  • Tracks financial transactions with spreadsheets and communicates discrepancies to accounting; updates the manager on budget and expenditures.
  • Orders and manages office, maintenance, and housekeeping supplies.
  • Logs monthly office supply budget charges and prepares statistical reports.
  • Ensures professionalism by adhering to policies on noise, mingling, and dress code.

Operations

  • Provides administrative support to the Employee Housing Manager, including call handling, photocopying, and document preparation.
  • Manages Rent Manager tasks: updating tenant accounts, processing rent, and handling account closures and security deposits.
  • Schedules and records seasonal and monthly room inspections; prepares eviction notices.
  • Collaborates with housekeeping and maintenance for key requests and issue tracking; coordinates spring cleaning and refurbishment projects.
  • Completes bi-weekly Facility Analysis inspections and files reports with Risk Management.
  • Prioritizes projects independently and contributes to team goals.

Communication

  • Handles confidential information with discretion and tact.
  • Supports clear communication by managing calls, messages, and orientation programs.
  • Maintains strong relationships with all on-mountain departments through effective communication.

Education:
  • High School Diploma or GED
  • Driving



Preferred Experience:
  • 1 year of previous administrative field skills and computer skills of word processing, Access, Excel and Microsoft Office.
