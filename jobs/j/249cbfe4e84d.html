About the job

Overview

The Parking & Transportation Supervisor is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations of all parking and transportation areas, including paid parking, lot operations, and enforcement, to ensure daily set-up and operations are efficient, safe, and enhance the guest arrival experience. This position leads parking enforcement, parking support, and frontline transportation team members, while managing parking systems and equipment, enforcing policies, streamlining communication across teams, and supporting accurate revenue collection. The supervisor plays a key role in maintaining a positive guest experience while upholding parking and transportation standards and ensuring operational effectiveness. The ideal candidate is organized, guest-focused, and capable of supervising front-line staff in a fast-paced, outdoor environment.

Come work and play at Seattle’s home mountain, The Summit at Snoqualmie. Located an hour from Seattle, at the junction where the Pacific Crest Trail crosses Interstate 90. With summer activities ramping up with spectacular Mountain Biking trails and Scenic Lift Rides, there is lots to do all year long.
