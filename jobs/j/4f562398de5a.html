WHAT WILL I BE DOING?

The purpose of this role is to maintain the grounds on resort to the highest standards, including general maintenance, landscaping and waste management.

  • Performs snow removal activities on Blue Mountain Resort property and Resort affiliated properties. Work includes, but not limited to; removing snow utilizing shovels and/or snow blowers, applying Ice Melt and/or sand as required.
  • Conducts ice patrols and assesses general hazards to ensure the safety of our guests and employees. Monitors material levels in SOS Bins.
  • Records detailed notes on activities and material applications throughout shift.
  • Makes basic repairs/maintenance of department equipment such as blowers.

WHAT DO YOU NEED FROM ME?

  • Valid Class “G” Driver’s License an asset.
  • High school or equivalent education preferred.
  • Must be physically fit; able to lift and carry heavy materials and perform repetitive tasks involving shoveling, bending, stretching, walking and kneeling.
  • Experience with snow removal an asset.
  • Knowledge of the Resort an asset.

BLUE MOUNTAIN RESORT COMPANY OVERVIEW

Blue Mountain Resort is Ontario’s largest mountain resort. Nestled on the shores of Southern Georgian Bay, on the traditional territory of the Anishinabek Nation: The People of the Three Fires known as Ojibway, Odawa, and Pottawatomie Nations. Only 90 minutes outside Toronto, Blue Mountain has become the four-season outdoor destination of the North. Blue Mountain is owned by Alterra Mountain Company.

ALTERRA COMPANY OVERVIEW

Alterra Mountain Company is a family of iconic year-round mountain destinations, the world’s largest heli-skiing operation, and Ikon Pass - the premier ski and snowboard season pass offering access to more than 50 iconic mountain destinations around the world.

Headquartered in Denver, Colorado and born out of a shared love of the mountains and adventure, Alterra Mountain Company exists to shape the future of mountain adventure.
