Job Summary:

Competition Services offers programs across the disciplines of Alpine, Freestyle, Race and Snowboard, and is seeking motivated and passionate coaches to join our staff. Through these programs, we look to provide opportunities for all our athletes to excel and enjoy their chosen sport. We are looking to fill various positions to meet our growing programs' needs. The ideal candidate is a self-starter, a multi-tasker, and a good communicator who is looking to share their passion for snow sports with the next generation, and willing to work weekends and some holidays.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $21.00/hr - $27.00/hr
  • Employment Type: Winter Seasonal 2026/2027
  • Shift Type: Part Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

  • Provide a safe, positive environment for athletes to develop fundamental skills and a love for the sport.
  • Work within guidelines and seasonal and daily plans to provide the best possible training environment for athletes.
  • Work with athletes to set goals and challenge and inspire them to achieve these goals on snow and off to reach their potential
  • As part of a coaching team, develop training ideas and implement plans based around seasonal and competition plans.
  • Other duties as assigned

Job Requirements:

  • Enthusiastic team player with excellent oral and written communication skills.
  • Prior coaching and/or racing experience along with USSS/USASA coaching certification (Level 100 or higher or international equivalent) preferred, or the willingness to complete relevant professional development training.
  • Ability to work as part of a team and independently to a common goal.
  • Ability to ski/ride and work safely in all snow surface/terrain conditions in harsh winter weather for prolonged periods of time.
  • Proven ability to positively inspire athletes of all ages and skill levels

The expected pay range is $21.00/hr - $27.00/hr. This is the pay range we reasonably expect to pay for this position. Individual compensation decisions are based on a variety of factors.
