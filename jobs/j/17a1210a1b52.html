About the job

Overview

There’s no better place to spend your days than surrounded by kids who are curious, energetic, and full of wonder — and few childcare programs offer the warmth, variety, and professional support of Playcare at Boyne Mountain Resort. As a Playcare Childcare Specialist, you’ll create a safe, nurturing, and developmentally rich environment for children ages 3 months to 12 years, supporting their growth across every dimension: physical, emotional, social, and cognitive.

Everything we do at Boyne Mountain is guided by our core values — L.E.A.D.S.: Long-Term Thinking, Excellence in Execution, Attitude is Everything, Develop Great People, and Serve First. In this role, you’ll live those values every time you comfort a fussy infant, guide a toddler through a new skill, or make a family feel truly seen and cared for. As a Boyne Mountain team member, you’ll also enjoy ski passes, golf passes, waterpark passes, and discounts on food, beverages, retail, daycare, and much more.


Responsibilities

  • Serve First - Provide attentive, hands-on supervision and care for children at all times, ensuring their physical and emotional safety. Honor and consistently uphold each family’s individual care plan, including dietary needs, sleep routines, behavioral guidance preferences, and screen time restrictions — because every family trusts you with what matters most to them.
  • Excellence in Execution - Follow established daily schedules, routines, and curriculum plans as assigned. Maintain accurate daily attendance records and required documentation; provide timely, real-time electronic updates to families. Maintain a clean, organized, and sanitary environment at all times, and adhere to all health, safety, Playcare, and resort policies and procedures.
  • Develop Great People - Plan and facilitate age-appropriate activities that support children’s cognitive, social, emotional, and physical development. Communicate openly, professionally, and respectfully with families and colleagues regarding children’s daily experiences, developmental progress, and any concerns. Participate actively in staff meetings, training sessions, and professional development opportunities.
  • Long-Term Thinking - Build consistent, trust-based relationships with children and families that make them feel seen, heard, and valued every visit. Report any safety concerns, incidents, or unusual behaviors to leadership promptly, keeping the long-term well-being of every child at the center of every decision.
  • Attitude is Everything - This role puts you in the middle of the action — busy, joyful, and occasionally unpredictable. You bring patience, warmth, and steady can-do energy to every shift, modeling calm and positivity for the children in your care and the team around you.
  • Assist children with personal care routines, including toileting, diapering, handwashing, meals, and sleep or rest periods.
  • Support meal preparation, feeding, and clean-up as needed.
  • Respond calmly and constructively to behavioral challenges using approved guidance techniques.

Qualifications

  • 18 years of age or older.
  • High school diploma or equivalent required; coursework or a degree in Early Childhood Education, Child Development, or a related field is preferred.
  • Prior experience working with children in a childcare, preschool, or educational setting preferred.
  • Current CPR and First Aid certification, or willingness to obtain upon hire.
  • Knowledge of child development principles and age-appropriate practices across infant, toddler, preschool, and school-age groups.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills, including problem-solving ability and attention to detail.
  • Patience, dependability, and a genuine passion for working with young children.
  • Ability to maintain composure and professionalism in dynamic or high-pressure situations.
  • Must successfully pass a background check.
  • Ability to lift up to 50 pounds, sit, stand, bend, kneel, and move quickly as needed to maintain child safety — and to remain active throughout the duration of a shift indoors and outdoors in varying weather conditions.

If you’re someone who lights up around kids, takes deep pride in their care, and wants your work to make a real difference every single day — Playcare at Boyne Mountain is ready for you.
