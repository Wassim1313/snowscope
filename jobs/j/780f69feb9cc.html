About the job

Overview

Sunday River is seeking full-time or part-time Base Lodge Custodians with high standards and a great work ethic to join our team. This position must perform all aspects of custodial duties, including bending, stooping, and lifting at least 50 pounds. Custodial work requires self-motivation, working with little supervision, excellent organizational skills, and the characteristics of a team player. The Base Lodge Custodian position also requires availability on nights, weekends, and holidays.


Responsibilities

  • Clean and maintain public area bathrooms, hallways, public common areas, and ski locker areas
  • Empty and clean all waste receptacles and transport waste to dumpsters
  • Vacuum, sweep, and scrub floors as necessary
  • Move furniture, dust furniture, and wash walls, ceilings, woodwork, windows, door panels, and sills as needed
  • Replace light bulbs
  • Comply with our recycling program
  • Shovel snow, sweep entries and walkways, and keep entries free of cobwebs and spiders
  • Respond positively and quickly to guest requests
  • Report maintenance issues, damages, or safety issues immediately to supervisor
  • Assist other departments in the completion of their duties as directed by supervisor

Qualifications

  • Must be at least 16 years old.
  • No previous experience is required for this position.

Compensation & Benefits

  • Wages range from $18 to $20 per hour depending on experience
  • Team Member Perks include:
  • Free Ski Pass and access to other Boyne Resorts
  • Resort discounts on dining, retail, lodging, and spa
  • Eligibility for affordable team member housing
