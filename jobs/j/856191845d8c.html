About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE: Server

LOCATION: Snowbird

STATUS: Part-time, Seasonal (Winter only)

SCHEDULE: Varies based on business needs

  • Must be able to work any day of the week including weekends and holidays.
  • EMPLOYEE HOUSING IS NOT AVAILABLE.

SUMMARY

At Snowbird, our Servers are more than order takers. They are hospitality professionals who create memorable dining experiences for guests from around the world. Servers deliver exceptional service by building genuine connections, anticipating guest needs, and ensuring every guest feels welcomed and valued.


Whether serving families, vacationers, conference attendees, or locals, Servers play an essential role in creating the outstanding experiences that make Snowbird a premier mountain destination.


WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Welcome guests with a friendly, professional, and engaging attitude.
  • Create a positive dining experience by anticipating guest needs and providing personalized service.
  • Present menus, daily specials, and beverage offerings with enthusiasm and product knowledge.
  • Accurately take and enter food and beverage orders.
  • Deliver food and beverages in a timely manner while maintaining service and presentation standards.
  • Monitor guest satisfaction throughout the dining experience and address questions or concerns promptly.
  • Process cash and credit card transactions accurately and responsibly.
  • Maintain cleanliness, organization, and readiness of dining and service areas.
  • Assist with setting and clearing tables according to restaurant standards.
  • Restock supplies and support inventory replenishment as needed.
  • Collaborate with kitchen staff, bartenders, hosts, and fellow team members to ensure seamless service.
  • Support opening, closing, and side-work responsibilities.
  • Follow all health, safety, sanitation, and responsible alcohol service guidelines.
  • Participate in training opportunities and team meetings.
  • Support special events, banquets, and resort operations as needed.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

  • High School Diploma or equivalent preferred.
  • Previous restaurant, hospitality, or customer service experience preferred but not required.
  • Outstanding communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment.
  • Positive attitude with a commitment to exceptional guest service.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team.
  • Professional appearance and demeanor.
  • Food Handler Permit and Alcohol Certification, or ability to obtain upon hire.
  • Must be at least 21 years of age.

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Must be available to work a flexible schedule that may include mornings, evenings, weekends, holidays, and peak business periods.
  • Scheduling will vary based on operational and business needs.

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Ability to stand and walk for extended periods.
  • Ability to lift, carry, and move up to 25 pounds.
  • Frequent bending, reaching, lifting, and carrying trays, dishes, and supplies.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced, high-volume environment with varying noise levels.
  • Must be able to navigate crowded dining and service areas safely and efficiently.
  • Travel to Snowbird Resort in winter conditions.
  • May be required to stay overnight in the event of weather-related road closures.

WHY WORK HERE

At Snowbird, work comes with incredible mountain perks and the opportunity to be part of a team that creates unforgettable guest experiences every day.

Benefits include:

  • Complimentary Snowbird season pass and resort access privileges
  • Discounted and complimentary lift tickets for friends and family
  • Discounts on dining, retail, lodging, mountain school, and other resort services
  • 401(k) program for eligible employees
  • Discounts and perks through POWDR partner resorts
  • Free UTA bus transportation and Snowbird employee shuttle services
  • Opportunities for professional growth and development
  • A fun, energetic workplace in a world-class mountain environment
