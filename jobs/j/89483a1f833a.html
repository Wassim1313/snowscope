About the job

Overview

The Brighton Store is located at the front of the Brighton Loop and serves as a one stop shop for day visitors, area residents, and multi-day guests. The Store offers a fryed food, soda bar, ice cream machine and a wide variety of outdoor apparel.

Join us for a season of satisfaction from a rewarding job in the “greatest snow on earth” lifestyle in the beautiful Wasatch Mountains.


Responsibilities

  • Be a friendly face greeting visitors from around the globe!

  • Become an expert in the goods sold at The Store and be able to offer assistance and advice to shoppers

  • Accurately maintain a cash draw and charge guests correctly for purchases

  • Complete opening and closing duties, cleaning, and restocking of goods as assigned

  • Work with and assist your teammates with creating an execeptional experience for our guests

Qualifications

  • 21+ years old
  • Current food handlers permit
  • Ability to lift up to 25 lbs unassisted
  • Passion for guest service and the hospitality industry
  • Able to remain calm, cool and collected under pressure
  • Flexible availability during the ski season (including weekends, evenings and holidays)

Perks:

  • $10.00 All Access Brighton Season Pass
  • FREE UTA ski bus access
  • Discounted Brighton lift tickets for friends and family
  • Access to discounted lift tickets at multiple ski areas
  • Employee discounts at all Brighton outlets and all Boyne Resorts
  • Discounted membership at Momentum Indoor Climbing gym
  • Access to ExpertVoice, Liberty Mountain, and Outdoor Prolink retail sites
