Job Summary:

Beaver Creek Club is one of eight Signature Clubs within Vail Resorts. Ideally situated at the base of Beaver Creek Mountain, the Club offers an exceptional blend of premier amenities, world-class skiing, and exclusive recreational and social experiences for members and their guests. Our Food & Beverage team delivers outstanding member experiences through breakfast service, Wine & Cheese events, après offerings, and personalized hospitality. We are seeking individuals with a positive, team-oriented attitude who are passionate about hospitality and excited to collaborate with colleagues in a fast-paced, member-focused environment. The ideal candidate is enthusiastic, dependable, and committed to creating memorable experiences that reflect the exceptional standards of Beaver Creek Club.

Eligible for the Staff Recognition Fund bonus, subject to meeting program requirements and eligibility criteria in effect at the time of payout.

Job Specifications:


  • Starting Wage: $22.00/hr - $29.22/hr
  • Employment Type: Winter Seasonal 2026/2027
  • Shift Type: Full Time and Part Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: Yes

Job Responsibilities:

  • Prepare and present a high-quality continental breakfast for Club Members, including baking, light food preparation, fruit cutting, plating, and garnishing in accordance with culinary team standards.
  • Monitor buffet quality, freshness, and temperature to ensure food safety and Club standards are maintained.
  • Assist with the preparation, presentation, and service of food offerings for Wine & Cheese, Après, and other Club events.
  • Prepare and organize breakfast items, workstations, and service areas for the following day's operations as directed by the culinary team.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and sanitary kitchen, dish area, and all back-of-house workspaces; complete breakdown, dishwashing, trash removal, cleaning, reset, and end-of-shift procedures in accordance with Club standards and food safety requirements.
  • Follow all food safety, sanitation, and health department regulations.
  • Greet members and guests by name whenever possible and provide personalized, exceptional service.
  • Communicate member preferences, special requests, and service concerns to the management team.
  • Work collaboratively with culinary, housekeeping, and Club operations teams to deliver an outstanding member experience.
  • Assist with inventory counts, organize and put away deliveries, and notify management of low stock levels.
  • Support special member events, holiday breakfasts, and Club functions as needed.
  • Perform other duties as assigned.

Job Requirements:

  • Must be able to work flexible hours, including mornings, weekends and holidays – required.
  • Must be able to work in fast-paced environment – required.
  • Must be willing to assist in other areas of the Club as needed – required.
  • Must be able to stand on feet and be moving for extended periods of time – required.
  • Must be able to lift 35 lbs. – required.
