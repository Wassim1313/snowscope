Job Summary:

The Quick Service Team Member is responsible for providing exceptional guest service while preparing, serving, and maintaining food and beverage offerings in a fast-paced resort environment. This role contributes to an outstanding guest experience by delivering quality products, maintaining cleanliness standards, ensuring food safety compliance, and supporting efficient outlet operations.

This position requires a positive attitude, strong teamwork, and the ability to perform effectively during high-volume service periods, weekends, holidays, and peak ski season.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $20.00 /hr
  • Employment Type: Winter Seasonal 2026/2027
  • Shift Type: Full Time and Part Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 16 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

Guest Service

  • Greet guests in a friendly, professional, and welcoming manner.
  • Provide prompt and courteous service while maintaining a positive attitude.
  • Answer guest questions regarding menu items, promotions, and resort offerings.
  • Resolve guest concerns or escalate issues to a supervisor when necessary.
  • Create a positive dining experience that enhances the overall resort visit.

Food & Beverage Service

  • Prepare and serve food and beverages according to established recipes and standards.
  • Ensure food is properly portioned, presented, and served at the correct temperature.
  • Replenish product displays and serving stations throughout the shift.
  • Maintain speed of service standards during high-volume periods.
  • Follow all food handling and sanitation procedures.

Cleanliness & Safety

  • Maintain a clean and organized work area at all times.
  • Regularly clean dining areas, counters, equipment, display cases, and guest seating areas.
  • Follow all health, sanitation, and food safety guidelines.
  • Properly handle and store food products.
  • Report safety concerns, equipment issues, or maintenance needs immediately.

Inventory & Product Management

  • Restock supplies, food products, condiments, and beverages as needed.
  • Assist with receiving and stocking deliveries.
  • Practice proper product rotation using FIFO (First In, First Out) procedures.
  • Help minimize waste by following portion and storage standards.

Team Work:

  • Work collaboratively with coworkers and supervisors to ensure smooth operations.
  • Support other outlets or workstations as business demands require.
  • Assist with opening, closing, and shift change procedures.
  • Participate in training and team development activities.

Job Requirements:

Required Qualifications

  • High school diploma or equivalent preferred.
  • Previous restaurant, retail, hospitality, or customer service experience preferred but not required.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong communication and guest service skills.
  • Basic math skills for cash handling.
  • Ability to follow procedures and recipes accurately.
  • Dependable attendance and punctuality.

Physical Requirements

  • Stand and walk for extended periods (up to 8 hours).
  • Lift, carry, and move up to 50 pounds.
  • Bend, reach, stoop, and perform repetitive motions.
  • Work in hot, cold, and noisy environments.
  • Ability to work weekends, holidays, evenings, and peak business periods.

Key Competencies

  • Guest Focus
  • Teamwork
  • Food Safety Awareness
  • Attention to Detail
  • Communication
  • Adaptability
  • Time Management
  • Positive Attitude

Preferred Certifications

  • ServSafe Food Handler Certification (or ability to obtain after hire).
  • Alcohol service certification where applicable.
