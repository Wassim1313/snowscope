About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE:               Bartender

LOCATION:     71 Restaurant, located inside the Cliff Lodge at Snowbird

STATUS:          Seasonal (Winter Only) 

SCHEDULE:     Shifts vary, may be morning or late evening, weekends, holidays, depending on business levels.

  • Must be able to work any day of the week including weekends and holidays.
  • EMPLOYEE HOUSING IS NOT AVAILABLE.

SUMMARY

Do you thrive in a fast-paced environment, enjoy connecting with people, and take pride in crafting exceptional beverages and guest experiences? Snowbird is looking for enthusiastic, service-focused Bartenders to join our Food & Beverage team.


As a Bartender, you'll play a key role in creating memorable experiences for guests from around the world while working in one of the most iconic mountain destinations in the country. You'll prepare and serve quality beverages, provide outstanding hospitality, and help create a welcoming atmosphere that keeps guests coming back.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Create and serve alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages according to established recipes, presentation standards, and Utah state liquor laws.
  • Deliver exceptional guest service by welcoming guests, making recommendations, and personalizing the dining experience.
  • Share menu knowledge, daily specials, and beverage offerings while using suggestive selling techniques to enhance the guest experience.
  • Anticipate guest needs and respond promptly and professionally to requests, questions, and concerns.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and fully stocked bar area throughout service.
  • Accurately process guest transactions, handle cash and credit card payments, and complete end-of-shift procedures.
  • Follow all food safety, sanitation, and responsible alcohol service standards.
  • Work collaboratively with servers, hosts, kitchen staff, and fellow bartenders to deliver seamless service.
  • Assist with opening, closing, restocking, and side work duties.
  • Help foster a positive, inclusive, and team-oriented work environment.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

  • Previous bartending experience preferred; experience in a high-volume restaurant, bar, or resort environment is a plus.
  • Must be at least 21 years of age.
  • Strong knowledge of beverage preparation, guest service, and responsible alcohol service practices.
  • Ability to learn menus, specialty cocktails, wine selections, and beverage offerings.
  • Excellent communication, interpersonal, and customer service skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment.
  • Comfortable handling cash, operating point-of-sale systems, and performing basic math calculations.
  • Ability to remain calm, friendly, and professional under pressure.
  • Food Handler Permit, Alcohol Server Certification (TIPS or equivalent), and any required local certifications must be obtained and maintained.

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Shifts vary, may be morning or late evening, weekends, holidays, depending on business levels.
  • Scheduling will vary based on operational and business needs.

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Ability to stand and walk for extended periods.
  • Frequently lift, carry, and move items weighing up to 25 pounds.
  • Ability to bend, reach, kneel, grasp, and perform repetitive motions throughout the shift.
  • Must be comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with fluctuating guest volumes.
  • May be required to remain overnight on occasion due to weather or canyon road closures.
  • Ability to work in varying mountain and winter weather conditions while commuting to and from Snowbird.

WHY WORK HERE

At Snowbird, work comes with incredible mountain perks and the opportunity to be part of a team that creates unforgettable guest experiences every day.

Benefits include:

  • Complimentary Snowbird season pass and resort access privileges
  • Discounted and complimentary lift tickets for friends and family

  • Discounts and perks through POWDR partner resorts
  • Free UTA bus transportation and Snowbird employee shuttle services
  • Opportunities for professional growth and development
  • A fun, energetic workplace in a world-class mountain environment
