What Will I Be Doing?

The Full-Time Snow School Trainer exemplifies our Snow School culture of creating revolutionary guest experiences and uniting the instructors around purpose and fun. Working closely with the Technical Trainer and Snow School leadership team, this role is responsible for delivering on-snow instructor training, supporting departmental orientation and maintaining quality assurance across our Snow School lessons. With a full time on-snow presence, this position will focus on instructor development, coaching and supporting our beginner learning areas to ensure exceptional guest experiences and high-quality instruction.

  • Lead and deliver instructor training sessions in alignment with CSIA, CASI, PSIC and resort standards, including being on our seasonal training calendar (January 4 – March 12).
  • Mentor and coach instructors through ongoing feedback, on-hill support, certification preparation and professional development opportunities.
  • Work collaboratively with Snow School Supervisors to support our beginner learning areas, providing on-snow coaching and quality assurance to maintain consistent teaching standards and positive guest and instructor experiences.
  • Collaborate with the Technical Trainer to identify opportunities for improvement and adapt training sessions to address instructor development needs and enhance lesson quality.
  • Support the delivery of departmental orientation, onboarding and on-snow training initiatives.

What Do We Need From You?

  • CSIA, CASI or PSIC Level 3 or 4 certification and active, current Course Conductor, Evaluator or Trainer status.
  • Proven experience delivering on-snow instructor training, with a strong understanding of teaching methodologies, technical skill development and learner-centred instruction.
  • Strong communication, coaching and mentorship skills, with the ability to provide constructive feedback and support instructor development.
  • Demonstrates a willingness to expand their knowledge and is receptive to feedback from leadership to further their own growth and expertise.
  • Post-secondary education is an asset.
  • Previous Snow School leadership experience is an asset.

BLUE MOUNTAIN RESORT COMPANY OVERVIEW

Blue Mountain Resort is Ontario’s largest mountain resort. Nestled on the shores of Southern Georgian Bay, on the traditional territory of the Anishinabek Nation: The People of the Three Fires known as Ojibway, Odawa, and Pottawatomie Nations. Only 90 minutes outside Toronto, Blue Mountain has become the four-season outdoor destination of the North. Blue Mountain is owned by Alterra Mountain Company.

ALTERRA COMPANY OVERVIEW

Alterra Mountain Company is a family of iconic year-round mountain destinations, the world’s largest heli-skiing operation, and Ikon Pass - the premier ski and snowboard season pass offering access to more than 50 iconic mountain destinations around the world.

Headquartered in Denver, Colorado and born out of a shared love of the mountains and adventure, Alterra Mountain Company exists to shape the future of mountain adventure.
