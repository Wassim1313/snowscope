About the job

Overview

Join Sunday River's Gould Academy Competition Program as a Coach! Coaches are responsible for the daily instruction and coaching of basic and advanced skiing/riding techniques and tactics in Alpine, Freestyle, Ski Cross, or Snowboard racing. Qualified candidates will have racing experience, possess effective coaching skills, and must be able to work in a team environment. Coaches are also the contact for participants' parents.


Responsibilities

  • Daily coaching of basic and advanced skiing/riding techniques and tactics in all GACP disciplines
  • Work with development team to prepare, implement, and review daily training schemes
  • Monitor athletes, ages 8-18, during scheduled training sessions, on-hill and "in-house"
  • Maintain group attendance each session
  • Be familiar with the use, maintenance, and clean-up of assigned training facilities
  • Be familiar with the use, maintenance, and storage of training related equipment
  • Ensure safety of all staff and participants
  • Assist with maintaining adequate course preparations for training and competitions
  • Abide by the GACP Operational Guidelines in the Coaches handbook
  • Abide by "Your Responsibility Code"
  • Maintain professional coaches license with United States Ski and Snowboard Association (USSS) and Maine Alpine Racing Association (MARA)
  • Assist with promotions, events and functions after hours when necessary
  • Utilize Flaik, including Check-in, Class Management, Time keeping, Bookings, and monitoring of daily schedules

Qualifications

  • Advanced Alpine, Freestyle, Snow Cross, or Snowboard competitive experience is required.
  • Competitive teaching or coaching experience, and/or previous racing experience in chosen discipline is preferred.
  • Must be an exemplary skier/rider, able to demonstrate appropriate techniques on all types of terrain (beginner, intermediate, advanced) and in all types of snow conditions.
  • Ability to work with others in a team environment.
  • To successfully perform this position, the candidate must possess a visible enthusiasm for competitive skiing/riding.
  • Must be able to ski/ride up to 6 hours per day.

Compensation & Benefits

  • Wages range from $16.50 to $25.00 per hour, depending on experience, with opportunities for professional growth and advancement
  • Team Member Perks include:
  • Free Ski Pass and access to other Boyne Resorts
  • Resort discounts on dining, retail, lodging, and spa
  • Eligibility for affordable team member housing
