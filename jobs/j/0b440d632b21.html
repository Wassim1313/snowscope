POSITION SUMMARY:


Sugarbush Resort is seeking an experienced Vehicle Maintenance Manager to oversee the daily operations of the vehicle maintenance shop. This leadership role is responsible for planning and supervising shop operations, managing budgets and inventory, and hiring, training, and leading maintenance staff. This is a full-time year-round position that pays between $65,000-80,000 annually depending on experience.


RESPONSIBILITIES:


  • Create daily task priorities, solve various mechanical problems.
  • Schedule people according to priorities and resources available.
  • Maintain budget and inventories.
  • Hire, train, supervise, and discipline employees.
  • Prioritize and plan staff assignments.
  • Provide training, advice, and/or hands on help with various maintenance tasks.
  • Assure that needed parts are ordered or in stock.
  • Communicate with other departments about their maintenance needs.
  • Routine paperwork i.e., ordering of parts, requisitions, work logs, department budget etc.
  • Create and manage long term schedule of maintenance of vehicles.
  • Ensure that all vehicles are maintained in the most cost effective and timely manner.
  • Daily supervision of staff to ensure tasks are completed correctly.
  • Handle discipline and performance appraisals.
  • Perform tasks of a vehicle mechanic.

QUALIFICATIONS:


  • Must be 18 years old with relevant mechanical experience.
  • High School Diploma required, Tech program a plus, and 2 years additional technical education.
  • 5-8 years' experience working on ski area related equipment.
  • 5 years' experience supervising employees. ASE certification required.
  • Must have a positive attitude and reliable transportation.
  • Vehicle Technical School education preferred.
  • Hours can include nights, weekends and holidays.
  • Must be able to lift 50 pounds.
  • Must be able to work in all weather conditions.
  • Must have strong basic reading and writing skills and the ability to keep records.
  • Must be able to use many types of safety equipment (eye protection, ear protection, hard hat, mask, etc.)
  • Must be able to drive or learn to drive loaders, snowmobiles, trucks, buses, and grooming vehicles.
