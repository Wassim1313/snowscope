About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE:               Restaurant Host

LOCATION:     71 Restaurant, located inside the Cliff Lodge at Snowbird

STATUS:          Schedule varies based on business needs

SCHEDULE:     Shifts vary, may be morning or late evening, weekends, holidays, depending on business levels.

  • Must be able to work any day of the week including weekends and holidays.
  • EMPLOYEE HOUSING IS NOT AVAILABLE.

SUMMARY 

Are you outgoing, welcoming, and passionate about creating exceptional first impressions? Snowbird is looking for friendly and organized Restaurant Hosts to join our Food & Beverage team.

As a Restaurant Host, you are often the first and last person guests interact with during their dining experience. You'll play a key role in creating a warm and inviting atmosphere by greeting guests, managing seating arrangements, and ensuring a smooth flow of service. Your hospitality, communication skills, and attention to detail help set the tone for an outstanding guest experience.

This is a great opportunity for someone who enjoys working with people, thrives in a fast-paced environment, and wants to be part of a fun and supportive mountain hospitality team.

WHAT YOU WILL DO 

  • Welcome guests with a friendly and professional attitude, creating a positive first impression.
  • Manage waitlists, reservations, and seating arrangements to ensure efficient restaurant operations.
  • Escort guests to their tables and introduce them to their server.
  • Present menus and provide information about restaurant offerings and daily specials when appropriate.
  • Monitor dining room flow and communicate seating needs with servers, managers, and other team members.
  • Ensure tables and dining areas are properly prepared before seating guests.
  • Assist guests with questions, requests, and concerns in a professional and timely manner.
  • Support guest satisfaction by checking in with guests and helping resolve issues when appropriate.
  • Assist servers, bussers, and other restaurant team members during busy service periods.
  • Thank guests as they depart and invite them to return.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and welcoming host stand and entrance area.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE 

  • Strong customer service skills and a passion for hospitality.
  • Positive attitude with excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to remain calm, organized, and professional in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and sound judgment when addressing guest needs.
  • Ability to multitask and effectively manage guest flow during busy periods.
  • Previous restaurant, hospitality, retail, or customer service experience preferred but not required.
  • High School Diploma or equivalent preferred.
  • Must be at least 18 years of age.
  • Food Handler Permit or ability to obtain upon hire.
  • Ability to work collaboratively with team members across restaurant and resort operations.

SCHEDULE 

  • Schedule varies based on business needs and operational demands.
  • Shifts may include mornings, evenings, weekends, and holidays.
  • Additional hours may be available during peak periods and special events.
  • Must be prepared to remain overnight on occasion due to weather conditions or canyon road closures.

WORKING CONDITIONS 

  • Ability to stand and walk for extended periods throughout the shift.
  • Ability to frequently move throughout the restaurant and assist with guest seating.
  • Occasionally lift and carry items weighing up to 30 pounds.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-energy restaurant environment.
  • Strong ability to multitask while maintaining attention to detail and guest service standards.
  • Ability to communicate effectively with guests, managers, and team members.
  • May experience varying business volumes throughout the day, including busy peak service periods.
  • Ability to work in varying mountain and winter weather conditions while commuting to and from Snowbird.
