POSITION SUMMARY

Shuttle Driver need! No CDL required. If you are looking for a full or part time gig and would like to enjoy all the perks of being a Steamboat Ski and Resort employee - apply today!

ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Driving a 14-passenger bus between the Meadows parking lot and the Gondola Square transportation center in a safe manner

  • Daily Department of Transportation (DOT) inspections

  • Provide excellent Guest Services (i.e. resort Information, directions, etc.)

  • Provide traffic control of vehicles entering and leaving parking lots, ensuring safe, steady flow, and pedestrian safety; manage parking lots efficiently by parking cars in straight, tight rows

  • Compliance with all company policies and procedures as well as any regulatory requirements

  • Other duties as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • 2 years of previous work history/education required

  • Must read, write, and speak clearly in English

  • Must have a valid driver's license and meet insurability standards

  • Must enjoy people, driving, and be outgoing and personable

  • Must present a professional, well-groomed appearance

  • Adhere to all safety policies

  • Interact and maintain positive relationships with guests, co-workers and management consistent with Steamboat Ski & Resort Company’s Service Excellence standards

  • Post offer confirmation required of driving history (past 3 years, must be free of moving violations)

  • Must have valid driver’s license without limitations or restrictions and meet company’s insurability standards (will be required to complete Motor Vehicle Record)

EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS

  • Must have a high school diploma or GED equivalent
