Job Summary:
The Hythe, a Luxury Collection Resort in Vail, is seeking a hardworking, detail-oriented team player to join our Reservations team as a Rooms Controller Team Lead. This role offers the unique benefit of being both a Vail Resorts employee and a Marriott associate, with competitive hotel discounts for employees as well as friends and family worldwide.
The Reservations Specialist is responsible for the setup, maintenance, and overall management of guest reservations prior to arrival. This role ensures a seamless pre- arrival experience by coordinating reservation details, processing deposits,communicating with guests, and preparing reservations for arrival.
Primary responsibilities include, but are not limited to, processing pre-arrival deposits, managing pre-arrival guest communications, maintaining market codes and reservation accuracy, serving as a liaison between the Reservations and Front Desk teams, and preparing daily arrivals. The Reservations Specialist also supports the day-to-day operations of the Reservations Department and assists with additional departmental tasks as needed to ensure exceptional guest service and operational efficiency.
