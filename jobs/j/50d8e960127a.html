Job Summary:

To function as a team member within Maintenance and other Departments. Ability to make competent decisions while thinking of the customers and resorts interest at all times. Use skill-based knowledge to perform assigned tasks in a professional manner at all times with focus on efficiency and quality of work. This position can cover many departments pertaining to repairs upkeep and preventative maintenance as requested. People skills planning, execution, control, communication is essential in completing tasks efficiency and keeping department informed on work status. Vail resorts core values are necessary to succeed in this function.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $22.00/hr - $27.70/hr
  • Employment Type: Year Round
  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

  • Assure that the integrity of the resort assets is maintained.
  • Abide by all safety rules and regulations from corporate, state and federal.
  • Complete preventative maintenance tasks room public areas and equipment.
  • Coordinate projects with sub-contractors when required.
  • Maintainstock for critical equipment for assigned areas.
  • Follow all safety procedures and alert supervisor to any unsafe conditions that may exist.
  • Work with other departments and area managers as required.
  • Keeps hand tools in a safe functional manner at all times.
  • Maintain job logs in FMX and update daily.
  • Maintain a validdriver license.
  • Assist in the training of entry level techs.
  • Repair and replace parts, piping valves and other fittings, dry wall application, painting and other repair and all other maintenance duties as assigned.
  • Good knowledge in general mechanics, carpentry, painting, drywall and troubleshooting.
  • Good knowledgeable with plumbing and systems, electrical, controls and kitchen equipment.
  • Knowledgeable on Lock out tag out and all other safety procedures, OSHA, EPA and MSDS.
  • Planning, organizing and controlling are key attributes.
  • Maintain good attitude and people skills even during high-pressure situations.
  • All other duties as assigned related to function.

Job Requirements:

  • 2 year: Trade school; Plumbing, electrical, mechanical. Min 2 years maintenance experience.
  • Min 2 years experience in maintenance or 2 years electrical, HVAC, plumbing experience
  • Optional certified trade. Clean driving license to be maintained.
  • Microsoft Office, Outlook, MaintainX
  • Read, write and speak English fluently
  • Public relations skills, good administrative and leadership skills
  • Ability to get things done, planning, organizing and controlling are key attributes.
