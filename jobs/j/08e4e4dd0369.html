RESORT OVERVIEW:


Sugarbush Resort is a year-round destination offering some of the best skiing and riding in the east, an award-winning ski school, golf, disc golf, an array of dining venues, and a fully appointed Health and Recreation Center. Do you want to join an all-star team dedicated to delivering an unparalleled guest experience? Make this your best season yet by joining the team at Sugarbush and taking advantage of everything Vermont has to offer.


BESIDES GETTING PAID TO WORK AT AN INCREDIBLE RESORT, WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU?:


Our investment in each employee includes a comprehensive wellness initiative, unwavering commitment to safety, growth opportunities, and a wide variety of resort benefits. Employees get to take advantage of our 4000+ acre playground including skiing, discounted golf, mountain activities, health club membership, resort discounts, and more, so it's easy to share your enthusiasm for Sugarbush with our guests.


POSITION SUMMARY:


Sugarbush Resort is seeking caring, energetic, and dependable Part-Time Caregivers to join our Sugarbush Day School team. In this role, you will help provide a safe, nurturing, and engaging environment for children ranging from 6 weeks to 6 years old, while ensuring compliance with all Vermont Non-Recurring Childcare regulations and program standards. Responsibilities include planning and implementing age-appropriate activities, supporting children's developmental growth, maintaining a safe and welcoming classroom environment, and building positive relationships with children, families, and fellow team members. Caregivers are also responsible for helping ensure that all equipment and facilities remain clean, safe, and well-maintained. We are currently hiring for part-time seasonal positions, with opportunities for growth into year-round or full-time employment for the right candidates. The starting pay $18/hour, with compensation based on experience.

Join our team and make a positive impact while working in the beautiful setting of Sugarbush Resort, where you'll help create meaningful experiences for children and families all winter long.


RESPONSIBILITIES:


  • Plan and implement activities to meet the physical, emotional, intellectual, and social needs of the children in the program for each day.
  • Carry out planned activities implemented by the Room Leader.
  • Feed the children according to schedule set forth.
  • Ensure that the equipment used daily, and facility (room area) are cleaned thoroughly and remain a safe environment at all times.
  • Be familiar with emergency procedures.
  • Ensure that children are supervised at all times and in compliance with Non-Recurring Childcare guild lines.
  • Provide opportunities for sleep and rest.
  • Provide a safe and secure environment for children to feel comfortable in.
  • Implement positive discipline when needed.
  • Clearly and effectively communicate with the children and other staff members.

QUALIFICATIONS:


  • High school education or a combination of education and experience from which equal skills and knowledge are acquired.
  • Three references in childcare.
  • One completed course in Early Childhood or Human/Child Development and at least 1-year experience in a childcare center.
  • Specialized skills in some form of activity applicable to children's interest and the school's programming.
  • Well-developed customer service skills.
  • The ability to handle customer complaints and special requests and interact effectively with customers and coworkers.
