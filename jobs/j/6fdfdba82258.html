About the job

Year Round

Classic, consistent quality from a winning team!

Deer Valley Resort is nestled in the Wasatch Mountains of Utah, in the historic mining town of Park City which has evolved into an exciting and diverse mountain ski town. Our staff consists of locals, transplants, seasonal workers from all 50 states and international students from around the globe. We are a diverse workforce where everyone feels welcome, appreciated, and valued. No matter if you are 14 or 84 years, there is a place for you in the Deer Valley family! (Last year over 1,100 staff members celebrated their fifth season or more at Deer Valley).

  • Ski perks (vary based on employment status) may include Deer Valley season pass, free skiing or riding at other Alterra Resorts and select local Utah resorts
  • Friends and family ski discounts, free staff ski lessons and discounted rentals (winter staff only)
  • Subsidized meals at Employee Dining Rooms offered for staff while on shift
  • Subsidized housing options available for seasonal full-time staff
  • Discounts for staff members at restaurants, shops, and service providers in Park City
  • Healthcare options are available for staff members
  • 401k plan with company match

PURPOSE OF POSITION: The Maintenance Manager is responsible for effectively, efficiently, and proactively maintaining the building, residences, and associated equipment to ensure a safe environment for guests, owners, and employees at the Lodges.


RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Recruit, manage, train, develop, schedule, and evaluate the performance of maintenance department staff
  • Provide direct supervision to the assistant maintenance manager, supervisor, and staff
  • Oversee completion of daily, weekly, monthly, and seasonal checklists and projects
  • Coach staff in updated maintenance practices, products, techniques, and standards
  • Provide support for department employees regarding technical problems, procedures, and obtaining parts
  • Maintain property in an “as new” condition, focusing on areas of attention with strong attention to detail
  • Ensure all safety protocols are met, documented, and kept current on an annual basis
  • Form and adhere to annual operating budgets
  • Assist with project management in formulation of capital expenditure requests up to planning, execution, and completion of the project
  • Work with and effectively communicate with outside vendors and build a strong working relationship with vendors, subcontractors, and suppliers
  • Oversee contractors and manage contracted services and research vendors to ensure best services and price value
  • Maintain appropriate inventory levels of chemicals, supplies, tools, operating equipment, and common repair parts
  • Other duties as assigned

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Thorough knowledge of a wide range of maintenance areas, including general repair work, basic construction, plumbing, refrigeration, pumps and motors, electrical, HVAC, painting, drywall repair, chiller, cooling tower, and closed loop HVAC systems
  • Experience in hiring, training, and supervising employees required
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills and the ability to work with other lodging maintenance matters
  • Safety-oriented
  • Exceptional dependability - must be willing to work a flexible schedule based on business demands and be on call as needed
  • Must be able to lift 50+ lbs.
  • Close, distance, and color vision and the ability to adjust focus
  • Able to sit, stand, crawl, kneel and adjust position as needed
  • Must have a valid Utah driver’s license and be able to operate company vehicles

DATES OF EMPLOYMENT:

  • Year Round

PAY RATE:

  • $85,000 – $95,000 annually, based on experience
