JOB TITLE: Building Maintenance Technician II
DIVISION: Maintenance
REPORTS TO: Maintenance Manager
LOCATION: Solitude Mountain Resort
CLASSIFICATION: Full-Time / Seasonal / Hourly


We are looking for someone who is motivated to maintain the interior and exterior infrastructure of Solitude Mountain Resort.


POSITION SUMMARY:

Are you looking for an amazing opportunity to help grow Solitude Mountain Resort? Apply now! Solitude is seeking an innovative individual who can effectively work independently or in teams with diverse groups. This position is responsible for maintaining all buildings and utilities, including alterations and installations involving electrical, plumbing, masonry, and general maintenance tasks that arise daily. This role includes both short- and long-term projects. We are excited to continue growing our team and offering the unique perks and benefits Solitude Mountain Resort provides.


KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Pool and Hot Tub Maintenance: Perform chemical testing and adjustments, maintain water levels, clean filters, calibrate feed systems, conduct acid washing, complete equipment repairs, and refill chemicals
  • Building Maintenance (Facilities, Lodging, and Private Homes under Contract): Perform repairs including plumbing, electrical, HVAC, carpentry, drywall, painting, and general upkeep
  • Troubleshooting and Repairs: Diagnose and repair faults in machinery, pumps, motors, lighting, and 3-phase electrical panels
  • Preventative Maintenance: Conduct scheduled inspections of infrastructure and report deficiencies to the Maintenance Manager
  • Safety and Compliance: Ensure adherence to OSHA and company safety standards, including proper PPE usage and chemical handling
  • Documentation: Accurately record daily maintenance activities, repair costs, and inventory adjustments

SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS:

  • Ability to inspect and adjust pools and hot tubs in compliance with Health Department requirements
  • Experience monitoring boilers and air movers and performing necessary repairs
  • Ability to complete work orders for resort buildings
  • Strong skills in inspecting buildings and performing general maintenance and repairs
  • Experience repairing restaurant equipment, including refrigeration systems
  • Ability to inspect, maintain, and repair exterior building features such as roofing, gutters, painting, and landscaping
  • Willingness to complete special projects as requested by Solitude Management
  • Ability to train Level I technicians and assist Level III technicians
  • Flexibility to assist other departments as needed

EDUCATION AND TRAINING:

  • Valid Driver’s License
  • 3–5 years of maintenance experience
  • Strong knowledge of building systems (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, mechanical, etc.)
  • CPO (Certified Pool Operator) certification

Please note that this job summary and outlined responsibilities reflect the general nature and level of work to be performed. It is not intended to be an exhaustive list of all duties, responsibilities, or skills required.
