Position Summary:

Seasonal position focused on preparing and cooking a variety of food for the cafe, including breakfast, lunch, and dinner options. This role requires working in a fast-paced environment and involves a strong emphasis on quality, freshness, and adherence to health and safety regulations.

Essential Functions and Major Responsibilities:

  • Execute all food preparation tasks, including chopping vegetables, portioning meats, and preparing sauces, in accordance with established recipes and quality standards.
  • Efficiently cook and assemble a variety of menu items—including breakfast, lunch, and dinner—on a cooking line. Must be proficient with a variety of cooking methods like grilling, frying, and sautéing.
  • Ensure all dishes are consistently prepared to meet the cafe's standards for quality and visual presentation.
  • Operate all kitchen equipment safely and efficiently.
  • Adhere strictly to all health, safety, and sanitation regulations, including proper food handling, storage, and cleaning procedures.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and well-stocked workstation always, to ensure smooth and efficient service.
  • Monitor inventory levels at your station and communicate any needs for supplies or ingredients to kitchen leadership.

Specific Job Skills:

License/Certifications- Idaho Food Safety Certification (training provided)

Technical- Cooking Skills/Basic kitchen skills

Physical Capabilities- Ability to stand and walk for up to 8 hours per shift. Will lift up to 25lbs frequently, up to 50lbs occasionally.

Additional- Exposed to fumes, odors, mechanical and burn hazards.
