About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.

TITLE: Housekeeper

LOCATION: Lodge at Snowbird

STATUS: Full-time, Regular

SCHEDULE: 8:00 am to 4:00 pm

  • Specific Shift: Any day of the week, 8:00 am to 4:00 pm (or until job is complete).
  • Weekend and holiday work required.

SUMMARY

As Housekeeper at the Lodge at Snowbird you will perform cleaning duties to ensure clean, orderly, attractive hotel rooms. You will obtain a list of assigned rooms that need to be cleaned. You will also assist other housekeepers as necessary to complete cleaning of the hotel rooms in a timely manner.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Clean guest rooms including making beds, dusting furniture, vacuuming, cleaning mirrors, emptying trash, scrubbing toilets, showers, sinks and floors with occasional condo kitchen cleaning.
  • Picks up spills, litter, and other items as necessary to maintain cleanliness of guest rooms and condominiums.
  • Deliver special items to guest rooms.
  • Sort, wash, and fold linen.
  • Maintain and clean housekeeping closets.
  • Perform other duties assigned by housekeeping director/supervisor.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

  • High School Diploma or equivalent preferred.
  • 6 months of job-specific work experience with demonstrated competence.
  • Must be organized and work well in a fast-paced environment.
  • Must be reliable and prompt to work.
  • Must be at least 18 years of age.
  • Demonstrate basic competency in reading, writing, speaking, simple arithmetic and interpersonal relations.
  • Must be reliable, mature, and able to work with minimal supervision.
  • Requires some exercise of judgment within policy guidelines and without supervisory approval.
  • Requires some problem solving and initiation of activities within job description and responsibility limitations.
  • Must have the ability to interact at any time with guests and other employees in a pleasant manner and maintain good working relationships with others and all departments at Snowbird.

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Specific Shift: Any day of the week, 8:00 am to 4:00 pm (or until job complete).
  • Weekend and holiday work required.

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Must be prepared to stay overnight, if necessary, on occasional times of road closures.
  • May experience unusual stress from continual work with public, winter conditions, snow night requirements and commuting to/from Snowbird.
  • Must be able to lift 30 lbs.
  • Must be able to stand for long periods of time.
  • Must demonstrate ability to bend, kneel, handle, reach, grasp and perform repetitive motions.
  • Daily work with cleaning chemicals can present some hazards and must use care with chemicals.
  • Able to push or pull beds, move chairs, and move tables.
  • Other/Additional: May experience some stress due to periodic guest relations, also during high occupancy periods.

WHY WORK HERE

  • Ski pass and comp ticket benefits
  • Discounts at Snowbird eateries, retail shops, mountain school, lodging and daycare for employees
  • Available medical, dental, vision and accident insurance – benefits are available based on position and are subject to change
  • 401k
  • Discounts with Powdr partners
  • Free transportation to work: UTA Bus, Rideshare Vans, and Employee Shuttles
