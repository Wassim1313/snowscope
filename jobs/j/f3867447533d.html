Job Summary:

The Resort Conference and Event Manager oversees planning to ensure our group/event guests have the highest quality experience possible while maintaining a profitable department. Prepares conference/event materials, writes detailed group resumes and Banquet Event Orders. Works closely with the F&B, culinary, hotel, mountain, CME & VRR teams to coordinate functions, events and all aspects of group experience (hotel events, transportation, lift tickets, ski school, racing, on mountain activities).

Job Responsibilities:



  • Acts as liaison between field sales person and customer throughout the event process (pre-event, event, post-event).
  • Oversees his/her customer experiences from file turnover through the post event phase until turnover back to sales.
  • Is actively involved in the operation through daily interaction with both the staff and guests.
  • Exhibits a professional demeanor through appearance and by maintaining a positive attitude toward all associates and guests.
  • Ensures that the guest experience is pleasant in regard to atmosphere, service and quality of food and beverage products by adhering to the resort performance standards.
  • Prepares professional correspondence for customers, internal booking reports and file maintenance. Prepares and adheres to event department budget.
  • Meets with meeting planners and conference planning managers as needed to coordinate function details.
  • Attends BEO and resume meetings. Communicates relevant information to the operational areas in an accurate and timely manner.
  • Ensures billing accuracy and conducts bill reviews with the clients prior to processing final bill.
  • Identifies operational challenges associated with his/her group and works with the hotel staff and customer to solve these challenges and/or develop alternative solutions.
  • Manages group room blocks and meeting space for assigned groups.
  • Develops strong working relationship with other department managers and directors to ensure communication and teamwork is at an optimum.
  • Complies with all resort policies, procedures and standards of operation.
  • Performs any additional tasks or projects as required.
