About the job

Woodward Park City, located just 20 minutes from Salt Lake City, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!

Woodward Park City is looking for a Seasonal Rental Technician to join our Guest Experience team. Reporting to the Guest Experience Manager, you will prepare, fit, and maintain rental equipment while providing a safe, efficient, and welcoming experience for every guest. This role is ideal for someone with mechanical aptitude, strong attention to detail, and a passion for action sports.

What's in It for You
  • Health insurance options for all employees.
  • Full access to Woodward Park City's world-class facilities.
  • Access to Snowbird and participating POWDR resorts.
  • Friends and family discounts, complimentary passes, plus food, retail, and gear discounts.
  • Growth opportunities across Woodward and POWDR.
  • A community that celebrates action sports and individual style.
What You'll Do
  • Welcome guests and provide a friendly, efficient rental experience.
  • Fit rental equipment and explain proper use and safety.
  • Inspect, tune, repair, and maintain equipment.
  • Prepare and organize rental inventory for daily operations.
  • Track equipment, maintenance, and inventory records.
  • Process rentals, reservations, and transactions accurately.
  • Complete opening, closing, cash-handling, and waiver procedures.
  • Keep the rental shop clean, organized, safe, and guest ready.
  • Support teammates and other resort operations as needed.
What We're Looking For
  • Friendly, guest-focused approach with clear communication skills.
  • Mechanical aptitude and interest in equipment tuning and repair.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to stay organized.
  • Ability to learn rental systems, equipment standards, and safety procedures.
  • Comfort working indoors and outdoors in winter conditions.
  • Ability to bend, twist, push, pull, stand for extended periods, and lift up to 50 pounds.
  • Availability to work evenings, weekends, holidays, and peak winter periods.
  • Previous rental, retail, equipment service, outdoor recreation, hospitality, or customer service experience is preferred.
  • Knowledge of action sports equipment and maintenance is preferred.
  • Comfortable using computers and digital Point of Sale (POS) systems in a customer service environment.
  • Support team members within the Guest Experience Department as needed

You make us Woodward. We are a welcoming community that celebrates individual stories, backgrounds, experiences, and identities where we all have "different" in common. We believe in the strength of representing all voices and having space for everyone. We believe in pushing boundaries and bringing our authentic selves to work while embracing uniqueness, creativity, and different points of view. Even if you don't think you meet all the qualifications listed for a job, we'd love to hear from you.
