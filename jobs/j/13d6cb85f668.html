JOB TITLE: Barista II


DIVISION: Food and Beverage

LOCATION: Little Dollies Waffle Shack


REPORTS TO: Executive Chef


CLASSIFICATION: Seasonal/ Full-Time/ Part-time/ Hourly



POSITION:


We are looking for a customer-focused Barista with strong communication, teamwork, and time management skills. Basic coffee knowledge is required, and attention to detail is essential in a fast-paced, high-volume environment. A positive attitude and commitment to great service are key.



JOB DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:


  • Preparing and serving coffee beverages to company standards


  • Managing customer orders with attention to detail and accuracy


  • Collaborating with team members to maintain smooth workflow during peak hours


  • Providing exceptional customer service and resolving guest concerns


  • Maintaining a clean and organized work area


  • Following opening, closing, and shift-change procedures


SPECIFIC JOB REQUIREMENTS:


  • Excellent listening and communication skills to effectively interact with coworkers and customers


  • Basic coffee knowledge, such as how to prepare common coffee beverages (advanced knowledge is a plus)


  • Exceptional customer service and interpersonal skills to interact in a positive manner and prevent or resolve conflicts


  • Strong teamwork abilities to work efficiently in a fast-paced and high-volume environment with other Baristas



  • Attention to Detail to manage specific customer requests and minimize errors


  • Time management and organizational skills to work efficiently and effectively in a high-volume workplace



Perks of Working Here:

Work & Play in the Mountains:

  • Embrace the stunning Wasatch Mountains.
  • Connect with fellow mountain enthusiasts.

Benefits:

  • Access to select Alterra Mountain Company Destinations.
  • Free Solitude season pass for you and eligible dependents.
  • Discounts on solitude lift tickets for friends and family.
  • Discounts on food, rentals, retail, and lessons.

Other Perks:

  • Comprehensive medical, dental, vision, and life insurance for eligible employees
  • Pro deals with outdoor brands.
  • Generous 401(k) plan with company match.
  • Free counseling service through Employee Assistance Program.
