Job Summary:

The Assistant Manager, Terrain Parks supports the planning, execution, and oversight of daily terrain park operations. This position partners with the Snow Surfaces Manager and Mountain Operations leadership team to deliver safe, innovative, and industry-leading terrain park experience while fostering employee engagement, operational excellence, and exceptional guest service.

The Assistant Manager is responsible for hiring, development and direct supervision of terrain park employees, supporting feature design and construction, ensuring compliance with all risk management standards, coordinating with cross-functional departments, and contributing to the development of a high-performing team culture aligned with Vail Resorts' mission, values, and Epic Service standards. This role will also support hiring, scheduling and employee management for all of Snow Surfaces. This role includes hands-on operational leadership and serves as the acting department leader when the Snow Surfaces Manager is unavailable.
