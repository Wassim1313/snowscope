About the job

Copper Mountain Resort is a vacation destination located 75 miles west of Denver, Colorado in the heart of the Rocky Mountains. Three vibrant, pedestrian-friendly village areas host lodging, retail outlets, restaurants, bars, and family activities. During the winter months, Copper's world-class terrain offers skiing and riding for all ages and abilities. Each summer, the mountain transforms into a playground for hikers, mountain bikers, and anyone seeking a genuine Colorado experience. Copper Mountain Resort is also home to Woodward Copper action-sports camps, programs, and training facility.

Job Title: Property Management Facilities Technician

Job Status: Year Round Full Time

Compensation and Benefits:

  • Compensation: Proposed hiring rate of $19.50 to $21.50+, actual pay will be adjusted based on experience.
  • Benefits: This position is eligible to enroll in company subsidized medical, dental, vision, flexible spending, HSA, life/disability options; season pass for employees/dependents and privileges at several other resorts. Several discount programs (i.e. food & beverage, retail/rental, tickets, etc.) also available.

Job Summary:

The Copper Association / Property Management team provides outstanding community association management and facility and maintenance services for Copper Mountain managed properties. It is the Facilities Technician’s responsibility to carry out all entry level duties in a manner to contribute to the team’s overall effectiveness. Facilities Technicians must be flexible with working schedule and be able to communicate effectively in person and via radio in the English language.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Maintain all HOA facilities (including but not limited to building, grounds, and equipment) with an emphasis on high quality common area cleaning, disinfection, and maintenance, to assure the consistent, outstanding presentation of properties under our management.
  • Common areas include common lobbies and entry ways, hallways, elevators, bathrooms, fitness rooms, garages, stairwells, saunas, steam rooms, games rooms, hot tub/pool areas, ski locker rooms, trash and recycling areas, and surrounding building grounds.
  • Deliver outstanding custodial services with a focus on cleanliness and customer service.
  • Ensure all facilities are fully operational, well-maintained, and provide a safe environment for owners, guests, and employees, and immediately report any potential safety or maintenance issues or concerns.
  • Assistance with grounds maintenance and snow removal.
  • Willingness to constructively receive and follow direction from Building Superintendents and Association Managers
  • Work cooperatively with other Copper employees and departments.

Other Duties as Assigned:

  • This job description is an overview of the scope of responsibilities for this job and is not intended to be an inclusive list of job tasks and expectations. With the evolution of POWDR-Copper Mountain, the responsibilities of this position may change. The employee must be prepared to accept new responsibilities and transfer others.
  • Functions as an ambassador of Copper Mountain, modeling and sharing the Copper “culture” with our guests and employees. Upholds the most professional image. Continually strives to exceed our guest’s expectations and create memories for our guests and staff.
