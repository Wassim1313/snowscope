Job Summary:
Stevens Pass is seeking a dependable and mechanically inclined Entry Water Treatment Plant Operator to support the operation, maintenance, and monitoring of the resort's potable water treatment and distribution systems. This position plays a critical role in providing safe and reliable drinking water for guests, employees, lodging facilities, food and beverage operations, and other resort infrastructure. Responsibilities include water treatment operations, water quality testing, SCADA monitoring, preventative maintenance, troubleshooting, regulatory compliance, and recordkeeping. Stevens Pass provides training and certification support, making this an ideal opportunity for candidates interested in developing a career in water treatment and utility operations.

Job Specifications:
  • Starting Wage: $21.00/hr. - $23.00/hr.
  • Employment Type: Year Round
  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

  • Operate and monitor water treatment equipment including pumps, motors, valves, filters, reservoirs, storage tanks, chemical feed systems, and related infrastructure.
  • Monitor and operate treatment systems through SCADA and other monitoring and control systems.
  • Conduct routine inspections of water treatment facilities, storage tanks, pump stations, and associated infrastructure.
  • Monitor water system performance and make operational adjustments in accordance with established procedures and training.
  • Assist in maintaining reliable water production and distribution throughout resort facilities.
  • Collect water samples and perform routine water quality testing including pH, chlorine residual, turbidity, temperature, and other required regulatory parameters.
  • Analyze, document, and maintain water quality test results, operational logs, and maintenance records.
  • Assist with regulatory reporting, compliance documentation, and adherence to applicable federal, state, and local drinking water regulations.
  • Perform preventative maintenance on pumps, valves, motors, chemical feed systems, plumbing systems, treatment equipment, and related infrastructure.
  • Assist with routine repairs and troubleshooting of water treatment and distribution system components.
  • Support maintenance of reservoirs, storage tanks, pumps, valves, and other utility infrastructure.
  • Identify operational abnormalities and report issues promptly to leadership.
  • Maintain treatment facilities, equipment, vehicles, and work areas in a clean, organized, and safe condition.
  • Safely operate company vehicles throughout the resort in varying weather and mountain conditions.
  • Support utility disruptions, severe weather events, and operational emergencies as needed.
  • Work effectively with other departments across the resort as part of the One Resort philosophy.
  • Stevens Pass will provide training and support to assist employees in obtaining required certifications.

Job Requirements:

  • High School Diploma or GED equivalent.
  • Valid driver's license and ability to pass a Motor Vehicle Records Check.
  • Ability to obtain and maintain a Washington State Water Treatment Plant Operator-in-Training (WTPO OIT) certification within six (6) months of employment.
  • Ability to obtain and maintain a Washington State Water Treatment Plant Operator Level 1 (WTPO 1) certification within twelve (12) months of employment.
  • Ability to work during hours outside normal business hours; work schedules vary throughout the year based on operational needs and water system requirements.
  • Ability to utilize electronic monitoring, reporting, and recordkeeping systems.
  • Strong mechanical aptitude and willingness to learn water treatment operations.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical manuals, schematics, procedures, and operational documentation.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Commitment to safe work practices and regulatory compliance.
  • Ability to regularly lift up to 50 pounds and occasionally lift up to 75 pounds.
  • Ability to work outdoors in varying weather conditions, including winter mountain environments.
  • Continued pursuit of professional development and water industry certifications is required.

Preferred Skills & Experience:

  • Previous experience in maintenance, mechanical systems, utilities operations, facility operations, construction trades, or other hands-on technical environments preferred but not required.
  • Experience working with pumps, motors, plumbing systems, mechanical equipment, automated control systems, or SCADA systems preferred.
