POSITION SUMMARY

The Retail Associate is responsible for providing excellent customer service, while selling merchandise at one of our village store locations. The Retail Associate will contribute to the customer experience, ensuring each customer leaves satisfied. The ideal Retail Associate will have a personal passion or professional experience within the outdoor industry and recreational activities. Our village store locations that currently have openings include, Burton, The North Face, First Run, & Mountain Sweets. Seasonal full-time and part-time positions available through out our retail portfolio. This role is compensated at $20.00/hour.


Recruitment for this role will be ongoing through December.


ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Assist the customer in finding the right product for their needs and desires
  • Accurately and efficiently completing customer transactions
  • Accurate cash handling and computer procedures
  • Merchandising the store as directed by manager, supervisor or lead
  • Answer the telephone according to Stratton’s telephone standard
  • Follow daily opening & closing procedures for your location
  • Participate in all retail/rental & corporate training programs
  • Know the product and services offered in your location as well as other Retail/Rental locations and the Resort as a whole
  • Continually exceed our guests’ expectations and create memories for both guests & employees

QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILL AND ABILITY REQUIREMENTS

  • Must be 16+ years of age
  • Previous retail, or customer service experience, preferred
  • Previous recreational industry experience, a plus
  • A passion for the outdoor industry, a plus
  • Strong computer literacy
  • Self-motivated
  • Strong communication, and organizational skills

PHYSICAL DEMANDS AND WORKING CONDITIONS

This position may be required to work evenings, weekends and holidays.
