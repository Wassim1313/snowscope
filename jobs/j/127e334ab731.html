About the job

Eldora is the backyard winter mountain playground for Greater Denver, Boulder County, and Colorado's northern Front Range. Eldora is located just outside of the charming mountain town of Nederland, Colorado, about 20 miles west of Boulder and less than 50 miles from downtown Denver.

Eldora strives to deliver Colorado’s Front Range the most memorable outdoor adventures to locals and out-of-town visitors. Eldora Mountain Resort employs up to 700 employees at peak season and has 680 acres of lift-accessible terrain for skiing and snowboarding, including a Woodward Mountain Park experience, and a world class Nordic Center.

Position Summary: Lift Operators at Eldora ensures the safe and efficient operation of ski lifts while providing excellent guest service. This role involves assisting guests with loading and unloading, monitoring lift functions, and maintaining a safe environment at the lift area. Lift Operators are key to creating a positive mountain experience by being attentive, friendly, and safety-focused in all interactions with guests and coworkers.

Essential Functions/Major Responsibilities:

  • Monitors lifts and personnel for Tramway compliance, this includes all signage and licensing, normal and emergency procedures, opening, operating, and closing procedures.

  • Maintains daily log books and related documents.

  • Participates in all phases of the job: opening, operational hours, and closing lifts.

  • Very thorough and accurate on all paperwork.

  • Exhibits support for Customer Service initiatives.

  • Must be flexible with schedule to accommodate opening and closing needs and special events.

  • Responsible for compliance with mountain safety practices to minimize injuries and unsafe practices.

  • Responsible for compliance with Tramway Safety Board and OSHA regulations.

  • Maintain the safe and continuous operation of the ski lifts.

  • Assist passengers in the safe loading and unloading of lifts, while providing excellent customer service by interacting with guests.

  • Maintain load and unload ramps and areas around all lift stations by shoveling and/or raking snow.

  • Organize the maze to provide orderly and friendly access to the lift and manage crowds for efficient loading.

  • Be familiar with all policies, procedures and rules regarding operating and riding a ski lift and ensure that these are followed by staff and guests.

  • Maintain a neat clean appearance including personal presentation, lift equipment and the surrounding area.

  • Works well in a team environment.

  • Adheres to all company safety policies and procedures, actively contributing to a safe working environment for self, coworkers, and guests.

  • All employees are expected to support Eldora’s corporate responsibility commitment to protect the environment by participating in our Sustainable Transportation Program. This includes choosing alternative transportation, carpooling, using the employee shuttle, and avoiding onsite parking on weekends and peak days. These actions help reduce Scope 3 emissions, lead by example, and ensure adequate parking for our guests.

  • Must be able to work weekends and holidays as required by resort operations.

Specific Job Skills:

  • High school degree or equivalent.

Physical Demands:

  • Bending, reaching, climbing, pushing, pulling & prolonged standing.

  • Eye/hand coordination & visual concentration on equipment.

  • Concentrated reading and writing.

  • Must be able to lift and carry items up to 50 lbs. 

  • Variation in temperature from hot to cold.

  • Be able to work on the mountain, which consists of being primarily outside and requires prolonged periods of standing and walking on slippery and/or uneven snow or icy surfaces.
