About the job

Overview

As a Snowmaking Foreman, you will oversee all day to day aspects of the snowmaking department and ensure daily goals and objectives are being hit safely and efficiently.


Responsibilities

  • All responsibilities of a Snowmaking Supervisor
  • Perate, inspect, and maintain all aspects of the snowmaking system alongside the Snowmaking Manager
  • Plan, coordinate, and supervise all year-round snowmaking, in conjunction with the Snowmaking Manager
  • Ensure effective communication between shifts, team leadership, and other Mountain Operations departments
  • Assist Snowmaking Manager in handling snowmaking personnel and related paperwork according to resort guidelines for hiring, employee evaluations, disciplinary procedures, and setting schedules for the snowmaking department
  • Continually operate the snowmaking system to maximium utilization based off system availabitlty
  • Take advantage of coachable moments for all levels of the snowmaking department and be alongside the team in the challenges and triumphs of the department
  • Stay positive, focused, and constructive when faced with setbacks, chalenges, and while working in an ever-changing environment
  • Operate as a servant leader
  • Produce high team member morale, motivation and guest satisfaction in an atmosphere of continuous improvement
  • Assist in teh end of season and yearly perfomance reviews of all snowmaking personnel
  • Act in place of the Snowmaking Manager when required

Qualifications

  • 5+ years of snowmaking experience (Preferred)
  • Strong communication, leadership and team builiding skills, skking/riding ability, must be able to work in extreme weather conditions (winter/summer)
  • Valid driver's license with no restrictions
  • Able to handle and manage confidential information
  • General knowledge of the ski industry and lift operations
  • Positive, solutions oriented leadership
  • Is flexible with hours and days of work

Compensation & Benefits

  • Wages range from $28-$32 per hour, depending on experience, with opportunities for professional growth and advancement
  • Team Member Perks include
  • Season Passes for self and dependents
  • Resort discounts for retail, dining, lessons, rentals, lodging and spa
  • Access to other Boyne Resorts
  • Gear allowance for full-time Team Members
  • Eligibility for affordable Team housing
