Position Summary (What you’ll do!)

You'll work alongside experienced technology professionals to support the systems and infrastructure that keep the resort operating smoothly.

Essential Functions/Major Responsibilities

  • Provide technical support for resort employees across Guest Services, Ski & Ride School, Rentals, Retail, Food & Beverage, Lodging, and Administration.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve hardware, software, printing, and connectivity issues.
  • Assist with the deployment, configuration, and maintenance of desktops, laptops, mobile devices, and tablets.
  • Support Wi-Fi and network connectivity troubleshooting across resort facilities.
  • Assist with point-of-sale systems used in retail, rental, ticketing, and food and beverage operations.
  • Help maintain technology inventory, asset tracking, and equipment lifecycle records.
  • Assist with user onboarding, account provisioning, and device setup.
  • Support endpoint management initiatives using Microsoft Intune and Microsoft 365 technologies.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation, knowledge base articles, and user guides.
  • Participate in seasonal technology deployments and winter operations readiness projects.
  • Support conference rooms, digital signage, guest-facing systems, and business applications.
  • Collaborate with infrastructure, networking, cybersecurity, and support teams on technology initiatives.
  • Perform other duties as assigned.

​

What You’ll Learn

  • Enterprise IT support and service management.
  • Networking and wireless infrastructure.
  • Microsoft 365 and Intune administration.
  • Endpoint deployment and management.
  • Cybersecurity fundamentals and best practices.
  • Hospitality and resort technology systems.
  • Technical documentation and operational processes.
  • Project planning and technology implementations.

​

What We’re Looking For


Required Qualifications

  • Currently pursuing a degree or certification in Information Technology, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Networking, or a related field.
  • Basic understanding of computer hardware, operating systems, and software applications.
  • Familiarity with Microsoft Windows and Microsoft 365 products.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Basic knowledge of networking concepts such as Wi-Fi, switches, and IP addressing.
  • Experience installing or supporting computer hardware and peripherals.
  • Familiarity with macOS, mobile devices, or endpoint management platforms.
  • Interest in outdoor recreation, hospitality, or resort operations.

Work Environment

  • This is not a desk-only position. You'll work throughout the resort supporting technology in offices, guest service areas, retail locations, restaurants, rental shops, lodging facilities, and mountain operations environments.
  • Ability to lift and move equipment up to 40 pounds.
  • Ability to walk between resort facilities and work in varying environments.
  • Comfortable working indoors and outdoors as needed.
  • Flexible schedule that may include weekends, holidays, and peak operational periods.

Why Join Mt. Bachelor

You'll gain hands-on experience supporting the technology behind exciting mountain destinations while working alongside experienced professionals who are invested in your development.
