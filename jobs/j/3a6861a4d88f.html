About the job

Woodward Tahoe (Boreal and Soda Springs), located in the beautiful Sierra Nevada mountain range, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!


KEEP THE MOUNTAIN MOVING


We are looking for an experienced Vehicle Maintenance Technician to maintain and repair the vehicles and equipment supporting Boreal and Soda Springs. This is a hands-on opportunity for a skilled mechanic who enjoys complex troubleshooting, takes pride in dependable repairs, and wants to work on a diverse fleet in an active mountain environment.


You’ll perform advanced diagnostics, preventative maintenance, and repairs on gasoline and diesel engines, electrical systems, hydraulics, brakes, fuel systems, hydrostatic systems, and small-engine equipment. You’ll work independently, share your expertise with developing technicians, and help keep our fleet safe, reliable, and ready for operation.


WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU


  • $25 - $35 per hour, based on experience, qualifications, and certifications.
  • Full-time, year-round employment.
  • Medical, dental, and vision insurance plans.
  • Flexible paid time off and a 401(k) with company match.
  • Annual performance bonus incentive program.
  • Complimentary access to participating POWDR resorts.
  • Development and advancement opportunities across Boreal, Woodward, and POWDR.

WHAT YOU’LL DO


  • Perform preventative, seasonal, and corrective maintenance according to manufacturer specifications and company standards.
  • Diagnose and repair gasoline and diesel engines, electrical systems, hydraulics, hydrostatic systems, brakes, fuel systems, and related components.
  • Troubleshoot complex mechanical and electrical problems and complete reliable repairs with minimal supervision.
  • Safely operate and test vehicles and equipment to diagnose concerns and verify completed work.
  • Complete emergency repairs and provide on-call support when operational needs require it.
  • Maintain accurate service records covering inspections, repairs, parts, maintenance, and equipment history.
  • Read and apply technical manuals, wiring diagrams, hydraulic schematics, diagnostic results, and manufacturer service information.
  • Complete minor welding, fabrication, and equipment-modification projects.
  • Help train less-experienced technicians and employees on equipment maintenance, safe operation, and vehicle check-out procedures.
  • Maintain a safe, organized, and efficient shop while following company policies and operating procedures.

WHAT YOU BRING


  • At least three years of professional experience repairing vehicles, heavy equipment, fleet equipment, or comparable machinery.
  • Strong knowledge of gasoline and diesel engines, electrical systems, hydraulics, brakes, fuel systems, and diagnostic procedures.
  • Proven ability to independently diagnose complex mechanical problems and return equipment to safe operating condition.
  • Ability to interpret technical manuals, wiring diagrams, hydraulic schematics, and manufacturer service information.
  • Strong attention to detail, sound judgment, and the ability to manage changing priorities.
  • A collaborative approach and willingness to share technical knowledge with others.
  • A valid U.S. driver’s license and acceptable driving record.
  • A personal toolbox with the metric and SAE tools needed for routine fleet work; Boreal provides required specialty tools.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS


  • ASE, manufacturer, automotive, diesel, or heavy-equipment certifications.
  • Fleet maintenance experience in a ski resort, construction, municipal, transportation, or agricultural environment.
  • Welding and fabrication experience.
  • Commercial driver’s license or previous experience training and mentoring mechanics.

PHYSICAL AND SCHEDULING REQUIREMENTS


This position works in both shop and outdoor mountain environments. The work involves vehicles, machinery, tools, fuels, lubricants, changing weather, and uneven or snow-covered terrain.

Candidates must be able to:

  • Lift and carry at least 50 pounds
  • Frequently stand, bend, reach, push, pull, and climb
  • Safely operate fleet vehicles and equipment after completing required training
  • Work outdoors in changing temperatures and weather conditions
  • Work weekends, holidays, early mornings, evenings, extended hours, or on-call assignments when operations require it
