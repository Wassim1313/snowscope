About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE: Service Bartender

LOCATION: Forklift Restaurant at Snowbird Center

STATUS: Seasonal position (Winter only)

SCHEDULE: Shifts vary, depending on business levels.

  • Must be able to work any day of the week including weekends and holidays.
  • EMPLOYEE HOUSING IS NOT AVAILABLE.

SUMMARY

Join Snowbird's Food & Beverage team and help create memorable guest experiences at one of Utah's premier mountain destinations. As a Service Bartender, you will prepare high-quality beverages, support restaurant operations, and provide exceptional service in a fast-paced environment. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys hospitality, takes pride in quality and consistency, and thrives as part of a collaborative team.


WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Prepare and serve alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages according to established recipes, quality standards, and Utah state liquor laws.
  • Deliver friendly, professional, and efficient service that exceeds guest expectations.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and fully stocked bar area throughout each shift.
  • Learn and communicate menu offerings, beverage selections, daily specials, and promotions.
  • Utilize suggestive selling techniques to enhance the guest experience and support business goals.
  • Anticipate guest needs and respond promptly and professionally to questions, concerns, and requests.
  • Accurately process guest orders, payments, and financial transactions using the point-of-sale system.
  • Complete required opening, closing, and side-work duties to support smooth restaurant operations.
  • Follow all food safety, sanitation, and workplace safety standards.
  • Collaborate with servers, bartenders, kitchen staff, and management to ensure exceptional service and efficient operations.
  • Maintain compliance with all local, state, and company alcohol service regulations.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

We're looking for team members who are passionate about hospitality, enjoy working with people, and take pride in delivering exceptional service.

  • High school diploma or equivalent preferred.
  • Previous bartending, restaurant, hospitality, or customer service experience preferred.
  • Must be at least 21 years of age.
  • Food Handler Permit, Utah Alcohol Server Certification (Tips/On-Premise Training), and Salt Lake County Sheriff Work Card, or the ability to obtain required certifications.
  • Strong customer service, communication, and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to multitask and remain organized in a fast-paced environment.
  • Basic math skills and ability to accurately handle financial transactions.
  • Ability to learn menus, recipes, wine selections, and beverage offerings.
  • Demonstrated professionalism, reliability, and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team.

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Schedule is based on business levels and operational needs.
  • Shifts may include mornings, afternoons, evenings, weekends, holidays, and peak business periods.
  • Additional hours may be available during holidays, special events, and busy seasons.

WORKING CONDITIONS

Physical Requirements

This position requires frequent movement and active participation throughout the shift.

  • Stand and walk for extended periods of time.
  • Frequently bend, reach, kneel, twist, grasp, and perform repetitive motions.
  • Lift, carry, and move supplies, beverage products, and service items weighing up to 25 pounds or more.
  • Transport trays, glassware, and service equipment safely through busy restaurant environments.

Work Environment

  • Fast-paced restaurant and bar environment with regular guest interaction.
  • Moderate to high noise levels during peak service periods.
  • Exposure to varying temperatures between kitchens, service stations, storage areas, and guest spaces.
  • May experience periods of high guest volume requiring multitasking and effective prioritization.

Additional Requirements

  • Must be prepared to remain overnight on rare occasions due to canyon road closures or severe weather conditions.
  • Must maintain all required certifications and comply with company policies regarding food safety, alcohol service, appearance standards, and professional conduct.

WHY WORK HERE

At Snowbird, work comes with incredible mountain perks and the opportunity to be part of a team that creates unforgettable guest experiences every day.

Benefits include:

  • Free UTA bus transportation and Snowbird employee shuttle services
  • Opportunities for professional growth and development
  • A fun, energetic workplace in a world-class mountain environment
