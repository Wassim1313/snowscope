About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE: Barista – Side Door

LOCATION: The Side Door at Snowbird Center

STATUS: Part-time, Seasonal

SCHEDULE: Includes weekends and holidays

SUMMARY

As a barista you will be responsible for taking orders, communicating with the kitchen and other team members, handling food and beverages, and ensuring an accurate account of all transactions, whilst demonstrating commitment to guest satisfaction.


WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Greets guests and employs suggestive selling techniques to enhance the guest experience and to increase company revenues.
  • Take orders and politely repeat them back to customer to ensure satisfaction is met
  • Address customer’s questions about menu items in a professional manner
  • Handle food and beverages that are requested by customer
  • Responsible, as assigned, for food preparation and presentation
  • Monitor and help manage the inventory of the kitchen and counter and make sure the food is stocked appropriately
  • Ensures standards of appearance and sanitation are maintained daily including busing tables and maintaining cleanliness of dining areas. Maintains good safety practices of employees and guests throughout the property. Follows state policies and regulations for serving alcoholic beverages in a responsible manner.
  • Must demonstrate competency in reading, writing and arithmetic with accuracy in math. Must be able to learn menus, be proficient in handling large sums of money and demonstrate ability to multi-task, e.g., ring several checks at once, serve several guests at once while maintaining interactions between other staff and guests.
  • Ability to handle, process, maintain and protect guest personal information, including credit card information, in compliance with all company or department policies and procedures according to PCI rules and requirements. This includes privacy and confidentiality, reporting, storage and disposal of sensitive information.  Will not share or disclose any guest personal or financial information to anyone unless authorized by the guest, manager or company or unless disclosure is permitted within departmental policies and procedures.
  • Knowledge and understanding of PCI DSS, including the safe and secure handling and maintenance of personal, financial and credit card information of guest/customers. Requires some exercise of judgement within policy guidelines and without supervisory approval. Additionally requires problem solving and initiation of activities within job description and a strong understanding of other Snowbird operations.
  • Assist with other restaurant positions as needed, including busing/cleaning

WHAT YOU WILL NEED DO GET THE JOB DONE

  • High School Diploma or equivalent
  • Barista experience is required
  • Great listening and speaking skills
  • Food Handler Permit and Alcohol Certification
  • Strong organizational skills
  • Must be at least 18 years of age
  • A positive attitude and ability to work well under pressure

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Schedule varies based on business needs

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Must be prepared to stay overnight, if necessary, on occasional times of road closures.
  • May experience unusual stress from continual work with public, winter conditions, snow night requirements and commuting to/from Snowbird.
  • Must be able to stand for long periods of time.
  • Work is routine in pleasant, comfortable surroundings. 
  • Must be able to lift 25 lbs.
  • Must have the ability to interact at any time with guests and other employees in a pleasant manner and maintain good working relationships with others and all departments at Snowbird.
  • Must demonstrate ability to bend, kneel, handle, reach, grasp and perform repetitive motions.

WHY WORK HERE

At Snowbird, work comes with incredible mountain perks and the opportunity to be part of a team that creates unforgettable guest experiences every day.

Benefits include:

  • Complimentary Snowbird season pass and resort access privileges
  • Discounted and complimentary lift tickets for friends and family
  • Discounts on dining, retail, lodging, mountain school, and other resort services
  • 401(k) program for eligible employees
  • Discounts and perks through POWDR partner resorts
  • Free UTA bus transportation and Snowbird employee shuttle services
  • Opportunities for professional growth and development
  • A fun, energetic workplace in a world-class mountain environment
