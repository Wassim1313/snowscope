Job Summary:


Service is the foundation of everything we stand for at Vail Resorts and our Snowmakers are no exception to creating this experience of a lifetime. Responsible for the manufactured snow production, Snowmakers are creative and tech savvy professionals laying the groundwork for an epic snow season.



Job Specifications:


  • Starting Wage: $25.25/hr - $30.61/hr


  • Employment Type: Summer Seasonal 2026


  • Shift Type: Part Time hours available


  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age


  • Housing Availability: No


Job Responsibilities:


  • Operate all transportation vehicles as per the standard operating procedures


  • Understand and teach proper snowmaking quality checks


  • Assist snowmaking lead with day-to-day operations


  • Understand and operate snowmaking pumps, compressors, manual and automated snowmaking guns


  • Maintain snowmaking equipment and buildings as directed


  • Ability to complete assigned tasks while unsupervised


  • Other duties as assigned



Job Requirements:



  • Valid driver’s license


  • Previous experience operating/maintaining heavy machinery; snowmaking experience preferred


  • Must be able to communicate fluently in English


  • Ability to work outdoors in alpine environments


  • Ability to work a flexible schedule including nights, holidays, and weekends


  • Ability to lift and carry up to 50lbs




The expected pay range is $25.25/hr - $30.61/hr. This is the pay range we reasonably expect to pay for this position. Individual compensation decisions are based on a variety of factors.
