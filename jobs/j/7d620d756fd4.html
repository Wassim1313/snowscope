POSITION SUMMARY:

The Lodging Maintenance Technician is a hands-on, guest-facing role on The Vintage Hotel’s three-person maintenance team, reporting to the Lodging Maintenance Supervisor. Technicians interact regularly with hotel guests, coworkers, and other departments, and keep the hotel and its grounds safe, functional, and looking their best year-round. This team is part of a larger group of 12 maintenance technicians across Winter Park Resort Lodging. To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily, with or without reasonable accommodation.

WHO WE’RE LOOKING FOR


We’re looking for a dependable, service-minded technician who enjoys solving problems and takes pride in a job well done. You’ll work closely with your supervisor and with Housekeeping and Front Desk staff to get rooms back in service quickly.

  • Hotel or hospitality maintenance experience preferred
  • Commercial pool and spa experience a plus
  • Water mitigation and restoration experience a plus


ESSENTIAL DUTIES:


  • Performs routine maintenance and repairs on HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and structural components
  • Completes preventive maintenance tasks according to the PM schedule
  • Completes assigned work orders and logs work in the electronic work order system
  • Accurately records hours and submits timecards in Workday
  • Conducts inspections to identify maintenance issues, fixing what can be resolved on the spot and reporting larger problems to the supervisor
  • Makes repairs such as patching drywall, painting, replacing light fixtures, fixing leaks, and replacing broken hardware
  • Performs routine pool and spa checks, including water chemistry testing, as directed by the supervisor
  • Responds promptly to maintenance requests and emergencies, including water leaks, and takes part in the on-call rotation
  • Communicates room status and repair updates to the supervisor, Housekeeping, and Front Desk to support room moves and quick turnarounds
  • Assists with installing and relocating furniture, fixtures, and equipment
  • Operates and maintains hand and power tools safely, and helps keep the workshop clean and organized
  • Helps keep shop inventory stocked and reports low supplies to the supervisor
  • Performs grounds maintenance, including snow removal, landscaping, and trash removal, to keep walkways safe and grounds well kept
  • Follows safety regulations and company policies, and takes part in regular safety meetings and training

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS


  • General: Valid Colorado driver’s license and a clean motor vehicle record; willingness to complete a background check as a condition of employment, if requested; flexibility to work varied shifts, including weekends, holidays, and on-call hours
  • Technical Knowledge: Ability to perform basic plumbing, electrical, carpentry, and general repairs; knowledge of maintenance safety procedures
  • Communication: Clear, friendly communication with guests and coworkers; regular status updates to the supervisor
  • Blueprint & Manual Reading: Ability to read blueprints, schematics, and technical manuals
  • Problem Solving: Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail; works well independently and as part of a team

EDUCATION & EXPERIENCE:


  • High school diploma or GED required
  • Technical certification or vocational training in a relevant trade (HVAC, electrical, plumbing) preferred
  • Minimum of two years of building maintenance or related experience
