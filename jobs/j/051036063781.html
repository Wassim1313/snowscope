Job Responsibilities:
  • Receive, inspect, and process incoming inventory, deliveries, and shipments while maintaining accurate records in company systems
  • Maintain inventory accuracy across multiple warehouse locations through cycle counts, audits, stock replenishment, and inventory organization
  • Monitor inventory levels and coordinate purchasing, receiving, and distribution activities to support resort operations
  • Safely operate forklifts, pallet jacks, company vehicles, and other equipment used in warehouse and logistics operations
  • Transport inventory, supplies, materials, and equipment to departments and locations throughout the resort
  • Provide warehouse counter service and support internal departments with inventory requests, material issuance, and delivery needs
  • Receive, sort, track, and distribute packages, freight, and mail for employees, guests, and business units across the resort
  • Coordinate outgoing shipments, carrier pickups, and vendor deliveries through USPS, UPS, FedEx, freight carriers, and local suppliers
  • Manage fuel inventory, monitor fuel usage, maintain fuel keys, and support fueling system operations
  • Monitor and maintain clean, organized, and secure warehouse facilities, storage areas, and inventory locations
  • Fill, inspect, handle, and transport propane cylinders while following all applicable regulations and safety procedures
  • Collect and coordinate environmental compliance activities, including required water sampling and documentation
  • Ensure compliance with company policies, OSHA standards, PPE requirements, and all safety regulations
  • Maintain clear communication with employees, vendors, and resort departments to support efficient operations and excellent service
  • Contribute to team goals and resort success by supporting special projects and performing additional duties as assigned

Education
  • High school diploma or GED or equivalent

Preferred Experience
  • Basic computer skills
  • 1 year previous work experience
  • 3 years driving vehicle with clean record
  • Valid Driver’s License good driving record & obtain forklift operator’s certificate within three months of hire date
  • Must pass the operating a propane dispenser quiz and be issued a certificate of instruction within 3 months of hire date.
