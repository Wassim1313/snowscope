About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE: General Gritts Assistant Manager

LOCATION: Snowbird Center

STATUS: Year-Round, Full-Time, Hourly

SCHEDULE: Varies


  • Must be able to work weekends, holidays, and a flexible schedule based on business needs.

SUMMARY

Support the General Gritts Manager in overseeing daily operations for a busy, multi-faceted store featuring a deli, grocery, liquor, and retail space. This role focuses on assisting with team leadership, maintaining high standards for service and cleanliness, and ensuring smooth operations across all departments.


WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Assist the Manager in supervising and supporting a team of up to 20 employees, including cashiers and deli staff.
  • Help with hiring, training, scheduling, and coaching team members to deliver excellent guest service.
  • Support store operations including inventory control, ordering, merchandising, and maintaining cleanliness standards.
  • Monitor and report on labor, food, and supply costs to help meet budget goals.
  • Participate in regular inventory counts and assist with ordering processes.
  • Ensure compliance with food safety, alcohol handling, and company policies.
  • Work floor shifts regularly, providing hands-on customer service and acting as a resource for staff.
  • Collaborate with the Manager and other leaders to improve product mix, promotions, and overall guest experience.
  • Uphold Snowbird’s standards for professionalism, appearance, and guest interaction.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

  • 1–3 years of experience in retail, grocery, or food service, preferably in a leadership or supervisory role.
  • Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to support and motivate a team.
  • Basic experience with inventory, ordering, and scheduling preferred.
  • Ability to safely operate kitchen equipment, including meat slicers and other appliances.
  • Familiarity with or willingness to learn YellowDog (inventory management), Coupa (procurement), Square (point of sale), and Microsoft Office tools.
  • Ability to manage sensitive guest information in compliance with company policies.
  • Strong communication, organization, and problem-solving skills.
  • Must be at least 21 years of age.

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Schedule varies and may include early mornings, evenings, weekends and holidays.

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Must be prepared to stay overnight during road closures.
  • May experience high guest volumes, tight deadlines, winter weather, and frequent interruptions.
  • Frequent standing, lifting, and multitasking in a fast-paced environment.

WHY WORK HERE

  • Free ski pass and comp ticket benefits
  • Discounts at Snowbird restaurants, retail shops, lodging, daycare, and Mountain School
  • Medical, dental, vision, and accident insurance (based on position)
  • 401(k) retirement plan
  • Discounts with Powdr partner resorts
  • Free employee transportation: UTA bus, rideshare vans, Alta Shuttle and Snowbird Employee Shuttles
