Job Summary:

The Senior Manager: Area Spas & Fitness is responsible for leading the spa, fitness, wellness, and retail operations at The Arrabelle at Vail Square and The Lodge at Vail. The position is accountable for the guest and employee experience, financial and operational performance, service quality, safety, staffing, facility standards, and business growth across both locations.

This leader develops and executes operating strategies that improve revenue, profitability, labor productivity, retail performance, and guest satisfaction. The position also identifies opportunities to align processes, share resources, and create greater consistency and efficiency between the two spa operations.

This is a dynamic, hands-on leadership role requiring strong business acumen, sound judgment, learning agility, and the ability to balance the distinct needs of two luxury resort spa operations. The successful leader builds high-performing teams, develops productive cross-functional relationships, and translates business strategy into consistent operational execution.

Job Responsibilities:



  • Provide strategic and operational leadership for spa, fitness, wellness, and retail operations across The Arrabelle at Vail Square and The Lodge at Vail.
  • Own the operating and financial performance of the assigned spa and fitness operations, including revenue, profitability, labor, operating expenses, retail sales, and productivity.
  • Develop and execute annual business plans, budgets, forecasts, staffing plans, and operating priorities.
  • Analyze financial and operational results, identify performance gaps, and implement actions that improve revenue, profitability, service, and efficiency.
  • Establish and maintain consistent luxury service standards, operating procedures, and quality expectations across both properties.
  • Lead the recruitment, hiring, training, scheduling, coaching, performance management, and development of spa and fitness leaders and team members.
  • Build staffing models that align employee coverage with business demand while supporting service quality and financial objectives.
  • Develop strategies to recruit and retain qualified therapists, estheticians, fitness professionals, and support staff.
  • Identify and implement shared staffing, services, resources, and operating practices across the two locations when doing so improves business performance or the guest experience.
  • Oversee treatment programming, fitness offerings, retail merchandising, inventory management, scheduling, reservations, and related operating systems.
  • Develop and execute strategies to increase treatment utilization, retail sales, guest capture, repeat visitation, and ancillary revenue.
  • Partner with Sales, Marketing, Revenue Management, Finance, and Resort Operations to create promotions, packages, partnerships, wellness programming, and group opportunities.
  • Monitor guest feedback, service results, and operating metrics; lead service recovery and continuous-improvement efforts.
  • Partner with Engineering and property leadership to maintain spa and fitness facilities, equipment, and guest-facing spaces.
  • Identify capital and facility needs and support the planning, prioritization, budgeting, and execution of approved projects.
  • Ensure compliance with company policies and applicable health, safety, sanitation, licensing, and operational requirements.
  • Develop strong working relationships with property leaders and cross-functional partners at each resort.
  • Demonstrate and reinforce Vail Resorts’ values through leadership decisions, employee interactions, and guest service.
  • Maintain high ethical and professional standards and serve as a positive example for the team.
  • Perform other duties as assigned.

Job Requirements:

Position Requirements

  • Demonstrated financial acumen, including budgeting, forecasting, labor management, expense control, revenue generation, and accountability for business results.
  • Demonstrated ability to lead and develop employees across multiple teams or operating locations.
  • Strong understanding of luxury guest service, spa operations, retail merchandising, and wellness programming.
  • Ability to interpret financial and operational data and translate findings into measurable action plans.
  • Ability to establish priorities, manage competing demands, and maintain attention to detail in a seasonal resort environment.
  • Strong communication, relationship-building, influencing, and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively within a matrixed organization and across property and corporate functions.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications and the ability to learn relevant spa, scheduling, purchasing, and financial systems.
  • Ability to speak, read, and write English in a professional manner.
  • Ability to work onsite and maintain schedule flexibility based on business and guest-service needs.

  • Multi-property, multi-unit, or area leadership experience preferred.
  • Luxury hotel, resort, or destination-spa experience preferred.
  • Experience overseeing spa retail, treatment operations, fitness programming, or wellness-related business development preferred.
