POSITION SUMMARY:

Join the team at Il Rifugio, Arapahoe Basin’s premier alpine restaurant and the highest dining destination in North America. Here, every shift is an opportunity to deliver exceptional service, stunning views, and memorable moments in a truly unique setting.

As a Server, you’ll be a key part of our Food & Beverage team, helping guests enjoy a refined, European-inspired dining experience in the heart of the Rockies. You’ll take pride in providing warm hospitality, expertly crafted food and beverages, and seamless service—all while working in one of the most breathtaking locations imaginable.

This position will start in mid-December and end in April.

Hourly pay range: $15.00/hour + Tips

ESSENTIAL DUTIES:

  • Welcome guests, seat tables, and deliver top-tier service with a personal touch

  • Serve food, wine, beer, and specialty coffee drinks with confidence and care

  • Maintain clean, organized service areas and follow opening/closing procedures

  • Handle cash transactions accurately and responsibly

  • Support sustainability efforts by sorting waste and educating guests on our eco-friendly practices

  • Uphold food safety standards and A-Basin’s service guidelines

  • Collaborate with your team to create a smooth, enjoyable experience for every guest

  • Other duties as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Previous serving or bartending experience (2+ years preferred)

  • TIPS certification and barista/wine/beer knowledge - preferred

  • Great organizational and multitasking skills

  • Friendly, team-oriented attitude with excellent communication

  • Must provide own, approved, non-slip footwear and personal ski/snowboard gear.

  • Available to work weekends and holidays.

PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS:

  • Use full range of body motion: bend, twist, kneel, push, pull and lift up to 50 lbs.

  • Intermediate skiing or snowboarding ability (must pass a ride test)

  • Stand and walk for 8+ hours.

  • Navigate stairs throughout your shift.

  • Withstand high altitude conditions at all times.

To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily- reasonable accommodations may be made to enable qualified individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.

WORKING CONDITIONS:

  • This role is based at high altitude (12,500 ft) in a table-service restaurant. While most work is indoors, outdoor exposure to cold and snowy conditions is possible. Expect frequent guest interaction, chairlift rides, and occasional snowmobile transport.
