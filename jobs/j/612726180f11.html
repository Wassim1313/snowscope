About the job

Overview

SIGN-ON BONUS PAYOUT RULES: Earn a $500 Sign-on Bonus! New Hire or Re-hire (must not have worked for Boyne Mountain within the past 6 months) will receive $250 Bonus after completing 30 days of employment, the 2nd payment of $250 will be paid after 90-days of employment.

As a Maintenance Technician you are responsible for completing daily work orders and service calls, and maintaining an accurate log of routine and preventative maintenance performed on Boyne Mountain Resort structures. Boyne Mountain also offers affordable onsite daycare! As a Boyne Mountain team member, there are so many awesome perks for you to take advantage of, such as: ski passes, golf passes, waterpark passes, and discounts for food, beverages, retail, daycare, and so much more! To learn more about Boyne Mountain Resort, employment perks, and employee testimonials, click here - Boyne Mountain Employment!


Responsibilities

  • Performing pool care and maintenance; boiler room water chemical testing and water softener care and maintenance
  • Conducting air conditioning and heat change over
  • Completing guest room care and maintenance
  • Performing food service equipment repair and maintenance
  • Reviewing and performing preventative maintenance on plant equipment
  • Assisting in snow removal
  • Performing infrastructure maintenance; water system inspection and maintenance
  • Maintaining accurate time and material repair billing records
  • Utilizing a motor vehicle to pick up supplies, parts and other materials as necessary

Qualifications

  • You are at least 18 years old
  • Achieved a high school diploma or general education degree (GED)
  • You are available to work evenings, weekends and holidays

  • You possess a working knowledge of general maintenance, plumbing, carpentry and electrical

  • You have a knowledge of inventory software and order processing systems is helpful

  • You are responsible for your own basic hand tools; Boyne Mountain will supply any specialty tools and equipment

  • You are able to crouch, bend, stand and/or sit in one position for prolonged periods of time
  • You possess a valid driver’s license
