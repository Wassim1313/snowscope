About the job

Overview

Lift Mechanics inspect, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair chairlifts across Sunday River’s eight peaks. The ideal candidate is mechanically inclined, safety-conscious, dependable, comfortable working outdoors in all weather conditions, and able to work independently and as part of a team.

This is a full-time, year-round position within Sunday River’s Lift Maintenance Department. Starting wages are $20.00 per hour for candidates with little to no experience in ski lift maintenance, with opportunities for advancement through training and skill development.


Responsibilities

  • Perform preventive and corrective maintenance in accordance with applicable ANSI B77.1 standards, manufacturer recommendations and specifications, and established department procedures.
  • Conduct routine inspections and evaluations of lift equipment, including daily, seasonal, and scheduled maintenance checks.
  • Troubleshoot, diagnose, adjust, repair, and rebuild mechanical, hydraulic, and electrical lift components.
  • Assist with lift maintenance projects, equipment upgrades, and major repairs.
  • Perform fabrication and welding as needed, based on qualifications and training.
  • Monitor lift performance and assist with operational decisions during high winds and adverse weather in accordance with established procedures.
  • Maintain accurate inspection, maintenance, and repair records.
  • Follow all safety procedures, including fall protection and lockout/tagout requirements.
  • Demonstrate teamwork, initiative, and accountability; assist other resort departments with snow removal, general maintenance, special events, and other duties as assigned.

Qualifications

  • Strong mechanical aptitude, a positive attitude, and a willingness to learn; prior lift maintenance experience is not required.
  • Previous mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, automotive, industrial, or equipment maintenance experience is preferred.
  • Ability to troubleshoot problems, think critically, follow directions, and work safely both independently and with a team.
  • Must be comfortable operating snowmobiles, ATVs, and UTVs in varying terrain and weather conditions, including snow, ice, and steep mountain terrain; previous experience is preferred and equipment-specific training will be provided as needed.
  • Ability to safely perform repetitive bending, stooping, climbing, and lifting up to 75 pounds.
  • Comfortable climbing and working at heights of 65 feet or more with appropriate fall protection.
  • Willingness to work outdoors year-round, including weekends, holidays, and additional hours as operational needs require.
  • Valid driver’s license required, with no alcohol-related convictions within the past three years and no more than three moving violations within the past three years.

Compenstation & Benefits

  • Starting wage: $20.00 per hour. Additional compensation and advancement opportunities depend on qualifications, experience, training, and demonstrated skills.

  • Team Works Perks include:
    • Free ski and golf passes for team members and eligible dependents, plus access to other Boyne Resorts.
    • Resort discounts on dining, retail, lodging, and spa services.
    • Benefits package includes health insurance, dental and vision insurance, 401(k), HSA match, life insurance, and paid time off (PTO).

If you enjoy hands-on mechanical work, solving problems, and being part of the team that keeps Sunday River’s lifts running safely and reliably, we encourage you to apply.
