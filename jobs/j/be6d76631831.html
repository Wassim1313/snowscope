About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE: Prep Cook (Cook 1)

LOCATION: Snowbird

STATUS: Full time, Seasonal (Winter Only)

SCHEDULE: Varied – Weekends/Holidays Required

SUMMARY

Snowbird is seeking motivated and dependable Prep Cook (Cook 1) team members to join our Food & Beverage operations. In this entry-level culinary role, you will provide essential support to kitchen operations by preparing ingredients, assisting with food production, maintaining food safety standards, and ensuring a clean and organized workspace. This position is ideal for individuals looking to grow their culinary skills while contributing to a high-quality dining experience for Snowbird guests.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Prepare ingredients by washing, chopping, slicing, measuring, and portioning food items according to recipes and production needs.
  • Assist with basic food preparation and cooking tasks under the direction of chefs and kitchen leadership.
  • Follow all food safety, sanitation, quality, and nutrition standards.
  • Stock, label, rotate, and organize ingredients and supplies to maintain efficient kitchen operations.
  • Help receive, store, and inventory food products according to established procedures.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organization of kitchen, prep, storage, and dishwashing areas.
  • Operate kitchen equipment safely and responsibly.
  • Wash dishes, sanitize work surfaces, remove waste, and complete assigned cleaning tasks.
  • Communicate effectively with chefs, supervisors, and fellow team members.
  • Support other kitchen positions and restaurant operations as needed.
  • Participate in ongoing training and skill development opportunities.
  • Contribute to a positive, team-oriented work environment focused on guest satisfaction.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

Required Qualifications

  • High School Diploma or equivalent.
  • Valid Food Handler Permit or ability to obtain one upon hire.
  • Basic understanding of food safety and sanitation practices.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to follow instructions and work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.
  • Positive attitude and willingness to learn.
  • Must be at least 18 years of age.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous restaurant, food service, or kitchen experience.
  • Experience working in high-volume food production environments.
  • Familiarity with commercial kitchen equipment and procedures.

SCHEDULE

  • Varies depending on business levels, includes weekends and holidays
  • Must be prepared for occasional overnight stays due to weather related road closures

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Ability to stand and walk for extended periods throughout a shift.
  • Ability to lift, carry, push, and pull up to 25 pounds regularly.
  • Ability to bend, kneel, squat, reach, grasp, and perform repetitive motions.
  • Comfortable working in busy kitchen environments with exposure to varying temperatures, including hot cooking areas, refrigeration units, and freezers.
  • Ability to work efficiently during periods of high guest volume and seasonal demand.
  • Must be able to commute to Snowbird Resort.
  • Employees may occasionally be required to remain onsite during severe weather conditions or road closures.
  • Must maintain positive working relationships with guests, coworkers, and employees throughout the resort.
