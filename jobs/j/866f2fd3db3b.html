POSITION SUMMARY

Responsible to develop and maintain excellent guest service in the areas of front desk, bell service, and shuttle operations. Oversees all front office and shuttle department activities at the main front desk and centralized shuttle operations. Sets policy, reviews procedures, designs and implements systems for coordination and communication with other key departments for guest / owner needs. Develops radio protocols, driver protocols, implements training procedures, hires and disciplines staff, and directly oversees the Front Office Manager. Reviews front office schedules and approves staffing needs. Develops and adheres to departmental budgets and monitors labor and department costs and charges. Assists executive team with shuttle contract negotiations, oversight of legal compliance, and reviews vendor & shuttle invoices. Performs fleet management processes related to leased & owned vehicles repairs, maintenance, filing insurance claims in event of damage/ accidents. Holds independent decision authority for guest & owner refunds and coordinates vendor access.


ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Ensures excellent guest service throughout the department.
  • Designs and implements department structure and procedures.
  • Recruits and retains a quality, customer-driven workforce.
  • Hires quality employees, trains, approves payroll, and actively participates in planning, assigning, and directing work, appraising performance, addressing complaints and resolving problems.
  • Develops and implements training procedures to assure development and retention of a quality workforce and to ensure health and safety protocols are followed by all staff. This includes initial training and orientation for new staff, as well as ongoing training for experienced staff.
  • Understands TRACK reservation system to act as back up on an as-needed basis.
  • Confers and cooperates with other department heads to ensure coordination of guest and owner services such as housekeeping, maintenance, and shuttle; willingly fulfills required task(s) if the need arises.
  • Assures adherence to management company policies and established operating and safety procedures.
  • Performs team development to create orderly systems between shuttle teams and front office teams. Greets guests and owners, maintains a secure key control system to ensure the safety of our guests, owners, and their property.
  • Manages shuttle fleet and seasonal shuttle staff, including hiring, training, and scheduling. Ensures each shuttle is maintained for safety and cleanliness.
  • Directly manage the entire fleet maintenance & repair records, needs, and ensures vehicles are safe & clean at all times.
  • Responds to insurance and police inquiries related to all shuttle and bell vehicle accidents, ensures all internal SOP’s are adhered to and completed.
  • The Manager must maintain a satisfactory driving record and valid driver’s license in order to comply with our insurance requirements of livery drivers.
  • Drives to and from properties in a safe and lawful manner, always mindful of safety and adherence to the rules of the road.
  • Communicates across all levels of the organization and companies including Resort Group, Steamboat Association Management, Mountain Resorts, Simply Steamboat, and MR Realty.
  • Compliance with all company policies and procedures as well as any regulatory requirements
  • Other duties as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • Must hold valid driver’s license and record in good standing for insurability purposes
  • 3 years of managing staff
  • 3 years of Guest Services experience
  • Communicate clearly in English, both verbal / non-verbal, and written
  • Interact and maintain positive relationships with guests, co-workers and management consistent with Steamboat Ski and Resort Company’s Service Excellence standards
  • Experience with managing a shuttle fleet, apps, and staff is a PLUS
  • Microsoft Office experience

EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS

  • Associate's degree (A. A.) or equivalent from two-year college or technical school; or six months to one-year related management experience and/or training; or equivalent combination of education and experience.
