About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.



TITLE: Massage Therapist

LOCATION: Cliff Spa at Snowbird

STATUS: Part-time or Full-time, Seasonal

SCHEDULE: Schedule varies based on business levels


SUMMARY

As a Massage Therapist at the Cliff Spa, you will provide massage services in a safe manner using appropriate and effective massage techniques while providing the guest with the highest level of customer service and professionalism to give them a world class resort spa experience.


WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Provide massage services to Cliff Spa clients; provide safe, appropriate and effective massage techniques.
  • Ascertain precautions/contradictions for massage prior to each session. Explain procedures and applies techniques appropriate to client needs and preferences.
  • Observe client reaction to massage and modifies as necessary. Solicits feedback and responds accordingly.
  • Maintain client confidentiality and sensitivity to age and gender related issues during all client interactions; report any unusual client interactions to supervisor immediately.
  • Responsible for cleaning and maintenance of massage rooms, refilling supplies, changing linens, etc.
  • Perform other related duties incidental to the work described herein.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE

  • High School Diploma or equivalent required.
  • 2+ years of job-specific work experience with demonstrated competence, preferably in a spa setting.
  • Must be at least 18 years of age.
  • Utah Massage Therapist License.
  • Knowledge and ability to provide up to 6 treatments in a row safely and competently.
  • Must have expertise in providing deep tissue or sports-oriented bodywork.
  • Ability to complete daily commission logs, do basic math and filing.
  • Must be reliable, mature and able to work with minimal supervision.
  • Exercise of judgment within policy guidelines and without supervisory approval.
  • Problem solving and initiation of activities within job description and responsibility limitations.

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Weekend and holiday availability required.
  • Shifts may vary based on business conditions.

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Must be prepared to stay overnight if necessary, on occasional times of road closures.
  • May experience unusual stress from high business volumes, deadlines, continual work with public, winter conditions, snow night requirements and commuting to/from Snowbird.
  • Must be able to lift 40 lbs.
  • Must be able to stand for long periods of time.
  • Must demonstrate ability to bend, kneel, handle, reach, grasp and perform repetitive motions.
  • Other/Additional: Must have ability to remain calm and focused on quality customer service in a sometimes stressful environment with last-minute changes, mistakes, and high expectations. Position requires an outgoing and friendly personality.

WHY WORK HERE

  • Ski pass and comp ticket benefits.
  • Discounts at Snowbird eateries, retail shops, mountain school, lodging and daycare for employees.
  • Discounts with Powdr partners.
  • Free transportation to work: UTA Bus, Rideshare vans, Canyon Transportation vans, and Employee Shuttles.

Spa-Specific Discounts

  • Complimentary spa access that includes use of pool, hot tub, fitness center, steam room, and sauna.
  • Complimentary yoga classes.
  • 30% discount on retail items.
  • $10 friend pass.
