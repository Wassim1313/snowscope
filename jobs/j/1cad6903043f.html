About the job

Overview

Our Snow Removal Crew plays a critical role in guest safety and first impressions across Big Sky Resort. This is one of the most impactful and respected teams on property—responsible for ensuring our walkways, plazas, and high-traffic areas are safe, clear, and accessible for thousands of guests every day.

This role is ideal for early risers, team players, and those who don’t mind putting in physical work outside while surrounded by some of the most breathtaking mountain views in the world. If you take pride in a job well done, enjoy working with your hands, and thrive in a high-accountability environment, we want to hear from you.

Skid steer experience is a plus, but not required. Positive attitude and reliability are key.


Responsibilities

  • Begin each workday at 4:00 AM to prepare resort areas before guest arrival

  • Shovel and sweep snow and ice from walkways, plazas, stairs, and other high-use areas

  • Set up appropriate signage and safety cones to mark hazards or closures

  • Operate snow removal tools and equipment as trained

  • Collect roadside trash and assist in basic exterior upkeep

  • Work outdoors in all winter conditions—snow, wind, sun, and everything in between

  • Must be available to work on April 27th, our end-of-season


Qualifications

  • Entry-level role with potential to grow within Mountain Operations
  • Ability to lift, push, and pull up to 50 pounds unassisted (up to 100 pounds assisted)
  • Repetitive movement and physical labor required
  • Must be punctual, dependable, and team oriented
  • Skid steer experience is a bonus, but not a requirement

Why join this crew:

  • Be a vital part of our resort’s success and guest safety

  • Work with a dependable, hardworking team that values trust and accountability

  • Stay active while surrounded by incredible mountain scenery every day

All full time seasonal team members can enjoy the following benefits:

  • FREE All Mountain Ski Pass and eligibility for Free or Discounted Tickets to other Montana Mountains and Boyne Resort properties
  • Discounted vouchers for skiing at Big Sky Resort for friends and family
  • Discounted ski/snowboard rentals with a free team member lesson
  • 25%-50% off F&B and Retail at all Boyne outlets
  • Eligibility for affordable Team Member Housing
  • Lodging discounts for friends and family
  • Discounted Lone Mountain Ranch Cross Country Ski Trail Pass
  • Wellness Day and Eligibility for PTO
  • Monthly team member events
