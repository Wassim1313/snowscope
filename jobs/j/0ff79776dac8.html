About the job

Eldora is the backyard winter mountain playground for Greater Denver, Boulder County, and Colorado's northern Front Range. Eldora is located just outside of the charming mountain town of Nederland, Colorado, about 20 miles west of Boulder and less than 50 miles from downtown Denver.

Eldora strives to deliver Colorado’s Front Range the most memorable outdoor adventures to locals and out-of-town visitors. Eldora Mountain Resort employs up to 700 employees at peak season and has 680 acres of lift-accessible terrain for skiing and snowboarding, including a Woodward Mountain Park experience, and a world class Nordic Center.

This role comes with amazing perks & benefits because we want our employees to Work Where You Play!

  • Free and discounted friends & family day passes


  • Free RTD EcoPass

Position Summary:The Terrain Park Lead will assist the Terrain Park Manager and the Terrain Park Supervisor in the overall delivery of the Terrain Park focusing on hand crew and daytime operations. This position will work closely with many mountain operations teams and play a leading role in the Terrain Park Department. This individual is responsible for overseeing day staff as well as helping with all operations of the parks. The Terrain Park Lead will also be responsible for the daily risk management of the terrain park venue including the completion of daily logs, signage, and feature maintenance. This position will assist in design, building and testing of features. The lead will be responsible for park crew safety, assisting with on mountain training and leading safety meetings.



Essential Functions/Major Responsibilities:


  • Work in partnership with the Terrain Park Manager and Supervisor to create a world class on hill product


  • Assist with all feature construction projects, special event builds and maintenance of terrain parks, half pipes and terrain based teaching areas.


  • Contribute to overall park design and layout


  • Oversee the performance, conduct and quality of work for team members during each shift.


  • Responsible for daily communication to Terrain Park Manager, Terrain Park Supervisor and Grooming Supervisor


  • Assist in creating a plan and delegating daily assignments


  • Represent department professionally and work with mountain operation departments to complete overall resort objectives


  • Talk to Terrain Park users and educate them on the proper etiquette and park safety.



  • Ensure proper branding within the Terrain Parks in accordance to the given guidelines.


  • Operate snowmobile to transport attendants and equipment to parks and pipes when/where needed.


  • Report accidents to patrol and assist when needed.


  • Maintain and repair Terrain Park equipment and maintain cleanliness of equipment storage areas.


  • Interact with park/pipe users and solicit feedback.


  • Open or close features depending on conditions.


  • Perform park/pipe daily duties (fencing, jump testing, handwork on features, dying features, etc)


  • Leads by example for all company safety policies and procedures, actively contributing to a safe working environment for self, coworkers, and guests.


  • All employees are expected to support Eldora’s corporate responsibility commitment to protect the environment by participating in our Sustainable Transportation Program. This includes choosing alternative transportation, carpooling, using the employee shuttle, and avoiding onsite parking on weekends and peak days. These actions help reduce Scope 3 emissions, lead by example, and ensure adequate parking for our guests.


  • Must be able to work weekends and holidays as required by resort operations.
