About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE: Mountain School Payroll & Admin

Coordinator

LOCATION: Snowbird

STATUS: Seasonal, Full-Time, Hourly

SCHEDULE: Variable – early mornings, weekdays, weekends and holidays


SUMMARY

The Mountain School Payroll & Administrative Coordinator plays a vital role in supporting the daily operations of Snowbird's Mountain School. This position provides administrative, payroll, and operational support to the Mountain School Manager and leadership team, ensuring accurate employee records, payroll preparation, onboarding coordination, reporting, and departmental administrative functions.


The ideal candidate is highly organized, detail-oriented, and thrives in a fast-paced environment. Success in this role requires excellent communication skills, exceptional attention to detail, strong organizational abilities, and the capacity to manage multiple priorities while maintaining confidentiality and accuracy.


WHAT YOU’LL DO


Payroll & Employee Records Support

  • Assist the Mountain School Manager with payroll preparation and payroll-related administrative tasks.
  • Review employee timekeeping records for completeness and accuracy.
  • Track payroll adjustments, missed punches, transfers, and pay-related corrections.
  • Assist with bi-weekly payroll processing and reporting.
  • Maintain accurate employee files, certifications, training records, and employment documentation.
  • Coordinate with Human Resources and Payroll to ensure employee information is current and accurate.
  • Prepare payroll reports and supporting documentation for management review.
  • Maintain confidentiality of payroll, employment, and personnel information.

Administrative & Operational Support

  • Provide administrative support to the Mountain School Manager and leadership team.
  • Assist with instructor scheduling, staffing updates, and workforce tracking.
  • Coordinate seasonal onboarding, orientation preparation, and employee documentation.
  • Prepare, organize, and maintain departmental records, forms, and correspondence.
  • Support invoice processing, expense tracking, reimbursements, and other administrative functions.
  • Assist with the development and maintenance of departmental calendars, schedules, and operational communications.
  • Prepare reports, spreadsheets, and documentation to support departmental planning and decision-making.

Employee & Department Support

  • Serve as a resource for employees regarding payroll, scheduling, onboarding, and administrative questions.
  • Coordinate with Human Resources, Payroll, and other resort departments to support Mountain School operations.
  • Provide exceptional customer service to instructors, coaches, managers, and internal stakeholders.
  • Assist with special projects, seasonal initiatives, and departmental events as assigned.
  • Support operational needs during peak periods and help ensure efficient day-to-day department functions.

WHAT YOU NEED TO GET THE JOB DONE


Experience & Education

  • 2-4 years of payroll, administrative, accounting, human resources, or related experience preferred.
  • Experience supporting large employee groups in a dynamic operational environment preferred.
  • Associate's or Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Accounting, Human Resources, or a related field preferred.

Technical Skills

  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, Outlook, and Office 365.
  • Experience with payroll, scheduling, timekeeping, or human resources systems.
  • Strong data entry skills with exceptional accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage and analyze reports, spreadsheets, and employee records.
  • Experience with Snowsports Management software such as RTP, Flaik, or similar platforms is preferred.

Core Competencies

  • Exceptional attention to detail and commitment to accuracy.
  • Strong organizational and time-management skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • High level of discretion and confidentiality when handling sensitive information.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Effective problem-solving abilities and sound judgment.
  • Self-motivated with the ability to work independently and collaboratively.
  • Flexible and adaptable to changing business needs and priorities.

Mental & Emotional Demands

  • Ability to maintain focus during extended periods of detailed administrative and computer-based work.
  • Ability to manage frequent interruptions while maintaining accuracy and professionalism.
  • Emotional resilience to navigate a dynamic seasonal environment and changing priorities.
  • Calm, courteous, and professional demeanor when interacting with employees and guests.

Physical Requirements

  • On-site presence required five days per week at Mountain School and resort locations.
  • Ability to sit or stand for extended periods while working on a computer.
  • Frequent use of standard office equipment.
  • Occasional movement between resort locations and facilities.
  • Ability to lift and carry office supplies and materials weighing up to 25 pounds.

WORK SCHEDULE

  • Variable shifts including early mornings, evenings, weekends, and holidays based on business needs.
  • Additional hours may be required during peak onboarding periods and payroll processing cycles.
  • Schedule flexibility required during the winter operating season.
  • Occasional collaboration with the sales and rental team as required.

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Primarily office-based with regular interaction with employees, managers, and resort departments.
  • Fast-paced environment with frequent interruptions and shifting priorities.
  • Requires strong attention to detail while balancing multiple responsibilities.
  • Occasional overnight stay may be required due to canyon closures or weather-related events.
