About the job

Overview

Sunday River's tuning technicians know their skis and snowboards, and provide excellent customer service along with the best equipment tunes and repairs in the business. This job is both fast-paced and detail driven. The ideal team player exudes a positive attitude, sense of ownership, safety first mindset, and an unsurpassed attention to detail no matter the task.


Responsibilities

Tune Shop Tech responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

  • Execute numerous functions related to a high-quality equipment tuning and repair shop
  • Work with customers to evaluate their equipment needs
  • Use a variety of hand tools and heavy machinery to tune and and reapair skis and snowboards
  • Ongoing safety training
  • Opening and closing a point of sale
  • Tracking and filing all paperwork
  • Maintaining a clean and organized area
  • Ordering/stocking/tracking inventory and repairs as needed

Qualifications

  • High school diploma or general education degree (GED); or one to three months related experience and/or training; or equivalent combination of education and experience
  • Previous rental shop and tuning knowledge preferred
  • Excellent customer service skills
  • Must be at least 16 years old

Compensation & Benefits

  • Wage ranges from $16 - $20 per hour, depending on experience
  • Team Member Perks include Season Pass, resort discounts, and access to other Boyne Resorts amenities.
