Job Summary:


The Team Lead Terrain Park Crew will deliver the Terrain Park experience for our employees and guests. This individual is responsible for the team's supervision, training, scheduling, and motivation. The team lead terrain park crew will perform a daily risk management assessment of the venue including the design/construction and layout of features and placement and integrity of all animation and signage.



Job Specifications:


  • Starting Wage: $23.00/hr -$30.61/hr


  • Employment Type: Winter Seasonal 2026/2027


  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available


  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age


  • Housing Availability: Yes



Job Responsibilities:


  • Training/coaching/supervising/leading of Terrain Park Attendants and Operators


  • Organize and conduct weekly safety meetings as well as document case specific Equipment and PPE Trainings


  • Lead all Terrain Park Attendants and Groomers efficiently through the daily Terrain Park Operations and nightly design and construction plan


  • Performs a daily assessment of risk, feature inspections, maintenance, and testing of all features


  • Directs leads, attendants, and operators to complete projects and general maintenance in an efficient and carefully planned manner


  • Assist Terrain Park Manager and Grooming team in Design and Construction planning for all parks, pipes, and special events


  • Assist Ski Patrol on accident investigations



  • Hiring, firing and scheduling of terrain park attendants


  • Act as liaison between Attendants and upper management and other departments


  • Assist in the development of nightly grooming plans for Terrain Parks


  • Responsible for park operations/decision making when manager is unavailable


  • Act as role model regarding employee development and guest service-related initiatives


  • Assist in all trainings for Terrain Park Attendants including the implementation of all necessary tools and equipment


  • Ongoing development of standard operating procedures and Best Practices


  • Other duties as assigned



Job Qualifications:


  • Must have 2 years of education


  • Minimum of 5 years of terrain park experience


  • Minimum of 2 years of supervisory experience


  • Must have a valid driver's license


  • Ability to work outside in alpine environment


  • Ability to lift and/or move up to 50 pounds


  • Must have own ski or ride equipment
