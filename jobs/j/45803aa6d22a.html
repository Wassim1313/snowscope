About the job

Overview

The Highlands is seeking a dedicated Call Center Agent to provide guest support from our on-site contact center. This full-time position offers the opportunity to assist guests across a variety of channels, working in an office environment handling phone, email, and chat inquiries while mastering our e-commerce platforms and reservation systems.

Responsibilities

Essential Job Functions (including but not limited to,):

  • Manage high-volume inbound calls, emails, and chat conversations simultaneously
  • Navigate multiple computer systems and databases to process complex reservations
  • Troubleshoot e-commerce transactions and online account issues
  • Document all guest interactions in Salesforce/RTP systems
  • Support Boyne Rewards program enrollment and point redemption
  • Collaborate with team members across multiple properties via internal systems
  • Maintain expert knowledge of all resort offerings through SharePoint and internal resources
  • Meet call quality metrics and response time standards
  • Provide backup support during peak periods for other Resort Services sales locations
  • Contribute to a positive team environment by participating in job sharing, and maintaining a friendly attitude in all interactions

Qualifications

Required Qualifications (knowledge, skills, certifications, education, etc):

  • High school diploma or equivalent; some college experience preferred
  • Previous hospitality, call center or phone-based customer service experience
  • Exceptional phone voice and professional writing skills
  • Advanced computer proficiency with ability to navigate 3+ systems simultaneously
  • Comfortable wearing a headset for extended periods
  • Strong focus and concentration in shared office environment
  • Ability to maintain professionalism during back-to-back calls
  • Proficient with Microsoft 365 applications.
  • Flexible schedule availability, including weekends and holidays
  • Able to problem-solve independently and adapt in a fast-paced environment
  • Familiar with CRM systems such as RTP or Salesforce

Work Environment:

  • Position requires extended periods at a computer (sitting or standing)
  • Noise levels can be high when all agents are on the phone

Position type and expected hours of work:

  • Typical 8-10 hour shifts 4-5 days a week
  • Weekends and holidays required; overtime may be needed during peak periods (especially Christmas break)
