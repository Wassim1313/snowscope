About the job

Overview

The Highlands at Harbor Springs is seeking motivated and dependable Prep Cooks to join our Food & Beverage team! Prep Cooks play an important role in delivering a great dining experience by preparing ingredients and food items for service, following recipes and food safety standards, and maintaining a clean, organized kitchen.

This full-time or part-time seasonal position offers flexible scheduling and the opportunity to enjoy the many perks and amenities that come with working at an exciting four-season resort.

Responsibilities

Essential Responsibilities & Duties;)

  • Prepare food items in advance according to menu requirements and service needs.
  • Follow recipes, preparation instructions, and cooking procedures established by the Chef.
  • Wash, peel, chop, and prepare vegetables; cut and portion meats; and prepare sauces, soups, and other menu items as needed.
  • Measure and mix ingredients accurately according to recipes and preparation guidelines.
  • Label, date, store, and rotate food items according to food safety standards.
  • Operate kitchen equipment, including ovens, grills, broilers, and other cooking appliances, safely and properly.
  • Maintain kitchen equipment, utensils, and food service items in clean and orderly condition.
  • Follow all food handling procedures, health regulations, and sanitation guidelines to maintain a safe and sanitary work environment.
  • Perform general cleaning duties, including dishwashing, mopping, waste removal, and cleaning kitchen surfaces, equipment, refrigerators, and worktables.
  • Complete closing duties, including cleaning and sanitizing equipment, properly shutting down appliances, and organizing kitchen areas, walk-in coolers, and storage spaces.
  • Assist with receiving, unloading, and storing deliveries and putting away orders.
  • Communicate absences and schedule changes promptly to the appropriate supervisor.
  • Perform additional duties and responsibilities as assigned.

Qualifications

  • Must be at least 18 years old.



  • High school diploma or GED preferred.



  • Previous kitchen experience preferred; six months is ideal.



  • ServSafe certification required or obtained within 30 days of hire.



  • Basic knife skills and knowledge of food preparation techniques.



  • Ability to multitask, follow recipes, and pay attention to detail.



  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.



  • Knowledge of food safety, sanitation, and proper food handling procedures.



  • Ability to stand for extended periods and occasionally lift up to 50 pounds.



  • Flexible availability, including weekends and holidays.
