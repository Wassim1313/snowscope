Job Summary:

Diagnose and repair common electrical and plumbing issues with minimal supervision. Perform basic interior and exterior maintenance on building structures, furniture, fixtures, and equipment. Supervise the M2 team and ensure tasks are completed effectively.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $23.00/hr - $30.61/hr
  • Employment Type: Year Round
  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: Yes

Job Responsibilities:

  • Performs duties as assigned by Assistant Manager of Building Maintenance.
  • May assist with Scheduling staff for appropriate coverage.
  • Assist and work with other Maintenance foremen as required.
  • Ensure compliance with company Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure Plan (SPCC) and all Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) on proper fuel handling and storage.
  • Ensure that all work is performed in a safe manner in a safe environment.
  • Observe all requirements for the GTLC Hazard Communication Program
  • Attends training and observes all applicable OSHA and other regulatory compliance programs and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) as required by human resources and the EH&S office. Programs include, but not limited to: permit-required confined spaces; respiratory protection; blood-borne pathogens; personal protective equipment (PPE); hearing conservation; lockout-tagout; spill prevention, control, and countermeasure plan; drivers safety; and, general proper operation of machinery and equipment.
  • Obtain all appropriate PPE for each employee to ensure maximum protection.
  • Maintenance Department staff members will be assigned other technical work within the department when maintenance crises occur within the company or work in their particular technical field is temporarily or permanently reduced.
  • Perform minor electrical, plumbing and general building maintenance.
  • Maintain adequate inventory of materials, equipment and tools required to perform routine tasks without delays.
  • Must clean up your job site and work area when finished with the assigned task.
  • Other duties as assigned

Hospitality

  • You must be committed to superior guest service and quality standards. As a team member of your department you play a key role ensuring the team’s success and achievement of meeting and exceeding our guests’ expectations. Our goal is to receive a minimum of 88% on our guest comment scores.

Environmental

  • The Grand Teton Lodge Company is entrusted with protecting the environment of Grand Teton National Park. As part of this mission you are responsible for carrying out our core environmental values: promote renewable energy, reduction of waste, protection of wildlife habitat, and environmental education. You should strive to preserve, protect and inspire your team members and our guests to join in this effort.

Health & Safety

  • You are responsible for working in a safe and healthy manner. You must be knowledgeable of the company’s work safe policies and procedures.

Job Requirements:

  • Prior supervisory experience
  • Must possess valid driver’s license, Successful completion of MVR

Other Requirements:

  • Organizational abilities
  • Capable of handling moderately strenuous activity

Preferred Skills:

  • Knowledge of Micro Main or other computerized maintenance program
  • 2 years plumbing and electrical experience
  • Basic carpentry experience
