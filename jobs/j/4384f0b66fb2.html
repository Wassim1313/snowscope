About the job

Overview

Sugarloaf is currently looking for an outgoing and fun-loving person to join our Skier Services team as a Rental Shop Cashier for the coming winter season. Rental Shop Cashiers are responsible for the fast-paced processing of ski and snowboard rental equipment sales. The right fit for this role will be energetic, outgoing and able to interact with guests in a friendly and fun manner.

This position will start at a wage of $16-20 per hour depending upon past experience and comes with great job perks. Come join our fun and supportive team!


Responsibilities

  • Make sure cash drawer is accurate at start and end of shift.
  • Data entry into computerized rental equipment system.
  • Assist guests with information about various rental, lift and ski school options.
  • Meet service level objectives and department goals as set forth by immediate supervisor.
  • Will be cross trained in all Rental Shop positions.

Qualifications

The ideal candidate for this role will:

  • be available for weekday, weekend, and holiday shifts as needed.
  • be friendly and courteous to guests and team members.
  • have a high school diploma or equivalent degree.
  • have previous retail and/or guest services experience.

Sugarloaf Team Member Benefits include:

  • Free lift pass for skiing and/or riding
  • Discounts at resort F&B and retail locations
  • Discounted ski/snowboard lessons and rentals
  • Access to Nordic trails and discounted equipment rentals at our Outdoor Center location
  • Discounted passes at other Boyne and industry partner resorts
