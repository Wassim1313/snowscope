Job Summary:

The Engineering Manager leads the daily engineering operation at The Arrabelle at Vail Square. This role oversees engineering personnel, sets priorities and staffing coverage, and coordinates maintenance and repair work across guest rooms, residences, public areas, and building systems.

The manager reports to the Director of Engineering and turns department priorities into staffing plans, preventive maintenance schedules, and work assignments. The manager tracks work quality, service response, labor, and expenses, and coordinates with hotel teams and contractors to limit disruption to guests and owners.


Job Specifications:


  • Starting Wage: $75,000.00 - $85,000.00

  • Shift Type: Full Time

Job Responsibilities:



  • Lead hiring, onboarding, scheduling, training, coaching, and performance reviews for the engineering team. Address performance concerns with the Director of Engineering and Human Resources.
  • Set staffing coverage and work priorities based on occupancy, arrivals, departures, group events, and seasonal demand. Review shift logs and set expectations for daily briefings and handoffs.
  • Run the preventive maintenance program for guest rooms, residences, public spaces, equipment, and grounds. Schedule work with Front Office, Housekeeping, and other affected teams.
  • Manage the work order system. Review response times, overdue work, repeat repairs, and preventive maintenance completion, then assign follow up.
  • Oversee maintenance of the central plant, boilers, heating and cooling, electrical and plumbing systems, kitchen and laundry equipment, pools and hot tubs, and fire and life safety systems.
  • Coordinate required inspections and service with qualified staff and contractors. Track records, deadlines, and corrective work.
  • Act on escalated guest and owner maintenance concerns. Communicate repair status and coordinate service recovery with hotel leadership.
  • Track labor, overtime, supplies, and repair spending. Review variances and support the Director of Engineering with operating budgets, forecasts, and capital planning.
  • Coordinate vendor bids, access, schedules, and approved project work. Check work quality and track completion, costs, and outstanding items.
  • Manage parts and tool inventory, review purchases within approval limits, and verify documentation for billable owner work under property procedures.
  • Train staff on safe work practices and emergency procedures. Lead the engineering response to equipment failures and building emergencies and notify property leadership.
  • Perform other duties as assigned.

Job Requirements:

Position Requirements

  • Ability to plan staffing, delegate work, coach employees, and address performance concerns.
  • Working knowledge of central plant operations, building controls, mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems, and fire and life safety systems.
  • Ability to read equipment manuals and drawings, use work order and office software, and interpret labor and expense reports.
  • Ability to explain technical issues and communicate work status to employees, guests, owners, and contractors.

Experience Requirements

  • Five years of building engineering or facilities maintenance experience, including three years supervising or managing a maintenance team.
  • Technical school, trade training, or equivalent work experience.
  • Hotel, resort, or comparable facility experience preferred.

License / Certification Requirements

  • Valid driver’s license.
  • Relevant trade or building systems certifications preferred. Maintain any credential required for assigned regulated work.
Working Conditions
  • Work onsite in guest areas, residences, mechanical spaces, and outdoor areas in winter conditions.
  • Work evenings, weekends, and holidays as needed and participate in emergency coverage.
  • Conduct property inspections and use protective equipment as required. Human Resources to confirm physical demands and lifting thresholds before posting.
