About the job

Overview

If you're someone who enjoys being outdoors, moving throughout the day, and being the first point of contact for thousands of mountain visitors—this is your chance to be a key player in delivering Big Sky Resort's signature guest experience.

Our Parking and Shuttle Attendants are the first to welcome guests when they arrive and the last to wave goodbye. This team sets the tone for the entire day by ensuring our base areas are safe, organized, and inviting. From maintaining clean and functional parking areas to operating guest shuttles, this role offers a unique opportunity to stay active, work independently, and meet new people every day.

Whether you're guiding traffic, driving a shuttle, or answering questions about the mountain, no two days are the same. Come ready to work outside in all weather conditions with a tight-knit crew who takes pride in their work and their view.


Responsibilities

  • Create an organized and welcoming experience for guests by setting up signage, cones, and walkways throughout the base area

  • Operate guest shuttles between parking lots and the Mountain Village Base Area

  • Provide helpful, friendly directions and information about Big Sky Resort and the surrounding area

  • Monitor and maintain cleanliness of parking areas and plazas throughout the day

  • Accurately collect and manage payments for paid parking areas

  • Rotate between the Big Sky Resort Base and Madison Base locations as scheduled

  • Assist with trash pickup and light equipment operations when needed


Qualifications

  • A positive attitude and strong team player mentality

  • A valid driver’s license and clean driving record

  • Comfortable working outdoors in a variety of weather conditions

  • Excellent communication and guest service skills

  • Must be able to stand, walk, lift, and move throughout your shift

All full time seasonal team members can enjoy the following benefits:

  • FREE All Mountain Ski Pass and eligibility for Free or Discounted Tickets to other Montana Mountains and Boyne Resort properties
  • Discounted vouchers for skiing at Big Sky Resort for friends and family
  • Discounted ski/snowboard rentals with a free team member lesson
  • 25%-50% off F&B and Retail at all Boyne outlets
  • Eligibility for affordable Team Member Housing
  • Lodging discounts for friends and family
  • Discounted Lone Mountain Ranch Cross Country Ski Trail Pass
  • Wellness Day and Eligibility for PTO
  • Monthly team member events
