PURPOSE OF POSITION: The Membership Development Coordinator supports the Membership Development Executive in building and serving the membership of the private club at Deer Valley. This role covers three distinct areas including but not limited to sales coordination, membership administration, and executive assistance. They manages the CRM and sales pipeline, coordinates prospect tours and follow-up, guides applicants from first inquiry through enrollment and onboarding, and serves as a polished, discreet point of contact for prospects and members. By handling the details, the Coordinator frees the Membership Development Executive to focus on long goal strategy, relationships, and sales.


RESPONSIBILITIES:

Membership Development Support

  • Maintain the CRM (Salesforce) and sales pipeline, including accurate prospect records, activity logging, and regular pipeline reports, in partnership with the CRM team
  • Track and route new inquiries and leads; schedule timely follow-up for the Membership Development Executive
  • Schedule and coordinate prospect tours, presentations, and on-mountain experiences, including logistics with the Club General Manager and Mountain Operations
  • Manage membership applications from receipt through qualification review, keeping files complete and status current
  • Prepare membership agreements and enrollment packets; track signatures, deposits, and required documents with Legal and Finance
  • Maintain the membership waitlist and keep prospective members informed of their status
  • Keep sales collateral and membership materials current, stocked, and ready for tours, in coordination with Marketing
  • Support referral programs and member-driven acquisition efforts

Member Relations & Onboarding

  • Coordinate new member onboarding and orientation with the Club General Manager, including welcome materials, account setup, and introductions
  • Draft and send member communications and updates on behalf of the Membership team
  • Maintain member records, preferences, and relationship notes in the CRM
  • Gather member feedback and support satisfaction surveys and reporting
  • Serve as the primary point of contact for prospects and newly enrolling members by phone and email, transitioning ongoing member service inquiries to Club Operations
  • Support member events and gatherings as needed

Executive Assistance & Administrative Support

  • Manage the Membership Development Executive's calendar, meetings, and follow-ups
  • Prepare meeting agendas, materials, and presentations; take minutes and track action items
  • Submit and reconcile expense reports and help track department spending
  • Maintain organized physical and digital files for prospects, members, and agreements
  • Coordinate with Finance on member billing, dues, and renewal records until a Membership Administrator is in place
  • Handle confidential member and financial information with discretion and in compliance with privacy policies
  • Perform other duties as assigned to support the Membership team and Club operations

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • 2+ years of experience in administrative, sales support, or client services roles; private club, luxury hospitality, real estate, or resort experience a plus
  • Experience with a CRM; Salesforce preferred
  • Proficient in Microsoft 365, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, and SharePoint
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills with a polished, professional, and courteous demeanor
  • Exceptional organizational skills, sound judgment, and proactive executive support; able to anticipate needs, independently manage competing priorities, and ensure consistent follow-through
  • Ability to handle confidential and sensitive information with discretion
  • Proactive approach to problem-solving and process improvement
  • Ability to work independently and in collaboration with others
  • Bachelor's degree preferred
  • Flexibility to work evenings, weekends, and holidays as business needs require, including peak sales periods and member events
  • Ability to lift, carry, or move up to 30 pounds occasionally and to move throughout the club and mountain during tours in winter conditions; skiing ability a plus

DATES OF EMPLOYMENT:
