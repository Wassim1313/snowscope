POSITION SUMMARY

The Transportation Manager provides leadership, strategic direction, and operational oversight for Steamboat Ski Resort's transportation operations, ensuring safe, efficient, and guest-focused transportation services throughout the winter season. This role is responsible for managing Parking supervisors, drivers, parking operations, shuttle operations, fleet utilization, regulatory compliance, staffing strategies, budget accountability, and guest service performance. The Transportation Manager serves as a key leader within the resort operation and is responsible for fostering a culture of safety, accountability, service excellence, and continuous improvement.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Provide overall leadership and management of the Transportation Department, including shuttle operations, parking services, and fleet utilization.
  • Develop and implement operational plans, staffing strategies, and transportation initiatives that align with departmental and resort objectives.
  • Ensure compliance with all applicable DOT, CDL, state, local, and company transportation regulations and policies.
  • Manage department supervisors and frontline staff, including hiring, onboarding, training, performance management, succession planning, discipline, and terminations.
  • Partner with Vehicle Maintenance to ensure fleet readiness, preventative maintenance scheduling, vehicle inspections, and operational reliability.
  • Oversee departmental safety programs and ensure all required training, certifications, and compliance documentation are maintained.
  • Monitor transportation demand and allocate resources appropriately to support changing operational needs.
  • Problem-solve in a highly effective manner and maintain positive relationships with guests and staff, including management, subordinates and co-workers
  • Supervise snow removal and ice build-up prevention around in and around parking lots
  • Oversee litter control in the vans and parking areas
  • Review incidents, accidents, and guest concerns, implementing corrective actions when necessary.
  • Promote and model Steamboat Ski Resort's Service Excellence Standards.
  • Create a culture of accountability, safety, teamwork, and guest service excellence.
  • Ensure effective staffing levels and workforce planning throughout the operating season.
  • Other duties as assigned.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • Previous management experience leading supervisors and frontline employees.
  • Knowledge of DOT regulations, CDL requirements, transportation safety, and compliance practices.
  • Demonstrated experience managing budgets, labor planning, scheduling, and operational performance metrics.
  • Proven leadership skills with the ability to build, develop, and retain high-performing teams.
  • Strong organizational, problem-solving, and decision-making abilities.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced seasonal operation.
  • Experience working with guest-facing operations and customer service programs.
  • Must pass all required background checks and meet company driving and insurability standards.
  • Must possess a valid driver's license without limitations that would prevent the performance of job duties.
  • Must be able to obtain and maintain a Colorado CDL with Passenger Endorsement.

EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS

  • High School Diploma or GED required.
  • Colorado CDL with Passenger Endorsement or ability to obtain within established timeframe.
  • Must meet SSRC minimum driving standards and pass motor vehicle record review.
  • Must be able to speak, read, and write English proficiently.

PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS

  • Must be at least 21 years of age.
  • Ability to lift and move up to 50 pounds. (box of ice melt)
  • Ability to work both indoors and outdoors in varying weather conditions.
  • Ability to inspect operational areas, vehicles, and facilities throughout the resort environment.
  • Ability to operate transportation vehicles and equipment as needed.
  • Expected to drive vehicles for extended periods of time often inclement weather conditions.
  • Ability to stand, walk, and navigate uneven terrain for extended periods.

WORKING CONDITIONS

Indoor/Outdoor: Regular exposure to winter weather, including snow, ice, wind, and extreme temperatures.

Hazardous Materials/Noise: Exposure to vehicle fuels, diesel exhaust fluid, cleaning products, and moderate to high noise environments.
