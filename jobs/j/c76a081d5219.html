About the job

Overview

Sugarloaf is looking for a dependable worker to join our team for the coming winter season as a second shift Hotel/Public Area Cleaning Attendant. The primary duty of a Public Area Attendant is to keep all common, office, and hotel areas clean. Essential job functions are to remove trash, vacuum, dust, clean windows and walls, mop, clean restrooms, and take care of recyclables. This position may also assist with deliveries for the housekeeping department and assist the department as needed.

This role typically works an afternoon/evening shift from 2pm to 10pm. Starting wage for this full-time winter season position is between $19-22 per hour depending upon previous experience.

If you take pride in your work and are looking for a spot on a fun and supportive team, please apply today!


Responsibilities

  • Keep all buildings, break rooms, offices, and service areas clean
  • Remove trash, vacuum, dust, clean windows and walls, mop, clean restrooms, and take care of recyclables
  • Perform deliveries as needed and when other departments need janitorial supplies
  • Assist housekeeping as needed
  • Complete daily records as required
  • Perform carpet cleaning if needed
  • Assist laundry room if needed

Qualifications

  • Must have valid Driver’s License and own vehicle
  • Must be able to work as part of a team, have good guest service skills and be safety conscious
  • Must be available for afternoon/evenings shifts and during our busy guest times (weekends/holidays included)
  • No experience necessary, we provide on the job training

Sugarloaf Team Member Benefits include:

  • Free lift pass for skiing and/or riding
  • Discounts at resort F&B and retail locations
  • Discounted ski/snowboard lessons and rentals
  • Access to Nordic trails and discounted equipment rentals at our Outdoor Center location
  • Discounted passes at other partner resorts
