About this position:

Transit bus drivers needed for the upcoming winter season in the beautiful Sierra Mountains (November-April).

  • Drivers transport guests to and from lodges, hotels, and nearby destinations.
  • Responsibilities: DMV-required inspections, paperwork, fueling, fluid checks, tire chaining in adverse weather, bus cleaning.
  • Variable schedule; some evening/night work. Part-time considered. Typically, 4 days/week, up to 10 hours/day. Enjoy 3 days off to play in the Sierra!

Requirements:

  • No more than 2 points on a current Driver’s license. Must have a current Class A or B license with passenger and air brake endorsements.
  • Safety Sensitive position subject to DOT drug and alcohol regulations.
  • Must lift 50 pounds.
  • 5 years driving experience.
  • Guest service or public relations experience preferred.
  • Professional, outgoing, and guest service-oriented demeanor.
  • Strong work ethic. Effective communication with guests, employees & management.
  • Ability to perform under pressure.
  • Follow instructions & company policies.
  • Training may be available for those without a CDL.
  • Qualified candidates only. Pay dependent on experience.
