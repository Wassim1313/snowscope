About the job

Overview

The Vehicle Maintenance Mechanic is a full-time, year-round position at Boyne Mountain Resort. The Mechanic role at Boyne Mountain is in a fast-paced, ever changing work environment from day to day. The mechanic is responsible for the preventative maintenance and repair of the Boyne Mountain fleet. This includes heavy equipment, snow grooming equipment, trucks, busses, vehicles, snowmobiles, fabrication and repair of miscellaneous small engines. The vehicle maintenance team ensures the safe performance and operation of the fleet, with minimum down-time and emergency repair at the most efficient costs to Boyne Mountain Resort while maintaining a fun, positive work environment.

Full time year round employment at Boyne Mountain Resort includes: Options for Health, Vision/Dental and Life Insurance, 401K, Paid Time Off, FREE All Access Ski/Golf Pass for yourself and spouse/dependents, Retail/F&B discounts at all Boyne outlets. Responsibilities

The Vehicle Maintenance Mechanic's typical job functions include (but not limited to):

  • Performing preventative maintenance, diagnostic troubleshooting and repairing jobs associated with the fleet

  • Assisting with ordering, inventory, parts and supplies for the repair facility’s and fleet

  • Ability to work in teams and solo to accomplish required tasks

  • Utilizing an accessible library of service, manuals, parts manuals, supply catalogs and other reference aids associated with the operation of a repair facility

  • Helping to maintain a comprehensive record system that tracks service, repair, and other needs associated with each vehicle

  • Maintaining a clean, safe and organized work environment
  • Helping to maintain communication channels to all departments regarding scheduling of repairs and maintenance
  • Mechanic must provide their own tools

Qualifications

This position may be for you if:

  • You possess a minimum of 1 year of experience in automotive and/or professional training
  • Have knowledge of all automotive systems including engine, transmission, axle, brake and electronics
  • Have achieved a High school diploma or general education degree (GED)
  • Obtained state and or other certifications are highly recommended
  • Possess a valid driver’s license
  • Fabrication skills are a bonus
