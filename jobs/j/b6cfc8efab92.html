Job Summary:

The Coffee Barista is responsible for preparing and serving high-quality coffee, espresso beverages, teas, and other menu items while providing exceptional guest service. This role ensures a clean, organized, and welcoming coffee bar environment, maintains product quality standards, and contributes to a positive guest experience.

Job Specifications:

  • Starting Wage: $12.14/hr - $16.04/hr
    • If hired into a tipped position, guaranteed to make a minimum of $20/hour, inclusive of tips
  • Employment Type: Year Round (10-month)
  • Shift Type: Full Time and Part Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No

Job Responsibilities:

  • Greet guests promptly and professionally.
  • Take food and beverage orders accurately.
  • Provide recommendations and answer questions regarding menu items.
  • Ensure guests receive timely and friendly service.
  • Resolve guest concerns or escalate issues to management when necessary.
  • Build positive relationships with regular and repeat guests.

Beverage Preparation

  • Prepare espresso-based drinks, coffee beverages, teas, and specialty drinks according to established recipes and standards.
  • Operate and maintain espresso machines, grinders, blenders, and brewing equipment.
  • Ensure beverage presentation and quality meet company standards.
  • Monitor beverage temperatures, portion sizes, and consistency.

Food Service

  • Prepare and serve pastries, sandwiches, and other light food items as applicable.
  • Follow food safety and sanitation standards at all times.
  • Maintain product freshness and proper storage procedures.

Cash Handling and Sales

  • Process guest transactions accurately using POS systems.
  • Handle cash, credit card, and digital payments responsibly.
  • Balance cash drawer and complete closing procedures as assigned.
  • Promote specialty beverages, seasonal offerings, and upselling opportunities.

Cleaning and Sanitation

  • Maintain a clean and organized work area throughout the shift.
  • Clean coffee equipment, counters, display cases, and dining areas.
  • Follow all health department regulations and company sanitation standards.
  • Complete opening, closing, and side work duties.
  • Other duties as assigned

Job Requirements:

Physical Requirements

  • Ability to stand and walk for extended periods.
  • Frequent lifting of up to 25 pounds.
  • Ability to bend, reach, stoop, and perform repetitive motions.
  • Capability to work in a fast-paced environment.

Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities

  • Knowledge of coffee preparation techniques and espresso equipment.
  • Excellent communication and guest engagement skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and quality standards.
  • Ability to multitask and prioritize tasks effectively.
  • Commitment to teamwork and hospitality excellence.

Performance Standards

  • Consistently delivers exceptional guest service.
  • Maintains quality and consistency of all beverages.
  • Follows food safety, sanitation, and brand standards.
  • Demonstrates reliability, punctuality, and professionalism.
  • Supports team members and contributes to a positive work environment.

Food Service & Merchandising

  • Prepare and serve pastries, sandwiches, and other light food items as applicable.
  • Stock and merchandise Grab & Go food items prepared by the culinary team.
  • Ensure all Grab & Go products are properly labeled, dated, rotated, and displayed according to food safety standards.
  • Communicate inventory needs and low-stock items to culinary and management teams.
  • Maintain strict adherence to FIFO (First In, First Out) inventory rotation procedures to ensure product freshness, quality, and food safety.
  • Monitor expiration dates and FFOB (First Expired, First Out/Best By Date) products to ensure items closest to expiration are sold first.
  • Remove expired, damaged, or unsellable products promptly and notify management of inventory concerns.
  • Maintain accurate food display levels and coordinate replenishment with the culinary team throughout the day.
  • Knowledge of food safety, product dating, labeling requirements, and temperature control standards.
  • Ability to maintain organized, attractive, and fully stocked Grab & Go displays while minimizing product waste.

Inventory and Organization

  • Monitor inventory levels and communicate replenishment needs.
  • Restock coffee, syrups, cups, lids, Grab & Go food items, pastries, and other supplies.
  • Coordinate with the culinary team to replenish food offerings throughout the day.
  • Assist with receiving, organizing, and storing deliveries.
  • Minimize waste by following proper rotation and portion control standards.

Job Qualifications:

  • High school diploma or equivalent preferred.
  • Previous barista, coffee shop, food service, or hospitality experience preferred.
  • Strong customer service and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
  • Basic math and cash handling skills.
  • Ability to follow recipes and operational standards.
  • Flexible schedule, including weekends, holidays, and early mornings.
