About the job

Woodward Park City, located just 20 minutes from Salt Lake City, is a year-round world-class action sports facility with a culture that comes straight from our main product: Fun. If this sounds like the kind of place you'd like to work, progress, and play, we'd love to hear from you!

Woodward Park City is looking for a Seasonal Outdoor Ticket Checker to join our Guest Experience team. Working outdoors at our mountain and skate park access points, you will help create a smooth, safe, and welcoming experience by validating tickets, passes, memberships, and guest access. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys helping people, pays attention to detail, and thrives in a fast-paced outdoor environment.

What's in It for You
  • Health insurance options for all employees.
  • Full access to Woodward Park City's world-class facilities.
  • Access to Snowbird and participating POWDR resorts.
  • Friends and family discounts, complimentary passes, plus food, retail, and gear discounts.
  • Growth opportunities across Woodward and POWDR.
  • A community that celebrates action sports and individual style.
What You'll Do
  • Welcome guests and create a positive first impression.
  • Scan and validate tickets, passes, memberships, and guest access.
  • Answer questions and provide accurate resort information.
  • Assist with guest access issues and help resolve entry concerns.
  • Monitor entry areas and help maintain a safe, organized guest experience.
  • Complete opening and closing tasks and keep work areas guest-ready.
  • Support teammates and other operations as needed.
What We're Looking For
  • Friendly, outgoing, and guest-focused with strong communication skills.
  • Comfortable working outdoors in all weather conditions and in a fast-paced environment.
  • Detail-oriented, dependable, and able to adapt to changing priorities.
  • Able to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Previous guest service, hospitality, recreation, or ticketing experience is a plus.
  • Passion for action sports and creating great guest experiences.
  • Available to work weekends, holidays, and peak operating periods.
  • Able to stand for extended periods and lift up to 50 pounds.

You make us Woodward. We are a welcoming community that celebrates individual stories, backgrounds, experiences, and identities where we all have "different" in common.
