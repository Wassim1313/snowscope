WHAT WILL I BE DOING?

We're looking for an HR Generalist who is passionate about people, thrives in a fast-paced environment, and enjoys supporting a diverse workforce in a resort setting. As part of the Human Resources team, the HR Generalist plays a key role in delivering human resources programs, employee relations support, workforce initiatives, and operational HR services that enhance the employee experience and support resort operations. This position partners with leaders and employees across the resort to ensure HR processes are efficient, compliant, and employee-focused.

  • Provides HR Operations support by responding to employee and leader inquiries, preparing employment-related documentation, and assisting with policy interpretation and routine employee relations matters.
  • Maintains HR Systems & Data through management of employee records, HRIS audits, troubleshooting Workday issues, and ensuring data accuracy throughout the employee lifecycle.
  • Coordinates and supports HR programs, training, onboarding, engagement, and performance management initiatives.
  • Supports compliance with employment legislation, company policies, and HR procedures while helping meet operational workforce needs.
  • Tracks HR metrics, generate reports, review timekeeping records, identify discrepancies, and coordinate corrections with leaders and Payroll.
  • Supports workforce compliance and immigration-related processes

WHAT DO WE NEED FROM YOU?

  • Minimum 2 years’ experience in Human Resources or equivalent leadership experience at Blue Mountain
  • Previous experience in HRIS systems (Workday an asset)
  • Strong computer skills and attention to detail required
  • HR designation (CHRP/CHRL) or in progress an asset
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities, maintain confidentiality, and support operational deadlines in a fast-paced environment

BLUE MOUNTAIN RESORT COMPANY OVERVIEW

Blue Mountain Resort is Ontario’s largest mountain resort. Nestled on the shores of Southern Georgian Bay, on the traditional territory of the Anishinabek Nation: The People of the Three Fires known as Ojibway, Odawa, and Pottawatomie Nations. Only 90 minutes outside Toronto, Blue Mountain has become the four-season outdoor destination of the North. Blue Mountain is owned by Alterra Mountain Company.

ALTERRA COMPANY OVERVIEW

Alterra Mountain Company is a family of iconic year-round mountain destinations, the world’s largest heli-skiing operation, and Ikon Pass - the premier ski and snowboard season pass offering access to more than 50 iconic mountain destinations around the world.

Headquartered in Denver, Colorado and born out of a shared love of the mountains and adventure, Alterra Mountain Company exists to shape the future of mountain adventure.
