Why Work with Us?

Each night, our team receives a requisition from all the restaurants on the mountain. As part of our vibrant team, you'll help ensure that these culinary essentials reach their destinations, come rain, snow, or sunshine.


What You’ll Do:

  • Early Starts: Begin your day bright and early, between 5-6 AM, to kick off your adventure.
  • Dynamic Duties: Split your time between the bustling warehouse and scenic mountain delivery routes.
  • Warehouse Wizardry: Manage inventory, stack boxes efficiently, and keep everything running smoothly.
  • Mountain Deliveries: Navigate the slopes and deliver food directly to the mountain’s restaurants, all while embracing the ever-changing weather.

This role is perfect for those who enjoy being active, working in a team environment, and taking on a variety of tasks throughout the day. It’s more than just a job—it’s an opportunity to be an integral part of the mountain’s daily rhythm.

Ready to hit the slopes and be the driving force behind our mountain’s culinary experience? Join us and make your mark in a role that’s as dynamic as the mountain itself!

  • Assist with inventory orders, documentation, and security.
  • Price, count, and label inventory; use forklifts and pallet jacks.
  • Handle deliveries, check for damage, and manage package sorting.
  • Perform courier duties, operate company vehicles, and ensure safety compliance.
  • Provide parts counter service, conduct inventory counts, and organize the storeroom.
  • Maintain a clean workspace and follow all safety policies.
