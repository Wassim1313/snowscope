Job Summary:

The Front Office Manager is responsible for leading all front office operations to ensure exceptional guest experiences, efficient departmental performance, and achievement of operational goals. This role currently oversees Front Desk, Night Audit, Hotel Operator and related guest-facing functions while maintaining service standards, financial performance, employee engagement, and compliance with company policies and procedures.


Job Specifications:


  • Starting Wage: $60,000.00 + annual bonus
  • Employment Type: Year Round
  • Shift Type: Full Time
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No


Job Responsibilities:



Guest Experience

  • Lead the front office team in delivering exceptional guest service and resolving guest concerns in a professional and timely manner.
  • Ensure all guests receive a warm welcome, accurate information, and efficient check-in/check-out experiences.
  • Monitor guest satisfaction scores and implement action plans to improve service performance.
  • Handle escalated guest concerns and service recovery situations.

Operations Management

  • Oversee daily front office operations, ensuring adherence to service standards and operating procedures.
  • Coordinate closely with Housekeeping, Maintenance, Reservations, Sales, and Food & Beverage teams to support seamless guest experiences.
  • Conduct daily stand-up meetings and department communications to include monthly front office meetings, 1:1 with front desk leads.
  • Ensure accurate room inventory management and availability controls.
  • Monitor compliance with credit card handling, audit, and security procedures.

Leadership & Team Development

  • Recruit, hire, train, coach, and develop front office team members.
  • Conduct regular performance evaluations and provide ongoing feedback.
  • Create employee schedules to ensure proper staffing levels while managing labor costs.
  • Foster a positive, inclusive, and engaged work environment.
  • Support employee development and succession planning initiatives.

Financial & Administrative Responsibilities

  • Manage departmental budgets and labor expenses.
  • Monitor revenue opportunities, upselling performance, and operational efficiencies.
  • Track key performance indicators including occupancy, guest satisfaction, labor costs, and productivity metrics.
  • Prepare operational reports and communicate results to leadership.

Safety & Compliance

  • Ensure compliance with company policies, safety procedures, and regulatory requirements.
  • Promote workplace safety and risk management practices.
  • Maintain confidentiality of guest and company information.
  • Participate in emergency response and incident management procedures.

Job Requirements:

Required

  • High school diploma or equivalent.
  • Minimum 3-5 years of hotel front office experience.
  • Minimum 2 years of leadership or supervisory experience.
  • Strong guest service and conflict resolution skills.
  • Excellent communication, organizational, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to work flexible schedules, including weekends, holidays, and evenings.

Preferred

  • Bachelor's degree in Hospitality Management, Business, or related field.
  • Resort or vacation ownership experience.
  • Experience with Opera or similar hotel property management systems.
