Position Summary

Oversees snow removal staff in the plowing of the Parking lot and Roads.


Essential Functions/Major Responsibilities

  • Communicate information between the manager and employees.
  • Create a work schedule for the crew.
  • Monitor payroll in Workday.
  • Establish a plan for each shift and lead operators in safe operations.
  • Ability to train employees on the safe and proper operation of equipment.
  • Watch for weather changes and equipment issues, and communicate with the manager clearly.
  • Clear parking lots and roadways of snow.
  • Clear snow from maintenance areas.
  • Clean ice and snow from equipment and complete equipment inspections each shift.
  • Tire chain building and repair for equipment.

Secondary Function

  • Assist mechanics in servicing equipment as needed.
  • Work with grooming in hauling of snow for day operations.
  • Ensure walls are cut and clean between storms.
  • Open and clear drains as the weather dictates.
  • Pull ice pack layer on roadways and parking lots.
  • Repair potholes in the lot/roads as needed.

Supervisory Responsibilities

  • Number of employees supervised: Up to 10.
  • Positions supervised: Plow operators 1 and 2.
  • Scheduling and assigning work: Assign employees to equipment and tasks, and follow up on the completion of tasks.
  • Training: Train employees on safe, proper, and efficient equipment operations.
  • Salary actions: None.
  • Disciplinary actions: Document action when needed.
  • Performance review: Mid-Season and End-of-Season reviews for operators.
  • Hiring/Termination: Work with the Fleet Maintenance & Snow Removal manager for hiring/terminations.

Interpersonal Contacts

Public Safety/Security, Grooming operations, Vehicle mechanics, Parking attendants, Lift Maintenance mechanics, Lift Services (tubing hill operations), and early arriving guests in parking lots.


Specific Job Skills

  • License/Certifications: Current and Valid Driver’s license
  • Technical: Experience with heavy equipment machinery.
  • Physical Capabilities: Able to work at night in variable weather conditions.

Education/Related Experience

  • Minimum education required: High School or equivalent
  • Minimum time in related position: 2 years either supervising staff or 2 years of heavy equipment operations
