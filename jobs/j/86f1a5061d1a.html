About the job

Overview

The Highlands at Harbor Springs is looking for caring, dependable, and enthusiastic individuals to join our team as Polar Penguins Childcare Attendants!

Polar Penguins is a unique hybrid childcare and snowsports program designed for children ages 2–4. This exciting program combines a nurturing childcare environment with age-appropriate indoor activities, outdoor snow play, and introductory ski experiences to create a fun, engaging, and memorable winter experience for our youngest guests.

As a Polar Penguins Childcare Attendant, you’ll play an important role in helping children feel safe, comfortable, and excited about their day at The Highlands. You’ll support their physical, social, emotional, and cognitive development through play, creative activities, daily routines, and outdoor adventures.

We’re looking for team members who are patient, attentive, and passionate about creating positive experiences for young children. Previous childcare experience is a plus, but a positive attitude, reliability, and a genuine love for working with children are what matter most.

This part-time, seasonal position offers flexible scheduling and the opportunity to enjoy the many perks of working at a premier Northern Michigan resort!

Responsibilities

Essential Duties and Responsibilities

  • Provide attentive supervision and ensure the safety, health, and well-being of children ages 2–4.
  • Welcome children and families, assist with check-in, and communicate professionally with parents and team members.
  • Assist with daily care needs, including feeding, diaper changes, toileting, dressing, and nap supervision.
  • Plan and participate in age-appropriate activities that promote physical, social, emotional, and cognitive development.
  • Engage children in indoor games, arts and crafts, stories, songs, and outdoor snow play.
  • Assist children with winter clothing and gear and support Snowsports instructors during outdoor activities.
  • Supervise snack and lunchtime routines and assist with setup and cleanup.
  • Maintain a clean, safe, and organized childcare environment, including sanitizing toys and surfaces.
  • Follow all childcare policies, safety procedures, and program guidelines.
  • Demonstrate patience, dependability, and professionalism while supporting a positive experience for children and families.
  • Complete daily preparation, housekeeping, and other duties as assigned by management.

Qualifications

Required Qualifications

  • Must be 18 or older with a high school diploma or GED.
  • Must pass a background check and complete required CPR, First Aid, and childcare training.
  • Must be dependable, patient, and enjoy working with children ages 2–4.
  • Must be available weekends and holidays and work in winter conditions.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Childcare experience or Early Childhood Education coursework.
  • Knowledge of child development; skiing knowledge is a plus but not required.

Physical Requirements

  • Ability to lift up to 50 pounds, bend, kneel, and sit on the floor.
  • Ability to actively supervise children during shifts of seven or more hours, indoors and outdoors.

Schedule

  • Part-time, seasonal position.
  • Typical hours: 9:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.
  • Weekends and holidays required; additional hours may be available during peak seasons.
