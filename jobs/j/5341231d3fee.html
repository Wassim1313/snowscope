About the job

Overview

Behind every crisp sheet, fluffy towel, and perfectly pressed uniform at Boyne Mountain is a Laundry Attendant who takes real pride in their work. As a Laundry Attendant, you’re responsible for the operation of our laundry facility — keeping things running smoothly and ensuring that every item that comes through meets the quality standards our guests expect.

Everything we do at Boyne Mountain is guided by our core values — L.E.A.D.S.: Long-Term Thinking, Excellence in Execution, Attitude is Everything, Develop Great People, and Serve First. Even in a role that happens behind the scenes, those values show up in every load, every fold, and every interaction with your teammates. As a Boyne Mountain team member, you’ll also enjoy ski passes, golf passes, waterpark passes, and discounts on food, beverages, retail, daycare, and much more.


Responsibilities

  • Excellence in Execution — Operate irons, Brauns, and towel machines, and manage the sort line according to established procedures. Every item that leaves this facility should reflect the quality Boyne Mountain guests count on.
  • Serve First — Sort, count, and verify quantities of articles against laundry requisitions to ensure other departments always have what they need, when they need it. Your accuracy and dependability keep the whole resort running smoothly.
  • Long-Term Thinking — Fold and place finished items in their appropriate storage areas with care and consistency, maintaining an organized facility that’s easy for the whole team to work in and keeps equipment in good condition over time.
  • Attitude is Everything — Show up ready to work, ready to support your teammates, and ready to take pride in a job well done — even when the work is physically demanding or the pace picks up. Your energy and reliability set the tone for everyone around you.
  • Develop Great People- in vesting time and build the skills and confindence and career trajectory of team members.

Qualifications

  • 18 years of age or older.
  • Available to work days 7am till 3:30pm, every 3rd Saturday, and holidays.
  • Able to lift up to 50 pounds and stand for extended periods throughout a shift.
  • Dependable, detail-oriented, and takes pride in doing the job right.
  • Available to work overtime in the busy peak season by staying over a few hours on 2nd shift
  • Have a positive attitude

If you’re someone who finds satisfaction in keeping things running right and takes pride in work that makes a difference for the whole team — we’d love to have you on board.
