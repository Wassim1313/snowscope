About the job

Volunteer

PART TIME HOURS:

  • 3 shifts per week
  • 16 SHIFTS DURING THE WINTER SEASON

WHAT WILL I BE DOING?

Reporting to the Supervisory team, Volunteer Patrollers are responsible for ensuring the safety of guests by monitoring trails and surrounding areas for hazards / surface conditions and managing them accordingly. The Volunteer Patroller will take a proactive approach in customer service active part in skier/ boarder education in an effort to reduce ski / snowboard injuries.

  • Monitor trail conditions and on-hill behaviors
  • Meet the operating expectations as they relate to guest experience, safety and effectiveness
  • Engaging guests in customer service, wayfinding, and general information about the resort as a whole
  • Render first aid and help as required, completing associated documentation
  • Perform incident investigations and ensure proper and complete documentation
  • Ensure all aspects of an incident are handled with a high standard of care.

WHAT DO WE NEED FROM YOU?

  • Must be 18 years of age
  • Valid driver’s license
  • Registered or hold current certification at a minimum level of First Responder or equivalent
  • Strong Intermediate skier / snowboarder. (able to ski with confidence, all areas of the resort)
  • Must possess excellent employee and public relations skills and be of a friendly and cooperative nature
  • Must be able to reason and react with quick, thoughtful judgment while remaining calm under pressure
  • Must be energetic, and dependable
  • Must be able and willing to work in a physically demanding outdoor environment
  • Knowledge of resort an asset
  • Successful applicants will be required to complete a criminal background check. (vulnerable sector)
