About the job

Create Your Experience of a Lifetime!

Come work and play in the mountains! Whether it’s your first-time seeing snow or you were born on the slopes, joining our team means discovering (or re-discovering!) a passion for the outdoors while building lifelong connections with teammates and guests from around the world.

With 40+ resorts across 3 continents, you can join our team for a season or stay to build a career. Job Summary:
This full time, year round Assistant Manager is responsible for leading public safety and guest services operations; including supporting a team that provides world-class guest service and creating a culture of safety and trust for our guests and employees. Guest Service and Public Safety areas include- Helping guests feel excited, happy, safe and welcome. It is your job to help guests and the team to find their epic day. Base Operations is often the first point of contact for a guest.

  • Starting Wage: $25.00/Hr - $28.60/Hr

  • Shift Type: Full Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: Yes

Job Responsibilities:

  • Providing Epic Service to our teams and our guests.
  • Snow Removal as needed
  • Assist guests & employees in navigating conflict
  • Be a leader through service- Develop and create leaders.
  • Interviewing, hiring, scheduling, payroll processing of employees and applicants.
  • Work closely with local law enforcement and regulatory agencies.
  • Handle internal and external investigations as assigned.
  • Training, staff development and progressive discipline, including proper documentation thereof.
  • Ensure everyone on the team goes home safely every day.
  • Assisting ticket staff as requested for fraudulent pass use
  • Manages departmental budgets and purchasing.
  • Facilitates resort lost and found.
  • Create and implement new policies and procedures when necessary.
  • Ensure daily shift pass down reports are completed and proper follow up with individuals and departments takes place.
  • Assist with traffic control, parking operations, and enforcement.
  • Assist with policy enforcement within employee housing.
  • Ensure the proper use of company equipment and vehicles.
  • Ensure prompt and accurate reporting in regard to correcting safety issues.
  • Participate in Base Operations weekly meetings.
  • Be an active participant in the Kirkwood Community as well as company initiatives and functions
  • Help plan and drive employee events.
  • Operate and conduct business with the Company Values in mind
  • Maintain a neat and clean appearance and meet the Company appearance and uniform policy
  • Collect all pertinent data from tenants, including vehicle information, emergency contacts, work departments, work supervisors, etc
  • Other duties as assigned.
