Job Summary:


The main responsibility for our Experienced Security is to serve our guests in the Resort. This role supports special events/groups, village concerts, holidays, and as-needed shift coverage for Guest Safety team members.


Job Specifications:


  • Starting Wage: $21.00/hr - $25.20/hr
  • Employment Type: Year Round
  • Shift Type: Full Time and Part Time hours available
  • Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age
  • Housing Availability: No



Job Responsibilities:


  • Interacting with property-owners, guests, and employees.
  • Assisting guests, residents, and employees with vehicle jump-starts, vehicle-unlocks, intrusion alarms and motor vehicle accidents.
  • Basic First Aid response.
  • Enacting general emergency procedures in conjunction with resort policies.
  • Basic understanding of emergency and law enforcement services and procedures.
  • Managing and resolving tier-two (transferred from other departments) customer service issues.
  • Enforcing all property regulations.
  • Traffic control and pedestrian safety on roadways within the resort and coming into the resort.
  • Investigating incidents.
  • Writing and filing reports.
  • Additional duties may include inspecting AEDs, managing and/or making keys, updating locks and keys and more.
  • Other duties as assigned.



Job Requirements:


  • High school diploma or equivalent - required.
  • Valid US Driver's license - required.
  • Extensive customer service skills and experience - required.
  • Clean driving record and background check - required.
  • Current Basic Life Support/CPR certification - required.
  • Able to obtain Advanced First Aid Certification within 3 months of employment - required.
  • Able to communicate effectively in English, both written and verbal - required.
  • Great interpersonal skills - required.
  • Able to work outside in all weather conditions - required.
  • Able to lift 50 pounds - required.
  • Able to stand for extended periods of time
  • Able to bend, crawl, climb, lift, kneel and crouch - required.
  • Able to work weekends, holidays and evenings - required.
  • Able to remain flexible in schedule, working nights, weekends, and holidays as needed - required.
  • College degree - preferred.
