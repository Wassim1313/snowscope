About the job

Year RoundPalisades TahoeWe share the spirit of these legendary mountains with the world.


To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential duties.

Job Summary: The Content and Social Media Coordinator supports the daily execution of Palisades Tahoe's social media strategy through content creation, publishing, and community engagement. Working closely with the Content and Social Media Manager and Photo and Video Specialist, this role helps capture and share the stories that make Palisades Tahoe unique.

This position requires strong creative instincts and an understanding of how content supports the resort's business goals. From building engagement to influencing purchase decisions, the focus is on creating content that connects with our audience and drives action.

Applicants must be at least 18 years of age.

Essential Job Responsibilities/Duties/Tasks include the following; other duties may be assigned:

  • Execute the resort's social media strategy, including content scheduling, publishing, and community management across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, and other platforms.
  • Capture and edit photo and video content for social media using mobile devices, GoPro cameras, and other production equipment.
  • Create content that supports resort business goals, from building awareness and engagement to driving website traffic, visitation, and purchases.
  • Identify and act on content opportunities across the resort, supporting seasonal campaigns, recurring content series, and real-time mountain storytelling.
  • Serve as an on-camera personality for Palisades Tahoe, including hosting social content, conducting interviews, and helping bring the resort's personality to life.
  • Engage with guests through social media comments and direct messages, providing helpful information while representing the Palisades Tahoe brand.
  • Monitor social trends and content performance, using audience insights to bring forward new ideas and improve results.
  • Support snow reporting operations two to three days per week during the winter season, working with Mountain Operations to communicate accurate conditions and resort information. Early morning start times are required on assigned days.
  • Work with resort departments, athletes, and other content creators to support content production and marketing initiatives.
  • Organize and maintain content within the resort's digital asset management system, while supporting blog updates, event coverage, and other marketing projects as needed.

Competencies and Job Requirements:

  • Strong understanding of social media platforms and what makes content effective, from audience engagement to driving business results.
  • Ability to capture and edit social content, particularly short-form video, using Adobe Creative Suite, CapCut, Instagram Edits, or similar tools.
  • Strong writing and storytelling skills, with an ability to communicate in the Palisades Tahoe brand voice.
  • Comfortable appearing on camera and interviewing others, with the ability to communicate naturally and represent the Palisades Tahoe brand.
  • Creative instincts and the ability to recognize content opportunities that connect with our audiences.
  • Familiarity with social media analytics and using performance data to improve content. Experience with Sprout Social preferred.
  • Strong organizational and communication skills, with the ability to work independently and meet deadlines in a fast-moving environment.
  • Understanding of mountain culture and the Palisades Tahoe guest experience.

Education and Experience:

Required:

  • Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Journalism, Film/Photography, or a related field, or equivalent work experience.
  • One to two years of experience in social media management, content creation, digital marketing, or a related field.
  • Demonstrated experience creating and publishing social content, including short-form video.
  • Intermediate skiing or snowboarding ability.

Preferred:

  • Experience in the ski resort, outdoor recreation, tourism, or hospitality industries.
  • Experience using social media analytics to measure engagement and business results.
  • Familiarity with digital asset management systems.
