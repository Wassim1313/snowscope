About the job

Snowbird is situated in the middle of the Wasatch Mountain Range and is not only home to awe-inspiring views and mighty terrain, it is also home to employees equally as remarkable. Snowbird is continually looking for the best, those who share a passion for these mountains and the outdoors. Snowbird is a unique resort with distinct summer and winter seasons and offers full-time, part-time and seasonal positions.


TITLE: Restaurant Cashier

LOCATION: Forklift Restaurant, located at Snowbird Center

STATUS: Seasonal (Winter Only)

SCHEDULE: Schedules vary based on business levels

  • Must be able to work any day of the week including weekends and holidays.
  • EMPLOYEE HOUSING IS NOT AVAILABLE.

SUMMARY

Snowbird is seeking friendly, energetic, and service-oriented Restaurant Cashiers to join our Food & Beverage team. In this role, you will provide exceptional guest service by taking food and beverage orders, processing transactions, answering menu questions, and helping create a welcoming dining experience. Successful candidates thrive in a fast-paced environment, enjoy interacting with guests, and are committed to accuracy, teamwork, and guest satisfaction.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Greet guests in a friendly and professional manner while creating a positive first impression.
  • Accurately take food and beverage orders and process cash and electronic transactions.
  • Utilize suggestive selling techniques to enhance the guest experience and support revenue goals.
  • Answer guest questions regarding menu offerings, ingredients, and dining options.
  • Ensure order accuracy by confirming selections and communicating effectively with kitchen staff and team members.
  • Assist with food and beverage preparation, presentation, and service as assigned.
  • Monitor and restock food, beverage, and service areas to ensure products remain available and presentable.
  • Maintain dining areas, service stations, and workspaces in accordance with cleanliness and sanitation standards.
  • Follow all food safety, responsible alcohol service, and guest service standards.
  • Safeguard guest information and process transactions in accordance with company policies and PCI compliance requirements.
  • Support other restaurant operations as needed, including bussing tables, cleaning, stocking, and assisting fellow team members.
  • Contribute to a positive, team-oriented environment focused on delivering exceptional guest experiences.

WHAT YOU WILL NEED DO GET THE JOB DONE

Required Qualifications

  • High School Diploma or equivalent.
  • Current Food Handler Permit or ability to obtain one upon hire.
  • Alcohol service certification or ability to obtain one as required by position.
  • Strong customer service and communication skills.
  • Ability to perform basic math calculations and accurately handle cash transactions.
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment while maintaining professionalism and accuracy.
  • Positive attitude and ability to work effectively as part of a team.
  • Must be at least 21 years of age.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous guest service, cashier, restaurant, food service, or hospitality experience.
  • Experience working in high-volume food and beverage operations.

WORK SCHEDULE​

  • Shifts vary depending on business needs.

WORKING CONDITIONS

  • Ability to stand and walk for extended periods throughout a shift.
  • Ability to lift and carry up to 25 pounds.
  • Ability to bend, kneel, squat, reach, grasp, and perform repetitive motions.
  • Comfortable working in high-volume guest service environments.
  • Ability to work efficiently under pressure while maintaining a positive attitude.
  • Must be able to commute to Snowbird Resort.
  • Employees may occasionally be required to remain onsite during severe weather conditions or road closures.
  • Must maintain positive working relationships with guests, coworkers, and employees throughout the resort.
