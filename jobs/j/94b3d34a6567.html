About the job

Overview

The role of a Sugarloaf Condo Houseperson is to service and assist with the needs of our guests, condo owners, and Housekeeping Department. A Condo Houseperson has a unique position within the department in that their responsibilities cannot be clearly defined, as every shift will be different. This position frequently interacts with guests in the hallways, rooms, and lobbies.

We are currently looking for someone to fill our PM shift role in this position. This shift typically works from 2pm to 10pm and may assist in our laundry room during down time.

The right candidate for this job will have a valid driver's license as you will be driving a work vehicle or your own vehicle to move between condos. This full-time seasonal position comes with great job perks and is expected to start at an hourly wage of $19.50.


Responsibilities

  • Be willing and able to help any guest encountered with enthusiasm and a smile
  • Be able to work as part of a team
  • Clean common area and hallways as needed
  • Deliver items to guest/owner rooms and condos
  • Organize linen drops
  • Assist housekeepers, laundry attendants, as well as other departments as needed
  • Become familiar with all room layouts in order to meet guest needs and respond to questions/requests

Qualifications

  • Must be a team player 
  • Must have good guest service skills and be safety conscious  
  • Must be able to lift up to 30 lbs. and do physical work 
  • Must have clean driving record and possess a valid driver’s license
  • Must be willing to work in changing weather conditions

Sugarloaf Team Member Benefits include:

  • Free lift pass for skiing and/or riding
  • Discounts at resort F&B and retail locations
  • Discounted ski/snowboard lessons and rentals
  • Access to Nordic trails and discounted equipment rentals at our Outdoor Center location
  • Discounted passes at other Boyne and partner resorts
