POSITION SUMMARY

Steamboat is looking for Assistant Manager's for our restaurant locations. If you have previous supervisor experience and have worked in a quick service environment, please apply. You will be considered for all restaurant locations. Join our team and the possibility of advancement in a dynamic and ever-growing field. Find your Steamboat Dream job today!!

An attached pdf or word document of your resume is required​.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES

  • Assist department manager with supervision of front of house staff

  • Be accountable for labor and cost of goods

  • Assist with monthly inventory

  • Assist employees with overall food preparation and customer service

  • Provide on the job training

  • Assist with management of daily operations

  • Perform scheduling duties and guest relations

  • Oversee building safety issues, ie: Food safety program, snow clearing, hazmat, repairs and maintenance

  • Ensure understanding and compliance with all company policies and procedures as well as any regulatory requirements

  • Other duties, as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • One year of restaurant front of house supervisory experience required, two years preferred

  • Must possess good organizational, interpersonal problem-solving leadership and team building skills

  • Adhere to all safety policies

  • Intermediate skiing/riding ability required for certain locations

  • Must have a valid driver’s license

  • Problem solve in a highly effective manner and maintain positive relationships with guests and staff, including management, subordinates and co-workers.

  • Be a consistent role model for company’s Service Excellence standards

  • Stand for long periods of time and climb stairs frequently

  • Must have Colorado approved Alcohol Server Training certificate or must attend a Colorado approved alcohol server training educational seminar within 30 days of employment

  • Must have current Food Manager and Allergy training certificate, or must attend Food Manager and Aller-train certification courses for Alterra Mountain company within 14 days of employment

EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS

  • High School Diploma or GED Equivalent
