About the job

Overview

As a Terrain Park Attendant you are responsible for maintaining all park elements in an effort to provide an enjoyable safe riding environment for our guests. As a Boyne Mountain team member, there are so many awesome perks for you to take advantage of, such as: ski passes, golf passes, waterpark passes, and discounts for food, beverages, retail, daycare, and so much more! To learn more about Boyne Mountain Resort, employment perks, and employee testimonials, click here - Boyne Mountain Employment!


Responsibilities

The Terrain Park Attendant's typical job functions include:

  • Daily examination and maintenance of all park elements
  • Assisting in creation of park construction including: shoveling, lifting, raking and digging
  • Keeping signage visible in the park
  • Knowing and educating guests on the terrain park etiquette
  • Monitoring all elements for risk and closing unsafe elements when appropriate
  • Completing daily terrain park forms

Qualifications

This position might be you if:

  • You are at least 18 years old
  • You are available to work weekends and holidays
  • You have achieved a high school diploma or general education degree (GED)
  • You enjoy working outside in various weather conditions
  • You are able to lift and or move up to 100 pounds
  • You are able to ski or ride all areas of the mountain and a majority of the terrain park
