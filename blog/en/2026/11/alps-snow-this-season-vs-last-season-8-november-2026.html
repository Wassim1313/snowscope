More snow than last winter in the Alps? 0 of 4 resorts ahead (8 November)

AlpsSnow vs past wintersPublished Sunday 8 November 2026 · 21:49 UTCData of 8 November2 min read

Is there more snow than a year ago? We compared today with 8 November last winter at 4 resorts in the Alps: 0 of them have more snow now, according to our snow model. The biggest change is at La Grave: 16 cm today against 84 cm a year ago.

Why the difference

At Bonneval-sur-Arc this winter so far, our model counts 0 days with a snowfall gain of 10 cm or more and 0 days that lost 5 cm or more to melt, rain or wind. The first 5 cm came on 21 October.

At La Grave this winter so far, our model counts 1 days with a snowfall gain of 10 cm or more and 0 days that lost 5 cm or more to melt, rain or wind. The first 5 cm came on 18 October.

At Orelle this winter so far, our model counts 0 days with a snowfall gain of 10 cm or more and 0 days that lost 5 cm or more to melt, rain or wind. The first 5 cm came on 20 October.

Early in the winter, one storm more or less makes the whole difference: these gaps say little about how the rest of the season will go.

Tips for skiers

  • A better start than last year is no promise for the holidays: watch the forecast, not only the snow already on the ground.
  • Model values describe an area at one height; a station measures one point. Read both before you decide.
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and dates on the resort's website.
  • Before any off-piste or touring day, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 8 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
