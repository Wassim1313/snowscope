Fresh snow in Western Canada: 56 cm in 72 hours at Ski Callaghan (7 November)

Western CanadaPowder alertsPublished Saturday 7 November 2026 · 15:18 UTCData of 6 November2 min read

Ski Callaghan got 56 cm of new snow in the last 72 hours, 9 cm of it in the last 24 hours. It now has about 48 cm on its upper slopes, according to our snow model.

Where the fresh snow fell

Elsewhere, Tweedsmuir Ski Club, Whistler Blackcomb and Mount Cain Alpine Park got 55 cm, 45 cm and 20 cm in the last 72 hours.

50 of 57 resorts in Western Canada have snow on their upper slopes today, and our model counts 300,720 km² of snow-covered ground.

What the stations measured

Keep in mind: resort values come from our snow model and describe an area, while stations measure one point.

Height, slopes and snow line

At Ski Callaghan, the freezing level reaches 1,120 m to 2,090 m on 7 November, above the top of the resort (998 m): rain or wet snow is possible on every slope that day.

What is coming next

More snow is on the way: by 10 November our forecast gives 103 cm at Whistler Blackcomb, 99 cm at Ski Callaghan and 74 cm at Mount Cain Alpine Park.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Safety and avalanche level

After fresh snow, wind builds drifts on sheltered slopes. Off-piste, the first days after a snowfall need the most care.

Tips for skiers

  • Fresh snow is best in the first hours after it stops: go early, before the sun and the crowds.
  • Look for snow high up and on north-facing slopes: they keep it longest, while south-facing slopes lose it first in the sun.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Ski Callaghan gets fresh snow.

About these numbers
