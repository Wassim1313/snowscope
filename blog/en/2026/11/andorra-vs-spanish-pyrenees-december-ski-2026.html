Pyrenees in December: Grandvalira and Spanish Resorts Compared

PyreneesGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 15:21 UTCData of 14 November4 min read

Planning a ski trip to the Pyrenees in December 2026? We look at the numbers for Grandvalira, Baqueira Beret, Astún-Candanchú, Boí Taüll, and Cauterets to see where to find the best snow and prices.

The match-up

The Pyrenees offer a mix of large and small ski areas across Andorra, Spain, and France. Grandvalira has 137 km of mapped runs and 53 mapped lifts. On the Spanish side, Baqueira Beret has 154 km of mapped runs and 36 mapped lifts. Astún-Candanchú offers 35 km of mapped runs, Boí Taüll has 35 km of mapped runs, and Cauterets on the French side provides 25 km of mapped runs.

Lift heights vary across the region. Grandvalira ranges from 1674 m to 2619 m, giving a vertical of 944 m. Baqueira Beret sits between 1504 m and 2600 m. Astún-Candanchú goes from 1659 m to 2272 m. Boí Taüll starts high at 2039 m and reaches 2747 m. Cauterets runs from 1725 m to 2420 m.

Snow: who wins December

Our snow history of 46 past winters shows that early December can be thin at the base across all these resorts. At Grandvalira, the base is at 1674 m with a usual December depth of 9 cm and a usual depth on 15 December of 8 cm. Baqueira Beret has a base at 1504 m with a usual December depth of 8 cm and 9 cm on 15 December. Astún-Candanchú has an 18 cm usual December depth at its 1659 m base, dropping to 13 cm on 15 December. Boí Taüll has a 2039 m base with a usual December depth of 16 cm and 17 cm on 15 December. Cauterets has a 1725 m base with a usual December depth of 23 cm and 21 cm on 15 December.

Higher up, the numbers improve. At mid-mountain, Grandvalira usual December depth is 27 cm, Baqueira Beret is 20 cm, Astún-Candanchú is 28 cm, Boí Taüll is 25 cm, and Cauterets is 33 cm. At the top of the lifts, Grandvalira reaches 45 cm, Baqueira Beret 30 cm, Astún-Candanchú 38 cm, Boí Taüll 34 cm, and Cauterets 42 cm. Looking at the trend in mid-mountain depth comparing the first 15 winters to the last 15 winters, Grandvalira moved from 21 cm to 25 cm, Baqueira Beret from 17 cm to 20 cm, Astún-Candanchú from 29 cm to 23 cm, Boí Taüll from 22 cm to 20 cm, and Cauterets from 35 cm to 23 cm.

Price: who wins

Official site checked costs show clear differences in day pass prices for the 2026-27 season. Grandvalira charges 62.50 EUR for an adult 1-day pass bought online for Monday 14 December 2026, and 72.00 EUR at the ticket office. Baqueira Beret lists an adult 1-day Baqueira Ticket at 74,00 €, a 6-day ticket at 359,00 €, and a 1-day pass with the rechargeable Baqueira Pass card at 70,00 €.

Astún-Candanchú charges 54.50 EUR for an adult 1-day joint pass bought online and 59.50 EUR at the ticket office, with a 5-day joint pass at 237.00 EUR. Boí Taüll lists an adult 1-day pass at 49.00 EUR. Cauterets charges 46 EUR for an adult 1-day public rate pass and 276 EUR for the 6-day pass.

The late-December rule

Share of winters with 30 cm or more at mid-mountain shows why timing matters. At Grandvalira, that share goes from 30 percent in the first week of December up to 65 percent between 22-28 December. At Baqueira Beret, it moves from 22 percent to 41 percent. At Astún-Candanchú, it goes from 35 percent to 63 percent. At Boí Taüll, it rises from 35 percent to 54 percent. At Cauterets, it goes from 43 percent to 67 percent. Waiting for the later weeks gives much better odds of coverage.

Verdict by type of trip

If you want a big mapped area and the deepest usual December snow at the top of this group, 45 cm, Grandvalira is a strong pick, especially if you book your pass online in advance. If you prefer a lower budget on the Spanish side, Boí Taüll offers a 1-day pass for 49.00 EUR with a high base at 2039 m. For travelers looking at Cauterets in France, the 46 EUR 1-day pass pairs with a top usual December depth of 42 cm.

Tips for skiers

  • Book your Grandvalira pass online before arrival to pay 62.50 EUR instead of the 72.00 EUR ticket office price.
  • Target the week of 22-28 December for the highest share of winters with 30 cm or more snow at mid-mountain across all resorts.
  • Check the avalanche bulletin before going off-piste in any of these areas.
  • Choose higher base resorts like Boí Taüll at 2039 m if you travel during the thin early-December weeks.
  • Use official resort sites to verify exact operating dates and active lift numbers for December 2026.
