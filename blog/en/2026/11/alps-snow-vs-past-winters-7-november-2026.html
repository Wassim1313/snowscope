Is it a good start?

At 3,369 m on La Grave the season is late: our model has only 3 cm today, below the usual 38 cm. Only 9 of the past 46 winters had less snow there on 7 November.

At 2,779 m on Orelle the season is late: our model has only 2 cm today, below the usual 28 cm. Only 6 of the past 46 winters had less snow there on 7 November.

The next days

More snow is on the way: by 10 November our forecast gives 33 cm at Les Deux Alpes, 32 cm at La Grave and 21 cm at Orelle.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Tips for skiers

  • Look for snow high up and on north-facing slopes: they keep it longest, while south-facing slopes lose it first in the sun.
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Early-season snow is thin. Stay on open, groomed pistes: rocks and grass can hide just under the surface.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when La Grave gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 6 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
