Pyrenees snow forecast: Cauterets Cirque du Lys +33 cm by 15 November

PyreneesSnow forecastsPowder alertsPublished Thursday 12 November 2026 · 10:25 UTCData of 12 November2 min read

Pyrenees snow report, 12 November. Looking ahead to 15 November, the forecast brings 33 cm at Cauterets Cirque du Lys, 29 cm at Piau-Engaly and 25 cm at Gourette. A word on the numbers: resort values are our model's estimate for the upper slopes, while each station measures a single spot on the date given. On the ground, the first thing we check is the shade: snow lasts where the sun cannot reach it.

What is on the ground

Of the places named here, Cauterets Cirque du Lys leads with about 11 cm on its upper slopes; Piau-Engaly 7 cm come next.

Across the region, 21 of 53 resorts in the Pyrenees have snow up high today, and our model finds 790 km² of white ground.

Top, middle, bottom

Warm air at Cauterets Cirque du Lys on 12 November: the freezing level goes from 2,530 m to 3,000 m, above the resort's top (2,420 m), so rain or wet snow can reach every slope.

On 12 November the freezing level at Piau-Engaly climbs to 2,540 m to 3,060 m, higher than the top of the lifts (2,527 m): expect rain or wet snow on every slope.

Warm air at Gourette on 12 November: the freezing level goes from 2,460 m to 3,020 m, above the resort's top (2,453 m), so rain or wet snow can reach every slope.

What to expect next

Between now and 15 November we expect 24 cm at Candanchu and 22 cm at Formigal.

More ground is white now: 710 km² on 10 November, 790 km² today, and 2,080 km² expected on 15 November.

Avalanche danger and safety

Tips for skiers

  • Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.
  • If the freezing level climbs above the lifts, the snow will be wet and heavy: head for the highest chairs.
  • Fresh snow has a short best-before time: the first hours. Early starts win.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Cauterets Cirque du Lys gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 12 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
