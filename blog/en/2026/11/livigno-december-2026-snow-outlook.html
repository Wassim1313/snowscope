Livigno in December 2026: 100 % white Christmas odds, the surest bet in our Italian list

AlpsGuidesPublished Tuesday 10 November 2026 · 12:19 UTCUpdated 10 November 2026Data of 10 November2 min read

Headline number: 100 %. That is the chance of a white Christmas at mid-mountain in Livigno, because in each of the last 45 winters there were at least 5 cm of snow there on 25 December. December 2026 is forecast warmer and wetter than usual; at this height, that barely matters.

Today, in numbers

Mid-mountain (2301 m): 4 cm. Top (2791 m): 4 cm. Base (1811 m): bare, for now. Seen from above, 83 % of the 60 km around Livigno is white. The coming seven days may add about 15 cm at mid-mountain and 23 cm at the top.

More snow than usual, or less?

No strong signal for most of the area: across Livigno, Bormio and the Engadin, 80 % of the mountains show no clear lean, 20 % lean towards less snow, and not one patch leans towards more.

For mid-mountain we give a 43 % chance of more new snow than in a usual December. Usual: 36 cm; outlook: 32 cm. On 31 December the snow on the ground is usually 62 cm; the outlook is 58 cm, most likely somewhere from 41 to 83 cm. Treat these as odds, not promises.

The European forecast, briefly

All 51 runs together put December 1.6 °C above usual over the area, and 80 % of them are warmer. Precipitation comes out 13 % above a usual December of 57 mm, with 59 % of the runs on the wet side. Warmth is usually bad news for snow, but not much here: at Livigno's height extra precipitation still falls as snow, and the link between warmth and less snow is weak (correlation -0.26).

Christmas odds

Mid-mountain was white on Christmas Day in all 45 past winters. Weighting those winters by this forecast still gives 100 %. A usual 25 December has 57 cm of snow there; the outlook says 54 cm. The past Decembers closest to this forecast: 2013, 2019 and 1994.

Tips for skiers

  • Christmas snow is your top priority? In our Italian list, Livigno's height is the safest bet.
  • Ski above mid-mountain for the best snow; early in December the base can stay thin.
  • Before you drive over the passes, look at the 7-day forecast and the SnowScope map for the next storm.

About these numbers

Method. Temperature and precipitation: the seasonal forecast of the European weather centre (ECMWF SEAS5, 51 runs started 1 November 2026, anomalies against its own 1993-2016 reforecasts, about 100 km grid). Snow: for every mountain cell of about 10 km we take our model of the past (46 winters, snow depth corrected for height) and the weather of each December from 1981 to 2025 (ERA5); past Decembers whose temperature and precipitation look like each forecast run count more, and their snow gives the odds. Snow today: the SnowScope snow map (500 m). A monthly outlook only tilts the odds: it cannot forecast a storm or a date.
