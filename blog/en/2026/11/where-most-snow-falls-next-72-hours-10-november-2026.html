The days ahead

We put the snow line about 300 m under the forecast freezing level. Ski above it for dry snow; below it, expect wet snow or rain.

Remember that a resort value is a model estimate for an area; a station tells you about one spot.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Tips for skiers

  • The best powder lasts a morning, not a week. Go early, before the sun and the crowds get to it.
  • When the freezing level rises above the resort, part of the snow melts as it falls: expect wet snow and stay near the top.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Whistler Blackcomb gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 10 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
