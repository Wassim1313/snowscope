58 EUR for a day in the southern sun at Vars-Risoul

AlpsGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 17:01 UTCData of 14 November5 min read

You can ski the Forêt Blanche area for 58 EUR for a single day or 298 EUR for a six-day pass this winter. This southern French resort offers 131 km of mapped runs across a vertical of 1090 m. Before you book, you need to know if the early snow is worth your hard-earned cash.

The cost of a Forêt Blanche trip

If you are like me and watch every euro, the price of the lift pass is the first thing you look at. For the 2026-27 winter, an adult 1-day Forêt Blanche pass for the Vars-Risoul area is 58 EUR. If you want to stay longer, the adult 6-day Forêt Blanche pass is 298 EUR. This gives you access to a mapped ski area with 38 lifts and 131 km of mapped runs.

The resort sits between 1632 m and 2722 m. This height is important because early season snow can be a gamble. You are paying for a large area, so you want to make sure the runs are actually open. In our snow history of 46 past winters, the usual December depth at the bottom of the lifts is 33 cm, which is not very deep for the start of the season.

Is the December snow good enough

Looking at our snow history from 1980-81 to 2025-26, the snow depth varies a lot. At mid-mountain, which is 2177 m, the usual December depth is 51 cm. However, in a thin December, which happens about 1 winter in 10, that depth can drop to just 20 cm. At the top of the lifts at 2722 m, things look better with a usual depth of 77 cm.

The base at 1632 m is the weakest spot. In a thin December, the depth there can be as low as 9 cm. If you get lucky and have a deep December, which also happens about 1 winter in 10, you could see 61 cm at the base and 160 cm at the top. Last winter, in December 2025-26, the average was 25 cm at the base and 57 cm at the top, which was a bit below the usual numbers.

Early versus late December chances

The timing of your trip in December changes your chances of finding good snow. In the first week of the month, from 1-7 December, there is only a 30 % share of winters with 30 cm or more at the base. By the last week, from 22-28 December, this share of winters rises to 74 %. If you want to see 50 cm or more at the base, that only happens in 30 % of past winters even in that final week.

Mid-mountain is a safer bet for your money. From 15-21 December, there is an 85 % share of winters with at least 30 cm of snow at 2177 m. The top of the lifts is the most reliable, with a 91 % share of winters having 30 cm or more as early as 8-14 December. By the end of the month, the top has 50 cm or more in 93 % of past winters.

Comparing with Serre Chevalier

It is worth looking at the bigger neighbour, Serre Chevalier. That resort has a larger mapped ski area with 157 km of runs and 70 lifts. For a trip from opening to 18 December 2026, an adult 6-day pass there is 276.00 EUR. This is cheaper than the 298 EUR pass at Vars-Risoul for those specific dates. However, from 19 December 2026 to 1 January 2027, the Serre Chevalier 6-day pass price jumps to 345.00 EUR.

Serre Chevalier also has more snow at the top. Its highest lift is at 2765 m, where the usual December depth is 111 cm. In our snow history, Serre Chevalier has 30 cm or more at its top in 100 % of past winters from 8 December onwards. At the base, which is lower at 1219 m, the usual depth is 30 cm, which is similar to the 33 cm at the Vars-Risoul base.

Recent trends and current outlook

Our snow history shows that December snow at mid-mountain has actually improved over time. In the middle of the first 15 winters (1980-81 to 1994-95), the December average was 42 cm. In the middle of the last 15 winters (2011-12 to 2025-26), that average rose to 62 cm. This is good news for skiers worried about the southern sun.

As of 13 November 2026, the snow now is quite thin, with 0 cm at the base and 10 cm at the top. However, the two-week outlook for mid-mountain and above shows a forecast range of 62 to 86 cm of new snow. This could provide a great start for the season if the forecast holds. The usual first lasting snow day at the top is 19 October, so the mountain is already behind that date.

Tips for skiers

  • Book your trip for the week of 22-28 December if you want a 74 % chance of 30 cm of snow at the base.
  • Stick to the runs above mid-mountain at 2177 m where the usual depth is 51 cm rather than 33 cm at the bottom.
  • Compare the 298 EUR pass with the 276.00 EUR early-season price at Serre Chevalier if you are travelling before 18 December.
  • Check the avalanche bulletin before going off-piste, especially if the forecast 62 to 86 cm of new snow arrives.
  • Buy your Forêt Blanche pass on the official site to ensure you get the checked price of 58 EUR for a day.
