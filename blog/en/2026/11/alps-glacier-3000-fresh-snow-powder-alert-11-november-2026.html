The forecast in short

Forecast to 14 November: 58 cm at La Grave, 33 cm at Glacier 3000 and 33 cm at Orcières Merlette. Treat the later days with care.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Snow line check

Glacier 3000, 12 November: freezing level 2,480 m to 2,900 m. Wet snow low down, winter up top.

Safety and avalanche level

After fresh snow, wind builds drifts on sheltered slopes. Off-piste, the first days after a snowfall need the most care.

Tips for skiers

  • Be on the first lift after a snowfall: by midday, sun and skiers have already changed the snow.
  • South-facing slopes take the most sun and melt first; high, north-facing slopes keep their snow the longest.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Glacier 3000 gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 11 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
