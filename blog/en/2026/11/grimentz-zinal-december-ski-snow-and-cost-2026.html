Grimentz-Zinal in December: High Snow and Old Village Charm

AlpsGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:31 UTCData of 14 November3 min read

Grimentz and Zinal are two historic villages connected by a cable car to form a single ski area. If you look for a low-cost trip in December 2026, this Swiss destination provides a mix of high-altitude reliability and traditional atmosphere.

Two villages, one ski area

The mapped ski area has 71 km of runs and 23 mapped lifts. You can move between the two villages easily using the cable car system. The lifts reach a highest point of 2873 m, while the lowest lift sits at 1552 m. This gives you a vertical drop of 1321 m to explore.

December depth by week

Our snow history of 46 past winters shows that snow at the bottom of the lifts is often thin in early December. In a usual December, the base has 27 cm of snow. However, the share of winters with 30 cm or more at the base increases as the month goes on, rising from 17 % in the first week to 63 % by the last week of December.

At mid-mountain, 2212 m, the conditions are more consistent. The usual December depth is 44 cm. In 85 % of past winters, you will find 30 cm or more of snow between 15-21 December. At the top of the lifts, 2873 m, the snow is very reliable. In 100 % of past winters, there is 30 cm or more of snow during the middle of the month.

Looking at long-term trends, the December average at the top was 99 cm in the first 15 winters, 1980-81 to 1994-95, and 92 cm in the last 15 winters, 2011-12 to 2025-26. We also see that the thinnest December on record at the top was 34 cm in the 2015-16 season, while the deepest was 175 cm in 1982-83.

Pass price

You can buy an adult 1-day Val d'Anniviers pass for CHF 75.00. If you plan to stay longer, an adult 6-day Val d'Anniviers pass costs CHF 339.00. These prices are valid for the winter 2026-27 season and cover Grimentz-Zinal, St-Luc Chandolin, and Vercorin.

Grimentz-Zinal vs St-Luc Chandolin

When comparing the two, St-Luc Chandolin has a mapped ski area with 56 km of runs and 18 mapped lifts. Its highest lift is at 2973 m, which is higher than the 2873 m top at Grimentz-Zinal. The usual December depth at the top of St-Luc Chandolin is 110 cm, compared to 99 cm at Grimentz-Zinal.

At the base, St-Luc Chandolin sits at 1682 m and has a usual December depth of 38 cm. Grimentz-Zinal's base is lower at 1552 m, which explains why the snow is thinner there early in the month. Both resorts share the same pricing for the Val d'Anniviers pass.

Who should pick it in December

Grimentz-Zinal is a good choice if you enjoy high-altitude skiing where the snow is most reliable. As of 13 November 2026, the snow now is 0 cm at the base, 1 cm at mid-mountain, and 1 cm at the top. The two-week new snow outlook for mid-mountain and above predicts a range of 63 cm to 108 cm, with a middle value of 86 cm.

If you travel early in December, focus your time on the upper parts of the mountain. The lower slopes are better suited for the weeks closer to the end of the month when the snow base has had more time to build.

Tips for skiers

  • Visit during the last week of December to have the best chance of 30 cm or more of snow at the base of the lifts.
  • Spend your time at the top of the lifts, 2873 m, where the snow is reliable even in thin years.
  • Check the avalanche bulletin before you go off-piste, as conditions change quickly in high-altitude areas.
  • Book your 6-day Val d'Anniviers pass on the official site to prepare for your trip.
