Les Karellis in December: Small resort with big snow for families

AlpsGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:29 UTCData of 14 November5 min read

Les Karellis in December is a quiet choice for parents who want reliable snow without a high price tag. This resort in the Maurienne valley is smaller than its neighbors, but it sits high enough to offer good skiing early in the season. If you are planning a family trip, the combination of a 1550 m base and affordable lift passes makes it a practical alternative to the giant ski areas.

A small resort with a high base

Les Karellis is a compact resort that is easy for families to navigate. The mapped ski area has 45 km of runs and 14 lifts, which is plenty for a week of skiing with children. The resort starts at 1550 m and goes up to 2484 m, giving a total vertical of 934 m. Because the village is at 1550 m, you often have snow right at the bottom of the lifts.

Our snow history of 46 past winters shows that the base is quite reliable for a resort of this size. The usual December depth at the base is 37 cm. In a deep winter, which happens in about 1 winter in 10, the depth can reach 73 cm. Even in a thin winter, which also happens in about 1 winter in 10, the base usually has at least 16 cm of snow. This height is a big advantage when other lower resorts are still waiting for a solid cover.

December snow week by week

The snow depth in Les Karellis improves significantly as December progresses. In the first week of the month, from 1-7 December, the base has 30 cm or more in 43 % of past winters. By the week of 22-28 December, this chance rises to 76 %. If you are looking for a deeper cover of 50 cm or more at the base, that happens in 50 % of past winters during the final week of the month.

Higher up the mountain, the numbers are even better for early visitors. At mid-mountain (2017 m), the usual depth on 15 December is 63 cm. In 98 % of past winters, there is at least 30 cm of snow at this height during the second half of the month. At the top of the lifts (2484 m), the usual depth is 98 cm on 15 December. In about 9 winters in 10, the top of the mountain has 50 cm or more of snow as early as the week of 15-21 December.

What a family pays for lifts

One of the main reasons we look at Les Karellis is the cost. For the winter 2026-27 season, an adult (18+) 1-day pass is 36.00 EUR. This is very low for a resort with a 934 m vertical drop. If you book a longer stay, an adult 6-day pass costs 191.00 EUR. These prices are checked from the official site and help a family keep the total trip cost down.

The resort is designed to be simple and affordable. With 14 lifts serving 45 km of runs, you are not paying for a massive network of hundreds of kilometers that young children might not even use. Instead, you pay for a functional area that has a high top lift at 2484 m, ensuring you can find good snow even if the village level is thin.

How it compares with Valloire-Valmeinier

It is useful to compare Les Karellis with Valloire-Valmeinier, which is a much larger neighbor. Valloire-Valmeinier has 131 km of mapped runs and 34 lifts, but it is also more expensive. An adult 6-day Galibier-Thabor pass there costs 278.50 EUR, and an adult 1-day pass is 54.90 EUR. While Valloire-Valmeinier reaches higher at 2738 m, its base is lower at 1406 m.

When we look at mid-mountain snow, the two are very similar. At 2017 m in Les Karellis, the usual December depth is 63 cm. In Valloire-Valmeinier at 2072 m, it is 66 cm. Both resorts have a 98 % chance of having 30 cm or more at mid-mountain in the second half of December. For a family, Les Karellis provides almost the same snow reliability as its larger neighbor but for a much lower price.

What to watch before booking

Our snow history shows that December in Les Karellis is actually getting snowier over time. At mid-mountain, the December average was 55 cm in the first 15 winters (1980-81 to 1994-95). In the last 15 winters (2011-12 to 2025-26), that average has risen to 64 cm. This trend is a good sign for those planning a trip in December 2026.

As of 13 November 2026, the season is starting well. There is currently 0 cm at the base and 3 cm at the top, but the next 7 days should bring 32 cm of new snow to the base and 86 cm to the top. The two-week outlook for mid-mountain and above shows a range of 68 cm to 104 cm of new snow. Always check the official site for the 191.00 EUR 6-day pass and keep an eye on the mountain reports as your trip approaches.

Tips for skiers

  • Book your trip for the second half of December when the chance of a 30 cm base rises to 70 % or more.
  • Stick to the mid-mountain runs at 2017 m if the base is thin, as there is a 98 % chance of snow there after 15 December.
  • Buy the adult 6-day pass on the official site for 191.00 EUR to get the best value.
  • Check the avalanche bulletin before skiing off-piste, especially if the top depth is near the usual 98 cm.
  • Compare the 36.00 EUR daily rate with nearby resorts if you only plan to ski for a few days.
