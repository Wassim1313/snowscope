Is there snow in France? Snow depth and new snow on 17 November 2026

Europe snow by countryPublished Tuesday 17 November 2026 · 12:53 UTCData of 17 November2 min read

The white area in France stands at about 8,720 km² today, 730 km² less than yesterday. Roughly 10 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills are covered.

Skiing conditions

The new snow freshens the upper slopes; lower down it may already be heavy or wet.

With more snow in the forecast, the next days should improve things further.

The latest snowfall

Three-day totals peak at 47 cm, around the Mont Blanc massif, the southern French Alps and the northern French Alps.

In the past day, up to 23 cm was added.

About 8,300 km² received at least 5 cm.

Shortlist for the weekend

The deepest snow of the country's resorts is at La Grave: 113 cm.

Next on the list: Orelle (108 cm), Val Thorens (108 cm), Les Deux Alpes (91 cm) and Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine (78 cm).

La Grave picked up 79 cm in the last three days and could add 92 cm by Friday 20 November.

Orelle picked up 74 cm in the last three days and could add 102 cm by Friday 20 November.

104 of the 224 resorts we follow in France have snow on their upper slopes.

Snow on the way?

Snow is in the forecast: up to 72 cm by the end of the third day, up to 87 cm within seven days.

The snowiest day looks like Friday 20 November, with up to 33 cm in the snowiest spots.

The second week adds up to 38 cm where it snows most.

Tips for skiers

  • Read the local avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
  • Fresh snow on a cold base sticks badly: take care on steep slopes.
  • Early-season snow is thin over rocks: ski the groomed runs first.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 17 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
