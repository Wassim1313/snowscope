Baqueira Beret in December: Snow Facts and Prices for Your Trip

PyreneesGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:29 UTCData of 14 November4 min read

Baqueira Beret, in the Val d'Aran, is one of the big names of the Pyrenees, but is it a good December bet? Our snow history shows a modest start to the season here, so the week you pick matters more than usual.

Val d'Aran and its heights

Baqueira Beret is located in Spain and offers a wide area to explore. The mapped ski area has 154 km of runs and 36 mapped lifts.

The resort spans from the bottom of the lifts at 1504 m up to the top of the lifts at 2600 m, giving a vertical drop of 1096 m.

December snow in numbers

Looking back at our snow history of 46 past winters from 1980-81 to 2025-26, December snow depth is often quite thin at the base. At 1504 m, the usual December depth is 8 cm, and the usual depth on 15 December is 9 cm. A thin December brings 0 cm or less about 1 winter in 10, while a deep December can reach 37 cm or more about 1 winter in 10.

Higher up at the mid-mountain height of 2052 m, the usual December depth is 20 cm, with 19 cm on 15 December. At the top of the lifts at 2600 m, the usual December depth is 30 cm, and the usual depth on 15 December is also 30 cm. At this top height, a deep December reaches 77 cm or more about 1 winter in 10.

If you look at the share of winters with 30 cm or more at mid-mountain, it rises as the month goes on: 22 percent in 1-7 December, 30 percent in 8-14 December, 35 percent in 15-21 December, and 41 percent in 22-28 December. At the top, the share of winters with 30 cm or more is 39 percent in 1-7 December, 46 percent in 8-14 December, 52 percent in 15-21 December, and 59 percent in 22-28 December.

The price, checked

Ski trips need a budget, so let us look at the checked costs from the official site for the 2026-27 season. An adult 1-day with the rechargeable 'Baqueira Pass' card costs 70,00 €. If you buy a standard adult 1-day 'Baqueira Ticket', the price is 74,00 €. For a longer stay, an adult 6-day 'Baqueira Ticket' is priced at 359,00 €.

Baqueira or Astún-Candanchú

It helps to compare Baqueira Beret with Astún-Candanchú, another Spanish destination. Astún-Candanchú has a mapped ski area with 35 km of runs and 17 mapped lifts, with heights ranging from 1659 m to 2272 m, giving a vertical of 613 m.

In our snow history for Astún-Candanchú, the base at 1659 m has a usual December depth of 18 cm and 13 cm on 15 December. Its top at 2272 m has a usual December depth of 38 cm and 29 cm on 15 December. For costs at Astún-Candanchú, an adult 1-day joint pass bought online for season 26-27 is 54.50 EUR, while at the ticket office it is 59.50 EUR, and an adult 5-day joint pass bought online is 237.00 EUR.

First snow and trend

In our snow history, the usual first lasting snow day at the base is 10 November. At mid-mountain it is 8 November, and at the top it arrives by 4 November.

Comparing past periods, the December average at mid-mountain was 17 cm in the middle of the first 15 winters (1980-81 to 1994-95) and 20 cm in the middle of last 15 winters (2011-12 to 2025-26). Last winter in December 2025 averaged 9 cm at the base, 14 cm at mid-mountain, and 19 cm at the top.

Snow now shows 0 cm at the base and mid-mountain, and 1 cm at the top, with 9 cm of new snow expected in the next 7 days at the top. The two-week new snow outlook for mid and above forecasts a middle of 14 cm with a range of 6 cm to 22 cm.

Tips for skiers

  • Aim for the later weeks of December, such as 22-28 December, when the share of winters with 30 cm or more reaches 59 percent at the top.
  • Stay higher up around the mid-mountain and top levels (2052 m to 2600 m) where natural snow depths are more reliable than at the base.
  • Buy the rechargeable 'Baqueira Pass' card on the official site to get the 1-day rate of 70,00 € instead of 74,00 €.
  • Always check the local avalanche bulletin before heading off-piste, especially early in the season when base layers are thin.
