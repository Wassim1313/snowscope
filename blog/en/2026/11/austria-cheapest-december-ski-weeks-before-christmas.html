Cheapest ski weeks in Austria before Christmas: 11 resorts, snow vs price

AlpsGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:29 UTCData of 14 November3 min read

The short answer: the best-value December weeks in Austria are in the high villages, not on the glaciers. Galtür (EUR 258 for 6 days until 18 December) and Kühtai (EUR 269 until 18 December) both had 30 cm or more at mid-mountain in the week of 15-21 December in over 90 % of our 46 winters of snow history.

How we compared

We took 11 Austrian resorts that are high enough to matter in December and looked at two things. First, snow: the share of winters with 30 cm or more on the ground at mid-mountain in the week of 15-21 December, and the same share for Christmas week in the last 15 winters. Second, the adult 6-day pass on each resort's official price list, using the cheaper early-season price where the list has one. The map numbers match the list below.

The winners: high villages with a low season

Kühtai (2) is the snow champion among the villages: its lowest lift is at 1939 m, mid-mountain had 30 cm in 98 % of winters in mid-December, and the low-season 6-day pass costs EUR 269 until 18 December. Galtür (3) is the cheapest week on this list at EUR 258 until 18 December, with 93 % of winters at 30 cm at mid-mountain.

Obertauern (1) is bigger and costs more, EUR 331 for 6 days until 25 December, with 80 % of winters at 30 cm in mid-December. All three have the same strength: you ski from the door, and no glacier ticket is needed.

The sure things: glaciers, at a price

The glaciers are close to certain at mid-mountain in mid-December: the Mölltaler Gletscher (8), Pitztal (9) and Stubai (10) had 30 cm in 100 % of our winters, Hintertux (7) in 87 %. You pay for that. Six days cost EUR 332 at the Mölltaler Gletscher in its low season (latest list 2025-26), EUR 355 at Stubai, EUR 399 at Hintertux, EUR 410 at Pitztal and EUR 396 to 440 at the Kitzsteinhorn (11).

Their weak point is the valley: Hintertux has 16 cm at its 1500 m bottom lift on a typical 15 December and the Kitzsteinhorn 6 cm at 765 m. You ski up high and ride down.

The gambles: small and low

Lachtal (4) and the Hochkar (5) are the budget day-trip hills: Lachtal from EUR 62 a day, the Hochkar with dynamic prices seen from EUR 32.90 on quiet December dates. Both had 30 cm at mid-mountain in mid-December in about two thirds to three quarters of winters (72 % and 63 %), and both have lost a lot of December snow.

The Turracher Höhe (6) is the one to avoid in December on snow grounds: 23 cm at mid-mountain on a typical 15 December, 39 % of winters with 30 cm in mid-December and only 27 % of recent Christmas weeks. Its 6-day pass is also not cheap, at EUR 353 in its low season.

How to choose

For a fixed week before Christmas on a budget: Galtür or Kühtai, booked to end by 18 December. For a fixed week with zero snow risk: the Mölltaler Gletscher is the cheapest glacier, Stubai the best compromise near Innsbruck. For a flexible weekend: the Hochkar or Lachtal, decided on the forecast. Look at the snow map a week before you go, whatever you book.

Tips for skiers

  • End your week by 18 December in Galtür or Kühtai to get the low-season price.
  • On glaciers plan to ski the upper half and download by lift; the valleys are rarely white in December.
  • Small hills: buy day by day and only when the forecast shows real snow.
  • Before any off-piste, read the regional avalanche report (Tyrol, Salzburg, Carinthia, Styria).
