Oulu snow depth: from bare to 7 cm overnight (13 November 2026)

ScandinaviaGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:29 UTCData of 13 November2 min read

Oulu went to bed brown and woke up white. The weather station at Kaukovainio, 3 km from the city centre, had bare ground on Thursday morning and 7 cm of snow on Friday. It is the city's first real cover of the winter, and it arrived a little later than usual.

Around the city

The snow came along the whole coast of the Bothnian Bay at once. Hailuoto, out on the island, measured 5 cm, Ruukki in Siikajoki 8 cm, Vaala Pelso inland 4 cm and Pudasjärvi airport 2 cm. Every one of them had bare ground the day before.

Late, then about normal

Our snow history for Oulu covers 46 past winters. Snow on the ground on 13 November is the rule, 33 winters out of 46, but it is usually thin: the middle value is 2 cm and one winter in four had 5 cm or more. Half the winters had their first lasting cover by 28 October, so this year's start is a couple of weeks late, while today's depth is actually above the usual for the date.

Recent winters on this date: 1 cm, bare, 5 cm, bare and 1 cm. By 1 December Oulu usually has about 9 cm, by mid-December 14 cm and by mid-January about 27 cm.

The weekend

A little more snow first: around 2 cm on Friday and 1 cm on Saturday, with temperatures just below zero. Sunday is mostly dry and slightly milder, up to about 3 °C. On Monday it turns to rain, from a couple of millimetres to about 8 mm depending on the forecast, at 1 to 4 °C. The new cover will shrink, and may not survive the start of next week.

Tips for skiers

  • Saturday is the best day to enjoy it: white, just below freezing, and no rain yet.
  • Fresh snow on the Oulu riverside paths hides the first ice patches: watch your step on the bridges.
  • Thin snow plus Monday's rain makes slush: waterproof shoes for the commute.

About these numbers

Snow depth today: measured at weather stations on the morning of 13 November 2026 (Finnish Meteorological Institute); a station reports the snow on its own small plot, so a few kilometres away and a hundred metres higher the ground can look different. Usual depth for the date: SnowScope snow history, 46 past winters (1980-81 to 2025-26), a model of the past at the city's height, not a station record. Weekend: two weather models (ECMWF IFS and DWD ICON, read through Open-Meteo), given as a range. Places and rules: the official pages listed below.
