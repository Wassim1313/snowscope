Obertauern in December: snow at the door, a cheaper pre-Christmas week

AlpsGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:29 UTCData of 14 November3 min read

The lowest lift in Obertauern starts at 1628 m, so the whole village sits where December snow usually lies. In our 46 winters of snow history, mid-mountain had 30 cm or more in the week of 15-21 December in 80 % of winters, and the 6-day pass costs EUR 331 until 25 December instead of EUR 368.

The short verdict

If you want a cheap December week in Austria without paying for a glacier, Obertauern is the obvious first call. The ski area runs from 1628 m to 2297 m, with 87 km of mapped runs and 28 lifts, and none of it is a valley-floor slope that needs luck.

The catch is honest and simple: early December is not yet safe. In the first week of the month only about half of our winters (57 %) had 30 cm at mid-mountain. Book the second half of the month and the odds look much better.

December, one week at a time

At mid-mountain (1962 m) the share of winters with 30 cm or more climbs through the month: 57 % in 1-7 December, 65 % in 8-14 December, 80 % in 15-21 December and 85 % in Christmas week. At the top (2297 m) the same weeks give 76, 80, 85 and 91 %.

A typical depth at mid-mountain grows from 34 cm on 1 December to 43 cm on 15 December and 62 cm on 31 December. In a thin year (1 winter in 10) 15 December has only 17 cm; in a deep one, 86 cm. In none of our 46 winters was the ground bare at the village on 15 December.

One warning from the long view: the December average at mid-mountain has dropped from 46 cm in the first 15 winters to 38 cm in the last 15, and last winter had just 18 cm there on 15 December.

What the lift pass costs

Obertauern's official price list for 2026-27 has two seasons. A day pass is EUR 72 in both. The saving is on longer passes: 6 days cost EUR 331 in season B, which runs from 20 November to 25 December, against EUR 368 in the main season.

So a week that ends before Christmas pays the lower season-B price on the pass, for the same lifts and, in most winters, enough snow.

Next to the Kitzsteinhorn

The Kitzsteinhorn above Kaprun is the classic December answer: a glacier with 137 cm at the top on a typical 15 December. But its lowest lift is at 765 m, where a typical 15 December has 6 cm, and its mid-mountain (1894 m) is close to Obertauern's: 40 cm and 76 % of winters with 30 cm in the week of 15-21 December.

The money side is clear. A 6-day pass at the Kitzsteinhorn costs EUR 396 in its early season and EUR 440 in the main season. Obertauern's pre-Christmas week is cheaper, and you ski from the hotel door.

Who should book it

Families and intermediate skiers who want ski-in, ski-out in December and a lower bill. Strong skiers who need deep snow should wait for Christmas week, when 41 % of our winters had 60 cm or more at mid-mountain, or look higher.

Tips for skiers

  • Pick 15-21 December or later: the odds of 30 cm at mid-mountain rise from 57 % to 80 % compared with the first week.
  • End your 6-day pass on or before 25 December to stay in the cheaper season B (EUR 331 instead of EUR 368).
  • Look at the snow map a week before you leave; last winter shows that a thin December still happens.
  • Off-piste after a storm: read the Salzburg avalanche report each morning.
