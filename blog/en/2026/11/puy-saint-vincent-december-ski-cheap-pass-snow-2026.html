44 EUR day passes and December snow at Puy-Saint-Vincent

AlpsGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 14:11 UTCData of 14 November4 min read

44 EUR for an adult 1-day pass makes Puy-Saint-Vincent a low-cost entry to December skiing in the Écrins. While the 1411 m base is often thin early in winter, our snow history shows reliable cover from mid-mountain up. Here is how it compares with Serre Chevalier.

A 44 EUR pass on a compact mountain

For winter 2026-27, the official Puy-Saint-Vincent site prices an adult 1-day pass at 44 EUR. That covers a mapped ski area of 42 km of runs and 12 mapped lifts, running from 1411 m to 2664 m for a 1253 m vertical.

A compact map keeps a short December trip simple and affordable. The real question is how well natural snow holds at each height before January.

Three heights, three December snow stories

At the bottom of the lifts (1411 m), December snow is plainly thin. In our snow history of 46 past winters (1980-81 to 2025-26), the usual December depth is 19 cm, dropping below 5 cm in 1 winter in 10 and topping 39 cm in 1 winter in 10.

Higher up, cover builds fast. Mid-mountain (2037 m) averages 55 cm in a usual December, with 1 winter in 10 below 21 cm and 1 winter in 10 above 96 cm. At the top (2664 m), a usual December reaches 106 cm (44 cm thin, 185 cm deep).

Which December weeks hold the snow

Week-by-week numbers show when to book. At the base (1411 m), 30 cm or more occurs in 15 % of past winters in 1-7 December, 20 % in 8-14 December, 28 % in 15-21 December, and 33 % in 22-28 December, while 50 cm or more appears in only 0 %, 2 %, 7 %, and 9 %.

At mid-mountain (2037 m), 30 cm or more holds in 61 % of past winters in 1-7 December, rising to 76 %, 89 %, and 93 % over the next three weeks, while 50 cm or more climbs from 35 % and 46 % to 54 % in 15-21 December and 70 % in 22-28 December. At the top (2664 m), 30 cm or more hits 98 % in 1-7 December and 100 % thereafter, with 50 cm or more at 74 %, 85 %, 93 %, and 96 %.

Against big neighbour Serre Chevalier

Serre Chevalier is much bigger, with 157 km of mapped runs and 70 mapped lifts from 1219 m to 2765 m (a 1546 m vertical). Its official site lists an adult 6-day pass at 276.00 EUR from opening to 18 December 2026 and 345.00 EUR from 19 December 2026 to 1 January 2027. Puy-Saint-Vincent only has a checked 44 EUR 1-day pass here, suited to shorter trips.

In our snow history, Serre Chevalier is deeper at the base (1219 m), averaging 30 cm in a usual December (8 cm thin, 59 cm deep) with 30 cm or more in 30 %, 41 %, 52 %, and 67 % of winters. Higher up, both are close: Serre Chevalier averages 58 cm at mid-mountain (1992 m; 24 cm thin, 103 cm deep) and 111 cm at the top (2765 m; 50 cm thin, 192 cm deep), hitting 30 cm or more in 65 %, 83 %, 93 %, and 96 % of winters mid-mountain and 50 cm or more in 78 %, 87 %, 96 %, and 96 % at the top.

First snow, long trends, early signs

Last winter (2025-26) had a modest December average at Puy-Saint-Vincent: 12 cm at the base, 42 cm mid-mountain, and 91 cm at the top. Usual first lasting snow arrives on 30 October at the base, 26 October mid-mountain, and 13 October at the top. Comparing the first 15 winters (1980-81 to 1994-95) with the last 15 (2011-12 to 2025-26), December averages rose from 13 cm to 23 cm at the base, 42 cm to 60 cm mid-mountain, and 87 cm to 114 cm at the top. Thin extremes hit 0 cm at the base in 1998-99 (13 cm mid, 38 cm top in 2015-16), while 1996-97 reached 52 cm, 114 cm, and 199 cm.

As a sign of how this season is starting, today's snow estimate on 13 November 2026 sits at 0 cm at the base and mid-mountain and 11 cm at the top, with an estimated 0 cm, 37 cm, and 70 cm of new snow over the next seven days. For 13 to 26 November 2026, the two-week outlook mid-mountain and above gives a forecast range of 69 cm to 108 cm (89 cm middle).

Tips for skiers

  • Aim for 15-21 December or 22-28 December, when 50 cm or more at mid-mountain (2037 m) rises to 54 % and 70 % of past winters.
  • Ski mainly between mid-mountain (2037 m) and the top (2664 m), since the 1411 m base averages 19 cm.
  • Check the avalanche bulletin before going off-piste, especially higher up where usual December snow hits 106 cm.
  • Book the 44 EUR 1-day pass on the official Puy-Saint-Vincent site, or compare Serre Chevalier's 276.00 EUR early-December 6-day pass.
