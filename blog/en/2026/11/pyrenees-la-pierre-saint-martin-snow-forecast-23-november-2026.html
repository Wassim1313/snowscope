La Pierre Saint-Martin snow forecast: up to 42 cm expected by 26 November (Pyrenees)

PyreneesSnow forecastsPowder alertsPublished Monday 23 November 2026 · 11:29 UTCData of 23 November3 min read

On 24 November the freezing level at Astun climbs to 1,960 m to 3,170 m, higher than the top of the lifts (2,272 m): expect rain or wet snow on every slope. And more is coming: by 26 November, the forecast brings 47 cm at Astun, 47 cm at Cauterets Cirque du Lys and 45 cm at Formigal. In the field we always carry a probe; numbers like these tell you where to go, the probe tells you what is under your skis.

What is on the ground

Among the resorts in this report, Cauterets Cirque du Lys has the deepest snow up high today, about 20 cm, ahead of Astun 16 cm and La Pierre Saint-Martin 13 cm.

Top, middle, bottom

Warm air at Cauterets Cirque du Lys on 24 November: the freezing level goes from 2,240 m to 3,060 m, above the resort's top (2,420 m), so rain or wet snow can reach every slope.

On 24 November the freezing level at Formigal climbs to 1,960 m to 3,090 m, higher than the top of the lifts (2,264 m): expect rain or wet snow on every slope.

At Astun, the difference between top and bottom is clear: 4 cm at the top (2,272 m), 0 cm at mid-mountain (1,965 m) and 0 cm at the base (1,659 m).

The days ahead

The snow is not done yet: we expect 44 cm at Artouste and 42 cm at La Pierre Saint-Martin by 26 November.

The snow has spread since 21 November, from 1,650 km² to 2,480 km² today; for 26 November our forecast gives 9,200 km².

Staying safe

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Tips for skiers

  • Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.
  • Air above freezing over the slopes turns snow into slush and water. The highest lifts keep the driest snow.
  • The best powder lasts a morning, not a week. Go early, before the sun and the crowds get to it.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Astun gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes on the date shown; they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
