Lake Como in December on a small budget: 3 days, a snow day and €40 saved per ski day

AlpsGuidesPublished Tuesday 10 November 2026 · 12:31 UTCData of 10 November3 min read

Lake Como in December is quiet, cheap and has snow an hour away. Our 3-day plan costs €92.30 for all trains, ferries, the funicular and one ski day, about what a single ski day pass costs in Cortina at Christmas (€89).

The plan on one map

Six stops: Brunate above Como (1), Bellagio (2), San Martino above Griante (3), Varenna (4), and two snow options, Piani di Bobbio (5) and Madesimo (6). All prices are the 2026-27 fares published by the operators. Beds and meals are not in the totals because we found no official prices for them, so add your own.

Day 1: Como and the balcony of Brunate

Take the regional train from Milano Centrale to Como San Giovanni (€5.40, about 40 minutes). From the lakefront the funicular climbs to Brunate (€7.20 return); walk up to the Faro Voltiano lighthouse for the view over the lake to the Alps, often white in December.

In the afternoon the ferry from Como to Bellagio costs €12. In winter there are far fewer boats, so check the winter timetable the day before.

Day 2: the central lake by ferry

Start at Punta Spartivento, the tip of Bellagio where the three arms of the lake meet: free. Then two ferry hops, Bellagio to Menaggio and Menaggio to Varenna, at €5.50 each. The central-lake day pass costs €17.50 and only pays off from the fourth crossing.

Above Cadenabbia a steep footpath climbs to the little church of San Martino on its rock ledge, one of the best free views of the lake. End in Varenna on the lakefront walk; Castello di Vezio above the village opens only on some winter days, so check before you climb.

Day 3: a snow day

From Lecco a bus goes up to Barzio, where the cable car to Piani di Bobbio is included in the ski pass: €49 a day in high season (weekends, 7-8 December and from 26 December to 6 January) and €41 on other days.

Madesimo, higher and bigger, is reached by bus from Chiavenna: €59 in high season, but €41 before Christmas, from 9 to 24 December. The train back from Varenna to Milano costs €7.70. The train to Lecco and the mountain buses are not in our total.

How much you save

All trains, ferries and the funicular of this plan cost €43.30; with a high-season ski day at Bobbio the total is €92.30, or €84.30 on a low-season day. One Dolomiti Superski day pass for Cortina costs €89 at Christmas.

Per ski day, Bobbio is €40 cheaper than Cortina (45 %) and Madesimo €30 cheaper (34 %). Over three ski days at Christmas that is €267 in Cortina against €147 at Bobbio: €120 saved on lift passes alone. Beds and meals are not compared here.

Will there be snow at Christmas?

Today Piani di Bobbio has 2 cm at the top (1860 m) after 14 cm last week, and Madesimo 1 cm at mid-mountain. For December the European seasonal forecast is about 1.6 °C warmer than usual here, with 12 % more precipitation.

At Piani di Bobbio's mid-mountain (1595 m), 41 of the last 45 winters had at least 5 cm of snow on 25 December. With this year's forecast the chance stays at 91 %, with about 17 cm that day instead of the usual 22 cm. Madesimo (1885 m) is safer: 44 of 45 winters, 99 % this year, about 33 cm. The chance of a snowier December than usual is 38 % at Bobbio and 41 % at Madesimo: no clear signal either way.

Tips for skiers

  • Buy single ferry tickets unless you plan four crossings or more.
  • Ski Madesimo before Christmas, from 9 to 24 December, at the pre-season price.
  • Check the winter ferry timetable and the SnowScope snow map the day before you go up.
