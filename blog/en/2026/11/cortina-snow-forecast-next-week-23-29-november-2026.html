    Cortina d'Ampezzo snow forecast 23–29 November 2026: only 5–21 cm, a light refresh

    AlpsGuidesPublished Monday 16 November 2026 · 11:51 UTCData of 16 November2 min read

    Next week is a quiet one for Cortina d'Ampezzo. Our forecast gives the upper slopes (around 2700 m) 13 cm between 23 and 29 November, with a low case of 5 cm and a high case of 21 cm, and the base around 1100 m only almost nothing (0 cm). It freshens the surface; it does not build a base.

    How the week of 23–29 November adds up in Cortina d'Ampezzo

    At the top (around 2700 m) the week totals 13 cm, 5–21 cm across our range. Mid-mountain around 1800 m gets 12 cm (4–21 cm), and the base around 1100 m almost nothing (0 cm) (0–1 cm).

    Most of the total arrives on Wednesday 25 (5 cm, up to 7) and Thursday 26 (6 cm, up to 9), counted at the top as the middle of our forecast and the high case.

    Where the good snow will be

    The snowiest day up top is Thursday 26 November, with 6 cm in the middle of our forecast (up to 9 cm). That is a fresh coat on the groomers, nothing more.

    Go high: depth is safest on the Tofane and the Faloria and Cristallo areas, and the best skiing should be on the north-facing Tofana runs above Pocol. Treat the town of Cortina as the weak point.

    Where the snow line sits

    Don't judge Cortina d'Ampezzo from the village. With 0 cm at the bottom and 13 cm at the top, the lower runs are near or under the snow line, and much of what falls there may be rain.

    The Dolomites usually get less from north-west storms than the western Alps, and this week is no exception.

    How nearby resorts compare

    Same week, same measure (top of each resort): Val Gardena (30 km away) 11 cm; Hintertux (71 km away) 14 cm; Mayrhofen (75 km away) 17 cm. Cortina d'Ampezzo itself: 13 cm.

    Nothing to separate them on snow alone; the choice comes down to the terrain you want.

    What 17–20 November leaves behind

    Little lands in Cortina d'Ampezzo before the weekend: 4 cm at the top from Tuesday 17 to Friday 20 November. Most of what the resort gets this fortnight comes in the second week.

    The range, not just the number

    Reasonably, with a caveat. The snow is coming to Cortina d'Ampezzo, but the top could end up anywhere between 5 and 21 cm, and the middle value of 13 cm is only the halfway mark.

    Storm timing tends to firm up first; totals keep moving until a day or two before.

    Tips for skiers

    • Off the groomed runs, new snow means new avalanche risk: the regional avalanche bulletin (bollettino valanghe) is the reference.
    • Check the resort's own opening date and lift status: snow on the mountain does not mean lifts are running.
    • With 13 cm at the top, this is not a week to chase powder in Cortina d'Ampezzo.
