Les Deux Alpes: how much fresh snow fell? 38 cm in 72 hours (15 November)

AlpsPowder alertsPublished Sunday 15 November 2026 · 17:06 UTCData of 15 November2 min read

Where did the most fresh snow fall in the Alps? In the last 72 hours La Grave received 41 cm of new snow, and 31 cm of that came in the last 24 hours. That leaves about 97 cm on its upper slopes, by our snow model. New snow on a cold morning is light; by the afternoon on a sunny slope it is a different material.

Fresh snow, place by place

Other resorts did well too: Les Deux Alpes, Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine and Crévoux got 38 cm, 37 cm and 37 cm over the last 72 hours.

There is snow at 851 of 1,178 resorts in the Alps today, over 63,570 km² of ground in total.

Which height to ski today

From the base to the top of La Grave: 76 cm at the base (3,196 m), 83 cm at mid-mountain (3,369 m) and 83 cm at the top (3,543 m).

Expect two seasons in one resort at La Grave on 16 November: a freezing level of 2,610 m to 3,210 m means wet snow below and wintry snow above.

The days ahead

The snow is not done yet: we expect 65 cm at La Grave, 54 cm at Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine and 51 cm at Les Deux Alpes by 18 November.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

What the stations saw

At the Sonnblick station (3,109 m), the gauge showed 55 cm of snow on the ground on 13 November, 23 cm of it new in 24 hours. On 13 November, the Sulden Madritsch station (2,825 m) measured 33 cm on the ground, including 23 cm of new snow in 24 hours. At the Schnals Teufelsegg station (3,035 m), the gauge showed 40 cm of snow on the ground on 13 November, 22 cm of it new in 24 hours.

A word on the numbers: resort values are our model's estimate for the upper slopes, while each station measures a single spot on the date given.

Avalanche danger and safety

For the La Grave area, today's official avalanche danger is 2 on the 5-level scale (Météo-France BERA).

After fresh snow, wind builds drifts on sheltered slopes. Off-piste, the first days after a snowfall need the most care.

Tips for skiers

  • New snow does not stay new for long. Start early, while it is still soft and untracked.
  • Ski the shady side: high, north-facing slopes hold their snow, while slopes facing south go bare first.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when La Grave gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 15 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
