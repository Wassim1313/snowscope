Montgenèvre vs Sestriere in December: High Base Wins Early

AlpsGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:29 UTCData of 14 November4 min read

If you pack the car for a December ski weekend on the French-Italian border, Montgenèvre beats Sestriere at village level. Its base sits at 1795 m with 56 cm of usual December snow, compared with 24 cm at Sestriere's 1600 m base. Higher up, both resorts deliver solid snow, yet Montgenèvre gives you safer early cover from the bottom of the lifts.

A Base Already High Up

For a weekend driver, starting high saves hassle. Montgenèvre's mapped ski area has 62 km of runs and 24 mapped lifts between 1795 m and 2581 m, giving 786 m of vertical. Because the bottom of the lifts sits at 1795 m, you start on higher ground right away.

Today's snow estimate on 13 November 2026 shows 0 cm at the base, 5 cm at mid-mountain (2188 m), and 11 cm at the top (2581 m). New snow over the next 7 days is estimated at 23 cm at the base, 36 cm at mid-mountain, and 52 cm at the top. Across the two-week outlook from 13 to 26 November 2026, mid-mountain and above carry a forecast range of 61 to 95 cm.

Typical Snow vs Thin Decembers

In our snow history of 46 past winters (1980-81 to 2025-26), Montgenèvre holds 56 cm in a usual December at the base, and 55 cm on 15 December. In a thin December (1 winter in 10), base depth drops below 22 cm, while a deep December (1 winter in 10) sits above 102 cm. Last winter (2025-26) averaged 37 cm at the base, and the thinnest December was 2015-16 with 13 cm, compared with 123 cm in the deepest December of 1996-97.

Climb higher and the snow builds further. Mid-mountain (2188 m) averages 65 cm in a usual December (64 cm on 15 December), ranging from 28 cm in a thin winter to 115 cm in a deep one. The top (2581 m) averages 78 cm, with 36 cm in thin winters and 139 cm in deep ones.

Timing matters for a weekend trip. At the base, 30 cm or more arrives in 61 % of past winters during 1-7 December and 76 % during 8-14 December, climbing to 89 % during 15-21 December and 96 % during 22-28 December (with 50 cm or more reaching 35 %, 46 %, 59 %, and 72 %). Mid-mountain sees 30 cm or more in 70 %, 87 %, 98 %, and 98 % of past winters week by week. At the top, 30 cm or more reaches 83 %, 91 %, 98 %, and 98 %.

What the Lift Pass Costs

For budget-minded weekend drivers, pass dates make a clear difference. On the official site, an adult 1-day Montgenèvre pass costs 45.50 EUR until 18 December 2026. From 19 December 2026, high season begins and the adult 1-day Montgenèvre pass costs 56.00 EUR. For a 6-day trip, an adult 6-day Montgenèvre + Monts de la Lune pass costs 301.00 EUR in high season from 19 December 2026.

Pass prices for Sestriere were not checked on the official site, so we do not give any price or claim which resort costs less.

Montgenèvre Against Sestriere Over the Border

Sestriere sits across the border in Italy, with a mapped ski area of 58 km of runs and 22 mapped lifts from 1600 m to 2786 m (1186 m vertical). Its higher top lifts hold 91 cm in a usual December (90 cm on 15 December), with 41 cm in a thin winter and 163 cm in a deep one.

Down at the bottom of the lifts, however, December is often thin at Sestriere's 1600 m base. Usual December depth is 24 cm (25 cm on 15 December), dropping below 6 cm in 1 thin winter in 10 and sitting above 57 cm in a deep one. Across the four weeks, Sestriere's base hits 30 cm or more in only 26 %, 33 %, 41 %, and 48 % of past winters (and 50 cm or more in 9 % to 17 %). At mid-mountain (2193 m), usual December depth is 51 cm.

How 46 Winters Have Shifted

Across our snow history, Montgenèvre's December averages were higher in the last 15 winters (2011-12 to 2025-26) than in the first 15 winters (1980-81 to 1994-95). At the base, the December middle average moved from 41 cm to 68 cm. Mid-mountain rose from 49 cm to 76 cm, and the top shifted from 62 cm to 89 cm, showing stronger recent early-season depth despite thin years like 2015-16.

Tips for skiers

  • Target 8-14 December or ski before 19 December 2026 if you want the 45.50 EUR adult 1-day pass alongside 30 cm or more at mid-mountain in 87 % to 98 % of past winters.
  • Stick to mid-mountain (2188 m) and the top (2581 m) if you drive up during 1-7 December, where 30 cm or more appears in 70 % and 83 % of past winters rather than 61 % at the base.
  • Book your Montgenèvre lift pass on the official site before high season starts on 19 December 2026 at 56.00 EUR.
  • Check the avalanche bulletin before skiing off-piste, especially after early-season snowfalls on thin base layers.
