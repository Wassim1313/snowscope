Trysil in December: Norway's big area, week by week, and how to pay less

ScandinaviaGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:29 UTCData of 14 November2 min read

Key figure: on a typical 15 December Trysil has 44 cm of snow at mid-mountain (758 m) in our 46 winters of snow history, and 83 % of winters had 30 cm or more there in the week of 15-21 December. That is a solid December for the biggest ski area in Norway, with 101 km of mapped runs and 40 lifts.

Early December

The first week is the weak one. In 1-7 December only 57 % of our winters had 30 cm at mid-mountain, and 30 % at the bottom (407 m). The top (1109 m) is safer at 76 %. Days are short this far north in December, so plan on fewer hours on the slopes and more on the sofa.

Mid-December

By 15-21 December the picture is good: 83 % of winters with 30 cm at mid-mountain and 89 % at the top. A typical 15 December has 26 cm at the bottom, 44 cm at mid-mountain and 60 cm at the top. Even a thin year (1 in 10) keeps 21 cm at mid-mountain.

Christmas week

Christmas week is the best of the month: 87 % of winters had 30 cm at mid-mountain and 93 % at the top, and 59 % had 60 cm or more up high. The bottom catches up late: the usual first day with 30 cm there is 18 December.

Then and now

The December average at mid-mountain fell from 52 cm in the first 15 winters to 41 cm in the last 15, and the share of Christmas weeks with 30 cm at the bottom dropped from 87 % to 60 %. The top held better: 100 % and 87 %.

Last winter shows both sides: 62 cm at the top on 15 December, but only 4 cm at the bottom on 24 December.

The bill

Trysil sells lift passes with dynamic prices in its operator's official online shop: the price depends on the date and how early you buy. On 13 November 2026 we could not read a fixed number on the official page, so we quote none. The rule of thumb with this kind of pricing is simple: buy online, early, and for dates outside the Christmas holidays.

Against Sälen

Sälen, across the border in Sweden, is the other big December choice for families. It is lower and flatter: our history gives one value for the whole area, 28 cm on a typical 15 December, and 48 % of winters with 30 cm in the week of 15-21 December. Trysil's mid-mountain beats that clearly, and its top even more.

Tips for skiers

  • Choose 15-21 December or Christmas week: the share of winters with 30 cm at mid-mountain rises from 57 % in the first week to 83 % and 87 %.
  • Buy the lift pass online as early as you can; with dynamic prices, late purchases for popular dates cost more.
  • Plan for short December days: start at first light and keep lunch short.
  • Pack for real cold: December in inland Norway can be far below freezing.
