Verbier snow forecast next week (23–29 November 2026): 24 cm at the top

AlpsGuidesPublished Monday 16 November 2026 · 11:31 UTCData of 16 November2 min read

Verbier has fresh snow on the way for the week of 23–29 November. At the top, around 2900 m, our forecast adds up to 24 cm, with 5 cm in a low case and 42 cm in a high one; mid-mountain gets 22 cm and the base around 900 m 9 cm.

This week in Verbier: the set-up

This week is the heavy one: 74 cm at the top between 17 and 20 November (range 56–91 cm), 0 cm at the base. Next week's snow falls onto that, which matters more than the size of the second round.

Next week, height by height

Our forecast splits Verbier's week like this: upper slopes 24 cm (low 5, high 42), middle of the mountain 22 cm, lower runs 9 cm (low 1, high 17).

Day by day, the upper mountain picks up most of it on Tuesday 24 (2 cm, up to 5), Wednesday 25 (5 cm, up to 7), Thursday 26 (7 cm, up to 13) and Sunday 29 (4 cm, up to 8). Outside those days expect little more than flurries.

Down at the base

At the base the total drops to 9 cm, roughly half of the top. Expect heavier, wetter snow on the lower runs and a snow line that wanders around village level on the mildest day.

Verbier is the big name of the 4 Vallées, the domain that also covers Nendaz, Veysonnaz and Thyon.

Verbier against its neighbours

For comparison, the upper slopes of the closest big resorts collect Chamonix (32 km away) 27 cm, Cervinia (35 km away) 23 cm and Avoriaz (37 km away) 29 cm; Verbier sits at 24 cm.

A few centimetres either way is inside the forecast's own margin, so Verbier and its neighbours are effectively level.

When and where to ski Verbier

The snowiest day up top is Thursday 26 November, with 7 cm in the middle of our forecast (up to 13 cm). That is a fresh coat on the groomers, nothing more.

Go high: depth is safest on Mont Fort and its glacier, and the best skiing should be on the shaded Mont Gelé and Tortin slopes. Treat Le Châble at the valley floor and the lower Savoleyres runs as the weak point.

Confidence, in plain words

Low. At the top our forecast spans 5 to 42 cm: one version of the week brings a decent fall, the other almost nothing. Check again midweek before committing.

Storm timing tends to firm up first; totals keep moving until a day or two before.

Tips for skiers

  • Off the groomed runs, new snow means new avalanche risk: the Swiss avalanche bulletin is the reference.
  • Opening dates are the resort's call; our numbers only say how much snow is coming.
  • Pack for wet weather in the village: the 9 cm at the base hides a lot of rain.
