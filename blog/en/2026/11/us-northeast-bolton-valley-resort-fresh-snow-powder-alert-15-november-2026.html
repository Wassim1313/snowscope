Fresh snow in the US Northeast compared: Bolton Valley Resort leads with 4 in in 72 hours (15 November)

US NortheastPowder alertsPublished Sunday 15 November 2026 · 20:24 UTCData of 15 November3 min read

The snow has pulled back since 13 November, from 6,880 sq mi (17,820 km²) to 6,039 sq mi (15,640 km²) today; for 18 November our forecast gives 382 sq mi (990 km²). Bolton Valley Resort picked up 4 in (10 cm) of fresh snow over the last 72 hours, 2 in (5 cm) of it in the last 24. That leaves about 3 in (8 cm) on its upper slopes, by our snow model. Field work in dry mountains teaches one thing: the map is a start, the slope itself has the last word.

What to expect next

Between now and 18 November we expect 0 in (1 cm) at Bolton Valley Resort and 0 in (1 cm) at Stowe.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

What the stations saw

On 13 November, the Lake Pleasant 2.3 WSW station (1,778 ft / 542 m) measured 4 in (9 cm) on the ground, including 4 in (9 cm) of new snow in 24 hours. At the Shrewsbury 0.8 W station (1,486 ft / 453 m), the gauge showed 3 in (8 cm) of snow on the ground on 13 November, 3 in (8 cm) of it new in 24 hours. On 13 November, the Rome 4.8 SSE station (561 ft / 171 m) measured 2 in (5 cm) on the ground, including 2 in (5 cm) of new snow in 24 hours.

The resort numbers describe an area and come from our snow model; the stations measure one point only.

Safety and avalanche level

After fresh snow, wind builds drifts on sheltered slopes. Off-piste, the first days after a snowfall need the most care.

Tips for skiers

  • Fresh snow has a short best-before time: the first hours. Early starts win.
  • South-facing slopes take the most sun and melt first; high, north-facing slopes keep their snow the longest.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Bolton Valley Resort gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 15 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
