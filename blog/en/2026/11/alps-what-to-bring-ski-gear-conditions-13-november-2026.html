The kit list

For powder: more width under your feet, a lens for flat light, a neck warmer for the spray.

Where and when, briefly

Early start, fewer tracks; off-piste, the first days after a snowfall carry the most risk.

Off-piste safety kit

Off the groomed runs, carry an avalanche transceiver, a shovel and a probe, know how to use them, and never go alone. Read the official avalanche bulletin first.

Tips for skiers

  • Local ski shops know the day's snow: rent there, or have your own skis serviced.
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and dates on the resort's website.
  • Before any off-piste or touring day, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • The 0 °C level is a forecast and can move by several hundred metres, which changes where rain turns to snow: check the map again the day before you go.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 13 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
