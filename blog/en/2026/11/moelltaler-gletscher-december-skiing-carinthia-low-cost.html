Mölltaler Gletscher in December: deep snow, small area, low-season price

AlpsGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:14 UTCData of 14 November3 min read

The catch first: the Mölltaler Gletscher is small, with 21 km of mapped runs and 9 lifts, and it is a long way up from the Möll valley. Everything else points the right way: its lowest lift is at 2103 m, where a typical 15 December has 66 cm in our 46 winters of snow history, and the published low season runs until Christmas Eve.

The catch

Do not come here for variety. Nine lifts and 21 km of runs fill two or three days for a keen skier, not a week. And the glacier sits at the head of a quiet Carinthian valley, so you need a car or the resort's own transport, and evenings are calm.

The other catch is about information: at the time of checking, the official price page still showed the 2025-26 list. Prices for this winter may move a little.

Why it still works

Height. The ski area starts at 2103 m and climbs to 3084 m. In December that means you ski on a full cover from the first lift. In the week of 15-21 December, 91 % of our winters had 30 cm or more at the bottom lift, and 100 % at mid-mountain (2593 m).

It is also one of the few places where Christmas week is close to certain for deep snow: in 98 % of our winters mid-mountain had 60 cm or more in the week of 22-28 December.

Snow by date

Typical depth at the bottom lift: 50 cm on 1 December, 66 cm on 15 December, 74 cm on 24 December. At mid-mountain: 93, 128 and 129 cm. Even a thin year (1 winter in 10) still has 28 cm at the bottom on 15 December, and there was no winter in 46 with bare ground there on that day.

The long view is less comfortable: the December average at mid-mountain fell from 117 cm in the first 15 winters to 92 cm in the last 15. Last winter had 58 cm there on 15 December. Deep, but well below the old normal.

What it costs

On the official list (2025-26, the latest published), an adult day pass costs EUR 69 in the low season, which runs until 24 December, and EUR 72 in the main season. A 6-day pass costs EUR 332 in the low season and EUR 376 in the main season.

So a ski week that ends before Christmas Eve gets the lower price, on a mountain that is snow-sure in that week.

Compared with the Kitzsteinhorn

The Kitzsteinhorn above Kaprun is the better-known glacier on the other side of the Hohe Tauern. Its 6-day pass costs EUR 396 in its early season and EUR 440 in the main season. Its top is deep (137 cm on a typical 15 December), but its mid-mountain at 1894 m has 40 cm and its base at 765 m only 6 cm.

The Mölltaler Gletscher is cheaper and deeper at every height you will actually ski in December. You give up size, nightlife and short transfers.

Tips for skiers

  • Combine it with a second area for a full week: three days on the glacier, the rest elsewhere in Carinthia.
  • Keep the pass within the low season (until 24 December) for EUR 332 instead of EUR 376 for 6 days.
  • Check this winter's price list on the official site before paying: the published list was still last season's.
  • Glacier terrain off the piste hides crevasses: go only with a guide and read the Carinthia avalanche report.
