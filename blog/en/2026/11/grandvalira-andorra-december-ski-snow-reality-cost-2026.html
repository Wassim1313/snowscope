Is Grandvalira Worth Skiing in December on a Budget?

PyreneesGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:29 UTCData of 14 November5 min read

Andorra has a name for budget skiing, so is Grandvalira a good December bet? Our snow history of 46 past winters (1980-81 to 2025-26) says natural snow at the base is thin all month and early December is unreliable. The trip is worth it in late December, high on the mountain, with a pass bought online.

The promise versus the snow numbers

Grandvalira in Andorra is big: the mapped ski area has 137 km of runs and 53 lifts between 1674 m and 2619 m. That looks reassuring until you check natural snow by height in our snow history. At the bottom of the lifts, at 1674 m, the usual December depth is 9 cm; a thin December, 1 winter in 10, is close to 0 cm, and a deep one tops 28 cm.

At mid-mountain at 2146 m, usual December snow is 27 cm, ranging from below 2 cm in a thin 1-in-10 winter to above 63 cm in a deep one. Up at the top at 2619 m, usual December depth is 45 cm, from below 5 cm in 1 winter in 10 to above 101 cm in a deep 1-in-10 winter. Usual depth on 15 December is 8 cm at the base, 25 cm at mid-mountain, and 42 cm at the top.

On 13 November, today's snow estimate is 0 cm at all three heights, with 0 cm of new snow expected in the next 7 days at the base and mid-mountain and 3 cm at the top. The two-week forecast range to 26 November for mid-mountain and above is 2 to 17 cm, with 10 cm in the middle, showing a slow start to the season.

Which December week gives you snow

Week-by-week shares show why early December is a gamble for a cautious skier. At the base at 1674 m, snow reaches 30 cm or more in only 2 % of past winters in 1-7 December and 7 % in each of the next three weeks, while 50 cm or more happens in 0 % all month. At mid-mountain at 2146 m, 30 cm or more occurs in 30 %, 41 %, 50 %, and 65 % of past winters across those four weeks.

The top of the lifts at 2619 m is where December becomes dependable later on. In 1-7 December, 30 cm or more turns up in 43 % of past winters and 50 cm or more in 28 %. By 8-14 December, those shares rise to 57 % and 39 %, then 63 % and 48 % in 15-21 December, and 76 % and 63 % in 22-28 December.

What the lift pass really costs

For Monday 14 December 2026, Grandvalira's official site lists an adult (18-64) 1-day pass bought online at 62.50 EUR, while the ticket-office price for that day is 72.00 EUR. Pass prices change by date, so checking early and buying online makes a clear difference. Other trip costs were not checked, so a cheaper pass alone does not guarantee a low-cost holiday.

Grandvalira compared with Baqueira Beret

In Spain, Baqueira Beret's mapped ski area has 154 km of runs and 36 mapped lifts from 1504 m to 2600 m. Yet Grandvalira has deeper usual natural snow in December across the mountain. Baqueira Beret's usual December depth is 8 cm at the base, 20 cm at mid-mountain at 2052 m, and 30 cm at the top at 2600 m. At mid-mountain, Baqueira reaches 30 cm or more in 22 %, 30 %, 35 %, and 41 % of past winters across the four December weeks. At the top, it hits 30 cm or more in 39 %, 46 %, 52 %, and 59 % of past winters, and 50 cm or more in 13 %, 22 %, 30 %, and 37 %.

For the 2026-27 season, Baqueira Beret's official site lists an adult 1-day Baqueira Ticket at 74,00 €, a 1-day pass with the rechargeable Baqueira Pass card at 70,00 €, and a 6-day Baqueira Ticket at 359,00 €. Grandvalira's checked online 1-day price of 62.50 EUR for 14 December is lower than Baqueira Beret's 1-day rates. However, Grandvalira's 72.00 EUR ticket-office rate sits between them, and Grandvalira prices change by date.

Are recent Decembers thinner than the past

Comparing the first 15 winters (1980-81 to 1994-95) with the last 15 winters (2011-12 to 2025-26), the middle December average stayed at 8 cm at the base, edged from 21 cm to 25 cm at mid-mountain, and rose from 35 cm to 41 cm at the top. Even so, last winter's December in 2025-26 was below that recent middle, averaging 8 cm at the base, 19 cm at mid-mountain, and 32 cm at the top. Year-to-year swings remain large: 1989-90 fell to 1 cm at both mid-mountain and the top, while 1996-97 reached 35 cm, 82 cm, and 129 cm.

Tips for skiers

  • Target 22-28 December if you can, when the top at 2619 m reaches 30 cm or more in 76 % of past winters and 50 cm or more in 63 %.
  • Plan your skiing between 2146 m and 2619 m, because the base at 1674 m averages only 9 cm in a usual December.
  • Book your lift pass on Grandvalira's official site to pay 62.50 EUR instead of 72.00 EUR for 14 December, and check prices for your exact dates.
  • Check the avalanche bulletin before any off-piste skiing, especially when early December snow cover is thin.
