Dévoluy snow outlook: in line for 24–32 cm by 16 November (Alps)

AlpsSnow forecastsPowder alertsPublished Friday 13 November 2026 · 13:16 UTCData of 13 November2 min read

The next few days look decidedly unsettled in the Alps, with the heaviest snow concentrated in a handful of resorts. Next round by 16 November: 35 cm at Orcières Merlette, 33 cm at La Grave and 24–32 cm at Dévoluy, according to our forecast. How sure are we? Resort values are model estimates for the upper slopes; stations are measurements, each at one point on the date shown. Confidence: good for the trend, lower for any single number.

Snow line check

On 13 November the freezing level at Orcières Merlette sits at 2,530 m to 2,770 m, over the top of the resort (2,731 m): rain or wet snow could fall on all slopes.

La Grave, 14 November: freezing level 2,700 m to 3,210 m. Wet snow low down, winter up top.

Dévoluy, 13 November: freezing level 2,560 m to 2,980 m, above the top (2,495 m). Rain or wet snow is possible on every slope.

Orcières Merlette by height, from our model: top 13 cm (2,731 m), middle 1 cm (2,278 m), base 0 cm (1,826 m).

Staying safe

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Tips for skiers

  • Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.
  • Freezing level above the top station? Expect wet snow and ski as high as the lifts allow.
  • Be on the first lift after a snowfall: by midday, sun and skiers have already changed the snow.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Orcières Merlette gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 13 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
