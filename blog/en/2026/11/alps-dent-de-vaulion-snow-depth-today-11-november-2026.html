Dent de Vaulion has the most snow in the Alps: 300 cm on the upper slopes (11 November)

AlpsWhere to skiPublished Wednesday 11 November 2026 · 18:44 UTCData of 11 November2 min read

At 1,308 m on Dent de Vaulion, today's 0 cm is more than this date brought in 34 of the past 46 winters (usual: 0 cm): a good amount of water is already stored in the snow. The biggest snow store in the Alps today is at Dent de Vaulion (Switzerland): about 300 cm on its upper slopes. L'Orient holds 121 cm and Ovronnaz 98 cm. Today 38,440 km² of ground in the Alps is covered by snow, our model says, and 623 of 1,179 resorts have snow on their upper slopes. For the rivers downstream, how long the snow stays matters as much as how much falls.

The next days in the forecast

By 14 November the forecast adds 33 cm at Glacier 3000, 30 cm at Ovronnaz and 21 cm at Crans-Montana to the snowpack.

At Dent de Vaulion the freezing level reaches 2,280 m to 2,800 m on 12 November, higher than the top (1,465 m): rain or wet snow is possible on all slopes, and some of the snow may turn to runoff.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

What the gauges recorded

Measured, not modelled: the Weißseespitze station (3,499 m) recorded 352 cm of snow on the ground on 10 November, with 60 cm of new snow in 24 hours. The Lanzada Passo Marinelli station (3,032 m) reported a snow depth of 35 cm on 11 November, 5 cm of it from the last 24 hours. Measured, not modelled: the Glacier de l'Evole station (2,800 m) recorded 33 cm of snow on the ground on 11 November, with 6 cm of new snow in 24 hours.

Two kinds of numbers here: model estimates for the upper slopes of each resort, and station measurements that are exact but cover one point on the date shown.

Tips for skiers

  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Early-season snow is thin. Stay on open, groomed pistes: rocks and grass can hide just under the surface.
  • A rule of thumb that is rarely wrong: high and north-facing keeps snow, south-facing loses it first.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Dent de Vaulion gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 11 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
