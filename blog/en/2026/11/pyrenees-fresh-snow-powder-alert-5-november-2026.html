Fresh snow in the Pyrenees: 15 cm in 72 hours at Rasos de Peguera (5 November)

PyreneesPublished Thursday 5 November 2026Data of 5 November2 min read

Rasos de Peguera got 15 cm of new snow in the last 72 hours. It now has about 20 cm on its upper slopes, according to our snow model.

Where the fresh snow fell

Estació d'esquí Port del Comte got 14 cm of new snow in the last 72 hours.

28 of 53 resorts in the Pyrenees have snow on their upper slopes today, and our model counts 820 km² of snow-covered ground.

Which height to ski

At Rasos de Peguera, the freezing level reaches 2,970 m to 3,520 m on 8 November, above the top of the resort (2,036 m): rain or wet snow is possible on every slope that day.

What is coming next

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Safety and avalanche level

After fresh snow, wind builds drifts on sheltered slopes. Off-piste, the first days after a snowfall need the most care.

Tips for skiers

  • Fresh snow is best in the first hours after it stops: go early, before the sun and the crowds.
  • Look for snow high up and on north-facing slopes: they keep it longest, while south-facing slopes lose it first in the sun.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Rasos de Peguera gets fresh snow.
