Pitztal Glacier in December: the high one that did not lose its snow

AlpsGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 14:54 UTCData of 14 November3 min read

Most December charts in the Alps point down. The Pitztal Glacier is the odd one out: in our 46 winters of snow history its December average at mid-mountain (2576 m) went from 77 cm in the first 15 winters to 85 cm in the last 15. Snow is the safe part of the deal here; the price is the part to think about.

December, then and now

At the top (3424 m) the December average rose from 142 cm in the first 15 winters to 174 cm in the last 15. Even the valley station (1728 m) went from 31 to 35 cm. That does not mean the climate got colder; it means this high, glaciated corner of Tyrol still turns most of its winter moisture into snow.

In Christmas week every one of the last 15 winters had 30 cm or more at mid-mountain, and the share with 30 cm at the bottom lift rose from 60 % to 67 %.

The heights that still deliver

Mid-mountain and above are the December zone. On a typical 15 December mid-mountain has 87 cm and the top 171 cm. In the week of 15-21 December all our winters had 30 cm or more at both heights, and in 85 % of winters mid-mountain had 60 cm or more in Christmas week.

The bottom is another story: 32 cm on a typical 15 December, and only 59 % of winters reached 30 cm in the week of 15-21 December. The usual first day with 30 cm down there is 10 December. Plan to ride the lift down.

Price for the week

The official 2026-27 price list uses one rate for the whole season: EUR 77 for an adult day pass and EUR 410 for 6 days. There is no pre-Christmas discount, so this is not a bargain week; it is a reliable one.

A useful extra on the same list: from 2-day passes upward, the ticket also includes one day at the Kaunertal Glacier, Winterberg Fendels or Hochzeiger. The nearby Rifflsee area opens on 12 December 2026.

The Stubai Glacier in the same years

The Stubai Glacier, in the next big valley to the east, has almost the same December snow at its middle height: 86 cm at 2450 m on a typical 15 December, and 100 % of winters with 30 cm in the week of 15-21 December. Its bottom lift (1692 m) is a little deeper than Pitztal's, at 40 cm.

On price, Stubai is slightly cheaper: EUR 75.50 a day and EUR 355 for 6 days in the main season. Pitztal's argument is height: its top, at 3424 m, is higher than Stubai's 3208 m.

Booking tips

If you want the safest December snow for a family that does not ski the very top, Stubai gives the same middle cover for a little less. If you want the high terrain, the extra glacier day and the longer trend in your favour, Pitztal earns its price.

Tips for skiers

  • Buy at least a 2-day pass: it adds one day at Kaunertal, Fendels or Hochzeiger at no extra cost.
  • In December ski from mid-mountain up (about 2576 m and higher) and download to the valley by lift.
  • At 3424 m it can be well below freezing in December: protect your face and fingers.
  • Glacier terrain off the piste: crevasses and avalanche risk, read the Tyrol avalanche report and take a guide.
