Ruka in December: a EUR 265 ski week, and which week to pick

ScandinaviaGuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 16:44 UTCData of 14 November2 min read

The money first, because it is simple: Ruka's official 2026-27 list gives EUR 58 for an adult day and EUR 265 for 6 days. The snow needs more care. On a typical 15 December our 46 winters of snow history give 36 cm, but in the first week of December only 39 % of winters had 30 cm or more.

A pre-Christmas week, costed

A 6-day adult pass at EUR 265 is one of the lowest fixed week prices in this series. The same list prices the day at EUR 58. Ruka and Pyhä share one price list, so the numbers are the same at both. Beds, flights and food are on top, and we do not quote those.

With a fixed list price, the saving is not in the ticket but in the dates: choose a week before the Christmas holidays, when accommodation is usually easier to find.

Which week of December to pick

The share of winters with 30 cm or more climbs fast through the month: 39 % in 1-7 December, 61 % in 8-14 December, 72 % in 15-21 December and 85 % in Christmas week. The usual first day with 30 cm is 5 December.

So early December is a coin flip at best on natural snow. From mid-month the odds turn in your favour, and Christmas week is the safest.

Height barely matters here

Ruka is a fell, a low rounded hill, with lifts between 286 m and 494 m, 25 km of mapped runs and 24 lifts. Our history gives one value for the whole area: the top is not much snowier than the bottom. A thin year (1 in 10) has 20 cm on 15 December, a deep one 46 cm. Never deep, rarely bare: in none of our 46 winters was the ground bare on 15 December.

The trend is flat to slightly better: the December average was 37 cm in the first 15 winters and 36 cm in the last 15, and the share of Christmas weeks with 30 cm rose from 80 % to 93 %.

Plan B: Pyhä-Luosto

Pyhä-Luosto, further north-west, has almost the same December: 37 cm on a typical 15 December and 72 % of winters with 30 cm in the week of 15-21 December. It uses the same prices, EUR 58 a day and EUR 265 for 6 days, and its official planned opening is 27 November 2026.

Choose Ruka for more lifts and a livelier village, Pyhä for a quieter week; the snow and the ticket will be about the same.

Tips for skiers

  • Book from 15 December on: 72 % of our winters had 30 cm that week, against 39 % in the first week.
  • Budget the lift pass at EUR 265 for 6 days and spend the effort on finding a cheaper bed before the holidays.
  • Expect short, dim days: plan your skiing around the few hours of light.
  • Bring proper cold-weather gear; December in Finnish Lapland can be bitterly cold.
