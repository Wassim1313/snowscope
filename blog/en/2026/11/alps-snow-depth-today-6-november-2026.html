Alps snow depth today: Bonneval-sur-Arc leads with 52 cm (6 November)

AlpsWhere to skiPublished Friday 6 November 2026 · 11:31 UTCData of 5 November2 min read

Bonneval-sur-Arc (France) has the deepest snow in the Alps today, with about 52 cm on its upper slopes. La Grave follows with 52 cm, then Orelle with 52 cm. Our snow model shows 39,100 km² of snow-covered ground in the Alps today, with snow at 725 of 1,178 resorts.

Today's snow

The snow cover shrank from 47,470 km² on 4 November to 39,100 km² today, and our forecast gives 40,240 km² on 9 November.

Height matters: at Bonneval-sur-Arc, our model gives 21 cm at the top (2,948 m), 6 cm at mid-mountain (2,371 m) and 1 cm at the base (1,794 m).

Is it a good start?

At 3,369 m on La Grave the season is late: our model has only 4 cm today, below the usual 36 cm. Only 9 of the past 46 winters had less snow there on 6 November.

At 2,779 m on Orelle the season is late: our model has only 1 cm today, below the usual 26 cm. Only 5 of the past 46 winters had less snow there on 6 November.

The next days

More snow is on the way: by 9 November our forecast gives 15 cm at La Grave, 15 cm at Les Deux Alpes and 7 cm at Bonneval-sur-Arc.

At Bonneval-sur-Arc, the freezing level reaches 2,250 m to 2,610 m on 9 November, so the lower slopes may get wet snow while the top stays wintry.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Tips for skiers

  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Early-season snow is thin. Stay on open, groomed pistes: rocks and grass can hide just under the surface.
  • Look for snow high up and on north-facing slopes: they keep it longest, while south-facing slopes lose it first in the sun.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Bonneval-sur-Arc gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 5 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
