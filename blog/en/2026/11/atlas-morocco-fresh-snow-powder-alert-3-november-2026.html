Fresh snow in the Atlas (Morocco): 14 cm in 72 hours at Oukaimden (3 November)

Oukaimden got 14 cm of new snow in the last 72 hours, 5 cm of it in the last 24 hours. It now has about 3 cm on its upper slopes, according to our snow model.

Height, slopes and snow line

Height matters: at Oukaimden, our model gives 2 cm at the top (3,260 m), 0 cm at mid-mountain (2,930 m) and 0 cm at the base (2,600 m).

At Oukaimden, the freezing level reaches 3,690 m to 4,130 m on 6 November, above the top of the resort (3,241 m): rain or wet snow is possible on every slope that day.

The next days

More snow is on the way: by 6 November our forecast gives 1 cm at Oukaimden.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Safety and avalanche level

After fresh snow, wind builds drifts on sheltered slopes. Off-piste, the first days after a snowfall need the most care.

Tips for skiers

  • Fresh snow is best in the first hours after it stops: go early, before the sun and the crowds.
  • Look for snow high up and on north-facing slopes: they keep it longest, while south-facing slopes lose it first in the sun.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Oukaimden gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 3 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
