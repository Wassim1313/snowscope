Atlas summits vs ski slopes: snow up to 6 cm on the top (11 November)

Atlas (Morocco)Where to skiSnow forecastsPublished Wednesday 11 November 2026 · 18:44 UTCData of 11 November1 min read

Less ground is white now: 40 km² on 9 November, only 20 km² today, and 10 km² expected on 14 November. Today the Atlas ski slopes are bare. Only the highest summits are white, by our model: about 20 km² of snowy ground, 6 cm at the most. In the High Atlas the snow comes in a few big storms, and the high north faces keep it longest.

What to expect next

Until 14 November, the forecast brings no snow worth the name to the resorts.

When the next storm arrives, keep an eye on Oukaïmeden: its lifts go up to 3,258 m, well above Habri (2,108 m) and Michlifen (2,070 m), which need colder storms to turn white.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Against other winters

From experience in these dry mountains, no: over the past 46 winters, the usual depth on this date at 1,970 m near Michlifen was 0 cm, so a bare start is normal for the Atlas.

Tips for skiers

  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Early-season snow is thin. Stay on open, groomed pistes: rocks and grass can hide just under the surface.
  • Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Habri gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 11 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
