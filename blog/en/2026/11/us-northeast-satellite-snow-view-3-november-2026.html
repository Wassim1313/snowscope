Our satellite layer keeps the last clear view of every place, up to 3 November. Here is what it shows at the resorts with the most snow in the US Northeast.

Where the snow is today

Tuckerman Ravine (United States) has the deepest snow in the US Northeast today, with about 9 cm (4 in) on its upper slopes. Attitash follows with 6 cm (2 in), then Sunday River with 6 cm (2 in).

15 of 111 resorts in the US Northeast have snow on their upper slopes today, and our model counts 360 km² of snow-covered ground.

The snow cover shrank from 1,050 km² on 1 November to 360 km² today, and our forecast gives 0 km² on 6 November.

What the stations measured

Satellites only see the ground on clear days, and a thin cover between trees or rocks can look patchy. Our snow model fills the cloudy days; together they give a reliable picture.

Keep in mind: resort values come from our snow model and describe an area, while stations measure one point.

Tips for skiers

  • Look for snow high up and on north-facing slopes: they keep it longest, while south-facing slopes lose it first in the sun.
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Early-season snow is thin. Stay on open, groomed pistes: rocks and grass can hide just under the surface.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Tuckerman Ravine gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 3 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
