Revelstoke snow forecast: up to 10–21 cm expected by 16 November (Western Canada)

Western CanadaSnow forecastsPowder alertsPublished Friday 13 November 2026 · 14:23 UTCData of 13 November2 min read

Whistler Blackcomb, 13 November: freezing level 1,500 m to 2,700 m, above the top (2,259 m). Rain or wet snow is possible on every slope. Forecast to 16 November: 88 cm at Whistler Blackcomb, 10–21 cm at Revelstoke and 20 cm at Mount Cain Alpine Park. Treat the later days with care. In the valley it will rain; at Whistler Blackcomb the snow starts at about 1,360 m. Small print that matters: resort values are model estimates for an area, stations measure a single point. By height and slope

On 13 November the freezing level at Revelstoke sits at 2,220 m to 3,170 m, over the top of the resort (2,224 m): rain or wet snow could fall on all slopes.

Mount Cain Alpine Park, 13 November: freezing level 1,680 m to 2,090 m, above the top (1,616 m). Rain or wet snow is possible on every slope.

Whistler Blackcomb by height, from our model: top 77 cm (2,259 m), middle 37 cm (1,456 m), base 0 cm (653 m).

Staying safe

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Tips for skiers

  • Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.
  • Air above freezing over the slopes turns snow into slush and water. The highest lifts keep the driest snow.
  • Fresh snow has a short best-before time: the first hours. Early starts win.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Whistler Blackcomb gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 13 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
