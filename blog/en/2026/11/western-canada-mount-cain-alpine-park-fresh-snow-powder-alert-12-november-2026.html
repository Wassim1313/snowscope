Mount Cain Alpine Park fresh snow compared with the region: 15 cm in 72 hours (12 November)

Western CanadaPowder alertsPublished Thursday 12 November 2026 · 15:46 UTCData of 12 November2 min read

Less ground is white now: 281,250 km² on 10 November, only 255,860 km² today, and 245,900 km² expected on 15 November. Tweedsmuir Ski Club picked up 47 cm of fresh snow over the last 72 hours, 8 cm of it in the last 24. That leaves about 70 cm on its upper slopes, by our snow model. In the field we always carry a probe; numbers like these tell you where to go, the probe tells you what is under your skis.

Fresh snow, place by place

Other resorts did well too: Whistler Blackcomb and Mount Cain Alpine Park got 40 cm and 15 cm over the last 72 hours.

Across the region, 39 of 57 resorts in Western Canada have snow up high today, and our model finds 255,860 km² of white ground.

Top, middle, bottom

At Tweedsmuir Ski Club, the difference between top and bottom is clear: 60 cm at the top (1,650 m), 58 cm at mid-mountain (1,619 m) and 56 cm at the base (1,588 m).

On 13 November the freezing level at Tweedsmuir Ski Club sits at 1,050 m to 1,610 m: wet snow is likely low down, while the top keeps winter snow.

What to expect next

The snow is not done yet: we expect 83 cm at Whistler Blackcomb, 37 cm at Mount Cain Alpine Park and 7 cm at Tweedsmuir Ski Club by 15 November.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

From the ground stations

A word on the numbers: resort values are our model's estimate for the upper slopes, while each station measures a single spot on the date given.

Safety and avalanche level

After fresh snow, wind builds drifts on sheltered slopes. Off-piste, the first days after a snowfall need the most care.

Tips for skiers

  • Fresh snow has a short best-before time: the first hours. Early starts win.
  • South-facing slopes take the most sun and melt first; high, north-facing slopes keep their snow the longest.
  • Before you ski off-piste or go touring, read the official avalanche bulletin (links below).
  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Tweedsmuir Ski Club gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 12 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
