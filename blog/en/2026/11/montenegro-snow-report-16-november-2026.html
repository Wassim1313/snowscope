Is there snow in Montenegro? Snow depth and new snow on 16 November 2026

Europe snow by countryPublished Monday 16 November 2026 · 20:42 UTCData of 16 November1 min read

The white area in Montenegro stands at about 80 km² today, 50 km² less than yesterday. In other words, 1 % of the mapped high ground is white.

Skiing conditions

The snow is retreating uphill, so plan on the higher lifts and avoid the low valley runs.

Keep an eye on the forecast: the coming snow could change the picture quickly.

New snow in the last three days

Three-day totals peak at 4 cm, around the Prokletije, Durmitor and Bjelasica.

At least 5 cm fell over some 70 km².

The resorts with the most snow

At the top of the list, Ski centar Savin kuk reports 13 cm.

Close behind: Kolašin 1600 (1 cm).

Ski centar Savin kuk picked up 15 cm in the last three days and could add 4 cm by Thursday 19 November.

At Kolašin 1600: 3 cm of fresh snow over three days, 0 cm more expected by Thursday 19 November.

2 of the 3 resorts we follow in Montenegro have snow on their upper slopes.

Snow on the way?

The week ahead could bring up to 15 cm, 0 cm of it in the next three days.

Monday 23 November stands out, with up to 9 cm in a day.

The second week adds up to 22 cm where it snows most.

Tips for skiers

  • Check the resort's own lift status before you travel.
  • Early-season snow is thin over rocks: ski the groomed runs first.
  • Snow above the tree line can be wind-packed: watch the ridges.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 16 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
