    Two-week snow outlook in Montenegro: 11 cm and 22 cm

    Europe snow by countryPublished Monday 16 November 2026 · 20:42 UTCData of 16 November2 min read

    The first week could bring up to 11 cm to the snowiest slopes in Montenegro, the second up to 22 cm.

    Two weeks ahead

    Two weeks out, the trend counts more than the totals: it tells you whether to book now or wait for the next storm.

    80 km² of snow-covered ground in Montenegro this Monday, down 50 km² on yesterday. That is 1 % of the high ground we map in Montenegro.

    Shortlist for the weekend

    At the top of the list, Ski centar Savin kuk reports 13 cm.

    Close behind: Kolašin 1600 (1 cm).

    At Ski centar Savin kuk: 15 cm of fresh snow over three days, 4 cm more expected by Thursday 19 November.

    At Kolašin 1600: 3 cm of fresh snow over three days, 0 cm more expected by Thursday 19 November.

    Snow lies on the upper slopes of 2 out of 3 resorts in Montenegro.

    How deep it is, height by height

    Height makes the difference: between 1,500 and 2,000 m, under 1 cm on average, 1 % covered; between 2,000 and 2,500 m, 2 cm on average, 49 % covered.

    Today's snow cover

    The snow line sits near 2,100 m. Mild days have cost 310 km² since the 390 km² of a week ago.

    New snow in the last three days

    Three-day totals peak at 4 cm, around the Prokletije, Durmitor and Bjelasica.

    At least 5 cm fell over some 70 km².

    The neighbours today

    For comparison, the neighbours on the same day: Bosnia and Herzegovina: 2 % white, deepest Skijalište Kozara with 24 cm, up to 15 cm in the week ahead; Albania: 1 % white, deepest Ski Pista Bigëll with 0 cm, up to 16 cm in the week ahead.

    Planning a day on the snow

    Between 2,000 m and 2,500 m the cover is patchy, about 49 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

    Below 2,000 m the slopes are still almost bare.

    Little new snow is expected in the next three days, so today's picture should hold for a weekend trip.

    On the ground

    The snow is retreating uphill, so plan on the higher lifts and avoid the low valley runs.

    With more snow in the forecast, the next days should improve things further.

    Tips for skiers

    • Check the resort's own lift status before you travel.
    • Read the local avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
    • Fresh snow on a cold base sticks badly: take care on steep slopes.

    About these numbers

    Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 16 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point.
