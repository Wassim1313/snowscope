Obergurgl snow forecast next week (23–29 November 2026): a minor top-up of 13 cm

AlpsGuidesPublished Monday 16 November 2026 · 11:42 UTCData of 16 November2 min read

Don't plan a powder trip to Obergurgl on next week's numbers alone. From Monday 23 to Sunday 29 November we expect 13 cm at the top (3–23 cm) and 11 cm at the base, so the week is a light refresh rather than a storm.

Top, middle and base

Our forecast splits Obergurgl's week like this: upper slopes 13 cm (low 3, high 23), middle of the mountain 12 cm, lower runs 11 cm (low 2, high 20).

The snow is not spread evenly. The days that matter at the top are Wednesday 25 (2 cm, up to 4) and Thursday 26 (6 cm, up to 10); the rest of the week is close to dry.

Next door, the same week

At the top, next week, our forecast gives Sölden (10 km away) 13 cm, Stubai Glacier (14 km away) 14 cm and Hintertux (52 km away) 14 cm, against 13 cm here.

The differences are small: the storm covers this whole corner of the Alps evenly, so pick on terrain and lifts, not on the snow total.

Down at the base

The base gets nearly as much as the top (11 against 13 cm), which means the snow line stays below the lowest lifts for most of the week. Snow down to the village is the likely picture.

Obergurgl is famous for snow that lasts, thanks to its height, not to big single storms.

First, this week's snow

Unlike the French side of the Alps, Obergurgl sees only 8 cm up high from 17 to 20 November (5–10 cm). So next week's snow is most of the fortnight's story here.

Picking your day in Obergurgl

Thursday 26 November brings the most, 6 cm at the top and 10 cm at best: enough to soften the pistes, not enough for a powder day.

The snow lies best on the Hohe Mut and the Wurmkogel above Hochgurgl. For the softest turns, look to the upper Hochgurgl slopes. Lower down, Obergurgl village, which is already high and cold will be the first to suffer if the air turns mild.

What could change

Treat this one with caution: 3 cm at the low end, 23 cm at the high end. A spread that wide a week out means the storm may still slide away from Obergurgl or sit right over it.

Storm timing tends to firm up first; totals keep moving until a day or two before.

Tips for skiers

  • Fresh snow on a young snowpack: read the regional avalanche service of the province before any off-piste plan.
  • Early season: confirm which lifts are open with Obergurgl itself before you travel.
  • With 13 cm at the top, this is not a week to chase powder in Obergurgl.
