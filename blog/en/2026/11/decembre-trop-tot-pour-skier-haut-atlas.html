Is December too early to ski in the High Atlas?

Atlas (Morocco)GuidesPublished Saturday 14 November 2026 · 13:29 UTCData of 14 November2 min read

Yes, most of the time, December is early in the High Atlas. On our 46-winter history, Oukaïmeden's mid-mountain has 18 cm of snow in a typical December, against 53 cm in March, its snowiest month. But early does not mean impossible.

The month-by-month scale

At mid-mountain, around 2929 m, the typical monthly snow depth climbs slowly: 3 cm in November, 18 cm in December, 28 cm in January, 44 cm in February and 53 cm in March, before dropping back to 11 cm in April. At the top, at 3258 m, it is 20 cm in December, 31 cm in January, 50 cm in February and 58 cm in March.

December therefore sits about a third of the way along. The peak of a typical winter reaches 92 cm at mid-mountain, but almost always after January.

Why the Atlas starts late

Here, snow does not build up in regular layers like in the Alps. It arrives in a few big storms, often separated by long dry, sunny spells that melt or transform it. A December can get a fine storm, then lose half its snow before Christmas.

The numbers show it: on 15 December, 20 % of winters have a nearly bare mid-mountain, while a generous December, one winter in ten, has 50 cm there.

The best week, in numbers

Over our 46 winters, the safest week at mid-mountain is week 9, late February: 59 % of winters have average snow of at least 50 cm there, for a typical height of 59 cm. In December, that same share never passes 13 % in any week.

The number of days with snow on the ground is 138 in a typical winter: the snow is there for a long time, but it is thin at the start.

So, December or not?

If you can only go in December, pick the second half of the month and wait for a snowfall: on 31 December, 48 % of winters have at least 25 cm at mid-mountain. If you have a choice, February and March are clearly better. Last winter showed both sides: a Christmas 2025 at 78 cm at mid-mountain, a winter that finished at 132 cm.

Tips for skiers

  • For a trip planned well in advance, aim for late February or March.
  • For December, stay flexible: leave after a storm, not on a fixed date.
  • Follow our map and the two-week forecast before booking transport.
  • Thin early-season snow: watch for rocks under the surface, stay on groomed runs.
