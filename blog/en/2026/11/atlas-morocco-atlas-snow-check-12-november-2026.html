Morocco snow report: white summits, bare ski slopes (12 November)

Atlas (Morocco)Where to skiSnow forecastsPublished Thursday 12 November 2026 · 11:59 UTCData of 12 November1 min read

Atlas snow report, 12 November. Atlas ski resorts today: no snow on the slopes. Snow only on the top summits: about 40 km² of white ground, 10 cm at most. The Atlas is small on a world map and large in our error bars: snow there is patchy and changes fast.

The forecast in short

Our forecast has no notable new snow at the resorts before 15 November.

Next storm, first place to check: Oukaïmeden, with lifts up to 3,258 m, far above Jbel Hebri (2,108 m) and Michlifen (2,070 m), which need colder storms to turn white.

Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.

Snow line check

On 15 November the freezing level at Jbel Hebri sits at 3,970 m to 4,120 m, over the top of the resort (2,108 m): rain or wet snow could fall on all slopes.

Tips for skiers

  • Snow on the map does not mean a resort is open: check lifts and opening dates on the resort's website.
  • Early-season snow is thin. Stay on open, groomed pistes: rocks and grass can hide just under the surface.
  • Forecasts a few days ahead can change: look at the map again the day before you travel.
  • Get a powder alert in the SnowScope app to know when Jbel Hebri gets fresh snow.

About these numbers

Resort snow depths are the deepest snow on the resort's upper slopes from the SnowScope snow model (about 1 km, run of 12 November); they are estimates for an area, not a measurement at one point. Station values are measurements on the date shown. Forecasts can change. Always read the official avalanche bulletin before going off-piste.
