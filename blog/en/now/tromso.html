Tromsø snow depth: 6 cm in town, 44 cm in the Lyngen hills (13 November 2026)

Tromsø has a thin white cover this Friday. The weather station on the island, 114 m up the hill above the town centre, measured 6 cm of snow on Friday morning, down from 8 cm on Thursday. The real snow is across the fjords: a station at 710 m in the Lyngen Alps has 44 cm.

Town, fjords and hills

The cover in town is new: on Monday the station still had bare ground, then the snow came mid-week and settled a little on Friday. Along the coast north-east of Tromsø, Nordstraum in Kvænangen has 8 cm. Inland, Bardufoss has only 3 cm.

Higher up it is a different season. Gjerdvassbu in Lyngen, at 710 m, has had a deep cover all week: 38 cm on Monday, 44 cm now. Across the border, Kilpisjärvi in Finland, 100 km away, has 13 cm.
