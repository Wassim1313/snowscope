Tromsø snow depth: 6 cm in town, 44 cm in the Lyngen hills (13 November 2026)

Early-season snow sits unevenly across Tromsø county this Friday. The island station in town shows just 6 cm, typical for mid-November at sea level, but climb inland and uphill to the Lyngen Alps and you find 44 cm—a usable base that arrived early in the week.

Where the snow actually is

The coast around Tromsø is thin. The town's station at 114 m measured 6 cm on Friday morning. Drive 45 km south-east to Lyngen Gjerdvassbu at 710 m and the depth jumps to 44 cm. That station has held a solid cover all week: it had 38 cm on Monday, so only 6 cm of new snow fell in four days, but what arrived has stayed cold and undisturbed.

Between these two extremes sits a patchy band. Bardufoss, 68 km south near sea level, has just 3 cm. The fishing settlement of Nordstraum in Kvænangen to the north-east, 115 km away on low ground, has 8 cm. Cross the border into Finland: Kilpisjärvi, 100 km east at higher elevation, shows 13 cm.
