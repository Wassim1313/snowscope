Funäsdalsfjällen snow: Tänndalen is skiing on a small area, Ramundberget waits (14 November 2026)

In Funäsdalsfjällen, the first skiing of the winter is already happening, on a very small scale. Tänndalen opened on 24 October and its lift status on 13 November said there is only a small area for the public, mostly at weekends. The nearest weather station, Ljusnedal at 585 m and 19 km from Tänndalen, measured 7 cm of snow that morning. Ramundberget, a little further north, reported 0 cm on its slopes and opens on 19 December.

Station and slopes

Ljusnedal had no snow until 10 November, then 2 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm on the next three mornings. Tänndalen's base is at 757 m and its top at 1020 m, so the slopes are higher than the station, and what is skiable is a small area the resort has prepared.

Our estimate of new snow over the last seven days is about 8 cm at Tänndalen and 7 cm at Ramundberget.
