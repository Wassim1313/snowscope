Pyhä and Luosto snow depth: 0 to 9 cm around the fells, a snowy week ahead (14 November 2026)

Around Pyhä and Luosto the snow is patchy and thin. On the morning of 13 November the weather station at Pelkosenniemi, 22 km away at 156 m, measured 1 cm; Sodankylä Tähtelä measured 0 cm; Kemijärvi airport, at 208 m, had 9 cm, and Savukoski 6 cm. Our 46-winter record gives 15 cm as the usual depth here on 14 November, so the area is behind. The next seven days should change that.

A thin, uneven start

The stations show two different Novembers. To the south and east, Kemijärvi and Savukoski have kept a thin cover all week, now 6 to 9 cm. To the west and north, Sodankylä and Pelkosenniemi are almost bare. The fells sit in between, and higher, so they likely hold a little more than the villages.

Our estimate of new snow over the last seven days is less than 1 cm, so this is old snow, not fresh.
