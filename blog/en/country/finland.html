Finland snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Finland about 281,340 km² of high ground is white today, 94 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is about the same as yesterday. The deepest resort is Pyhä with 29 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 3 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 1 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 3 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Monday 30 November, with up to 3 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Kainuu hills.

The second week could add up to 16 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 0-500 m: 10 cm on average, 93 % white; 500-1,000 m: 17 cm on average, 100 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 45 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 0 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 0 m the ground is almost fully white (93 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Rukatunturi (494 m), Salla (462 m) and Suomutunturi (400 m).

Little new snow is expected in the next three days, so today's picture should hold for a weekend trip.

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Finland right now?

Yes. About 281,340 km² of high ground is white, 94 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Pyhä with 29 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Finland today?

Pyhä leads with 29 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Suomutunturi (26 cm), Rukatunturi (25 cm) and Kiririnteet (24 cm).

Will it snow in Finland this week?

Up to 1 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 3 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Monday 30 November, with up to 3 cm.

From what height is there snow in Finland?

Most of the ground is white from about 0 m up.

How much new snow fell in Finland in the last three days?

Up to 12 cm on the snowiest slopes, 4 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 118,600 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Kainuu hills.

How many ski resorts in Finland have snow?

58 of the 67 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
