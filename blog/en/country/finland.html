Finland snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Finland about 183,210 km² of high ground is white today, 61 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 23,090 km² more than yesterday. The deepest resort is Kallin with 24 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 16 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 16 November about 157,360 km² was white; today it is 183,210 km².

The snow cover grew by 25,850 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 11 cm (to 16 November).

This season against a normal year

3 of the 5 deepest resorts are above their usual snow depth for 17 November.

Kallin: 13 cm at about 40 m, a normal year has 4 cm; Salla: 19 cm at about 356 m, a normal year has 17 cm; Pyhä: 13 cm at about 285 m, a normal year has 16 cm; Rukatunturi: 16 cm at about 390 m, a normal year has 15 cm; Suomutunturi: 13 cm at about 285 m, a normal year has 14 cm.

These depths are for one height on each mountain, below the top.

In past winters the best snow at Kallin usually came early March / mid-March.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 9 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 16 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Wednesday 18 November, with up to 6 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Kainuu hills and the Ruka fells.

The second week could add up to 5 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 0-500 m: 4 cm on average, 61 % white; 500-1,000 m: 13 cm on average, 99 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 39 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 100 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 500 m the ground is almost fully white (99 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 0 m and 500 m the cover is patchy, about 61 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Rukatunturi (494 m), Salla (462 m) and Suomutunturi (400 m).
