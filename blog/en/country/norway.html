Norway snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Norway about 250,530 km² of high ground is white today, 89 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is about the same as yesterday. The deepest resort is Riksgränsen with 167 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 33 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

This season against a normal year

0 of the 5 deepest resorts are above their usual snow depth for 19 November.

Riksgränsen: 56 cm at about 708 m, a normal year has 61 cm; Sulitjelma Fjellandsby: 49 cm at about 465 m, a normal year has 52 cm; Tromsø Alpinpark: 17 cm at about 222 m, a normal year has 38 cm; Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter: 31 cm at about 2,015 m, a normal year has 84 cm; Steilia: 31 cm at about 455 m, a normal year has 58 cm.

These depths are for one height on each mountain, below the top.

In past winters the best snow at Riksgränsen usually came early April / mid-April.

Planning a trip

Above 0 m the ground is almost fully white (80 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter (2,180 m), Riksgränsen (900 m) and Sulitjelma Fjellandsby (542 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Saturday 21 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Norway right now?

Yes. About 250,530 km² of high ground is white, 89 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Riksgränsen with 167 cm on its upper slopes.

Riksgränsen leads with 167 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Sulitjelma Fjellandsby (126 cm), Tromsø Alpinpark (92 cm) and Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter (61 cm).
