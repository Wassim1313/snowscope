Norway snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Norway about 265,930 km² of high ground is white today, 94 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is about the same as yesterday. The deepest resort is Riksgränsen with 166 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 44 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

On 21 November about 260,290 km² was white; today it is 265,930 km².

This season against a normal year

0 of the 5 deepest resorts are above their usual snow depth for 27 November.

Riksgränsen: 60 cm at about 708 m, a normal year has 70 cm; Sulitjelma Fjellandsby: 47 cm at about 465 m, a normal year has 62 cm; Tromsø Alpinpark: 21 cm at about 222 m, a normal year has 45 cm; Steilia: 36 cm at about 455 m, a normal year has 69 cm; Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter: 28 cm at about 2,015 m, a normal year has 92 cm.

These depths are for one height on each mountain, below the top.

In past winters the best snow at Riksgränsen usually came early April / mid-April.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 14 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 44 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Thursday 3 December, with up to 17 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Lyngen Alps.

Planning a trip

Above 0 m the ground is almost fully white (89 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter (2,180 m), Riksgränsen (900 m) and Sulitjelma Fjellandsby (542 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Thursday 3 December: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.
