Albania snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Albania about 7,320 km² of high ground is white today, 53 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 6,150 km² more than yesterday. The deepest resort is Ski Pista Bigëll with 9 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 10 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 10 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 10 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Sunday 29 November, with up to 10 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Albanian Alps, Mount Tomorr and Mount Korab.

The second week could add up to 21 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 1 cm on average, 28 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 2 cm on average, 63 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 7 cm on average, 91 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 24 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,100 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 1,500 m the ground is almost fully white (91 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 500 m and 1,500 m the cover is patchy, about 28 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Ski Pista Bigëll (1,678 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Sunday 29 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.
