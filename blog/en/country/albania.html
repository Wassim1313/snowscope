Albania snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Albania about 1,200 km² of high ground is white today, 9 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 340 km² less than yesterday. Up to 22 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 21 November about 560 km² was white; today it is 1,200 km².

The snow cover grew by 640 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 21 cm (to 23 November).

Snowiest resorts today

None of the resorts we follow in Albania has snow on its upper slopes yet.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 22 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 22 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 12 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Albanian Alps, Mount Korab and Mount Tomorr.

The second week could add up to 47 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 5 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 1 cm on average, 35 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 16 cm on average, 91 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,800 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (91 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,500 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 35 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

If you can choose the day, go after Saturday 28 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Albania right now?

Yes. About 1,200 km² of high ground is white, 9 % of the mapped mountains.

Will it snow in Albania this week?

Up to 22 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 22 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 12 cm.

From what height is there snow in Albania?

Most of the ground is white from about 1,800 m up.

How much new snow fell in Albania in the last three days?

Up to 11 cm on the snowiest slopes, 3 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 290 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Albanian Alps, Mount Korab and Mount Tomorr.
