Andorra snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Andorra about 340 km² of high ground is white today, 77 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is about the same as yesterday. The deepest resort is Canaro with 22 cm on its upper slopes. Little or no new snow is expected in the week ahead.

This season against a normal year

0 of the 4 deepest resorts are above their usual snow depth for 28 November.

Canaro: 5 cm at about 2,108 m, a normal year has 12 cm; Ordino-Arcalis: 9 cm at about 2,284 m, a normal year has 13 cm; Pal-Arinsal: 5 cm at about 2,024 m, a normal year has 10 cm; GrandValira: 5 cm at about 2,146 m, a normal year has 10 cm.

These depths are for one height on each mountain, below the top.

In past winters the best snow at Canaro usually came mid-February / late February.

Snow by height

Height by height: 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 2 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 2 cm on average, 64 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 7 cm on average, 99 % white; above 2,500 m: 9 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,700 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (99 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,500 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 64 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Ordino-Arcalis (2,626 m), GrandValira (2,619 m) and Canaro (2,500 m).

Little new snow is expected in the next three days, so today's picture should hold for a weekend trip.
