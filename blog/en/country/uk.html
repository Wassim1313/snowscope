UK snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In the UK about 14,950 km² of high ground is white today, 40 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 3,560 km² more than yesterday. The deepest resort is Nevis Range with 33 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 16 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 10 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 16 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Sunday 29 November, with up to 8 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to Glencoe, Lochaber (Ben Nevis) and Glenshee and the Grampians.

Snow by height

Height by height: 0-500 m: 1 cm on average, 25 % white; 500-1,000 m: 7 cm on average, 77 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 20 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 500 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 1,000 m the ground is almost fully white (100 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 0 m and 1,000 m the cover is patchy, about 25 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Nevis Range (1,190 m), Glencoe (1,079 m) and Glenshee (1,028 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Sunday 29 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.
