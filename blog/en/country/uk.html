UK snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In the UK about 4,850 km² of high ground is white today, 13 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is about the same as yesterday. The deepest resort is Yad Moss with 24 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 16 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 9 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 16 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Tuesday 24 November, with up to 9 cm.

The second week could add up to 14 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 0-500 m: 0 cm on average, 4 % white; 500-1,000 m: 2 cm on average, 35 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 8 cm on average, 98 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 700 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 1,000 m the ground is almost fully white (98 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 500 m and 1,000 m the cover is patchy, about 35 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Nevis Range (1,190 m), Glencoe (1,079 m) and Lecht 2090 (776 m).
