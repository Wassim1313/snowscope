Slovakia snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Slovakia about 2,340 km² of high ground is white today, 13 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 70 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Ski centrum Dedinky with 42 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 15 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 16 November about 2,020 km² was white; today it is 2,340 km².

The snow cover grew by 320 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 7 cm (to 17 November).

Snowiest resorts today

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 9 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 15 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Thursday 19 November, with up to 5 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the High Tatras, Malá Fatra and the Low Tatras.

The second week could add up to 27 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 5 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 2 cm on average, 50 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 12 cm on average, 100 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 20 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,200 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 1,500 m the ground is almost fully white (100 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,000 m and 1,500 m the cover is patchy, about 50 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Kubínská hola (1,390 m), Ski Zábava Hruštín (1,141 m) and Vernár (1,001 m).

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Slovakia right now?

Yes. About 2,340 km² of high ground is white, 13 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Ski centrum Dedinky with 42 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Slovakia today?

Ski centrum Dedinky leads with 42 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Ski Grúniky (31 cm), Vernár (31 cm) and Kubínská hola (29 cm).

Will it snow in Slovakia this week?

Up to 9 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 15 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Thursday 19 November, with up to 5 cm.

From what height is there snow in Slovakia?

Most of the ground is white from about 1,200 m up.

How much new snow fell in Slovakia in the last three days?

Up to 7 cm on the snowiest slopes, 6 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 680 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the High Tatras, Malá Fatra and the Low Tatras.

How many ski resorts in Slovakia have snow?

44 of the 107 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
