Slovakia snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Slovakia about 18,600 km² of high ground is white today, 100 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is about the same as yesterday. The deepest resort is Vysoké Tatry with 32 cm on its upper slopes. Little or no new snow is expected in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 21 November about 2,680 km² was white; today it is 18,600 km².

The snow cover grew by 15,920 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 17 cm (to 21 November).

Snowiest resorts today

Vysoké Tatry leads with 32 cm on its upper slopes.

Then come Ski Podjavorník (29 cm), Roháče (29 cm), Jasna Low Tatras (25 cm) and Martinské Hole (24 cm).

Where the snow is coming next

The first flakes are expected around Saturday 28 November.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to Malá Fatra, the High Tatras and the Low Tatras.

The second week could add up to 12 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 6 cm on average, 100 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 9 cm on average, 100 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 21 cm on average, 100 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 28 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 500 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 500 m the ground is almost fully white (100 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Vysoké Tatry (2,182 m), Jasna Low Tatras (2,003 m) and Roháče (1,496 m).

Little new snow is expected in the next three days, so today's picture should hold for a weekend trip.

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Slovakia right now?

Yes. About 18,600 km² of high ground is white, 100 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Vysoké Tatry with 32 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Slovakia today?

Vysoké Tatry leads with 32 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Ski Podjavorník (29 cm), Roháče (29 cm) and Jasna Low Tatras (25 cm).

Will it snow in Slovakia this week?

The first flakes are expected around Saturday 28 November. The last three days brought the most fresh snow to Malá Fatra, the High Tatras and the Low Tatras.

From what height is there snow in Slovakia?

Most of the ground is white from about 500 m up.

How much new snow fell in Slovakia in the last three days?

Up to 11 cm on the snowiest slopes, 3 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 18,420 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to Malá Fatra, the High Tatras and the Low Tatras.

How many ski resorts in Slovakia have snow?

107 of the 107 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
