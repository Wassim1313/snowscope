Slovakia snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Slovakia about 2,470 km² of high ground is white today, 13 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 230 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Vysoké Tatry with 33 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 10 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

On 16 November about 2,020 km² was white; today it is 2,470 km².

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 7 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 10 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Thursday 26 November, with up to 6 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to Malá Fatra, the High Tatras and the Low Tatras.

The second week could add up to 22 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 4 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 2 cm on average, 61 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 18 cm on average, 100 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 28 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,100 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 1,500 m the ground is almost fully white (100 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,000 m and 1,500 m the cover is patchy, about 61 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Vysoké Tatry (2,182 m), Jasna Low Tatras (2,003 m) and Štrbské Pleso (1,822 m).

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Slovakia right now?

Yes. About 2,470 km² of high ground is white, 13 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Vysoké Tatry with 33 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Slovakia today?

Vysoké Tatry leads with 33 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Roháče (27 cm), Jasna Low Tatras (23 cm) and Štrbské Pleso (20 cm).

Will it snow in Slovakia this week?

Up to 7 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 10 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Thursday 26 November, with up to 6 cm.

From what height is there snow in Slovakia?

Most of the ground is white from about 1,100 m up.

How much new snow fell in Slovakia in the last three days?

Up to 8 cm on the snowiest slopes, 0 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 760 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to Malá Fatra, the High Tatras and the Low Tatras.

How many ski resorts in Slovakia have snow?

42 of the 107 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
