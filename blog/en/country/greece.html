Greece snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Greece about 1,490 km² of high ground is white today, 6 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 400 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Kaimaktsalan with 30 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 30 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 30 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 30 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Sunday 29 November, with up to 20 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to Mount Olympus, the Pindus and the Vermio mountains.

The second week could add up to 22 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 1 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 8 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 1 cm on average, 23 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 3 cm on average, 52 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 2,200 m up.

Planning a trip

Between 2,000 m and 2,500 m the cover is patchy, about 52 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Kaimaktsalan (2,470 m), Χιονοδρομικό Κέντρο 3-5 Πηγάδια (1,977 m) and Elatohori (1,913 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Sunday 29 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.
