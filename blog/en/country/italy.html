Italy snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Italy about 24,510 km² of high ground is white today, 26 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is about the same as yesterday. The deepest resort is Pfelders with 118 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 62 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 40 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 62 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Sunday 22 November, with up to 37 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Aosta Valley, the Piedmont Alps and the central Apennines.

The second week could add up to 23 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 1 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 17 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 3 cm on average, 66 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 11 cm on average, 94 % white; above 2,500 m: 27 cm on average, 99 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,500 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (94 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,500 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 66 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Courmayeur (2,749 m), Pila ski (2,723 m) and La Thuile ski (2,608 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Sunday 22 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Italy right now?

Yes. About 24,510 km² of high ground is white, 26 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Pfelders with 118 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Italy today?

Pfelders leads with 118 cm on its upper slopes. Then come La Thuile ski (72 cm), Courmayeur (67 cm) and Prali (57 cm).

Will it snow in Italy this week?

Up to 40 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 62 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Sunday 22 November, with up to 37 cm.

From what height is there snow in Italy?

Most of the ground is white from about 1,500 m up.

How much new snow fell in Italy in the last three days?

Up to 55 cm on the snowiest slopes, 8 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 20,450 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Aosta Valley, the Piedmont Alps and the central Apennines.

How many ski resorts in Italy have snow?

164 of the 238 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
