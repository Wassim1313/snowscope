Italy snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Italy about 33,540 km² of high ground is white today, 36 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 1,360 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Courmayeur with 75 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 3 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 21 November about 24,510 km² was white; today it is 33,540 km².

The snow cover grew by 9,030 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 57 cm (to 22 November).

This season against a normal year

4 of the 5 deepest resorts are above their usual snow depth for 27 November.

Courmayeur: 97 cm at about 1,976 m, a normal year has 32 cm; La Thuile ski: 90 cm at about 2,038 m, a normal year has 37 cm; Prali: 31 cm at about 2,173 m, a normal year has 21 cm; Bardonecchia: 42 cm at about 2,028 m, a normal year has 32 cm; Schnalstal: 43 cm at about 2,602 m, a normal year has 55 cm.

These depths are for one height on each mountain, below the top.

In past winters the best snow at Courmayeur usually came late February / early March.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 3 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 3 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 3 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Lombardy Alps, the Carnic and Julian Alps and the Dolomites.

The second week could add up to 18 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 3 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 3 cm on average, 39 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 11 cm on average, 82 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 19 cm on average, 99 % white; above 2,500 m: 33 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,300 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 1,500 m the ground is almost fully white (82 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,000 m and 1,500 m the cover is patchy, about 39 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Schnalstal (3,200 m), Courmayeur (2,749 m) and Bardonecchia (2,708 m).
