Italy snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Italy about 20,940 km² of high ground is white today, 22 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 3,530 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Monterosa Ski with 128 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 58 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 42 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 58 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Sunday 22 November, with up to 34 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Aosta Valley, the Piedmont Alps and the central Apennines.

The second week could add up to 16 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 6 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 3 cm on average, 61 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 10 cm on average, 94 % white; above 2,500 m: 27 cm on average, 99 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,700 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (94 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,500 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 61 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Valtournenche (3,091 m), Monterosa Ski (3,030 m) and Courmayeur (2,749 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Sunday 22 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.

165 of the 237 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
