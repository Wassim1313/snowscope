Slovenia snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Slovenia about 6,790 km² of high ground is white today, 93 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 170 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Sella Nevea with 50 cm on its upper slopes. Little or no new snow is expected in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 22 November about 1,880 km² was white; today it is 6,790 km².

The snow cover grew by 4,910 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 54 cm (to 27 November).

This season against a normal year

5 of the 5 deepest resorts are above their usual snow depth for 28 November.

Sella Nevea: 37 cm at about 1,622 m, a normal year has 6 cm; Vogel: 20 cm at about 1,152 m, a normal year has 2 cm; Kranjska Gora: 14 cm at about 1,188 m, a normal year has 4 cm; Krvavec: 12 cm at about 1,718 m, a normal year has 3 cm; Pokljuka: 24 cm at about 1,413 m, a normal year has 5 cm.

These depths are for one height on each mountain, below the top.

In past winters the best snow at Sella Nevea usually came late February / early March.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 7 cm on average, 89 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 12 cm on average, 100 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 26 cm on average, 100 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 39 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 600 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 500 m the ground is almost fully white (89 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Sella Nevea (2,137 m), Krvavec (1,963 m) and Vogel (1,731 m).

Little new snow is expected in the next three days, so today's picture should hold for a weekend trip.
