Slovenia snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Slovenia about 2,100 km² of high ground is white today, 29 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 820 km² more than yesterday. The deepest resort is Sella Nevea with 33 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 39 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 18 November about 260 km² was white; today it is 2,100 km².

The snow cover grew by 1,840 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 30 cm (to 19 November).

This season against a normal year

4 of the 5 deepest resorts are above their usual snow depth for 24 November.

Sella Nevea: 21 cm at about 1,622 m, a normal year has 4 cm; Vogel: 5 cm at about 1,152 m, a normal year has 1 cm; Soriška planina: 2 cm at about 1,404 m, a normal year has 3 cm; Golte: 3 cm at about 1,424 m, a normal year has 1 cm; Pokljuka: 6 cm at about 1,413 m, a normal year has 4 cm.

These depths are for one height on each mountain, below the top.

In past winters the best snow at Sella Nevea usually came late February / early March.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 39 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 39 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Thursday 26 November, with up to 24 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Julian Alps, the Kamnik-Savinja Alps and Pohorje.

The second week could add up to 8 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 15 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 1 cm on average, 46 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 12 cm on average, 99 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 25 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,300 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 1,500 m the ground is almost fully white (99 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,000 m and 1,500 m the cover is patchy, about 46 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Sella Nevea (2,137 m), Vogel (1,731 m) and Golte (1,570 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Thursday 26 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.
