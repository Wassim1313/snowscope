Croatia snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Croatia about 840 km² of high ground is white today, 10 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 740 km² more than yesterday. The deepest resort is Platak with 6 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 10 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 6 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 10 cm over the week.

Snow by height

Planning a trip

Between 1,000 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 29 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Platak (1,354 m) and Sljeme (1,029 m).
