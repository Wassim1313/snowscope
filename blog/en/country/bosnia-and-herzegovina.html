Bosnia and Herzegovina snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Bosnia and Herzegovina about 3,290 km² of high ground is white today, 12 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 540 km² more than yesterday. The deepest resort is Jahorina olimpijski centar with 16 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 15 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 21 November about 60 km² was white; today it is 3,290 km².

The snow cover grew by 3,230 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 8 cm (to 25 November).

Snowiest resorts today

Jahorina olimpijski centar leads with 16 cm on its upper slopes.

Then come Malo polje Igman (ZOI '84) (14 cm), Skijalište Kozara (12 cm), Ponijeri ski centar (12 cm) and OC Bjelašnica ZOI '84 (11 cm).

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 15 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 15 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 13 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to Bjelašnica and Jahorina, Vlašić and the Dinara range.

The second week could add up to 22 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 7 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 13 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 1 cm on average, 40 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,700 m up.

Planning a trip

Between 1,500 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 40 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: OC Bjelašnica ZOI '84 (2,056 m), Jahorina olimpijski centar (1,886 m) and Malo polje Igman (ZOI '84) (1,490 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Saturday 28 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Bosnia and Herzegovina right now?

Yes. About 3,290 km² of high ground is white, 12 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Jahorina olimpijski centar with 16 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Bosnia and Herzegovina today?

Jahorina olimpijski centar leads with 16 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Malo polje Igman (ZOI '84) (14 cm), Skijalište Kozara (12 cm) and Ponijeri ski centar (12 cm).

Will it snow in Bosnia and Herzegovina this week?

Up to 15 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 15 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 13 cm.

From what height is there snow in Bosnia and Herzegovina?

Most of the ground is white from about 1,700 m up.

How much new snow fell in Bosnia and Herzegovina in the last three days?

Up to 5 cm on the snowiest slopes, 3 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 370 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to Bjelašnica and Jahorina, Vlašić and the Dinara range.

How many ski resorts in Bosnia and Herzegovina have snow?

7 of the 12 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
