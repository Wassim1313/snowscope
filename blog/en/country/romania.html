Romania snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Romania about 4,170 km² of high ground is white today, 6 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 2,460 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Borsec with 27 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 12 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 16 November about 6,630 km² was white; today it is 4,170 km².

The snow cover shrank by 2,460 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 8 cm (to 16 November).

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 8 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 12 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Friday 20 November, with up to 4 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Retezat, the Parâng Mountains and the Rodna Mountains.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 1 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 8 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 2 cm on average, 49 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 9 cm on average, 97 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,700 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (97 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,500 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 49 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Straja (1,865 m), Parâng (1,768 m) and Havas Bucsin (1,494 m).

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Romania right now?

Yes. About 4,170 km² of high ground is white, 6 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Borsec with 27 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Romania today?

Borsec leads with 27 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Havas Bucsin (26 cm), Straja (19 cm) and Pârtia de schi Veresvirág (19 cm).

Will it snow in Romania this week?

Up to 8 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 12 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Friday 20 November, with up to 4 cm.

From what height is there snow in Romania?

Most of the ground is white from about 1,700 m up.

How much new snow fell in Romania in the last three days?

Up to 4 cm on the snowiest slopes, 1 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 600 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Retezat, the Parâng Mountains and the Rodna Mountains.

How many ski resorts in Romania have snow?

20 of the 52 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
