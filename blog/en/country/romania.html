Romania snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Romania about 33,910 km² of high ground is white today, 51 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 5,650 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Muntele Mic with 42 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 6 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 6 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 6 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 8 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Retezat, the Parâng Mountains and the Rodna Mountains.

The second week could add up to 13 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 2 cm on average, 31 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 7 cm on average, 88 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 18 cm on average, 100 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 30 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 900 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 1,000 m the ground is almost fully white (88 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 500 m and 1,000 m the cover is patchy, about 31 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Balea Lac (2,032 m), Șureanu (1,968 m) and Muntele Mic (1,793 m).

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Romania right now?

Yes. About 33,910 km² of high ground is white, 51 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Muntele Mic with 42 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Romania today?

Muntele Mic leads with 42 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Șureanu (39 cm), Parâng (32 cm) and Rausor (30 cm).

Will it snow in Romania this week?

Up to 6 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 6 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 8 cm.

From what height is there snow in Romania?

Most of the ground is white from about 900 m up.

How much new snow fell in Romania in the last three days?

Up to 14 cm on the snowiest slopes, 5 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 16,580 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Retezat, the Parâng Mountains and the Rodna Mountains.

How many ski resorts in Romania have snow?

45 of the 52 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
