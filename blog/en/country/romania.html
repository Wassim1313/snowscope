Romania snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Romania about 7,710 km² of high ground is white today, 12 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 2,770 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Havas Bucsin with 31 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 21 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

On 16 November about 6,630 km² was white; today it is 7,710 km².

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 6 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 21 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Monday 23 November, with up to 9 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Retezat, the Rodna Mountains and the Parâng Mountains.

The second week could add up to 8 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 2 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 1 cm on average, 21 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 3 cm on average, 72 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 10 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,500 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (100 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,500 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 72 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Ski Vidra (1,974 m), Straja (1,865 m) and Havas Bucsin (1,494 m).

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Romania right now?

Yes. About 7,710 km² of high ground is white, 12 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Havas Bucsin with 31 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Romania today?

Havas Bucsin leads with 31 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Pârtia de schi Rarău (22 cm), Pârtia de schi Veresvirág (21 cm) and Ski Vidra (18 cm).

Will it snow in Romania this week?

Up to 6 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 21 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Monday 23 November, with up to 9 cm.

From what height is there snow in Romania?

Most of the ground is white from about 1,500 m up.

How much new snow fell in Romania in the last three days?

Up to 5 cm on the snowiest slopes, 2 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 880 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Retezat, the Rodna Mountains and the Parâng Mountains.

How many ski resorts in Romania have snow?

27 of the 52 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
