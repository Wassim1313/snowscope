Romania snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Romania about 7,300 km² of high ground is white today, 11 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 3,940 km² more than yesterday. The deepest resort is Balea Lac with 16 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 37 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 18 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 37 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Monday 23 November, with up to 13 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Rodna Mountains, the Retezat and the Călimani Mountains.

The second week could add up to 17 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 1 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 21 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 3 cm on average, 75 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 14 cm on average, 99 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,500 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (99 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,500 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 75 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Balea Lac (2,032 m), Ski Vidra (1,974 m) and Șureanu (1,968 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Monday 23 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Romania right now?

Yes. About 7,300 km² of high ground is white, 11 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Balea Lac with 16 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Romania today?

Balea Lac leads with 16 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Ski Vidra (13 cm), Șureanu (10 cm) and Parâng (9 cm).

Will it snow in Romania this week?

Up to 18 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 37 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Monday 23 November, with up to 13 cm.

From what height is there snow in Romania?

Most of the ground is white from about 1,500 m up.

How much new snow fell in Romania in the last three days?

Up to 7 cm on the snowiest slopes, 4 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 1,630 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Rodna Mountains, the Retezat and the Călimani Mountains.

How many ski resorts in Romania have snow?

22 of the 52 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
