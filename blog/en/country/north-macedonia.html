North Macedonia snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In North Macedonia about 1,270 km² of high ground is white today, 8 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 150 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Скијачки центар Попова шапка with 21 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 32 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 32 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 32 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 19 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Mavrovo mountains, the Šar Mountains and Pelister.

Snow by height

Height by height: 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 6 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 1 cm on average, 30 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 10 cm on average, 88 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,900 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (88 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,500 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 30 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Скијачки центар Попова шапка (2,376 m) and Mavrovo (1,875 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Saturday 28 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.
