North Macedonia snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In North Macedonia about 170 km² of high ground is white today, 1 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 70 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Скијачки центар Попова шапка with 1 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 47 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 19 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 47 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Monday 23 November, with up to 17 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Mavrovo mountains, the Šar Mountains and Pelister.

Snow by height

Height by height: 1,500-2,000 m: 0 cm on average, 2 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 1 cm on average, 35 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 2,300 m up.

Planning a trip

Between 2,000 m and 2,500 m the cover is patchy, about 35 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Скијачки центар Попова шапка (2,376 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Monday 23 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.

From what height is there snow in North Macedonia?
