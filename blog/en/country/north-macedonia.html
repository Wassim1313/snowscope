North Macedonia snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In North Macedonia about 3,960 km² of high ground is white today, 25 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 3,740 km² more than yesterday. The deepest resort is Скијачки центар Попова шапка with 15 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 36 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 19 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 36 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Thursday 26 November, with up to 18 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Mavrovo mountains, the Šar Mountains and Pelister.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 2 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 1 cm on average, 34 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 3 cm on average, 83 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 11 cm on average, 96 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,300 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 1,500 m the ground is almost fully white (83 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,000 m and 1,500 m the cover is patchy, about 34 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Скијачки центар Попова шапка (2,376 m), Mavrovo (1,875 m) and Pelister (1,772 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Thursday 26 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.
