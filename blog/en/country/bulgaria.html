Bulgaria snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Bulgaria about 6,920 km² of high ground is white today, 22 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 820 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Bansko with 30 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 36 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 35 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 36 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 25 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Pirin Mountains and the Rila Mountains.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 0 cm on average, 4 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 1 cm on average, 32 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 3 cm on average, 68 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 9 cm on average, 93 % white; above 2,500 m: 23 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 1,400 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (93 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

Between 1,000 m and 2,000 m the cover is patchy, about 32 % white: expect grass and stones between the snow.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Borovets (2,538 m), Bansko (2,516 m) and Kartala (2,245 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Saturday 28 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Bulgaria right now?

Yes. About 6,920 km² of high ground is white, 22 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Bansko with 30 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Bulgaria today?

Bansko leads with 30 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Borovets (23 cm), Kartala (19 cm) and Malyovista (16 cm).

Will it snow in Bulgaria this week?

Up to 35 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 36 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 25 cm.

From what height is there snow in Bulgaria?

Most of the ground is white from about 1,400 m up.

How much new snow fell in Bulgaria in the last three days?

Up to 6 cm on the snowiest slopes, 6 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 390 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Pirin Mountains and the Rila Mountains.

How many ski resorts in Bulgaria have snow?

11 of the 11 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
