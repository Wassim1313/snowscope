Bulgaria snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Bulgaria about 30,390 km² of high ground is white today, 97 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 23,510 km² more than yesterday. The deepest resort is Bansko with 45 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 22 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

The last seven days

On 22 November about 420 km² was white; today it is 30,390 km².

The snow cover grew by 29,970 km² over these days.

The biggest three-day snowfall of the period reached 34 cm (to 28 November).

Snowiest resorts today

Bansko leads with 45 cm on its upper slopes.

Then come Borovets (37 cm), Kartala (29 cm), Чепеларе (23 cm) and Malyovista (23 cm).

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 22 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 22 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Sunday 29 November, with up to 20 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Rila Mountains, the Pirin Mountains and the Balkan Mountains.

The second week could add up to 13 cm where it snows most.

Snow by height

Height by height: 500-1,000 m: 9 cm on average, 95 % white; 1,000-1,500 m: 12 cm on average, 100 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 15 cm on average, 100 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 24 cm on average, 100 % white; above 2,500 m: 40 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 600 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 500 m the ground is almost fully white (95 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Borovets (2,538 m), Bansko (2,516 m) and Kartala (2,245 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Sunday 29 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.

Questions and answers

Is there snow in Bulgaria right now?

Yes. About 30,390 km² of high ground is white, 97 % of the mapped mountains. The deepest resort is Bansko with 45 cm on its upper slopes.

Where is the most snow in Bulgaria today?

Bansko leads with 45 cm on its upper slopes. Then come Borovets (37 cm), Kartala (29 cm) and Чепеларе (23 cm).

Will it snow in Bulgaria this week?

Up to 22 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 22 cm over the week. The snowiest day looks like Sunday 29 November, with up to 20 cm.

From what height is there snow in Bulgaria?

Most of the ground is white from about 600 m up.

How much new snow fell in Bulgaria in the last three days?

Up to 34 cm on the snowiest slopes, 29 cm of it in the last 24 hours. At least 5 cm fell over some 25,950 km². The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Rila Mountains, the Pirin Mountains and the Balkan Mountains.

How many ski resorts in Bulgaria have snow?

11 of the 11 resorts we follow have snow on their upper slopes today. Snow on top does not mean the lifts are open: check the resort before you go.
