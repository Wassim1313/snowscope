Bulgaria snow report: snow depth, new snow and the week ahead

In Bulgaria about 1,370 km² of high ground is white today, 4 % of the country's mapped mountains and hills. That is 260 km² less than yesterday. The deepest resort is Bansko with 24 cm on its upper slopes. Up to 30 cm of new snow could fall on the snowiest slopes in the week ahead.

Where the snow is coming next

Up to 26 cm is expected on the snowiest slopes in the next three days, and up to 30 cm over the week.

The snowiest day looks like Saturday 28 November, with up to 19 cm.

The last three days brought the most fresh snow to the Rila Mountains, the Pirin Mountains and Vitosha.

Snow by height

Height by height: 1,000-1,500 m: 0 cm on average, 1 % white; 1,500-2,000 m: 0 cm on average, 13 % white; 2,000-2,500 m: 7 cm on average, 86 % white; above 2,500 m: 15 cm on average, 100 % white.

Most of the ground is white from about 2,000 m up.

Planning a trip

Above 2,000 m the ground is almost fully white (86 % covered), so runs up there have a continuous base.

The resorts with the highest lifts are the safest bet: Borovets (2,538 m), Bansko (2,516 m) and Kartala (2,245 m).

If you can choose the day, go after Saturday 28 November: the fresh snow then has time to settle and the roads to be cleared.
