Measure the snow with your phone

Any phone works. Take one photo of your ski poles in the snow, send it here, and we read the snow depth for you. It then appears on the community snow map with everyone else's measurements. Every accepted measurement earns you 7 days of SnowScope Pro in the app (every peak named, 3D videos, no ads), up to 2 months. No subscription needed.

Take ONE photo

Step back so both poles are fully in the picture, top to bottom. Keep location on in your camera so we know where the snow is.

The difference between the two poles is the snow depth. A ruler or an avalanche probe standing in the snow works too: just photograph it so the numbers are readable.
