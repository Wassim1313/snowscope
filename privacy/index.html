SnowScope privacy policy

Version 1.1 — 27 October 2026. Publisher: Atlas Labs. Contact: atlaslabsdaba@gmail.com.

SnowScope shows snow forecasts, snow maps and ski-touring information. We collect as little as possible: no analytics SDK and no selling of data. Free users may see ads (see below); during the current test phase all features are free and no ads are shown.

What the app sends, and why

Data When Where it goes Why Kept
IP address, requested resort / map area Every screen that loads data Our server (access logs) Serve forecasts, security 14 days
Random install id (no name or email) First start Our server Keeps favourites and alert settings without an account Until you delete your data
Account: email address + password hash Only if you create an account Our server Sign-in, restore Premium and settings on a new phone Until you delete the account
Favourite resorts, alert rules, phone time zone When you use favourites or alerts Our server Powder alerts, quiet hours Until you delete them or your data
A map point you tap When you ask for the snow or forecast there Our server Return the values 14 days (logs)
Push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) If you allow notifications Our server + Google Firebase Deliver the alerts you set Until the token stops working
Google Play purchase token When you subscribe Our server + Google Play Verify Premium While subscribed, then as required by law
GPS track Only while a recording you started runs Stays on your phone Distance, vertical, GPX export Until you delete it; leaves the phone only if you share it
Camera image Only while the skyline camera is open Stays on your phone (not stored, not sent) Draw peak names over the view Not kept
Snow-depth photo (Measure snow) Only when you measure Our server, which reads the depth and deletes the photo right away Measure snow depth Not kept — unless you tick "share as observation": then the photo, its depth and the map point are shown to other users on the snow-photos layer (location metadata in the image is removed)
App language and phone time zone With every request / device registration Our server Answer in your language, no alerts at night Until you delete your data
Crash report (error text, app version, phone model) Only after the app crashed Our server Fix bugs 90 days

Location is used only while you use the map's "my location", record a track or open the skyline camera. SnowScope has no background-location permission.

Services the phone contacts directly

They see your IP address and the area requested, nothing else from us: OpenFreeMap (map tiles), Amazon Web Services public datasets (terrain tiles), OpenStreetMap Overpass mirrors (peak names for the skyline camera), GitHub Pages (the app's server address) and Cloudflare (which carries the traffic to our server). Weather data from Open-Meteo is fetched by our server, so Open-Meteo does not see you.

Advertising (free version only)

When advertising is active (not during the current test phase), free users may see Google AdMob ads. Google may use the advertising ID and cookies to show and measure ads; in the EU/UK/Switzerland we ask for your consent first (Google's consent form), and you can change it in About → Privacy options. Premium users see no ads. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Hotel and chalet links

"Hotels & chalets nearby" opens Booking.com (or a partner such as Stay22) in your browser with only the place, dates and number of guests in the link — never your account, id or position. Those sites have their own privacy policies; we may earn a commission from bookings.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play; we never see card details. Cancel any time in Google Play → Subscriptions.

Delete your data

In the app: menu → About → "Delete my data" (or "Delete account" when signed in). This erases everything stored on our server for your install or account. Uninstalling the app removes what is stored on the phone. You can also write to atlaslabsdaba@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Access, correction, deletion, portability and objection: atlaslabsdaba@gmail.com. Controller: Atlas Labs (contact above). You may also complain to your data-protection authority.

Safety

Snow and weather values are model forecasts, not observations. SnowScope never replaces the official avalanche bulletin; the danger levels shown are copied unchanged from the official services, with a link to their bulletin.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the app's data use changes.
