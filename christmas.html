HOW THE XMAS PROJECTION WORKS

We start from today's snowpack at each resort. We add the snowfall of every run of the ECMWF and GFS ensemble forecasts, as far ahead as they go (ECMWF to early December, GFS to about 22 December). Then we add how the snow changed until 25 December in each of the last 46 winters at the same place and height.

That gives thousands of possible Christmases. The range shows the middle 60 % of them, and the tick shows the most likely one. When the two ensembles disagree, the range gets wider. We will keep updating it as Christmas gets closer.
