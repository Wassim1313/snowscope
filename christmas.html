HOW TO READ IT

Snow now is the actual snowpack today on the upper slopes. Christmas snow is a projection: Christmas is 5 weeks away, so it is a likely range, not a promise.

We start from today's snowpack at each resort, add the snowfall of every run of the ECMWF and GFS ensemble forecasts as far ahead as they go (ECMWF to early December, GFS to about 22 December), then add how the snow changed until 25 December in each of the last 46 winters at the same place and height. The range is the middle 60 % of those Christmases; the yellow tick is the most likely one. We update it as Christmas gets closer.

  • Ski pass: LOW < €45 ≤ MID ≤ €65 < HIGH (adult day pass).
  • Hotels around: typical Christmas-week lodging price level around the resort (editorial estimate).
  • Pistes: size + altitude: ≥120 km = 2 pts, ≥50 km = 1, top ≥2900 m = +1; HIGH ≥2, MID 1, LOW 0.
  • Christmas week budget: per person: 6-day pass + 7 nights (LOW €55 / MID €95 / HIGH €170 per night, sharing) + 7 days food (FR €40, IT €38, DE €42, AT €45, CH €60 per day); estimates. Compared with a 1,000 € budget per person.
