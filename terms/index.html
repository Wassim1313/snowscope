1. What SnowScope is

SnowScope shows snow maps and forecasts, a powder score, piste and resort information, official avalanche danger levels copied from the national services, summit information (Summit AI: summits in sight, climb times, difficulty, routes), community snow reports, a trip planner and hotel links. The app is an information tool for adults (18+).

2. Mountain safety — read this

  • Everything in the app is an estimate. Snow depths, new snow, powder scores, piste quality, snow lines and 3D views are computed from forecasts, terrain and satellite data; they are not measurements and can be wrong by a lot.
  • Summit AI climb times, distances, difficulties and routes are computed from public terrain and trail data for a fit, experienced walker or ski tourer in good conditions. They do not account for your fitness, your equipment, the weather, the snow, avalanche danger, closed trails, glaciers, rockfall or darkness. "No marked trail" means we have no trail data — it never means the summit is safe or reachable. A route line is never a recommendation.
  • Avalanche danger levels are shown exactly as published by the official services, with a link to their bulletin. Levels can be missing, outdated or wrong in the app; always read the full official bulletin before going into terrain where avalanches are possible.
  • SnowScope never replaces the official avalanche bulletin, the mountain weather forecast, a mountain guide, local advice, your own training or your own judgement. Going into the mountains is dangerous and is your own decision and responsibility. Do not use the app while skiing, driving or moving in exposed terrain.
