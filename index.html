    About SnowScope

    SnowScope is a free snow map for skiers, snowboarders and ski tourers. It shows how much snow lies on every slope today, where fresh snow fell and how much is coming over the next days, for 3,720 ski resorts in Europe, North America and the Atlas mountains of Morocco. Every resort has its own page with today's snow, the next days and how this winter compares with past winters.

    How to use the map

    • Tap any slope on the map to see the snow depth at that spot and its height.
    • Use the day bar to go back two days or look at the coming days.
    • Open a resort to see its snow, the next 7 days and places to stay nearby.
    • Set a free powder alert to get a message when fresh snow is coming.
