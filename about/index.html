About SnowScope

SnowScope shows where the snow is: how deep it lies on every slope today, where fresh snow fell and how much is coming, for 3,720 ski resorts in Europe, North America and Morocco.

Who we are

SnowScope is made by Atlas Labs, a small team of hydrology, snow and Earth-observation specialists. We have spent years measuring and modelling mountain snow for water management, and we built SnowScope to put that work in the hands of skiers, snowboarders, ski tourers and everyone who lives with snow.

What you will find here

  • The snow map: snow depth and fresh snow on every slope, for the last days and the next days.
  • A page for every ski resort: today's snow on the upper slopes, the next 7 days, the season dates when they are announced, how this winter compares with past winters, nearby resorts and places to stay.
  • Snow reports: short daily reports by region and country, and longer guides that explain the snow.
  • Powder alerts: a free message when fresh snow is coming to the resorts you follow.
  • The SnowScope app for Android, with the sharpest map and offline regions.

How SnowScope is paid for

The map and the resort pages are free. SnowScope is paid for by an optional Pro subscription, by advertising, and by partner links to places to stay, which are always marked “Partner” and may earn us a commission at no cost to you. Advertisers never influence the snow numbers or what we write.

How we work

Read our editorial policy to see how the numbers and the reports are made and checked. Questions, corrections or ideas: contact us.

Mountains are dangerous. SnowScope is an information tool, not a safety service: always read the official avalanche bulletin and follow the rules of the resort before you go.
