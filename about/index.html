SnowScope: snow map, powder and Summit AI

SnowScope is an Android app by Atlas Labs for skiers, snowboarders and ski tourers in the Alps, the Pyrenees, Scandinavia and North America. It shows where the snow is on a sharp, terrain-shaped map, scores powder for every resort, colours the pistes by how good they are to ski right now, shows the official avalanche danger levels, and with Summit AI names every summit in sight and tells you how long it takes to climb it from where you stand.

What the app does

  • Summit AI: every summit in sight up to 120 km with its name, height and distance; climb time on foot or on skis from where you stand, difficulty in plain words, an honest "no marked trail"; an AR camera that locks on to the peak; record a short clip with an end card and share it.
  • Snow depth and new snow on a terrain-shaped map, day by day for the past week and the next 7 days.
  • Powder score 0–10 per resort, powder alerts for your resorts, your region or the mountains near you.
  • Pistes coloured by how good they are to ski now, and the best runs of each resort.
  • Official avalanche danger levels with the bulletin link, steep and risky slopes.
  • 3D view of any area with the latest satellite view of the snow; offline regions.
  • Community snow reports with photos; measure snow depth with your poles, skis, probe or a ruler; the Snow Challenge.
  • Trip planner, season opening dates, snowmaking, hotels and chalets near each resort.
  • English, French, German, Italian and Spanish. Free with ads; optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase; optional Pro subscription.

SnowScope never replaces the official avalanche bulletin, a mountain guide or your own judgement.
