    About Ski Eldorado

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Ski Eldorado one of the smaller places to ski in US Northeast. The slopes start at 719 m and reach 889 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ski Eldorado (2.0 km, north-east).

    Snow at Ski Eldorado through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 804 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 December and lasts to 24 April: about 19 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 65 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 43 cm, with 68 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 15 March, with about 83 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 50 cm to 111 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 170 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 719 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Salmon Mountain (981 m), Mount Kent (956 m) and Mont D'Urban (914 m).

    Questions about Ski Eldorado

    What is the best month to ski at Ski Eldorado?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Ski Eldorado at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 43 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Eldorado?

    The top is at 889 m and the base at 719 m.

    Who is Ski Eldorado best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
