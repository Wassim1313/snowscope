    About NASPA Ski Garden

    Neither huge nor tiny, NASPA Ski Garden offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 210 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 431 m and 682 m above sea level. Ippon Sugi is the next resort, 1.2 km away to the north.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Gala Yuzawa Snow Resort (3.5 km north-west) has about 19 km of pistes against 4.0 km here. Gala Yuzawa Snow Resort reaches 1,178 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at NASPA Ski Garden today: about 17 cm up high. 21 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 12 cm by Monday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    251 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 431 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
