    Fellhorn/Kanzelwand in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Fellhorn/Kanzelwand (1,442 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 43 cm, with a normal range of 26 cm–54 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 86 cm; the middle half of winters range from 67 cm to 104 cm.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 93 cm.

    By 15 December 20 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 41 %.

    Best winter on record: 1998-99, peaking at 198 cm. Worst: 2022-23, which never passed 59 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 153 days.

    So far this winter: 6 cm on 19 November (usual: 10 cm).

    About Fellhorn/Kanzelwand

    Fellhorn/Kanzelwand is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 37 km of pistes put it at number 169 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 918 m and 1,965 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf, 1.8 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Fellhorn/Kanzelwand

    Fellhorn/Kanzelwand has 70 graded runs adding up to about 37 km. Most of the length is red (43%, about 16 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 2.2 km of black or expert runs. 5.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,047 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 918 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 12 lifts break down into 2 cable cars, 3 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 drag lifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Trettachspitze at 2,594 m, standing 629 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 1,442 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 December and lasts to 1 May: about 20 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 43 cm; a snowy December gave 70 cm, a dry one 13 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 93 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 53 cm and 128 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 52 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.
