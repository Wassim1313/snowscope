    About Forsteralm

    Forsteralm is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 744 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 678 m and 825 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Königsberg (12 km, south).

    Forsteralm and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Hochkar: about 19 km of pistes, 23 km to the south-east. For more reliable snow, Hochkar usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 21 weeks, against 0 here. The highest nearby is Hochkar, topping out at 1,788 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Forsteralm has 7 graded runs adding up to about 2.5 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.6 km (64%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    147 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 678 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hochkar reaches 1,788 m. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Alpl at 1,404 m, standing 579 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Forsteralm through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 752 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 36 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 46 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 23 cm, around 4 February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February.

    Quick answers

    When does Forsteralm have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Forsteralm at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Forsteralm at?

    Between 678 m and 825 m, so 147 m of vertical drop.

    Is Forsteralm good for beginners?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Forsteralm?

    Hochkar, 23 km to the south-east, with about 19 km of pistes.
