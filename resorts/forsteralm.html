    About Forsteralm

    Forsteralm is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 744 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 678 m and 825 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Königsberg (12 km, south).

    Forsteralm and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Hochkar: about 19 km of pistes, 23 km to the south-east. The highest nearby is Hochkar, topping out at 1,788 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    147 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 678 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hochkar reaches 1,788 m.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Forsteralm include 4 chalets: Almchalet Austall, Almchalet Hirschkogel Hütte, Almchalet Jagdhütte and Almchalet Almhütte.
