    About Hoodoo

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hoodoo offers about 20 km of pistes (number 100 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 1,427 m and reach 1,728 m at the top.

    The pistes of Hoodoo

    In all, 47 marked runs cover roughly 20 km. The largest share is green circle, with 9.8 km (48%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 5.0 km.

    How much snow Hoodoo usually gets

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,578 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-May. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for early January and late March. A normal season tops out near 214 cm, around 4 March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 93 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 182 cm, a dry one 29 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 159 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 46 cm and 275 cm.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 5 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Hoodoo. The last seven days brought 13 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 28 cm by now.

    The mountain at Hoodoo

    The vertical drop is 301 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,427 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 6 lifts break down into 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Maxwell Butte (1,831 m), 6.8 km to the north, about 103 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Hoodoo

    When does Hoodoo have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Hoodoo have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 93 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Hoodoo?

    The top is at 1,728 m and the base at 1,427 m.

    Is Hoodoo good for beginners?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 9.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Hoodoo have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
