    What 46 winters say about Hoodoo

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Hoodoo (5,177 ft (1,578 m)), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 37 in (93 cm); half of the winters sit between 20 in (50 cm) and 56 in (141 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 73 in (185 cm) (49 in (124 cm)–96 in (243 cm)).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 84 in (214 cm).

    By 15 December 70 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 78 %.

    1998-99 was the big one (160 in (406 cm)); 2014-15 the lean one (19 in (47 cm)).

    Expect 12 in (30 cm) or more from late November until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 175 days.

    Hoodoo: quick answers

    Is there snow in Hoodoo at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 37 in (93 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 78 % of 46 winters. Right now: 4.3 in (11 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Hoodoo?

    March: the snow peaks at about 84 in (214 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How deep does the snow get at Hoodoo?

    About 84 in (214 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 19 in (47 cm) in the leanest winter (2014-15) and 160 in (406 cm) in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high do the lifts go at Hoodoo?

    The top is at 1,728 m and the base at 1,427 m.

    How many km of pistes does Hoodoo have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 6 lifts.

    About Hoodoo

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hoodoo offers about 20 km of pistes (number 100 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 1,427 m and reach 1,728 m at the top.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 11 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Hoodoo. The last seven days brought 7 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    The mountain at Hoodoo

    The vertical drop is 301 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,427 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.
