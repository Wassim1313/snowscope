    About Cauterets Pont d'Espagne

    About 3.0 km of pistes make Cauterets Pont d'Espagne one of the smaller places to ski in the Pyrenees. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,507 m up to 1,746 m. Cauterets Cirque du Lys is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the north.

    Cauterets Pont d'Espagne and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Gourette (18 km north-west) has about 30 km of pistes against 3.0 km here. Cauterets Cirque du Lys has the longer snow season: around 19 weeks of solid cover, compared with 15 at Cauterets Pont d'Espagne. The highest nearby is Luz-Ardiden, topping out at 2,485 m.

    The pistes of Cauterets Pont d'Espagne

    The piste map lists 6 graded runs, about 2.9 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.6 km (57%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,626 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 December and lasts to 13 April: about 15 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 19 cm to 96 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 26 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 60 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 58 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 28 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Lifts and terrain

    239 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,507 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Luz-Ardiden, for one, climbs to 2,485 m. To get up there are 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 2 button lifts. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grande Fache at 3,005 m, standing 1,259 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hôtellerie Pont d'Espagne (hotel), Chalet du Clot (mountain hut), Refuge d'Ilhéou (mountain hut) and Hôtellerie de la Fruitière (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Cauterets Pont d'Espagne: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Cauterets Pont d'Espagne?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Cauterets Pont d'Espagne at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    What altitude is Cauterets Pont d'Espagne at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,746 m; the lowest starts at 1,507 m (239 m of vertical).

    Is Cauterets Pont d'Espagne good for beginners?

    Yes: 70% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Cauterets Pont d'Espagne?

    Gourette, 18 km to the north-west, with about 30 km of pistes.
