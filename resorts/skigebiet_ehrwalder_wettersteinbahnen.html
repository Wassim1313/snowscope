    About Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen offers about 12 km of pistes (number 449 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,003 m and 1,486 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ehrwalder Alm, 3.7 km to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen has 22 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. Most of the length is blue (76%, about 9.1 km). With 76% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The mountain at Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen

    From top to bottom that makes 483 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 1 T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Zugspitze, 2,962 m high and 4.1 km north-east of the resort.

    How much snow Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen usually gets

    Count on about 13 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,244 m) in an ordinary winter, from early January to early April. A normal season tops out near 47 cm, around 26 February. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 25 cm (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 43 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 26 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Year to year the snow is steady, with 28 cm to 66 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    When does Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    How much snow does Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen at?

    Between 1,003 m and 1,486 m, so 483 m of vertical drop.

    Is Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen suitable for families?

    Yes: 76% of the graded pistes, about 9.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Ehrwalder Wettersteinbahnen?

    Garmisch-Classic, 12 km to the north-east, with about 38 km of pistes.
