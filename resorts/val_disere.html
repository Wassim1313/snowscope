    About Tignes

    About 211 km of pistes make Tignes one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Alps (number 10). Its lifts climb from 1,559 m to 3,447 m. The closest other ski area is Bonneval-sur-Arc (14 km, south-east).

    The mountain at Tignes

    Skied top to bottom, Tignes gives 1,888 m of descent: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. With its base at 1,559 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 3,447 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 cable cars, 6 funiculars, 7 gondolas, 38 chairlifts, 7 T-bars, 7 button lifts, 4 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 12 moving carpets, 84 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 12 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aiguille de la Grande Sassière at 3,747 m, standing 300 m above the top lift.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 51 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Tignes. 36 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 81 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 47 cm at mid-mountain. The 2026/27 season at Tignes is announced to start on 21 November 2026 and to run until 2 May 2027.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,503 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 12 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 185 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is usually deepest around 17 April, with about 187 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and early April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 99 cm (lean years 53 cm, snowy years 147 cm). Most winters land between 119 cm and 208 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks.

    Tignes: common questions

    When does Tignes have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Tignes at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 99 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Tignes?

    The announced opening day is 21 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 2 May 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Tignes?

    Between 1,559 m and 3,447 m, so 1,888 m of vertical drop.

    Is Tignes good for beginners?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 117 km, are easy.
