    About Gitschberg Jochtal

    Around 27 km of marked runs place Gitschberg Jochtal among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 222 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,305 m up to 2,497 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Terenten, 10.0 km to the east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,901 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 December and keeps it until about 25 May, roughly 25 weeks. Most winters land between 55 cm and 112 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 79 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 47 cm, with 78 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 88 cm, around 20 February.

    What there is to ski at Gitschberg Jochtal

    In all, 39 marked runs cover roughly 27 km. The largest share is red, with 14 km (52%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 3.0 km of difficult runs.

    How Gitschberg Jochtal is laid out

    1,192 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,305 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 14 lifts break down into 7 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Ribigenspitz at 2,939 m, standing 442 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Gitschberg Jochtal

    When does Gitschberg Jochtal have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Gitschberg Jochtal have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 47 cm deep.

    How high is Gitschberg Jochtal?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,497 m; the lowest starts at 1,305 m (1,192 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Gitschberg Jochtal best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Gitschberg Jochtal have?

    About 27 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
