    Orsa Grönklitt in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Orsa Grönklitt (476 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 34 cm; half of the winters sit between 22 cm and 43 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 56 cm; the middle half of winters range from 44 cm to 67 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 57 cm.

    By 15 December 11 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 24 %.

    1987-88 was the big one (119 cm); 1995-96 the lean one (33 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to mid-April, about 127 days.

    Plan a trip to Orsa Grönklitt

    Common questions

    Does Orsa Grönklitt have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 34 cm, and only 24 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 26 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Orsa Grönklitt?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 57 cm). For a sure base, mid-December to mid-April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Orsa Grönklitt?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 57 cm in early February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 119 cm (1987-88), the thinnest 33 cm (1995-96).

    How high do the lifts go at Orsa Grönklitt?

    Between 393 m and 560 m, so 167 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Orsa Grönklitt?

    About 13 km of pistes and 15 lifts.

    About Orsa Grönklitt

    With some 13 km of runs, Orsa Grönklitt belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 393 m up to 560 m.
