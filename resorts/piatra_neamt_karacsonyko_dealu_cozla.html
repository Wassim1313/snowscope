    About Piatra Neamt (Karácsonykő)

    Piatra Neamt (Karácsonykő) is a small ski area by the standards of the Carpathians, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 382 m and reach 629 m at the top.

    How Piatra Neamt (Karácsonykő) is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Piatra Neamt (Karácsonykő) gives 247 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 382 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.
