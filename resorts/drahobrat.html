    About Драгобрат

    Around 10 km of marked runs place Драгобрат among the larger resorts of the Carpathians, number 14 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,323 m to 1,706 m. Its nearest neighbour is Буковель, 18 km north-east, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 21 graded runs, about 10 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.0 km, or 70%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 383 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,323 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 14 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 7 button lifts and 1 drag lift. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
