    About Ellegglifte

    Ellegglifte is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 939 m and reach 996 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Buron (3.2 km, south).

    Ellegglifte and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 9.7 km south-west to Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte, with about 8.0 km of runs. Nesselwang has the longer snow season: around 14 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Ellegglifte. Nesselwang reaches 1,468 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Snow this week

    The mountain at Ellegglifte

    From top to bottom that makes 57 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 939 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Edelsberg at 1,630 m, standing 634 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 968 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 30 cm, a dry one 1 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 7 cm to 51 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 9 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. The snow is usually deepest around 11 February, with about 27 cm.

    Questions about Ellegglifte

    What is the best month to ski at Ellegglifte?

    Aim for early January and mid-February.

    Is there snow at Ellegglifte at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Ellegglifte?

    Its highest lift reaches 996 m; the lowest starts at 939 m (57 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Ellegglifte?

    Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte, 9.7 km to the south-west, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Ellegglifte have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
