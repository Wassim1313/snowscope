    About Mountain High

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mountain High offers about 19 km of pistes (number 102 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 2,008 m and reach 2,494 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Mt. Baldy, 13 km south-east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Mountain High

    Mountain High has 41 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 8.0 km, or 44%. Good skiers will like it here; 36% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 6.6 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Mountain High in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at about 2,251 m, the middle of the ski area stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 486 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,008 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. The lift system counts 12 lifts: 8 chairlifts and 4 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Pine Mountain (2,942 m), 7.5 km to the south-east, about 448 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Mountain High

    How high do the lifts go at Mountain High?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,494 m; the lowest starts at 2,008 m (486 m of vertical).

    Does Mountain High have steep runs?

    Yes: about 6.6 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How big is Mountain High?

    About 19 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
