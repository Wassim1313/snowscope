    About Wintersportgebiet Wurmberg

    Around 11 km of marked runs place Wintersportgebiet Wurmberg among the larger resorts of Central Europe, number 17 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 720 m up to 968 m. Its nearest neighbour is Rathauslifte Braunlage, 2.4 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Wintersportgebiet Wurmberg

    The piste map lists 20 graded runs, about 11 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 9.0 km (81%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Wintersportgebiet Wurmberg

    The vertical drop is 248 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 920 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 4 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 844 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 30 January, with about 10 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 27 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 25 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.
