    About Torgon

    Neither huge nor tiny, Torgon offers about 23 km of pistes (number 272 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,335 m and reach 1,979 m at the top. The closest other ski area is La Chapelle d'Abondance (3.2 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Torgon

    Counting every graded piste, there are 36 runs and around 23 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 9.6 km (43%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes. 0.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,657 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 26 weeks of good cover. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 160 cm, around 19 February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 115 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 90 cm to 206 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 75 cm; a snowy December gave 123 cm, a dry one 24 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    644 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,335 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 10 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 4 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Mont de Grange (2,432 m), 6.2 km to the south-west, about 453 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Torgon?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Torgon have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 75 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Torgon at?

    Between 1,335 m and 1,979 m, so 644 m of vertical drop.

    Is Torgon good for beginners?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Torgon?

    Les Portes du Soleil, 10.0 km to the south-west, with about 406 km of pistes.
