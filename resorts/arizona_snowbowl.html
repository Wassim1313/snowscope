    Arizona Snowbowl in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Arizona Snowbowl (about 10,335 ft (3,150 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 13 in (32 cm), with a normal range of 1.6 in (4 cm)–37 in (95 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 19 in (48 cm) (3.9 in (10 cm)–69 in (175 cm)).

    January is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 29 in (73 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 28 % of winters, and by 1 January in 46 %.

    The deepest winter was 1992-93 (264 in (670 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2017-18 (19 in (49 cm)).

    Expect 12 in (30 cm) or more from early December until early April in a normal winter, roughly 125 days.
