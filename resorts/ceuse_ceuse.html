    About Céüse

    Céüse has about 0.1 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,999 m and reach 2,008 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Laye, 17 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    9 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,999 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Pic de Céüse (2,016 m), la Manche (1,825 m) and Tête de Combe Noire (1,792 m).

    Snow at Céüse through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,004 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 8 December and keeps it until about 23 April, roughly 20 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 78 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 151 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 220 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 61 cm to 301 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 70 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For about 14 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Gîte les Izoards (chalet), Hôtel Logis Muret (hotel), Domaine de Tiko (chalet) and Les Couleurs du Temps (guesthouse).

    Quick answers

    When does Céüse have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    Is there snow at Céüse at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 78 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Céüse?

    The top is at 2,008 m and the base at 1,999 m.
