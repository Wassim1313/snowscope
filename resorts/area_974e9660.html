    About Парковая гора

    Neither huge nor tiny, Парковая гора offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 48 of 86 in Russia). Its lifts climb from 76 m to 255 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Горный Воздух, 3.2 km to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Парковая гора

    Парковая гора has 4 graded runs adding up to about 2.5 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 2.5 km, or 100%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Парковая гора is laid out

    179 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 76 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Quick answers

    How high is Парковая гора?

    The top is at 255 m and the base at 76 m.

    Can beginners ski at Парковая гора?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Парковая гора have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
