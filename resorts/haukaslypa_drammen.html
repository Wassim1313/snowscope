    About Haukåsløypa Drammen

    Haukåsløypa Drammen has about 0.4 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 93 m up to 216 m. Its nearest neighbour is Høgås alpinsenter, 24 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Haukåsløypa Drammen and the resorts around it

    Snow at Haukåsløypa Drammen through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 154 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 January and lasts to 29 March: about 12 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 52 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 46 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 5 cm to 79 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Haukåsløypa Drammen gives 123 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Gruvåsen, 412 m high and 4.2 km west of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Globus Hotel (hotel), Scandic Ambassadeur Drammen (hotel), Quality Hotel River Station (hotel) and Høvik overnatting B&B (guesthouse).

    Questions about Haukåsløypa Drammen

    When does Haukåsløypa Drammen have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    How much snow does Haukåsløypa Drammen have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    How high is Haukåsløypa Drammen?

    Its highest lift reaches 216 m; the lowest starts at 93 m (123 m of vertical).

    How big is Haukåsløypa Drammen?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
