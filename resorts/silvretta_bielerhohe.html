    About Silvretta-Bielerhöhe

    Neither huge nor tiny, Silvretta-Bielerhöhe offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 792 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,955 m and 2,072 m above sea level. Galtür is the next resort, 5.9 km away to the north-east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,014 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 139 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Most winters land between 97 cm and 182 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 77 cm, from 41 cm in a poor year to 105 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 152 cm, around 22 March.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 3.4 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 3.4 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Silvretta Montafon: about 102 km of pistes, 17 km to the north-west. When it comes to snow, Silvretta-Bielerhöhe leads the group with roughly 28 weeks of a 30 cm base. The highest nearby is Scuol Motta Naluns, topping out at 2,792 m.

    How Silvretta-Bielerhöhe is laid out

    117 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,955 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Silvrettahorn, 3,244 m high and 6.7 km south of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Silvretta-Bielerhöhe include Piz Buin (hotel), Silvretta-Haus (hotel), Madlenerhaus (mountain hut) and Pension Tirolerhof (hotel).

    Silvretta-Bielerhöhe: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Silvretta-Bielerhöhe?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Silvretta-Bielerhöhe at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 77 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Silvretta-Bielerhöhe at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,072 m; the lowest starts at 1,955 m (117 m of vertical).

    Is Silvretta-Bielerhöhe good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Silvretta-Bielerhöhe?

    Silvretta Montafon, 17 km to the north-west, with about 102 km of pistes.
