    About Sauris

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sauris offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 786 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,201 m to 1,408 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sella Ciampigotto, 7.8 km to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 2.9 km. Black runs make up the biggest part: 1.0 km, or 34%. Steep ground is the speciality: 34% of the length is graded difficult.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,304 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 27 December and keeps it until about 9 April, roughly 15 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 45 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 26 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 4 cm in a poor year to 50 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 24 cm to 81 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 25 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early March.

    The mountain at Sauris

    From top to bottom that makes 207 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Golzentipp/Obertilliach reaches 2,225 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Creton di Clap Grande, 2,487 m high and 7.3 km north of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Albergo Neider (hotel), Albergo Ristorante Borgo Velt (hotel), Bed and Breakfast Pa' Mairlan (guesthouse) and Albergo Morgenleit (hotel).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Sauris?

    Aim for early January and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Can you ski at Sauris at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    What altitude is Sauris at?

    The top is at 1,408 m and the base at 1,201 m.

    Does Sauris have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.0 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sauris?

    Zoncolan, 19 km to the east, with about 27 km of pistes.
