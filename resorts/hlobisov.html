    About Hlobišov

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hlobišov offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 128 of 312 in the Carpathians). The ski area spans the mountain from 581 m up to 803 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hronec (vlek Majer), 2.5 km to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Hlobišov has 2 graded runs adding up to about 1.9 km. Most of the length is red (100%, about 1.9 km). Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Hlobišov today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Hlobišov hold around 24 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 2 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    The mountain at Hlobišov

    From top to bottom that makes 222 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 581 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a button lift.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Hlobišov at?

    The top is at 803 m and the base at 581 m.

    What level of skier is Hlobišov best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hlobišov?

    Ski Čierny Balog, 5.6 km to the south-east, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Hlobišov?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
