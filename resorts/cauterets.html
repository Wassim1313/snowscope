    About Cauterets Cirque du Lys

    With roughly 25 km of runs, Cauterets Cirque du Lys sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. Its lifts climb from 1,725 m to 2,420 m. Cauterets Pont d'Espagne is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 49 graded runs, about 22 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 54%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How much snow Cauterets Cirque du Lys usually gets

    At about 2,072 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 December and lasts to 26 April: about 19 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 43 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 94 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 53 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Year to year the snow is steady, with 41 cm to 165 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 110 cm, around 3 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 4 weeks.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 695 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,725 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Grand Barbat (2,813 m), 3.2 km to the south-west, about 393 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Cauterets Cirque du Lys

    When is the best time to ski at Cauterets Cirque du Lys?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Cauterets Cirque du Lys at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 43 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Cauterets Cirque du Lys?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 18 April 2027.

    What altitude is Cauterets Cirque du Lys at?

    The top is at 2,420 m and the base at 1,725 m.

    Is Cauterets Cirque du Lys good for beginners?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.
