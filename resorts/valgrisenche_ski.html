    About Valgrisenche Ski

    Valgrisenche Ski is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,615 m and reach 1,798 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Rhemes (7.2 km, south-east).

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Valgrisenche Ski today: about 64 cm up high. 26 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 41 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Les Arcs: about 168 km of pistes, 21 km to the west. For higher slopes, Les Arcs climbs to 3,205 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Valgrisenche Ski gives 183 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,615 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Tignes reaches 3,447 m.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Albergo Grande Sassière (hotel), Albergo Perret (hotel), Hotel Maison des Myrtilles (hotel) and B&B Maison Bovard (guesthouse).
