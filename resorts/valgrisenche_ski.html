    About Valgrisenche Ski

    Valgrisenche Ski is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,615 m and reach 1,798 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Rhemes (7.2 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 6 graded runs, about 2.5 km in total. The largest share is black, with 1.0 km (40%). With 40% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Les Arcs: about 168 km of pistes, 21 km to the west. Les Arcs has the longer snow season: around 28 weeks of solid cover, compared with 24 at Valgrisenche Ski. For higher slopes, Les Arcs climbs to 3,205 m.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Valgrisenche Ski today: about 42 cm up high. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 33 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Valgrisenche Ski gives 183 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,615 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Tignes reaches 3,447 m. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grande Rousse Nord at 3,607 m, standing 1,809 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Valgrisenche Ski usually gets

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,706 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to early May. A normal season tops out near 112 cm, around 5 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 75 cm to 133 cm in mid-February in most winters. For about 5 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 62 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 33 cm in a poor year to 92 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 59 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Albergo Grande Sassière (hotel), Albergo Perret (hotel), Hotel Maison des Myrtilles (hotel) and B&B Maison Bovard (guesthouse).

    Quick answers

    When does Valgrisenche Ski have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Is there snow at Valgrisenche Ski at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 62 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Valgrisenche Ski?

    The top is at 1,798 m and the base at 1,615 m.

    Is Valgrisenche Ski good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.0 km of its graded pistes (40%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Valgrisenche Ski?

    Les Arcs, 21 km to the west, with about 168 km of pistes.
