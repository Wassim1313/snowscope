    About Skiareál Klínovec

    About 20 km of pistes make Skiareál Klínovec one of the ten biggest ski areas of Central Europe (number 4). The ski area spans the mountain from 752 m up to 1,240 m. Its nearest neighbour is Boží Dar, 0.9 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 32 runs and around 19 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 60%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skiareál Klínovec gives 488 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 752 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,240 m. To get up there are 5 chairlifts, 4 button lifts and 4 moving carpets. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    How much snow Skiareál Klínovec usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 996 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 24 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 43 cm). Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 69 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Skiareál Klínovec?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    How much snow does Skiareál Klínovec have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Skiareál Klínovec at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,240 m; the lowest starts at 752 m (488 m of vertical).

    Is Skiareál Klínovec suitable for families?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    How big is Skiareál Klínovec?

    About 20 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
