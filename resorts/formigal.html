    About Formigal

    About 104 km of pistes make Formigal one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Pyrenees (number 3). The slopes start at 1,502 m and reach 2,264 m at the top. Astun is the next resort, 8.1 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 109 graded runs, about 103 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 36 km, or 35%. Good skiers will like it here; 37% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 33 km of difficult runs. 4.8 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How much snow Formigal usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,883 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 December and lasts to 26 April: about 19 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 37 cm and 143 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 39 cm; a snowy December gave 83 cm, a dry one 6 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 52 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 98 cm.

    The mountain at Formigal

    762 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,502 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 13 chairlifts, 7 drag lifts and 5 moving carpets, 25 lifts in all. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pala de Ip at 2,779 m, standing 515 m above the top lift.

    Formigal: common questions

    When does Formigal have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Formigal have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 39 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Formigal?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Formigal at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,264 m; the lowest starts at 1,502 m (762 m of vertical).

    Is Formigal good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 38 km of its graded pistes (37%) are difficult.
