    About Avers

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Avers sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,981 m and reach 2,515 m at the top. Bivio is the next resort, 7.7 km away to the east.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,248 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 61 cm; a snowy December gave 100 cm, a dry one 33 cm. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 115 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 112 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Year to year the snow is steady, with 71 cm to 134 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Runs by difficulty

    Avers has 11 graded runs adding up to about 8.2 km. The largest share is black, with 4.9 km (60%). With 60% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 4.9 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Avers

    From top to bottom that makes 534 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 1,981 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow, 3 lifts in all. Above the ski area rises Piz Platta (3,392 m), 4.7 km to the north, about 877 m higher than the top lift.

    Avers and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 15 km north to Savognin, with about 44 km of runs. For snow it is second in the group, behind Bivio (28 weeks of good cover, 26 here).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Avers?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Avers at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 61 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Avers?

    The top is at 2,515 m and the base at 1,981 m.

    Is Avers good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 4.9 km of its graded pistes (60%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Avers?

    Savognin, 15 km to the north, with about 44 km of pistes.
