    About Lachtal

    With some 25 km of runs, Lachtal belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,572 m and reach 2,199 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Lachtal, 1.8 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,886 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 68 cm and 146 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 55 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 88 cm, a dry one 15 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 103 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 128 cm.

    What there is to ski at Lachtal

    The piste map lists 42 graded runs, about 25 km in total. The largest share is red, with 16 km (63%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.9 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    627 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,572 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,199 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Schießeck (2,275 m), Steineck (2,260 m) and Hühnerkogel (2,242 m).

    Questions about Lachtal

    When does Lachtal have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Lachtal at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 55 cm deep.

    How high is Lachtal?

    Between 1,572 m and 2,199 m, so 627 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Lachtal best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 63% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Lachtal?

    About 25 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
