    About Gföllberglift

    Gföllberglift is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,108 m and 1,316 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Jöchelspitze, 2.8 km to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 2.2 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 64%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Gföllberglift today and the next days

    Snow on the ground at Gföllberglift today: about 4 cm up high. 4 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 31 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 10 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 208 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Holzgauer Wetterspitze, 2,895 m high and 6.5 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 12 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,212 m) in an ordinary winter, from late December to mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 64 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 36 cm and 100 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 23 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 30 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 6 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    Quick answers

    When does Gföllberglift have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Gföllberglift at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 30 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Gföllberglift?

    The top is at 1,316 m and the base at 1,108 m.

    Is Gföllberglift suitable for families?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Gföllberglift?

    Fellhorn/Kanzelwand, 13 km to the north-west, with about 37 km of pistes.
