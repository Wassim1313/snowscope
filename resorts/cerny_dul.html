    About Černý Důl

    Černý Důl is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 5.0 km of pistes put it at number 50 of 257. The slopes start at 616 m and reach 892 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Skiareál Špička, 3.4 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Černý Důl has 5 graded runs adding up to about 4.7 km. The largest share is red, with 3.1 km (66%). Intermediate runs make up 66% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The mountain at Černý Důl

    Skied top to bottom, Černý Důl gives 276 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 616 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 5 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Černá Hora-Pec (4.0 km north-east) has about 44 km of pistes against 5.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Pec pod Sněžkou does better, with about 8 weeks of a good base instead of 2. The highest nearby is Černá Hora-Pec, topping out at 1,280 m.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Černý Důl has roughly 2 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 4 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 6 cm at mid-mountain).

    How much snow Černý Důl usually gets

    At about 754 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 February and lasts to 28 February: about 2 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. A normal season tops out near 32 cm, around 25 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 56 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 11 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 43 cm, a dry one 1 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Černý Důl?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    How much snow does Černý Důl have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 11 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Černý Důl?

    Between 616 m and 892 m, so 276 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Černý Důl best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Černý Důl?

    Černá Hora-Pec, 4.0 km to the north-east, with about 44 km of pistes.
