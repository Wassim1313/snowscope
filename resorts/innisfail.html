    What 46 winters say about Innisfail

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Innisfail (942 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 13 cm, with a normal range of 7 cm–18 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 15 cm; the middle half of winters range from 7 cm to 25 cm.

    The snowiest month is January, when the pack peaks at about 19 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 0 % of winters, and by 1 January in 4 %.

    1996-97 was the big one (63 cm); 1994-95 the lean one (11 cm).

    A 30 cm base is rare here: most winters stay below it.
