    The mountain at Innisfail

    24 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 930 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 942 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-January. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 19 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 34 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 29 cm, a dry one 3 cm.
