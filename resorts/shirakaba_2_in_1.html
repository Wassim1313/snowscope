    About Shirakaba 2 in 1

    Neither huge nor tiny, Shirakaba 2 in 1 offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 152 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,567 m up to 1,830 m. The closest other ski area is Shirakabako Royal Hill (1.9 km, west).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Shirakaba 2 in 1 gives 263 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,567 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 1,830 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
