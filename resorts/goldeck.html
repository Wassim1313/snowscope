    About Goldeck

    With some 24 km of runs, Goldeck belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 546 m up to 2,117 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bergbahn Weissensee, 15 km to the south-west.

    The mountain at Goldeck

    From top to bottom that makes 1,571 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The lowest slopes are at only 546 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
