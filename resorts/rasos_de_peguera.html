    About Rasos de Peguera

    Compact and local, Rasos de Peguera has about 6.0 km of runs, small for the Pyrenees. Skiing here happens between 1,826 m and 2,036 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Estació d'esquí Port del Comte (18 km, west).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 5.8 km. Most of the length is red (64%, about 3.7 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 210 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 1,826 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at GrandValira, reach 2,619 m. Getting up the mountain: 4 drag lifts and 1 rope tow, 5 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The skyline is dominated by Cap de la Gallina Pelada, 2,321 m high and 5.7 km north-west of the resort.

    Snow at Rasos de Peguera through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,931 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 23 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten), with 14 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 7 cm.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Xalet Refugi Rasos de Peguera (mountain hut), La Torre d'Espinalbet (chalet), Els Noguers (hotel) and Refugi Serra d'Ensija (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Rasos de Peguera

    When does Rasos de Peguera have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Rasos de Peguera at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Rasos de Peguera?

    The top is at 2,036 m and the base at 1,826 m.

    What level of skier is Rasos de Peguera best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 64% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rasos de Peguera?

    GrandValira, 46 km to the north, with about 136 km of pistes.
