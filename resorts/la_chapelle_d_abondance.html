    About La Chapelle d'Abondance

    With some 27 km of runs, La Chapelle d'Abondance belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 994 m to 1,797 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Espace Liberté, 2.7 km to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 48 runs and around 27 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 43%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at La Chapelle d'Abondance through a normal winter

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,396 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 71 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 52 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 87 cm, a dry one 13 cm. For about 4 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 56 cm and 142 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 106 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March.

    How La Chapelle d'Abondance is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 803 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 994 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Les Portes du Soleil, reach 2,250 m. Its 11 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 4 button lifts and 1 drag lift. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Mont de Grange, 2,432 m high and 3.5 km south-west of the resort.

    Questions about La Chapelle d'Abondance

    What is the best month to ski at La Chapelle d'Abondance?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at La Chapelle d'Abondance at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 52 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is La Chapelle d'Abondance at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,797 m; the lowest starts at 994 m (803 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at La Chapelle d'Abondance?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 19 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near La Chapelle d'Abondance?

    Les Portes du Soleil, 7.5 km to the south, with about 406 km of pistes.
