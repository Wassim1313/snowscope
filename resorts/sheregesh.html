    About Шерегеш

    Only 1 of the 86 ski areas of Russia is larger than Шерегеш, which has some 65 km of runs. The slopes start at 577 m and reach 1,462 m at the top.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 87 graded runs, about 65 km in total. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 25 km, or 39%. With 70% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How Шерегеш is laid out

    885 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. At 577 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 23 lifts: 4 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 10 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 3 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Шерегеш at?

    Between 577 m and 1,462 m, so 885 m of vertical drop.

    Is Шерегеш good for beginners?

    Yes: 70% of the graded pistes, about 45 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Шерегеш have?

    About 65 km of pistes and 23 lifts.
