    The mountain at Měděnec

    Skied top to bottom, Měděnec gives 119 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 657 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Skiareál Klínovec, reach 1,240 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.
