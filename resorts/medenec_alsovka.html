    How much snow Měděnec usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 716 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-December and early February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 14 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 5 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 25 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 37 cm.

    The mountain at Měděnec

    Skied top to bottom, Měděnec gives 119 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 657 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Skiareál Klínovec, reach 1,240 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    Questions about Měděnec

    When does Měděnec have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-December and early February.

    How much snow does Měděnec have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 5 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Měděnec at?

    Between 657 m and 776 m, so 119 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Měděnec best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Měděnec?

    Skiareál Klínovec, 13 km to the west, with about 20 km of pistes.
