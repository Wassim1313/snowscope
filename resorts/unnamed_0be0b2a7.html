    About Skilift Weißenbach

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skilift Weißenbach one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 970 m and 1,074 m above sea level. Dobel-Skilift Schönwald is the next resort, 1.4 km away to the south-east.

    How Skilift Weißenbach compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 9.0 km east to Skilift Schlossberg Oberkirnach, with about 2.0 km of runs. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 104 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,170 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,074 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Obereck at 1,178 m, standing 104 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,022 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for late December and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 7 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; a snowy December gave 18 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 26 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Skilift Weißenbach?

    Aim for late December and early March.

    Can you ski at Skilift Weißenbach at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 4 cm).

    What altitude is Skilift Weißenbach at?

    The top is at 1,074 m and the base at 970 m.

    Who is Skilift Weißenbach best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 65% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilift Weißenbach?

    Skilift Schlossberg Oberkirnach, 9.0 km to the east, with about 2.0 km of pistes.
