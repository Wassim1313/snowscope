    About Divcibare

    Divcibare is a small ski area by the standards of the Balkans, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 918 m and reach 1,088 m at the top.

    The mountain at Divcibare

    From top to bottom that makes 170 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 918 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a chairlift.
