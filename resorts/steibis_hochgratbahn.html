    About Steibis

    Steibis is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 725 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 860 m to 1,704 m. Its nearest neighbour is Steibis, 4.5 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Steibis through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,282 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 December and keeps it until about 7 May, roughly 19 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 30 cm and 92 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm, with 51 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 60 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 41 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    How Steibis is laid out

    The vertical drop is 844 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 860 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,704 m. The only lift is a gondola. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Hochgrat, 1,834 m high and 1.1 km south-east of the resort.

    Steibis and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.8 km south-east to Balderschwang, with about 26 km of runs. Only Hochhäderich keeps a solid base longer: about 20 weeks against 19 here.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Steibis?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Can you ski at Steibis at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 31 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Steibis at?

    The top is at 1,704 m and the base at 860 m.

    Who is Steibis best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Steibis?

    Balderschwang, 5.8 km to the south-east, with about 26 km of pistes.
