    About Steibis

    Steibis is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 725 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 860 m to 1,704 m. Its nearest neighbour is Steibis, 4.5 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Steibis is laid out

    The vertical drop is 844 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 860 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,704 m.

    Steibis and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.8 km south-east to Balderschwang, with about 26 km of runs.

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Steibis include Staufner Haus (mountain hut), Alpengasthof Höfle (hotel), Berggasthof Oberstiegalpe (hostel) and Berghüs Schratt (hotel).
