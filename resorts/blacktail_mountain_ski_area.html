    About Blacktail Mountain

    With roughly 22 km of runs, Blacktail Mountain sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,597 m up to 2,030 m.

    The pistes of Blacktail Mountain

    Blacktail Mountain has 23 graded runs adding up to about 22 km. The largest share is blue square, with 11 km (51%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 5.4 km of black or expert runs.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    How Blacktail Mountain is laid out

    433 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,597 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts and 1 rope tow, 4 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Blacktail Mountain (2,058 m), Kerr Mountain (1,859 m) and Wild Bill Mountain (1,831 m).

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,814 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 104 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 55 cm (lean years 22 cm, snowy years 87 cm). For about 3 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 44 cm and 154 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 37 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Blacktail Mountain: common questions

    When does Blacktail Mountain have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Blacktail Mountain at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 55 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Blacktail Mountain?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,030 m; the lowest starts at 1,597 m (433 m of vertical).

    Who is Blacktail Mountain best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 51% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Blacktail Mountain?

    About 22 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
