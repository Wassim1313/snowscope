    About Engelberg Titlis

    Around 42 km of marked runs place Engelberg Titlis among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 139 by length. Skiing here happens between 997 m and 3,040 m above sea level. Engelberg is the next resort, 5.0 km away to the north.

    The mountain at Engelberg Titlis

    2,043 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Below about 1,197 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 3,040 m.
