    How Lyžařský vlek Popelka is laid out

    54 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 797 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 4 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
