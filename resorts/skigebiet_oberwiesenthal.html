    About Oberwiesenthal

    Oberwiesenthal is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 11 km of pistes put it at number 15 of 257. Skiing here happens between 907 m and 1,211 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Boží Dar, 2.6 km to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Oberwiesenthal has 22 graded runs adding up to about 10 km. Most of the length is blue (81%, about 8.5 km). With 81% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Oberwiesenthal is laid out

    The vertical drop is 304 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 907 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 cable car, 1 chairlift, 6 drag lifts and 6 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 6 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,059 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 76 cm. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 27 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 47 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Oberwiesenthal

    What is the best month to ski at Oberwiesenthal?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Oberwiesenthal at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    What altitude is Oberwiesenthal at?

    Between 907 m and 1,211 m, so 304 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Oberwiesenthal?

    Yes: 81% of the graded pistes, about 8.5 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Oberwiesenthal have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
