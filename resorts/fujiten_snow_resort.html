    About Fujiten Snow Resort

    Fujiten Snow Resort is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 164 of 385 in Japan by length. Its lifts climb from 1,290 m to 1,478 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kamui Misaka Ski, 17 km to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 4.9 km in total. Green runs make up the biggest part: 1.8 km, or 37%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 188 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,478 m. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    Fujiten Snow Resort: common questions

    What altitude is Fujiten Snow Resort at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,478 m; the lowest starts at 1,290 m (188 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Fujiten Snow Resort?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Fujiten Snow Resort?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
