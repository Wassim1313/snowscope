    About Lackenhof

    Lackenhof is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 384 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 815 m up to 1,431 m. Maiszinken is the next resort, 6.7 km away to the west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    616 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,015 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
