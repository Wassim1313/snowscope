    About Lackenhof

    Lackenhof is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 384 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 815 m up to 1,431 m. Maiszinken is the next resort, 6.7 km away to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Lackenhof has 16 graded runs adding up to about 14 km. The largest share is blue, with 6.5 km (46%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 2.8 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,123 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 25 December and keeps it until about 2 April, roughly 14 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 30 cm, with 73 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 22 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 56 cm. Most winters land between 16 cm and 94 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    616 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,015 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Ötscher (1,893 m), 2.8 km to the east, about 462 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Lackenhof

    When does Lackenhof have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Is there snow at Lackenhof at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 30 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Lackenhof?

    Between 815 m and 1,431 m, so 616 m of vertical drop.

    Is Lackenhof good for beginners?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 6.5 km, are easy.

    How big is Lackenhof?

    About 15 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
