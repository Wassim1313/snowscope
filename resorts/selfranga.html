    About Selfranga

    Neither huge nor tiny, Selfranga offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 790 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,185 m to 1,355 m. The closest other ski area is Parsenn (4.9 km, west).

    The pistes of Selfranga

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 2.8 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.8 km, or 66%. With 66% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Selfranga today and the next days

    Snow on the ground at Selfranga today: about 3 cm up high. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 19 cm by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 9 cm at mid-mountain.

    How Selfranga is laid out

    The vertical drop is 170 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Parsenn, reach 2,811 m. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 T-bar, 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Pischahorn (2,980 m), 6.9 km to the south-east, about 1,625 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,270 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to late April, about 14 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 29 cm to 58 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 24 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 7 cm in a poor year to 37 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 44 cm, around 21 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 36 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Quick answers

    When does Selfranga have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late April.

    Is there snow at Selfranga at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    What altitude is Selfranga at?

    The top is at 1,355 m and the base at 1,185 m.

    Is Selfranga good for beginners?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Selfranga?

    Parsenn, 4.9 km to the west, with about 81 km of pistes.
