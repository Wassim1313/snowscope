    About Riesneralm

    Riesneralm is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 27 km of pistes put it at number 227 of 1,056. The slopes start at 966 m and reach 1,819 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Planneralm, 8.3 km to the east.

    The pistes of Riesneralm

    Riesneralm has 42 graded runs adding up to about 27 km. The largest share is blue, with 13 km (47%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,392 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 December and keeps it until about 29 April, roughly 19 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 35 cm, with 58 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 70 cm, around 29 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 36 cm and 87 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 42 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    The mountain at Riesneralm

    Skied top to bottom, Riesneralm gives 853 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 966 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 4 moving carpets, 8 lifts in all. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Hochwart (2,301 m), 7.4 km to the south, about 482 m higher than the top lift.

    Riesneralm: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Riesneralm?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Riesneralm at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 35 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Riesneralm?

    Between 966 m and 1,819 m, so 853 m of vertical drop.

    Is Riesneralm suitable for families?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    How big is Riesneralm?

    About 27 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
