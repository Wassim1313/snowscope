    About Riedbergerhorn

    Compact and local, Riedbergerhorn has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,050 m to 1,417 m. Its nearest neighbour is Balderschwang, 1.9 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Riedbergerhorn through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,234 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 3 January and keeps it until about 5 May, roughly 18 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 36 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Most winters land between 26 cm and 85 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 28 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 47 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 54 cm.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 2.6 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 1.8 km (69%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How Riedbergerhorn is laid out

    367 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 button lift, 4 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Hochgrat, 1,834 m high and 5.9 km north-west of the resort.

    Riedbergerhorn and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 1.9 km west to Balderschwang, with about 26 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Grasgehren usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 23 weeks, against 18 here. For higher slopes, Steibis climbs to 1,704 m.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Riedbergerhorn?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early May.

    How much snow does Riedbergerhorn have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 28 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Riedbergerhorn?

    The top is at 1,417 m and the base at 1,050 m.

    Who is Riedbergerhorn best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Riedbergerhorn?

    Balderschwang, 1.9 km to the west, with about 26 km of pistes.
