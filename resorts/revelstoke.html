    About Revelstoke

    Only 4 of the 53 ski areas of Canada West are larger than Revelstoke, which has some 84 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 514 m to 2,224 m.

    The pistes of Revelstoke

    In all, 83 marked runs cover roughly 84 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 29 km, or 35%. With 30% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 25 km.

    Snow at Revelstoke through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,369 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early November to late May or later, about 30 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 178 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 130 cm and 225 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 25 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 15 March, with about 191 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 131 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 170 cm in the best years.

    How Revelstoke is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,710 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Because the lifts start as low as 514 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Mount Cartier (2,591 m), 7.0 km to the south-east, about 367 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Revelstoke?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at Revelstoke at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 131 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Revelstoke?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026.

    How high is Revelstoke?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,224 m; the lowest starts at 514 m (1,710 m of vertical).

    Is Revelstoke good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 25 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.
