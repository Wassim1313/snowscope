    The snow at Revelstoke, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Revelstoke (about 1,369 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 131 cm of cover (112 cm to 152 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 171 cm, between 155 cm and 199 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 191 cm.

    100 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 100 % by 1 January.

    2025-26 was the big one (276 cm); 2000-01 the lean one (146 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late October to early June, about 222 days.

    Revelstoke: quick answers

    Does Revelstoke have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 131 cm at Christmas, and 100 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 65 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Revelstoke?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 191 cm). For a sure base, late October to early June is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Revelstoke get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 191 cm in mid-March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 276 cm (2025-26), the thinnest 146 cm (2000-01).

    How high is Revelstoke?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,224 m; the lowest starts at 514 m (1,710 m of vertical).

    How big is Revelstoke?

    About 84 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
