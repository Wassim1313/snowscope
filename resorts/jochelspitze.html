    About Jöchelspitze

    Neither huge nor tiny, Jöchelspitze offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 655 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,211 m up to 1,762 m. Gföllberglift is the next resort, 2.8 km away to the south-west.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,486 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 22 weeks of good cover. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 4 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 49 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 17 cm, snowy years 75 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 69 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Most winters land between 67 cm and 148 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 111 cm.

    What there is to ski at Jöchelspitze

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 5.1 km in total. Most of the length is blue (65%, about 3.3 km). With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 551 m: runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The skyline is dominated by Holzgauer Wetterspitze, 2,895 m high and 7.2 km south of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Jöchelspitze?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Jöchelspitze at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 49 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Jöchelspitze?

    Between 1,211 m and 1,762 m, so 551 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Jöchelspitze?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 3.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Jöchelspitze have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
