    About Кукисвумчорр

    Neither huge nor tiny, Кукисвумчорр offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 41 of 86 in Russia). Skiing here happens between 376 m and 874 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Большой Вудъявр (7.0 km, south).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 3.7 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.1 km, or 57%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The mountain at Кукисвумчорр

    Skied top to bottom, Кукисвумчорр gives 498 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 3 T-bars and 1 rope tow, 4 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
