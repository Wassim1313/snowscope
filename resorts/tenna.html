    About Tenna

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Tenna one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 1,656 m and reach 1,792 m at the top. Sarn Heinzenberg is the next resort, 4.6 km away to the south-east.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 14 km north-west to Laax, with about 152 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Laax usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 28 weeks, against 25 here. Laax reaches 2,974 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,724 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 64 cm and 125 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 93 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 2 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 28 February, with about 103 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm, with 75 cm in the best years.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Tenna has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.4 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.4 km (100%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Tenna hold around 14 cm of snow today. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 19 cm by Wednesday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 27 cm at mid-mountain).

    Lifts and terrain

    136 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,656 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Laax, for one, climbs to 2,974 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Piz Fess (Bös Fess) at 2,880 m, standing 1,088 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Berghotel Alpenblick (hotel), Ferienwohnung (www.tennaferien.ch) (chalet), Rössli (hotel) and Hotel Reich (hotel).

    Questions about Tenna

    What is the best month to ski at Tenna?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Tenna at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 54 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Tenna at?

    Between 1,656 m and 1,792 m, so 136 m of vertical drop.

    Is Tenna suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Tenna?

    Laax, 14 km to the north-west, with about 152 km of pistes.
