    About Hütegglift Weerberg

    Hütegglift Weerberg is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,253 m to 1,511 m. Kolsassberg is the next resort, 2.8 km away to the west.
