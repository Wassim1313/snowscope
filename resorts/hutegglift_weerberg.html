    About Hütegglift Weerberg

    Hütegglift Weerberg is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,253 m to 1,511 m. Kolsassberg is the next resort, 2.8 km away to the west.

    How Hütegglift Weerberg compares nearby

    For more terrain, Hochzillertal-Hochfügen (11 km east) has about 82 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Kellerjoch does better, with about 20 weeks of a good base instead of 6. Hochzillertal-Hochfügen reaches 2,374 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How Hütegglift Weerberg is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 258 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Wildofen at 2,553 m, standing 1,042 m above the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Hütegglift Weerberg has roughly 16 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 26 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 13 cm at mid-mountain.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,382 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 3 February and lasts to 18 March: about 6 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 26 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 19 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 31 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 21 cm and 46 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early May. The snow is usually deepest around 24 February, with about 36 cm.

    Hütegglift Weerberg: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Hütegglift Weerberg?

    Aim for late February and early May; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    How much snow does Hütegglift Weerberg have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 19 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Hütegglift Weerberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,511 m; the lowest starts at 1,253 m (258 m of vertical).

    Is Hütegglift Weerberg good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Hütegglift Weerberg?

    Hochzillertal-Hochfügen, 11 km to the east, with about 82 km of pistes.
