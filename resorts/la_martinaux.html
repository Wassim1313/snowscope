    About La Martinaux

    About 2.0 km of pistes make La Martinaux one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,485 m to 1,681 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les Giettes, 7.1 km to the west.

    Snow at La Martinaux through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,583 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 27 November and keeps it until about 25 May, roughly 26 weeks. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 118 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 90 cm and 182 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 146 cm, around 5 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 76 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 32 cm, snowy years 121 cm).

    The pistes of La Martinaux

    The piste map lists 3 graded runs, about 2.2 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 65%. With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at La Martinaux today: about 41 cm up high. 40 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 27 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday. An ordinary winter would have around 34 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    196 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,485 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Grand Muveran, 3,051 m high and 6.6 km east of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Cabane des Martinaux (mountain hut), Auberge de Morcles (guesthouse), Cabane de la Tourche (mountain hut) and Grand Hôtel des Bains (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about La Martinaux

    When does La Martinaux have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at La Martinaux at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 76 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is La Martinaux at?

    The top is at 1,681 m and the base at 1,485 m.

    Is La Martinaux good for beginners?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near La Martinaux?

    Champéry, 17 km to the west, with about 82 km of pistes.
