    About Todtnauberg

    Todtnauberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 355 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 981 m and 1,374 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Todtnauberg, 0.7 km to the south-west.

    The mountain at Todtnauberg

    From top to bottom that makes 393 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,181 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 4 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Feldberg, 1,494 m high and 4.4 km north-east of the resort.

    Snow at Todtnauberg through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,178 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 16 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 60 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 36 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and early March.

    Questions about Todtnauberg

    When is the best time to ski at Todtnauberg?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    How much snow does Todtnauberg have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Todtnauberg at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,374 m; the lowest starts at 981 m (393 m of vertical).

    Who is Todtnauberg best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 88% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Todtnauberg?

    About 17 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
