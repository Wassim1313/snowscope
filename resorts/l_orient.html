    About L'Orient

    Neither huge nor tiny, L'Orient offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 657 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,027 m and reach 1,287 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Le Brassus, 3.0 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    L'Orient has 6 graded runs adding up to about 4.8 km. Black runs make up the biggest part: 2.2 km, or 45%. Good skiers will like it here; 45% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 2.2 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at L'Orient

    From top to bottom that makes 260 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Mont Tendre, 1,679 m high and 5.2 km east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,157 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 10 cm; a snowy December gave 42 cm, a dry one 0 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 20 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 47 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Questions about L'Orient

    When is the best time to ski at L'Orient?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Is there snow at L'Orient at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at L'Orient?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,287 m; the lowest starts at 1,027 m (260 m of vertical).

    Does L'Orient have steep runs?

    Yes: about 2.2 km of its graded pistes (45%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near L'Orient?

    L'Abbaye, 7.6 km to the north-east, with about 7.0 km of pistes.
