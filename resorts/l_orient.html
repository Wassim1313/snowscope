    About L'Orient

    Neither huge nor tiny, L'Orient offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 657 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,027 m and reach 1,287 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Le Brassus, 3.0 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Questions about L'Orient

    How high do the lifts go at L'Orient?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,287 m; the lowest starts at 1,027 m (260 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near L'Orient?

    L'Abbaye, 7.6 km to the north-east, with about 7.0 km of pistes.

    How big is L'Orient?
