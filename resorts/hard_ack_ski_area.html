    About Hard'Ack

    Hard'Ack has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 189 m and 235 m above sea level. Smugglers' Notch Resort is the next resort, 36 km away to the south-east.

    Other resorts close by

    Smugglers' Notch Resort has the longer snow season: around 16 weeks of solid cover, compared with 9 at Hard'Ack. The highest nearby is Jay Peak Resort, topping out at 1,166 m.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 212 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 January and lasts to 23 March: about 9 weeks. A normal season tops out near 45 cm, around 16 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 42 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 18 cm to 70 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    The mountain at Hard'Ack

    From top to bottom that makes 46 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 189 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Jay Peak Resort, reach 1,166 m. The only lift is a rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bellevue Hill at 401 m, standing 166 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 2 hotels: Hampton Inn St. Albans and La Quinta Inn & Suites St. Albans.

    Quick answers

    When does Hard'Ack have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    Can you ski at Hard'Ack at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Hard'Ack?

    Its highest lift reaches 235 m; the lowest starts at 189 m (46 m of vertical).

    How big is Hard'Ack?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
