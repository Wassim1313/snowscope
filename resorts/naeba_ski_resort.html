    About Naeba

    Naeba is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 19 km of pistes put it at number 18 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 882 m up to 1,785 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tashiro Area, 6.8 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Naeba

    Naeba has 29 graded runs adding up to about 19 km. Most of the length is red (41%, about 7.9 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.7 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    903 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 882 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 15 lifts break down into 3 gondolas and 12 chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    Naeba: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Naeba?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,785 m; the lowest starts at 882 m (903 m of vertical).

    Who is Naeba best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 41% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Naeba have?

    About 19 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
