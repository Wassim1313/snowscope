    About Święta Góra

    Święta Góra is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 530 m to 699 m. The closest other ski area is Žacléř (11 km, south-west).

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 4 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Święta Góra. The last seven days brought 6 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 6 cm at mid-mountain.

    The mountain at Święta Góra

    The vertical drop is 169 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 530 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a drag lift.

    How much snow Święta Góra usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 614 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 49 cm. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 26 February. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 34 cm in a big one.
