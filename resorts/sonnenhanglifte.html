    About Sonnenhanglifte

    Sonnenhanglifte is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 3.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,043 m and 1,145 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Spieserlifte, 1.8 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 2.9 km in total. Most of the length is blue (63%, about 1.8 km). With 63% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Sonnenhanglifte is laid out

    The vertical drop is 102 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Tannheim reaches 1,803 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Ponten, 2,045 m high and 6.0 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 7 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,094 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 20 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 35 cm). A normal season tops out near 34 cm, around 7 February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 16 cm to 56 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Sonnenhanglifte?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Sonnenhanglifte at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Sonnenhanglifte?

    Between 1,043 m and 1,145 m, so 102 m of vertical drop.

    Is Sonnenhanglifte suitable for families?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Sonnenhanglifte?

    Hindelang Oberjoch, 2.9 km to the south, with about 22 km of pistes.
