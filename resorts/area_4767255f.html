    About Горнолыжный центр "Лоза"

    Compact and local, Горнолыжный центр "Лоза" has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Russia. Its lifts climb from 170 m to 227 m.

    What there is to ski at Горнолыжный центр "Лоза"

    Горнолыжный центр "Лоза" has 4 graded runs adding up to about 1.1 km. Most of the length is blue (35%, about 0.4 km). With 69% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Горнолыжный центр "Лоза"

    Skied top to bottom, Горнолыжный центр "Лоза" gives 57 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 227 m is the third highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 1 drag lift. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    How high do the lifts go at Горнолыжный центр "Лоза"?
