    About Moléson

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Moléson sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,109 m to 1,975 m. Its nearest neighbour is La Chia, 3.6 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Moléson

    Skied top to bottom, Moléson gives 866 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 1 cable car, 4 funiculars, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Dent de Lys (2,014 m), Le Moléson (2,002 m) and Crête du Moléson (1,938 m).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 10 marked runs cover roughly 9.0 km. The largest share is blue, with 5.2 km (58%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,542 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 55 cm to 160 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 111 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 113 cm, around 7 April. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 64 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 18 cm, snowy years 108 cm).

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Moléson include Plan-Francey (hostel), Cabane des Clés (mountain hut), Le Gros Plané (mountain hut) and Chalet Le Pliano (chalet). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Moléson

    What is the best month to ski at Moléson?

    Aim for early March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Moléson at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 64 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Moléson?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,975 m; the lowest starts at 1,109 m (866 m of vertical).

    Is Moléson good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 5.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Moléson?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
