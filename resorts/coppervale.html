    About Coppervale

    Coppervale is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,604 m and reach 1,774 m at the top.

    The pistes of Coppervale

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 2.2 km. The largest share is green circle, with 1.0 km (46%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Coppervale hold around 31 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 5 cm at mid-mountain).

    The mountain at Coppervale

    The vertical drop is 170 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,604 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hamilton Mountain at 2,237 m, standing 463 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,689 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 16 January and keeps it until about 27 March, roughly 10 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 46 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 12 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 8 cm to 82 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 49 cm). For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Coppervale?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Can you ski at Coppervale at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Coppervale at?

    Between 1,604 m and 1,774 m, so 170 m of vertical drop.

    Is Coppervale suitable for families?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Coppervale?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
