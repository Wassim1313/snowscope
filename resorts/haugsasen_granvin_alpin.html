    How much snow Haugsåsen (Granvin Alpin) usually gets

    At about 470 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 118 cm and 286 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 115 cm, with 169 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 224 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 24 weeks. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 242 cm.

    Questions about Haugsåsen (Granvin Alpin)

    When does Haugsåsen (Granvin Alpin) have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Haugsåsen (Granvin Alpin) have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 115 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
