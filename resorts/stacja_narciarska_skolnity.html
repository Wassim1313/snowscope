    About Stacja Narciarska Skolnity

    Neither huge nor tiny, Stacja Narciarska Skolnity offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 176 of 312 in the Carpathians). The slopes start at 465 m and reach 664 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Partecznik (1.3 km, east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 1.9 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.3 km, or 69%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    199 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 465 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet, 2 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
