    About Shiga Kogen Hasuike

    Neither huge nor tiny, Shiga Kogen Hasuike offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 217 of 385 in Japan). Its lifts climb from 1,504 m to 1,574 m. The closest other ski area is Shiga Kogen Maruike (0.3 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Shiga Kogen Hasuike

    Shiga Kogen Hasuike has 10 graded runs adding up to about 3.5 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 3.0 km, or 85%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 12 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Shiga Kogen Hasuike. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 70 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,504 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Shiga Kogen Hasuike at?

    The top is at 1,574 m and the base at 1,504 m.

    Is Shiga Kogen Hasuike good for beginners?

    Yes: 85% of the graded pistes, about 3.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Shiga Kogen Hasuike?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
