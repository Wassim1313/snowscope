    About Marela

    Marela is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 468 m to 897 m. Its nearest neighbour is Celjska koca, 26 km east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Marela compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 37 km north-west to Krvavec, with about 16 km of runs. Krvavec has the longer snow season: around 4 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Marela. The highest nearby is Krvavec, topping out at 1,963 m.

    What there is to ski at Marela

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 2.3 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 52%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 682 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 26 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, with 15 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 4 cm, around 30 January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    How Marela is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Marela gives 429 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 468 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 2 T-bars, 3 lifts in all. Above the ski area rises Črni vrh (1,204 m), 7.2 km to the north, about 307 m higher than the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Gostišče Kum (guesthouse), Planinski dom na Zasavski Sveti gori (mountain hut), CŠOD Prvine (hostel) and Prenočišča Vidrgar (hostel).

    Marela: common questions

    When does Marela have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Can you ski at Marela at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Marela at?

    Between 468 m and 897 m, so 429 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Marela?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Marela?

    Krvavec, 37 km to the north-west, with about 16 km of pistes.
