    About Skagahøgdi skisenter

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Skagahøgdi skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 439 m to 873 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gol Skisenter, 0.5 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Skagahøgdi skisenter

    The piste map lists 15 graded runs, about 9.1 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.7 km, or 52%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 25 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Skagahøgdi skisenter. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 38 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    How Skagahøgdi skisenter is laid out

    The vertical drop is 434 m: runs of a moderate length. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hemsedal skisenter reaches 1,439 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Above the ski area rises Syningen (1,047 m), 7.3 km to the west, about 174 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Skagahøgdi skisenter usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 656 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 59 cm to 106 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 75 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 51 cm, from 34 cm in a poor year to 82 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 12 March, with about 86 cm.

    Questions about Skagahøgdi skisenter

    When does Skagahøgdi skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Skagahøgdi skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 51 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Skagahøgdi skisenter?

    The top is at 873 m and the base at 439 m.

    Can beginners ski at Skagahøgdi skisenter?

    Yes: 86% of the graded pistes, about 7.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Skagahøgdi skisenter?

    Hemsedal skisenter, 26 km to the north-west, with about 48 km of pistes.
