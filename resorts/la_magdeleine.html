    How La Magdeleine is laid out

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Petit Tournalin (3,207 m), Becca Trecaré (3,032 m) and Becca di Nana (3,010 m).

    How much snow La Magdeleine usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,645 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 December and keeps it until about 13 May, roughly 21 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 56 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Most winters land between 42 cm and 80 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 35 cm; a snowy December gave 56 cm, a dry one 19 cm. A normal season tops out near 65 cm, around 7 March.

    La Magdeleine: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at La Magdeleine?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at La Magdeleine at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 35 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
