    About Ifen

    With roughly 21 km of runs, Ifen sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,257 m to 2,019 m. Its nearest neighbour is Heuberg Arena/Walmendingerhorn, 2.9 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,638 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 55 cm (lean years 23 cm, snowy years 86 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 10 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 100 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 128 cm, around 3 March. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 76 cm to 163 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Runs by difficulty

    Ifen has 32 graded runs adding up to about 21 km. The largest share is blue, with 10 km (50%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.2 km.

    The mountain at Ifen

    From top to bottom that makes 762 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 1 chairlift and 1 button lift, 4 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The skyline is dominated by Widderstein, 2,533 m high and 7.3 km south of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf: about 108 km of pistes, 6.9 km to the east. Its snow is the most reliable of the group: a 30 cm base lasts about 25 weeks in a typical winter, longer than at any neighbour listed.

    Questions about Ifen

    When does Ifen have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Ifen have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 55 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Ifen?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,019 m; the lowest starts at 1,257 m (762 m of vertical).

    Is Ifen suitable for families?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 10 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Ifen?

    Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf, 6.9 km to the east, with about 108 km of pistes.
