    About Gresse en Vercors

    Gresse en Vercors is a mid-sized ski area with about 13 km of pistes, number 411 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,247 m up to 1,696 m. The closest other ski area is Station du Col de Rousset (11 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 19 graded runs, about 13 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.2 km, or 40%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.7 km.

    The mountain at Gresse en Vercors

    449 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 8 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Grand Veymont (2,341 m), 2.5 km to the south-west, about 645 m higher than the top lift.

    How Gresse en Vercors compares nearby

    For more terrain, Villard de Lans (15 km north) has about 56 km of pistes against 13 km here. For more reliable snow, Les Signaraux usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 18 weeks, against 7 here. Villard de Lans reaches 2,055 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    This week's snow

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 7 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,472 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 81 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 38 cm, around 16 February. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 87 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Gresse en Vercors have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    How much snow does Gresse en Vercors have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 16 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Gresse en Vercors?

    The top is at 1,696 m and the base at 1,247 m.

    Is Gresse en Vercors good for beginners?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 6.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Gresse en Vercors?

    Villard de Lans, 15 km to the north, with about 56 km of pistes.
