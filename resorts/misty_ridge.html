    About Misty Ridge

    Compact and local, Misty Ridge has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Canada West. Skiing here happens between 596 m and 668 m above sea level.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.0 km to ski. Most of the length is blue square (52%, about 0.5 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The mountain at Misty Ridge

    From top to bottom that makes 72 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 596 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a T-bar.

    The snow right now

    Misty Ridge has no snow cover yet today. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (8 cm).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 632 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 8 cm to 47 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and mid-January. A normal season tops out near 24 cm, around 1 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 38 cm).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Misty Ridge?

    Aim for early January and mid-January.

    Is there snow at Misty Ridge at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Misty Ridge?

    Between 596 m and 668 m, so 72 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Misty Ridge?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 0.5 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Misty Ridge have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
