    About Embach

    Neither huge nor tiny, Embach offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 741 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,009 m and reach 1,290 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Goldegger Buchberg, 6.8 km to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 3.0 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.5 km (52%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Embach today: about 35 cm up high. The last seven days brought 14 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 10 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (12 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 281 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Bernkogel (2,325 m), 5.6 km to the south-east, about 1,035 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,150 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 January and lasts to 7 March: about 6 weeks. A normal season tops out near 34 cm, around 16 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 21 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 38 cm, a dry one 4 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 19 cm and 48 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 26 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and mid-February.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Embach?

    Aim for late January and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Embach have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 21 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Embach?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,290 m; the lowest starts at 1,009 m (281 m of vertical).

    Is Embach good for beginners?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Embach?

    Hochkönig, 11 km to the north, with about 82 km of pistes.
