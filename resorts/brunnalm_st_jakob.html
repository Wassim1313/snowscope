    About Brunnalm / St. Jakob

    Brunnalm / St. Jakob is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 280 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,387 m up to 2,682 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Virgen, 13 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,295 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,387 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,682 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Weiße Spitze (2,962 m), 3.0 km to the south, about 280 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Brunnalm / St. Jakob usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,034 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 November and keeps it until about 26 May, roughly 27 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 64 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 101 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 110 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 68 cm and 133 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 118 cm, around 21 March.

    Brunnalm / St. Jakob: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Brunnalm / St. Jakob?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Brunnalm / St. Jakob at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 64 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Brunnalm / St. Jakob at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,682 m; the lowest starts at 1,387 m (1,295 m of vertical).

    Who is Brunnalm / St. Jakob best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 47% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Brunnalm / St. Jakob have?

    About 22 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
