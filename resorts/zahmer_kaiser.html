    About Zahmer Kaiser

    Compact and local, Zahmer Kaiser has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 692 m and 789 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Sachrang, 6.2 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Zahmer Kaiser usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 740 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 January and keeps it until about 9 March, roughly 7 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 12 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 40 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 13 cm and 70 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 20 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 46 cm in a big one.

    How Zahmer Kaiser is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 97 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 692 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gamshalt at 2,289 m, standing 1,500 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Wildauerhof (hotel), Hotel Garni Tirol (hotel), Brandauerhof (hotel) and Ausserfeldhof (chalet).

    Zahmer Kaiser: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Zahmer Kaiser?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    How much snow does Zahmer Kaiser have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Zahmer Kaiser?

    Between 692 m and 789 m, so 97 m of vertical drop.

    Is there a larger resort near Zahmer Kaiser?

    Hochkössen, 11 km to the east, with about 15 km of pistes.

    How big is Zahmer Kaiser?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
