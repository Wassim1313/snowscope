    About Nad Nádražím

    Neither huge nor tiny, Nad Nádražím offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 69 of 257 in Central Europe). The slopes start at 790 m and reach 906 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Železná Ruda (0.9 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 3.7 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 2.1 km (57%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 848 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 28 cm, around 10 February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 41 cm). Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 65 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    Lifts and terrain

    116 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 990 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 3 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Nad Nádražím?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    How much snow does Nad Nádražím have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    How high is Nad Nádražím?

    Between 790 m and 906 m, so 116 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Nad Nádražím?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    How big is Nad Nádražím?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
