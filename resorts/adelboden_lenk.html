    The snow at Adelboden, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Adelboden (1,728 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 72 cm, with a normal range of 43 cm–91 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 132 cm; the middle half of winters range from 110 cm to 157 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 145 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 50 % of winters, and by 1 January in 83 %.

    1998-99 was the big one (279 cm); 2010-11 the lean one (119 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late May, about 188 days.

    Adelboden: quick answers

    Does Adelboden have snow over Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 72 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 83 % of 46 winters. Right now: 32 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Adelboden?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 145 cm). For a sure base, late November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Adelboden get?

    About 145 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 119 cm in the leanest winter (2010-11) and 279 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high do the lifts go at Adelboden?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,193 m; the lowest starts at 1,263 m (930 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Adelboden have?

    About 83 km of pistes and 21 lifts.

    About Adelboden

    Adelboden is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 83 km of pistes put it at number 64 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,263 m up to 2,193 m. Its nearest neighbour is Adelboden, 4.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.
