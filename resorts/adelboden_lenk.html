    About Adelboden

    Adelboden is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 83 km of pistes put it at number 64 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,263 m up to 2,193 m. Its nearest neighbour is Adelboden, 4.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Adelboden

    The piste map lists 147 graded runs, about 83 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 44 km, or 53%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How much snow Adelboden usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,728 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 November and keeps it until about 29 May, roughly 26 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 88 cm and 183 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 72 cm, from 28 cm in a poor year to 112 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 15 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 137 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 145 cm, around 25 March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 930 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 1 cable car, 5 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift, 3 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Wildstrubel (3,244 m), 7.1 km to the south, about 1,051 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Adelboden have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Adelboden have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 72 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Adelboden open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Adelboden?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,193 m; the lowest starts at 1,263 m (930 m of vertical).

    Is Adelboden suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 44 km, are easy.
