    About Szklana Góra Ski

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Szklana Góra Ski one of the smaller places to ski in the Carpathians. Skiing here happens between 391 m and 559 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Skomielna Czarna, 9.9 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Szklana Góra Ski is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Szklana Góra Ski gives 168 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 391 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Szklana Góra Ski?

    Its highest lift reaches 559 m; the lowest starts at 391 m (168 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Szklana Góra Ski have?
