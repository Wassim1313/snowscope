    The snow at Grand Tourmalet, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Grand Tourmalet (1,962 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 35 cm, with a normal range of 16 cm–53 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 82 cm (50 cm–109 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 85 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 28 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 39 %.

    Best winter on record: 2012-13, peaking at 198 cm. Worst: 1992-93, which never passed 45 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until late April in a normal winter, roughly 137 days.

    So far this winter: 20 cm on 27 November (usual: 12 cm).

    About Grand Tourmalet

    Only 5 of the 49 ski areas of the Pyrenees are larger than Grand Tourmalet, which has some 54 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 1,459 m to 2,464 m. The closest other ski area is Saint-Lary (13 km, south-east).

    What there is to ski at Grand Tourmalet

    Counting every graded piste, there are 80 runs and around 54 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (56%, about 30 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 1.8 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How much snow Grand Tourmalet usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,962 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 17 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 43 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 35 cm, with 80 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 24 cm to 136 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 85 cm, around 4 March.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,005 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,459 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,464 m is the third highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 3 cable cars, 14 chairlifts, 5 button lifts, 4 drag lifts and 3 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Pic de Néouvielle (3,091 m), 7.8 km to the south, about 627 m higher than the top lift.

That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 20 cm at mid-mountain). The 2026/27 season at Grand Tourmalet is announced to start on 5 December 2026 and to run until 29 March 2027.
