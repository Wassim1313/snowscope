    About Pyhätunturi

    Around 22 km of marked runs place Pyhätunturi among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 28 by length. Its lifts climb from 194 m to 489 m. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Luosto, 7.2 km to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 30 graded runs, about 15 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 8.6 km, or 56%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at Pyhätunturi

    The vertical drop is 295 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 3 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet, 13 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Ukko-Luosto (514 m), Huttutunturi (418 m) and Kapusta (398 m).

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 342 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 22 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 52 cm and 78 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 67 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 60 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 42 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 53 cm in the best years.

    Pyhätunturi: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Pyhätunturi?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Pyhätunturi at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 42 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Pyhätunturi at?

    The top is at 489 m and the base at 194 m.

    Can beginners ski at Pyhätunturi?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 10 km, are easy.

    How big is Pyhätunturi?

    About 22 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
