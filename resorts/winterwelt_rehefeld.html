    About Winterwelt Rehefeld

    Neither huge nor tiny, Winterwelt Rehefeld offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 142 of 257 in Central Europe). The slopes start at 689 m and reach 846 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Bouřňák, 4.3 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 2.5 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.3 km, or 52%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 157 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 689 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 chairlift and 1 rope tow.

    How much snow Winterwelt Rehefeld usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 768 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and early March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 36 cm. A normal season tops out near 13 cm, around 31 January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 4 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 26 cm).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Winterwelt Rehefeld?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    How much snow does Winterwelt Rehefeld have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Winterwelt Rehefeld?

    The top is at 846 m and the base at 689 m.

    Is Winterwelt Rehefeld suitable for families?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Winterwelt Rehefeld?
