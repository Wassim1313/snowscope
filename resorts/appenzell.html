    About Appenzell

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Appenzell sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 812 m up to 1,164 m. The closest other ski area is Ebenalp-Horn (3.8 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Appenzell

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 5.5 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 3.0 km, or 55%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Appenzell

    From top to bottom that makes 352 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 812 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,164 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Höch Nideri (2,246 m), 7.6 km to the south-west, about 1,082 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Appenzell through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 988 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Most winters land between 8 cm and 51 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm; a snowy December gave 28 cm, a dry one 2 cm. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 20 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 9 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then.

    Questions about Appenzell

    When does Appenzell have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Appenzell have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Appenzell?

    Between 812 m and 1,164 m, so 352 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Appenzell best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 55% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Appenzell?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
