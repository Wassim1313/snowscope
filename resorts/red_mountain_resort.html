    About Red

    About 69 km of pistes make Red one of the ten biggest ski areas of Canada West (number 10). The slopes start at 1,155 m and reach 2,037 m at the top.

    The pistes of Red

    Counting every graded piste, there are 83 runs and around 68 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 25 km (37%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 20 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Red

    The vertical drop is 882 m: enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 6 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet, 8 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Old Glory Mountain (2,376 m), 7.1 km to the north-west, about 339 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Red usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,596 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May, about 27 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 97 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 132 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 92 cm and 171 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 145 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 155 cm, around 30 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 18 weeks.

    Quick answers

    When does Red have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Red at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 97 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Red?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Red?

    The top is at 2,037 m and the base at 1,155 m.

    Is Red good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 20 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.
