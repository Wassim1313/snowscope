    About "Erste Berg" Masserberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, "Erste Berg" Masserberg offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 161 of 257 in Central Europe). Its lifts climb from 741 m to 821 m. Its nearest neighbour is Heubach, 3.4 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    "Erste Berg" Masserberg and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Silbersattel Steinach: about 3.0 km of pistes, 15 km to the south-east. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    The mountain at "Erste Berg" Masserberg

    From top to bottom that makes 80 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 741 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a button lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 781 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 23 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 13 cm, around 24 January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 35 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-December and early January.
