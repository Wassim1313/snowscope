    About Zuberec

    With some 5.0 km of runs, Zuberec belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. Skiing here happens between 744 m and 976 m above sea level. Ski Uhliská is the next resort, 6.6 km away to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Zuberec has 6 graded runs adding up to about 4.7 km. Most of the length is blue (59%, about 2.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Zuberec gives 232 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 944 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 976 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
