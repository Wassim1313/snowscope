    What 46 winters say about Isola 2000

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Isola 2000 (2,214 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 29 cm; half of the winters sit between 9 cm and 40 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 55 cm, between 30 cm and 67 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 55 cm.

    7 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 20 % by 1 January.

    2013-14 was the big one (129 cm); 2006-07 the lean one (14 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April, about 136 days.

    Today's 5 cm compares with a usual 4 cm for 19 November.

    About Isola 2000

    Isola 2000 is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 55 km of pistes put it at number 107 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,828 m and 2,601 m above sea level. La Colmiane is the next resort, 14 km away to the south-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Isola 2000 has 65 graded runs adding up to about 55 km. The largest share is blue, with 25 km (45%). With 63% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. 1.3 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 773 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 1,828 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its top at 2,601 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 20 lifts break down into 1 funicular, 2 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 8 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Rocca della Paur, 2,972 m high and 7.8 km north-east of the resort.

    How much snow Isola 2000 usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,214 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 December and lasts to 3 April: about 13 weeks. Most winters land between 12 cm and 85 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 55 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 29 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 61 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 22 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.
