    What 46 winters say about Isola 2000

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Isola 2000 (2,214 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 29 cm; half of the winters sit between 9 cm and 40 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 55 cm, between 30 cm and 67 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 55 cm.

    7 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 20 % by 1 January.

    2013-14 was the big one (129 cm); 2006-07 the lean one (14 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April, about 136 days.

    Questions about Isola 2000

    Does Isola 2000 have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 29 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 20 % of 46 winters. Right now: 52 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Isola 2000?

    February: the snow peaks at about 55 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April.

    How deep does the snow get at Isola 2000?

    About 55 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-February); between 14 cm in the leanest winter (2006-07) and 129 cm in the biggest (2013-14).

    How high is Isola 2000?

    The top is at 2,601 m and the base at 1,828 m.

    How big is Isola 2000?

    About 55 km of pistes and 20 lifts.

    About Isola 2000

    Isola 2000 is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 55 km of pistes put it at number 107 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,828 m and 2,601 m above sea level. La Colmiane is the next resort, 14 km away to the south-east.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Isola 2000 has roughly 52 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 18 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 22 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 773 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 1,828 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its top at 2,601 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
