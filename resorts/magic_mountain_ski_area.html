    About Magic Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Magic Mountain offers about 12 km of pistes (number 45 of 93 in US Northeast). The slopes start at 420 m and reach 869 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Timber Ridge (2.5 km, east).

    The pistes of Magic Mountain

    Magic Mountain has 23 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 5.8 km, or 47%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 1.2 km of difficult runs.

    How much snow Magic Mountain usually gets

    At about 644 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 January and lasts to 26 March: about 8 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 13 cm to 76 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 19 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 40 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 42 cm, around 1 March.

    The mountain at Magic Mountain

    From top to bottom that makes 449 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 420 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Glebe Mountain (891 m), Turkey Mountain (675 m) and Timber Ridge Mountain (635 m).

    Magic Mountain: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Magic Mountain?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does Magic Mountain have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 19 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Magic Mountain?

    Between 420 m and 869 m, so 449 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Magic Mountain?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 5.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Magic Mountain?

    Stratton, 15 km to the south-west, with about 72 km of pistes.
