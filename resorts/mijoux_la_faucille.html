    About Mijoux

    Mijoux is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 385 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 997 m and reach 1,540 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Lajoux (2.8 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 53 graded runs, about 15 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 7.8 km (53%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Mijoux gives 543 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 997 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 2 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Colomby de Gex at 1,689 m, standing 149 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Mijoux usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,268 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 January and keeps it until about 10 March, roughly 7 weeks. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 86 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 20 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 90 cm). By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 41 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Mijoux?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    Is there snow at Mijoux at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Mijoux at?

    Between 997 m and 1,540 m, so 543 m of vertical drop.

    Is Mijoux suitable for families?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Mijoux have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
