    About Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,013 m and 1,175 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Klug Lifte (5.4 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte

    Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte has 12 graded runs adding up to about 4.8 km. Most of the length is blue (88%, about 4.2 km). With 88% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 162 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Koralpe reaches 2,065 m. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Schwarzkogel at 1,550 m, standing 375 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,094 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 28 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 15 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 40 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte?

    The top is at 1,175 m and the base at 1,013 m.

    Can beginners ski at Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte?

    Yes: 88% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte?
