    About Luossavaarabacken

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Luossavaarabacken sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 569 m to 724 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 3.2 km. Most of the length is red (72%, about 2.3 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Luossavaarabacken today and the next days

    Snow on the ground at Luossavaarabacken today: about 13 cm up high. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 42 cm by now.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 155 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Kiirunavaara (733 m), Luossavaara (726 m) and Kurravaara (604 m).

    Snow at Luossavaarabacken through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 646 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 November and keeps it until about 18 May, roughly 26 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 80 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Most winters land between 60 cm and 96 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 75 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 57 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 38 cm in a poor year to 69 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Luossavaarabacken

    When does Luossavaarabacken have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Luossavaarabacken have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 57 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Luossavaarabacken at?

    The top is at 724 m and the base at 569 m.

    Who is Luossavaarabacken best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 72% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Luossavaarabacken have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
