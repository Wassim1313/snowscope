    About Garmisch-Classic

    Garmisch-Classic is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 38 km of pistes put it at number 163 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 727 m up to 2,047 m. Eckbauer is the next resort, 4.4 km away to the north-east.

    How Garmisch-Classic is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,320 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 927 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 18 lifts: 2 cable cars, 2 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 7 T-bars, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Zugspitze, 2,962 m high and 7.8 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,387 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 35 cm, with 57 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 49 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 64 cm, around 29 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 41 cm and 89 cm.

    What there is to ski at Garmisch-Classic

    Garmisch-Classic has 88 graded runs adding up to about 38 km. The largest share is red, with 19 km (50%). Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 6.9 km of black or expert runs.

    Garmisch-Classic: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Garmisch-Classic?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Garmisch-Classic at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 35 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Garmisch-Classic?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,047 m; the lowest starts at 727 m (1,320 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Garmisch-Classic best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Does Garmisch-Classic have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 19 km of its pistes (50%).
