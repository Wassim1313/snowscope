    About Liptovská Teplička

    With some 5.0 km of runs, Liptovská Teplička belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. Its lifts climb from 952 m to 1,255 m. Its nearest neighbour is Liptovská Teplička, 2.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Liptovská Teplička

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 4.8 km to ski. Most of the length is red (67%, about 3.2 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 303 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 952 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. All 4 lifts here are button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
