    About Tahko

    Around 18 km of marked runs place Tahko among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 36 by length. Skiing here happens between 97 m and 293 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Kasurila, 31 km south-west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Tahko

    Counting every graded piste, there are 52 runs and around 18 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 7.6 km (42%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 3.8 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Tahko usually gets

    At about 195 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 December and lasts to 28 April: about 19 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 64 cm, around 27 February. By mid-April the typical depth is 53 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm (lean years 15 cm, snowy years 47 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    196 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 1 button lift, 7 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Välimäki (314 m) and Tahkomäki (296 m).

    Tahko: common questions

    When does Tahko have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Tahko at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    What altitude is Tahko at?

    Between 97 m and 293 m, so 196 m of vertical drop.

    Is Tahko suitable for families?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 7.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Tahko have?

    About 18 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
