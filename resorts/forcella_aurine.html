    About Forcella Aurine

    Compact and local, Forcella Aurine has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,302 m up to 1,443 m. The closest other ski area is San Martino di Castrozza (14 km, west).

    Forcella Aurine and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 19 km north-west to San Pellegrino, with about 53 km of runs. For higher slopes, Marmolada climbs to 3,235 m.

    Snow this week

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Forcella Aurine gives 141 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,302 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Marmolada reaches 3,235 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rifugio Scarpa - Gurekian all'Agner (mountain hut), Rifugio Malga Cavallera (mountain hut), Hotel Ristorante Stella Alpina (hotel) and Hotel Chalet Giasenei (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
