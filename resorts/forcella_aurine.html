    About Forcella Aurine

    Compact and local, Forcella Aurine has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,302 m up to 1,443 m. The closest other ski area is San Martino di Castrozza (14 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Forcella Aurine has 4 graded runs adding up to about 2.0 km. Most of the length is blue (62%, about 1.2 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Forcella Aurine and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 19 km north-west to San Pellegrino, with about 53 km of runs. Marmolada has the longer snow season: around 24 weeks of solid cover, compared with 8 at Forcella Aurine. For higher slopes, Marmolada climbs to 3,235 m.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Forcella Aurine hold around 1 cm of snow today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 7 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Forcella Aurine gives 141 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,302 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Marmolada reaches 3,235 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Monte Agner, 2,872 m high and 5.2 km north of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,372 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 January and lasts to 19 March: about 8 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 17 cm to 63 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 43 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 23 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 37 cm.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rifugio Scarpa - Gurekian all'Agner (mountain hut), Rifugio Malga Cavallera (mountain hut), Hotel Ristorante Stella Alpina (hotel) and Hotel Chalet Giasenei (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Forcella Aurine have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Forcella Aurine at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    What altitude is Forcella Aurine at?

    The top is at 1,443 m and the base at 1,302 m.

    Is Forcella Aurine good for beginners?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Forcella Aurine?

    San Pellegrino, 19 km to the north-west, with about 53 km of pistes.
