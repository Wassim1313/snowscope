    The mountain at Ylläs

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Yllästunturi (719 m), Keskinenlaki (615 m) and Kesänkitunturi (535 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 279 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 29 November and lasts to 11 May: about 24 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 64 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 70 cm, around 7 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 55 cm to 84 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 45 cm, from 32 cm in a poor year to 59 cm in a big one.

    Ylläs: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ylläs?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Ylläs have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
