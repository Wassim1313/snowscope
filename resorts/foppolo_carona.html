    About Foppolo-Carona

    Neither huge nor tiny, Foppolo-Carona offers about 16 km of pistes (number 363 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,595 m to 2,149 m. The closest other ski area is Piazzatorre (7.5 km, south-west).

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,872 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 December and lasts to 24 April: about 18 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 81 cm. Most winters land between 32 cm and 117 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 33 cm, with 66 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 38 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 30 runs and around 16 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 66%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 1.7 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Foppolo-Carona gives 554 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,595 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 2 cable cars, 6 chairlifts and 4 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Masoni at 2,663 m, standing 514 m above the top lift.

    Foppolo-Carona: common questions

    When does Foppolo-Carona have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Foppolo-Carona at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 33 cm deep.

    How high is Foppolo-Carona?

    Between 1,595 m and 2,149 m, so 554 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Foppolo-Carona best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Foppolo-Carona?

    About 16 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
