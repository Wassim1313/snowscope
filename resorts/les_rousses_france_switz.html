    About Les Rousses

    With roughly 12 km of runs, Les Rousses sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,140 m up to 1,446 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Trélasse, 3.4 km to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Les Rousses has 38 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (51%, about 5.9 km). With 73% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Les Rousses is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 306 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts, 5 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by La Dôle, 1,678 m high and 4.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow at Les Rousses through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,293 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early February to early March, about 5 weeks of good cover. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 82 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 18 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 65 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 44 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    Questions about Les Rousses

    When is the best time to ski at Les Rousses?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Can you ski at Les Rousses at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 18 cm deep.

    How high is Les Rousses?

    The top is at 1,446 m and the base at 1,140 m.

    Is Les Rousses good for beginners?

    Yes: 73% of the graded pistes, about 8.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Les Rousses have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
