    About Lake City Ski Hill

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Lake City Ski Hill one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Its lifts climb from 2,701 m to 2,768 m. Lee's Ski Hill is the next resort, 31 km away to the west.

    How Lake City Ski Hill compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 47 km west to Telluride, with about 101 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Cimarron Mountain Club does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 13. For higher slopes, Telluride climbs to 3,812 m.

    What there is to ski at Lake City Ski Hill

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 2.1 km in total. Most of the length is green circle (74%, about 1.5 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Lake City Ski Hill today: about 21 cm up high. The last seven days brought 7 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 15 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Lake City Ski Hill gives 67 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,701 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Telluride, reach 3,812 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Crystal Peak at 3,939 m, standing 1,171 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Lake City Ski Hill through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,734 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to mid-April, about 13 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 24 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The snow is usually deepest around 23 March, with about 49 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 24 cm (lean years 9 cm, snowy years 43 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 26 cm to 62 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Lake City Ski Hill

    When is the best time to ski at Lake City Ski Hill?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Lake City Ski Hill at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 24 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Lake City Ski Hill at?

    Between 2,701 m and 2,768 m, so 67 m of vertical drop.

    Is Lake City Ski Hill suitable for families?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Lake City Ski Hill?

    Telluride, 47 km to the west, with about 101 km of pistes.
