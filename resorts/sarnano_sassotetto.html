    About Sarnano

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Sarnano sits in the middle of the size table of the Apennines. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,287 m up to 1,587 m. Bolognola is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the south-east.

    The pistes of Sarnano

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 6.9 km. Most of the length is red (50%, about 3.4 km). Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How Sarnano is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Sarnano gives 300 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Area Sciistica Ussita Frontignan reaches 1,964 m. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 4 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    Sarnano: common questions

    What altitude is Sarnano at?

    The top is at 1,587 m and the base at 1,287 m.

    Is Sarnano good for beginners?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 2.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Sarnano?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
