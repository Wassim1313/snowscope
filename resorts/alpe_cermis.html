    About Alpe Cermis

    Alpe Cermis is a mid-sized ski area with about 18 km of pistes, number 334 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 862 m to 2,250 m. Its nearest neighbour is Jochgrimm, 9.6 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Alpe Cermis is laid out

    1,388 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 862 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 3 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Cimon de le Sute, 2,616 m high and 6.8 km south-east of the resort.

    What there is to ski at Alpe Cermis

    Alpe Cermis has 30 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. The largest share is red, with 10 km (57%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.1 km.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,556 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 9 February and keeps it until about 13 March, roughly 5 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 19 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 18 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 43 cm). Most winters land between 13 cm and 60 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 32 cm.

    Questions about Alpe Cermis

    What is the best month to ski at Alpe Cermis?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Alpe Cermis at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 18 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Alpe Cermis?

    The top is at 2,250 m and the base at 862 m.

    Who is Alpe Cermis best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 57% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Alpe Cermis have?

    About 18 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
