    The snow at Espace Diamant, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Espace Diamant (about 1,528 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 65 cm; half of the winters sit between 40 cm and 92 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 128 cm; the middle half of winters range from 106 cm to 154 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 132 cm.

    By 15 December 50 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 76 %.

    1983-84 was the big one (225 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (90 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to late May (180 days).

    Skiing Espace Diamant: the practical side

    The pistes run from 1,007 m to 2,048 m (1,041 m of vertical), with 199 km of marked runs and 85 lifts.

    Questions about Espace Diamant

    Is there snow in Espace Diamant at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 65 cm at Christmas, and 76 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 74 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Espace Diamant?

    February: the snow peaks at about 132 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Espace Diamant get?

    About 132 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-February); between 90 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 225 cm in the biggest (1983-84).

    What altitude is Espace Diamant at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,048 m; the lowest starts at 1,007 m (1,041 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Espace Diamant have?

    About 199 km of pistes and 85 lifts.
