    About Espace Diamant

    Around 199 km of marked runs place Espace Diamant among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 13 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,007 m and 2,048 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Evasion Mont Blanc, 10 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 320 runs and around 199 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 106 km (53%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 6.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How much snow Espace Diamant usually gets

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,528 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 102 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 65 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 21 cm in a poor year to 109 cm in a big one. For about 13 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 79 cm and 176 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 19 February, with about 132 cm.

    The mountain at Espace Diamant

    1,041 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 2 gondolas, 28 chairlifts, 36 button lifts, 5 drag lifts, 4 rope tows and 9 moving carpets, 85 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 9 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Montagne d'Outray, 2,346 m high and 7.8 km south-east of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Le Very (hotel), Le Calgary (hotel), Refuge du Lachat (mountain hut) and Les chalets d'émeraude (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Espace Diamant?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Espace Diamant have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 65 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Espace Diamant?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 10 April 2027.

    What altitude is Espace Diamant at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,048 m; the lowest starts at 1,007 m (1,041 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Espace Diamant?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 135 km, are easy.
