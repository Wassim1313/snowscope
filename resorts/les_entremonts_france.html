    Snow at Les Entremonts through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 647 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 18 cm, around 19 January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 41 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 39 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and mid-February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    When is the best time to ski at Les Entremonts?
