    About Saas Fee

    With some 48 km of runs, Saas Fee belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,780 m and reach 3,556 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Saas-Almagell, 3.5 km to the east.

    How Saas Fee is laid out

    1,776 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,780 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 3,556 m. To get up there are 2 cable cars, 4 funiculars, 6 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 8 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 3 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Dom at 4,545 m, standing 989 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Saas Fee usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 2,668 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 81 cm, from 48 cm in a poor year to 123 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 143 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and early April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 136 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 18 weeks. Most winters land between 94 cm and 172 cm by mid-February.

    What there is to ski at Saas Fee

    The piste map lists 82 graded runs, about 48 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 22 km, or 44%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 2.3 km of difficult runs. 3.2 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Saas Fee: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Saas Fee?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Saas Fee at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 81 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Saas Fee?

    It opened on 3 October 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Saas Fee?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,556 m; the lowest starts at 1,780 m (1,776 m of vertical).

    Is Saas Fee good for beginners?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 22 km, are easy.
