    About Cima Piazzi

    Neither huge nor tiny, Cima Piazzi offers about 22 km of pistes (number 281 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,338 m to 2,366 m. Bormio Ski is the next resort, 6.3 km away to the south-east.

    The pistes of Cima Piazzi

    Counting every graded piste, there are 28 runs and around 22 km to ski. Most of the length is red (76%, about 16 km). Intermediate runs make up 76% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How Cima Piazzi is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,028 m: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,338 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Passo Stelvio reaches 3,355 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 3 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Cima de' Piazzi (3,439 m), 6.5 km to the south, about 1,073 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,852 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 44 cm and 91 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 30 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. A normal season tops out near 66 cm, around 11 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm, from 19 cm in a poor year to 68 cm in a big one.

    Cima Piazzi: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Cima Piazzi?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Cima Piazzi at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 42 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Cima Piazzi?

    Between 1,338 m and 2,366 m, so 1,028 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Cima Piazzi best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 76% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Cima Piazzi?

    Livigno, 15 km to the north-west, with about 83 km of pistes.
