    About Tomamu

    With some 22 km of runs, Tomamu belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Its lifts climb from 587 m to 1,172 m. The closest other ski area is Minami Furano (11 km, north-west).

    The mountain at Tomamu

    The vertical drop is 585 m: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 587 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 1,172 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
