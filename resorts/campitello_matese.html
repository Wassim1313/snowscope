    About Campitello Matese

    Only 4 of the 40 ski areas of the Apennines are larger than Campitello Matese, which has some 12 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,418 m up to 1,824 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 16 graded runs, about 12 km in total. Most of the length is red (77%, about 9.2 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 1.3 km of black or expert runs.

    How Campitello Matese is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 406 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,418 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 4 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    How high is Campitello Matese?

    The top is at 1,824 m and the base at 1,418 m.

    Who is Campitello Matese best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 77% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Campitello Matese have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
