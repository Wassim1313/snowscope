    The snow at Feldberg, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Feldberg (1,189 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 8 cm; half of the winters sit between 1 cm and 23 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 16 cm; the middle half of winters range from 4 cm to 40 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 16 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    2 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 7 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 2005-06 (139 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2022-23 (13 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early January until early March in a normal winter, roughly 37 days.

    Getting to Feldberg and what it costs

    Feldberg: quick answers

    Can you ski Feldberg at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 8 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 7 % of 46 winters. Right now: 4 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Feldberg?

    February: the snow peaks at about 16 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early January to early March.

    How snowy is Feldberg?

    About 16 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-February); between 13 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 139 cm in the biggest (2005-06).

    What altitude is Feldberg at?

    The top is at 1,446 m and the base at 932 m.

    How many km of pistes does Feldberg have?

    About 22 km of pistes and 20 lifts.
