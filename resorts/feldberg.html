    About Feldberg

    Feldberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 283 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 932 m up to 1,446 m. The closest other ski area is Hofeck (3.8 km, south).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Feldberg has 50 graded runs adding up to about 22 km. The largest share is blue, with 11 km (49%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.5 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 514 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The base at 932 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,446 m. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts, 6 T-bars, 3 button lifts and 7 moving carpets, 20 lifts in all. First-timers get 7 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Feldberg (1,494 m), Baldenweger Buck (1,461 m) and Seebuck (1,449 m).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,189 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 60 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 16 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 8 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 36 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March.

    Feldberg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Feldberg?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    Can you ski at Feldberg at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Feldberg at?

    The top is at 1,446 m and the base at 932 m.

    Is Feldberg good for beginners?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Feldberg have?

    About 22 km of pistes and 20 lifts.
