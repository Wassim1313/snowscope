    About Badger Mountain

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Badger Mountain one of the smaller places to ski in US West. The slopes start at 954 m and reach 1,062 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Echo Valley, 37 km to the north.

    How Badger Mountain compares nearby

    Of the 4 ski areas compared here, Badger Mountain is the third biggest by piste length. Mission Ridge has the longer snow season: around 21 weeks of solid cover, compared with 11 at Badger Mountain. The highest nearby is Mission Ridge, topping out at 2,064 m.

    The pistes of Badger Mountain

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 1.7 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 54%. With 54% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow this week

    Lifts and terrain

    108 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 954 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,062 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows. The skyline is dominated by Badger Mountain, 1,292 m high and 4.8 km south-west of the resort.

    Snow at Badger Mountain through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,008 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 28 December and keeps it until about 12 March, roughly 11 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. A normal season tops out near 49 cm, around 18 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 9 cm and 76 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 29 cm, from 11 cm in a poor year to 57 cm in a big one. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Badger Mountain: common questions

    When does Badger Mountain have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Badger Mountain at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 29 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Badger Mountain?

    The top is at 1,062 m and the base at 954 m.

    Is Badger Mountain good for beginners?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Badger Mountain?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
