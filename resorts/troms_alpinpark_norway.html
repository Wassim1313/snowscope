    Lifts and terrain

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Skarsfjellet - Stuoračazačohkka (843 m), Blåfjellet - Davit Geađgečohkka (778 m) and Blåmannen - Lulit Geađgečohkka (766 m).

    Snow at Tromsø Alpinpark through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 222 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 10 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 73 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 112 cm, a dry one 47 cm. Most winters land between 95 cm and 162 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 8 April, with about 156 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 152 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Tromsø Alpinpark?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at Tromsø Alpinpark at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 73 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
