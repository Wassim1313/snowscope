    About Langenbruck

    Langenbruck is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 810 m up to 908 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Schwengimatt, 8.7 km to the south-west.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Hohe Winde (13 km west) has about 6.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. Grenchenberg reaches 1,363 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Snow at Langenbruck through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 859 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; a snowy December gave 16 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 6 cm. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 25 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Langenbruck gives 98 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 810 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Vogelberg at 1,204 m, standing 296 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Erica (hotel), Gasthof zum Kreuz (hotel), Gasthof zum Ochsen (guesthouse) and Taverne (guesthouse).

    Questions about Langenbruck

    When is the best time to ski at Langenbruck?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Langenbruck at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Langenbruck?

    The top is at 908 m and the base at 810 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Langenbruck?

    Hohe Winde, 13 km to the west, with about 6.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Langenbruck?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
