    About Les Entremonts

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Les Entremonts sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,105 m and reach 1,425 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les Entremonts, 1.7 km to the south-east.

    How much snow Les Entremonts usually gets

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,265 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. The snow is usually deepest around 19 March, with about 122 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 58 cm; a snowy December gave 129 cm, a dry one 15 cm. For about 8 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 106 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 40 cm and 145 cm.

    The mountain at Les Entremonts

    The vertical drop is 320 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,425 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises "Granier, sommet Sud" (1,934 m), 5.0 km to the south-east, about 509 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Les Entremonts

    When does Les Entremonts have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Les Entremonts at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 58 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Les Entremonts?

    Between 1,105 m and 1,425 m, so 320 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Les Entremonts best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Les Entremonts have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
