    About Passo Lavazè

    Compact and local, Passo Lavazè has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,763 m to 1,837 m. Its nearest neighbour is Jochgrimm, 3.0 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Latemar Dolomites: about 40 km of pistes, 4.8 km to the east. For higher slopes, Latemar Dolomites climbs to 2,423 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Passo Lavazè gives 74 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,763 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.
