    About Avantura park

    Avantura park is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 36 of 51 in the Balkans by length. The slopes start at 966 m and reach 1,485 m at the top. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Obudojevica, 0.5 km to the west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Avantura park gives 519 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 966 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,485 m is the second highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 gondola and 1 chairlift. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns.
