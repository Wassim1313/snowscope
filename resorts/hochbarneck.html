    About Hochbärneck

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hochbärneck offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 696 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 862 m up to 1,055 m. Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben is the next resort, 5.5 km away to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 3.8 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.2 km, or 85%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Hochbärneck compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.0 km south-west to Lackenhof, with about 15 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Lackenhof usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 14 weeks, against 9 here. For higher slopes, Gemeindealpe Mitterbach climbs to 1,622 m.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Hochbärneck today: about 15 cm up high. The last seven days brought 4 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Friday.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 958 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to late March, about 9 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 18 February, with about 42 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 23 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 56 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 8 cm to 72 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 193 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 862 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Hochkar, reach 1,788 m. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Ötscher, 1,893 m high and 7.0 km south of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Hochbärneck include Almhaus Hochbärneck (guesthouse), Schindlhütte (guesthouse), Erlebnisdorf Sulzbichl (hostel) and Hotel Winterbach (hotel).

    Questions about Hochbärneck

    When is the best time to ski at Hochbärneck?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    Is there snow at Hochbärneck at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 23 cm deep.

    How high is Hochbärneck?

    Between 862 m and 1,055 m, so 193 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Hochbärneck?

    Yes: 85% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Hochbärneck?

    Lackenhof, 8.0 km to the south-west, with about 15 km of pistes.
