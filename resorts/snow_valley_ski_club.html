    About Snow Valley Ski Club

    Compact and local, Snow Valley Ski Club has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Canada West. Skiing here happens between 627 m and 669 m above sea level. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Edmonton Ski Club, 7.9 km to the north-east.

    How much snow Snow Valley Ski Club usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 648 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 16 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 35 cm). Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 27 cm. Most winters land between 8 cm and 47 cm by mid-February.

    The mountain at Snow Valley Ski Club

    Skied top to bottom, Snow Valley Ski Club gives 42 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 627 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Rabbit Hill (713 m), Bellamy Hill (686 m) and Grierson Hill (678 m).
