    About Snow Valley Ski Club

    Compact and local, Snow Valley Ski Club has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Canada West. Skiing here happens between 627 m and 669 m above sea level. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Edmonton Ski Club, 7.9 km to the north-east.

    The mountain at Snow Valley Ski Club

    Skied top to bottom, Snow Valley Ski Club gives 42 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 627 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 hotels: Hotel Selkirk, Varscona Hotel On Whyte, Wyndham Edmonton Hotel and Conference Centre and Travelodge.
