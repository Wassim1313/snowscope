    About Tatrapoma F Ťaskovka

    Tatrapoma F Ťaskovka has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 737 m and reach 815 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski centrum Oravská Polhora, 8.1 km to the north.

    The mountain at Tatrapoma F Ťaskovka

    Skied top to bottom, Tatrapoma F Ťaskovka gives 78 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 737 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.
