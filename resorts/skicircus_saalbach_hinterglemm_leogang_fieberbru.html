    About Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L

    Only 7 of the 1,056 ski areas of the Alps are larger than Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L, which has some 267 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 795 m to 2,090 m. The closest other ski area is Buchensteinwand (Pillersee) (9.2 km, north-west).

    The mountain at Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L

    Skied top to bottom, Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L gives 1,295 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 795 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 2,090 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 31 gondolas, 22 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 11 button lifts, 6 drag lifts, 3 rope tows and 6 moving carpets, 83 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 6 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Großes Zinthorn at 2,486 m, standing 396 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,442 m) in an ordinary winter, from late December to mid-May. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 58 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 30 cm and 70 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 40 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 29 cm; a snowy December gave 48 cm, a dry one 12 cm.

    Questions about Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L

    When is the best time to ski at Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 29 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L?

    How high is Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L?

    Is Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L good for beginners?
