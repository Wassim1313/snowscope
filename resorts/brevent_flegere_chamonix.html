    About Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix)

    With some 41 km of runs, Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix) belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,047 m and reach 2,486 m at the top. Les Planards (Chamonix) is the next resort, 2.8 km away to the south.

    How Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix) is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix) gives 1,439 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 2 cable cars, 2 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 5 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 18 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Aiguille Verte (4,121 m), 7.9 km to the east, about 1,635 m higher than the top lift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix) has 64 graded runs adding up to about 40 km. The largest share is black, with 13 km (32%). Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 13 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,766 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 November and keeps it until about 19 May, roughly 25 weeks. Most winters land between 95 cm and 203 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 88 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 159 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 74 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 119 cm, a dry one 34 cm.
