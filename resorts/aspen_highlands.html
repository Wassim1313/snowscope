    About Aspen Highlands

    Aspen Highlands is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 43 km of pistes put it at number 43 of 175. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,466 m up to 3,527 m. There are 5 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Buttermilk, 3.3 km to the north.

    The pistes of Aspen Highlands

    The piste map lists 76 graded runs, about 43 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 26 km (59%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 17 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,061 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,466 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The skyline is dominated by Highland Peak, 3,757 m high and 4.1 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,996 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 November and lasts to 20 May: about 27 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 134 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 99 cm to 188 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 158 cm, around 30 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 85 cm (lean years 41 cm, snowy years 130 cm). For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Aspen Highlands: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Aspen Highlands?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Aspen Highlands have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 85 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Aspen Highlands at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,527 m; the lowest starts at 2,466 m (1,061 m of vertical).

    Does Aspen Highlands have steep runs?

    Yes: about 17 km of its graded pistes (41%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Aspen Highlands?

    Aspen Snowmass, 8.5 km to the north-west, with about 120 km of pistes.
