    About Le Tourchet (Chamonix)

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Le Tourchet (Chamonix) one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 1,005 m and reach 1,068 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Les Bossons, 3.3 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Megève: about 141 km of pistes, 12 km to the south-west. If snow is the priority, Passy does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 18. Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix) reaches 2,486 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How Le Tourchet (Chamonix) is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Le Tourchet (Chamonix) gives 63 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Les Grands Montets, reach 2,768 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts. Above the ski area rises Rocher de la Tournette (4,677 m), 7.9 km to the south-east, about 3,609 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Le Tourchet (Chamonix) through a normal winter

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,036 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 36 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 44 cm and 97 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 19 February, with about 71 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm, from 10 cm in a poor year to 62 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Le Tourchet (Chamonix)?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Le Tourchet (Chamonix) at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 36 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Le Tourchet (Chamonix)?

    The top is at 1,068 m and the base at 1,005 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Le Tourchet (Chamonix)?

    Megève, 12 km to the south-west, with about 141 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Le Tourchet (Chamonix) have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
