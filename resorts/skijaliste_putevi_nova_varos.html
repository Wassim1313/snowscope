    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 199 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 997 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Tornik, for one, climbs to 1,486 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 button lift.
