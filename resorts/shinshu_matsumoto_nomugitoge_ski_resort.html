    About Shinshu-Matsumoto Nomugitoge

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Shinshu-Matsumoto Nomugitoge sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The slopes start at 1,434 m and reach 2,114 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Yabuhara Kogen (9.8 km, south).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 680 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,434 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Shinshu-Matsumoto Nomugitoge hold around 28 cm of snow today. 16 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 10 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.
