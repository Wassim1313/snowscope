    About Biberwier

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Biberwier sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,013 m and reach 1,823 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lermoos Grubigstein, 4.4 km to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Biberwier

    Counting every graded piste, there are 25 runs and around 9.5 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (73%, about 7.0 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Berwang (13 km north-west) has about 28 km of pistes against 10 km here. If snow is the priority, Zugspitze does better, with about 26 weeks of a good base instead of 19. For higher slopes, Zugspitze climbs to 2,627 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,418 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 62 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 39 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 33 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 16 cm, snowy years 51 cm). Most winters land between 39 cm and 79 cm by mid-February.

    Biberwier today and the next days

    Today there are about 24 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Biberwier. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 22 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (13 cm).

    The mountain at Biberwier

    810 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 3 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Hochplattig (2,768 m), 7.1 km to the east, about 945 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Waldhaus Talblick (guesthouse), DORMERO BeHo Zugspitze (hotel), Hotel Alpina-Regina (hotel) and Hotel Monte Vita Tirol (hotel).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Biberwier?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Biberwier at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 33 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Biberwier at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,823 m; the lowest starts at 1,013 m (810 m of vertical).

    Is Biberwier good for beginners?

    Yes: 73% of the graded pistes, about 7.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Biberwier?

    Berwang, 13 km to the north-west, with about 28 km of pistes.
