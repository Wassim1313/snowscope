    About Glarus

    Compact and local, Glarus has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 510 m and reach 669 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Mugi, 3.6 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Glarus compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 14 km south-east to Elm, with about 28 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Braunwald does better, with about 25 weeks of a good base instead of 0. Elm reaches 2,100 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    What there is to ski at Glarus

    Glarus has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.5 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.9 km (62%). With 62% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes. 0.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    159 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 510 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Flumserberg reaches 2,218 m. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bächistock at 2,914 m, standing 2,245 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 590 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 11 cm, with 26 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 16 February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 6 cm to 47 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 8 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Glarus?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Glarus at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).

    What altitude is Glarus at?

    Between 510 m and 669 m, so 159 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Glarus?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Glarus?

    Elm, 14 km to the south-east, with about 28 km of pistes.
