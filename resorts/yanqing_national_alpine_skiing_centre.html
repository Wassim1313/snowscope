    About Yanqing National Alpine Skiing C

    Yanqing National Alpine Skiing C ranks number 9 of 92 ski areas in China by piste length, with about 26 km of runs. The slopes start at 928 m and reach 2,181 m at the top. Shijinglong is the next resort, 14 km away to the east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,253 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 928 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 5 gondolas, 4 chairlifts and 2 drag lifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Yanqing National Alpine Skiing C has 21 graded runs adding up to about 26 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 51%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.
