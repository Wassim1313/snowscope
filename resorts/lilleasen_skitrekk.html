    About Lilleåsen skitrekk

    Lilleåsen skitrekk is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 551 m and 713 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Stordal alpinsenter, 17 km to the north.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 31 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 632 m) in an ordinary winter, from late October to late May or later. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 129 cm and 274 cm. A normal season tops out near 227 cm, around 9 April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 130 cm; a snowy December gave 182 cm, a dry one 88 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 224 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 25 weeks.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 162 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Skorene (1,789 m), 6.6 km to the south, about 1,076 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Nord Hotel Og Restaurant (hotel), Fjordglytt Overnatting (guesthouse), Petrines Gjestegiveri (guesthouse) and Rellingsætra (chalet).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Lilleåsen skitrekk?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    Can you ski at Lilleåsen skitrekk at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 130 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Lilleåsen skitrekk at?

    Between 551 m and 713 m, so 162 m of vertical drop.
