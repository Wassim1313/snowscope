    Snow at Black Mountain of Maine through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 456 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 25 December and keeps it until about 14 April, roughly 16 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 29 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 68 cm, around 3 March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 30 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 8 cm in a poor year to 53 cm in a big one.     How Black Mountain of Maine is laid out

    The vertical drop is 318 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 297 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 1 T-bar, 3 lifts in all. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Black Mountain (715 m), Whitecap Mountain (655 m) and South Twin Mountain (655 m).
