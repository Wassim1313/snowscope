    About Spitzenberg und Köpfle

    Spitzenberg und Köpfle is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 718 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 906 m and 1,064 m above sea level. Hofeck is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the north-west.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 985 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for late December and early January. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 24 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 9 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 31 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    The mountain at Spitzenberg und Köpfle

    Skied top to bottom, Spitzenberg und Köpfle gives 158 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,106 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Feldberg, reach 1,446 m. To get up there are 3 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Seebuck, 1,449 m high and 7.0 km north of the resort.
