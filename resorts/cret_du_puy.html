    About Crêt-du-Puy

    Crêt-du-Puy is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 610 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 870 m and 1,256 m above sea level. Bugnenets-Savagnières is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Crêt-du-Puy

    Crêt-du-Puy has 8 graded runs adding up to about 6.1 km. The largest share is blue, with 4.1 km (68%). With 68% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Crêt-du-Puy gives 386 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 870 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. Above the ski area rises Chasseral (1,607 m), 6.8 km to the north-east, about 351 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Crêt-du-Puy through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,063 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 18 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 8 cm; a snowy December gave 41 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 60 cm.

    Questions about Crêt-du-Puy

    When does Crêt-du-Puy have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Crêt-du-Puy at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Crêt-du-Puy?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,256 m; the lowest starts at 870 m (386 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Crêt-du-Puy?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 4.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Crêt-du-Puy?

    Bugnenets-Savagnières, 3.9 km to the north-east, with about 16 km of pistes.
