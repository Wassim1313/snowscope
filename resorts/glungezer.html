    About Glungezer

    Glungezer is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 398 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 940 m and 2,307 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Vögelsberg, 6.2 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Glungezer gives 1,367 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 940 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Rosenjoch (2,796 m), 6.0 km to the south, about 489 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Glungezer usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,624 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 31 December and keeps it until about 5 May, roughly 18 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 46 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 28 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 13 cm in a poor year to 44 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 32 cm and 64 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 54 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Glungezer?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Can you ski at Glungezer at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 28 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Glungezer?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,307 m; the lowest starts at 940 m (1,367 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Glungezer best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 70% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Glungezer?

    About 14 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
