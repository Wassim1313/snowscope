    About Glungezer

    Glungezer is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 398 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 940 m and 2,307 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Vögelsberg, 6.2 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Glungezer gives 1,367 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 940 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
