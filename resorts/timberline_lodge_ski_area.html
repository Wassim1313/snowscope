    What 46 winters say about Timberline Lodge

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Timberline Lodge (about 6,234 ft (1,900 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 69 in (174 cm); half of the winters sit between 33 in (85 cm) and 105 in (267 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 139 in (352 cm); the middle half of winters range from 81 in (206 cm) to 175 in (444 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 169 in (429 cm).

    78 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 89 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 2007-08 (427 in (1085 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2014-15 (40 in (101 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-May, about 176 days.

    So far this winter: 8.7 in (22 cm) on 27 November (usual: 19 in (49 cm)).

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 69 in (174 cm) at Christmas, and 89 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 33 in (83 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Timberline Lodge?

    How much snow does Timberline Lodge get?

    About 169 in (429 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (mid-March); between 40 in (101 cm) in the leanest winter (2014-15) and 427 in (1085 cm) in the biggest (2007-08).

    When does Timberline Lodge have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    What altitude is Timberline Lodge at?

    About Timberline Lodge

    With some 53 km of runs, Timberline Lodge belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,214 m up to 2,586 m. The closest other ski area is Mt. Hood Meadows (4.5 km, east).

    How Timberline Lodge is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Timberline Lodge gives 1,372 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,586 m. To get up there are 8 chairlifts, 3 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Mount Hood (3,429 m), 5.8 km to the north, about 843 m higher than the top lift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Timberline Lodge has 61 graded runs adding up to about 53 km. Most of the length is blue square (52%, about 28 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 12 km of difficult runs.

    Snow at Timberline Lodge through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,900 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 November and keeps it until about 8 May, roughly 24 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and late February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 174 cm (lean years 31 cm, snowy years 308 cm). Most winters land between 54 cm and 518 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 429 cm, around 18 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 312 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.
