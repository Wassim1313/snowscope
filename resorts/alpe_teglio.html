    About Alpe Teglio

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Alpe Teglio sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,670 m to 2,480 m. Aprica is the next resort, 11 km away to the south-east.

    How much snow Alpe Teglio usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,075 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 26 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 90 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm (lean years 24 cm, snowy years 91 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 53 cm and 113 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 90 cm.

    What there is to ski at Alpe Teglio

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 7.7 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.0 km, or 52%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 810 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,670 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,480 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Vetta di Ron (3,137 m), 7.7 km to the north-west, about 657 m higher than the top lift.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Aprica (11 km south-east) has about 38 km of pistes against 8.0 km here. For more reliable snow, Diavolezza-Lagalb usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 29 weeks, against 26 here. For higher slopes, Diavolezza-Lagalb climbs to 3,003 m.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Alpe Teglio?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Alpe Teglio have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 54 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Alpe Teglio?

    Between 1,670 m and 2,480 m, so 810 m of vertical drop.

    Is Alpe Teglio suitable for families?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Alpe Teglio?

    Aprica, 11 km to the south-east, with about 38 km of pistes.
