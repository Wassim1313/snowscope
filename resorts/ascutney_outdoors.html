    About Ascutney Outdoors

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ascutney Outdoors offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 57 of 93 in US Northeast). The ski area spans the mountain from 236 m up to 372 m. The closest other ski area is Arrowhead (16 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 25 graded runs, about 5.1 km in total. Most of the length is green circle (46%, about 2.3 km). No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them.

    How Ascutney Outdoors is laid out

    136 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 236 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 T-bar, 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Mt Ascutney (954 m), 2.3 km to the south, about 582 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 4 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 304 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-February to mid-March. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 29 February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 10 cm to 65 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 15 cm; a snowy December gave 36 cm, a dry one 4 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Ascutney Outdoors

    When does Ascutney Outdoors have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Ascutney Outdoors at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 15 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Ascutney Outdoors?

    The top is at 372 m and the base at 236 m.

    Is Ascutney Outdoors suitable for families?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 2.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Ascutney Outdoors?

    Okemo Mountain, 22 km to the west, with about 70 km of pistes.
