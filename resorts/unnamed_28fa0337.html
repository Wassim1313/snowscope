    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 37 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,099 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Kandellifte, reach 1,201 m.
