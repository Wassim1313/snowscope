    About Wennser Gletscher

    Wennser Gletscher is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 814 m to 858 m. Wildkogel is the next resort, 4.6 km away to the west.

    Wennser Gletscher and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser: about 238 km of pistes, 24 km to the north. If snow is the priority, Weißsee Gletscherwelt does better, with about 28 weeks of a good base instead of 0. The highest nearby is Weißsee Gletscherwelt, topping out at 2,555 m.

    The mountain at Wennser Gletscher

    44 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 814 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kaprun reaches 3,024 m. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Graukogel at 2,834 m, standing 1,976 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 836 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 10 cm and 36 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 10 cm; a snowy December gave 27 cm, a dry one 0 cm. A normal season tops out near 22 cm, around 18 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 17 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Questions about Wennser Gletscher

    When does Wennser Gletscher have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at Wennser Gletscher at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 10 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Wennser Gletscher?

    Its highest lift reaches 858 m; the lowest starts at 814 m (44 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Wennser Gletscher?

    SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser, 24 km to the north, with about 238 km of pistes.

    How big is Wennser Gletscher?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
