    This week's snow

    Today there are about 15 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Torsbustaden. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 13 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 188 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 24 cm and 88 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 68 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 37 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 13 cm in a poor year to 56 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Torsbustaden

    What is the best month to ski at Torsbustaden?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Torsbustaden at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 31 cm deep.
