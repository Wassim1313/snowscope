    About Ylläs

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Ylläs sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 396 m to 399 m. Ylläs is the next resort, 0.5 km away to the north-west.

    The pistes of Ylläs

    The piste map lists 37 graded runs, about 11 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 6.2 km, or 55%. Intermediate runs make up 55% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Snow at Ylläs through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 398 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 November and keeps it until about 11 May, roughly 24 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 45 cm, with 59 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 64 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 55 cm to 84 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 70 cm, around 7 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March.

    How Ylläs is laid out

    3 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Yllästunturi at 719 m, standing 320 m above the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find 4 chalets: Villa Mahama, Villa Kuusi, Villa Elina and Röllykkä.

    Ylläs: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ylläs?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Ylläs at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Ylläs?

    Its highest lift reaches 399 m; the lowest starts at 396 m (3 m of vertical).

    Who is Ylläs best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 55% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Ylläs have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 1 lift.
