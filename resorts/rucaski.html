    About Rucaski

    Neither huge nor tiny, Rucaski offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 671 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,483 m and 1,789 m above sea level. Crissolo is the next resort, 9.7 km away to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Rucaski

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 4.6 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.4 km, or 52%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 306 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,483 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Pontechianale reaches 2,659 m. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Frioland at 2,738 m, standing 949 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,636 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 January and keeps it until about 23 April, roughly 14 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 22 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 54 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 50 cm, around 3 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 33 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Year to year the snow is steady, with 15 cm to 87 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Questions about Rucaski

    When does Rucaski have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late April.

    How much snow does Rucaski have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Rucaski?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,789 m; the lowest starts at 1,483 m (306 m of vertical).

    Is Rucaski suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Rucaski?

    Crissolo, 9.7 km to the south-west, with about 12 km of pistes.
