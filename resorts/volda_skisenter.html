    About Volda skisenter

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Volda skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 322 m to 654 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ørsta skisenter, 7.0 km to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 6.6 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.0 km, or 45%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 29 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Volda skisenter. The last seven days brought 47 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 21 cm by Friday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (40 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 332 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Stryn Vinterski, for one, climbs to 974 m. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Kyrkjetinden (1,070 m), 7.9 km to the north, about 416 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 488 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 November and lasts to 18 May: about 26 weeks. A normal season tops out near 180 cm, around 20 March. Most winters land between 89 cm and 209 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 86 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 40 cm in a poor year to 139 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 155 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Questions about Volda skisenter

    When is the best time to ski at Volda skisenter?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Volda skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 86 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Volda skisenter?

    The top is at 654 m and the base at 322 m.

    Is Volda skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 3.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Volda skisenter have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
