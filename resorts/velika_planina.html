    About Velika planina

    Compact and local, Velika planina has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,408 m to 1,664 m. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Krvavec, 8.4 km to the west.

    How Velika planina is laid out

    256 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,408 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 cable car, 1 chairlift and 2 button lifts. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Planjava - Zahodni vrh at 2,397 m, standing 733 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,536 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 January and keeps it until about 1 March, roughly 3 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; a snowy December gave 67 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 37 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 103 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Questions about Velika planina

    When is the best time to ski at Velika planina?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Is there snow at Velika planina at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Velika planina?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,664 m; the lowest starts at 1,408 m (256 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Velika planina best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 61% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Velika planina?

    Krvavec, 8.4 km to the west, with about 16 km of pistes.
