    Velika planina in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Velika planina (about 1,536 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 13 cm, with a normal range of 5 cm–34 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 27 cm (10 cm–82 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 37 cm.

    9 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 22 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 2005-06 (285 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2019-20 (15 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early January to late March, about 77 days.

    About Velika planina

    Compact and local, Velika planina has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,408 m to 1,664 m. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Krvavec, 8.4 km to the west.

    How Velika planina is laid out

    256 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,408 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
