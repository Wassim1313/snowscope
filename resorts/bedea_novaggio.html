    About Bedea-Novaggio

    Compact and local, Bedea-Novaggio has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 626 m to 683 m. Its nearest neighbour is Forcora Ski, 9.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Bedea-Novaggio and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Forcora Ski: about 1.0 km of pistes, 9.9 km to the north-west. For higher slopes, Cimetta climbs to 1,646 m.

    The mountain at Bedea-Novaggio

    The vertical drop is 57 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 626 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Piana di Vigezzo, for one, climbs to 2,053 m.
