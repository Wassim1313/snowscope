    The snow at Val Cenis, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Val Cenis (2,022 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 54 cm; half of the winters sit between 39 cm and 68 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 97 cm; the middle half of winters range from 75 cm to 109 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 103 cm.

    39 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 57 % by 1 January.

    2017-18 was the big one (181 cm); 1988-89 the lean one (67 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 165 days.

    Questions about Val Cenis

    Is there snow in Val Cenis at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 54 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 57 % of 46 winters. Right now: 128 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Val Cenis?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 103 cm). For a sure base, early December to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Val Cenis?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 103 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 181 cm (2017-18), the thinnest 67 cm (1988-89).

    How high do the lifts go at Val Cenis?

    Between 1,306 m and 2,737 m, so 1,431 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Val Cenis have?

    About 89 km of pistes and 32 lifts.
