    About Val Cenis

    With some 89 km of runs, Val Cenis belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,306 m and 2,737 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise, 3.3 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 140 marked runs cover roughly 89 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 38 km, or 42%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Val Cenis has roughly 58 cm of snow on the ground today. 30 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 62 cm by Sunday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 24 cm now. The 2026/27 season at Val Cenis is announced to start on 12 December 2026.

    How Val Cenis is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,431 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Starting at 1,306 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 14 chairlifts, 12 button lifts and 4 moving carpets, 32 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pointe de Ronce at 3,612 m, standing 875 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 2,022 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 6 December and lasts to 15 May: about 23 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 63 cm to 120 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 54 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 86 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 2 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 103 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 55 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Val Cenis?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Val Cenis have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 54 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Val Cenis?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Val Cenis?

    Between 1,306 m and 2,737 m, so 1,431 m of vertical drop.

    Is Val Cenis suitable for families?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 60 km, are easy.
