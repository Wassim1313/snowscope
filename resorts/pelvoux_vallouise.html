    About Pelvoux-Vallouise

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pelvoux-Vallouise offers about 15 km of pistes (number 388 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,231 m and reach 2,219 m at the top. Puy-Saint-Vincent is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Pelvoux-Vallouise

    The piste map lists 34 graded runs, about 15 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 6.2 km, or 42%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Pelvoux-Vallouise has roughly 33 cm of snow on the ground today. 30 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 52 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (20 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 988 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Its 7 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 4 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Mont Pelvoux - Pointe Puiseux (3,943 m), 7.5 km to the north-west, about 1,724 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Pelvoux-Vallouise usually gets

    At about 1,725 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 December and lasts to 20 April: about 19 weeks. A normal season tops out near 76 cm, around 6 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 90 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm, with 68 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 36 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Pelvoux-Vallouise: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Pelvoux-Vallouise?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Pelvoux-Vallouise at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Pelvoux-Vallouise?

    Between 1,231 m and 2,219 m, so 988 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Pelvoux-Vallouise?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 9.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Pelvoux-Vallouise?

    Serre-Chevalier, 9.5 km to the north-east, with about 157 km of pistes.
