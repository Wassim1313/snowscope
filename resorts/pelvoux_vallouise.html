    Pelvoux-Vallouise in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Pelvoux-Vallouise (1,725 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 38 cm of cover (27 cm to 53 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 74 cm, between 56 cm and 84 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 76 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 28 % of winters, and by 1 January in 37 %.

    The deepest winter was 2017-18 (141 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2004-05 (42 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 143 days.

    Skiing Pelvoux-Vallouise: the practical side

    The pistes run from 1,231 m to 2,219 m (988 m of vertical), with 15 km of marked runs and 7 lifts.

    About Pelvoux-Vallouise

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pelvoux-Vallouise offers about 15 km of pistes (number 388 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,231 m and reach 2,219 m at the top. Puy-Saint-Vincent is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the south.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Pelvoux-Vallouise has roughly 100 cm of snow on the ground today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 36 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.
