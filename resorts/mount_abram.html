    About Mount Abram

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mount Abram offers about 18 km of pistes (number 35 of 93 in US Northeast). Its lifts climb from 290 m to 606 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sunday River, 17 km to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 43 graded runs, about 18 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 9.2 km, or 52%. Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.4 km.

    There is no snow on the ground at Mount Abram yet. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 7 cm at mid-mountain.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 448 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 31 December and keeps it until about 10 April, roughly 15 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 34 cm and 96 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 19 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 65 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 28 cm, with 47 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    316 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 290 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Abram (604 m), Long Mountain (557 m) and Round Mountain (557 m).

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Chapman Inn (hotel), The Bethel Resort & Suites (hotel) and paws inn (hostel).

    Mount Abram: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Mount Abram?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    How much snow does Mount Abram have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 28 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Mount Abram at?

    The top is at 606 m and the base at 290 m.

    What level of skier is Mount Abram best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Mount Abram?

    Sunday River, 17 km to the north-west, with about 86 km of pistes.
