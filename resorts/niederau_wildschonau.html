    About Niederau (Wildschönau)

    With roughly 19 km of runs, Niederau (Wildschönau) sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 833 m and reach 1,564 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Juwel, 2.9 km to the west.

    What there is to ski at Niederau (Wildschönau)

    Counting every graded piste, there are 41 runs and around 19 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 6.9 km, or 37%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 5.5 km.

    How Niederau (Wildschönau) is laid out

    The vertical drop is 731 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 833 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,564 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 11 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Schwaigberghorn, 1,990 m high and 7.7 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,198 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 January and lasts to 11 March: about 7 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 21 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 22 cm to 54 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 38 cm, around 18 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 28 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Niederau (Wildschönau)?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Niederau (Wildschönau) have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 21 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Niederau (Wildschönau) at?

    The top is at 1,564 m and the base at 833 m.

    Is Niederau (Wildschönau) good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 5.5 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Niederau (Wildschönau) have?

    About 19 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
