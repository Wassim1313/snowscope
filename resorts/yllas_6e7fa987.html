    Lifts and terrain

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Yllästunturi (719 m), Keskinenlaki (615 m) and Kesänkitunturi (535 m).

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 279 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 64 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. A normal season tops out near 70 cm, around 7 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 55 cm and 84 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 45 cm (lean years 32 cm, snowy years 59 cm).

    Ylläs: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ylläs?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Ylläs at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
