    Snow at Le Haut-du-Tôt through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 757 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and early March. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 20 January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 37 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 26 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Le Haut-du-Tôt: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Le Haut-du-Tôt?

    Aim for mid-January and early March.

    Can you ski at Le Haut-du-Tôt at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).
