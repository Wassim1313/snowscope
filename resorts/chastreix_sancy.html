    About Chastreix-Sancy

    Chastreix-Sancy is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 13 km of pistes put it at number 12 of 257. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,385 m up to 1,709 m. Super Besse is the next resort, 4.9 km away to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 27 marked runs cover roughly 13 km. Most of the length is blue (39%, about 5.1 km). With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Chastreix-Sancy is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 324 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,385 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. All 7 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Snow at Chastreix-Sancy through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,547 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 7 cm, around 26 January. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; a snowy December gave 15 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 33 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February.

    Chastreix-Sancy: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Chastreix-Sancy?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Chastreix-Sancy at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 1 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Chastreix-Sancy?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,709 m; the lowest starts at 1,385 m (324 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Chastreix-Sancy?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 7.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Chastreix-Sancy?

    About 13 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
