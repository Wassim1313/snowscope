    About Petzen

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Petzen sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 634 m and 1,904 m above sea level. Golte is the next resort, 19 km away to the south-east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,270 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The lowest slopes are at only 634 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 drag lift. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Kordeževa glava / Kordeschkopf (2,126 m), 2.6 km to the south, about 222 m higher than the top lift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Petzen has 15 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. The largest share is red, with 6.8 km (65%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Krvavec (31 km south-west) has about 16 km of pistes against 11 km here. For more reliable snow, Saualpe Eberstein usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 11 weeks, against 1 here.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,269 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 31 January and lasts to 8 February: about 1 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 97 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 33 cm, around 5 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 12 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 1 cm in a poor year to 58 cm in a big one.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Petzen include Petzen (hotel), Werkhof Bistrica (hotel), Planinski dom na Peci (mountain hut) and Wackendorfer Alm (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Petzen: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Petzen?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early February.

    Is there snow at Petzen at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Petzen?

    The top is at 1,904 m and the base at 634 m.

    What level of skier is Petzen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 65% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Petzen?

    Krvavec, 31 km to the south-west, with about 16 km of pistes.
