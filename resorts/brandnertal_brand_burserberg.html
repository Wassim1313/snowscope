    What 46 winters say about Brandnertal

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Brandnertal (about 1,430 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 30 cm of cover (20 cm to 36 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 55 cm; the middle half of winters range from 43 cm to 68 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 62 cm.

    By 15 December 2 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 7 %.

    Best winter on record: 1998-99, peaking at 120 cm. Worst: 2022-23, which never passed 40 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 141 days.

    Getting to Brandnertal and what it costs

    Common questions

    Can you ski Brandnertal at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 30 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 7 % of 46 winters. Right now: 69 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Brandnertal?

    March: the snow peaks at about 62 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to early May.

    How much snow does Brandnertal get?

    About 62 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 40 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 120 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    What altitude is Brandnertal at?

    About Brandnertal

    Brandnertal is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 45 km of pistes put it at number 131 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 889 m up to 1,971 m. The closest other ski area is Tschardund (7.9 km, north-west).

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Brandnertal hold around 69 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 64 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Brandnertal gives 1,082 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 889 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
