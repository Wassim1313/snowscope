    About Ski Eldorado

    Ski Eldorado is a small ski area by the standards of US Northeast, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 671 m and reach 797 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ski Eldorado (2.0 km, south-west).

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 734 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 42 cm, from 16 cm in a poor year to 68 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 48 cm to 106 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 56 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 80 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    126 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 671 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Above the ski area rises Salmon Mountain (981 m), 7.9 km to the south, about 184 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Ski Eldorado

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Eldorado?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Ski Eldorado at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 42 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Eldorado?

    Its highest lift reaches 797 m; the lowest starts at 671 m (126 m of vertical).
