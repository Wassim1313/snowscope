    About Skilift Wurmstein

    Skilift Wurmstein is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 596 m up to 725 m. The closest other ski area is Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb (7.9 km, south).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.1 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 0.7 km (59%). With 59% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    129 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 596 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 660 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 28 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 10 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and late December. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 19 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Quick answers

    When does Skilift Wurmstein have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    How much snow does Skilift Wurmstein have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilift Wurmstein?

    The top is at 725 m and the base at 596 m.

    Can beginners ski at Skilift Wurmstein?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilift Wurmstein?

    Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb, 7.9 km to the south, with about 1.0 km of pistes.
