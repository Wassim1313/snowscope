    The mountain at Happylift

    Skied top to bottom, Happylift gives 31 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 984 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Stuhleck, reach 1,767 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Stuhleck at 1,782 m, standing 767 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Happylift usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,000 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 27 cm, around 14 February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 15 cm, with 43 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 4 cm to 53 cm.
