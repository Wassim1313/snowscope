    About Eriz

    Neither huge nor tiny, Eriz offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 612 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,056 m up to 1,379 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Habkern, 5.5 km to the south-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 5.7 km in total. Most of the length is blue (47%, about 2.7 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 1.5 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 16 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,218 m in an ordinary winter, from early January to late April. By mid-April the typical depth is 37 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 53 cm. Most winters land between 24 cm and 77 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 28 cm (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 42 cm).

    How Eriz is laid out

    323 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 2 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The skyline is dominated by Furggenguetsch, 2,197 m high and 5.9 km east of the resort.

    Questions about Eriz

    When does Eriz have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    How much snow does Eriz have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 28 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Eriz?

    Between 1,056 m and 1,379 m, so 323 m of vertical drop.

    Is Eriz suitable for families?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 2.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Eriz?

    Sigriswill-Schwanden, 8.3 km to the south-west, with about 12 km of pistes.
