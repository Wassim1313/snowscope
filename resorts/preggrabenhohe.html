    About Preggrabenhöhe

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Preggrabenhöhe one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 908 m and 986 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Schilifte Kleinlobming (18 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. Freeride runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 100%. Steep ground is the speciality: 100% of the length is graded difficult. 1.2 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Preggrabenhöhe today: about 6 cm up high. The last seven days brought 11 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 7 cm at mid-mountain.

    The mountain at Preggrabenhöhe

    From top to bottom that makes 78 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 908 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Salzstiegl, for one, climbs to 1,705 m. Above the ski area rises Lenzmoarkogel (1,991 m), 6.7 km to the south-east, about 1,005 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Preggrabenhöhe usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 947 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 40 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 22 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Preggrabenhöhe: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Preggrabenhöhe?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Preggrabenhöhe at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Preggrabenhöhe at?

    Between 908 m and 986 m, so 78 m of vertical drop.

    Is Preggrabenhöhe good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.2 km of its graded pistes (100%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Preggrabenhöhe?

    Präbichl, 27 km to the north, with about 14 km of pistes.
