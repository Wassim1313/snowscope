    About La Pierre Saint-Martin

    La Pierre Saint-Martin is a mid-sized ski area with about 25 km of pistes, number 27 of 49 in the Pyrenees by length. The slopes start at 1,519 m and reach 2,146 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Candanchu (27 km, south-east).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, La Pierre Saint-Martin gives 627 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,519 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
