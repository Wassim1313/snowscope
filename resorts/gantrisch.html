    About Gantrisch

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Gantrisch sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,047 m up to 1,636 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gatrisch, 1.4 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,342 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 26 December and keeps it until about 22 April, roughly 17 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 35 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 29 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 49 cm). A normal season tops out near 60 cm, around 1 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 25 cm to 87 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 3.9 km. Most of the length is blue (91%, about 3.6 km). With 91% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Gantrisch

    The vertical drop is 589 m: runs of a moderate length. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,636 m. Above the ski area rises Ochsen (2,188 m), 4.1 km to the south, about 552 m higher than the top lift.

    How Gantrisch compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Gantrisch: about 11 km of pistes, 2.2 km to the north-east. For more reliable snow, Gantrisch usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 22 weeks, against 17 here.

    Questions about Gantrisch

    When does Gantrisch have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Gantrisch at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 29 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Gantrisch at?

    Between 1,047 m and 1,636 m, so 589 m of vertical drop.

    Is Gantrisch suitable for families?

    Yes: 91% of the graded pistes, about 3.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Gantrisch?

    Gantrisch, 2.2 km to the north-east, with about 11 km of pistes.
