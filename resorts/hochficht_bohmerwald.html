    About Hochficht-Böhmerwald

    Hochficht-Böhmerwald ranks number 9 of 257 ski areas in Central Europe by piste length, with about 16 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 856 m and 1,332 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skilift Heindlmühle, 17 km to the south.

    What there is to ski at Hochficht-Böhmerwald

    Hochficht-Böhmerwald has 23 graded runs adding up to about 16 km. Most of the length is blue (49%, about 7.8 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Hochficht-Böhmerwald gives 476 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 856 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,332 m. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 2 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
