    About Kolsassberg

    Compact and local, Kolsassberg has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 754 m to 846 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wildstättlift Wattenberg, 2.8 km to the south-west.

    How Kolsassberg is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Kolsassberg gives 92 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 754 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Hochzillertal-Hochfügen, reach 2,374 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Hirzer (2,725 m), 8.0 km to the south, about 1,879 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 800 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Year to year the snow is steady, with 12 cm to 36 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 12 cm; a snowy December gave 27 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 24 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Questions about Kolsassberg

    When is the best time to ski at Kolsassberg?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at Kolsassberg at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Kolsassberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 846 m; the lowest starts at 754 m (92 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Kolsassberg?

    Glungezer, 10 km to the south-west, with about 14 km of pistes.

    How big is Kolsassberg?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
