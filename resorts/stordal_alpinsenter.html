    About Stordal alpinsenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Stordal alpinsenter offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 130 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 394 m up to 792 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lilleåsen skitrekk, 17 km to the south.

    The pistes of Stordal alpinsenter

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 4.7 km in total. Most of the length is green (beginner) (57%, about 2.6 km). With 57% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 29 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 593 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 98 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 146 cm, a dry one 63 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 105 cm and 220 cm. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 185 cm. For about 21 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 184 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    The mountain at Stordal alpinsenter

    Skied top to bottom, Stordal alpinsenter gives 398 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Seljebottinden (1,531 m), 7.9 km to the south-east, about 739 m higher than the top lift.

    Stordal alpinsenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Stordal alpinsenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Stordal alpinsenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 98 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Stordal alpinsenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 792 m; the lowest starts at 394 m (398 m of vertical).

    Is Stordal alpinsenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 2.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Stordal alpinsenter have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
