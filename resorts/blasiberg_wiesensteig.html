    About Bläsiberg Wiesensteig

    Bläsiberg Wiesensteig is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 65 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Skiing here happens between 612 m and 756 m above sea level. Skilift Halde is the next resort, 3.6 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Bläsiberg Wiesensteig

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 3.5 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.2 km, or 63%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Bläsiberg Wiesensteig is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bläsiberg Wiesensteig gives 144 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 612 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 684 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; a snowy December gave 17 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 24 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 6 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and late December. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Bläsiberg Wiesensteig?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    How much snow does Bläsiberg Wiesensteig have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Bläsiberg Wiesensteig?

    Its highest lift reaches 756 m; the lowest starts at 612 m (144 m of vertical).

    Is Bläsiberg Wiesensteig suitable for families?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Bläsiberg Wiesensteig?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
