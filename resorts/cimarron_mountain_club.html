    About Cimarron Mountain Club

    Cimarron Mountain Club is a mid-sized ski area with about 21 km of pistes, number 98 of 175 in US West by length. Its lifts climb from 2,991 m to 3,461 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lee's Ski Hill, 31 km south, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 3,226 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May or later. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 15 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 30 March, with about 168 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 123 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 66 cm and 187 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 77 cm (lean years 29 cm, snowy years 138 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March.

    How Cimarron Mountain Club is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Cimarron Mountain Club gives 470 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,991 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.

    What there is to ski at Cimarron Mountain Club

    Counting every graded piste, there are 41 runs and around 20 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 7.5 km (38%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Experts can count on some 5.6 km of difficult runs.

    Quick answers

    When does Cimarron Mountain Club have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Cimarron Mountain Club have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 77 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Cimarron Mountain Club?

    The top is at 3,461 m and the base at 2,991 m.

    Does Cimarron Mountain Club have steep runs?

    Yes: about 5.6 km of its graded pistes (28%) are difficult.
