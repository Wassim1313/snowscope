    About Przełęcz Sokola

    Compact and local, Przełęcz Sokola has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 757 m and 837 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Górnik (Rzeczka) (0.4 km, west).

    The mountain at Przełęcz Sokola

    The vertical drop is 80 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 957 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 5 T-bars and 1 button lift. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
