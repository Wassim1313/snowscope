    About Gokazan

    Gokazan is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 28 m to 117 m. Its nearest neighbour is Engaru Rock Valley, 20 km south-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Gokazan is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Gokazan gives 89 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 28 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Engaru Rock Valley, reach 416 m. The only lift is a chairlift.
