    About Lyžařský vlek Visalaje

    The mountain at Lyžařský vlek Visalaje

    Skied top to bottom, Lyžařský vlek Visalaje gives 76 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 703 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 button lift and 1 rope tow.
