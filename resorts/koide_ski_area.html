    About Koide

    Koide is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 288 of 385 in Japan by length. The slopes start at 129 m and reach 258 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Yakushi, 3.9 km to the east.

    How Koide compares nearby

    Snow this week

    Up high, Koide has roughly 16 cm of snow on the ground today. 23 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Tuesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 129 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 129 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Muikamachi Hakkaisan, for one, climbs to 1,168 m.
