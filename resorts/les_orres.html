    About Les Orres

    With some 54 km of runs, Les Orres belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,554 m and reach 2,700 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Crévoux (7.6 km, north-east).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Les Orres gives 1,146 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,554 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.

    Other resorts close by

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Les Orres?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,700 m; the lowest starts at 1,554 m (1,146 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Les Orres?

    Espace Lumière, 17 km to the south, with about 185 km of pistes.

    How big is Les Orres?

    About 54 km of pistes and 21 lifts.
