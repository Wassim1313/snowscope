    About Ski Juwel

    Ski Juwel is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 715 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 654 m up to 1,229 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau, 6.6 km to the south-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Ski Juwel has 10 graded runs adding up to about 3.8 km. The largest share is red, with 2.8 km (72%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Ski Juwel

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Juwel gives 575 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 654 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Vorderes Sonnwendjoch at 2,224 m, standing 995 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Ski Juwel through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 942 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the typical depth is 19 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and early February. The snow is usually deepest around 29 January, with about 28 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 17 cm, with 33 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 14 cm and 43 cm by mid-February.

    Ski Juwel: common questions

    When does Ski Juwel have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and early February.

    How much snow does Ski Juwel have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 17 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Juwel?

    The top is at 1,229 m and the base at 654 m.

    Who is Ski Juwel best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 72% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Ski Juwel?

    Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau, 6.6 km to the south-east, with about 69 km of pistes.
