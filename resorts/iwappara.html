    About Iwappara

    Around 10 km of marked runs place Iwappara among the larger resorts of Japan, number 73 by length. The slopes start at 396 m and reach 984 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Yuzawa Park (2.8 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 15 runs and around 10 km to ski. Green runs make up the biggest part: 6.5 km, or 65%. With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The mountain at Iwappara

    From top to bottom that makes 588 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. At 396 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 984 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 7 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.
