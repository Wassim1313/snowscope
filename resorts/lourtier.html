    About Lourtier

    Lourtier is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,098 m and reach 1,192 m at the top. 4 Vallées is the next resort, 9.2 km away to the north.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is 4 Vallées: about 175 km of pistes, 9.2 km to the north. 4 Vallées has the longer snow season: around 27 weeks of solid cover, compared with 16 at Lourtier. The highest nearby is 4 Vallées, topping out at 3,304 m.

    The mountain at Lourtier

    94 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: 4 Vallées, for one, climbs to 3,304 m. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Petit-Combin, 3,663 m high and 7.4 km south of the resort.

    Snow at Lourtier through a normal winter

    At about 1,145 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 January and lasts to 30 April: about 16 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 24 cm to 52 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 37 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 25 cm; a snowy December gave 40 cm, a dry one 11 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. A normal season tops out near 41 cm, around 1 March.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hotel & Spa La Vallée (hotel), Cabane Brunet (mountain hut), Hôtel du Grand Combin (hotel) and Cabane de Louvie (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Lourtier: common questions

    When does Lourtier have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late April.

    Can you ski at Lourtier at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Lourtier at?

    The top is at 1,192 m and the base at 1,098 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Lourtier?

    4 Vallées, 9.2 km to the north, with about 175 km of pistes.

    How big is Lourtier?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
