    The snow right now

    Up high, Băile Homorod has roughly 3 cm of snow on the ground today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Friday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 52 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 727 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Madarasi Hargita, reach 1,676 m. The only lift is a drag lift.
