    About Ski Santa Fe

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski Santa Fe offers about 29 km of pistes (number 82 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 3,151 m to 3,686 m. Terrain Park is the next resort, 0.8 km away to the west.

    How much snow Ski Santa Fe usually gets

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 3,418 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 43 cm, with 177 cm in the best years. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 153 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 107 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 19 cm to 311 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 535 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 3,151 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. To get up there are 5 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Santa Fe Baldy (3,847 m), 5.4 km to the north-east, about 161 m higher than the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 80 marked runs cover roughly 28 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 40%. With 33% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 9.3 km of difficult runs.

    Quick answers

    When does Ski Santa Fe have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Ski Santa Fe have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 43 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Ski Santa Fe at?

    The top is at 3,686 m and the base at 3,151 m.

    Does Ski Santa Fe have steep runs?

    Yes: about 9.3 km of its graded pistes (33%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Ski Santa Fe have?

    About 29 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
