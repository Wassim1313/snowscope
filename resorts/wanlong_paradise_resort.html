    About Wanlong Paradise Resort

    Only 4 of the 92 ski areas of China are larger than Wanlong Paradise Resort, which has some 31 km of runs. The slopes start at 1,535 m and reach 2,102 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Genting Resort Secret Garden, 2.5 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 33 runs and around 31 km to ski. The largest share is black diamond, with 14 km (46%). With 46% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 14 km.

    The mountain at Wanlong Paradise Resort

    567 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,535 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 9 lifts break down into 5 gondolas, 3 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
