    About Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci

    Around 35 km of marked runs place Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 179 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,537 m to 2,233 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Splügen-Tambo, 13 km to the north.

    The pistes of Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci

    In all, 70 marked runs cover roughly 35 km. Most of the length is blue (42%, about 15 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.6 km. 2.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci gives 696 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,537 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 3 funiculars, 1 gondola, 8 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Pizzo di Emet, 3,211 m high and 5.3 km north-east of the resort.

    Snow at Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,885 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 69 cm, around 11 April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm; a snowy December gave 65 cm, a dry one 16 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 43 cm and 89 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 68 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci: common questions

    When does Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 34 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci?

    Can beginners ski at Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci?
