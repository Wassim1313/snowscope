    The snow at Le Semnoz, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Le Semnoz (about 1,552 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 74 cm; half of the winters sit between 22 cm and 128 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 125 cm, between 75 cm and 170 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 136 cm.

    41 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 65 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 500 cm. Worst: 2022-23, which never passed 46 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to early May (151 days).

    Questions about Le Semnoz

    Is there snow in Le Semnoz at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 74 cm at Christmas, and 65 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 20 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Le Semnoz?

    March: the snow peaks at about 136 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to early May.

    How snowy is Le Semnoz?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 136 cm in late March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 500 cm (1981-82), the thinnest 46 cm (2022-23).

    How high is Le Semnoz?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,693 m; the lowest starts at 1,412 m (281 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Le Semnoz have?

    About 16 km of pistes and 9 lifts.

    About Le Semnoz

    Neither huge nor tiny, Le Semnoz offers about 16 km of pistes (number 367 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,412 m to 1,693 m. The closest other ski area is Montmin (11 km, east).
