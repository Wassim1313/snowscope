    About Les Prés-d'Orvin

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Les Prés-d'Orvin sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,025 m and 1,227 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Nods, 7.7 km to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 10 graded runs, about 4.3 km in total. The largest share is red, with 2.8 km (66%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 202 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 2 T-bars and 3 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Mont Sujet (1,382 m), 2.2 km to the south-west, about 155 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Les Prés-d'Orvin usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,126 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 7 cm, around 23 January. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 19 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 29 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Other resorts close by

    Les Prés-d'Orvin: common questions

    When does Les Prés-d'Orvin have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Les Prés-d'Orvin have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Prés-d'Orvin?

    Between 1,025 m and 1,227 m, so 202 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Les Prés-d'Orvin best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Les Prés-d'Orvin?

    Bugnenets-Savagnières, 12 km to the west, with about 16 km of pistes.
