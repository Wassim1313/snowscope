    About Kesselmannlifte

    Compact and local, Kesselmannlifte has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 793 m to 857 m. Its nearest neighbour is Schmiedhornlift, 0.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 19 km south-east to Postalm, with about 8.0 km of runs. The highest nearby is Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen), topping out at 1,470 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 64 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 993 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Rossfeld, for one, climbs to 1,537 m.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Botenwirt (guesthouse), Pension Schierl (hotel), Bayerhammer (guesthouse) and Haus Leitgeb (guesthouse).
