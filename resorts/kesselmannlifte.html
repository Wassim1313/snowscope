    About Kesselmannlifte

    Compact and local, Kesselmannlifte has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 793 m to 857 m. Its nearest neighbour is Schmiedhornlift, 0.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 19 km south-east to Postalm, with about 8.0 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Postalm usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 22 weeks, against 0 here. The highest nearby is Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen), topping out at 1,470 m.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Kesselmannlifte today: about 1 cm up high. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 9 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 64 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 993 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Rossfeld, for one, climbs to 1,537 m. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 T-bar, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Wieserhörndl (1,567 m), 6.2 km to the south, about 710 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 825 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 28 cm, around 6 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 18 cm, with 38 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Year to year the snow is steady, with 10 cm to 48 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    When does Kesselmannlifte have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Can you ski at Kesselmannlifte at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 18 cm deep.

    What altitude is Kesselmannlifte at?

    The top is at 857 m and the base at 793 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Kesselmannlifte?

    Postalm, 19 km to the south-east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Kesselmannlifte have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
