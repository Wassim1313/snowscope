    About Kelly Canyon

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kelly Canyon offers about 24 km of pistes (number 90 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 1,770 m to 2,037 m.

    What there is to ski at Kelly Canyon

    The piste map lists 56 graded runs, about 24 km in total. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 46%. No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.8 km.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Kelly Canyon today: about 30 cm up high. The last seven days brought 27 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (21 cm).

    How Kelly Canyon is laid out

    267 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,770 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 4 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts and 1 rope tow. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Kelly Mountain (2,071 m), Lookout Mountain (2,046 m) and Table Rock (1,665 m).

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,904 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-April, about 19 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 46 cm, with 80 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 34 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 48 cm and 117 cm. A normal season tops out near 89 cm, around 28 February.

    Questions about Kelly Canyon

    When is the best time to ski at Kelly Canyon?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Kelly Canyon at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 46 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Kelly Canyon?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,037 m; the lowest starts at 1,770 m (267 m of vertical).

    Is Kelly Canyon suitable for families?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Kelly Canyon have?

    About 24 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
