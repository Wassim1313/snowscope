    The snow at Furutangen skisenter, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Furutangen skisenter (602 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 46 cm; half of the winters sit between 33 cm and 56 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 74 cm; the middle half of winters range from 65 cm to 87 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 77 cm.

    20 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 57 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1987-88 (142 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1995-96 (55 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 155 days.

    Questions about Furutangen skisenter

    Is there snow in Furutangen skisenter at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 46 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 57 % of 46 winters. Right now: 6 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Furutangen skisenter?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 77 cm). For a sure base, early December to early May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Furutangen skisenter get?

    About 77 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-March); between 55 cm in the leanest winter (1995-96) and 142 cm in the biggest (1987-88).

    How high do the lifts go at Furutangen skisenter?

    The top is at 661 m and the base at 543 m.
