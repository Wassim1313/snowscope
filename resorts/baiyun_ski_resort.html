    About Baiyun

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Baiyun sits in the middle of the size table of China. Its lifts climb from 1,679 m to 1,880 m. Silk Road is the next resort, 6.7 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 4.0 km in total. The largest share is black diamond, with 2.7 km (67%). With 67% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 2.7 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    201 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,679 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are gondolas. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Baiyun at?

    The top is at 1,880 m and the base at 1,679 m.

    Does Baiyun have steep runs?

    Yes: about 2.7 km of its graded pistes (67%) are difficult.

    How big is Baiyun?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
