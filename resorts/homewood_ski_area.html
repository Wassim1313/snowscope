    About Homewood

    Neither huge nor tiny, Homewood offers about 35 km of pistes (number 68 of 175 in US West). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,903 m up to 2,403 m. Its nearest neighbour is Homewood, 0.2 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 47 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Homewood. 18 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 58 cm by Saturday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 500 m: runs of a moderate length. At 1,903 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Heavenly reaches 3,037 m.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Tahoma Meadows Cottages (guesthouse), Sunnyside Restaurant & Lodge (hotel) and The Cottage Inn (hotel).
