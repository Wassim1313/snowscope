    About Mariánská Hora

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Mariánská Hora sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The slopes start at 682 m and reach 737 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Selský dvůr (0.9 km, south-west).

    How Mariánská Hora compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Jizerky: about 10 km of pistes, 1.5 km to the south-west. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Mariánská Hora gives 55 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 682 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 button lift and 1 drag lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 710 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 27 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 9 cm; a snowy December gave 43 cm, a dry one 1 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 48 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February.

    Questions about Mariánská Hora

    When does Mariánská Hora have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Is there snow at Mariánská Hora at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 9 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Mariánská Hora?

    Its highest lift reaches 737 m; the lowest starts at 682 m (55 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Mariánská Hora?

    Jizerky, 1.5 km to the south-west, with about 10 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Mariánská Hora have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
