    About Mariánská Hora

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Mariánská Hora sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The slopes start at 682 m and reach 737 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Selský dvůr (0.9 km, south-west).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Mariánská Hora gives 55 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 682 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
