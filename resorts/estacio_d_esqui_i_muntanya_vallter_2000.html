    About Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt

    With roughly 14 km of runs, Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. The slopes start at 2,010 m and reach 2,495 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Estació de muntanya Vall de Núri, 8.6 km to the west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    485 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,010 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 5 drag lifts and 3 moving carpets. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Bastiments, 2,881 m high and 1.8 km west of the resort.

    How much snow Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,252 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm, with 30 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 52 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 18 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 mountain huts: Refugi d'Ulldeter, Refugio de pastor, Refuge du Ras de La Carança and Refugi de Coma de Vaca. Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt

    What is the best month to ski at Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    How much snow does Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt have at Christmas?

    What altitude is Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt at?

    Can beginners ski at Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt?
