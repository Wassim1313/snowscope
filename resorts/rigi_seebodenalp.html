    About Rigi Seebodenalp

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Rigi Seebodenalp sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,032 m and 1,220 m above sea level. Rigi is the next resort, 1.9 km away to the south-east.

    How much snow Rigi Seebodenalp usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,126 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 December and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 20 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 29 cm and 83 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 48 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 56 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 50 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Rigi Seebodenalp has 3 graded runs adding up to about 2.7 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.1 km (80%). With 80% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 15 km south to Klewenalp, with about 18 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Klewenalp does better, with about 25 weeks of a good base instead of 20. The highest nearby is Klewenalp, topping out at 1,934 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Rigi Seebodenalp gives 188 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car and 1 T-bar, 2 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Rigi, 1,798 m high and 1.4 km south-east of the resort.

    Questions about Rigi Seebodenalp

    When is the best time to ski at Rigi Seebodenalp?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    How much snow does Rigi Seebodenalp have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 32 cm deep.

    What altitude is Rigi Seebodenalp at?

    The top is at 1,220 m and the base at 1,032 m.

    Is Rigi Seebodenalp good for beginners?

    Yes: 80% of the graded pistes, about 2.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rigi Seebodenalp?

    Klewenalp, 15 km to the south, with about 18 km of pistes.
