    The snow at Rigi Seebodenalp, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Rigi Seebodenalp (about 1,126 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 32 cm, with a normal range of 22 cm–45 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 55 cm; the middle half of winters range from 41 cm to 70 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 56 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 4 % of winters, and by 1 January in 11 %.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 128 cm. Worst: 2022-23, which never passed 34 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late December to early May (140 days).

    Rigi Seebodenalp: quick answers

    Is there snow in Rigi Seebodenalp at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 32 cm, and only 11 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is no snow.

    Which month has the most snow at Rigi Seebodenalp?

    February: the snow peaks at about 56 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to early May.

    How much snow does Rigi Seebodenalp get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 56 cm in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 128 cm (1981-82), the thinnest 34 cm (2022-23).

    What altitude is Rigi Seebodenalp at?

    The top is at 1,220 m and the base at 1,032 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rigi Seebodenalp?

    Klewenalp, 15 km to the south, with about 18 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Rigi Seebodenalp have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.

    About Rigi Seebodenalp

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Rigi Seebodenalp sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,032 m and 1,220 m above sea level. Rigi is the next resort, 1.9 km away to the south-east.

    Other resorts close by
