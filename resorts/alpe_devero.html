    The snow at Alpe Devero, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Alpe Devero (about 1,920 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 34 cm, with a normal range of 25 cm–48 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 61 cm, between 51 cm and 76 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 72 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 15 % of winters, and by 1 January in 20 %.

    The deepest winter was 2023-24 (136 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2004-05 (50 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to mid-May (156 days).

    This winter so far: 50 cm on 22 November, against a usual 13 cm for the date.

    Alpe Devero: quick answers

    Is there snow in Alpe Devero at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 34 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 20 % of 46 winters. Right now: 30 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Alpe Devero?

    March: the snow peaks at about 72 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to mid-May.

    How deep does the snow get at Alpe Devero?

    About 72 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early April); between 50 cm in the leanest winter (2004-05) and 136 cm in the biggest (2023-24).

    How high is Alpe Devero?

    The top is at 2,201 m and the base at 1,640 m.

    What level of skier is Alpe Devero best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 78% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Alpe Devero?

    San Domenico, 6.4 km to the south-west, with about 20 km of pistes.

    Alpe Devero is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 640 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,640 m to 2,201 m. Its nearest neighbour is San Domenico, 6.4 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Alpe Devero

    561 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,640 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Helsenhorn (3,272 m), 4.8 km to the west, about 1,071 m higher than the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Alpe Devero has roughly 30 cm of snow on the ground today. 11 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 20 cm by Wednesday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 18 cm now.

    Snow at Alpe Devero through a normal winter

    At about 1,920 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 December and lasts to 18 May: about 22 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 29 March, with about 72 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 71 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 89 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 34 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 21 cm, snowy years 55 cm).
