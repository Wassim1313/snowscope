    About Formiguères

    Neither huge nor tiny, Formiguères offers about 17 km of pistes (number 33 of 49 in the Pyrenees). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,738 m up to 2,305 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les Angles, 5.6 km to the south.

    Snow at Formiguères through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,022 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to late March, about 7 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 40 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 4 cm to 78 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm, with 52 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 12 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    What there is to ski at Formiguères

    The piste map lists 32 graded runs, about 17 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 6.9 km, or 39%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Formiguères gives 567 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,738 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,305 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift, 3 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets, 7 lifts in all. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Puig Peric, 2,810 m high and 5.8 km west of the resort.

    Formiguères: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Formiguères?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does Formiguères have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Formiguères?

    The top is at 2,305 m and the base at 1,738 m.

    Is Formiguères suitable for families?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 10 km, are easy.

    How big is Formiguères?

    About 17 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
