    About Hörnli

    Hörnli is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 897 m to 974 m. Its nearest neighbour is Schwellbrunn, 3.8 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Hörnli and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.8 km north to Degersheim, with about 5.0 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Wolzenalp (Krummenau) does better, with about 14 weeks of a good base instead of 0.

    What there is to ski at Hörnli

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.2 km to ski. Most of the length is red (56%, about 0.7 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    How Hörnli is laid out

    77 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 897 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Wolzenalp reaches 1,461 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Hinderfallenchopf (1,531 m), 6.5 km to the south-east, about 557 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Hörnli usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 936 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 17 cm, around 13 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 35 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 8 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 22 cm).

    Questions about Hörnli

    When is the best time to ski at Hörnli?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Hörnli have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Hörnli?

    Its highest lift reaches 974 m; the lowest starts at 897 m (77 m of vertical).

    Is Hörnli good for beginners?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 0.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Hörnli?

    Degersheim, 5.8 km to the north, with about 5.0 km of pistes.
