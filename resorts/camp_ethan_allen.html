    About Camp Ethan Allen

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Camp Ethan Allen one of the smaller places to ski in US Northeast. The ski area spans the mountain from 233 m up to 293 m. The closest other ski area is Cochran's (9.8 km, south).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Stowe (14 km north-east) has about 67 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Stowe does better, with about 16 weeks of a good base instead of 4. The highest nearby is Stowe, topping out at 1,112 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 60 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 233 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Oxbow Mountain at 747 m, standing 454 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 263 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 6 February and keeps it until about 7 March, roughly 4 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 12 cm to 61 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 37 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 35 cm, around 13 February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Questions about Camp Ethan Allen

    When does Camp Ethan Allen have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    How much snow does Camp Ethan Allen have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Camp Ethan Allen?

    Its highest lift reaches 293 m; the lowest starts at 233 m (60 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Camp Ethan Allen?

    Stowe, 14 km to the north-east, with about 67 km of pistes.

    How big is Camp Ethan Allen?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
