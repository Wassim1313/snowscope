    About Warth-Schröcken

    Warth-Schröcken is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 52 km of pistes put it at number 117 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,513 m to 2,051 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lech/Zürs, 7.0 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    How much snow Warth-Schröcken usually gets

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,782 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 111 cm to 232 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 160 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 189 cm, around 24 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 79 cm (lean years 31 cm, snowy years 118 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 538 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,513 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 14 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 12 chairlifts and 1 button lift. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Braunarlspitze at 2,649 m, standing 598 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Warth-Schröcken

    What is the best month to ski at Warth-Schröcken?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Warth-Schröcken have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 79 cm deep.

    When does Warth-Schröcken open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Warth-Schröcken?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,051 m; the lowest starts at 1,513 m (538 m of vertical).

    Is Warth-Schröcken suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 27 km, are easy.
