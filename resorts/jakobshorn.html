    About Jakobshorn

    Jakobshorn is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 37 km of pistes put it at number 167 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,531 m to 2,562 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Schatzalp/Strela, 2.8 km to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 60 marked runs cover roughly 37 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 22 km, or 60%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 6.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Jakobshorn is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,031 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,531 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 13 lifts break down into 3 cable cars, 6 chairlifts and 4 T-bars. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Älplihorn at 3,006 m, standing 444 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Jakobshorn usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,046 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 66 cm and 123 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 98 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 109 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 52 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 72 cm, a dry one 29 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 8 weeks.

    Questions about Jakobshorn

    When does Jakobshorn have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Jakobshorn at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 52 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Jakobshorn?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,562 m; the lowest starts at 1,531 m (1,031 m of vertical).

    Is Jakobshorn suitable for families?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 22 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Jakobshorn have?

    About 37 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
