    About Area Sciistica Abetone

    With some 39 km of runs, Area Sciistica Abetone belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,258 m up to 1,889 m. The closest other ski area is Comprensorio de Le Polle (9.1 km, north).

    The pistes of Area Sciistica Abetone

    In all, 87 marked runs cover roughly 39 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 24 km, or 62%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.5 km.

    How Area Sciistica Abetone is laid out

    631 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 10 chairlifts, 4 button lifts and 3 moving carpets, 18 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Cimone at 2,165 m, standing 276 m above the top lift.
