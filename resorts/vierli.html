    About Vierli

    Neither huge nor tiny, Vierli offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 135 of 262 in Scandinavia). Skiing here happens between 929 m and 1,116 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Rauland skisenter (2.1 km, west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 5.1 km to ski. Most of the length is red (62%, about 3.2 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 187 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,116 m is the third highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 4 drag lifts and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bosnuten at 1,431 m, standing 315 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,022 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 77 cm, from 49 cm in a poor year to 108 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. A normal season tops out near 136 cm, around 3 April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 128 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 87 cm and 157 cm by mid-February.

    Vierli: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Vierli?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Vierli at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 77 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Vierli at?

    Between 929 m and 1,116 m, so 187 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Vierli best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 62% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Vierli?

    Rauland skisenter, 2.1 km to the west, with about 11 km of pistes.
