    About Geilo

    With some 13 km of runs, Geilo belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 779 m to 1,065 m. The closest other ski area is Ski Geilo (1.9 km, north).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 20 graded runs, about 13 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.4 km, or 40%. With 55% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 922 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 November and keeps it until about 26 May, roughly 27 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 4 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 60 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 90 cm, a dry one 40 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 95 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is usually deepest around 20 March, with about 103 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 70 cm and 120 cm.

    The mountain at Geilo

    From top to bottom that makes 286 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 9 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 4 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Havsdalshovda, 1,173 m high and 4.9 km north of the resort.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Geilo hold around 11 cm of snow today. More is on the way: about 5 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 45 cm by now.

    Questions about Geilo

    When does Geilo have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Geilo at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 60 cm deep.

    What altitude is Geilo at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,065 m; the lowest starts at 779 m (286 m of vertical).

    Is Geilo suitable for families?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 7.5 km, are easy.

    How big is Geilo?

    About 13 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
