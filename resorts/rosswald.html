    About Rosswald

    Rosswald is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 670 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,835 m to 2,514 m. The closest other ski area is Rothwald (3.5 km, south).

    Rosswald and the resorts around it

    How Rosswald is laid out

    679 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 1,835 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.

    Rosswald today and the next days

    Up high, Rosswald has roughly 46 cm of snow on the ground today. 17 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 34 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.
