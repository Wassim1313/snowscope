    About Obermutten

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Obermutten one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,730 m up to 1,865 m. Lantsch/Lenz is the next resort, 6.4 km away to the east.

    Snow at Obermutten through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,798 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 11 December and keeps it until about 14 May, roughly 22 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 39 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 60 cm, a dry one 21 cm. A normal season tops out near 77 cm, around 22 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 90 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 69 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    The pistes of Obermutten

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. Most of the length is blue (52%, about 0.7 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Obermutten and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km north-east to Arosa Lenzerheide, with about 164 km of runs. Arosa Lenzerheide has the longer snow season: around 25 weeks of solid cover, compared with 22 at Obermutten. For higher slopes, Arosa Lenzerheide climbs to 2,854 m.

    How Obermutten is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Obermutten gives 135 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,730 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Piz Curvér at 2,972 m, standing 1,107 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Obermutten?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Obermutten at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Obermutten at?

    Between 1,730 m and 1,865 m, so 135 m of vertical drop.

    Is Obermutten suitable for families?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Obermutten?

    Arosa Lenzerheide, 11 km to the north-east, with about 164 km of pistes.
