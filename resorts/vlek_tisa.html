    About vlek Tisá

    vlek Tisá has about 0.4 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 516 m up to 576 m. The closest other ski area is Zadní Telnice (9.0 km, south-west).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 546 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 8 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 25 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, with 21 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and late December.

    The mountain at vlek Tisá

    Skied top to bottom, vlek Tisá gives 60 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 516 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Bouřňák reaches 870 m. The only lift is a button lift.

    Questions about vlek Tisá

    When is the best time to ski at vlek Tisá?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    Can you ski at vlek Tisá at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    What altitude is vlek Tisá at?

    Between 516 m and 576 m, so 60 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does vlek Tisá have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
