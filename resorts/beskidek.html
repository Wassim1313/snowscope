    About Beskidek

    Beskidek has about 0.2 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 530 m up to 545 m. Beskid Sport Arena is the next resort, 1.3 km away to the west.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Beskidek hold around 10 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    Lifts and terrain

    15 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 530 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at COS Szczyrk, reach 1,244 m. The only lift is a moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
