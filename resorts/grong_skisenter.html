    About Grong skisenter

    With some 16 km of runs, Grong skisenter belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 235 m and 684 m above sea level.

    What there is to ski at Grong skisenter

    Counting every graded piste, there are 20 runs and around 16 km to ski. The largest share is green (beginner), with 6.9 km (42%). With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 449 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Geitfjellet (872 m), 5.3 km to the north-west, about 188 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Grong skisenter has roughly 8 cm of snow on the ground today. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 30 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 460 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 50 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 29 cm, snowy years 84 cm). Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 116 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 102 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 8 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 65 cm to 146 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Heia Gjestegård (hotel), Rundtjønnhytta (chalet) and Grong Vertshus (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Grong skisenter?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Grong skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 50 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Grong skisenter at?

    Its highest lift reaches 684 m; the lowest starts at 235 m (449 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Grong skisenter?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Grong skisenter have?

    About 16 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
