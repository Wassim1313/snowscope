    About Urabandai

    Neither huge nor tiny, Urabandai offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 268 of 385 in Japan). Its lifts climb from 908 m to 1,104 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hoshino Resorts Nekoma Mountain, 4.2 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 2.6 km to ski. Most of the length is beginner (50%, about 1.3 km). With 88% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Urabandai

    196 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,108 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Is Urabandai suitable for families?
