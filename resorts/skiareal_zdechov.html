    About Skiareál Zděchov

    Skiareál Zděchov is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 519 m up to 593 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lyžařský areál Lužná, 5.0 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Skiareál Zděchov

    74 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 519 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Kohútka, reach 939 m.

    Skiareál Zděchov: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Skiareál Zděchov?

    Its highest lift reaches 593 m; the lowest starts at 519 m (74 m of vertical).
