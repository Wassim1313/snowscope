    About Selce

    Neither huge nor tiny, Selce offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 159 of 312 in the Carpathians). The ski area spans the mountain from 464 m up to 638 m. The closest other ski area is Šachtičky (3.0 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 1.8 km in total. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Selce is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 174 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 464 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Donovaly, for one, climbs to 1,356 m. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 T-bars and 1 button lift.

    Questions about Selce

    How high is Selce?

    Its highest lift reaches 638 m; the lowest starts at 464 m (174 m of vertical).

    Is Selce suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Selce?

    Donovaly, 10 km to the north, with about 9.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Selce have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
