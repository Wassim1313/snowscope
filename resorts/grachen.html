    About Grächen

    With roughly 17 km of runs, Grächen sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,620 m up to 2,863 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gspon, 4.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Grächen is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Grächen gives 1,243 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Starting at 1,620 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The lift system counts 15 lifts: 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 1 button lift and 4 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Balfrin (3,795 m), 7.0 km to the south, about 932 m higher than the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Grächen

    The piste map lists 32 graded runs, about 17 km in total. The largest share is red, with 11 km (67%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Snow at Grächen through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,242 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 14 December and keeps it until about 24 May, roughly 23 weeks. Most winters land between 44 cm and 79 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 63 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 64 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm (lean years 23 cm, snowy years 56 cm). For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Grächen: common questions

    When does Grächen have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late May.

    Can you ski at Grächen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Grächen?

    The top is at 2,863 m and the base at 1,620 m.

    Who is Grächen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Grächen?

    Unterbäch, 10 km to the north-west, with about 37 km of pistes.
