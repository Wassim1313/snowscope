    About Le Schlumpf

    Le Schlumpf is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 675 m up to 1,014 m. The closest other ski area is Ballon d'Alsace (5.3 km, west).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.3 km west to Ballon d'Alsace, with about 8.0 km of runs. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    Snow at Le Schlumpf through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 844 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm, with 36 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 27 January, with about 9 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and early February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 52 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    How Le Schlumpf is laid out

    339 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 675 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Haute Bers at 1,252 m, standing 238 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Le Schlumpf

    When does Le Schlumpf have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Can you ski at Le Schlumpf at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 4 cm).

    What altitude is Le Schlumpf at?

    The top is at 1,014 m and the base at 675 m.

    What level of skier is Le Schlumpf best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Le Schlumpf?

    Ballon d'Alsace, 5.3 km to the west, with about 8.0 km of pistes.
