    About Golzentipp/Obertilliach

    Golzentipp/Obertilliach is a mid-sized ski area with about 11 km of pistes, number 464 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,431 m up to 2,225 m. Lanalift Anras is the next resort, 7.9 km away to the north.

    Snow at Golzentipp/Obertilliach through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,828 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 58 cm to 127 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 82 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 89 cm, around 3 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 49 cm; a snowy December gave 92 cm, a dry one 20 cm.

    The mountain at Golzentipp/Obertilliach

    From top to bottom that makes 794 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,431 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,225 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola and 4 T-bars, 5 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Eggenkofel (2,591 m), 5.0 km to the north-east, about 366 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Golzentipp/Obertilliach have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Golzentipp/Obertilliach at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 49 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Golzentipp/Obertilliach?

    Between 1,431 m and 2,225 m, so 794 m of vertical drop.

    Is Golzentipp/Obertilliach good for beginners?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 6.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Golzentipp/Obertilliach?

    About 11 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
