    About Les Paccots

    Les Paccots is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 522 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,064 m and 1,487 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Les Pléiades (3.5 km, south-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 20 graded runs, about 9.4 km in total. Most of the length is blue (64%, about 6.0 km). With 64% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How much snow Les Paccots usually gets

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,276 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 56 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 105 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 43 cm and 139 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 90 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 94 cm, around 17 February.

    The mountain at Les Paccots

    The vertical drop is 423 m: runs of a moderate length. All 9 lifts here are button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Dent de Lys at 2,014 m, standing 527 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Les Paccots have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Les Paccots at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 56 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Les Paccots at?

    Between 1,064 m and 1,487 m, so 423 m of vertical drop.

    Is Les Paccots good for beginners?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 6.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Les Paccots?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
