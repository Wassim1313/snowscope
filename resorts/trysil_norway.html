    How much snow Trysil usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 569 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 10 December and keeps it until about 2 May, roughly 21 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 44 cm, with 67 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 62 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 11 March, with about 76 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 57 cm to 99 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Trysil: common questions

    When does Trysil have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Trysil at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 44 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
