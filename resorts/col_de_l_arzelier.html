    About Col de l'Arzelier

    Col de l'Arzelier has about 0.1 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,157 m to 1,164 m. The closest other ski area is Villard de Lans (4.4 km, north-west).

    How Col de l'Arzelier is laid out

    The vertical drop is 7 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Villard de Lans, for one, climbs to 2,055 m. The highest named summit within 8 km is La Grande Moucherolle at 2,285 m, standing 1,121 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Col de l'Arzelier through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,160 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 62 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 28 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 61 cm.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find La Soldanelle (mountain hut), Gîte Le Pas de l'Oeille (guesthouse), Gîte l'Ancienne École (chalet) and Chalet de Pré Clos (chalet).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Col de l'Arzelier?

    Aim for early January and mid-March.

    Is there snow at Col de l'Arzelier at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    What altitude is Col de l'Arzelier at?

    Between 1,157 m and 1,164 m, so 7 m of vertical drop.
