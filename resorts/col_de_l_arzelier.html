    About Col de l'Arzelier

    Col de l'Arzelier is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 1,157 m to 1,164 m. The closest other ski area is Villard de Lans (4.4 km, north-west).

    How Col de l'Arzelier is laid out

    The vertical drop is 7 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Villard de Lans, for one, climbs to 2,055 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find La Soldanelle (mountain hut), Gîte Le Pas de l'Oeille (guesthouse), Gîte l'Ancienne École (chalet) and Chalet de Pré Clos (chalet).
