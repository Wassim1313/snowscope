    About Lanalift Anras

    Lanalift Anras has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,265 m to 1,376 m. The closest other ski area is Skilift Compedal (3.7 km, east).

    How Lanalift Anras is laid out

    The vertical drop is 111 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Sillian, reach 2,399 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Gölbner, 2,943 m high and 7.5 km north-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,320 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the typical depth is 12 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the best snow, aim for early January and early February. A normal season tops out near 25 cm, around 1 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm, with 38 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 11 cm and 49 cm.
