    About Eastlink Park

    Compact and local, Eastlink Park has about 4.0 km of runs, small for Canada West. Its lifts climb from 700 m to 750 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 4.0 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 2.1 km, or 52%. Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    The mountain at Eastlink Park

    The vertical drop is 50 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 700 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Eastlink Park has roughly 9 cm of snow on the ground today. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 8 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Snow at Eastlink Park through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 725 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 29 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 44 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 10 cm to 54 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and mid-February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Questions about Eastlink Park

    When is the best time to ski at Eastlink Park?

    Aim for mid-January and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Eastlink Park at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Eastlink Park?

    Between 700 m and 750 m, so 50 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Eastlink Park best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Eastlink Park have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
