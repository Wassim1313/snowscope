    About San Vito di Cadore

    Neither huge nor tiny, San Vito di Cadore offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 597 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,096 m up to 1,569 m. Cristallo is the next resort, 11 km away to the north.

    The pistes of San Vito di Cadore

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 7.1 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.9 km, or 69%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How San Vito di Cadore is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, San Vito di Cadore gives 473 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Cortina d'Ampezzo reaches 2,739 m. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Antelao (3,264 m), 3.0 km to the south-east, about 1,695 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at San Vito di Cadore through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,332 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 12 cm and 44 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 15 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 30 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 8 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 23 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early February.

    Questions about San Vito di Cadore

    What is the best month to ski at San Vito di Cadore?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Can you ski at San Vito di Cadore at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 15 cm deep.

    What altitude is San Vito di Cadore at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,569 m; the lowest starts at 1,096 m (473 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is San Vito di Cadore best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near San Vito di Cadore?

    Cortina d'Ampezzo, 11 km to the north-west, with about 113 km of pistes.
