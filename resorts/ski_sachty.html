    About Ski Šachty

    Around 5.0 km of marked runs place Ski Šachty among the larger resorts of Central Europe, number 60 by length. Skiing here happens between 511 m and 707 m above sea level. Lyžařský areál Metlák is the next resort, 0.5 km away to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 4.3 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.3 km (52%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Šachty gives 196 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 511 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 707 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 4 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    How Ski Šachty compares nearby

    In this group of 7, Ski Šachty is number 2 for piste length. If snow is the priority, Paseky nad Jizerou does better, with about 7 weeks of a good base instead of 4.

    Snow at Ski Šachty through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 609 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to late February, about 4 weeks of good cover. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 49 cm). Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 57 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 33 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February.

    Quick answers

    When does Ski Šachty have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from late January to late February.

    Can you ski at Ski Šachty at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Ski Šachty?

    Its highest lift reaches 707 m; the lowest starts at 511 m (196 m of vertical).

    Is Ski Šachty suitable for families?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 2.3 km, are easy.

    How big is Ski Šachty?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
