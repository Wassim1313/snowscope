    About Ski Šachty

    Around 5.0 km of marked runs place Ski Šachty among the larger resorts of Central Europe, number 60 by length. Skiing here happens between 511 m and 707 m above sea level. Lyžařský areál Metlák is the next resort, 0.5 km away to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Šachty gives 196 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 511 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 707 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
