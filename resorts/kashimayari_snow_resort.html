    About Kashimayari Snow Resort

    Kashimayari Snow Resort is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 9.0 km of pistes put it at number 92 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 842 m up to 1,333 m. The closest other ski area is Hakuba Sanosaka Snow Resort (2.7 km, north-east).

    The pistes of Kashimayari Snow Resort

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 8.6 km. The largest share is red, with 4.8 km (56%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.6 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 491 m: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 842 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 5 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    What altitude is Kashimayari Snow Resort at?

    What level of skier is Kashimayari Snow Resort best for?

    Hakuba Happo One, 12 km to the north, with about 30 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Kashimayari Snow Resort have?
