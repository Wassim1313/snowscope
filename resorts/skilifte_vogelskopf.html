    About Skilifte Vogelskopf

    Skilifte Vogelskopf is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 877 m up to 974 m. The closest other ski area is Ausflugszentrum Ruhestein (1.1 km, north).

    The mountain at Skilifte Vogelskopf

    97 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,077 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Skizirkus Unterstmatt, reach 1,114 m.
