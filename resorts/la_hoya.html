    About La Hoya

    With roughly 10 km of runs, La Hoya sits in the middle of the size table of Andes. The slopes start at 1,424 m and reach 2,081 m at the top.

    The pistes of La Hoya

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 10 km. The largest share is blue, with 5.1 km (50%). With 50% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    GradeRunsLengthShare

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 657 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,424 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 8 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts, 3 T-bars and 1 rope tow.

    La Hoya: common questions

    How high is La Hoya?

    Between 1,424 m and 2,081 m, so 657 m of vertical drop.

    Is La Hoya good for beginners?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 5.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does La Hoya have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
