    About Kocierz

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Kocierz sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 643 m up to 732 m. Czarny Groń is the next resort, 4.4 km away to the east.

    Kocierz and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Stacja Narciarska Czarny Groń (5.4 km east) has about 2.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. For higher slopes, Szyndzielnia climbs to 1,008 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 89 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 643 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
