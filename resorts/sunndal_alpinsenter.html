    What 46 winters say about Sunndal alpinsenter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Sunndal alpinsenter (about 368 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 85 cm, with a normal range of 70 cm–113 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 141 cm; the middle half of winters range from 114 cm to 172 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 164 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 83 % of winters, and by 1 January in 100 %.

    Best winter on record: 1996-97, peaking at 267 cm. Worst: 2025-26, which never passed 117 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early November to late May (198 days).

    Getting to Sunndal alpinsenter and what it costs

    Questions about Sunndal alpinsenter

    Is there snow in Sunndal alpinsenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 85 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 100 % of 46 winters. Right now: 19 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Sunndal alpinsenter?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 164 cm). For a sure base, early November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Sunndal alpinsenter?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 164 cm in early April. The deepest of 46 winters reached 267 cm (1996-97), the thinnest 117 cm (2025-26).

    How high is Sunndal alpinsenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 525 m; the lowest starts at 211 m (314 m of vertical).

    How big is Sunndal alpinsenter?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.

    About Sunndal alpinsenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sunndal alpinsenter offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 131 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 211 m up to 525 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Surnadal alpinsenter, 21 km to the north.
