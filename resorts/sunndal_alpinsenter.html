    About Sunndal alpinsenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sunndal alpinsenter offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 131 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 211 m up to 525 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Surnadal alpinsenter, 21 km to the north.

    What there is to ski at Sunndal alpinsenter

    Sunndal alpinsenter has 10 graded runs adding up to about 5.1 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 3.5 km, or 69%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Snow at Sunndal alpinsenter through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 368 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. The snow is usually deepest around 7 April, with about 164 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 163 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 97 cm and 189 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 19 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 85 cm (lean years 51 cm, snowy years 135 cm).

    This week's snow

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Sunndal alpinsenter gives 314 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Grøvelnebba (1,428 m), 6.3 km to the south, about 903 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Sunndal alpinsenter

    When does Sunndal alpinsenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Sunndal alpinsenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 85 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Sunndal alpinsenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 525 m; the lowest starts at 211 m (314 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Sunndal alpinsenter best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Sunndal alpinsenter?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
