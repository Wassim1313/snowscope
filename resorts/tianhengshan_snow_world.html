    About Tianhengshan Snow World

    Tianhengshan Snow World has about 0.4 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 131 m up to 186 m. Yueliang Wan is the next resort, 16 km away to the west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Tianhengshan Snow World gives 55 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 131 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Jihua Changshoushan, for one, climbs to 451 m.

    Tianhengshan Snow World: common questions

    How high is Tianhengshan Snow World?

    Its highest lift reaches 186 m; the lowest starts at 131 m (55 m of vertical).
