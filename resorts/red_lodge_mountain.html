    About Red Lodge Mountain

    Red Lodge Mountain is a mid-sized ski area with about 33 km of pistes, number 73 of 175 in US West by length. Its lifts climb from 2,161 m to 2,858 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Beartooth Basin Summer, 24 km to the south.

    The pistes of Red Lodge Mountain

    Counting every graded piste, there are 58 runs and around 33 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 14 km (43%). Good skiers will like it here; 39% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 13 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,510 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early November to late May or later, about 29 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 163 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 168 cm, around 1 April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 87 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 54 cm, snowy years 149 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 20 weeks. Most winters land between 94 cm and 197 cm by mid-February.

    How Red Lodge Mountain is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 697 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. At 2,161 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Its 7 lifts break down into 6 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Nichols Peak (2,859 m), Grizzly Peak (2,851 m) and Towne Point (2,429 m).

    Red Lodge Mountain: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Red Lodge Mountain?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at Red Lodge Mountain at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 87 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Red Lodge Mountain?

    Between 2,161 m and 2,858 m, so 697 m of vertical drop.

    Does Red Lodge Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 13 km of its graded pistes (39%) are difficult.

    How big is Red Lodge Mountain?

    About 33 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
