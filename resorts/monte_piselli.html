    About Monte Piselli

    Monte Piselli is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 20 of 40 in the Apennines by length. Skiing here happens between 1,429 m and 1,649 m above sea level. Prato Selva is the next resort, 30 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Monte Piselli

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 6.0 km. The largest share is freeride, with 3.2 km (53%). Good skiers will like it here; 68% of the runs are steep. 3.2 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    220 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,429 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Monte Piselli: common questions

    How high is Monte Piselli?

    Between 1,429 m and 1,649 m, so 220 m of vertical drop.

    Does Monte Piselli have steep runs?

    Yes: about 4.1 km of its graded pistes (68%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Monte Piselli have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
