    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 32 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Alpe Cermis reaches 2,250 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Monte Fregasoga (2,447 m), 7.8 km to the north-east, about 1,394 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Pradis_ci usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,037 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 10 cm, with 50 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 10 January, with about 16 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and mid-March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 55 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.
