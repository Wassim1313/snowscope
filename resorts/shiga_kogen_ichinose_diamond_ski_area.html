    About Shiga Kogen Ichinose Diamond

    Around 11 km of marked runs place Shiga Kogen Ichinose Diamond among the larger resorts of Japan, number 60 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,606 m and 1,710 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Shiga Kogen Ichinose Yamanokami, 0.4 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Shiga Kogen Ichinose Diamond

    The piste map lists 17 graded runs, about 11 km in total. Green runs make up the biggest part: 7.8 km, or 72%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Shiga Kogen Ichinose Diamond gives 104 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,606 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Shiga Kogen Yakebitaiyama reaches 1,998 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
