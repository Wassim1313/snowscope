    About Schwärzenlifte Eschach

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Schwärzenlifte Eschach sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,005 m and reach 1,067 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wengen, 4.9 km to the south-west.

    Snow at Schwärzenlifte Eschach through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,036 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 6 January and keeps it until about 10 April, roughly 14 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 26 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 50 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 44 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 28 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Year to year the snow is steady, with 17 cm to 80 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    Runs by difficulty

    Schwärzenlifte Eschach has 11 graded runs adding up to about 2.8 km. Most of the length is blue (83%, about 2.3 km). With 83% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 62 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,067 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 4 drag lifts and 9 moving carpets. First-timers get 9 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Ursersberg (1,129 m), Hauptmannsberg (1,129 m) and Änger (1,123 m).

    Schwärzenlifte Eschach: common questions

    When does Schwärzenlifte Eschach have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Can you ski at Schwärzenlifte Eschach at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 26 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Schwärzenlifte Eschach?

    The top is at 1,067 m and the base at 1,005 m.

    Is Schwärzenlifte Eschach good for beginners?

    Yes: 83% of the graded pistes, about 2.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Schwärzenlifte Eschach have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
