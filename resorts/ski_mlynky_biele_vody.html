    About Ski Mlynky Biele Vody

    Ski Mlynky Biele Vody is a small ski area by the standards of the Carpathians, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 782 m to 870 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ski centrum Dedinky, 0.3 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 88 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 982 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 2 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    How Ski Mlynky Biele Vody compares nearby
