    About Skiareál Peklák

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skiareál Peklák one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 399 m up to 544 m. Its nearest neighbour is Přívrat, 3.4 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Skiareál Peklák

    The vertical drop is 145 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 399 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift.
