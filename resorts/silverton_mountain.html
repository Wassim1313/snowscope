    What 46 winters say about Silverton Mountain

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Silverton Mountain (10,643 ft (3,244 m)), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 31 in (78 cm), with a normal range of 19 in (49 cm)–42 in (107 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 47 in (120 cm) (35 in (90 cm)–57 in (145 cm)).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 54 in (136 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 65 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 78 %.

    The deepest winter was 1986-87 (93 in (237 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (20 in (51 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs mid-November to early May (176 days).

    So far this winter: 31 in (78 cm) on 21 November (usual: 10 in (26 cm)).

    Silverton Mountain: quick answers

    Does Silverton Mountain have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 31 in (78 cm) at Christmas, and 78 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 35 in (90 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Silverton Mountain?

    March: the snow peaks at about 54 in (136 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to early May.

    How deep does the snow get at Silverton Mountain?

    About 54 in (136 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (mid-March); between 20 in (51 cm) in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 93 in (237 cm) in the biggest (1986-87).

    When does Silverton Mountain have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    How high do the lifts go at Silverton Mountain?

    The top is at 3,345 m and the base at 3,143 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Silverton Mountain?

    Telluride, 16 km to the west, with about 101 km of pistes.

    About Silverton Mountain

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Silverton Mountain one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Its lifts climb from 3,143 m to 3,345 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kendall Mountain, 7.3 km south, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Silverton Mountain through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 3,244 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 November and keeps it until about 8 May, roughly 24 weeks. For about 14 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 94 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 136 cm, around 9 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 71 cm and 173 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 78 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 16 cm, snowy years 128 cm).

    This week's snow

    Up high, Silverton Mountain has roughly 90 cm of snow on the ground today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Tuesday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 43 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    202 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 3,143 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its top at 3,345 m is the third highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a chairlift. The skyline is dominated by Tower Mountain, 4,119 m high and 3.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 16 km west to Telluride, with about 101 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Cimarron Mountain Club does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 24. Telluride reaches 3,812 m, the most of the resorts listed.
