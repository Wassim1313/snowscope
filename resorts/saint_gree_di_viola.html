    About Saint Grée di Viola

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Saint Grée di Viola sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,053 m to 1,739 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sangiacomo Cardini, 5.9 km to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,396 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 70 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 8 February, with about 16 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm, with 36 cm in the best years.

    Lifts and terrain

    686 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bric di Mezzodì at 1,945 m, standing 206 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Albergo Alpi (hotel), Agriturismo La Crosa (guesthouse), B&B Antico Convento (guesthouse) and Chalets Mongioie (chalet).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Saint Grée di Viola?

    Aim for mid-February and early March.

    Is there snow at Saint Grée di Viola at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Saint Grée di Viola at?

    Between 1,053 m and 1,739 m, so 686 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Saint Grée di Viola best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 46% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Saint Grée di Viola?

    Artesina, 16 km to the west, with about 39 km of pistes.
