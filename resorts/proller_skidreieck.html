    About Pröller Skidreieck

    With some 5.0 km of runs, Pröller Skidreieck belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 762 m up to 1,034 m. The closest other ski area is Sankt Englmar (Grün-Pröller) (2.4 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 4.8 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.9 km, or 60%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 272 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 762 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 898 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 11 February, with about 27 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 0 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 77 cm.

    Questions about Pröller Skidreieck

    When does Pröller Skidreieck have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Pröller Skidreieck at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Pröller Skidreieck?

    The top is at 1,034 m and the base at 762 m.

    Is Pröller Skidreieck good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 2.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Pröller Skidreieck?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
