    About Varkaus

    Varkaus is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 78 m and 124 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Pääskyvuori, 39 km east, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 15 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 101 m in an ordinary winter, from early January to mid-April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 29 cm and 67 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 31 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 26 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 44 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 51 cm, around 26 February.

    The mountain at Varkaus

    46 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar.

    Varkaus today and the next days

    Varkaus has no snow cover yet today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 8 cm at mid-mountain).

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Varkaus include Hotel Oscar (hotel), Hotelli Vanha Postitalo (hotel), Pusinharjun majatalo (guesthouse) and Kirvesranta (chalet).

    Varkaus: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Varkaus?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Varkaus at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 26 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Varkaus at?

    Between 78 m and 124 m, so 46 m of vertical drop.
