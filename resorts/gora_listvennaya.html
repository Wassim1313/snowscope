    About Гора Лиственная (Gora Listvennay

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Гора Лиственная gives 69 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 4 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Гора Волчиха (Volchikha) reaches 499 m. All 4 lifts here are T-bars. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
