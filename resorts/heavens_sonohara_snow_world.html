    About Heavens Sonohara Snow World

    Neither huge nor tiny, Heavens Sonohara Snow World offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 167 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,376 m up to 1,583 m. Its nearest neighbour is Jibusaka, 12 km south, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 4.3 km in total. Most of the length is green (79%, about 3.4 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    207 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,376 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 gondola, 2 chair-gondolas and 3 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns.
