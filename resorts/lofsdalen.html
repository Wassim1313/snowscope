    About Lofsdalen

    Lofsdalen is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 27 km of pistes put it at number 20 of 262. Its lifts climb from 629 m to 1,106 m. Its nearest neighbour is Idre Fjäll, 35 km south-west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Lofsdalen

    Lofsdalen has 32 graded runs adding up to about 27 km. The largest share is blue, with 9.2 km (34%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Lofsdalen gives 477 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,106 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 4 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 10 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Svanhågna (1,176 m), Hovärken (1,125 m) and Härdalsstöten (1,090 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 868 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 9 December and lasts to 5 May: about 21 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 65 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 56 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 86 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 40 cm, from 20 cm in a poor year to 63 cm in a big one.

    Lofsdalen: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Lofsdalen?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Lofsdalen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 40 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Lofsdalen?

    Between 629 m and 1,106 m, so 477 m of vertical drop.

    Is Lofsdalen suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Lofsdalen have?

    About 27 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
