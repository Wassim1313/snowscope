    About Jihua Changshoushan

    Neither huge nor tiny, Jihua Changshoushan offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 30 of 92 in China). The slopes start at 250 m and reach 451 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Longzhu Erlongshan, 17 km north-east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Jihua Changshoushan

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 5.7 km in total. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 4.1 km, or 72%. Good skiers will like it here; 72% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 4.1 km of black or expert runs.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 201 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 250 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
