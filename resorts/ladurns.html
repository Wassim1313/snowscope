    About Ladurns

    Ladurns is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 436 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,138 m up to 2,022 m. The closest other ski area is Rosskopf (5.5 km, south-east).

    The pistes of Ladurns

    Counting every graded piste, there are 14 runs and around 12 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 5.6 km, or 48%. No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.1 km.

    Ladurns and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 18 km north to Stubai, with about 95 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Stubai does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 17. For higher slopes, Stubai climbs to 3,208 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,580 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 26 December and keeps it until about 24 April, roughly 17 weeks. A normal season tops out near 47 cm, around 16 February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 30 cm to 67 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 29 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 49 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 34 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 884 m: enough height for proper long runs. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 1 button lift. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. Above the ski area rises Pflerscher Tribulaun - Tribulaun di Fleres (3,097 m), 5.4 km to the north-west, about 1,075 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Ladurns?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Ladurns at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 29 cm deep.

    What altitude is Ladurns at?

    Between 1,138 m and 2,022 m, so 884 m of vertical drop.

    Is Ladurns suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 5.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ladurns?

    Stubai, 18 km to the north, with about 95 km of pistes.
