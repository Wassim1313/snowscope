    About Horní Kouty

    Horní Kouty is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 524 m to 649 m. Its nearest neighbour is Monínec, 24 km west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    125 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 524 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Snow at Horní Kouty through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 586 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 15 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 7 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for late January and early March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 18 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Horní Kouty have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early March.

    Can you ski at Horní Kouty at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Horní Kouty?

    The top is at 649 m and the base at 524 m.
