    Snow at Schulenberg through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 574 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 7 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 21 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 21 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February.
