    Les Ménuires in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Les Ménuires (about 2,112 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 95 cm; half of the winters sit between 65 cm and 120 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 168 cm (134 cm–188 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 177 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    74 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 98 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1994-95 (279 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2021-22 (143 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-November to early June (197 days).

    Questions about Les Ménuires

    Can you ski Les Ménuires at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 95 cm at Christmas, and 98 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 112 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Les Ménuires?

    March: the snow peaks at about 177 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to early June.

    How much snow does Les Ménuires get?

    About 177 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 143 cm in the leanest winter (2021-22) and 279 cm in the biggest (1994-95).

    How high do the lifts go at Les Ménuires?

    The top is at 2,838 m and the base at 1,386 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Les Ménuires?

    Les Trois Vallées, 3.5 km to the north-east, with about 542 km of pistes.

    How big is Les Ménuires?

    About 120 km of pistes and 33 lifts.

    About Les Ménuires

    Around 120 km of marked runs place Les Ménuires among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 38 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,386 m and 2,838 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les Trois Vallées, 3.5 km to the north-east.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Les Ménuires has roughly 112 cm of snow on the ground today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 85 cm by Tuesday.

    From top to bottom that makes 1,452 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,386 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
