    About Les Ménuires

    Around 120 km of marked runs place Les Ménuires among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 38 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,386 m and 2,838 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les Trois Vallées, 3.5 km to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Les Ménuires

    In all, 164 marked runs cover roughly 120 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 74 km, or 61%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 9.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,452 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,386 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 33 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 funicular, 6 gondolas, 11 chairlifts, 5 button lifts and 9 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 9 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Cime de Caron, 3,195 m high and 7.9 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,112 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 177 cm. For about 19 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 115 cm to 205 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 95 cm, with 139 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 165 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Les Ménuires?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Les Ménuires at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 95 cm deep.

    When does Les Ménuires open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 18 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Ménuires?

    The top is at 2,838 m and the base at 1,386 m.

    Can beginners ski at Les Ménuires?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 83 km, are easy.
