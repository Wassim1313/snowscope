    About Les Ménuires

    Around 120 km of marked runs place Les Ménuires among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 38 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,386 m and 2,838 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les Trois Vallées, 3.5 km to the north-east.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Les Ménuires has roughly 70 cm of snow on the ground today. 25 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 86 cm by Saturday.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,452 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,386 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
