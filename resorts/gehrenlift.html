    About Gehrenlift

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Gehrenlift sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 633 m and 707 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Ochsenkopf (2.2 km, south-east).

    How Gehrenlift compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Ochsenkopf: about 5.0 km of pistes, 2.2 km to the south-east. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell. The highest nearby is Ochsenkopf, topping out at 1,012 m.

    How much snow Gehrenlift usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 670 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 31 January, with about 13 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 37 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 4 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 22 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually mid-December and late December.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 74 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 633 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Ochsenkopf reaches 1,012 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Questions about Gehrenlift

    What is the best month to ski at Gehrenlift?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    Is there snow at Gehrenlift at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 4 cm).

    What altitude is Gehrenlift at?

    Its highest lift reaches 707 m; the lowest starts at 633 m (74 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Gehrenlift?

    Ochsenkopf, 2.2 km to the south-east, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Gehrenlift?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
