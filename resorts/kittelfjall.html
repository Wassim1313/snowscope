    About Kittelfjäll

    Around 12 km of marked runs place Kittelfjäll among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 58 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 538 m up to 897 m. The closest other ski area is Klimpfjällsbackarna (39 km, south-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 26 runs and around 12 km to ski. Most of the length is green (beginner) (40%, about 4.7 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    359 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow, 5 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Grönfjället, 1,376 m high and 6.4 km north-east of the resort.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Kittelfjäll has roughly 22 cm of snow on the ground today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 42 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Snow at Kittelfjäll through a normal winter

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 718 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 106 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 110 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 79 cm to 125 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 67 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 47 cm, snowy years 86 cm).

    Questions about Kittelfjäll

    What is the best month to ski at Kittelfjäll?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Kittelfjäll at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 67 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Kittelfjäll?

    Between 538 m and 897 m, so 359 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kittelfjäll good for beginners?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 9.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Kittelfjäll have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
