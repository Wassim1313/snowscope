    About Yakurai Garden

    Compact and local, Yakurai Garden has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Japan. Its lifts climb from 214 m to 315 m. Its nearest neighbour is Spring Valley Izumi Kogen, 17 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Yakurai Garden compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 21 km south-west to Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle, with about 8.0 km of runs. For higher slopes, Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle climbs to 1,029 m.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Yakurai Garden hold around 2 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Saturday.

    How Yakurai Garden is laid out

    101 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 214 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Onikobe reaches 1,038 m. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Yakurai Garden at?

    Its highest lift reaches 315 m; the lowest starts at 214 m (101 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Yakurai Garden?

    Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle, 21 km to the south-west, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Yakurai Garden?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
