    About Rangger Köpfl

    Rangger Köpfl is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 628 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,243 m up to 1,925 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sonnenlift Axams, 4.9 km to the east.

    How much snow Rangger Köpfl usually gets

    At about 1,584 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 December and lasts to 10 May: about 20 weeks. A normal season tops out near 62 cm, around 1 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 48 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Most winters land between 37 cm and 74 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 31 cm, with 47 cm in the best years.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Rangger Köpfl has 6 graded runs adding up to about 6.0 km. The largest share is blue, with 3.4 km (56%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Rangger Köpfl is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 682 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Peider Spitze (2,808 m), 7.2 km to the west, about 883 m higher than the top lift.

    How Rangger Köpfl compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.8 km south-east to Axamer Lizum, with about 38 km of runs. Seefeld has the longer snow season: around 24 weeks of solid cover, compared with 20 at Rangger Köpfl. Axamer Lizum reaches 2,334 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Rangger Köpfl: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Rangger Köpfl?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    How much snow does Rangger Köpfl have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 31 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Rangger Köpfl?

    The top is at 1,925 m and the base at 1,243 m.

    Is Rangger Köpfl good for beginners?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 3.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Rangger Köpfl?

    Axamer Lizum, 8.8 km to the south-east, with about 38 km of pistes.
