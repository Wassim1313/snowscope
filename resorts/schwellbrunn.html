    About Schwellbrunn

    Compact and local, Schwellbrunn has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,022 m and 1,083 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Degersheim (2.9 km, north-west).

    Schwellbrunn and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Degersheim: about 5.0 km of pistes, 2.9 km to the north-west. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    61 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Hochalp, 1,530 m high and 8.0 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,052 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 10 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 7 cm and 50 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 28 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 28 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and late February.

    Questions about Schwellbrunn

    When is the best time to ski at Schwellbrunn?

    Aim for early January and late February.

    Is there snow at Schwellbrunn at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    What altitude is Schwellbrunn at?

    The top is at 1,083 m and the base at 1,022 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Schwellbrunn?

    Degersheim, 2.9 km to the north-west, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Schwellbrunn?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
