    About La Pagliara

    La Pagliara is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 1,397 m to 1,872 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Camigliatello Silano, 6.0 km to the north-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, La Pagliara gives 475 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,397 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 2 button lifts. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.
