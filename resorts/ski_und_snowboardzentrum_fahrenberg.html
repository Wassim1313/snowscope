    About Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 668 m up to 781 m. Skilift Wurmstein is the next resort, 7.9 km away to the north.

    How Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb gives 113 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 668 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all.
