    About Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 668 m up to 781 m. Skilift Wurmstein is the next resort, 7.9 km away to the north.

    How Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb gives 113 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 668 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all.

    Snow at Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 724 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; a snowy December gave 19 cm, a dry one 0 cm. A normal season tops out near 10 cm, around 24 January. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and late December. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 28 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    Can you ski at Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Ski und Snowboardzentrum Fahrenb?

    Its highest lift reaches 781 m; the lowest starts at 668 m (113 m of vertical).
