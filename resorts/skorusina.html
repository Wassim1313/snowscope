    About Skorušiná

    Around 6.0 km of marked runs place Skorušiná among the larger resorts of the Carpathians, number 42 by length. Its lifts climb from 699 m to 1,031 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kokava, 8.0 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skorušiná gives 332 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 699 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
