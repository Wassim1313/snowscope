    About Area sciistica Pian del Falco

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Area sciistica Pian del Falco one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,365 m and 1,531 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Area Sciistica Campigna, 2.1 km north-west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Area sciistica Pian del Falco in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,448 m) stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    The mountain at Area sciistica Pian del Falco

    The vertical drop is 166 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,365 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,531 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Falco at 1,657 m, standing 126 m above the top lift.
