    About Val d'Allos

    With some 26 km of runs, Val d'Allos belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,514 m to 2,415 m. Val d'Allos is the next resort, 8.5 km away to the north-west.

    The pistes of Val d'Allos

    The piste map lists 34 graded runs, about 26 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 43%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 4.2 km of black or expert runs. 0.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Val d'Allos is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 901 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,514 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 3 chairlifts and 3 button lifts. The skyline is dominated by Le Grand Cheval de Bois, 2,838 m high and 7.1 km north of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 1,964 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 December and lasts to 12 April: about 17 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 28 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm, with 78 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 72 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Most winters land between 23 cm and 110 cm by mid-February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Val d'Allos?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Val d'Allos have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 34 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Val d'Allos?

    The top is at 2,415 m and the base at 1,514 m.

    Who is Val d'Allos best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 43% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Val d'Allos?

    Espace Lumière, 12 km to the north, with about 185 km of pistes.
