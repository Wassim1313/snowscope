    About Smugglers' Notch Resort

    Only 9 of the 93 ski areas of US Northeast are larger than Smugglers' Notch Resort, which has some 49 km of runs. The slopes start at 340 m and reach 1,112 m at the top. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Stowe, 4.9 km to the south.

    Lifts and terrain

    772 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 340 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
