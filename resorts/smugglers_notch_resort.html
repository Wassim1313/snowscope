    About Smugglers' Notch Resort

    Only 9 of the 93 ski areas of US Northeast are larger than Smugglers' Notch Resort, which has some 49 km of runs. The slopes start at 340 m and reach 1,112 m at the top. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Stowe, 4.9 km to the south.

    What there is to ski at Smugglers' Notch Resort

    Smugglers' Notch Resort has 110 graded runs adding up to about 49 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 28 km, or 57%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 11 km of difficult runs. 0.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 726 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early April, about 16 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 36 cm; a snowy December gave 64 cm, a dry one 9 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 37 cm and 110 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 76 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    772 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 340 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 6 chairlifts, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mount Mansfield at 1,340 m, standing 228 m above the top lift.
