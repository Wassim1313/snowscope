    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 849 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 43 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm, with 30 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 14 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and late February.
