    About Fjellsyn Skisenter

    Fjellsyn Skisenter is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 748 m and 843 m above sea level. Dombas Skiheiser is the next resort, 35 km away to the west.

    Other resorts close by

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Fjellsyn Skisenter hold around 13 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 37 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    The mountain at Fjellsyn Skisenter

    Skied top to bottom, Fjellsyn Skisenter gives 95 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Oppdal Skisenter, reach 1,327 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mesæterhøe at 1,425 m, standing 582 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    At about 796 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 November and lasts to 12 May: about 24 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 50 cm, from 30 cm in a poor year to 64 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 74 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 60 cm to 88 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 81 cm.

    Questions about Fjellsyn Skisenter

    What is the best month to ski at Fjellsyn Skisenter?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Fjellsyn Skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 50 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Fjellsyn Skisenter at?

    Between 748 m and 843 m, so 95 m of vertical drop.
