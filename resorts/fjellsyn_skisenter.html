    The mountain at Fjellsyn Skisenter

    Skied top to bottom, Fjellsyn Skisenter gives 95 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Oppdal Skisenter, reach 1,327 m.
