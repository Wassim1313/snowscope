    About Spieljoch

    Neither huge nor tiny, Spieljoch offers about 16 km of pistes (number 375 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 640 m and 2,049 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Kellerjoch, 6.8 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Spieljoch

    1,409 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The lowest slopes are at only 640 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Of the 7 ski areas compared here, Spieljoch is the third biggest by piste length. For higher slopes, Hochzillertal-Hochfügen climbs to 2,374 m.

    How high do the lifts go at Spieljoch?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,049 m; the lowest starts at 640 m (1,409 m of vertical).
