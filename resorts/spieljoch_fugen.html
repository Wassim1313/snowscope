    About Spieljoch

    Neither huge nor tiny, Spieljoch offers about 16 km of pistes (number 375 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 640 m and 2,049 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Kellerjoch, 6.8 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Spieljoch

    1,409 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The lowest slopes are at only 640 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 3 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hüttenkogel at 2,386 m, standing 337 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,344 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 December and keeps it until about 26 April, roughly 17 weeks. Most winters land between 30 cm and 67 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 52 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 34 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 27 cm (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 47 cm).

    What there is to ski at Spieljoch

    Spieljoch has 19 graded runs adding up to about 16 km. The largest share is red, with 11 km (66%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 2.3 km of black or expert runs.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Spieljoch?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Spieljoch have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 27 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Spieljoch?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,049 m; the lowest starts at 640 m (1,409 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Spieljoch best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Spieljoch?

    About 16 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
