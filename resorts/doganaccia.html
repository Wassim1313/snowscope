    About Doganaccia

    Neither huge nor tiny, Doganaccia offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 611 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,433 m and 1,667 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Corno alle Scale (3.6 km, east).

    How Doganaccia compares nearby

    For more terrain, Area Sciistica Abetone (10.0 km west) has about 39 km of pistes against 6.0 km here. Corno alle Scale reaches 1,940 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How Doganaccia is laid out

    234 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,433 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    The snow right now

    Doganaccia has no snow cover yet today. The coming days should add about 11 cm by Saturday.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Villa Basilewsky (hotel), Rifugio Duca Degli Abruzzi - Lago Scaffaiolo (mountain hut), Rifugio Malghe (mountain hut) and Capanno Tassoni (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
