    About Doganaccia

    Neither huge nor tiny, Doganaccia offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 611 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,433 m and 1,667 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Corno alle Scale (3.6 km, east).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 5.8 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.4 km, or 59%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,550 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 1 cm (snow lay here in 4 winters out of ten), with 16 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 2 February, with about 4 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 52 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    How Doganaccia is laid out

    234 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,433 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 1 cable car, 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Corno alle Scale (1,944 m), 4.6 km to the east, about 277 m higher than the top lift.

    Doganaccia: common questions

    When does Doganaccia have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Is there snow at Doganaccia at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 4 winters out of ten, usually about 1 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Doganaccia?

    The top is at 1,667 m and the base at 1,433 m.

    Can beginners ski at Doganaccia?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 3.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Doganaccia?

    Area Sciistica Abetone, 10.0 km to the west, with about 39 km of pistes.
