    About Chamois

    Chamois is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 484 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,814 m to 2,494 m. The closest other ski area is La Magdeleine (3.8 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Chamois

    In all, 16 marked runs cover roughly 10 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 8.1 km, or 81%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Snow at Chamois through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,154 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 December and keeps it until about 27 May, roughly 25 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 58 cm and 103 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 1 April, with about 88 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 85 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 50 cm (lean years 27 cm, snowy years 75 cm). For the best snow, aim for early March and early April.

    Snow this week

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 680 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 1,814 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 cable car, 3 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Grand-Tournalin - Punta Nord, 3,379 m high and 5.6 km north-east of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hotel Bellevue (hotel), Maison Cly (hotel), Rifugio L'Ermitage (mountain hut) and Maison du Soleil (guesthouse).

    Chamois: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Chamois?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Chamois have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 50 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Chamois?

    The top is at 2,494 m and the base at 1,814 m.

    What level of skier is Chamois best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 81% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Chamois?

    Valtournenche, 6.5 km to the north, with about 27 km of pistes.
