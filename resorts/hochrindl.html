    About Hochrindl

    Hochrindl is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 432 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,465 m up to 1,838 m. The closest other ski area is Flattnitz (8.9 km, north).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 373 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,465 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Hochrindl today: about 16 cm up high. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 11 cm by Saturday.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find JUFA Nockberge (hotel), Hiaslhütte (chalet), Pressl (chalet) and FeWo Keuschnig (chalet).

    Hochrindl: common questions

    How high is Hochrindl?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,838 m; the lowest starts at 1,465 m (373 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Hochrindl have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
