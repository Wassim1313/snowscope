    About Hochrindl

    Hochrindl is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 432 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,465 m up to 1,838 m. The closest other ski area is Flattnitz (8.9 km, north).

    The pistes of Hochrindl

    Counting every graded piste, there are 23 runs and around 12 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 6.4 km, or 52%. Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 2.9 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Hochrindl through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,652 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 21 January and keeps it until about 27 March, roughly 10 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. A normal season tops out near 40 cm, around 21 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 20 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 60 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 21 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 43 cm in a big one.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 373 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,465 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 4 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow, 7 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bretthöhe at 2,320 m, standing 482 m above the top lift.

    Hochrindl: common questions

    When does Hochrindl have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    Can you ski at Hochrindl at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 21 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Hochrindl?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,838 m; the lowest starts at 1,465 m (373 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Hochrindl best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Hochrindl have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
