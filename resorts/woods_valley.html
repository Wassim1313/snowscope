    About Woods Valley

    Neither huge nor tiny, Woods Valley offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 56 of 93 in US Northeast). The slopes start at 174 m and reach 323 m at the top. Val Bialas is the next resort, 26 km away to the south-east.

    The pistes of Woods Valley

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 5.9 km. Most of the length is green circle (39%, about 2.3 km). With 37% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 2.2 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Woods Valley

    149 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 174 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 1 T-bar, 3 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Meszler Hill at 445 m, standing 122 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 248 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 4 February and keeps it until about 23 February, roughly 3 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 15 cm; a snowy December gave 35 cm, a dry one 3 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 10 cm to 65 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 36 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Questions about Woods Valley

    What is the best month to ski at Woods Valley?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    How much snow does Woods Valley have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 15 cm deep.

    What altitude is Woods Valley at?

    The top is at 323 m and the base at 174 m.

    Does Woods Valley have steep runs?

    Yes: about 2.2 km of its graded pistes (37%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Woods Valley have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
