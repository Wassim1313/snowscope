    About Bear Paw Ski Bowl

    About 6.0 km of pistes make Bear Paw Ski Bowl one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Skiing here happens between 1,517 m and 1,782 m above sea level.

    Runs by difficulty

    Bear Paw Ski Bowl has 27 graded runs adding up to about 6.2 km. Most of the length is green circle (49%, about 3.0 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 1.4 km of difficult runs.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 8 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Bear Paw Ski Bowl. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 12 cm by now.

    The mountain at Bear Paw Ski Bowl

    265 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,517 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Baldy Mountain at 2,103 m, standing 321 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,650 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 5 January and lasts to 14 March: about 10 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 56 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 145 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 22 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 59 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Bear Paw Ski Bowl?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Bear Paw Ski Bowl have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high is Bear Paw Ski Bowl?

    Between 1,517 m and 1,782 m, so 265 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Bear Paw Ski Bowl?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 3.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Bear Paw Ski Bowl have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
