    About Домбай

    About 18 km of pistes make Домбай one of the ten biggest ski areas of Russia (number 10). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,600 m up to 3,142 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Домбай has 23 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. Most of the length is blue (71%, about 13 km). With 75% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,542 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,600 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts and 4 drag lifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    Домбай: common questions

    What altitude is Домбай at?

    Between 1,600 m and 3,142 m, so 1,542 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Домбай?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How big is Домбай?

    About 18 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
