    About Hohentauern

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Hohentauern sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,242 m and 1,724 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Kaiserau, 11 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,483 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 December and keeps it until about 28 April, roughly 19 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 54 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 48 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 36 cm and 92 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 73 cm.

    What there is to ski at Hohentauern

    The piste map lists 15 graded runs, about 8.1 km in total. Most of the length is red (70%, about 5.6 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Hohentauern gives 482 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Its 5 lifts break down into 4 T-bars and 1 button lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Großer Bösenstein, 2,448 m high and 5.5 km west of the resort.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Hohentauern?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Hohentauern have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 31 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Hohentauern at?

    The top is at 1,724 m and the base at 1,242 m.

    What level of skier is Hohentauern best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 70% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Hohentauern have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
