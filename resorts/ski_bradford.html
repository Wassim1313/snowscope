    About Ski Bradford

    Ski Bradford is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 68 of 93 in US Northeast by length. The slopes start at 14 m and reach 81 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Nashoba Valley, 39 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 2.7 km to ski. Most of the length is black diamond (61%, about 1.6 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 61% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 1.6 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 48 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 9 cm, around 9 February. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 40 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 17 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    The mountain at Ski Bradford

    The vertical drop is 67 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 14 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 2 rope tows and 3 moving carpets, 9 lifts in all. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Town Farm Hill (117 m), Baldpate Hill (107 m) and Osgood Hill (105 m).

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 2 hotels: Hampton and Best Western Plus Merrimack Valley.

    Quick answers

    When does Ski Bradford have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    How much snow does Ski Bradford have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 3 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Bradford?

    Its highest lift reaches 81 m; the lowest starts at 14 m (67 m of vertical).

    Does Ski Bradford have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.6 km of its graded pistes (61%) are difficult.

    How big is Ski Bradford?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
