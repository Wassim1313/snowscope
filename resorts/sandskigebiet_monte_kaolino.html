    About Sandskigebiet Monte Kaolino

    Sandskigebiet Monte Kaolino has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 436 m and 532 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Etzelwang, 28 km west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Sandskigebiet Monte Kaolino usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 484 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and late January. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 6 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 13 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 17 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    96 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 436 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Klausenlift Mehlmeisel reaches 801 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 funicular and 1 drag lift, 2 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up.

    Quick answers

    When does Sandskigebiet Monte Kaolino have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Is there snow at Sandskigebiet Monte Kaolino at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; the higher slopes do better.
