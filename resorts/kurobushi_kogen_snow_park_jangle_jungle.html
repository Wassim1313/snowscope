    About Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Skiing here happens between 711 m and 1,029 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Hanagasa Kogen (11 km, north).

    What there is to ski at Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle

    Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle has 6 graded runs adding up to about 7.5 km. Most of the length is green (70%, about 5.3 km). With 70% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 318 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 711 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,029 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Questions about Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle

    How high is Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,029 m; the lowest starts at 711 m (318 m of vertical).

    Is Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle good for beginners?

    Yes: 70% of the graded pistes, about 5.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
