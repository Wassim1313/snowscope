    About Pleasant Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pleasant Mountain offers about 15 km of pistes (number 42 of 93 in US Northeast). The slopes start at 160 m and reach 538 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Cranmore, 23 km west, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Pleasant Mountain

    Pleasant Mountain has 41 graded runs adding up to about 15 km. The largest share is blue square, with 8.9 km (60%). Intermediate runs make up 60% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 3.5 km of difficult runs.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Pleasant Mountain has roughly 7 cm of snow on the ground today. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 5 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 349 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early April, about 14 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 63 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 14 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 27 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 48 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 31 cm and 99 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Pleasant Mountain gives 378 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 160 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 5 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Pleasant Mountain (611 m), Pleasant Mountain - Big Bald Peak (587 m) and Pleasant Mountain - North Peak (580 m).

    Questions about Pleasant Mountain

    What is the best month to ski at Pleasant Mountain?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Is there snow at Pleasant Mountain at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 27 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Pleasant Mountain at?

    Between 160 m and 538 m, so 378 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Pleasant Mountain best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 60% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Pleasant Mountain?

    Attitash, 33 km to the west, with about 39 km of pistes.
