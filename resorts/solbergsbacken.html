    About Solbergsbacken

    Solbergsbacken is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 359 m and 577 m above sea level.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 468 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 60 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 48 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 33 cm (lean years 20 cm, snowy years 52 cm). Most winters land between 41 cm and 77 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 218 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Solbergsbacken hold around 36 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 6 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 18 cm at mid-mountain.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Solbergsbacken include 2 hostels: Lägergården Solberg and Villa Solberg.

    Questions about Solbergsbacken

    When is the best time to ski at Solbergsbacken?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Solbergsbacken at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 33 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Solbergsbacken at?

    Its highest lift reaches 577 m; the lowest starts at 359 m (218 m of vertical).
