    Snow at Hornindal through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 601 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 November and lasts to the end of May: about 30 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 234 cm. Most winters land between 125 cm and 274 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 124 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 169 cm, a dry one 78 cm. For about 24 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 223 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April.

    Hornindal: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Hornindal?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Hornindal have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 124 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
