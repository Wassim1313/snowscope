    About Gaberl

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Gaberl sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,302 m up to 1,549 m. Salzstiegl is the next resort, 6.1 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 3.5 km to ski. Most of the length is red (69%, about 2.4 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 247 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,302 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Speikkogel (1,993 m), 8.0 km to the south-west, about 444 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,426 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 25 February and keeps it until about 3 March, roughly 1 weeks. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 32 cm. Most winters land between 4 cm and 45 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 17 cm; a snowy December gave 43 cm, a dry one 3 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Gaberl have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late February to early March.

    How much snow does Gaberl have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Gaberl?

    The top is at 1,549 m and the base at 1,302 m.

    What level of skier is Gaberl best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Gaberl have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
