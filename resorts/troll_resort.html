    About Troll Resort

    With roughly 34 km of runs, Troll Resort sits in the middle of the size table of Canada West. Skiing here happens between 1,013 m and 1,531 m above sea level.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 37 runs and around 33 km to ski. The largest share is black diamond, with 13 km (40%). With 40% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 13 km.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,272 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 November and lasts to 9 May: about 25 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 66 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 40 cm, snowy years 94 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 74 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 94 cm, around 7 February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 56 cm and 116 cm.

    The mountain at Troll Resort

    518 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. All 5 lifts here are T-bars. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Troll Resort today: about 21 cm up high. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 36 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Questions about Troll Resort

    When does Troll Resort have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to early May.

    Is there snow at Troll Resort at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 66 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Troll Resort?

    Between 1,013 m and 1,531 m, so 518 m of vertical drop.

    Is Troll Resort good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 13 km of its graded pistes (40%) are difficult.

    How big is Troll Resort?

    About 34 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
