    About Namari Onsen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Namari Onsen offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 209 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 224 m and 438 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Choei Yuda (22 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Namari Onsen

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 4.0 km to ski. The largest share is black, with 1.3 km (32%). With 32% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 1.3 km of black or expert runs.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Namari Onsen today: about 17 cm up high. The last seven days brought 36 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 11 cm by Thursday.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 214 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 224 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Iwate Kogen Snow Park, for one, climbs to 1,207 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
