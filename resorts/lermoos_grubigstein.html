    About Lermoos Grubigstein

    Lermoos Grubigstein is a mid-sized ski area with about 23 km of pistes, number 268 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 987 m and 2,055 m above sea level. Biberwier is the next resort, 4.4 km away to the south-east.

    The pistes of Lermoos Grubigstein

    The piste map lists 47 graded runs, about 23 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 54%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 0.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,521 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 December and keeps it until about 7 May, roughly 20 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm; a snowy December gave 56 cm, a dry one 19 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 72 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 52 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 89 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    How Lermoos Grubigstein is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,068 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 987 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 3 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Westliche Griesspitze at 2,741 m, standing 686 m above the top lift.
