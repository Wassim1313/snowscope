    About Skiareál Benecko

    Skiareál Benecko is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 71 of 257 in Central Europe by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 668 m up to 853 m. Its nearest neighbour is Family aréna Cinkáč, 0.6 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Skiareál Benecko

    Skiareál Benecko has 12 graded runs adding up to about 3.9 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.9 km, or 49%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skiareál Benecko gives 185 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 668 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 853 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 5 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 4 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
