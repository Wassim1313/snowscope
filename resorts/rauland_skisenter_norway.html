    About Rauland skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Rauland skisenter offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 126 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 826 m to 1,066 m. Rauland skisenter is the next resort, 1.3 km away to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 240 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning.
