    About Rauland skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Rauland skisenter offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 126 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 826 m to 1,066 m. Rauland skisenter is the next resort, 1.3 km away to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Rauland skisenter

    Rauland skisenter has 12 graded runs adding up to about 4.5 km. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 2.4 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How much snow Rauland skisenter usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 946 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and early April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 74 cm, with 102 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 126 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 87 cm to 155 cm in mid-February in most winters. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. A normal season tops out near 133 cm, around 5 April.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 240 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The highest named summit within 8 km is Brurenuten at 1,207 m, standing 141 m above the top lift.

    Rauland skisenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Rauland skisenter?

    Aim for mid-March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Rauland skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 74 cm deep.

    How high is Rauland skisenter?

    The top is at 1,066 m and the base at 826 m.

    Is Rauland skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rauland skisenter?

    Rauland skisenter, 1.3 km to the north, with about 11 km of pistes.
