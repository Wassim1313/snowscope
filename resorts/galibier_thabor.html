    About Galibier-Thabor

    With some 131 km of runs, Galibier-Thabor belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,406 m to 2,738 m. Its nearest neighbour is Les Karellis, 7.7 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 177 graded runs, about 131 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 58 km, or 45%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Galibier-Thabor gives 1,332 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,406 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 34 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 17 chairlifts, 9 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 4 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Roche Noire at 3,067 m, standing 329 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,072 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 150 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 162 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 87 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 48 cm in a poor year to 126 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 108 cm and 189 cm by mid-February.

    Galibier-Thabor: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Galibier-Thabor?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Galibier-Thabor have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 87 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Galibier-Thabor?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    What altitude is Galibier-Thabor at?

    The top is at 2,738 m and the base at 1,406 m.

    Is Galibier-Thabor good for beginners?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 80 km, are easy.
