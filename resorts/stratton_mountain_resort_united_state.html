    About Stratton

    Stratton ranks number 5 of 93 ski areas in US Northeast by piste length, with about 72 km of runs. The slopes start at 607 m and reach 1,183 m at the top. Stratton is the next resort, 0.5 km away to the south.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    576 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 607 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 17 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 9 chairlifts, 1 drag lift and 6 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 6 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Stratton Mountain (1,194 m), The Pinnacle (769 m) and Mundal Hill (647 m).

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 895 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 January and lasts to 27 March: about 9 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 45 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm, with 41 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Year to year the snow is steady, with 14 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Stratton include Mountain Ridge (hotel), Mid Mountain Lodge (mountain hut), Black Bear Lodge (hotel) and Main Base Lodge (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Stratton

    When is the best time to ski at Stratton?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does Stratton have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Stratton?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,183 m; the lowest starts at 607 m (576 m of vertical).

    Is Stratton good for beginners?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 30 km, are easy.

    How big is Stratton?

    About 72 km of pistes and 17 lifts.
