    About Kolašin 1450

    About 19 km of pistes make Kolašin 1450 one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Balkans (number 9). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,425 m up to 1,915 m. The closest other ski area is Kolašin 1600 (2.5 km, north-east).

    The pistes of Kolašin 1450

    In all, 26 marked runs cover roughly 19 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 68%. With 68% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 490 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,425 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 1,915 m is the third highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. Its 5 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 3 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    Questions about Kolašin 1450

    How high is Kolašin 1450?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,915 m; the lowest starts at 1,425 m (490 m of vertical).

    Is Kolašin 1450 good for beginners?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How big is Kolašin 1450?

    About 19 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
