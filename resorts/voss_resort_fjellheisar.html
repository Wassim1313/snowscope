    About Voss Resort Fjellheisar

    Voss Resort Fjellheisar is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 23 km of pistes put it at number 26 of 262. Its lifts climb from 283 m to 948 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Haugsåsen (Granvin Alpin), 22 km to the south-east.

    The pistes of Voss Resort Fjellheisar

    Voss Resort Fjellheisar has 39 graded runs adding up to about 23 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 54%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How much snow Voss Resort Fjellheisar usually gets

    Count on about 29 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 616 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 270 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 142 cm and 340 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 284 cm, around 19 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 24 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 136 cm, with 199 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    665 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 6 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Lønahorgi at 1,415 m, standing 467 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Voss Resort Fjellheisar

    When is the best time to ski at Voss Resort Fjellheisar?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Voss Resort Fjellheisar have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 136 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Voss Resort Fjellheisar at?

    The top is at 948 m and the base at 283 m.

    Is Voss Resort Fjellheisar good for beginners?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Voss Resort Fjellheisar have?

    About 23 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
