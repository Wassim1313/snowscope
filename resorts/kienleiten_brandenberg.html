    About Kienleiten

    Kienleiten has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 913 m and reach 975 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Rofanlifte Steinberg, 8.1 km to the west.

    Kienleiten and the resorts around it

    Rofan Seilbahn has the longer snow season: around 18 weeks of solid cover, compared with 11 at Kienleiten. The highest nearby is Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau, topping out at 2,026 m.

    Snow at Kienleiten through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 944 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to early April, about 11 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 19 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 4 cm in a poor year to 40 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 21 cm and 67 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 41 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 25 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    The mountain at Kienleiten

    Skied top to bottom, Kienleiten gives 62 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 913 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau, reach 2,026 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Marchspitze at 2,004 m, standing 1,029 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Haus Schönblick (chalet), Kohlerhof (guesthouse), Schafflerhof (guesthouse) and Haflingerhof (guesthouse).

    Kienleiten: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Kienleiten?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    How much snow does Kienleiten have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 19 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Kienleiten at?

    Its highest lift reaches 975 m; the lowest starts at 913 m (62 m of vertical).

    How big is Kienleiten?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
