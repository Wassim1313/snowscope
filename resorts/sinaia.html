    About Sinaia

    Sinaia ranks number 3 of 312 ski areas in the Carpathians by piste length, with about 30 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,024 m up to 2,085 m. There are 5 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Buşteni, 7.3 km to the north.

    What there is to ski at Sinaia

    Counting every graded piste, there are 52 runs and around 27 km to ski. Most of the length is red (77%, about 21 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 3.5 km of black or expert runs.

    How Sinaia is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,061 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,085 m. Its 10 lifts break down into 2 cable cars, 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
