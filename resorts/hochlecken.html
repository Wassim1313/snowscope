    About Hochlecken

    Hochlecken is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 697 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 769 m and 1,076 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Feuerkogel, 9.1 km to the east.

    How Hochlecken compares nearby

    For more terrain, Loser-Altaussee (23 km south-east) has about 21 km of pistes against 4.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Postalm does better, with about 22 weeks of a good base instead of 10. For higher slopes, Loser-Altaussee climbs to 1,765 m.

    What there is to ski at Hochlecken

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 4.3 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 2.2 km (51%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 10 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 922 m in an ordinary winter, from late January to early April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 17 cm to 54 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 3 cm in a poor year to 37 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 29 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. A normal season tops out near 39 cm, around 29 February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    The mountain at Hochlecken

    307 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 969 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 3 T-bars and 2 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Großer Höllkogel at 1,862 m, standing 786 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hochleckenhaus (mountain hut), Bramosen (hotel), Hanslmann (guesthouse) and Gasthaus Föttinger (hotel).

    Hochlecken: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hochlecken?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to early April.

    How much snow does Hochlecken have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 18 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Hochlecken?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,076 m; the lowest starts at 769 m (307 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Hochlecken?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hochlecken?

    Loser-Altaussee, 23 km to the south-east, with about 21 km of pistes.
