    About Vögelsberg

    Vögelsberg is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 867 m and 970 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Wildstättlift Wattenberg, 1.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Glungezer (6.2 km south-west) has about 14 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. For more reliable snow, Kellerjoch usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 20 weeks, against 0 here. The highest nearby is Glungezer, topping out at 2,307 m.

    The mountain at Vögelsberg

    Skied top to bottom, Vögelsberg gives 103 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,067 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Haneburger, 2,596 m high and 7.0 km south of the resort.

    How much snow Vögelsberg usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 918 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the typical depth is 18 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm, with 27 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 26 cm. Most winters land between 14 cm and 39 cm by mid-February.

    Vögelsberg: common questions

    When does Vögelsberg have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Vögelsberg have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    How high is Vögelsberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 970 m; the lowest starts at 867 m (103 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Vögelsberg?

    Glungezer, 6.2 km to the south-west, with about 14 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Vögelsberg have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
