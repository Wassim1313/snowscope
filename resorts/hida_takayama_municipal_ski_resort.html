    About Hida Takayama Municipal

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hida Takayama Municipal offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 199 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,308 m up to 1,534 m. Honokidaira is the next resort, 5.9 km away to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Hida Takayama Municipal has 6 graded runs adding up to about 4.1 km. The largest share is green, with 2.1 km (50%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Hida Takayama Municipal gives 226 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,308 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Hida Takayama Municipal: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Hida Takayama Municipal?

    Between 1,308 m and 1,534 m, so 226 m of vertical drop.

    Is Hida Takayama Municipal suitable for families?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 2.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hida Takayama Municipal?

    Blue Resort Norikura, 15 km to the east, with about 13 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Hida Takayama Municipal have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
