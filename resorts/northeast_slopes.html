    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 102 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 247 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows, 3 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Leech Hill at 625 m, standing 276 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 298 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to mid-March, about 6 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 64 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 19 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 37 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 42 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Questions about Northeast Slopes

    When is the best time to ski at Northeast Slopes?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Northeast Slopes at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 19 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Northeast Slopes at?

    Its highest lift reaches 349 m; the lowest starts at 247 m (102 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Northeast Slopes best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 77% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Northeast Slopes?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
