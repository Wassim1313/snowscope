    How Luosto is laid out

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Ukko-Luosto (514 m), Lampivaara (365 m) and Pikku-Luosto (365 m).

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 223 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 59 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 66 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 42 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 27 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 52 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Luosto

    When does Luosto have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Luosto have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 42 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
