    About Kegelberg

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Kegelberg sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 580 m up to 744 m. Skilift Klingenthal Jägerstraße is the next resort, 6.0 km away to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 2.3 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 52%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow cannons cover about 0.9 km of the runs (41%), so the main runs can open even when natural snow is late.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Kegelberg gives 164 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 580 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 drag lifts and 1 rope tow.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.4 km north-west to Skiwelt Schöneck, with about 3.0 km of runs.

    Questions about Kegelberg

    What altitude is Kegelberg at?

    Between 580 m and 744 m, so 164 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kegelberg suitable for families?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Does Kegelberg have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 0.9 km of its pistes (41%).

    Which bigger ski area is near Kegelberg?

    Skiwelt Schöneck, 8.4 km to the north-west, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Kegelberg have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
