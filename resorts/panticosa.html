    About Panticosa

    Neither huge nor tiny, Panticosa offers about 19 km of pistes (number 32 of 49 in the Pyrenees). Skiing here happens between 1,154 m and 2,202 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Formigal (14 km, north-west).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Panticosa gives 1,048 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs.

    Panticosa and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Formigal (14 km north-west) has about 104 km of pistes against 19 km here. The highest nearby is Cauterets Cirque du Lys, topping out at 2,420 m.

    Panticosa: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Panticosa?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,202 m; the lowest starts at 1,154 m (1,048 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Panticosa?

    Formigal, 14 km to the north-west, with about 104 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Panticosa have?

    About 19 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
