    About Panticosa

    Neither huge nor tiny, Panticosa offers about 19 km of pistes (number 32 of 49 in the Pyrenees). Skiing here happens between 1,154 m and 2,202 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Formigal (14 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Panticosa

    The piste map lists 36 graded runs, about 19 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 8.7 km, or 47%. Intermediate runs make up 47% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 2.3 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Panticosa gives 1,048 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts, 4 drag lifts and 5 moving carpets, 15 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Punda dero Garmo Negro (3,064 m), 7.8 km to the north, about 862 m higher than the top lift.

    Panticosa and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Formigal (14 km north-west) has about 104 km of pistes against 19 km here. If snow is the priority, Formigal does better, with about 19 weeks of a good base instead of 8. The highest nearby is Cauterets Cirque du Lys, topping out at 2,420 m.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Panticosa has roughly 2 cm of snow on the ground today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Sunday. The 2026/27 season at Panticosa is announced to start on 27 November 2026 and to run until 4 April 2027.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,678 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to mid-March, about 8 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 15 cm, with 52 cm in the best years. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 85 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 11 February, with about 42 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Panticosa: common questions

    When does Panticosa have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Panticosa have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in 8 winters out of ten, about 15 cm deep.

    When does Panticosa open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Panticosa?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,202 m; the lowest starts at 1,154 m (1,048 m of vertical).

    Is Panticosa suitable for families?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 7.7 km, are easy.
