    About U Karla

    U Karla is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 676 m up to 742 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Velká Úpa, 2.0 km to the north-west.

    U Karla and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 3.2 km south-west to Černá Hora-Pec, with about 44 km of runs. For higher slopes, Černá Hora-Pec climbs to 1,280 m.

    How U Karla is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 66 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 676 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Černá Hora-Pec reaches 1,280 m.
