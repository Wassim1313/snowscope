    About Lee's Ski Hill

    Lee's Ski Hill has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 2,401 m and 2,421 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Silverton Mountain (16 km, south).

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Lee's Ski Hill today: about 11 cm up high. The last seven days brought 12 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 13 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 20 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 2,401 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Telluride, for one, climbs to 3,812 m. The only lift is a rope tow. Above the ski area rises Potosi Peak (4,195 m), 7.8 km to the south-west, about 1,774 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Lee's Ski Hill through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,411 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 13 January and keeps it until about 6 April, roughly 12 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 10 cm and 76 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 19 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 46 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 49 cm.

    Questions about Lee's Ski Hill

    What is the best month to ski at Lee's Ski Hill?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    Can you ski at Lee's Ski Hill at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 19 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Lee's Ski Hill at?

    Between 2,401 m and 2,421 m, so 20 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Lee's Ski Hill have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
