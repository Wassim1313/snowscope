    About Spitze

    Spitze has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 879 m and 925 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Grub-Kaien, 1.8 km to the east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 46 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 879 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a rope tow. Above the ski area rises Gäbris (1,250 m), 7.0 km to the south, about 325 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow on the ground at Spitze today: about 3 cm up high. The last seven days brought 4 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Friday.

    How much snow Spitze usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 902 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the typical depth is 19 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 8 cm to 53 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 16 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 31 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 27 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Spitze?

    Aim for early March and late March.

    Can you ski at Spitze at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 16 cm deep.

    What altitude is Spitze at?

    The top is at 925 m and the base at 879 m.

    How big is Spitze?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
