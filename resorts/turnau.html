    About Turnau

    Neither huge nor tiny, Turnau offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 729 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 776 m up to 1,240 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Aflenzer Bürgeralm, 8.8 km to the west.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Turnau gives 464 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Below about 976 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Aflenzer Bürgeralm, reach 1,798 m.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Gasthof Marktwirt (hotel), Urlaubsbauernhof Payer (guesthouse), Ferienhaus Kirchleit'n (guesthouse) and Gasthof Puregger (guesthouse).
