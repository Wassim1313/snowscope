    About Turnau

    Neither huge nor tiny, Turnau offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 729 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 776 m up to 1,240 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Aflenzer Bürgeralm, 8.8 km to the west.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Aflenzer Bürgeralm (8.8 km west) has about 18 km of pistes against 4.0 km here. Aflenzer Bürgeralm has the longer snow season: around 19 weeks of solid cover, compared with 10 at Turnau. Aflenzer Bürgeralm reaches 1,798 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Snow at Turnau through a normal winter

    At about 1,008 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 January and lasts to 22 March: about 10 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 22 cm, with 46 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 16 cm and 68 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 12 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. A normal season tops out near 43 cm, around 14 February.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Turnau gives 464 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Below about 976 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Aflenzer Bürgeralm, reach 1,798 m. To get up there are 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 drag lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Rauschkogel, 1,720 m high and 6.7 km north of the resort.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Turnau?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Is there snow at Turnau at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 22 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Turnau at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,240 m; the lowest starts at 776 m (464 m of vertical).

    Who is Turnau best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 55% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Turnau?

    Aflenzer Bürgeralm, 8.8 km to the west, with about 18 km of pistes.
