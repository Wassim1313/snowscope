    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 818 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late December at roughly 3 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten), with 11 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 0 cm to 10 cm in mid-February in most winters. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and mid-January.

    Questions about Gateway Parks

    When is the best time to ski at Gateway Parks?
