    About Maverick Mountain

    Maverick Mountain is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 123 of 175 in US West by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,127 m up to 2,569 m.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Maverick Mountain today: about 50 cm up high. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 39 cm at mid-mountain).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 18 graded runs, about 12 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 5.3 km (45%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 2.4 km of difficult runs.

    How Maverick Mountain is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 442 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. At 2,127 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. The only lift is a chairlift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Comet Mountain at 3,114 m, standing 545 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Maverick Mountain through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,348 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 November and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 24 weeks. Most winters land between 66 cm and 126 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 103 cm, around 4 April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 90 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 59 cm, with 93 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 7 weeks.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Maverick Mountain?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    How much snow does Maverick Mountain have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 59 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Maverick Mountain?

    Between 2,127 m and 2,569 m, so 442 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Maverick Mountain best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 45% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Maverick Mountain?

    About 12 km of pistes and 1 lift.
