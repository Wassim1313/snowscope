    About Hachimantai Resort Panorama Ski

    Hachimantai Resort Panorama Ski is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 10 km of pistes put it at number 70 of 385. Skiing here happens between 557 m and 975 m above sea level. Hachimantai Resort Shimokura is the next resort, 3.1 km away to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Hachimantai Resort Panorama Ski

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 10 km. Most of the length is green (77%, about 7.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Hachimantai Resort Panorama Ski hold around 27 cm of snow today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 19 cm by Saturday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    418 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 557 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Amihari Onsen, for one, climbs to 1,324 m. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    Quick answers

    How high is Hachimantai Resort Panorama Ski?

    The top is at 975 m and the base at 557 m.

    Is Hachimantai Resort Panorama Ski suitable for families?

    Yes: 77% of the graded pistes, about 7.8 km, are easy.

    How big is Hachimantai Resort Panorama Ski?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
