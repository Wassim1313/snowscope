    About Ghisoni

    Compact and local, Ghisoni has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Apennines. The slopes start at 1,592 m and reach 1,871 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Val d'Ese, 8.6 km south, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 279 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,592 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 button lifts and 1 drag lift.

    The pistes of Ghisoni

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 2.9 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.6 km (57%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.
