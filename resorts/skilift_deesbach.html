    About Skilift Deesbach

    Skilift Deesbach has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 658 m up to 722 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kirchberglift am Fröbelturm, 1.3 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Skilift Deesbach usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 690 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-December and late December. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 21 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 28 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 30 January, with about 12 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Deesbach gives 64 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 658 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Winterwelt Schmiedefeld, for one, climbs to 878 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Skilift Deesbach?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    How much snow does Skilift Deesbach have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).

    What altitude is Skilift Deesbach at?

    Its highest lift reaches 722 m; the lowest starts at 658 m (64 m of vertical).

    How big is Skilift Deesbach?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
