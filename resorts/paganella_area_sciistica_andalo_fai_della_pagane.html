    About Paganella

    Paganella is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 34 km of pistes put it at number 184 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,025 m and reach 2,124 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Monte Bondone (13 km, south).

    Runs by difficulty

    Paganella has 59 graded runs adding up to about 34 km. Most of the length is red (66%, about 22 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.5 km of black or expert runs.

    How Paganella is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,099 m: enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,124 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 4 gondolas, 11 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 16 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Fibion at 2,664 m, standing 540 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Paganella through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,574 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 February and keeps it until about 20 March, roughly 7 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 19 cm to 53 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late March and mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 34 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 36 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 27 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Paganella?

    Aim for late March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Paganella at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Paganella at?

    The top is at 2,124 m and the base at 1,025 m.

    What level of skier is Paganella best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Paganella?

    About 34 km of pistes and 16 lifts.
