    About Sundance Resort

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sundance Resort offers about 32 km of pistes (number 75 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 1,859 m and reach 2,514 m at the top. Snowbird is the next resort, 22 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Sundance Resort

    The piste map lists 80 graded runs, about 32 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (49%, about 16 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 8.6 km.

    How Sundance Resort is laid out

    655 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,859 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Getting up the mountain: 7 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Mount Timpanogos, 3,581 m high and 4.9 km west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,186 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 84 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 38 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 46 cm; a snowy December gave 90 cm, a dry one 13 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 119 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Sundance Resort?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Sundance Resort at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 46 cm deep.

    What altitude is Sundance Resort at?

    Between 1,859 m and 2,514 m, so 655 m of vertical drop.

    Is Sundance Resort good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 8.6 km of its graded pistes (27%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Sundance Resort?

    Park City, 31 km to the north, with about 256 km of pistes.
