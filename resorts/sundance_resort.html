    The snow at Sundance Resort, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Sundance Resort (about 7,172 ft (2,186 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 18 in (46 cm) of cover (12 in (31 cm) to 26 in (66 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 32 in (82 cm) (23 in (59 cm)–43 in (109 cm)).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 33 in (84 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 33 % of winters, and by 1 January in 54 %.

    1983-84 was the big one (73 in (185 cm)); 2025-26 the lean one (9.4 in (24 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs early December to mid-April (136 days).

    Questions about Sundance Resort

    Does Sundance Resort have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 18 in (46 cm), and only 54 % of the past 46 winters had 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 18 in (46 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Sundance Resort?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 33 in (84 cm)). For a sure base, early December to mid-April is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How snowy is Sundance Resort?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 33 in (84 cm) in mid-February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 73 in (185 cm) (1983-84), the thinnest 9.4 in (24 cm) (2025-26).

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sundance Resort offers about 32 km of pistes (number 75 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 1,859 m and reach 2,514 m at the top. Snowbird is the next resort, 22 km away to the north.
