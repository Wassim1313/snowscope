    Ski amadé in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Ski amadé (about 1,654 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 54 cm of cover (38 cm to 73 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 115 cm; the middle half of winters range from 86 cm to 138 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 133 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 41 % of winters, and by 1 January in 70 %.

    The deepest winter was 1999-00 (207 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (87 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-November until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 180 days.

    Ski amadé: quick answers

    Does Ski amadé have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 54 cm at Christmas, and 70 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 17 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Ski amadé?

    March: the snow peaks at about 133 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Ski amadé get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 133 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 207 cm (1999-00), the thinnest 87 cm (2025-26).

    What altitude is Ski amadé at?

    The top is at 2,642 m and the base at 667 m.

    How many km of pistes does Ski amadé have?

    About 605 km of pistes and 239 lifts.

    About Ski amadé

    Only 1 of the 1,056 ski areas of the Alps is larger than Ski amadé, which has some 605 km of runs. The slopes start at 667 m and reach 2,642 m at the top. Flachau is the next resort, 2.5 km away to the north.

    How Ski amadé is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,975 m: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. The lowest slopes are at only 667 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 2,642 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
