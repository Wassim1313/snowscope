    About Paloisvuori

    Paloisvuori is a ski area.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 105 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 December and lasts to 24 April: about 18 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 44 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Most winters land between 37 cm and 72 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 31 cm (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 43 cm). Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 58 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Paloisvuori

    What is the best month to ski at Paloisvuori?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Paloisvuori at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 31 cm deep.
