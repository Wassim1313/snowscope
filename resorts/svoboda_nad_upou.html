    About Svoboda nad Úpou

    Compact and local, Svoboda nad Úpou has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 563 m and 644 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Košťálka, 1.0 km to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. Most of the length is blue (54%, about 0.7 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Svoboda nad Úpou has roughly 25 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 5 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    81 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 563 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Černá Hora-Pec reaches 1,280 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 2 button lifts, 3 lifts in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 604 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 41 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 19 cm, around 1 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 7 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 31 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and mid-February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Svoboda nad Úpou?

    Aim for mid-January and mid-February.

    Is there snow at Svoboda nad Úpou at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Svoboda nad Úpou at?

    Between 563 m and 644 m, so 81 m of vertical drop.

    Is Svoboda nad Úpou good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Svoboda nad Úpou?

    Černá Hora-Pec, 5.6 km to the north-west, with about 44 km of pistes.
