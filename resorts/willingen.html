    About Willingen

    Only 9 of the 257 ski areas of Central Europe are larger than Willingen, which has some 15 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 577 m up to 838 m. Willingen is the next resort, 1.2 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Willingen

    In all, 40 marked runs cover roughly 14 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.3 km, or 50%. Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How Willingen is laid out

    The vertical drop is 261 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 577 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 838 m. The lift system counts 15 lifts: 1 funicular, 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 4 button lifts and 5 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts.

    Willingen: common questions

    What altitude is Willingen at?

    The top is at 838 m and the base at 577 m.

    Is Willingen suitable for families?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 7.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Willingen have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
