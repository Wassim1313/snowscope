    About Dvoračky

    Neither huge nor tiny, Dvoračky offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 98 of 257 in Central Europe). The slopes start at 1,055 m and reach 1,124 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Skiareál Horní Domky (1.3 km, west).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Dvoračky gives 69 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,124 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
