    About Master Ski

    Master Ski is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 4.0 km of pistes put it at number 67 of 312. Its lifts climb from 567 m to 679 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Master-Ski, 0.5 km to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Master Ski

    Counting every graded piste, there are 18 runs and around 4.3 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.7 km (63%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Master Ski today: about 13 cm up high. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 112 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 567 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Jaworzyna Krynicka reaches 1,108 m. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.

    Questions about Master Ski

    What altitude is Master Ski at?

    Its highest lift reaches 679 m; the lowest starts at 567 m (112 m of vertical).

    Is Master Ski good for beginners?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 2.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Master Ski?

    Słotwiny Arena, 7.6 km to the north-west, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Master Ski?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
