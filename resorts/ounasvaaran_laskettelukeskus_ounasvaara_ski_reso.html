    About Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus

    Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 94 m and reach 195 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Ounasvaara, 0.3 km east, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus compares nearby

    Of the 3 ski areas compared here, Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus is the third biggest by piste length. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus today: about 18 cm up high. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 23 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The mountain at Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus

    101 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Santavaara (215 m), Korkalovaara (213 m) and Ounasvaara (203 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 144 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 20 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 53 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 62 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 47 cm and 73 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 36 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 21 cm, snowy years 50 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Quick answers

    When does Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 36 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus?

    Its highest lift reaches 195 m; the lowest starts at 94 m (101 m of vertical).

    Is Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 0.5 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
