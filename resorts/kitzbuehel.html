    About KitzSki

    KitzSki is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 186 km of pistes put it at number 15 of 1,056. The slopes start at 762 m and reach 1,993 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is St Johann in Tirol, 10 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow KitzSki usually gets

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,378 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to early May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 28 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 48 cm). Most winters land between 31 cm and 73 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 39 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 59 cm.

    How KitzSki is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,231 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 962 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,993 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 63 lifts: 2 cable cars, 11 gondolas, 28 chairlifts, 7 T-bars, 1 button lift and 14 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 14 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Schützkogel (2,069 m), Schwarzkogel (2,030 m) and Kitzbüheler Horn (1,996 m).

    Questions about KitzSki

    When does KitzSki have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    How much snow does KitzSki have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 28 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does KitzSki open for the 2026/27 season?

    It opened on 6 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is KitzSki?

    Between 762 m and 1,993 m, so 1,231 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at KitzSki?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 93 km, are easy.
