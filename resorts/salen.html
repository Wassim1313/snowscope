    What 46 winters say about Sälen

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Sälen (726 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 34 cm; half of the winters sit between 23 cm and 45 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 61 cm; the middle half of winters range from 49 cm to 72 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 64 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 15 % of winters, and by 1 January in 35 %.

    1987-88 was the big one (120 cm); 1995-96 the lean one (38 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April, about 138 days.

    Today's 13 cm compares with a usual 9 cm for 19 November.

    Skiing Sälen: the practical side

    Lifts: 98. Pistes: 90 km. Heights: 567 m at the bottom, 886 m at the top (319 m of vertical).

    A day pass costs about 715 kr; a 6-day pass 3,124 kr. Nights from around 700 kr per person.

    Nearest airports: Oslo Gardermoen (OSL, 152 km), Are Ostersund (OSD, 238 km), Trondheim Vaernes (TRD, 280 km).

    About Sälen

    About 90 km of pistes make Sälen one of the ten biggest ski areas of Scandinavia (number 3). Its lifts climb from 567 m to 886 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Högfjället, 2.2 km to the south-east.

    The pistes of Sälen

    The mountain at Sälen

    The vertical drop is 319 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 886 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 98 lifts break down into 9 chairlifts, 16 T-bars, 42 button lifts, 21 drag lifts and 10 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. For small children there are 10 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Hundfjället (925 m), Storfjället (924 m) and Mellanfjället (910 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 726 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 December and keeps it until about 25 April, roughly 19 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm; a snowy December gave 59 cm, a dry one 13 cm. Most winters land between 42 cm and 83 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 46 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 64 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.
