    What 46 winters say about Bonneval-sur-Arc

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Bonneval-sur-Arc (2,371 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 80 cm of cover (64 cm to 104 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 130 cm (103 cm–152 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 146 cm.

    70 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 87 % by 1 January.

    2003-04 was the big one (240 cm); 2021-22 the lean one (102 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-November until late May in a normal winter, roughly 189 days.

    Questions about Bonneval-sur-Arc

    Does Bonneval-sur-Arc have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 80 cm at Christmas, and 87 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 97 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Bonneval-sur-Arc?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 146 cm). For a sure base, mid-November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Bonneval-sur-Arc?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 146 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 240 cm (2003-04), the thinnest 102 cm (2021-22).

    What altitude is Bonneval-sur-Arc at?

    Between 1,794 m and 2,948 m, so 1,154 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bonneval-sur-Arc?

    About Bonneval-sur-Arc

    Bonneval-sur-Arc is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 307 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,794 m to 2,948 m. Bessans is the next resort, 6.2 km away to the south-west.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Bonneval-sur-Arc has roughly 97 cm of snow on the ground today. 27 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 40 cm by Monday.

    The mountain at Bonneval-sur-Arc

    1,154 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,794 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,948 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    Nearby alternatives compared
