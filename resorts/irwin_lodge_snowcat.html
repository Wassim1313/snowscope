    About Irwin Lodge Snowcat

    Irwin Lodge Snowcat is a ski area. The closest other ski area is Crested Butte (14 km, east).

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Irwin Lodge Snowcat today: about 62 cm up high. The last seven days brought 23 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (53 cm).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 3,029 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to mid-May, about 27 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 154 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 187 cm, around 30 March. Most winters land between 113 cm and 230 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 18 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 99 cm; a snowy December gave 155 cm, a dry one 49 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Irwin Lodge Snowcat?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Irwin Lodge Snowcat have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 99 cm deep.
