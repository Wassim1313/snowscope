    About Thaler Höhe

    Thaler Höhe is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 727 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 923 m to 1,163 m. The closest other ski area is Liftanlagen Oberwilhams (3.3 km, north).

    How Thaler Höhe is laid out

    240 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,123 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.
