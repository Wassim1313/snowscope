    About Thaler Höhe

    Thaler Höhe is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 727 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 923 m to 1,163 m. The closest other ski area is Liftanlagen Oberwilhams (3.3 km, north).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Thaler Höhe has 8 graded runs adding up to about 4.0 km. The largest share is blue, with 3.5 km (86%). With 86% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Thaler Höhe is laid out

    240 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,123 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 3 drag lifts and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Leckereck at 1,501 m, standing 338 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 4 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,043 m) in an ordinary winter, from early February to early March. The snow is usually deepest around 11 February, with about 32 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 20 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 39 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 22 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Most winters land between 12 cm and 57 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March.

    Thaler Höhe: common questions

    When does Thaler Höhe have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    How much snow does Thaler Höhe have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Thaler Höhe?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,163 m; the lowest starts at 923 m (240 m of vertical).

    Is Thaler Höhe suitable for families?

    Yes: 86% of the graded pistes, about 3.5 km, are easy.

    How big is Thaler Höhe?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
