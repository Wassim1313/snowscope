    About Ski areál Šacberk

    Ski areál Šacberk has about 0.5 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 533 m up to 611 m. The closest other ski area is Luka nad Jihlavou (12 km, south-east).

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 78 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 533 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all.
