    About Фристайл

    Neither huge nor tiny, Фристайл offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 47 of 86 in Russia). The slopes start at 123 m and reach 158 m at the top.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Фристайл gives 35 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are gondolas. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns.

    Questions about Фристайл

    How high do the lifts go at Фристайл?

    Its highest lift reaches 158 m; the lowest starts at 123 m (35 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Фристайл have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
