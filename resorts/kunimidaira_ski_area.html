    About Kunimidaira

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Kunimidaira sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The slopes start at 401 m and reach 667 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Matsurube (10 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Kunimidaira

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 4.1 km. Most of the length is green (45%, about 1.9 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 266 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 401 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Kunimidaira has roughly 25 cm of snow on the ground today. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Tuesday.

    Kunimidaira: common questions

    What altitude is Kunimidaira at?

    The top is at 667 m and the base at 401 m.

    Can beginners ski at Kunimidaira?

    Yes: 45% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.
