    About Praz de Lys Sommand

    Praz de Lys Sommand is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 47 km of pistes put it at number 127 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,246 m to 1,960 m. The closest other ski area is Roc d'Enfer (6.4 km, north).

    The pistes of Praz de Lys Sommand

    The piste map lists 93 graded runs, about 47 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 24 km, or 50%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,603 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. Most winters land between 68 cm and 185 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 140 cm, around 5 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 92 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 63 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 16 cm in a poor year to 106 cm in a big one.

    The mountain at Praz de Lys Sommand

    The vertical drop is 714 m: enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 1,960 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 23 lifts break down into 6 chairlifts, 15 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Le Roc d'Enfer at 2,244 m, standing 284 m above the top lift.

    Praz de Lys Sommand: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Praz de Lys Sommand?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Praz de Lys Sommand have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 63 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Praz de Lys Sommand open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 19 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Praz de Lys Sommand?

    Between 1,246 m and 1,960 m, so 714 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Praz de Lys Sommand?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 28 km, are easy.
