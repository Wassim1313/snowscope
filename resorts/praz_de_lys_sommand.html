    What 46 winters say about Praz de Lys Sommand

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Praz de Lys Sommand (1,603 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 63 cm of cover (33 cm to 88 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 131 cm (96 cm–158 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 140 cm.

    By 15 December 43 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 70 %.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 265 cm. Worst: 2022-23, which never passed 66 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to mid-May (172 days).

    Questions about Praz de Lys Sommand

    Is there snow in Praz de Lys Sommand at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 63 cm at Christmas, and 70 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 16 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Praz de Lys Sommand?

    February: the snow peaks at about 140 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Praz de Lys Sommand get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 140 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 265 cm (1981-82), the thinnest 66 cm (2022-23).

    How high is Praz de Lys Sommand?

    Between 1,246 m and 1,960 m, so 714 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Praz de Lys Sommand?

    About 47 km of pistes and 23 lifts.

    About Praz de Lys Sommand

    Praz de Lys Sommand is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 47 km of pistes put it at number 127 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,246 m to 1,960 m. The closest other ski area is Roc d'Enfer (6.4 km, north).

    The mountain at Praz de Lys Sommand

    The vertical drop is 714 m: enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 1,960 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
