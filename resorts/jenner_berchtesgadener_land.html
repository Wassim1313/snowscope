    About Jenner (Berchtesgadener Land)

    Compact and local, Jenner (Berchtesgadener Land) has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,081 m to 1,432 m. Grünsteinlift (Berchtesgaden) is the next resort, 4.7 km away to the north-west.

    How much snow Jenner (Berchtesgadener Land) usually gets

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,256 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to early May. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 85 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm (lean years 8 cm, snowy years 60 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 24 February, with about 67 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 42 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 351 m: runs of a moderate length. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 2 gondolas and 1 chairlift. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Watzmann-Mittelspitze, 2,713 m high and 8.0 km south-west of the resort.

    Jenner (Berchtesgadener Land): common questions

    When does Jenner (Berchtesgadener Land) have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Is there snow at Jenner (Berchtesgadener Land) at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 31 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Jenner (Berchtesgadener Land) at?

    Between 1,081 m and 1,432 m, so 351 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Jenner (Berchtesgadener Land) best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Jenner (Berchtesgadener Land)?

    Rossfeld, 8.1 km to the north-east, with about 10 km of pistes.
