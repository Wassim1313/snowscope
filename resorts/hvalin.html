    About Хвалынский (Hvalin)

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Хвалынский (Hvalin) sits in the middle of the size table of Russia. Its lifts climb from 118 m to 237 m.

    The pistes of Хвалынский (Hvalin)

    Хвалынский (Hvalin) has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.7 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 1.0 km, or 62%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Хвалынский (Hvalin) is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Хвалынский (Hvalin) gives 119 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 118 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.

    Questions about Хвалынский (Hvalin)

    How high is Хвалынский (Hvalin)?

    Its highest lift reaches 237 m; the lowest starts at 118 m (119 m of vertical).

    Is Хвалынский (Hvalin) good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Хвалынский (Hvalin) have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
