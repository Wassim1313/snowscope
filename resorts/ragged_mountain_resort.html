    About Ragged

    With roughly 18 km of runs, Ragged sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 337 m to 673 m. The closest other ski area is Proctor (5.7 km, south).

    The mountain at Ragged

    Skied top to bottom, Ragged gives 336 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 337 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
