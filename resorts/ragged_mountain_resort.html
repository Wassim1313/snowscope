    About Ragged

    With roughly 18 km of runs, Ragged sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 337 m to 673 m. The closest other ski area is Proctor (5.7 km, south).

    The pistes of Ragged

    Counting every graded piste, there are 38 runs and around 17 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 7.7 km (46%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.0 km.

    Snow at Ragged through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 505 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 31 January and keeps it until about 22 March, roughly 7 weeks. Most winters land between 10 cm and 68 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 40 cm, around 18 February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 17 cm; a snowy December gave 37 cm, a dry one 4 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    The mountain at Ragged

    Skied top to bottom, Ragged gives 336 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 337 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 3 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Ragged Mountain (689 m), The Pinnacle (689 m) and Dickinson Hill (579 m).

    Questions about Ragged

    When is the best time to ski at Ragged?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    Can you ski at Ragged at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 17 cm deep.

    What altitude is Ragged at?

    The top is at 673 m and the base at 337 m.

    Is Ragged good for beginners?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 6.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ragged have?

    About 18 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
