    About Gatrisch

    Gatrisch is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 692 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,292 m and 1,593 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Gantrisch, 1.4 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Gatrisch usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,442 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 December and lasts to 29 April: about 19 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 49 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 33 cm and 98 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 70 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm, with 55 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    301 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Ochsen, 2,188 m high and 5.4 km south of the resort.

    Questions about Gatrisch

    When does Gatrisch have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Gatrisch at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Gatrisch?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,593 m; the lowest starts at 1,292 m (301 m of vertical).

    Is Gatrisch good for beginners?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Gatrisch?

    Gantrisch, 2.6 km to the east, with about 11 km of pistes.
