    About Ski Juwel

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Ski Juwel one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 916 m to 1,017 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Juwel, 1.8 km to the north-east.

    Ski Juwel and the resorts around it

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Ski Juwel hold around 19 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    The mountain at Ski Juwel

    From top to bottom that makes 101 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 916 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 hotels: Jugendhotel Angerhof, Talhof, Hotel Haflingerhof and Hotel Silberberger.
