    About Ski Juwel

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Ski Juwel one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 916 m to 1,017 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Juwel, 1.8 km to the north-east.

    The pistes of Ski Juwel

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.8 km (67%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Ski Juwel hold around 17 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 4 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 16 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (6 cm).

    The mountain at Ski Juwel

    From top to bottom that makes 101 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 916 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 2 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Schwaigberghorn, 1,990 m high and 7.1 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 966 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 18 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Year to year the snow is steady, with 15 cm to 44 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 28 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 17 cm, from 3 cm in a poor year to 34 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February.

    Ski Juwel: common questions

    When does Ski Juwel have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Ski Juwel at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 17 cm deep.

    How high is Ski Juwel?

    The top is at 1,017 m and the base at 916 m.

    Is Ski Juwel suitable for families?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ski Juwel?

    Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau, 8.3 km to the south-west, with about 69 km of pistes.
