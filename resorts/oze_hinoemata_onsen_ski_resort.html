    The mountain at Oze Hinoemata Onsen

    Skied top to bottom, Oze Hinoemata Onsen gives 158 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 923 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Marunuma Kogen reaches 1,991 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
