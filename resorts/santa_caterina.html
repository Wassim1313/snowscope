    About Santa Caterina

    With some 26 km of runs, Santa Caterina belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,723 m up to 2,879 m. Its nearest neighbour is Bormio Ski, 9.6 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Santa Caterina compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Ponte di Legno: about 56 km of pistes, 17 km to the south. The highest nearby is Passo Stelvio, topping out at 3,355 m.

    From top to bottom that makes 1,156 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,723 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.

    Santa Caterina: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Santa Caterina?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,879 m; the lowest starts at 1,723 m (1,156 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Santa Caterina?

    Ponte di Legno, 17 km to the south, with about 56 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Santa Caterina have?

    About 26 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
