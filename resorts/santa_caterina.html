    About Santa Caterina

    With some 26 km of runs, Santa Caterina belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,723 m up to 2,879 m. Its nearest neighbour is Bormio Ski, 9.6 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 49 runs and around 25 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 19 km (76%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.7 km.

    How much snow Santa Caterina usually gets

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,301 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-May. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 79 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 88 cm. Most winters land between 54 cm and 114 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 51 cm; a snowy December gave 88 cm, a dry one 25 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,156 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,723 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets, 8 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Punta San Matteo (3,678 m), 6.5 km to the south-east, about 799 m higher than the top lift.

    Santa Caterina: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Santa Caterina?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Santa Caterina at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 51 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Santa Caterina?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,879 m; the lowest starts at 1,723 m (1,156 m of vertical).

    Who is Santa Caterina best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 76% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Santa Caterina?

    Ponte di Legno, 17 km to the south, with about 56 km of pistes.
