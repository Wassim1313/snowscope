    About Mount Greylock Ski Club

    Compact and local, Mount Greylock Ski Club has about 1.0 km of runs, small for US Northeast. The ski area spans the mountain from 383 m up to 481 m. Its nearest neighbour is Jiminy Peak Resort, 11 km south-west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 98 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 383 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Bascom Lodge (hotel), Guest House at Field Farm (guesthouse), The 1896 House (hotel) and Harbour House Inn B&B (hotel).
