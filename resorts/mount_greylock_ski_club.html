    About Mount Greylock Ski Club

    Compact and local, Mount Greylock Ski Club has about 1.0 km of runs, small for US Northeast. The ski area spans the mountain from 383 m up to 481 m. Its nearest neighbour is Jiminy Peak Resort, 11 km south-west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 98 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 383 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 2 lifts here are rope tows. The skyline is dominated by Mount Greylock, 1,063 m high and 3.9 km east of the resort.

    How much snow Mount Greylock Ski Club usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 432 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 23 cm, around 16 February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 58 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm, with 28 cm in the best years.

    Mount Greylock Ski Club: common questions

    When does Mount Greylock Ski Club have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Mount Greylock Ski Club have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Mount Greylock Ski Club?

    Its highest lift reaches 481 m; the lowest starts at 383 m (98 m of vertical).

    Does Mount Greylock Ski Club have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.6 km of its graded pistes (46%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Mount Greylock Ski Club?

    Mount Snow, 44 km to the north-east, with about 61 km of pistes.
