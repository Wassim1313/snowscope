    About Snowy Range

    Neither huge nor tiny, Snowy Range offers about 19 km of pistes (number 103 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 2,674 m and reach 2,939 m at the top.

    What there is to ski at Snowy Range

    Counting every graded piste, there are 35 runs and around 19 km to ski. Most of the length is blue square (46%, about 8.7 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 4.4 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Snowy Range through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,806 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 26 November and keeps it until about 12 May, roughly 24 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 85 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 58 cm and 122 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 100 cm, around 24 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 55 cm; a snowy December gave 86 cm, a dry one 26 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    How Snowy Range is laid out

    265 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,674 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Bald Mountain (2,922 m) and Corner Mountain (2,889 m).

    Snowy Range: common questions

    When does Snowy Range have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Snowy Range at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 55 cm deep.

    What altitude is Snowy Range at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,939 m; the lowest starts at 2,674 m (265 m of vertical).

    Who is Snowy Range best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 46% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Snowy Range?

    About 19 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
