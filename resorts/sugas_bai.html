    About Șugaș Băi

    Compact and local, Șugaș Băi has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Carpathians. Its lifts climb from 773 m to 922 m. Its nearest neighbour is Baile Tusnad, 32 km north-east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Șugaș Băi

    From top to bottom that makes 149 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 773 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Poiana Brașov, for one, climbs to 1,772 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.
