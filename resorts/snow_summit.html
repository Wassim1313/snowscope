    About Snow Summit

    Snow Summit is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 101 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 2,122 m and 2,494 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Bear Mountain (2.9 km, east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 36 runs and around 20 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 10 km, or 50%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 3.3 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    372 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,122 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 10 chairlifts and 5 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Sugarloaf Mountain at 3,029 m, standing 535 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,308 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 4 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; a snowy December gave 9 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 28 cm.

    Questions about Snow Summit

    When does Snow Summit have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early March.

    Is there snow at Snow Summit at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 1 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Snow Summit?

    The top is at 2,494 m and the base at 2,122 m.

    Who is Snow Summit best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Snow Summit have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
