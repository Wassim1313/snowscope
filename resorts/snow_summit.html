    About Snow Summit

    Snow Summit is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 101 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 2,122 m and 2,494 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Bear Mountain (2.9 km, east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 36 runs and around 20 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 10 km, or 50%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 3.3 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    372 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,122 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 10 chairlifts and 5 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Sugarloaf Mountain at 3,029 m, standing 535 m above the top lift.
