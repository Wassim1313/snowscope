    About Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee)

    Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee) is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,410 m up to 1,621 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ohlstadt, 6.8 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee) through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,516 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 6 December and lasts to 15 May: about 23 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 47 cm (lean years 21 cm, snowy years 79 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-April at roughly 103 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 97 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 53 cm to 128 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Lifts and terrain

    211 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,410 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 cable car and 1 T-bar. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Simetsberg, 1,840 m high and 5.7 km south-west of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 10 km north-east to Ötzlifte Kochel-Rabenkopf, with about 2.0 km of runs. Its snow is the most reliable of the group: a 30 cm base lasts about 23 weeks in a typical winter, longer than at any neighbour listed.

    Questions about Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee)

    What is the best month to ski at Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee)?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee) have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 47 cm deep.

    How high is Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee)?

    The top is at 1,621 m and the base at 1,410 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee)?

    Ötzlifte Kochel-Rabenkopf, 10 km to the north-east, with about 2.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee) have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
