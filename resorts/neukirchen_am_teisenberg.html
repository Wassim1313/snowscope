    About Neukirchen am Teisenberg

    Compact and local, Neukirchen am Teisenberg has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 711 m up to 817 m. Its nearest neighbour is Wolfsberg (Siegsdorf), 6.7 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Neukirchen am Teisenberg has roughly 14 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 10 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 6 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 106 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 711 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Teisenberg (1,333 m), 2.3 km to the south-east, about 516 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Neukirchen am Teisenberg usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 764 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 36 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the typical depth is 5 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 28 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 17 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Neukirchen am Teisenberg?

    Aim for mid-March and late March.

    Is there snow at Neukirchen am Teisenberg at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).

    What altitude is Neukirchen am Teisenberg at?

    The top is at 817 m and the base at 711 m.

    How big is Neukirchen am Teisenberg?
