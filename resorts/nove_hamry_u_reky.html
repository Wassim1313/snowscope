    About Nové Hamry

    Nové Hamry is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 685 m and 763 m above sea level. Nové Hamry is the next resort, 0.7 km away to the north-east.

    Snow at Nové Hamry through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 724 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm, with 28 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 37 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March. A normal season tops out near 16 cm, around 31 January. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    78 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 685 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Skiareál Plešivec reaches 1,012 m. The only lift is a button lift.

    Questions about Nové Hamry

    When does Nové Hamry have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Can you ski at Nové Hamry at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 6 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Nové Hamry?

    Between 685 m and 763 m, so 78 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Nové Hamry?

    Skiareál Plešivec, 8.3 km to the east, with about 6.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Nové Hamry?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
