    About Dartmouth Skiway

    Neither huge nor tiny, Dartmouth Skiway offers about 16 km of pistes (number 40 of 93 in US Northeast). Its lifts climb from 280 m to 580 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Grill Hill, 16 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 42 graded runs, about 16 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 8.5 km, or 53%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 3.1 km of difficult runs.

    Snow at Dartmouth Skiway through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 430 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to late March, about 8 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 19 cm, with 36 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 42 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 14 cm to 66 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    How Dartmouth Skiway is laid out

    The vertical drop is 300 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 280 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 4 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Smarts Mountain (983 m), 7.1 km to the north-east, about 403 m higher than the top lift.

    Dartmouth Skiway: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Dartmouth Skiway?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does Dartmouth Skiway have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 19 cm deep.

    How high is Dartmouth Skiway?

    The top is at 580 m and the base at 280 m.

    What level of skier is Dartmouth Skiway best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 53% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Dartmouth Skiway?

    About 16 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
