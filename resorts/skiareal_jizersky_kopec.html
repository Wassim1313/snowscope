    About Skiareál Jizerský kopec

    Skiareál Jizerský kopec has about 0.2 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 742 m to 794 m. The closest other ski area is Skiareál Mýtiny (1.9 km, east).

    How Skiareál Jizerský kopec is laid out

    The vertical drop is 52 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 942 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 794 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a button lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 768 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 9 February and lasts to 28 February: about 2 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 12 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 48 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 33 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 55 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Quick answers

    When does Skiareál Jizerský kopec have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    Can you ski at Skiareál Jizerský kopec at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).

    What altitude is Skiareál Jizerský kopec at?

    The top is at 794 m and the base at 742 m.

    How many km of pistes does Skiareál Jizerský kopec have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
