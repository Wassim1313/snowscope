    About Puchis Welt in Puchberg

    Puchis Welt in Puchberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 592 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 767 m to 1,207 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Rohr im Gebirge, 13 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 6.7 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.5 km, or 67%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 440 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The base at 767 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 1 drag lift. Above the ski area rises Klosterwappen (2,076 m), 3.2 km to the south-west, about 869 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Puchis Welt in Puchberg through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 987 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early February to late February, about 3 weeks of good cover. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 64 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 35 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 15 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 46 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Forellenhof (hotel), Almreserlhaus (mountain hut), Edelweißhütte (mountain hut) and Berghaus Hochschneeberg (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Puchis Welt in Puchberg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Puchis Welt in Puchberg?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    How much snow does Puchis Welt in Puchberg have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 15 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Puchis Welt in Puchberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,207 m; the lowest starts at 767 m (440 m of vertical).

    Who is Puchis Welt in Puchberg best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Puchis Welt in Puchberg?

    Stuhleck, 23 km to the south, with about 22 km of pistes.
