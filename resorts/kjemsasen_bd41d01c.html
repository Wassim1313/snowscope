    How Kjemsasen is laid out

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Vardefjell (640 m), Tuva (463 m) and Storfjellet (436 m).

    How much snow Kjemsasen usually gets

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 442 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 115 cm and 215 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 188 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 194 cm, around 31 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 94 cm; a snowy December gave 130 cm, a dry one 57 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks.

    Quick answers

    When does Kjemsasen have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Kjemsasen have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 94 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
