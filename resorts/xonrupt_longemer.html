    About Xonrupt-Longemer

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Xonrupt-Longemer one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 807 m up to 972 m. Gérardmer is the next resort, 2.4 km away to the south.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 6.9 km south-east to La Bresse, with about 24 km of runs. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell. For higher slopes, La Bresse climbs to 1,347 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 3 graded runs, about 1.5 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.0 km, or 71%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 890 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 26 January. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 21 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-January and early March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 35 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 165 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,007 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at La Bresse, reach 1,347 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Haut de Falimont, 1,306 m high and 7.9 km south-east of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Questions about Xonrupt-Longemer

    When is the best time to ski at Xonrupt-Longemer?

    Aim for mid-January and early March.

    Can you ski at Xonrupt-Longemer at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Xonrupt-Longemer?

    Between 807 m and 972 m, so 165 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Xonrupt-Longemer?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Xonrupt-Longemer?

    La Bresse, 6.9 km to the south-east, with about 24 km of pistes.
