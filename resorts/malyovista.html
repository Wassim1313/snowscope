    About Malyovista

    Neither huge nor tiny, Malyovista offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 31 of 51 in the Balkans). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,708 m up to 1,974 m. Its nearest neighbour is Panichishte, 6.0 km north-west, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Borovets: about 43 km of pistes, 17 km to the east. For higher slopes, Borovets climbs to 2,538 m.

    The mountain at Malyovista

    The vertical drop is 266 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,708 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Borovets, for one, climbs to 2,538 m.

    Malyovista: common questions

    What altitude is Malyovista at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,974 m; the lowest starts at 1,708 m (266 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Malyovista?

    Borovets, 17 km to the east, with about 43 km of pistes.

    How big is Malyovista?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
