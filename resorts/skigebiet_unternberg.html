    About Unternberg

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Unternberg one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 718 m and 937 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Skilift Maiergschwendt (2.2 km, north).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 2.0 km in total. Most of the length is red (38%, about 0.8 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 31% of the length is graded difficult.

    The mountain at Unternberg

    The vertical drop is 219 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 718 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 2 drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Sonntagshorn, 1,961 m high and 7.6 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 828 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 2 February and lasts to 8 March: about 5 weeks. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 17 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 38 cm, a dry one 1 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 9 cm to 65 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 36 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Unternberg?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    How much snow does Unternberg have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Unternberg at?

    Its highest lift reaches 937 m; the lowest starts at 718 m (219 m of vertical).

    Is Unternberg good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 0.6 km of its graded pistes (31%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Unternberg?

    Heutal, 7.9 km to the south, with about 9.0 km of pistes.
