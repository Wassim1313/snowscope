    Lifts and terrain

    113 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mont Planachat (1,234 m), La Chèvre (1,231 m) and Crêt Fury (1,079 m).

    Snow at Terre Ronde through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,136 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 23 January, with about 21 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 116 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 7 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 64 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Terre Ronde

    When does Terre Ronde have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Terre Ronde at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 7 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Terre Ronde?

    Between 1,080 m and 1,193 m, so 113 m of vertical drop.

    Does Terre Ronde have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.4 km of its graded pistes (38%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Terre Ronde?

    Le Semnoz, 42 km to the south-east, with about 16 km of pistes.
