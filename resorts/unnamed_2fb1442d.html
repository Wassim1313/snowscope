    About Skilift Oberer Schlossberg

    Skilift Oberer Schlossberg has about 0.5 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 919 m to 971 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skilift Schlossberg Oberkirnach, 0.6 km to the east.

    Snow at Skilift Oberer Schlossberg through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 945 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late December and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 7 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm; a snowy December gave 18 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 27 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 52 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 919 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Spitzberg (1,058 m), Bosberg (1,052 m) and Rappeneck (1,047 m).

    Skilift Oberer Schlossberg: common questions

    When does Skilift Oberer Schlossberg have the most snow?
