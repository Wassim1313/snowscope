    About am Engländer

    am Engländer is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 131 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Its lifts climb from 422 m to 481 m. Its nearest neighbour is Winterloch Heigenbrücken, 6.1 km south-east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at am Engländer

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.4 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.4 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How am Engländer is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 59 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 422 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
