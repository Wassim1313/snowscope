    About Superbagnères

    Neither huge nor tiny, Superbagnères offers about 30 km of pistes (number 19 of 49 in the Pyrenees). Its lifts climb from 1,463 m to 2,122 m. Peyragudes is the next resort, 8.9 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Superbagnères has 70 graded runs adding up to about 30 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 50%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 659 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,463 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 5 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pic de Maupas / Pico de Maupás at 3,109 m, standing 987 m above the top lift.

That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 8 cm at mid-mountain). The 2026/27 season at Superbagnères is announced to start on 4 December 2026 and to run until 29 March 2027.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 8 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,792 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 1 February, with about 53 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 55 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 96 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Questions about Superbagnères

    What is the best month to ski at Superbagnères?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Superbagnères at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Superbagnères?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 29 March 2027.

    How high is Superbagnères?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,122 m; the lowest starts at 1,463 m (659 m of vertical).

    Is Superbagnères good for beginners?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 19 km, are easy.
