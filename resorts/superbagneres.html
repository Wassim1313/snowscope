    About Superbagnères

    Neither huge nor tiny, Superbagnères offers about 30 km of pistes (number 19 of 49 in the Pyrenees). Its lifts climb from 1,463 m to 2,122 m. Peyragudes is the next resort, 8.9 km away to the west.

    Superbagnères and the resorts around it

    Of the 7 ski areas compared here, Superbagnères is the third biggest by piste length. For higher slopes, Aramón Cerler climbs to 2,618 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 659 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,463 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Superbagnères hold around 2 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Monday.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Superbagnères include Village Club du Soleil (hotel), Résidence L'Ourson du Lys (hotel), Chalet L'Ourson du Lys (chalet) and Castel De La Pique (hotel).

    Questions about Superbagnères

    How high is Superbagnères?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,122 m; the lowest starts at 1,463 m (659 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Superbagnères have?

    About 30 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
