    What 46 winters say about Kicking Horse Resort

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Kicking Horse Resort (1,843 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 92 cm; half of the winters sit between 77 cm and 113 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 116 cm, between 101 cm and 137 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 128 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 96 % of winters, and by 1 January in 100 %.

    2021-22 was the big one (193 cm); 2000-01 the lean one (95 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early November to early June, about 208 days.

    Most years, yes: around 92 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 100 % of 46 winters. Right now: 39 cm.
