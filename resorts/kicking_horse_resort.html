    About Kicking Horse Resort

    With roughly 46 km of runs, Kicking Horse Resort sits in the middle of the size table of Canada West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,262 m up to 2,424 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 58 marked runs cover roughly 46 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 16 km, or 35%. Steep ground is the speciality: 33% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 15 km.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 30 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,843 m in an ordinary winter, from early November to late May or later. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 18 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 92 cm; a snowy December gave 130 cm, a dry one 66 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 88 cm to 149 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 122 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 128 cm, around 1 March.

    The mountain at Kicking Horse Resort

    The vertical drop is 1,162 m: enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Ozone (2,504 m), Whitetooth (2,443 m) and Terminator Peak (2,408 m).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Kicking Horse Resort?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Kicking Horse Resort have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 92 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Kicking Horse Resort open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Kicking Horse Resort?

    Between 1,262 m and 2,424 m, so 1,162 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kicking Horse Resort good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 15 km of its graded pistes (33%) are difficult.
