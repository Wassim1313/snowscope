    About Weinebene

    Neither huge nor tiny, Weinebene offers about 13 km of pistes (number 421 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,598 m to 1,843 m. The closest other ski area is Koralpe (5.7 km, south-west).

    The mountain at Weinebene

    The vertical drop is 245 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,598 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,843 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.

    Weinebene today and the next days

    Today there are about 19 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Weinebene. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 14 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Weinebene include Grünangerhütte (mountain hut), Grillitschhütte (mountain hut), Gasthof Pfeifferstocker (guesthouse) and Hotel Koralpe (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
