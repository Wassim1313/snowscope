    About Weinebene

    Neither huge nor tiny, Weinebene offers about 13 km of pistes (number 421 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,598 m to 1,843 m. The closest other ski area is Koralpe (5.7 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Weinebene has 26 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. The largest share is red, with 6.6 km (57%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.4 km.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,720 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 January and lasts to 18 March: about 8 weeks. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 92 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 19 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 49 cm). A normal season tops out near 49 cm, around 27 February.

    The mountain at Weinebene

    The vertical drop is 245 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,598 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,843 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 6 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 8 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Großer Speikkogel (2,140 m), 5.9 km to the south-west, about 297 m higher than the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Weinebene include Grünangerhütte (mountain hut), Grillitschhütte (mountain hut), Gasthof Pfeifferstocker (guesthouse) and Hotel Koralpe (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Weinebene

    What is the best month to ski at Weinebene?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Weinebene at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 19 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Weinebene?

    The top is at 1,843 m and the base at 1,598 m.

    What level of skier is Weinebene best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 57% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Weinebene?

    About 13 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
