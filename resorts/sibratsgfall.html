    How Sibratsgfäll is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 190 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,041 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Diedamskopf, for one, climbs to 2,056 m.
