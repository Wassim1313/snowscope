    How Sibratsgfäll is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 190 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,041 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Diedamskopf, for one, climbs to 2,056 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hoher Ifen at 2,230 m, standing 1,199 m above the top lift.
