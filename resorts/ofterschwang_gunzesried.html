    About Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried

    Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 387 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 880 m up to 1,403 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang, 4.6 km to the south.

    The pistes of Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried

    The piste map lists 24 graded runs, about 15 km in total. The largest share is red, with 8.3 km (56%). Intermediate runs make up 56% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 2.0 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 15 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,142 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early January to mid-April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 30 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 49 cm. Most winters land between 24 cm and 79 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 27 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 45 cm, a dry one 7 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried gives 523 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,080 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts and 3 T-bars. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The skyline is dominated by Riedberger Horn, 1,787 m high and 7.4 km south-west of the resort.

    Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 27 cm deep.

    What altitude is Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried at?

    Between 880 m and 1,403 m, so 523 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 56% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
