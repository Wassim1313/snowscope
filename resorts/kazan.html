    About Казань (Kazan)

    Neither huge nor tiny, Казань (Kazan) offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 43 of 86 in Russia). Its lifts climb from 58 m to 220 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Казань (Kazan) has 7 graded runs adding up to about 3.9 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 41%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.
