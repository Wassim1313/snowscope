    About Erlebniswelt Ernstthal

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Erlebniswelt Ernstthal sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 721 m and 831 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Silbersattel Steinach, 6.5 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Erlebniswelt Ernstthal gives 110 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 721 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
