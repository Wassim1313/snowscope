    About Queyras

    With some 26 km of runs, Queyras belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,549 m to 2,400 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Queyras, 6.6 km to the west.

    What there is to ski at Queyras

    The piste map lists 37 graded runs, about 26 km in total. Most of the length is red (64%, about 17 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 2.5 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    851 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,549 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 6 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 8 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Gran Queyron / Grand Queyron, 3,060 m high and 6.8 km north-east of the resort.

    The snow right now

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,974 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 December and lasts to 15 April: about 17 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 32 cm, from 13 cm in a poor year to 72 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 30 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 80 cm, around 5 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 29 cm and 115 cm.

    Questions about Queyras

    When is the best time to ski at Queyras?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Queyras at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 32 cm deep.

    How high is Queyras?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,400 m; the lowest starts at 1,549 m (851 m of vertical).

    Who is Queyras best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 64% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Queyras have?

    About 26 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
