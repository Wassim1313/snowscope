    About Solvalla Swinghill

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Solvalla Swinghill one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 39 m to 106 m. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Serena Ski, 10 km to the east.

    How Solvalla Swinghill is laid out

    The vertical drop is 67 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mustakorvenkallio (114 m), Ilomäki (90 m) and Mustakallio (90 m).

    Snow at Solvalla Swinghill through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 72 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 28 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 6 cm to 53 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm; a snowy December gave 25 cm, a dry one 0 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.
