    About Puy-Saint-Vincent

    Puy-Saint-Vincent is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 42 km of pistes put it at number 140 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,411 m up to 2,664 m. Pelvoux-Vallouise is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Puy-Saint-Vincent

    Puy-Saint-Vincent has 73 graded runs adding up to about 41 km. Most of the length is blue (47%, about 19 km). With 63% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Puy-Saint-Vincent gives 1,253 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,411 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,664 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 12 lifts break down into 8 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Pointe de l'Aiglière (3,307 m), 5.1 km to the west, about 643 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Puy-Saint-Vincent usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,038 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 26 November and keeps it until about 21 May, roughly 25 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 120 cm. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 100 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 73 cm and 142 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 69 cm (lean years 33 cm, snowy years 108 cm).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Puy-Saint-Vincent?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Puy-Saint-Vincent at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 69 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Puy-Saint-Vincent open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    What altitude is Puy-Saint-Vincent at?

    The top is at 2,664 m and the base at 1,411 m.

    Can beginners ski at Puy-Saint-Vincent?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 26 km, are easy.
