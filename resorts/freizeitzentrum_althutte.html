    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 790 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 26 January. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 9 cm, with 36 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 55 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 35 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 772 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Großer Arber, reach 1,437 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    What is the best month to ski at Freizeitzentrum Althütte?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Freizeitzentrum Althütte at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Freizeitzentrum Althütte?

    How big is Freizeitzentrum Althütte?
