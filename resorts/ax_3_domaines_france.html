    This week's snow

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,734 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 26 January and keeps it until about 23 February, roughly 4 weeks. A normal season tops out near 38 cm, around 2 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 6 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 84 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; a snowy December gave 45 cm, a dry one 1 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Ax 3 Domaines?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to late February.

    Is there snow at Ax 3 Domaines at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).
