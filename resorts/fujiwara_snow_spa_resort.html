    About Fujiwara Snow&Spa Resort

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Fujiwara Snow&Spa Resort sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Skiing here happens between 838 m and 966 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Minakami Kogen, 1.1 km to the north-east.

    How Fujiwara Snow&Spa Resort is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Fujiwara Snow&Spa Resort gives 128 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 838 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kawaba reaches 1,843 m.
