    About Anzère

    Anzère is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 38 km of pistes put it at number 159 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,516 m and 2,411 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Crans-Montana, 7.3 km to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 146 graded runs, about 37 km in total. Most of the length is red (44%, about 16 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 7.0 km of difficult runs.

    How much snow Anzère usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,964 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 November and lasts to 30 May: about 26 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 68 cm (lean years 37 cm, snowy years 98 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 117 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For about 14 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 78 cm and 157 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 130 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 895 m: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,516 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 3 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Wildhorn at 3,250 m, standing 839 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Anzère

    What is the best month to ski at Anzère?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Anzère at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 68 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Anzère?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,411 m; the lowest starts at 1,516 m (895 m of vertical).

    Who is Anzère best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 44% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Anzère have?

    About 38 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
