    About Skilifte Herrischried

    Compact and local, Skilifte Herrischried has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 873 m to 966 m. Todtmoos is the next resort, 10 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Skilifte Herrischried

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 2.4 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.4 km (56%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 920 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and early February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 6 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 23 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 18 cm).

    The mountain at Skilifte Herrischried

    Skied top to bottom, Skilifte Herrischried gives 93 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 873 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Feldberg, for one, climbs to 1,446 m. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Rohrenkopf, 1,170 m high and 7.2 km north-west of the resort.

    Questions about Skilifte Herrischried

    When does Skilifte Herrischried have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and early February.

    Can you ski at Skilifte Herrischried at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Skilifte Herrischried?

    Between 873 m and 966 m, so 93 m of vertical drop.

    Is Skilifte Herrischried suitable for families?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Skilifte Herrischried?

    Spießhorn Menzenschwand, 19 km to the north, with about 7.0 km of pistes.
