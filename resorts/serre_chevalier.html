    Serre-Chevalier in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Serre-Chevalier (1,992 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 71 cm, with a normal range of 49 cm–90 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 126 cm (99 cm–140 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 129 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 57 % of winters, and by 1 January in 85 %.

    Best winter on record: 2017-18, peaking at 217 cm. Worst: 2004-05, which never passed 81 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to late May, about 184 days.

    So far this winter: 63 cm on 25 November (usual: 34 cm).

    About Serre-Chevalier

    Around 157 km of marked runs place Serre-Chevalier among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 25 by length. The slopes start at 1,219 m and reach 2,765 m at the top. Pelvoux-Vallouise is the next resort, 9.5 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of Serre-Chevalier

    In all, 274 marked runs cover roughly 156 km. The largest share is blue, with 54 km (34%). With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. 1.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Serre-Chevalier is laid out

    1,546 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,765 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 70 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 5 gondolas, 20 chairlifts, 22 button lifts, 5 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 15 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 15 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Pic de Clouzis, 3,465 m high and 7.4 km west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,992 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 November and lasts to 22 May: about 26 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 12 weeks. Most winters land between 80 cm and 152 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 71 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 36 cm, snowy years 109 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 103 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 129 cm, around 5 March.
