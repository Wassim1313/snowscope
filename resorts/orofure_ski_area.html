    About Orofure

    Orofure is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 227 m up to 360 m. Sanlaiva is the next resort, 10.0 km away to the east.

    What there is to ski at Orofure

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 1.9 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 48%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts.

    The mountain at Orofure

    Skied top to bottom, Orofure gives 133 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 227 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Questions about Orofure

    What altitude is Orofure at?

    Its highest lift reaches 360 m; the lowest starts at 227 m (133 m of vertical).

    Is Orofure good for beginners?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Orofure?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
