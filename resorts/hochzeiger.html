    About Hochzeiger

    With some 35 km of runs, Hochzeiger belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,467 m and reach 2,483 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Galtwiesenlift, 4.1 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 57 marked runs cover roughly 35 km. Most of the length is red (51%, about 18 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 7.4 km.

    Snow at Hochzeiger through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,975 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May or later, about 26 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 87 cm, around 24 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 81 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Year to year the snow is steady, with 54 cm to 101 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 46 cm, with 67 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Hochzeiger gives 1,016 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,467 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,483 m is the second highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 3 button lifts. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Above the ski area rises Wildgrat (2,971 m), 4.4 km to the south-east, about 488 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Hochzeiger

    When is the best time to ski at Hochzeiger?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Hochzeiger have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 46 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Hochzeiger at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,483 m; the lowest starts at 1,467 m (1,016 m of vertical).

    Who is Hochzeiger best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 51% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Hochzeiger have?

    About 35 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
