    About Hochzeiger

    With some 35 km of runs, Hochzeiger belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,467 m and reach 2,483 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Galtwiesenlift, 4.1 km to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Hochzeiger gives 1,016 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,467 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,483 m is the second highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km.
