    About Látky

    Látky is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 226 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Its lifts climb from 935 m to 1,035 m. The closest other ski area is Ski Látky (2.0 km, south-west).

    The mountain at Látky

    Skied top to bottom, Látky gives 100 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,135 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,035 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
