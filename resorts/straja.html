    About Straja

    Straja is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 9.0 km of pistes put it at number 26 of 312. Its lifts climb from 1,087 m to 1,865 m. Its nearest neighbour is Parâng, 19 km north-east, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Straja

    Straja has 21 graded runs adding up to about 9.1 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 6.1 km, or 67%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.1 km of black or expert runs.

    How Straja is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Straja gives 778 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 gondola, 6 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 button lifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    How high do the lifts go at Straja?

    Who is Straja best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Straja?
