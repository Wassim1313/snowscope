    About Obergurgl-Hochgurgl

    With some 66 km of runs, Obergurgl-Hochgurgl belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,784 m up to 3,024 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sölden, 9.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Obergurgl-Hochgurgl

    Counting every graded piste, there are 120 runs and around 66 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 39 km, or 60%. With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The mountain at Obergurgl-Hochgurgl

    The vertical drop is 1,240 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,784 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 1 funicular, 11 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Großer Ramolkogel (3,549 m), 7.5 km to the south-west, about 525 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Obergurgl-Hochgurgl usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,404 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 13 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 16 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 89 cm and 167 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 74 cm; a snowy December gave 108 cm, a dry one 46 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 138 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 147 cm, around 28 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Obergurgl-Hochgurgl?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Obergurgl-Hochgurgl have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 74 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Obergurgl-Hochgurgl open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 19 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 18 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Obergurgl-Hochgurgl?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,024 m; the lowest starts at 1,784 m (1,240 m of vertical).

    Is Obergurgl-Hochgurgl good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 39 km, are easy.
