    The snow at Obergurgl-Hochgurgl, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Obergurgl-Hochgurgl (2,404 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 74 cm; half of the winters sit between 56 cm and 98 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 135 cm, between 107 cm and 158 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 147 cm.

    By 15 December 65 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 85 %.

    2000-01 was the big one (241 cm); 2024-25 the lean one (104 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early November until early June in a normal winter, roughly 218 days.

    About Obergurgl-Hochgurgl

    With some 66 km of runs, Obergurgl-Hochgurgl belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,784 m up to 3,024 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sölden, 9.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Obergurgl-Hochgurgl

    The vertical drop is 1,240 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,784 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
