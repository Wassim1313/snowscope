    About Jasenská dolina

    Jasenská dolina is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 4.0 km of pistes put it at number 64 of 312. The slopes start at 541 m and reach 746 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Hrádok, 12 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Jasenská dolina and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Malinô Brdo (19 km east) has about 9.0 km of pistes against 4.0 km here. The highest nearby is Martinské Hole, topping out at 1,452 m.

    Jasenská dolina today and the next days

    Up high, Jasenská dolina has roughly 16 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Saturday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 205 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 541 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Vrátna reaches 1,529 m.
