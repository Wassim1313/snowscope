    About Buscat

    Buscat is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 122 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,485 m up to 1,671 m. The closest other ski area is Buscat / Băișoara (2.8 km, east).

    The pistes of Buscat

    The piste map lists 3 graded runs, about 2.3 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.2 km (52%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 186 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,485 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Buscat: common questions

    How high is Buscat?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,671 m; the lowest starts at 1,485 m (186 m of vertical).

    Is Buscat suitable for families?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Buscat?

    Pârtiile Mărișel, 22 km to the north-west, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Buscat have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
