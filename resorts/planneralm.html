    About Planneralm

    Planneralm is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 401 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,589 m and reach 1,957 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Riesneralm, 8.3 km to the west.

    How much snow Planneralm usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,773 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 November and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 23 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 104 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 60 cm, from 18 cm in a poor year to 96 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 79 cm and 171 cm. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 144 cm, around 5 March.

    What there is to ski at Planneralm

    Counting every graded piste, there are 30 runs and around 14 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 8.8 km, or 61%. Intermediate runs make up 61% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 2.7 km of black or expert runs.

    How Planneralm is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Planneralm gives 368 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,589 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Hochrettelstein, 2,220 m high and 3.2 km north-east of the resort.

    Questions about Planneralm

    When is the best time to ski at Planneralm?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Is there snow at Planneralm at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 60 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Planneralm?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,957 m; the lowest starts at 1,589 m (368 m of vertical).

    Who is Planneralm best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 61% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Planneralm?

    About 14 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
