    About Sestriere

    Around 58 km of marked runs place Sestriere among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 100 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,600 m to 2,786 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pragelato, 4.6 km to the north-east.

    The mountain at Sestriere

    1,186 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,600 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
