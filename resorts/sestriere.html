    Sestriere in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Sestriere (about 2,193 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 60 cm, with a normal range of 46 cm–80 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 111 cm (90 cm–134 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 122 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    50 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 74 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 2017-18 (222 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2004-05 (81 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 177 days.

    Questions about Sestriere

    Does Sestriere have snow over Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 60 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 74 % of 46 winters. Right now: 53 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Sestriere?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 122 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Sestriere?

    About 122 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 81 cm in the leanest winter (2004-05) and 222 cm in the biggest (2017-18).

    How high do the lifts go at Sestriere?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,786 m; the lowest starts at 1,600 m (1,186 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Sestriere have?

    About 58 km of pistes and 22 lifts.
