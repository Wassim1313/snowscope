    About Sestriere

    Around 58 km of marked runs place Sestriere among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 100 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,600 m to 2,786 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pragelato, 4.6 km to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 124 runs and around 57 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 25 km (44%). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 8.4 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Sestriere

    1,186 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,600 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 22 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 8 chairlifts, 8 button lifts and 4 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Punta Ciatagnera, 3,294 m high and 7.9 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 2,193 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 2 December and lasts to 15 May: about 24 weeks. For about 10 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 122 cm, around 5 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 68 cm to 147 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 94 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 60 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 31 cm in a poor year to 109 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Sestriere

    When does Sestriere have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Sestriere have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 60 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Sestriere open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Sestriere?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,786 m; the lowest starts at 1,600 m (1,186 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Sestriere?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 27 km, are easy.
