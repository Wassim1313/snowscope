    About Hintertuxer Gletscher

    Hintertuxer Gletscher is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 49 km of pistes put it at number 122 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,500 m and 3,245 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Grünberglift Navis (11 km, north-west).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Hintertuxer Gletscher gives 1,745 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,500 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 7 gondolas, 6 chairlifts, 7 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet, 23 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Olperer, 3,476 m high and 3.9 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,372 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. Most winters land between 66 cm and 129 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 116 cm. For about 13 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 60 cm (lean years 37 cm, snowy years 86 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 111 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Runs by difficulty

    Hintertuxer Gletscher has 88 graded runs adding up to about 48 km. Most of the length is red (53%, about 25 km). Intermediate runs make up 53% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 4.7 km of difficult runs.

    Quick answers

    When does Hintertuxer Gletscher have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Hintertuxer Gletscher have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 60 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Hintertuxer Gletscher?

    It opened on 2 October 2026.

    What altitude is Hintertuxer Gletscher at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,245 m; the lowest starts at 1,500 m (1,745 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Hintertuxer Gletscher best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 53% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
