    About Winterberglifte

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Winterberglifte one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 882 m up to 947 m. Skilift Weißenbach is the next resort, 4.4 km away to the south.

    How Winterberglifte compares nearby

    For more terrain, Kandellifte (15 km south-west) has about 3.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. The highest nearby is Kandellifte, topping out at 1,201 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 914 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 3 cm; a snowy December gave 15 cm, a dry one 0 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 21 cm. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 21 January. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January.

    How Winterberglifte is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 65 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,082 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kandellifte reaches 1,201 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Obereck, 1,178 m high and 6.4 km south-west of the resort.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Winterberglifte?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Can you ski at Winterberglifte at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 3 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Winterberglifte?

    The top is at 947 m and the base at 882 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Winterberglifte?

    Kandellifte, 15 km to the south-west, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Winterberglifte have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
