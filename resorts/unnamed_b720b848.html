    About Skilift Dottingen

    Compact and local, Skilift Dottingen has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 744 m and reach 823 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Beiwald, 3.1 km to the north-west.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen (11 km west) has about 6.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Dottingen gives 79 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 744 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Gänsewag (861 m), Sternberg (844 m) and Banholz (804 m).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 784 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 5 cm, around 25 January. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and late January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, with 11 cm in the best years. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 20 cm.

    Questions about Skilift Dottingen

    What is the best month to ski at Skilift Dottingen?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    How much snow does Skilift Dottingen have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    What altitude is Skilift Dottingen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 823 m; the lowest starts at 744 m (79 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilift Dottingen?

    Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen, 11 km to the west, with about 6.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Skilift Dottingen have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
