    About Canmore

    Canmore is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 191 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 216 m up to 450 m. Its nearest neighbour is Asahiyama, 5.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 234 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 216 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Canmore hold around 7 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 12 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.
