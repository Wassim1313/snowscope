    About Hubelweid

    Hubelweid has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,102 m to 1,148 m. Homberg is the next resort, 2.2 km away to the north.

    Hubelweid and the resorts around it

    If snow is the priority, Niederhorn does better, with about 23 weeks of a good base instead of 13. For higher slopes, Niederhorn climbs to 1,938 m.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Hubelweid today: about 7 cm up high. The last seven days brought 29 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 13 cm by now.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 46 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Sigriswiler Rothorn (2,051 m), 6.9 km to the east, about 903 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Hubelweid usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,125 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 12 January and lasts to 21 April: about 13 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 26 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 9 cm in a poor year to 42 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 33 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 18 cm to 66 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 18 February, with about 45 cm.

    Hubelweid: common questions

    When does Hubelweid have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late April.

    Can you ski at Hubelweid at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Hubelweid?

    The top is at 1,148 m and the base at 1,102 m.

    How many km of pistes does Hubelweid have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
