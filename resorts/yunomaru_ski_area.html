    About Yunomaru

    Compact and local, Yunomaru has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Japan. The slopes start at 1,724 m and reach 1,844 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Takamine Mountain Park (4.1 km, south-east).

    How Yunomaru compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 14 km north-west to Sugadairakogen Pine Beak, with about 16 km of runs. The highest nearby is Palcall Tsumagoi Resort Ski & Ho, topping out at 2,089 m.

    How Yunomaru is laid out

    120 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,724 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
