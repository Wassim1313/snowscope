    About Gurtis

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gurtis offers about 11 km of pistes (number 465 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 918 m and reach 1,389 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tschardund, 4.0 km to the south-east.

    Gurtis and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Brandnertal (12 km south-east) has about 45 km of pistes against 11 km here. Malbun reaches 1,991 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 471 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,118 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Gurtis hold around 16 cm of snow today. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 18 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Gurtis include Saminapark Aparthotel & Boardinghouse (hotel), Naturfreundehaus Feldkircher Hütte (mountain hut), Hütte Schiverein Tisis (chalet) and Gutwinski (hotel).
