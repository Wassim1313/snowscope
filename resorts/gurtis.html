    About Gurtis

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gurtis offers about 11 km of pistes (number 465 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 918 m and reach 1,389 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tschardund, 4.0 km to the south-east.

    Snow at Gurtis through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,154 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 December and keeps it until about 3 May, roughly 19 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 31 cm and 82 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 11 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 27 February, with about 55 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 41 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    What there is to ski at Gurtis

    In all, 18 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. The largest share is red, with 4.5 km (40%). With 48% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 5.4 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 471 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,118 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 2 button lifts. The skyline is dominated by Galinakopf, 2,198 m high and 5.1 km south of the resort.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Gurtis hold around 1 cm of snow today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 29 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 13 cm by now.

    Questions about Gurtis

    When does Gurtis have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    How much snow does Gurtis have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 32 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Gurtis at?

    Between 918 m and 1,389 m, so 471 m of vertical drop.

    Does Gurtis have steep runs?

    Yes: about 5.4 km of its graded pistes (48%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Gurtis?

    Brandnertal, 12 km to the south-east, with about 45 km of pistes.
