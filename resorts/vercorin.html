    About Vercorin

    Neither huge nor tiny, Vercorin offers about 19 km of pistes (number 332 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,320 m to 2,369 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Nax, 5.2 km to the south-west.

    The pistes of Vercorin

    The piste map lists 41 graded runs, about 19 km in total. The largest share is red, with 9.7 km (50%). Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 1.5 km of black or expert runs. 1.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Vercorin today: about 41 cm up high. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 27 cm now.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,844 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 6 December and keeps it until about 24 May, roughly 24 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 61 cm to 123 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 89 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 102 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 54 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 30 cm in a poor year to 77 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 2 weeks.

    The mountain at Vercorin

    Skied top to bottom, Vercorin gives 1,049 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,320 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Saint-Luc reaches 2,973 m. Its 12 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 2 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 4 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Becs de Bosson, 3,143 m high and 7.8 km south of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Vercorin include Swiss Peak Resorts (hotel), L'A de Bran (mountain hut), Mazots de la Source (hotel) and Hostellerie d'Orzival (hotel).

    Questions about Vercorin

    What is the best month to ski at Vercorin?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Vercorin at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 54 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Vercorin at?

    Between 1,320 m and 2,369 m, so 1,049 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Vercorin best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Vercorin?

    Crans-Montana, 11 km to the north, with about 74 km of pistes.
