    About Vercorin

    Neither huge nor tiny, Vercorin offers about 19 km of pistes (number 332 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,320 m to 2,369 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Nax, 5.2 km to the south-west.

    The mountain at Vercorin

    Skied top to bottom, Vercorin gives 1,049 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,320 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Saint-Luc reaches 2,973 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Vercorin include Swiss Peak Resorts (hotel), L'A de Bran (mountain hut), Mazots de la Source (hotel) and Hostellerie d'Orzival (hotel).
