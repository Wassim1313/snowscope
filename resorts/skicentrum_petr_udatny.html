    About Skicentrum Petr Udatný

    Skicentrum Petr Udatný is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 130 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Its lifts climb from 557 m to 686 m. Skiareál Studenov is the next resort, 0.6 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 1.5 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 1.5 km (100%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Skicentrum Petr Udatný hold around 4 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 9 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 129 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 557 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 chairlift and 6 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 622 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 January and keeps it until about 29 February, roughly 4 weeks. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 58 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 49 cm, a dry one 2 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 34 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Skicentrum Petr Udatný?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from late January to late February.

    Can you ski at Skicentrum Petr Udatný at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Skicentrum Petr Udatný?

    The top is at 686 m and the base at 557 m.

    Is Skicentrum Petr Udatný suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Skicentrum Petr Udatný?

    Skiareál Horní Domky, 3.4 km to the east, with about 9.0 km of pistes.
