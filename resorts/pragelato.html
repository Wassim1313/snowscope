    About Pragelato

    Pragelato has about 0.3 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,525 m to 1,566 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sestriere, 4.6 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pragelato and the resorts around it

    Sansicario has the longer snow season: around 26 weeks of solid cover, compared with 13 at Pragelato. The highest nearby is Sestriere, topping out at 2,786 m.

    Pragelato today and the next days

    Today there are about 2 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Pragelato. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 29 cm by Sunday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 10 cm now.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 41 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,525 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sestriere, for one, climbs to 2,786 m. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 1 button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Punta Rognosa at 3,282 m, standing 1,716 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 1,546 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 January and lasts to 4 April: about 13 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 25 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 58 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 18 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 29 cm and 81 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 58 cm.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Albergo Passet (hotel), Trampolini (hotel), Casa San Giuseppe (hotel) and Rifugio Mulino di Laval (mountain hut).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Pragelato?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    How much snow does Pragelato have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Pragelato?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,566 m; the lowest starts at 1,525 m (41 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Pragelato have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
