    About Pragelato

    Pragelato is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 1,525 m to 1,566 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sestriere, 4.6 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pragelato today and the next days

    Today there are about 6 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Pragelato. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 17 cm by Thursday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 41 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,525 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sestriere, for one, climbs to 2,786 m.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Albergo Passet (hotel), Trampolini (hotel), Casa San Giuseppe (hotel) and Rifugio Mulino di Laval (mountain hut).

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Pragelato?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,566 m; the lowest starts at 1,525 m (41 m of vertical).
