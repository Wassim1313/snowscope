    About Balme

    Balme is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 31 km of pistes put it at number 195 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,262 m to 2,252 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Vormaine, 2.2 km to the south.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Balme has roughly 200 cm of snow on the ground today. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 116 cm by Saturday.

    The mountain at Balme

    From top to bottom that makes 990 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,252 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
