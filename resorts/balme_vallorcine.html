    About Balme

    Balme is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 31 km of pistes put it at number 195 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,262 m to 2,252 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Vormaine, 2.2 km to the south.

    The pistes of Balme

    In all, 98 marked runs cover roughly 30 km. Most of the length is red (52%, about 16 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Snow at Balme through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,757 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 27 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 19 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 158 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 92 cm; a snowy December gave 146 cm, a dry one 45 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 108 cm and 224 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 177 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    The mountain at Balme

    From top to bottom that makes 990 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,252 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 9 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts and 5 button lifts. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Aiguille de Chardonnet (3,824 m), 7.2 km to the south-east, about 1,572 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Balme?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Balme at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 92 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Balme?

    Between 1,262 m and 2,252 m, so 990 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Balme?

    Yes: 45% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How big is Balme?

    About 31 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
