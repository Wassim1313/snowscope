    How much snow Gol Skisenter usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 718 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 November and keeps it until about 14 May, roughly 25 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 13 March, with about 90 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 54 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 84 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 64 cm and 109 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 80 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Questions about Gol Skisenter

    When is the best time to ski at Gol Skisenter?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Gol Skisenter have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 54 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
