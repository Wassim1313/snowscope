    The snow at Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl (about 1,560 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 43 cm, with a normal range of 28 cm–57 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 89 cm; the middle half of winters range from 67 cm to 108 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 103 cm.

    By 15 December 28 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 48 %.

    The deepest winter was 1999-00 (149 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (65 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to early May (172 days).

    Questions about Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl

    Does Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 43 cm, and only 48 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 18 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl?

    March: the snow peaks at about 103 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to early May.

    How much snow does Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 103 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 149 cm (1999-00), the thinnest 65 cm (2025-26).

    What altitude is Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl at?

    Between 1,011 m and 2,109 m, so 1,098 m of vertical drop.
