    About Grand Sunpia Inawashiro Resort

    With some 9.0 km of runs, Grand Sunpia Inawashiro Resort belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Skiing here happens between 771 m and 1,198 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Inawashiro Snow Paradise (2.7 km, east).

    The pistes of Grand Sunpia Inawashiro Resort

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 9.5 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 5.2 km, or 55%. With 93% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Grand Sunpia Inawashiro Resort is laid out

    The vertical drop is 427 m: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 771 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 1 gondola and 3 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.
