    About Cherry Peak

    Cherry Peak is a mid-sized ski area with about 11 km of pistes, number 124 of 175 in US West by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,764 m up to 2,132 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Beaver Mountain, 18 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Cherry Peak has 36 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 4.5 km, or 40%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.3 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,948 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 58 cm and 160 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 53 cm, from 28 cm in a poor year to 109 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 121 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 7 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 68 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    How Cherry Peak is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Cherry Peak gives 368 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,764 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 4 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Naomi Peak at 3,041 m, standing 909 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Cherry Peak

    When is the best time to ski at Cherry Peak?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Cherry Peak at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 53 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Cherry Peak?

    The top is at 2,132 m and the base at 1,764 m.

    Is Cherry Peak good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 3.3 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.

    How big is Cherry Peak?

    About 11 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
