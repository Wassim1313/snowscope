    Oukaïmeden in past winters

    42 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Oukaïmeden (about 2,929 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 18 cm, with a normal range of 5 cm–35 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 42 cm; the middle half of winters range from 22 cm to 73 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 57 cm.

    By 15 December 12 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 14 %.

    The deepest winter was 1994-95 (207 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2000-01 (41 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 127 days.

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Oukaïmeden at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 18 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 14 % of 42 winters. Right now: no snow.

    What is the best time to ski Oukaïmeden?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 57 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Oukaïmeden?

    About 57 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 41 cm in the leanest winter (2000-01) and 207 cm in the biggest (1994-95).

    How high is Oukaïmeden?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,258 m; the lowest starts at 2,600 m (658 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Oukaïmeden have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 1 lift.
