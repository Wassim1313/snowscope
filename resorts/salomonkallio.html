    About Salomonkallio

    Salomonkallio is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 17 m and 42 m above sea level. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hittenharju, 14 km to the south.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Salomonkallio gives 25 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Salomonkallio today: about 2 cm up high. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Tuesday.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 30 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 6 cm to 36 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 21 cm, a dry one 0 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 6 February, with about 22 cm.

    Questions about Salomonkallio

    What is the best month to ski at Salomonkallio?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    How much snow does Salomonkallio have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Salomonkallio?

    Between 17 m and 42 m, so 25 m of vertical drop.
