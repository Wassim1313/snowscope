    About Harrington Hill

    Harrington Hill has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 302 m up to 352 m. Its nearest neighbour is Grill Hill, 14 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Harrington Hill is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 50 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 302 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a rope tow. Above the ski area rises Williams Hill (591 m), 7.2 km to the north-west, about 239 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 327 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early February to mid-March, about 6 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 13 cm and 66 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 17 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 38 cm). By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 38 cm, around 17 February.

    Questions about Harrington Hill

    What is the best month to ski at Harrington Hill?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    How much snow does Harrington Hill have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 17 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Harrington Hill at?

    Its highest lift reaches 352 m; the lowest starts at 302 m (50 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Harrington Hill have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
