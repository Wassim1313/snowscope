    About Feuerkogel

    Feuerkogel is a mid-sized ski area with about 11 km of pistes, number 462 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,050 m up to 1,676 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hochlecken, 9.1 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Feuerkogel usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,363 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 20 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 35 cm; a snowy December gave 67 cm, a dry one 7 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 81 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 42 cm and 108 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 74 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    What there is to ski at Feuerkogel

    Feuerkogel has 29 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 8.4 km, or 78%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    626 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Großer Höllkogel (1,862 m), 4.3 km to the west, about 186 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Hüttendorf Feuerkogel (chalet), Naturfreundehaus- Kranabethhütte (mountain hut), Berggasthof Feuerkogel (mountain hut) and Berggasthof Edelweiss (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Feuerkogel: common questions

    When does Feuerkogel have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Feuerkogel at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 35 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Feuerkogel?

    Between 1,050 m and 1,676 m, so 626 m of vertical drop.

    Is Feuerkogel suitable for families?

    Yes: 78% of the graded pistes, about 8.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Feuerkogel have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
