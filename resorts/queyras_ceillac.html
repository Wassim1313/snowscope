    About Queyras

    Neither huge nor tiny, Queyras offers about 18 km of pistes (number 342 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,652 m up to 2,449 m. The closest other ski area is Queyras (8.5 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Queyras has 32 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 69%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Snow at Queyras through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,050 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 27 November and keeps it until about 19 May, roughly 25 weeks. A normal season tops out near 123 cm, around 8 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 66 cm and 145 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 86 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For about 10 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 59 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 35 cm, snowy years 111 cm).

    The mountain at Queyras

    Skied top to bottom, Queyras gives 797 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,652 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 4 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pics de la Font Sancte at 3,385 m, standing 936 m above the top lift.

    Queyras: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Queyras?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Queyras at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 59 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Queyras at?

    Between 1,652 m and 2,449 m, so 797 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Queyras best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Queyras?

    Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul, 13 km to the south-west, with about 131 km of pistes.
