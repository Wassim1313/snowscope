    About Urnäsch

    Compact and local, Urnäsch has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 831 m and reach 1,083 m at the top. Gonten is the next resort, 4.3 km away to the east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Appenzell (8.7 km east) has about 6.0 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    The pistes of Urnäsch

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.8 km. The largest share is red, with 1.4 km (74%). Intermediate runs make up 74% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. 0.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Urnäsch gives 252 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 831 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Säntis (2,502 m), 7.8 km to the south-east, about 1,419 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 957 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 34 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm; a snowy December gave 20 cm, a dry one 0 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 19 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Urnäsch?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Urnäsch have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Urnäsch?

    The top is at 1,083 m and the base at 831 m.

    Does Urnäsch have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.5 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Urnäsch?

    Appenzell, 8.7 km to the east, with about 6.0 km of pistes.
