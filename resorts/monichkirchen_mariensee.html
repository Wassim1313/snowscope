    About Mönichkirchen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mönichkirchen offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 524 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 861 m up to 1,428 m. The closest other ski area is Wexl Arena (6.6 km, north).

    The pistes of Mönichkirchen

    Mönichkirchen has 13 graded runs adding up to about 9.2 km. The largest share is blue, with 5.0 km (54%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Mönichkirchen is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Mönichkirchen gives 567 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 861 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,428 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hochwechsel at 1,743 m, standing 315 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Mönichkirchen usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,144 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 29 January, with about 12 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 31 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 7 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 22 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Mönichkirchen

    When is the best time to ski at Mönichkirchen?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Mönichkirchen at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 7 cm).

    How high is Mönichkirchen?

    Between 861 m and 1,428 m, so 567 m of vertical drop.

    Is Mönichkirchen suitable for families?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 5.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Mönichkirchen have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
