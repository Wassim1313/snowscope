    About Grand Targhee Resort

    With some 56 km of runs, Grand Targhee Resort belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,318 m up to 2,999 m. The closest other ski area is Jackson Hole (22 km, south).

    The pistes of Grand Targhee Resort

    In all, 93 marked runs cover roughly 56 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 34 km, or 61%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 8.3 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Grand Targhee Resort through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,658 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early November to late May or later, about 29 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 22 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 156 cm to 251 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 207 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 133 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 93 cm, snowy years 182 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 1 April, with about 236 cm.

    The mountain at Grand Targhee Resort

    The vertical drop is 681 m: runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,318 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. To get up there are 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Littles Peak (3,258 m), 6.6 km to the east, about 259 m higher than the top lift.
