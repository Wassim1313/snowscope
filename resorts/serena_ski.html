    About Serena Ski

    Compact and local, Serena Ski has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The slopes start at 45 m and reach 97 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Solvalla Swinghill (10 km, west).

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Vihti: about 5.0 km of pistes, 21 km to the west.

    Skied top to bottom, Serena Ski gives 52 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning.
