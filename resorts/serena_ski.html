    About Serena Ski

    Compact and local, Serena Ski has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The slopes start at 45 m and reach 97 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Solvalla Swinghill (10 km, west).

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Vihti: about 5.0 km of pistes, 21 km to the west. Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus has the longer snow season: around 8 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Serena Ski.

    The pistes of Serena Ski

    Counting every graded piste, there are 14 runs and around 1.4 km to ski. The largest share is black, with 0.6 km (43%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Serena Ski gives 52 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 4 T-bars and 1 button lift. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mustakorvenkallio (114 m), Petikonhuippu (109 m) and Hernemäenkallio (84 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 71 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 28 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 6 cm to 53 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm; a snowy December gave 26 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Questions about Serena Ski

    When is the best time to ski at Serena Ski?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Serena Ski have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Serena Ski?

    The top is at 97 m and the base at 45 m.

    Can beginners ski at Serena Ski?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Serena Ski?

    Vihti, 21 km to the west, with about 5.0 km of pistes.
