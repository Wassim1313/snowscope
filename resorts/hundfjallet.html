    About Hundfjället

    With some 23 km of runs, Hundfjället belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 580 m and 831 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Tandådalen, 2.7 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Hundfjället

    Counting every graded piste, there are 29 runs and around 22 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 9.2 km, or 42%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Advanced skiers have about 2.6 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    251 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 7 T-bars, 6 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Stor-Närfjället (931 m), Hundfjället (925 m) and Källfjället (903 m).

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 706 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 December and keeps it until about 27 April, roughly 19 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 66 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 36 cm (lean years 13 cm, snowy years 61 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 50 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 44 cm and 85 cm by mid-February.

    Hundfjället: common questions

    When does Hundfjället have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Hundfjället have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 36 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Hundfjället?

    Between 580 m and 831 m, so 251 m of vertical drop.

    Is Hundfjället good for beginners?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 9.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hundfjället?

    Sälen, 6.9 km to the east, with about 90 km of pistes.
