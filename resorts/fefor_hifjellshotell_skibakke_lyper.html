    About Fefor Høifjellshotell Skibakke &

    Fefor Høifjellshotell Skibakke & is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 181 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Skiing here happens between 943 m and 1,072 m above sea level. Gålå Alpinsenter is the next resort, 7.7 km away to the south-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 2.1 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.1 km (100%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    129 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Storslåkampen, 1,428 m high and 7.5 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,008 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 97 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 64 cm, from 42 cm in a poor year to 94 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 92 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Most winters land between 73 cm and 122 cm by mid-February.

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Fefor Høifjellshotell Skibakke & at Christmas?

    How high is Fefor Høifjellshotell Skibakke &?

    Can beginners ski at Fefor Høifjellshotell Skibakke &?
