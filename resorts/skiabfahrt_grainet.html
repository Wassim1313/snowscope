    About Skiabfahrt Grainet

    Skiabfahrt Grainet has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 675 m to 764 m. Skilift Herzogsreut is the next resort, 4.2 km away to the north.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 720 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 38 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 58 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The snow is usually deepest around 23 January, with about 20 cm.

    How Skiabfahrt Grainet is laid out

    The vertical drop is 89 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 675 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Mitterdorf, reach 1,142 m. The only lift is a T-bar.

    Questions about Skiabfahrt Grainet

    When is the best time to ski at Skiabfahrt Grainet?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Skiabfahrt Grainet at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Skiabfahrt Grainet at?

    Between 675 m and 764 m, so 89 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Skiabfahrt Grainet have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
