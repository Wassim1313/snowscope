    About Eagle Point

    Neither huge nor tiny, Eagle Point offers about 24 km of pistes (number 88 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 2,843 m and reach 3,222 m at the top.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 75 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Eagle Point. The last seven days brought 23 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 36 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The pistes of Eagle Point

    In all, 45 marked runs cover roughly 24 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 14 km, or 58%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 5.6 km of difficult runs.

    How Eagle Point is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Eagle Point gives 379 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,843 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 4 chairlifts and 1 button lift. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Delano Peak at 3,710 m, standing 488 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Eagle Point usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 3,032 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 November and lasts to 11 May: about 24 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 130 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 15 weeks. Most winters land between 44 cm and 192 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 162 cm, around 17 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 71 cm; a snowy December gave 144 cm, a dry one 18 cm.

    Eagle Point: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Eagle Point?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Eagle Point at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 71 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Eagle Point at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,222 m; the lowest starts at 2,843 m (379 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Eagle Point best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 58% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Eagle Point?

    About 24 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
