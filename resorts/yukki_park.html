    About Спортивный комплекс "Юкки" ГАУ Л

    The mountain at Спортивный комплекс "Юкки" ГАУ Л

    How much snow Спортивный комплекс "Юкки" ГАУ Л usually gets

    At about 66 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 February and lasts to 11 March: about 4 weeks. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 31 cm, around 8 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 15 cm; a snowy December gave 32 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 10 cm to 55 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Спортивный комплекс "Юкки" ГАУ Л

    When is the best time to ski at Спортивный комплекс "Юкки" ГАУ Л?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Спортивный комплекс "Юкки" ГАУ Л at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 15 cm deep.

    How high is Спортивный комплекс "Юкки" ГАУ Л?

    Its highest lift reaches 78 m; the lowest starts at 55 m (23 m of vertical).

    How big is Спортивный комплекс "Юкки" ГАУ Л?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
