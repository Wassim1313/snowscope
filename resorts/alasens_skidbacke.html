    About Ålåsens Skidbacke

    Compact and local, Ålåsens Skidbacke has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 451 m up to 502 m. The closest other ski area is Åkersjöns Skidbacke (30 km, west).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Ålåsens Skidbacke today: about 31 cm up high. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 35 cm by now.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 476 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to early May. The snow is usually deepest around 25 March, with about 82 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 75 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 48 cm; a snowy December gave 75 cm, a dry one 28 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 50 cm to 101 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 51 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Tjärnafjället at 760 m, standing 258 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Ålåsens Skidbacke

    When is the best time to ski at Ålåsens Skidbacke?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Is there snow at Ålåsens Skidbacke at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 48 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Ålåsens Skidbacke at?

    The top is at 502 m and the base at 451 m.

    How big is Ålåsens Skidbacke?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
