    How Schwarzer Mann is laid out

    The vertical drop is 85 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 607 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 692 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.

    The pistes of Schwarzer Mann

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 1.4 km in total. Most of the length is blue (57%, about 0.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.
