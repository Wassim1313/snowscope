    About Borovets

    Only 1 of the 51 ski areas of the Balkans is larger than Borovets, which has some 43 km of runs. The slopes start at 1,329 m and reach 2,538 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Malyovista (17 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Borovets

    Counting every graded piste, there are 49 runs and around 42 km to ski. The largest share is green (beginner), with 20 km (47%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,209 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Starting at 1,329 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 14 lifts: 2 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 6 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    Quick answers

    How high is Borovets?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,538 m; the lowest starts at 1,329 m (1,209 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Borovets?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 28 km, are easy.

    How big is Borovets?

    About 43 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
