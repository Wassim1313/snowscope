    About Valtournenche

    With some 27 km of runs, Valtournenche belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,559 m and 3,091 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Chamois, 6.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Valtournenche is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Valtournenche gives 1,532 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,559 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 3,091 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 gondola and 5 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. Above the ski area rises Gobba di Rollin (3,899 m), 6.5 km to the north-east, about 808 m higher than the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 38 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Valtournenche. The last seven days brought 58 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 22 cm at mid-mountain).

    How much snow Valtournenche usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,325 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 December and keeps it until about 26 May, roughly 22 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 18 cm in a poor year to 47 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. Most winters land between 34 cm and 66 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 53 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 54 cm, around 3 April.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Valtournenche?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from late December to late May.

    Is there snow at Valtournenche at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 31 cm deep.

    What altitude is Valtournenche at?

    Between 1,559 m and 3,091 m, so 1,532 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Valtournenche best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 60% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Valtournenche have?

    About 27 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
