    How much snow Torsbustaden alpinsenter usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 188 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 17 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm; a snowy December gave 56 cm, a dry one 13 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 68 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 37 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Most winters land between 24 cm and 88 cm by mid-February.

    Quick answers

    When does Torsbustaden alpinsenter have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.
