    About Bačova roveň

    Bačova roveň is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 118 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Skiing here happens between 987 m and 1,353 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Čertovica, 2.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Bačova roveň has 4 graded runs adding up to about 2.3 km. Most of the length is red (51%, about 1.2 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.1 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 366 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 987 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 T-bar and 2 button lifts.
