    About Hergiswil

    Hergiswil has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 834 m to 937 m. Its nearest neighbour is Pilatus, 2.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    For more reliable snow, Klewenalp usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 25 weeks, against 8 here. The highest nearby is Pilatus, topping out at 2,063 m.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Hergiswil hold around 1 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 2 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 8 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    103 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 834 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Pilatus reaches 2,063 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car and 1 button lift, 2 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The skyline is dominated by Tomlishorn, 2,128 m high and 3.3 km west of the resort.

    How much snow Hergiswil usually gets

    Count on about 8 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 886 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to late March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 18 cm and 54 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 23 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 22 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 32 cm, a dry one 8 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 38 cm.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Hergiswil include Pilatus Kulm (hotel), Brunni (guesthouse), Pilatus Bellevue (hotel) and Hotel Roggerli (hotel).

    Hergiswil: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hergiswil?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Can you ski at Hergiswil at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 22 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Hergiswil?

    The top is at 937 m and the base at 834 m.

    How big is Hergiswil?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
