    What 46 winters say about Heutal

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Heutal (1,246 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 40 cm, with a normal range of 24 cm–59 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 76 cm, between 59 cm and 107 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 88 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 24 % of winters, and by 1 January in 41 %.

    1981-82 was the big one (276 cm); 2006-07 the lean one (47 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to mid-May, about 162 days.

    Questions about Heutal

    Is there snow in Heutal at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 40 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 41 % of 46 winters. Right now: 14 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Heutal?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 88 cm). For a sure base, early December to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Heutal get?

    About Heutal

    Neither huge nor tiny, Heutal offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 513 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 961 m up to 1,531 m. Its nearest neighbour is Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, 6.1 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Heutal gives 570 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 961 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.
