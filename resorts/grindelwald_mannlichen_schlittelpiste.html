    Grindelwald in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Grindelwald (1,582 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 30 cm; half of the winters sit between 20 cm and 36 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 56 cm; the middle half of winters range from 47 cm to 65 cm.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 58 cm.

    By 15 December 2 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 2 %.

    The deepest winter was 1998-99 (104 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2022-23 (33 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late December to early May (134 days).

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 30 cm, and only 2 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 14 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Grindelwald?

    How snowy is Grindelwald?
