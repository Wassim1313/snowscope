    About Grindelwald

    Neither huge nor tiny, Grindelwald offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 546 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 940 m and 2,223 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Grindelwald, 2.2 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Grindelwald has 42 graded runs adding up to about 7.9 km. Most of the length is red (49%, about 3.9 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.0 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,283 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 940 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 3 cable cars, 4 funiculars, 123 mountain railways, 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 4 rope tows and 3 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Mönch, 4,107 m high and 7.0 km south of the resort.

    Snow at Grindelwald through a normal winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,582 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to early May. Year to year the snow is steady, with 37 cm to 77 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 30 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 13 cm, snowy years 42 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 58 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 37 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Questions about Grindelwald

    When does Grindelwald have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Can you ski at Grindelwald at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 30 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Grindelwald?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Grindelwald?

    The top is at 2,223 m and the base at 940 m.

    Who is Grindelwald best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 49% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
