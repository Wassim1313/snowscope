    About Tannenberg

    Tannenberg is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 789 m and 847 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Beckenhalde (7.2 km, south-east).

    The mountain at Tannenberg

    The vertical drop is 58 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 789 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are B+B (guesthouse), Gasthaus Kreuz (hotel), Revier Hotel Säntispark (hotel) and Towerhotel (hotel).
