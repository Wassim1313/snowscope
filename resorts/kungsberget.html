    About Kungsberget

    Kungsberget is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 16 km of pistes put it at number 40 of 262. Skiing here happens between 114 m and 308 m above sea level.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 32 marked runs cover roughly 16 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (40%, about 6.5 km). With 77% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How Kungsberget is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Kungsberget gives 194 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 5 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 3 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Kungsberget (306 m) and Vettåsen (259 m).

    Snow at Kungsberget through a normal winter

    At about 211 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 January and lasts to 7 March: about 4 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 33 cm, around 31 January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 17 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 1 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 7 cm to 56 cm in mid-February in most winters. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Kungsberget?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Is there snow at Kungsberget at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 17 cm deep.

    How high is Kungsberget?

    The top is at 308 m and the base at 114 m.

    Can beginners ski at Kungsberget?

    Yes: 77% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    How big is Kungsberget?

    About 16 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
