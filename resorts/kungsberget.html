    What 46 winters say about Kungsberget

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Kungsberget (211 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 17 cm of cover (7 cm to 26 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 31 cm (20 cm–49 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 33 cm.

    0 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 4 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 87 cm. Worst: 1995-96, which never passed 19 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early January to late March (93 days).

    Kungsberget: quick answers

    Can you ski Kungsberget at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 17 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 4 % of 46 winters. Right now: 17 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Kungsberget?

    February: the snow peaks at about 33 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early January to late March.

    How snowy is Kungsberget?

    About 33 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late January); between 19 cm in the leanest winter (1995-96) and 87 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    How high is Kungsberget?

    The top is at 308 m and the base at 114 m.

    How big is Kungsberget?

    About 16 km of pistes and 13 lifts.

    Kungsberget is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 16 km of pistes put it at number 40 of 262. Skiing here happens between 114 m and 308 m above sea level.
