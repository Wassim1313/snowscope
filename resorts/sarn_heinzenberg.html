    About Sarn Heinzenberg

    With roughly 16 km of runs, Sarn Heinzenberg sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,309 m and reach 2,050 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Tschappina Heinzenberg, 3.4 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Sarn Heinzenberg

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 16 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 16 km (100%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Sarn Heinzenberg

    From top to bottom that makes 741 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,309 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Piz Fess (Bös Fess) at 2,880 m, standing 830 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Sarn Heinzenberg through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,680 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to mid-May, about 21 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 61 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Most winters land between 41 cm and 82 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 21 March, with about 69 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 35 cm, from 19 cm in a poor year to 53 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When does Sarn Heinzenberg have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Sarn Heinzenberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 35 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Sarn Heinzenberg?

    The top is at 2,050 m and the base at 1,309 m.

    Can beginners ski at Sarn Heinzenberg?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 16 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Sarn Heinzenberg have?

    About 16 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
