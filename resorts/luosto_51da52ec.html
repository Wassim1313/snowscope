    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 223 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 4 December and keeps it until about 8 May, roughly 22 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Most winters land between 52 cm and 78 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm, with 52 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 59 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 66 cm, around 7 March.

    Luosto: common questions

    When does Luosto have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Luosto at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 42 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
