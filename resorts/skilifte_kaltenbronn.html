    About Skilifte Kaltenbronn

    Skilifte Kaltenbronn is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 862 m up to 953 m. Mehliskopf is the next resort, 15 km away to the south-west.

    How Skilifte Kaltenbronn is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 91 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 862 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Skizirkus Unterstmatt, reach 1,114 m. All 2 lifts here are T-bars.
