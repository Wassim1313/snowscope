    About Skilifte Kaltenbronn

    Skilifte Kaltenbronn is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 862 m up to 953 m. Mehliskopf is the next resort, 15 km away to the south-west.

    How Skilifte Kaltenbronn compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 19 km south-west to Skizirkus Unterstmatt, with about 5.0 km of runs. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 908 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for early January and late January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 4 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 27 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 9 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 42 cm.
