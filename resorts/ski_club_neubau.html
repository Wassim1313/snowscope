    About Ski-Club Neubau

    Ski-Club Neubau is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 767 m up to 803 m. The closest other ski area is Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift (2.2 km, west).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 36 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 767 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.
