    About Crévacol

    Crévacol is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 24 km of pistes put it at number 252 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,647 m and 2,450 m above sea level. La Fouly is the next resort, 12 km away to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 28 graded runs, about 24 km in total. Most of the length is red (42%, about 10 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 5.8 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Crévacol through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,048 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 26 weeks of good cover. For about 10 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 71 cm to 134 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 59 cm; a snowy December gave 92 cm, a dry one 32 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 105 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 112 cm.

    How Crévacol is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 803 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,647 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grande Rochère at 3,328 m, standing 878 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Crévacol?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Crévacol at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 59 cm deep.

    How high is Crévacol?

    The top is at 2,450 m and the base at 1,647 m.

    What level of skier is Crévacol best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 42% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Crévacol have?

    About 24 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
