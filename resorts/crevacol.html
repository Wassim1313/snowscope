    About Crévacol

    Crévacol is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 24 km of pistes put it at number 253 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,647 m and 2,450 m above sea level. La Fouly is the next resort, 12 km away to the north.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Crévacol hold around 104 cm of snow today. 45 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 31 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    How Crévacol is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 803 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,647 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Hôtel des Alpes (hotel), Hôtel Suisse (hotel), B&B La Vieille Cloche (guesthouse) and Hotel Italia (hotel).
