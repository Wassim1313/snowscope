    About Stuhleck

    Stuhleck is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 291 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 800 m up to 1,767 m. Its nearest neighbour is Semmering Hirschenkogel, 6.2 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Stuhleck usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,284 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 31 December and keeps it until about 11 March, roughly 10 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 25 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 63 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 10 cm to 96 cm. A normal season tops out near 52 cm, around 28 February. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    What there is to ski at Stuhleck

    In all, 32 marked runs cover roughly 22 km. The largest share is blue, with 16 km (73%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Stuhleck is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 967 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,000 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 6 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 12 lifts in all. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Stuhleck (1,782 m), Amundsenhöhe (1,666 m) and Pretul (1,656 m).

    Stuhleck: common questions

    When does Stuhleck have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Stuhleck at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 25 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Stuhleck?

    Between 800 m and 1,767 m, so 967 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Stuhleck?

    Yes: 73% of the graded pistes, about 16 km, are easy.

    How big is Stuhleck?

    About 22 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
