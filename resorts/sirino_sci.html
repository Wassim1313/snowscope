    About Sirino Sci

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sirino Sci offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 26 of 40 in the Apennines). Its lifts climb from 1,423 m to 1,879 m. Its nearest neighbour is Viggiano, 27 km north, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Sirino Sci has 14 graded runs adding up to about 5.1 km. Most of the length is blue (62%, about 3.2 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Sirino Sci

    456 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,423 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 3 button lifts and 1 drag lift, 5 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
