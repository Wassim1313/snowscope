    About Oguna Hotaka

    Around 10 km of marked runs place Oguna Hotaka among the larger resorts of Japan, number 76 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,242 m up to 1,803 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Katashina Ogna Hotaka Snow Resor, 3.2 km to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Oguna Hotaka

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 9.6 km. The largest share is red, with 4.3 km (45%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 1.6 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 561 m: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,803 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 9 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Oguna Hotaka. The last seven days brought 18 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 27 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Oguna Hotaka at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,803 m; the lowest starts at 1,242 m (561 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Oguna Hotaka best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 45% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Oguna Hotaka have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
