    About Monte Pora

    Monte Pora is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 350 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,379 m and 1,866 m above sea level. Presolana is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the north.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,622 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 20 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 92 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 63 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 131 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 44 cm, from 10 cm in a poor year to 87 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 34 graded runs, about 17 km in total. The largest share is red, with 8.0 km (48%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.3 km.

    The mountain at Monte Pora

    487 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,379 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 8 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Presolana Occidentale, 2,521 m high and 7.9 km north-west of the resort.

    Questions about Monte Pora

    When does Monte Pora have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Monte Pora at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 44 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Monte Pora?

    The top is at 1,866 m and the base at 1,379 m.

    Can beginners ski at Monte Pora?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 7.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Monte Pora have?

    About 17 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
