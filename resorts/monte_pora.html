    About Monte Pora

    Monte Pora is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 350 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,379 m and 1,866 m above sea level. Presolana is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the north.

    The mountain at Monte Pora

    487 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,379 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
