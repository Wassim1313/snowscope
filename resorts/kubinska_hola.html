    About Kubínská hola

    Kubínská hola is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 9.0 km of pistes put it at number 23 of 312. The slopes start at 742 m and reach 1,390 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ski Zábava Hruštín (3.3 km, north).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    648 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 742 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,390 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 12 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Kubínská hola. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.
