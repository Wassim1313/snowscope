    About Kubínská hola

    Kubínská hola is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 9.0 km of pistes put it at number 23 of 312. The slopes start at 742 m and reach 1,390 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ski Zábava Hruštín (3.3 km, north).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Kubínská hola has 19 graded runs adding up to about 9.4 km. Most of the length is red (48%, about 4.5 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.0 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    648 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 742 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,390 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 9 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 6 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.
