    About Kitashiga Komaruyama

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kitashiga Komaruyama offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 250 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 795 m and 1,110 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Ryuoo Ski Park (1.8 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 2.8 km to ski. Most of the length is red (51%, about 1.4 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How Kitashiga Komaruyama is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 315 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 795 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 5 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts and 1 drag lift. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
