    About Nové Hamry

    Nové Hamry is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 114 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Its lifts climb from 696 m to 825 m. The closest other ski area is Nové Hamry (0.7 km, south-west).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 760 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 41 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 29 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 1 February, with about 17 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 129 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 696 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 4 lifts here are button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Nové Hamry: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Nové Hamry?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    How much snow does Nové Hamry have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 6 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Nové Hamry?

    Between 696 m and 825 m, so 129 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Nové Hamry best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 76% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Nové Hamry have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
