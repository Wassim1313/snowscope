    About Aprica

    Around 38 km of marked runs place Aprica among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 160 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,157 m to 2,254 m. The closest other ski area is Alpe Teglio (11 km, north-west).

    How much snow Aprica usually gets

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,706 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 66 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 80 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 49 cm to 110 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 44 cm, with 82 cm in the best years.

    The pistes of Aprica

    Counting every graded piste, there are 59 runs and around 38 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 19 km, or 49%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 6.0 km of difficult runs.

    How Aprica is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Aprica gives 1,097 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 5 button lifts and 4 moving carpets, 15 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Monte Torena, 2,911 m high and 7.0 km south-west of the resort.

    Aprica: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Aprica?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Aprica have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 44 cm deep.

    What altitude is Aprica at?

    Between 1,157 m and 2,254 m, so 1,097 m of vertical drop.

    Is Aprica good for beginners?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 19 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Aprica have?

    About 38 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
