    The snow at Aprica, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Aprica (about 1,706 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 44 cm, with a normal range of 30 cm–65 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 72 cm (58 cm–90 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 80 cm.

    By 15 December 33 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 50 %.

    The deepest winter was 2000-01 (195 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2006-07 (51 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to early May (166 days).

    Common questions

    Does Aprica have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 44 cm, and only 50 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 51 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Aprica?

    March: the snow peaks at about 80 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to early May.

    How snowy is Aprica?

    About 80 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 51 cm in the leanest winter (2006-07) and 195 cm in the biggest (2000-01).

    What altitude is Aprica at?

    Between 1,157 m and 2,254 m, so 1,097 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Aprica have?

    About 38 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
