    About Aprica

    Around 38 km of marked runs place Aprica among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 160 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,157 m to 2,254 m. The closest other ski area is Alpe Teglio (11 km, north-west).

    How Aprica is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Aprica gives 1,097 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
