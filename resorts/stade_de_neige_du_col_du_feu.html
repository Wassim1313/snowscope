    About Stade de neige du Col du Feu

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Stade de neige du Col du Feu one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,082 m and 1,160 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Hirmentaz, 6.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Praz de Lys Sommand: about 47 km of pistes, 17 km to the south. If snow is the priority, Praz de Lys Sommand does better, with about 25 weeks of a good base instead of 18. Praz de Lys Sommand reaches 1,960 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    This week's snow

    There is no snow on the ground at Stade de neige du Col du Feu yet. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 12 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 8 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,121 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late April, about 18 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 39 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. A normal season tops out near 50 cm, around 24 February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 30 cm; a snowy December gave 72 cm, a dry one 6 cm. Most winters land between 17 cm and 79 cm by mid-February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    78 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Praz de Lys Sommand, reach 1,960 m. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Mont Billiat (1,894 m), 5.7 km to the east, about 734 m higher than the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Hôtel de l'Union (hotel), Hôtel de la Poste (hotel), Ecosse (chalet) and Auberge du Moulin de Léré (guesthouse).

    Questions about Stade de neige du Col du Feu

    What is the best month to ski at Stade de neige du Col du Feu?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Stade de neige du Col du Feu at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 30 cm deep.

    How high is Stade de neige du Col du Feu?

    The top is at 1,160 m and the base at 1,082 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Stade de neige du Col du Feu?

    Praz de Lys Sommand, 17 km to the south, with about 47 km of pistes.

    How big is Stade de neige du Col du Feu?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
