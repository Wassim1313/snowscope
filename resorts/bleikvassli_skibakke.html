    About Bleikvassli skibakke

    Bleikvassli skibakke has about 0.2 km of pistes. The slopes start at 322 m and reach 362 m at the top. Kjemsasen is the next resort, 23 km away to the west.

    Bleikvassli skibakke and the resorts around it

    Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. For higher slopes, Hemavan climbs to 1,136 m.

    Snow at Bleikvassli skibakke through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 342 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. For about 22 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 118 cm to 225 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 199 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 208 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 98 cm; a snowy December gave 138 cm, a dry one 60 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 40 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gjetardalstinden at 1,026 m, standing 664 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Bleikvassli skibakke include Lomtjønnstua (chalet).
