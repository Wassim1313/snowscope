    About Skilift Au

    Skilift Au has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 503 m up to 537 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wendelstein, 11 km to the south.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 34 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 503 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Wendelstein, for one, climbs to 1,718 m. The only lift is a rope tow. Above the ski area rises Breitenstein (1,622 m), 7.9 km to the south, about 1,085 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 520 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 14 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-December at roughly 4 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 17 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and mid-January.

    Skilift Au: common questions

    When does Skilift Au have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-December and mid-January.

    How much snow does Skilift Au have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilift Au?

    The top is at 537 m and the base at 503 m.

    How big is Skilift Au?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
