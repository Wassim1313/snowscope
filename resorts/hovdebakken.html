    About Hovdebakken

    Hovdebakken is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 171 m up to 257 m. The closest other ski area is Lierberget (26 km, east).

    Hovdebakken and the resorts around it

    If snow is the priority, Sjusjøen Skisenter does better, with about 26 weeks of a good base instead of 18. For higher slopes, Sjusjøen Skisenter climbs to 950 m.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Hovdebakken today: about 3 cm up high. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 18 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    86 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Sjusjøen Skisenter, reach 950 m. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Skonhovdhøgda (579 m), 7.0 km to the west, about 322 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Hovdebakken through a normal winter

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 214 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 46 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 68 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 34 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 62 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 44 cm to 89 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Quality Hotel Strand (hotel), Kløverhotellet (hotel), Brusveen gård (guesthouse) and Gjøvik Vandrerhjem Hovdetun (hostel).

    Hovdebakken: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hovdebakken?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Hovdebakken at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 34 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Hovdebakken at?

    The top is at 257 m and the base at 171 m.
