    About Muikamachi Hakkaisan

    Muikamachi Hakkaisan is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 11 km of pistes put it at number 59 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 358 m up to 1,168 m. Hakkai Sanroku is the next resort, 4.5 km away to the north.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 810 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. At 358 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,168 m.
