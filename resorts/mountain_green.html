    About Mountain Green

    About 4.0 km of pistes make Mountain Green one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Its lifts climb from 1,628 m to 1,777 m. The closest other ski area is Wasatch Peaks Ranch (11 km, south-west).

    How Mountain Green compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Powder Mountain: about 130 km of pistes, 24 km to the north. Powder Mountain has the longer snow season: around 25 weeks of solid cover, compared with 13 at Mountain Green. The highest nearby is Wasatch Peaks Ranch, topping out at 2,843 m.

    The pistes of Mountain Green

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 3.7 km. The largest share is green circle, with 1.9 km (50%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Mountain Green is laid out

    The vertical drop is 149 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,628 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Wasatch Peaks Ranch, for one, climbs to 2,843 m. The only lift is a chairlift. The skyline is dominated by Durst Mountain, 2,819 m high and 5.4 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 13 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,702 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to late March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 29 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 62 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 11 cm to 95 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 63 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February.

    Mountain Green: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Mountain Green?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to late March.

    How much snow does Mountain Green have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 29 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Mountain Green?

    Between 1,628 m and 1,777 m, so 149 m of vertical drop.

    Is Mountain Green suitable for families?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Mountain Green?

    Powder Mountain, 24 km to the north, with about 130 km of pistes.
