    About Strandafjellet

    Strandafjellet is a ski area. The closest other ski area is Strandafjellet (0.2 km, north).

    How much snow Strandafjellet usually gets

    At about 521 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 208 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 204 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 113 cm, with 163 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 115 cm and 250 cm. For about 23 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Questions about Strandafjellet

    When is the best time to ski at Strandafjellet?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Strandafjellet at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 113 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
