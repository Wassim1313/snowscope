    About Normafa

    Normafa has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 434 m and reach 478 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Normafa sípálya (0.2 km, west).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Normafa gives 44 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 434 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 478 m is the second highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km.
