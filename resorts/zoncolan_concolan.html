    About Zoncolan

    Zoncolan is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 27 km of pistes put it at number 232 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 890 m up to 1,963 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sappada, 19 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Zoncolan

    Counting every graded piste, there are 68 runs and around 27 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 12 km (45%). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.9 km.

    Snow at Zoncolan through a normal winter

    At about 1,426 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 December and lasts to 29 April: about 18 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 46 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 56 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 29 cm; a snowy December gave 57 cm, a dry one 6 cm. Most winters land between 29 cm and 96 cm by mid-February.

    How Zoncolan is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,073 m: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,090 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 16 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 5 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 9 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 9 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Monte Crostis (2,250 m), 7.8 km to the north, about 287 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Zoncolan?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Zoncolan at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 29 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Zoncolan?

    Between 890 m and 1,963 m, so 1,073 m of vertical drop.

    Is Zoncolan good for beginners?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Zoncolan have?

    About 27 km of pistes and 16 lifts.
