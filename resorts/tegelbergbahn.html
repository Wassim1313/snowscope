    About Tegelbergbahn

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Tegelbergbahn sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 815 m and reach 1,716 m at the top. Nachwuchsförderzentrum "Reithlif is the next resort, 0.7 km away to the west.

    Snow at Tegelbergbahn through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,266 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 January and keeps it until about 26 April, roughly 14 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 44 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 42 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 24 cm; a snowy December gave 40 cm, a dry one 10 cm. Most winters land between 24 cm and 66 cm by mid-February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    901 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 815 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 1 cable car, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 3 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Hochplatte (2,082 m), 5.7 km to the east, about 366 m higher than the top lift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 5.0 km to ski. Most of the length is red (66%, about 3.4 km). Intermediate runs make up 66% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Tegelbergbahn: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Tegelbergbahn?

    Aim for early March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late April.

    How much snow does Tegelbergbahn have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Tegelbergbahn?

    Between 815 m and 1,716 m, so 901 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Tegelbergbahn best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Tegelbergbahn?

    Hahnenkamm, 13 km to the south-west, with about 9.0 km of pistes.
