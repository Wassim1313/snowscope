    About Kačenčina sjezdovka

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kačenčina sjezdovka offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 168 of 257 in Central Europe). The slopes start at 666 m and reach 736 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Olešnice v Orlických horách, 2.3 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Kačenčina sjezdovka compares nearby

    For more terrain, Skiareál Olešnice v Orlických ho (2.3 km south-west) has about 2.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. For higher slopes, Zieleniec climbs to 1,016 m.

    How Kačenčina sjezdovka is laid out

    70 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 666 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    Questions about Kačenčina sjezdovka

    How high do the lifts go at Kačenčina sjezdovka?

    Its highest lift reaches 736 m; the lowest starts at 666 m (70 m of vertical).
