    About Cristallo

    Neither huge nor tiny, Cristallo offers about 18 km of pistes (number 335 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,686 m and 2,352 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cortina d'Ampezzo, 3.7 km to the south-west.

    Snow at Cristallo through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,019 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 88 cm, around 29 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 56 cm to 120 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 86 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 47 cm, with 89 cm in the best years.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 30 marked runs cover roughly 18 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 60%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 4.7 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    666 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,686 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 7 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 5 chairlifts and 1 button lift. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Monte Cristallo, 3,221 m high and 2.9 km north-east of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 3.7 km south-west to Cortina d'Ampezzo, with about 113 km of runs. For snow it is second in the group, behind Misurina (24 weeks of good cover, 24 here). The highest nearby is Cortina d'Ampezzo, topping out at 2,739 m.

    Questions about Cristallo

    What is the best month to ski at Cristallo?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Cristallo have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 47 cm deep.

    What altitude is Cristallo at?

    The top is at 2,352 m and the base at 1,686 m.

    Is Cristallo good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 4.7 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Cristallo?

    Cortina d'Ampezzo, 3.7 km to the south-west, with about 113 km of pistes.
