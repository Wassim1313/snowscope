    About Öjberget

    Öjberget is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 14 m up to 56 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 42 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Öjberget (55 m), Nyback berget (48 m) and Mobergen (45 m).

    Snow at Öjberget through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 35 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 10 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 27 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 43 cm. A normal season tops out near 25 cm, around 8 March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early March.

    Öjberget: common questions

    When does Öjberget have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Can you ski at Öjberget at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Öjberget at?

    The top is at 56 m and the base at 14 m.

    How big is Öjberget?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
