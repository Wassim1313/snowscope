    About Hurdal Skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hurdal Skisenter offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 123 of 262 in Scandinavia). The slopes start at 302 m and reach 523 m at the top. Hurdal Skisenter is the next resort, 0.1 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Hurdal Skisenter

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 3.6 km to ski. The largest share is green (beginner), with 1.9 km (52%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at Hurdal Skisenter through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 412 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 17 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 62 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 41 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm, from 11 cm in a poor year to 58 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 32 cm and 84 cm by mid-February.

    The mountain at Hurdal Skisenter

    From top to bottom that makes 221 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Fjellsjøkampen at 812 m, standing 289 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Hurdal Skisenter?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Hurdal Skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 31 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Hurdal Skisenter at?

    The top is at 523 m and the base at 302 m.

    Can beginners ski at Hurdal Skisenter?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 2.6 km, are easy.

    How big is Hurdal Skisenter?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
