    About St. Mary

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, St. Mary sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The slopes start at 447 m and reach 935 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Miyagi Zao Sumikawasu Snow Park, 9.9 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at St. Mary

    St. Mary has 7 graded runs adding up to about 2.9 km. Beginner runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 42%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    488 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 447 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.

    St. Mary: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at St. Mary?

    Between 447 m and 935 m, so 488 m of vertical drop.

    Is St. Mary good for beginners?

    Yes: 76% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near St. Mary?

    Zao Onsen, 11 km to the south-west, with about 39 km of pistes.
