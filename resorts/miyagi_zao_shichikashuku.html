    About Miyagi Zao Shichikashuku

    Miyagi Zao Shichikashuku is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 208 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 540 m up to 957 m. The closest other ski area is Miyagi Zao Shiroishi (14 km, north-east).

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 19 km north to Zao Onsen, with about 39 km of runs. For higher slopes, Zao Onsen climbs to 1,661 m.

    Miyagi Zao Shichikashuku today and the next days

    Up high, Miyagi Zao Shichikashuku has roughly 21 cm of snow on the ground today. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Tuesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 417 m: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 540 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.
