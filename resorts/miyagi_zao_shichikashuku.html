    About Miyagi Zao Shichikashuku

    Miyagi Zao Shichikashuku is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 208 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 540 m up to 957 m. The closest other ski area is Miyagi Zao Shiroishi (14 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 4.1 km to ski. Green runs make up the biggest part: 1.9 km, or 45%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Miyagi Zao Shichikashuku today and the next days

    Up high, Miyagi Zao Shichikashuku has roughly 3 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Tuesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 417 m: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 540 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.
