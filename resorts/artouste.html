    About Artouste

    Artouste is a small ski area by the standards of the Pyrenees, with about 8.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,775 m to 2,038 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gourette, 6.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Artouste and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Formigal: about 104 km of pistes, 14 km to the south. The highest nearby is Gourette, topping out at 2,453 m.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Artouste has roughly 2 cm of snow on the ground today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 23 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    263 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,775 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Luz-Ardiden, reach 2,485 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Artouste include Hôtel Restaurant Vignau (hotel), Maison des Gardes (chalet), Chambres d'Hôtes L'Estibère (guesthouse) and Hébergement associatif Béarn Pont de Camps (hostel).
