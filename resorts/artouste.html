    About Artouste

    Artouste is a small ski area by the standards of the Pyrenees, with about 8.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,775 m to 2,038 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gourette, 6.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,906 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 December and lasts to 27 April: about 19 weeks. A normal season tops out near 103 cm, around 2 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 40 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 88 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 36 cm and 158 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 60 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Artouste has 22 graded runs adding up to about 7.9 km. The largest share is blue, with 3.9 km (49%). With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    263 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,775 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Luz-Ardiden, reach 2,485 m. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 5 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Pic du Midi d'Ossau (2,884 m), 7.4 km to the south-west, about 846 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Artouste

    What is the best month to ski at Artouste?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Artouste at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 40 cm deep.

    What altitude is Artouste at?

    Between 1,775 m and 2,038 m, so 263 m of vertical drop.

    Is Artouste good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 4.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Artouste?

    Formigal, 14 km to the south, with about 104 km of pistes.
