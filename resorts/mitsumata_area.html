    About Mitsumata Area

    Mitsumata Area is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 9.0 km of pistes put it at number 95 of 385. The slopes start at 627 m and reach 1,052 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Kandatsu Kogen, 4.2 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Mitsumata Area and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Tashiro Area (5.5 km south-west) has about 16 km of pistes against 9.0 km here. For higher slopes, Kagura Area climbs to 1,838 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 425 m: runs of a moderate length. At 627 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 20 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Mitsumata Area. 16 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.
