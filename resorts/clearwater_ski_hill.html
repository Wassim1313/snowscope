    About Clearwater Ski Hill

    Neither huge nor tiny, Clearwater Ski Hill offers about 10 km of pistes (number 35 of 53 in Canada West). The slopes start at 418 m and reach 720 m at the top.

    What there is to ski at Clearwater Ski Hill

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 9.5 km to ski. Most of the length is green circle (51%, about 4.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 6 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Clearwater Ski Hill. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 40 cm by now.

    Lifts and terrain

    302 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 418 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 569 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-April. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 87 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 64 cm and 116 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 64 cm; a snowy December gave 95 cm, a dry one 45 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 44 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Clearwater Ski Hill?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-April.

    How much snow does Clearwater Ski Hill have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 64 cm deep.

    How high is Clearwater Ski Hill?

    Between 418 m and 720 m, so 302 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Clearwater Ski Hill?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 4.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Clearwater Ski Hill have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
