    About Megève

    Around 141 km of marked runs place Megève among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 30 by length. Skiing here happens between 820 m and 2,371 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Evasion Mont Blanc (1.8 km, south-west).

    What there is to ski at Megève

    In all, 175 marked runs cover roughly 141 km. Most of the length is red (38%, about 54 km). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 13 km of difficult runs. 7.8 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Megève is laid out

    1,551 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 820 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 55 lifts break down into 2 cable cars, 1 funicular, 8 gondolas, 18 chairlifts, 19 button lifts, 5 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Mont Joly, 2,524 m high and 3.4 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Megève usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,596 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 7 December and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 22 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 55 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 5 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 118 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 68 cm and 151 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 52 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 90 cm, a dry one 23 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Megève?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Megève at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 52 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Megève open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Megève at?

    The top is at 2,371 m and the base at 820 m.

    Can beginners ski at Megève?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 66 km, are easy.
