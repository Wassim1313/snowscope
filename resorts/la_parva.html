    About La Parva

    La Parva ranks number 3 of 30 ski areas in Andes by piste length, with about 57 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,647 m up to 3,572 m. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being El Colorado, 1.9 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 93 marked runs cover roughly 57 km. The largest share is red, with 29 km (51%). Intermediate runs make up 51% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 10 km of black or expert runs.

    How La Parva is laid out

    925 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. At 2,647 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 5 chairlifts, 9 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 15 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
