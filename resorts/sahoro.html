    About Sahoro

    Sahoro ranks number 10 of 385 ski areas in Japan by piste length, with about 24 km of runs. The slopes start at 408 m and reach 1,044 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Shintokuyama, 9.8 km south, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Sahoro

    Sahoro has 43 graded runs adding up to about 24 km. The largest share is green, with 11 km (47%). Good skiers will like it here; 33% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 7.8 km.

    How Sahoro is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 636 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 408 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 1,044 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 9 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts and 1 button lift. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.
