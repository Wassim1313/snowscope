    About Wurzeralm

    Neither huge nor tiny, Wurzeralm offers about 23 km of pistes (number 275 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 807 m and reach 1,859 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Hinterstoder (11 km, north-west).

    What there is to ski at Wurzeralm

    Counting every graded piste, there are 64 runs and around 23 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (48%, about 11 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,333 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 19 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 57 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 42 cm and 108 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 78 cm, around 1 March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 36 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 7 cm in a poor year to 63 cm in a big one.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,052 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 807 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 19 lifts: 11 funiculars, 2 chairlifts, 3 T-bars and 3 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Warscheneck at 2,388 m, standing 529 m above the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Sonnalm (hotel), Naturfreundehaus (mountain hut), Linzer Haus / Wurzeralm (mountain hut) and Fuchsalm (chalet). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Wurzeralm

    What is the best month to ski at Wurzeralm?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Wurzeralm have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 36 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Wurzeralm?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,859 m; the lowest starts at 807 m (1,052 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Wurzeralm?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    Does Wurzeralm have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 9.0 km of its pistes (39%).
