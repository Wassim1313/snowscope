    About Crissolo

    Crissolo is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 430 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,537 m up to 2,308 m. Its nearest neighbour is Rucaski, 9.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Crissolo

    Crissolo has 9 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. Most of the length is red (41%, about 4.9 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 2.2 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Crissolo gives 771 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,537 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monviso at 3,841 m, standing 1,533 m above the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Crissolo today: about 22 cm up high. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 17 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 11 cm at mid-mountain.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,922 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 December and lasts to 20 April: about 16 weeks. Most winters land between 21 cm and 94 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 34 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. A normal season tops out near 65 cm, around 5 March. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 28 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 61 cm).

    Questions about Crissolo

    When does Crissolo have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Crissolo have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 28 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Crissolo?

    The top is at 2,308 m and the base at 1,537 m.

    Is Crissolo suitable for families?

    Yes: 40% of the graded pistes, about 4.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Crissolo have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
