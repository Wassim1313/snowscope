    About Oberhelfenschwil

    Compact and local, Oberhelfenschwil has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 801 m and 865 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mosnang, 5.5 km to the west.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Degersheim: about 5.0 km of pistes, 6.4 km to the east. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. Hemberg reaches 1,128 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 64 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 801 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Atzmännig, for one, climbs to 1,194 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Chellenspitz (1,201 m), 7.6 km to the south-west, about 336 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 833 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 35 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 16 cm, around 22 January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 10 cm, with 27 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 7 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and late January.

    Oberhelfenschwil: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Oberhelfenschwil?

    Aim for early January and late January.

    Is there snow at Oberhelfenschwil at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    What altitude is Oberhelfenschwil at?

    Its highest lift reaches 865 m; the lowest starts at 801 m (64 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Oberhelfenschwil?

    Degersheim, 6.4 km to the east, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Oberhelfenschwil?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
