    About Oberhelfenschwil

    Compact and local, Oberhelfenschwil has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 801 m and 865 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mosnang, 5.5 km to the west.

    Oberhelfenschwil today and the next days

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 64 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 801 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Atzmännig, for one, climbs to 1,194 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Gasthaus Rössli (hotel), Gästehaus Sonne (hotel), Auto Rüegg AG (chalet) and Wirkstatt Auboden (hotel).
