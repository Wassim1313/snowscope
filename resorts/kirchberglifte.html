    About Kirchberglifte

    Kirchberglifte has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 797 m up to 853 m. Hirschberglifte is the next resort, 1.5 km away to the north.

    Kirchberglifte today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Kirchberglifte hold around 2 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Friday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 56 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 797 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Wallberg, for one, climbs to 1,616 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Halserspitz at 1,862 m, standing 1,009 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Kirchberglifte usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 825 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 9 cm; a snowy December gave 25 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 38 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 18 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and late January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Questions about Kirchberglifte

    What is the best month to ski at Kirchberglifte?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Can you ski at Kirchberglifte at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Kirchberglifte?

    Between 797 m and 853 m, so 56 m of vertical drop.
