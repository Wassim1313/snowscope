    About Powderhorn

    With roughly 37 km of runs, Powderhorn sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The slopes start at 2,494 m and reach 2,997 m at the top.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Powderhorn today: about 27 cm up high. 4 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 18 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Powderhorn gives 503 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,494 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Slopeside (hotel) and Nordic Council Warming Hut (mountain hut).
