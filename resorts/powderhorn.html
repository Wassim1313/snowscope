    About Powderhorn

    With roughly 37 km of runs, Powderhorn sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The slopes start at 2,494 m and reach 2,997 m at the top.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 56 runs and around 37 km to ski. Most of the length is black diamond (40%, about 15 km). Good skiers will like it here; 44% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 16 km of difficult runs.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Powderhorn today: about 87 cm up high. The last seven days brought 13 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 30 cm at mid-mountain).

    How much snow Powderhorn usually gets

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,746 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 63 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 20 cm in a poor year to 119 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 10 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 129 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 46 cm and 177 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 97 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Powderhorn gives 503 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,494 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 5 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Slopeside (hotel) and Nordic Council Warming Hut (mountain hut).

    Powderhorn: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Powderhorn?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Powderhorn at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 63 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Powderhorn?

    The top is at 2,997 m and the base at 2,494 m.

    Does Powderhorn have steep runs?

    Yes: about 16 km of its graded pistes (44%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Powderhorn have?

    About 37 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
