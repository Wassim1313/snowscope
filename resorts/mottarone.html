    About Mottarone

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Mottarone sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,298 m and reach 1,489 m at the top. Pian di Sole is the next resort, 16 km away to the north-east.

    How much snow Mottarone usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,394 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 December and lasts to 17 May: about 21 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 67 cm, a dry one 14 cm. Most winters land between 30 cm and 125 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 72 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 78 cm.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 27 runs and around 10 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 4.2 km (41%). Steep ground is the speciality: 43% of the length is graded difficult. 3.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 191 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 4 button lifts and 3 moving carpets. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Cerano at 1,701 m, standing 212 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Mottarone include Casa della neve (hotel), Albergo Ristorante Bar Eden (hotel), Rifugio Genziana (mountain hut) and Rifugio Gran Baita CAI Omegna (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Mottarone

    When does Mottarone have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Mottarone at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 31 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Mottarone at?

    Between 1,298 m and 1,489 m, so 191 m of vertical drop.

    Is Mottarone good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 4.5 km of its graded pistes (43%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Mottarone have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
