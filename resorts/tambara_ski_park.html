    About Tambara Ski Park

    Tambara Ski Park is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 117 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,243 m up to 1,556 m. Kawaba is the next resort, 3.4 km away to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 8.3 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.0 km, or 48%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 313 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,556 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    Tambara Ski Park: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Tambara Ski Park?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,556 m; the lowest starts at 1,243 m (313 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Tambara Ski Park?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 3.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Tambara Ski Park?

    Hodaigi, 5.5 km to the north, with about 12 km of pistes.

    How big is Tambara Ski Park?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
