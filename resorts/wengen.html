    About Wengen

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Wengen one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 801 m to 901 m. Skilift Weitnau is the next resort, 4.8 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Wengen

    The piste map lists 3 graded runs, about 1.2 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 0.7 km, or 58%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 100 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 801 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Immenstadt reaches 1,412 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hauchenberg at 1,244 m, standing 343 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Wengen usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 851 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 51 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm; a snowy December gave 32 cm, a dry one 1 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early March. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 22 cm, around 19 February.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Wengen?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Is there snow at Wengen at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Wengen at?

    The top is at 901 m and the base at 801 m.

    Is Wengen suitable for families?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 0.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Wengen?

    Schwärzenlifte Eschach, 4.9 km to the north-east, with about 3.0 km of pistes.
