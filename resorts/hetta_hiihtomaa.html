    The snow at Hetta Hiihtomaa, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Hetta Hiihtomaa (about 356 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 46 cm; half of the winters sit between 40 cm and 54 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 68 cm, between 61 cm and 75 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 73 cm.

    17 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 52 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1996-97 (105 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2002-03 (56 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 170 days.

    Questions about Hetta Hiihtomaa

    Does Hetta Hiihtomaa have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 46 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 52 % of 46 winters. Right now: 7 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Hetta Hiihtomaa?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 73 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Hetta Hiihtomaa get?

    About 73 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 56 cm in the leanest winter (2002-03) and 105 cm in the biggest (1996-97).

    How high is Hetta Hiihtomaa?

    The top is at 405 m and the base at 307 m.

    How big is Hetta Hiihtomaa?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
