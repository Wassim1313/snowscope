    The snow at Hetta Hiihtomaa, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Hetta Hiihtomaa (about 356 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 46 cm; half of the winters sit between 40 cm and 54 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 68 cm, between 61 cm and 75 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 73 cm.

    17 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 52 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1996-97 (105 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2002-03 (56 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 170 days.

    Today's 8 cm compares with a usual 29 cm for 27 November.

    Questions about Hetta Hiihtomaa

    Does Hetta Hiihtomaa have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 46 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 52 % of 46 winters. Right now: 8 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Hetta Hiihtomaa?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 73 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Hetta Hiihtomaa get?

    About 73 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 56 cm in the leanest winter (2002-03) and 105 cm in the biggest (1996-97).

    When does Hetta Hiihtomaa have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How high is Hetta Hiihtomaa?

    The top is at 405 m and the base at 307 m.

    Is Hetta Hiihtomaa suitable for families?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    About Hetta Hiihtomaa

    Compact and local, Hetta Hiihtomaa has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 307 m to 405 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.3 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 0.8 km, or 59%. With 59% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at Hetta Hiihtomaa through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 356 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 November and lasts to 13 May: about 24 weeks. A normal season tops out near 73 cm, around 29 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 69 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 55 cm and 85 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 46 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 33 cm in a poor year to 58 cm in a big one.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    98 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Paljasselkä (457 m), Iso Onnasvaara (448 m) and Jyppyrä (408 m).
