    About Comprensorio de Le Polle

    Comprensorio de Le Polle is a mid-sized ski area with about 19 km of pistes, number 321 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,305 m and reach 1,853 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Cimone (1.9 km, east).

    The mountain at Comprensorio de Le Polle

    548 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,305 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Albergo Edelweiss (hotel), Albergo Passo del Lupo (hotel), Rifugio Pian Cavallaro (mountain hut) and Alpe di Sara (guesthouse).
