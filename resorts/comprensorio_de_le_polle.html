    About Comprensorio de Le Polle

    Comprensorio de Le Polle is a mid-sized ski area with about 19 km of pistes, number 321 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,305 m and reach 1,853 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Cimone (1.9 km, east).

    The pistes of Comprensorio de Le Polle

    Counting every graded piste, there are 19 runs and around 19 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 12 km (62%). Intermediate runs make up 62% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 2.8 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Comprensorio de Le Polle

    548 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,305 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 4 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Monte Cimone (2,165 m), 2.0 km to the south, about 312 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,579 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 5 cm, around 22 January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 68 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 1 cm (snow lay here in 5 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 22 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Comprensorio de Le Polle: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Comprensorio de Le Polle?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    How much snow does Comprensorio de Le Polle have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 5 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Comprensorio de Le Polle?

    Between 1,305 m and 1,853 m, so 548 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Comprensorio de Le Polle best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 62% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Comprensorio de Le Polle have?

    About 19 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
