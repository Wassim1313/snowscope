    About Dixie Summit

    Dixie Summit has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,621 m up to 1,655 m.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 12 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Dixie Summit. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 12 cm at mid-mountain).

    How much snow Dixie Summit usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,638 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 47 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 88 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 48 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. A normal season tops out near 83 cm, around 22 February. Most winters land between 26 cm and 121 cm by mid-February.

    How Dixie Summit is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 34 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,621 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a rope tow. Above the ski area rises Dixie Butte (2,316 m), 5.5 km to the north-west, about 661 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Dixie Summit

    What is the best month to ski at Dixie Summit?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Dixie Summit at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 47 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Dixie Summit?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,655 m; the lowest starts at 1,621 m (34 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Dixie Summit have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
