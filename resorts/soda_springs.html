    About Soda Springs

    About 7.0 km of pistes make Soda Springs one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Its lifts climb from 2,064 m to 2,228 m. Boreal is the next resort, 3.4 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of Soda Springs

    Soda Springs has 17 graded runs adding up to about 6.7 km. The largest share is green circle, with 3.6 km (53%). With 53% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    Other resorts close by

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Soda Springs hold around 25 cm of snow today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 72 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 10 cm at mid-mountain).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,146 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 69 cm, with 156 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 87 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 159 cm, around 18 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 50 cm to 244 cm in mid-February in most winters. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 164 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,064 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley reaches 2,665 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets, 6 lifts in all. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Castle Peak, 2,773 m high and 6.0 km north-east of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hutchinson Lodge (hostel), Schull Lodge (hotel), Clair Tappaan Lodge, Sierra Club (hostel) and Heidelmann Lodge, The Nature Friends (hostel).

    Questions about Soda Springs

    When is the best time to ski at Soda Springs?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Soda Springs have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 69 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Soda Springs at?

    The top is at 2,228 m and the base at 2,064 m.

    Can beginners ski at Soda Springs?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 3.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Soda Springs?

    Sugar Bowl Resort, 4.7 km to the south-east, with about 38 km of pistes.
