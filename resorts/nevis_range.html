    About Nevis Range

    Nevis Range has about 20 km of pistes. The slopes start at 634 m and reach 1,190 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Glencoe, 25 km south, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 556 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 10 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    The pistes of Nevis Range

    Counting every graded piste, there are 39 runs and around 19 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 8.4 km (45%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.
