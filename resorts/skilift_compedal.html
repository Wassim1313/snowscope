    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,474 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 December and keeps it until about 25 March, roughly 13 weeks. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 36 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 26 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 27 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 55 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 21 cm to 62 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Skilift Compedal?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late March.

    How much snow does Skilift Compedal have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 27 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Skilift Compedal at?

    Between 1,296 m and 1,651 m, so 355 m of vertical drop.

    Is Skilift Compedal suitable for families?
