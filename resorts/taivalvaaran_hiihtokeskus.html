    About Taivalvaaran Hiihtokeskus

    Taivalvaaran Hiihtokeskus is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 200 m and 280 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Taivalkoski ski jumping hill (0.1 km, north).

    Snow at Taivalvaaran Hiihtokeskus through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 240 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 41 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 53 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 76 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 67 cm, around 8 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 57 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    How Taivalvaaran Hiihtokeskus is laid out

    80 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Suvantovaara (318 m), Kivivaara (261 m) and Repovaara (259 m).

    Taivalvaaran Hiihtokeskus: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Taivalvaaran Hiihtokeskus?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Taivalvaaran Hiihtokeskus at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 41 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Taivalvaaran Hiihtokeskus?

    Its highest lift reaches 280 m; the lowest starts at 200 m (80 m of vertical).
