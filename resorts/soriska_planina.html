    About Soriška planina

    Soriška planina is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 635 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,283 m up to 1,526 m. The closest other ski area is Kozji Hrbet (5.0 km, north-west).

    Snow at Soriška planina through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,404 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 January and lasts to 26 March: about 10 weeks. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 86 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 13 March, with about 46 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 59 cm in the best years.

    How Soriška planina is laid out

    The vertical drop is 243 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,526 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chairlift and 4 button lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Konjski vrh (1,878 m), 7.7 km to the west, about 352 m higher than the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Soriška planina

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 5.3 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.3 km, or 81%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Soriška planina?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    How much snow does Soriška planina have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Soriška planina?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,526 m; the lowest starts at 1,283 m (243 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Soriška planina best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 81% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Soriška planina?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
