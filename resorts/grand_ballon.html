    About Grand Ballon

    Grand Ballon is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 617 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,065 m up to 1,307 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Le Markstein, 7.2 km to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    242 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.

    Grand Ballon and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is La Bresse: about 24 km of pistes, 18 km to the north-west.
