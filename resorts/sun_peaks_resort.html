    About Sun Peaks Resort

    About 156 km of pistes make Sun Peaks Resort one of the ten biggest ski areas of Canada West (number 2). Skiing here happens between 1,200 m and 2,087 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Drop Zone Terrain Park (0.7 km, east).

    What there is to ski at Sun Peaks Resort

    The piste map lists 253 graded runs, about 155 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 70 km, or 45%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 46 km.

    How Sun Peaks Resort is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 887 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 8 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 3 moving carpets, 13 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Tod (2,131 m), West Bowl Peak (2,093 m) and Mount Cahilty (1,883 m).

    How much snow Sun Peaks Resort usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,644 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to early May, about 24 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 82 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Most winters land between 67 cm and 113 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. A normal season tops out near 93 cm, around 28 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 63 cm; a snowy December gave 86 cm, a dry one 46 cm.

    Sun Peaks Resort: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Sun Peaks Resort?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Is there snow at Sun Peaks Resort at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 63 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Sun Peaks Resort open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 21 November 2026.

    What altitude is Sun Peaks Resort at?

    The top is at 2,087 m and the base at 1,200 m.

    Does Sun Peaks Resort have steep runs?

    Yes: about 46 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.
