    About Pal-Arinsal

    With some 39 km of runs, Pal-Arinsal belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Pyrenees. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,554 m up to 2,494 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ordino-Arcalis, 9.6 km to the north.

    What there is to ski at Pal-Arinsal

    In all, 61 marked runs cover roughly 39 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 21 km, or 53%. With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    940 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,554 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 2 gondolas, 12 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 6 drag lifts and 9 moving carpets, 31 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 9 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Pic de Comapedrosa (2,943 m), 5.2 km to the north, about 449 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Pal-Arinsal through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,024 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 16 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 12 cm to 117 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 25 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 30 cm; a snowy December gave 72 cm, a dry one 6 cm. A normal season tops out near 75 cm, around 21 February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Pal-Arinsal?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Pal-Arinsal at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 30 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Pal-Arinsal open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Pal-Arinsal at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,494 m; the lowest starts at 1,554 m (940 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Pal-Arinsal?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 24 km, are easy.
