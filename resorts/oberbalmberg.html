    About Oberbalmberg

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Oberbalmberg sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,064 m and 1,267 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hohe Winde, 11 km to the north-east.

    The pistes of Oberbalmberg

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 2.7 km in total. Most of the length is blue (68%, about 1.9 km). With 68% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Oberbalmberg

    The vertical drop is 203 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Hasenmatt (1,445 m), 6.7 km to the west, about 178 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,166 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 36 cm. A normal season tops out near 9 cm, around 27 January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 20 cm, a dry one 0 cm. For the best snow, aim for late January and early March.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Oberbalmberg?

    Aim for late January and early March.

    How much snow does Oberbalmberg have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).

    How high is Oberbalmberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,267 m; the lowest starts at 1,064 m (203 m of vertical).

    Is Oberbalmberg suitable for families?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Oberbalmberg?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
