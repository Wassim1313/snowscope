    About La Grave

    La Grave is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 703 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 3,196 m to 3,543 m. Le Chazelet is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the north.

    How La Grave is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 347 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 3,196 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its top at 3,543 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 cable cars and 1 button lift, 3 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The skyline is dominated by Grand Pic de la Meije, 3,982 m high and 3.0 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 30 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 3,370 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early November to late May or later. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-April and late April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 187 cm (lean years 106 cm, snowy years 278 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 326 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 26 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 182 cm to 325 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-April at roughly 329 cm.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 3.6 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.6 km, or 100%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at La Grave?

    Aim for mid-April and late April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at La Grave at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 187 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is La Grave at?

    Between 3,196 m and 3,543 m, so 347 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at La Grave?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near La Grave?

    Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine, 18 km to the north-west, with about 163 km of pistes.
