    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,045 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 73 cm; a snowy December gave 107 cm, a dry one 43 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 123 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 130 cm, around 29 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 84 cm and 154 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Mosvatn Skipark?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Mosvatn Skipark have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 73 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
