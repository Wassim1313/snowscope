    About Shiga Kogen Tannenomori Okojo

    Shiga Kogen Tannenomori Okojo has about 0.5 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,694 m up to 1,774 m. Its nearest neighbour is Shiga Kogen Takamagahara Mammoth, 0.4 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Shiga Kogen Tannenomori Okojo is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Shiga Kogen Tannenomori Okojo gives 80 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,694 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a chairlift.
