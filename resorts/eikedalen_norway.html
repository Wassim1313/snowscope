    Snow at Eikedalen through a normal winter

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 589 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May or later. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 222 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 20 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 200 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 93 cm; a snowy December gave 147 cm, a dry one 33 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 99 cm and 267 cm.

    Questions about Eikedalen

    What is the best month to ski at Eikedalen?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Eikedalen at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 93 cm deep.
