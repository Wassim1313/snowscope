    About Budor skitrekk

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Budor skitrekk sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 630 m up to 743 m. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lierberget, 12 km to the south-west.

    The pistes of Budor skitrekk

    Budor skitrekk has 9 graded runs adding up to about 3.8 km. Most of the length is blue (68%, about 2.6 km). With 79% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 113 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 743 m is the second highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Nordhue (767 m), Kollmyrkampen (760 m) and Svaen (758 m).

    How much snow Budor skitrekk usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 686 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 2 December and lasts to 5 May: about 22 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 21 March, with about 87 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 49 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 78 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 63 cm and 114 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 79 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Questions about Budor skitrekk

    When is the best time to ski at Budor skitrekk?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Budor skitrekk have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 49 cm deep.

    How high is Budor skitrekk?

    The top is at 743 m and the base at 630 m.

    Is Budor skitrekk suitable for families?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 3.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Budor skitrekk?

    Hafjell, 54 km to the north-west, with about 39 km of pistes.
