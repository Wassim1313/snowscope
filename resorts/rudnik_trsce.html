    About Rudnik Tršće

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Rudnik Tršće one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 830 m and 1,019 m above sea level. Platak is the next resort, 16 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Rudnik Tršće

    Rudnik Tršće has 6 graded runs adding up to about 1.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.2 km (72%). With 72% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Rudnik Tršće

    The vertical drop is 189 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,030 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Of the 5 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 2 lifts here are button lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Belica at 1,354 m, standing 335 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Rudnik Tršće through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 924 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 21 January, with about 8 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 6 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 29 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 52 cm.

    Rudnik Tršće: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Rudnik Tršće?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Rudnik Tršće at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 6 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Rudnik Tršće?

    The top is at 1,019 m and the base at 830 m.

    Can beginners ski at Rudnik Tršće?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Rudnik Tršće have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
