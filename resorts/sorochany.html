    About Сорочаны (Sorochany)

    Neither huge nor tiny, Сорочаны (Sorochany) offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 29 of 86 in Russia). Its lifts climb from 141 m to 225 m. The closest other ski area is Волен (Volen) (5.3 km, west).

    The pistes of Сорочаны (Sorochany)

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 5.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.1 km (38%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 1.2 km of black or expert runs.

    How Сорочаны (Sorochany) is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 84 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 3 T-bars, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
