    About Dolní Morava sport & relax resor

    With some 9.0 km of runs, Dolní Morava sport & relax resor belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 663 m up to 1,232 m. Skiareál Větrný vrch is the next resort, 2.0 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of Dolní Morava sport & relax resor

    Counting every graded piste, there are 20 runs and around 8.9 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (61%, about 5.4 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Dolní Morava sport & relax resor

    Skied top to bottom, Dolní Morava sport & relax resor gives 569 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 663 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 3 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
