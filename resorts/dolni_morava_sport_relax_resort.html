    About Dolní Morava sport & relax resor

    With some 9.0 km of runs, Dolní Morava sport & relax resor belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 663 m up to 1,232 m. Skiareál Větrný vrch is the next resort, 2.0 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of Dolní Morava sport & relax resor

    Counting every graded piste, there are 20 runs and around 8.9 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (61%, about 5.4 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 948 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 January and lasts to 3 March: about 7 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 39 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm; a snowy December gave 55 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 75 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    The mountain at Dolní Morava sport & relax resor

    Skied top to bottom, Dolní Morava sport & relax resor gives 569 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 663 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 3 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Dolní Morava sport & relax resor: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Dolní Morava sport & relax resor?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    How much snow does Dolní Morava sport & relax resor have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Dolní Morava sport & relax resor at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,232 m; the lowest starts at 663 m (569 m of vertical).

    Is Dolní Morava sport & relax resor suitable for families?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 5.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Dolní Morava sport & relax resor?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
