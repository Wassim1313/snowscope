    Riserva Bianca in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Riserva Bianca (about 1,559 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 13 cm; half of the winters sit between 2 cm and 25 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 27 cm; the middle half of winters range from 4 cm to 43 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 28 cm.

    2 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 7 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1995-96, peaking at 125 cm. Worst: 2024-25, which never passed 13 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late December until late March in a normal winter, roughly 72 days.

    Questions about Riserva Bianca

    Is there snow in Riserva Bianca at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 13 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 7 % of 46 winters. Right now: no snow.

    Which month has the most snow at Riserva Bianca?

    February: the snow peaks at about 28 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to late March.

    How snowy is Riserva Bianca?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 28 cm in early February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 125 cm (1995-96), the thinnest 13 cm (2024-25).

    What altitude is Riserva Bianca at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,054 m; the lowest starts at 1,064 m (990 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Riserva Bianca have?

    About 42 km of pistes and 18 lifts.

    About Riserva Bianca

    With some 42 km of runs, Riserva Bianca belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,064 m up to 2,054 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lurisia Monte Pigna, 16 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.
