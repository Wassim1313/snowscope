    About Riserva Bianca

    With some 42 km of runs, Riserva Bianca belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,064 m up to 2,054 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lurisia Monte Pigna, 16 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Riserva Bianca

    Riserva Bianca has 63 graded runs adding up to about 42 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 24 km, or 57%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 9.4 km of black or expert runs.

    How Riserva Bianca is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Riserva Bianca gives 990 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,054 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 gondola, 11 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 4 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Rocca dell'Abisso / Roche de l'Abisse (2,755 m), 6.2 km to the south-west, about 701 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,559 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 70 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm, with 40 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 28 cm, around 9 February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March.

    Quick answers

    When does Riserva Bianca have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March.

    How much snow does Riserva Bianca have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Riserva Bianca at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,054 m; the lowest starts at 1,064 m (990 m of vertical).

    Who is Riserva Bianca best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 57% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Riserva Bianca have?

    About 42 km of pistes and 18 lifts.
