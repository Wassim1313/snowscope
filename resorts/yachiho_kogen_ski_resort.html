    About Yachiho Kōgen

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Yachiho Kōgen sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,643 m up to 1,831 m. The closest other ski area is Chateraise Ski Valley Koumi (3.2 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 3.8 km. The largest share is green, with 1.9 km (48%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    Lifts and terrain

    188 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,643 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 1,831 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Yachiho Kōgen at?

    The top is at 1,831 m and the base at 1,643 m.

    Can beginners ski at Yachiho Kōgen?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Yachiho Kōgen?

    Shirakaba Kogen Kokusai Ski, 11 km to the north-west, with about 11 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Yachiho Kōgen have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
