    About Farellones

    Compact and local, Farellones has about 6.0 km of runs, small for Andes. Its lifts climb from 2,339 m to 2,620 m. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being El Colorado, 3.5 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 13 graded runs, about 6.1 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.4 km, or 39%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 281 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,339 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Valle Nevado reaches 3,675 m. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.
