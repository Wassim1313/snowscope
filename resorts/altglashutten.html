    How much snow Schwarzenbachlift usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,066 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 11 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 38 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 6 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 25 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 151 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,190 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Baldenweger Buck (1,461 m), 7.5 km to the west, about 320 m higher than the top lift.

    Schwarzenbachlift: common questions

    When does Schwarzenbachlift have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Can you ski at Schwarzenbachlift at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Schwarzenbachlift?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,141 m; the lowest starts at 990 m (151 m of vertical).

    Does Schwarzenbachlift have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.1 km of its graded pistes (38%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Schwarzenbachlift?

    Feldberg, 6.5 km to the west, with about 22 km of pistes.
