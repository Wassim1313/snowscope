    About Snow Ruyi Ski Jumping Centre

    About 31 km of pistes make Snow Ruyi Ski Jumping Centre one of the ten biggest ski areas of China (number 4). Skiing here happens between 1,664 m and 2,120 m above sea level. National Biathlon Centre is the next resort, 1.4 km away to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 28 runs and around 31 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 13 km (43%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 7.1 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Snow Ruyi Ski Jumping Centre

    456 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,664 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 3 gondolas, 2 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.
