    About Noeux-les-Mines

    Compact and local, Noeux-les-Mines has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 54 m to 114 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    60 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 54 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.

    Quick answers

    How high is Noeux-les-Mines?

    Its highest lift reaches 114 m; the lowest starts at 54 m (60 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Noeux-les-Mines have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
