    About Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows

    Neither huge nor tiny, Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows offers about 38 km of pistes (number 55 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 1,897 m and reach 2,589 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley, 4.4 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows

    Counting every graded piste, there are 93 runs and around 38 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 28 km (73%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 8.9 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows gives 692 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,897 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 13 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Granite Chief at 2,749 m, standing 160 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,243 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 December and lasts to 12 May: about 23 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 64 cm, from 11 cm in a poor year to 132 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 148 cm. Most winters land between 59 cm and 219 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 86 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows: common questions

    When does Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 64 cm deep.

    How high is Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows?

    The top is at 2,589 m and the base at 1,897 m.

    What level of skier is Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows?

    About 38 km of pistes and 17 lifts.
