    About Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows

    Neither huge nor tiny, Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows offers about 38 km of pistes (number 55 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 1,897 m and reach 2,589 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley, 4.4 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows gives 692 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,897 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
