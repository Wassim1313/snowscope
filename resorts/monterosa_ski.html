    About Monterosa Ski

    With some 72 km of runs, Monterosa Ski belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,207 m and 3,030 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Antagnod (11 km, west).

    How Monterosa Ski is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,823 m: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Its top at 3,030 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Monterosa Ski has roughly 112 cm of snow on the ground today. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 33 cm by Saturday.
