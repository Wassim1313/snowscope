    About Monterosa Ski

    With some 72 km of runs, Monterosa Ski belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,207 m and 3,030 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Antagnod (11 km, west).

    How Monterosa Ski is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,823 m: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Its top at 3,030 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 37 lifts break down into 3 cable cars, 8 funiculars, 5 gondolas, 12 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 7 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 7 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Liskamm Ostgipfel at 4,527 m, standing 1,497 m above the top lift.

    The pistes of Monterosa Ski

    The piste map lists 96 graded runs, about 72 km in total. The largest share is red, with 45 km (63%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 15 km.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,118 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 December and lasts to 21 May: about 24 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 41 cm, with 70 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 84 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 46 cm to 99 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 76 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Quick answers

    When does Monterosa Ski have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Monterosa Ski at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 41 cm deep.

    When does Monterosa Ski open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Monterosa Ski?

    Between 1,207 m and 3,030 m, so 1,823 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Monterosa Ski best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 63% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
