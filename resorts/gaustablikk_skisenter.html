    About Gaustablikk Skisenter

    With some 17 km of runs, Gaustablikk Skisenter belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 709 m to 1,250 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gaustablikk Skisenter, 0.2 km to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 21 graded runs, about 17 km in total. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 78%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    541 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 5 T-bars and 2 button lifts, 9 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gaustatoppen at 1,883 m, standing 633 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 980 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 118 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 25 March, with about 125 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 72 cm (lean years 44 cm, snowy years 106 cm). Most winters land between 78 cm and 153 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Gaustablikk Skisenter

    When does Gaustablikk Skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Gaustablikk Skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 72 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Gaustablikk Skisenter?

    The top is at 1,250 m and the base at 709 m.

    Is Gaustablikk Skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 84% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Gaustablikk Skisenter have?

    About 17 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
