    What 46 winters say about Gaustablikk Skisenter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Gaustablikk Skisenter (about 980 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 72 cm of cover (55 cm to 88 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 109 cm; the middle half of winters range from 94 cm to 130 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 125 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 63 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 87 %.

    The deepest winter was 2000-01 (217 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1995-96 (84 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to late May, about 199 days.

    Gaustablikk Skisenter: quick answers

    Is there snow in Gaustablikk Skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 72 cm at Christmas, and 87 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 9 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Gaustablikk Skisenter?

    March: the snow peaks at about 125 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to late May.

    How deep does the snow get at Gaustablikk Skisenter?

    About 125 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 84 cm in the leanest winter (1995-96) and 217 cm in the biggest (2000-01).

    How high do the lifts go at Gaustablikk Skisenter?

    The top is at 1,250 m and the base at 709 m.

    How many km of pistes does Gaustablikk Skisenter have?

    About 17 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
