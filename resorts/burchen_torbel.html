    About Bürchen

    Bürchen is a mid-sized ski area with about 19 km of pistes, number 320 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,478 m and reach 2,508 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Unterbäch, 3.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Bürchen has 41 graded runs adding up to about 19 km. Most of the length is blue (41%, about 7.7 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 4.7 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Bürchen usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,993 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 62 cm to 116 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 100 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 90 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 54 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 78 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,030 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,478 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 chairlift, 4 T-bars and 2 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Schwarzhorn at 3,201 m, standing 693 m above the top lift.

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Bürchen include Chalet Müsli (chalet), Bürchnerhof (hotel), Ronalp (hotel) and Hotel Alpenrösli (hotel).

    Questions about Bürchen

    What is the best month to ski at Bürchen?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Bürchen have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 54 cm deep.

    How high is Bürchen?

    The top is at 2,508 m and the base at 1,478 m.

    Is Bürchen good for beginners?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 7.7 km, are easy.

    Can Bürchen make its own snow?

    Yes, on about 8.2 km of its pistes (44%).
