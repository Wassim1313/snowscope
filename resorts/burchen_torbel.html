    The snow at Bürchen, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Bürchen (about 1,993 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 54 cm, with a normal range of 38 cm–69 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 92 cm (77 cm–106 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 100 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    39 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 63 % by 1 January.

    1994-95 was the big one (148 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (79 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early December to late May (184 days).

    Skiing Bürchen: the practical side

    Lifts: 7. Pistes: 19 km. Heights: 1,478 m at the bottom, 2,508 m at the top (1,030 m of vertical).

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Bürchen at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 54 cm at Christmas, and 63 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 47 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Bürchen?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 100 cm). For a sure base, early December to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Bürchen?

    About 100 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 79 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 148 cm in the biggest (1994-95).

    How high is Bürchen?

    The top is at 2,508 m and the base at 1,478 m.

    How many km of pistes does Bürchen have?

    About 19 km of pistes and 7 lifts.

    About Bürchen

    Bürchen is a mid-sized ski area with about 19 km of pistes, number 320 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,478 m and reach 2,508 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Unterbäch, 3.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,030 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,478 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
