    About Horben

    Horben has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 755 m and reach 798 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Röhrenmoos, 20 km to the north.

    The mountain at Horben

    43 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 755 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Pilatus reaches 2,063 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 776 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm; a snowy December gave 34 cm, a dry one 4 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Most winters land between 7 cm and 50 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 29 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Horben

    When does Horben have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Horben have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Horben at?

    Its highest lift reaches 798 m; the lowest starts at 755 m (43 m of vertical).

    How big is Horben?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
