    About Rothwald

    Rothwald is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 530 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,754 m up to 2,260 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Rosswald, 3.5 km to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    Snow at Rothwald through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,007 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 90 cm; a snowy December gave 129 cm, a dry one 53 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 154 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 108 cm to 197 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 160 cm, around 6 April. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    How Rothwald is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Rothwald gives 506 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,754 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 2 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Leone at 3,552 m, standing 1,292 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hotel Post (hotel), Berghotel Klenenhorn (hotel), Simplonblick (hotel) and Alpenruh (chalet).

    Questions about Rothwald

    When is the best time to ski at Rothwald?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Rothwald at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 90 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Rothwald?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,260 m; the lowest starts at 1,754 m (506 m of vertical).

    Is Rothwald suitable for families?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 5.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rothwald?

    Belalp, 13 km to the north-west, with about 40 km of pistes.
