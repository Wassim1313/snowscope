    About Rothwald

    Rothwald is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 530 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,754 m up to 2,260 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Rosswald, 3.5 km to the north.

    How Rothwald is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Rothwald gives 506 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,754 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hotel Post (hotel), Berghotel Klenenhorn (hotel), Simplonblick (hotel) and Alpenruh (chalet).
