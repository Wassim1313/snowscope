    About Guzet-Neige

    Guzet-Neige is a mid-sized ski area with about 28 km of pistes, number 20 of 49 in the Pyrenees by length. The slopes start at 1,103 m and reach 2,049 m at the top. Tavascan is the next resort, 13 km away to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Guzet-Neige

    Guzet-Neige has 36 graded runs adding up to about 27 km. Most of the length is blue (45%, about 12 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Guzet-Neige is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 946 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 4 chairlifts, 10 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pic de Certascan at 2,853 m, standing 804 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,576 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 January and lasts to 8 March: about 6 weeks. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 80 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 5 February, with about 36 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 6 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 11 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 43 cm).

    Guzet-Neige: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Guzet-Neige?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Guzet-Neige at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).

    When does the ski season start at Guzet-Neige?

    The announced opening day is 19 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 21 March 2027.

    How high is Guzet-Neige?

    Between 1,103 m and 2,049 m, so 946 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Guzet-Neige?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.
