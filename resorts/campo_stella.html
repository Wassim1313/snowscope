    About Campo Stella

    Campo Stella ranks number 9 of 40 ski areas in the Apennines by piste length, with about 10 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 1,170 m to 1,722 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Terminillo, 4.5 km to the south.

    The pistes of Campo Stella

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 9.3 km. The largest share is blue, with 6.9 km (74%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Campo Stella is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 552 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Terminillo, reach 1,847 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Campo Stella at?

    The top is at 1,722 m and the base at 1,170 m.

    Is Campo Stella good for beginners?

    Yes: 80% of the graded pistes, about 7.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Campo Stella have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
