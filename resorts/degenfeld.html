    About Degenfeld

    Neither huge nor tiny, Degenfeld offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 158 of 257 in Central Europe). Its lifts climb from 536 m to 582 m. Waldstetten is the next resort, 3.4 km away to the west.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 559 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 5 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 1 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 13 cm). For the best snow, aim for late December and late January. A normal season tops out near 5 cm, around 24 January. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 19 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    How Degenfeld is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Degenfeld gives 46 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 536 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Halde, reach 822 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Degenfeld: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Degenfeld?

    Aim for late December and late January.

    Is there snow at Degenfeld at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 5 winters out of ten, usually about 1 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Degenfeld?

    Between 536 m and 582 m, so 46 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Degenfeld?

    Hirtenteich, 11 km to the north-east, with about 4.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Degenfeld have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
