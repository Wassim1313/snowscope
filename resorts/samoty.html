    About Samoty

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Samoty sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 780 m to 898 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Debrník, 0.6 km to the south.

    What there is to ski at Samoty

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 1.9 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.9 km, or 100%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 839 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 64 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 28 cm, around 11 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 12 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 41 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 118 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 780 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Samoty have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    How much snow does Samoty have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 12 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Samoty?

    Its highest lift reaches 898 m; the lowest starts at 780 m (118 m of vertical).

    Is Samoty good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Samoty?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
