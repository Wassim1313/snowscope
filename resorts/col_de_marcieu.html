    About Col de Marcieu

    Neither huge nor tiny, Col de Marcieu offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 738 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,022 m and reach 1,270 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Saint-Hilaire-du-Touvet, 4.9 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Col de Marcieu through a normal winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,146 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 44 cm, with 97 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 73 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 59 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 23 cm to 111 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    The pistes of Col de Marcieu

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 3.0 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 48%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Col de Marcieu

    248 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse, reach 1,743 m. All 3 lifts here are button lifts. Above the ski area rises Dent de Crolles (2,062 m), 6.5 km to the south-west, about 792 m higher than the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find 4 chalets: La Salamandre du Glésy, Chalet Djurdjura, Chalet Cervin and Chalet Everest.

    Col de Marcieu: common questions

    When does Col de Marcieu have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Col de Marcieu at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 44 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Col de Marcieu at?

    The top is at 1,270 m and the base at 1,022 m.

    Can beginners ski at Col de Marcieu?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Col de Marcieu?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
