    About Горнолыжный курорт "Черневская г

    Compact and local, Горнолыжный курорт "Черневская г has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Russia. The ski area spans the mountain from 153 m up to 191 m. The closest other ski area is Горнолыжный склон "ТРЦ Вейпарк" (6.5 km, east).

    How Горнолыжный курорт "Черневская г is laid out

    The vertical drop is 38 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 T-bar and 3 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
