    About Ski Juwel

    Ski Juwel is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 928 m and 975 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Juwel, 1.8 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 8 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Ski Juwel. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 15 cm by Sunday.

    How Ski Juwel is laid out

    The vertical drop is 47 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 928 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau reaches 2,026 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Tirolerhof (hotel), Jugendhotel Angerhof (hotel), Pension Schneerose (guesthouse) and Gästehaus Steiner (guesthouse).

    Questions about Ski Juwel

    How high is Ski Juwel?

    Its highest lift reaches 975 m; the lowest starts at 928 m (47 m of vertical).
