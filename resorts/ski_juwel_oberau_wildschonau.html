    About Ski Juwel

    Ski Juwel has about 0.3 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 928 m and 975 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Juwel, 1.8 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 7 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Ski Juwel. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 6 cm by Wednesday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 7 cm at mid-mountain).

    How Ski Juwel is laid out

    The vertical drop is 47 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 928 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau reaches 2,026 m. To get up there are 1 T-bar, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Feldalphorn, 1,923 m high and 6.8 km south of the resort.

    How much snow Ski Juwel usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 952 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 27 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 16 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 17 cm, from 3 cm in a poor year to 34 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 14 cm and 42 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and early February.

    Questions about Ski Juwel

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Juwel?

    Aim for mid-January and early February.

    Is there snow at Ski Juwel at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 17 cm deep.

    How high is Ski Juwel?

    Its highest lift reaches 975 m; the lowest starts at 928 m (47 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Ski Juwel have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
