    About Stevens Pass

    Stevens Pass is a mid-sized ski area with about 30 km of pistes, number 79 of 175 in US West by length. Its lifts climb from 1,166 m to 1,727 m. Its nearest neighbour is Leavenworth Ski Hill, 34 km south-east, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,446 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 29 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 311 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 198 cm and 386 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 157 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 110 cm in a poor year to 228 cm in a big one. For about 25 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 335 cm.

    The pistes of Stevens Pass

    In all, 71 marked runs cover roughly 29 km. Most of the length is blue square (66%, about 19 km). Intermediate runs make up 66% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 5.5 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Stevens Pass gives 561 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 14 lifts: 10 chairlifts, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Jim Hill Mountain, 2,062 m high and 6.1 km east of the resort.

    Questions about Stevens Pass

    When does Stevens Pass have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Stevens Pass at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 157 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Stevens Pass?

    Between 1,166 m and 1,727 m, so 561 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Stevens Pass best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Stevens Pass?

    About 30 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
