    The snow at Pfelders, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Pfelders (about 2,050 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 48 cm; half of the winters sit between 36 cm and 69 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 90 cm, between 69 cm and 106 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 93 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 37 % of winters, and by 1 January in 54 %.

    Best winter on record: 2000-01, peaking at 153 cm. Worst: 1995-96, which never passed 70 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 184 days.

    So far this winter: 30 cm on 25 November (usual: 28 cm).

    About Pfelders

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Pfelders sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,600 m and reach 2,500 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Obergurgl-Hochgurgl (10 km, north).

    The pistes of Pfelders

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 7.3 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 3.9 km (53%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Pfelders today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Pfelders hold around 27 cm of snow today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 12 cm by Saturday. An ordinary winter would have around 32 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 900 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,600 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift and 2 button lifts. The skyline is dominated by Hochwilde - Cima Altissima, 3,482 m high and 6.1 km south-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,050 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 93 cm, around 2 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 48 cm (lean years 30 cm, snowy years 77 cm). For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 84 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Year to year the snow is steady, with 62 cm to 114 cm in mid-February in most winters.
