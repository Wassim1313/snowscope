    About Malesco

    Malesco has about 0.4 km of pistes. The slopes start at 794 m and reach 911 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Piana di Vigezzo, 5.2 km to the north.

    Other resorts close by

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Malesco hold around 3 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 9 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 117 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 794 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Bosco Gurin, reach 2,326 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pizzo Ragno at 2,289 m, standing 1,378 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Malesco through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 852 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 22 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 30 cm, a dry one 0 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 42 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Questions about Malesco

    When is the best time to ski at Malesco?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    How much snow does Malesco have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 9 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Malesco?

    Between 794 m and 911 m, so 117 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Malesco have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
