    About Bourg-Saint-Pierre

    Bourg-Saint-Pierre has about 0.4 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,579 m and 1,631 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Vichères-Bavon (5.1 km, north-west).

    Bourg-Saint-Pierre and the resorts around it

    For more reliable snow, Crévacol usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 26 weeks, against 20 here. The highest nearby is Crévacol, topping out at 2,450 m.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 10 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Bourg-Saint-Pierre. 23 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 57 cm by Saturday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 11 cm now.

    How Bourg-Saint-Pierre is laid out

    52 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,579 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Grand Combin de Grafeneire (4,314 m), 7.3 km to the east, about 2,683 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Bourg-Saint-Pierre usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,605 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to early May, about 20 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 30 cm, with 47 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 37 cm to 77 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 41 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 59 cm.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Bivouac Napoléon (hotel), Hôtel du Vieux Moulin (hotel), Hôtel du Grand Saint Bernard (hotel) and Café de Dranse (guesthouse).

    Bourg-Saint-Pierre: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Bourg-Saint-Pierre?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Can you ski at Bourg-Saint-Pierre at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 30 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Bourg-Saint-Pierre at?

    The top is at 1,631 m and the base at 1,579 m.

    How many km of pistes does Bourg-Saint-Pierre have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
