    About Shiga Kogen Yokoteyama

    With some 14 km of runs, Shiga Kogen Yokoteyama belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Skiing here happens between 1,705 m and 2,303 m above sea level. Shiga Kogen Kumanoyu is the next resort, 1.7 km away to the west.

    What there is to ski at Shiga Kogen Yokoteyama

    The piste map lists 22 graded runs, about 14 km in total. The largest share is green, with 8.3 km (61%). With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    598 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,705 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,303 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 7 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.
