    About Snowsun Horný Vadičov

    Snowsun Horný Vadičov is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 543 m to 632 m. Lyžiarske a rekreačné stredisko is the next resort, 6.0 km away to the west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 89 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 543 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    Questions about Snowsun Horný Vadičov

    How high is Snowsun Horný Vadičov?

    Its highest lift reaches 632 m; the lowest starts at 543 m (89 m of vertical).
