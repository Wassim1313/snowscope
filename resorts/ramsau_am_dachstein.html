    About Ramsau am Dachstein

    With roughly 21 km of runs, Ramsau am Dachstein sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,131 m to 1,494 m. Reiteralm is the next resort, 6.9 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Ramsau am Dachstein

    Counting every graded piste, there are 44 runs and around 21 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 17 km, or 83%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,312 m) in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 66 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 36 cm and 78 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 41 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 33 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 10 cm in a poor year to 58 cm in a big one.

    How Ramsau am Dachstein is laid out

    The vertical drop is 363 m: runs of a moderate length. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp, for one, climbs to 2,072 m. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 1 chair-gondola, 6 T-bars, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hoher Dachstein at 2,995 m, standing 1,501 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Questions about Ramsau am Dachstein

    When is the best time to ski at Ramsau am Dachstein?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Ramsau am Dachstein at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 33 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Ramsau am Dachstein at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,494 m; the lowest starts at 1,131 m (363 m of vertical).

    Is Ramsau am Dachstein suitable for families?

    Yes: 83% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ramsau am Dachstein?

    Schladming Dachstein, 7.2 km to the south-east, with about 179 km of pistes.
