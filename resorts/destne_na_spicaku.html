    About Deštné

    Deštné has about 0.5 km of pistes. The slopes start at 652 m and reach 718 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Deštné v Orlických horách (1.4 km, south-east).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 685 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 32 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 24 cm, a dry one 0 cm. A normal season tops out near 13 cm, around 24 January.

    The mountain at Deštné

    From top to bottom that makes 66 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 652 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a button lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Deštné?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Deštné at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 5 cm).

    How high is Deštné?

    Its highest lift reaches 718 m; the lowest starts at 652 m (66 m of vertical).

    How big is Deštné?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 1 lift.
