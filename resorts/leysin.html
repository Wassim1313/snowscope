    About Leysin

    Around 25 km of marked runs place Leysin among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 248 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,237 m up to 2,192 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Forclaz VD, 3.9 km to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 42 graded runs, about 25 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 17 km, or 69%. With 69% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. 0.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How much snow Leysin usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,714 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 26 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 97 cm; a snowy December gave 151 cm, a dry one 38 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 128 cm to 252 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 174 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 200 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 19 weeks.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 955 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 1 funicular, 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pic Chaussy at 2,351 m, standing 159 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Leysin

    When is the best time to ski at Leysin?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Leysin at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 97 cm deep.

    How high is Leysin?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,192 m; the lowest starts at 1,237 m (955 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Leysin?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Leysin have?

    About 25 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
