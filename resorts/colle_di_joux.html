    About Colle di Joux

    Colle di Joux is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 688 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,604 m up to 1,953 m. Brusson is the next resort, 4.7 km away to the north-east.

    Colle di Joux and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Torgnon (14 km north-west) has about 13 km of pistes against 4.0 km here. For higher slopes, Chamois climbs to 2,494 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 349 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,604 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Valtournenche, reach 3,091 m.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Etoile de Neige, Stella Alpina, Hôtel du Foyer and L'Abreny.
