    About Torsbustaden alpinsenter

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Torsbustaden alpinsenter one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. The slopes start at 224 m and reach 238 m at the top. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Torsbustaden, 0.1 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 1.8 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.0 km (57%). With 57% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Torsbustaden alpinsenter usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 231 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 December and lasts to 24 April: about 18 weeks. A normal season tops out near 73 cm, around 2 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 47 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 29 cm and 93 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm (lean years 16 cm, snowy years 61 cm).

    The mountain at Torsbustaden alpinsenter

    14 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Meråker alpinsenter reaches 617 m. The only lift is a T-bar.

    What altitude is Torsbustaden alpinsenter at?

    Is Torsbustaden alpinsenter suitable for families?
