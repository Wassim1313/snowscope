    About Hilbert

    Hilbert has about 0.4 km of pistes. The slopes start at 671 m and reach 697 m at the top. Harrachov is the next resort, 1.7 km away to the south-east.

    Hilbert and the resorts around it

    Harrachov has the longer snow season: around 5 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Hilbert. The highest nearby is Harrachov, topping out at 1,021 m.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Hilbert has roughly 5 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 6 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    26 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 671 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 684 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and late February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 50 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 28 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 43 cm, a dry one 1 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Quick answers

    When does Hilbert have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and late February.

    Can you ski at Hilbert at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    What altitude is Hilbert at?

    Between 671 m and 697 m, so 26 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Hilbert have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
