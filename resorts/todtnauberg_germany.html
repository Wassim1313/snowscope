    Snow at Todtnauberg through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 771 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 16 cm, a dry one 0 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 20 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and early February. The snow is usually deepest around 21 January, with about 6 cm.
