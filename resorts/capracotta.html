    About Capracotta

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Capracotta one of the smaller places to ski in the Apennines. Its lifts climb from 1,385 m to 1,638 m. The closest other ski area is Valle Fura (18 km, north-west).

    The mountain at Capracotta

    From top to bottom that makes 253 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,385 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Roccaraso, for one, climbs to 2,129 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 button lift.

    Quick answers

    How high is Capracotta?

    The top is at 1,638 m and the base at 1,385 m.

    Who is Capracotta best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Capracotta?

    Roccaraso, 20 km to the west, with about 67 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Capracotta have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
