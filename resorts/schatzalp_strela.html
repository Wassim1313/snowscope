    About Schatzalp/Strela

    Schatzalp/Strela is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 559 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,608 m and 2,352 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Bündalift (2.2 km, east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 10 marked runs cover roughly 7.6 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 5.7 km, or 75%. With 75% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Schatzalp/Strela usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,980 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 December and lasts to 26 May: about 25 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 66 cm to 123 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 106 cm, around 27 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 51 cm (lean years 28 cm, snowy years 70 cm). For about 6 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 96 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    744 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,608 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 4 funiculars, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 3 drag lifts and 1 rope tow, 10 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Weissflue (2,843 m), 3.9 km to the north-west, about 491 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Schatzalp/Strela?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Schatzalp/Strela at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 51 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Schatzalp/Strela?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,352 m; the lowest starts at 1,608 m (744 m of vertical).

    Is Schatzalp/Strela good for beginners?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 5.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Schatzalp/Strela?

    Parsenn, 5.3 km to the north, with about 81 km of pistes.
