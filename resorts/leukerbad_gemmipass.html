    About Leukerbad

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Leukerbad one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 1,383 m and reach 2,343 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Leukerbad (Torrent) (4.1 km, south-east).

    How Leukerbad compares nearby

    For more terrain, Adelboden (9.9 km north-west) has about 83 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. The highest nearby is Crans-Montana, topping out at 2,872 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.1 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 0.7 km (64%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes. 0.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Leukerbad is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 960 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,383 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 7 lifts break down into 2 cable cars, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Balmhorn, 3,697 m high and 7.0 km east of the resort.

    Snow at Leukerbad through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,863 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 8 December and keeps it until about 24 May, roughly 24 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 105 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 109 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 55 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 80 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 66 cm and 136 cm by mid-February. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Questions about Leukerbad

    When is the best time to ski at Leukerbad?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Leukerbad at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 55 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Leukerbad at?

    The top is at 2,343 m and the base at 1,383 m.

    Can beginners ski at Leukerbad?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Leukerbad?

    Adelboden, 9.9 km to the north-west, with about 83 km of pistes.
