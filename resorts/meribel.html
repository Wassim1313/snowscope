    About Méribel

    With some 142 km of runs, Méribel belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,132 m to 2,920 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les Trois Vallées, 3.4 km to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    Méribel has 217 graded runs adding up to about 140 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 60 km, or 43%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 3.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,788 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its 46 lifts break down into 14 gondolas, 15 chairlifts, 8 button lifts, 3 rope tows and 6 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 6 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Aiguille du Borgne (3,138 m), 7.7 km to the south, about 218 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,026 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 27 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 87 cm and 164 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 130 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 70 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 40 cm in a poor year to 107 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 140 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Méribel have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Méribel have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 70 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Méribel?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 18 April 2027.

    What altitude is Méribel at?

    The top is at 2,920 m and the base at 1,132 m.

    Can beginners ski at Méribel?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 78 km, are easy.
