    About Pikku-Syote

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Pikku-Syote sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 256 m to 384 m. Taivalvaara is the next resort, 24 km away to the east.

    The mountain at Pikku-Syote

    From top to bottom that makes 128 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Iso-Syöte (430 m), Pikku-Syöte (387 m) and Maaselkä (385 m).

    Pikku-Syote today and the next days

    How much snow Pikku-Syote usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 320 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 14 December and keeps it until about 2 May, roughly 20 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm; a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 23 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 12 March, with about 67 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 55 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Year to year the snow is steady, with 48 cm to 76 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Hotelli Pikku-Syöte (hotel), Alarinteentie 1 (chalet), Retkeilymaja Syöte (hostel) and Kuurankukka (chalet).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Pikku-Syote?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Pikku-Syote at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 38 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Pikku-Syote at?

    Its highest lift reaches 384 m; the lowest starts at 256 m (128 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Pikku-Syote have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
