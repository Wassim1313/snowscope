    About Mouthe

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Mouthe sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 939 m to 1,183 m. Chaux-Neuve is the next resort, 6.9 km away to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Mouthe

    Mouthe has 6 graded runs adding up to about 5.5 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (46%, about 2.5 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Mouthe

    Skied top to bottom, Mouthe gives 244 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 939 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 4 lifts break down into 3 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Gros Crêt (1,408 m), 5.1 km to the south, about 225 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,061 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 40 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early March. A normal season tops out near 18 cm, around 14 February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 50 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Mouthe include Chalet De la Source (guesthouse), La Chaumière (guesthouse), Clarines (chalet) and Les écrins du Val de Mouthe (chalet).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Mouthe?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    How much snow does Mouthe have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Mouthe?

    Between 939 m and 1,183 m, so 244 m of vertical drop.

    Is Mouthe good for beginners?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 4.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Mouthe?

    Metabief, 10 km to the east, with about 26 km of pistes.
