    About Gerlosstein / Hainzenberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gerlosstein / Hainzenberg offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 544 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 920 m and 1,830 m above sea level. Hochzillertal-Hochfügen is the next resort, 9.1 km away to the north-west.

    Gerlosstein / Hainzenberg and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Zillertal Arena: about 123 km of pistes, 11 km to the east. Zillertal Arena reaches 2,501 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 28 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Gerlosstein / Hainzenberg. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Saturday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    910 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,120 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Gerlosstein / Hainzenberg include 4 hotels: Sonnalm, Ferienhotel Jörglerhof, Alpen Wohlfühlhotel Dörflwirt and Alpenhotel Ramsauerhof.
