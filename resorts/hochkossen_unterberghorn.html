    About Hochkössen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hochkössen offers about 15 km of pistes (number 383 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 613 m to 1,684 m. The closest other ski area is Lärchenhof (8.5 km, south-east).

    How Hochkössen is laid out

    1,071 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 613 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Scheibenwaldhütte (mountain hut), Sternenhof (hotel), Bärenhütte (mountain hut) and Schwendterwirt (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Hochkössen: common questions

    What altitude is Hochkössen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,684 m; the lowest starts at 613 m (1,071 m of vertical).

    How big is Hochkössen?

    About 15 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
