    About Hochkössen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hochkössen offers about 15 km of pistes (number 383 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 613 m to 1,684 m. The closest other ski area is Lärchenhof (8.5 km, south-east).

    How much snow Hochkössen usually gets

    At about 1,148 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 31 December and lasts to 29 April: about 17 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 46 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 27 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 54 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 25 cm to 72 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 50 cm, around 22 February.

    What there is to ski at Hochkössen

    In all, 22 marked runs cover roughly 15 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 8.9 km, or 59%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Hochkössen is laid out

    1,071 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 613 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 9 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Unterberghorn (1,773 m), Fellhorn (1,765 m) and Hefferthorn (1,735 m).

    Near the lifts you will find Scheibenwaldhütte (mountain hut), Sternenhof (hotel), Bärenhütte (mountain hut) and Schwendterwirt (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Hochkössen: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hochkössen?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Hochkössen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 27 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Hochkössen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,684 m; the lowest starts at 613 m (1,071 m of vertical).

    Is Hochkössen good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 8.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Hochkössen?

    About 15 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
