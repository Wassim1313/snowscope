    About Glomfjord Alpinsenter

    Glomfjord Alpinsenter is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 166 m and 358 m above sea level.

    How Glomfjord Alpinsenter compares nearby

    Snow at Glomfjord Alpinsenter through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 262 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 11 April, with about 162 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 74 cm; a snowy December gave 115 cm, a dry one 43 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 159 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 93 cm and 174 cm.

    The mountain at Glomfjord Alpinsenter

    192 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Sætertindan (1,260 m), 6.9 km to the south-west, about 902 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Glomfjord Alpinsenter include Glomfjord hotell (hotel), Glomfjord Overnatting (guesthouse), Øvre Navar (chalet) and Ruffehytta (chalet).

    Glomfjord Alpinsenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Glomfjord Alpinsenter?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Glomfjord Alpinsenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 74 cm deep.

    What altitude is Glomfjord Alpinsenter at?

    Its highest lift reaches 358 m; the lowest starts at 166 m (192 m of vertical).

    How big is Glomfjord Alpinsenter?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
