    About Otaru Tenguyama

    Neither huge nor tiny, Otaru Tenguyama offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 215 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 205 m up to 529 m. The closest other ski area is Asarigawa Onsen (6.0 km, south-east).

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 13 km south to Kiroro Resort, with about 25 km of runs. For higher slopes, Kiroro Resort climbs to 1,180 m.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Otaru Tenguyama today: about 17 cm up high. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    The mountain at Otaru Tenguyama

    324 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 205 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.

    Quick answers

    How high is Otaru Tenguyama?

    The top is at 529 m and the base at 205 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Otaru Tenguyama?

    Kiroro Resort, 13 km to the south, with about 25 km of pistes.

    How big is Otaru Tenguyama?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
