    About Otaru Tenguyama

    Neither huge nor tiny, Otaru Tenguyama offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 215 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 205 m up to 529 m. The closest other ski area is Asarigawa Onsen (6.0 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 4.5 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.8 km, or 62%. Intermediate runs make up 62% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.3 km.

    The mountain at Otaru Tenguyama

    324 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 205 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car and 2 chairlifts, 3 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up.

    Quick answers

    How high is Otaru Tenguyama?

    The top is at 529 m and the base at 205 m.

    Does Otaru Tenguyama have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.3 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Otaru Tenguyama?

    Kiroro Resort, 13 km to the south, with about 25 km of pistes.

    How big is Otaru Tenguyama?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
