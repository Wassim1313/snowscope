    About La Trélasse

    About 1.0 km of pistes make La Trélasse one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 1,192 m and reach 1,218 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Les Rousses (3.4 km, west).

    La Trélasse and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Les Rousses: about 32 km of pistes, 5.2 km to the north. For more reliable snow, Saint-Cergue usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 8 weeks, against 2 here. Les Rousses reaches 1,663 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 26 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 1 button lift and 3 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises La Dôle (1,678 m), 2.9 km to the south, about 460 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow La Trélasse usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,205 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 11 February and keeps it until about 23 February, roughly 2 weeks. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 65 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 34 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 15 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 53 cm in a big one.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hôtel Club Côté Dôle (hotel), Hôtel Restaurant Arbez Franco-Suisse (hotel), Cabane du ski-club de Nyon (chalet) and Chalet de La Pile-Dessus (mountain hut).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at La Trélasse?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    How much snow does La Trélasse have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 15 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is La Trélasse at?

    Between 1,192 m and 1,218 m, so 26 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near La Trélasse?

    Les Rousses, 5.2 km to the north, with about 32 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does La Trélasse have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
