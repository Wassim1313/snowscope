    About Ski Nezdice

    Ski Nezdice skis between 670 m and 745 m: 75 m of vertical. 1 lift opens up around 1 km of pistes. It is one of the smaller ski areas of Central Europe, often quieter than the big names. It tops out lower than the resorts around it (996 m on average), so its snow depends more on cold spells.
