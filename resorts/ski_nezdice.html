    About Ski Nezdice

    Ski Nezdice is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 670 m and 745 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski areál Kašperky, 4.8 km to the south-west.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The pistes of Ski Nezdice

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. Most of the length is blue (38%, about 0.5 km). With 31% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    75 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 670 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Nové Hutě reaches 1,146 m. The only lift is a button lift.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Ski Nezdice at?

    Between 670 m and 745 m, so 75 m of vertical drop.

    Does Ski Nezdice have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.4 km of its graded pistes (31%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Ski Nezdice?

    Nové Hutě, 15 km to the south, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Ski Nezdice have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
