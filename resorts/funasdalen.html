    Funäsdalen in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Funäsdalen (784 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 45 cm of cover (37 cm to 55 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 72 cm (62 cm–81 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 79 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 26 % of winters, and by 1 January in 54 %.

    Best winter on record: 1987-88, peaking at 126 cm. Worst: 1995-96, which never passed 52 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to early May (164 days).

    Today's 7 cm compares with a usual 26 cm for 27 November.

    About Funäsdalen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Funäsdalen offers about 10 km of pistes (number 74 of 262 in Scandinavia). Skiing here happens between 621 m and 947 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Tänndalen, 16 km west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 22 runs and around 10 km to ski. Most of the length is red (39%, about 3.9 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 1.6 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 326 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 3 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Funäsdalsberget (977 m) and Säteråsen (834 m).

    Snow at Funäsdalen through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 784 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 December and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 23 weeks. A normal season tops out near 79 cm, around 28 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 58 cm and 88 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 72 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 45 cm, with 63 cm in the best years.
