    About Bardonecchia

    With some 35 km of runs, Bardonecchia belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,274 m and reach 2,384 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bardonecchia, 5.2 km to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Bardonecchia

    The piste map lists 49 graded runs, about 35 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 17 km, or 47%. Intermediate runs make up 47% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 1.6 km of black or expert runs.

    How Bardonecchia is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bardonecchia gives 1,110 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Serre-Chevalier reaches 2,765 m. To get up there are 7 chairlifts, 6 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Rocca Bernauda / Roche Bernaude at 3,222 m, standing 838 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Bardonecchia through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,829 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 22 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 96 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 59 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 60 cm and 114 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 51 cm; a snowy December gave 82 cm, a dry one 26 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Bardonecchia have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Bardonecchia at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 51 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Bardonecchia?

    The top is at 2,384 m and the base at 1,274 m.

    Is Bardonecchia suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bardonecchia?

    Montgenèvre, 14 km to the south, with about 62 km of pistes.
