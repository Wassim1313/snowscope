    About Qinggeli Langshan

    Qinggeli Langshan is one of the bigger ski areas of China: about 9.0 km of pistes put it at number 22 of 92. The slopes start at 1,232 m and reach 1,940 m at the top.

    Runs by difficulty

    Qinggeli Langshan has 3 graded runs adding up to about 6.9 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 6.9 km, or 100%. Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    GradeRunsLengthShare
    Blue square3 runs6.9 km100%

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    The mountain at Qinggeli Langshan

    From top to bottom that makes 708 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 gondolas and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Qinggeli Langshan hold around 13 cm of snow today. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Qinggeli Langshan: common questions

    How high is Qinggeli Langshan?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,940 m; the lowest starts at 1,232 m (708 m of vertical).

    Who is Qinggeli Langshan best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Qinggeli Langshan?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
