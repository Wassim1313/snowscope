    About Nesfjellet Alpinsenter

    Around 15 km of marked runs place Nesfjellet Alpinsenter among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 44 by length. Skiing here happens between 735 m and 1,093 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Gol Skisenter (21 km, north).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 23 runs and around 15 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 6.8 km, or 47%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The mountain at Nesfjellet Alpinsenter

    358 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 3 button lifts and 2 drag lifts, 8 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Fetjafjell (1,212 m), 6.5 km to the south, about 119 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Nesfjellet Alpinsenter usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 914 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 26 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 20 March, with about 95 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 87 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 64 cm and 116 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 57 cm, with 87 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Nesfjellet Alpinsenter?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Nesfjellet Alpinsenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 57 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Nesfjellet Alpinsenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,093 m; the lowest starts at 735 m (358 m of vertical).

    Is Nesfjellet Alpinsenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 9.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Nesfjellet Alpinsenter?

    About 15 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
