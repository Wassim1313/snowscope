    When the snow is good

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 556 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early January to late April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 54 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 83 cm, around 10 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 20 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 56 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 30 cm to 110 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Gilja Alpin?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    How much snow does Gilja Alpin have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.
