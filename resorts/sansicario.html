    About Sansicario

    Around 34 km of marked runs place Sansicario among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 185 by length. The slopes start at 1,549 m and reach 2,675 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sestriere, 5.5 km to the east.

    What there is to ski at Sansicario

    Counting every graded piste, there are 61 runs and around 34 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 25 km (75%). Intermediate runs make up 75% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 2.7 km of difficult runs.

    Snow at Sansicario through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,112 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 23 November and keeps it until about 22 May, roughly 26 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 127 cm. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 108 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Year to year the snow is steady, with 76 cm to 152 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 65 cm; a snowy December gave 116 cm, a dry one 34 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,126 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,549 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 1 funicular, 3 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 4 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Punta Muta at 3,171 m, standing 496 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Sansicario have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Sansicario at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 65 cm deep.

    How high is Sansicario?

    Between 1,549 m and 2,675 m, so 1,126 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Sansicario best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 75% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Sansicario?

    Montgenèvre, 9.3 km to the south-west, with about 62 km of pistes.
