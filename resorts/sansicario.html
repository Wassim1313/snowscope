    About Sansicario

    Around 34 km of marked runs place Sansicario among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 185 by length. The slopes start at 1,549 m and reach 2,675 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sestriere, 5.5 km to the east.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,126 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,549 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
