    About Saariselka

    Compact and local, Saariselka has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 267 m and 433 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Saariselkä, 0.4 km north-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 350 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 December and lasts to 11 May: about 23 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 48 cm to 73 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 59 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 39 cm; a snowy December gave 54 cm, a dry one 27 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 63 cm.

    How Saariselka is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 166 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars and 1 button lift, 3 lifts in all. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Kivipää (469 m), Iisakkipää (454 m) and Vahtamapää (452 m).

    Questions about Saariselka

    When does Saariselka have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Saariselka at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 39 cm deep.

    How high is Saariselka?

    Its highest lift reaches 433 m; the lowest starts at 267 m (166 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Saariselka have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
