    How Skiverein Sehmatal e.V. Skilift is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 92 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 724 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Skiareál Klínovec, for one, climbs to 1,240 m.
