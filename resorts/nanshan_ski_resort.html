    About Nanshan

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Nanshan sits in the middle of the size table of China. The ski area spans the mountain from 72 m up to 253 m. The closest other ski area is Yunfoshan (16 km, north).

    What there is to ski at Nanshan

    In all, 30 marked runs cover roughly 8.2 km. The largest share is green circle, with 4.2 km (52%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    Lifts and terrain

    181 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 72 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 4 drag lifts and 15 moving carpets. First-timers get 15 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Questions about Nanshan

    How high is Nanshan?

    Its highest lift reaches 253 m; the lowest starts at 72 m (181 m of vertical).

    Is Nanshan suitable for families?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Nanshan?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 23 lifts.
