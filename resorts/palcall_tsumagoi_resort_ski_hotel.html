    About Palcall Tsumagoi Resort Ski & Ho

    With some 15 km of runs, Palcall Tsumagoi Resort Ski & Ho belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,386 m up to 2,089 m. The closest other ski area is Minenohara Kogen (8.6 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Palcall Tsumagoi Resort Ski & Ho

    Palcall Tsumagoi Resort Ski & Ho has 16 graded runs adding up to about 15 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.8 km, or 50%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 703 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,386 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 5 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.
