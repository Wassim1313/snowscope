    About Lienz

    Neither huge nor tiny, Lienz offers about 21 km of pistes (number 296 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,672 m and 2,272 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Lienzer Bergbahnen (4.2 km, south-west).

    How much snow Lienz usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,972 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May, about 28 weeks of good cover. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 72 cm and 143 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 67 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 106 cm, a dry one 38 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 15 April, with about 123 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 123 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Lifts and terrain

    600 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,672 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Above the ski area rises Hoher Perschitzkopf (3,125 m), 7.8 km to the north, about 853 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Sporthotel Hochlienz (hotel), Alpengasthof Bidner (guesthouse), AV Jugendheim (mountain hut) and Berggasthaus Pension Zettersfeld (guesthouse).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Lienz?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Lienz at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 67 cm deep.

    How high is Lienz?

    Between 1,672 m and 2,272 m, so 600 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Lienz best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 59% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Lienz?

    About 21 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
