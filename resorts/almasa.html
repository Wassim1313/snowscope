    About Almåsa

    Almåsa is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 88 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Skiing here happens between 417 m and 702 m above sea level. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Åkersjöns Skidbacke, 30 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Almåsa has 7 graded runs adding up to about 7.7 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 3.2 km (42%). With 64% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 32 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Almåsa. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 32 cm at mid-mountain.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 560 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 1 December and lasts to 8 May: about 23 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 76 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 86 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 48 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 31 cm, snowy years 76 cm). Most winters land between 48 cm and 103 cm by mid-February.

    Lifts and terrain

    285 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 5 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Almåsaberget (706 m) and Hällberget (640 m).

    Quick answers

    When does Almåsa have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Almåsa have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 48 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Almåsa at?

    The top is at 702 m and the base at 417 m.

    Is Almåsa suitable for families?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 5.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Almåsa?

    Åre, 51 km to the west, with about 116 km of pistes.
