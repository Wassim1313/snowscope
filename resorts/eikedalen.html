    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 589 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 26 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 27 weeks. Most winters land between 99 cm and 267 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 200 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 222 cm, around 23 March. For about 20 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 93 cm, from 33 cm in a poor year to 147 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Eikedalen?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Eikedalen at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 93 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
