    About Snobowl Bike and

    Snobowl Bike and is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,932 m up to 2,129 m.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 12 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Snobowl Bike and. The last seven days brought 19 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (5 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 197 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,932 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 rope tow.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,030 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 31 December and keeps it until about 12 March, roughly 10 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm, with 68 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 66 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 116 cm.

    Snobowl Bike and: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Snobowl Bike and?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Snobowl Bike and at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    What altitude is Snobowl Bike and at?

    Between 1,932 m and 2,129 m, so 197 m of vertical drop.
