    About Okunakayamakogen

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Okunakayamakogen sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Skiing here happens between 646 m and 1,003 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Tayama (21 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 6.1 km to ski. The largest share is black, with 2.8 km (46%). Steep ground is the speciality: 46% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 2.8 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 357 m: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 646 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 5 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Okunakayamakogen hold around 6 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 19 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Okunakayamakogen: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Okunakayamakogen?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,003 m; the lowest starts at 646 m (357 m of vertical).

    Is Okunakayamakogen good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 2.8 km of its graded pistes (46%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Okunakayamakogen have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
