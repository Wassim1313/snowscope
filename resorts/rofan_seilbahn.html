    About Rofan Seilbahn

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Rofan Seilbahn sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 976 m up to 1,864 m. The closest other ski area is Planberg- & Wiesenlifte Pertisau (4.3 km, west).

    How much snow Rofan Seilbahn usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,420 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 5 January and lasts to 12 May: about 18 weeks. A normal season tops out near 46 cm, around 29 February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 25 cm; a snowy December gave 42 cm, a dry one 11 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 27 cm and 60 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 40 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Rofan Seilbahn gives 888 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,176 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car and 2 chairlifts, 3 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hochiss at 2,299 m, standing 435 m above the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 7.4 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 5.6 km (76%). Intermediate runs make up 76% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 1.2 km of black or expert runs.

    Questions about Rofan Seilbahn

    What is the best month to ski at Rofan Seilbahn?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Rofan Seilbahn at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 25 cm deep.

    How high is Rofan Seilbahn?

    The top is at 1,864 m and the base at 976 m.

    Who is Rofan Seilbahn best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 76% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Rofan Seilbahn?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
