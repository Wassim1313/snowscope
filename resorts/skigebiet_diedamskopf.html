    The snow at Diedamskopf, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Diedamskopf (about 1,447 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 48 cm, with a normal range of 31 cm–62 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 97 cm; the middle half of winters range from 74 cm to 118 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 111 cm.

    28 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 50 % by 1 January.

    1998-99 was the big one (234 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (66 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 165 days.

    Questions about Diedamskopf

    Does Diedamskopf have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 48 cm, and only 50 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 33 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Diedamskopf?

    February: the snow peaks at about 111 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Diedamskopf get?

    About 111 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 66 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 234 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high do the lifts go at Diedamskopf?

    The top is at 2,056 m and the base at 838 m.

    How big is Diedamskopf?

    About Diedamskopf

    With some 36 km of runs, Diedamskopf belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 838 m and reach 2,056 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ifen (7.4 km, east).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Diedamskopf gives 1,218 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 838 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 2,056 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
