    About Diedamskopf

    With some 36 km of runs, Diedamskopf belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 838 m and reach 2,056 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ifen (7.4 km, east).

    What there is to ski at Diedamskopf

    Diedamskopf has 60 graded runs adding up to about 36 km. Most of the length is blue (44%, about 16 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 6.6 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Diedamskopf gives 1,218 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 838 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 2,056 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 3 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Hochkünzelspitze (2,397 m), 7.1 km to the south, about 341 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Diedamskopf usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,447 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 December and lasts to 10 May: about 21 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 60 cm to 145 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 111 cm, around 4 March. For about 3 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 68 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 48 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 77 cm in the best years.

    Questions about Diedamskopf

    When does Diedamskopf have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Diedamskopf have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 48 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Diedamskopf?

    The top is at 2,056 m and the base at 838 m.

    Can beginners ski at Diedamskopf?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 16 km, are easy.

    How big is Diedamskopf?

    About 36 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
