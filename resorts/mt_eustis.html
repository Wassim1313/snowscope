    Common questions

    Does Mt. Eustis have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 9.8 in (25 cm) at Christmas on average, with a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 9 % of 46 winters. Right now: no snow.

    When is the best month to ski Mt. Eustis?

    February: the snow peaks at about 21 in (54 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from early January to late March.

    How deep does the snow get at Mt. Eustis?

    About 21 in (54 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (mid-February); between 9.1 in (23 cm) in the leanest winter (2015-16) and 44 in (112 cm) in the biggest (2000-01).

    What altitude is Mt. Eustis at?

    Between 302 m and 384 m, so 82 m of vertical drop.

    Mt. Eustis has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 302 m and reach 384 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cannon Mountain, 15 km to the south-east.

    There is no snow on the ground at Mt. Eustis yet. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 8 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    82 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 302 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Towns Mountain, 671 m high and 7.4 km north of the resort.

    How much snow Mt. Eustis usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 343 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 January and lasts to 28 March: about 12 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 25 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 6 cm in a poor year to 43 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 54 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 24 cm and 78 cm.
