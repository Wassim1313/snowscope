    About Nevelandia

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Nevelandia one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,281 m and 1,295 m above sea level. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Piancavallo, 1.7 km to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.5 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.5 km, or 100%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Nevelandia has roughly 14 cm of snow on the ground today. 16 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 14 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Ski Civetta, for one, climbs to 2,078 m. All 2 lifts here are moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Cimon del Cavallo, 2,251 m high and 2.8 km north-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,288 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 51 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 24 cm in a big one. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 24 cm.

    Questions about Nevelandia

    When is the best time to ski at Nevelandia?

    Aim for mid-March and late March.

    How much snow does Nevelandia have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Nevelandia at?

    The top is at 1,295 m and the base at 1,281 m.

    Is Nevelandia suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Nevelandia?

    Piancavallo, 1.7 km to the west, with about 15 km of pistes.
