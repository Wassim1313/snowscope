    About Laax

    With some 152 km of runs, Laax belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,082 m to 2,974 m. Elm is the next resort, 9.7 km away to the north-west.

    The mountain at Laax

    Skied top to bottom, Laax gives 1,892 m of descent: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,974 m. The lift system counts 37 lifts: 2 cable cars, 13 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 5 button lifts, 5 rope tows and 3 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Piz Segnas, 3,098 m high and 7.0 km north of the resort.

    What there is to ski at Laax

    In all, 153 marked runs cover roughly 150 km. Most of the length is blue (38%, about 57 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Experts can count on some 12 km of difficult runs. 28 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow at Laax through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,028 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 125 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For about 14 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 67 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 97 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 79 cm to 153 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 129 cm, around 22 March.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Laax hold around 12 cm of snow today. 10 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 32 cm by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 34 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track. The 2026/27 season at Laax is announced to start on 28 November 2026 and to run until 11 April 2027.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Berghaus Nagens (hotel), Laaxer Hof (hotel), Segneshütte (mountain hut) and Rocksresort (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Laax?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Laax at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 67 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Laax open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high is Laax?

    The top is at 2,974 m and the base at 1,082 m.

    Does Laax have steep runs?

    Yes: about 40 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.
