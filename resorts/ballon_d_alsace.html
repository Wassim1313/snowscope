    About Ballon d'Alsace

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ballon d'Alsace offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 541 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 816 m and 1,150 m above sea level. Saint-Maurice-sur-Moselle is the next resort, 2.8 km away to the north.

    What there is to ski at Ballon d'Alsace

    Counting every graded piste, there are 18 runs and around 7.5 km to ski. The largest share is green (beginner), with 2.6 km (35%). Steep ground is the speciality: 32% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 1.2 km of difficult runs. 1.2 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Ballon d'Alsace gives 334 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,016 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,150 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 9 drag lifts and 1 rope tow. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Haute Bers (1,252 m), 6.2 km to the north-east, about 102 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 983 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 5 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 55 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 15 cm, around 14 February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 83 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Ballon d'Alsace include Auberge Fluhr (hotel), Chalet du Ski Club de Beaucourt (mountain hut), Foyer communautaire Jean Devillers (mountain hut) and Refuge des amis de la natures (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Ballon d'Alsace: common questions

    When does Ballon d'Alsace have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Is there snow at Ballon d'Alsace at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Ballon d'Alsace at?

    The top is at 1,150 m and the base at 816 m.

    Is Ballon d'Alsace suitable for families?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 3.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ballon d'Alsace have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
