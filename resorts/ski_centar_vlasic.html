    About Ski centar Vlašić

    Ski centar Vlašić is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 25 of 51 in the Balkans by length. Its lifts climb from 1,229 m to 1,524 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 7.1 km in total. Most of the length is red (100%, about 7.1 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Ski centar Vlašić gives 295 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chairlift and 4 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
