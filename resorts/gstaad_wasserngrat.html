    About Gstaad

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Gstaad sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,272 m and 1,936 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wispile, 2.5 km to the south-west.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,604 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-May. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 96 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 107 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 6 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 63 cm to 138 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 47 cm (lean years 17 cm, snowy years 74 cm).

    What there is to ski at Gstaad

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 3.6 km. The largest share is red, with 1.9 km (53%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.7 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Gstaad gives 664 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The only lift is a chairlift. The skyline is dominated by Giferspitz, 2,542 m high and 2.9 km south-east of the resort.

    The upper slopes of Gstaad hold around 38 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 57 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 25 cm at mid-mountain).

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Palace Gstaad, Le Grand Bellevue, Alphorn and Hotel Gstaaderhof.

    Questions about Gstaad

    When is the best time to ski at Gstaad?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Gstaad at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 47 cm deep.

    What altitude is Gstaad at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,936 m; the lowest starts at 1,272 m (664 m of vertical).

    Does Gstaad have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.7 km of its graded pistes (47%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Gstaad?

    Schönried, 5.8 km to the north, with about 59 km of pistes.
