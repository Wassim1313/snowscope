    About Mount Rose Ski Tahoe

    Mount Rose Ski Tahoe is a mid-sized ski area with about 33 km of pistes, number 72 of 175 in US West by length. The slopes start at 2,310 m and reach 2,916 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Diamond Peak (7.9 km, south).

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Mount Rose Ski Tahoe hold around 50 cm of snow today. 18 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 52 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Mount Rose Ski Tahoe gives 606 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 2,310 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
