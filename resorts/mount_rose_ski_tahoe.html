    About Mount Rose Ski Tahoe

    Mount Rose Ski Tahoe is a mid-sized ski area with about 33 km of pistes, number 72 of 175 in US West by length. The slopes start at 2,310 m and reach 2,916 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Diamond Peak (7.9 km, south).

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Mount Rose Ski Tahoe hold around 53 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 83 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. An ordinary winter would have around 13 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The pistes of Mount Rose Ski Tahoe

    Counting every graded piste, there are 80 runs and around 33 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 37%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 9.6 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Mount Rose Ski Tahoe gives 606 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 2,310 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 8 chairlifts and 4 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Mount Rose, 3,285 m high and 3.9 km north-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    At about 2,613 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 5 December and lasts to 7 May: about 22 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 84 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 204 cm). For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 126 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. A normal season tops out near 204 cm, around 21 March. Most winters land between 57 cm and 303 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Mount Rose Ski Tahoe

    When does Mount Rose Ski Tahoe have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Mount Rose Ski Tahoe at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 84 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Mount Rose Ski Tahoe?

    Between 2,310 m and 2,916 m, so 606 m of vertical drop.

    Is Mount Rose Ski Tahoe good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 9.6 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Mount Rose Ski Tahoe?

    Northstar at Tahoe Resort, 22 km to the west, with about 83 km of pistes.
