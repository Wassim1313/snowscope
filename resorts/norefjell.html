    About Norefjell

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Norefjell sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The slopes start at 194 m and reach 995 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Norefjell, 0.5 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 23 marked runs cover roughly 7.4 km. Most of the length is red (34%, about 2.5 km). With 54% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Norefjell gives 801 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 995 m is the third highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Grindefjell (1,414 m), 7.7 km to the north-west, about 419 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 594 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 84 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 65 cm and 132 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 55 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 34 cm, snowy years 89 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 11 March, with about 99 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Norefjell

    When is the best time to ski at Norefjell?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Norefjell have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 55 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Norefjell at?

    The top is at 995 m and the base at 194 m.

    Can beginners ski at Norefjell?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Norefjell have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
