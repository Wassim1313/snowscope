    About Krößbach

    Krößbach has about 0.4 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,103 m up to 1,171 m. The closest other ski area is Stubai (2.2 km, north-east).

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Krößbach today: about 16 cm up high. The last seven days brought 21 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 8 cm now.

    The mountain at Krößbach

    The vertical drop is 68 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Stubai reaches 3,208 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Habicht (3,277 m), 4.1 km to the south-east, about 2,106 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,137 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 25 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 28 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 25 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 16 cm and 35 cm.

    Questions about Krößbach

    What is the best month to ski at Krößbach?

    Aim for early March and late March.

    How much snow does Krößbach have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    What altitude is Krößbach at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,171 m; the lowest starts at 1,103 m (68 m of vertical).

    How big is Krößbach?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
