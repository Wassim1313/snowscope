    About Sella Nevea

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Sella Nevea sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,107 m and 2,137 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Tarvisio, 14 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Sella Nevea usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,622 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 January and lasts to 30 March: about 12 weeks. A normal season tops out near 53 cm, around 3 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm, with 62 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 7 cm to 97 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 12 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,030 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,137 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Jôf Fuart, 2,666 m high and 7.4 km north of the resort.

    What there is to ski at Sella Nevea

    The piste map lists 29 graded runs, about 10 km in total. The largest share is red, with 5.7 km (56%). Intermediate runs make up 56% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 1.6 km of difficult runs.

    Questions about Sella Nevea

    When does Sella Nevea have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    How much snow does Sella Nevea have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Sella Nevea at?

    Between 1,107 m and 2,137 m, so 1,030 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Sella Nevea best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 56% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Sella Nevea?

    Nassfeld, 29 km to the north-west, with about 70 km of pistes.
