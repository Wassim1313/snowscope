    The snow at Wolf Creek, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Wolf Creek (about 11,010 ft (3,356 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 41 in (104 cm), with a normal range of 22 in (56 cm)–60 in (152 cm).

    Mid-February typically sees 65 in (165 cm), between 43 in (108 cm) and 81 in (206 cm) in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 72 in (183 cm).

    By 15 December 72 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 85 %.

    The deepest winter was 1984-85 (140 in (356 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (30 in (75 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to late May, about 205 days.

    Questions about Wolf Creek

    Can you ski Wolf Creek at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 41 in (104 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 85 % of 46 winters. Right now: 36 in (92 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Wolf Creek?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 72 in (183 cm)). For a sure base, mid-November to late May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How snowy is Wolf Creek?

    About 72 in (183 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (mid-March); between 30 in (75 cm) in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 140 in (356 cm) in the biggest (1984-85).

    How high do the lifts go at Wolf Creek?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,585 m; the lowest starts at 3,128 m (457 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Wolf Creek have?

    About 50 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
