    About Tajima Farm Park

    Compact and local, Tajima Farm Park has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Japan. Its lifts climb from 366 m to 560 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ojiro, 6.0 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Tajima Farm Park

    Tajima Farm Park has 4 graded runs adding up to about 1.6 km. Most of the length is green (81%, about 1.3 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Tajima Farm Park

    194 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 366 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Hachi Kita, reach 1,217 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Tajima Farm Park at?

    The top is at 560 m and the base at 366 m.

    Is Tajima Farm Park suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Tajima Farm Park?

    Hachi Kita, 13 km to the south, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Tajima Farm Park?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
