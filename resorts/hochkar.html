    About Hochkar

    Hochkar is a mid-sized ski area with about 19 km of pistes, number 323 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,378 m to 1,788 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hochkar, 1.6 km to the south-west.

    How much snow Hochkar usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,583 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 5 December and keeps it until about 1 May, roughly 21 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 60 cm to 168 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 129 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 52 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 12 cm in a poor year to 97 cm in a big one. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 90 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    What there is to ski at Hochkar

    In all, 40 marked runs cover roughly 18 km. Most of the length is red (55%, about 10 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.7 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 410 m: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,378 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 1,788 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 6 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Hochkar (1,808 m), Noten (1,780 m) and Schmalzmauer (1,759 m).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Hochkar?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Hochkar at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 52 cm deep.

    What altitude is Hochkar at?

    The top is at 1,788 m and the base at 1,378 m.

    What level of skier is Hochkar best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 55% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Hochkar?

    About 19 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
