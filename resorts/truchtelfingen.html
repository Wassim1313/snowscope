    About Truchtelfingen

    Truchtelfingen is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 781 m to 834 m. The closest other ski area is Albstadt-Tailfingen (2.0 km, north-east).

    The mountain at Truchtelfingen

    From top to bottom that makes 53 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 981 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Skilift Albstadt-Tailfingen, for one, climbs to 932 m.
