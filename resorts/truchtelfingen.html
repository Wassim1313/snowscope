    About Truchtelfingen

    Truchtelfingen has about 0.2 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 781 m to 834 m. The closest other ski area is Albstadt-Tailfingen (2.0 km, north-east).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 808 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 3 cm, with 13 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 22 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 22 January, with about 6 cm.

    The mountain at Truchtelfingen

    From top to bottom that makes 53 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 981 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Skilift Albstadt-Tailfingen, for one, climbs to 932 m. The only lift is a rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Weichenwang at 988 m, standing 154 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Truchtelfingen

    When is the best time to ski at Truchtelfingen?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    How much snow does Truchtelfingen have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 3 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Truchtelfingen?

    Its highest lift reaches 834 m; the lowest starts at 781 m (53 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Truchtelfingen have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
