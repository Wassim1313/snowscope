    About Ebenalp-Horn

    Ebenalp-Horn is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 485 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 846 m and reach 1,701 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Brülisau, 2.9 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Ebenalp-Horn through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,274 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 January and keeps it until about 14 May, roughly 19 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 48 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 25 cm and 88 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 26 cm (lean years 8 cm, snowy years 44 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 18 February, with about 56 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Ebenalp-Horn gives 855 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 846 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 cable car, 3 T-bars and 2 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The skyline is dominated by Säntis, 2,502 m high and 6.8 km south-west of the resort.

    Questions about Ebenalp-Horn

    When is the best time to ski at Ebenalp-Horn?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Ebenalp-Horn at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    How high is Ebenalp-Horn?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,701 m; the lowest starts at 846 m (855 m of vertical).

    Who is Ebenalp-Horn best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 80% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Ebenalp-Horn?

    About 10 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
