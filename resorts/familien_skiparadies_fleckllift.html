    About Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift

    Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 707 m and 727 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Skischaukel Hempelsberg und Geie (0.9 km, south).

    Snow at Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 717 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-December and early January. A normal season tops out near 13 cm, around 31 January. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 37 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 4 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 23 cm in the best years.

    How Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift gives 20 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 707 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Ochsenkopf, for one, climbs to 1,012 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-December and early January.

    Can you ski at Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift?

    The top is at 727 m and the base at 707 m.

    How many km of pistes does Familien Skiparadies Fleckllift have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
