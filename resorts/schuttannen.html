    About Schuttannen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Schuttannen offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 787 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,151 m and 1,319 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Heumöserlift, 2.1 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,235 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to early May. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 52 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 29 cm; a snowy December gave 48 cm, a dry one 10 cm. A normal season tops out near 56 cm, around 16 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 28 cm and 87 cm.

    What there is to ski at Schuttannen

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 3.1 km in total. Most of the length is blue (89%, about 2.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Damüls-Mellau: about 88 km of pistes, 12 km to the south-east. Laterns-Gapfohl has the longer snow season: around 22 weeks of solid cover, compared with 18 at Schuttannen. For higher slopes, Damüls-Mellau climbs to 2,021 m.

    How Schuttannen is laid out

    The vertical drop is 168 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hoher Freschen at 2,004 m, standing 685 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Alpenrose (hotel), Gasthaus-Pension Alpenheim (hotel), Kolping Feriendorf Ebnit (hotel) and Schönermannalpe (mountain hut).

    Schuttannen: common questions

    When does Schuttannen have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    How much snow does Schuttannen have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 29 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Schuttannen at?

    The top is at 1,319 m and the base at 1,151 m.

    Is Schuttannen good for beginners?

    Yes: 89% of the graded pistes, about 2.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Schuttannen?

    Damüls-Mellau, 12 km to the south-east, with about 88 km of pistes.
