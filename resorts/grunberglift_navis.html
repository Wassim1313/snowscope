    About Grünberglift Navis

    Grünberglift Navis is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,385 m and 1,576 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bergeralm, 9.8 km to the south-west.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Hintertuxer Gletscher (10 km south-east) has about 49 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Hintertuxer Gletscher has the longer snow season: around 28 weeks of solid cover, compared with 15 at Grünberglift Navis. For higher slopes, Hintertuxer Gletscher climbs to 3,245 m.

    Snow at Grünberglift Navis through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,480 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to early April, about 15 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 37 cm to 74 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 16 cm in a poor year to 49 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 58 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 29 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Grünberglift Navis has roughly 27 cm of snow on the ground today. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 18 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 191 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,385 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Lizumer Reckner (2,886 m), 6.3 km to the east, about 1,310 m higher than the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find 4 mountain huts: Peeralm, Stöcklalm, Seapen Alm and Poltnalm. Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Grünberglift Navis: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Grünberglift Navis?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Is there snow at Grünberglift Navis at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 31 cm deep.

    How high is Grünberglift Navis?

    The top is at 1,576 m and the base at 1,385 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Grünberglift Navis?

    Hintertuxer Gletscher, 10 km to the south-east, with about 49 km of pistes.

    How big is Grünberglift Navis?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
