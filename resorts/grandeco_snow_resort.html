    About Grandeco Snow Resort

    Grandeco Snow Resort is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 13 km of pistes put it at number 42 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,011 m up to 1,581 m. Tengendai Kogen is the next resort, 6.9 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Grandeco Snow Resort

    In all, 21 marked runs cover roughly 13 km. Beginner runs make up the biggest part: 6.3 km, or 50%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 570 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola and 4 chairlifts, 5 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns.

    Grandeco Snow Resort: common questions

    What altitude is Grandeco Snow Resort at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,581 m; the lowest starts at 1,011 m (570 m of vertical).

    Is Grandeco Snow Resort suitable for families?

    Yes: 84% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How big is Grandeco Snow Resort?

    About 13 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
