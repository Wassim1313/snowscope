    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 673 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early February. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 17 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 1 cm in a poor year to 32 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 50 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Questions about Skilifte Geiersberg

    When does Skilifte Geiersberg have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Can you ski at Skilifte Geiersberg at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.
