    About Saas-Almagell

    Saas-Almagell is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 497 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,673 m up to 2,344 m. The closest other ski area is Saas Fee (3.5 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Saas-Almagell

    Counting every graded piste, there are 25 runs and around 9.3 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.4 km, or 58%. Intermediate runs make up 58% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Saas-Almagell today: about 24 cm up high. The last seven days brought 41 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 18 cm).

    The mountain at Saas-Almagell

    From top to bottom that makes 671 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,673 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Lenzspitze (4,294 m), 7.8 km to the west, about 1,950 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Saas-Almagell through a normal winter

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,008 m in an ordinary winter, from early January to early May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 40 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Year to year the snow is steady, with 32 cm to 70 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 27 cm; a snowy December gave 45 cm, a dry one 18 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 50 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    Questions about Saas-Almagell

    When does Saas-Almagell have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early May.

    How much snow does Saas-Almagell have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 27 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Saas-Almagell at?

    The top is at 2,344 m and the base at 1,673 m.

    Can beginners ski at Saas-Almagell?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 3.9 km, are easy.

    Can Saas-Almagell make its own snow?

    Yes, on about 6.4 km of its pistes (69%).
