    About Saas-Almagell

    Saas-Almagell is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 497 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,673 m up to 2,344 m. The closest other ski area is Saas Fee (3.5 km, west).

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Saas-Almagell today: about 55 cm up high. 14 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 25 cm by Wednesday.

    The mountain at Saas-Almagell

    From top to bottom that makes 671 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,673 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 hotels: Pirmin Zurbriggen, Monte-Moro, Mattmarkblick and Portjengrat.
