    About Bündalift

    Bündalift is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,556 m up to 1,696 m. Schatzalp/Strela is the next resort, 2.2 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 1.3 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 0.8 km (58%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Bündalift is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bündalift gives 140 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,556 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Parsenn, reach 2,811 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pischahorn at 2,980 m, standing 1,284 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,626 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 December and keeps it until about 25 April, roughly 19 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 48 cm and 92 cm. A normal season tops out near 71 cm, around 17 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm (lean years 16 cm, snowy years 53 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 35 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Questions about Bündalift

    When does Bündalift have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Bündalift have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 38 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Bündalift?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,696 m; the lowest starts at 1,556 m (140 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Bündalift?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bündalift?

    Parsenn, 5.2 km to the north, with about 81 km of pistes.
