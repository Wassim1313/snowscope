    About Bündalift

    Bündalift is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,556 m up to 1,696 m. Schatzalp/Strela is the next resort, 2.2 km away to the west.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Parsenn (5.2 km north) has about 81 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. For higher slopes, Parsenn climbs to 2,811 m.

    How Bündalift is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bündalift gives 140 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,556 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Parsenn, reach 2,811 m.
