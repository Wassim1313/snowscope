    About Graukogel

    Graukogel is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 489 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,076 m and 2,006 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Gastein, 6.0 km to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 9.9 km to ski. Most of the length is red (55%, about 5.4 km). Intermediate runs make up 55% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.7 km.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Graukogel has roughly 41 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday. An ordinary winter would have around 18 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 930 m: enough height for proper long runs. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 1 T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Tischlerkarkopf, 3,004 m high and 7.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow at Graukogel through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,541 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late March, about 14 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 28 February, with about 64 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 28 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 37 cm and 78 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 33 cm, from 13 cm in a poor year to 54 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Graukogel?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late March.

    Can you ski at Graukogel at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 33 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Graukogel?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,006 m; the lowest starts at 1,076 m (930 m of vertical).

    Who is Graukogel best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 55% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Graukogel?

    Ski Gastein, 6.0 km to the north-west, with about 74 km of pistes.
