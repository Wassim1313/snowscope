    About Selvino

    Compact and local, Selvino has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 944 m up to 1,097 m. Conca dell'Alben is the next resort, 11 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Selvino

    Selvino has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.8 km. Most of the length is red (100%, about 1.8 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Selvino

    Skied top to bottom, Selvino gives 153 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,144 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Foppolo-Carona, for one, climbs to 2,149 m. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Segredònt at 1,548 m, standing 451 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,020 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 10 cm, with 29 cm in the best years. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 7 cm to 49 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 5 March. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Selvino?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Selvino at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Selvino?

    The top is at 1,097 m and the base at 944 m.

    What level of skier is Selvino best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Selvino?

    Foppolo-Carona, 29 km to the north, with about 16 km of pistes.
