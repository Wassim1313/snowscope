    About Cosmic Hill

    Cosmic Hill is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 188 m to 233 m. Mad River Glen is the next resort, 15 km away to the south-west.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 210 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 January and lasts to 24 March: about 9 weeks. A normal season tops out near 45 cm, around 10 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 21 cm, with 42 cm in the best years. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Year to year the snow is steady, with 20 cm to 69 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    How Cosmic Hill is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Cosmic Hill gives 45 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 188 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Sugarbush Resort reaches 1,238 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Bald Mountain (776 m), 4.4 km to the south, about 543 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Cosmic Hill include 4 guesthouses: Wilder Farm Inn, White Birch Cottage, 1824 House Inn and Mad River Inn B&B.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Cosmic Hill?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    Can you ski at Cosmic Hill at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 21 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Cosmic Hill at?

    The top is at 233 m and the base at 188 m.
