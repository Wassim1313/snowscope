    About Mendip Snowsport Centre

    Mendip Snowsport Centre has about 0.1 km of pistes. The slopes start at 58 m and reach 93 m at the top.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 35 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 58 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. All 4 lifts here are button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
