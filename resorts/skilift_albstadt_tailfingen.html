    About Skilift Albstadt-Tailfingen

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Skilift Albstadt-Tailfingen one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 819 m and 932 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Albstadt-Tailfingen, 0.1 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 113 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 819 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Skilift Albstadt-Tailfingen today: about 3 cm up high. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Wednesday.
