    About Azuga

    Azuga is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 5.0 km of pistes put it at number 43 of 312. Skiing here happens between 976 m and 1,526 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Buşteni (5.5 km, south-west).

    What there is to ski at Azuga

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 4.8 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.8 km, or 100%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    How Azuga is laid out

    550 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 976 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Quick answers

    How high is Azuga?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,526 m; the lowest starts at 976 m (550 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Azuga best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Azuga?

    Sinaia, 11 km to the south-west, with about 30 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Azuga have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
