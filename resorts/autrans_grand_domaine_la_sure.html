    About Autrans

    With roughly 12 km of runs, Autrans sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,262 m to 1,605 m. Its nearest neighbour is Autrans, 2.7 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 27 graded runs, about 12 km in total. The largest share is red, with 5.1 km (45%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 2.3 km of difficult runs.

    How much snow Autrans usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,434 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early April, about 15 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 104 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 34 cm (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 139 cm). Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 10 cm to 211 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 17 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February.

    How Autrans is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 343 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 chairlift, 4 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Charande, 1,709 m high and 4.8 km south of the resort.

    Questions about Autrans

    When does Autrans have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early April.

    Can you ski at Autrans at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Autrans?

    Between 1,262 m and 1,605 m, so 343 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Autrans best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 45% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Autrans?

    About 12 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
