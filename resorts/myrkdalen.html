    About Myrkdalen

    With some 23 km of runs, Myrkdalen belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 446 m and 1,067 m above sea level. Voss Resort Fjellheisar is the next resort, 24 km away to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 28 graded runs, about 23 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 50%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 5.1 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 756 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early November to late May or later, about 30 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 276 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 25 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 149 cm to 344 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 26 March, with about 291 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 144 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 207 cm in the best years.

    How Myrkdalen is laid out

    The vertical drop is 621 m: runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 3 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bjørndalskamben at 1,402 m, standing 335 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Myrkdalen?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at Myrkdalen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 144 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Myrkdalen?

    The top is at 1,067 m and the base at 446 m.

    What level of skier is Myrkdalen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Myrkdalen?

    About 23 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
