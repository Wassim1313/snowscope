    About Fridingen Antoni

    Fridingen Antoni is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 674 m and 781 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Aggershausen, 12 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Fridingen Antoni gives 107 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 674 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Böttingen, for one, climbs to 964 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Fridingen Antoni include Hotel Gasthof Sonne (hotel), Löwen (guesthouse), Berghaus Knopfmacher (hotel) and Hotel Landhaus Donautal (hotel).

    Questions about Fridingen Antoni

    What altitude is Fridingen Antoni at?

    Its highest lift reaches 781 m; the lowest starts at 674 m (107 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Fridingen Antoni?

    Ebingen, 20 km to the north, with about 2.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Fridingen Antoni?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
