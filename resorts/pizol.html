    About Pizol

    Around 40 km of marked runs place Pizol among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 150 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 513 m up to 2,250 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being St. Margrethenberg, 5.1 km to the east.

    How Pizol is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Pizol gives 1,737 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 513 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
