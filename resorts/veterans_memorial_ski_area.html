    About Veterans Memorial

    Compact and local, Veterans Memorial has about 2.0 km of runs, small for US Northeast. Skiing here happens between 236 m and 306 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Proctor, 11 km west, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 13 graded runs, about 2.2 km in total. Most of the length is green circle (58%, about 1.3 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 70 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 236 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Tucker Mountain (495 m), 8.0 km to the north-west, about 189 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Veterans Memorial through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 271 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 18 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 30 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 7 cm to 62 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Questions about Veterans Memorial

    What is the best month to ski at Veterans Memorial?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Veterans Memorial have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Veterans Memorial?

    The top is at 306 m and the base at 236 m.

    Is Veterans Memorial good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Veterans Memorial?

    Mount Sunapee, 33 km to the west, with about 31 km of pistes.
