    About Turracher Höhe

    With some 34 km of runs, Turracher Höhe belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,392 m and 2,193 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Heidialm, 7.2 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 62 graded runs, about 32 km in total. The largest share is red, with 18 km (57%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,792 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to late April, about 16 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 52 cm, around 21 February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 27 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 50 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 31 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Year to year the snow is steady, with 29 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    801 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,392 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 chair-gondola, 6 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 15 lifts in all. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Eisenhut (2,441 m), 5.2 km to the north-east, about 248 m higher than the top lift.

    Turracher Höhe: common questions

    When does Turracher Höhe have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    Can you ski at Turracher Höhe at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 27 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Turracher Höhe at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,193 m; the lowest starts at 1,392 m (801 m of vertical).

    Is Turracher Höhe good for beginners?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How big is Turracher Höhe?

    About 34 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
