    About Fregattbacken

    Fregattbacken is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 16 m to 48 m. Skistar Hammarbybacken is the next resort, 9.4 km away to the west.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 32 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 31 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 7 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 16 cm in a big one.

    How Fregattbacken is laid out

    32 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Flottsbro, reach 102 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Himlaberget (70 m), Hjortberget (65 m) and Ekholmsnäsberget (63 m).

    Questions about Fregattbacken

    What is the best month to ski at Fregattbacken?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Is there snow at Fregattbacken at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Fregattbacken?

    Between 16 m and 48 m, so 32 m of vertical drop.
