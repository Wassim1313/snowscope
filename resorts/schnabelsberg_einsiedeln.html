    About Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln

    Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 948 m and 1,013 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bennau /Einsiedeln, 0.5 km to the north-west.

    How Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 65 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 948 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Brunni-Haggenegg reaches 1,500 m. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar. Above the ski area rises Spital (1,574 m), 7.1 km to the south, about 561 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln through a normal winter

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 980 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to late March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 22 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 61 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 22 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 37 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 7 February, with about 38 cm.

    Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    How much snow does Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high is Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln?

    Between 948 m and 1,013 m, so 65 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln?

    Biberegg, 7.2 km to the south-west, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
