    About Alvaneu

    Compact and local, Alvaneu has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,142 m up to 1,197 m. The closest other ski area is Lantsch/Lenz (5.5 km, west).

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Arosa Lenzerheide (10 km north-west) has about 164 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Bergün/Bravuogn does better, with about 26 weeks of a good base instead of 0. For higher slopes, Arosa Lenzerheide climbs to 2,854 m.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 2 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Alvaneu. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 11 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 6 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 55 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Arosa Lenzerheide reaches 2,854 m. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Corn da Tinizong, 3,172 m high and 7.3 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,170 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 March and lasts to 27 March: about 0 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 28 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-April and late April. A normal season tops out near 30 cm, around 25 March. Most winters land between 18 cm and 36 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 17 cm, with 26 cm in the best years.

    Alvaneu: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Alvaneu?

    Aim for mid-April and late April; the cover is usually good from late March to late March.

    Can you ski at Alvaneu at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 17 cm deep.

    What altitude is Alvaneu at?

    The top is at 1,197 m and the base at 1,142 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Alvaneu?

    Arosa Lenzerheide, 10 km to the north-west, with about 164 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Alvaneu have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
