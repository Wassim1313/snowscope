    About Nybroberget

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Nybroberget sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 156 m and 325 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Romme Alpin, 4.5 km south-west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Nybroberget

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 2.9 km in total. Most of the length is black (36%, about 1.0 km). With 36% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.0 km.

    The mountain at Nybroberget

    From top to bottom that makes 169 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Gethällsklack (395 m), Solklinten (390 m) and Stora berget (329 m).

    How much snow Nybroberget usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 240 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 6 cm and 47 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 32 cm, a dry one 1 cm. A normal season tops out near 27 cm, around 11 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Questions about Nybroberget

    What is the best month to ski at Nybroberget?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Can you ski at Nybroberget at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Nybroberget?

    Its highest lift reaches 325 m; the lowest starts at 156 m (169 m of vertical).

    Is Nybroberget good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.0 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Nybroberget have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
