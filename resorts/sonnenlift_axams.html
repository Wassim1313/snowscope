    About Sonnenlift Axams

    Compact and local, Sonnenlift Axams has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 916 m up to 994 m. Axamer Lizum is the next resort, 4.2 km away to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 78 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 916 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Axamer Lizum, reach 2,334 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Große Ochsenwand at 2,700 m, standing 1,706 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Sonnenlift Axams through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 955 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 13 cm and 33 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 11 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 21 cm). Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 24 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and early May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 18 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.
