    About Vals

    Vals is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 395 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,282 m up to 2,849 m. The closest other ski area is Obersaxen (15 km, north).

    The mountain at Vals

    From top to bottom that makes 1,567 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,849 m.
