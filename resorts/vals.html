    About Vals

    Vals is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 395 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,282 m up to 2,849 m. The closest other ski area is Obersaxen (15 km, north).

    The mountain at Vals

    From top to bottom that makes 1,567 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,849 m. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 3 T-bars. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Piz Terri, 3,149 m high and 7.6 km west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,066 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 November and keeps it until about 29 May, roughly 27 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 27 March, with about 118 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 114 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 71 cm and 147 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 64 cm; a snowy December gave 98 cm, a dry one 32 cm.

    Runs by difficulty

    Vals has 14 graded runs adding up to about 15 km. Most of the length is red (54%, about 8.4 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 3.5 km of black or expert runs.

    Questions about Vals

    What is the best month to ski at Vals?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Vals at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 64 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Vals?

    The top is at 2,849 m and the base at 1,282 m.

    What level of skier is Vals best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 54% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Vals?

    Obersaxen, 15 km to the north, with about 92 km of pistes.
