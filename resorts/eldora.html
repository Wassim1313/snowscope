    About Eldora

    Neither huge nor tiny, Eldora offers about 30 km of pistes (number 78 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 2,821 m to 3,232 m. The closest other ski area is Winter Park Resort (18 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 67 graded runs, about 30 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (54%, about 16 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 11 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Eldora

    The vertical drop is 411 m: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,821 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 7 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Skyscraper Peak (3,774 m), 7.9 km to the west, about 542 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Eldora through a normal winter

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 3,026 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 97 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 43 cm and 111 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 52 cm (lean years 17 cm, snowy years 75 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 79 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Eldora include Caribou Lodge (mountain hut), Tennessee Mountain Cabin (mountain hut), The Boulder Creek Lodge (hotel) and Sundance Lodge building 2 (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Eldora?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Eldora at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 52 cm deep.

    How high is Eldora?

    The top is at 3,232 m and the base at 2,821 m.

    Is Eldora good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 11 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How big is Eldora?

    About 30 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
