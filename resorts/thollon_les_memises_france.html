    Snow at Thollon-les-Memises through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 715 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 18 cm, around 13 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 22 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 3 cm to 31 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Thollon-les-Memises

    What is the best month to ski at Thollon-les-Memises?

    Can you ski at Thollon-les-Memises at Christmas?
