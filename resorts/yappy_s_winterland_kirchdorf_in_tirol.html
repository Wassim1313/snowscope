    Lifts and terrain

    60 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 641 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: KitzSki, for one, climbs to 1,993 m. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Maukspitze (2,231 m), 7.4 km to the west, about 1,530 m higher than the top lift.
