    About Blizzard Mountain

    About 6.0 km of pistes make Blizzard Mountain one of the smaller places to ski in US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,703 m up to 1,934 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Blizzard Mountain has 11 graded runs adding up to about 5.6 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 2.2 km, or 39%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.3 km.

    Blizzard Mountain today and the next days

    Up high, Blizzard Mountain has roughly 13 cm of snow on the ground today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 5 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 231 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,703 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Black Cap (2,294 m), 5.0 km to the west, about 360 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Blizzard Mountain usually gets

    Count on about 12 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,818 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to late March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm, with 60 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 55 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 5 cm to 83 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Blizzard Mountain

    What is the best month to ski at Blizzard Mountain?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to late March.

    Is there snow at Blizzard Mountain at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Blizzard Mountain?

    The top is at 1,934 m and the base at 1,703 m.

    How big is Blizzard Mountain?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
