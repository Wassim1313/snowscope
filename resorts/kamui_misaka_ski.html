    About Kamui Misaka Ski

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Kamui Misaka Ski sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 879 m up to 1,037 m. Its nearest neighbour is Fujiten Snow Resort, 17 km south, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 52 km north-west to Sun Meadows Kiyosato, with about 6.0 km of runs. For higher slopes, Sun Meadows Kiyosato climbs to 1,880 m.

    How Kamui Misaka Ski is laid out

    The vertical drop is 158 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 879 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 4 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Chateraise Ski Valley Nobeyama reaches 1,624 m.

    Questions about Kamui Misaka Ski

    How high is Kamui Misaka Ski?

    The top is at 1,037 m and the base at 879 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Kamui Misaka Ski?

    How big is Kamui Misaka Ski?
