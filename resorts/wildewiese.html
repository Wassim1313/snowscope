    About Wildewiese

    Around 6.0 km of marked runs place Wildewiese among the larger resorts of Central Europe, number 49 by length. Its lifts climb from 527 m to 646 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skipiste "Am Kohlberg", 17 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Wildewiese has 15 graded runs adding up to about 5.7 km. Most of the length is blue (56%, about 3.2 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Wildewiese

    From top to bottom that makes 119 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 527 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 7 lifts break down into 5 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
