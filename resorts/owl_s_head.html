    About Owl's Head

    Owl's Head is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 38 of 93 in US Northeast by length. Skiing here happens between 238 m and 712 m above sea level. There are 5 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mont Sutton, 21 km to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 31 graded runs, about 17 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 7.0 km (41%). With 30% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 5.2 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    474 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 238 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Getting up the mountain: 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 6 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mont Owl's Head (747 m), Mont Bear (668 m) and Mont Sugar Loaf (658 m).

    How much snow Owl's Head usually gets

    Count on about 13 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 475 m in an ordinary winter, from early January to late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 26 cm, with 46 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 30 cm and 77 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 5 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 56 cm.

    Questions about Owl's Head

    When is the best time to ski at Owl's Head?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Is there snow at Owl's Head at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    How high is Owl's Head?

    Between 238 m and 712 m, so 474 m of vertical drop.

    Is Owl's Head good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 5.2 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Owl's Head?

    Bromont Montagne d'Expérience, 37 km to the north-west, with about 46 km of pistes.
