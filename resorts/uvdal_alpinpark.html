    About Uvdal Alpinpark

    Uvdal Alpinpark is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 19 km of pistes put it at number 34 of 262. The ski area spans the mountain from 697 m up to 1,209 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Uvdal Alpinpark, 0.1 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 15 km to ski. Most of the length is green (beginner) (67%, about 10 km). With 81% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 512 m: runs of a moderate length. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,209 m. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 chairlift and 3 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Skinnuten (1,384 m), 7.7 km to the west, about 175 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 953 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 November and lasts to 21 May: about 26 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 85 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 51 cm, with 82 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 59 cm and 108 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 91 cm.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Nordre Brostrud (hotel), Jønndalen Høyfjellseter (chalet) and Imingfjell Turistheim (mountain hut).

    Uvdal Alpinpark: common questions

    When does Uvdal Alpinpark have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Uvdal Alpinpark at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 51 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Uvdal Alpinpark at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,209 m; the lowest starts at 697 m (512 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Uvdal Alpinpark?

    Yes: 81% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    How big is Uvdal Alpinpark?

    About 19 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
