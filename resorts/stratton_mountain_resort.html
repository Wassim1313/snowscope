    About Stratton

    Only 3 of the 93 ski areas of US Northeast are larger than Stratton, which has some 72 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 607 m to 1,183 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Stratton, 0.5 km to the south.

    How Stratton is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 576 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 607 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Stratton hold around 21 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 16 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 2 cm by Saturday.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Mountain Ridge (hotel), Black Bear Lodge (hotel), Mid Mountain Lodge (mountain hut) and Main Base Lodge (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
