    About Stratton

    Only 3 of the 93 ski areas of US Northeast are larger than Stratton, which has some 72 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 607 m to 1,183 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Stratton, 0.5 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 146 graded runs, about 72 km in total. The largest share is green circle, with 30 km (42%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 17 km of difficult runs.

    How Stratton is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 576 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 607 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 17 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 9 chairlifts, 1 drag lift and 6 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 6 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Stratton Mountain (1,194 m), The Pinnacle (769 m) and Mundal Hill (647 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 9 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 895 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to late March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 14 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 41 cm). In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 45 cm.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Mountain Ridge (hotel), Black Bear Lodge (hotel), Mid Mountain Lodge (mountain hut) and Main Base Lodge (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Stratton?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does Stratton have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    When does Stratton open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 25 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Stratton?

    The top is at 1,183 m and the base at 607 m.

    Is Stratton good for beginners?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 30 km, are easy.
