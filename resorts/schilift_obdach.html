    About Schilift Obdach

    Schilift Obdach is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 897 m and 1,176 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Rieseralm (7.5 km, north-west).

    The mountain at Schilift Obdach

    Skied top to bottom, Schilift Obdach gives 279 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 897 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Klippitztörl reaches 1,817 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Geierkogel at 2,185 m, standing 1,009 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Schilift Obdach usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,036 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 30 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 27 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 15 January, with about 14 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Schilift Obdach?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Schilift Obdach at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Schilift Obdach?

    Between 897 m and 1,176 m, so 279 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Schilift Obdach best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Schilift Obdach?

    Klippitztörl, 12 km to the south, with about 13 km of pistes.
