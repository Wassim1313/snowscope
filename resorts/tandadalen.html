    Tandådalen in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Tandådalen (about 741 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 36 cm, with a normal range of 24 cm–47 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 63 cm, between 50 cm and 74 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 66 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    15 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 37 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1987-88 (122 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1995-96 (40 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-December until late April in a normal winter, roughly 140 days.

    This winter so far: 7 cm on 28 November, against a usual 16 cm for the date.

    Common questions

    Can you ski Tandådalen at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 36 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 37 % of 46 winters. Right now: 11 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Tandådalen?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 66 cm). For a sure base, mid-December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Tandådalen get?

    About 66 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 40 cm in the leanest winter (1995-96) and 122 cm in the biggest (1987-88).

    When does Tandådalen have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How high is Tandådalen?

    Can beginners ski at Tandådalen?

    About Tandådalen

    Around 20 km of marked runs place Tandådalen among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 33 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 625 m up to 857 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hundfjället, 2.7 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    232 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 857 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 24 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 8 T-bars, 11 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Stor-Närfjället (931 m), Hundfjället (925 m) and Storfjället (924 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 741 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 December and lasts to 27 April: about 19 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 66 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 44 cm to 85 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 36 cm, with 61 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 50 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.
