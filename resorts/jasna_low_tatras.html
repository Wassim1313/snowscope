    About Jasna Low Tatras

    About 47 km of pistes make Jasna Low Tatras one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Carpathians (number 2). Skiing here happens between 943 m and 2,003 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skicentrum Pavčina Lehota, 8.4 km to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,060 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 943 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
