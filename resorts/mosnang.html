    About Mosnang

    Compact and local, Mosnang has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 721 m up to 833 m. Gähwil is the next resort, 4.7 km away to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.6 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (58%, about 0.9 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Mosnang gives 112 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 921 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Atzmännig, reach 1,194 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Tweralpspitz at 1,332 m, standing 499 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Mosnang through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 777 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for early January and late January. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 12 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 30 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 25 cm in the best years.

    Mosnang: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Mosnang?

    Aim for early January and late January.

    Is there snow at Mosnang at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Mosnang at?

    Between 721 m and 833 m, so 112 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Mosnang?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Mosnang?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
