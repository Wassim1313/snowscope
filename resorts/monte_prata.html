    About Monte Prata

    Monte Prata is a small ski area by the standards of the Apennines, with about 3.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,558 m to 1,784 m. The closest other ski area is Monte Prata (0.4 km, west).

    The mountain at Monte Prata

    The vertical drop is 226 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,558 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 1,784 m is the second highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.3 km north-west to Area Sciistica Ussita Frontignan, with about 18 km of runs.

    Quick answers

    How high is Monte Prata?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,784 m; the lowest starts at 1,558 m (226 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Monte Prata?

    Area Sciistica Ussita Frontignan, 5.3 km to the north-west, with about 18 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Monte Prata have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
