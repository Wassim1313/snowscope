    The snow at Kläppen, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Kläppen (510 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 31 cm; half of the winters sit between 19 cm and 44 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 58 cm, between 45 cm and 66 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 59 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    9 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 24 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1987-88, peaking at 116 cm. Worst: 1991-92, which never passed 32 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-December until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 126 days.

    So far this winter: 7 cm on 27 November (usual: 12 cm).

    Around 27 km of marked runs place Kläppen among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 19 by length. Its lifts climb from 355 m to 665 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lindvallen, 18 km north-west, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Kläppen has roughly 11 cm of snow on the ground today. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 18 cm by now.

    The mountain at Kläppen

    Skied top to bottom, Kläppen gives 310 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Högfjället, for one, climbs to 886 m. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 7 T-bars, 9 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Skärhön (696 m), Norra Klevklitten (593 m) and Hemberget (577 m).

    How much snow Kläppen usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 510 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 December and keeps it until about 18 April, roughly 17 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 35 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 53 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 59 cm. Most winters land between 34 cm and 80 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March.
