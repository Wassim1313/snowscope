    About Nesselwang

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Nesselwang sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 907 m and reach 1,468 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Buron (3.5 km, west).

    How much snow Nesselwang usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,188 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 January and keeps it until about 21 April, roughly 14 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 38 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 21 cm and 68 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 24 cm (lean years 8 cm, snowy years 40 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 40 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-April.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 7.2 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 5.4 km (75%). With 75% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Nesselwang gives 561 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 907 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,468 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 7 lifts break down into 2 chair-gondolas, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Breitenberg, 1,838 m high and 7.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Questions about Nesselwang

    When does Nesselwang have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late April.

    Can you ski at Nesselwang at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    How high is Nesselwang?

    The top is at 1,468 m and the base at 907 m.

    Is Nesselwang good for beginners?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 5.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Nesselwang?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
