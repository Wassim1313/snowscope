    About Foncine le Haut

    Foncine le Haut is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 902 m and 1,030 m above sea level. Chaux-Neuve is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the north-east.

    Foncine le Haut and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 18 km east to L'Abbaye, with about 7.0 km of runs. For more reliable snow, L'Abbaye usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 5 weeks, against 0 here. The highest nearby is L'Abbaye, topping out at 1,435 m.

    The mountain at Foncine le Haut

    From top to bottom that makes 128 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 902 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Les Rousses, for one, climbs to 1,663 m. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 button lift and 2 rope tows. The highest named summit within 8 km is Le Pré d'Haut at 1,274 m, standing 244 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 966 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 8 cm; a snowy December gave 43 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 56 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 21 January, with about 16 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Foncine le Haut: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Foncine le Haut?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Foncine le Haut have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Foncine le Haut?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,030 m; the lowest starts at 902 m (128 m of vertical).

    Is Foncine le Haut suitable for families?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Foncine le Haut?

    L'Abbaye, 18 km to the east, with about 7.0 km of pistes.
