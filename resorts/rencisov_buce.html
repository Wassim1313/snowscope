    About Renčišov

    Renčišov is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 720 m up to 960 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Dubovica, 3.7 km to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    240 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 720 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.

    Renčišov today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Renčišov hold around 6 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.
