    About Lac Blanc

    With roughly 12 km of runs, Lac Blanc sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 856 m up to 1,205 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Le Tanet, 6.2 km to the south.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 349 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 856 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.
