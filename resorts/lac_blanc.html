    About Lac Blanc

    With roughly 12 km of runs, Lac Blanc sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 856 m up to 1,205 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Le Tanet, 6.2 km to the south.

    What there is to ski at Lac Blanc

    Counting every graded piste, there are 31 runs and around 12 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 6.6 km (57%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 349 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 856 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 11 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 7 button lifts and 3 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gazon du Faing at 1,306 m, standing 101 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,030 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 5 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 27 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten), with 17 cm in the best years.

    Quick answers

    When does Lac Blanc have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Lac Blanc at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Lac Blanc?

    Between 856 m and 1,205 m, so 349 m of vertical drop.

    Is Lac Blanc suitable for families?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 4.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Lac Blanc have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
