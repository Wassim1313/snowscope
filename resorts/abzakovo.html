    About Абзаково (Abzakovo)

    Around 15 km of marked runs place Абзаково (Abzakovo) among the larger resorts of Russia, number 11 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 511 m up to 817 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being ГЛЦ Металлург-Магнитогорск, 25 km to the south.

    The pistes of Абзаково (Abzakovo)

    Абзаково (Abzakovo) has 14 graded runs adding up to about 9.9 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.3 km, or 44%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 1.0 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 306 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 511 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 8 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 6 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
