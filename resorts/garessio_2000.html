    About Garessio 2000

    Neither huge nor tiny, Garessio 2000 offers about 12 km of pistes (number 431 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,315 m up to 1,933 m. Its nearest neighbour is Saint Grée di Viola, 6.6 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Garessio 2000

    The piste map lists 13 graded runs, about 12 km in total. Freeride runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 100%. Good skiers will like it here; 100% of the runs are steep. 12 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Garessio 2000 gives 618 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,315 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The skyline is dominated by Cima Ferrarine, 2,249 m high and 7.7 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,624 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 7 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 49 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 20 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 74 cm.

    Garessio 2000: common questions

    When does Garessio 2000 have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Garessio 2000 at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 7 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Garessio 2000?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,933 m; the lowest starts at 1,315 m (618 m of vertical).

    Does Garessio 2000 have steep runs?

    Yes: about 12 km of its graded pistes (100%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Garessio 2000?

    Artesina, 15 km to the west, with about 39 km of pistes.
