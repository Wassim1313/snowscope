    About Rofanlifte Steinberg

    Compact and local, Rofanlifte Steinberg has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,013 m and reach 1,256 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Kienleiten (8.1 km, east).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,134 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 January and keeps it until about 21 April, roughly 16 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 25 cm, with 45 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 26 cm and 70 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 46 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 35 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    The pistes of Rofanlifte Steinberg

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.8 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 1.8 km (100%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    243 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Hochiss (2,299 m), 6.3 km to the south-west, about 1,043 m higher than the top lift.

    Rofanlifte Steinberg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Rofanlifte Steinberg?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    Can you ski at Rofanlifte Steinberg at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Rofanlifte Steinberg?

    The top is at 1,256 m and the base at 1,013 m.

    Can beginners ski at Rofanlifte Steinberg?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Rofanlifte Steinberg?

    Christlum, 8.9 km to the west, with about 15 km of pistes.
