    About Mayrhofen Hippach

    Mayrhofen Hippach is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 113 km of pistes put it at number 42 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 616 m to 2,468 m. Skilift Floiten Ginzling is the next resort, 8.2 km away to the south.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Mayrhofen Hippach gives 1,852 m of descent: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. The lowest slopes are at only 616 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 54 lifts: 2 cable cars, 8 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 17 chairlifts, 6 T-bars, 7 button lifts, 4 rope tows and 9 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 9 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Roßkopf, 2,971 m high and 7.9 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,542 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 December and lasts to 9 May: about 22 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 82 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 54 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm, with 65 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 47 cm and 99 cm by mid-February.

    What there is to ski at Mayrhofen Hippach

    In all, 220 marked runs cover roughly 113 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 53 km, or 47%. No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. Experts can count on some 13 km of difficult runs.

    Mayrhofen Hippach: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Mayrhofen Hippach?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Mayrhofen Hippach at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Mayrhofen Hippach?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    What altitude is Mayrhofen Hippach at?

    Between 616 m and 2,468 m, so 1,852 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Mayrhofen Hippach?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 54 km, are easy.
