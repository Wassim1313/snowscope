    The snow at Mayrhofen Hippach, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Mayrhofen Hippach (about 1,542 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 38 cm, with a normal range of 28 cm–53 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 75 cm (58 cm–87 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 82 cm.

    By 15 December 20 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 33 %.

    1999-00 was the big one (128 cm); 2016-17 the lean one (60 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 173 days.

    Questions about Mayrhofen Hippach

    Does Mayrhofen Hippach have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 38 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 33 % of 46 winters. Right now: 25 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Mayrhofen Hippach?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 82 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Mayrhofen Hippach?

    About 82 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 60 cm in the leanest winter (2016-17) and 128 cm in the biggest (1999-00).

    About Mayrhofen Hippach

    Mayrhofen Hippach is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 113 km of pistes put it at number 42 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 616 m to 2,468 m. Skilift Floiten Ginzling is the next resort, 8.2 km away to the south.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Mayrhofen Hippach gives 1,852 m of descent: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. The lowest slopes are at only 616 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.
