    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,350 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 27 November and keeps it until about 2 May, roughly 23 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and late February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 56 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 78 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 57 cm and 102 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm (lean years 39 cm, snowy years 77 cm).

    Questions about Drop Zone Terrain Park

    What is the best month to ski at Drop Zone Terrain Park?

    Aim for early January and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    How much snow does Drop Zone Terrain Park have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 54 cm deep.
