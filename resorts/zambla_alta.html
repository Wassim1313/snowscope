    Zambla Alta is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 1,232 m to 1,293 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Conca dell'Alben, 2.9 km to the south-west.

    The mountain at Zambla Alta

    From top to bottom that makes 61 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Colere, for one, climbs to 2,174 m.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Zambla Alta include Albergo Ristorante Neve (hotel), Il fienile del nonno (guesthouse), Rifugio Alpe Grem (mountain hut) and Rifugio SABA Ca' Arera (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
