    About Seeberg / Seewiesen

    Seeberg / Seewiesen is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 674 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,158 m and 1,403 m above sea level. Aflenzer Bürgeralm is the next resort, 8.0 km away to the south-west.

    Snow at Seeberg / Seewiesen through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,280 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 December and lasts to 23 April: about 19 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 83 cm. Most winters land between 37 cm and 116 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 40 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 78 cm, a dry one 9 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 43 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 4.8 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.5 km, or 52%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Seeberg / Seewiesen is laid out

    The vertical drop is 245 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Ringkarwand, 2,041 m high and 6.8 km west of the resort.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Seeberg / Seewiesen has roughly 20 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 11 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (22 cm).

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Familien- & Jugendhotel Seetalerhof (hotel), Hotel Restaurant Seeberghof (hotel), Strobl-Hütte (mountain hut) and Kobenkogelhütte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Seeberg / Seewiesen

    When does Seeberg / Seewiesen have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Seeberg / Seewiesen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 40 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Seeberg / Seewiesen at?

    The top is at 1,403 m and the base at 1,158 m.

    Can beginners ski at Seeberg / Seewiesen?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Seeberg / Seewiesen?

    Aflenzer Bürgeralm, 8.0 km to the south-west, with about 18 km of pistes.
