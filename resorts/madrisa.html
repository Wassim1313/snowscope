    About Madrisa

    With some 27 km of runs, Madrisa belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,152 m to 2,615 m. Its nearest neighbour is Selfranga, 5.9 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,463 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,615 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Schildflue at 2,887 m, standing 272 m above the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Madrisa

    Madrisa has 25 graded runs adding up to about 27 km. Most of the length is red (57%, about 16 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.8 km. 2.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow at Madrisa through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,884 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 27 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 27 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 134 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 117 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 84 cm to 160 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 64 cm, from 34 cm in a poor year to 87 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Madrisa

    What is the best month to ski at Madrisa?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Madrisa at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 64 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Madrisa at?

    Between 1,152 m and 2,615 m, so 1,463 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Madrisa best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 57% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Madrisa have?

    About 27 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
