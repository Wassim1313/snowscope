    About Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla

    Around 11 km of marked runs place Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 64 by length. The slopes start at 209 m and reach 356 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk (16 km, south-west).

    What there is to ski at Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla

    Counting every graded piste, there are 23 runs and around 11 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (41%, about 4.6 km). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 1.6 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla

    Skied top to bottom, Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla gives 147 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 1 button lift and 4 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Iso Tuomivaara (387 m), Kuusivaara (342 m) and Latvavaara (342 m).

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 282 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 December and lasts to 1 May: about 20 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 66 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 48 cm and 78 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm, from 23 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 56 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla: common questions

    When does Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 38 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla?

    Its highest lift reaches 356 m; the lowest starts at 209 m (147 m of vertical).

    Is Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla good for beginners?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 5.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
