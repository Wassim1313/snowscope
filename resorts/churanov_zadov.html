    About Churáňov

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Churáňov sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 888 m to 1,080 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lyžařský areál Kobyla, 0.9 km to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.4 km. The largest share is red, with 1.1 km (77%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How Churáňov is laid out

    The vertical drop is 192 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,088 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 button lift.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Nové Hutě: about 3.0 km of pistes, 4.1 km to the south. Nové Hutě has the longer snow season: around 5 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Churáňov.

    Snow at Churáňov through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 984 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 12 cm; a snowy December gave 38 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 5 cm to 60 cm. A normal season tops out near 25 cm, around 11 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and mid-February.

    Questions about Churáňov

    What is the best month to ski at Churáňov?

    Aim for late January and mid-February.

    How much snow does Churáňov have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Churáňov?

    Between 888 m and 1,080 m, so 192 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Churáňov best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 77% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Churáňov?

    Nové Hutě, 4.1 km to the south, with about 3.0 km of pistes.
