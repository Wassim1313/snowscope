    About Méaudre

    Neither huge nor tiny, Méaudre offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 625 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,003 m and 1,573 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Les Coulmes, 2.6 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Méaudre compares nearby

    In this group of 7, Méaudre is number 3 for piste length. The highest nearby is Montagnes de Lans, topping out at 1,789 m.
