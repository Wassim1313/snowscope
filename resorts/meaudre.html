    About Méaudre

    Neither huge nor tiny, Méaudre offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 625 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,003 m and 1,573 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Les Coulmes, 2.6 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Méaudre

    Méaudre has 13 graded runs adding up to about 6.3 km. The largest share is blue, with 4.4 km (70%). With 79% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    570 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 5 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Roche de Méaudre (1,628 m), Crête de Chalimont (1,607 m) and Gros Martel (1,556 m).

    Snow at Méaudre through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,288 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 10 January and keeps it until about 10 March, roughly 9 weeks. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 168 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 56 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 101 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Méaudre

    What is the best month to ski at Méaudre?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to early March.

    Is there snow at Méaudre at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Méaudre?

    The top is at 1,573 m and the base at 1,003 m.

    Is Méaudre suitable for families?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 5.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Méaudre have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
