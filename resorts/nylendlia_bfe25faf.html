    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 189 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to mid-March, about 8 weeks of good cover. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 39 cm, around 19 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 6 cm and 62 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 1 cm in a poor year to 36 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Nylendlia?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Nylendlia at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.
