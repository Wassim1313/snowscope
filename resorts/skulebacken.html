    About Skulebacken

    Neither huge nor tiny, Skulebacken offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 171 of 262 in Scandinavia). Skiing here happens between 38 m and 286 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Bjästabacken, 16 km north-east, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Skulebacken has 7 graded runs adding up to about 1.8 km. The largest share is red, with 0.8 km (44%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Skulebacken usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 162 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 January and keeps it until about 9 April, roughly 10 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 24 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Most winters land between 18 cm and 69 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 7 February, with about 42 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 18 cm; a snowy December gave 39 cm, a dry one 2 cm.

    Skulebacken: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Skulebacken?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to early April.

    Is there snow at Skulebacken at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Skulebacken at?

    Between 38 m and 286 m, so 248 m of vertical drop.

    Is Skulebacken good for beginners?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Skulebacken have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
