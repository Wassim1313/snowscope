    What 46 winters say about Tamarack Resort

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Tamarack Resort (about 6,260 ft (1,908 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 31 in (79 cm) of cover (25 in (63 cm) to 38 in (96 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 53 in (134 cm) (43 in (110 cm)–61 in (154 cm)).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 59 in (149 cm).

    By 15 December 72 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 96 %.

    The deepest winter was 1998-99 (100 in (253 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2000-01 (38 in (97 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to late May, about 182 days.

    Common questions

    Can you ski Tamarack Resort at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 31 in (79 cm) at Christmas, and 96 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 17 in (44 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Tamarack Resort?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 59 in (149 cm)). For a sure base, late November to late May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Tamarack Resort?

    About 59 in (149 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (late February); between 38 in (97 cm) in the leanest winter (2000-01) and 100 in (253 cm) in the biggest (1998-99).
