    About Tamarack Resort

    With some 58 km of runs, Tamarack Resort belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,488 m up to 2,328 m. Little Ski Hill is the next resort, 27 km away to the north.

    Snow at Tamarack Resort through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,908 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 November and lasts to 23 May: about 26 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 97 cm to 183 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 79 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 111 cm, a dry one 48 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 126 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 16 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 149 cm.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 87 runs and around 58 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 24 km (42%). Steep ground is the speciality: 34% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 19 km of difficult runs.

    How Tamarack Resort is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 840 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,488 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 5 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mica Ridge (2,413 m), Lone Tree (2,388 m) and Tamarack Summit (2,338 m).

    Questions about Tamarack Resort

    When does Tamarack Resort have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Tamarack Resort at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 79 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Tamarack Resort?

    The top is at 2,328 m and the base at 1,488 m.

    Does Tamarack Resort have steep runs?

    Yes: about 19 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Tamarack Resort have?

    About 58 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
