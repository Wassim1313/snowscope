    How Montagne de Lure is laid out

    54 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,591 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 4 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Chabanon reaches 2,028 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Signal de Lure, 1,826 m high and 2.0 km north-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,618 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 0 cm (snow lay here in 5 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 20 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 4 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 79 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.
