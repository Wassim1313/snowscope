    About Estación Invernal y de Montaña S

    About 13 km of pistes make Estación Invernal y de Montaña S one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Cantabrian Mountains (number 5). Its lifts climb from 1,680 m to 2,019 m. Its nearest neighbour is Estación Invernal y de Montaña S, 1.0 km north-west, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 28 runs and around 13 km to ski. Most of the length is red (44%, about 5.8 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.6 km.

    How Estación Invernal y de Montaña S is laid out

    The vertical drop is 339 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,680 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,019 m is the highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 3 moving carpets. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts.
