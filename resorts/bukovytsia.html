    About Bukovytsia

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Bukovytsia sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 406 m up to 566 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 1.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.9 km (55%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Bukovytsia gives 160 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 406 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 6 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Slavske reaches 1,233 m. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
