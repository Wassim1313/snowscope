    About Kamui Ski Links

    With some 18 km of runs, Kamui Ski Links belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Skiing here happens between 146 m and 732 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Santa present park, 10 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Kamui Ski Links

    Counting every graded piste, there are 36 runs and around 18 km to ski. Green runs make up the biggest part: 9.3 km, or 50%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Kamui Ski Links is laid out

    586 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 146 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 5 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    Kamui Ski Links: common questions

    How high is Kamui Ski Links?

    Its highest lift reaches 732 m; the lowest starts at 146 m (586 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Kamui Ski Links?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 9.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Kamui Ski Links have?

    About 18 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
