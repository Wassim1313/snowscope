    About Fröhnd

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Fröhnd sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 698 m and 985 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Belchen (6.4 km, north).

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Todtnauberg: about 17 km of pistes, 12 km to the north-east. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. For higher slopes, Todtnauberg climbs to 1,374 m.

    How much snow Fröhnd usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 842 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and late January. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 22 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 21 January, with about 6 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 3 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 18 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 287 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 698 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Todtnauberg reaches 1,374 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Belchen (1,414 m), 6.7 km to the north, about 429 m higher than the top lift.

    Fröhnd: common questions

    When does Fröhnd have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Is there snow at Fröhnd at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 3 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Fröhnd?

    The top is at 985 m and the base at 698 m.

    Can beginners ski at Fröhnd?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 3.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Fröhnd?

    Todtnauberg, 12 km to the north-east, with about 17 km of pistes.
