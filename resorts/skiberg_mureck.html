    About Skiberg Mureck

    Skiberg Mureck is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 235 m and 244 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje, 27 km south-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Skiberg Mureck in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at mid-mountain (about 240 m) stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 9 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 235 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kope reaches 1,535 m. The only lift is a rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Rujev kogel, 412 m high and 5.3 km south-west of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Reineldis (hotel), Koh Wai Paradise Resort (guesthouse), Vina Kauran (guesthouse) and Domačija Kapl (guesthouse).
