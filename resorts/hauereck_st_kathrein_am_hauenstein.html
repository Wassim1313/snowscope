    About Hauereck

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hauereck offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 753 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 996 m and reach 1,302 m at the top. Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa is the next resort, 7.8 km away to the south-east.

    Hauereck and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 10 km north-east to Stuhleck, with about 22 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Stuhleck usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 10 weeks, against 0 here. For higher slopes, Stuhleck climbs to 1,767 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Hauereck has 3 graded runs adding up to about 3.1 km. The largest share is red, with 3.1 km (100%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Hauereck

    306 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 996 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Amundsenhöhe, 1,666 m high and 5.1 km north-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,149 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 30 January. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 36 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 5 cm to 45 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Hauereck: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Hauereck?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at Hauereck at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    What altitude is Hauereck at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,302 m; the lowest starts at 996 m (306 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Hauereck best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hauereck?

    Stuhleck, 10 km to the north-east, with about 22 km of pistes.
