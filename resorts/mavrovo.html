    About Mavrovo

    Mavrovo is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 14 of 51 in the Balkans by length. The slopes start at 1,268 m and reach 1,875 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Хотел Радика, 6.8 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Mavrovo has 23 graded runs adding up to about 14 km. The largest share is red, with 5.8 km (42%). Good skiers will like it here; 34% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 4.8 km of difficult runs.

    How Mavrovo is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 607 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts and 11 button lifts, 14 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
