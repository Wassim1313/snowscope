    About Rifflsee

    Neither huge nor tiny, Rifflsee offers about 18 km of pistes (number 343 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,674 m and 2,804 m above sea level. Pitztaler Gletscher is the next resort, 4.5 km away to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 36 runs and around 18 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 7.8 km (43%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 4.0 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,239 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 111 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 63 cm; a snowy December gave 88 cm, a dry one 37 cm. A normal season tops out near 121 cm, around 24 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 12 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 79 cm to 146 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,130 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,674 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts and 2 button lifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Watzespitze at 3,532 m, standing 728 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Rifflsee

    What is the best month to ski at Rifflsee?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Rifflsee at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 63 cm deep.

    How high is Rifflsee?

    The top is at 2,804 m and the base at 1,674 m.

    Who is Rifflsee best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 43% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rifflsee?

    Sölden, 8.8 km to the east, with about 107 km of pistes.
