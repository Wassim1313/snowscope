    About Passy

    Passy is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 556 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,329 m to 1,722 m. Its nearest neighbour is Les Houches, 7.9 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,526 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. Most winters land between 124 cm and 250 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 101 cm, from 43 cm in a poor year to 158 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 202 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 154 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For about 20 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    The pistes of Passy

    In all, 16 marked runs cover roughly 7.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 3.6 km (47%). With 63% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Passy

    Skied top to bottom, Passy gives 393 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,329 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 3 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Tête à l'Âne (2,804 m), 4.5 km to the north-east, about 1,082 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Passy include Refuge Le Châtelet d'Ayères (mountain hut), Résidence Le Fontenay Mont-Blanc (hotel), Hotel Sabaudia (hotel) and Refuge de Platé (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Passy have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Passy have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 101 cm deep.

    How high is Passy?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,722 m; the lowest starts at 1,329 m (393 m of vertical).

    Is Passy good for beginners?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 4.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Passy?

    Le Grand Massif, 10.0 km to the north-west, with about 174 km of pistes.
