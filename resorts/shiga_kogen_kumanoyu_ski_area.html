    About Shiga Kogen Kumanoyu

    Neither huge nor tiny, Shiga Kogen Kumanoyu offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 130 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 1,680 m and 1,952 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Shiga Kogen Yokoteyama, 1.7 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Shiga Kogen Kumanoyu

    In all, 18 marked runs cover roughly 6.6 km. Most of the length is green (41%, about 2.7 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.8 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    272 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,680 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. All 5 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.
