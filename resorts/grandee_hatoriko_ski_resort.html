    About Grandee Hatoriko

    Grandee Hatoriko is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 11 km of pistes put it at number 55 of 385. Skiing here happens between 955 m and 1,270 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Tenei (7.1 km, north-west).

    What there is to ski at Grandee Hatoriko

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 11 km to ski. Most of the length is beginner (58%, about 6.3 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Grandee Hatoriko is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 315 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 955 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
