    About Balderschwang

    Balderschwang is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 26 km of pistes put it at number 233 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,002 m up to 1,487 m. Riedbergerhorn is the next resort, 1.9 km away to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 53 graded runs, about 26 km in total. Most of the length is blue (56%, about 14 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,244 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early May, about 18 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 55 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 27 cm and 86 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 37 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 28 cm, with 47 cm in the best years.

    How Balderschwang is laid out

    485 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 6 T-bars and 2 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Hochgrat, 1,834 m high and 4.9 km north-west of the resort.

    Questions about Balderschwang

    When does Balderschwang have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early May.

    Is there snow at Balderschwang at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 28 cm deep.

    What altitude is Balderschwang at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,487 m; the lowest starts at 1,002 m (485 m of vertical).

    Is Balderschwang suitable for families?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Balderschwang have?

    About 26 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
