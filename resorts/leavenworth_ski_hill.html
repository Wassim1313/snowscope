    About Leavenworth Ski Hill

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Leavenworth Ski Hill one of the smaller places to ski in US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 438 m up to 519 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Stevens Pass, 34 km to the north-west.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 42 km south-east to Mission Ridge, with about 33 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Stevens Pass does better, with about 29 weeks of a good base instead of 0. The highest nearby is Mission Ridge, topping out at 2,064 m.

    How Leavenworth Ski Hill is laid out

    The vertical drop is 81 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 438 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Mission Ridge, reach 2,064 m. Getting up the mountain: 3 rope tows and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Icicle Ridge South at 2,142 m, standing 1,623 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 478 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 11 cm and 36 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 15 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 8 cm in a poor year to 27 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 23 February.
