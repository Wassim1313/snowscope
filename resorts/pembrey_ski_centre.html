    About Pembrey

    Pembrey has about 0.2 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 11 m up to 32 m.

    The snow right now

    Pembrey has no snow cover yet today.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Pembrey gives 21 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 11 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a button lift.
