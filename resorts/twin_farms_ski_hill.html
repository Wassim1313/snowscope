    About Twin Farms Ski Hill

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Twin Farms Ski Hill sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. The slopes start at 470 m and reach 579 m at the top. Saskadena Six is the next resort, 7.3 km away to the south-east.
