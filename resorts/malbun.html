    About Malbun

    Neither huge nor tiny, Malbun offers about 17 km of pistes (number 349 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,474 m to 1,991 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Brandnertal, 9.4 km to the east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,732 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 27 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 130 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 150 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 74 cm (lean years 32 cm, snowy years 99 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 96 cm to 173 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 517 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,474 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets, 8 lifts in all. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Panüelerkopf, 2,860 m high and 6.3 km south-east of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Malbun?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Malbun have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 74 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Malbun?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,991 m; the lowest starts at 1,474 m (517 m of vertical).

    Is Malbun suitable for families?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 8.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Malbun have?

    About 17 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
