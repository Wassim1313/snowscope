    About Malbun

    Neither huge nor tiny, Malbun offers about 17 km of pistes (number 349 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,474 m to 1,991 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Brandnertal, 9.4 km to the east.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 517 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,474 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
