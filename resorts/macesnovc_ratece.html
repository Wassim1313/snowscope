    About Macesnovc Rateče

    Macesnovc Rateče is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 862 m and reach 1,179 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kranjska Gora, 2.6 km to the east.

    How Macesnovc Rateče compares nearby

    For more terrain, Tarvisio (14 km west) has about 27 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. For higher slopes, Sella Nevea climbs to 2,137 m.

    How Macesnovc Rateče is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Macesnovc Rateče gives 317 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 862 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Sella Nevea, reach 2,137 m.
