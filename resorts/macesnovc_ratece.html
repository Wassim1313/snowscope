    About Macesnovc Rateče

    Macesnovc Rateče is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 862 m and reach 1,179 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kranjska Gora, 2.6 km to the east.

    How Macesnovc Rateče compares nearby

    For more terrain, Tarvisio (14 km west) has about 27 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Sella Nevea does better, with about 12 weeks of a good base instead of 0. For higher slopes, Sella Nevea climbs to 2,137 m.

    How Macesnovc Rateče is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Macesnovc Rateče gives 317 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 862 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Sella Nevea, reach 2,137 m. The only lift is a chairlift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mangart at 2,679 m, standing 1,500 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Macesnovc Rateče through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,020 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 42 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 5 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. A normal season tops out near 19 cm, around 2 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early April and mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 41 cm.

    Questions about Macesnovc Rateče

    When is the best time to ski at Macesnovc Rateče?

    Aim for early April and mid-April.

    Is there snow at Macesnovc Rateče at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Macesnovc Rateče?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,179 m; the lowest starts at 862 m (317 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Macesnovc Rateče best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Macesnovc Rateče?

    Tarvisio, 14 km to the west, with about 27 km of pistes.
