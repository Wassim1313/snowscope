    About Tirolina

    Tirolina is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 3.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 852 m and 1,075 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Tirolina Hinterthiersee, 0.1 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Tirolina usually gets

    At about 964 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 January and lasts to 10 April: about 13 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 22 cm to 80 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 25 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 53 cm). Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 52 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 26 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 3.3 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (51%, about 1.6 km). With 51% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Tirolina today: about 9 cm up high. The last seven days brought 4 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 15 cm by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 9 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 223 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 852 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Großer Traithen, 1,852 m high and 7.6 km north of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Tirolina?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    How much snow does Tirolina have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 25 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Tirolina?

    The top is at 1,075 m and the base at 852 m.

    Is Tirolina good for beginners?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    How big is Tirolina?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
