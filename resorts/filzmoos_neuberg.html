    About Filzmoos-Neuberg

    With roughly 14 km of runs, Filzmoos-Neuberg sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 968 m to 1,585 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Monte Popolo, 7.6 km to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Filzmoos-Neuberg

    The piste map lists 17 graded runs, about 14 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 8.8 km, or 64%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,276 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 December and lasts to 1 May: about 19 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 32 cm and 76 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 32 cm, with 56 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 41 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 64 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    The mountain at Filzmoos-Neuberg

    From top to bottom that makes 617 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 968 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 3 T-bars and 1 button lift. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Above the ski area rises Torstein (2,948 m), 7.6 km to the north-east, about 1,363 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Filzmoos-Neuberg

    What is the best month to ski at Filzmoos-Neuberg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Is there snow at Filzmoos-Neuberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 32 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Filzmoos-Neuberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,585 m; the lowest starts at 968 m (617 m of vertical).

    Is Filzmoos-Neuberg good for beginners?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 8.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Filzmoos-Neuberg?

    Reiteralm, 9.8 km to the south-east, with about 28 km of pistes.
