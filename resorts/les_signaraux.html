    About Les Signaraux

    Compact and local, Les Signaraux has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,280 m to 1,420 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being L'Alpe du Grand Serre, 12 km to the north-east.

    Snow at Les Signaraux through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,350 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 December and keeps it until about 22 April, roughly 18 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 43 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 97 cm, a dry one 4 cm. A normal season tops out near 77 cm, around 15 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 22 cm to 117 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 40 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 2.5 km to ski. Most of the length is red (67%, about 1.6 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Les Signaraux

    140 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Le Sénépy, 1,769 m high and 3.2 km south-west of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Les Signaraux include L'oriel du Sénépy (chalet), Les Aveilles (hotel), La Pouya (mountain hut) and Hôtel Murtel La Mure (hotel).

    Les Signaraux: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Les Signaraux?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Les Signaraux at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 43 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Les Signaraux?

    The top is at 1,420 m and the base at 1,280 m.

    Who is Les Signaraux best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Les Signaraux?

    Villard de Lans, 18 km to the north-west, with about 56 km of pistes.
