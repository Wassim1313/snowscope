    About Slavske

    With some 10 km of runs, Slavske belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 759 m and reach 1,233 m at the top. Slavske is the next resort, 2.1 km away to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 14 graded runs, about 9.6 km in total. The largest share is black, with 4.4 km (46%). Steep ground is the speciality: 46% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 4.4 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 474 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 759 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 5 T-bars and 1 rope tow, 7 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Slavske?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,233 m; the lowest starts at 759 m (474 m of vertical).

    Is Slavske suitable for families?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 3.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Slavske?
