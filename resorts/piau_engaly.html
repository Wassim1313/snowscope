    About Piau-Engaly

    With roughly 32 km of runs, Piau-Engaly sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,762 m up to 2,527 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Saint-Lary, 9.4 km to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 59 runs and around 32 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 21 km, or 65%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,144 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 December and keeps it until about 22 April, roughly 18 weeks. Most winters land between 28 cm and 139 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 50 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 40 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 80 cm). A normal season tops out near 98 cm, around 8 March.

    How Piau-Engaly is laid out

    765 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,762 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 8 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pic Long at 3,192 m, standing 665 m above the top lift.

    Piau-Engaly: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Piau-Engaly?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Piau-Engaly at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 40 cm deep.

    When does Piau-Engaly open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Piau-Engaly at?

    The top is at 2,527 m and the base at 1,762 m.

    Is Piau-Engaly good for beginners?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 23 km, are easy.
