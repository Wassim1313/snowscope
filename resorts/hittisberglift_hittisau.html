    About Hittisberglift Hittisau

    Compact and local, Hittisberglift Hittisau has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 809 m and 996 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Hochhäderich (3.5 km, north).

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Hittisberglift Hittisau hold around 9 cm of snow today. 10 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 11 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (7 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 187 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 809 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau), for one, climbs to 1,704 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Winterstaude at 1,877 m, standing 881 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Hittisberglift Hittisau through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 902 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 7 cm and 46 cm. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 11 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 29 cm, a dry one 1 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 9 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.
