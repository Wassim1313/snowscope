    About Alpe del Nevegal

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Alpe del Nevegal sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,048 m and 1,664 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Piancavallo, 17 km east, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Alpe del Nevegal

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.9 km, or 54%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.3 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Alpe del Nevegal usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,356 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 January and keeps it until about 22 March, roughly 10 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 44 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 16 cm to 87 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 10 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 18 cm; a snowy December gave 50 cm, a dry one 3 cm.

    How Alpe del Nevegal is laid out

    616 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 3 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Col Visentin at 1,776 m, standing 112 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Alpe del Nevegal include Slalom (hotel), Checco Zaino (guesthouse), Albergo Miari (hotel) and Albergo Pineta (hotel).

    Questions about Alpe del Nevegal

    What is the best month to ski at Alpe del Nevegal?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Can you ski at Alpe del Nevegal at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Alpe del Nevegal?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,664 m; the lowest starts at 1,048 m (616 m of vertical).

    Who is Alpe del Nevegal best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 54% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Alpe del Nevegal have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
