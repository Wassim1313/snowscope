    About Torsbustaden

    Torsbustaden has about 0.1 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 224 m and 397 m above sea level. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Torsbustaden alpinsenter, 0.0 km to the north-east.

    Other resorts close by

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 310 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. Most winters land between 39 cm and 103 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 63 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 40 cm (lean years 20 cm, snowy years 68 cm). Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 83 cm.

    The mountain at Torsbustaden

    173 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 5 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The only lift is a T-bar.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 10 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Torsbustaden. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 16 cm at mid-mountain).

    Quick answers

    When does Torsbustaden have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Torsbustaden at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 40 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Torsbustaden?

    Between 224 m and 397 m, so 173 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Torsbustaden?

    About 0.1 km of pistes and 1 lift.
