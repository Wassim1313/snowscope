    About Netschbuehl Eggiwill

    Netschbuehl Eggiwill is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 773 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 716 m and reach 1,014 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Chuderhüsi (4.1 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Netschbuehl Eggiwill has 3 graded runs adding up to about 2.9 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.1 km (73%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Netschbuehl Eggiwill

    Skied top to bottom, Netschbuehl Eggiwill gives 298 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 916 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Marbachegg reaches 1,472 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Pfyffer (1,315 m), 6.9 km to the east, about 301 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 865 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 15 cm, around 9 February. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 18 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 30 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.
