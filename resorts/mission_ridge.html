    What 46 winters say about Mission Ridge

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Mission Ridge (about 5,682 ft (1,732 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 22 in (55 cm), with a normal range of 16 in (40 cm)–35 in (88 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 40 in (101 cm) (30 in (75 cm)–53 in (134 cm)).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 46 in (118 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 48 % of winters, and by 1 January in 72 %.

    1996-97 was the big one (141 in (357 cm)); 1980-81 the lean one (21 in (53 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to late April, about 146 days.

    Questions about Mission Ridge

    Is there snow in Mission Ridge at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 22 in (55 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 72 % of 46 winters. Right now: 11 in (28 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Mission Ridge?

    March: the snow peaks at about 46 in (118 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to late April.

    How snowy is Mission Ridge?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 46 in (118 cm) in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 141 in (357 cm) (1996-97), the thinnest 21 in (53 cm) (1980-81).

    How high do the lifts go at Mission Ridge?

    The top is at 2,064 m and the base at 1,401 m.

    How many km of pistes does Mission Ridge have?

    About 33 km of pistes and 7 lifts.

    About Mission Ridge

    Mission Ridge is a mid-sized ski area with about 33 km of pistes, number 71 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 1,401 m and 2,064 m above sea level.

    How Mission Ridge is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 663 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,401 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
