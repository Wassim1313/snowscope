    About Mission Ridge

    Mission Ridge is a mid-sized ski area with about 33 km of pistes, number 71 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 1,401 m and 2,064 m above sea level.

    How Mission Ridge is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 663 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,401 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 7 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mission Peak (2,081 m), Wenatchee Mountain (2,053 m) and Beehive Mountain (1,383 m).

    What there is to ski at Mission Ridge

    Mission Ridge has 51 graded runs adding up to about 33 km. The largest share is blue square, with 21 km (64%). Intermediate runs make up 64% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 8.8 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Mission Ridge through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,732 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 December and lasts to 28 April: about 21 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 7 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 69 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 118 cm, around 1 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 39 cm and 162 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 55 cm, from 26 cm in a poor year to 111 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When does Mission Ridge have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    Is there snow at Mission Ridge at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 55 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Mission Ridge?

    The top is at 2,064 m and the base at 1,401 m.

    Is Mission Ridge good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 8.8 km of its graded pistes (27%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Mission Ridge have?

    About 33 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
