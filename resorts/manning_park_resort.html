    What 46 winters say about Manning Park Resort

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Manning Park Resort (1,574 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 115 cm, with a normal range of 93 cm–138 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 162 cm; the middle half of winters range from 144 cm to 191 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 185 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 98 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 100 %.

    1998-99 was the big one (309 cm); 2000-01 the lean one (134 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early November until mid-June in a normal winter, roughly 220 days.

    About Manning Park Resort

    Manning Park Resort is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 25 of 53 in Canada West by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,359 m up to 1,789 m.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 110 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Manning Park Resort. The last seven days brought 13 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    How Manning Park Resort is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Manning Park Resort gives 430 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,359 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.
