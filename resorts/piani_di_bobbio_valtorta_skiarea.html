    About Piani di Bobbio

    With roughly 18 km of runs, Piani di Bobbio sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,330 m and 1,860 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Piani di Artavaggio (4.4 km, south-east).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Piani di Bobbio gives 530 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,330 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,860 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Piani di Bobbio today: about 10 cm up high. The last seven days brought 6 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Monday.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rifugio Campelli (hotel), Rifugio Ratti-Cassin (mountain hut), Rifugio Stella (mountain hut) and Rifugio Gran Baita (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
