    About Piani di Bobbio

    With roughly 18 km of runs, Piani di Bobbio sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,330 m and 1,860 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Piani di Artavaggio (4.4 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Piani di Bobbio has 29 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. Most of the length is red (53%, about 9.6 km). Intermediate runs make up 53% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 3.7 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Piani di Bobbio gives 530 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,330 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,860 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 13 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 5 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 5 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Pizzo dei Tre Signori, 2,554 m high and 6.1 km north-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,595 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to early April. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 17 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 11 cm to 79 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 42 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 23 cm, with 59 cm in the best years.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rifugio Campelli (hotel), Rifugio Ratti-Cassin (mountain hut), Rifugio Stella (mountain hut) and Rifugio Gran Baita (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Piani di Bobbio have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    How much snow does Piani di Bobbio have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 23 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Piani di Bobbio at?

    The top is at 1,860 m and the base at 1,330 m.

    Who is Piani di Bobbio best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 53% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Piani di Bobbio?

    About 18 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
