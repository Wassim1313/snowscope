    About Annaberg

    Annaberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 479 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 892 m and 1,293 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben (7.8 km, north-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 18 marked runs cover roughly 9.6 km. Most of the length is blue (79%, about 7.7 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,092 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 9 January and lasts to 21 March: about 10 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 22 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 57 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 10 February, with about 39 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 8 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. Most winters land between 9 cm and 66 cm by mid-February.

    How Annaberg is laid out

    401 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The base at 892 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 2 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets, 8 lifts in all. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Großer Sulzberg (1,400 m), 4.6 km to the south, about 107 m higher than the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Lackenhof (15 km west) has about 15 km of pistes against 10 km here. If snow is the priority, Lackenhof does better, with about 14 weeks of a good base instead of 10. Gemeindealpe Mitterbach reaches 1,622 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Annaberg?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Is there snow at Annaberg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 22 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Annaberg at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,293 m; the lowest starts at 892 m (401 m of vertical).

    Is Annaberg suitable for families?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 7.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Annaberg?

    Lackenhof, 15 km to the west, with about 15 km of pistes.
