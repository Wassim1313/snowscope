    About Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul

    Around 131 km of marked runs place Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 32 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,632 m up to 2,722 m. Crévoux is the next resort, 6.1 km away to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul

    In all, 188 marked runs cover roughly 131 km. Most of the length is blue (50%, about 65 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 1.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul gives 1,090 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,632 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,722 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 38 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 1 chair-gondola, 18 chairlifts, 11 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 5 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Mont Tailland (2,938 m), 3.2 km to the south-west, about 216 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,177 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 November and keeps it until about 17 May, roughly 24 weeks. Most winters land between 67 cm and 150 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 59 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 110 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 122 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 94 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.
