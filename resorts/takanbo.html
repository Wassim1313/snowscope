    About Takanbo

    Neither huge nor tiny, Takanbo offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 267 of 385 in Japan). Its lifts climb from 417 m to 742 m. Its nearest neighbour is Taira, 9.0 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Takanbo compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour: about 9.0 km of pistes, 16 km to the west. For higher slopes, Hida Kawai climbs to 1,213 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 325 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 417 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.
