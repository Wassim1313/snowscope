    About Gantrisch

    Gantrisch is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 745 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,451 m and reach 1,628 m at the top. Gatrisch is the next resort, 2.4 km away to the north-east.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,540 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 December and keeps it until about 20 May, roughly 22 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 45 cm and 121 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 69 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 39 cm; a snowy December gave 64 cm, a dry one 12 cm. A normal season tops out near 88 cm, around 2 March.

    The pistes of Gantrisch

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 3.0 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.0 km, or 67%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Gantrisch

    The vertical drop is 177 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,451 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Ochsen, 2,188 m high and 5.8 km south-east of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Gantrisch (5.0 km east) has about 11 km of pistes against 3.0 km here. Only Gantrisch keeps a solid base longer: about 22 weeks against 22 here.

    Questions about Gantrisch

    When does Gantrisch have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Gantrisch have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Gantrisch?

    The top is at 1,628 m and the base at 1,451 m.

    What level of skier is Gantrisch best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Gantrisch?

    Gantrisch, 5.0 km to the east, with about 11 km of pistes.
