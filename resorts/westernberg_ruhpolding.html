    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 61 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 666 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, for one, climbs to 1,853 m.
