    About Mishima Choei

    Mishima Choei has about 0.2 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 313 m up to 375 m. Its nearest neighbour is Fairy Land Kaneyama, 8.6 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Mishima Choei today and the next days

    Up high, Mishima Choei has roughly 10 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 13 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    The mountain at Mishima Choei

    62 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 313 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Aizu Kogen Daikura, for one, climbs to 1,219 m.
