    About Stacja narciarska Małe Ciche

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Stacja narciarska Małe Ciche sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. Skiing here happens between 875 m and 1,090 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Wysoki Wierch (2.8 km, north-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Stacja narciarska Małe Ciche has 11 graded runs adding up to about 3.9 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 3.9 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Stacja narciarska Małe Ciche is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 215 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 875 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 6 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
