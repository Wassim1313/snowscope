    About Warner Canyon

    Neither huge nor tiny, Warner Canyon offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 130 of 175 in US West). Skiing here happens between 1,744 m and 1,975 m above sea level.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 21 runs and around 9.0 km to ski. Most of the length is black diamond (57%, about 5.1 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 57% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 5.1 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 231 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,744 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a chairlift. Above the ski area rises Stairstep Butte (2,209 m), 5.1 km to the north-east, about 234 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow this week

    Snow at Warner Canyon through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,860 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 December and keeps it until about 6 April, roughly 16 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 15 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. A normal season tops out near 68 cm, around 22 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 35 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 86 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 4 cm to 120 cm.

    Warner Canyon: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Warner Canyon?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early April.

    How much snow does Warner Canyon have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 35 cm deep.

    What altitude is Warner Canyon at?

    Between 1,744 m and 1,975 m, so 231 m of vertical drop.

    Does Warner Canyon have steep runs?

    Yes: about 5.1 km of its graded pistes (57%) are difficult.

    How big is Warner Canyon?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
