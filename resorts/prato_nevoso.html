    About Prato Nevoso

    With roughly 21 km of runs, Prato Nevoso sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,461 m up to 1,921 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Artesina, 2.9 km to the west.

    The pistes of Prato Nevoso

    The piste map lists 40 graded runs, about 21 km in total. Most of the length is blue (48%, about 10 km). No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.1 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 460 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,461 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets, 6 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Monte Mongioie (2,630 m), 7.9 km to the south, about 709 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,691 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 5 February, with about 28 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 47 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 77 cm.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Galassia (hotel), Rifugio Balma (mountain hut), Albergo Miramonti (hotel) and Rifugio FIE Artesina (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Prato Nevoso have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at Prato Nevoso at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 7 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Prato Nevoso?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,921 m; the lowest starts at 1,461 m (460 m of vertical).

    Is Prato Nevoso suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 10 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Prato Nevoso have?

    About 21 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
