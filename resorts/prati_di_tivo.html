    About Prati di Tivo

    Prati di Tivo is a small ski area by the standards of the Apennines, with about 4.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,399 m and reach 1,807 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Prato Selva (6.0 km, north-west).

    Prati di Tivo and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Campo Felice: about 24 km of pistes, 34 km to the south. The highest nearby is Campo Imperatore, topping out at 2,190 m.

    The mountain at Prati di Tivo

    408 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,399 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.
