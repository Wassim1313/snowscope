    About Prati di Tivo

    Prati di Tivo is a small ski area by the standards of the Apennines, with about 4.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,399 m and reach 1,807 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Prato Selva (6.0 km, north-west).

    What there is to ski at Prati di Tivo

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 3.6 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.7 km (75%). With 75% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Prati di Tivo

    408 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,399 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 chair-gondola, 2 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Prati di Tivo: common questions

    What altitude is Prati di Tivo at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,807 m; the lowest starts at 1,399 m (408 m of vertical).

    Is Prati di Tivo suitable for families?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 2.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Prati di Tivo?

    Campo Felice, 34 km to the south, with about 24 km of pistes.

    How big is Prati di Tivo?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
