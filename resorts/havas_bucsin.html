    About Havas Bucsin

    Neither huge nor tiny, Havas Bucsin offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 92 of 312 in the Carpathians). Its lifts climb from 1,189 m to 1,346 m. Bujdosó is the next resort, 0.9 km away to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 2.6 km in total. The largest share is red, with 1.5 km (57%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The mountain at Havas Bucsin

    157 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 3 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Questions about Havas Bucsin

    How high do the lifts go at Havas Bucsin?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,346 m; the lowest starts at 1,189 m (157 m of vertical).

    Is Havas Bucsin suitable for families?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 1.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Havas Bucsin have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
