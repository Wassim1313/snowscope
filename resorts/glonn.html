    About Glonn

    Glonn has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 573 m and 604 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Skilift Waldsportpark Ebersberg, 14 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Glonn

    31 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 573 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Rauher Berg (675 m), Taubenberg (618 m) and Kollmannsbrunnberg (600 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 588 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. The snow is usually deepest around 27 January, with about 5 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Most winters land between 0 cm and 15 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm, with 10 cm in the best years.

    Questions about Glonn

    What is the best month to ski at Glonn?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    How much snow does Glonn have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Glonn?

    Its highest lift reaches 604 m; the lowest starts at 573 m (31 m of vertical).
