    About Grill Hill

    Grill Hill has about 0.4 km of pistes. The slopes start at 128 m and reach 153 m at the top. Storrs Hill is the next resort, 11 km away to the south.

    How much snow Grill Hill usually gets

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 140 m in an ordinary winter, from early February to mid-March. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 11 cm and 65 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm; a snowy December gave 37 cm, a dry one 4 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 36 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 25 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 128 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Dartmouth Skiway reaches 580 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Griggs Mountain at 518 m, standing 365 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Grill Hill

    What is the best month to ski at Grill Hill?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Grill Hill at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Grill Hill?

    Its highest lift reaches 153 m; the lowest starts at 128 m (25 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Grill Hill have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
