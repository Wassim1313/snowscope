    About Bodental

    Bodental is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,008 m and reach 1,149 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Španov vrh, 13 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Krvavec: about 16 km of pistes, 30 km to the south-east. The highest nearby is Krvavec, topping out at 1,963 m.

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Bodental include Berggasthof-Pension Lausegger (hotel), Gasthof Sereinig (guesthouse), Deutscher Peter (guesthouse) and Koča na Ljubelju (mountain hut).
