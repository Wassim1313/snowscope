    About Bodental

    Bodental is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,008 m and reach 1,149 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Španov vrh, 13 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Krvavec: about 16 km of pistes, 30 km to the south-east. For more reliable snow, Krvavec usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 4 weeks, against 0 here. The highest nearby is Krvavec, topping out at 1,963 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 1.5 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.5 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 141 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hochstuhl / Stol at 2,237 m, standing 1,088 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Bodental usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,078 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 54 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 76 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 5 February, with about 28 cm.

    Questions about Bodental

    When does Bodental have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at Bodental at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    How high is Bodental?

    The top is at 1,149 m and the base at 1,008 m.

    Can beginners ski at Bodental?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Bodental?

    Krvavec, 30 km to the south-east, with about 16 km of pistes.
