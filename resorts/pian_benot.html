    About Pian Benot

    Pian Benot is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,628 m up to 2,057 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ala di Stura, 11 km to the north-east.

    How much snow Pian Benot usually gets

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,842 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 32 cm, from 16 cm in a poor year to 63 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 65 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Most winters land between 34 cm and 86 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 45 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Pian Benot gives 429 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,628 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 4 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Punta Sulè at 3,384 m, standing 1,327 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Pian Benot

    When does Pian Benot have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Pian Benot at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 32 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Pian Benot?

    Between 1,628 m and 2,057 m, so 429 m of vertical drop.
