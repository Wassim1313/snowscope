    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Pian Benot gives 429 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,628 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Pian Benot has roughly 33 cm of snow on the ground today. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 16 cm by Monday.
