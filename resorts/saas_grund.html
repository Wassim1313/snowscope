    About Saas Grund

    Neither huge nor tiny, Saas Grund offers about 21 km of pistes (number 302 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,553 m and 3,142 m above sea level. Saas-Balen is the next resort, 3.5 km away to the north-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,589 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,553 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 3,142 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 66 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Saas Grund. 20 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 34 cm by Monday.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Bergrestaurant Kreuzboden (mountain hut), Eden (hotel), Krone (hotel) and Weissmieshütte SAC (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
