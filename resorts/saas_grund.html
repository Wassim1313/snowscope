    About Saas Grund

    Neither huge nor tiny, Saas Grund offers about 21 km of pistes (number 302 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,553 m and 3,142 m above sea level. Saas-Balen is the next resort, 3.5 km away to the north-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,589 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,553 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 3,142 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 2 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Weissmies (4,011 m), 3.8 km to the east, about 869 m higher than the top lift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 37 marked runs cover roughly 20 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 76%. Intermediate runs make up 76% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. 2.0 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,348 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. By mid-April the typical depth is 75 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 77 cm, around 7 April. Most winters land between 45 cm and 94 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 40 cm; a snowy December gave 63 cm, a dry one 25 cm.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Bergrestaurant Kreuzboden (mountain hut), Eden (hotel), Krone (hotel) and Weissmieshütte SAC (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Saas Grund: common questions

    When does Saas Grund have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and early April; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Saas Grund at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 40 cm deep.

    What altitude is Saas Grund at?

    The top is at 3,142 m and the base at 1,553 m.

    What level of skier is Saas Grund best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 76% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Can Saas Grund make its own snow?

    Yes, on about 12 km of its pistes (60%).
