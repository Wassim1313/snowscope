    About Belvedere

    Belvedere is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 40 km of pistes put it at number 146 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,440 m to 2,423 m. Val di Fassa is the next resort, 5.0 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 70 graded runs, about 33 km in total. Most of the length is red (83%, about 27 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.2 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,932 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 December and keeps it until about 26 April, roughly 18 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 80 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 42 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 33 cm; a snowy December gave 63 cm, a dry one 8 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 52 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 983 m: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,440 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Marmolada, for one, climbs to 3,235 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 cable cars, 6 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 20 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Punta Penia at 3,343 m, standing 920 m above the top lift.

    Belvedere: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Belvedere?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Belvedere have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 33 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Belvedere?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Belvedere?

    Between 1,440 m and 2,423 m, so 983 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Belvedere best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 83% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
