    About Komáří vížka

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Komáří vížka sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 643 m and 770 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zadní Telnice, 5.2 km to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 1.4 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 69%. With 69% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    127 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 643 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 3 button lifts, 4 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Komáří vížka: common questions

    How high is Komáří vížka?

    Its highest lift reaches 770 m; the lowest starts at 643 m (127 m of vertical).

    Is Komáří vížka suitable for families?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Komáří vížka?

    Zadní Telnice, 5.2 km to the north-east, with about 7.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Komáří vížka have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
