    About Bystré

    Bystré has about 0.4 km of pistes. The slopes start at 524 m and reach 610 m at the top. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski areál Olešnice na Moravě, 9.8 km to the south.

    Snow at Bystré through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 567 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 24 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 22 January, with about 8 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 3 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 18 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and late January.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 86 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 524 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Bystré?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Can you ski at Bystré at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Bystré?

    Between 524 m and 610 m, so 86 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Bystré?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
