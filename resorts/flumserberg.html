    The snow at Flumserberg, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Flumserberg (about 1,442 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 37 cm, with a normal range of 28 cm–47 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 67 cm; the middle half of winters range from 58 cm to 84 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 76 cm.

    By 15 December 13 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 20 %.

    1998-99 was the big one (151 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (48 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to mid-May (160 days).

    So far this winter: 8 cm on 19 November (usual: 11 cm).

    About Flumserberg

    With some 59 km of runs, Flumserberg belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 667 m to 2,218 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Amden Arvenbüel, 10 km to the north-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,551 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 667 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 2,218 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 24 lifts: 4 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 2 rope tows and 6 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 6 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Magerrain at 2,523 m, standing 305 m above the top lift.

    The pistes of Flumserberg

    In all, 116 marked runs cover roughly 58 km. Most of the length is red (55%, about 31 km). Intermediate runs make up 55% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 8.8 km. 4.8 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow at Flumserberg through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,442 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to mid-May, about 22 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 37 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 15 cm in a poor year to 53 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 60 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 46 cm and 97 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 28 February, with about 76 cm.
