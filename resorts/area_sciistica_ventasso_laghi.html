    About Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi

    Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 641 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,314 m and reach 1,503 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi (10 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi

    Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi has 9 graded runs adding up to about 4.4 km. Most of the length is red (100%, about 4.4 km). Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,408 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 44 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 1 cm (snow lay here in 5 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 15 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 4 cm, around 4 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio (13 km north-west) has about 12 km of pistes against 5.0 km here. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell. The highest nearby is Area Sciisitica Febbio, topping out at 2,060 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    189 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,314 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. All 2 lifts here are button lifts. The skyline is dominated by Monte Casarola, 1,978 m high and 7.9 km south-west of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Ristorante Albergo Lago Calamone (hotel), Albergo Ristorante La Cicogna (hotel), Rifugio Venusta (mountain hut) and Bivacco Le Cadoniche (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    Can you ski at Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 5 winters out of ten, usually about 1 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi?

    Between 1,314 m and 1,503 m, so 189 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi?

    Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio, 13 km to the north-west, with about 12 km of pistes.
