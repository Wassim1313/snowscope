    About Wakkanaishi Komadori

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Wakkanaishi Komadori one of the smaller places to ski in Japan. The slopes start at 5 m and reach 56 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kamiyuchi, 14 km to the south.

    How Wakkanaishi Komadori is laid out

    The vertical drop is 51 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 5 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Oniwaki Ishiyama, for one, climbs to 166 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Wakkanaishi Komadori hold around 15 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 12 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.
