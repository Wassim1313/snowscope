    How Skilift Grünberg is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 72 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Zugspitze reaches 2,627 m. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 3 moving carpets. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Östliche Griesspitze, 2,747 m high and 7.4 km north-east of the resort.

    How much snow Skilift Grünberg usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,067 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 12 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 21 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 33 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 25 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 20 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.
