    About COS Szczyrk

    Around 10 km of marked runs place COS Szczyrk among the larger resorts of the Carpathians, number 13 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 539 m up to 1,244 m. Szczyrk is the next resort, 1.9 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of COS Szczyrk

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 10 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.7 km, or 45%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.9 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, COS Szczyrk gives 705 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. At 539 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Questions about COS Szczyrk

    What altitude is COS Szczyrk at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,244 m; the lowest starts at 539 m (705 m of vertical).

    Is COS Szczyrk suitable for families?

    Yes: 45% of the graded pistes, about 4.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does COS Szczyrk have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
