    About Stubai

    Stubai is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 95 km of pistes put it at number 54 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 959 m and 3,208 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Sonnenbergbahn (0.6 km, north-west).

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 2,249 m of vertical, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Below about 1,159 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 10 gondolas, 10 chairlifts, 11 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 10 moving carpets, 46 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 10 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Habicht (3,277 m), Glättespitze (3,133 m) and Uelasgratspitze (3,038 m).

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,084 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 November and lasts to the end of May: about 27 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 92 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 27 March, with about 94 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 50 cm, from 30 cm in a poor year to 72 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 59 cm to 108 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    The pistes of Stubai

    Stubai has 245 graded runs adding up to about 95 km. Most of the length is blue (45%, about 43 km). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. The steepest pistes add up to about 5.9 km. 6.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Quick answers

    When does Stubai have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Stubai at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 50 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Stubai open for the 2026/27 season?

    It opened on 9 October 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Stubai?

    The top is at 3,208 m and the base at 959 m.

    Is Stubai good for beginners?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 44 km, are easy.
