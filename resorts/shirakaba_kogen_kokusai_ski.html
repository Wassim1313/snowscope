    About Shirakaba Kogen Kokusai Ski

    Shirakaba Kogen Kokusai Ski is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 11 km of pistes put it at number 61 of 385. Skiing here happens between 1,558 m and 1,825 m above sea level. Shirakaba Resort Ikenotaira Snow is the next resort, 3.8 km away to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 10 km in total. The largest share is green, with 7.8 km (75%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Shirakaba Kogen Kokusai Ski is laid out

    267 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,558 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
