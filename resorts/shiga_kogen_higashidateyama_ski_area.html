    About Shiga Kogen Higashidateyama

    Neither huge nor tiny, Shiga Kogen Higashidateyama offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 218 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 1,675 m and 1,985 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Shiga Kogen Takamagahara Mammoth, 0.7 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 3.6 km. The largest share is green, with 2.3 km (63%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Shiga Kogen Higashidateyama is laid out

    The vertical drop is 310 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,675 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The only lift is a gondola. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Shiga Kogen Higashidateyama at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,985 m; the lowest starts at 1,675 m (310 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Shiga Kogen Higashidateyama?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 2.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Shiga Kogen Higashidateyama have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
