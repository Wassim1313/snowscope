    About Gsieser Tal

    Gsieser Tal is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 752 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,226 m to 1,731 m. Its nearest neighbour is Antholz, 9.9 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Gsieser Tal has 4 graded runs adding up to about 3.1 km. Most of the length is blue (86%, about 2.6 km). With 86% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Gsieser Tal today: about 22 cm up high. 18 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (12 cm).

    How Gsieser Tal is laid out

    The vertical drop is 505 m: runs of a moderate length. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Brunnalm / St. Jakob reaches 2,682 m. All 3 lifts here are button lifts. Above the ski area rises Rote Wand - Croda Rossa (2,818 m), 7.8 km to the north, about 1,087 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,478 m in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 42 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 14 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Year to year the snow is steady, with 16 cm to 62 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 34 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Gsieser Tal?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Gsieser Tal have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Gsieser Tal?

    Between 1,226 m and 1,731 m, so 505 m of vertical drop.

    Is Gsieser Tal good for beginners?

    Yes: 86% of the graded pistes, about 2.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Gsieser Tal?

    Drei Zinnen, 19 km to the south-east, with about 101 km of pistes.
