    About Haboro Chomin Skijo

    Haboro Chomin Skijo is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 280 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 39 m up to 197 m. The closest other ski area is Shosanbetsu-mura (24 km, north).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Haboro Chomin Skijo gives 158 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 39 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
