    About Camigliatello Silano

    Neither huge nor tiny, Camigliatello Silano offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 28 of 40 in the Apennines). The slopes start at 1,370 m and reach 1,756 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Fago del Soldato, 3.4 km to the north-west.
