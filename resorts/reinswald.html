    About Reinswald

    With roughly 14 km of runs, Reinswald sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,562 m up to 2,439 m. The closest other ski area is Rittner Horn (12 km, south).

    How Reinswald is laid out

    877 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,562 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,439 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
