    About Reinswald

    With roughly 14 km of runs, Reinswald sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,562 m up to 2,439 m. The closest other ski area is Rittner Horn (12 km, south).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,000 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 December and keeps it until about 24 May, roughly 25 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 87 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 47 cm, from 20 cm in a poor year to 76 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 58 cm and 111 cm. A normal season tops out near 88 cm, around 27 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Runs by difficulty

    Reinswald has 14 graded runs adding up to about 14 km. Most of the length is red (76%, about 11 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.3 km.

    How Reinswald is laid out

    877 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,562 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,439 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 4 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Weißkopf - Punta Valcenai (2,638 m), 7.4 km to the north-east, about 199 m higher than the top lift.

    Reinswald and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Gitschberg Jochtal (22 km north-east) has about 27 km of pistes against 14 km here. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 3 of 7).

    Questions about Reinswald

    What is the best month to ski at Reinswald?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Reinswald at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 47 cm deep.

    How high is Reinswald?

    Between 1,562 m and 2,439 m, so 877 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Reinswald best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 76% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Reinswald?

    Gitschberg Jochtal, 22 km to the north-east, with about 27 km of pistes.
