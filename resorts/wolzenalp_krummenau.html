    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,130 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 January and lasts to 17 April: about 14 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 31 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 39 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 22 cm; a snowy December gave 37 cm, a dry one 7 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 18 cm and 62 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March.

    Questions about Wolzenalp (Krummenau)

    What is the best month to ski at Wolzenalp (Krummenau)?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Wolzenalp (Krummenau) at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 22 cm deep.
