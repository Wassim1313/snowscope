    About Sulzberg

    Sulzberg is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 968 m to 1,003 m. Hagenberg is the next resort, 1.8 km away to the north-west.

    How Sulzberg compares nearby

    For more terrain, Steibis (7.7 km east) has about 16 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Hochhäderich has the longer snow season: around 20 weeks of solid cover, compared with 1 at Sulzberg. The highest nearby is Hochhäderich, topping out at 1,499 m.

    The mountain at Sulzberg

    The vertical drop is 35 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 968 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Fluh (1,391 m), 7.4 km to the east, about 388 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow this week

    Sulzberg has no snow cover yet today. The last seven days brought 2 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 17 cm by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 7 cm at mid-mountain.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 986 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 February and lasts to 15 February: about 1 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 15 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Year to year the snow is steady, with 9 cm to 53 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 19 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 36 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 11 February, with about 30 cm.

    Questions about Sulzberg

    What is the best month to ski at Sulzberg?

    Aim for mid-February and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to mid-February.

    How much snow does Sulzberg have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 19 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Sulzberg at?

    The top is at 1,003 m and the base at 968 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sulzberg?

    Steibis, 7.7 km to the east, with about 16 km of pistes.

    How big is Sulzberg?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
