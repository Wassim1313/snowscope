    About Serlesbahnen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Serlesbahnen offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 632 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 959 m and reach 1,680 m at the top. Schlick 2000 is the next resort, 7.5 km away to the west.

    Serlesbahnen and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Axamer Lizum: about 38 km of pistes, 9.1 km to the north-west. Axamer Lizum reaches 2,334 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How Serlesbahnen is laid out

    The vertical drop is 721 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 959 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Serlesbahnen include Ochsenhütte (hostel), Waldrast Natur Resort (hotel), Alpenstolz (hotel) and Hotel Bergkranz (hotel).
