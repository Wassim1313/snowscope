    About Serlesbahnen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Serlesbahnen offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 632 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 959 m and reach 1,680 m at the top. Schlick 2000 is the next resort, 7.5 km away to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 5.9 km. The largest share is blue, with 3.4 km (57%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Serlesbahnen is laid out

    The vertical drop is 721 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 959 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Kesselspitze, 2,727 m high and 6.0 km south-west of the resort.

    How much snow Serlesbahnen usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,320 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 December and keeps it until about 29 March, roughly 14 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 15 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 27 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 61 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 39 cm to 80 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Serlesbahnen?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late March.

    How much snow does Serlesbahnen have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 32 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Serlesbahnen?

    Between 959 m and 1,680 m, so 721 m of vertical drop.

    Is Serlesbahnen suitable for families?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 3.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Serlesbahnen?

    Axamer Lizum, 9.1 km to the north-west, with about 38 km of pistes.
