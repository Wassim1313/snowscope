    About Großer Arber

    Großer Arber is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 11 km of pistes put it at number 14 of 257. Skiing here happens between 1,049 m and 1,437 m above sea level. Skilift Riedlberg is the next resort, 5.5 km away to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Großer Arber has 19 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. The largest share is red, with 3.7 km (35%). Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 3.4 km of difficult runs.

    How much snow Großer Arber usually gets

    At about 1,243 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 January and lasts to 8 March: about 8 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 20 cm, with 65 cm in the best years. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 8 cm to 108 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 53 cm, around 21 February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Großer Arber gives 388 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 2 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Quick answers

    When does Großer Arber have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    Is there snow at Großer Arber at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Großer Arber at?

    Between 1,049 m and 1,437 m, so 388 m of vertical drop.

    Is Großer Arber good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 3.4 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.

    How big is Großer Arber?

    About 11 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
