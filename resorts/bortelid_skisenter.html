    About Bortelid Skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Bortelid Skisenter offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 120 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 579 m up to 805 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ljosland skisenter, 9.5 km to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Bortelid Skisenter

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 4.5 km to ski. Most of the length is green (beginner) (37%, about 1.7 km). With 72% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Bortelid Skisenter gives 226 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 4 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 6 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Skrivarknuten (986 m), 3.7 km to the north-west, about 181 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Bortelid Skisenter has roughly 14 cm of snow on the ground today. The coming days should add about 12 cm by Sunday. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 19 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    How much snow Bortelid Skisenter usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 692 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to mid-May, about 21 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 117 cm, around 12 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 44 cm (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 77 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 60 cm and 140 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 8 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 98 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Quick answers

    When does Bortelid Skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Bortelid Skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 44 cm deep.

    What altitude is Bortelid Skisenter at?

    Between 579 m and 805 m, so 226 m of vertical drop.

    Is Bortelid Skisenter good for beginners?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Bortelid Skisenter?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
