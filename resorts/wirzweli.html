    About Wirzweli

    Wirzweli is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,216 m up to 1,332 m. Bannalp is the next resort, 7.2 km away to the south-east.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,274 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 December and keeps it until about 12 May, roughly 21 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 39 cm, from 16 cm in a poor year to 53 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Most winters land between 44 cm and 102 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 78 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 58 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Wirzweli and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Klewenalp: about 18 km of pistes, 9.5 km to the east. If snow is the priority, Bannalp does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 21. The highest nearby is Pilatus, topping out at 2,063 m.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Wirzweli today: about 12 cm up high. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 31 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 16 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Wirzweli gives 116 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 4 cable cars, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The skyline is dominated by Rigidalstock, 2,593 m high and 7.8 km south-east of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Bergrestaurant Gummenalp (hotel), Berggasthaus Arviblick (guesthouse), Gruppenhaus Lueg is Tal (guesthouse) and Alphütte Langboden (chalet).

    Wirzweli: common questions

    When does Wirzweli have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Wirzweli at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Wirzweli?

    The top is at 1,332 m and the base at 1,216 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Wirzweli?

    Klewenalp, 9.5 km to the east, with about 18 km of pistes.

    How big is Wirzweli?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
