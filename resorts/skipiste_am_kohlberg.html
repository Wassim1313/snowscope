    About Skipiste "Am Kohlberg"

    Skipiste "Am Kohlberg" has about 0.4 km of pistes. The slopes start at 436 m and reach 510 m at the top. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wildewiese, 17 km to the east.

    The mountain at Skipiste "Am Kohlberg"

    From top to bottom that makes 74 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 436 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Bödefeld, for one, climbs to 799 m. The only lift is a rope tow.

    Skipiste "Am Kohlberg": common questions

    How high is Skipiste "Am Kohlberg"?

    Its highest lift reaches 510 m; the lowest starts at 436 m (74 m of vertical).
