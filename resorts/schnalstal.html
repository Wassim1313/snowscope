    About Schnalstal

    With some 33 km of runs, Schnalstal belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,005 m up to 3,200 m. Ramudla is the next resort, 9.1 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of Schnalstal

    Schnalstal has 50 graded runs adding up to about 32 km. The largest share is red, with 16 km (50%). Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 7.1 km.

    The mountain at Schnalstal

    From top to bottom that makes 1,195 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,005 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 11 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts and 4 drag lifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Weißkugel - Palla Bianca (3,738 m), 5.2 km to the north-west, about 538 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Schnalstal usually gets

    Count on about 30 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,602 m) in an ordinary winter, from early November to late May or later. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 104 cm and 209 cm. A normal season tops out near 172 cm, around 29 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 171 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For about 21 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 93 cm; a snowy December gave 144 cm, a dry one 54 cm.

    Questions about Schnalstal

    When does Schnalstal have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at Schnalstal at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 93 cm deep.

    What altitude is Schnalstal at?

    The top is at 3,200 m and the base at 2,005 m.

    Who is Schnalstal best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Schnalstal have?

    About 33 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
