    About Hübeli

    Compact and local, Hübeli has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 720 m and 832 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wasen, 13 km to the west.

    Hübeli and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 15 km south-east to Heiligkreuz, with about 3.0 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Heiligkreuz does better, with about 19 weeks of a good base instead of 0. Heiligkreuz reaches 1,460 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How Hübeli is laid out

    112 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 720 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Langis, reach 1,506 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Napf at 1,408 m, standing 576 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Hübeli usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 776 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 11 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm; a snowy December gave 23 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 29 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Quick answers

    When does Hübeli have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Hübeli at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 6 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Hübeli at?

    Between 720 m and 832 m, so 112 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Hübeli best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Hübeli?

    Heiligkreuz, 15 km to the south-east, with about 3.0 km of pistes.
