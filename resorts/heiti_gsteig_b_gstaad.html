    About Heiti

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Heiti one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,223 m up to 1,351 m. Glacier 3000 is the next resort, 5.5 km away to the south-west.

    Heiti and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets (12 km south-west) has about 61 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Glacier 3000 does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 11. The highest nearby is Glacier 3000, topping out at 2,931 m.

    What there is to ski at Heiti

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.2 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (59%, about 0.7 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 22 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Heiti. 23 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 23 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 9 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 128 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Glacier 3000 reaches 2,931 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Wildhorn at 3,250 m, standing 1,899 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,287 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to late March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 21 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 24 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 39 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 25 cm and 67 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 46 cm.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Heiti (hotel), Bären (hotel), Sanetsch (hotel) and Ferienlager Heiti (chalet).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Heiti?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Can you ski at Heiti at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Heiti?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,351 m; the lowest starts at 1,223 m (128 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Heiti?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Heiti?

    Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets, 12 km to the south-west, with about 61 km of pistes.
