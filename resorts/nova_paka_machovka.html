    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Nová Paka gives 67 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 432 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Herlíkovice, for one, climbs to 1,005 m.
