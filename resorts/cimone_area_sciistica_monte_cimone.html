    About Cimone

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Cimone sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,458 m and reach 1,674 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Cimoncino, 1.7 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Cimone is laid out

    The vertical drop is 216 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,458 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
