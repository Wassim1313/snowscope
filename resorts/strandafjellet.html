    About Strandafjellet

    Strandafjellet is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 20 km of pistes put it at number 32 of 262. Its lifts climb from 447 m to 1,139 m. The closest other ski area is Strandafjellet (0.2 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Strandafjellet

    Counting every graded piste, there are 22 runs and around 20 km to ski. Most of the length is green (beginner) (55%, about 11 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The mountain at Strandafjellet

    692 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,139 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Dalmannshornet, 1,476 m high and 6.1 km south-west of the resort.

    Snow at Strandafjellet through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 793 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late October to late May or later, about 31 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. The snow is usually deepest around 23 March, with about 294 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 160 cm (lean years 108 cm, snowy years 222 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 157 cm and 343 cm. For about 26 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 291 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Questions about Strandafjellet

    What is the best month to ski at Strandafjellet?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    Can you ski at Strandafjellet at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 160 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Strandafjellet?

    The top is at 1,139 m and the base at 447 m.

    Can beginners ski at Strandafjellet?

    Yes: 70% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Strandafjellet have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
