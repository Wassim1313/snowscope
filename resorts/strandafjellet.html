    About Strandafjellet

    Strandafjellet is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 20 km of pistes put it at number 32 of 262. Its lifts climb from 447 m to 1,139 m. The closest other ski area is Strandafjellet (0.2 km, south).

    The mountain at Strandafjellet

    692 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,139 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
