    Bald Mountain in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Bald Mountain (4,088 ft (1,246 m)), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 22 in (55 cm), with a normal range of 14 in (35 cm)–32 in (82 cm).

    Mid-February typically sees 37 in (93 cm), between 29 in (74 cm) and 45 in (115 cm) in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 44 in (111 cm).

    46 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 70 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1996-97 (96 in (243 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 1980-81 (16 in (41 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to early May, about 151 days.

    So far this winter: 0 in (0 cm) on 26 November (usual: 9.1 in (23 cm)).

    Questions about Bald Mountain

    Is there snow in Bald Mountain at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 22 in (55 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 70 % of 46 winters. Right now: 5.5 in (14 cm).

    What is the best time to ski Bald Mountain?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 44 in (111 cm) in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 96 in (243 cm) (1996-97), the thinnest 16 in (41 cm) (1980-81).

    About Bald Mountain

    Bald Mountain is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 7.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,147 m to 1,344 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 22 marked runs cover roughly 6.4 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 3.8 km, or 59%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,246 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and early February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 39 cm and 136 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 56 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For about 4 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is usually deepest around 28 February, with about 111 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 55 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 19 cm, snowy years 96 cm).

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 14 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Bald Mountain. The last seven days brought 7 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 10 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 33 cm by now.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 197 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bald Mountain at 1,535 m, standing 191 m above the top lift.
