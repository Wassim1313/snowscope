    About Ski Gastein

    With some 74 km of runs, Ski Gastein belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 843 m and reach 2,294 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Graukogel (6.0 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 82 runs and around 74 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 35 km (48%). Intermediate runs make up 48% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 3.3 km of black or expert runs. 1.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,451 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,043 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The lift system counts 23 lifts: 7 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 5 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Silberpfennig, 2,600 m high and 6.1 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,568 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 53 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 87 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 42 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 19 cm in a poor year to 69 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 100 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Lackenbauer (guesthouse), Jausenstation Hartlgut (guesthouse), Stubneralm (mountain hut) and Cesta Grand Aktivhotel (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Gastein?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Ski Gastein at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 42 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Ski Gastein?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Gastein?

    The top is at 2,294 m and the base at 843 m.

    Is Ski Gastein suitable for families?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 34 km, are easy.
