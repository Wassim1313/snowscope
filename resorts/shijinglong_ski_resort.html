    About Shijinglong

    Neither huge nor tiny, Shijinglong offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 53 of 92 in China). Skiing here happens between 526 m and 796 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Yanqing National Alpine Skiing C (14 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 2.5 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (60%, about 1.5 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    The mountain at Shijinglong

    Skied top to bottom, Shijinglong gives 270 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 526 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 796 m is the second highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 4 moving carpets, 7 lifts in all. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.
