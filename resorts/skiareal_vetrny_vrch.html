    About Skiareál Větrný vrch

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Skiareál Větrný vrch sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 653 m to 806 m. Dolní Morava sport & relax resor is the next resort, 2.0 km away to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.5 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.5 km (100%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Skiareál Větrný vrch is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Skiareál Větrný vrch gives 153 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 653 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 2 lifts here are T-bars.
