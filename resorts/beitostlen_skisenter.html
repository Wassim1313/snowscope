    About Beitostølen Skisenter

    Around 11 km of marked runs place Beitostølen Skisenter among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 62 by length. The slopes start at 901 m and reach 1,099 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Alpinsenteret i Raudalen (5.8 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 22 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Most of the length is blue (34%, about 3.7 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Beitostølen Skisenter usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,000 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 November and lasts to the end of May: about 30 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 81 cm; a snowy December gave 111 cm, a dry one 59 cm. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 91 cm and 147 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 122 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 127 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 198 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Bitihorn, 1,607 m high and 7.2 km north-west of the resort.

    Beitostølen Skisenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Beitostølen Skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Beitostølen Skisenter have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 81 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Beitostølen Skisenter?

    The top is at 1,099 m and the base at 901 m.

    Is Beitostølen Skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 7.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Beitostølen Skisenter have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
