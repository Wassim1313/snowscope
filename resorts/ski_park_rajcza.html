    About Ski Park Rajcza

    Ski Park Rajcza is a ski area. The slopes start at 505 m and reach 559 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zwardoń Ski, 7.7 km to the west.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Park Rajcza gives 54 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 505 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Zwardoń Ski, for one, climbs to 953 m.

    Ski Park Rajcza: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Park Rajcza?

    Its highest lift reaches 559 m; the lowest starts at 505 m (54 m of vertical).
