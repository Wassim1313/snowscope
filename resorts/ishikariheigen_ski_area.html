    About Ishikariheigen

    Ishikariheigen is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 38 m and 153 m above sea level. Hokkaido Greenland White Park is the next resort, 25 km away to the south-east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Sapporo Teine: about 14 km of pistes, 31 km to the south-west. Sapporo Teine reaches 986 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The snow right now

    Ishikariheigen has no snow cover yet today. The last seven days brought 14 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 115 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 38 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sapporo Teine, for one, climbs to 986 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Ishikariheigen?

    Its highest lift reaches 153 m; the lowest starts at 38 m (115 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Ishikariheigen?

    Sapporo Teine, 31 km to the south-west, with about 14 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Ishikariheigen have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
