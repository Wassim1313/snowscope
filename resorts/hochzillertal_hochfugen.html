    Hochzillertal-Hochfügen in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Hochzillertal-Hochfügen (1,464 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 31 cm; half of the winters sit between 21 cm and 42 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 53 cm (41 cm–62 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 57 cm.

    By 15 December 7 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 15 %.

    2018-19 was the big one (95 cm); 1989-90 the lean one (41 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to early May, about 160 days.

    So far this winter: 27 cm on 27 November (usual: 16 cm).

    About Hochzillertal-Hochfügen

    Hochzillertal-Hochfügen is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 82 km of pistes put it at number 68 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 553 m to 2,374 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Spieljoch, 7.6 km to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,821 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. At 553 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 2,374 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 6 gondolas, 11 chairlifts, 8 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 4 rope tows and 2 moving carpets, 35 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Dreispitzkopf (2,604 m), 7.5 km to the south-west, about 230 m higher than the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Hochzillertal-Hochfügen

    Hochzillertal-Hochfügen has 167 graded runs adding up to about 82 km. The largest share is red, with 34 km (41%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 17 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,464 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 December and keeps it until about 5 May, roughly 19 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 57 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 33 cm and 70 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 38 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 31 cm, from 14 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one.
