    About Malá Úpa

    With some 5.0 km of runs, Malá Úpa belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The slopes start at 982 m and reach 1,241 m at the top. Ski Vojtěch is the next resort, 0.8 km away to the north.

    What there is to ski at Malá Úpa

    The piste map lists 11 graded runs, about 5.4 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 3.3 km (60%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    259 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 982 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 4 T-bars, 3 button lifts and 1 rope tow. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    What altitude is Malá Úpa at?

    Is Malá Úpa suitable for families?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 3.3 km, are easy.

    How big is Malá Úpa?
