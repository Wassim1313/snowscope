    About Skilift Grün/Maibrunn

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skilift Grün/Maibrunn one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 724 m to 818 m. The closest other ski area is Skilift Kapellenberg (2.4 km, south-east).

    Skilift Grün/Maibrunn and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Geißkopf (20 km south-east) has about 8.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Geißkopf does better, with about 5 weeks of a good base instead of 0. For higher slopes, Geißkopf climbs to 1,108 m.

    The pistes of Skilift Grün/Maibrunn

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.2 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.2 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Skilift Grün/Maibrunn usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 771 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 0 cm in a poor year to 44 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 22 cm, around 14 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 62 cm.

    How Skilift Grün/Maibrunn is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Grün/Maibrunn gives 94 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 724 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Geißkopf, reach 1,108 m. All 8 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Questions about Skilift Grün/Maibrunn

    When does Skilift Grün/Maibrunn have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Skilift Grün/Maibrunn at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilift Grün/Maibrunn?

    Between 724 m and 818 m, so 94 m of vertical drop.

    Is Skilift Grün/Maibrunn good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilift Grün/Maibrunn?

    Geißkopf, 20 km to the south-east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.
