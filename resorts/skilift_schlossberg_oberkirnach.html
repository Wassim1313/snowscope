    About Skilift Schlossberg Oberkirnach

    Compact and local, Skilift Schlossberg Oberkirnach has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 884 m and reach 951 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Skilift Oberer Schlossberg (0.6 km, west).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 918 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 25 cm. For the best snow, aim for late January and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 17 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 7 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Schlossberg Oberkirnach gives 67 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 884 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Kandellifte, for one, climbs to 1,201 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Spitzberg, 1,058 m high and 7.5 km west of the resort.
