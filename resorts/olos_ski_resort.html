    About Olos

    Neither huge nor tiny, Olos offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 99 of 262 in Scandinavia). The slopes start at 331 m and reach 503 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Pallas, 17 km north-east, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Olos

    The piste map lists 30 graded runs, about 6.6 km in total. The largest share is red, with 3.4 km (52%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    When the snow is good

    At about 417 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 November and lasts to 12 May: about 24 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 46 cm, with 61 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 64 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Year to year the snow is steady, with 55 cm to 84 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 69 cm.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Olos has roughly 4 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 31 cm by now.

    The mountain at Olos

    From top to bottom that makes 172 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Olostunturi (470 m), Juuvanrova (429 m) and Tunturivierimaa (370 m).

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Lapland Hotel Olos (hotel), Luppo (chalet), Olosneuvos (chalet) and Torassieppi (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Olos?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Olos at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 46 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Olos?

    Its highest lift reaches 503 m; the lowest starts at 331 m (172 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Olos best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Olos?

    Ylläs, 42 km to the south-east, with about 18 km of pistes.
