    What 46 winters say about Damüls-Mellau

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Damüls-Mellau (about 1,355 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 36 cm, with a normal range of 21 cm–47 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 68 cm (51 cm–84 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 73 cm.

    13 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 24 % by 1 January.

    1998-99 was the big one (153 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (43 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to early May, about 137 days.

    Getting to Damüls-Mellau and what it costs

    Lifts: 25. Pistes: 88 km. Heights: 689 m at the bottom, 2,021 m at the top (1,332 m of vertical).

    How deep does the snow get at Damüls-Mellau?

    About Damüls-Mellau

    Damüls-Mellau is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 88 km of pistes put it at number 60 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 689 m up to 2,021 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Damüls-Mellau-Faschina, 0.4 km to the east.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,332 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Because the lifts start as low as 689 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
