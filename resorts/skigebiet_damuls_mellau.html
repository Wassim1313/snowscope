    About Damüls-Mellau

    Damüls-Mellau is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 88 km of pistes put it at number 60 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 689 m up to 2,021 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Damüls-Mellau-Faschina, 0.4 km to the east.

    The pistes of Damüls-Mellau

    Damüls-Mellau has 167 graded runs adding up to about 84 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 30 km, or 35%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 22 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,332 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Because the lifts start as low as 689 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 13 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 7 moving carpets, 25 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 7 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Zitterklapfen (2,403 m), 7.9 km to the south-east, about 382 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Damüls-Mellau has roughly 18 cm of snow on the ground today. 11 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 42 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 13 cm). The 2026/27 season at Damüls-Mellau is announced to start on 4 December 2026 and to run until 4 April 2027.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,355 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 36 cm (lean years 13 cm, snowy years 58 cm). A normal season tops out near 73 cm, around 3 March. Most winters land between 40 cm and 104 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 43 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    Questions about Damüls-Mellau

    What is the best month to ski at Damüls-Mellau?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Damüls-Mellau have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 36 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Damüls-Mellau?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Damüls-Mellau?

    The top is at 2,021 m and the base at 689 m.

    Does Damüls-Mellau have steep runs?

    Yes: about 22 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.
