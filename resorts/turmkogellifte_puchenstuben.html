    About Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben

    Neither huge nor tiny, Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 728 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 904 m up to 1,137 m. Hochbärneck is the next resort, 5.5 km away to the west.

    The pistes of Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 3.9 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 3.9 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben is laid out

    233 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,104 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hennesteck at 1,334 m, standing 197 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,020 m in an ordinary winter, from early February to mid-March. A normal season tops out near 33 cm, around 13 February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 20 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 50 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 6 cm to 57 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Pension Inge Buder (guesthouse), Hotel Winterbach (hotel), Erlebnisdorf Sulzbichl (hostel) and Melkstatt Hütte (chalet).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    What altitude is Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,137 m; the lowest starts at 904 m (233 m of vertical).

    Is Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Turmkogellifte Puchenstuben?

    Lackenhof, 11 km to the south-west, with about 15 km of pistes.
