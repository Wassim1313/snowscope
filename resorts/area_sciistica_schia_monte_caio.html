    About Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio

    Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 423 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,199 m and 1,488 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi, 13 km south-east, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio

    The piste map lists 14 graded runs, about 12 km in total. Most of the length is red (67%, about 8.3 km). Intermediate runs make up 67% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 2.1 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,344 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 38 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 22 January, with about 3 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 1 cm (snow lay here in 4 winters out of ten), with 12 cm in the best years.

    The mountain at Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio

    289 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Monte Caio (1,575 m), Punta Fegni (1,485 m) and Corno di Caneto (1,425 m).

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio include Normena Residence & Wellness (hotel), Da Berto (hotel) and Ostello di Corniglio (hostel).

    Quick answers

    When does Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March.

    Can you ski at Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 4 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,488 m; the lowest starts at 1,199 m (289 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
