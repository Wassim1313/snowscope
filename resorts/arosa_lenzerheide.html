    About Arosa Lenzerheide

    Arosa Lenzerheide is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 164 km of pistes put it at number 22 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,240 m and reach 2,854 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pradaschier, 4.9 km to the north-west.

    The mountain at Arosa Lenzerheide

    From top to bottom that makes 1,614 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,854 m.
