    The snow at Arosa Lenzerheide, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Arosa Lenzerheide (about 2,047 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 52 cm of cover (39 cm to 68 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 94 cm (70 cm–106 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 99 cm.

    43 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 63 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 2000-01 (173 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2016-17 (72 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until late May in a normal winter, roughly 186 days.

    So far this winter: 34 cm on 23 November (usual: 26 cm).

    Common questions

    Does Arosa Lenzerheide have snow over Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 52 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 63 % of 46 winters. Right now: 30 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Arosa Lenzerheide?

    March: the snow peaks at about 99 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late May.

    How deep does the snow get at Arosa Lenzerheide?

    About 99 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 72 cm in the leanest winter (2016-17) and 173 cm in the biggest (2000-01).

    What altitude is Arosa Lenzerheide at?

    Is Arosa Lenzerheide good for beginners?

    About Arosa Lenzerheide

    Arosa Lenzerheide is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 164 km of pistes put it at number 22 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,240 m and reach 2,854 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pradaschier, 4.9 km to the north-west.

    The mountain at Arosa Lenzerheide

    From top to bottom that makes 1,614 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,854 m. To get up there are 4 cable cars, 1 funicular, 4 gondolas, 18 chairlifts, 15 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 5 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 5 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aroser Rothorn at 2,980 m, standing 126 m above the top lift.

    The pistes of Arosa Lenzerheide

    Arosa Lenzerheide has 294 graded runs adding up to about 164 km. The largest share is blue, with 93 km (57%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 4.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,047 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 99 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 58 cm to 118 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 89 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 52 cm, from 29 cm in a poor year to 78 cm in a big one.
