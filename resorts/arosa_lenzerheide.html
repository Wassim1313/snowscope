    About Arosa Lenzerheide

    Arosa Lenzerheide is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 164 km of pistes put it at number 22 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,240 m and reach 2,854 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pradaschier, 4.9 km to the north-west.

    The mountain at Arosa Lenzerheide

    From top to bottom that makes 1,614 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,854 m. To get up there are 4 cable cars, 1 funicular, 4 gondolas, 18 chairlifts, 15 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 5 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 5 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aroser Rothorn at 2,980 m, standing 126 m above the top lift.

    The pistes of Arosa Lenzerheide

    Arosa Lenzerheide has 293 graded runs adding up to about 164 km. The largest share is blue, with 93 km (57%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 4.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,047 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 99 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 58 cm to 118 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 89 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 52 cm, from 29 cm in a poor year to 78 cm in a big one.

    Arosa Lenzerheide: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Arosa Lenzerheide?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Arosa Lenzerheide have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 52 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Arosa Lenzerheide?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    What altitude is Arosa Lenzerheide at?

    Between 1,240 m and 2,854 m, so 1,614 m of vertical drop.

    Is Arosa Lenzerheide good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 95 km, are easy.
