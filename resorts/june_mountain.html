    About June Mountain

    With roughly 27 km of runs, June Mountain sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The slopes start at 2,309 m and reach 3,089 m at the top. Mammoth Mountain is the next resort, 14 km away to the south-east.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of June Mountain hold around 77 cm of snow today. 60 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 7 cm by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 16 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Runs by difficulty

    June Mountain has 33 graded runs adding up to about 26 km. Most of the length is blue square (47%, about 12 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 8.0 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 780 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 2,309 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 6 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by San Joaquin Mountain, 3,522 m high and 4.7 km south-west of the resort.

    How much snow June Mountain usually gets

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,699 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to late April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 52 cm and 179 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 124 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 60 cm; a snowy December gave 126 cm, a dry one 14 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 72 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 8 weeks.

    June Mountain: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at June Mountain?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    Can you ski at June Mountain at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 60 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at June Mountain?

    The top is at 3,089 m and the base at 2,309 m.

    Does June Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 8.0 km of its graded pistes (31%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does June Mountain have?

    About 27 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
