    About La Bresse

    With some 24 km of runs, La Bresse belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 876 m and 1,347 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is La Bresse, 3.4 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    La Bresse has 45 graded runs adding up to about 23 km. The largest share is blue, with 13 km (55%). With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How La Bresse is laid out

    The vertical drop is 471 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,076 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 15 lifts break down into 6 chairlifts, 7 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Hohneck (1,363 m), Kastelberg (1,350 m) and Rothenbachkopf (1,316 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,112 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 29 January, with about 14 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 5 cm, with 50 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 78 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does La Bresse have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    How much snow does La Bresse have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 5 cm).

    How high is La Bresse?

    Between 876 m and 1,347 m, so 471 m of vertical drop.

    Is La Bresse suitable for families?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    How big is La Bresse?

    About 24 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
