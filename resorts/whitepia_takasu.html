    About WhitePIA Takasu

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, WhitePIA Takasu sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Skiing here happens between 1,066 m and 1,394 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Washigatake, 1.2 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of WhitePIA Takasu

    The piste map lists 16 graded runs, about 6.3 km in total. Most of the length is red (45%, about 2.8 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 1.7 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at WhitePIA Takasu

    The vertical drop is 328 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.

    This week's snow

    Up high, WhitePIA Takasu has roughly 20 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 32 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 22 cm by Sunday.

    Questions about WhitePIA Takasu

    How high do the lifts go at WhitePIA Takasu?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,394 m; the lowest starts at 1,066 m (328 m of vertical).

    Does WhitePIA Takasu have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.7 km of its graded pistes (28%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near WhitePIA Takasu?

    Dynaland, 8.1 km to the north-west, with about 13 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does WhitePIA Takasu have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
