    About Saskadena Six

    Saskadena Six is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 47 of 93 in US Northeast by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 223 m up to 412 m. Its nearest neighbour is Twin Farms Ski Hill, 7.3 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Saskadena Six

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 318 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 February and keeps it until about 21 March, roughly 7 weeks. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Year to year the snow is steady, with 13 cm to 66 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 18 February, with about 38 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 17 cm, with 39 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Saskadena Six gives 189 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 223 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 chairlifts and 1 T-bar. Above the ski area rises Pinnacle (776 m), 6.7 km to the west, about 364 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Saskadena Six

    When is the best time to ski at Saskadena Six?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early February to late March.

    How much snow does Saskadena Six have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Saskadena Six?

    Its highest lift reaches 412 m; the lowest starts at 223 m (189 m of vertical).

    Does Saskadena Six have steep runs?

    Yes: about 2.7 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    How big is Saskadena Six?

    About 10 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
