    About Hallsta Ski

    Hallsta Ski is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 158 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Skiing here happens between 76 m and 320 m above sea level.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 26 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Hallsta Ski. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 11 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 9 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Hallsta Ski gives 244 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Lidberget (375 m), Skällmyrberget (356 m) and Gammbodberget (348 m).

    How much snow Hallsta Ski usually gets

    Count on about 15 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 198 m) in an ordinary winter, from early January to mid-April. By mid-April the typical depth is 26 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 49 cm, around 21 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 26 cm (lean years 8 cm, snowy years 48 cm). Most winters land between 25 cm and 73 cm by mid-February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Hallsta Ski?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Hallsta Ski have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 26 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Hallsta Ski?

    The top is at 320 m and the base at 76 m.

    How big is Hallsta Ski?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
