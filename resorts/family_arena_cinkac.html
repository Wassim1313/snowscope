    About Family aréna Cinkáč

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Family aréna Cinkáč sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 674 m and 794 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skiareál Benecko, 0.6 km to the south-east.

    The pistes of Family aréna Cinkáč

    Snow at Family aréna Cinkáč through a normal winter

    Count on about 6 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 734 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 51 cm, a dry one 2 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 37 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 64 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Family aréna Cinkáč gives 120 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 674 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Family aréna Cinkáč: common questions

    When does Family aréna Cinkáč have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Family aréna Cinkáč have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Family aréna Cinkáč?

    The top is at 794 m and the base at 674 m.

    Can beginners ski at Family aréna Cinkáč?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Family aréna Cinkáč?

    Skiareál Benecko, 0.6 km to the south-east, with about 4.0 km of pistes.
