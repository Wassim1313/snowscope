    About Ski centrum Strachan

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski centrum Strachan offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 166 of 312 in the Carpathians). Skiing here happens between 982 m and 1,128 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Strednica (1.0 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Ski centrum Strachan

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 1.5 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 1.5 km (100%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Ski centrum Strachan is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Ski centrum Strachan gives 146 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,182 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.
