    About Курорт Красная Поляна

    About 26 km of pistes make Курорт Красная Поляна one of the ten biggest ski areas of Russia (number 8). The ski area spans the mountain from 899 m up to 2,283 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Альпика-Сервис, 2.6 km to the east.

    The pistes of Курорт Красная Поляна

    The piste map lists 37 graded runs, about 26 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 14 km, or 52%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. 2.0 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Курорт Красная Поляна is laid out

    1,384 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 899 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 2,283 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 4 gondolas, 7 chairlifts and 2 drag lifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.

    Курорт Красная Поляна: common questions

    What altitude is Курорт Красная Поляна at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,283 m; the lowest starts at 899 m (1,384 m of vertical).

    Is Курорт Красная Поляна suitable for families?

    Yes: 40% of the graded pistes, about 10 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Курорт Красная Поляна have?

    About 26 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
