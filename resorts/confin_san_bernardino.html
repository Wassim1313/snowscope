    About Confin / San Bernardino

    Confin / San Bernardino is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 309 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,640 m to 2,504 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being San Bernadino, 2.6 km to the east.

    What there is to ski at Confin / San Bernardino

    In all, 21 marked runs cover roughly 20 km. Most of the length is red (42%, about 8.4 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 4.1 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    At about 2,072 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 November and lasts to 24 May: about 25 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm (lean years 29 cm, snowy years 88 cm). For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 97 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 89 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 62 cm and 127 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 864 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,640 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,504 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars and 1 rope tow. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. Above the ski area rises Vogelbärg (3,217 m), 7.9 km to the west, about 713 m higher than the top lift.

    Confin / San Bernardino: common questions

    When does Confin / San Bernardino have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Confin / San Bernardino at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 54 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Confin / San Bernardino at?

    The top is at 2,504 m and the base at 1,640 m.

    Who is Confin / San Bernardino best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 42% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Confin / San Bernardino?

    About 20 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
