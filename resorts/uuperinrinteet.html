    About Uuperinrinteet

    Uuperinrinteet is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 12 m to 78 m. The closest other ski area is Mielakan Rinnekeskus (32 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Uuperinrinteet

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 1.5 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 0.6 km, or 37%. Steep ground is the speciality: 37% of the length is graded difficult.

    How much snow Uuperinrinteet usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 45 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to mid-March, about 7 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 34 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 8 cm and 57 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm; a snowy December gave 31 cm, a dry one 1 cm.

    Uuperinrinteet: common questions

    When does Uuperinrinteet have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Uuperinrinteet have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Uuperinrinteet?

    The top is at 78 m and the base at 12 m.

    Does Uuperinrinteet have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.5 km of its graded pistes (37%) are difficult.

    How big is Uuperinrinteet?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
