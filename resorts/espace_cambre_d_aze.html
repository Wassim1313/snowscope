    About Espace Cambre d'Aze

    Neither huge nor tiny, Espace Cambre d'Aze offers about 25 km of pistes (number 26 of 49 in the Pyrenees). Skiing here happens between 1,631 m and 2,377 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is La Quillane (7.5 km, north).

    What there is to ski at Espace Cambre d'Aze

    Espace Cambre d'Aze has 53 graded runs adding up to about 25 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (41%, about 11 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The mountain at Espace Cambre d'Aze

    The vertical drop is 746 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,631 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 15 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 11 drag lifts and 3 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Pic de les Nou Fonts, 2,861 m high and 7.0 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow at Espace Cambre d'Aze through a normal winter

    Count on about 1 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,004 m in an ordinary winter, from early February to early February. By mid-April the typical depth is 9 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. The snow is usually deepest around 3 February, with about 32 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 47 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 67 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    Questions about Espace Cambre d'Aze

    When does Espace Cambre d'Aze have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early February to early February.

    Can you ski at Espace Cambre d'Aze at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    When does Espace Cambre d'Aze open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 14 March 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Espace Cambre d'Aze?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,377 m; the lowest starts at 1,631 m (746 m of vertical).

    Is Espace Cambre d'Aze suitable for families?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.
