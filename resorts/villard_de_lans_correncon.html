    About Villard de Lans

    With some 56 km of runs, Villard de Lans belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,108 m up to 2,055 m. Col de l'Arzelier is the next resort, 4.4 km away to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Villard de Lans

    Villard de Lans has 87 graded runs adding up to about 56 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (34%, about 19 km). With 68% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 947 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,055 m. Its 25 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 6 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 9 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 5 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is La Grande Moucherolle at 2,285 m, standing 230 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Villard de Lans usually gets

    Count on about 12 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,582 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 13 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 118 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 59 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 22 cm; a snowy December gave 107 cm, a dry one 2 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Villard de Lans?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Can you ski at Villard de Lans at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 22 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Villard de Lans?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Villard de Lans?

    The top is at 2,055 m and the base at 1,108 m.

    Can beginners ski at Villard de Lans?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 38 km, are easy.
