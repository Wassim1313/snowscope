    About Zinal / Grimentz

    Around 71 km of marked runs place Zinal / Grimentz among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 78 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,552 m up to 2,873 m. Its nearest neighbour is Vercorin, 8.8 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 105 runs and around 69 km to ski. Most of the length is red (36%, about 25 km). Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 22 km of difficult runs.

    How Zinal / Grimentz is laid out

    1,321 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,552 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 3 cable cars, 3 gondolas, 5 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 8 button lifts and 3 moving carpets, 23 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Sommet Nord (Les Diablons), 3,592 m high and 7.7 km east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,212 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May or later, about 26 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 61 cm and 116 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 98 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm, with 79 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 93 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Questions about Zinal / Grimentz

    When does Zinal / Grimentz have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Zinal / Grimentz have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 54 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Zinal / Grimentz open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 21 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high is Zinal / Grimentz?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,873 m; the lowest starts at 1,552 m (1,321 m of vertical).

    Does Zinal / Grimentz have steep runs?

    Yes: about 22 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.
