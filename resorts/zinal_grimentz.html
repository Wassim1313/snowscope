    About Zinal / Grimentz

    Around 71 km of marked runs place Zinal / Grimentz among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 78 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,552 m up to 2,873 m. Its nearest neighbour is Vercorin, 8.8 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Zinal / Grimentz today and the next days

    Today there are about 65 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Zinal / Grimentz. 23 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 66 cm by Saturday.

    How Zinal / Grimentz is laid out

    1,321 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,552 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
