    About Villnöß

    Villnöß is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,244 m and 1,487 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Brixen Plose, 5.5 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Dolomiti Superski: about 1,100 km of pistes, 17 km to the south-east. For more reliable snow, Dolomiti Superski usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 21 weeks, against 0 here. The highest nearby is Dolomiti Superski, topping out at 3,235 m.

    The mountain at Villnöß

    243 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Dolomiti Superski, for one, climbs to 3,235 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Sas Rigais, 3,025 m high and 4.9 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,366 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 25 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 10 cm to 44 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 8 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 30 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Villnöß

    When is the best time to ski at Villnöß?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    How much snow does Villnöß have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Villnöß?

    The top is at 1,487 m and the base at 1,244 m.

    Is Villnöß suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Villnöß?

    Dolomiti Superski, 17 km to the south-east, with about 1,100 km of pistes.
