    About Le Brassus

    Neither huge nor tiny, Le Brassus offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 659 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,062 m up to 1,366 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being L'Orient, 3.0 km to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Le Brassus

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 4.7 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.3 km, or 49%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 1.1 km of difficult runs.

    How Le Brassus is laid out

    The vertical drop is 304 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 2 lifts in all. Above the ski area rises Mont Tendre (1,679 m), 7.3 km to the east, about 313 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Le Brassus usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,214 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 58 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 26 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 11 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 48 cm). By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Le Brassus?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Le Brassus have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 11 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Le Brassus?

    Between 1,062 m and 1,366 m, so 304 m of vertical drop.

    Is Le Brassus good for beginners?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 2.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Le Brassus have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
