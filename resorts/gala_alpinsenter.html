    About Gålå Alpinsenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gålå Alpinsenter offers about 10 km of pistes (number 75 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 786 m to 1,091 m. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Fefor Høifjellshotell Skibakke &, 7.7 km to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 19 runs and around 10 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 5.5 km, or 53%. With 81% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at Gålå Alpinsenter through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 938 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 November and keeps it until about 21 May, roughly 27 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 73 cm and 122 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. A normal season tops out near 97 cm, around 16 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 63 cm (lean years 41 cm, snowy years 95 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 92 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    The mountain at Gålå Alpinsenter

    From top to bottom that makes 305 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,091 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Valsfjellet (1,148 m), Måsårandin (1,040 m) and Skjerellkampen (1,033 m).

    Questions about Gålå Alpinsenter

    When is the best time to ski at Gålå Alpinsenter?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Gålå Alpinsenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 63 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Gålå Alpinsenter at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,091 m; the lowest starts at 786 m (305 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Gålå Alpinsenter?

    Yes: 81% of the graded pistes, about 8.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Gålå Alpinsenter have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
