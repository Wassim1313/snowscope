    About Familienparadies Kranzberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Familienparadies Kranzberg offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 613 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,021 m up to 1,342 m. Karwendelbahn is the next resort, 2.4 km away to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Familienparadies Kranzberg has 17 graded runs adding up to about 6.1 km. Most of the length is red (68%, about 4.1 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Familienparadies Kranzberg today: about 28 cm up high. The last seven days brought 30 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 13 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 321 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 7 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 5 T-bars and 1 button lift. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Wettersteinwand at 2,482 m, standing 1,140 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Familienparadies Kranzberg usually gets

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,182 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 22 cm, from 7 cm in a poor year to 41 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 27 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 43 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. Most winters land between 23 cm and 67 cm by mid-February.

    Familienparadies Kranzberg: common questions

    When does Familienparadies Kranzberg have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    Can you ski at Familienparadies Kranzberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Familienparadies Kranzberg?

    Between 1,021 m and 1,342 m, so 321 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Familienparadies Kranzberg best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 68% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Familienparadies Kranzberg?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
