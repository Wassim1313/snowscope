    About Schindelberg Linden

    Compact and local, Schindelberg Linden has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 920 m to 1,088 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Heimenschwand, 1.3 km to the south.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Sigriswill-Schwanden (13 km south) has about 12 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. Sigriswill-Schwanden has the longer snow season: around 22 weeks of solid cover, compared with 6 at Schindelberg Linden. For higher slopes, Sigriswill-Schwanden climbs to 1,460 m.

    The pistes of Schindelberg Linden

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.6 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 100%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Schindelberg Linden is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Schindelberg Linden gives 168 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 920 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sigriswill-Schwanden, for one, climbs to 1,460 m. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. Above the ski area rises Bichsel (1,208 m), 7.8 km to the south-east, about 120 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,004 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 January and lasts to 9 March: about 6 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 17 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 20 cm; a snowy December gave 35 cm, a dry one 6 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 35 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 12 cm to 54 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Schindelberg Linden

    When does Schindelberg Linden have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Schindelberg Linden at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Schindelberg Linden?

    The top is at 1,088 m and the base at 920 m.

    Is Schindelberg Linden good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Schindelberg Linden?

    Sigriswill-Schwanden, 13 km to the south, with about 12 km of pistes.
