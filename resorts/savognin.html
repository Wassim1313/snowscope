    About Savognin

    With some 44 km of runs, Savognin belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,592 m and 2,700 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Savognin, 1.1 km to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Savognin

    The piste map lists 56 graded runs, about 44 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 21 km, or 48%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.9 km.

    Snow at Savognin through a normal winter

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,146 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 57 cm and 107 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 48 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 26 cm in a poor year to 77 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 88 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 92 cm, around 26 March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,108 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,592 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 6 T-bars. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Above the ski area rises Piz Forbesch (3,262 m), 6.6 km to the south, about 562 m higher than the top lift.

    Savognin: common questions

    When does Savognin have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Savognin at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 48 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Savognin?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 29 March 2027.

    How high is Savognin?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,700 m; the lowest starts at 1,592 m (1,108 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Savognin?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 21 km, are easy.
