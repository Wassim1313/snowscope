    About Font d'Urle Chaud Clapier

    With roughly 16 km of runs, Font d'Urle Chaud Clapier sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,273 m and reach 1,541 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Station du Col de Rousset, 11 km to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 49 graded runs, about 16 km in total. Most of the length is blue (36%, about 5.9 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 268 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 9 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Serre de Montue at 1,706 m, standing 165 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,407 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to mid-February, about 3 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 114 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 10 cm, with 58 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 34 cm, around 30 January. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Font d'Urle Chaud Clapier?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-February.

    Is there snow at Font d'Urle Chaud Clapier at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    How high is Font d'Urle Chaud Clapier?

    Between 1,273 m and 1,541 m, so 268 m of vertical drop.

    Is Font d'Urle Chaud Clapier suitable for families?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How big is Font d'Urle Chaud Clapier?

    About 16 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
