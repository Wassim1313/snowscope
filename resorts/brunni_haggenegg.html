    About Brunni-Haggenegg

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Brunni-Haggenegg sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,092 m up to 1,500 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skilift Brunni, 1.1 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.
