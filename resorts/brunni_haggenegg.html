    About Brunni-Haggenegg

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Brunni-Haggenegg sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,092 m up to 1,500 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skilift Brunni, 1.1 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 8.7 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.6 km, or 53%. Intermediate runs make up 53% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,296 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 December and lasts to 6 May: about 20 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 65 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 33 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 14 cm, snowy years 48 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 43 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 36 cm and 83 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    How Brunni-Haggenegg is laid out

    The vertical drop is 408 m: runs of a moderate length. All 3 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Grosser Mythen, 1,898 m high and 2.1 km south of the resort.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Brunni-Haggenegg today: about 28 cm up high. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 16 cm at mid-mountain).

    Questions about Brunni-Haggenegg

    When does Brunni-Haggenegg have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Brunni-Haggenegg at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 33 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Brunni-Haggenegg?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,500 m; the lowest starts at 1,092 m (408 m of vertical).

    Is Brunni-Haggenegg suitable for families?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 4.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Brunni-Haggenegg?

    Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue, 2.3 km to the south, with about 27 km of pistes.
