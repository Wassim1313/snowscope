    About St. Margrethenberg

    About 2.0 km of pistes make St. Margrethenberg one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 1,190 m and reach 1,457 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Skilift Mastrils (2.7 km, south-east).

    Snow at St. Margrethenberg through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,324 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 100 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 77 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 40 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 16 cm in a poor year to 55 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 60 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    What there is to ski at St. Margrethenberg

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 2.4 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.3 km, or 57%. With 57% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at St. Margrethenberg

    Skied top to bottom, St. Margrethenberg gives 267 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Rossstall, 2,456 m high and 8.0 km west of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Grand Hotel Quellenhof, Schloss Wartenstein, Schloss Ragaz and Esos Hotel Quelle.

    Quick answers

    When does St. Margrethenberg have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does St. Margrethenberg have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 40 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is St. Margrethenberg at?

    The top is at 1,457 m and the base at 1,190 m.

    Can beginners ski at St. Margrethenberg?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near St. Margrethenberg?

    Pizol, 5.1 km to the west, with about 40 km of pistes.
