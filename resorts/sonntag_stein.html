    About Sonntag Stein

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sonntag Stein offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 634 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,293 m to 1,753 m. Fontanella-Faschina is the next resort, 5.1 km away to the north-west.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,523 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 89 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For about 7 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 74 cm and 151 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 54 cm (lean years 22 cm, snowy years 79 cm). For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 120 cm, around 2 March.

    The pistes of Sonntag Stein

    In all, 18 marked runs cover roughly 6.3 km. The largest share is blue, with 3.7 km (59%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Sonntag Stein is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Sonntag Stein gives 460 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Diedamskopf, for one, climbs to 2,056 m. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Rote Wand (2,703 m), 6.5 km to the south-east, about 950 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Alpen-Chalet Partnom (chalet), Hütte Martina (chalet), Wandfluhhütte (chalet) and Das Schäfer (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Sonntag Stein?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Sonntag Stein have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 54 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Sonntag Stein?

    Between 1,293 m and 1,753 m, so 460 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Sonntag Stein?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 3.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sonntag Stein?

    Damüls-Mellau-Faschina, 8.6 km to the north-west, with about 101 km of pistes.
