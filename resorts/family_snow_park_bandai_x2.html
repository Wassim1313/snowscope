    About Family Snow Park Bandai X2

    Neither huge nor tiny, Family Snow Park Bandai X2 offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 235 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 647 m up to 849 m. The closest other ski area is Inawashiro Snow Paradise (2.4 km, south-west).

    How Family Snow Park Bandai X2 compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 7.0 km west to Hoshino Resorts Nekoma Mountain, with about 23 km of runs. The highest nearby is Hoshino Resorts Nekoma Mountain, topping out at 1,335 m.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Family Snow Park Bandai X2 today: about 27 cm up high. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Family Snow Park Bandai X2 gives 202 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 647 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Grandeco Snow Resort, reach 1,581 m.
