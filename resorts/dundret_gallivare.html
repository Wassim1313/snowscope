    About Dundret / Gallivare

    Dundret / Gallivare is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 12 km of pistes put it at number 57 of 262. The ski area spans the mountain from 425 m up to 758 m.

    The pistes of Dundret / Gallivare

    In all, 24 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. The largest share is blue, with 5.3 km (46%). With 51% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 592 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 November and lasts to 14 May: about 25 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 76 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 54 cm and 91 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 51 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 35 cm in a poor year to 66 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 69 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 333 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 7 drag lifts, 8 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Dundret (821 m), Västtoppen (787 m) and Linbanetoppen (721 m).

    Dundret / Gallivare: common questions

    When does Dundret / Gallivare have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Dundret / Gallivare have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 51 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Dundret / Gallivare?

    Between 425 m and 758 m, so 333 m of vertical drop.

    Is Dundret / Gallivare suitable for families?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 5.8 km, are easy.

    How big is Dundret / Gallivare?

    About 12 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
