    How Lądek-Zdrój is laid out

    122 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 471 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Dolní Morava sport & relax resor, reach 1,232 m. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
