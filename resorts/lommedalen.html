    About Lommedalen

    Lommedalen is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 164 m up to 310 m. The closest other ski area is Skimore Oslo (11 km, east).

    How much snow Lommedalen usually gets

    Count on about 13 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 237 m) in an ordinary winter, from early January to early April. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 21 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 46 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 10 February, with about 55 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 15 cm to 75 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then.

    The mountain at Lommedalen

    From top to bottom that makes 146 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Vidvangshøgda Sør (553 m), 7.6 km to the north, about 243 m higher than the top lift.

    How Lommedalen compares nearby

    Of the 7 ski areas compared here, Lommedalen is the third biggest by piste length. Ringkollen alpinbakke has the longer snow season: around 18 weeks of solid cover, compared with 13 at Lommedalen. The highest nearby is Ringkollen alpinbakke, topping out at 607 m.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Lommedalen include Brunkollen turisthytte (chalet), Sætra (chalet), Gjestehus (hotel) and Svartvannshytta (chalet).

    Questions about Lommedalen

    When is the best time to ski at Lommedalen?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Can you ski at Lommedalen at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 21 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Lommedalen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 310 m; the lowest starts at 164 m (146 m of vertical).

    How big is Lommedalen?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
