    About Le Collet d'Allevard

    With some 25 km of runs, Le Collet d'Allevard belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,421 m and reach 2,084 m at the top. Stade des Neiges du Barioz is the next resort, 9.2 km away to the south-west.

    How much snow Le Collet d'Allevard usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,752 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 November and lasts to 26 May: about 26 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 19 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 136 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 89 cm, with 138 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 174 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 98 cm to 216 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    How Le Collet d'Allevard is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 663 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,421 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 5 drag lifts, 10 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The skyline is dominated by Pic du Frêne, 2,807 m high and 7.1 km south-east of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When does Le Collet d'Allevard have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Le Collet d'Allevard at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 89 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Le Collet d'Allevard?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,084 m; the lowest starts at 1,421 m (663 m of vertical).

    Is Le Collet d'Allevard good for beginners?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    How big is Le Collet d'Allevard?

    About 25 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
