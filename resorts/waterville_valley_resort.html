    About Waterville Valley Resort

    Around 31 km of marked runs place Waterville Valley Resort among the larger resorts of US Northeast, number 23 by length. Its lifts climb from 556 m to 1,163 m. Its nearest neighbour is Campton Mountain, 12 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Waterville Valley Resort

    Waterville Valley Resort has 84 graded runs adding up to about 31 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 16 km, or 52%. Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 11 km.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 860 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 December and lasts to 16 April: about 17 weeks. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 77 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 33 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 54 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 37 cm to 108 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    The mountain at Waterville Valley Resort

    Skied top to bottom, Waterville Valley Resort gives 607 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 556 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 10 lifts break down into 6 chairlifts and 4 T-bars. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Above the ski area rises Mount Osceola (1,315 m), 4.3 km to the north, about 152 m higher than the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    Questions about Waterville Valley Resort

    What is the best month to ski at Waterville Valley Resort?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Waterville Valley Resort at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 32 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Waterville Valley Resort at?

    The top is at 1,163 m and the base at 556 m.

    Does Waterville Valley Resort have steep runs?

    Yes: about 11 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Waterville Valley Resort?

    Loon, 13 km to the north-west, with about 46 km of pistes.
