    About Skizentrum Pfronten

    Neither huge nor tiny, Skizentrum Pfronten offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 721 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 862 m to 1,264 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden, 2.8 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Skizentrum Pfronten today and the next days

    Up high, Skizentrum Pfronten has roughly 24 cm of snow on the ground today. 10 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 11 cm at mid-mountain).

    The mountain at Skizentrum Pfronten

    From top to bottom that makes 402 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The base at 862 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 1 button lift, 3 drag lifts and 2 rope tows. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Gimpel (2,173 m), 8.0 km to the south-east, about 909 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 4 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,063 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early February to early March. Most winters land between 16 cm and 54 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 7 cm in a poor year to 35 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early April. By mid-April the typical depth is 25 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 32 cm.

    Skizentrum Pfronten: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Skizentrum Pfronten?

    Aim for mid-February and early April; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Can you ski at Skizentrum Pfronten at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Skizentrum Pfronten?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,264 m; the lowest starts at 862 m (402 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Skizentrum Pfronten best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Skizentrum Pfronten?

    Füssener Jöchle-Grän, 6.2 km to the south, with about 9.0 km of pistes.
