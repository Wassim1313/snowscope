    About Sahis

    Sahis is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 103 m to 171 m.

    Sahis and the resorts around it

    Its snow is the most reliable of the group: a 30 cm base lasts about 17 weeks in a typical winter, longer than at any neighbour listed.

    How much snow Sahis usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 137 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late April, about 17 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 44 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 29 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 44 cm, a dry one 11 cm. Most winters land between 34 cm and 68 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 55 cm, around 1 March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 68 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Savivuori (187 m), Halkovuori (161 m) and Roviovuori (152 m).

    Questions about Sahis

    What is the best month to ski at Sahis?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Sahis at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 29 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Sahis at?

    The top is at 171 m and the base at 103 m.

    How many km of pistes does Sahis have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
