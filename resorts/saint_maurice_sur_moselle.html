    About Saint-Maurice-sur-Moselle

    Saint-Maurice-sur-Moselle has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,081 m up to 1,142 m. Ballon d'Alsace is the next resort, 2.8 km away to the south.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Saint-Maurice-sur-Moselle gives 61 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Haute Bers, 1,252 m high and 5.6 km east of the resort.

    How much snow Saint-Maurice-sur-Moselle usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,112 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 106 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 29 January, with about 20 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm, with 73 cm in the best years.

    Saint-Maurice-sur-Moselle: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Saint-Maurice-sur-Moselle?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Saint-Maurice-sur-Moselle at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm; the higher slopes do better.
