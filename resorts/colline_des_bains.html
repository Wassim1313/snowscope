    About Colline des Bains

    Colline des Bains is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 740 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,001 m and reach 1,038 m at the top. Villard de Lans is the next resort, 4.8 km away to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 3.4 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (100%, about 3.4 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Colline des Bains

    37 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Villard de Lans reaches 2,055 m. All 3 lifts here are moving carpets. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by La Grande Moucherolle, 2,285 m high and 6.8 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,020 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 30 January, with about 24 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late December and early March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 58 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 63 cm, a dry one 0 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Colline des Bains: common questions

    When does Colline des Bains have the most snow?

    Aim for late December and early March.

    Can you ski at Colline des Bains at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Colline des Bains?

    Between 1,001 m and 1,038 m, so 37 m of vertical drop.

    Is Colline des Bains good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Colline des Bains?

    Villard de Lans, 4.8 km to the south, with about 56 km of pistes.
