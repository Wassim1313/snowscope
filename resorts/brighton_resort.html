    What is the best time to ski Brighton Resort?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 90 in (229 cm)). For a sure base, early November to late May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Brighton Resort?

    About 90 in (229 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early April); between 59 in (151 cm) in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 164 in (417 cm) in the biggest (2022-23).

    When does Brighton Resort have the most snow?

    About Brighton Resort

    Brighton Resort is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 47 km of pistes put it at number 41 of 175. Its lifts climb from 2,666 m to 3,185 m. Its nearest neighbour is Solitude, 2.7 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Brighton Resort

    Counting every graded piste, there are 90 runs and around 47 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 23 km, or 48%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 14 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,926 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 November and lasts to 27 May: about 28 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 121 cm, from 68 cm in a poor year to 222 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 130 cm and 283 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. A normal season tops out near 229 cm, around 1 April.     The mountain at Brighton Resort

    From top to bottom that makes 519 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. At 2,666 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 6 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Twin Peaks (west) at 3,502 m, standing 317 m above the top lift.
