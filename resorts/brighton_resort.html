    The snow at Brighton Resort, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Brighton Resort (about 9,600 ft (2,926 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 48 in (121 cm) of cover (35 in (89 cm) to 66 in (167 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 82 in (209 cm) (62 in (157 cm)–98 in (250 cm)).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 90 in (229 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 89 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 98 %.

    2022-23 was the big one (164 in (417 cm)); 2025-26 the lean one (59 in (151 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs early November to late May (203 days).

    Does Brighton Resort have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 48 in (121 cm) at Christmas, and 98 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 31 in (80 cm).

    What is the best time to ski Brighton Resort?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 90 in (229 cm)). For a sure base, early November to late May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Brighton Resort?

    About 90 in (229 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early April); between 59 in (151 cm) in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 164 in (417 cm) in the biggest (2022-23).
