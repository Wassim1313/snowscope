    About Brighton Resort

    Brighton Resort is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 47 km of pistes put it at number 41 of 175. Its lifts climb from 2,666 m to 3,185 m. Its nearest neighbour is Solitude, 2.7 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Brighton Resort today: about 60 cm up high. 19 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 39 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    The mountain at Brighton Resort

    From top to bottom that makes 519 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. At 2,666 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
