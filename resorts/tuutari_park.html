    About Туутари-Парк (Tuutari-park)

    Туутари-Парк (Tuutari-park) is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 174 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Skiing here happens between 111 m and 173 m above sea level.

    What there is to ski at Туутари-Парк (Tuutari-park)

    The piste map lists 10 graded runs, about 2.5 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.9 km, or 77%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Туутари-Парк (Tuutari-park) is laid out

    The vertical drop is 62 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 5 T-bars and 1 moving carpet, 6 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Ореховая гора (176 m), Кирхгоф (170 m) and Воронья гора (147 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 2 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 142 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early February to late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 36 cm). Most winters land between 11 cm and 52 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for early January and late February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 30 cm.
