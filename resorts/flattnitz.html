    About Flattnitz

    Neither huge nor tiny, Flattnitz offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 543 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,390 m to 1,516 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hochrindl, 8.9 km to the south.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Kreischberg (16 km north) has about 40 km of pistes against 8.0 km here. The highest nearby is Turracher Höhe, topping out at 2,193 m.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Flattnitz hold around 3 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Thursday.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Flattnitz gives 126 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,390 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Turracher Höhe reaches 2,193 m.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hütte Bockbühel (chalet), Hütte Eisenhut (chalet), Alpenhof Ladinig (hotel) and Alpengasthof Isopp (guesthouse).
