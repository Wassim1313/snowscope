    About Flattnitz

    Neither huge nor tiny, Flattnitz offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 543 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,390 m to 1,516 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hochrindl, 8.9 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 8.4 km. The largest share is blue, with 3.9 km (46%). With 33% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 2.8 km of black or expert runs.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Flattnitz hold around 11 cm of snow today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 6 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Flattnitz gives 126 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,390 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Turracher Höhe reaches 2,193 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Eisenhut (2,441 m), 6.3 km to the west, about 925 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Flattnitz usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,453 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 9 cm and 46 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 10 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 28 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 15 cm (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 34 cm).

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hütte Bockbühel (chalet), Hütte Eisenhut (chalet), Alpenhof Ladinig (hotel) and Alpengasthof Isopp (guesthouse).

    Flattnitz: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Flattnitz?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Can you ski at Flattnitz at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 15 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Flattnitz?

    The top is at 1,516 m and the base at 1,390 m.

    Can beginners ski at Flattnitz?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 3.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Flattnitz?

    Kreischberg, 16 km to the north, with about 40 km of pistes.
