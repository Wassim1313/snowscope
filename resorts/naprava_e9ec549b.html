    Snow at Náprava through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 783 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 7 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 31 cm in the best years. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 46 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 18 cm, around 31 January.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Náprava?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Can you ski at Náprava at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 7 cm, so check before you go.
