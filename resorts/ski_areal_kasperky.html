    How much snow Ski areál Kašperky usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 755 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 15 cm, around 1 February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 38 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 7 cm, with 27 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    How Ski areál Kašperky is laid out

    The vertical drop is 32 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 939 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Nové Hutě reaches 1,146 m. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 button lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Ski areál Kašperky?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Ski areál Kašperky at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 7 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Ski areál Kašperky?

    Its highest lift reaches 771 m; the lowest starts at 739 m (32 m of vertical).

    How big is Ski areál Kašperky?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
