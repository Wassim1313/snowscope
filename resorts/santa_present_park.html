    About Santa present park

    Neither huge nor tiny, Santa present park offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 183 of 385 in Japan). The slopes start at 164 m and reach 322 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Inosawa Citizen, 2.9 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Santa present park

    The piste map lists 6 graded runs, about 3.6 km in total. The largest share is green, with 3.6 km (100%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Santa present park is laid out

    158 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 164 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 5 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    Quick answers

    How high is Santa present park?

    Its highest lift reaches 322 m; the lowest starts at 164 m (158 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Santa present park?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.6 km, are easy.

    How big is Santa present park?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
