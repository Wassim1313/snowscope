    About Zao Onsen

    Only 2 of the 385 ski areas of Japan are larger than Zao Onsen, which has some 39 km of runs. The slopes start at 792 m and reach 1,661 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zao Liza World, 4.0 km to the south.

    The pistes of Zao Onsen

    In all, 91 marked runs cover roughly 39 km. Beginner runs make up the biggest part: 16 km, or 41%. With 76% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Lifts and terrain

    869 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 792 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 35 lifts break down into 2 cable cars, 2 gondolas and 31 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.

    Zao Onsen: common questions

    What altitude is Zao Onsen at?

    Between 792 m and 1,661 m, so 869 m of vertical drop.

    Is Zao Onsen good for beginners?

    Yes: 76% of the graded pistes, about 30 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Zao Onsen have?

    About 39 km of pistes and 35 lifts.
