    About Jahodná

    Jahodná is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 130 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. The slopes start at 566 m and reach 704 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Skipark Erika (11 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 2.1 km. The largest share is red, with 1.7 km (82%). Intermediate runs make up 82% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How Jahodná is laid out

    The vertical drop is 138 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 566 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 4 T-bars and 2 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
