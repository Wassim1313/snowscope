    About Евразия (Euroasia)

    Neither huge nor tiny, Евразия (Euroasia) offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 26 of 86 in Russia). Skiing here happens between 329 m and 602 m above sea level.

    What there is to ski at Евразия (Euroasia)

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 6.5 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 2.9 km, or 45%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.
