    About Горнолыжный комплекс "Лисья гора

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Горнолыжный комплекс "Лисья гора sits in the middle of the size table of Russia. Its lifts climb from 136 m to 196 m. Экстремальный парк "Фристайл" is the next resort, 18 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Горнолыжный комплекс "Лисья гора

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 1.8 km to ski. Most of the length is red (39%, about 0.7 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    The mountain at Горнолыжный комплекс "Лисья гора

    Skied top to bottom, Горнолыжный комплекс "Лисья гора gives 60 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 3 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 2 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
