    About Chateraise Ski Valley Nobeyama

    Neither huge nor tiny, Chateraise Ski Valley Nobeyama offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 192 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 1,406 m and 1,624 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Sun Meadows Kiyosato, 8.5 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Chateraise Ski Valley Nobeyama

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 3.5 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 45%. With 31% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 1.1 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Chateraise Ski Valley Nobeyama

    From top to bottom that makes 218 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,406 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.
