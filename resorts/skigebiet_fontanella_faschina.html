    About Fontanella-Faschina

    Fontanella-Faschina is a mid-sized ski area with about 13 km of pistes, number 418 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,449 m up to 1,946 m. The closest other ski area is Damüls (2.9 km, north-west).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,698 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 3 December and keeps it until about 24 May, roughly 25 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. A normal season tops out near 142 cm, around 3 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 60 cm, from 23 cm in a poor year to 93 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 84 cm to 179 cm in mid-February in most winters. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 109 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Fontanella-Faschina gives 497 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,449 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Zitterklapfen (2,403 m), 5.6 km to the east, about 457 m higher than the top lift.

    Fontanella-Faschina: common questions

    When does Fontanella-Faschina have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Fontanella-Faschina have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 60 cm deep.

    What altitude is Fontanella-Faschina at?

    The top is at 1,946 m and the base at 1,449 m.

    Is Fontanella-Faschina good for beginners?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 7.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Fontanella-Faschina?

    Damüls-Mellau-Faschina, 3.6 km to the north, with about 101 km of pistes.
