    About Bardonecchia

    With roughly 20 km of runs, Bardonecchia sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,349 m and reach 2,708 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bardonecchia, 5.2 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Bardonecchia hold around 61 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 57 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (38 cm).

    The mountain at Bardonecchia

    The vertical drop is 1,359 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Starting at 1,349 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,708 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Cima del Vallonetto, 3,217 m high and 7.9 km east of the resort.

    How much snow Bardonecchia usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,028 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 26 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 67 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 102 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 102 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Most winters land between 80 cm and 146 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 123 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Bardonecchia?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Bardonecchia have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 67 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Bardonecchia?

    The top is at 2,708 m and the base at 1,349 m.

    Who is Bardonecchia best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 93% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Bardonecchia?

    Sauze D'Oulx, 13 km to the south-east, with about 57 km of pistes.
