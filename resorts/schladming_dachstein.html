    The snow at Schladming Dachstein, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Schladming Dachstein (1,364 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 31 cm, with a normal range of 19 cm–39 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 55 cm (44 cm–70 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 64 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    9 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 17 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1986-87, peaking at 102 cm. Worst: 2025-26, which never passed 42 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to late April (140 days).

    Schladming Dachstein: quick answers

    Does Schladming Dachstein have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 31 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 17 % of 46 winters. Right now: 19 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Schladming Dachstein?

    February: the snow peaks at about 64 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April.

    How deep does the snow get at Schladming Dachstein?

    About 64 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 42 cm in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 102 cm in the biggest (1986-87).

    How high is Schladming Dachstein?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,004 m; the lowest starts at 724 m (1,280 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Schladming Dachstein have?

    About 179 km of pistes and 73 lifts.

    About Schladming Dachstein

    Schladming Dachstein is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 179 km of pistes put it at number 17 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 724 m up to 2,004 m. Planai & Hochwurzen is the next resort, 1.6 km away to the south-west.

    The mountain at Schladming Dachstein

    The vertical drop is 1,280 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 724 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 2,004 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
