    About Schladming Dachstein

    Schladming Dachstein is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 179 km of pistes put it at number 17 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 724 m up to 2,004 m. Planai & Hochwurzen is the next resort, 1.6 km away to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 320 runs and around 177 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 94 km). With 54% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The mountain at Schladming Dachstein

    The vertical drop is 1,280 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 724 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 2,004 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 cable car, 1 funicular, 13 gondolas, 2 chair-gondolas, 21 chairlifts, 13 T-bars, 11 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 2 rope tows and 8 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 8 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Scheichenspitze, 2,667 m high and 7.9 km north-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,364 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 34 cm and 76 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 37 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 64 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm, from 9 cm in a poor year to 53 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Schladming Dachstein

    When does Schladming Dachstein have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Schladming Dachstein have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 31 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Schladming Dachstein?

    The announced opening day is 20 November 2026.

    How high is Schladming Dachstein?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,004 m; the lowest starts at 724 m (1,280 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Schladming Dachstein?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 95 km, are easy.
