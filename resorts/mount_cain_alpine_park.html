    About Mount Cain Alpine Park

    Mount Cain Alpine Park is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 30 of 53 in Canada West by length. Skiing here happens between 1,190 m and 1,616 m above sea level.

    Mount Cain Alpine Park today and the next days

    Today there are about 89 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Mount Cain Alpine Park. The last seven days brought 64 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 62 cm at mid-mountain.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 25 graded runs, about 14 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 5.1 km (37%). Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.4 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    426 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Mount Abel, 1,822 m high and 2.3 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,403 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 120 cm and 333 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 160 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 83 cm in a poor year to 229 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 25 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and mid-April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 335 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in mid-April at roughly 335 cm.

    Questions about Mount Cain Alpine Park

    When is the best time to ski at Mount Cain Alpine Park?

    Aim for early January and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Mount Cain Alpine Park have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 160 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Mount Cain Alpine Park?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,616 m; the lowest starts at 1,190 m (426 m of vertical).

    Is Mount Cain Alpine Park good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 4.4 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Mount Cain Alpine Park have?

    About 14 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
