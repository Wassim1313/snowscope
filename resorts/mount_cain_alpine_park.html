    Mount Cain Alpine Park in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Mount Cain Alpine Park (about 1,403 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 160 cm; half of the winters sit between 108 cm and 196 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 258 cm; the middle half of winters range from 198 cm to 288 cm.

    The snowiest month is April, when the pack peaks at about 335 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 91 % of winters, and by 1 January in 98 %.

    Best winter on record: 1998-99, peaking at 546 cm. Worst: 2014-15, which never passed 120 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-November to early June (207 days).

    This winter so far: 23 cm on 25 November, against a usual 67 cm for the date.

    Questions about Mount Cain Alpine Park

    Is there snow in Mount Cain Alpine Park at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 160 cm at Christmas, and 98 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 121 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Mount Cain Alpine Park?

    April: the snow peaks at about 335 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to early June.

    How much snow does Mount Cain Alpine Park get?

    Is Mount Cain Alpine Park good for advanced skiers?

    About Mount Cain Alpine Park

    Mount Cain Alpine Park is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 30 of 53 in Canada West by length. Skiing here happens between 1,190 m and 1,616 m above sea level.

    Mount Cain Alpine Park today and the next days

    Today there are about 121 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Mount Cain Alpine Park. 41 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 42 cm by Saturday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 90 cm at mid-mountain.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 25 graded runs, about 14 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 5.1 km (37%). Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.4 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    426 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Mount Abel, 1,822 m high and 2.3 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,403 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 120 cm and 333 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 160 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 83 cm in a poor year to 229 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 25 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and mid-April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 335 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in mid-April at roughly 335 cm.
