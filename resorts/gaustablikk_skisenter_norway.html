    How much snow Gaustablikk Skisenter usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 892 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 November and keeps it until about 23 May, roughly 27 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 121 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 68 cm; a snowy December gave 103 cm, a dry one 41 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 76 cm to 153 cm in mid-February in most winters. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 111 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Questions about Gaustablikk Skisenter

    When is the best time to ski at Gaustablikk Skisenter?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Gaustablikk Skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 68 cm deep.
