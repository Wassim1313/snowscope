    About Loser-Altaussee

    Neither huge nor tiny, Loser-Altaussee offers about 21 km of pistes (number 297 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 854 m to 1,765 m. Its nearest neighbour is Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp, 16 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Loser-Altaussee

    The piste map lists 26 graded runs, about 21 km in total. Most of the length is blue (60%, about 12 km). With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,310 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 December and keeps it until about 1 May, roughly 19 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 44 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 35 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 67 cm, a dry one 7 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 37 cm to 92 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 69 cm, around 26 February.

    How Loser-Altaussee is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 911 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,054 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,765 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 2 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Großer Wildenkogel, 2,093 m high and 6.4 km north of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When does Loser-Altaussee have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Loser-Altaussee at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 35 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Loser-Altaussee?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,765 m; the lowest starts at 854 m (911 m of vertical).

    Is Loser-Altaussee good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Loser-Altaussee?

    Dachstein West, 25 km to the south-west, with about 39 km of pistes.
