    About White Pine Resort

    Neither huge nor tiny, White Pine Resort offers about 13 km of pistes (number 122 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 2,571 m and reach 2,845 m at the top.

    The snow right now

    Up high, White Pine Resort has roughly 55 cm of snow on the ground today. 4 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 38 cm at mid-mountain.

    Runs by difficulty

    White Pine Resort has 29 graded runs adding up to about 13 km. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 5.5 km, or 42%. Good skiers will like it here; 42% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 5.5 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    274 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,571 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Photographers Point (3,076 m), 7.4 km to the north-east, about 231 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at White Pine Resort through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,708 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 November and keeps it until about 24 May, roughly 26 weeks. For about 10 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 123 cm, around 31 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 112 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 79 cm and 139 cm. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 66 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 42 cm in a poor year to 92 cm in a big one.

    Questions about White Pine Resort

    When is the best time to ski at White Pine Resort?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does White Pine Resort have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 66 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at White Pine Resort?

    The top is at 2,845 m and the base at 2,571 m.

    Does White Pine Resort have steep runs?

    Yes: about 5.5 km of its graded pistes (42%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does White Pine Resort have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
