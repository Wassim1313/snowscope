    About Za Nádražím

    Za Nádražím has about 0.2 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 835 m to 860 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski&Bike Špičák, 0.7 km to the north-west.

    How Za Nádražím compares nearby

    Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell. For higher slopes, Ski&Bike Špičák climbs to 1,200 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 848 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 19 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and early February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 34 cm, a dry one 1 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 50 cm.

    How Za Nádražím is laid out

    25 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 835 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Ski&Bike Špičák, reach 1,200 m. The only lift is a button lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Za Nádražím have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Can you ski at Za Nádražím at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Za Nádražím at?

    Between 835 m and 860 m, so 25 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Za Nádražím have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
