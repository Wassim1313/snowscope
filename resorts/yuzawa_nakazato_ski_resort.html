    About Yuzawa Nakazato

    Yuzawa Nakazato is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 160 of 385 in Japan by length. Its lifts climb from 464 m to 698 m. Nakazato Snow Wood is the next resort, 0.8 km away to the south.
