    The snow at Sun Valley, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Sun Valley (about 7,441 ft (2,268 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 30 in (77 cm); half of the winters sit between 19 in (49 cm) and 41 in (104 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 46 in (117 cm) (38 in (97 cm)–59 in (150 cm)).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 54 in (138 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 65 % of winters, and by 1 January in 78 %.

    The deepest winter was 1998-99 (99 in (251 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2000-01 (30 in (75 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs late November to mid-May (171 days).

    Common questions

    Can you ski Sun Valley at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 30 in (77 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 78 % of 46 winters. Right now: 20 in (50 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Sun Valley?

    March: the snow peaks at about 54 in (138 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Sun Valley get?

    About 54 in (138 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 30 in (75 cm) in the leanest winter (2000-01) and 99 in (251 cm) in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high is Sun Valley?

    The top is at 2,780 m and the base at 1,756 m.

    How many km of pistes does Sun Valley have?
