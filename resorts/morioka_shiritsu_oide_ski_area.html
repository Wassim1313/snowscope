    About Morioka Shiritsu Oide

    Morioka Shiritsu Oide has about 0.5 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 253 m and 312 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Hachimantai Resort Panorama Ski, 13 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Morioka Shiritsu Oide hold around 9 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 20 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.

    The mountain at Morioka Shiritsu Oide

    The vertical drop is 59 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 253 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Amihari Onsen, for one, climbs to 1,324 m. All 2 lifts here are rope tows.

    Quick answers

    How high is Morioka Shiritsu Oide?

    Its highest lift reaches 312 m; the lowest starts at 253 m (59 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Morioka Shiritsu Oide have?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
