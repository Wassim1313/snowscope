    About Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai

    Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 39 of 53 in Canada West by length. Skiing here happens between 617 m and 684 m above sea level.

    What there is to ski at Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai

    Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai has 26 graded runs adding up to about 8.0 km. Most of the length is blue square (41%, about 3.3 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 1.9 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai

    From top to bottom that makes 67 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 617 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 3 T-bars and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 650 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Most winters land between 8 cm and 46 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 16 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 34 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 10 February, with about 26 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Can you ski at Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai?

    Its highest lift reaches 684 m; the lowest starts at 617 m (67 m of vertical).

    Who is Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 41% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Tawaitnaw Cross Country Ski Trai have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
