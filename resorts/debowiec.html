    About Dębowiec

    Neither huge nor tiny, Dębowiec offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 200 of 312 in the Carpathians). The ski area spans the mountain from 420 m up to 524 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Szyndzielnia, 1.3 km to the south.

    How Dębowiec is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Dębowiec gives 104 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 420 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; COS Szczyrk reaches 1,244 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 chairlift and 1 rope tow.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Dębowiec?

    The top is at 524 m and the base at 420 m.
