    About Willamette Pass

    Willamette Pass is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 97 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 1,560 m and 2,034 m above sea level.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 474 m: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,560 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Willamette Pass today: about 10 cm up high. The last seven days brought 22 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Shelter Cove Resort (hotel) and Betchtel Shelter (mountain hut).
