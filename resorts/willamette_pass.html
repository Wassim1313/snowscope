    About Willamette Pass

    Willamette Pass is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 97 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 1,560 m and 2,034 m above sea level.

    What there is to ski at Willamette Pass

    The piste map lists 30 graded runs, about 22 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 55%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 6.2 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,797 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 November and keeps it until about 24 May, roughly 25 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early January and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 166 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 68 cm and 225 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 191 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 85 cm; a snowy December gave 156 cm, a dry one 28 cm. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 474 m: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,560 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 6 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Maiden Peak, 2,379 m high and 5.1 km north-east of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Willamette Pass?

    Aim for early January and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Willamette Pass have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 85 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Willamette Pass?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,034 m; the lowest starts at 1,560 m (474 m of vertical).

    Is Willamette Pass good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 6.2 km of its graded pistes (28%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Willamette Pass have?

    About 22 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
