    About La Molina

    Only 8 of the 49 ski areas of the Pyrenees are larger than La Molina, which has some 49 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,653 m up to 2,518 m. The closest other ski area is Masella (3.2 km, north-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 64 runs and around 49 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (39%, about 19 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,086 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to late March, about 10 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 48 cm, around 9 February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 8 cm to 75 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 12 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 23 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 48 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 865 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,653 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,518 m. Its 17 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 3 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include la Tosa (2,536 m), Mirador de la Tosa (2,526 m) and Puig de Comabella (2,482 m).

    Questions about La Molina

    When does La Molina have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Can you ski at La Molina at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 23 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at La Molina?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at La Molina?

    Between 1,653 m and 2,518 m, so 865 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at La Molina?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 26 km, are easy.
