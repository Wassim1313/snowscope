    La Molina in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at La Molina (2,086 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 23 cm, with a normal range of 10 cm–33 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 43 cm (23 cm–62 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 48 cm.

    7 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 11 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 128 cm. Worst: 1989-90, which never passed 17 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late December until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 119 days.

    Questions about La Molina

    Is there snow in La Molina at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 23 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 11 % of 46 winters. Right now: no snow.

    Which month has the most snow at La Molina?

    February: the snow peaks at about 48 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to mid-April.

    How much snow does La Molina get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 48 cm in early February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 128 cm (1981-82), the thinnest 17 cm (1989-90).

    How high do the lifts go at La Molina?

    Between 1,653 m and 2,518 m, so 865 m of vertical drop.

    About 49 km of pistes and 17 lifts.

    About La Molina

    Only 8 of the 49 ski areas of the Pyrenees are larger than La Molina, which has some 49 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,653 m up to 2,518 m. The closest other ski area is Masella (3.2 km, north-west).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 865 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,653 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,518 m.
