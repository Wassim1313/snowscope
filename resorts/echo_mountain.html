    About Echo Mountain

    Compact and local, Echo Mountain has about 4.0 km of runs, small for US West. Skiing here happens between 3,067 m and 3,249 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Black Mountain (20 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 3.8 km to ski. Most of the length is green circle (66%, about 2.5 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    How much snow Echo Mountain usually gets

    At about 3,158 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 November and lasts to 21 May: about 25 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 59 cm, with 97 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 42 cm and 137 cm. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 135 cm. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 106 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    The snow right now

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 182 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 3,067 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet, 2 lifts in all. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Chief Mountain at 3,566 m, standing 317 m above the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Indian Hot Springs (hotel), Columbine Inn (hotel), H and H Motor Lodge (hotel) and Miner's Pick Bed & Breakfast (guesthouse).

    Questions about Echo Mountain

    What is the best month to ski at Echo Mountain?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Echo Mountain at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 59 cm deep.

    What altitude is Echo Mountain at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,249 m; the lowest starts at 3,067 m (182 m of vertical).

    Is Echo Mountain good for beginners?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Echo Mountain?

    Winter Park Resort, 30 km to the north-west, with about 112 km of pistes.
