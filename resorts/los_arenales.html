    About Los Arenales

    Los Arenales is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,519 m up to 1,809 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Quebrada Honda Ski & Snow, 4.3 km to the east.

    How Los Arenales is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Los Arenales gives 290 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,519 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,809 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
