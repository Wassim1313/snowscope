    About Tirolina Hinterthiersee

    Tirolina Hinterthiersee is a ski area. Its nearest neighbour is Tirolina, 0.1 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Tirolina Hinterthiersee through a normal winter

    At about 925 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 January and lasts to 6 April: about 12 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 24 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 50 cm, a dry one 4 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 49 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 22 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Year to year the snow is steady, with 21 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Tirolina Hinterthiersee

    When is the best time to ski at Tirolina Hinterthiersee?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    How much snow does Tirolina Hinterthiersee have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 24 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
