    About Farchant

    Compact and local, Farchant has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 677 m up to 779 m. The closest other ski area is Oberau (3.9 km, north).

    Farchant and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Garmisch-Classic: about 38 km of pistes, 8.1 km to the south-west. Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau has the longer snow season: around 19 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Farchant. The highest nearby is Garmisch-Classic, topping out at 2,047 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Farchant gives 102 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 677 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Krottenkopf at 2,086 m, standing 1,307 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Farchant through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 728 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 20 January, with about 12 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and late January. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 7 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 20 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 31 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.
