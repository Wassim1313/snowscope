    About Menthières

    Menthières is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,070 m to 1,189 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Menthières, 0.6 km to the north.

    What there is to ski at Menthières

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 1.6 km in total. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 1.3 km, or 79%. With 79% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Menthières

    From top to bottom that makes 119 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Lélex, for one, climbs to 1,659 m. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Crêt de la Goutte, 1,621 m high and 2.1 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,130 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 16 January and keeps it until about 23 February, roughly 5 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 42 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 13 cm; a snowy December gave 84 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 143 cm.

    Menthières: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Menthières?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late February.

    Is there snow at Menthières at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Menthières?

    The top is at 1,189 m and the base at 1,070 m.

    Is Menthières suitable for families?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Menthières?

    Lélex, 16 km to the north-east, with about 21 km of pistes.
