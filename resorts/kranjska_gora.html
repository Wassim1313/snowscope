    About Kranjska Gora

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kranjska Gora offers about 22 km of pistes (number 290 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 807 m to 1,568 m. Macesnovc Rateče is the next resort, 2.6 km away to the west.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 761 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 807 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Kranjska Gora hold around 6 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 10 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.
