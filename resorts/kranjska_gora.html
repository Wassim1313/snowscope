    About Kranjska Gora

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kranjska Gora offers about 22 km of pistes (number 290 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 807 m to 1,568 m. Macesnovc Rateče is the next resort, 2.6 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 60 marked runs cover roughly 22 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 10.0 km, or 46%. Intermediate runs make up 46% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 2.2 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 761 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 807 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 2 funiculars, 6 chairlifts, 7 button lifts and 2 moving carpets, 17 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Velika Ponca at 2,602 m, standing 1,034 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,188 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 22 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 5 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 44 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 43 cm).

    Kranjska Gora: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Kranjska Gora?

    Aim for late March and early April.

    Can you ski at Kranjska Gora at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Kranjska Gora?

    The top is at 1,568 m and the base at 807 m.

    Can beginners ski at Kranjska Gora?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 9.7 km, are easy.

    How big is Kranjska Gora?

    About 22 km of pistes and 17 lifts.
