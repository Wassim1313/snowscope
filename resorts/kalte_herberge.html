    About Kalte Herberge

    Kalte Herberge is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,006 m up to 1,074 m. The closest other ski area is Schneeberglifte Wadau (3.2 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 2.0 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.4 km (71%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Kalte Herberge

    68 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 3 moving carpets. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Steinbühl (1,140 m), Steinberg (1,140 m) and Bossenbühl (1,127 m).

    How much snow Kalte Herberge usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,040 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 38 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 26 cm). Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 12 cm.

    Kalte Herberge: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Kalte Herberge?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Is there snow at Kalte Herberge at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 6 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Kalte Herberge?

    Between 1,006 m and 1,074 m, so 68 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kalte Herberge suitable for families?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Kalte Herberge?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
