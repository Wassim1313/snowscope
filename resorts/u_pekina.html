    About U Pekina

    Compact and local, U Pekina has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Carpathians. The slopes start at 513 m and reach 621 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lázeňský vrch, 15 km to the west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 108 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 513 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Švýcárna reaches 1,307 m. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts.

    U Pekina: common questions

    How high is U Pekina?

    Its highest lift reaches 621 m; the lowest starts at 513 m (108 m of vertical).

    Is U Pekina suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near U Pekina?

    Miroslav, 16 km to the west, with about 2.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does U Pekina have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
