    About Bleymard Mont Lozère

    With some 8.0 km of runs, Bleymard Mont Lozère belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The slopes start at 1,361 m and reach 1,570 m at the top. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Croix de Bauzon, 33 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 209 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,361 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,466 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 23 January, with about 6 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm, with 12 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 38 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February.

    Quick answers

    When does Bleymard Mont Lozère have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    How much snow does Bleymard Mont Lozère have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 1 cm).

    What altitude is Bleymard Mont Lozère at?

    The top is at 1,570 m and the base at 1,361 m.

    Is Bleymard Mont Lozère suitable for families?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 3.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Bleymard Mont Lozère have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
