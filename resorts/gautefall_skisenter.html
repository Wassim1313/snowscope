    About Gautefall skisenter

    Gautefall skisenter is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 76 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 449 m to 640 m. Vrådal Panorama is the next resort, 32 km away to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Gautefall skisenter has 16 graded runs adding up to about 10 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (57%, about 5.9 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 191 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 5 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Nokkedalsfjell (729 m), Jørundskårheia (706 m) and Store Djupsanfjellet (689 m).

    Snow at Gautefall skisenter through a normal winter

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 544 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 98 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 74 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 53 cm and 130 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm, from 11 cm in a poor year to 73 cm in a big one.

    Gautefall skisenter: common questions

    When does Gautefall skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Gautefall skisenter have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 38 cm deep.

    What altitude is Gautefall skisenter at?

    Between 449 m and 640 m, so 191 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Gautefall skisenter?

    Yes: 81% of the graded pistes, about 8.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Gautefall skisenter have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
