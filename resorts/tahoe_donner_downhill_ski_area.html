    About Tahoe Donner Downhill

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Tahoe Donner Downhill sits in the middle of the size table of US West. Skiing here happens between 2,070 m and 2,219 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Donner Ski Ranch (7.1 km, south-west).

    What there is to ski at Tahoe Donner Downhill

    Tahoe Donner Downhill has 17 graded runs adding up to about 8.4 km. Most of the length is blue square (50%, about 4.2 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 2,144 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 December and lasts to 5 May: about 21 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 64 cm; a snowy December gave 145 cm, a dry one 8 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 48 cm and 225 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 83 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 145 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 149 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 2,070 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley, for one, climbs to 2,665 m. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Castle Peak (2,773 m), 7.6 km to the west, about 554 m higher than the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Tahoe Donner Downhill include Donner Lake Village Resort (hotel), Truckee Donner Lodge (hotel), The Inn at Truckee (hotel) and Frog Lake Backcountry Huts (mountain hut).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Tahoe Donner Downhill?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Tahoe Donner Downhill at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 64 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Tahoe Donner Downhill?

    The top is at 2,219 m and the base at 2,070 m.

    What level of skier is Tahoe Donner Downhill best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Tahoe Donner Downhill?

    Northstar at Tahoe Resort, 15 km to the south-east, with about 83 km of pistes.
