    About Skiareál Vurmovka

    Skiareál Vurmovka is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 129 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Skiing here happens between 740 m and 829 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Aldrov, 1.2 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 1.6 km. Most of the length is blue (69%, about 1.1 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skiareál Vurmovka gives 89 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 740 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 drag lift.
