    About Langyashan

    Langyashan is a small ski area by the standards of China, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 133 m and reach 171 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Shenhu Ski Field, 12 km south, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Langyashan gives 38 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 133 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 5 lifts here are moving carpets. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts.

    Quick answers

    How high is Langyashan?

    Its highest lift reaches 171 m; the lowest starts at 133 m (38 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Langyashan have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
