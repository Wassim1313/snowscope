    About Ørsta skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ørsta skisenter offers about 10 km of pistes (number 79 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 320 m to 854 m. The closest other ski area is Volda skisenter (7.0 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Ørsta skisenter

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 9.5 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 4.4 km (47%). With 62% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Ørsta skisenter gives 534 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 3 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Storetinden at 1,408 m, standing 554 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 587 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and mid-April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 202 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 107 cm and 252 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 22 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 103 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 55 cm, snowy years 152 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 24 March, with about 208 cm.

    Questions about Ørsta skisenter

    When is the best time to ski at Ørsta skisenter?

    Aim for late March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Ørsta skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 103 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Ørsta skisenter at?

    The top is at 854 m and the base at 320 m.

    Is Ørsta skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 5.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ørsta skisenter have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
