    About Alpe Cainallo

    Alpe Cainallo has about 0.1 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,245 m and reach 1,258 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Pian delle Betulle, 5.7 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more reliable snow, Pian delle Betulle usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 15 weeks, against 3 here. The highest nearby is Pescegallo, topping out at 1,984 m.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 1 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Alpe Cainallo. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 13 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Foppolo-Carona reaches 2,149 m. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Grigna Settentrionale (2,410 m), 4.0 km to the south-east, about 1,152 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,252 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 16 February and keeps it until about 6 March, roughly 3 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 15 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 53 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 18 February, with about 31 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 7 cm to 75 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 8 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rifugio Cainallo (hotel), Santa Maria (hotel), Tremej (guesthouse) and Albergo La Lucciola (hotel).

    Alpe Cainallo: common questions

    When does Alpe Cainallo have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to early March.

    Is there snow at Alpe Cainallo at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 15 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Alpe Cainallo?

    The top is at 1,258 m and the base at 1,245 m.

    How many km of pistes does Alpe Cainallo have?

    About 0.1 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
