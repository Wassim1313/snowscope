    About Ski Vidra

    With some 10 km of runs, Ski Vidra belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,322 m up to 1,974 m. Rânca is the next resort, 13 km away to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 10 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 5.3 km, or 52%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Ski Vidra

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Vidra gives 652 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,322 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,974 m. Its 6 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.
