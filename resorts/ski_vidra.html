    About Ski Vidra

    With some 10 km of runs, Ski Vidra belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,322 m up to 1,974 m. Rânca is the next resort, 13 km away to the south.

    The mountain at Ski Vidra

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Vidra gives 652 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,322 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,974 m.
