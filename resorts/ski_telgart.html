    About Ski Telgárt

    Ski Telgárt is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 5.0 km of pistes put it at number 54 of 312. Its lifts climb from 905 m to 1,308 m. Its nearest neighbour is Vernár, 9.2 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 15 runs and around 4.5 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (39%, about 1.7 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 35% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 1.6 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 403 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 905 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,308 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 6 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
