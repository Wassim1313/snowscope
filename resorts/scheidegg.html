    About Scheidegg

    Compact and local, Scheidegg has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 825 m up to 936 m. Hagenberg is the next resort, 6.4 km away to the south-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 3 graded runs, about 1.6 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.6 km (100%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 111 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,025 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Hirschberg (1,095 m), 5.2 km to the south, about 159 m higher than the top lift.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Scheidegg hold around 10 cm of snow today. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday. An ordinary winter would have around 8 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 880 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 23 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 18 cm, from 3 cm in a poor year to 37 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 28 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 11 cm and 55 cm.

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Scheidegg have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 18 cm deep.

    Between 825 m and 936 m, so 111 m of vertical drop.
