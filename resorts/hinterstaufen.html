    About Hündle-Thalkirchdorf

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hündle-Thalkirchdorf offers about 18 km of pistes (number 338 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 744 m and reach 1,183 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Steibis (4.9 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Hündle-Thalkirchdorf

    Hündle-Thalkirchdorf has 28 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. The largest share is blue, with 11 km (62%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Hündle-Thalkirchdorf gives 439 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 744 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 6 drag lifts, 8 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Hochgrat (1,834 m), 5.5 km to the south, about 651 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 964 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 3 February and lasts to 7 March: about 5 weeks. Most winters land between 11 cm and 58 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 12 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 18 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 34 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 33 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Hündle-Thalkirchdorf

    What is the best month to ski at Hündle-Thalkirchdorf?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    How much snow does Hündle-Thalkirchdorf have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 18 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Hündle-Thalkirchdorf?

    The top is at 1,183 m and the base at 744 m.

    Is Hündle-Thalkirchdorf suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Hündle-Thalkirchdorf have?

    About 18 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
