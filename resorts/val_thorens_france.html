    About Val Thorens

    With some 115 km of runs, Val Thorens belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,822 m and reach 3,175 m at the top. Val Thorens is the next resort, 0.7 km away to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    Val Thorens has 189 graded runs adding up to about 115 km. Most of the length is blue (48%, about 56 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Val Thorens today and the next days

    How Val Thorens is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Val Thorens gives 1,353 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. A high base at 1,822 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. The lift system counts 27 lifts: 3 cable cars, 7 gondolas, 9 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 5 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aiguille de Péclet at 3,561 m, standing 386 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Val Thorens through a normal winter

    At about 2,498 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 12 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 10 March, with about 213 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 136 cm to 230 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 192 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and early April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 110 cm; a snowy December gave 162 cm, a dry one 59 cm. For about 22 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Val Thorens: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Val Thorens?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Val Thorens at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 110 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Val Thorens open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 21 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 2 May 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Val Thorens?

    The top is at 3,175 m and the base at 1,822 m.

    Is Val Thorens suitable for families?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 68 km, are easy.
