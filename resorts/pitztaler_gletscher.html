    About Pitztaler Gletscher

    Pitztaler Gletscher is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 32 km of pistes put it at number 194 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,728 m and reach 3,424 m at the top. Rifflsee is the next resort, 4.5 km away to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Pitztaler Gletscher gives 1,696 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Starting at 1,728 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 funicular, 2 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Wildspitze at 3,768 m, standing 344 m above the top lift.

    The pistes of Pitztaler Gletscher

    Counting every graded piste, there are 34 runs and around 31 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 21 km (69%). Intermediate runs make up 69% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.1 km.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,576 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early November to late May or later, about 29 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 109 cm and 210 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For about 20 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 93 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 55 cm in a poor year to 132 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 161 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 178 cm.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Gletscherstube (guesthouse), Braunschweiger Hütte (mountain hut), Hotel Restaurant Berghof Steinbock (hotel) and Hotel Wanderlux Pure Resort (chalet).

    Questions about Pitztaler Gletscher

    What is the best month to ski at Pitztaler Gletscher?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Pitztaler Gletscher have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 93 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Pitztaler Gletscher?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,424 m; the lowest starts at 1,728 m (1,696 m of vertical).

    Who is Pitztaler Gletscher best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Pitztaler Gletscher have?

    About 32 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
