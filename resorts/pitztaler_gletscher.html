    About Pitztaler Gletscher

    Pitztaler Gletscher is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 32 km of pistes put it at number 194 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,728 m and reach 3,424 m at the top. Rifflsee is the next resort, 4.5 km away to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Pitztaler Gletscher gives 1,696 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Starting at 1,728 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 51 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Pitztaler Gletscher. 4 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 16 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Gletscherstube (guesthouse), Braunschweiger Hütte (mountain hut), Hotel Restaurant Berghof Steinbock (hotel) and Hotel Wanderlux Pure Resort (chalet).

    Questions about Pitztaler Gletscher

    How high do the lifts go at Pitztaler Gletscher?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,424 m; the lowest starts at 1,728 m (1,696 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Pitztaler Gletscher have?

    About 32 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
