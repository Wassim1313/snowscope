    About Hakuba Sanosaka Snow Resort

    Hakuba Sanosaka Snow Resort is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 166 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 791 m up to 1,146 m. Kashimayari Snow Resort is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 5.5 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 2.1 km (38%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 1.4 km of difficult runs.

    How Hakuba Sanosaka Snow Resort is laid out

    355 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The base at 791 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Hakuba Happo One, reach 1,827 m. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Hakuba Sanosaka Snow Resort?

    The top is at 1,146 m and the base at 791 m.

    Is Hakuba Sanosaka Snow Resort good for advanced skiers?

    How many km of pistes does Hakuba Sanosaka Snow Resort have?
