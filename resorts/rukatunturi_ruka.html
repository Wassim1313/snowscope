    Rukatunturi in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Rukatunturi (390 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 40 cm, with a normal range of 34 cm–46 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 64 cm; the middle half of winters range from 56 cm to 70 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 65 cm.

    7 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 30 % by 1 January.

    1997-98 was the big one (105 cm); 1987-88 the lean one (53 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early December to early May (153 days).

    Today's 25 cm compares with a usual 22 cm for 24 November.

    Skiing Rukatunturi: the practical side

    Lifts: 24. Pistes: 25 km. Heights: 286 m at the bottom, 494 m at the top (208 m of vertical).

    Count on 56 € for a day pass (265 € for 6 days) and from 85 € a night per person.

    Airports: Kuusamo (KAO, 20 km), Rovaniemi (RVN, 156 km), Kittila (KTT, 258 km).

    About Rukatunturi

    With some 25 km of runs, Rukatunturi belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 286 m up to 494 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kiririnteet, 39 km west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Rukatunturi has 94 graded runs adding up to about 25 km. The largest share is blue, with 15 km (61%). With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 208 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 24 lifts: 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts, 9 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 6 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 6 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Ruka (500 m), Valtavaara (487 m) and Konttainen (433 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 390 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 December and lasts to 8 May: about 22 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 65 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 73 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 61 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 40 cm; a snowy December gave 50 cm, a dry one 23 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.
