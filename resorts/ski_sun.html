    About Ski&Sun

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski&Sun offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 88 of 257 in Central Europe). The slopes start at 613 m and reach 1,053 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skiareál Černá Říčka, 14 km to the south.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 440 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 613 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,053 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
