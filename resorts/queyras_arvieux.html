    About Queyras

    Queyras is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 403 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,692 m and 2,105 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Queyras, 11 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Queyras

    The piste map lists 28 graded runs, about 14 km in total. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 7.2 km, or 51%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,898 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 November and keeps it until about 19 May, roughly 26 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 108 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 80 cm and 169 cm. A normal season tops out near 143 cm, around 6 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 73 cm, with 132 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks.

    The mountain at Queyras

    Skied top to bottom, Queyras gives 413 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,692 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Queyras, for one, climbs to 2,805 m. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 chairlift, 4 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Pic de Rochebrune, 3,320 m high and 5.7 km north-east of the resort.

    Questions about Queyras

    When is the best time to ski at Queyras?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Queyras at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 73 cm deep.

    What altitude is Queyras at?

    The top is at 2,105 m and the base at 1,692 m.

    Can beginners ski at Queyras?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 8.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Queyras?

    Montgenèvre, 16 km to the north, with about 62 km of pistes.
