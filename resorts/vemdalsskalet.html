    About Vemdalsskalet

    Vemdalsskalet is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 25 km of pistes put it at number 23 of 262. Its lifts climb from 590 m to 944 m. Its nearest neighbour is Björnrike, 7.4 km south, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 48 runs and around 24 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (39%, about 9.6 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 354 m: runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 6 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets, 13 lifts in all. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Oxsjövålen (1,023 m), Skorvdalsfjället (1,009 m) and Varggranshågna (966 m).

    How much snow Vemdalsskalet usually gets

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 767 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 56 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 44 cm and 85 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 39 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 23 cm in a poor year to 63 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 65 cm.

    Questions about Vemdalsskalet

    What is the best month to ski at Vemdalsskalet?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Vemdalsskalet have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Vemdalsskalet at?

    Between 590 m and 944 m, so 354 m of vertical drop.

    Is Vemdalsskalet suitable for families?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Vemdalsskalet have?

    About 25 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
