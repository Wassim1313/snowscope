    About Bjorli Skisenter

    Around 15 km of marked runs place Bjorli Skisenter among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 43 by length. Skiing here happens between 573 m and 1,213 m above sea level.

    What there is to ski at Bjorli Skisenter

    In all, 12 marked runs cover roughly 14 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (47%, about 6.4 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Lifts and terrain

    640 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 4 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Løyfthøa (1,444 m), 7.7 km to the north, about 231 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 893 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late October to late May or later, about 31 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 214 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 133 cm (lean years 85 cm, snowy years 173 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-April at roughly 218 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 124 cm to 259 cm in mid-February in most winters. For about 25 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Bjorli Skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    Can you ski at Bjorli Skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 133 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Bjorli Skisenter at?

    The top is at 1,213 m and the base at 573 m.

    Can beginners ski at Bjorli Skisenter?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 8.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Bjorli Skisenter?

    About 15 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
