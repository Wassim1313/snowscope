    About Ratschings-Jaufen

    With some 25 km of runs, Ratschings-Jaufen belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,290 m and 2,142 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Rosskopf, 10 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 31 graded runs, about 24 km in total. Most of the length is red (73%, about 18 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,716 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 23 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 74 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 51 cm, with 82 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 63 cm and 121 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 93 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March.

    This week's snow

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 852 m: enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 6 chairlifts, 1 drag lift and 4 moving carpets, 12 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Ratschinger Weißen - La Bianca (2,822 m), 7.8 km to the west, about 680 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 hotels: Alpura, Schölzhorn, Hotel Jägerhof and Sporthotel Ratschings.

    Ratschings-Jaufen: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ratschings-Jaufen?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Ratschings-Jaufen at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 51 cm deep.

    What altitude is Ratschings-Jaufen at?

    The top is at 2,142 m and the base at 1,290 m.

    What level of skier is Ratschings-Jaufen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Ratschings-Jaufen?

    About 25 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
