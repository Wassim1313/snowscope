    About Heroldstatt

    Heroldstatt has about 0.2 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 751 m to 778 m. The closest other ski area is Beiningen (13 km, south-east).

    Snow at Heroldstatt through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 764 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 20 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 12 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January. A normal season tops out near 5 cm, around 23 January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    27 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 951 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The only lift is a drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Heroldstatt (849 m), Grimmelberg (841 m) and Ludwigshöhe (799 m).

    Heroldstatt: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Heroldstatt?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    How much snow does Heroldstatt have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    What altitude is Heroldstatt at?

    The top is at 778 m and the base at 751 m.

    How many km of pistes does Heroldstatt have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
