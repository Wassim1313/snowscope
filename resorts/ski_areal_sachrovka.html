    About Ski areál Sachrovka

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Ski areál Sachrovka one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 620 m and 876 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Modrá hvězda, 0.3 km to the east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 2.4 km north-east to Skiareál Horní Domky, with about 9.0 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Skiareál Horní Domky usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 9 weeks, against 6 here. For higher slopes, Skiareál Horní Domky climbs to 1,308 m.

    What there is to ski at Ski areál Sachrovka

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.2 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.2 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How much snow Ski areál Sachrovka usually gets

    Count on about 6 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 748 m in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 38 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 6 cm to 65 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm, with 52 cm in the best years.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 256 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 620 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    Quick answers

    When does Ski areál Sachrovka have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Is there snow at Ski areál Sachrovka at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Ski areál Sachrovka?

    The top is at 876 m and the base at 620 m.

    Is Ski areál Sachrovka good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ski areál Sachrovka?

    Skiareál Horní Domky, 2.4 km to the north-east, with about 9.0 km of pistes.
