    About Ferguson Ridge

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ferguson Ridge offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 131 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 1,561 m to 1,752 m.

    What there is to ski at Ferguson Ridge

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 7.5 km. Most of the length is black diamond (32%, about 2.4 km). With 32% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 2.4 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,656 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to early May. Most winters land between 69 cm and 142 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. For about 7 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 76 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 120 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 60 cm; a snowy December gave 90 cm, a dry one 37 cm.

    Ferguson Ridge today and the next days

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 191 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,561 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Aneroid Mountain North, 2,880 m high and 7.3 km south-west of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 2 hotels: Wallowa Lake Lodge and Wallowa Lake Resort.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Ferguson Ridge?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Is there snow at Ferguson Ridge at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 60 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Ferguson Ridge at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,752 m; the lowest starts at 1,561 m (191 m of vertical).

    Is Ferguson Ridge suitable for families?

    Yes: 40% of the graded pistes, about 3.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Ferguson Ridge?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
