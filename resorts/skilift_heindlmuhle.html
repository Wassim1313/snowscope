    The mountain at Skilift Heindlmühle

    From top to bottom that makes 87 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 619 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hochficht-Böhmerwald reaches 1,332 m. The only lift is a drag lift.
