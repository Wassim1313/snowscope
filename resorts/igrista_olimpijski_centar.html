    About Igrišta olimpijski centar

    Neither huge nor tiny, Igrišta olimpijski centar offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 30 of 51 in the Balkans). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,020 m up to 1,346 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sportski centar Milići, 12 km to the east.
