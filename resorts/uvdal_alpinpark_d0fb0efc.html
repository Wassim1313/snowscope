    About Uvdal Alpinpark

    Uvdal Alpinpark is a ski area. Its nearest neighbour is Uvdal Alpinpark, 0.1 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Uvdal Alpinpark through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,086 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 November and lasts to 25 May: about 28 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. Most winters land between 72 cm and 123 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 25 March, with about 107 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 100 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 60 cm, from 38 cm in a poor year to 90 cm in a big one. For about 6 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Uvdal Alpinpark: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Uvdal Alpinpark?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Uvdal Alpinpark have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 60 cm deep.
