    About Chabanon

    Chabanon is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 26 km of pistes put it at number 234 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,534 m and reach 2,028 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Le Grand Puy (7.7 km, east).

    The pistes of Chabanon

    Chabanon has 43 graded runs adding up to about 26 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 11 km (42%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,781 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 December and lasts to 19 April: about 18 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 36 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Most winters land between 22 cm and 160 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 98 cm, around 22 February. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 39 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 109 cm in the best years.

    How Chabanon is laid out

    494 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,534 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Les Orres, for one, climbs to 2,700 m. The lift system counts 12 lifts: 1 chairlift and 11 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Sommet de l'Oratoire (2,072 m), Tête Grosse (2,032 m) and Clot de bouc (1,962 m).

    Chabanon: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Chabanon?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Chabanon at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 39 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Chabanon at?

    Between 1,534 m and 2,028 m, so 494 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Chabanon?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 16 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Chabanon?

    Espace Lumière, 23 km to the east, with about 185 km of pistes.
