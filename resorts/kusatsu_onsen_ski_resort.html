    About Kusatsu Onsen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kusatsu Onsen offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 142 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,244 m up to 1,620 m. Its nearest neighbour is Shiga Kogen Shibutoge, 5.7 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Shiga Kogen Yokoteyama (7.3 km north-west) has about 14 km of pistes against 6.0 km here. For higher slopes, Shiga Kogen Yokoteyama climbs to 2,303 m.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Kusatsu Onsen hold around 5 cm of snow today. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 11 cm by Sunday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Kusatsu Onsen gives 376 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Shiga Kogen Yokoteyama, reach 2,303 m.
