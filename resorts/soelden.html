    About Sölden

    With some 107 km of runs, Sölden belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,346 m and reach 3,247 m at the top. Pitztaler Gletscher is the next resort, 8.4 km away to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,901 m of vertical, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. The bottom station at 1,346 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 39 lifts break down into 2 funiculars, 9 gondolas, 13 chairlifts, 6 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 6 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 6 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Hohe Geige (3,394 m), 7.7 km to the north-west, about 147 m higher than the top lift.

    The pistes of Sölden

    In all, 169 marked runs cover roughly 105 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 59 km, or 56%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,296 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 88 cm to 163 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 136 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 122 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 72 cm; a snowy December gave 98 cm, a dry one 44 cm.

    Sölden: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Sölden?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Sölden at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 72 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Sölden?

    It opened on 9 October 2026; the last ski day is planned for 2 May 2027.

    What altitude is Sölden at?

    Between 1,346 m and 3,247 m, so 1,901 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Sölden?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 59 km, are easy.
