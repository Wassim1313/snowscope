    About Sölden

    With some 107 km of runs, Sölden belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,346 m and reach 3,247 m at the top. Pitztaler Gletscher is the next resort, 8.4 km away to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,901 m of vertical, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. The bottom station at 1,346 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
