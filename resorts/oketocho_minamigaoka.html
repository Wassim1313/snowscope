    About Oketocho Minamigaoka

    Oketocho Minamigaoka is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 240 m up to 320 m. The closest other ski area is Rubeshibe-cho Happodai (10 km, north).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Oketocho Minamigaoka gives 80 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 240 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
