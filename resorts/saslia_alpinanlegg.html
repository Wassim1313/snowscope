    About Såslia alpinanlegg

    Såslia alpinanlegg has about 0.4 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 188 m and 296 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Daltrøa alpinbakke (25 km, north).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Vassfjellet skisenter has the longer snow season: around 23 weeks of solid cover, compared with 20 at Såslia alpinanlegg. For higher slopes, Vassfjellet skisenter climbs to 656 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 242 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 December and lasts to 3 May: about 20 weeks. A normal season tops out near 84 cm, around 1 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 44 cm to 108 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 68 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm; a snowy December gave 65 cm, a dry one 18 cm.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 12 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Såslia alpinanlegg. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 18 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 15 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The mountain at Såslia alpinanlegg

    Skied top to bottom, Såslia alpinanlegg gives 108 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Vennafjellet (697 m), 6.7 km to the north-west, about 401 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Såslia alpinanlegg include Selbusjøen Hotel & Gjestegård (hotel) and Selbukåten (chalet).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Såslia alpinanlegg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Såslia alpinanlegg have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 42 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Såslia alpinanlegg?

    Between 188 m and 296 m, so 108 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Såslia alpinanlegg?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
