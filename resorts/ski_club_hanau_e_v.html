    About Ski Club Hanau e.V.

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Ski Club Hanau e.V. sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 669 m up to 842 m. The closest other ski area is Arnsberglifte (3.9 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Ski Club Hanau e.V. has 9 graded runs adding up to about 3.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.8 km (50%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at Ski Club Hanau e.V.

    173 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 669 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 842 m is the third highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
