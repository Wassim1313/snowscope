    Almenwelt Lofer in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Almenwelt Lofer (about 1,151 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 32 cm of cover (20 cm to 48 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 52 cm; the middle half of winters range from 40 cm to 73 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 66 cm.

    11 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 30 % by 1 January.

    1981-82 was the big one (141 cm); 2006-07 the lean one (35 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to early May, about 155 days.

    Almenwelt Lofer: quick answers

    Can you ski Almenwelt Lofer at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 32 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 30 % of 46 winters. Right now: 11 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Almenwelt Lofer?

    February: the snow peaks at about 66 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to early May.

    How deep does the snow get at Almenwelt Lofer?

    About 66 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late February); between 35 cm in the leanest winter (2006-07) and 141 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    How high do the lifts go at Almenwelt Lofer?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,674 m; the lowest starts at 628 m (1,046 m of vertical).

    How big is Almenwelt Lofer?

    About 23 km of pistes and 11 lifts.

    About Almenwelt Lofer

    Neither huge nor tiny, Almenwelt Lofer offers about 23 km of pistes (number 271 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 628 m and reach 1,674 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Heutal (7.1 km, north).

    Lifts and terrain

    1,046 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 628 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,674 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
