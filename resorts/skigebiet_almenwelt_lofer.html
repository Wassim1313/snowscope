    About Almenwelt Lofer

    Neither huge nor tiny, Almenwelt Lofer offers about 23 km of pistes (number 271 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 628 m and reach 1,674 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Heutal (7.1 km, north).

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,151 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 December and keeps it until about 2 May, roughly 19 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 32 cm to 86 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 32 cm; a snowy December gave 65 cm, a dry one 7 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 66 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 46 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 34 runs and around 23 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 14 km (60%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,046 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 628 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,674 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet, 11 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Großes Ochsenhorn at 2,513 m, standing 839 m above the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Lippnkasa Almhütte (mountain hut), Steger Kaserei (mountain hut), Pension Bräuschmied (hotel) and Landhaus Eva-Marie (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Almenwelt Lofer: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Almenwelt Lofer?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Almenwelt Lofer have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 32 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Almenwelt Lofer?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,674 m; the lowest starts at 628 m (1,046 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Almenwelt Lofer?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How big is Almenwelt Lofer?

    About 23 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
