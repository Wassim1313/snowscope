    About Каменный мыс

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Каменный мыс sits in the middle of the size table of Russia. The ski area spans the mountain from 33 m up to 98 m.

    The pistes of Каменный мыс

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 4.3 km to ski. Most of the length is red (40%, about 1.7 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 1.2 km of black or expert runs.

    How Каменный мыс is laid out

    65 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 1 rope tow. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Каменный мыс: common questions

    How high is Каменный мыс?

    The top is at 98 m and the base at 33 m.

    Does Каменный мыс have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.2 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Каменный мыс have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
