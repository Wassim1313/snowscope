    About Hagenberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hagenberg offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 695 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 613 m and reach 918 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Sulzberg, 1.8 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Hagenberg

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 4.0 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 3.0 km (74%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Hagenberg

    From top to bottom that makes 305 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 613 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Kojenstein, 1,300 m high and 7.5 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 766 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually late December and late January. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 11 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 26 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 36 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 19 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Hagenberg?

    Aim for late December and late January.

    Is there snow at Hagenberg at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 11 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Hagenberg?

    Between 613 m and 918 m, so 305 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Hagenberg?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 3.0 km, are easy.
