    About Spökbacken

    Spökbacken is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 29 m and 74 m above sea level. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Yxbacken, 32 km to the south.

    Spökbacken and the resorts around it

    In this group of 4, Spökbacken is number 3 for piste length. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold.

    Snow at Spökbacken through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 52 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 10 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 21 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 31 cm.

    How Spökbacken is laid out

    45 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 74 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Questions about Spökbacken

    When is the best time to ski at Spökbacken?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    How much snow does Spökbacken have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 4 cm).

    What altitude is Spökbacken at?

    Between 29 m and 74 m, so 45 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Spökbacken have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
