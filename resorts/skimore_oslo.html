    About Skimore Oslo

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Skimore Oslo sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 153 m up to 530 m. Its nearest neighbour is Oslo Vinterpark, 0.3 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Skimore Oslo

    Counting every graded piste, there are 34 runs and around 11 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 3.7 km (33%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 15 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 342 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to early April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 22 cm to 80 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 60 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 25 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 48 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 16 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skimore Oslo gives 377 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 530 m is the highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts and 3 moving carpets, 11 lifts in all. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Tryvannshøgda (530 m), Råkollen (528 m) and Kobberhaugene (526 m).

    Quick answers

    When does Skimore Oslo have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Can you ski at Skimore Oslo at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 25 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Skimore Oslo?

    Between 153 m and 530 m, so 377 m of vertical drop.

    Is Skimore Oslo good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 6.8 km, are easy.

    How big is Skimore Oslo?

    About 11 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
