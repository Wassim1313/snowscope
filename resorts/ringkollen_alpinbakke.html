    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 564 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to mid-April, about 18 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 46 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Most winters land between 37 cm and 95 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm, with 62 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 67 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 87 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 607 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Ringkollen (702 m), Gyrihaugen (682 m) and Svarthøgda (654 m).

    Ringkollen alpinbakke: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Ringkollen alpinbakke?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Ringkollen alpinbakke at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Ringkollen alpinbakke at?

    Between 520 m and 607 m, so 87 m of vertical drop.
