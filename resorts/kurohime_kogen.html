    About Kurohime Kogen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kurohime Kogen offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 113 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 782 m up to 1,178 m. Myoko Suginohara is the next resort, 4.9 km away to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Kurohime Kogen

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 7.9 km to ski. Most of the length is red (36%, about 2.8 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 1.6 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Kurohime Kogen today: about 19 cm up high. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 396 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 782 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Kurohime Kogen at?

    Between 782 m and 1,178 m, so 396 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kurohime Kogen suitable for families?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 3.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Kurohime Kogen?

    Myoko Suginohara, 4.9 km to the north-west, with about 21 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Kurohime Kogen have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
