    About Dolní Lomná

    Neither huge nor tiny, Dolní Lomná offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 202 of 312 in the Carpathians). Skiing here happens between 486 m and 670 m above sea level. Skiareál Severka is the next resort, 3.4 km away to the south-east.

    Dolní Lomná and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 3.4 km south-east to Skiareál Severka, with about 3.0 km of runs. Skiareál Severka reaches 955 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 184 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 486 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Skiareál Severka reaches 955 m.

    Quick answers

    How high is Dolní Lomná?

    The top is at 670 m and the base at 486 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Dolní Lomná?

    Skiareál Severka, 3.4 km to the south-east, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Dolní Lomná have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
