    About Estació de muntanya Vall de Núri

    About 6.0 km of pistes make Estació de muntanya Vall de Núri one of the smaller places to ski in the Pyrenees. Its lifts climb from 1,964 m to 2,259 m. Its nearest neighbour is Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt, 8.6 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Estació de muntanya Vall de Núri and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 20 km south-west to La Molina, with about 49 km of runs. For higher slopes, La Molina climbs to 2,518 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    295 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 1,964 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Estació de muntanya Vall de Núri hold around 5 cm of snow today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Estació de muntanya Vall de Núri include Hotel Vall de Núria (hotel), Hostal Les Roquetes (guesthouse), Refugi de Coma de Vaca (mountain hut) and Can Bonada (chalet).
