    How much snow Hummelfjell usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 810 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 December and keeps it until about 13 May, roughly 23 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 78 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 49 cm (lean years 31 cm, snowy years 69 cm). Most winters land between 57 cm and 96 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 85 cm, around 20 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Hummelfjell: common questions

    When does Hummelfjell have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Hummelfjell at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 49 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
