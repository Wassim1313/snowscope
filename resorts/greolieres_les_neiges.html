    About Gréolières-Les-Neiges

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gréolières-Les-Neiges offers about 23 km of pistes (number 267 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,399 m to 1,765 m. L'Audibergue is the next resort, 17 km away to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 39 marked runs cover roughly 23 km. The largest share is blue, with 10 km (44%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at Gréolières-Les-Neiges

    From top to bottom that makes 366 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,399 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 1 chairlift, 7 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Cime du Cheiron (1,778 m), Jérusalem (1,768 m) and La Croix de Verse (1,706 m).

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,582 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to early March, about 5 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 36 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 155 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 57 cm, a dry one 0 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Questions about Gréolières-Les-Neiges

    When does Gréolières-Les-Neiges have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Gréolières-Les-Neiges have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 8 cm).

    What altitude is Gréolières-Les-Neiges at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,765 m; the lowest starts at 1,399 m (366 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Gréolières-Les-Neiges?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Gréolières-Les-Neiges have?

    About 23 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
