    About Mt. Baldy

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mt. Baldy offers about 15 km of pistes (number 113 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 1,974 m to 2,611 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mountain High, 13 km to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 27 marked runs cover roughly 15 km. The largest share is blue square, with 5.6 km (37%). Steep ground is the speciality: 44% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 6.6 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Mt. Baldy

    637 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,974 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mount San Antonio at 3,068 m, standing 457 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Mt. Baldy in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at about 2,292 m, the middle of the ski area stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Quick answers

    How high is Mt. Baldy?

    Between 1,974 m and 2,611 m, so 637 m of vertical drop.

    Does Mt. Baldy have steep runs?

    Yes: about 6.6 km of its graded pistes (44%) are difficult.

    How big is Mt. Baldy?

    About 15 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
