    About Piazzatorre

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Piazzatorre sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,067 m and 1,776 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Foppolo-Carona (7.5 km, north-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Piazzatorre has 3 graded runs adding up to about 4.0 km. Most of the length is red (62%, about 2.5 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Piazzatorre and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 17 km west to Piani di Bobbio, with about 18 km of runs. Foppolo-Carona has the longer snow season: around 18 weeks of solid cover, compared with 2 at Piazzatorre. For higher slopes, Foppolo-Carona climbs to 2,149 m.

    How Piazzatorre is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 709 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Monte Spondone (2,445 m), 6.5 km to the east, about 669 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,422 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 February and lasts to 12 March: about 2 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm; a snowy December gave 31 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 8 cm to 53 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 33 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 8 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then.

    Questions about Piazzatorre

    What is the best month to ski at Piazzatorre?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to mid-March.

    How much snow does Piazzatorre have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Piazzatorre at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,776 m; the lowest starts at 1,067 m (709 m of vertical).

    Who is Piazzatorre best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 62% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Piazzatorre?

    Piani di Bobbio, 17 km to the west, with about 18 km of pistes.
