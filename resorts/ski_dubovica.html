    About Ski Dubovica

    Ski Dubovica is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 4.0 km of pistes put it at number 75 of 312. The ski area spans the mountain from 521 m up to 782 m. Its nearest neighbour is Renčišov, 3.7 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Ski Dubovica has 8 graded runs adding up to about 4.0 km. The largest share is red, with 3.6 km (89%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Dubovica gives 261 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 521 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    This week's snow

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Dubovica?

    Its highest lift reaches 782 m; the lowest starts at 521 m (261 m of vertical).

    Who is Ski Dubovica best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 89% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Ski Dubovica have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
