    About Mont Serein

    Mont Serein is a mid-sized ski area with about 11 km of pistes, number 469 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,388 m up to 1,809 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 31 runs and around 11 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.6 km, or 67%. Intermediate runs make up 67% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The mountain at Mont Serein

    The vertical drop is 421 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,388 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 7 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Mont Ventoux (1,910 m), 1.0 km to the south-east, about 101 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Mont Serein usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,598 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 5 winters out of ten, typically 0 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 16 cm in a big one. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 80 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 7 cm.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Chalet Ventoux Serein (hotel), Le Chalet Liotard (chalet), La Bergerie des Salamandres (guesthouse) and Auberge de Brantes (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Mont Serein?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Is there snow at Mont Serein at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 5 winters out of ten, typically 0 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Mont Serein at?

    Between 1,388 m and 1,809 m, so 421 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Mont Serein best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Mont Serein have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
