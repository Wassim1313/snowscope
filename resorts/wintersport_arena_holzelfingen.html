    About Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 710 m up to 795 m. The closest other ski area is Engstingen (4.7 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 18 graded runs, about 5.7 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 3.5 km (62%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 752 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 18 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and late January. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 4 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm, with 10 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 85 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 710 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 5 T-bars and 1 drag lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Hohfleck (872 m), Sternberg (844 m) and Feldberg (838 m).

    Questions about Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen

    When is the best time to ski at Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    How much snow does Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    What altitude is Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen at?

    Between 710 m and 795 m, so 85 m of vertical drop.

    Is Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 3.5 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
