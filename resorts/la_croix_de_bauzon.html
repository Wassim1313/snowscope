    About La Croix de Bauzon

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, La Croix de Bauzon sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,259 m up to 1,486 m. Bleymard Mont Lozère is the next resort, 33 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    La Croix de Bauzon has 5 graded runs adding up to about 1.5 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 0.9 km (57%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    227 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 5 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
