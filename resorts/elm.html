    About Elm

    With some 28 km of runs, Elm belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,016 m and 2,100 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hätzingen, 9.2 km to the north-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,084 m: enough height for proper long runs.

    Elm: common questions

    How high is Elm?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,100 m; the lowest starts at 1,016 m (1,084 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Elm have?

    About 28 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
