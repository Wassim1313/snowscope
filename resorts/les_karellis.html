    About Les Karellis

    With some 45 km of runs, Les Karellis belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,550 m to 2,484 m. Albiez-Montrond is the next resort, 4.0 km away to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 55 runs and around 45 km to ski. Most of the length is red (35%, about 16 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 0.8 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow at Les Karellis through a normal winter

    At about 2,017 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 November and lasts to the end of May: about 27 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 148 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and early April. A normal season tops out near 155 cm, around 5 March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 85 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 47 cm, snowy years 123 cm). Most winters land between 102 cm and 183 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 18 weeks.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Les Karellis has roughly 37 cm of snow on the ground today. 39 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 83 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 34 cm at mid-mountain). The 2026/27 season at Les Karellis is announced to start on 19 December 2026 and to run until 10 April 2027.

    The mountain at Les Karellis

    From top to bottom that makes 934 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,550 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 6 chairlifts, 5 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 14 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by La Grande Chible, 2,932 m high and 4.4 km south-west of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are La Gamelle (hotel), Cabane des chasseurs (mountain hut), Hôtel du Galibier (hotel) and Hôtel de Lancheton (hotel).

    Les Karellis: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Les Karellis?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Les Karellis have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 85 cm deep.

    When does Les Karellis open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 19 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 10 April 2027.

    What altitude is Les Karellis at?

    The top is at 2,484 m and the base at 1,550 m.

    Is Les Karellis good for beginners?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 25 km, are easy.
