    About Les Karellis

    With some 45 km of runs, Les Karellis belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,550 m to 2,484 m. Albiez-Montrond is the next resort, 4.0 km away to the west.

    How Les Karellis compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 13 km west to Les Sybelles, with about 153 km of runs. For higher slopes, Orelle climbs to 3,221 m.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Les Karellis has roughly 43 cm of snow on the ground today. 26 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 72 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    The mountain at Les Karellis

    From top to bottom that makes 934 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,550 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are La Gamelle (hotel), Cabane des chasseurs (mountain hut), Hôtel du Galibier (hotel) and Hôtel de Lancheton (hotel).
