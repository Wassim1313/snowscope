    The snow at Les Karellis, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Les Karellis (about 2,017 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 85 cm; half of the winters sit between 56 cm and 108 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 151 cm (119 cm–165 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 155 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 67 % of winters, and by 1 January in 98 %.

    The deepest winter was 2019-20 (245 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2004-05 (127 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to late May, about 194 days.

    Les Karellis: quick answers

    Does Les Karellis have snow over Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 85 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 98 % of 46 winters. Right now: 133 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Les Karellis?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 155 cm). For a sure base, mid-November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Les Karellis get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 155 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 245 cm (2019-20), the thinnest 127 cm (2004-05).

    What altitude is Les Karellis at?

    The top is at 2,484 m and the base at 1,550 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Les Karellis?

    Les Sybelles, 13 km to the west, with about 153 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Les Karellis have?

    About 45 km of pistes and 14 lifts.

    About Les Karellis

    With some 45 km of runs, Les Karellis belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,550 m to 2,484 m. Albiez-Montrond is the next resort, 4.0 km away to the west.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Les Karellis has roughly 133 cm of snow on the ground today. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 33 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.
