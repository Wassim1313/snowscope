    About Sampeyre

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sampeyre offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 596 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 947 m up to 1,851 m. Pian Munè is the next resort, 8.9 km away to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Sampeyre has 7 graded runs adding up to about 7.2 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.5 km, or 76%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Sampeyre and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 29 km north-west to Queyras, with about 30 km of runs. Queyras has the longer snow season: around 22 weeks of solid cover, compared with 6 at Sampeyre. The highest nearby is Queyras, topping out at 2,805 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 904 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 947 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Queyras, reach 2,805 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 1 button lift, 3 lifts in all. Above the ski area rises Cima delle Lobbie (3,015 m), 7.8 km to the north-west, about 1,164 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Sampeyre through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,399 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 January and keeps it until about 9 March, roughly 6 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 34 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 58 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 32 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Sampeyre

    When is the best time to ski at Sampeyre?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Is there snow at Sampeyre at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Sampeyre?

    Between 947 m and 1,851 m, so 904 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Sampeyre best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 76% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sampeyre?

    Queyras, 29 km to the north-west, with about 30 km of pistes.
