    About Sampeyre

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sampeyre offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 596 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 947 m up to 1,851 m. Pian Munè is the next resort, 8.9 km away to the north-east.

    Sampeyre and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 29 km north-west to Queyras, with about 30 km of runs. The highest nearby is Queyras, topping out at 2,805 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 904 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 947 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Queyras, reach 2,805 m.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Albergo Alte Alpi, Hotel Monte Nebin, Albergo Loganda Garzino and Hotel Ristorante Torinetto.
