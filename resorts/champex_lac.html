    About Champex-Lac

    Neither huge nor tiny, Champex-Lac offers about 11 km of pistes (number 457 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,468 m and 2,194 m above sea level. Vichères-Bavon is the next resort, 6.1 km away to the south-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 20 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 7.8 km, or 69%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Champex-Lac is laid out

    726 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,468 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 2 chairlifts and 2 button lifts. The skyline is dominated by Aiguille de la Varappe, 3,518 m high and 7.3 km south-west of the resort.

    Champex-Lac and the resorts around it

    Of the 7 ski areas compared here, Champex-Lac is the third biggest by piste length. For more reliable snow, Balme usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 27 weeks, against 21 here.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 36 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Champex-Lac. 40 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 88 cm by Sunday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 16 cm).

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,831 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 December and keeps it until about 14 May, roughly 21 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 45 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 48 cm to 100 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 76 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 39 cm, with 62 cm in the best years.

    Quick answers

    When does Champex-Lac have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Champex-Lac at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 39 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Champex-Lac?

    Between 1,468 m and 2,194 m, so 726 m of vertical drop.

    Is Champex-Lac suitable for families?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 7.8 km, are easy.

    How big is Champex-Lac?

    About 11 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
