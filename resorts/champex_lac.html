    The snow at Champex-Lac, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Champex-Lac (about 1,831 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 39 cm; half of the winters sit between 26 cm and 51 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 75 cm (64 cm–87 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 76 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    17 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 28 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1998-99 (123 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2016-17 (54 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to mid-May (153 days).

    Champex-Lac: quick answers

    Is there snow in Champex-Lac at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 39 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 28 % of 46 winters. Right now: 77 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Champex-Lac?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 76 cm). For a sure base, mid-December to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Champex-Lac get?

    About 76 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-February); between 54 cm in the leanest winter (2016-17) and 123 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high do the lifts go at Champex-Lac?

    Between 1,468 m and 2,194 m, so 726 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Champex-Lac?

    About 11 km of pistes and 4 lifts.

    About Champex-Lac

    Neither huge nor tiny, Champex-Lac offers about 11 km of pistes (number 457 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,468 m and 2,194 m above sea level. Vichères-Bavon is the next resort, 6.1 km away to the south-east.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 77 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Champex-Lac. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 61 cm by Tuesday.

    How Champex-Lac is laid out

    726 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,468 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
