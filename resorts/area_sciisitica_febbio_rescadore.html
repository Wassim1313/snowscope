    About Area Sciisitica Febbio

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Area Sciisitica Febbio sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,149 m and reach 2,060 m at the top. Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi is the next resort, 14 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 20 marked runs cover roughly 9.4 km. Most of the length is blue (39%, about 3.7 km). Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 3.0 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Area Sciisitica Febbio

    From top to bottom that makes 911 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,060 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Monte Cusna (2,120 m), Monte La Piella (2,077 m) and Sasso del Morto (2,076 m).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,604 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 5 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 1 cm, with 24 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 21 January. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 74 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and early March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Questions about Area Sciisitica Febbio

    What is the best month to ski at Area Sciisitica Febbio?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    Is there snow at Area Sciisitica Febbio at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 5 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Area Sciisitica Febbio?

    Between 1,149 m and 2,060 m, so 911 m of vertical drop.

    Is Area Sciisitica Febbio good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 3.0 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.

    How big is Area Sciisitica Febbio?

    About 10 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
