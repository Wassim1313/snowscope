    About Les Egaux

    Compact and local, Les Egaux has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 949 m and 1,081 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Col de Porte, 3.5 km to the south-west.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse: about 7.0 km of pistes, 5.1 km to the north-east. If snow is the priority, Col de Porte does better, with about 23 weeks of a good base instead of 18. The highest nearby is Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse, topping out at 1,743 m.

    What there is to ski at Les Egaux

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.7 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 71%. With 71% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,015 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 December and keeps it until about 25 April, roughly 18 weeks. A normal season tops out near 63 cm, around 16 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 38 cm, with 93 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 21 cm and 101 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 44 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    The mountain at Les Egaux

    Skied top to bottom, Les Egaux gives 132 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 949 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse, reach 1,743 m. All 4 lifts here are button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Chamechaude, 2,082 m high and 3.1 km south of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 4 chalets: Gîte Les Clarines, Notre Dame de Vette, Gîte Amalthée and Les chalets de Saint-Hugues.

    Questions about Les Egaux

    When does Les Egaux have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Les Egaux at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 38 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Egaux?

    The top is at 1,081 m and the base at 949 m.

    Is Les Egaux suitable for families?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Les Egaux?

    Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse, 5.1 km to the north-east, with about 7.0 km of pistes.
