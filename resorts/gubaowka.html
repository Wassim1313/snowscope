    About Gubałówka

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gubałówka offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 207 of 312 in the Carpathians). The slopes start at 1,045 m and reach 1,111 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Lipki (0.7 km, south).

    The mountain at Gubałówka

    66 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 2 funiculars and 4 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up.
