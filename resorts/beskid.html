    About Beskid

    Beskid is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 190 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 619 m up to 752 m. The closest other ski area is Orawka (7.0 km, south-west).

    The mountain at Beskid

    From top to bottom that makes 133 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 619 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
