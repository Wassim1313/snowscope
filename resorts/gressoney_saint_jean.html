    About Gressoney-Saint-Jean

    Gressoney-Saint-Jean is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 545 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,369 m and reach 2,043 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Brusson, 5.6 km to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Gressoney-Saint-Jean has 25 graded runs adding up to about 8.3 km. The largest share is blue, with 4.6 km (55%). With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Gressoney-Saint-Jean today and the next days

    Today there are about 18 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Gressoney-Saint-Jean. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 20 cm by Wednesday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 12 cm at mid-mountain.

    The mountain at Gressoney-Saint-Jean

    Skied top to bottom, Gressoney-Saint-Jean gives 674 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,369 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Punta di Ciampono (3,233 m), 7.8 km to the north, about 1,190 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,706 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 6 January and lasts to 10 March: about 9 weeks. Most winters land between 26 cm and 69 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 26 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 24 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 49 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 47 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Gressoney-Saint-Jean have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to early March.

    Is there snow at Gressoney-Saint-Jean at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 24 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Gressoney-Saint-Jean at?

    The top is at 2,043 m and the base at 1,369 m.

    Can beginners ski at Gressoney-Saint-Jean?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 5.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Gressoney-Saint-Jean?

    Monterosa Ski, 12 km to the north, with about 72 km of pistes.
