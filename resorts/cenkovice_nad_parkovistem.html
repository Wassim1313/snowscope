    About Čenkovice

    Čenkovice is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 153 of 257 in Central Europe by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 721 m up to 825 m. Its nearest neighbour is TJ Čenkovice, 1.0 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    104 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 721 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a button lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 773 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 1 February and lasts to 26 February: about 1 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 60 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; a snowy December gave 43 cm, a dry one 0 cm. A normal season tops out near 31 cm, around 24 February.

    Quick answers

    When does Čenkovice have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    How much snow does Čenkovice have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Čenkovice?

    Its highest lift reaches 825 m; the lowest starts at 721 m (104 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Čenkovice?

    Horský resort Buková hora, 1.0 km to the east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Čenkovice?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
