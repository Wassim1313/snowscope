    The mountain at Górka Środulska

    Skied top to bottom, Górka Środulska gives 49 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 271 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 3 lifts here are button lifts.

    Questions about Górka Środulska

    How high do the lifts go at Górka Środulska?

    Its highest lift reaches 320 m; the lowest starts at 271 m (49 m of vertical).
