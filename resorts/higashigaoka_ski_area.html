    About Higashigaoka

    Higashigaoka is a ski area. The slopes start at 15 m and reach 68 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Toyotomi, 6.3 km north, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Higashigaoka

    From top to bottom that makes 53 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 15 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a T-bar.
