    About Ventron

    Ventron has about 0.1 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 858 m up to 1,108 m. Bussang Larcenaire is the next resort, 2.6 km away to the south.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 250 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,058 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The skyline is dominated by Tête des Neufs Bois, 1,229 m high and 7.0 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Ventron usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 983 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 76 cm. A normal season tops out near 19 cm, around 17 February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Ventron

    What is the best month to ski at Ventron?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Ventron at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Ventron?

    Between 858 m and 1,108 m, so 250 m of vertical drop.
