    About Senjojiki

    Senjojiki is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 1,693 m and 2,642 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Komagane, 7.8 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Senjojiki

    The vertical drop is 949 m: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,693 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The only lift is a cable car. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns.
