    About Hakodate Nanae Snowpark

    Around 14 km of marked runs place Hakodate Nanae Snowpark among the larger resorts of Japan, number 31 by length. Its lifts climb from 257 m to 949 m. Its nearest neighbour is Niyama Kogen, 11 km west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 692 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 257 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 949 m is the highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km.
