    About Ruokolahden Freeski

    Compact and local, Ruokolahden Freeski has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 77 m and 127 m above sea level.

    Snow at Ruokolahden Freeski through a normal winter

    At about 102 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 29 December and lasts to 16 April: about 16 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 27 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 8 cm in a poor year to 40 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 53 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Most winters land between 31 cm and 67 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 33 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    50 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Ruokolahden Freeski today: about 4 cm up high. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 12 cm by now.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rantalinna (hotel), Hirsiranta (hotel), Hotelli Immalanjärvi (hotel) and Vuoksen Aalto Oy (hostel).

    Questions about Ruokolahden Freeski

    What is the best month to ski at Ruokolahden Freeski?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Ruokolahden Freeski at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 27 cm deep.

    What altitude is Ruokolahden Freeski at?

    The top is at 127 m and the base at 77 m.

    How big is Ruokolahden Freeski?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
