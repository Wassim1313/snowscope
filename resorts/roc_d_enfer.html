    About Roc d'Enfer

    Around 28 km of marked runs place Roc d'Enfer among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 216 by length. Its lifts climb from 947 m to 1,793 m. The closest other ski area is Praz de Lys Sommand (6.4 km, south).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 55 marked runs cover roughly 28 km. The largest share is red, with 16 km (59%). Intermediate runs make up 59% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 846 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 947 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 7 button lifts and 4 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The skyline is dominated by Le Roc d'Enfer, 2,244 m high and 2.6 km south-east of the resort.

    How Roc d'Enfer compares nearby

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 17 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Roc d'Enfer. 30 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 71 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 11 cm at mid-mountain).

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,370 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 December and lasts to 25 April: about 19 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 83 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 118 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 39 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 68 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 50 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find La Grand Joux (chalet), Refuge de Chavan (mountain hut), La Halte du Moulin (hotel) and Le Bois des Luison (hotel).

    Roc d'Enfer: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Roc d'Enfer?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Roc d'Enfer at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Roc d'Enfer?

    The top is at 1,793 m and the base at 947 m.

    What level of skier is Roc d'Enfer best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 59% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Roc d'Enfer?

    About 28 km of pistes and 16 lifts.
