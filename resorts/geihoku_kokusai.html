    About Geihoku Kokusai

    Around 10 km of marked runs place Geihoku Kokusai among the larger resorts of Japan, number 69 by length. Its lifts climb from 697 m to 1,117 m. The closest other ski area is Osa (4.4 km, north).

    What there is to ski at Geihoku Kokusai

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 9.8 km. The largest share is red, with 3.4 km (35%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 2.5 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Geihoku Kokusai

    The vertical drop is 420 m: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 697 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    Questions about Geihoku Kokusai

    How high do the lifts go at Geihoku Kokusai?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,117 m; the lowest starts at 697 m (420 m of vertical).

    Is Geihoku Kokusai good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 2.5 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Geihoku Kokusai have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
