    Notre Dame du Pré today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Notre Dame du Pré hold around 19 cm of snow today. 15 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 102 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 22 cm at mid-mountain.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,652 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 83 cm and 168 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 118 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 136 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 68 cm (lean years 32 cm, snowy years 108 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and early April.

    Staying nearby

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Notre Dame du Pré?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Notre Dame du Pré at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 68 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
