    About Galtür

    With some 27 km of runs, Galtür belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,628 m up to 2,295 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Silvretta-Bielerhöhe, 5.9 km to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Galtür

    In all, 52 marked runs cover roughly 27 km. The largest share is red, with 18 km (65%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 5.3 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,962 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May or later. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. A normal season tops out near 155 cm, around 24 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 92 cm and 184 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 73 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 102 cm, a dry one 37 cm. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 138 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    How Galtür is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Galtür gives 667 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,628 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 9 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Südliche Finsterkarspitze at 3,012 m, standing 717 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Galtür?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Galtür have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 73 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Galtür at?

    Between 1,628 m and 2,295 m, so 667 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Galtür best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 65% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Galtür?

    Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun, 14 km to the east, with about 163 km of pistes.
