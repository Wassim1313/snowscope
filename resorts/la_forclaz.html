    About La Forclaz

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, La Forclaz sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,697 m and reach 2,202 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Evolène (5.9 km, north-west).

    La Forclaz today and the next days

    The upper slopes of La Forclaz hold around 82 cm of snow today. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    The mountain at La Forclaz

    505 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,697 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Zermatt reaches 3,872 m.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hôtel - Gîte des Alpes (hotel), Alpiness Coliving (hotel), le Mayen à Etienne (chalet) and le Mayen à Olivier (chalet).
