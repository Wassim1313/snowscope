    About La Forclaz

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, La Forclaz sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,697 m and reach 2,202 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Evolène (5.9 km, north-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    La Forclaz has 25 graded runs adding up to about 7.6 km. The largest share is red, with 3.3 km (44%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.8 km.

    La Forclaz today and the next days

    The upper slopes of La Forclaz hold around 38 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 61 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 17 cm now.

    The mountain at La Forclaz

    505 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,697 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Zermatt reaches 3,872 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow, 3 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grand Cornier at 3,969 m, standing 1,767 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,950 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 December and keeps it until about 17 May, roughly 22 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm (lean years 19 cm, snowy years 52 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 45 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 41 cm to 82 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 66 cm.

    La Forclaz: common questions

    When does La Forclaz have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How much snow does La Forclaz have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    How high is La Forclaz?

    Between 1,697 m and 2,202 m, so 505 m of vertical drop.

    Who is La Forclaz best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 44% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Can La Forclaz make its own snow?

    Yes, on about 0.7 km of its pistes (9%).
