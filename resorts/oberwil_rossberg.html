    About Oberwil Rossberg

    Oberwil Rossberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 777 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,148 m and 1,467 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Rossberg Oberwil, 0.0 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 3.3 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.1 km (64%). With 64% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Oberwil Rossberg gives 319 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Grimmialp, reach 1,828 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Ochsen (2,188 m), 7.0 km to the north, about 721 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Oberwil Rossberg through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,308 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 8 January and keeps it until about 15 April, roughly 14 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 25 cm to 72 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 51 cm, around 18 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 24 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 42 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 30 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Oberwil Rossberg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Oberwil Rossberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Oberwil Rossberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,467 m; the lowest starts at 1,148 m (319 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Oberwil Rossberg?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 2.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Oberwil Rossberg?

    Wiriehorn, 8.4 km to the south-east, with about 10 km of pistes.
