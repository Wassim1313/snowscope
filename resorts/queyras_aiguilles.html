    Queyras in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Queyras (1,460 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 28 cm, with a normal range of 18 cm–38 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 54 cm, between 43 cm and 72 cm in a normal winter.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 60 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 11 % of winters, and by 1 January in 13 %.

    Best winter on record: 2017-18, peaking at 129 cm. Worst: 2006-07, which never passed 23 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to early April (101 days).

    Today's 26 cm compares with a usual 7 cm for 22 November.

    Queyras: quick answers

    Does Queyras have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 28 cm, and only 13 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 9 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Queyras?

    February: the snow peaks at about 60 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to early April.

    How much snow does Queyras get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 60 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 129 cm (2017-18), the thinnest 23 cm (2006-07).

    The mountain at Queyras

    From top to bottom that makes 23 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,449 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Queyras, for one, climbs to 2,805 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets, 3 lifts in all. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Petit Rochebrune, 3,078 m high and 6.1 km north of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 13 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,460 m in an ordinary winter, from early January to early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 28 cm (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 61 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 16 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 60 cm. Most winters land between 29 cm and 82 cm by mid-February.
