    With roughly 25 km of runs, Mont Orford sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. Skiing here happens between 316 m and 837 m above sea level. Parc du Mont Hatley is the next resort, 23 km away to the east.

    How Mont Orford is laid out

    The vertical drop is 521 m: runs of a moderate length. At 316 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 837 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
