    About Mont Orford

    With roughly 25 km of runs, Mont Orford sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. Skiing here happens between 316 m and 837 m above sea level. Parc du Mont Hatley is the next resort, 23 km away to the east.

    The pistes of Mont Orford

    Counting every graded piste, there are 36 runs and around 24 km to ski. Most of the length is green circle (43%, about 11 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 3.7 km of black or expert runs.

    How Mont Orford is laid out

    The vertical drop is 521 m: runs of a moderate length. At 316 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 837 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 5 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mont Orford (853 m), Mont Alfred-DesRochers (770 m) and Pic de l'Ours (740 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 12 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 576 m in an ordinary winter, from early January to late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm, with 44 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 29 cm and 72 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 52 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Questions about Mont Orford

    When does Mont Orford have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Can you ski at Mont Orford at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Mont Orford?

    The top is at 837 m and the base at 316 m.

    Is Mont Orford suitable for families?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Mont Orford have?

    About 25 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
