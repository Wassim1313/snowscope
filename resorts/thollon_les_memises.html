    Thollon-les-Mémises in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Thollon-les-Mémises (1,476 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 83 cm of cover (48 cm to 113 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 111 cm (80 cm–131 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 148 cm.

    59 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 83 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1981-82 (360 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2022-23 (90 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to mid-May (165 days).

    Common questions

    Can you ski Thollon-les-Mémises at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 83 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 83 % of 46 winters. Right now: 24 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Thollon-les-Mémises?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 148 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Thollon-les-Mémises?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 148 cm in late March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 360 cm (1981-82), the thinnest 90 cm (2022-23).

    What altitude is Thollon-les-Mémises at?

    About Thollon-les-Mémises

    Thollon-les-Mémises is a mid-sized ski area with about 19 km of pistes, number 331 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,078 m up to 1,873 m. The closest other ski area is Thollon-les-Memises (2.0 km, north-west).
