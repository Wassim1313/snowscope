    About Riksgränsen

    With some 13 km of runs, Riksgränsen belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 516 m to 900 m. Its nearest neighbour is Björkliden, 23 km east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Riksgränsen

    In all, 22 marked runs cover roughly 13 km. Most of the length is red (38%, about 4.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Riksgränsen today: about 170 cm up high. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 65 cm at mid-mountain).

    Snow at Riksgränsen through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 708 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 October and lasts to the end of May: about 32 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 6 April, with about 196 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 121 cm and 220 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early April and mid-April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 23 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 105 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 152 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 196 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    384 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Among the 4 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Narvikfjellet reaches 1,002 m. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 2 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Vássečohkka, 1,412 m high and 4.7 km east of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Hotell Riksgränsen (hotel), Niehku Mountain Villa (hotel), Arctic Lodge (hotel) and Riksgränsen Vandrarhem (hostel).

    Questions about Riksgränsen

    What is the best month to ski at Riksgränsen?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    Can you ski at Riksgränsen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 105 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Riksgränsen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 900 m; the lowest starts at 516 m (384 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Riksgränsen?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 7.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Riksgränsen?

    About 13 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
