    About Val d'Ese

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Val d'Ese sits in the middle of the size table of the Apennines. The slopes start at 1,613 m and reach 1,811 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Ghisoni, 8.6 km north, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 5.2 km in total. Most of the length is red (70%, about 3.7 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Val d'Ese gives 198 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,613 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 1,811 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
