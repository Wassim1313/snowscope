    About Gaaler Lifte

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gaaler Lifte offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 614 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 861 m and 1,224 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Schilifte Kleinlobming, 17 km to the south-east.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Gaaler Lifte has roughly 20 cm of snow on the ground today. 11 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (10 cm).

    The mountain at Gaaler Lifte

    363 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The base at 861 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Kleiner Ringkogel, 2,110 m high and 7.1 km north-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,042 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 17 cm; a snowy December gave 41 cm, a dry one 3 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 28 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 5 cm to 45 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Gaaler Lifte?

    Aim for early March and late March.

    Is there snow at Gaaler Lifte at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Gaaler Lifte?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,224 m; the lowest starts at 861 m (363 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Gaaler Lifte?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 2.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Gaaler Lifte have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
