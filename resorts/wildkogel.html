    What 46 winters say about Wildkogel

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Wildkogel (about 1,470 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 30 cm, with a normal range of 21 cm–44 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 60 cm (45 cm–71 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 69 cm.

    By 15 December 11 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 22 %.

    The deepest winter was 1999-00 (101 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2016-17 (43 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 160 days.

    So far this winter: 1 cm on 19 November (usual: 11 cm).

    About Wildkogel

    With some 69 km of runs, Wildkogel belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 814 m and 2,125 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wennser Gletscher, 4.6 km to the east.

    Snow at Wildkogel through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,470 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to early May, about 19 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 69 cm, around 29 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 42 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 30 cm, with 54 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 35 cm and 80 cm by mid-February.

    The mountain at Wildkogel

    Skied top to bottom, Wildkogel gives 1,311 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 814 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 21 lifts: 5 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 6 T-bars, 3 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Großer Finagl at 2,730 m, standing 605 m above the top lift.
