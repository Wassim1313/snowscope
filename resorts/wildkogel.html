    About Wildkogel

    With some 69 km of runs, Wildkogel belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 814 m and 2,125 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wennser Gletscher, 4.6 km to the east.

    The mountain at Wildkogel

    Skied top to bottom, Wildkogel gives 1,311 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 814 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.

    Wildkogel and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 22 km north to SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser, with about 238 km of runs. For higher slopes, Zillertal Arena climbs to 2,501 m.
