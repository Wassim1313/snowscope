    About Tyin-Filefjell

    Around 15 km of marked runs place Tyin-Filefjell among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 46 by length. Its lifts climb from 833 m to 1,251 m. Its nearest neighbour is Solheisen, 32 km south, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 17 graded runs, about 15 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 5.9 km (38%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.4 km.

    Snow at Tyin-Filefjell through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,042 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late October to late May or later, about 31 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 115 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 77 cm in a poor year to 149 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 176 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 24 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 179 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 122 cm to 205 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    How Tyin-Filefjell is laid out

    418 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Vardhovdtinden, 1,456 m high and 5.6 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow this week

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Filefjellstuene (mountain hut), Tyinkrysset fjellstue (mountain hut), Kyrkjestølen (guesthouse) and Tyinstølen Turisthytte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Tyin-Filefjell have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    How much snow does Tyin-Filefjell have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 115 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Tyin-Filefjell?

    The top is at 1,251 m and the base at 833 m.

    Is Tyin-Filefjell suitable for families?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 6.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Tyin-Filefjell have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
