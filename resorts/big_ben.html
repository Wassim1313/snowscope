    How much snow Big Ben usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 65 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 31 January and lasts to 14 March: about 6 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm, with 29 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 38 cm. Most winters land between 21 cm and 57 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Big Ben

    When is the best time to ski at Big Ben?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Big Ben have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 16 cm deep.
