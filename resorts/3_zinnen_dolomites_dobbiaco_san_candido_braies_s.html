    The snow at 3 Zinnen Dolomites (Dobbiaco, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of 3 Zinnen Dolomites (Dobbiaco (about 1,678 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 33 cm of cover (20 cm to 49 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 52 cm; the middle half of winters range from 43 cm to 66 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 56 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 15 % of winters, and by 1 January in 26 %.

    The deepest winter was 2013-14 (142 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2016-17 (42 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early December to early May (164 days).
