    About Wildcat Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Wildcat Mountain offers about 26 km of pistes (number 27 of 93 in US Northeast). The ski area spans the mountain from 593 m up to 1,211 m. The closest other ski area is Tuckerman Ravine (3.6 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Wildcat Mountain has 50 graded runs adding up to about 26 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 48%. Intermediate runs make up 48% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 5.8 km.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 618 m: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 593 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,211 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Above the ski area rises Mount Washington (1,917 m), 5.6 km to the west, about 706 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    At about 902 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 December and lasts to 23 April: about 19 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 58 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Most winters land between 46 cm and 114 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm; a snowy December gave 62 cm, a dry one 11 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 88 cm.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Wildcat Mountain include Joe Dodge Lodge (hotel), Carter Notch Hut (mountain hut), Harvard Cabin (mountain hut) and Dana Place Inn (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Wildcat Mountain: common questions

    When does Wildcat Mountain have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Wildcat Mountain have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 38 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Wildcat Mountain?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,211 m; the lowest starts at 593 m (618 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Wildcat Mountain best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 48% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Wildcat Mountain?

    About 26 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
