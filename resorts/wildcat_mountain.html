    About Wildcat Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Wildcat Mountain offers about 26 km of pistes (number 27 of 93 in US Northeast). The ski area spans the mountain from 593 m up to 1,211 m. The closest other ski area is Tuckerman Ravine (3.6 km, west).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 618 m: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 593 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,211 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Wildcat Mountain include Joe Dodge Lodge (hotel), Carter Notch Hut (mountain hut), Harvard Cabin (mountain hut) and Dana Place Inn (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
