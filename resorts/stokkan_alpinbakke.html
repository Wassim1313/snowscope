    About Stokkan Alpinbakke

    Stokkan Alpinbakke is a ski area. The slopes start at 195 m and reach 273 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Gråkallparken, 12 km west, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Stokkan Alpinbakke today and the next days

    Today there are about 8 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Stokkan Alpinbakke. The last seven days brought 11 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 16 cm by Saturday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Stokkan Alpinbakke gives 78 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Vassfjellet skisenter, reach 656 m.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Scandic Lerkendal, Scandic Solsiden, Singsaker Sommerhotell and Clarion Hotel Trondheim.

    Questions about Stokkan Alpinbakke

    How high do the lifts go at Stokkan Alpinbakke?

    Its highest lift reaches 273 m; the lowest starts at 195 m (78 m of vertical).
