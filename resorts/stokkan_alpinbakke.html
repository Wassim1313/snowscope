    About Stokkan Alpinbakke

    Stokkan Alpinbakke is a ski area. The slopes start at 195 m and reach 273 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Gråkallparken, 12 km west, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    Stokkan Alpinbakke today and the next days

    Today there are about 12 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Stokkan Alpinbakke. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 4 cm by Tuesday. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 22 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Stokkan Alpinbakke gives 78 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Vassfjellet skisenter, reach 656 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Liaåsen at 429 m, standing 156 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Stokkan Alpinbakke through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 234 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 76 cm, around 2 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 53 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 39 cm and 95 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm; a snowy December gave 56 cm, a dry one 18 cm.

    Questions about Stokkan Alpinbakke

    When does Stokkan Alpinbakke have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Stokkan Alpinbakke at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 36 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Stokkan Alpinbakke?

    Its highest lift reaches 273 m; the lowest starts at 195 m (78 m of vertical).
