    About Hausberglift

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Hausberglift one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 781 m to 896 m. The closest other ski area is Lärchenhof (4.5 km, west).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.4 km north to Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, with about 37 km of runs. The highest nearby is Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, topping out at 1,853 m.

    How Hausberglift is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 115 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 981 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.
