    About Hausberglift

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Hausberglift one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 781 m to 896 m. The closest other ski area is Lärchenhof (4.5 km, west).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.4 km north to Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, with about 37 km of runs. Heutal has the longer snow season: around 22 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Hausberglift. The highest nearby is Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, topping out at 1,853 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    How Hausberglift is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 115 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 981 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Mitterhorn (2,506 m), 6.0 km to the south-east, about 1,610 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 838 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Year to year the snow is steady, with 10 cm to 50 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 15 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 11 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 28 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and late January.

    Hausberglift: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Hausberglift?

    Aim for early January and late January.

    Can you ski at Hausberglift at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 15 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Hausberglift?

    Its highest lift reaches 896 m; the lowest starts at 781 m (115 m of vertical).

    Is Hausberglift good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hausberglift?

    Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, 5.4 km to the north, with about 37 km of pistes.
