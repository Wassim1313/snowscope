    About Bellows Falls Ski Tow

    Bellows Falls Ski Tow is a small ski area by the standards of US Northeast, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 127 m to 197 m. Timber Ridge is the next resort, 24 km away to the west.

    The mountain at Bellows Falls Ski Tow

    Skied top to bottom, Bellows Falls Ski Tow gives 70 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 127 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Stratton, for one, climbs to 1,183 m. The only lift is a rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hartley Hill at 443 m, standing 246 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 162 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm, with 27 cm in the best years. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 49 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 12 February, with about 22 cm.
