    About Banff Sunshine Village

    About 77 km of pistes make Banff Sunshine Village one of the ten biggest ski areas of Canada West (number 8). The slopes start at 1,661 m and reach 2,686 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Mt Norquay, 17 km to the north-east.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Banff Sunshine Village has roughly 52 cm of snow on the ground today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    How Banff Sunshine Village is laid out

    1,025 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,661 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,686 m.
