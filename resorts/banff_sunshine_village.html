    About Banff Sunshine Village

    About 77 km of pistes make Banff Sunshine Village one of the ten biggest ski areas of Canada West (number 8). The slopes start at 1,661 m and reach 2,686 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Mt Norquay, 17 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 152 marked runs cover roughly 76 km. Most of the length is black diamond (41%, about 31 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 41% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 31 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,174 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 13 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 98 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 69 cm and 121 cm. A normal season tops out near 102 cm, around 12 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 72 cm (lean years 46 cm, snowy years 108 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 3 weeks.

    How Banff Sunshine Village is laid out

    1,025 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,661 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,686 m. Getting up the mountain: 3 gondolas, 9 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 14 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Mount Bourgeau (2,930 m), 5.1 km to the north, about 244 m higher than the top lift.

    Banff Sunshine Village: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Banff Sunshine Village?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Banff Sunshine Village at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 72 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Banff Sunshine Village open for the 2026/27 season?

    It opened on 6 November 2026.

    How high is Banff Sunshine Village?

    Between 1,661 m and 2,686 m, so 1,025 m of vertical drop.

    Does Banff Sunshine Village have steep runs?

    Yes: about 31 km of its graded pistes (41%) are difficult.
