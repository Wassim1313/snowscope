    About Las Araucarias (Formerly Llaima)

    Las Araucarias (Formerly Llaima) is a mid-sized ski area with about 11 km of pistes, number 17 of 30 in Andes by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,479 m up to 1,910 m. Its nearest neighbour is Nevados de Vilcún, 1.7 km south, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 10 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Most of the length is black (77%, about 8.4 km). Good skiers will like it here; 77% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 8.4 km.

    How Las Araucarias (Formerly Llaima) is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Las Araucarias (Formerly Llaima) gives 431 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,479 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
