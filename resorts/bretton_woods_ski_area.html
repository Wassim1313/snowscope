    About Bretton Woods

    Around 41 km of marked runs place Bretton Woods among the larger resorts of US Northeast, number 13 by length. The slopes start at 487 m and reach 912 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tuckerman Ravine, 15 km to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 88 runs and around 41 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 21 km, or 50%. Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 8.0 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 700 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 33 cm (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 57 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 38 cm to 105 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 75 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 38 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    How Bretton Woods is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bretton Woods gives 425 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 487 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 moving carpets, 9 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Mount Field (1,301 m), 6.9 km to the south, about 389 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Bretton Woods have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Bretton Woods at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 33 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Bretton Woods at?

    The top is at 912 m and the base at 487 m.

    What level of skier is Bretton Woods best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Bretton Woods?

    About 41 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
