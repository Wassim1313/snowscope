    About Горнолыжный клуб “Чертовицы”

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Горнолыжный клуб “Чертовицы” one of the smaller places to ski in Russia. The slopes start at 115 m and reach 154 m at the top.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Горнолыжный клуб “Чертовицы” has 7 graded runs adding up to about 1.2 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.6 km (46%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 39 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 115 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 4 button lifts and 2 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
