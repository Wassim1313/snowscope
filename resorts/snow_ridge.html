    About Snow Ridge

    Snow Ridge is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 48 of 93 in US Northeast by length. Its lifts climb from 398 m to 538 m. Its nearest neighbour is McCauley Mountain, 37 km east, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Snow Ridge

    In all, 19 marked runs cover roughly 9.6 km. Most of the length is blue square (47%, about 4.5 km). Intermediate runs make up 47% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.9 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 468 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 January and lasts to 27 March: about 11 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 25 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 51 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 60 cm. Most winters land between 27 cm and 83 cm by mid-February.

    Lifts and terrain

    140 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 398 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 4 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Gomer Hill, 643 m high and 5.4 km west of the resort.

    Questions about Snow Ridge

    What is the best month to ski at Snow Ridge?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Can you ski at Snow Ridge at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 25 cm deep.

    How high is Snow Ridge?

    Its highest lift reaches 538 m; the lowest starts at 398 m (140 m of vertical).

    Does Snow Ridge have steep runs?

    Yes: about 3.9 km of its graded pistes (41%) are difficult.

    How big is Snow Ridge?

    About 10 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
