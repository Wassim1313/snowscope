    About Ski areál Razula

    Compact and local, Ski areál Razula has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 577 m up to 779 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Horal, 2.8 km to the east.

    What there is to ski at Ski areál Razula

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.2 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 100%. Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The mountain at Ski areál Razula

    202 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 577 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 779 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 2 lifts here are T-bars.
