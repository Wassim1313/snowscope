    About Tuckerman Ravine

    Tuckerman Ravine is a ski area. Its nearest neighbour is Wildcat Mountain, 3.6 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 867 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 38 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 61 cm, a dry one 12 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 113 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 84 cm, around 15 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 54 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    The mountain at Tuckerman Ravine

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Washington (1,917 m), Ball Crag (1,843 m) and Agiocochook Crag (1,748 m).

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Tuckerman Ravine hold around 25 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 4 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 18 cm by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 10 cm at mid-mountain.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Joe Dodge Lodge (hotel), Harvard Cabin (mountain hut), Lakes of the Clouds Hut (mountain hut) and Dana Place Inn (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Tuckerman Ravine: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Tuckerman Ravine?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Tuckerman Ravine have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
