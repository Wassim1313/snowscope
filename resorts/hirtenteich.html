    About Hirtenteich

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Hirtenteich sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The slopes start at 573 m and reach 689 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Olstalb-Lifte Aalen, 7.4 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Hirtenteich is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Hirtenteich gives 116 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 573 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.
