    About Germundö Alpin

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Germundö Alpin one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 2 m and 41 m above sea level.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 22 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 24 February, with about 7 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 26 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 12 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 39 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 button lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Hjortö stugor & stockhus (chalet), Kvarnbo Pensionat (guesthouse), Runneröds stugor (chalet) and Stalldalen (hotel).

    Questions about Germundö Alpin

    When is the best time to ski at Germundö Alpin?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at Germundö Alpin at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Germundö Alpin at?

    The top is at 41 m and the base at 2 m.

    How many km of pistes does Germundö Alpin have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
