    About Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret)

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret) sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 648 m to 722 m. The closest other ski area is Uvdal Alpinpark (0.3 km, east).

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 11 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret). Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 28 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    How Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret) is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret) gives 74 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 6 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Uvdal Alpinpark reaches 1,209 m. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Synhovd (1,437 m), 7.8 km to the south-west, about 715 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret) through a normal winter

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 685 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. Most winters land between 31 cm and 80 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm; a snowy December gave 63 cm, a dry one 10 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. A normal season tops out near 64 cm, around 11 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 50 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret): common questions

    When does Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret) have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret) at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 36 cm deep.

    What altitude is Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret) at?

    Its highest lift reaches 722 m; the lowest starts at 648 m (74 m of vertical).

    Who is Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret) best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Uvdal alpinsenter (Senteret)?

    Ski Geilo, 32 km to the north-west, with about 35 km of pistes.
