    About Chalmazel

    Around 9.0 km of marked runs place Chalmazel among the larger resorts of Central Europe, number 21 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,108 m to 1,592 m. Prabouré is the next resort, 12 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Chalmazel

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 8.8 km. The largest share is red, with 3.7 km (42%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.4 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    484 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 1 chairlift and 7 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Questions about Chalmazel

    How high is Chalmazel?

    The top is at 1,592 m and the base at 1,108 m.

    Is Chalmazel suitable for families?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 3.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Chalmazel have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
