    About Sakae Club

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Sakae Club sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The slopes start at 396 m and reach 800 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Matsunoyama Onsen, 6.7 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Sakae Club

    The piste map lists 10 graded runs, about 6.1 km in total. The largest share is green, with 2.9 km (48%). No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them.

    How Sakae Club is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 404 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 396 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.
