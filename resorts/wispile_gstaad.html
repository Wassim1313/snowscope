    About Wispile

    Wispile is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 638 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,044 m up to 1,933 m. Gstaad is the next resort, 2.5 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of Wispile

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 5.6 km in total. Most of the length is blue (58%, about 3.2 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,488 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 December and lasts to 8 May: about 20 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 74 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 49 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 96 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm, from 12 cm in a poor year to 54 cm in a big one.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Wispile gives 889 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 1 gondola and 3 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Giferspitz (2,542 m), 4.6 km to the east, about 609 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Wispile

    What is the best month to ski at Wispile?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Wispile at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Wispile?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,933 m; the lowest starts at 1,044 m (889 m of vertical).

    Is Wispile good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Wispile?

    Schönried, 8.0 km to the north-east, with about 59 km of pistes.
