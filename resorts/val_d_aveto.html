    About Val d'Aveto

    Val d'Aveto is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 680 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,276 m and reach 1,777 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Area Sciistica Pian del Poggio M (27 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Val d'Aveto

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 5.0 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.9 km, or 57%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Val d'Aveto gives 501 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,777 m is the highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Monte Maggiorasca (1,798 m), Monte Bue (1,772 m) and Monte Nero (1,752 m).

    How much snow Val d'Aveto usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,526 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 26 January, with about 3 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 50 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 1 cm (snow lay here in 4 winters out of ten), with 8 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rifugio Prato della Cipolla (mountain hut), Rifugio Monte Bue (mountain hut), Albergo Ristorante La Baita (hotel) and La Soldanella (guesthouse). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Val d'Aveto have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    How much snow does Val d'Aveto have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 4 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Val d'Aveto at?

    Between 1,276 m and 1,777 m, so 501 m of vertical drop.

    Is Val d'Aveto good for beginners?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Val d'Aveto?

    Area Sciistica Schia Monte Caio, 55 km to the east, with about 12 km of pistes.
