    About Harpefossen skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Harpefossen skisenter offers about 10 km of pistes (number 77 of 262 in Scandinavia). Skiing here happens between 248 m and 812 m above sea level. Volda skisenter is the next resort, 25 km away to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 9.8 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 5.1 km, or 51%. With 57% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Harpefossen skisenter

    564 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. All 6 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Felden, 1,272 m high and 6.2 km north of the resort.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Harpefossen skisenter has roughly 34 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 9 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Monday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 48 cm at mid-mountain.

    Snow at Harpefossen skisenter through a normal winter

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 530 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 89 cm, from 46 cm in a poor year to 134 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 198 cm, around 21 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 98 cm to 234 cm in mid-February in most winters. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 185 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Harpefossen skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Harpefossen skisenter have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 89 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Harpefossen skisenter?

    The top is at 812 m and the base at 248 m.

    Is Harpefossen skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 5.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Harpefossen skisenter have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
