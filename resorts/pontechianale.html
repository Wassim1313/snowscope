    What 46 winters say about Pontechianale

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Pontechianale (2,129 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 46 cm of cover (31 cm to 73 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 85 cm; the middle half of winters range from 57 cm to 119 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 98 cm.

    By 15 December 30 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 43 %.

    Best winter on record: 2017-18, peaking at 217 cm. Worst: 2006-07, which never passed 45 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until late April in a normal winter, roughly 152 days.

    This winter so far: 4 cm on 19 November, against a usual 13 cm for the date.

    Pontechianale: quick answers

    Does Pontechianale have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 46 cm, and only 43 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 29 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Pontechianale?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 98 cm). For a sure base, early December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Pontechianale get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 98 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 217 cm (2017-18), the thinnest 45 cm (2006-07).

    How high is Pontechianale?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,659 m; the lowest starts at 1,599 m (1,060 m of vertical).

    Is Pontechianale good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 3.2 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Pontechianale?

    Queyras, 16 km to the north-west, with about 30 km of pistes.

    About Pontechianale

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pontechianale offers about 11 km of pistes (number 472 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,599 m to 2,659 m. The closest other ski area is Crissolo (11 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 12 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. The largest share is red, with 5.7 km (52%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.2 km.

    The mountain at Pontechianale

    The vertical drop is 1,060 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,599 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 3 button lifts, 4 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monviso at 3,841 m, standing 1,182 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Pontechianale through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,129 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 9 December and keeps it until about 29 April, roughly 20 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 50 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 46 cm, with 98 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 98 cm, around 7 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 41 cm and 133 cm.
