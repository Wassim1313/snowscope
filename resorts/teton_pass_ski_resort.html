    About Teton Pass

    Neither huge nor tiny, Teton Pass offers about 15 km of pistes (number 114 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 1,887 m to 2,163 m.

    The pistes of Teton Pass

    In all, 39 marked runs cover roughly 15 km. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 6.6 km, or 44%. Good skiers will like it here; 44% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.6 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,025 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 November and keeps it until about 18 May, roughly 25 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 28 February, with about 148 cm. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 122 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 75 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 106 cm, a dry one 39 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 78 cm and 193 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 276 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 1,887 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mount Wright at 2,701 m, standing 538 m above the top lift.

    Teton Pass: common questions

    When does Teton Pass have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Teton Pass at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 75 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Teton Pass?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,163 m; the lowest starts at 1,887 m (276 m of vertical).

    Does Teton Pass have steep runs?

    Yes: about 6.6 km of its graded pistes (44%) are difficult.

    How big is Teton Pass?

    About 15 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
