    About Teton Pass

    Neither huge nor tiny, Teton Pass offers about 15 km of pistes (number 114 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 1,887 m to 2,163 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 276 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 1,887 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter.
