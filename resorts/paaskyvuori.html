    About Pääskyvuori

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Pääskyvuori one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 85 m up to 180 m. Its nearest neighbour is Varkaus, 39 km west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 132 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 6 January and keeps it until about 17 April, roughly 15 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 32 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Most winters land between 27 cm and 62 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 47 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 24 cm (lean years 8 cm, snowy years 38 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 95 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 T-bars and 1 button lift.

    Pääskyvuori: common questions

    When does Pääskyvuori have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Pääskyvuori have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Pääskyvuori at?

    The top is at 180 m and the base at 85 m.

    How big is Pääskyvuori?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
