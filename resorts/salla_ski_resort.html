    About Salla

    Neither huge nor tiny, Salla offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 102 of 262 in Scandinavia). Skiing here happens between 251 m and 462 m above sea level.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 11 graded runs, about 7.2 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.2 km, or 59%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Lifts and terrain

    211 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 7 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Sallatunturi (479 m), Sallatunturi (477 m) and Ruuhitunturi (439 m).

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 356 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 5 December and keeps it until about 8 May, roughly 22 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 60 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 40 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 27 cm, snowy years 51 cm). Most winters land between 53 cm and 74 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 66 cm.

    Salla: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Salla?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Salla at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 40 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Salla?

    Its highest lift reaches 462 m; the lowest starts at 251 m (211 m of vertical).

    Is Salla good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Salla?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
