    About Rossberg Oberwil

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Rossberg Oberwil sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,148 m up to 1,467 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Oberwil Rossberg, 0.0 km to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 3.3 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.1 km (64%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Rossberg Oberwil

    From top to bottom that makes 319 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Grimmialp, for one, climbs to 1,828 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Ochsen, 2,188 m high and 7.0 km north of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,308 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 January and lasts to 15 April: about 14 weeks. A normal season tops out near 51 cm, around 18 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 24 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 42 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 25 cm and 72 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 30 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Rossberg Oberwil: common questions

    When does Rossberg Oberwil have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Rossberg Oberwil at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Rossberg Oberwil at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,467 m; the lowest starts at 1,148 m (319 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Rossberg Oberwil?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 2.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rossberg Oberwil?

    Wiriehorn, 8.4 km to the south-east, with about 10 km of pistes.
