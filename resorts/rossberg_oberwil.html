    About Rossberg Oberwil

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Rossberg Oberwil sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,148 m up to 1,467 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Oberwil Rossberg, 0.0 km to the north.

    The mountain at Rossberg Oberwil

    From top to bottom that makes 319 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Grimmialp, for one, climbs to 1,828 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Niderhornhütte (mountain hut), Team House (hostel), Rawyl (hotel) and Chalet Betula (guesthouse).
