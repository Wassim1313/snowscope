    About Stryn Sommerski

    Stryn Sommerski is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 118 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Skiing here happens between 1,067 m and 1,562 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Stryn Vinterski (35 km, west).

    The mountain at Stryn Sommerski

    495 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Raudeggi at 1,938 m, standing 376 m above the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Stryn Sommerski

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 6.3 km in total. Most of the length is red (100%, about 6.3 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Snow at Stryn Sommerski through a normal winter

    At about 1,314 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 October and lasts to the end of May: about 33 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 22 March, with about 274 cm. For about 28 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 170 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 222 cm, a dry one 112 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 163 cm and 317 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 263 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Questions about Stryn Sommerski

    What is the best month to ski at Stryn Sommerski?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-October to late May.

    Is there snow at Stryn Sommerski at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 170 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Stryn Sommerski?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,562 m; the lowest starts at 1,067 m (495 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Stryn Sommerski best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Stryn Sommerski have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
