    About Langis

    Compact and local, Langis has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,448 m up to 1,506 m. The closest other ski area is Heiligkreuz (7.6 km, north-west).

    Langis and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 12 km south-west to Sörenberg, with about 38 km of runs. Sörenberg reaches 2,257 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The mountain at Langis

    Skied top to bottom, Langis gives 58 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,448 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Langis today: about 43 cm up high. 10 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 30 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Langis include Berghotel Langis (hotel), Gästehaus Kaltbad (guesthouse), Skihütte Brend (mountain hut) and Landgasthof Grossteil (hotel).
