    About Langis

    Compact and local, Langis has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,448 m up to 1,506 m. The closest other ski area is Heiligkreuz (7.6 km, north-west).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,477 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late May, about 23 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 63 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 94 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 73 cm, around 3 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 41 cm, from 18 cm in a poor year to 55 cm in a big one.

    The mountain at Langis

    Skied top to bottom, Langis gives 58 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,448 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Fürstein (2,040 m), 4.0 km to the west, about 534 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Langis today: about 4 cm up high. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 38 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 16 cm at mid-mountain).

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Langis include Berghotel Langis (hotel), Gästehaus Kaltbad (guesthouse), Skihütte Brend (mountain hut) and Landgasthof Grossteil (hotel).

    Questions about Langis

    When is the best time to ski at Langis?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late May.

    How much snow does Langis have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 41 cm deep.

    What altitude is Langis at?

    Between 1,448 m and 1,506 m, so 58 m of vertical drop.

    Is there a larger resort near Langis?

    Sörenberg, 12 km to the south-west, with about 38 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Langis have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
