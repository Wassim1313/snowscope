    About Macugnaga

    Neither huge nor tiny, Macugnaga offers about 10 km of pistes (number 494 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,703 m up to 2,877 m. Macugnaga is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 13 graded runs, about 9.9 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.2 km, or 73%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 1.9 km of difficult runs.

    How much snow Macugnaga usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,290 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late May, about 24 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 39 cm and 90 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm, from 21 cm in a poor year to 57 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 69 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 68 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,174 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,703 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 2 cable cars, 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Rimpfischhorn, 4,198 m high and 8.0 km north-west of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Rifugio Gaspare Oberto - Paolo Maroli (mountain hut), Rifugio Gaspare Oberto e Paolo Maroli (mountain hut), Casa Alpina De Filippi (hotel) and Hotel Flora (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Macugnaga: common questions

    When does Macugnaga have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late May.

    Is there snow at Macugnaga at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    What altitude is Macugnaga at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,877 m; the lowest starts at 1,703 m (1,174 m of vertical).

    Who is Macugnaga best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Macugnaga?

    Saas Fee, 12 km to the north, with about 48 km of pistes.
