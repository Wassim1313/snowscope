    About Wasen

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Wasen one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 845 m up to 949 m. The closest other ski area is Walterswil (8.5 km, north).

    How Wasen compares nearby

    For more terrain, Netschbuehl Eggiwill (16 km south) has about 3.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Heiligkreuz has the longer snow season: around 19 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Wasen. For higher slopes, Heiligkreuz climbs to 1,460 m.

    The pistes of Wasen

    Wasen has 4 graded runs adding up to about 1.2 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.2 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Wasen

    The vertical drop is 104 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 845 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Farnli-Esel (1,382 m), 7.4 km to the south-east, about 433 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Wasen usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 897 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 34 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 7 cm, with 22 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 18 February, with about 16 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Wasen: common questions

    When does Wasen have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Wasen at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 7 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Wasen?

    Between 845 m and 949 m, so 104 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Wasen?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Wasen?

    Netschbuehl Eggiwill, 16 km to the south, with about 3.0 km of pistes.
