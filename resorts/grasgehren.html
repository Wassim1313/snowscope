    About Grasgehren

    Neither huge nor tiny, Grasgehren offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 510 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,436 m and reach 1,674 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Riedbergerhorn (3.4 km, north-west).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,555 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late May. A normal season tops out near 95 cm, around 29 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 59 cm and 122 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 72 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 47 cm, from 20 cm in a poor year to 71 cm in a big one.

    How Grasgehren is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Grasgehren gives 238 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,436 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 3 T-bars. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Torkopf (1,930 m), 8.0 km to the south-west, about 256 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Wannenkopfhütte (mountain hut), Berghaus Schwaben (mountain hut), Schwabenhof (hotel) and Berggasthaus Rohrmoos (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Grasgehren

    When does Grasgehren have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late May.

    How much snow does Grasgehren have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 47 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Grasgehren?

    Between 1,436 m and 1,674 m, so 238 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Grasgehren best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 65% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Grasgehren?

    Balderschwang, 5.0 km to the west, with about 26 km of pistes.
