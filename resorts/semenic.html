    About Semenic

    Around 10 km of marked runs place Semenic among the larger resorts of the Carpathians, number 18 by length. The slopes start at 1,186 m and reach 1,428 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Casa Baraj, 2.7 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Semenic

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 9.8 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 9.2 km, or 94%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Semenic

    The vertical drop is 242 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 4 lifts break down into 2 T-bars and 2 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Semenic at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,428 m; the lowest starts at 1,186 m (242 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Semenic best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 94% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Semenic have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
