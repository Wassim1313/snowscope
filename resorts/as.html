    About Aš

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Aš sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 646 m and 750 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Aš (0.1 km, south-west).

    How Aš compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Skiwelt Schöneck: about 3.0 km of pistes, 19 km to the north-east. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 104 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 646 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    Snow at Aš through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 698 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 3 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 19 cm). By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 27 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the best snow, aim for mid-December and late December. A normal season tops out near 10 cm, around 25 January.

    Aš: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Aš?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    Can you ski at Aš at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).

    What altitude is Aš at?

    The top is at 750 m and the base at 646 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Aš?

    Skiwelt Schöneck, 19 km to the north-east, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Aš?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
