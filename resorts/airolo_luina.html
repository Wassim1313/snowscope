    About Airolo Lüina

    Airolo Lüina has about 0.5 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,178 m and 1,251 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Airolo Pesciüm (2.3 km, south).

    Airolo Lüina today and the next days

    Today there are about 13 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Airolo Lüina. The last seven days brought 29 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 12 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 73 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Gemsstock, for one, climbs to 2,958 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Pizzo Centrale, 2,999 m high and 5.3 km north of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,214 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 January and lasts to 24 March: about 10 weeks. Most winters land between 20 cm and 72 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 51 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 18 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 22 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 51 cm in a big one.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Albergo Motta (hotel), Hotel des Alpes (hotel), Hotel Forni (hotel) and B&B Dotta (guesthouse).

    Questions about Airolo Lüina

    When does Airolo Lüina have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Can you ski at Airolo Lüina at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Airolo Lüina?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,251 m; the lowest starts at 1,178 m (73 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Airolo Lüina have?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
