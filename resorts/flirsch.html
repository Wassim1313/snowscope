    About Flirsch

    Flirsch has about 0.3 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,158 m to 1,219 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Arlberg, 6.3 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Flirsch hold around 17 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 23 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 12 cm now.

    Lifts and terrain

    61 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: St. Anton/St. Christoph/Stuben, for one, climbs to 2,642 m. The skyline is dominated by Hoher Riffler (Nord), 3,167 m high and 4.8 km south-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,188 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to mid-March, about 8 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 22 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 5 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 26 cm to 63 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 42 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 27 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Basur (hotel), Troschana (hotel), Hotel Pezina (hotel) and Haus Dorfschmied (guesthouse).

    Questions about Flirsch

    What is the best month to ski at Flirsch?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Flirsch at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Flirsch?

    The top is at 1,219 m and the base at 1,158 m.
