    About Piesendorf

    Piesendorf is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 846 m and reach 1,076 m at the top. Lechnerberglifte is the next resort, 6.8 km away to the east.

    What there is to ski at Piesendorf

    Piesendorf has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.7 km. The largest share is red, with 1.1 km (67%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How Piesendorf is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 230 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 846 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kaprun reaches 3,024 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows, 3 lifts in all. Above the ski area rises Tristkogel (2,642 m), 7.2 km to the south, about 1,566 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 961 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Most winters land between 14 cm and 38 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 21 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 26 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 29 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Waidachhof (hotel), Neue Bubenreuther Hütte (mountain hut), tauernhex (hotel) and Haus Törghele (guesthouse).

    Questions about Piesendorf

    What is the best month to ski at Piesendorf?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Piesendorf have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Piesendorf?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,076 m; the lowest starts at 846 m (230 m of vertical).

    Who is Piesendorf best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Piesendorf?

    Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L, 14 km to the north, with about 267 km of pistes.
