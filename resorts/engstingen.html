    About Engstingen

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Engstingen sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 697 m up to 775 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wintersport Arena Holzelfingen, 4.7 km to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 4.3 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.7 km, or 62%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 736 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The snow is usually deepest around 20 January, with about 4 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 17 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 10 cm in a big one.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Engstingen gives 78 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 697 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Skilift Albstadt-Tailfingen, reach 932 m. To get up there are 3 drag lifts and 3 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Hohfleck (872 m), Sternberg (844 m) and Feldberg (838 m).

    Engstingen: common questions

    When does Engstingen have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Is there snow at Engstingen at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    How high is Engstingen?

    Its highest lift reaches 775 m; the lowest starts at 697 m (78 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Engstingen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 62% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Engstingen?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
