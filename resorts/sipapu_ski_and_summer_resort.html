    About Sipapu Ski and Summer Resort

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sipapu Ski and Summer Resort offers about 13 km of pistes (number 119 of 175 in US West). The ski area spans the mountain from 2,498 m up to 2,821 m. Its nearest neighbour is Angel Fire Resort, 37 km north-east, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 35 runs and around 12 km to ski. Most of the length is blue square (48%, about 5.7 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.4 km.

    This week's snow

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Sipapu Ski and Summer Resort gives 323 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,498 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Ski Santa Fe, for one, climbs to 3,686 m. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 4 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Ripley Point at 3,594 m, standing 773 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,660 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 75 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 25 cm, around 3 March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 43 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February.

    Questions about Sipapu Ski and Summer Resort

    When is the best time to ski at Sipapu Ski and Summer Resort?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Is there snow at Sipapu Ski and Summer Resort at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    What altitude is Sipapu Ski and Summer Resort at?

    The top is at 2,821 m and the base at 2,498 m.

    What level of skier is Sipapu Ski and Summer Resort best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 48% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sipapu Ski and Summer Resort?

    Angel Fire Resort, 37 km to the north-east, with about 53 km of pistes.
