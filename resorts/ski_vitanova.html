    About Ski Vitanová

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski Vitanová offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 110 of 312 in the Carpathians). Skiing here happens between 724 m and 952 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Skipark Meander Oravice (4.4 km, south).

    The pistes of Ski Vitanová

    Ski Vitanová has 5 graded runs adding up to about 3.0 km. Most of the length is blue (61%, about 1.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Ski Vitanová

    The vertical drop is 228 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 724 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 button lift.
