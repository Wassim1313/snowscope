    About Kanazawa Municipal

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kanazawa Municipal offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 287 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 388 m and 537 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is IOX-AROSA, 5.5 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 1.8 km. Most of the length is green (66%, about 1.2 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 13 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Kanazawa Municipal. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 4 cm by Saturday.

    How Kanazawa Municipal is laid out

    149 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 388 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Quick answers

    How high is Kanazawa Municipal?

    Its highest lift reaches 537 m; the lowest starts at 388 m (149 m of vertical).

    Is Kanazawa Municipal suitable for families?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Kanazawa Municipal?

    Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour, 21 km to the south, with about 9.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Kanazawa Municipal?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
