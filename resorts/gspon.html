    About Gspon

    Gspon is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,866 m up to 2,192 m. Its nearest neighbour is Visperterminen, 3.3 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km north-west to Unterbäch, with about 37 km of runs. Visperterminen has the longer snow season: around 26 weeks of solid cover, compared with 22 at Gspon. The highest nearby is Saas Grund, topping out at 3,142 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 2.4 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 73%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Gspon today: about 39 cm up high. 4 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 28 cm by Sunday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 19 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 326 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 1,866 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Saas Grund, for one, climbs to 3,142 m. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 2 cable cars and 2 T-bars. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Böshorn, 3,269 m high and 7.2 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,029 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 December and lasts to 18 May: about 22 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm; a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 22 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 61 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 56 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 41 cm to 74 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    Quick answers

    When does Gspon have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Gspon at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 36 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Gspon at?

    The top is at 2,192 m and the base at 1,866 m.

    What level of skier is Gspon best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Does Gspon have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 1.9 km of its pistes (79%).
