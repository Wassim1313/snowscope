    About Șureanu

    Șureanu is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 11 km of pistes put it at number 12 of 312. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,685 m up to 1,968 m. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Parâng, 19 km to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 18 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Most of the length is red (57%, about 6.3 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.5 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 283 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,685 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
