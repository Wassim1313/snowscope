    About White Pass

    White Pass is a mid-sized ski area with about 36 km of pistes, number 66 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 1,367 m and 1,987 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Crystal Mountain, 35 km north, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at White Pass through a normal winter

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,677 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 23 weeks. Most winters land between 149 cm and 299 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 30 March, with about 264 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 123 cm, with 188 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 232 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 620 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,367 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 5 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Hogback Mountain (2,052 m), Cramer Mountain (1,826 m) and Round Mountain (1,817 m).

    What there is to ski at White Pass

    The piste map lists 55 graded runs, about 36 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 20 km (57%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.4 km.

    Quick answers

    When does White Pass have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at White Pass at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 123 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at White Pass?

    Between 1,367 m and 1,987 m, so 620 m of vertical drop.

    Who is White Pass best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 57% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is White Pass?

    About 36 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
