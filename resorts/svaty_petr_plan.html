    About Svatý Petr

    With some 11 km of runs, Svatý Petr belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 702 m up to 1,196 m. The closest other ski area is MAJAsport (2.4 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Svatý Petr

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.2 km, or 66%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 494 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 902 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts and 2 T-bars, 5 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.
