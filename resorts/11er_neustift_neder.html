    About 11er Neustift/Neder

    Neither huge nor tiny, 11er Neustift/Neder offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 505 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 980 m up to 1,808 m. Stubai is the next resort, 3.4 km away to the west.

    The pistes of 11er Neustift/Neder

    11er Neustift/Neder has 46 graded runs adding up to about 9.3 km. Most of the length is freeride (62%, about 5.7 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 62% of the length is graded difficult. 5.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 828 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 980 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 2 T-bars and 2 moving carpets, 5 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Habicht at 3,277 m, standing 1,469 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,394 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early February to early April, about 7 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 33 cm, around 13 February. By mid-April the typical depth is 29 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 21 cm and 43 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 19 cm (lean years 9 cm, snowy years 29 cm).

    Questions about 11er Neustift/Neder

    When does 11er Neustift/Neder have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to early April.

    Can you ski at 11er Neustift/Neder at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 19 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is 11er Neustift/Neder?

    The top is at 1,808 m and the base at 980 m.

    Does 11er Neustift/Neder have steep runs?

    Yes: about 5.7 km of its graded pistes (62%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near 11er Neustift/Neder?

    Stubai, 3.4 km to the west, with about 95 km of pistes.
