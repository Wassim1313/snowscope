    About 11er Neustift/Neder

    Neither huge nor tiny, 11er Neustift/Neder offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 505 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 980 m up to 1,808 m. Stubai is the next resort, 3.4 km away to the west.

    How 11er Neustift/Neder compares nearby

    For more terrain, Stubai (3.4 km west) has about 95 km of pistes against 9.0 km here. Stubai reaches 3,208 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 828 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 980 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 24 cm of snow on the upper slopes of 11er Neustift/Neder. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Saturday.

    Where to stay
