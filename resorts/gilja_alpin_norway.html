    The snow right now

    Today there are about 17 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Gilja Alpin. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (5 cm).

    How much snow Gilja Alpin usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 556 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to late April, about 17 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 10 March, with about 83 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 54 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 56 cm, a dry one 5 cm. Most winters land between 30 cm and 110 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Gilja Alpin

    When does Gilja Alpin have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    How much snow does Gilja Alpin have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.
