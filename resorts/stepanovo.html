    About Степаново (Stepanovo)

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Степаново (Stepanovo) sits in the middle of the size table of Russia. Its lifts climb from 140 m to 240 m. Яхрома (Yakhroma) is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of Степаново (Stepanovo)

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 3.5 km in total. The largest share is red, with 2.6 km (73%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How Степаново (Stepanovo) is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Степаново (Stepanovo) gives 100 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 240 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 6 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts and 2 rope tows. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
