    About Skilift Weitnau

    Compact and local, Skilift Weitnau has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 806 m up to 907 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Liftanlagen Oberwilhams, 2.6 km to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.4 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 1.4 km (100%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    101 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 806 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Salmaser Höhe, 1,254 m high and 7.7 km south of the resort.

    Snow at Skilift Weitnau through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 856 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 12 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 32 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 44 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 20 cm.

    Questions about Skilift Weitnau

    What is the best month to ski at Skilift Weitnau?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Can you ski at Skilift Weitnau at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 12 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilift Weitnau?

    Its highest lift reaches 907 m; the lowest starts at 806 m (101 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Skilift Weitnau?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Skilift Weitnau?

    Thaler Höhe, 5.9 km to the south, with about 4.0 km of pistes.
