    About Mont Saxonnex

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mont Saxonnex offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 584 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,037 m and 1,544 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf, 3.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 6.6 km. Most of the length is blue (58%, about 3.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Mont Saxonnex and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 15 km east to Le Grand Massif, with about 174 km of runs. Le Grand Massif has the longer snow season: around 27 weeks of solid cover, compared with 21 at Mont Saxonnex. For higher slopes, Le Grand Massif climbs to 2,484 m.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Mont Saxonnex today: about 18 cm up high. 34 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 79 cm by Sunday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 13 cm at mid-mountain).

    The mountain at Mont Saxonnex

    The vertical drop is 507 m: runs of a moderate length. All 6 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Pointe Blanche, 2,438 m high and 5.0 km south-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,290 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 13 December and keeps it until about 6 May, roughly 21 weeks. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 87 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 118 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 43 cm; a snowy December gave 71 cm, a dry one 11 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 56 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Mont Saxonnex: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Mont Saxonnex?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Mont Saxonnex at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 43 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Mont Saxonnex?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,544 m; the lowest starts at 1,037 m (507 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Mont Saxonnex?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 4.3 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Mont Saxonnex?

    Le Grand Massif, 15 km to the east, with about 174 km of pistes.
