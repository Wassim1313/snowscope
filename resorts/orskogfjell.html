    About Orskogfjell

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Orskogfjell one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 315 m and 703 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Ørskogfjell skisenter (0.1 km, south-west).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 509 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 146 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. Most winters land between 79 cm and 182 cm by mid-February. For about 17 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 75 cm; a snowy December gave 112 cm, a dry one 43 cm. A normal season tops out near 151 cm, around 28 March.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.4 km. Most of the length is black (75%, about 1.0 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 75% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 1.0 km of difficult runs.

    How Orskogfjell compares nearby

    For more terrain, Strandafjellet (28 km south) has about 20 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Strandafjellet does better, with about 31 weeks of a good base instead of 28. The highest nearby is Strandafjellet, topping out at 1,139 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 388 m: runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Sprovstind (1,194 m), 2.8 km to the north-east, about 491 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Orskogfjell today: about 37 cm up high. The last seven days brought 23 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (41 cm).

    Orskogfjell: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Orskogfjell?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Orskogfjell have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 75 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Orskogfjell?

    The top is at 703 m and the base at 315 m.

    Is Orskogfjell good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.0 km of its graded pistes (75%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Orskogfjell?

    Strandafjellet, 28 km to the south, with about 20 km of pistes.
