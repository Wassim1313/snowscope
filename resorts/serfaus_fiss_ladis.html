    The snow at Serfaus Fiss Ladis, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Serfaus Fiss Ladis (about 2,020 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 61 cm; half of the winters sit between 48 cm and 74 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 112 cm, between 91 cm and 133 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 118 cm.

    By 15 December 50 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 76 %.

    1998-99 was the big one (203 cm); 2024-25 the lean one (79 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to early June, about 213 days.

    Questions about Serfaus Fiss Ladis

    Is there snow in Serfaus Fiss Ladis at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 61 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 76 % of 46 winters. Right now: 48 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Serfaus Fiss Ladis?

    March: the snow peaks at about 118 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to early June.

    How much snow does Serfaus Fiss Ladis get?

    About 118 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 79 cm in the leanest winter (2024-25) and 203 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high is Serfaus Fiss Ladis?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,817 m; the lowest starts at 1,222 m (1,595 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Serfaus Fiss Ladis have?

    About 169 km of pistes and 48 lifts.

    About Serfaus Fiss Ladis

    With some 169 km of runs, Serfaus Fiss Ladis belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,222 m to 2,817 m. Its nearest neighbour is Patschiedlift, 7.0 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,595 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
