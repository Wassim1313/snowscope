    About Serfaus Fiss Ladis

    With some 169 km of runs, Serfaus Fiss Ladis belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,222 m to 2,817 m. Its nearest neighbour is Patschiedlift, 7.0 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,595 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 11 gondolas, 17 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 10 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 10 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Furgler, 3,004 m high and 4.3 km west of the resort.

    The pistes of Serfaus Fiss Ladis

    In all, 257 marked runs cover roughly 167 km. The largest share is red, with 89 km (53%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 21 km of black or expert runs. 21 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,020 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 November and lasts to the end of May: about 27 weeks. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 61 cm, with 88 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 118 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 108 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Most winters land between 76 cm and 145 cm by mid-February.

    Serfaus Fiss Ladis: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Serfaus Fiss Ladis?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Serfaus Fiss Ladis at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 61 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Serfaus Fiss Ladis?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Serfaus Fiss Ladis?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,817 m; the lowest starts at 1,222 m (1,595 m of vertical).

    Does Serfaus Fiss Ladis have steep runs?

    Yes: about 42 km of its graded pistes (25%) are difficult.
