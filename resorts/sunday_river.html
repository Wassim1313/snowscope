    The snow at Sunday River, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Sunday River (about 1,975 ft (602 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 11 in (29 cm); half of the winters sit between 6.3 in (16 cm) and 16 in (40 cm).

    Mid-February typically sees 25 in (63 cm), between 19 in (48 cm) and 30 in (76 cm) in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 27 in (68 cm).

    4 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 17 % by 1 January.

    2018-19 was the big one (53 in (135 cm)); 2005-06 the lean one (16 in (40 cm)).

    Expect 12 in (30 cm) or more from mid-December until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 117 days.

    Questions about Sunday River

    Can you ski Sunday River at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 11 in (29 cm) at Christmas on average, with a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 17 % of 46 winters. Right now: 9.8 in (25 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Sunday River?

    March: the snow peaks at about 27 in (68 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-December to mid-April.

    How snowy is Sunday River?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 27 in (68 cm) in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 53 in (135 cm) (2018-19), the thinnest 16 in (40 cm) (2005-06).

    How high do the lifts go at Sunday River?

    Between 249 m and 955 m, so 706 m of vertical drop.

    About Sunday River

    Sunday River ranks number 2 of 93 ski areas in US Northeast by piste length, with about 86 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 249 m up to 955 m. Mount Abram is the next resort, 17 km away to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 706 m: enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 249 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
