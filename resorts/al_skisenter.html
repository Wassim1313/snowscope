    About Ål skisenter

    With some 13 km of runs, Ål skisenter belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 567 m to 1,037 m. The closest other ski area is Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter (13 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Ål skisenter has 26 graded runs adding up to about 13 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 4.8 km, or 38%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Ål skisenter is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Ål skisenter gives 470 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Primhovda (1,133 m), Skurvehovda (1,122 m) and Hovdun (982 m).

    How much snow Ål skisenter usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 802 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 November and keeps it until about 22 May, roughly 26 weeks. Most winters land between 62 cm and 113 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 57 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 87 cm, a dry one 38 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 95 cm, around 14 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 88 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Ål skisenter: common questions

    When does Ål skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Ål skisenter have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 57 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Ål skisenter?

    Between 567 m and 1,037 m, so 470 m of vertical drop.

    Is Ål skisenter good for beginners?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 7.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ål skisenter?

    Hemsedal skisenter, 21 km to the north, with about 48 km of pistes.
