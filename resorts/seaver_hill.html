    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 282 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 February and keeps it until about 13 March, roughly 6 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 12 cm to 66 cm in mid-February in most winters. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 37 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 16 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 37 cm).

    Seaver Hill: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Seaver Hill?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Seaver Hill at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
