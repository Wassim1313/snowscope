    About Tschardund

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Tschardund one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 830 m and 974 m above sea level. Gurtis is the next resort, 4.0 km away to the north-west.

    Tschardund and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Brandnertal: about 45 km of pistes, 7.9 km to the south-east. Malbun reaches 1,991 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Tschardund today and the next days

    Up high, Tschardund has roughly 1 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 15 cm by Wednesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 144 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,030 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Golm, for one, climbs to 2,103 m.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 chalets: Stüba Wasserfall, Kühbruck, Märchenwald and Das Krichle.
