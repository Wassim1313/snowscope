    About Tschardund

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Tschardund one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 830 m and 974 m above sea level. Gurtis is the next resort, 4.0 km away to the north-west.

    Tschardund and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Brandnertal: about 45 km of pistes, 7.9 km to the south-east. For more reliable snow, Malbun usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 27 weeks, against 5 here. Malbun reaches 1,991 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The pistes of Tschardund

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 1.1 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.1 km (100%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Tschardund today and the next days

    Up high, Tschardund has roughly 15 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 31 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Saturday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 9 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 144 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,030 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Golm, for one, climbs to 2,103 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Fundelkopf at 2,401 m, standing 1,427 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Tschardund through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 902 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early February to early March, about 5 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 14 cm and 54 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 12 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 32 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 32 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 chalets: Stüba Wasserfall, Kühbruck, Märchenwald and Das Krichle.

    Quick answers

    When does Tschardund have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Is there snow at Tschardund at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Tschardund at?

    The top is at 974 m and the base at 830 m.

    Is Tschardund suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Tschardund?

    Brandnertal, 7.9 km to the south-east, with about 45 km of pistes.
