    About Meiringen

    Around 51 km of marked runs place Meiringen among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 119 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,052 m and 2,433 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Melchsee-Frutt (4.8 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Meiringen

    Meiringen has 58 graded runs adding up to about 51 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 27 km, or 53%. Intermediate runs make up 53% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 5.4 km.

    How Meiringen is laid out

    1,381 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The lift system counts 16 lifts: 1 cable car, 4 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 1 drag lift and 2 rope tows. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Tällistock, 2,579 m high and 7.3 km east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,742 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 December and keeps it until about 26 May, roughly 25 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 122 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 134 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 59 cm (lean years 29 cm, snowy years 76 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 12 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 77 cm to 145 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    When does Meiringen have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Meiringen at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 59 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Meiringen?

    The announced opening day is 19 December 2026.

    What altitude is Meiringen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,433 m; the lowest starts at 1,052 m (1,381 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Meiringen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 53% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
