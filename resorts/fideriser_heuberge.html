    About Fideriser Heuberge

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Fideriser Heuberge sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,953 m and reach 2,354 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Hochwang (5.9 km, west).

    How much snow Fideriser Heuberge usually gets

    At about 2,154 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 November and lasts to the end of May: about 27 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 62 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 85 cm, a dry one 35 cm. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 119 cm, around 25 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 76 cm to 139 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 117 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The mountain at Fideriser Heuberge

    401 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 1,953 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. All 3 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Weissflue, 2,843 m high and 6.2 km south-east of the resort.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Fideriser Heuberge?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Fideriser Heuberge have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 62 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Fideriser Heuberge at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,354 m; the lowest starts at 1,953 m (401 m of vertical).

    Is Fideriser Heuberge good for beginners?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Fideriser Heuberge?

    Parsenn, 7.6 km to the east, with about 81 km of pistes.
