    About Kurumayama Kogen SKYPARK

    Kurumayama Kogen SKYPARK is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 123 of 385 in Japan by length. Skiing here happens between 1,569 m and 1,911 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Echo Valley, 1.6 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Kurumayama Kogen SKYPARK

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 7.3 km. Most of the length is green (51%, about 3.7 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Kurumayama Kogen SKYPARK gives 342 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,569 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,911 m. All 5 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
