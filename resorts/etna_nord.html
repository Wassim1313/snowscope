    About Etna Nord

    Etna Nord is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 11 of 40 in the Apennines by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,807 m up to 2,333 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Etna Sud/Nicolosi, 8.9 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 10 marked runs cover roughly 8.9 km. The largest share is red, with 6.7 km (75%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 526 m: runs of a moderate length. At 1,807 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 3 button lifts, 4 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    The snow right now

    There is no snow on the ground at Etna Nord yet. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Etna Nord at?

    The top is at 2,333 m and the base at 1,807 m.

    What level of skier is Etna Nord best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 75% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Etna Nord?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
