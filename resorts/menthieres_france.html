    About Menthières

    Menthières has about 0.3 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,076 m to 1,103 m. Its nearest neighbour is Menthières, 0.6 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Menthières is laid out

    The vertical drop is 27 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Lélex reaches 1,659 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Crêt de la Goutte, 1,621 m high and 2.7 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,090 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 January and lasts to 18 February: about 3 weeks. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The snow is usually deepest around 12 February, with about 38 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 12 cm; a snowy December gave 77 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 127 cm.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Menthières include La Biolaz (guesthouse), Chalet de Varambon (chalet), Hôtel Ducret (hotel) and Le Sorgia (hotel).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Menthières?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-February.

    Can you ski at Menthières at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 12 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Menthières?

    The top is at 1,103 m and the base at 1,076 m.

    How big is Menthières?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
