    About Tweedsmuir Ski Club

    Tweedsmuir Ski Club is a ski area. The slopes start at 1,588 m and reach 1,650 m at the top.

    Snow at Tweedsmuir Ski Club through a normal winter

    Count on about 31 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,619 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late October to late May or later. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 169 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For about 25 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 124 cm, from 88 cm in a poor year to 178 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 118 cm to 207 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 164 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 82 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Tweedsmuir Ski Club. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Monday. An ordinary winter would have around 90 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Tweedsmuir Ski Club gives 62 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,588 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a rope tow.
