    About Sitzmark Ski Hill

    Sitzmark Ski Hill is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 5.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,339 m up to 1,492 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 4.9 km. The largest share is black diamond, with 2.2 km (44%). With 44% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 2.2 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Sitzmark Ski Hill gives 153 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,339 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 rope tow. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Bimetalic Mountain (1,504 m), Knob Hill (1,486 m) and Buster Mountain (1,413 m).

    How much snow Sitzmark Ski Hill usually gets

    At about 1,416 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 December and lasts to 15 April: about 18 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 38 cm, from 16 cm in a poor year to 64 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 63 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 34 cm and 80 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 31 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    Quick answers

    When does Sitzmark Ski Hill have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Sitzmark Ski Hill at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 38 cm deep.

    What altitude is Sitzmark Ski Hill at?

    The top is at 1,492 m and the base at 1,339 m.

    Does Sitzmark Ski Hill have steep runs?

    Yes: about 2.2 km of its graded pistes (44%) are difficult.

    How big is Sitzmark Ski Hill?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
