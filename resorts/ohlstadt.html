    The mountain at Ohlstadt

    The vertical drop is 37 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 719 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau, for one, climbs to 1,671 m. The only lift is a rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hohe Kisten at 1,922 m, standing 1,166 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 738 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 15 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 10 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 25 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 34 cm.
