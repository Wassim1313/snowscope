    About Sljeme

    Compact and local, Sljeme has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 735 m and 1,029 m above sea level.

    The pistes of Sljeme

    In all, 10 marked runs cover roughly 3.5 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 46%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    294 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 935 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Sljeme (1,033 m), Veliki Plazur (869 m) and Stol (866m) (866 m).

    Snow at Sljeme in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at mid-mountain (about 882 m) stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Apartmanska kuća Snježna Kraljica (hotel), Tomislavov dom (hotel), Grofica (mountain hut) and Runolist (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Sljeme?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,029 m; the lowest starts at 735 m (294 m of vertical).

    Is Sljeme suitable for families?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 2.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Sljeme have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
