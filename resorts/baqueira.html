    Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret (about 2,052 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 26 cm, with a normal range of 10 cm–44 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 67 cm; the middle half of winters range from 31 cm to 89 cm.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 70 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 15 % of winters, and by 1 January in 26 %.

    Best winter on record: 2012-13, peaking at 152 cm. Worst: 1989-90, which never passed 26 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to mid-April, about 119 days.

    This winter so far: 0 cm on 19 November, against a usual 3 cm for the date.

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 26 cm, and only 26 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 2 cm.

    About Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret

    Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret tops the size table of the Pyrenees with roughly 154 km of runs. The slopes start at 1,504 m and reach 2,600 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Espot (20 km, south-east).

    What there is to ski at Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret

    The piste map lists 153 graded runs, about 154 km in total. Most of the length is blue (41%, about 64 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Experts can count on some 16 km of difficult runs. 8.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How much snow Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret usually gets

    Count on about 14 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,052 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early January to mid-April. Most winters land between 9 cm and 105 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 26 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 3 cm in a poor year to 66 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 70 cm, around 29 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 23 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,096 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,504 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 36 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 18 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts, 4 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 7 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 7 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pic de Moredo at 2,766 m, standing 166 m above the top lift.
