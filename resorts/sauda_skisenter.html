    About Sauda skisenter

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Sauda skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The slopes start at 354 m and reach 816 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Gullingen, 27 km south-east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 15 graded runs, about 8.7 km in total. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 3.4 km, or 39%. With 62% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at Sauda skisenter through a normal winter

    At about 585 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 2 December and lasts to the end of May: about 26 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 74 cm, with 122 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 18 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 187 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Most winters land between 102 cm and 241 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 209 cm, around 22 March.

    The mountain at Sauda skisenter

    From top to bottom that makes 462 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hustveitsata at 1,187 m, standing 371 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Sauda skisenter?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Sauda skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 74 cm deep.

    What altitude is Sauda skisenter at?

    The top is at 816 m and the base at 354 m.

    Is Sauda skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 5.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Sauda skisenter?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
