    About Högfjället

    Högfjället is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 11 km of pistes put it at number 65 of 262. Its lifts climb from 694 m to 886 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sälen, 2.2 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Högfjället

    Counting every graded piste, there are 16 runs and around 11 km to ski. The largest share is green (beginner), with 8.7 km (82%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Högfjället

    192 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 14 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 5 button lifts, 7 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Hundfjället (925 m), Storfjället (924 m) and Mellanfjället (910 m).

    How much snow Högfjället usually gets

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 790 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 46 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 64 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 42 cm and 83 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm, from 13 cm in a poor year to 59 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Högfjället

    What is the best month to ski at Högfjället?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Högfjället at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 34 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Högfjället at?

    Its highest lift reaches 886 m; the lowest starts at 694 m (192 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Högfjället?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Högfjället?

    Sälen, 2.2 km to the north-west, with about 90 km of pistes.
