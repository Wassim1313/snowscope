    About Nerskogen skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Nerskogen skisenter offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 145 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 714 m to 1,060 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Oppdal Skisenter, 17 km to the south.

    Snow at Nerskogen skisenter through a normal winter

    Count on about 30 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 887 m in an ordinary winter, from early November to late May or later. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 179 cm, around 1 April. Most winters land between 94 cm and 201 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 172 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 100 cm, with 133 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks.

    The pistes of Nerskogen skisenter

    Counting every graded piste, there are 5 runs and around 4.1 km to ski. Most of the length is green (beginner) (38%, about 1.6 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Nerskogen skisenter

    Skied top to bottom, Nerskogen skisenter gives 346 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Brattskarven (1,578 m), 7.4 km to the south, about 518 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Nerskogen skisenter

    When does Nerskogen skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Nerskogen skisenter have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 100 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Nerskogen skisenter?

    Between 714 m and 1,060 m, so 346 m of vertical drop.

    Is Nerskogen skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 2.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Nerskogen skisenter have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
