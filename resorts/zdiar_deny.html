    About Ždiar

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ždiar offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 116 of 312 in the Carpathians). The slopes start at 883 m and reach 1,134 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Bachledova dolina (1.5 km, north).

    How Ždiar is laid out

    The vertical drop is 251 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 883 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km south-west to Vysoké Tatry, with about 13 km of runs. The highest nearby is Vysoké Tatry, topping out at 2,182 m.
