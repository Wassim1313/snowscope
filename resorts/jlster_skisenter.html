    About Jølster Skisenter

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Jølster Skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 243 m to 759 m. The closest other ski area is Sunnfjord skisenter (19 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Jølster Skisenter

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 5.1 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 3.1 km (60%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Jølster Skisenter through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 501 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 29 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 130 cm; a snowy December gave 180 cm, a dry one 71 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 256 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 139 cm and 313 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 273 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 24 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    516 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 T-bar and 3 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Sanddalsfjellet (1,193 m), 6.6 km to the east, about 434 m higher than the top lift.

    Jølster Skisenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Jølster Skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Jølster Skisenter have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 130 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Jølster Skisenter?

    The top is at 759 m and the base at 243 m.

    Is Jølster Skisenter good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 3.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Jølster Skisenter?

    Sogndal skisenter, 51 km to the south-east, with about 12 km of pistes.
