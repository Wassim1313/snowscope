    About Roubion-Les-Buisses

    Neither huge nor tiny, Roubion-Les-Buisses offers about 15 km of pistes (number 391 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,421 m and reach 1,909 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Valberg, 6.5 km to the west.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,665 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 December and lasts to 8 April: about 16 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 15 cm to 145 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 81 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 24 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 39 cm, with 107 cm in the best years.

    The piste map lists 21 graded runs, about 15 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 9.6 km, or 62%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.1 km of black or expert runs.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Roubion-Les-Buisses hold around 15 cm of snow today. 32 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 15 cm at mid-mountain.

    The mountain at Roubion-Les-Buisses

    488 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,421 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Mont Autcellier, 2,204 m high and 6.1 km north of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Chalet des Buisses (guesthouse), Le Rupicapra (chalet), Génépi Hotel (hotel) and L'Escapade (hotel).

    Questions about Roubion-Les-Buisses

    When does Roubion-Les-Buisses have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early April.

    Can you ski at Roubion-Les-Buisses at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 39 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Roubion-Les-Buisses at?

    Between 1,421 m and 1,909 m, so 488 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Roubion-Les-Buisses best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 62% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Roubion-Les-Buisses?

    Auron, 19 km to the north-west, with about 62 km of pistes.
