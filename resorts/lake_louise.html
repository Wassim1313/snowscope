    About Lake Louise

    About 81 km of pistes make Lake Louise one of the ten biggest ski areas of Canada West (number 7). Skiing here happens between 1,647 m and 2,643 m above sea level.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Lake Louise has 117 graded runs adding up to about 80 km. The largest share is green circle, with 29 km (36%). Good skiers will like it here; 36% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 28 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,145 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early November to late May or later, about 30 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 76 cm to 127 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 105 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 78 cm, from 51 cm in a poor year to 113 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 9 March, with about 108 cm.

    The mountain at Lake Louise

    996 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,647 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 1 gondola, 9 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mount Richardson at 3,086 m, standing 443 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Lake Louise

    What is the best month to ski at Lake Louise?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Can you ski at Lake Louise at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 78 cm deep.

    When does Lake Louise open for the 2026/27 season?

    It opened on 6 November 2026.

    What altitude is Lake Louise at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,643 m; the lowest starts at 1,647 m (996 m of vertical).

    Does Lake Louise have steep runs?

    Yes: about 28 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.
