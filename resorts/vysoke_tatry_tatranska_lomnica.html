    About Vysoké Tatry

    Vysoké Tatry ranks number 9 of 312 ski areas in the Carpathians by piste length, with about 13 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 888 m and 2,182 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Starý Smokovec, 5.3 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,294 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,088 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
