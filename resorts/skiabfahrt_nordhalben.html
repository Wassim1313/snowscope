    About Skiabfahrt Nordhalben

    Skiabfahrt Nordhalben has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 562 m and reach 624 m at the top. Ludwigsstadt is the next resort, 15 km away to the north-west.

    How Skiabfahrt Nordhalben compares nearby

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 593 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 21 cm in a big one. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 31 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and late December. The snow is usually deepest around 30 January, with about 13 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 62 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 562 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Silbersattel Steinach reaches 840 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Skiabfahrt Nordhalben?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    Can you ski at Skiabfahrt Nordhalben at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Skiabfahrt Nordhalben?

    Its highest lift reaches 624 m; the lowest starts at 562 m (62 m of vertical).

    How big is Skiabfahrt Nordhalben?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
