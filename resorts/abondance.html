    About Abondance

    Neither huge nor tiny, Abondance offers about 10 km of pistes (number 478 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 920 m up to 1,689 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Chapelle d'Abondance, 5.7 km to the east.

    The pistes of Abondance

    Counting every graded piste, there are 18 runs and around 9.9 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (56%, about 5.5 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Abondance has roughly 29 cm of snow on the ground today. 39 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 32 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (12 cm).

    The mountain at Abondance

    From top to bottom that makes 769 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 920 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Les Portes du Soleil reaches 2,250 m. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 1 gondola, 7 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Mont de Grange (2,432 m), 4.8 km to the south-east, about 743 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Abondance through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,304 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 December and lasts to 23 April: about 17 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 28 cm and 93 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 6 cm in a poor year to 56 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 66 cm, around 16 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 38 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Where to stay

    Questions about Abondance

    When does Abondance have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Abondance at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 31 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Abondance?

    Between 920 m and 1,689 m, so 769 m of vertical drop.

    Is Abondance suitable for families?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 6.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Abondance?

    Les Portes du Soleil, 6.7 km to the south-east, with about 406 km of pistes.
