    About Abondance

    Neither huge nor tiny, Abondance offers about 10 km of pistes (number 478 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 920 m up to 1,689 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Chapelle d'Abondance, 5.7 km to the east.

    Abondance and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Les Portes du Soleil (6.7 km south-east) has about 406 km of pistes against 10 km here. Les Portes du Soleil reaches 2,250 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Abondance has roughly 6 cm of snow on the ground today. 25 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 57 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.

    The mountain at Abondance

    From top to bottom that makes 769 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 920 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Les Portes du Soleil reaches 2,250 m.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Abondance include Le Mont Jorat (hotel), Les Touristes (hotel), Chalet des Praz (chalet) and Les Quatre Chamois (chalet).
