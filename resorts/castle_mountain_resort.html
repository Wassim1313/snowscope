    About Castle

    Castle is one of the bigger ski areas of Canada West: about 55 km of pistes put it at number 13 of 53. The slopes start at 1,409 m and reach 2,259 m at the top. Pass Powderkeg ski is the next resort, 32 km away to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Castle has 93 graded runs adding up to about 50 km. The largest share is green circle, with 20 km (39%). Steep ground is the speciality: 35% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 17 km of difficult runs.

    How Castle is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 850 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,409 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 5 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Mount Haig (2,610 m), 3.0 km to the south-west, about 351 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,834 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 November and keeps it until about 16 May, roughly 25 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 58 cm; a snowy December gave 84 cm, a dry one 40 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 89 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 94 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 63 cm to 114 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    When does Castle have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Castle at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 58 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Castle open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026.

    What altitude is Castle at?

    Between 1,409 m and 2,259 m, so 850 m of vertical drop.

    Does Castle have steep runs?

    Yes: about 17 km of its graded pistes (35%) are difficult.
