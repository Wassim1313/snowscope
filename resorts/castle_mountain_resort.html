    Castle in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Castle (about 1,834 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 58 cm of cover (47 cm to 70 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 81 cm, between 68 cm and 100 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 94 cm.

    50 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 80 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1996-97, peaking at 174 cm. Worst: 1991-92, which never passed 78 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 173 days.

    Questions about Castle

    Can you ski Castle at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 58 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 80 % of 46 winters. Right now: 23 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Castle?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 94 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Castle get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 94 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 174 cm (1996-97), the thinnest 78 cm (1991-92).

    What altitude is Castle at?

    Between 1,409 m and 2,259 m, so 850 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Castle have?

    About 55 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
