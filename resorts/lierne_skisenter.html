    About Lierne skisenter

    Lierne skisenter is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 446 m to 585 m.

    How Lierne skisenter compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning: about 9.0 km of pistes, 48 km to the north-east. For more reliable snow, Borgefjellsenteret usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 29 weeks, against 26 here. For higher slopes, Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning climbs to 923 m.

    How Lierne skisenter is laid out

    139 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Holandsfjellet (725 m), 1.9 km to the north, about 140 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Lierne skisenter today: about 10 cm up high. The last seven days brought 2 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 35 cm by now.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 516 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 60 cm, from 38 cm in a poor year to 84 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 117 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 118 cm, around 24 March. Most winters land between 74 cm and 135 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and mid-April.

    Lierne skisenter: common questions

    When does Lierne skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Lierne skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 60 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Lierne skisenter at?

    Between 446 m and 585 m, so 139 m of vertical drop.

    Is there a larger resort near Lierne skisenter?

    Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning, 48 km to the north-east, with about 9.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Lierne skisenter?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
