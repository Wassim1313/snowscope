    About Pass Powderkeg ski

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Pass Powderkeg ski sits in the middle of the size table of Canada West. Its lifts climb from 1,292 m to 1,587 m. The closest other ski area is Castle (32 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Pass Powderkeg ski

    Counting every graded piste, there are 31 runs and around 8.6 km to ski. Most of the length is green circle (46%, about 4.0 km). Good skiers will like it here; 34% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.0 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Pass Powderkeg ski gives 295 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Turtle Mountain, 2,210 m high and 4.0 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,440 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early April, about 13 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 27 cm, with 49 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 22 cm and 66 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 46 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 18 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Questions about Pass Powderkeg ski

    When is the best time to ski at Pass Powderkeg ski?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Can you ski at Pass Powderkeg ski at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 27 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Pass Powderkeg ski?

    The top is at 1,587 m and the base at 1,292 m.

    Is Pass Powderkeg ski good for beginners?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Pass Powderkeg ski?

    Fernie Alpine Resort, 50 km to the west, with about 82 km of pistes.
