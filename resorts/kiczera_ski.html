    About Kiczera Ski

    Kiczera Ski is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 94 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Skiing here happens between 473 m and 631 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Karlików, 12 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Kiczera Ski gives 158 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 473 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
