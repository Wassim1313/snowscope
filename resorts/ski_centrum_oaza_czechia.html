    The mountain at Ski Centrum Oáza

    50 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 543 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Švýcárna, reach 1,307 m. The only lift is a button lift.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Centrum Oáza?

    Its highest lift reaches 593 m; the lowest starts at 543 m (50 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Ski Centrum Oáza have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
