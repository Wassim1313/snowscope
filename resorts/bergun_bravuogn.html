    About Bergün/Bravuogn

    Neither huge nor tiny, Bergün/Bravuogn offers about 13 km of pistes (number 407 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,367 m and reach 2,557 m at the top. Alvaneu is the next resort, 11 km away to the north-west.

    Snow at Bergün/Bravuogn through a normal winter

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,962 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 63 cm and 124 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 83 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 56 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 32 cm, snowy years 85 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 2 weeks. A normal season tops out near 100 cm, around 19 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    The pistes of Bergün/Bravuogn

    Bergün/Bravuogn has 19 graded runs adding up to about 13 km. Most of the length is red (66%, about 8.3 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    How Bergün/Bravuogn is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,190 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,367 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,557 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Piz Ela (3,339 m), 5.0 km to the south-west, about 782 m higher than the top lift.

    How Bergün/Bravuogn compares nearby

    For more terrain, Corviglia (14 km south) has about 68 km of pistes against 13 km here. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 3 of 7). For higher slopes, Corviglia climbs to 3,021 m.

    Questions about Bergün/Bravuogn

    When is the best time to ski at Bergün/Bravuogn?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Bergün/Bravuogn at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 56 cm deep.

    What altitude is Bergün/Bravuogn at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,557 m; the lowest starts at 1,367 m (1,190 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Bergün/Bravuogn best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Bergün/Bravuogn?

    Corviglia, 14 km to the south, with about 68 km of pistes.
