    About Bergün/Bravuogn

    Neither huge nor tiny, Bergün/Bravuogn offers about 13 km of pistes (number 407 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,367 m and reach 2,557 m at the top. Alvaneu is the next resort, 11 km away to the north-west.

    How Bergün/Bravuogn is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,190 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,367 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,557 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.

    How Bergün/Bravuogn compares nearby

    For more terrain, Corviglia (14 km south) has about 68 km of pistes against 13 km here. For higher slopes, Corviglia climbs to 3,021 m.
