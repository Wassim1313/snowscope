    Lifts and terrain

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Vardefjell (640 m), Tuva (463 m) and Nordåsen (307 m).

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 442 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. By mid-April the typical depth is 188 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 94 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 57 cm, snowy years 130 cm). For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Most winters land between 115 cm and 215 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 194 cm.

    Kjemsasen: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Kjemsasen?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Kjemsasen have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 94 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
