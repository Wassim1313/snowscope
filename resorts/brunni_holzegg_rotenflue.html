    About Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue

    Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 27 km of pistes put it at number 219 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 817 m to 1,568 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skilift Brunni, 1.7 km to the north-east.

    How Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue gives 751 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,017 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 12 lifts break down into 3 cable cars, 1 gondola, 5 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Klingenstock, 1,935 m high and 7.9 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,192 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 25 December and keeps it until about 29 April, roughly 18 weeks. A normal season tops out near 55 cm, around 1 March. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 37 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Most winters land between 30 cm and 73 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 30 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 44 cm).

    Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 30 cm deep.

    How high is Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue?

    The top is at 1,568 m and the base at 817 m.

    Is Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue have?

    About 27 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
