    About Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue

    Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 27 km of pistes put it at number 219 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 817 m to 1,568 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skilift Brunni, 1.7 km to the north-east.

    How Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue gives 751 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,017 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 7 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue. 10 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 21 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.
