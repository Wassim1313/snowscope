    About Les Deux Alpes

    Les Deux Alpes is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 108 km of pistes put it at number 44 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,284 m to 3,511 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Grave, 10 km to the east.

    How Les Deux Alpes is laid out

    2,227 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 46 lifts: 3 funiculars, 5 gondolas, 3 chair-gondolas, 13 chairlifts, 6 T-bars, 6 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 8 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 8 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Aiguille du Plat de la Selle (3,595 m), Dôme de la Lauze (3,568 m) and Dôme de Puy Salié (3,417 m).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 225 runs and around 107 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (59%, about 63 km). With 77% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Les Deux Alpes today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Les Deux Alpes hold around 91 cm of snow today. 33 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 68 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 31 cm at mid-mountain. The 2026/27 season at Les Deux Alpes is announced to start on 28 November 2026 and to run until 2 May 2027.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,398 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May or later. By mid-April the typical depth is 179 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and early April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 124 cm to 220 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 106 cm (lean years 56 cm, snowy years 152 cm). Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 193 cm. For about 20 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Questions about Les Deux Alpes

    What is the best month to ski at Les Deux Alpes?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Les Deux Alpes at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 106 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Les Deux Alpes?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 2 May 2027.

    What altitude is Les Deux Alpes at?

    Between 1,284 m and 3,511 m, so 2,227 m of vertical drop.

    Is Les Deux Alpes suitable for families?

    Yes: 77% of the graded pistes, about 83 km, are easy.
