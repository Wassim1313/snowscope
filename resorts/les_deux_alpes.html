    About Les Deux Alpes

    Les Deux Alpes is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 108 km of pistes put it at number 44 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,284 m to 3,511 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Grave, 10 km to the east.

    How Les Deux Alpes is laid out

    2,227 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.

    Les Deux Alpes today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Les Deux Alpes hold around 83 cm of snow today. 19 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 79 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.
