    About Marbachegg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Marbachegg offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 551 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 879 m and reach 1,472 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bumbach, 4.1 km to the south.

    What there is to ski at Marbachegg

    The piste map lists 6 graded runs, about 7.9 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 5.6 km (71%). With 71% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at Marbachegg through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,176 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to mid-April, about 13 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 19 cm and 57 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 23 cm; a snowy December gave 35 cm, a dry one 6 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 29 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. A normal season tops out near 40 cm, around 21 February.

    How Marbachegg is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Marbachegg gives 593 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 879 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,472 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 2 T-bars and 1 drag lift, 4 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Furggenguetsch at 2,197 m, standing 725 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Marbachegg?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Marbachegg have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 23 cm deep.

    What altitude is Marbachegg at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,472 m; the lowest starts at 879 m (593 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Marbachegg?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 5.6 km, are easy.

    How big is Marbachegg?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
