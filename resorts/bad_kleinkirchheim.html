    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Bad Kleinkirchheim gives 1,025 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 4 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 3 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 5 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 5 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Klomnock, 2,331 m high and 7.4 km north of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 9 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,524 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to late March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 13 cm to 60 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 21 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 40 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 17 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 39 cm.
