    About Mörlialp

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mörlialp offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 664 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,356 m up to 1,837 m. The closest other ski area is Sörenberg (6.3 km, west).

    Snow at Mörlialp through a normal winter

    At about 1,596 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 December and lasts to 22 May: about 24 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 106 cm. For about 2 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 64 cm and 123 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 86 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 50 cm; a snowy December gave 67 cm, a dry one 24 cm.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 5.1 km to ski. Most of the length is red (57%, about 2.9 km). Intermediate runs make up 57% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 1.2 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 481 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,356 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Brienzer Rothorn at 2,348 m, standing 511 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Mörlialp

    When does Mörlialp have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Mörlialp at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 50 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Mörlialp at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,837 m; the lowest starts at 1,356 m (481 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Mörlialp best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 57% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Mörlialp?

    Meiringen, 12 km to the south-east, with about 51 km of pistes.
