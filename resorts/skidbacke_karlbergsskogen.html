    How much snow Skidbacke Karlbergsskogen usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 26 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 22 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 37 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 6 February, with about 11 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February.
