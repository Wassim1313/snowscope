    About Cranmore

    Cranmore is a mid-sized ski area with about 24 km of pistes, number 29 of 93 in US Northeast by length. Its lifts climb from 166 m to 510 m. The closest other ski area is Attitash (11 km, west).

    The pistes of Cranmore

    In all, 59 marked runs cover roughly 24 km. Most of the length is blue square (48%, about 12 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 6.2 km of difficult runs.

    How Cranmore is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Cranmore gives 344 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 166 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Wildcat Mountain, for one, climbs to 1,211 m. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 5 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Kearsarge North, 991 m high and 5.2 km north of the resort.

    Snow at Cranmore through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 338 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 4 January and keeps it until about 5 April, roughly 13 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 26 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 44 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 30 cm and 97 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 60 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 10 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Cranmore: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Cranmore?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Can you ski at Cranmore at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    What altitude is Cranmore at?

    Its highest lift reaches 510 m; the lowest starts at 166 m (344 m of vertical).

    Does Cranmore have steep runs?

    Yes: about 6.2 km of its graded pistes (25%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Cranmore have?

    About 24 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
