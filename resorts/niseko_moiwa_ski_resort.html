    About Niseko Moiwa

    Niseko Moiwa is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 128 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 345 m up to 789 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Niseko United, 3.8 km to the east.

    The mountain at Niseko Moiwa

    The vertical drop is 444 m: runs of a moderate length. At 345 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
