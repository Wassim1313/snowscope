    About Silvretta Montafon

    Around 102 km of marked runs place Silvretta Montafon among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 47 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 689 m up to 2,421 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kristberg-Silbertal, 7.1 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Silvretta Montafon

    Counting every graded piste, there are 212 runs and around 102 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 54 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,732 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The lowest slopes are at only 689 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 37 lifts: 2 cable cars, 8 gondolas, 18 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 5 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Valschavieler Maderer, 2,769 m high and 7.8 km east of the resort.

    Snow at Silvretta Montafon through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,555 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 40 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 85 cm, around 21 February. Most winters land between 56 cm and 116 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 45 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 65 cm in the best years.

    Questions about Silvretta Montafon

    What is the best month to ski at Silvretta Montafon?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Silvretta Montafon at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 45 cm deep.

    When does Silvretta Montafon open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high is Silvretta Montafon?

    Between 689 m and 2,421 m, so 1,732 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Silvretta Montafon?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 55 km, are easy.
