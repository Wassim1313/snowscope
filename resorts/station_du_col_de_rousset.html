    About Station du Col de Rousset

    Station du Col de Rousset is a mid-sized ski area with about 19 km of pistes, number 329 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,256 m up to 1,692 m. Font d'Urle Chaud Clapier is the next resort, 11 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 33 marked runs cover roughly 19 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 58%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,474 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to mid-March, about 9 weeks of good cover. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 65 cm, around 13 February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 189 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 94 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Station du Col de Rousset gives 436 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 9 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 7 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Roc de Peyrolle (2,016 m), 8.0 km to the south, about 324 m higher than the top lift.

    Station du Col de Rousset: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Station du Col de Rousset?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Station du Col de Rousset at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in 8 winters out of ten, about 18 cm deep.

    How high is Station du Col de Rousset?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,692 m; the lowest starts at 1,256 m (436 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Station du Col de Rousset?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How big is Station du Col de Rousset?

    About 19 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
