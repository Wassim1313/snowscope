    About Hakkoda Kokusai

    Hakkoda Kokusai is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 165 of 385 in Japan by length. The slopes start at 664 m and reach 849 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is 八甲田ロープウェー, 1.0 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 185 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 664 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 849 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Hakkoda Kokusai has roughly 13 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 16 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.
