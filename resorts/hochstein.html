    About Hochstein

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hochstein offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 618 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 681 m to 1,510 m. Lienzer Bergbahnen is the next resort, 2.6 km away to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 6.4 km in total. Black runs make up the biggest part: 4.4 km, or 69%. Steep ground is the speciality: 69% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.4 km.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Hochstein has roughly 9 cm of snow on the ground today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 5 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Hochstein gives 829 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Because the lifts start as low as 681 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift and 1 button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Spitzkofel at 2,717 m, standing 1,207 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Hochstein usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,096 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 27 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for late January and mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 32 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 30 December, with about 15 cm.

    Questions about Hochstein

    When is the best time to ski at Hochstein?

    Aim for late January and mid-February.

    How much snow does Hochstein have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Hochstein?

    The top is at 1,510 m and the base at 681 m.

    Is Hochstein good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 4.4 km of its graded pistes (69%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hochstein?

    Lienzer Bergbahnen, 2.6 km to the north-east, with about 27 km of pistes.
