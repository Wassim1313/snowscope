    How Valdres is laid out

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 13 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Valdres. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 36 cm by now.

    Snow at Valdres through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 754 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 23 November and keeps it until about 15 May, roughly 25 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 82 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 19 March, with about 91 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 65 cm and 112 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 57 cm (lean years 38 cm, snowy years 90 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Valdres include Danebu Kongsgaard (hotel), Bekkjedalen (chalet) and Aurdal Fjellpark (chalet).

    Questions about Valdres

    When does Valdres have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Valdres have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 57 cm deep.
