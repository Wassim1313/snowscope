    About Skilift Kapellenberg

    Skilift Kapellenberg has about 0.2 km of pistes. The slopes start at 834 m and reach 877 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Sankt Englmar (Grün-Pröller), 1.9 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Skilift Kapellenberg has roughly 8 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 2 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (6 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    43 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 834 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a T-bar.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 856 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 72 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm, with 49 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 25 cm, around 27 January.
