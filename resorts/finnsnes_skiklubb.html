    About Finnsnes Skiklubb

    Neither huge nor tiny, Finnsnes Skiklubb offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 183 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 112 m up to 335 m.

    Snow this week

    How Finnsnes Skiklubb is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Finnsnes Skiklubb gives 223 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning.
