    About Ски центар Копаоник

    Only 4 of the 51 ski areas of the Balkans are larger than Ски центар Копаоник, which has some 29 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,502 m up to 1,975 m. Kopaonik is the next resort, 0.7 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 34 runs and around 26 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 10 km, or 39%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Experts can count on some 6.5 km of difficult runs.

    How Ски центар Копаоник is laid out

    473 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,502 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 22 lifts break down into 10 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 7 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
