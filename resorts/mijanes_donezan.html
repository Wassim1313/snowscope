    About Mijanès-Donezan

    About 10 km of pistes make Mijanès-Donezan one of the smaller places to ski in the Pyrenees. Its lifts climb from 1,535 m to 2,015 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ascou, 3.8 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Mijanès-Donezan

    Counting every graded piste, there are 18 runs and around 10.0 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (46%, about 4.6 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 11 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Mijanès-Donezan. The last seven days brought 19 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (6 cm).

    How Mijanès-Donezan is laid out

    The vertical drop is 480 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,535 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Les Angles, for one, climbs to 2,358 m. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Pic de Baxouillade (2,546 m), 7.0 km to the south, about 531 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Mijanès-Donezan usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,775 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 19 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 62 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 7 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 33 cm in a big one.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Mijanès-Donezan include Relais de Pailheres (hotel), La Pradaille (chalet), Centre de Vacance Le Saint-Bernard (hostel) and Refuge d'Ourtiset (mountain hut).

    Quick answers

    When does Mijanès-Donezan have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Mijanès-Donezan at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 7 cm).

    How high is Mijanès-Donezan?

    The top is at 2,015 m and the base at 1,535 m.

    Can beginners ski at Mijanès-Donezan?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 6.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Mijanès-Donezan?

    Les Angles, 17 km to the south, with about 35 km of pistes.
