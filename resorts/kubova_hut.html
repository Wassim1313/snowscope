    About Kubova Huť

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Kubova Huť sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The slopes start at 980 m and reach 1,098 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Horní Vltavice (3.1 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Kubova Huť

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.0 km to ski. Most of the length is black (50%, about 0.5 km). Good skiers will like it here; 50% of the runs are steep.

    Lifts and terrain

    118 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 980 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Kubova Huť at?

    The top is at 1,098 m and the base at 980 m.

    Is Kubova Huť good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 0.5 km of its graded pistes (50%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Kubova Huť?

    Mitterdorf, 14 km to the south-west, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Kubova Huť have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
