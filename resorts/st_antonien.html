    About St. Antönien

    About 3.0 km of pistes make St. Antönien one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,402 m and 1,648 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pany, 5.2 km to the south-west.

    From top to bottom that makes 246 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,402 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
