    About St. Antönien

    About 3.0 km of pistes make St. Antönien one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,402 m and 1,648 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pany, 5.2 km to the south-west.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,525 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 December and lasts to 19 May: about 24 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 61 cm and 128 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 75 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 97 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 48 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 18 cm in a poor year to 68 cm in a big one.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 3.1 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.4 km, or 77%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    St. Antönien today and the next days

    Today there are about 18 cm of snow on the upper slopes of St. Antönien. 10 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 25 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 246 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,402 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Großer Turm (2,830 m), 6.0 km to the north, about 1,182 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about St. Antönien

    When is the best time to ski at St. Antönien?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at St. Antönien at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 48 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is St. Antönien at?

    Between 1,402 m and 1,648 m, so 246 m of vertical drop.

    Is St. Antönien suitable for families?

    Yes: 77% of the graded pistes, about 2.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near St. Antönien?

    Golm, 12 km to the north, with about 28 km of pistes.
