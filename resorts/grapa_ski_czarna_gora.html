    About Grapa SKI Czarna Góra

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Grapa SKI Czarna Góra sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. Skiing here happens between 740 m and 893 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bania Ski, 1.4 km to the west.

    What there is to ski at Grapa SKI Czarna Góra

    Grapa SKI Czarna Góra has 4 graded runs adding up to about 2.4 km. Most of the length is blue (61%, about 1.4 km). With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 153 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 940 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
