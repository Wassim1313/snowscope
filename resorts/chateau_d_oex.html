    Château-d'Oex today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Château-d'Oex hold around 23 cm of snow today. 27 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 57 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (11 cm).

    Snow at Château-d'Oex through a normal winter

    At about 1,338 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 December and lasts to 30 April: about 19 weeks. A normal season tops out near 68 cm, around 17 February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 44 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 34 cm and 97 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 35 cm, with 56 cm in the best years.

    Quick answers

    When does Château-d'Oex have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Château-d'Oex at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 35 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
