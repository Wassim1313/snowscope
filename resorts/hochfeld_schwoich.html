    About Hochfeld

    Hochfeld is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 629 m to 721 m. Its nearest neighbour is Schneeberglifte Thiersee, 6.8 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km south-east to SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser, with about 238 km of runs. For more reliable snow, SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 20 weeks, against 0 here. The highest nearby is SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser, topping out at 1,865 m.

    What there is to ski at Hochfeld

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.3 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.3 km, or 100%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 92 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 629 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser, for one, climbs to 1,865 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 2 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Zettenkaiser at 1,968 m, standing 1,247 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Hochfeld through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 675 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 33 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 11 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 18 February. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and mid-February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 11 cm to 49 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Hochfeld

    What is the best month to ski at Hochfeld?

    Aim for late January and mid-February.

    How much snow does Hochfeld have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Hochfeld?

    Between 629 m and 721 m, so 92 m of vertical drop.

    Is Hochfeld suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hochfeld?

    SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser, 11 km to the south-east, with about 238 km of pistes.
