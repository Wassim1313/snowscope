    About Val Gardena

    Val Gardena is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 118 km of pistes put it at number 39 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,258 m and reach 2,507 m at the top. Seiser Alm is the next resort, 8.1 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Val Gardena has 189 graded runs adding up to about 106 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 55 km, or 52%. Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 15 km of black or expert runs.

    How Val Gardena is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,249 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,507 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 cable car, 27 funiculars, 8 gondolas, 19 chairlifts, 16 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 7 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 7 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Saslonch - Langkofel - Sassolungo, 3,181 m high and 4.2 km south of the resort.

    How much snow Val Gardena usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,882 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 27 December and keeps it until about 26 April, roughly 17 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 51 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 38 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Year to year the snow is steady, with 32 cm to 80 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 28 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 59 cm in the best years.

    Val Gardena: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Val Gardena?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Val Gardena at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 28 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Val Gardena at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,507 m; the lowest starts at 1,258 m (1,249 m of vertical).

    Who is Val Gardena best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Val Gardena?

    About 118 km of pistes and 79 lifts.
