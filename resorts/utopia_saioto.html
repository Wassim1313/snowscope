    About Utopia Saioto

    Utopia Saioto is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 223 of 385 in Japan by length. The slopes start at 736 m and reach 1,142 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Mizuho Highland (6.4 km, east).

    How Utopia Saioto is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 406 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Below about 936 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
