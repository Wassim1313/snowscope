    About Chaux-Neuve

    Chaux-Neuve is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,010 m and 1,132 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Foncine le Haut (4.1 km, south-west).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, L'Abbaye (14 km east) has about 7.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. The highest nearby is L'Abbaye, topping out at 1,435 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    122 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Dent de Vaulion, reach 1,465 m.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Chaux-Neuve include Les Tremplins (hotel), Le Passant (guesthouse), Les Enguenelles (guesthouse) and Les écrins du Val de Mouthe (chalet).

    Chaux-Neuve: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Chaux-Neuve?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,132 m; the lowest starts at 1,010 m (122 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Chaux-Neuve?

    L'Abbaye, 14 km to the east, with about 7.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Chaux-Neuve have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
