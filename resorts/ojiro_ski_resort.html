    About Ojiro

    Ojiro is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 213 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 613 m up to 861 m. The closest other ski area is Tajima Farm Park (6.0 km, north).

    Runs by difficulty

    Ojiro has 8 graded runs adding up to about 3.6 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 2.0 km, or 55%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Ojiro gives 248 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 613 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 3 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.

    Ojiro: common questions

    What altitude is Ojiro at?

    Its highest lift reaches 861 m; the lowest starts at 613 m (248 m of vertical).

    Is Ojiro good for beginners?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Ojiro?

    Hachi Kita, 7.4 km to the south-east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Ojiro have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
