    About Vrátna

    Around 12 km of marked runs place Vrátna among the larger resorts of the Carpathians, number 11 by length. The slopes start at 612 m and reach 1,529 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Snowsun Horný Vadičov (13 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 32 graded runs, about 12 km in total. Most of the length is red (40%, about 4.8 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 38% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 4.6 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    917 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. At 612 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,529 m. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 9 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
