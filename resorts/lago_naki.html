    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Лаго-Наки (Lago-Naki) gives 190 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,613 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 3 T-bars and 4 rope tows, 7 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
