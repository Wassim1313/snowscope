    About Grafenherberge (Rankenlift)

    Compact and local, Grafenherberge (Rankenlift) has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,076 m up to 1,248 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sudelfeld, 0.6 km to the south-west.

    How Grafenherberge (Rankenlift) compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Sudelfeld: about 26 km of pistes, 0.6 km to the south-west. For more reliable snow, Oberer Maxlraineralm usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 21 weeks, against 12 here. The highest nearby is Wendelstein, topping out at 1,718 m.

    The pistes of Grafenherberge (Rankenlift)

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.7 km. Most of the length is blue (51%, about 0.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    172 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Dürrmiesing (1,863 m), 7.8 km to the west, about 615 m higher than the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Snow at Grafenherberge (Rankenlift) through a normal winter

    Count on about 12 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,162 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to early April. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 49 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 27 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 25 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 54 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 18 cm and 81 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Grafenherberge (Rankenlift)

    When does Grafenherberge (Rankenlift) have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    Can you ski at Grafenherberge (Rankenlift) at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 25 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Grafenherberge (Rankenlift)?

    The top is at 1,248 m and the base at 1,076 m.

    Is Grafenherberge (Rankenlift) good for beginners?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Grafenherberge (Rankenlift)?

    Sudelfeld, 0.6 km to the south-west, with about 26 km of pistes.
