    About Limanowa

    Limanowa is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 229 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 612 m up to 774 m. The closest other ski area is Stacja Narciarska Laskowa-Kamion (6.8 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.1 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.1 km, or 100%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Limanowa

    162 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 612 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.

    Questions about Limanowa

    How high is Limanowa?

    Between 612 m and 774 m, so 162 m of vertical drop.

    Is Limanowa good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Limanowa?

    Stacja Narciarska Laskowa-Kamion, 6.8 km to the north-west, with about 4.0 km of pistes.
