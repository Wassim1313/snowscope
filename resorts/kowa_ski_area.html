    About Kowa

    Kowa has about 0.4 km of pistes. The slopes start at 14 m and reach 45 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Teshio Chomin (20 km, north).

    Kowa and the resorts around it

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 31 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 14 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Otoineppu Otoifuji reaches 247 m. The only lift is a rope tow.

    Kowa today and the next days

    Kowa has no snow cover yet today. The last seven days brought 15 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 13 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Kowa at?

    Its highest lift reaches 45 m; the lowest starts at 14 m (31 m of vertical).

    How big is Kowa?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
