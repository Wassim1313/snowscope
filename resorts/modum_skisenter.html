    About Modum skisenter

    Modum skisenter is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 115 m and 460 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Lommedalen (25 km, east).

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 288 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to mid-April, about 18 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 37 cm and 97 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 68 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 34 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 13 cm in a poor year to 62 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 42 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Modum skisenter hold around 7 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 19 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 7 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The mountain at Modum skisenter

    345 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Lauvkollen (704 m), 5.8 km to the east, about 244 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Tyrifjord hotell (hotel).

    Modum skisenter: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Modum skisenter?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Modum skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Modum skisenter?

    Between 115 m and 460 m, so 345 m of vertical drop.
