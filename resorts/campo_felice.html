    About Campo Felice

    Only 1 of the 40 ski areas of the Apennines is larger than Campo Felice, which has some 24 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 1,419 m and 1,944 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ovindoli, 6.7 km south-east, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 41 graded runs, about 24 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 9.2 km, or 38%. Good skiers will like it here; 35% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 5.1 km of difficult runs. 3.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Campo Felice gives 525 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,419 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,944 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 9 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    Campo Felice: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Campo Felice?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,944 m; the lowest starts at 1,419 m (525 m of vertical).

    Does Campo Felice have steep runs?

    Yes: about 8.4 km of its graded pistes (35%) are difficult.

    How big is Campo Felice?

    About 24 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
