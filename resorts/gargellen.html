    About Gargellen

    Gargellen is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 27 km of pistes put it at number 221 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,427 m and reach 2,272 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Madrisa (6.1 km, south-west).

    How Gargellen compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Silvretta Montafon: about 102 km of pistes, 10 km to the north-east. Madrisa reaches 2,615 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Gargellen gives 845 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,427 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Gargellen hold around 26 cm of snow today. 11 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 38 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Where to stay
