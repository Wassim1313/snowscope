    About Gargellen

    Gargellen is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 27 km of pistes put it at number 221 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,427 m and reach 2,272 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Madrisa (6.1 km, south-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 52 graded runs, about 27 km in total. Most of the length is blue (72%, about 19 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Snow at Gargellen through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,850 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 November and lasts to the end of May: about 27 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 26 March, with about 165 cm. For about 17 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 103 cm and 199 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 81 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 40 cm, snowy years 110 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 153 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Gargellen gives 845 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,427 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 3 button lifts. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The skyline is dominated by Isentällispitz, 2,872 m high and 7.0 km south-east of the resort.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Gargellen?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Gargellen at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 81 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Gargellen?

    The top is at 2,272 m and the base at 1,427 m.

    Is Gargellen suitable for families?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 20 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Gargellen?

    Silvretta Montafon, 10 km to the north-east, with about 102 km of pistes.
