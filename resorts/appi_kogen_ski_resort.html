    About Appi Kogen

    Appi Kogen ranks number 5 of 385 ski areas in Japan by piste length, with about 31 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 628 m to 1,269 m. Hachimantai Resort Shimokura is the next resort, 9.7 km away to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 40 marked runs cover roughly 30 km. The largest share is green, with 17 km (56%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Appi Kogen is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 641 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 628 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,269 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 1 gondola and 7 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Appi Kogen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,269 m; the lowest starts at 628 m (641 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Appi Kogen?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Appi Kogen have?

    About 31 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
