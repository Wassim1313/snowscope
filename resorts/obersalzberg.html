    About Skilifte Obersalzberg

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Skilifte Obersalzberg sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 793 m to 952 m. Rossfeld is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 2.8 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.5 km, or 87%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Skilifte Obersalzberg

    The vertical drop is 159 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 993 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Rossfeld reaches 1,537 m. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The skyline is dominated by Hoher Göll, 2,522 m high and 5.1 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Skilifte Obersalzberg usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 872 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 28 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 12 cm to 41 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 25 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-November and mid-January.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Skilifte Obersalzberg?

    Aim for mid-November and mid-January.

    How much snow does Skilifte Obersalzberg have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Skilifte Obersalzberg?

    Between 793 m and 952 m, so 159 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Skilifte Obersalzberg best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 87% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilifte Obersalzberg?

    Rossfeld, 4.1 km to the east, with about 10 km of pistes.
