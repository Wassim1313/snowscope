    About Skilifte Obersalzberg

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Skilifte Obersalzberg sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 793 m to 952 m. Rossfeld is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the east.

    How Skilifte Obersalzberg compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Rossfeld: about 10 km of pistes, 4.1 km to the east. For higher slopes, Rossfeld climbs to 1,537 m.

    The mountain at Skilifte Obersalzberg

    The vertical drop is 159 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 993 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Rossfeld reaches 1,537 m.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Kempinski Hotel Berchtesgaden (hotel), Pension Angerer (guesthouse), Jugendhotel Seimler (hotel) and Hotel Edelweiss (hotel).
