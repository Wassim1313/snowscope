    About Shirakabako Royal Hill

    Shirakabako Royal Hill is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 185 of 385 in Japan by length. Skiing here happens between 1,448 m and 1,713 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Shirakaba Resort Ikenotaira Snow, 1.5 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 5.1 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.2 km, or 44%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    The mountain at Shirakabako Royal Hill

    From top to bottom that makes 265 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,448 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.
