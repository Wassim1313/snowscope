    About Phoenix Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Phoenix Mountain offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 37 of 53 in Canada West). Its lifts climb from 1,265 m to 1,479 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 16 runs and around 7.6 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 3.9 km (51%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 1.9 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,372 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 23 November and keeps it until about 12 May, roughly 25 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 96 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 68 cm to 129 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 76 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 43 cm, snowy years 102 cm). For about 8 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 110 cm.

    The mountain at Phoenix Mountain

    214 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Mount Pelly, 1,606 m high and 7.9 km north of the resort.

    Questions about Phoenix Mountain

    When is the best time to ski at Phoenix Mountain?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Phoenix Mountain have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 76 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Phoenix Mountain?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,479 m; the lowest starts at 1,265 m (214 m of vertical).

    Who is Phoenix Mountain best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 51% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Phoenix Mountain have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
