    About St. Martin im Tennengebirge

    Compact and local, St. Martin im Tennengebirge has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 946 m up to 1,150 m. The closest other ski area is Lammertallift (3.7 km, north).

    What there is to ski at St. Martin im Tennengebirge

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 2.5 km in total. Most of the length is red (100%, about 2.5 km). Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The snow right now

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 204 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,146 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Flachau reaches 1,977 m. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Bleikogel, 2,411 m high and 7.7 km north-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 9 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,048 m in an ordinary winter, from late January to late March. A normal season tops out near 37 cm, around 20 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 27 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 22 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 42 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 21 cm to 52 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February.

    St. Martin im Tennengebirge: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at St. Martin im Tennengebirge?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    Is there snow at St. Martin im Tennengebirge at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is St. Martin im Tennengebirge?

    The top is at 1,150 m and the base at 946 m.

    Who is St. Martin im Tennengebirge best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near St. Martin im Tennengebirge?

    Dachstein West, 12 km to the north-east, with about 39 km of pistes.
