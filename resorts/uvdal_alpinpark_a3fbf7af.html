    About Uvdal Alpinpark

    Uvdal Alpinpark is a ski area. Uvdal Alpinpark is the next resort, 0.3 km away to the south-east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,086 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 November and keeps it until about 25 May, roughly 28 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 6 weeks. A normal season tops out near 107 cm, around 25 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 100 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 60 cm, from 38 cm in a poor year to 90 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 72 cm to 123 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Uvdal Alpinpark: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Uvdal Alpinpark?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Uvdal Alpinpark at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 60 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
