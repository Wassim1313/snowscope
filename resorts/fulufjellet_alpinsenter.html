    About Fulufjellet alpinsenter

    Fulufjellet alpinsenter is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 90 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 514 m to 737 m. The closest other ski area is Näsfjället (9.6 km, east).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 7.7 km in total. Most of the length is green (beginner) (42%, about 3.2 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How much snow Fulufjellet alpinsenter usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 626 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 December and lasts to 30 April: about 20 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 51 cm and 90 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 55 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 69 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 38 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 64 cm, a dry one 13 cm.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 9 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Fulufjellet alpinsenter. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 21 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    How Fulufjellet alpinsenter is laid out

    The vertical drop is 223 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Trysil reaches 1,109 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Västertangen (971 m), 4.6 km to the north, about 234 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Fulufjellet alpinsenter

    What is the best month to ski at Fulufjellet alpinsenter?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Fulufjellet alpinsenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Fulufjellet alpinsenter?

    Between 514 m and 737 m, so 223 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Fulufjellet alpinsenter?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 4.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Fulufjellet alpinsenter?

    Trysil, 30 km to the west, with about 101 km of pistes.
