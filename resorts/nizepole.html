    About Nizepole

    Nizepole is a small ski area by the standards of the Balkans, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,316 m and reach 1,443 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Pelister, 6.5 km north, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nizepole and the resorts around it

    The mountain at Nizepole

    From top to bottom that makes 127 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,316 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Kaimaktsalan, reach 2,470 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.
