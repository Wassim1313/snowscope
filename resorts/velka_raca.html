    About Veľká Rača

    Only 9 of the 312 ski areas of the Carpathians are larger than Veľká Rača, which has some 12 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 637 m and 1,044 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Športcentrum Oščadnica, 4.2 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 27 marked runs cover roughly 12 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 5.5 km, or 44%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Veľká Rača is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Veľká Rača gives 407 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 637 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,044 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 chair-gondola, 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow, 6 lifts in all.
