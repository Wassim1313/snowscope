    About La Poya (Chamonix)

    Compact and local, La Poya (Chamonix) has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,347 m to 1,501 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Balme, 2.9 km to the east.

    What there is to ski at La Poya (Chamonix)

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 1.6 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 59%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of La Poya (Chamonix) hold around 18 cm of snow today. 48 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 94 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 15 cm now.

    The mountain at La Poya (Chamonix)

    The vertical drop is 154 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,347 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 2 button lifts and 2 rope tows, 4 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aiguille du Tour at 3,542 m, standing 2,041 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,424 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 60 cm and 127 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 97 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 49 cm, from 18 cm in a poor year to 80 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 40 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at La Poya (Chamonix)?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does La Poya (Chamonix) have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 49 cm deep.

    How high is La Poya (Chamonix)?

    The top is at 1,501 m and the base at 1,347 m.

    Can beginners ski at La Poya (Chamonix)?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near La Poya (Chamonix)?

    Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix), 8.3 km to the south-west, with about 41 km of pistes.
