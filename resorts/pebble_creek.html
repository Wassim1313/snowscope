    About Pebble Creek

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pebble Creek offers about 24 km of pistes (number 91 of 175 in US West). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,945 m up to 2,594 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 58 graded runs, about 24 km in total. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 8.9 km, or 37%. Steep ground is the speciality: 41% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 9.3 km. 0.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 649 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 1,945 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts. The skyline is dominated by Bonneville Peak, 2,812 m high and 1.5 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow at Pebble Creek through a normal winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,270 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to late April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 80 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-February. For about 14 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 97 cm to 195 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 84 cm, from 46 cm in a poor year to 140 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 28 February, with about 165 cm.

    Pebble Creek: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Pebble Creek?

    Aim for early January and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late November to late April.

    Is there snow at Pebble Creek at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 84 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Pebble Creek?

    Between 1,945 m and 2,594 m, so 649 m of vertical drop.

    Is Pebble Creek good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 9.9 km of its graded pistes (41%) are difficult.

    How big is Pebble Creek?

    About 24 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
