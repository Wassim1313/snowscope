    About Brezovica

    Brezovica is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 751 m to 922 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Vitanová, 4.9 km to the east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 171 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 751 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a drag lift.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Brezovica hold around 1 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.
