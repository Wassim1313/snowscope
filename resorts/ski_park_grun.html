    About Ski Park Grůň

    With some 4.0 km of runs, Ski Park Grůň belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. Skiing here happens between 700 m and 845 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is FELA Zlatník, 4.9 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Ski Park Grůň

    From top to bottom that makes 145 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 700 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
