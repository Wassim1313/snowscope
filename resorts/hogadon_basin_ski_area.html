    About Hogadon Basin

    Hogadon Basin is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 7.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,234 m up to 2,412 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 20 marked runs cover roughly 5.9 km. Most of the length is black diamond (46%, about 2.7 km). Good skiers will like it here; 46% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 2.7 km of black or expert runs.

    How Hogadon Basin is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Hogadon Basin gives 178 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,234 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    How much snow Hogadon Basin usually gets

    Count on about 16 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,323 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 37 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 10 cm in a poor year to 70 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 82 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 32 cm and 144 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 26 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    Hogadon Basin: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hogadon Basin?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Hogadon Basin have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 37 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Hogadon Basin?

    The top is at 2,412 m and the base at 2,234 m.

    Is Hogadon Basin good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 2.7 km of its graded pistes (46%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Hogadon Basin have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
