    About Nozawa Onsen

    Nozawa Onsen ranks number 2 of 385 ski areas in Japan by piste length, with about 42 km of runs. The slopes start at 565 m and reach 1,644 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Togari Onsen, 7.8 km to the west.

    The pistes of Nozawa Onsen

    Nozawa Onsen has 55 graded runs adding up to about 42 km. The largest share is green, with 17 km (41%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 12 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,079 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. At 565 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 17 lifts: 2 gondolas, 14 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
