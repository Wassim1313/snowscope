    About Aeschiallmend

    Neither huge nor tiny, Aeschiallmend offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 565 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 914 m and 1,381 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Faltschen, 2.2 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Aeschiallmend

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 6.8 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (46%, about 3.1 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Aeschiallmend today and the next days

    Snow on the ground at Aeschiallmend today: about 17 cm up high. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 17 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 467 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 914 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars and 2 rope tows, 4 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Dreispitz (2,520 m), 6.3 km to the south, about 1,139 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Aeschiallmend through a normal winter

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,148 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 29 cm; a snowy December gave 46 cm, a dry one 11 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 32 cm and 75 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 54 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 36 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Aeschiallmend: common questions

    When does Aeschiallmend have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Aeschiallmend at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 29 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Aeschiallmend?

    The top is at 1,381 m and the base at 914 m.

    Can beginners ski at Aeschiallmend?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 3.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Aeschiallmend?

    Sigriswill-Schwanden, 9.3 km to the north, with about 12 km of pistes.
