    About Val Palot

    Compact and local, Val Palot has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,071 m and reach 1,326 m at the top. Montecampione is the next resort, 5.5 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of Val Palot

    Val Palot has 2 graded runs adding up to about 1.7 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 100%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.5 km north-east to Montecampione, with about 20 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Monte Pora usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 20 weeks, against 2 here. For higher slopes, Colere climbs to 2,174 m.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Val Palot has roughly 6 cm of snow on the ground today. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 19 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday. An ordinary winter would have around 5 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    How Val Palot is laid out

    The vertical drop is 255 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Colere, for one, climbs to 2,174 m. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Muffetto at 2,062 m, standing 736 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Val Palot usually gets

    Count on about 2 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,198 m in an ordinary winter, from late February to mid-March. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 3 March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 43 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 11 cm to 75 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Albergo Ristorante Nuova Stella (hotel), Rifugio Colle di San Zeno (hotel), Agriturismo Belvedere (guesthouse) and Rifugio Medelet (mountain hut).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Val Palot?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late February to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Val Palot at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Val Palot?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,326 m; the lowest starts at 1,071 m (255 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Val Palot?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Val Palot?

    Montecampione, 5.5 km to the north-east, with about 20 km of pistes.
