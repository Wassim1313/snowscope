    About Eckbauer

    Neither huge nor tiny, Eckbauer offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 716 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 730 m and reach 1,226 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Garmisch-Classic (4.4 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Eckbauer

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 4.0 km. The largest share is red, with 3.4 km (85%). Intermediate runs make up 85% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Eckbauer hold around 5 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 4 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 5 cm at mid-mountain).

    Lifts and terrain

    496 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Below about 930 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 1 drag lift. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The highest named summit within 8 km is "Partenkirchner Dreitorspitze, Westgipfel" at 2,633 m, standing 1,407 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Eckbauer through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 978 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 11 cm to 41 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 23 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 9 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 27 cm).

    Questions about Eckbauer

    What is the best month to ski at Eckbauer?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Eckbauer at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Eckbauer at?

    The top is at 1,226 m and the base at 730 m.

    Who is Eckbauer best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 85% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Eckbauer?

    Garmisch-Classic, 4.4 km to the south-west, with about 38 km of pistes.
