    About Eckbauer

    Neither huge nor tiny, Eckbauer offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 716 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 730 m and reach 1,226 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Garmisch-Classic (4.4 km, south-west).

    Other resorts close by

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Eckbauer hold around 4 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    Lifts and terrain

    496 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Below about 930 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.
