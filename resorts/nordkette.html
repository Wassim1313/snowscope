    About Nordkette

    Nordkette is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 554 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 867 m and 2,269 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Patscherkofel Ski, 10 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Nordkette gives 1,402 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 867 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 2 cable cars, 13 funiculars, 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Großer Lafatscher, 2,696 m high and 7.5 km north-east of the resort.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 17 graded runs, about 7.4 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.4 km, or 73%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. 1.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,568 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to mid-May. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 26 cm and 49 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early May and mid-May. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 40 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 23 cm, from 11 cm in a poor year to 35 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 34 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Nordkette: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Nordkette?

    Aim for early May and mid-May; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Nordkette at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 23 cm deep.

    How high is Nordkette?

    The top is at 2,269 m and the base at 867 m.

    What level of skier is Nordkette best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Nordkette?

    Axamer Lizum, 12 km to the south-west, with about 38 km of pistes.
