    About Nordkette

    Nordkette is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 554 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 867 m and 2,269 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Patscherkofel Ski, 10 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Nordkette gives 1,402 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 867 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
