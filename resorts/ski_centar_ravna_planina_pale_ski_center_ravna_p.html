    About Ski centar "Ravna Planina"

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski centar "Ravna Planina" offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 24 of 51 in the Balkans). The slopes start at 921 m and reach 1,291 m at the top. Ravna planina is the next resort, 0.8 km away to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 14 graded runs, about 6.5 km in total. Most of the length is blue (74%, about 4.8 km). With 74% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Ski centar "Ravna Planina" gives 370 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 921 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; OC Bjelašnica ZOI '84 reaches 2,056 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
