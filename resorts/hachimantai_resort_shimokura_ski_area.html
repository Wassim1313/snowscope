    About Hachimantai Resort Shimokura

    Hachimantai Resort Shimokura is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 108 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 604 m up to 1,123 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hachimantai Resort Panorama Ski, 3.1 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 7.6 km to ski. Green runs make up the biggest part: 3.5 km, or 47%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 1.4 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Hachimantai Resort Shimokura gives 519 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 604 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.
