    About Skilift Chrachenhütten Blumenste

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skilift Chrachenhütten Blumenste one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 736 m and 816 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Gantrisch (4.5 km, west).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 80 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 936 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Gantrisch, for one, climbs to 1,636 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Stockhorn at 2,190 m, standing 1,374 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 776 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 26 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 15 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 7 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 19 cm in a big one.
