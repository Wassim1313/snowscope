    About Takamine Mountain Park

    Takamine Mountain Park is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 221 of 385 in Japan by length. Its lifts climb from 1,881 m to 2,046 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Yunomaru, 4.1 km to the north-west.

    How Takamine Mountain Park is laid out

    165 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,881 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    What there is to ski at Takamine Mountain Park

    Takamine Mountain Park has 9 graded runs adding up to about 4.4 km. The largest share is green, with 2.5 km (58%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Questions about Takamine Mountain Park

    How high is Takamine Mountain Park?

    Between 1,881 m and 2,046 m, so 165 m of vertical drop.

    Is Takamine Mountain Park good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Takamine Mountain Park?

    Palcall Tsumagoi Resort Ski & Ho, 17 km to the north, with about 15 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Takamine Mountain Park have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
