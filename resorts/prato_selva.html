    About Prato Selva

    About 4.0 km of pistes make Prato Selva one of the smaller places to ski in the Apennines. Its lifts climb from 1,376 m to 1,772 m. The closest other ski area is Prati di Tivo (6.0 km, south-east).

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Campo Felice: about 24 km of pistes, 35 km to the south. For higher slopes, Campo Imperatore climbs to 2,190 m.

    The mountain at Prato Selva

    From top to bottom that makes 396 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,376 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.

    Prato Selva: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Prato Selva?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,772 m; the lowest starts at 1,376 m (396 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Prato Selva?

    Campo Felice, 35 km to the south, with about 24 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Prato Selva have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
