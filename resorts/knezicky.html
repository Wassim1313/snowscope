    About Kněžický

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Kněžický sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The slopes start at 537 m and reach 709 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Herlíkovice (2.6 km, north).

    The pistes of Kněžický

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 3.3 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.4 km, or 72%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Kněžický gives 172 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 537 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 drag lift, 4 lifts in all.

    How much snow Kněžický usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 623 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 37 cm, a dry one 1 cm. A normal season tops out near 24 cm, around 19 February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 47 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February.

    Questions about Kněžický

    When is the best time to ski at Kněžický?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    How much snow does Kněžický have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Kněžický at?

    The top is at 709 m and the base at 537 m.

    What level of skier is Kněžický best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 72% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Kněžický have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
