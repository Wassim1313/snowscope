    About U Steni

    U Steni is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 908 m and 989 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Rusiń-Ski (2.8 km, north-east).

    The mountain at U Steni

    The vertical drop is 81 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 908 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 989 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
