    About SnowWorld Landgraaf

    Compact and local, SnowWorld Landgraaf has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 155 m up to 220 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Dousberg, 27 km to the west.

    How SnowWorld Landgraaf is laid out

    65 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 155 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.

    How high do the lifts go at SnowWorld Landgraaf?

    How big is SnowWorld Landgraaf?
