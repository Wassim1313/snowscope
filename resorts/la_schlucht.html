    About La Schlucht

    About 2.0 km of pistes make La Schlucht one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,140 m to 1,257 m. The closest other ski area is Gaschney 360 (2.9 km, south-east).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 3.9 km south-west to La Bresse, with about 24 km of runs. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 1.6 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 100%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, La Schlucht gives 117 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 drag lift, 2 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Hohneck, 1,363 m high and 2.4 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,198 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 55 cm in a big one. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 98 cm. A normal season tops out near 17 cm, around 29 January. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at La Schlucht?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Is there snow at La Schlucht at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 5 cm).

    How high is La Schlucht?

    The top is at 1,257 m and the base at 1,140 m.

    Is La Schlucht suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near La Schlucht?

    La Bresse, 3.9 km to the south-west, with about 24 km of pistes.
