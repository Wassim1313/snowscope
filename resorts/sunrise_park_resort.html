    About Sunrise Park Resort

    Sunrise Park Resort is a mid-sized ski area with about 42 km of pistes, number 48 of 175 in US West by length. The slopes start at 2,852 m and reach 3,321 m at the top.

    The pistes of Sunrise Park Resort

    The piste map lists 51 graded runs, about 42 km in total. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 22 km, or 52%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Snow at Sunrise Park Resort through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 3,086 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 4 January and keeps it until about 25 March, roughly 12 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 5 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 212 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 99 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 10 February, with about 71 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 469 m: runs of a moderate length. At 2,852 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Getting up the mountain: 6 chairlifts and 1 rope tow, 7 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The highest named summit within 8 km is Baldy Peak at 3,477 m, standing 156 m above the top lift.

    Sunrise Park Resort: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Sunrise Park Resort?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Is there snow at Sunrise Park Resort at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    How high is Sunrise Park Resort?

    Between 2,852 m and 3,321 m, so 469 m of vertical drop.

    Is Sunrise Park Resort good for beginners?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 22 km, are easy.

    How big is Sunrise Park Resort?

    About 42 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
