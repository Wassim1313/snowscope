    About Domaine Autrans

    Domaine Autrans is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 282 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,003 m to 1,605 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Méaudre, 2.9 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 44 runs and around 22 km to ski. Most of the length is red (37%, about 8.1 km). With 53% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The mountain at Domaine Autrans

    The vertical drop is 602 m: runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 14 lifts: 3 chairlifts, 5 button lifts, 5 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Charande (1,709 m), 6.5 km to the north-east, about 104 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Domaine Autrans through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,304 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 January and keeps it until about 10 March, roughly 10 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 21 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 108 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 60 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 182 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Questions about Domaine Autrans

    When is the best time to ski at Domaine Autrans?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to early March.

    How much snow does Domaine Autrans have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 21 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in 8 winters out of ten.

    What altitude is Domaine Autrans at?

    The top is at 1,605 m and the base at 1,003 m.

    Is Domaine Autrans suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Domaine Autrans have?

    About 22 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
