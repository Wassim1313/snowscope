    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 476 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 December and lasts to 11 April: about 16 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 54 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 18 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. Most winters land between 35 cm and 89 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 64 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Ski de montagne?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.
