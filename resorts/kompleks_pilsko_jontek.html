    About Kompleks Pilsko-Jontek

    With some 10 km of runs, Kompleks Pilsko-Jontek belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 722 m and reach 1,433 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Kolej Baba (1.4 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Kompleks Pilsko-Jontek

    The piste map lists 16 graded runs, about 10 km in total. The largest share is red, with 6.0 km (60%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Kompleks Pilsko-Jontek

    Skied top to bottom, Kompleks Pilsko-Jontek gives 711 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 722 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,433 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 3 T-bars and 2 button lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Questions about Kompleks Pilsko-Jontek

    How high is Kompleks Pilsko-Jontek?

    The top is at 1,433 m and the base at 722 m.

    Can beginners ski at Kompleks Pilsko-Jontek?

    Yes: 40% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Kompleks Pilsko-Jontek have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
