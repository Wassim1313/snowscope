    About Kasina SKI

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kasina SKI offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 133 of 312 in the Carpathians). Its lifts climb from 574 m to 863 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lubomierz, 14 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Kasina SKI

    Kasina SKI has 3 graded runs adding up to about 2.3 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.1 km (48%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Kasina SKI gives 289 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 574 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 863 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 1 button lift.
