    About Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi

    Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,316 m up to 1,481 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Virgen, 6.3 km to the east.

    The pistes of Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi

    Counting every graded piste, there are 5 runs and around 1.9 km to ski. Most of the length is red (42%, about 0.8 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi gives 165 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,316 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Hoher Eichham, 3,371 m high and 5.0 km north-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,398 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 2 February and lasts to 2 March: about 4 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 20 cm and 52 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 19 cm, with 37 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 8 February, with about 33 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 22 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Is there snow at Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 19 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,481 m; the lowest starts at 1,316 m (165 m of vertical).

    Does Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.5 km of its graded pistes (25%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi?

    Kals-Matrei, 18 km to the east, with about 50 km of pistes.
