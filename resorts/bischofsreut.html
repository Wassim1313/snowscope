    About Bischofsreut

    Bischofsreut is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 886 m to 937 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being České Žleby, 4.3 km to the north-east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Bischofsreut gives 51 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 886 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.
