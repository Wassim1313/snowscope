    About Les Marecottes

    With roughly 14 km of runs, Les Marecottes sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,122 m and reach 2,195 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Balme, 11 km to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 27 runs and around 14 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (70%, about 10 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Les Marecottes is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,073 m: enough height for proper long runs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Haute Cime (3,257 m), 7.4 km to the north-west, about 1,062 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Les Marecottes through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,658 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 November and lasts to 27 May: about 26 weeks. A normal season tops out near 159 cm, around 1 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 83 cm, with 131 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 98 cm to 204 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 140 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Les Marecottes?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Les Marecottes at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 83 cm deep.

    What altitude is Les Marecottes at?

    Between 1,122 m and 2,195 m, so 1,073 m of vertical drop.

    Is Les Marecottes suitable for families?

    Yes: 70% of the graded pistes, about 10 km, are easy.

    How big is Les Marecottes?

    About 14 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
