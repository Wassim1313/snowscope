    About Rondane Skianlegg

    Compact and local, Rondane Skianlegg has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 879 m to 1,020 m. Rondane Skianlegg is the next resort, 0.0 km away to the north.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 950 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May, about 27 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 92 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 67 cm (lean years 47 cm, snowy years 90 cm). Most winters land between 76 cm and 118 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 98 cm, around 17 March.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.1 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 0.6 km (59%). With 59% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 141 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Gålå Alpinsenter, for one, climbs to 1,091 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Skjerrelhøa at 1,371 m, standing 351 m above the top lift.

    How Rondane Skianlegg compares nearby

    For more terrain, Kvitfjell (45 km south-east) has about 38 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 5 of 7).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Rondane Skianlegg?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Rondane Skianlegg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 67 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Rondane Skianlegg at?

    Between 879 m and 1,020 m, so 141 m of vertical drop.

    Is Rondane Skianlegg good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 0.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Rondane Skianlegg?

    Kvitfjell, 45 km to the south-east, with about 38 km of pistes.
