    About Corralco

    Only 7 of the 30 ski areas of Andes are larger than Corralco, which has some 26 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 1,529 m to 2,392 m. The closest other ski area is Ski No Limit (7.1 km, south-east).

    What there is to ski at Corralco

    In all, 28 marked runs cover roughly 25 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 8.3 km (33%). With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The mountain at Corralco

    The vertical drop is 863 m: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,529 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    Corralco: common questions

    How high is Corralco?

    Between 1,529 m and 2,392 m, so 863 m of vertical drop.

    Is Corralco good for beginners?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 16 km, are easy.

    How big is Corralco?

    About 26 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
