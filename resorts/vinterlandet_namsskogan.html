    The snow at Vinterlandet Namsskogan, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Vinterlandet Namsskogan (162 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 55 cm of cover (42 cm to 74 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 102 cm, between 87 cm and 141 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 130 cm.

    43 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 74 % by 1 January.

    1996-97 was the big one (257 cm); 2018-19 the lean one (90 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 174 days.

    So far this winter: 6 cm on 19 November (usual: 15 cm).

    Questions about Vinterlandet Namsskogan

    Can you ski Vinterlandet Namsskogan at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 55 cm at Christmas, and 74 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 7 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Vinterlandet Namsskogan?

    March: the snow peaks at about 130 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How snowy is Vinterlandet Namsskogan?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 130 cm in late March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 257 cm (1996-97), the thinnest 90 cm (2018-19).

    When does Vinterlandet Namsskogan have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    What altitude is Vinterlandet Namsskogan at?

    Between 142 m and 182 m, so 40 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Vinterlandet Namsskogan?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.

    About Vinterlandet Namsskogan

    Vinterlandet Namsskogan has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 142 m to 182 m. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Borgefjellsenteret, 35 km to the north-east.

    Other resorts close by

    Vinterlandet Namsskogan today and the next days

    Today there are about 7 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Vinterlandet Namsskogan. The last seven days brought 14 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (23 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 40 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Grong skisenter, reach 684 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Snow at Vinterlandet Namsskogan through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 162 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 December and keeps it until about 18 May, roughly 24 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 122 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 69 cm and 170 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 55 cm; a snowy December gave 96 cm, a dry one 32 cm. A normal season tops out near 130 cm, around 22 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks.
