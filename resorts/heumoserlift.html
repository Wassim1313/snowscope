    About Heumöserlift

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Heumöserlift sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,039 m and reach 1,216 m at the top. Schuttannen is the next resort, 2.1 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Heumöserlift

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 2.6 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.6 km (100%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Damüls-Mellau: about 88 km of pistes, 12 km to the south-east. Laterns-Gapfohl has the longer snow season: around 22 weeks of solid cover, compared with 11 at Heumöserlift. For higher slopes, Damüls-Mellau climbs to 2,021 m.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 26 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Heumöserlift. 19 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 15 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 11 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 177 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Damüls-Mellau, for one, climbs to 2,021 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hoher Freschen at 2,004 m, standing 788 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,128 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 January and keeps it until about 22 April, roughly 11 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 20 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 32 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 38 cm, around 16 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 19 cm and 59 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 33 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and mid-April.

    Questions about Heumöserlift

    When does Heumöserlift have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and mid-April; the cover is usually good from late January to late April.

    How much snow does Heumöserlift have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    What altitude is Heumöserlift at?

    Between 1,039 m and 1,216 m, so 177 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Heumöserlift?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Heumöserlift?

    Damüls-Mellau, 12 km to the south-east, with about 88 km of pistes.
