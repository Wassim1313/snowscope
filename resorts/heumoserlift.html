    About Heumöserlift

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Heumöserlift sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,039 m and reach 1,216 m at the top. Schuttannen is the next resort, 2.1 km away to the north.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Damüls-Mellau: about 88 km of pistes, 12 km to the south-east. For higher slopes, Damüls-Mellau climbs to 2,021 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 177 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Damüls-Mellau, for one, climbs to 2,021 m.
