    About Angel Fire Resort

    With some 53 km of runs, Angel Fire Resort belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of US West. Skiing here happens between 2,618 m and 3,256 m above sea level. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Taos Ski Valley, 28 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Angel Fire Resort

    The piste map lists 101 graded runs, about 53 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (48%, about 25 km). Intermediate runs make up 48% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 13 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    638 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,618 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 6 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Agua Fria Peak at 3,364 m, standing 108 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Angel Fire Resort through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,937 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 31 January and keeps it until about 10 March, roughly 6 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 38 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 7 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 9 cm to 108 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 3 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Angel Fire Resort

    When does Angel Fire Resort have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Angel Fire Resort at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Angel Fire Resort open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026.

    What altitude is Angel Fire Resort at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,256 m; the lowest starts at 2,618 m (638 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Angel Fire Resort best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 48% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
