    About Brentonico

    With roughly 22 km of runs, Brentonico sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,190 m and 1,581 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Prà Alpesina (4.7 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Brentonico

    In all, 26 marked runs cover roughly 22 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 56%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Brentonico is laid out

    391 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Paganella, reach 2,124 m. The lift system counts 11 lifts: 3 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 6 moving carpets. For small children there are 6 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Cima Longino, 2,179 m high and 7.8 km south-west of the resort.

    Snow at Brentonico through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,386 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 10 cm to 79 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 18 March, with about 29 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 8 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm, with 43 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    Questions about Brentonico

    When does Brentonico have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Brentonico have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Brentonico?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,581 m; the lowest starts at 1,190 m (391 m of vertical).

    Is Brentonico good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How big is Brentonico?

    About 22 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
