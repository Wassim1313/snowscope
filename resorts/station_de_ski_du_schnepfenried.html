    About du Schnepfenried

    Neither huge nor tiny, du Schnepfenried offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 534 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,037 m and 1,251 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Gaschney 360 (6.0 km, north).

    How du Schnepfenried is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, du Schnepfenried gives 214 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 6 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Hohneck (1,363 m), 6.4 km to the north, about 112 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow du Schnepfenried usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,144 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm, with 56 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and mid-February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 86 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 16 cm.

    du Schnepfenried: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at du Schnepfenried?

    Aim for mid-January and mid-February.

    Can you ski at du Schnepfenried at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is du Schnepfenried?

    Between 1,037 m and 1,251 m, so 214 m of vertical drop.

    Who is du Schnepfenried best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 46% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is du Schnepfenried?
