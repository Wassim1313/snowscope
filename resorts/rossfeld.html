    About Rossfeld

    Rossfeld is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 496 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,351 m and reach 1,537 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zinkenlifte, 2.3 km to the north.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,444 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 37 cm (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 68 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 66 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 47 cm and 102 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 91 cm.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 9.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 8.8 km (91%). With 91% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Rossfeld

    186 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,351 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,537 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 drag lift, 4 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hoher Göll at 2,522 m, standing 985 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Rossfeld have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Rossfeld at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 37 cm deep.

    How high is Rossfeld?

    The top is at 1,537 m and the base at 1,351 m.

    Is Rossfeld suitable for families?

    Yes: 91% of the graded pistes, about 8.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Rossfeld have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
