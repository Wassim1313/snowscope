    About Serra da Estrela

    Serra da Estrela has about 5.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,850 m and 1,986 m above sea level. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Ski Parque, 15 km to the north-east.

    The pistes of Serra da Estrela

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 5.3 km in total. The largest share is red, with 1.9 km (36%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 1.1 km of black or expert runs.

    How Serra da Estrela is laid out

    The vertical drop is 136 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,850 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
