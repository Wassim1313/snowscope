    About Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk

    Neither huge nor tiny, Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 100 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 165 m up to 346 m. Hiihtokeskus Ukkohalla is the next resort, 16 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 6.7 km. The largest share is black, with 3.3 km (49%). Steep ground is the speciality: 49% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.3 km.

    How Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk is laid out

    The vertical drop is 181 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 2 button lifts and 5 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 256 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 16 December and keeps it until about 1 May, roughly 20 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm, with 52 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 48 cm and 79 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 57 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 66 cm.

    Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk at?

    The top is at 346 m and the base at 165 m.

    Is Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 3.3 km of its graded pistes (49%) are difficult.

    How big is Paljakan matkailu- ja hiihtokesk?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
