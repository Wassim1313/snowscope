    About San Domenico

    San Domenico is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 316 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,374 m up to 2,490 m. Alpe Devero is the next resort, 6.4 km away to the north-east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,932 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. The snow is usually deepest around 7 April, with about 151 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 96 cm to 187 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 148 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for early March and early April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 78 cm; a snowy December gave 123 cm, a dry one 48 cm. For about 17 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 44 runs and around 19 km to ski. Most of the length is red (48%, about 9.2 km). Intermediate runs make up 48% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 4.9 km of difficult runs.

    How San Domenico is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,116 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,374 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Leone at 3,552 m, standing 1,062 m above the top lift.

    San Domenico and the resorts around it

    In this group of 7, San Domenico is number 2 for piste length. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 3 of 7). The highest nearby is Aletsch Arena, topping out at 2,869 m.

    Questions about San Domenico

    When is the best time to ski at San Domenico?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does San Domenico have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 78 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is San Domenico at?

    Between 1,374 m and 2,490 m, so 1,116 m of vertical drop.

    Is San Domenico good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 4.9 km of its graded pistes (25%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does San Domenico have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
