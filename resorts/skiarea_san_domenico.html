    About San Domenico

    San Domenico is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 316 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,374 m up to 2,490 m. Alpe Devero is the next resort, 6.4 km away to the north-east.

    How San Domenico is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,116 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,374 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
