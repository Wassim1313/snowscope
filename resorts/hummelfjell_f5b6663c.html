    Snow at Hummelfjell through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 810 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 85 cm, around 20 March. Most winters land between 57 cm and 96 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 78 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 49 cm, from 31 cm in a poor year to 69 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Hummelfjell?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Hummelfjell at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 49 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
