    The snow at Astun, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Astun (1,966 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 34 cm; half of the winters sit between 15 cm and 67 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 93 cm, between 52 cm and 126 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 100 cm.

    26 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 43 % by 1 January.

    2012-13 was the big one (237 cm); 1989-90 the lean one (51 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until late April in a normal winter, roughly 152 days.

    So far this winter: 2 cm on 22 November (usual: 5 cm).

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Astun at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 34 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 43 % of 46 winters. Right now: 13 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Astun?

    February: the snow peaks at about 100 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late April.

    How deep does the snow get at Astun?

    About 100 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 51 cm in the leanest winter (1989-90) and 237 cm in the biggest (2012-13).

    How high do the lifts go at Astun?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,272 m; the lowest starts at 1,659 m (613 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Astun best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 41% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    About Astun

    Neither huge nor tiny, Astun offers about 35 km of pistes (number 13 of 49 in the Pyrenees). Skiing here happens between 1,659 m and 2,272 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Candanchu, 4.6 km to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 65 marked runs cover roughly 34 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 14 km, or 41%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 7.6 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Astun usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,966 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 46 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 86 cm). A normal season tops out near 100 cm, around 6 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 155 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Lifts and terrain

    613 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,659 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 17 lifts break down into 6 chairlifts, 7 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Pic du Midi d'Ossau (2,884 m), 6.5 km to the north-east, about 612 m higher than the top lift.
