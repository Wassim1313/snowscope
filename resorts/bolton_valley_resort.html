    About Bolton Valley Resort

    Around 31 km of marked runs place Bolton Valley Resort among the larger resorts of US Northeast, number 20 by length. Skiing here happens between 475 m and 950 m above sea level. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Camp Ethan Allen, 10.0 km to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 76 runs and around 31 km to ski. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 43%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 8.3 km.

    How Bolton Valley Resort is laid out

    The vertical drop is 475 m: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 475 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 5 chairlifts and 1 rope tow. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bolton Mountain at 1,123 m, standing 173 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Bolton Valley Resort include The Inn at Bolton Valley (hotel), Lotus Mountain Retreat (hotel), Wilderness Warming Hut (mountain hut) and Nebraska Knoll Sugar House (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Bolton Valley Resort?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    How much snow does Bolton Valley Resort have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 30 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Bolton Valley Resort?

    The top is at 950 m and the base at 475 m.

    Is Bolton Valley Resort good for beginners?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Bolton Valley Resort have?

    About 31 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
