    The snow at Bolton Valley Resort, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Bolton Valley Resort (2,336 ft (712 m)), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 12 in (30 cm), with a normal range of 5.9 in (15 cm)–16 in (41 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 25 in (63 cm) (17 in (42 cm)–30 in (76 cm)).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 26 in (66 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 11 % of winters, and by 1 January in 13 %.

    2000-01 was the big one (51 in (130 cm)); 2015-16 the lean one (11 in (28 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-December to early April, about 109 days.

    This winter so far: 0.4 in (1 cm) on 22 November, against a usual 1.2 in (3 cm) for the date.

    Bolton Valley Resort: quick answers

    Does Bolton Valley Resort have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 12 in (30 cm) at Christmas on average, with a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 13 % of 46 winters. Right now: 3.5 in (9 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Bolton Valley Resort?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 26 in (66 cm)). For a sure base, mid-December to early April is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How much snow does Bolton Valley Resort get?

    About 26 in (66 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (mid-February); between 11 in (28 cm) in the leanest winter (2015-16) and 51 in (130 cm) in the biggest (2000-01).

    How high is Bolton Valley Resort?

    The top is at 950 m and the base at 475 m.

    Is Bolton Valley Resort good for beginners?

    How Bolton Valley Resort is laid out

    The vertical drop is 475 m: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 475 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 5 chairlifts and 1 rope tow. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bolton Mountain at 1,123 m, standing 173 m above the top lift.

    Snow on the ground at Bolton Valley Resort today: about 9 cm up high. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 3 cm by Wednesday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 10 cm at mid-mountain).
