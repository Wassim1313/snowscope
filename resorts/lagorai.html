    About Lagorai

    Neither huge nor tiny, Lagorai offers about 10 km of pistes (number 493 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,597 m to 2,043 m. Its nearest neighbour is Monte Avena, 15 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Lagorai compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 21 km north-east to San Martino di Castrozza, with about 43 km of runs. San Martino di Castrozza reaches 2,351 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How Lagorai is laid out

    The vertical drop is 446 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,597 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.

    Lagorai today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Lagorai hold around 19 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 18 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 30 cm by Thursday.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Lagorai include Chalet Paradiso (chalet), Albergo Passo Brocon (hotel), Hotel Pizzo degli Uccelli (hotel) and Maso Lagorai (chalet).

    Quick answers

    How high is Lagorai?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,043 m; the lowest starts at 1,597 m (446 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Lagorai?

    San Martino di Castrozza, 21 km to the north-east, with about 43 km of pistes.

    How big is Lagorai?

    About 10 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
