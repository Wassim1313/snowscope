    About Lagorai

    Neither huge nor tiny, Lagorai offers about 10 km of pistes (number 493 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,597 m to 2,043 m. Its nearest neighbour is Monte Avena, 15 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Lagorai through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,820 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 December and lasts to 1 May: about 20 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 75 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 121 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 65 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 43 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 5 cm in a poor year to 92 cm in a big one.

    How Lagorai is laid out

    The vertical drop is 446 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,597 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 5 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by il Passetto, 2,589 m high and 7.3 km north-west of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Lagorai?

    Aim for early January and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Lagorai at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 43 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Lagorai?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,043 m; the lowest starts at 1,597 m (446 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Lagorai?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 4.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Lagorai?

    San Martino di Castrozza, 21 km to the north-east, with about 43 km of pistes.
