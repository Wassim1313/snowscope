    About Горбова

    Neither huge nor tiny, Горбова offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 186 of 312 in the Carpathians). The ski area spans the mountain from 147 m up to 188 m. Sun Valley (Сонячна Долина) is the next resort, 13 km away to the north-west.

    There is no snow on the ground at Горбова yet.

    How Горбова is laid out

    The vertical drop is 41 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 147 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.
