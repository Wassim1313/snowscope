    About Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,010 m and reach 1,190 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Schneeland Wenigzell, 6.4 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa

    Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa has 6 graded runs adding up to about 2.6 km. Most of the length is red (51%, about 1.3 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    From top to bottom that makes 180 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 button lift and 2 drag lifts, 3 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Steinriegel, 1,577 m high and 7.9 km north-west of the resort.

    Snow at Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,100 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 21 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 28 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 43 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa?

    Between 1,010 m and 1,190 m, so 180 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa?

    Stuhleck, 12 km to the north, with about 22 km of pistes.
