    Snow at Familienschiberg St. Jakob im Wa through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,100 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 21 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 28 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 43 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.
