    About Mont Rigaud

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Mont Rigaud sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 58 m to 167 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 5.5 km to ski. Most of the length is black diamond (41%, about 2.3 km). Good skiers will like it here; 41% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 2.3 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Mont Rigaud usually gets

    At about 112 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 10 January and lasts to 27 March: about 11 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 51 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm, with 52 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 30 cm to 70 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    How Mont Rigaud is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 109 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 58 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mont Rigaud (228 m), Sommet de la Croix du Mont-Rigaud (222 m) and Mont Oscar (171 m).

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 3 hotels: Auberge du Mont-Rigaud, Travelodge Wyndham Rigaud and Aubegre des Gallant.

    Questions about Mont Rigaud

    When is the best time to ski at Mont Rigaud?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Can you ski at Mont Rigaud at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    What altitude is Mont Rigaud at?

    Between 58 m and 167 m, so 109 m of vertical drop.

    Does Mont Rigaud have steep runs?

    Yes: about 2.3 km of its graded pistes (41%) are difficult.

    How big is Mont Rigaud?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
