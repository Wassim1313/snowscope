    About Długa Polana

    Długa Polana is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 123 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Its lifts climb from 683 m to 773 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zadział, 2.3 km to the west.

    The pistes of Długa Polana

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.4 km. Most of the length is blue (55%, about 0.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Długa Polana gives 90 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 683 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet, 2 lifts in all. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
