    About Szklarska Poręba

    Szklarska Poręba is a ski area. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pietkiewiczówka, 1.5 km to the south-west.

    Snow at Szklarska Poręba through a normal winter

    Count on about 6 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 887 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. A normal season tops out near 39 cm, around 21 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 64 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 1 cm in a poor year to 54 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Szklarska Poręba

    When does Szklarska Poręba have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Szklarska Poręba at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.
