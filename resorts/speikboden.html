    About Speikboden

    Speikboden is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 31 km of pistes put it at number 200 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 938 m and 2,398 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Klausberg, 3.6 km to the north-east.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,668 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 41 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 68 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 43 cm and 92 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 61 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 70 cm, around 20 February.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,460 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 938 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 3 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Schwarzenbachspitz - Monte Rio Nero, 3,246 m high and 7.6 km north of the resort.

    Runs by difficulty

    Speikboden has 33 graded runs adding up to about 31 km. Most of the length is blue (58%, about 18 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Quick answers

    When does Speikboden have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Speikboden at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 41 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Speikboden at?

    The top is at 2,398 m and the base at 938 m.

    Is Speikboden suitable for families?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 18 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Speikboden have?

    About 31 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
