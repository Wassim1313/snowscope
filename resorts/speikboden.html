    About Speikboden

    Speikboden is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 31 km of pistes put it at number 200 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 938 m and 2,398 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Klausberg, 3.6 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,460 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 938 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.
