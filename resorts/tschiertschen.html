    About Tschiertschen

    With roughly 20 km of runs, Tschiertschen sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,355 m up to 2,378 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pradaschier, 6.3 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 41 marked runs cover roughly 20 km. The largest share is red, with 9.2 km (46%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 1.7 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Tschiertschen through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,866 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 23 March, with about 92 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 55 cm and 110 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 81 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 46 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 68 cm in the best years.

    How Tschiertschen is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,023 m: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,355 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aroser Rothorn at 2,980 m, standing 602 m above the top lift.

    How Tschiertschen compares nearby

    In this group of 7, Tschiertschen is number 2 for piste length. Fideriser Heuberge has the longer snow season: around 27 weeks of solid cover, compared with 23 at Tschiertschen. The highest nearby is Arosa Lenzerheide, topping out at 2,854 m.

    Tschiertschen: common questions

    When does Tschiertschen have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Tschiertschen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 46 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Tschiertschen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,378 m; the lowest starts at 1,355 m (1,023 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Tschiertschen?

    Yes: 45% of the graded pistes, about 9.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Tschiertschen have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
