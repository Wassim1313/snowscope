    About Montgenèvre

    With some 62 km of runs, Montgenèvre belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,795 m up to 2,581 m. The closest other ski area is Claviere (3.6 km, east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 114 marked runs cover roughly 61 km. Most of the length is blue (43%, about 26 km). With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 2,188 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 November and lasts to 25 May: about 27 weeks. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 119 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 76 cm, from 40 cm in a poor year to 134 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 144 cm. Most winters land between 86 cm and 177 cm by mid-February.

    How Montgenèvre is laid out

    786 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,795 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 3 gondolas, 2 chair-gondolas, 9 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 3 drag lifts and 3 moving carpets, 24 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Mont Chaberton (3,132 m), 4.6 km to the north-east, about 551 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Montgenèvre

    What is the best month to ski at Montgenèvre?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Montgenèvre at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 76 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Montgenèvre open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 17 April 2027.

    How high is Montgenèvre?

    Between 1,795 m and 2,581 m, so 786 m of vertical drop.

    Is Montgenèvre suitable for families?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 39 km, are easy.
