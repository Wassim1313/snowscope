    About Hakkai Sanroku

    Hakkai Sanroku is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 239 of 385 in Japan by length. The slopes start at 212 m and reach 415 m at the top. Muikamachi Hakkaisan is the next resort, 4.5 km away to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 6 graded runs, about 3.3 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.0 km, or 62%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    How Hakkai Sanroku is laid out

    The vertical drop is 203 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 212 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Hakkai Sanroku: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Hakkai Sanroku?

    Its highest lift reaches 415 m; the lowest starts at 212 m (203 m of vertical).

    Who is Hakkai Sanroku best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 62% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hakkai Sanroku?

    Muikamachi Hakkaisan, 4.5 km to the south, with about 11 km of pistes.

    How big is Hakkai Sanroku?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
