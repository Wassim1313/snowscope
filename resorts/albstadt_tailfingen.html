    About Albstadt-Tailfingen

    The mountain at Albstadt-Tailfingen

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 731 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 22 January, with about 4 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 17 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, with 10 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January.
