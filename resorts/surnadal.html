    About Surnadal

    Compact and local, Surnadal has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 106 m up to 471 m. Its nearest neighbour is Surnadal alpinsenter, 0.7 km south-west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Surnadal and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Sunndal alpinsenter (22 km south) has about 5.0 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Nerskogen skisenter does better, with about 30 weeks of a good base instead of 24. The highest nearby is Nerskogen skisenter, topping out at 1,060 m.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Surnadal hold around 22 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 30 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 23 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 27 cm at mid-mountain).

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 365 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Its top at 471 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Honnstadknyken (1,071 m), 7.0 km to the east, about 600 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 288 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 28 November and keeps it until about 16 May, roughly 24 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 109 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. A normal season tops out near 124 cm, around 23 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 59 cm (lean years 36 cm, snowy years 93 cm). For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 70 cm and 145 cm by mid-February.

    Surnadal: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Surnadal?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Surnadal at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 59 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Surnadal?

    The top is at 471 m and the base at 106 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Surnadal?

    Sunndal alpinsenter, 22 km to the south, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Surnadal have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
