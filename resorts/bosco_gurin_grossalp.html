    About Bosco Gurin

    Bosco Gurin is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 346 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,482 m and 2,326 m above sea level. Val Formazza is the next resort, 7.5 km away to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    844 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,482 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Capanna Grossalp (mountain hut), Hotel Walser (hotel), Casa Moni (guesthouse) and Rifugio Sonnenberg (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
