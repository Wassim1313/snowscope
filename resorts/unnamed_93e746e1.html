    About Dobel-Skilift Schönwald

    Dobel-Skilift Schönwald is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 987 m and reach 1,053 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skilift Weißenbach, 1.4 km to the north-west.

    How Dobel-Skilift Schönwald compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 7.9 km east to Skilift Schlossberg Oberkirnach, with about 2.0 km of runs. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 66 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,187 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. Above the ski area rises Obereck (1,178 m), 6.5 km to the west, about 125 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,020 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm, with 18 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 19 January, with about 7 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late December and early March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 26 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.
