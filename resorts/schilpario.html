    How Schilpario is laid out

    75 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Aprica reaches 2,254 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Gleno at 2,882 m, standing 1,667 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,178 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 February and keeps it until about 11 March, roughly 3 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early January and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 36 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 17 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 37 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 8 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 9 cm to 59 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Questions about Schilpario

    What is the best month to ski at Schilpario?

    Aim for early January and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-February to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Schilpario at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Schilpario?

    Between 1,140 m and 1,215 m, so 75 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Schilpario?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 1 lift.
