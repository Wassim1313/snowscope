    About Luz-Ardiden

    With roughly 25 km of runs, Luz-Ardiden sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. Its lifts climb from 1,690 m to 2,485 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cauterets Cirque du Lys, 7.6 km to the west.

    The pistes of Luz-Ardiden

    The piste map lists 37 graded runs, about 23 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 54%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.9 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 795 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,690 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,485 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 6 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Pic d'Ardiden, 2,988 m high and 5.6 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,088 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 41 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 93 cm, a dry one 8 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 52 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 107 cm. For about 3 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 39 cm and 162 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Luz-Ardiden

    What is the best month to ski at Luz-Ardiden?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Luz-Ardiden at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 41 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Luz-Ardiden?

    The announced opening day is 25 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 28 March 2027.

    What altitude is Luz-Ardiden at?

    Between 1,690 m and 2,485 m, so 795 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Luz-Ardiden best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 54% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
