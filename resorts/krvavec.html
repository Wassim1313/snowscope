    About Krvavec

    Krvavec is a mid-sized ski area with about 16 km of pistes, number 366 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,474 m and reach 1,963 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Velika planina (8.4 km, east).

    How Krvavec is laid out

    489 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,474 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 1,963 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grintovec at 2,558 m, standing 595 m above the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 39 runs and around 15 km to ski. Most of the length is red (52%, about 7.7 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 1.4 km of black or expert runs. 0.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow at Krvavec through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,718 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 28 January and keeps it until about 5 March, roughly 4 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm, with 59 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 38 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 96 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Krvavec?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Krvavec at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 11 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Krvavec?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,963 m; the lowest starts at 1,474 m (489 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Krvavec best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Krvavec?

    About 16 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
