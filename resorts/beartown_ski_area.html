    About Beartown

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Beartown one of the smaller places to ski in US Northeast. Skiing here happens between 262 m and 302 m above sea level.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Whiteface Mountain (49 km south-west) has about 37 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Whiteface Mountain does better, with about 16 weeks of a good base instead of 5. Whiteface Mountain reaches 1,333 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How Beartown is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Beartown gives 40 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 262 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Rand Hill at 459 m, standing 157 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Beartown usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 282 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 3 February and lasts to 9 March: about 5 weeks. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 36 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 16 cm and 61 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 17 cm (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 33 cm).

    Beartown: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Beartown?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Can you ski at Beartown at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Beartown?

    The top is at 302 m and the base at 262 m.

    Is Beartown suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 0.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Beartown?

    Whiteface Mountain, 49 km to the south-west, with about 37 km of pistes.
