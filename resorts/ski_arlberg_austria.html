    About Ski Arlberg

    Compact and local, Ski Arlberg has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,146 m and reach 1,851 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Flirsch, 6.3 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 2.8 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.4 km, or 84%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 13 km north-west to Ski Arlberg, with about 272 km of runs. For more reliable snow, St. Anton/St. Christoph/Stuben usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 27 weeks, against 15 here. The highest nearby is St. Anton/St. Christoph/Stuben, topping out at 2,642 m.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 22 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Ski Arlberg. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 31 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 8 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Arlberg gives 705 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hoher Riffler (Nord) at 3,167 m, standing 1,316 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Ski Arlberg through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,498 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 January and lasts to 30 April: about 15 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 22 cm, with 38 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 37 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 31 cm and 67 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 48 cm.

    Ski Arlberg: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Arlberg?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late April.

    Can you ski at Ski Arlberg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 22 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Arlberg?

    The top is at 1,851 m and the base at 1,146 m.

    What level of skier is Ski Arlberg best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 84% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Ski Arlberg?

    Ski Arlberg, 13 km to the north-west, with about 272 km of pistes.
