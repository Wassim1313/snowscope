    About Mount Washington Alpine Resort

    With some 61 km of runs, Mount Washington Alpine Resort belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Canada West. Skiing here happens between 1,081 m and 1,569 m above sea level.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 163 runs and around 53 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 22 km, or 40%. Good skiers will like it here; 37% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 20 km.

    This week's snow

The 2026/27 season at Mount Washington Alpine Resort is announced to start on 11 December 2026 and to run until 4 April 2027.

    How Mount Washington Alpine Resort is laid out

    The vertical drop is 488 m: runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 5 chairlifts and 5 rope tows, 10 lifts in all. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Washington (1,588 m), Mount Allan Brooks (1,500 m) and Strata Mountain (1,440 m).

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,325 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 100 cm and 337 cm. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 282 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 278 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For about 25 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 147 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 58 cm in a poor year to 224 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Mount Washington Alpine Resort

    When is the best time to ski at Mount Washington Alpine Resort?

    Aim for early January and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Mount Washington Alpine Resort have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 147 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Mount Washington Alpine Resort open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Mount Washington Alpine Resort?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,569 m; the lowest starts at 1,081 m (488 m of vertical).

    Is Mount Washington Alpine Resort good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 20 km of its graded pistes (37%) are difficult.
