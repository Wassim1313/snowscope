    Mount Washington Alpine Resort in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Mount Washington Alpine Resort (1,325 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 147 cm, with a normal range of 92 cm–187 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 234 cm; the middle half of winters range from 178 cm to 266 cm.

    The snowiest month is April, when the pack peaks at about 282 cm.

    85 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 96 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1998-99, peaking at 552 cm. Worst: 2014-15, which never passed 92 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-November until mid-June in a normal winter, roughly 214 days.

    Mount Washington Alpine Resort: quick answers

    Is there snow in Mount Washington Alpine Resort at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 147 cm at Christmas, and 96 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 2 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Mount Washington Alpine Resort?

    April: the snow peaks at about 282 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-June.

    How snowy is Mount Washington Alpine Resort?

    About 282 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 92 cm in the leanest winter (2014-15) and 552 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high is Mount Washington Alpine Resort?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,569 m; the lowest starts at 1,081 m (488 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Mount Washington Alpine Resort have?

    About 61 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
