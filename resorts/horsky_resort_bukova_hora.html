    About Horský resort Buková hora

    With some 8.0 km of runs, Horský resort Buková hora belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 554 m and 955 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Čenkovice, 1.0 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Horský resort Buková hora

    Horský resort Buková hora has 16 graded runs adding up to about 8.1 km. Most of the length is blue (49%, about 4.0 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Horský resort Buková hora

    401 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 554 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 4 lifts in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 754 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 1 February. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 57 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm; a snowy December gave 42 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Horský resort Buková hora?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Horský resort Buková hora at Christmas?

    Can beginners ski at Horský resort Buková hora?
