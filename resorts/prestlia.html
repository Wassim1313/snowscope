    About Prestlia

    Prestlia has about 0.3 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 26 m to 71 m. Heggesåsen Alpinbakke is the next resort, 20 km away to the north-east.

    How Prestlia compares nearby

    Torsbustaden has the longer snow season: around 20 weeks of solid cover, compared with 15 at Prestlia. The highest nearby is Torsbustaden, topping out at 397 m.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Prestlia today: about 13 cm up high. The last seven days brought 2 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 11 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 45 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Kverkilberget, 200 m high and 5.0 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 15 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 48 m in an ordinary winter, from early January to mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 25 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 9 cm, snowy years 49 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 58 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 24 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. Most winters land between 19 cm and 81 cm by mid-February.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Husfrua Gårdshotell (guesthouse), Jektvolden Fjordhotell (hotel), Jegtvolden Fjordhotell (hotel) and Strømnes (guesthouse).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Prestlia?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Prestlia have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Prestlia?

    The top is at 71 m and the base at 26 m.

    How many km of pistes does Prestlia have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
