    About Äänemäki

    Äänemäki is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 118 m up to 187 m. Its nearest neighbour is Paavonrinteet, 25 km west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Äänemäki usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 152 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to mid-April, about 15 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 50 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 33 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 26 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 42 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 26 cm and 63 cm.

    How Äänemäki is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Äänemäki gives 69 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 drag lifts and 1 rope tow. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Äänemäki (190 m) and Tärttävuori (189 m).

    Questions about Äänemäki

    What is the best month to ski at Äänemäki?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Äänemäki have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    What altitude is Äänemäki at?

    The top is at 187 m and the base at 118 m.
