    About Kupres

    Kupres is a ski area. The slopes start at 1,300 m and reach 1,716 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ski Ivan (1.5 km, north-west).

    How Kupres is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Kupres gives 416 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,300 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The only lift is a chairlift.
