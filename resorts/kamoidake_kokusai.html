    About Kamoidake Kokusai

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Kamoidake Kokusai sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Its lifts climb from 222 m to 466 m. Shintotsukawacho Sotchidake is the next resort, 17 km away to the west.

    The pistes of Kamoidake Kokusai

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 3.6 km to ski. Most of the length is red (43%, about 1.5 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts.

    How Kamoidake Kokusai is laid out

    244 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 222 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 466 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 3 chairlifts and 1 T-bar. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
