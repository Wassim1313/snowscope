    About Aspen Mountain

    Aspen Mountain is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 59 km of pistes put it at number 28 of 175. The slopes start at 2,426 m and reach 3,431 m at the top. Aspen Highlands is the next resort, 3.7 km away to the west.

    The pistes of Aspen Mountain

    Counting every graded piste, there are 138 runs and around 59 km to ski. Most of the length is blue square (53%, about 31 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 28 km of difficult runs.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,928 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to mid-May. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 162 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 86 cm, from 38 cm in a poor year to 133 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 100 cm to 190 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 132 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 16 weeks.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,005 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,426 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 gondola and 5 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Highland Peak, 3,757 m high and 6.4 km south-west of the resort.

    Questions about Aspen Mountain

    When is the best time to ski at Aspen Mountain?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Aspen Mountain have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 86 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Aspen Mountain?

    The announced opening day is 26 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 18 April 2027.

    How high is Aspen Mountain?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,431 m; the lowest starts at 2,426 m (1,005 m of vertical).

    Does Aspen Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 28 km of its graded pistes (47%) are difficult.
