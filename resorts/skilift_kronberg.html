    The mountain at Skilift Kronberg

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Kronberg gives 89 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 543 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Feuerkogel, for one, climbs to 1,676 m. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Rossmoos at 1,015 m, standing 383 m above the top lift.
