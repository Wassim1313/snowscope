    The mountain at Skilift Kronberg

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Kronberg gives 89 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 543 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Feuerkogel, for one, climbs to 1,676 m.
