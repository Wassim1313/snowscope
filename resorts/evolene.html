    About Evolène

    Around 26 km of marked runs place Evolène among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 236 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,385 m and 2,625 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is La Forclaz (5.9 km, south-east).

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 67 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Evolène. 11 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 53 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,240 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,385 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.
