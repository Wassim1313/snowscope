    About Evolène

    Around 26 km of marked runs place Evolène among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 236 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,385 m and 2,625 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is La Forclaz (5.9 km, south-east).

    The pistes of Evolène

    In all, 33 marked runs cover roughly 26 km. Most of the length is red (41%, about 11 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. The steepest pistes add up to about 5.2 km.

    About 2.4 km of pistes (9%) have snowmaking, mostly the busiest runs and the way back to the bottom.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,240 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,385 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Aiguilles Rouges d'Arolla (3,646 m), 6.7 km to the south, about 1,021 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 48 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Evolène. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 17 cm).

    How much snow Evolène usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 2,005 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 December and lasts to 18 May: about 22 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 45 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 41 cm and 82 cm. A normal season tops out near 66 cm, around 17 February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm (lean years 19 cm, snowy years 53 cm).

    Evolène: common questions

    When does Evolène have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Evolène at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Evolène?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,625 m; the lowest starts at 1,385 m (1,240 m of vertical).

    Who is Evolène best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 41% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Does Evolène have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 2.4 km of its pistes (9%).
