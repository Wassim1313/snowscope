    About Javornik

    Javornik has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 814 m up to 879 m. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cerkno, 30 km to the north.

    How much snow Javornik usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 846 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 5 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 35 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 19 cm in a big one.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    65 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 814 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Srednja gora, 1,275 m high and 3.2 km south of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Javornik include Pirnatova koča na Javorniku (mountain hut), Gostilna Turk (hotel), Turistična kmetija Pr'Mark (guesthouse) and Požarjeva koča (chalet).

    Javornik: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Javornik?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Is there snow at Javornik at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    What altitude is Javornik at?

    Its highest lift reaches 879 m; the lowest starts at 814 m (65 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Javornik have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
