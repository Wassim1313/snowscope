    About Winterpol Karpacz Biały Jar

    Compact and local, Winterpol Karpacz Biały Jar has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 715 m to 793 m. Karpacz / Śnieżka is the next resort, 0.3 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of Winterpol Karpacz Biały Jar

    The piste map lists 3 graded runs, about 1.5 km in total. Most of the length is blue (64%, about 0.9 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 78 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 715 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 2 moving carpets, 3 lifts in all. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 754 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-February to late February, about 2 weeks of good cover. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 54 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 10 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 40 cm). In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 32 cm, around 22 February.

    Questions about Winterpol Karpacz Biały Jar

    When does Winterpol Karpacz Biały Jar have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    Can you ski at Winterpol Karpacz Biały Jar at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Winterpol Karpacz Biały Jar?

    Between 715 m and 793 m, so 78 m of vertical drop.

    Is Winterpol Karpacz Biały Jar suitable for families?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Winterpol Karpacz Biały Jar have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
