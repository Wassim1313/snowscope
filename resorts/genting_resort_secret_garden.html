    About Genting Resort Secret Garden

    Genting Resort Secret Garden ranks number 7 of 92 ski areas in China by piste length, with about 28 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 1,655 m and 2,103 m above sea level. Wanlong Paradise Resort is the next resort, 2.5 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Genting Resort Secret Garden has 41 graded runs adding up to about 28 km. The largest share is blue square, with 14 km (50%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 12 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    448 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,655 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,103 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
