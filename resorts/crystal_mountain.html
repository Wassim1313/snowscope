    Crystal Mountain in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Crystal Mountain (about 5,604 ft (1,708 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 69 in (176 cm); half of the winters sit between 51 in (129 cm) and 85 in (215 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 119 in (302 cm); the middle half of winters range from 96 in (245 cm) to 142 in (361 cm).

    The snowiest month is April, when the pack peaks at about 150 in (380 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 93 % of winters, and by 1 January in 98 %.

    The deepest winter was 1998-99 (244 in (620 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2004-05 (59 in (150 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-June, about 221 days.

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Crystal Mountain at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 69 in (176 cm) at Christmas, and 98 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 15 in (38 cm).

    What is the best time to ski Crystal Mountain?

    April: the snow peaks at about 150 in (380 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-June.

    How deep does the snow get at Crystal Mountain?

    About 150 in (380 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 59 in (150 cm) in the leanest winter (2004-05) and 244 in (620 cm) in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high is Crystal Mountain?

    The top is at 2,072 m and the base at 1,344 m.

    How many km of pistes does Crystal Mountain have?

    About 39 km of pistes and 11 lifts.

    About Crystal Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Crystal Mountain offers about 39 km of pistes (number 52 of 175 in US West). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,344 m up to 2,072 m. The closest other ski area is White Pass (35 km, south).

    The mountain at Crystal Mountain

    Skied top to bottom, Crystal Mountain gives 728 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,344 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
