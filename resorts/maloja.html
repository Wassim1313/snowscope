    About Maloja

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Maloja sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,821 m to 2,133 m. Casaccia is the next resort, 3.1 km away to the west.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 15 km north-east to Corviglia, with about 68 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Bivio usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 28 weeks, against 14 here. The highest nearby is Corvatsch-Furtschellas, topping out at 3,296 m.

    What there is to ski at Maloja

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 3.1 km to ski. Most of the length is red (61%, about 1.9 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.2 km.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Maloja hold around 6 cm of snow today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Friday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 13 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Maloja gives 312 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,821 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 1 button lift and 2 rope tows. The skyline is dominated by Piz Bacun, 3,244 m high and 6.5 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,977 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late March, about 14 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 61 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 63 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 25 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Most winters land between 40 cm and 84 cm by mid-February.

    Quick answers

    When does Maloja have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late March.

    Can you ski at Maloja at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 32 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Maloja at?

    Between 1,821 m and 2,133 m, so 312 m of vertical drop.

    Does Maloja have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.2 km of its graded pistes (39%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Maloja?

    Corviglia, 15 km to the north-east, with about 68 km of pistes.
