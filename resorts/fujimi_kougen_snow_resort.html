    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 190 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Pilatus Tateshina Snow Resort reaches 2,222 m. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.
