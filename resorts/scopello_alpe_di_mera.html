    About Scopello / Alpe di Mera

    With roughly 15 km of runs, Scopello / Alpe di Mera sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 684 m and 1,737 m above sea level. Bielmonte is the next resort, 8.8 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Scopello / Alpe di Mera

    The piste map lists 26 graded runs, about 15 km in total. Most of the length is red (73%, about 11 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.7 km of difficult runs.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Scopello / Alpe di Mera hold around 8 cm of snow today. 4 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 33 cm by Friday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (7 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Scopello / Alpe di Mera gives 1,053 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Because the lifts start as low as 684 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Monte Bo (2,556 m), 7.6 km to the south-west, about 819 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Scopello / Alpe di Mera usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,210 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to mid-March, about 6 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 43 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 10 cm to 61 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 34 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Rifugio Camparient (mountain hut), La Casera Bianca (guesthouse), Baita Neva (chalet) and Hotel al Cantuccio (hotel).

    Scopello / Alpe di Mera: common questions

    When does Scopello / Alpe di Mera have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Scopello / Alpe di Mera have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Scopello / Alpe di Mera?

    Between 684 m and 1,737 m, so 1,053 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Scopello / Alpe di Mera best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Scopello / Alpe di Mera have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
