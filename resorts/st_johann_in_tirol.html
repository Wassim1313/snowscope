    About St Johann in Tirol

    St Johann in Tirol is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 34 km of pistes put it at number 186 of 1,056. The slopes start at 666 m and reach 1,601 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Yappy's Winterland Kirchdorf in, 6.1 km to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 54 graded runs, about 34 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 20 km (59%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 935 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. At 666 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. To get up there are 5 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Kitzbüheler Horn (1,996 m), 2.5 km to the south, about 395 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow St Johann in Tirol usually gets

    At about 1,134 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 January and lasts to 29 March: about 7 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 19 cm; a snowy December gave 37 cm, a dry one 5 cm. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 27 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 34 cm. Most winters land between 19 cm and 51 cm by mid-February.

    St Johann in Tirol: common questions

    When does St Johann in Tirol have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does St Johann in Tirol have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 19 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is St Johann in Tirol at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,601 m; the lowest starts at 666 m (935 m of vertical).

    Is St Johann in Tirol suitable for families?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 21 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does St Johann in Tirol have?

    About 34 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
