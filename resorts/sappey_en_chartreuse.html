    About Sappey-en-Chartreuse

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Sappey-en-Chartreuse one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 969 m and 1,104 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Col de Porte, 4.2 km to the north.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,036 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 December and lasts to 27 April: about 19 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 24 cm to 109 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 68 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 50 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm, from 10 cm in a poor year to 100 cm in a big one.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Sappey-en-Chartreuse has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.0 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.6 km (62%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse (12 km north) has about 7.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Col de Porte does better, with about 23 weeks of a good base instead of 19. For higher slopes, Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse climbs to 1,743 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    135 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 969 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse, for one, climbs to 1,743 m. Getting up the mountain: 4 button lifts and 1 drag lift, 5 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Chamechaude at 2,082 m, standing 978 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Gîte du Chant de l'Eau (guesthouse), Chalet Robert (chalet), La Jacobine (hostel) and Auberge du Sappey (hostel).

    Questions about Sappey-en-Chartreuse

    When is the best time to ski at Sappey-en-Chartreuse?

    Aim for mid-February and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Sappey-en-Chartreuse at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 42 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Sappey-en-Chartreuse?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,104 m; the lowest starts at 969 m (135 m of vertical).

    Is Sappey-en-Chartreuse good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Sappey-en-Chartreuse?

    Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse, 12 km to the north, with about 7.0 km of pistes.
