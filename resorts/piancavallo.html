    About Piancavallo

    Piancavallo is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 389 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,267 m and reach 1,812 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Nevelandia, 1.7 km east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Piancavallo

    In all, 59 marked runs cover roughly 15 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 80%. Intermediate runs make up 80% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Piancavallo gives 545 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 6 chairlifts, 4 drag lifts and 6 moving carpets. First-timers get 6 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Cimon del Cavallo at 2,251 m, standing 439 m above the top lift.

    How Piancavallo compares nearby

    In this group of 7, Piancavallo has the most pistes. Sella Ciampigotto has the longer snow season: around 18 weeks of solid cover, compared with 7 at Piancavallo. For higher slopes, Forni di Sopra climbs to 2,068 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 1,540 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 January and lasts to 17 March: about 7 weeks. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 9 cm to 65 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 11 cm; a snowy December gave 31 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 10 March, with about 35 cm.

    Piancavallo: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Piancavallo?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Piancavallo at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Piancavallo?

    Between 1,267 m and 1,812 m, so 545 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Piancavallo best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 80% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Piancavallo have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 16 lifts.
