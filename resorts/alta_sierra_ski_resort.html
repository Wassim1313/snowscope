    About Alta Sierra

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Alta Sierra one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Its lifts climb from 2,037 m to 2,153 m.

    The pistes of Alta Sierra

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 2.0 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (46%, about 0.9 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Alta Sierra gives 116 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,037 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Black Mountain at 2,267 m, standing 114 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,095 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early January to late February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 178 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 12 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 73 cm). Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 39 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-January.

    Alta Sierra: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Alta Sierra?

    Aim for early January and mid-January; the cover is usually good from early January to late February.

    Can you ski at Alta Sierra at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 12 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Alta Sierra at?

    Between 2,037 m and 2,153 m, so 116 m of vertical drop.

    Does Alta Sierra have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.5 km of its graded pistes (25%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Alta Sierra have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
