    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Alta Sierra gives 116 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,037 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Black Mountain at 2,267 m, standing 114 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,095 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early January to late February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 178 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.
