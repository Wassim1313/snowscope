    About Yabuli Sun Mountain

    Yabuli Sun Mountain is one of the bigger ski areas of China: about 16 km of pistes put it at number 17 of 92. Its lifts climb from 456 m to 996 m. Its nearest neighbour is Yabuli Sports Training Base, 1.5 km south-east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Yabuli Sun Mountain

    In all, 16 marked runs cover roughly 16 km. The largest share is blue square, with 7.6 km (48%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.9 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Yabuli Sun Mountain gives 540 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 456 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts and 4 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts.
