    About Kozákov

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kozákov offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 172 of 257 in Central Europe). Skiing here happens between 631 m and 724 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski areál Struhy, 6.7 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 1.4 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 67%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 93 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 631 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 724 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 2 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    How Kozákov compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Ski Šachty: about 5.0 km of pistes, 14 km to the north-east. For more reliable snow, Ski Šachty usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 4 weeks, against 1 here.

    This week's snow

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 678 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-February to late February, about 1 weeks of good cover. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 59 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 32 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 12 cm; a snowy December gave 51 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February.

    Quick answers

    When does Kozákov have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    Is there snow at Kozákov at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 12 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Kozákov?

    The top is at 724 m and the base at 631 m.

    Can beginners ski at Kozákov?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Kozákov?

    Ski Šachty, 14 km to the north-east, with about 5.0 km of pistes.
