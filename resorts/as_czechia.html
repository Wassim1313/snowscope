    About Aš

    Neither huge nor tiny, Aš offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 149 of 257 in Central Europe). Its lifts climb from 646 m to 747 m. The closest other ski area is Aš (0.1 km, west).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Aš gives 101 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 646 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.

    Aš and the resorts around it
