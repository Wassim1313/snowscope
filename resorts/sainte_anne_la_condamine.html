    About Sainte-Anne la Condamine

    With roughly 17 km of runs, Sainte-Anne la Condamine sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,835 m and reach 2,409 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Crévoux (11 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Sainte-Anne la Condamine has 20 graded runs adding up to about 17 km. Most of the length is blue (51%, about 8.5 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,122 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 65 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 29 cm in a poor year to 129 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 72 cm and 168 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 136 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 97 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Sainte-Anne la Condamine gives 574 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,835 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 3 button lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Le Grand Bérard, 3,048 m high and 4.2 km west of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Sainte-Anne la Condamine?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Sainte-Anne la Condamine at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 65 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Sainte-Anne la Condamine?

    Between 1,835 m and 2,409 m, so 574 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Sainte-Anne la Condamine?

    Yes: 73% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Sainte-Anne la Condamine?

    Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul, 15 km to the north, with about 131 km of pistes.
