    Sainte-Anne la Condamine in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Sainte-Anne la Condamine (2,122 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 65 cm of cover (50 cm to 96 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 126 cm; the middle half of winters range from 96 cm to 151 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 136 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 50 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 78 %.

    The deepest winter was 2017-18 (250 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2004-05 (82 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 177 days.

    Sainte-Anne la Condamine: quick answers

    Can you ski Sainte-Anne la Condamine at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 65 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 78 % of 46 winters. Right now: 71 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Sainte-Anne la Condamine?

    March: the snow peaks at about 136 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Sainte-Anne la Condamine get?

    About 136 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 82 cm in the leanest winter (2004-05) and 250 cm in the biggest (2017-18).

    How high do the lifts go at Sainte-Anne la Condamine?

    Between 1,835 m and 2,409 m, so 574 m of vertical drop.

    Is there a larger resort near Sainte-Anne la Condamine?

    Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul, 15 km to the north, with about 131 km of pistes.

    How big is Sainte-Anne la Condamine?

    About 17 km of pistes and 5 lifts.

    About Sainte-Anne la Condamine

    With roughly 17 km of runs, Sainte-Anne la Condamine sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,835 m and reach 2,409 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Crévoux (11 km, north-west).

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Sainte-Anne la Condamine hold around 71 cm of snow today. 39 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 28 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Sainte-Anne la Condamine gives 574 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,835 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most.

    Near the lifts you will find Le Chalet Sainte-Anne (hotel), La Condaminoise (guesthouse), Villa Morelia (hotel) and Le Sans Souci (hotel).
