    About Koktokay

    About 30 km of pistes make Koktokay one of the ten biggest ski areas of China (number 6). The slopes start at 1,864 m and reach 3,077 m at the top.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Koktokay gives 1,213 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 1,864 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. To get up there are 5 gondolas, 3 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 16 graded runs, about 29 km in total. Most of the length is green circle (57%, about 17 km). With 64% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Questions about Koktokay

    How high is Koktokay?

    Between 1,864 m and 3,077 m, so 1,213 m of vertical drop.

    Is Koktokay suitable for families?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 19 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Koktokay have?

    About 30 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
