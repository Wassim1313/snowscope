    About Žacléř

    Žacléř is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 76 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Its lifts climb from 569 m to 810 m. The closest other ski area is Prkenný Důl (1.0 km, south-east).

    What there is to ski at Žacléř

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 3.8 km. Most of the length is blue (80%, about 3.1 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Žacléř

    From top to bottom that makes 241 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 569 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 810 m is the highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow, 3 lifts in all.

    Snow at Žacléř through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 690 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm; a snowy December gave 36 cm, a dry one 1 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 50 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 26 February.

    Questions about Žacléř

    What is the best month to ski at Žacléř?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Žacléř have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Žacléř?

    The top is at 810 m and the base at 569 m.

    Can beginners ski at Žacléř?

    Yes: 80% of the graded pistes, about 3.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Žacléř have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
