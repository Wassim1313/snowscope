    About Monte Popolo

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Monte Popolo sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,218 m to 1,601 m. Radstadt is the next resort, 5.5 km away to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Monte Popolo

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 7.2 km. The largest share is red, with 5.5 km (77%). Intermediate runs make up 77% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Ski amadé: about 605 km of pistes, 10 km to the south-west. For more reliable snow, Ski amadé usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 23 weeks, against 18 here. Ski amadé reaches 2,642 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Snow at Monte Popolo through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,410 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late April, about 18 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 38 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 58 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 29 cm and 72 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 30 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 50 cm in the best years.

    Monte Popolo today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Monte Popolo hold around 24 cm of snow today. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (16 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Monte Popolo gives 383 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 chairlift and 1 button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Rossbrand at 1,770 m, standing 169 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Monte Popolo include Schwaiger (hotel), Reitlehenalm (mountain hut), Gasthof Ebnerwirt (hotel) and Unterbuchstein (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Monte Popolo have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Monte Popolo have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 30 cm deep.

    What altitude is Monte Popolo at?

    The top is at 1,601 m and the base at 1,218 m.

    What level of skier is Monte Popolo best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 77% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Monte Popolo?

    Ski amadé, 10 km to the south-west, with about 605 km of pistes.
