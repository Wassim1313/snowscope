    About Centro Ski Pucón

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Centro Ski Pucón sits in the middle of the size table of Andes. The slopes start at 1,250 m and reach 1,538 m at the top.

    What there is to ski at Centro Ski Pucón

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Most of the length is blue (40%, about 4.2 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 2.0 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 288 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Quick answers

    How high is Centro Ski Pucón?

    Between 1,250 m and 1,538 m, so 288 m of vertical drop.

    Is Centro Ski Pucón suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 5.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Centro Ski Pucón have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
