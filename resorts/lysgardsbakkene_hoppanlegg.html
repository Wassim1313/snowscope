    About Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg

    Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg has about 0.3 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 369 m and 462 m above sea level. Hafjell is the next resort, 12 km away to the north.

    How Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg compares nearby

    Sjusjøen Skisenter has the longer snow season: around 26 weeks of solid cover, compared with 23 at Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg. For higher slopes, Skeikampen climbs to 1,114 m.

    Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg today and the next days

    Snow on the ground at Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg today: about 16 cm up high. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 36 cm by now.

    Snow at Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 416 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 27 November and keeps it until about 7 May, roughly 23 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 51 cm, from 30 cm in a poor year to 79 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 88 cm, around 8 March. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 66 cm to 116 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 74 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    The mountain at Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg

    The vertical drop is 93 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Skeikampen, for one, climbs to 1,114 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar, 2 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Grøtåshaugen, 923 m high and 7.7 km north-east of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Birkebeineren Hotel & Apartments (hotel), Scandic Lillehammer Hotel (hotel), Gardsjord (guesthouse) and Aksjemøllen - by Classic Norway Hotels (hotel).

    Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    How much snow does Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 51 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg?

    Between 369 m and 462 m, so 93 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
