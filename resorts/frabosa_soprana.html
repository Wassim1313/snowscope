    About Frabosa Soprana

    Frabosa Soprana is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 312 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 887 m and reach 1,735 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Prato Nevoso, 3.5 km to the south.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Frabosa Soprana gives 848 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 887 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Monesi di Triora reaches 2,157 m.
