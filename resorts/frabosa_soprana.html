    About Frabosa Soprana

    Frabosa Soprana is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 312 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 887 m and reach 1,735 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Prato Nevoso, 3.5 km to the south.

    Snow at Frabosa Soprana through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,311 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 37 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 8 February, with about 19 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 58 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Frabosa Soprana gives 848 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 887 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Monesi di Triora reaches 2,157 m. To get up there are 5 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Monte Mondolé (2,382 m), 6.8 km to the south-west, about 647 m higher than the top lift.

    Frabosa Soprana: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Frabosa Soprana?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Is there snow at Frabosa Soprana at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Frabosa Soprana at?

    Between 887 m and 1,735 m, so 848 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Frabosa Soprana best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 70% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Frabosa Soprana have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
