    About Longchaumois Rosset

    Longchaumois Rosset has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,018 m up to 1,060 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Serra, 5.9 km to the south-east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    42 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Les Rousses, reach 1,663 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Le Crêt Pela at 1,495 m, standing 435 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,039 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 12 February, with about 26 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 12 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 48 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 56 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March.
