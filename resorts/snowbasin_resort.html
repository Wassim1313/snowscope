    About Snowbasin Resort

    Around 86 km of marked runs place Snowbasin Resort among the larger resorts of US West, number 18 by length. The slopes start at 1,939 m and reach 2,835 m at the top. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Nordic Valley Resort, 12 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 156 marked runs cover roughly 86 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 44 km, or 52%. Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 34 km of black or expert runs.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,387 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to early May. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 99 cm (lean years 30 cm, snowy years 201 cm). For about 19 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 59 cm and 233 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 320 cm, around 30 March. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 243 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 896 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,939 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 1 cable car, 2 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Ogden (2,917 m), Allen Peak (2,885 m) and De Moisy Peak (2,847 m).

    Questions about Snowbasin Resort

    What is the best month to ski at Snowbasin Resort?

    Aim for early February and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Can you ski at Snowbasin Resort at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 99 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Snowbasin Resort open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Snowbasin Resort?

    The top is at 2,835 m and the base at 1,939 m.

    Does Snowbasin Resort have steep runs?

    Yes: about 34 km of its graded pistes (39%) are difficult.
