    Snowbasin Resort in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Snowbasin Resort (about 7,831 ft (2,387 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 39 in (99 cm) of cover (24 in (60 cm) to 58 in (148 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 62 in (157 cm) (46 in (116 cm)–85 in (215 cm)).

    About Snowbasin Resort

    Around 86 km of marked runs place Snowbasin Resort among the larger resorts of US West, number 18 by length. The slopes start at 1,939 m and reach 2,835 m at the top. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Nordic Valley Resort, 12 km to the north.

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,387 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to early May.     Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 896 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,939 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 1 cable car, 2 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Ogden (2,917 m), Allen Peak (2,885 m) and De Moisy Peak (2,847 m).
