    About Argentera

    Neither huge nor tiny, Argentera offers about 16 km of pistes (number 357 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,631 m up to 2,519 m. The closest other ski area is Larche (11 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Argentera

    Counting every graded piste, there are 18 runs and around 16 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 12 km (73%). Intermediate runs make up 73% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 1.9 km of difficult runs. 1.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow at Argentera through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,075 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 5 December and keeps it until about 2 May, roughly 21 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 48 cm and 154 cm. A normal season tops out near 112 cm, around 7 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 6 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 64 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 53 cm, from 21 cm in a poor year to 113 cm in a big one.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 888 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,631 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 3 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Monte Oronaye / Tête de Moïse, 3,104 m high and 6.7 km north of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Roburent (hotel), Rifugio Becchi Rossi (mountain hut), B&B del Villaggio (guesthouse) and Rifugio Gardetta (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Argentera?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Argentera at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 53 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Argentera?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,519 m; the lowest starts at 1,631 m (888 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Argentera best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Argentera?

    Auron, 18 km to the south, with about 62 km of pistes.
