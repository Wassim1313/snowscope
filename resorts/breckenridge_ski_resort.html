    About Breckenridge

    Breckenridge is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 113 km of pistes put it at number 11 of 175. The slopes start at 2,918 m and reach 3,913 m at the top. Copper Mountain is the next resort, 8.2 km away to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 159 marked runs cover roughly 112 km. The largest share is blue square, with 48 km (43%). Steep ground is the speciality: 34% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 38 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 995 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 2,918 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its top at 3,913 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 35 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 20 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts and 10 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 10 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Pacific Peak (4,227 m), 7.4 km to the south-west, about 314 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Breckenridge through a normal winter

    Count on about 29 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 3,416 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 102 cm and 185 cm. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 88 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 46 cm in a poor year to 130 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 157 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 173 cm.

    Questions about Breckenridge

    When is the best time to ski at Breckenridge?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Breckenridge at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 88 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Breckenridge at?

    Between 2,918 m and 3,913 m, so 995 m of vertical drop.

    Is Breckenridge good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 38 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.

    Does Breckenridge have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 50 km of its pistes (44%).
