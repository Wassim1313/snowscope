    The snow at Breckenridge, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Breckenridge (about 11,207 ft (3,416 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 35 in (88 cm) of cover (24 in (60 cm) to 42 in (106 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 54 in (136 cm), between 45 in (115 cm) and 64 in (163 cm) in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 68 in (173 cm).

    By 15 December 76 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 91 %.

    1983-84 was the big one (104 in (263 cm)); 1980-81 the lean one (43 in (109 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from early November to mid-June, about 214 days.

    Breckenridge: quick answers

    Is there snow in Breckenridge at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 35 in (88 cm) at Christmas, and 91 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 12 in (30 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Breckenridge?

    March: the snow peaks at about 68 in (173 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from early November to mid-June.

    How snowy is Breckenridge?

    About 68 in (173 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early April); between 43 in (109 cm) in the leanest winter (1980-81) and 104 in (263 cm) in the biggest (1983-84).

    What altitude is Breckenridge at?

    Between 2,918 m and 3,913 m, so 995 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Breckenridge?

    About 113 km of pistes and 35 lifts.
