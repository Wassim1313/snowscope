    About Skilift am Seimberg

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skilift am Seimberg one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. The slopes start at 608 m and reach 703 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski alpin am Inselsberg, 4.4 km to the north.

    How Skilift am Seimberg compares nearby

    For more terrain, Ski- und Rodelarena Wasserkuppe (50 km south-west) has about 5.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. The highest nearby is Ski- und Rodelarena Wasserkuppe, topping out at 926 m.

    How much snow Skilift am Seimberg usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 656 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 24 cm. A normal season tops out near 9 cm, around 26 January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 22 cm, a dry one 0 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-December and late December. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    How Skilift am Seimberg is laid out

    95 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 608 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Ski- und Rodelarena Wasserkuppe reaches 926 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Skilift am Seimberg?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    Is there snow at Skilift am Seimberg at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    How high is Skilift am Seimberg?

    Between 608 m and 703 m, so 95 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Skilift am Seimberg?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
