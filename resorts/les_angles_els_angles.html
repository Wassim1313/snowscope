    About Les Angles

    With roughly 35 km of runs, Les Angles sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. The slopes start at 1,635 m and reach 2,358 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Les Angles, 1.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Les Angles

    The piste map lists 67 graded runs, about 35 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 44%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 3.1 km of difficult runs.

    How Les Angles is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 723 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,635 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The lift system counts 21 lifts: 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 4 button lifts, 9 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Puig Peric at 2,810 m, standing 452 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Les Angles usually gets

    Count on about 4 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,996 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 38 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 51 cm). Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 77 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 11 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Questions about Les Angles

    What is the best month to ski at Les Angles?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Les Angles at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    When does the ski season start at Les Angles?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026.

    How high is Les Angles?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,358 m; the lowest starts at 1,635 m (723 m of vertical).

    Is Les Angles suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.
