    About Les Angles

    With roughly 35 km of runs, Les Angles sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. The slopes start at 1,635 m and reach 2,358 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Les Angles, 1.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.
