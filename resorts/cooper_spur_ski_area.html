    About Cooper Spur

    Compact and local, Cooper Spur has about 3.0 km of runs, small for US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,204 m up to 1,293 m. The closest other ski area is Mt. Hood Meadows (9.7 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 2.5 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 1.8 km, or 69%. Intermediate runs make up 69% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Mt. Hood Meadows (9.7 km south-west) has about 57 km of pistes against 3.0 km here. Mt. Hood Meadows has the longer snow season: around 24 weeks of solid cover, compared with 21 at Cooper Spur. Timberline Lodge reaches 2,586 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How Cooper Spur is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Cooper Spur gives 89 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Timberline Lodge, for one, climbs to 2,586 m. The only lift is a chairlift. Above the ski area rises Pulpit Rock (2,663 m), 7.9 km to the south-west, about 1,370 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,248 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 71 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 14 cm in a poor year to 135 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 12 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 82 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 28 cm and 221 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 147 cm, around 21 February.

    Cooper Spur: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Cooper Spur?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Cooper Spur have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 71 cm deep.

    What altitude is Cooper Spur at?

    The top is at 1,293 m and the base at 1,204 m.

    What level of skier is Cooper Spur best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Cooper Spur?

    Mt. Hood Meadows, 9.7 km to the south-west, with about 57 km of pistes.
