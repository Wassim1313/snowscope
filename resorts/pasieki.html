    About Pasieki

    Compact and local, Pasieki has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 518 m up to 613 m. The closest other ski area is Siglany (1.1 km, north-west).

    How Pasieki compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 2.0 km north-east to Nowa Osada, with about 3.0 km of runs. The highest nearby is Stożek, topping out at 934 m.

    How Pasieki is laid out

    The vertical drop is 95 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 518 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
