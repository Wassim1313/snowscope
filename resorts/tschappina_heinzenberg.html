    About Tschappina Heinzenberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Tschappina Heinzenberg offers about 18 km of pistes (number 344 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,547 m and 2,163 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sarn Heinzenberg, 3.4 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 18 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 9.6 km, or 53%. With 53% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Tschappina Heinzenberg gives 616 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,547 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 4 lifts here are T-bars. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Piz Beverin (2,997 m), 5.0 km to the south, about 834 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,855 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 12 December and lasts to 14 May: about 22 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 37 cm (lean years 19 cm, snowy years 57 cm). Most winters land between 43 cm and 85 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 66 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 73 cm, around 23 March.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Tschappina Heinzenberg?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Tschappina Heinzenberg have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 37 cm deep.

    What altitude is Tschappina Heinzenberg at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,163 m; the lowest starts at 1,547 m (616 m of vertical).

    Is Tschappina Heinzenberg good for beginners?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 9.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Tschappina Heinzenberg have?

    About 18 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
