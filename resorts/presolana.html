    About Presolana

    Neither huge nor tiny, Presolana offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 781 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,200 m and 1,349 m above sea level. Monte Pora is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the south.

    How Presolana compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Monte Pora: about 17 km of pistes, 3.9 km to the south. Monte Pora has the longer snow season: around 20 weeks of solid cover, compared with 13 at Presolana. Colere reaches 2,174 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    What there is to ski at Presolana

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 3.3 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 2.5 km (75%). With 75% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes. 0.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 13 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,274 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 51 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 78 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 27 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 26 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 54 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually early March and early April.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Presolana has roughly 7 cm of snow on the ground today. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 12 cm by Saturday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 11 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Presolana gives 149 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets, 5 lifts in all. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Presolana Occidentale, 2,521 m high and 4.2 km north-west of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Spampatti (hotel), Albergo Alpino Ristorante La Vecchia Cantoniera (hotel), Hotel Cristallino (hotel) and Malga Scanapà (chalet).

    Questions about Presolana

    What is the best month to ski at Presolana?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Presolana have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 26 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Presolana?

    The top is at 1,349 m and the base at 1,200 m.

    Is Presolana good for beginners?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Presolana?

    Monte Pora, 3.9 km to the south, with about 17 km of pistes.
