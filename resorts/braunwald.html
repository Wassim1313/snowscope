    About Braunwald

    With roughly 15 km of runs, Braunwald sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,234 m to 1,900 m. The closest other ski area is Hätzingen (3.5 km, east).

    How much snow Braunwald usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,567 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 December and keeps it until about 23 May, roughly 25 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 131 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 58 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 23 cm in a poor year to 82 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 80 cm and 165 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 113 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    The mountain at Braunwald

    The vertical drop is 666 m: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,900 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 20 lifts: 14 funiculars, 1 gondola, 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift and 3 rope tows. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Above the ski area rises Bächistock (2,914 m), 4.7 km to the north, about 1,014 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Braunwald have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Braunwald have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 58 cm deep.

    What altitude is Braunwald at?

    Between 1,234 m and 1,900 m, so 666 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Braunwald best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 62% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Braunwald have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 20 lifts.
