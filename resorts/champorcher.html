    About Champorcher

    Champorcher is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 380 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,444 m and 2,489 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Cogne (17 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Champorcher

    Champorcher has 35 graded runs adding up to about 15 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 9.8 km, or 65%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 4.4 km of difficult runs.

    How much snow Champorcher usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,966 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 22 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 50 cm to 112 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 90 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 69 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 42 cm, with 77 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,045 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,444 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets, 7 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Mont Glacier (3,185 m), 3.8 km to the north-west, about 696 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Champorcher

    What is the best month to ski at Champorcher?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Champorcher have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 42 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Champorcher?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,489 m; the lowest starts at 1,444 m (1,045 m of vertical).

    Does Champorcher have steep runs?

    Yes: about 4.4 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Champorcher have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
