    About Vichères-Bavon

    Vichères-Bavon is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 396 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,595 m to 2,264 m. The closest other ski area is Bourg-Saint-Pierre (5.1 km, south-east).

    Snow this week

    Up high, Vichères-Bavon has roughly 51 cm of snow on the ground today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 16 cm).

    The mountain at Vichères-Bavon

    From top to bottom that makes 669 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,595 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow, 4 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Grand Darray (3,514 m), 7.6 km to the west, about 1,250 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Vichères-Bavon usually gets

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,930 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to mid-May. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 40 cm and 82 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 65 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm; a snowy December gave 53 cm, a dry one 18 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 45 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Vichères-Bavon: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Vichères-Bavon?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Vichères-Bavon at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Vichères-Bavon?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,264 m; the lowest starts at 1,595 m (669 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Vichères-Bavon?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 7.8 km, are easy.

    How big is Vichères-Bavon?

    About 15 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
