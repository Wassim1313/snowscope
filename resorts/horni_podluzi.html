    About Horní Podluží

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Horní Podluží sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The slopes start at 452 m and reach 570 m at the top. Waltersdorf is the next resort, 7.5 km away to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Horní Podluží

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 2.0 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.5 km, or 73%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Horní Podluží usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 511 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 6 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 18 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually late December and mid-January. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 23 cm.

    How Horní Podluží is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Horní Podluží gives 118 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 452 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 3 button lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Questions about Horní Podluží

    What is the best month to ski at Horní Podluží?

    Aim for late December and mid-January.

    Is there snow at Horní Podluží at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    How high is Horní Podluží?

    Its highest lift reaches 570 m; the lowest starts at 452 m (118 m of vertical).

    Is Horní Podluží suitable for families?

    Yes: 73% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Horní Podluží have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
