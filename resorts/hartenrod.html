    About Hartenrod

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Hartenrod sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The slopes start at 374 m and reach 494 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Skihang Dautphetal-Holzhausen, 8.4 km north-east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Hartenrod

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.5 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 0.9 km (63%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 120 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 374 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Familienskigebiet Sahnehang, reach 825 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Hartenrod?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Is there snow at Hartenrod at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 4 winters out of ten, typically 0 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Hartenrod?

    Its highest lift reaches 494 m; the lowest starts at 374 m (120 m of vertical).

    Is Hartenrod suitable for families?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Hartenrod have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
