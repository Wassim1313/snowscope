    About Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000

    With some 36 km of runs, Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Pyrenees. Skiing here happens between 1,716 m and 2,215 m above sea level. Les Angles is the next resort, 4.9 km away to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 92 runs and around 36 km to ski. Most of the length is green (beginner) (36%, about 13 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How much snow Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 usually gets

    Count on about 6 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,966 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 16 cm, with 57 cm in the best years. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 6 cm to 85 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 15 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. A normal season tops out near 45 cm, around 2 February. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    The mountain at Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000

    Skied top to bottom, Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 gives 499 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,716 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt reaches 2,495 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 9 chairlifts, 7 button lifts, 3 drag lifts and 5 moving carpets, 25 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Pic du Petit Castellà (2,420 m), 7.7 km to the north-west, about 205 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 16 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 29 March 2027.

    What altitude is Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,215 m; the lowest starts at 1,716 m (499 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 22 km, are easy.
