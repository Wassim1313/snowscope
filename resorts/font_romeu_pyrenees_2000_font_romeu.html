    The snow at Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 (1,966 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 16 cm, with a normal range of 9 cm–33 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 35 cm; the middle half of winters range from 18 cm to 70 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 45 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 7 % of winters, and by 1 January in 15 %.

    The deepest winter was 1980-81 (136 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1997-98 (18 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late December to mid-April (114 days).

    Questions about Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000

    Can you ski Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 16 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 15 % of 46 winters. Right now: 2 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000?

    February: the snow peaks at about 45 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to mid-April.

    How snowy is Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 45 cm in early February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 136 cm (1980-81), the thinnest 18 cm (1997-98).

    What altitude is Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,215 m; the lowest starts at 1,716 m (499 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 have?

    About 36 km of pistes and 25 lifts.

    About Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000

    With some 36 km of runs, Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Pyrenees. Skiing here happens between 1,716 m and 2,215 m above sea level. Les Angles is the next resort, 4.9 km away to the north.

    The mountain at Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000

    Skied top to bottom, Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 gives 499 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,716 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt reaches 2,495 m.
