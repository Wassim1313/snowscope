    Around 53 km of marked runs place Le Sauze 1400 / Le Super-Sauze 1 among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 112 by length. The slopes start at 1,374 m and reach 2,388 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Pra-Loup 1600 (9.0 km, west).
