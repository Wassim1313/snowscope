    About Brüsti

    Brüsti is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,426 m and 1,600 m above sea level. Haldi is the next resort, 6.5 km away to the east.

    Brüsti and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Biel (10 km north-east) has about 7.0 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 3 of 7). For higher slopes, Biel climbs to 1,940 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 2.1 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 75%. With 75% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,513 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 November and lasts to 23 May: about 25 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 7 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 74 cm to 147 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 61 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 30 cm, snowy years 78 cm). For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 123 cm, around 5 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 85 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Brüsti gives 174 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,426 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 3 cable cars, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Schlossberg at 3,133 m, standing 1,533 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Brüsti include Alp Grat (mountain hut), 54 Hochgenuss (hotel), Visionary Hospitality (hotel) and Hotel Albert (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When does Brüsti have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Brüsti at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 61 cm deep.

    What altitude is Brüsti at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,600 m; the lowest starts at 1,426 m (174 m of vertical).

    Is Brüsti good for beginners?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Brüsti?

    Biel, 10 km to the north-east, with about 7.0 km of pistes.
