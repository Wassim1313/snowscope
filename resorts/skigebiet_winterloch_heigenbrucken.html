    About Winterloch Heigenbrücken

    Winterloch Heigenbrücken is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 317 m and 454 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is am Engländer (6.1 km, north-west).

    Snow at Winterloch Heigenbrücken in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at mid-mountain (about 386 m) stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 60 km north-east to Skilifte Kreuzberg, with about 8.0 km of runs. For higher slopes, Skilifte Kreuzberg climbs to 881 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    137 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 317 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow.

    Questions about Winterloch Heigenbrücken

    What altitude is Winterloch Heigenbrücken at?

    Its highest lift reaches 454 m; the lowest starts at 317 m (137 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Winterloch Heigenbrücken?

    Skilifte Kreuzberg, 60 km to the north-east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Winterloch Heigenbrücken have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
