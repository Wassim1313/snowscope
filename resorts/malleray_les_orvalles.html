    About Malleray

    Malleray has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 781 m to 842 m. Grenchenberg is the next resort, 8.5 km away to the east.

    How Malleray compares nearby

    Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell. For higher slopes, Grenchenberg climbs to 1,363 m.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 812 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 20 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and late January. The snow is usually deepest around 26 January, with about 5 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 12 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 61 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 781 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Bugnenets-Savagnières, reach 1,448 m. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Le Buement (1,338 m), 7.9 km to the east, about 496 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Cabane La Rochette (mountain hut), Chambres d'hôtes Sous-Moron (guesthouse), Hotel-Restaurant La Werdtberg (hotel) and Chalet du ski-club Petit-Val (chalet).

    Malleray: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Malleray?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Can you ski at Malleray at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Malleray?

    The top is at 842 m and the base at 781 m.

    How many km of pistes does Malleray have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
