    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,589 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 55 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 45 cm and 97 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 84 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm; a snowy December gave 61 cm, a dry one 12 cm.
