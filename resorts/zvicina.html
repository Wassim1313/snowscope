    About Zvičina

    Neither huge nor tiny, Zvičina offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 145 of 257 in Central Europe). The ski area spans the mountain from 657 m up to 660 m. The closest other ski area is Mostek (5.1 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Zvičina

    Zvičina has 3 graded runs adding up to about 2.2 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.6 km (72%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 3 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 657 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a rope tow.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 658 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 48 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 26 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm; a snowy December gave 38 cm, a dry one 1 cm.

    Zvičina: common questions

    When does Zvičina have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Zvičina at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Zvičina?

    The top is at 660 m and the base at 657 m.

    Can beginners ski at Zvičina?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Zvičina?

    Černý Důl, 21 km to the north, with about 5.0 km of pistes.
