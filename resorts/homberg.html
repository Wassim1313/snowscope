    About Homberg

    Homberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 759 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 952 m up to 1,103 m. Hubelweid is the next resort, 2.2 km away to the south.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 6.7 km south-east to Sigriswill-Schwanden, with about 12 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Niederhorn does better, with about 23 weeks of a good base instead of 7. The highest nearby is Niederhorn, topping out at 1,938 m.

    The mountain at Homberg

    151 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,152 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Merra, 1,953 m high and 7.9 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 7 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,028 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 21 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Year to year the snow is steady, with 13 cm to 55 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 21 cm, from 7 cm in a poor year to 36 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 36 cm.

    Homberg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Homberg?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Homberg have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 21 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Homberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,103 m; the lowest starts at 952 m (151 m of vertical).

    Does Homberg have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.9 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Homberg?

    Sigriswill-Schwanden, 6.7 km to the south-east, with about 12 km of pistes.
