    About Splügen-Tambo

    Neither huge nor tiny, Splügen-Tambo offers about 20 km of pistes (number 317 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,484 m to 2,211 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being San Bernadino, 12 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Splügen-Tambo has 49 graded runs adding up to about 20 km. Most of the length is blue (50%, about 10 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 0.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow on the ground at Splügen-Tambo today: about 14 cm up high. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 13 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (18 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    727 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,484 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pizzo Tambo at 3,280 m, standing 1,069 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Splügen-Tambo through a normal winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,848 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. A normal season tops out near 68 cm, around 20 February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 30 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Year to year the snow is steady, with 42 cm to 90 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm, from 17 cm in a poor year to 64 cm in a big one.

    Splügen-Tambo: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Splügen-Tambo?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Splügen-Tambo at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 36 cm deep.

    How high is Splügen-Tambo?

    Between 1,484 m and 2,211 m, so 727 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Splügen-Tambo?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 10 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Splügen-Tambo?

    Savognin, 20 km to the east, with about 44 km of pistes.
