    About Splügen-Tambo

    Neither huge nor tiny, Splügen-Tambo offers about 20 km of pistes (number 317 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,484 m to 2,211 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being San Bernadino, 12 km to the south-west.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 20 km east to Savognin, with about 44 km of runs. The highest nearby is Vals, topping out at 2,849 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    727 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,484 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Suretta, Piz Tambo, Weiss Kreuz and Bodenhaus.
