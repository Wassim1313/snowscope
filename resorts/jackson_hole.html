    About Jackson Hole

    With some 78 km of runs, Jackson Hole belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of US West. Skiing here happens between 1,929 m and 3,169 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Snow King Mountain (17 km, south-east).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,240 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,929 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 3 gondolas, 10 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets, 17 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Prospectors Mountain at 3,417 m, standing 248 m above the top lift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 138 marked runs cover roughly 78 km. Most of the length is blue square (59%, about 46 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 25 km.

    How much snow Jackson Hole usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 2,549 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. Most winters land between 128 cm and 208 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 169 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 189 cm, around 30 March. For about 21 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 106 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 71 cm, snowy years 145 cm).

    Questions about Jackson Hole

    What is the best month to ski at Jackson Hole?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Jackson Hole have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 106 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Jackson Hole?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026.

    What altitude is Jackson Hole at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,169 m; the lowest starts at 1,929 m (1,240 m of vertical).

    Is Jackson Hole good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 25 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.
