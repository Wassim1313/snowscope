    The snow at Jackson Hole, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Jackson Hole (8,363 ft (2,549 m)), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 42 in (106 cm), with a normal range of 35 in (90 cm)–50 in (128 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 65 in (166 cm) (54 in (138 cm)–75 in (190 cm)).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 74 in (189 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 100 % of winters, and by 1 January in 100 %.

    The deepest winter was 1981-82 (118 in (300 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2000-01 (54 in (138 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs early November to mid-June (218 days).

    Questions about Jackson Hole

    Can you ski Jackson Hole at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 42 in (106 cm) at Christmas, and 100 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 16 in (41 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Jackson Hole?

    March: the snow peaks at about 74 in (189 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from early November to mid-June.

    How much snow does Jackson Hole get?

    About 74 in (189 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early April); between 54 in (138 cm) in the leanest winter (2000-01) and 118 in (300 cm) in the biggest (1981-82).

    What altitude is Jackson Hole at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,169 m; the lowest starts at 1,929 m (1,240 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Jackson Hole have?

    About 78 km of pistes and 17 lifts.

    About Jackson Hole

    With some 78 km of runs, Jackson Hole belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of US West. Skiing here happens between 1,929 m and 3,169 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Snow King Mountain (17 km, south-east).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,240 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,929 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most.
