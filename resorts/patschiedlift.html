    About Patschiedlift

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Patschiedlift one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 1,370 m and reach 1,543 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Venet, 4.6 km to the north-east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 7.0 km south to Serfaus Fiss Ladis, with about 169 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Serfaus Fiss Ladis usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 27 weeks, against 13 here. For higher slopes, Serfaus Fiss Ladis climbs to 2,817 m.

    The mountain at Patschiedlift

    Skied top to bottom, Patschiedlift gives 173 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,370 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Rotpleiskopf, 2,936 m high and 6.8 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,456 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 January and keeps it until about 27 April, roughly 13 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 41 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 23 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 10 cm in a poor year to 35 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 35 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Most winters land between 27 cm and 51 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    Questions about Patschiedlift

    What is the best month to ski at Patschiedlift?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late April.

    Is there snow at Patschiedlift at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 23 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Patschiedlift?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,543 m; the lowest starts at 1,370 m (173 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Patschiedlift?

    Serfaus Fiss Ladis, 7.0 km to the south, with about 169 km of pistes.

    How big is Patschiedlift?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
