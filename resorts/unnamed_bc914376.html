    About Skilift Lamm

    Skilift Lamm has about 0.4 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 899 m up to 936 m. The closest other ski area is Skilift Zuflucht (4.8 km, north-west).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 918 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 18 cm). For the best snow, aim for late December and early February. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 6 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 30 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    37 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 899 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kandellifte reaches 1,201 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Lettstädter Höhe (966 m), Rossbühl (963 m) and Sandkopf (954 m).

    Skilift Lamm: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Skilift Lamm?

    Aim for late December and early February.

    Is there snow at Skilift Lamm at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    What altitude is Skilift Lamm at?

    Between 899 m and 936 m, so 37 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Skilift Lamm?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
