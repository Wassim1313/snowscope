    About Vihti

    Vihti is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 136 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The slopes start at 49 m and reach 123 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Solvalla Swinghill, 13 km south-east, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 3.6 km. Black runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 33%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 86 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 10 February and keeps it until about 18 March, roughly 5 weeks. A normal season tops out near 32 cm, around 12 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 27 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 9 cm and 55 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 74 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 6 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts.

    Vihti: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Vihti?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Vihti at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Vihti?

    Between 49 m and 123 m, so 74 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Vihti?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    How big is Vihti?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
