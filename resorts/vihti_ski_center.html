    About Vihti

    Vihti is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 136 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The slopes start at 49 m and reach 123 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Solvalla Swinghill, 13 km south-east, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 74 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
