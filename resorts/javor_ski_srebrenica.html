    About Javor ski Srebrenica

    Javor ski Srebrenica has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 784 m to 827 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sportski centar Milići, 23 km north-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Javor ski Srebrenica

    The vertical drop is 43 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 984 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Avantura park, reach 1,485 m. The only lift is a T-bar.
