    About Vaset

    Neither huge nor tiny, Vaset offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 155 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 860 m to 1,047 m. The closest other ski area is Brattstølen Skisenter (18 km, south-east).

    What there is to ski at Vaset

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 4.3 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.2 km, or 52%. Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Snow at Vaset through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 954 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 16 November and keeps it until about 26 May, roughly 28 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 66 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 48 cm, snowy years 100 cm). A normal season tops out near 104 cm, around 16 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. Most winters land between 77 cm and 124 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 98 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 4 weeks.

    How Vaset is laid out

    187 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,047 m is the third highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Grønsennknippa (1,367 m), 6.7 km to the west, about 320 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Vaset?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Vaset at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 66 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Vaset at?

    Between 860 m and 1,047 m, so 187 m of vertical drop.

    Is Vaset suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Vaset have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
