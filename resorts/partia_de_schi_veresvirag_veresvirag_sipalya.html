    About Pârtia de schi Veresvirág

    Pârtia de schi Veresvirág is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 148 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 822 m up to 1,096 m. Havas Bucsin is the next resort, 15 km away to the west.
