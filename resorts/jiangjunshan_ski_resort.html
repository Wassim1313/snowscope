    About Jiangjunshan

    With some 22 km of runs, Jiangjunshan belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of China. Its lifts climb from 847 m to 1,319 m. The closest other ski area is Terrain Park (0.7 km, north).

    The pistes of Jiangjunshan

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 18 km. Most of the length is black diamond (44%, about 7.8 km). With 44% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 7.8 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 472 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 847 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 15 lifts break down into 4 gondolas, 2 chair-gondolas, 3 chairlifts and 6 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 6 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Jiangjunshan?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,319 m; the lowest starts at 847 m (472 m of vertical).

    Is Jiangjunshan good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 7.8 km of its graded pistes (44%) are difficult.

    How big is Jiangjunshan?

    About 22 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
