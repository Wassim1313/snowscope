    About Cheggio

    Compact and local, Cheggio has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,461 m to 1,540 m. Domobianca is the next resort, 9.3 km away to the east.

    How Cheggio compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 15 km west to Saas Fee, with about 48 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Saas Fee usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 28 weeks, against 13 here. Saas Fee reaches 3,556 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    What there is to ski at Cheggio

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.0 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.0 km, or 100%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Cheggio is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 79 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,461 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Saas Fee, for one, climbs to 3,556 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pizzo d'Andolla at 3,654 m, standing 2,114 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 13 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,500 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to late April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 31 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 23 cm and 54 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 18 cm; a snowy December gave 36 cm, a dry one 12 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 39 cm.

    Cheggio: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Cheggio?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late April.

    Can you ski at Cheggio at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 18 cm deep.

    How high is Cheggio?

    The top is at 1,540 m and the base at 1,461 m.

    Is Cheggio suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Cheggio?

    Saas Fee, 15 km to the west, with about 48 km of pistes.
