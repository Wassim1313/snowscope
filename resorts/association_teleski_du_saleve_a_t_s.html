    About Association Téléski du Salève (A

    Compact and local, Association Téléski du Salève has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,168 m and 1,280 m above sea level. Massif des Brasses is the next resort, 25 km away to the east.

    Snow at Association Téléski du Salève through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,224 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 13 December and keeps it until about 29 April, roughly 20 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 50 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 101 cm, a dry one 5 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 28 cm and 122 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 79 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 101 cm.

    This week's snow

    Lifts and terrain

    112 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Le Grand Piton (1,379 m), Petit Salève (899 m) and Mont Gosse (628 m).

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Association Téléski du Salève include 4 hotels: Best Western, Hôtel Ibis Archamps Porte de Genève, Ibis Budget and Buffalo Hotel.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Association Téléski du Salève?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Association Téléski du Salève at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 50 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Association Téléski du Salève at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,280 m; the lowest starts at 1,168 m (112 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Association Téléski du Salève?

    Lélex, 26 km to the north-west, with about 21 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Association Téléski du Salève have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
