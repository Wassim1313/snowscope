    About Silver

    With roughly 38 km of runs, Silver sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,272 m up to 1,904 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lookout Pass, 32 km east, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Silver has 78 graded runs adding up to about 38 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 18 km, or 47%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 13 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,588 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 November and lasts to 10 May: about 24 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 19 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 186 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 89 cm to 219 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 100 cm, with 129 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 209 cm, around 27 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and late February.

    How Silver is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 632 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Its 7 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Kellogg Peak (1,920 m), Wardner Peak (1,884 m) and Silver Hill (1,822 m).

    Silver: common questions

    When does Silver have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    How much snow does Silver have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 100 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Silver?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,904 m; the lowest starts at 1,272 m (632 m of vertical).

    Is Silver good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 13 km of its graded pistes (35%) are difficult.

    How big is Silver?

    About 38 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
