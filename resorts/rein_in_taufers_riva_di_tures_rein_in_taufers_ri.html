    About Rein in Taufers

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Rein in Taufers sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,581 m up to 1,833 m. The closest other ski area is Klausberg (6.9 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 4.0 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.8 km, or 70%. Intermediate runs make up 70% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How much snow Rein in Taufers usually gets

    Count on about 14 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,707 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 66 cm. Most winters land between 40 cm and 86 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 35 cm (lean years 14 cm, snowy years 58 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 23 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February.

    How Rein in Taufers is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Rein in Taufers gives 252 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,581 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Hochgall - Monte Collalto (3,436 m), 7.5 km to the south-east, about 1,603 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Rein in Taufers

    When is the best time to ski at Rein in Taufers?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late March.

    How much snow does Rein in Taufers have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 35 cm deep.

    How high is Rein in Taufers?

    The top is at 1,833 m and the base at 1,581 m.

    What level of skier is Rein in Taufers best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 70% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Rein in Taufers?

    Klausberg, 9.1 km to the west, with about 60 km of pistes.
