    About Ratzi

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Ratzi sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 948 m to 1,840 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Biel, 2.6 km to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Ratzi has 5 graded runs adding up to about 4.8 km. Most of the length is blue (67%, about 3.2 km). With 67% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Ratzi is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Ratzi gives 892 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 948 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 5 cable cars and 1 T-bar, 6 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Schächentaler Windgällen at 2,764 m, standing 924 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,394 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late May, about 23 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 53 cm and 112 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 72 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 90 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 46 cm, with 60 cm in the best years.

    Ratzi: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ratzi?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late May.

    How much snow does Ratzi have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 46 cm deep.

    What altitude is Ratzi at?

    The top is at 1,840 m and the base at 948 m.

    Can beginners ski at Ratzi?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Ratzi?

    Stoos, 11 km to the north-west, with about 19 km of pistes.
