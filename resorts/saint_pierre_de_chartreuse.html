    About Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse

    Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 595 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,083 m and reach 1,743 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les Egaux, 5.1 km to the south-west.

    Snow at Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse through a normal winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,413 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to late April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 44 cm and 169 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 102 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 62 cm, from 16 cm in a poor year to 138 cm in a big one. For about 10 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 131 cm, around 24 March. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    660 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,743 m. Its 10 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 button lift, 7 drag lifts and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Dent de Crolles (2,062 m), 5.2 km to the south, about 319 m higher than the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 15 runs and around 7.2 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 3.9 km (54%). With 74% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    Is there snow at Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 62 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse?

    The top is at 1,743 m and the base at 1,083 m.

    Is Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse suitable for families?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 5.3 km, are easy.

    How big is Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
