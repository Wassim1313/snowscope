    About Skihang Mühlleiten

    Skihang Mühlleiten has about 0.2 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 834 m to 866 m. Bublava is the next resort, 4.5 km away to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    32 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 834 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 866 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a drag lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 850 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 51 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 36 cm). A normal season tops out near 21 cm, around 1 February.

    Questions about Skihang Mühlleiten

    When does Skihang Mühlleiten have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Is there snow at Skihang Mühlleiten at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 8 cm).

    What altitude is Skihang Mühlleiten at?

    Between 834 m and 866 m, so 32 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Skihang Mühlleiten?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
