    About Sovata

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Sovata one of the smaller places to ski in the Carpathians. The slopes start at 939 m and reach 1,175 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bogdan, 8.9 km to the south-east.

    The mountain at Sovata

    From top to bottom that makes 236 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 939 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a chairlift.
