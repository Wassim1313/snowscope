    About Steg

    Compact and local, Steg has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 711 m to 999 m. Fischenthal is the next resort, 2.2 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Steg

    Steg has 6 graded runs adding up to about 3.2 km. Most of the length is blue (50%, about 1.6 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    288 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 911 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 3 T-bars and 1 button lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Höchhand, 1,314 m high and 5.7 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Steg usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 855 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 February and lasts to 8 April: about 3 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 28 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 32 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 31 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 12 cm to 58 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Steg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Steg?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to early April.

    Can you ski at Steg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Steg at?

    The top is at 999 m and the base at 711 m.

    Is Steg good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Steg have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
