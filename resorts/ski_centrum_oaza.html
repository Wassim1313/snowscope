    The mountain at Ski Centrum Oáza

    108 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 494 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Švýcárna reaches 1,307 m. All 3 lifts here are button lifts.
