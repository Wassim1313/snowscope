    About Pian del Frais

    Pian del Frais is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 526 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,461 m up to 1,886 m. The closest other ski area is Pragelato (13 km, south).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 8.4 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 4.8 km (57%). Intermediate runs make up 57% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Pian del Frais hold around 13 cm of snow today. 20 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 30 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 15 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Snow at Pian del Frais through a normal winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,674 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 40 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 105 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 38 cm; a snowy December gave 82 cm, a dry one 18 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 76 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 425 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,461 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Above the ski area rises Cima Ciantiplagna (2,849 m), 3.2 km to the south, about 963 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Pian del Frais

    When is the best time to ski at Pian del Frais?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Pian del Frais have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Pian del Frais at?

    The top is at 1,886 m and the base at 1,461 m.

    Is Pian del Frais suitable for families?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 3.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Pian del Frais?

    Sestriere, 17 km to the south-west, with about 58 km of pistes.
