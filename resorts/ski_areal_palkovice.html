    About Ski areál Palkovice

    Ski areál Palkovice is a small ski area by the standards of the Carpathians, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 339 m and reach 443 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski areál Opálená, 6.5 km to the south.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Ski Malenovice: about 2.0 km of pistes, 9.2 km to the south-east. For higher slopes, Ondrášova dolina climbs to 768 m.

    How Ski areál Palkovice is laid out

    104 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 339 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at TJ Radegast Sihly, reach 780 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 2 lifts in all.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Ski areál Palkovice?

    Its highest lift reaches 443 m; the lowest starts at 339 m (104 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Ski areál Palkovice have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
