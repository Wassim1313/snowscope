    About Elatohori

    Elatohori is a mid-sized ski area with about 11 km of pistes, number 18 of 51 in the Balkans by length. Skiing here happens between 1,412 m and 1,913 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Seli (30 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 5.1 km, or 48%. With 83% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Elatohori is laid out

    501 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,412 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 2 drag lifts.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Elatohori hold around 28 cm of snow today. More is on the way: about 12 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.
