    About Asahidake

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Asahidake sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Skiing here happens between 1,105 m and 1,589 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Daisetsuzan Sounkyo Kurodake, 11 km to the north-east.

    The mountain at Asahidake

    The vertical drop is 484 m: runs of a moderate length. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The only lift is a cable car. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 6.3 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 3.5 km (54%). Intermediate runs make up 54% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 2.9 km of difficult runs.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Asahidake at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,589 m; the lowest starts at 1,105 m (484 m of vertical).

    Is Asahidake good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 2.9 km of its graded pistes (46%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Asahidake have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
