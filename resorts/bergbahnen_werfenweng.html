    About Bergbahnen Werfenweng

    With roughly 22 km of runs, Bergbahnen Werfenweng sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 898 m and reach 1,828 m at the top. The closest other ski area is St. Martin im Tennengebirge (7.0 km, east).

    How much snow Bergbahnen Werfenweng usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,363 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 12 December and keeps it until about 11 May, roughly 22 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 39 cm, with 68 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 62 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 89 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 47 cm and 105 cm.

    What there is to ski at Bergbahnen Werfenweng

    Counting every graded piste, there are 30 runs and around 22 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 56%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Bergbahnen Werfenweng gives 930 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,098 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 3 drag lifts. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Raucheck, 2,430 m high and 6.0 km north-west of the resort.

    Questions about Bergbahnen Werfenweng

    When does Bergbahnen Werfenweng have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Bergbahnen Werfenweng at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 39 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Bergbahnen Werfenweng?

    Between 898 m and 1,828 m, so 930 m of vertical drop.

    Is Bergbahnen Werfenweng good for beginners?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Bergbahnen Werfenweng have?

    About 22 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
