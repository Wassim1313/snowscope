    About Bergbahnen Werfenweng

    With roughly 22 km of runs, Bergbahnen Werfenweng sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 898 m and reach 1,828 m at the top. The closest other ski area is St. Martin im Tennengebirge (7.0 km, east).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Bergbahnen Werfenweng gives 930 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,098 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 17 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Bergbahnen Werfenweng. The last seven days brought 12 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.
