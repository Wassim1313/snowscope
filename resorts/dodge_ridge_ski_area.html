    What 46 winters say about Dodge Ridge

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Dodge Ridge (7,356 ft (2,242 m)), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 26 in (67 cm) of cover (14 in (36 cm) to 43 in (110 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 49 in (125 cm), between 31 in (80 cm) and 63 in (159 cm) in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 55 in (139 cm).

    48 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 70 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 2022-23, peaking at 138 in (351 cm). Worst: 2013-14, which never passed 25 in (63 cm).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from early December to early May, about 157 days.

    So far this winter: 17 in (43 cm) on 28 November (usual: 5.9 in (15 cm)).

    Questions about Dodge Ridge

    Does Dodge Ridge have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 26 in (67 cm) at Christmas, and 70 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 30 in (77 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Dodge Ridge?

    March: the snow peaks at about 55 in (139 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Dodge Ridge get?

    About 55 in (139 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (mid-March); between 25 in (63 cm) in the leanest winter (2013-14) and 138 in (351 cm) in the biggest (2022-23).

    When does Dodge Ridge have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How high do the lifts go at Dodge Ridge?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,482 m; the lowest starts at 2,002 m (480 m of vertical).

    Does Dodge Ridge have steep runs?

    Yes: about 14 km of its graded pistes (41%) are difficult.

    About Dodge Ridge

    Neither huge nor tiny, Dodge Ridge offers about 34 km of pistes (number 69 of 175 in US West). The ski area spans the mountain from 2,002 m up to 2,482 m. Summit Ranger District Ski Trail is the next resort, 0.8 km away to the south-west.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Dodge Ridge today: about 77 cm up high. The last seven days brought 2 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 28 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 24 cm at mid-mountain).

    What there is to ski at Dodge Ridge

    Counting every graded piste, there are 61 runs and around 34 km to ski. Most of the length is blue square (52%, about 18 km). Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 14 km.

    Snow at Dodge Ridge through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,242 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 9 December and keeps it until about 1 May, roughly 21 weeks. A normal season tops out near 139 cm, around 17 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 67 cm, with 146 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 12 weeks. Most winters land between 47 cm and 213 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 77 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    The mountain at Dodge Ridge

    The vertical drop is 480 m: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,002 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Getting up the mountain: 7 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 3 moving carpets, 11 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Burst Rock, 2,781 m high and 5.9 km east of the resort.
