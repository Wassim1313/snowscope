    About Pallas

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Pallas sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The slopes start at 438 m and reach 754 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Olos, 17 km south-west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Pallas

    Counting every graded piste, there are 25 runs and around 11 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 5.5 km, or 50%. With 50% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Pallas gives 316 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 754 m is the highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Taivaskero (809 m), Pyhäkero (775 m) and Laukukero (762 m).

    How much snow Pallas usually gets

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 596 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 66 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 46 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 59 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 56 cm and 84 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 28 February, with about 70 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Pallas?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Pallas at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 46 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Pallas at?

    Its highest lift reaches 754 m; the lowest starts at 438 m (316 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Pallas?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 5.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Pallas?

    Levin hiihtokeskus, 44 km to the south-east, with about 50 km of pistes.
