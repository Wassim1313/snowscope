    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 532 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 26 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 15 cm, a dry one 0 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 16 January, with about 3 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-January.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    79 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 492 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Vogel reaches 1,731 m. All 2 lifts here are rope tows. Above the ski area rises Altemaver (1,679 m), 6.5 km to the west, about 1,108 m higher than the top lift.
