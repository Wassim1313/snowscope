    About Mont Sutton

    Mont Sutton is one of the bigger ski areas of US Northeast: about 38 km of pistes put it at number 17 of 93. Its lifts climb from 397 m to 852 m. The closest other ski area is Jay Peak Resort (20 km, south).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    455 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 397 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.
