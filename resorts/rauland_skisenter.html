    About Rauland skisenter

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Rauland skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 821 m to 1,123 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Rauland skisenter, 1.3 km to the south.

    The pistes of Rauland skisenter

    The piste map lists 21 graded runs, about 11 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 3.8 km, or 35%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 302 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 6 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Brurenuten (1,207 m), Svinefjell (1,180 m) and Bjønnsbufjellet (1,140 m).

    How much snow Rauland skisenter usually gets

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 972 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. Year to year the snow is steady, with 87 cm to 156 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and early April. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 128 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 134 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 75 cm (lean years 50 cm, snowy years 105 cm).

    Questions about Rauland skisenter

    When is the best time to ski at Rauland skisenter?

    Aim for mid-March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Rauland skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 75 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Rauland skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,123 m; the lowest starts at 821 m (302 m of vertical).

    Is Rauland skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 6.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Rauland skisenter have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
