    About Heubach

    Heubach is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 680 m to 768 m. Its nearest neighbour is "Erste Berg" Masserberg, 3.4 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Heubach compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 18 km south-east to Silbersattel Steinach, with about 3.0 km of runs. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.0 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 0.7 km (71%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Heubach through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 724 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 22 cm). Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 31 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 25 January, with about 12 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-December and early January. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    88 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 680 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Winterwelt Schmiedefeld, reach 878 m. To get up there are 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Heubach: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Heubach?

    Aim for mid-December and early January.

    Is there snow at Heubach at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Heubach?

    Between 680 m and 768 m, so 88 m of vertical drop.

    Is Heubach suitable for families?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Heubach?

    Silbersattel Steinach, 18 km to the south-east, with about 3.0 km of pistes.
