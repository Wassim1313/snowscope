    About Pec pod Sněžkou

    Pec pod Sněžkou is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 13 km of pistes put it at number 13 of 257. Skiing here happens between 816 m and 1,210 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being vlek Kladenka, 1.5 km to the east.

    The pistes of Pec pod Sněžkou

    The piste map lists 34 graded runs, about 13 km in total. Most of the length is red (49%, about 6.1 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,013 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 January and lasts to 11 March: about 8 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 8 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 62 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 48 cm.

    The mountain at Pec pod Sněžkou

    The vertical drop is 394 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 816 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 16 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 5 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    Questions about Pec pod Sněžkou

    When does Pec pod Sněžkou have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Pec pod Sněžkou at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Pec pod Sněžkou at?

    Between 816 m and 1,210 m, so 394 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Pec pod Sněžkou?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 6.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Pec pod Sněžkou have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 16 lifts.
