    About Pec pod Sněžkou

    Pec pod Sněžkou is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 13 km of pistes put it at number 13 of 257. Skiing here happens between 816 m and 1,210 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being vlek Kladenka, 1.5 km to the east.

    The pistes of Pec pod Sněžkou

    The piste map lists 34 graded runs, about 13 km in total. Most of the length is red (49%, about 6.1 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Pec pod Sněžkou

    The vertical drop is 394 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 816 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 16 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 5 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.
