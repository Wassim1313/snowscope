    Evasion Mont Blanc in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Evasion Mont Blanc (1,632 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 73 cm; half of the winters sit between 46 cm and 104 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 145 cm; the middle half of winters range from 120 cm to 170 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 147 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 57 % of winters, and by 1 January in 83 %.

    The deepest winter was 1994-95 (257 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2010-11 (113 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to late May (186 days).

    Questions about Evasion Mont Blanc

    Is there snow in Evasion Mont Blanc at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 73 cm at Christmas, and 83 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 162 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Evasion Mont Blanc?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 147 cm). For a sure base, late November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Evasion Mont Blanc get?

    About 147 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 113 cm in the leanest winter (2010-11) and 257 cm in the biggest (1994-95).

    What altitude is Evasion Mont Blanc at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,444 m; the lowest starts at 820 m (1,624 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Evasion Mont Blanc have?

    About 293 km of pistes and 106 lifts.

    About Evasion Mont Blanc

    About 293 km of pistes make Evasion Mont Blanc one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Alps (number 6). The ski area spans the mountain from 820 m up to 2,444 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Megève, 1.8 km to the north-east.

    The mountain at Evasion Mont Blanc

    From top to bottom that makes 1,624 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 820 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
