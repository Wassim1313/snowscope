    About Storeskar skisenter

    Storeskar skisenter is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 92 m up to 313 m. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lifjell Skisenter, 16 km to the south-west.

    Storeskar skisenter today and the next days

    Today there are about 13 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Storeskar skisenter. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 11 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 221 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Gaustablikk Skisenter, reach 1,250 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    Snow at Storeskar skisenter through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 202 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 18 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 35 cm, from 12 cm in a poor year to 66 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 12 February, with about 77 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 47 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 37 cm and 114 cm.

    Storeskar skisenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Storeskar skisenter?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Storeskar skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 35 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Storeskar skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 313 m; the lowest starts at 92 m (221 m of vertical).
