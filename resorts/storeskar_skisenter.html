    Storeskar skisenter is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 92 m up to 313 m. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lifjell Skisenter, 16 km to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 221 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Gaustablikk Skisenter, reach 1,250 m.
