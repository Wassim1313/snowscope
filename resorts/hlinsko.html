    About Hlinsko

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hlinsko offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 167 of 257 in Central Europe). Skiing here happens between 556 m and 646 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Svatá Anna (16 km, west).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Hlinsko gives 90 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 556 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 3 drag lifts and 1 rope tow, 4 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
