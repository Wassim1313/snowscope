    Snow at Skiareál Novako through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,038 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 72 cm. A normal season tops out near 28 cm, around 23 February. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 10 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 46 cm in a big one.

    How Skiareál Novako is laid out

    The vertical drop is 19 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.
