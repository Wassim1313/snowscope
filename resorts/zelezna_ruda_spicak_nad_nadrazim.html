    About Železná Ruda (Špičák

    Železná Ruda is a ski area. Slavoj is the next resort, 0.5 km away to the north.

    How much snow Železná Ruda usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 874 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-February to late February, about 2 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 30 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 67 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm, with 43 cm in the best years.

    Quick answers

    When does Železná Ruda have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    How much snow does Železná Ruda have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.
