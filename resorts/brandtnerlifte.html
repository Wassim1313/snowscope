    About Brandtnerlifte

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Brandtnerlifte one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 421 m and reach 517 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Kirchschlag (22 km, east).

    How Brandtnerlifte compares nearby

    In this group of 3, Brandtnerlifte is number 3 for piste length. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. The highest nearby is Kirchschlag, topping out at 884 m.

    How Brandtnerlifte is laid out

    96 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 421 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Mayrhofberg (655 m), 6.4 km to the south-west, about 138 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 469 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Most winters land between 0 cm and 15 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 23 January, with about 5 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 12 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Kaiserhof (hotel), Gasthof Ernst (hotel), Gasthof in der Exlau (hotel) and Gasthaus in der Exlau (guesthouse).

    Brandtnerlifte: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Brandtnerlifte?

    Aim for late January and early March.

    Is there snow at Brandtnerlifte at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Brandtnerlifte?

    Between 421 m and 517 m, so 96 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Brandtnerlifte have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
