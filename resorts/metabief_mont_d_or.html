    About Metabief

    Metabief is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 26 km of pistes put it at number 239 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,013 m and reach 1,418 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs (5.9 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 50 graded runs, about 26 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 10 km, or 38%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Metabief is laid out

    The vertical drop is 405 m: runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts and 8 drag lifts, 12 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Dent de Vaulion (1,483 m), Le Mont d'Or (1,463 m) and le Mont d'Or (1,461 m).

    Snow at Metabief through a normal winter

    Count on about 1 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,216 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-February to late February. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 11 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 49 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 78 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 33 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hôtel Restaurant Les Sapins (hotel), Chalet du Gros Morond (mountain hut), Cabane de la Gymn "Mont d'Or - Bellevue" (chalet) and Les Gentianes (hotel).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Metabief?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    Can you ski at Metabief at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 11 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Metabief?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,418 m; the lowest starts at 1,013 m (405 m of vertical).

    Is Metabief good for beginners?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 18 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Metabief have?

    About 26 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
