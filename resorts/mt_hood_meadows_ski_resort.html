    About Mt. Hood Meadows

    Mt. Hood Meadows is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 57 km of pistes put it at number 31 of 175. Skiing here happens between 1,380 m and 2,226 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Timberline Lodge (4.5 km, west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 94 runs and around 57 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 33 km (58%). Intermediate runs make up 58% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 14 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 846 m: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,380 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 11 lifts break down into 10 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Mount Hood (3,429 m), 5.0 km to the north-west, about 1,203 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,803 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to early May, about 24 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for early January and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 164 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 285 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 49 cm and 480 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 388 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 279 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For about 21 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Timberline Lodge (hotel), Silcox Hut (mountain hut), Aubrey Watzek Lodge (mountain hut) and Tilly Jane Guard Station (chalet). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Mt. Hood Meadows

    When does Mt. Hood Meadows have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    How much snow does Mt. Hood Meadows have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 164 cm deep.

    When does Mt. Hood Meadows open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 24 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Mt. Hood Meadows?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,226 m; the lowest starts at 1,380 m (846 m of vertical).

    Is Mt. Hood Meadows good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 14 km of its graded pistes (25%) are difficult.
