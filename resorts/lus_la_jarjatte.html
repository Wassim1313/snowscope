    About Lus la Jarjatte

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Lus la Jarjatte sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,180 m and 1,519 m above sea level. Dévoluy is the next resort, 11 km away to the east.

    What there is to ski at Lus la Jarjatte

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 6.3 km. Most of the length is blue (67%, about 4.2 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Dévoluy (11 km east) has about 70 km of pistes against 6.0 km here. For more reliable snow, Dévoluy usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 23 weeks, against 8 here. For higher slopes, Dévoluy climbs to 2,495 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 339 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Dévoluy, for one, climbs to 2,495 m. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Le Grand Ferrand at 2,758 m, standing 1,239 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,350 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to mid-March, about 8 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 46 cm in a big one. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 7 cm to 79 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. A normal season tops out near 46 cm, around 17 February.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Lus la Jarjatte include Gite des Chabottes (chalet), Refuge du col de la Croix (mountain hut), Les Trois Marmottes (hotel) and L'Envie des Mets (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Lus la Jarjatte?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Lus la Jarjatte at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in 8 winters out of ten, about 16 cm deep.

    What altitude is Lus la Jarjatte at?

    Between 1,180 m and 1,519 m, so 339 m of vertical drop.

    Is Lus la Jarjatte good for beginners?

    Yes: 86% of the graded pistes, about 5.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Lus la Jarjatte?

    Dévoluy, 11 km to the east, with about 70 km of pistes.
