    About Alberschwende

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Alberschwende one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 737 m and reach 938 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Schneiderkopflifte, 4.0 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 4.4 km south to Bödele, with about 21 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau) usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 22 weeks, against 3 here. Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau) reaches 1,704 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    What there is to ski at Alberschwende

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 2.5 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 68%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 201 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 737 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Above the ski area rises Hochälpele (1,463 m), 5.5 km to the south-west, about 525 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 838 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 5 February and keeps it until about 26 February, roughly 3 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 17 cm, with 32 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 9 cm and 55 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 14 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 32 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Alberschwende?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    Can you ski at Alberschwende at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Alberschwende at?

    Its highest lift reaches 938 m; the lowest starts at 737 m (201 m of vertical).

    Is Alberschwende suitable for families?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 1.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Alberschwende?

    Bödele, 4.4 km to the south, with about 21 km of pistes.
