    About Levin hiihtokeskus

    About 50 km of pistes make Levin hiihtokeskus one of the ten biggest ski areas of Scandinavia (number 4). Its lifts climb from 197 m to 530 m. The closest other ski area is Levi (2.7 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Levin hiihtokeskus

    In all, 144 marked runs cover roughly 50 km. Most of the length is blue (44%, about 22 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at Levin hiihtokeskus

    Skied top to bottom, Levin hiihtokeskus gives 333 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 6 chairlifts, 10 T-bars, 5 button lifts and 4 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Levi (531 m), Kätkätunturi (502 m) and Petsukkavaara (228 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 364 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 November and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 23 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 44 cm (lean years 30 cm, snowy years 56 cm). Most winters land between 54 cm and 80 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 60 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 67 cm.

    Questions about Levin hiihtokeskus

    What is the best month to ski at Levin hiihtokeskus?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Is there snow at Levin hiihtokeskus at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 44 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Levin hiihtokeskus?

    It opened on 2 October 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Levin hiihtokeskus?

    The top is at 530 m and the base at 197 m.

    Is Levin hiihtokeskus suitable for families?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 28 km, are easy.
