    About Kampenwand

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kampenwand offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 578 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 621 m to 1,482 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sachrang, 11 km to the south-west.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,052 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to mid-April, about 15 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 56 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 17 cm and 84 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 24 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 52 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 34 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    The mountain at Kampenwand

    The vertical drop is 861 m: enough height for proper long runs. Because the lifts start as low as 621 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts and 3 T-bars. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Geigelstein, 1,808 m high and 6.1 km south of the resort.

    What there is to ski at Kampenwand

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 6.8 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 3.0 km, or 44%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski.

    Kampenwand: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Kampenwand?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Kampenwand at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Kampenwand?

    Between 621 m and 1,482 m, so 861 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Kampenwand?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 3.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Kampenwand have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
