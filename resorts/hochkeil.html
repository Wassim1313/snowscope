    About Hochkeil

    Hochkeil is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 287 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 606 m and 1,748 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is St. Johann im Pongau (8.7 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 20 runs and around 22 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 11 km (50%). Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 1.2 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Hochkeil

    Skied top to bottom, Hochkeil gives 1,142 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. At 606 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 2 funiculars, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The skyline is dominated by Hochkönig, 2,941 m high and 6.4 km west of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 13 km south-east to Snow Space Salzburg, with about 85 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Bergbahnen Werfenweng does better, with about 22 weeks of a good base instead of 3. The highest nearby is Snow Space Salzburg, topping out at 1,977 m.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Hochkeil has roughly 9 cm of snow on the ground today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 13 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 7 cm at mid-mountain).

    Snow at Hochkeil through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,177 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 February and lasts to 15 March: about 3 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 15 cm, with 27 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 32 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and mid-May. Most winters land between 17 cm and 39 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 26 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Hochkeil: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hochkeil?

    Aim for late March and mid-May; the cover is usually good from late February to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Hochkeil at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 15 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Hochkeil?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,748 m; the lowest starts at 606 m (1,142 m of vertical).

    Is Hochkeil suitable for families?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 9.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Hochkeil?

    Snow Space Salzburg, 13 km to the south-east, with about 85 km of pistes.
