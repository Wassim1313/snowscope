    About Skilift Fischbach

    Compact and local, Skilift Fischbach has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,029 m and 1,139 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Schwarzenbachlift (3.6 km, west).

    Skilift Fischbach and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Feldberg: about 22 km of pistes, 10 km to the west. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell. Feldberg reaches 1,446 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Snow at Skilift Fischbach through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,084 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 36 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 9 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 24 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Fischbach gives 110 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bärhalde at 1,324 m, standing 185 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Skilift Fischbach

    When does Skilift Fischbach have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Is there snow at Skilift Fischbach at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 5 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilift Fischbach?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,139 m; the lowest starts at 1,029 m (110 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Skilift Fischbach?

    Feldberg, 10 km to the west, with about 22 km of pistes.

    How big is Skilift Fischbach?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
