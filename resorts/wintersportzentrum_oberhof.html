    About Wintersportzentrum Oberhof

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Wintersportzentrum Oberhof one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 718 m and 832 m above sea level. Winterwelt Schmiedefeld is the next resort, 13 km away to the south-east.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Winterwelt Schmiedefeld (13 km south-east) has about 2.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    Lifts and terrain

    114 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 718 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Snow at Wintersportzentrum Oberhof through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 775 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-December and late December. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 11 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 3 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 22 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 27 cm.
