    About Antuco

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Antuco sits in the middle of the size table of Andes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,394 m up to 1,898 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 5.9 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 3.1 km, or 53%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.5 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    504 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,394 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Antuco?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,898 m; the lowest starts at 1,394 m (504 m of vertical).

    Is Antuco good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.5 km of its graded pistes (25%) are difficult.

    How big is Antuco?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
