    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 29 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 236 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Gore Mountain, for one, climbs to 1,092 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Black Spruce Mountain at 716 m, standing 451 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Double H Ranch (Hidden Valley) usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 250 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; a snowy December gave 32 cm, a dry one 3 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 29 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 8 cm to 62 cm.
