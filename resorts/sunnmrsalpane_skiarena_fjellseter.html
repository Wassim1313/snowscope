    About Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter

    Compact and local, Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The slopes start at 367 m and reach 743 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sunnmørsalpene, 0.2 km to the south.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The pistes of Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.8 km to ski. The largest share is black, with 1.1 km (60%). Steep ground is the speciality: 60% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 1.1 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 555 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 10 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 219 cm, around 1 April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 120 cm to 262 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 215 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For about 23 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 117 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 170 cm in the best years.

    The mountain at Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter

    376 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Above the ski area rises Ystevasshornet (1,330 m), 6.7 km to the south, about 587 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 117 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter?

    Its highest lift reaches 743 m; the lowest starts at 367 m (376 m of vertical).

    Is Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.1 km of its graded pistes (60%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter?

    Strandafjellet, 6.8 km to the south-east, with about 20 km of pistes.
