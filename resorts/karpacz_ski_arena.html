    About Karpacz

    Karpacz is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 7.0 km of pistes put it at number 35 of 257. The ski area spans the mountain from 833 m up to 1,350 m. Karpacz / Śnieżka is the next resort, 1.5 km away to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 20 runs and around 7.0 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 4.7 km (67%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,092 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 January and lasts to 11 March: about 8 weeks. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 49 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 63 cm). Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 5 cm to 86 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 517 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,033 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,350 m. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Karpacz?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Karpacz have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Karpacz at?

    The top is at 1,350 m and the base at 833 m.

    Is Karpacz suitable for families?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 4.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Karpacz have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
