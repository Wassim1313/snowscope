    Parsenn in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Parsenn (1,906 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 60 cm, with a normal range of 41 cm–72 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 112 cm (88 cm–130 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 119 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 46 % of winters, and by 1 January in 78 %.

    Best winter on record: 1998-99, peaking at 201 cm. Worst: 2024-25, which never passed 88 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to early June, about 187 days.

    Questions about Parsenn

    Can you ski Parsenn at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 60 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 78 % of 46 winters. Right now: 69 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Parsenn?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 119 cm). For a sure base, late November to early June is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Parsenn?

    About 119 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 88 cm in the leanest winter (2024-25) and 201 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high is Parsenn?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,811 m; the lowest starts at 1,000 m (1,811 m of vertical).

    How big is Parsenn?

    About 81 km of pistes and 39 lifts.

    About Parsenn

    Parsenn is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 81 km of pistes put it at number 70 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,000 m and 2,811 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Selfranga, 4.9 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,811 m: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. The base at 1,000 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,811 m.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Parsenn hold around 69 cm of snow today. 14 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 20 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.
