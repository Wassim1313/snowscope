    About Ollomont

    Ollomont is a ski area. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Crévacol, 13 km to the west.

    Snow at Ollomont through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,623 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 December and lasts to 23 May: about 24 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 82 cm, around 5 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 45 cm, with 65 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 56 cm and 94 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 76 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Ollomont: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ollomont?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Ollomont at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 45 cm deep.
