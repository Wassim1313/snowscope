    About Bukovka

    Bukovka is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 152 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Skiing here happens between 567 m and 712 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Josefův Důl, 0.2 km to the west.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 2.4 km south-east to Jizerky, with about 10 km of runs. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    How much snow Bukovka usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 640 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm, with 36 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 43 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 20 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Bukovka gives 145 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 567 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 button lift.
