    About Skiareál Pařez

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skiareál Pařez one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 620 m and 680 m above sea level. Skiareál Studenov is the next resort, 0.1 km away to the north-east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 650 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 January and lasts to 2 March: about 5 weeks. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm; a snowy December gave 51 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 35 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 5 cm to 60 cm.

    How Skiareál Pařez is laid out

    The vertical drop is 60 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 620 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Skiareál Horní Domky, reach 1,308 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    Questions about Skiareál Pařez

    When is the best time to ski at Skiareál Pařez?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Skiareál Pařez at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Skiareál Pařez?

    Between 620 m and 680 m, so 60 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skiareál Pařez?

    Paseky nad Jizerou, 3.7 km to the south-west, with about 7.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Skiareál Pařez have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
