    About Rochers-de-Naye

    Neither huge nor tiny, Rochers-de-Naye offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 558 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,357 m and 2,015 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Les Prévondes, 3.4 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,686 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 26 November and keeps it until about 26 May, roughly 26 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 95 cm; a snowy December gave 149 cm, a dry one 37 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 169 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 196 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 19 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Most winters land between 125 cm and 248 cm by mid-February.

    The mountain at Rochers-de-Naye

    The vertical drop is 658 m: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,357 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts and 1 rope tow, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Tour d'Aï at 2,330 m, standing 315 m above the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Rochers-de-Naye

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 8.2 km to ski. Black runs make up the biggest part: 3.1 km, or 38%. With 91% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 4.6 km of difficult runs. 2.8 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Rochers-de-Naye: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Rochers-de-Naye?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Rochers-de-Naye at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 95 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Rochers-de-Naye?

    The top is at 2,015 m and the base at 1,357 m.

    Is Rochers-de-Naye good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 7.4 km of its graded pistes (91%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Rochers-de-Naye?

    Les Mosses, 11 km to the east, with about 26 km of pistes.
