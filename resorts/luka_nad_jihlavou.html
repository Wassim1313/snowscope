    About Luka nad Jihlavou

    Neither huge nor tiny, Luka nad Jihlavou offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 179 of 257 in Central Europe). Skiing here happens between 464 m and 553 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski areál Šacberk, 12 km north-west, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Luka nad Jihlavou compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Čeřínek: about 1.0 km of pistes, 21 km to the west. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 508 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 16 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 20 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 9 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    The mountain at Luka nad Jihlavou

    Skied top to bottom, Luka nad Jihlavou gives 89 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 464 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 drag lift, 2 lifts in all.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Luka nad Jihlavou?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Is there snow at Luka nad Jihlavou at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).

    How high is Luka nad Jihlavou?

    Its highest lift reaches 553 m; the lowest starts at 464 m (89 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Luka nad Jihlavou?

    Čeřínek, 21 km to the west, with about 1.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Luka nad Jihlavou have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
