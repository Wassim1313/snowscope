    About Saint George

    Compact and local, Saint George has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 977 m and 1,143 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Le Brassus, 6.6 km to the north-west.

    Saint George and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Les Rousses (14 km west) has about 32 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. For higher slopes, Les Rousses climbs to 1,663 m.

    The mountain at Saint George

    From top to bottom that makes 166 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 977 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Les Rousses reaches 1,663 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are The Guesthouse (chalet), Au cavalier (hotel), Chalet le Muguet (chalet) and Chalet la Nouvelle Lune (chalet).
