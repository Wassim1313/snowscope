    About Saint George

    Compact and local, Saint George has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 977 m and 1,143 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Le Brassus, 6.6 km to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Saint George has 6 graded runs adding up to about 1.9 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.1 km (59%). With 59% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Saint George through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,060 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to late February, about 3 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 37 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 14 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 70 cm). In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 102 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    The mountain at Saint George

    From top to bottom that makes 166 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 977 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Les Rousses reaches 1,663 m. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grand Cunay at 1,574 m, standing 431 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are The Guesthouse (chalet), Au cavalier (hotel), Chalet le Muguet (chalet) and Chalet la Nouvelle Lune (chalet).

    Questions about Saint George

    When is the best time to ski at Saint George?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late February.

    Can you ski at Saint George at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    What altitude is Saint George at?

    The top is at 1,143 m and the base at 977 m.

    Is Saint George good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 1.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Saint George?

    Les Rousses, 14 km to the west, with about 32 km of pistes.
