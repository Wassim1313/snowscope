    About Sangiacomo Cardini

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sangiacomo Cardini offers about 19 km of pistes (number 327 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 974 m and reach 1,605 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Saint Grée di Viola, 5.9 km to the east.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km west to Artesina, with about 39 km of runs. For higher slopes, Artesina climbs to 2,091 m.

    The mountain at Sangiacomo Cardini

    631 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 974 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Monesi di Triora, reach 2,157 m.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Sangiacomo Cardini include Chalets Mongioie (chalet), Albergo Nazionale (hotel), Albergo Alpi (hotel) and Agriturismo La Crosa (guesthouse).
