    About Lyžařský vlek Filip

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Lyžařský vlek Filip sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 574 m and 661 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zásada, 3.4 km to the south.

    When the snow is good

    At about 618 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 February and lasts to 28 February: about 3 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm, with 49 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. The snow is usually deepest around 12 February, with about 32 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 59 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Lyžařský vlek Filip gives 87 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 574 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 button lift.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Lyžařský vlek Filip hold around 4 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 5 cm at mid-mountain).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Lyžařský vlek Filip?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    How much snow does Lyžařský vlek Filip have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).

    How high is Lyžařský vlek Filip?

    Its highest lift reaches 661 m; the lowest starts at 574 m (87 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Lyžařský vlek Filip?

    Jizerky, 3.5 km to the north, with about 10 km of pistes.

    How big is Lyžařský vlek Filip?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
