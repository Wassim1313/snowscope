    About SNØ Oslo

    Compact and local, SNØ Oslo has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The slopes start at 168 m and reach 241 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Liabakken (2.3 km, north-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    SNØ Oslo has 5 graded runs adding up to about 1.1 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 0.8 km (70%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, SNØ Oslo gives 73 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Slengfehøgda (420 m), 7.8 km to the north-west, about 179 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow SNØ Oslo usually gets

    Count on about 7 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 204 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 33 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 19 February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 5 cm to 60 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    SNØ Oslo: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at SNØ Oslo?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at SNØ Oslo at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at SNØ Oslo?

    Its highest lift reaches 241 m; the lowest starts at 168 m (73 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at SNØ Oslo?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.1 km, are easy.

    How big is SNØ Oslo?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
