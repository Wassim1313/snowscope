    About Ehrwalder Alm

    Ehrwalder Alm is a mid-sized ski area with about 18 km of pistes, number 336 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,112 m to 1,920 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zugspitze, 3.3 km to the north.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,516 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. Year to year the snow is steady, with 61 cm to 118 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 65 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 45 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 24 cm, snowy years 69 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 97 cm, around 28 February.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 23 runs and around 16 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 11 km (69%). With 69% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Ehrwalder Alm gives 808 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts and 2 T-bars. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Zugspitze at 2,962 m, standing 1,042 m above the top lift.

    Ehrwalder Alm and the resorts around it

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Eriro (hotel), Gasthof Ehrwalder Alm (mountain hut), Hochfeldern Alm (hostel) and Iglu-Dorf Zugspitze (hotel).

    Questions about Ehrwalder Alm

    What is the best month to ski at Ehrwalder Alm?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Ehrwalder Alm at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 45 cm deep.

    What altitude is Ehrwalder Alm at?

    The top is at 1,920 m and the base at 1,112 m.

    Is Ehrwalder Alm suitable for families?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How big is Ehrwalder Alm?

    About 18 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
