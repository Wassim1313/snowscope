    About Katschberg-Aineck

    With some 52 km of runs, Katschberg-Aineck belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,054 m and reach 2,211 m at the top. Petersbründllift St. Michael is the next resort, 2.5 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Katschberg-Aineck

    Katschberg-Aineck has 76 graded runs adding up to about 52 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 35 km, or 67%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 5.9 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,632 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 December and keeps it until about 30 April, roughly 19 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 49 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 67 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 32 cm, with 55 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 36 cm and 83 cm by mid-February.

    The mountain at Katschberg-Aineck

    From top to bottom that makes 1,157 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 18 lifts break down into 4 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Kareck at 2,481 m, standing 270 m above the top lift.

    Katschberg-Aineck: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Katschberg-Aineck?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Katschberg-Aineck at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 32 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Katschberg-Aineck?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026.

    What altitude is Katschberg-Aineck at?

    Between 1,054 m and 2,211 m, so 1,157 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Katschberg-Aineck best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
