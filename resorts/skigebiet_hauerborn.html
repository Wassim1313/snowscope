    About Hauerborn

    Hauerborn has about 0.5 km of pistes. The slopes start at 336 m and reach 416 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sauerberg Frammersbach, 8.5 km to the west.

    The mountain at Hauerborn

    80 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 336 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Skilifte Kreuzberg, for one, climbs to 881 m. The only lift is a button lift.

    Hauerborn: common questions

    What altitude is Hauerborn at?

    Its highest lift reaches 416 m; the lowest starts at 336 m (80 m of vertical).

    How big is Hauerborn?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 1 lift.
