    About Kessel-Lifte Inzell

    Compact and local, Kessel-Lifte Inzell has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 717 m and reach 799 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Westernberg (6.4 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 1.6 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (58%, about 1.0 km). With 58% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Kessel-Lifte Inzell is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Kessel-Lifte Inzell gives 82 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 917 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Zwiesel (1,782 m), 7.1 km to the east, about 983 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 758 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 14 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 34 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 27 cm).

    Kessel-Lifte Inzell: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Kessel-Lifte Inzell?

    Aim for mid-January and early March.

    How much snow does Kessel-Lifte Inzell have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Kessel-Lifte Inzell?

    Between 717 m and 799 m, so 82 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kessel-Lifte Inzell good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Kessel-Lifte Inzell?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
