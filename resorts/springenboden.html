    About Springenboden

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Springenboden sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,207 m and 1,510 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wiriehorn, 3.6 km to the south-west.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Wiriehorn (3.6 km south-west) has about 10 km of pistes against 6.0 km here. For more reliable snow, Grimmialp usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 23 weeks, against 20 here. For higher slopes, Grimmialp climbs to 1,828 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 5.7 km. The largest share is blue, with 3.7 km (65%). With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,358 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 36 cm, from 13 cm in a poor year to 57 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 53 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Most winters land between 44 cm and 102 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 74 cm, around 29 February.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Springenboden hold around 27 cm of snow today. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 35 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (14 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 303 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Männliflue at 2,652 m, standing 1,142 m above the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Rothbad (hotel), Gasthaus Gsässweid (guesthouse), Sennhütte Ottenschwand (mountain hut) and Haus der Naturfreunde Gwatt (chalet).

    Springenboden: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Springenboden?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Springenboden at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 36 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Springenboden at?

    Between 1,207 m and 1,510 m, so 303 m of vertical drop.

    Is Springenboden good for beginners?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 3.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Springenboden?

    Wiriehorn, 3.6 km to the south-west, with about 10 km of pistes.
