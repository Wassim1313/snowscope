    About Pico

    Pico is one of the bigger ski areas of US Northeast: about 31 km of pistes put it at number 22 of 93. Skiing here happens between 610 m and 1,199 m above sea level. Killington Resort is the next resort, 6.0 km away to the south-east.

    How Pico is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Pico gives 589 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 610 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
