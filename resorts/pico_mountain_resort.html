    About Pico

    Pico is one of the bigger ski areas of US Northeast: about 31 km of pistes put it at number 22 of 93. Skiing here happens between 610 m and 1,199 m above sea level. Killington Resort is the next resort, 6.0 km away to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 66 marked runs cover roughly 31 km. Most of the length is blue square (57%, about 17 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 8.4 km.

    How Pico is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Pico gives 589 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 610 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 6 chairlifts and 1 rope tow. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Killington Peak (1,293 m), Pico Peak (1,206 m) and Little Killington (1,194 m).

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 904 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 9 January and lasts to 31 March: about 12 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 56 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 47 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 25 cm and 85 cm by mid-February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Pico?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Can you ski at Pico at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Pico?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,199 m; the lowest starts at 610 m (589 m of vertical).

    Does Pico have steep runs?

    Yes: about 8.4 km of its graded pistes (27%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Pico have?

    About 31 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
