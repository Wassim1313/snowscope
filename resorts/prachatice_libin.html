    About Prachatice

    Prachatice has about 0.5 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 844 m and 897 m above sea level. Kubova Huť is the next resort, 16 km away to the west.

    Snow at Prachatice through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 870 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 28 January, with about 15 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 7 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 29 cm). In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 44 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 53 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 844 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow, 3 lifts in all.

    Prachatice: common questions

    When does Prachatice have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Can you ski at Prachatice at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 7 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Prachatice?

    Its highest lift reaches 897 m; the lowest starts at 844 m (53 m of vertical).

    How big is Prachatice?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
