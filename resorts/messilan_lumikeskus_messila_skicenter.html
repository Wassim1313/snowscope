    About Messilän Lumikeskus

    Messilän Lumikeskus is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 115 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The slopes start at 84 m and reach 195 m at the top.

    Runs by difficulty

    Snow at Messilän Lumikeskus through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 140 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 January and keeps it until about 2 April, roughly 11 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 19 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 33 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 9 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 42 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 20 cm and 58 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    The mountain at Messilän Lumikeskus

    111 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 7 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Questions about Messilän Lumikeskus

    What is the best month to ski at Messilän Lumikeskus?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    Is there snow at Messilän Lumikeskus at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 19 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Messilän Lumikeskus at?

    The top is at 195 m and the base at 84 m.

    What level of skier is Messilän Lumikeskus best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Messilän Lumikeskus?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
