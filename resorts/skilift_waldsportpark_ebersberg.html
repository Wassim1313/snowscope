    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Waldsportpark Ebersberg gives 26 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 569 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Wendelstein, reach 1,718 m. The only lift is a drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Taubenberg (618 m), Ludwigshöhe (618 m) and Dachsberg (612 m).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 582 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January. Most winters land between 0 cm and 14 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 9 January, with about 3 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 9 cm in a big one.
