    About Tobadill

    Compact and local, Tobadill has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,186 m and reach 1,356 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Patschiedlift, 5.6 km to the east.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.7 km south-east to Serfaus Fiss Ladis, with about 169 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Serfaus Fiss Ladis usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 27 weeks, against 13 here. For higher slopes, Serfaus Fiss Ladis climbs to 2,817 m.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Tobadill hold around 5 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 9 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Tobadill gives 170 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Serfaus Fiss Ladis, for one, climbs to 2,817 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Parseierspitze at 3,036 m, standing 1,680 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,271 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 28 January and keeps it until about 25 April, roughly 13 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 21 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 35 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 34 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 26 cm and 52 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 38 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Tobadill?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to late April.

    Is there snow at Tobadill at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 21 cm deep.

    How high is Tobadill?

    The top is at 1,356 m and the base at 1,186 m.

    Is Tobadill good for beginners?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Tobadill?

    Serfaus Fiss Ladis, 8.7 km to the south-east, with about 169 km of pistes.
