    About Folgarida-Marilleva

    With some 41 km of runs, Folgarida-Marilleva belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,290 m and 2,167 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta (6.7 km, south).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 90 marked runs cover roughly 41 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 23 km, or 55%. With 56% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,728 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late April, about 17 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 30 cm and 76 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 39 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 48 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 29 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 50 cm, a dry one 8 cm.

    How Folgarida-Marilleva is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Folgarida-Marilleva gives 877 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 7 gondolas, 13 chairlifts, 4 button lifts and 3 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Corno di Flavona, 2,914 m high and 7.3 km south-east of the resort.

    Folgarida-Marilleva: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Folgarida-Marilleva?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Folgarida-Marilleva have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 29 cm deep.

    When does Folgarida-Marilleva open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 3 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    What altitude is Folgarida-Marilleva at?

    The top is at 2,167 m and the base at 1,290 m.

    Is Folgarida-Marilleva good for beginners?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 23 km, are easy.
