    About Les Portes du Soleil

    Only 3 of the 1,056 ski areas of the Alps are larger than Les Portes du Soleil, which has some 406 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 920 m and 2,250 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Avoriaz, 5.2 km to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 818 graded runs, about 400 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 205 km (51%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 0.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,330 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 920 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 2 cable cars, 11 gondolas, 76 chairlifts, 6 T-bars, 54 button lifts, 31 drag lifts, 5 rope tows and 9 moving carpets, 194 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 9 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Les Hauts Forts (2,466 m), 6.8 km to the south, about 216 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,585 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 5 December and lasts to 14 May: about 23 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 92 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 57 cm, from 18 cm in a poor year to 95 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 19 February, with about 121 cm. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 73 cm to 154 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    When does Les Portes du Soleil have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Les Portes du Soleil at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 57 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Les Portes du Soleil open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Portes du Soleil?

    The top is at 2,250 m and the base at 920 m.

    Can beginners ski at Les Portes du Soleil?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 242 km, are easy.
