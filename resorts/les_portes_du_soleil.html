    Les Portes du Soleil in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Les Portes du Soleil (about 1,585 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 57 cm; half of the winters sit between 35 cm and 80 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 115 cm, between 88 cm and 134 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 121 cm.

    39 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 67 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1981-82 (201 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2022-23 (77 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 168 days.

    Common questions

    Can you ski Les Portes du Soleil at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 57 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 67 % of 46 winters. Right now: 37 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Les Portes du Soleil?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 121 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Les Portes du Soleil?

    About 121 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-February); between 77 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 201 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    How high do the lifts go at Les Portes du Soleil?

    The top is at 2,250 m and the base at 920 m.

    About Les Portes du Soleil

    Only 3 of the 1,056 ski areas of the Alps are larger than Les Portes du Soleil, which has some 406 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 920 m and 2,250 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Avoriaz, 5.2 km to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,330 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 920 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
