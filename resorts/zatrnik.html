    About Zatrnik

    Zatrnik has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 910 m and 935 m above sea level. Pokljuka is the next resort, 7.7 km away to the west.

    Zatrnik and the resorts around it

    How much snow Zatrnik usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 922 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 16 January, with about 12 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 9 cm, with 32 cm in the best years. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 26 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early April. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Zatrnik gives 25 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 910 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 3 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Debela peč, 2,014 m high and 7.6 km west of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Tourist farm Anž'k (guesthouse), Penzion Zaka (hotel), Turistična kmetija Frčej (guesthouse) and Garni Pension Kuralt (hotel).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Zatrnik?

    Aim for early January and early April.

    How much snow does Zatrnik have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Zatrnik?

    The top is at 935 m and the base at 910 m.

    How many km of pistes does Zatrnik have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
