    About Zatrnik

    Zatrnik is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 910 m and 935 m above sea level. Pokljuka is the next resort, 7.7 km away to the west.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 1 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Zatrnik. The coming days should add about 13 cm by Friday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Zatrnik gives 25 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 910 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Tourist farm Anž'k (guesthouse), Penzion Zaka (hotel), Turistična kmetija Frčej (guesthouse) and Garni Pension Kuralt (hotel).
