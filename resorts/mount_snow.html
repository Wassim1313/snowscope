    About Mount Snow

    Only 8 of the 93 ski areas of US Northeast are larger than Mount Snow, which has some 61 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 591 m and 1,093 m above sea level. The Hermitage Club at Haystack M is the next resort, 4.5 km away to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 119 runs and around 61 km to ski. Most of the length is blue square (65%, about 39 km). Intermediate runs make up 65% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 10 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 9 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 842 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to late March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 21 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 41 cm). Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 46 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Most winters land between 15 cm and 78 cm by mid-February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 502 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 591 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 19 lifts: 13 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 5 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Snow (1,092 m), Haystack Mountain (1,044 m) and Rice Hill (895 m).

    Mount Snow: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Mount Snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does Mount Snow have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 21 cm deep.

    When does Mount Snow open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 21 November 2026.

    What altitude is Mount Snow at?

    The top is at 1,093 m and the base at 591 m.

    What level of skier is Mount Snow best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 65% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
