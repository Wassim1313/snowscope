    The snow at Mount Snow, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Mount Snow (about 2,762 ft (842 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 8.3 in (21 cm) of cover (3.9 in (10 cm) to 11 in (28 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 16 in (40 cm) (11 in (28 cm)–23 in (58 cm)).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 18 in (46 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 0 % of winters, and by 1 January in 9 %.

    The deepest winter was 2000-01 (45 in (114 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2015-16 (5.5 in (14 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late December to late March, about 95 days.

    Mount Snow: quick answers

    Can you ski Mount Snow at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 8.3 in (21 cm), and only 9 % of the past 46 winters had 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 1.2 in (3 cm).

    What is the best time to ski Mount Snow?

    February: the snow peaks at about 18 in (46 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late December to late March.

    How snowy is Mount Snow?

    About 18 in (46 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 5.5 in (14 cm) in the leanest winter (2015-16) and 45 in (114 cm) in the biggest (2000-01).

    What altitude is Mount Snow at?

    The top is at 1,093 m and the base at 591 m.

    How many km of pistes does Mount Snow have?

    About 61 km of pistes and 19 lifts.
