    About Whiteface Mountain

    Around 37 km of marked runs place Whiteface Mountain among the larger resorts of US Northeast, number 18 by length. Its lifts climb from 372 m to 1,333 m. Its nearest neighbour is North County School, 17 km south, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 961 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. At 372 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 8 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 11 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Whiteface Mountain (1,466 m), 2.2 km to the south-west, about 133 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 16 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 852 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm, from 11 cm in a poor year to 56 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 20 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. A normal season tops out near 73 cm, around 18 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 39 cm and 94 cm.

    What there is to ski at Whiteface Mountain

    Whiteface Mountain has 85 graded runs adding up to about 37 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 16 km, or 43%. Steep ground is the speciality: 36% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 13 km of difficult runs.

    Whiteface Mountain: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Whiteface Mountain?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Whiteface Mountain have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    What altitude is Whiteface Mountain at?

    Between 372 m and 1,333 m, so 961 m of vertical drop.

    Is Whiteface Mountain good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 13 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How big is Whiteface Mountain?

    About 37 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
