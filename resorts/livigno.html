    What 46 winters say about Livigno

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Livigno (2,301 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 57 cm; half of the winters sit between 38 cm and 80 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 95 cm, between 70 cm and 112 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 99 cm.

    48 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 63 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 2000-01, peaking at 187 cm. Worst: 2022-23, which never passed 67 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late May, about 193 days.

    Questions about Livigno

    Is there snow in Livigno at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 57 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 63 % of 46 winters. Right now: 56 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Livigno?

    March: the snow peaks at about 99 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late May.

    How deep does the snow get at Livigno?

    About 99 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 67 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 187 cm in the biggest (2000-01).

    What altitude is Livigno at?

    The top is at 2,791 m and the base at 1,811 m.

    How many km of pistes does Livigno have?

    About 83 km of pistes and 37 lifts.

    About Livigno

    With some 83 km of runs, Livigno belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,811 m to 2,791 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cima Piazzi, 15 km to the south-east.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 56 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Livigno. 4 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Saturday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 980 m: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,811 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most.
