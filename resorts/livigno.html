    About Livigno

    With some 83 km of runs, Livigno belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,811 m to 2,791 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cima Piazzi, 15 km to the south-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 150 runs and around 82 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 47 km (57%). Intermediate runs make up 57% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 8.9 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,301 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 57 cm; a snowy December gave 91 cm, a dry one 29 cm. A normal season tops out near 99 cm, around 5 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 96 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 63 cm and 132 cm by mid-February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 980 m: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,811 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 6 gondolas, 14 chairlifts, 11 button lifts and 6 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 6 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Monte Cassa del Ferro, 3,140 m high and 7.9 km north-east of the resort.

    Questions about Livigno

    When is the best time to ski at Livigno?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Livigno have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 57 cm deep.

    When does Livigno open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 2 May 2027.

    What altitude is Livigno at?

    The top is at 2,791 m and the base at 1,811 m.

    Who is Livigno best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 57% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
