    About Brundage Mountain

    Around 49 km of marked runs place Brundage Mountain among the larger resorts of US West, number 39 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,792 m and 2,318 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Little Ski Hill (8.0 km, south).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,055 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 109 cm and 200 cm. For about 19 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 176 cm, around 29 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 154 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 93 cm; a snowy December gave 130 cm, a dry one 60 cm.

    What there is to ski at Brundage Mountain

    In all, 98 marked runs cover roughly 49 km. Most of the length is blue square (40%, about 20 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 31% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 15 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 526 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,792 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 6 lifts break down into 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Questions about Brundage Mountain

    When is the best time to ski at Brundage Mountain?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Brundage Mountain have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 93 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Brundage Mountain open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Brundage Mountain?

    The top is at 2,318 m and the base at 1,792 m.

    Does Brundage Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 15 km of its graded pistes (31%) are difficult.
