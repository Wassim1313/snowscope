    About Espot

    Neither huge nor tiny, Espot offers about 20 km of pistes (number 31 of 49 in the Pyrenees). Skiing here happens between 1,511 m and 2,290 m above sea level. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tavascan, 17 km to the north-east.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret: about 154 km of pistes, 20 km to the north-west. Estació d'esquí de Port-Ainé has the longer snow season: around 16 weeks of solid cover, compared with 10 at Espot. The highest nearby is Boí Taüll, topping out at 2,747 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 779 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,511 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Pic de Peguera, 2,983 m high and 5.8 km west of the resort.

    Snow at Espot through a normal winter

    Count on about 10 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,900 m) in an ordinary winter, from early January to mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm; a snowy December gave 61 cm, a dry one 3 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 8 cm to 105 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 4 February, with about 61 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 12 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Questions about Espot

    When is the best time to ski at Espot?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Espot at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Espot at?

    The top is at 2,290 m and the base at 1,511 m.

    Can beginners ski at Espot?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Espot?

    Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret, 20 km to the north-west, with about 154 km of pistes.
