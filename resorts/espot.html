    About Espot

    Neither huge nor tiny, Espot offers about 20 km of pistes (number 31 of 49 in the Pyrenees). Skiing here happens between 1,511 m and 2,290 m above sea level. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tavascan, 17 km to the north-east.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret: about 154 km of pistes, 20 km to the north-west. The highest nearby is Boí Taüll, topping out at 2,747 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 779 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,511 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
