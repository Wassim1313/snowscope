    About Val di Fassa

    Val di Fassa is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 78 km of pistes put it at number 72 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,331 m and 2,435 m above sea level. Buffaure is the next resort, 2.2 km away to the south-east.

    The pistes of Val di Fassa

    Val di Fassa has 121 graded runs adding up to about 70 km. The largest share is red, with 47 km (67%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 9.7 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Val di Fassa gives 1,104 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,331 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 2 cable cars, 8 gondolas, 20 chairlifts, 5 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Gran Vernel, 3,210 m high and 7.9 km east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 15 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,883 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 29 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 25 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 55 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 45 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 27 cm to 73 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    When does Val di Fassa have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Val di Fassa at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 25 cm deep.

    When does Val di Fassa open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Val di Fassa?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,435 m; the lowest starts at 1,331 m (1,104 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Val di Fassa best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
