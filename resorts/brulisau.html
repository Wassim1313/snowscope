    About Brülisau

    Brülisau is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 735 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 905 m up to 1,067 m. The closest other ski area is Ebenalp-Horn (2.9 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Brülisau

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 2.9 km in total. The largest share is red, with 2.9 km (100%). Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Brülisau today: about 12 cm up high. The last seven days brought 26 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 11 cm at mid-mountain).

    How much snow Brülisau usually gets

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 986 m in an ordinary winter, from early February to early March. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. By mid-April the typical depth is 23 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 36 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 34 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 12 cm to 60 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    The mountain at Brülisau

    162 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,105 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Fählentürm, 2,223 m high and 7.8 km south-west of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Gasthaus Rössli, Hotel Restaurant Alpenblick, Hotel Krone and Frohe Aussicht.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Brülisau?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Is there snow at Brülisau at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Brülisau?

    The top is at 1,067 m and the base at 905 m.

    What level of skier is Brülisau best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Brülisau?

    Ebenalp-Horn, 2.9 km to the west, with about 10 km of pistes.
