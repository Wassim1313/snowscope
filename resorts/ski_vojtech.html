    About Ski Vojtěch

    Ski Vojtěch has about 0.1 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,029 m to 1,062 m. Its nearest neighbour is Malá Úpa, 0.8 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,046 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 13 January and keeps it until about 11 March, roughly 8 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 53 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 7 cm and 86 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 17 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 63 cm).

    How Ski Vojtěch is laid out

    The vertical drop is 33 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift.

    Ski Vojtěch today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Ski Vojtěch hold around 5 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 10 cm at mid-mountain.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Ski Vojtěch?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Ski Vojtěch at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 17 cm deep.

    How high is Ski Vojtěch?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,062 m; the lowest starts at 1,029 m (33 m of vertical).

    How big is Ski Vojtěch?

    About 0.1 km of pistes and 1 lift.
