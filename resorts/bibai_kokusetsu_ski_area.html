    About Bibai Kokusetsu

    Neither huge nor tiny, Bibai Kokusetsu offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 233 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 133 m up to 309 m. Katsurazawa Kokusetsu is the next resort, 8.2 km away to the south.

    Bibai Kokusetsu and the resorts around it

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 176 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 133 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 13 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Bibai Kokusetsu. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 12 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.
