    About Skiareál Plešivec

    With some 6.0 km of runs, Skiareál Plešivec belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The slopes start at 707 m and reach 1,012 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Plešivec, 0.2 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Skiareál Plešivec has 9 graded runs adding up to about 5.8 km. The largest share is blue, with 4.0 km (69%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Skiareál Plešivec is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Skiareál Plešivec gives 305 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 907 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,012 m is the highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 860 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm, with 36 cm in the best years. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 52 cm. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 21 cm.

    Questions about Skiareál Plešivec

    What is the best month to ski at Skiareál Plešivec?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Skiareál Plešivec at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 8 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Skiareál Plešivec?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,012 m; the lowest starts at 707 m (305 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Skiareál Plešivec?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Skiareál Plešivec have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
