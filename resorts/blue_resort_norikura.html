    About Blue Resort Norikura

    Around 13 km of marked runs place Blue Resort Norikura among the larger resorts of Japan, number 39 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,477 m and 1,973 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hirayu Onsen, 8.6 km to the north-west.

    How Blue Resort Norikura is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Blue Resort Norikura gives 496 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,477 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
