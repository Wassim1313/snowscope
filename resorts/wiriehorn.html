    About Wiriehorn

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Wiriehorn sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 998 m to 1,725 m. The closest other ski area is Springenboden (3.6 km, north-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 19 runs and around 9.8 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 5.6 km (57%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How much snow Wiriehorn usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,362 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 40 cm (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 65 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 19 February, with about 84 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 57 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 50 cm and 119 cm by mid-February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Wiriehorn gives 727 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 998 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 4 T-bars. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Männliflue at 2,652 m, standing 927 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rothbad (hotel), Staldenmaadhütte (mountain hut), Rinderalphütte (mountain hut) and Gasthaus Gsässweid (guesthouse). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Wiriehorn

    When does Wiriehorn have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Wiriehorn at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 40 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Wiriehorn?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,725 m; the lowest starts at 998 m (727 m of vertical).

    Is Wiriehorn good for beginners?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Wiriehorn?

    About 10 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
