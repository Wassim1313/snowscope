    About Unazuki Snow Park

    Unazuki Snow Park is a ski area. The slopes start at 246 m and reach 434 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Tsugaike Kogen (25 km, east).

    This week's snow

    Up high, Unazuki Snow Park has roughly 3 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Tuesday.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Unazuki Snow Park gives 188 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 246 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hakuba Happo One reaches 1,827 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Questions about Unazuki Snow Park

    What altitude is Unazuki Snow Park at?

    Its highest lift reaches 434 m; the lowest starts at 246 m (188 m of vertical).
