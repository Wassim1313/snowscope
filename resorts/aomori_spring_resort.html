    About Aomori Spring Resort

    Aomori Spring Resort is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 19 km of pistes put it at number 16 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 396 m up to 916 m. The closest other ski area is Iwakisan Hyakuzawa (6.9 km, south-east).

    The mountain at Aomori Spring Resort

    520 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 396 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.
