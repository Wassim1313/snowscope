    Questions about Candanchu

    Is there snow in Candanchu at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 31 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 41 % of 46 winters. Right now: 41 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Candanchu?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 90 cm). For a sure base, early December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Candanchu?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 90 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 221 cm (2012-13), the thinnest 46 cm (1989-90).

    When does Candanchu have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How high do the lifts go at Candanchu?

    The top is at 2,238 m and the base at 1,514 m.

    Does Candanchu have steep runs?

    Yes: about 11 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.

    About Candanchu

    Neither huge nor tiny, Candanchu offers about 33 km of pistes (number 16 of 49 in the Pyrenees). Its lifts climb from 1,514 m to 2,238 m. The closest other ski area is Astun (4.6 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 61 runs and around 33 km to ski. Most of the length is red (34%, about 11 km). Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 6.7 km of difficult runs. 4.0 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,876 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 31 cm, with 80 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 28 cm and 141 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 38 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 90 cm.

    The mountain at Candanchu

    From top to bottom that makes 724 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,514 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 6 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 13 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Punta d'a Tronquera (2,693 m), 7.7 km to the south-east, about 455 m higher than the top lift.
