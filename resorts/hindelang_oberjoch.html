    About Hindelang Oberjoch

    Hindelang Oberjoch is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 286 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,128 m and reach 1,556 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Spieserlifte, 2.4 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,342 m) in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. A normal season tops out near 49 cm, around 4 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 28 cm and 73 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 28 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 46 cm). For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 36 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Hindelang Oberjoch gives 428 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,556 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 4 chairlifts and 1 drag lift. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gaishorn at 2,247 m, standing 691 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Hindelang Oberjoch

    When is the best time to ski at Hindelang Oberjoch?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Hindelang Oberjoch have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 28 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Hindelang Oberjoch at?

    Between 1,128 m and 1,556 m, so 428 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Hindelang Oberjoch best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 81% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Hindelang Oberjoch have?

    About 22 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
