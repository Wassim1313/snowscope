    About Weißsee Gletscherwelt

    Neither huge nor tiny, Weißsee Gletscherwelt offers about 12 km of pistes (number 452 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,483 m and 2,555 m above sea level. Kaprun is the next resort, 10 km away to the north-east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,019 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 November and keeps it until about 29 May, roughly 28 weeks. A normal season tops out near 145 cm, around 27 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For about 13 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 91 cm to 172 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 73 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 42 cm in a poor year to 107 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 105 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    How Weißsee Gletscherwelt is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,072 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,483 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 3 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 2 button lifts. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Romariswandköpfe at 3,511 m, standing 956 m above the top lift.

    The pistes of Weißsee Gletscherwelt

    Weißsee Gletscherwelt has 22 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 8.9 km, or 77%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Berghotel Rudolfshütte (hotel), Kalser Tauernhaus (mountain hut) and Fürthermoaralm/Ebmatten (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Weißsee Gletscherwelt: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Weißsee Gletscherwelt?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Weißsee Gletscherwelt have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 73 cm deep.

    How high is Weißsee Gletscherwelt?

    The top is at 2,555 m and the base at 1,483 m.

    What level of skier is Weißsee Gletscherwelt best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 77% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Weißsee Gletscherwelt?

    Kals-Matrei, 15 km to the south, with about 50 km of pistes.
