    About Hors-Piste

    Hors-Piste is a small ski area by the standards of US Northeast, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 201 m and reach 436 m at the top. Bromont Montagne d'Expérience is the next resort, 9.4 km away to the south.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Bromont Montagne d'Expérience (9.4 km south) has about 46 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Jay Peak Resort does better, with about 16 weeks of a good base instead of 12. Jay Peak Resort reaches 1,166 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Runs by difficulty

    Hors-Piste has 2 graded runs adding up to about 1.5 km. The largest share is freeride, with 1.5 km (100%). Steep ground is the speciality: 100% of the length is graded difficult. 1.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 12 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 318 m) in an ordinary winter, from early January to late March. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 53 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 24 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 44 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 29 cm and 73 cm.

    How Hors-Piste is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 235 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 201 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Mont Sutton, reach 852 m. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mont Shefford (526 m), Mont Shefford Sud (494 m) and Mont Shefford Est (488 m).

    Questions about Hors-Piste

    When is the best time to ski at Hors-Piste?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    How much snow does Hors-Piste have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Hors-Piste?

    Between 201 m and 436 m, so 235 m of vertical drop.

    Is Hors-Piste good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.5 km of its graded pistes (100%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hors-Piste?

    Bromont Montagne d'Expérience, 9.4 km to the south, with about 46 km of pistes.
