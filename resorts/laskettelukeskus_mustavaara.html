    About Laskettelukeskus Mustavaara

    Compact and local, Laskettelukeskus Mustavaara has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 113 m up to 201 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 157 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 18 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 33 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 52 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 46 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 62 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 76 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    The pistes of Laskettelukeskus Mustavaara

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.4 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 0.8 km (60%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 88 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 3 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Quick answers

    When does Laskettelukeskus Mustavaara have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Laskettelukeskus Mustavaara have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 33 cm deep.

    How high is Laskettelukeskus Mustavaara?

    Between 113 m and 201 m, so 88 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Laskettelukeskus Mustavaara best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 60% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Laskettelukeskus Mustavaara?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
