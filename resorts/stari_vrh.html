    The snow at Stari Vrh, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Stari Vrh (888 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 6 cm; half of the winters sit between 2 cm and 13 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 11 cm (5 cm–31 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 13 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 0 % of winters, and by 1 January in 2 %.

    2005-06 was the big one (97 cm); 2019-20 the lean one (8 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late January until mid-March in a normal winter, roughly 52 days.

    Today's 14 cm compares with a usual 1 cm for 26 November.

    About Stari Vrh

    Neither huge nor tiny, Stari Vrh offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 600 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 593 m and 1,183 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Rudno, 7.1 km to the north.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Stari Vrh gives 590 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 593 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Blegoš at 1,562 m, standing 379 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Stari Vrh usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 888 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 40 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 13 cm, around 3 February. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm, with 26 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.
