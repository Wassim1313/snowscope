    How Le Tanet is laid out

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Hohneck (1,363 m), Haut de Falimont (1,306 m) and Gazon du Faing (1,306 m).

    Snow at Le Tanet through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 779 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 20 cm. A normal season tops out near 4 cm, around 19 January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 5 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 1 cm; a snowy December gave 14 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and late January.

    Le Tanet: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Le Tanet?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Can you ski at Le Tanet at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 5 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 1 cm).
