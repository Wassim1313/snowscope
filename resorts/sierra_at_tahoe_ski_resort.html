    About Sierra-at-Tahoe

    Sierra-at-Tahoe is a mid-sized ski area with about 27 km of pistes, number 84 of 175 in US West by length. Its lifts climb from 2,030 m to 2,693 m. The closest other ski area is Kirkwood (13 km, south).

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Sierra-at-Tahoe hold around 122 cm of snow today. 55 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 14 cm by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 18 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 41 graded runs, about 27 km in total. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 41%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.6 km.

    How Sierra-at-Tahoe is laid out

    The vertical drop is 663 m: runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,030 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 9 chairlifts and 4 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Little Round Top at 2,924 m, standing 231 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Sierra-at-Tahoe through a normal winter

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,362 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. Most winters land between 69 cm and 243 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 76 cm, from 10 cm in a poor year to 153 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 99 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 162 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 15 weeks.

    Questions about Sierra-at-Tahoe

    When is the best time to ski at Sierra-at-Tahoe?

    Aim for early January and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Sierra-at-Tahoe at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 76 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Sierra-at-Tahoe at?

    Between 2,030 m and 2,693 m, so 663 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Sierra-at-Tahoe?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Sierra-at-Tahoe?

    Heavenly, 21 km to the north-east, with about 85 km of pistes.
