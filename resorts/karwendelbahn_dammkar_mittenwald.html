    About Karwendelbahn (Dammkar-Mittenwal

    Karwendelbahn is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 932 m up to 2,246 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Familienparadies Kranzberg, 2.4 km to the north-west.

    How Karwendelbahn is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,314 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 932 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,246 m. The only lift is a cable car. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The highest named summit within 8 km is Große Riedlkarspitze at 2,585 m, standing 339 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Karwendelbahn through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,589 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 9 December and keeps it until about 12 May, roughly 22 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 44 cm, with 71 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 27 February, with about 95 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 60 cm and 116 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 67 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Karwendelbahn?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Karwendelbahn at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 44 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Karwendelbahn?

    Between 932 m and 2,246 m, so 1,314 m of vertical drop.
