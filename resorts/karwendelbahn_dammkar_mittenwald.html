    About Karwendelbahn (Dammkar-Mittenwal

    Karwendelbahn is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 932 m up to 2,246 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Familienparadies Kranzberg, 2.4 km to the north-west.

    How Karwendelbahn is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,314 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 932 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,246 m.
