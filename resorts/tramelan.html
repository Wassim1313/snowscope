    About Tramelan

    Neither huge nor tiny, Tramelan offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 562 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 925 m to 1,209 m. Les Breuleux is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the west.

    How Tramelan compares nearby

    In this group of 7, Tramelan is number 2 for piste length. The highest nearby is Bugnenets-Savagnières, topping out at 1,448 m.

    How Tramelan is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 284 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 925 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Tramelan today: about 2 cm up high. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Tramelan include Chez Marie (guesthouse), Vent du Nord (guesthouse), Auberge du Hameau (hostel) and Chalet Mont Crosin (hotel).
