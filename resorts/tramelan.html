    About Tramelan

    Neither huge nor tiny, Tramelan offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 562 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 925 m to 1,209 m. Les Breuleux is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the west.

    What there is to ski at Tramelan

    Tramelan has 9 graded runs adding up to about 8.2 km. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 4.4 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Tramelan is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 284 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 925 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Montagne du Droit (1,263 m), Montbautier (1,160 m) and Petit Crêt (1,022 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,067 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 29 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 20 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 7 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Tramelan?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Tramelan at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 4 cm).

    How high is Tramelan?

    The top is at 1,209 m and the base at 925 m.

    Is Tramelan good for beginners?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 4.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Tramelan?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
