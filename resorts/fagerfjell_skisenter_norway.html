    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 143 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Flesebekk-ble, 1,272 m high and 6.5 km west of the resort.

    Fagerfjell Skisenter today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Fagerfjell Skisenter hold around 19 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 13 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (25 cm).

    How much snow Fagerfjell Skisenter usually gets

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 684 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 86 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 99 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 59 cm and 134 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 51 cm; a snowy December gave 88 cm, a dry one 30 cm.

    Fagerfjell Skisenter: common questions

    When does Fagerfjell Skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Fagerfjell Skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 51 cm deep.

    How high is Fagerfjell Skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 755 m; the lowest starts at 612 m (143 m of vertical).
