    The snow at Fagerfjell Skisenter, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Fagerfjell Skisenter (about 684 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 51 cm of cover (40 cm to 73 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 90 cm, between 76 cm and 112 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 99 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 41 % of winters, and by 1 January in 65 %.

    The deepest winter was 1987-88 (190 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1991-92 (70 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 165 days.

    Common questions

    Can you ski Fagerfjell Skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 51 cm at Christmas, and 65 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 20 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Fagerfjell Skisenter?

    March: the snow peaks at about 99 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How deep does the snow get at Fagerfjell Skisenter?

    About 99 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 70 cm in the leanest winter (1991-92) and 190 cm in the biggest (1987-88).

    How high is Fagerfjell Skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 755 m; the lowest starts at 612 m (143 m of vertical).
