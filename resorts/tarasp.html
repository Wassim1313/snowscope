    About Tarasp

    Tarasp has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,409 m and 1,449 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Scuol Motta Naluns, 4.8 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    For more reliable snow, Minschuns usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 28 weeks, against 7 here. For higher slopes, Scuol Motta Naluns climbs to 2,792 m.

    This week's snow

    The mountain at Tarasp

    40 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,409 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun, reach 2,868 m. The only lift is a rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Piz Pisoc, 3,173 m high and 3.7 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 7 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,429 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 23 cm (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 36 cm). For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 40 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 27 cm to 53 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 27 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Tarasp: common questions

    When does Tarasp have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    Is there snow at Tarasp at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 23 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Tarasp?

    The top is at 1,449 m and the base at 1,409 m.

    How big is Tarasp?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
