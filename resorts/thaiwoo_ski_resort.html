    About Thaiwoo

    With some 18 km of runs, Thaiwoo belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of China. Its lifts climb from 1,655 m to 2,152 m. National Biathlon Centre is the next resort, 4.2 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Thaiwoo

    The piste map lists 25 graded runs, about 18 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (56%, about 10 km). Intermediate runs make up 56% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 4.6 km of difficult runs.

    How Thaiwoo is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Thaiwoo gives 497 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,655 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts and 6 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 6 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    Quick answers

    How high is Thaiwoo?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,152 m; the lowest starts at 1,655 m (497 m of vertical).

    Does Thaiwoo have steep runs?

    Yes: about 4.6 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Thaiwoo?

    Snow Ruyi Ski Jumping Centre, 5.5 km to the north-east, with about 31 km of pistes.

    How big is Thaiwoo?

    About 18 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
