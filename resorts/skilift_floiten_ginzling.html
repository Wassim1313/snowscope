    About Skilift Floiten Ginzling

    Skilift Floiten Ginzling has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 989 m and 1,024 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Mayrhofen Hippach (8.2 km, north).

    The mountain at Skilift Floiten Ginzling

    From top to bottom that makes 35 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 989 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Hintertuxer Gletscher, for one, climbs to 3,245 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Kleiner Mörchner, 3,198 m high and 7.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow at Skilift Floiten Ginzling through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,006 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 28 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm; a snowy December gave 25 cm, a dry one 3 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 24 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Year to year the snow is steady, with 15 cm to 36 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Skilift Floiten Ginzling

    Aim for early March and late March.

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).
