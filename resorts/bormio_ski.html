    About Bormio Ski

    With some 32 km of runs, Bormio Ski belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,197 m and reach 3,010 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Cima Piazzi (6.3 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 56 marked runs cover roughly 30 km. The largest share is red, with 23 km (75%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.7 km.

    How Bormio Ski is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bormio Ski gives 1,813 m of descent: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 6 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 3 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Monte Cristallo (3,439 m), 7.5 km to the north-east, about 429 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,104 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm, with 59 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 38 cm to 84 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 61 cm, around 14 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 55 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Bormio Ski: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Bormio Ski?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Bormio Ski have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Bormio Ski?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,010 m; the lowest starts at 1,197 m (1,813 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Bormio Ski best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 75% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Bormio Ski have?

    About 32 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
