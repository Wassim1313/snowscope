    Snow at Salzstiegl through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,038 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The snow is usually deepest around 4 February, with about 14 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 37 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 9 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 26 cm).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Salzstiegl?

    Aim for mid-March and late March.

    Is there snow at Salzstiegl at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 9 cm).
