    About Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast

    Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 31 km of pistes put it at number 196 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,102 m and reach 2,412 m at the top. Obersaxen is the next resort, 7.5 km away to the south-east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,310 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 4 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Cavistrau Grond (3,251 m), 7.9 km to the west, about 839 m higher than the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 47 runs and around 31 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 21 km (67%). Intermediate runs make up 67% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 3.8 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,757 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 24 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 97 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 5 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 54 cm; a snowy December gave 70 cm, a dry one 28 cm. A normal season tops out near 104 cm, around 26 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 64 cm and 123 cm.

    Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast: common questions

    When does Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 54 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,412 m; the lowest starts at 1,102 m (1,310 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast have?

    About 31 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
