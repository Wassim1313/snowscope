    About Loon

    Loon is one of the bigger ski areas of US Northeast: about 46 km of pistes put it at number 12 of 93. Its lifts climb from 262 m to 929 m. The closest other ski area is Kanc (2.2 km, west).

    The pistes of Loon

    In all, 79 marked runs cover roughly 46 km. Most of the length is blue square (55%, about 25 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 9.5 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Loon

    From top to bottom that makes 667 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 262 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The lift system counts 12 lifts: 1 gondola, 10 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Mount Liberty (1,353 m), 7.4 km to the north, about 424 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 596 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early April, about 14 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 61 cm, around 17 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 16 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 25 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 7 cm in a poor year to 43 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 27 cm and 90 cm by mid-February.

    Loon: common questions

    When does Loon have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Is there snow at Loon at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Loon?

    Between 262 m and 929 m, so 667 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Loon best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 55% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Loon have?

    About 46 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
