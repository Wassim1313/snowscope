    About The Hermitage Club at Haystack M

    The Hermitage Club at Haystack M is a mid-sized ski area with about 23 km of pistes, number 31 of 93 in US Northeast by length. The slopes start at 549 m and reach 977 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Mount Snow, 4.5 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The Hermitage Club at Haystack M has 59 graded runs adding up to about 23 km. Most of the length is blue square (43%, about 9.8 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 4.2 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow The Hermitage Club at Haystack M usually gets

    Count on about 9 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 763 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to late March. Most winters land between 14 cm and 76 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 20 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 40 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 45 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 428 m: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 549 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Mount Snow (1,092 m), 4.5 km to the north-west, about 115 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at The Hermitage Club at Haystack M?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does The Hermitage Club at Haystack M have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is The Hermitage Club at Haystack M at?

    Its highest lift reaches 977 m; the lowest starts at 549 m (428 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is The Hermitage Club at Haystack M best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 43% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near The Hermitage Club at Haystack M?

    Stratton, 20 km to the north, with about 72 km of pistes.
