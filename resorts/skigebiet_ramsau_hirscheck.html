    About Ramsau

    Compact and local, Ramsau has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,035 m and 1,382 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Götschenkopf, 2.9 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Ramsau usually gets

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,208 m in an ordinary winter, from early January to late April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 32 cm to 82 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 40 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 64 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 27 cm, from 7 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 3.2 km in total. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 3.2 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Ramsau is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 347 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 3 drag lifts, 4 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Hocheck (2,651 m), 7.8 km to the south, about 1,269 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Ramsau

    What is the best month to ski at Ramsau?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    Is there snow at Ramsau at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 27 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Ramsau?

    The top is at 1,382 m and the base at 1,035 m.

    Is Ramsau suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ramsau?

    Rossfeld, 14 km to the east, with about 10 km of pistes.
