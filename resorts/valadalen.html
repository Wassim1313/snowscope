    About Vålådalen

    Vålådalen is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 604 m and reach 751 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Trillevallen, 14 km north-east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Vålådalen usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 678 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 November and lasts to 16 May: about 25 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 86 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is usually deepest around 27 March, with about 89 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 54 cm to 100 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 48 cm, from 32 cm in a poor year to 69 cm in a big one.

    How Vålådalen is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 147 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Åre, for one, climbs to 1,318 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Ottfjället Östertoppen at 1,265 m, standing 514 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Vålådalen include Per Olsgården (hostel), Vallbo fjällgård (hostel) and Ottsjö Fjällhotell (hotel).

    Questions about Vålådalen

    When is the best time to ski at Vålådalen?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Vålådalen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 48 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Vålådalen at?

    The top is at 751 m and the base at 604 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Vålådalen?

    Åre, 28 km to the north, with about 116 km of pistes.

    How big is Vålådalen?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
