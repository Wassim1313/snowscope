    About Planai & Hochwurzen

    Around 65 km of marked runs place Planai & Hochwurzen among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 87 by length. The slopes start at 743 m and reach 1,901 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Schladming Dachstein (1.6 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Planai & Hochwurzen

    In all, 119 marked runs cover roughly 65 km. Most of the length is red (47%, about 30 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 3.8 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Planai & Hochwurzen

    From top to bottom that makes 1,158 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 743 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 26 lifts break down into 1 funicular, 7 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 8 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts and 5 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Schiedeck (2,339 m), 7.7 km to the south, about 438 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Planai & Hochwurzen through a normal winter

    At about 1,322 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 December and lasts to 25 April: about 18 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 35 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 37 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 53 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 67 cm.

    Planai & Hochwurzen: common questions

    When does Planai & Hochwurzen have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Planai & Hochwurzen at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 31 cm deep.

    When does Planai & Hochwurzen open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Planai & Hochwurzen?

    Between 743 m and 1,901 m, so 1,158 m of vertical drop.

    Is Planai & Hochwurzen suitable for families?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 31 km, are easy.
