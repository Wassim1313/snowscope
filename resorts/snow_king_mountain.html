    About Snow King Mountain

    With roughly 18 km of runs, Snow King Mountain sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,902 m up to 2,416 m. The closest other ski area is Jackson Hole (17 km, north-west).

    What there is to ski at Snow King Mountain

    Snow King Mountain has 39 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 7.3 km, or 41%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 5.1 km.

    Snow at Snow King Mountain through a normal winter

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,159 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to mid-May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 87 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 57 cm in a poor year to 119 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 128 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 158 cm, around 29 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 110 cm and 184 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    How Snow King Mountain is laid out

    514 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,902 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 4 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Snow King Mountain (2,378 m), East Gros Ventre Butte (2,259 m) and High School Butte (2,070 m).

    Snow King Mountain: common questions

    When does Snow King Mountain have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Snow King Mountain at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 87 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Snow King Mountain?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,416 m; the lowest starts at 1,902 m (514 m of vertical).

    Does Snow King Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 5.1 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.

    How big is Snow King Mountain?

    About 18 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
