    About Monte Valbella (Asiago)

    Monte Valbella (Asiago) is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 441 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,134 m up to 1,139 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being leMelette, 6.0 km to the north.

    Snow at Monte Valbella (Asiago) through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,136 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 46 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 13 cm, around 21 January. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm, with 30 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Monte Valbella (Asiago): common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Monte Valbella (Asiago)?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Monte Valbella (Asiago) have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Monte Valbella (Asiago)?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,139 m; the lowest starts at 1,134 m (5 m of vertical).

    Is Monte Valbella (Asiago) suitable for families?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 7.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Monte Valbella (Asiago)?

    About 12 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
