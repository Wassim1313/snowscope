    About Akita Hachimantai

    Akita Hachimantai is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 272 of 385 in Japan by length. The slopes start at 998 m and reach 1,196 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Appi Kogen (14 km, east).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 198 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 998 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a gondola. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.
