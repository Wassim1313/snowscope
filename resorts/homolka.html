    About Homôľka

    Homôľka is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 211 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Its lifts climb from 750 m to 849 m. The closest other ski area is Mojtín (11 km, north-east).

    Homôľka and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km north-east to Mojtín, with about 4.0 km of runs. For higher slopes, Fačkovské sedlo climbs to 1,071 m.

    The pistes of Homôľka

    Homôľka has 6 graded runs adding up to about 1.2 km. Most of the length is red (100%, about 1.2 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 99 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 750 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 3 lifts here are button lifts.
