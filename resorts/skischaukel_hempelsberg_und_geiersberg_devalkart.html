    How Skischaukel Hempelsberg und Geie is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Skischaukel Hempelsberg und Geie gives 105 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 639 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.
