    About Bannalp

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Bannalp one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 1,661 m and reach 1,792 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Engelberg (4.7 km, south-west).

    How much snow Bannalp usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,726 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 28 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 27 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. Most winters land between 88 cm and 171 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 70 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 34 cm in a poor year to 89 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 136 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 149 cm, around 30 March.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 2.0 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 2.0 km (100%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Bannalp has roughly 28 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 66 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 39 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    131 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,661 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 8 lifts break down into 6 cable cars and 2 T-bars. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Urirotstock, 2,928 m high and 7.4 km east of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 mountain huts: Chrüzhütte, Berggasthaus Urnerstafel, Berggasthaus Bannalpsee and Haghütte. Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Bannalp

    When is the best time to ski at Bannalp?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Bannalp at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 70 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Bannalp at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,792 m; the lowest starts at 1,661 m (131 m of vertical).

    Is Bannalp suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bannalp?

    Engelberg Titlis, 9.6 km to the south, with about 42 km of pistes.
