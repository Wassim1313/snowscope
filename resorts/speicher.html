    About Speicher

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Speicher one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 920 m and reach 1,024 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Beckenhalde, 4.0 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Speicher

    From top to bottom that makes 104 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,120 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.
