    About Speicher

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Speicher one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 920 m and reach 1,024 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Beckenhalde, 4.0 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Speicher

    From top to bottom that makes 104 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,120 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Gäbris, 1,250 m high and 4.3 km south-east of the resort.

    The pistes of Speicher

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 100%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Speicher through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 972 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the typical depth is 14 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 14 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 15 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 32 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 8 cm to 51 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Speicher?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Can you ski at Speicher at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 15 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Speicher?

    Between 920 m and 1,024 m, so 104 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Speicher?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Speicher?

    Grub-Kaien, 6.0 km to the north-east, with about 3.0 km of pistes.
