    The snow at L'Audibergue, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at L'Audibergue (1,455 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 3 cm, with a normal range of 0 cm–12 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 12 cm (0 cm–36 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 16 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    2 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 7 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 2008-09, peaking at 198 cm. Worst: 2024-25, which never passed 10 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-January to early March (41 days).

    About L'Audibergue

    Neither huge nor tiny, L'Audibergue offers about 17 km of pistes (number 348 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,277 m and reach 1,633 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Gréolières-Les-Neiges, 17 km north-east, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.
