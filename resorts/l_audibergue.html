    About L'Audibergue

    Neither huge nor tiny, L'Audibergue offers about 17 km of pistes (number 348 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,277 m and reach 1,633 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Gréolières-Les-Neiges, 17 km north-east, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 26 graded runs, about 17 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 8.3 km, or 48%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 356 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Valberg, for one, climbs to 2,012 m. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 chairlift, 5 button lifts and 1 drag lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Cime de Bausson (1,657 m), Pic de l'Aiglo (1,645 m) and Bauroux (1,644 m).

    How much snow L'Audibergue usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,455 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 3 cm, with 32 cm in the best years. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 16 cm, around 22 February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 77 cm.

    L'Audibergue: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at L'Audibergue?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Is there snow at L'Audibergue at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 3 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is L'Audibergue at?

    Between 1,277 m and 1,633 m, so 356 m of vertical drop.

    Is L'Audibergue good for beginners?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 8.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does L'Audibergue have?

    About 17 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
