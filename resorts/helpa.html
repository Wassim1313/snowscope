    About Heľpa

    Heľpa has about 0.4 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 744 m up to 818 m. The closest other ski area is Polomka (8.3 km, south-west).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Heľpa gives 74 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 744 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Čertovica, for one, climbs to 1,419 m. The only lift is a button lift.
