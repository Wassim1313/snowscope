    About Les Giettes

    Compact and local, Les Giettes has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,280 m and reach 1,694 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Martinaux, 7.1 km to the east.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Les Giettes today: about 66 cm up high. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Friday.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 414 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Ovronnaz, reach 2,456 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Chindonne (hotel), Whitepod (hotel), Vieux Manoir (hotel) and Entre deux Nants (guesthouse).

    Quick answers

    How high is Les Giettes?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,694 m; the lowest starts at 1,280 m (414 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Les Giettes?

    Espace Liberté, 12 km to the north-west, with about 103 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Les Giettes have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
