    About Burglift Stans

    Compact and local, Burglift Stans has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 568 m and 672 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kellerjoch, 4.4 km to the south.

    How Burglift Stans compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.7 km south-east to Spieljoch, with about 16 km of runs. The highest nearby is Spieljoch, topping out at 2,049 m.

    The snow right now

    There is no snow on the ground at Burglift Stans yet. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    Lifts and terrain

    104 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 568 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Spieljoch reaches 2,049 m.

    Questions about Burglift Stans

    What altitude is Burglift Stans at?

    Its highest lift reaches 672 m; the lowest starts at 568 m (104 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Burglift Stans?

    Spieljoch, 8.7 km to the south-east, with about 16 km of pistes.

    How big is Burglift Stans?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
