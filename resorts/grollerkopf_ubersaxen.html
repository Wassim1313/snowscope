    About Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen

    Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 750 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,014 m and 1,194 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Laterns-Gapfohl, 5.4 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 3.3 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.3 km, or 100%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Laterns-Gapfohl: about 24 km of pistes, 5.4 km to the east. For more reliable snow, Laterns-Gapfohl usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 22 weeks, against 9 here. For higher slopes, Laterns-Gapfohl climbs to 1,770 m.

    Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen today and the next days

    Today there are about 8 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 21 cm by Friday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 8 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 180 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Damüls, for one, climbs to 2,021 m. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Tälispitze, 2,000 m high and 7.1 km east of the resort.

    Snow at Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,104 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 27 January and keeps it until about 20 April, roughly 9 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 10 February, with about 35 cm. Most winters land between 18 cm and 56 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 31 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 19 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 32 cm).

    Questions about Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen

    When is the best time to ski at Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen?

    Aim for mid-February and mid-April; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 19 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,194 m; the lowest starts at 1,014 m (180 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.3 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen?

    Laterns-Gapfohl, 5.4 km to the east, with about 24 km of pistes.
