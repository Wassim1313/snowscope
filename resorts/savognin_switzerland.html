    About Savognin

    Savognin is a ski area. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Savognin, 1.1 km to the south-west.

    How much snow Savognin usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,009 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 5 December and keeps it until about 22 May, roughly 24 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 53 cm and 105 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 46 cm (lean years 24 cm, snowy years 72 cm). A normal season tops out near 88 cm, around 26 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 84 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Savognin: common questions

    When does Savognin have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Savognin at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 46 cm deep.
