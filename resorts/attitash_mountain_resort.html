    About Attitash

    Around 39 km of marked runs place Attitash among the larger resorts of US Northeast, number 15 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 190 m up to 695 m. Cranmore is the next resort, 11 km away to the east.

    What there is to ski at Attitash

    In all, 87 marked runs cover roughly 39 km. The largest share is blue square, with 20 km (52%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 10 km of difficult runs.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 15 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 442 m) in an ordinary winter, from late December to early April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 21 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 65 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 35 cm to 102 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 29 cm, with 50 cm in the best years.

    How Attitash is laid out

    505 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 190 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 8 lifts break down into 7 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Bear Mountain (976 m), 7.4 km to the south-west, about 281 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Attitash

    What is the best month to ski at Attitash?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Is there snow at Attitash at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 29 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Attitash?

    Between 190 m and 695 m, so 505 m of vertical drop.

    Is Attitash good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 10 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    How big is Attitash?

    About 39 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
