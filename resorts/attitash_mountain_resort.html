    The snow at Attitash, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Attitash (1,450 ft (442 m)), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 11 in (29 cm) of cover (5.9 in (15 cm) to 15 in (37 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 24 in (62 cm), between 18 in (46 cm) and 30 in (76 cm) in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 26 in (65 cm).

    By 15 December 2 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 17 %.

    2007-08 was the big one (56 in (143 cm)); 2005-06 the lean one (14 in (35 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs late December to mid-April (109 days).

    Skiing Attitash: the practical side

    The pistes run from 623 ft (190 m) to 2,280 ft (695 m) (1,657 ft (505 m) of vertical), with 24 mi of marked runs and 8 lifts.

    Attitash: quick answers

    Does Attitash have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 11 in (29 cm) at Christmas on average, with a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 17 % of 46 winters. Right now: 1.2 in (3 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Attitash?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 26 in (65 cm)). For a sure base, late December to mid-April is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How much snow does Attitash get?

    About 26 in (65 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 14 in (35 cm) in the leanest winter (2005-06) and 56 in (143 cm) in the biggest (2007-08).

    How high do the lifts go at Attitash?

    Between 190 m and 695 m, so 505 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Attitash?

    About 39 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
