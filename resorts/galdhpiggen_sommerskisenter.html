    About Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 1,850 m to 2,180 m.

    How Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter gives 330 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Galdhøpiggen (2,469 m), 3.8 km to the south-west, about 289 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter today: about 63 cm up high. The last seven days brought 39 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 88 cm by now.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 2.6 km. Most of the length is red (100%, about 2.6 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,015 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-October to late May or later, about 33 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 124 cm and 216 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 15 April, with about 200 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 132 cm, from 95 cm in a poor year to 160 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 200 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 26 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and mid-April.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter include Juvasshytta (mountain hut), Elveseter Hotell (hotel), Spiterstulen turisthytte (mountain hut) and Raubergstulen Turisthytte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter?

    Aim for mid-March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-October to late May.

    Is there snow at Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 132 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,180 m; the lowest starts at 1,850 m (330 m of vertical).

    Who is Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Galdhøpiggen Sommerskisenter?

    Tyin-Filefjell, 53 km to the south, with about 15 km of pistes.
