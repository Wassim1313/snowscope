    About Mount Prospect Ski Tow

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Mount Prospect Ski Tow one of the smaller places to ski in US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 438 m to 511 m. Mt. Eustis is the next resort, 24 km away to the south-west.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Bretton Woods (24 km south) has about 41 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Tuckerman Ravine has the longer snow season: around 19 weeks of solid cover, compared with 14 at Mount Prospect Ski Tow. For higher slopes, Cannon Mountain climbs to 1,231 m.

    How much snow Mount Prospect Ski Tow usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 474 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 January and keeps it until about 7 April, roughly 14 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 26 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 50 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 14 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Year to year the snow is steady, with 31 cm to 89 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 61 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    How Mount Prospect Ski Tow is laid out

    The vertical drop is 73 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 438 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Prospect Mountain, 621 m high and 0.5 km south-east of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Mount Prospect Ski Tow include Cabot Inn & Suites (hotel), Roger's Motel (hotel), Mountain View Grand Resort & Spa (hotel) and Spalding Inn (guesthouse).

    Questions about Mount Prospect Ski Tow

    When is the best time to ski at Mount Prospect Ski Tow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    How much snow does Mount Prospect Ski Tow have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    What altitude is Mount Prospect Ski Tow at?

    Between 438 m and 511 m, so 73 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Mount Prospect Ski Tow?

    Bretton Woods, 24 km to the south, with about 41 km of pistes.

    How big is Mount Prospect Ski Tow?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
