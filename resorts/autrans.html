    About Autrans

    Autrans is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 985 m and 1,633 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Autrans, 2.7 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 648 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The base at 985 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,633 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Montagnes de Lans: about 23 km of pistes, 11 km to the south.
