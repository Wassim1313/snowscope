    How much snow Payolle usually gets

    Count on about 7 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,590 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 49 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 88 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 46 cm.

    Payolle: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Payolle?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Is there snow at Payolle at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).
