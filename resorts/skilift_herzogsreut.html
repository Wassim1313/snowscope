    About Skilift Herzogsreut

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skilift Herzogsreut one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 794 m and 853 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skiabfahrt Grainet, 4.2 km to the south.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 824 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 24 cm, around 8 February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; a snowy December gave 40 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 65 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    59 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 994 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The only lift is a drag lift.
