    About Maniva

    Maniva is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 439 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,472 m and 2,082 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Gaver, 10 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Maniva through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,777 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 December and keeps it until about 3 May, roughly 20 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 39 cm, from 7 cm in a poor year to 82 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 106 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 77 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 28 cm and 117 cm.

    The mountain at Maniva

    610 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,472 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 8 lifts break down into 5 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Punta Setteventi, 2,251 m high and 3.1 km north of the resort.

    Maniva: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Maniva?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Maniva at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Maniva?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,082 m; the lowest starts at 1,472 m (610 m of vertical).

    Is Maniva good for beginners?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 6.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Maniva?

    Montecampione, 14 km to the west, with about 20 km of pistes.
