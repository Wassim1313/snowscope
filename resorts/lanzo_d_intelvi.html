    About Lanzo d'Intelvi

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Lanzo d'Intelvi one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,087 m and 1,198 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Bedea-Novaggio (12 km, west).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Lanzo d'Intelvi gives 111 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Pian delle Betulle, for one, climbs to 1,721 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 2 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Monte Generoso, 1,701 m high and 4.4 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,142 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 40 cm). For the best snow, aim for early January and late February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 6 cm to 69 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 27 cm.

    Questions about Lanzo d'Intelvi

    When does Lanzo d'Intelvi have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and late February.

    Can you ski at Lanzo d'Intelvi at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Lanzo d'Intelvi?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,198 m; the lowest starts at 1,087 m (111 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Lanzo d'Intelvi?

    Pian delle Betulle, 31 km to the east, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Lanzo d'Intelvi have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
