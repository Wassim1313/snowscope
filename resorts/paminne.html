    About Påminne

    Påminne is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 11 m and reach 89 m at the top.

    How Påminne compares nearby

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 50 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 42 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 19 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 5 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 18 cm in a big one.

    Påminne: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Påminne?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    How much snow does Påminne have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 5 cm).

    What altitude is Påminne at?

    Its highest lift reaches 89 m; the lowest starts at 11 m (78 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Påminne?

    Vihti, 53 km to the north-east, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Påminne have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
