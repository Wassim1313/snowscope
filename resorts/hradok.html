    About Hrádok

    Hrádok has about 0.4 km of pistes. The slopes start at 547 m and reach 664 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Martinské Hole, 5.5 km to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 117 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 547 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a button lift.
