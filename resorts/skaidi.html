    About Skaidi

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Skaidi sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 60 m to 290 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 175 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 14 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 66 cm to 111 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 104 cm, around 30 March. For about 3 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 58 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 79 cm, a dry one 36 cm. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 100 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    What there is to ski at Skaidi

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 4.1 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.0 km, or 48%. Intermediate runs make up 48% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The mountain at Skaidi

    Skied top to bottom, Skaidi gives 230 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 27 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Skaidi. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 17 cm by Thursday. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 38 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Skaidi include Repparfjord Camping og Misjonssenter (chalet) and Skaidi Hotel (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When does Skaidi have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Skaidi at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 58 cm deep.

    How high is Skaidi?

    The top is at 290 m and the base at 60 m.

    What level of skier is Skaidi best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 48% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Skaidi have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
