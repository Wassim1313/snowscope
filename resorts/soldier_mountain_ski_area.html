    About Soldier Mountain

    With roughly 22 km of runs, Soldier Mountain sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,763 m up to 2,188 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Rotarun, 38 km to the east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,976 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late April, about 21 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 61 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 113 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 5 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 60 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 27 cm, snowy years 104 cm). Most winters land between 60 cm and 161 cm by mid-February.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 41 runs and around 22 km to ski. Most of the length is green circle (35%, about 7.6 km). With 34% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 7.4 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    425 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,763 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Smoky Dome, 3,077 m high and 7.9 km west of the resort.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Soldier Mountain?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    Is there snow at Soldier Mountain at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 60 cm deep.

    How high is Soldier Mountain?

    Between 1,763 m and 2,188 m, so 425 m of vertical drop.

    Is Soldier Mountain good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 7.4 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.

    How big is Soldier Mountain?

    About 22 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
