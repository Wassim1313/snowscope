    About Renåfjellet alpinsenter

    Renåfjellet alpinsenter is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 127 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 639 m to 857 m. Its nearest neighbour is Engerdal Østfjell, 36 km east, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Renåfjellet alpinsenter and the resorts around it

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 748 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to early May. A normal season tops out near 78 cm, around 20 March. Most winters land between 59 cm and 94 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 48 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 27 cm, snowy years 67 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 66 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Lifts and terrain

    218 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Renåfjellet, 1,028 m high and 3.7 km south-east of the resort.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 14 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Renåfjellet alpinsenter. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 26 cm by now.

    Staying nearby

    Questions about Renåfjellet alpinsenter

    When does Renåfjellet alpinsenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    How much snow does Renåfjellet alpinsenter have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 48 cm deep.

    What altitude is Renåfjellet alpinsenter at?

    Between 639 m and 857 m, so 218 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Renåfjellet alpinsenter?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
