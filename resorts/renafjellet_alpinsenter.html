    Renåfjellet alpinsenter in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Renåfjellet alpinsenter (about 748 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 48 cm of cover (36 cm to 58 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 75 cm (66 cm–87 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 78 cm.

    By 15 December 28 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 59 %.

    Best winter on record: 1987-88, peaking at 125 cm. Worst: 1995-96, which never passed 57 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to early May (160 days).

    Questions about Renåfjellet alpinsenter

    Can you ski Renåfjellet alpinsenter at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 48 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 59 % of 46 winters. Right now: 14 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Renåfjellet alpinsenter?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 78 cm). For a sure base, late November to early May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Renåfjellet alpinsenter get?

    About 78 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-March); between 57 cm in the leanest winter (1995-96) and 125 cm in the biggest (1987-88).

    What altitude is Renåfjellet alpinsenter at?

    Between 639 m and 857 m, so 218 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Renåfjellet alpinsenter?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
