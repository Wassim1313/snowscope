    About Suomutunturi

    Neither huge nor tiny, Suomutunturi offers about 10 km of pistes (number 81 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 170 m up to 400 m.

    What there is to ski at Suomutunturi

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 9.6 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.0 km, or 52%. Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 2.3 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 230 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 4 lifts here are T-bars. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Suomutunturi today: about 25 cm up high. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 24 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Snow at Suomutunturi through a normal winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 285 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 37 cm, with 48 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 54 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 9 February, with about 61 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Most winters land between 46 cm and 70 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Suomutunturi

    When is the best time to ski at Suomutunturi?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Suomutunturi at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 37 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Suomutunturi?

    Its highest lift reaches 400 m; the lowest starts at 170 m (230 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Suomutunturi best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Suomutunturi?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
