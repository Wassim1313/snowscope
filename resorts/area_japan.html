    About 湯殿山スキー場

    Neither huge nor tiny, 湯殿山スキー場 offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 189 of 385 in Japan). Its lifts climb from 533 m to 699 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mount Gassan, 6.9 km to the south-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 4.2 km. Most of the length is red (55%, about 2.3 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    166 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 533 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
