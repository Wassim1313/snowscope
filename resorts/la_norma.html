    About La Norma

    Around 29 km of marked runs place La Norma among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 209 by length. The slopes start at 1,352 m and reach 2,723 m at the top. Valfréjus is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the south-west.

    La Norma today and the next days

    The upper slopes of La Norma hold around 132 cm of snow today. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 30 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.

    The mountain at La Norma

    1,371 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,352 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise (13 km north-east) has about 208 km of pistes against 29 km here. For higher slopes, Orelle climbs to 3,221 m.
