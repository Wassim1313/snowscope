    About La Norma

    Around 29 km of marked runs place La Norma among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 209 by length. The slopes start at 1,352 m and reach 2,723 m at the top. Valfréjus is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of La Norma

    The piste map lists 44 graded runs, about 29 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 40%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 0.3 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    La Norma today and the next days

    The upper slopes of La Norma hold around 58 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 75 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 31 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The mountain at La Norma

    1,371 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,352 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts, 5 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aiguille de Scolette / Pierre Menue at 3,506 m, standing 783 m above the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise (13 km north-east) has about 208 km of pistes against 29 km here. If snow is the priority, Orelle does better, with about 29 weeks of a good base instead of 25. For higher slopes, Orelle climbs to 3,221 m.

    How much snow La Norma usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,038 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For about 8 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 88 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 113 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 74 cm and 132 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 62 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 92 cm in the best years.

    Questions about La Norma

    What is the best month to ski at La Norma?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at La Norma at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 62 cm deep.

    How high is La Norma?

    Between 1,352 m and 2,723 m, so 1,371 m of vertical drop.

    Is La Norma suitable for families?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 16 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near La Norma?

    Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise, 13 km to the north-east, with about 208 km of pistes.
