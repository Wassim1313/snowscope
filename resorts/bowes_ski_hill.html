    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,571 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 November and keeps it until about 8 May, roughly 24 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 90 cm and 155 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 128 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 77 cm (lean years 47 cm, snowy years 105 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 102 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.
