    About Cari

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Cari sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,651 m up to 2,280 m. Prato Leventina is the next resort, 5.0 km away to the south-west.

    How much snow Cari usually gets

    At about 1,966 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 November and lasts to the end of May: about 26 weeks. Most winters land between 69 cm and 138 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 7 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm (lean years 31 cm, snowy years 86 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 106 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 113 cm, around 30 March.

    How Cari is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 629 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,651 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,280 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 2 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Scopi, 3,190 m high and 7.9 km north of the resort.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Cari has 24 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. Most of the length is blue (55%, about 6.1 km). With 55% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Cari?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Cari at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 54 cm deep.

    How high is Cari?

    Between 1,651 m and 2,280 m, so 629 m of vertical drop.

    Is Cari suitable for families?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 6.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Cari have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
