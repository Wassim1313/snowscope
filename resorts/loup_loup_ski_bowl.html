    About Loup Loup Ski Bowl

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Loup Loup Ski Bowl sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,237 m up to 1,611 m.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,424 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to late April. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 99 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 60 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 28 cm in a poor year to 97 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 56 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 57 cm to 129 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    How Loup Loup Ski Bowl is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Loup Loup Ski Bowl gives 374 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Beaver Mountain, 1,916 m high and 8.0 km north-east of the resort.

    The pistes of Loup Loup Ski Bowl

    The piste map lists 19 graded runs, about 10 km in total. The largest share is green circle, with 5.4 km (52%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Loup Loup Ski Bowl?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late April.

    How much snow does Loup Loup Ski Bowl have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 60 cm deep.

    What altitude is Loup Loup Ski Bowl at?

    The top is at 1,611 m and the base at 1,237 m.

    Is Loup Loup Ski Bowl good for beginners?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 5.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Loup Loup Ski Bowl have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
