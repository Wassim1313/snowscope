    About Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs

    Compact and local, Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,076 m to 1,223 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Baulmes, 5.2 km to the north-east.

    The pistes of Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.9 km (55%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs has roughly 7 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 28 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 5 cm at mid-mountain.

    The mountain at Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs

    The vertical drop is 147 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses reaches 1,558 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Le Suchet, 1,588 m high and 4.2 km east of the resort.

    How much snow Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs usually gets

    Count on about 1 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,150 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-February to late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 72 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 34 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 13 cm; a snowy December gave 60 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Questions about Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs

    When is the best time to ski at Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    Can you ski at Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs at?

    The top is at 1,223 m and the base at 1,076 m.

    Can beginners ski at Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs?

    Metabief, 5.9 km to the south-west, with about 26 km of pistes.
