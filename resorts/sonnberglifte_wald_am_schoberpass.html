    About Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas

    Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 723 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 851 m and 1,145 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hohentauern, 15 km to the west.

    The pistes of Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 3.8 km to ski. Most of the length is red (47%, about 1.8 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The mountain at Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas

    294 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 851 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Lachtal, for one, climbs to 2,199 m. All 3 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Zeiritzkampel, 2,125 m high and 5.5 km north-east of the resort.

    Snow at Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 998 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to early March, about 5 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 12 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 56 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 13 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 22 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 45 cm).

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Pension Leitner (guesthouse), Jagdhütte Zeiritz 1599m (mountain hut), Treglwangerhof (hotel) and Kammerlalm (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas

    When does Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas?

    The top is at 1,145 m and the base at 851 m.

    Who is Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 47% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sonnberglifte Wald am Schoberpas?

    Lachtal, 32 km to the south-west, with about 25 km of pistes.
