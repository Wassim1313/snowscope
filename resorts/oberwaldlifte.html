    About Oberwaldlifte

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Oberwaldlifte one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 790 m and 870 m above sea level. Schmiedhornlift is the next resort, 0.5 km away to the south-east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 20 km south-east to Postalm, with about 8.0 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Postalm does better, with about 22 weeks of a good base instead of 1. Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen) reaches 1,470 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Oberwaldlifte has roughly 9 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 10 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (9 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 80 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 790 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Rossfeld, reach 1,537 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all. Above the ski area rises Wieserhörndl (1,567 m), 7.3 km to the south, about 697 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Oberwaldlifte through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 830 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 February and lasts to 8 March: about 1 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 39 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 30 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 11 cm and 52 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 17 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Oberwaldlifte: common questions

    When does Oberwaldlifte have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to early March.

    How much snow does Oberwaldlifte have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 18 cm deep.

    How high is Oberwaldlifte?

    Between 790 m and 870 m, so 80 m of vertical drop.

    Is there a larger resort near Oberwaldlifte?

    Postalm, 20 km to the south-east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Oberwaldlifte have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
