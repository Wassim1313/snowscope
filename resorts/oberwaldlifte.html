    About Oberwaldlifte

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Oberwaldlifte one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 790 m and 870 m above sea level. Schmiedhornlift is the next resort, 0.5 km away to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 80 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 790 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Rossfeld, reach 1,537 m.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Pension Wald (guesthouse), Arabella Jagdhof Resort am Fuschlsee, a Tribute Portfolio Ho (hotel), Pension Schierl (hotel) and Botenwirt (guesthouse).
