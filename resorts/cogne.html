    The mountain at Cogne

    Skied top to bottom, Cogne gives 690 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,587 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Torre del Gran San Pietro at 3,692 m, standing 1,415 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,932 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 December and lasts to 24 March: about 14 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 65 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 27 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Year to year the snow is steady, with 40 cm to 85 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm; a snowy December gave 58 cm, a dry one 18 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    Cogne: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Cogne?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late March.

    How much snow does Cogne have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 34 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Cogne at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,277 m; the lowest starts at 1,587 m (690 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Cogne best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 77% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Cogne?

    Pila ski, 9.4 km to the north-west, with about 56 km of pistes.
