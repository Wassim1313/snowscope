    About Χιονοδρομικό Κέντρο Καρπενησίου

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Χιονοδρομικό Κέντρο Καρπενησίου sits in the middle of the size table of the Balkans. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,814 m up to 2,041 m. The closest other ski area is Karamanoli (38 km, north).

    How Χιονοδρομικό Κέντρο Καρπενησίου is laid out

    227 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,814 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most.
