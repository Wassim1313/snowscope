    About Furano

    Only 7 of the 385 ski areas of Japan are larger than Furano, which has some 26 km of runs. The slopes start at 235 m and reach 1,064 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Nakafurano Hokusei, 11 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Furano

    The piste map lists 55 graded runs, about 25 km in total. The largest share is green, with 14 km (54%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 829 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Because the lifts start as low as 235 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 1,064 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Furano?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,064 m; the lowest starts at 235 m (829 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Furano?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Furano have?

    About 26 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
