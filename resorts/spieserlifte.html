    About Spieserlifte

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Spieserlifte sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,068 m and 1,374 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Sonnenhanglifte (1.8 km, east).

    What there is to ski at Spieserlifte

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 4.2 km in total. The largest share is red, with 2.3 km (54%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Snow at Spieserlifte through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,221 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 7 January and keeps it until about 5 April, roughly 13 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 25 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 8 cm, snowy years 40 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 28 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Most winters land between 22 cm and 68 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 43 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February.

    From top to bottom that makes 306 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Ponten at 2,045 m, standing 671 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Spieserlifte?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Can you ski at Spieserlifte at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Spieserlifte at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,374 m; the lowest starts at 1,068 m (306 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Spieserlifte?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Spieserlifte?

    Hindelang Oberjoch, 2.4 km to the south, with about 22 km of pistes.
