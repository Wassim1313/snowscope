    About Skiareál Pyšná

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skiareál Pyšná one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 619 m and 735 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Svahová, 1.5 km to the west.

    What there is to ski at Skiareál Pyšná

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 1.5 km. Most of the length is blue (73%, about 1.1 km). With 73% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 677 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 11 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 3 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 23 cm in a big one. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 31 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Skiareál Pyšná?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    Can you ski at Skiareál Pyšná at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 3 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Skiareál Pyšná at?

    Its highest lift reaches 735 m; the lowest starts at 619 m (116 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Skiareál Pyšná?

    Yes: 73% of the graded pistes, about 1.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Skiareál Pyšná?

    Holzhau, 22 km to the north-east, with about 5.0 km of pistes.
