    The mountain at Dutch Henry Ski Hill

    Skied top to bottom, Dutch Henry Ski Hill gives 25 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 3,031 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Breckenridge, for one, climbs to 3,913 m. The only lift is a rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Prospect Mountain at 3,844 m, standing 788 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Dutch Henry Ski Hill through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 3,044 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to early May, about 23 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. For about 8 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 57 cm (lean years 27 cm, snowy years 93 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 88 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 118 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 64 cm and 146 cm.
