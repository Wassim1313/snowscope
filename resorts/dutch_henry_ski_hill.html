    What 46 winters say about Dutch Henry Ski Hill

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Dutch Henry Ski Hill (about 9,987 ft (3,044 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 22 in (57 cm), with a normal range of 16 in (40 cm)–30 in (77 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 39 in (98 cm); the middle half of winters range from 30 in (77 cm) to 48 in (121 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 46 in (118 cm).

    43 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 70 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1984-85 (83 in (210 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 1980-81 (27 in (68 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to early May, about 173 days.

    When is the best month to ski Dutch Henry Ski Hill?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 46 in (118 cm)). For a sure base, late November to early May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.
