    About Kreischberg

    Around 40 km of marked runs place Kreischberg among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 148 by length. The slopes start at 863 m and reach 2,116 m at the top. Flattnitz is the next resort, 16 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Kreischberg

    In all, 63 marked runs cover roughly 39 km. Most of the length is blue (42%, about 16 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 7.7 km of black or expert runs.

    How Kreischberg is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,253 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,063 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 3 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 4 T-bars and 2 rope tows. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Goldachnock (2,171 m), Prankerhöhe (2,166 m) and Kirbisch (2,140 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,490 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 6 January and keeps it until about 19 April, roughly 15 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 24 cm to 62 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 26 cm; a snowy December gave 41 cm, a dry one 7 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 45 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 31 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Questions about Kreischberg

    When does Kreischberg have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Kreischberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Kreischberg?

    The top is at 2,116 m and the base at 863 m.

    Is Kreischberg suitable for families?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 16 km, are easy.

    How big is Kreischberg?

    About 40 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
