    What 46 winters say about Kreischberg

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Kreischberg (about 1,490 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 26 cm of cover (14 cm to 33 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 43 cm; the middle half of winters range from 32 cm to 52 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 45 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 0 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 2 %.

    Best winter on record: 1991-92, peaking at 116 cm. Worst: 2016-17, which never passed 26 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late December to late April (119 days).

    Kreischberg: quick answers

    Is there snow in Kreischberg at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 26 cm, and only 2 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 21 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Kreischberg?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 45 cm). For a sure base, late December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Kreischberg?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 45 cm in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 116 cm (1991-92), the thinnest 26 cm (2016-17).

    How high do the lifts go at Kreischberg?

    The top is at 2,116 m and the base at 863 m.

    How big is Kreischberg?

    About 40 km of pistes and 11 lifts.

    About Kreischberg

    Around 40 km of marked runs place Kreischberg among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 148 by length. The slopes start at 863 m and reach 2,116 m at the top. Flattnitz is the next resort, 16 km away to the south.

    How Kreischberg is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,253 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,063 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.
