    About Pescasseroli

    Pescasseroli ranks number 6 of 40 ski areas in the Apennines by piste length, with about 11 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,237 m up to 1,811 m. The closest other ski area is Scanno (13 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 13 graded runs, about 11 km in total. Most of the length is red (37%, about 4.0 km). Good skiers will like it here; 33% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.4 km. 1.1 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    574 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Its 5 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    How high is Pescasseroli?

    Between 1,237 m and 1,811 m, so 574 m of vertical drop.

    Is Pescasseroli good for advanced skiers?
