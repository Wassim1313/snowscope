    About Bjørkelangen skisenter

    Bjørkelangen skisenter is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 158 m to 259 m. The closest other ski area is Marikollen skisenter (28 km, west).

    The mountain at Bjørkelangen skisenter

    The vertical drop is 101 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Elgheia at 388 m, standing 129 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 208 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 28 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 50 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 31 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Quick answers

    When does Bjørkelangen skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at Bjørkelangen skisenter at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Bjørkelangen skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 259 m; the lowest starts at 158 m (101 m of vertical).
