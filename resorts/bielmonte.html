    About Bielmonte

    With roughly 21 km of runs, Bielmonte sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,227 m to 1,620 m. Scopello / Alpe di Mera is the next resort, 8.8 km away to the north.

    What there is to ski at Bielmonte

    In all, 40 marked runs cover roughly 21 km. Most of the length is red (73%, about 15 km). Intermediate runs make up 73% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.1 km.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 8 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Bielmonte. 27 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 11 cm at mid-mountain.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 1,424 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 January and lasts to 4 May: about 16 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 39 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 19 cm and 78 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 48 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 23 cm; a snowy December gave 54 cm, a dry one 12 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 393 m: runs of a moderate length. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Monterosa Ski, reach 3,030 m. To get up there are 5 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Punta del Manzo at 2,463 m, standing 843 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Albergo Ristorante Bucaneve (hotel), Albergo La Pineta (hotel), Agriturismo Alpe Moncerchio (chalet) and Rifugio Monte Marca (guesthouse).

    Bielmonte: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Bielmonte?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early May.

    Can you ski at Bielmonte at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 23 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Bielmonte?

    Between 1,227 m and 1,620 m, so 393 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Bielmonte best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Bielmonte?

    About 21 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
