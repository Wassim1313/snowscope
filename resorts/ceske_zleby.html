    About České Žleby

    České Žleby is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 919 m to 992 m. Its nearest neighbour is Bischofsreut, 4.3 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, České Žleby gives 73 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,119 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.
