    About Bear Canyon

    About 3.0 km of pistes make Bear Canyon one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Skiing here happens between 1,677 m and 1,859 m above sea level. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Bridger Bowl, 24 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Bear Canyon has 9 graded runs adding up to about 2.9 km. The largest share is blue square, with 1.2 km (41%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Bear Canyon today: about 20 cm up high. The coming days should add about 13 cm by Friday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 13 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Bear Canyon gives 182 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,677 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mount Ellis at 2,542 m, standing 683 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 16 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,768 m) in an ordinary winter, from late December to mid-April. By mid-April the typical depth is 31 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 33 cm, with 60 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 62 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 37 cm to 88 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    Questions about Bear Canyon

    When does Bear Canyon have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Bear Canyon at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 33 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Bear Canyon?

    Between 1,677 m and 1,859 m, so 182 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Bear Canyon best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 41% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bear Canyon?

    Big Sky Resort, 53 km to the south-west, with about 236 km of pistes.
