    About La Robella

    Neither huge nor tiny, La Robella offers about 11 km of pistes (number 466 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 778 m to 1,429 m. Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses is the next resort, 4.2 km away to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    La Robella has 13 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. The largest share is blue, with 5.7 km (53%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at La Robella

    The vertical drop is 651 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 978 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 drag lift. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Le Chasseron at 1,607 m, standing 178 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,104 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 24 cm, around 18 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 61 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 45 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Questions about La Robella

    When is the best time to ski at La Robella?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Is there snow at La Robella at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 10 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is La Robella?

    The top is at 1,429 m and the base at 778 m.

    Can beginners ski at La Robella?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 6.5 km, are easy.

    How big is La Robella?

    About 11 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
