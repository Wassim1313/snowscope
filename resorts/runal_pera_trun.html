    About Runal Péra Trun

    Runal Péra Trun is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 712 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 921 m up to 1,041 m. Its nearest neighbour is Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast, 10 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Runal Péra Trun

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 3.8 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 3.8 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Runal Péra Trun

    The vertical drop is 120 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 921 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Laax, reach 2,974 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Stoc Grond at 3,422 m, standing 2,381 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 981 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. By mid-April the typical depth is 38 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 55 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 31 cm (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 45 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 35 cm to 73 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February.

    Runal Péra Trun: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Runal Péra Trun?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Runal Péra Trun at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 31 cm deep.

    What altitude is Runal Péra Trun at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,041 m; the lowest starts at 921 m (120 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Runal Péra Trun?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Runal Péra Trun?

    Obersaxen, 12 km to the east, with about 92 km of pistes.
