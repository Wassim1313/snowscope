    About Rauland skisenter

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Rauland skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 882 m up to 1,123 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Rauland skisenter, 1.3 km to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Rauland skisenter

    In all, 10 marked runs cover roughly 5.1 km. The largest share is red, with 3.7 km (72%). Intermediate runs make up 72% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 241 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The skyline is dominated by Lifjell, 1,314 m high and 8.0 km east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,002 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 75 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 106 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 127 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 86 cm and 156 cm. For about 17 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 134 cm.

    Questions about Rauland skisenter

    When does Rauland skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Rauland skisenter have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 75 cm deep.

    How high is Rauland skisenter?

    Between 882 m and 1,123 m, so 241 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Rauland skisenter best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 72% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rauland skisenter?

    Rauland skisenter, 2.2 km to the north-west, with about 11 km of pistes.
