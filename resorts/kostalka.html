    About Košťálka

    Neither huge nor tiny, Košťálka offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 171 of 257 in Central Europe). Skiing here happens between 630 m and 764 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Svoboda nad Úpou (1.0 km, south-east).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Černá Hora-Pec: about 44 km of pistes, 4.6 km to the north-west. vlek Kladenka has the longer snow season: around 8 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Košťálka. For higher slopes, Černá Hora-Pec climbs to 1,280 m.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Košťálka today: about 25 cm up high. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 6 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 134 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 630 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    How much snow Košťálka usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 697 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 22 February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 49 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Košťálka: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Košťálka?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Can you ski at Košťálka at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Košťálka?

    The top is at 764 m and the base at 630 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Košťálka?

    Černá Hora-Pec, 4.6 km to the north-west, with about 44 km of pistes.

    How big is Košťálka?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
