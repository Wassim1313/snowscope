    About Ancelle

    With some 23 km of runs, Ancelle belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,323 m to 1,798 m. Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes is the next resort, 2.8 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Ancelle

    The piste map lists 58 graded runs, about 23 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 51%. With 67% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,560 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 82 cm, around 15 February. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm; a snowy December gave 70 cm, a dry one 14 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 64 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 41 cm and 110 cm.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 28 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Ancelle. The last seven days brought 51 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 21 cm by Tuesday. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 25 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    How Ancelle is laid out

    475 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,323 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Orcières Merlette reaches 2,731 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 11 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 14 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Grande Autane (2,782 m), 6.1 km to the north-east, about 984 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Le Chamois (hotel), Hotel Le Torrent (hotel), Gîte l'Establa (chalet) and Les Fauvettes (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When does Ancelle have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Ancelle have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 42 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Ancelle?

    The top is at 1,798 m and the base at 1,323 m.

    Is Ancelle suitable for families?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ancelle have?

    About 23 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
