    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 26 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 145 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Jihua Changshoushan, reach 451 m.
