    About Ski Cooper

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski Cooper offers about 32 km of pistes (number 74 of 175 in US West). Skiing here happens between 3,226 m and 3,579 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Dutch Henry Ski Hill (13 km, south).

    Ski Cooper and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Vail: about 215 km of pistes, 31 km to the north. For higher slopes, Breckenridge climbs to 3,913 m.

    How Ski Cooper is laid out

    The vertical drop is 353 m: runs of a moderate length. A high base at 3,226 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Ski Cooper hold around 41 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.
