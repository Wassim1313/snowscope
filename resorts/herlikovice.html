    About Herlíkovice

    With some 10 km of runs, Herlíkovice belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 535 m up to 1,005 m. Lyžařský vlek Popelka is the next resort, 1.3 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Herlíkovice

    Counting every graded piste, there are 25 runs and around 9.8 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 4.2 km (43%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at Herlíkovice

    From top to bottom that makes 470 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 535 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,005 m. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 3 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    How much snow Herlíkovice usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 770 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 31 January and lasts to 4 March: about 5 weeks. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 60 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 49 cm, a dry one 2 cm. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 23 February.

    Questions about Herlíkovice

    When does Herlíkovice have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Herlíkovice have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Herlíkovice at?

    Between 535 m and 1,005 m, so 470 m of vertical drop.

    Is Herlíkovice suitable for families?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 5.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Herlíkovice?

    About 10 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
