    About Magic

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Magic sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,055 m up to 2,244 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 35 graded runs, about 8.8 km in total. The largest share is black diamond, with 3.6 km (41%). Good skiers will like it here; 41% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 3.6 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 189 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,055 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Trapper Peak, 2,400 m high and 7.3 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 41 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Magic. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 14 cm at mid-mountain).

    Snow at Magic through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,150 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 December and keeps it until about 9 April, roughly 16 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 10 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 91 cm. Most winters land between 21 cm and 130 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 40 cm, with 91 cm in the best years.

    Magic: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Magic?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early April.

    How much snow does Magic have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 40 cm deep.

    How high is Magic?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,244 m; the lowest starts at 2,055 m (189 m of vertical).

    Is Magic good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 3.6 km of its graded pistes (41%) are difficult.

    How big is Magic?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
