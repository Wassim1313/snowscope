    About Burladingen

    Burladingen is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 743 m to 897 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bitz, 3.6 km to the south.

    The pistes of Burladingen

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.2 km (100%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 154 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 743 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Burg (976 m), Braunhardsberg (969 m) and Raichberg (956 m).

    How much snow Burladingen usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 820 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 21 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 12 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. A normal season tops out near 5 cm, around 22 January.

    Burladingen: common questions

    When does Burladingen have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Burladingen at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    How high is Burladingen?

    Between 743 m and 897 m, so 154 m of vertical drop.

    Is Burladingen good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Burladingen have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
