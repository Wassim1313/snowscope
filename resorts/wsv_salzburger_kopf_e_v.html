    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 26 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 625 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.
