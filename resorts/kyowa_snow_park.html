    About Kyowa Snow Park

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Kyowa Snow Park sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 179 m up to 440 m. Its nearest neighbour is Taiheizan Opus, 19 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 10 marked runs cover roughly 5.4 km. The largest share is green, with 3.6 km (66%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 261 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 179 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
