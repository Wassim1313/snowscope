    What 46 winters say about San Pellegrino

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at San Pellegrino (1,864 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 29 cm; half of the winters sit between 15 cm and 44 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 44 cm (34 cm–63 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 51 cm.

    11 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 20 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 2013-14 (155 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2016-17 (32 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early December to mid-April (123 days).

    Common questions

    Is there snow in San Pellegrino at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 29 cm, and only 20 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 31 cm.

    When is the best month to ski San Pellegrino?

    February: the snow peaks at about 51 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to mid-April.

    How snowy is San Pellegrino?

    About 51 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 32 cm in the leanest winter (2016-17) and 155 cm in the biggest (2013-14).

    What altitude is San Pellegrino at?

    Between 1,216 m and 2,513 m, so 1,297 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does San Pellegrino have?

    About 53 km of pistes and 18 lifts.

    About San Pellegrino

    With some 53 km of runs, San Pellegrino belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,216 m and 2,513 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Passo Rolle, 7.3 km to the south.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, San Pellegrino gives 1,297 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,513 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    How San Pellegrino compares nearby

    In this group of 7, San Pellegrino has the most pistes. The highest nearby is Marmolada, topping out at 3,235 m.
