    About Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte

    Neither huge nor tiny, Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 536 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 974 m to 1,315 m. The closest other ski area is Spieserlifte (7.2 km, south-east).

    What there is to ski at Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte

    Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte has 8 graded runs adding up to about 7.7 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 7.7 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 341 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 974 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 4 lifts break down into 3 T-bars and 1 drag lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Übelhorn at 1,738 m, standing 423 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,144 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 3 February and keeps it until about 18 April, roughly 6 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. A normal season tops out near 35 cm, around 26 February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 16 cm to 58 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 35 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 30 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,315 m; the lowest starts at 974 m (341 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 7.7 km, are easy.

    How big is Adelharz- und Breitensteinlifte?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
