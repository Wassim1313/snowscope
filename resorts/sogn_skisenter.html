    About Sogn skisenter

    Sogn skisenter is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 13 km of pistes put it at number 55 of 262. Skiing here happens between 322 m and 990 m above sea level. Sogndal skisenter is the next resort, 13 km away to the west.

    The pistes of Sogn skisenter

    The piste map lists 11 graded runs, about 13 km in total. Most of the length is black (51%, about 6.6 km). With 51% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 6.6 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 656 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 31 October and lasts to the end of May: about 31 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 226 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 140 cm, with 193 cm in the best years. For about 25 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 130 cm and 294 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 27 March, with about 246 cm.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Sogn skisenter has roughly 27 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 91 cm by now.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 668 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Krokberg, 1,356 m high and 5.8 km north-west of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Sogn skisenter include Eikum Hotel (hotel), Hafslo gjestehus (guesthouse), Walaker Hotell (hotel) and Villa Solvorn (guesthouse).

    Sogn skisenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Sogn skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    Can you ski at Sogn skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 140 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Sogn skisenter at?

    The top is at 990 m and the base at 322 m.

    Does Sogn skisenter have steep runs?

    Yes: about 6.6 km of its graded pistes (51%) are difficult.

    How big is Sogn skisenter?

    About 13 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
