    About Olstalb-Lifte Aalen

    Olstalb-Lifte Aalen is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 115 of 257 in Central Europe by length. The slopes start at 524 m and reach 663 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Hirtenteich (7.4 km, south-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Olstalb-Lifte Aalen has 2 graded runs adding up to about 1.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.9 km (59%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Olstalb-Lifte Aalen through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 594 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 1 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 13 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late December and late January. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 5 cm, around 23 January. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 19 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 139 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 524 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Olstalb-Lifte Aalen have the most snow?

    Aim for late December and late January.

    Is there snow at Olstalb-Lifte Aalen at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 1 cm).

    How high is Olstalb-Lifte Aalen?

    Between 524 m and 663 m, so 139 m of vertical drop.

    Is Olstalb-Lifte Aalen good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Olstalb-Lifte Aalen?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
