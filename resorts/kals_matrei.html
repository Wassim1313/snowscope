    About Kals-Matrei

    With some 50 km of runs, Kals-Matrei belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 928 m and reach 2,422 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Virgen, 12 km to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 87 runs and around 49 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 22 km (44%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. The steepest pistes add up to about 9.9 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Kals-Matrei gives 1,494 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 928 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 1 funicular, 3 gondolas, 5 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts and 3 moving carpets, 16 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Muntanitz at 3,232 m, standing 810 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Kals-Matrei usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,675 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 10 December and lasts to 5 May: about 21 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 37 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 41 cm and 91 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 67 cm, around 19 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 37 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 19 cm in a poor year to 62 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Kals-Matrei

    When is the best time to ski at Kals-Matrei?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Kals-Matrei have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 37 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Kals-Matrei?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026.

    What altitude is Kals-Matrei at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,422 m; the lowest starts at 928 m (1,494 m of vertical).

    Is Kals-Matrei good for beginners?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 22 km, are easy.
