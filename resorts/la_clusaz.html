    About La Clusaz

    Around 96 km of marked runs place La Clusaz among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 51 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,030 m and 2,476 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Le Grand-Bornand (9.0 km, north).

    The mountain at La Clusaz

    1,446 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 cable car, 4 gondolas, 2 chair-gondolas, 14 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 24 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 3 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises La Grande Balmaz (2,616 m), 4.9 km to the east, about 140 m higher than the top lift.

    What there is to ski at La Clusaz

    The piste map lists 147 graded runs, about 96 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 47 km (49%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow La Clusaz usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,753 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 29 November and lasts to 26 May: about 26 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 155 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For about 17 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 80 cm, with 132 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 100 cm and 217 cm. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 181 cm.

    Questions about La Clusaz

    When is the best time to ski at La Clusaz?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at La Clusaz at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 80 cm deep.

    When does La Clusaz open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 25 April 2027.

    How high is La Clusaz?

    The top is at 2,476 m and the base at 1,030 m.

    Is La Clusaz good for beginners?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 55 km, are easy.
