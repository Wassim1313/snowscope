    About Courchevel

    Around 151 km of marked runs place Courchevel among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 28 by length. The slopes start at 1,258 m and reach 2,695 m at the top. Méribel is the next resort, 5.3 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of Courchevel

    Counting every graded piste, there are 288 runs and around 150 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 54 km (36%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 2.0 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Courchevel gives 1,437 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Les Trois Vallées, for one, climbs to 3,221 m. The lift system counts 59 lifts: 2 cable cars, 2 funiculars, 8 gondolas, 17 chairlifts, 17 button lifts, 2 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 10 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 10 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aiguille du Fruit at 3,048 m, standing 353 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Courchevel usually gets

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,976 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 69 cm; a snowy December gave 106 cm, a dry one 39 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 86 cm and 163 cm. A normal season tops out near 139 cm, around 7 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 128 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and early April.

    Courchevel: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Courchevel?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Courchevel have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 69 cm deep.

    When does Courchevel open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 18 April 2027.

    What altitude is Courchevel at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,695 m; the lowest starts at 1,258 m (1,437 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Courchevel?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 86 km, are easy.
