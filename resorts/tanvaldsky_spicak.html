    About Tanvaldský Špičák

    Tanvaldský Špičák is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 6.0 km of pistes put it at number 48 of 257. The ski area spans the mountain from 539 m up to 807 m. The closest other ski area is Jizerky (0.6 km, north-west).

    How Tanvaldský Špičák is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Tanvaldský Špičák gives 268 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 539 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 807 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Tanvaldský Špičák hold around 3 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 6 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.
