    Snow at Kingvale through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,144 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 8 December and keeps it until about 5 May, roughly 21 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 12 weeks. A normal season tops out near 158 cm, around 17 March. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 50 cm to 244 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 69 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 156 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 87 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Questions about Kingvale

    When is the best time to ski at Kingvale?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Kingvale have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 69 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
