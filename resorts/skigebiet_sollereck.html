    Söllereck in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Söllereck (1,200 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 28 cm, with a normal range of 15 cm–40 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 46 cm (35 cm–57 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 47 cm.

    4 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 9 % by 1 January.

    1998-99 was the big one (103 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (29 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late December to mid-April (111 days).

    So far this winter: 12 cm on 26 November (usual: 10 cm).

    Questions about Söllereck

    Is there snow in Söllereck at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 28 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 9 % of 46 winters. Right now: 30 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Söllereck?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 47 cm). For a sure base, late December to mid-April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Söllereck?

    About 47 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 29 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 103 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    What altitude is Söllereck at?

    Between 955 m and 1,445 m, so 490 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Söllereck?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 7.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Söllereck?

    About Söllereck

    Neither huge nor tiny, Söllereck offers about 11 km of pistes (number 476 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 955 m up to 1,445 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf, 3.5 km to the south-west.

    The pistes of Söllereck

    Söllereck has 20 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. Most of the length is blue (64%, about 7.2 km). With 64% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 30 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Söllereck. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 13 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The mountain at Söllereck

    490 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The base at 955 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 11 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars, 3 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hochgehrenspitze at 2,251 m, standing 806 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 14 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,200 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to early April. By mid-April the typical depth is 28 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 47 cm, around 2 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 26 cm and 72 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 28 cm; a snowy December gave 48 cm, a dry one 11 cm.
