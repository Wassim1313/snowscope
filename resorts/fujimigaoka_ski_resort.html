    About Fujimigaoka

    Fujimigaoka is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 70 m and 118 m above sea level. Kushukohan is the next resort, 14 km away to the north.

    The mountain at Fujimigaoka

    Skied top to bottom, Fujimigaoka gives 48 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 70 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
