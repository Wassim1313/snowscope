    About Prali

    Neither huge nor tiny, Prali offers about 12 km of pistes (number 443 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,477 m and 2,488 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Prali (1.2 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 12 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.7 km, or 41%. Good skiers will like it here; 36% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 4.2 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,011 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,477 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 4 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 2 drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gran Queyron / Grand Queyron at 3,060 m, standing 572 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Prali usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,982 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 12 December and keeps it until about 30 April, roughly 20 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 52 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 85 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 37 cm, from 14 cm in a poor year to 85 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 32 cm and 114 cm.

    Prali: common questions

    When does Prali have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Prali at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 37 cm deep.

    What altitude is Prali at?

    Between 1,477 m and 2,488 m, so 1,011 m of vertical drop.

    Does Prali have steep runs?

    Yes: about 4.2 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How big is Prali?

    About 12 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
