    About Feistritzsattel

    Compact and local, Feistritzsattel has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,118 m and reach 1,282 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Arabichl, 4.2 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,200 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 13 January and keeps it until about 27 February, roughly 7 weeks. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 77 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 39 cm, around 15 February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 15 cm; a snowy December gave 44 cm, a dry one 3 cm.

    Runs by difficulty

    Feistritzsattel has 2 graded runs adding up to about 1.1 km. Most of the length is red (100%, about 1.1 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Feistritzsattel and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Stuhleck: about 22 km of pistes, 6.6 km to the west. Arabichl has the longer snow season: around 10 weeks of solid cover, compared with 7 at Feistritzsattel. The highest nearby is Stuhleck, topping out at 1,767 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    164 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Stuhleck (1,782 m), 4.7 km to the west, about 500 m higher than the top lift.

    Feistritzsattel: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Feistritzsattel?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late February.

    Can you ski at Feistritzsattel at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 15 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Feistritzsattel?

    The top is at 1,282 m and the base at 1,118 m.

    Who is Feistritzsattel best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Feistritzsattel?

    Stuhleck, 6.6 km to the west, with about 22 km of pistes.
