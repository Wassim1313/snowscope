    About Hidaka Kokusai

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hidaka Kokusai offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 138 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 253 m up to 749 m. The closest other ski area is Kokusetsu Shimukappu Central (13 km, north).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    496 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 253 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Hidaka Kokusai today: about 22 cm up high. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 12 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.
