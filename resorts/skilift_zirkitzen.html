    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 55 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,125 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Mölltaler Gletscher reaches 3,084 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Mulleter Seichenkopf, 2,918 m high and 6.7 km north-west of the resort.

    Snow at Skilift Zirkitzen through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 952 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the typical depth is 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 11 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 27 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 6 cm to 34 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 17 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Skilift Zirkitzen?

    Is there snow at Skilift Zirkitzen at Christmas?

    What altitude is Skilift Zirkitzen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 980 m; the lowest starts at 925 m (55 m of vertical).
