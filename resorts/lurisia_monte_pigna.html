    About Lurisia Monte Pigna

    Neither huge nor tiny, Lurisia Monte Pigna offers about 16 km of pistes (number 368 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 830 m and reach 1,754 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Artesina (5.0 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 34 graded runs, about 16 km in total. Most of the length is red (71%, about 12 km). Intermediate runs make up 71% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 2.6 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,292 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 53 cm. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 18 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 35 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    How Lurisia Monte Pigna is laid out

    The vertical drop is 924 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 830 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Monte Mondolé (2,382 m), 7.0 km to the south-east, about 628 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Lurisia Monte Pigna include Everest (hotel), Residence Le Fonti (hotel), Topazio (hotel) and Il Ciliegio di Lurisia (guesthouse).

    Questions about Lurisia Monte Pigna

    When is the best time to ski at Lurisia Monte Pigna?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Is there snow at Lurisia Monte Pigna at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Lurisia Monte Pigna?

    The top is at 1,754 m and the base at 830 m.

    Who is Lurisia Monte Pigna best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 71% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Lurisia Monte Pigna?

    Riserva Bianca, 16 km to the south-west, with about 42 km of pistes.
