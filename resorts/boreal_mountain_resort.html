    Boreal in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Boreal (about 7,434 ft (2,266 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 32 in (82 cm), with a normal range of 15 in (39 cm)–54 in (138 cm).

    Mid-February typically sees 64 in (162 cm), between 44 in (111 cm) and 86 in (218 cm) in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 78 in (199 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    61 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 78 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1982-83 (168 in (427 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2014-15 (33 in (83 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 174 days.

    Boreal: quick answers

    Can you ski Boreal at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 32 in (82 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 78 % of 46 winters. Right now: 36 in (92 cm).

    What is the best time to ski Boreal?

    March: the snow peaks at about 78 in (199 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How snowy is Boreal?

    About 78 in (199 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (mid-March); between 33 in (83 cm) in the leanest winter (2014-15) and 168 in (427 cm) in the biggest (1982-83).

    How high do the lifts go at Boreal?

    The top is at 2,347 m and the base at 2,186 m.

    How many km of pistes does Boreal have?

    About 14 km of pistes and 7 lifts.

    About Boreal

    Neither huge nor tiny, Boreal offers about 14 km of pistes (number 116 of 175 in US West). Skiing here happens between 2,186 m and 2,347 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Donner Ski Ranch (1.5 km, south-east).
