    About Saint-Cergue

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Saint-Cergue sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,036 m and reach 1,185 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Trélasse, 3.8 km to the west.

    Snow at Saint-Cergue through a normal winter

    At about 1,110 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 January and lasts to 10 March: about 8 weeks. A normal season tops out near 47 cm, around 17 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 119 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 19 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 89 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    The pistes of Saint-Cergue

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 2.4 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 1.8 km (75%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Saint-Cergue

    From top to bottom that makes 149 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 5 lifts break down into 3 button lifts and 2 rope tows. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is La Dôle at 1,678 m, standing 493 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Saint-Cergue: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Saint-Cergue?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    Can you ski at Saint-Cergue at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 19 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Saint-Cergue at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,185 m; the lowest starts at 1,036 m (149 m of vertical).

    Is Saint-Cergue suitable for families?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Saint-Cergue?

    Les Rousses, 7.9 km to the north-west, with about 32 km of pistes.
