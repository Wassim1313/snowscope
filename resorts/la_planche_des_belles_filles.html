    About La Planche-des-Belles-Filles

    La Planche-des-Belles-Filles is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 766 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 983 m and reach 1,147 m at the top. Ballon d'Alsace is the next resort, 6.9 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of La Planche-des-Belles-Filles

    La Planche-des-Belles-Filles has 7 graded runs adding up to about 2.8 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.1 km, or 41%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, La Planche-des-Belles-Filles gives 164 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 983 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 3 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Ballon d'Alsace at 1,248 m, standing 101 m above the top lift.

    How much snow La Planche-des-Belles-Filles usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,065 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm, with 65 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 29 January, with about 18 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 96 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    La Planche-des-Belles-Filles: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at La Planche-des-Belles-Filles?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Is there snow at La Planche-des-Belles-Filles at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 6 cm).

    What altitude is La Planche-des-Belles-Filles at?

    The top is at 1,147 m and the base at 983 m.

    Can beginners ski at La Planche-des-Belles-Filles?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 1.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does La Planche-des-Belles-Filles have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
