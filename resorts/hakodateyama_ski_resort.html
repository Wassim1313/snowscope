    About Hakodateyama

    Hakodateyama is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 240 of 385 in Japan by length. Skiing here happens between 551 m and 684 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Makino Kogen, 7.6 km to the north-east.

    Other resorts close by

    What there is to ski at Hakodateyama

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 2.8 km in total. Most of the length is red (43%, about 1.2 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 32% of the length is graded difficult.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 133 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 551 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 4 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up.

    What altitude is Hakodateyama at?

    The top is at 684 m and the base at 551 m.

    Does Hakodateyama have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.9 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Hakodateyama?

    Biwako Valley, 26 km to the south, with about 6.0 km of pistes.
