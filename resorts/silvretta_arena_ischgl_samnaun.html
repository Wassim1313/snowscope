    Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun (2,110 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 74 cm, with a normal range of 54 cm–87 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 138 cm; the middle half of winters range from 116 cm to 165 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 155 cm.

    By 15 December 59 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 87 %.

    Best winter on record: 1998-99, peaking at 280 cm. Worst: 2024-25, which never passed 107 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-June, about 213 days.

    Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun: quick answers

    Does Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 74 cm at Christmas, and 87 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 79 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun?

    March: the snow peaks at about 155 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-June.

    How snowy is Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun?

    About Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun

    Around 163 km of marked runs place Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 24 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,352 m up to 2,868 m. The closest other ski area is Kappl (11 km, north).

    How Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun is laid out

    1,516 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,352 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,868 m.
