    About Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun

    Around 163 km of marked runs place Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 24 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,352 m up to 2,868 m. The closest other ski area is Kappl (11 km, north).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 271 runs and around 162 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 83 km, or 51%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 27 km. 9.1 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun is laid out

    1,516 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,352 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,868 m. Its 46 lifts break down into 3 cable cars, 6 gondolas, 25 chairlifts, 6 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 3 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Piz Rots (3,097 m), 5.5 km to the south, about 229 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun usually gets

    At about 2,110 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 74 cm, from 40 cm in a poor year to 103 cm in a big one. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 93 cm to 184 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 133 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 22 March, with about 155 cm.

    Questions about Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun

    When does Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 74 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun?

    The announced opening day is 26 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 2 May 2027.

    What altitude is Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun at?

    Between 1,352 m and 2,868 m, so 1,516 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Silvretta Arena Ischgl/Samnaun best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 51% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
