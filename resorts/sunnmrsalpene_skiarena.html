    About Sunnmørsalpene

    Sunnmørsalpene is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 152 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 356 m up to 872 m. The closest other ski area is Sunnmørsalpane Fjellseter (0.2 km, north).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 5 runs and around 3.8 km to ski. Most of the length is green (beginner) (57%, about 2.2 km). With 57% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 614 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early November to late May or later, about 30 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 130 cm to 281 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 128 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 83 cm, snowy years 183 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 24 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 234 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 239 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 516 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Ystevasshornet at 1,330 m, standing 458 m above the top lift.

    Sunnmørsalpene: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Sunnmørsalpene?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Sunnmørsalpene have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 128 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Sunnmørsalpene?

    Between 356 m and 872 m, so 516 m of vertical drop.

    Is Sunnmørsalpene suitable for families?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Sunnmørsalpene have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
