    About Cochran's

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Cochran's one of the smaller places to ski in US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 117 m to 215 m. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Camp Ethan Allen, 9.8 km to the north.

    The pistes of Cochran's

    Cochran's has 8 graded runs adding up to about 2.1 km. Most of the length is blue square (40%, about 0.8 km). Good skiers will like it here; 37% of the runs are steep.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    98 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 117 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sugarbush Resort, for one, climbs to 1,238 m. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows. Above the ski area rises Robbins Mountain (624 m), 5.0 km to the south-east, about 409 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 166 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; a snowy December gave 31 cm, a dry one 3 cm. A normal season tops out near 27 cm, around 13 February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 8 cm to 52 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Cochran's?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Is there snow at Cochran's at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Cochran's?

    The top is at 215 m and the base at 117 m.

    Is Cochran's good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 0.8 km of its graded pistes (37%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Cochran's?

    Stowe, 22 km to the north-east, with about 67 km of pistes.
