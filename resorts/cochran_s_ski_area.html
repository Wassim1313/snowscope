    About Cochran's

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Cochran's one of the smaller places to ski in US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 117 m to 215 m. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Camp Ethan Allen, 9.8 km to the north.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 22 km north-east to Stowe, with about 67 km of runs. The highest nearby is Stowe, topping out at 1,112 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    98 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 117 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sugarbush Resort, for one, climbs to 1,238 m.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Gale's Retreat (chalet).

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Cochran's?

    The top is at 215 m and the base at 117 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Cochran's?

    Stowe, 22 km to the north-east, with about 67 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Cochran's have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
