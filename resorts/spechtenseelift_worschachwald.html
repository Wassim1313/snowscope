    About Spechtenseelift

    Spechtenseelift is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,099 m and reach 1,259 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf, 7.9 km to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 1.8 km. The largest share is red, with 1.8 km (100%). Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How much snow Spechtenseelift usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,179 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 65 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 33 cm, with 63 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 36 cm and 95 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 40 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    The snow right now

    How Spechtenseelift is laid out

    The vertical drop is 160 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp, reach 2,072 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grimming at 2,351 m, standing 1,092 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Spechtenseelift include Wörschachwalderhof (guesthouse), Spechtenseehütte (mountain hut), Landgasthof Moessner (hotel) and Der Hechl (hotel).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Spechtenseelift?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Spechtenseelift at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 33 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Spechtenseelift?

    Between 1,099 m and 1,259 m, so 160 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Spechtenseelift best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Spechtenseelift?

    Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf, 7.9 km to the west, with about 35 km of pistes.
