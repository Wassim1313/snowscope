    About Jungfraujoch Snow Fun Park

    Jungfraujoch Snow Fun Park has about 0.5 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 3,439 m up to 3,461 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Grindelwald, 5.9 km to the north.

    How Jungfraujoch Snow Fun Park is laid out

    The vertical drop is 22 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 3,439 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 3,461 m. All 2 lifts here are moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Jungfrau (4,158 m), 2.3 km to the south-west, about 697 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Jungfraujoch Snow Fun Park through a normal winter

    Count on about 30 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 3,450 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early November to late May or later. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 177 cm and 336 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 26 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 179 cm; a snowy December gave 268 cm, a dry one 106 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-April at roughly 394 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-April and late April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 394 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Mönchsjochhütte (mountain hut), Guggihütte (mountain hut), Berglihütte (mountain hut) and Desalpes (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Jungfraujoch Snow Fun Park have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-April and late April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Jungfraujoch Snow Fun Park have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 179 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Jungfraujoch Snow Fun Park at?

    Between 3,439 m and 3,461 m, so 22 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Jungfraujoch Snow Fun Park have?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
