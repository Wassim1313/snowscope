    About Canyon (Ski Red Deer)

    Canyon (Ski Red Deer) is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 34 of 53 in Canada West by length. Skiing here happens between 816 m and 969 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Innisfail (36 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 30 marked runs cover roughly 9.5 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 4.4 km, or 46%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 2.5 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Canyon (Ski Red Deer) gives 153 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 816 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.

    Other resorts close by

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 892 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 23 cm, around 11 February. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and late January. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 5 cm to 41 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 15 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 32 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Canyon (Ski Red Deer)?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    How much snow does Canyon (Ski Red Deer) have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 15 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Canyon (Ski Red Deer) open for the 2026/27 season?

    How high is Canyon (Ski Red Deer)?

    Its highest lift reaches 969 m; the lowest starts at 816 m (153 m of vertical).

    Does Canyon (Ski Red Deer) have steep runs?

    Yes: about 2.5 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.
