    About Vrådal Panorama

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Vrådal Panorama sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 267 m and 724 m above sea level. There are 5 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Morgedal, 19 km to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 6.6 km in total. The largest share is green (beginner), with 3.1 km (47%). With 93% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Vrådal Panorama usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 496 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 December and lasts to 9 May: about 23 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 66 cm and 137 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 47 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 80 cm, a dry one 23 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 90 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is usually deepest around 28 March, with about 104 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 5 weeks.

    The mountain at Vrådal Panorama

    457 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 4 drag lifts and 3 moving carpets, 8 lifts in all. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Sveinsheii at 1,056 m, standing 332 m above the top lift.

    Vrådal Panorama: common questions

    When does Vrådal Panorama have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Vrådal Panorama at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 47 cm deep.

    What altitude is Vrådal Panorama at?

    Its highest lift reaches 724 m; the lowest starts at 267 m (457 m of vertical).

    Is Vrådal Panorama suitable for families?

    Yes: 93% of the graded pistes, about 6.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Vrådal Panorama?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
