    About Karwendel Bergbahn

    Karwendel Bergbahn is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 516 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 961 m to 1,491 m. The closest other ski area is Planberg- & Wiesenlifte Pertisau (0.6 km, north-east).

    How Karwendel Bergbahn is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Karwendel Bergbahn gives 530 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 961 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.

    Karwendel Bergbahn today and the next days

    Snow on the ground at Karwendel Bergbahn today: about 26 cm up high. The last seven days brought 15 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Friday.
