    About Karwendel Bergbahn

    Karwendel Bergbahn is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 516 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 961 m to 1,491 m. The closest other ski area is Planberg- & Wiesenlifte Pertisau (0.6 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Karwendel Bergbahn

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 8.6 km. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 4.5 km). With 53% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Karwendel Bergbahn is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Karwendel Bergbahn gives 530 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 961 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Sonnjoch (2,458 m), 7.2 km to the west, about 967 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,226 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to early April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 25 cm and 59 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 28 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is usually deepest around 27 February, with about 44 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 22 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 9 cm in a poor year to 37 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Karwendel Bergbahn?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    Can you ski at Karwendel Bergbahn at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Karwendel Bergbahn?

    Between 961 m and 1,491 m, so 530 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Karwendel Bergbahn?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 4.5 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Karwendel Bergbahn have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
