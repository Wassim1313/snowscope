    About Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh

    Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 789 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,166 m and 1,219 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Seefeld (1.8 km, south).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 5 runs and around 2.4 km to ski. The largest share is green (beginner), with 2.4 km (100%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh today: about 8 cm up high. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 17 cm by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (10 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh gives 53 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Karwendelbahn, reach 2,246 m. Getting up the mountain: 3 T-bars and 6 moving carpets, 9 lifts in all. 6 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hohe Munde Ostgipfel at 2,592 m, standing 1,373 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 12 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,192 m) in an ordinary winter, from early January to late March. A normal season tops out near 41 cm, around 15 February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 24 cm, with 40 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 28 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 27 cm and 58 cm.

    Questions about Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh

    When does Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Can you ski at Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 24 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,219 m; the lowest starts at 1,166 m (53 m of vertical).

    Is Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
