    About Tendo Kogen

    Tendo Kogen is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 222 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 599 m up to 676 m. The closest other ski area is Kurobushi Kogen Snow Park Jangle (11 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 3.5 km to ski. The largest share is green, with 3.0 km (84%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    77 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 599 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Zao Onsen, reach 1,661 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Is Tendo Kogen good for beginners?

    Which bigger ski area is near Tendo Kogen?

    How many km of pistes does Tendo Kogen have?
