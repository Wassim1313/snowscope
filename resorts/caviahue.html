    About Caviahue

    Caviahue is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 13 of 30 in Andes by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,683 m up to 2,111 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 17 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 12 km (71%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 428 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,683 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 11 lifts: 4 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 5 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
