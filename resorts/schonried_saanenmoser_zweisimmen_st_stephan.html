    About Schönried

    Schönried is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 59 km of pistes put it at number 98 of 1,056. The slopes start at 944 m and reach 2,002 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Gstaad, 5.8 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 71 marked runs cover roughly 59 km. The largest share is blue, with 37 km (62%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,058 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,144 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 16 lifts break down into 4 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 4 T-bars and 1 button lift. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Giferspitz (2,542 m), 6.9 km to the south, about 540 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,473 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 69 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 36 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 57 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 50 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 40 cm to 93 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    When does Schönried have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Schönried at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 36 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Schönried open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 20 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Schönried?

    Between 944 m and 2,002 m, so 1,058 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Schönried?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 37 km, are easy.
