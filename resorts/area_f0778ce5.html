    About Полазна

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Полазна sits in the middle of the size table of Russia. Its lifts climb from 116 m to 225 m. Ельники is the next resort, 17 km away to the south.

    The mountain at Полазна

    From top to bottom that makes 109 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 225 m is the highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km.
