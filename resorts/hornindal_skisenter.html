    About Hornindal skisenter

    Hornindal skisenter is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 121 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The slopes start at 391 m and reach 485 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hornindal, 0.1 km to the south.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Ørsta skisenter (22 km north-west) has about 10 km of pistes against 5.0 km here. For more reliable snow, Breimsbygda Skisenter usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 32 weeks, against 29 here. Stryn Vinterski reaches 974 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 438 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 29 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 211 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 224 cm, around 23 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 112 cm, with 158 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and mid-April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 23 weeks. Most winters land between 114 cm and 266 cm by mid-February.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Hornindal skisenter hold around 26 cm of snow today. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 19 cm by Tuesday. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 68 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    The mountain at Hornindal skisenter

    Skied top to bottom, Hornindal skisenter gives 94 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Stryn Vinterski reaches 974 m. The highest named summit within 8 km is Storhornet at 1,600 m, standing 1,115 m above the top lift.

    Hornindal skisenter: common questions

    When does Hornindal skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Hornindal skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 112 cm deep.

    What altitude is Hornindal skisenter at?

    The top is at 485 m and the base at 391 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hornindal skisenter?

    Ørsta skisenter, 22 km to the north-west, with about 10 km of pistes.

    How big is Hornindal skisenter?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
