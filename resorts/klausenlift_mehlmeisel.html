    About Klausenlift Mehlmeisel

    Neither huge nor tiny, Klausenlift Mehlmeisel offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 170 of 257 in Central Europe). Skiing here happens between 666 m and 801 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Skischaukel Hempelsberg und Geie, 4.8 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Klausenlift Mehlmeisel

    The vertical drop is 135 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 666 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 801 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Snow at Klausenlift Mehlmeisel through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 734 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 38 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; a snowy December gave 23 cm, a dry one 0 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 30 January, with about 13 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and early January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.
