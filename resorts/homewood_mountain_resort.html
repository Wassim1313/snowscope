    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,080 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 December and lasts to 5 May: about 22 weeks. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 134 cm. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 62 cm (lean years 16 cm, snowy years 129 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 78 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 57 cm and 199 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Homewood?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Homewood at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 62 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
