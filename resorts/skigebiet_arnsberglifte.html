    About Arnsberglifte

    Arnsberglifte is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 14 km of pistes put it at number 11 of 257. The ski area spans the mountain from 588 m up to 834 m. The closest other ski area is Skilifte Kreuzberg (2.6 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. The largest share is blue, with 6.1 km (57%). With 57% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    246 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 588 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 4 lifts break down into 3 drag lifts and 1 rope tow. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Arnsberglifte: common questions

    How high is Arnsberglifte?

    Its highest lift reaches 834 m; the lowest starts at 588 m (246 m of vertical).

    Is Arnsberglifte good for beginners?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 6.1 km, are easy.

    How big is Arnsberglifte?

    About 14 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
