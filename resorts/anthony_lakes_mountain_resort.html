    About Anthony Lakes

    Neither huge nor tiny, Anthony Lakes offers about 13 km of pistes (number 117 of 175 in US West). Skiing here happens between 2,174 m and 2,418 m above sea level.

    The mountain at Anthony Lakes

    244 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,174 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 chairlift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Twin Mountain, 2,712 m high and 7.0 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,296 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 25 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and late February. For about 17 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 77 cm to 172 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 74 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 126 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 29 March, with about 167 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 147 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 23 marked runs cover roughly 13 km. The largest share is black diamond, with 4.5 km (34%). Steep ground is the speciality: 34% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 4.5 km of black or expert runs.

    Anthony Lakes: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Anthony Lakes?

    Aim for early January and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Anthony Lakes have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 74 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Anthony Lakes?

    Between 2,174 m and 2,418 m, so 244 m of vertical drop.

    Is Anthony Lakes good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 4.5 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Anthony Lakes have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
