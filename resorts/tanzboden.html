    About Tanzboden

    Tanzboden is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 3.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 866 m and 1,178 m above sea level. Wolzenalp is the next resort, 4.3 km away to the south-east.

    Snow at Tanzboden through a normal winter

    Count on about 16 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,022 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to mid-April. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 32 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 50 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 22 cm and 78 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 27 cm (lean years 9 cm, snowy years 46 cm).

    The pistes of Tanzboden

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Tanzboden gives 312 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 866 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Above the ski area rises Speer (1,950 m), 6.8 km to the south, about 772 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Tanzboden have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Tanzboden have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 27 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Tanzboden?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,178 m; the lowest starts at 866 m (312 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Tanzboden?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.
