    What 46 winters say about Little Ski Hill

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Little Ski Hill (5,387 ft (1,642 m)), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 32 in (81 cm), with a normal range of 24 in (60 cm)–39 in (100 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 56 in (143 cm); the middle half of winters range from 45 in (114 cm) to 65 in (166 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 64 in (162 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 72 % of winters, and by 1 January in 96 %.

    1998-99 was the big one (109 in (278 cm)); 2000-01 the lean one (37 in (95 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 170 days.

    Little Ski Hill: quick answers

    Is there snow in Little Ski Hill at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 32 in (81 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 96 % of 46 winters. Right now: 6.3 in (16 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Little Ski Hill?

    March: the snow peaks at about 64 in (162 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How snowy is Little Ski Hill?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 64 in (162 cm) in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 109 in (278 cm) (1998-99), the thinnest 37 in (95 cm) (2000-01).

    What altitude is Little Ski Hill at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,702 m; the lowest starts at 1,581 m (121 m of vertical).

    How big is Little Ski Hill?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
