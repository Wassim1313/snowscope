    About Little Ski Hill

    Little Ski Hill is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 3.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,581 m and 1,702 m above sea level. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Brundage Mountain, 8.0 km to the north.

    Snow at Little Ski Hill through a normal winter

    At about 1,642 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 November and lasts to 11 May: about 24 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 162 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 107 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Year to year the snow is steady, with 97 cm to 191 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 81 cm (lean years 48 cm, snowy years 113 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 15 weeks.

    The pistes of Little Ski Hill

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 3.1 km to ski. Most of the length is green circle (67%, about 2.1 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 121 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,581 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a T-bar.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Little Ski Hill include Shore Lodge (hotel), Brundage Bungalows (hotel), Discovery Cottage #5 (chalet) and Discovery Cottage #4 (chalet).

    Quick answers

    When does Little Ski Hill have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Little Ski Hill at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 81 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Little Ski Hill at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,702 m; the lowest starts at 1,581 m (121 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Little Ski Hill?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 2.1 km, are easy.

    How big is Little Ski Hill?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
