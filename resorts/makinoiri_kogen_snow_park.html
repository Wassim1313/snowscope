    About Makinoiri Kogen Snow Park

    Makinoiri Kogen Snow Park is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 9.0 km of pistes put it at number 94 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 617 m up to 1,269 m. Its nearest neighbour is The Cupid of Romance, 0.7 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Makinoiri Kogen Snow Park

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 3.3 km in total. Most of the length is black (79%, about 2.6 km). With 79% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.6 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Makinoiri Kogen Snow Park gives 652 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 617 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 7 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.
