    About Walterswil

    Walterswil is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 679 m to 774 m. The closest other ski area is Wasen (8.5 km, south).

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 25 km south to Netschbuehl Eggiwill, with about 3.0 km of runs. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell. The highest nearby is Oberbalmberg, topping out at 1,267 m.

    The pistes of Walterswil

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.3 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 1.3 km (100%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How much snow Walterswil usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 726 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 18 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 22 January. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; a snowy December gave 15 cm, a dry one 0 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 95 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 679 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Oberbalmberg reaches 1,267 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Gitzichnübeli, 997 m high and 6.9 km south of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Romantik Hotel Bären, Krone Huttwil, Bahnhof and Hotel guter Hirte.

    Walterswil: common questions

    When does Walterswil have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Is there snow at Walterswil at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 4 cm).

    How high is Walterswil?

    Between 679 m and 774 m, so 95 m of vertical drop.

    Is Walterswil suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Walterswil?

    Netschbuehl Eggiwill, 25 km to the south, with about 3.0 km of pistes.
