    About La Pinilla

    La Pinilla has about 12 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,484 m to 2,036 m.

    The mountain at La Pinilla

    552 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,484 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 6 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    What there is to ski at La Pinilla

    Counting every graded piste, there are 29 runs and around 12 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.3 km, or 62%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.
