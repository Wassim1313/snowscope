    About Harrachov

    Harrachov is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 6.0 km of pistes put it at number 42 of 257. Its lifts climb from 669 m to 1,021 m. Hilbert is the next resort, 1.7 km away to the north-west.

    The pistes of Harrachov

    In all, 12 marked runs cover roughly 6.3 km. Most of the length is blue (52%, about 3.3 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 352 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 669 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 7 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 2 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 845 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm; a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 62 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 21 February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Harrachov?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Harrachov have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Harrachov at?

    The top is at 1,021 m and the base at 669 m.

    Is Harrachov good for beginners?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 3.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Harrachov have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
