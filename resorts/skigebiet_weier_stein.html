    About Weißer Stein

    Weißer Stein is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 616 m up to 677 m. Schwarzer Mann is the next resort, 16 km away to the south.

    The mountain at Weißer Stein

    From top to bottom that makes 61 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 616 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
