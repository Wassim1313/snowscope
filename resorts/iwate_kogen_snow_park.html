    About Iwate Kogen Snow Park

    Around 9.0 km of marked runs place Iwate Kogen Snow Park among the larger resorts of Japan, number 90 by length. Skiing here happens between 646 m and 1,207 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Amihari Onsen, 2.7 km to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 9.1 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 6.3 km, or 70%. With 70% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Iwate Kogen Snow Park

    The vertical drop is 561 m: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 646 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 5 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up.
