    The snow at Realp, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Realp (about 1,594 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 38 cm, with a normal range of 27 cm–58 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 74 cm (56 cm–95 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 80 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    22 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 41 % by 1 January.

    2013-14 was the big one (158 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (38 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to early May (143 days).

    So far this winter: 48 cm on 24 November (usual: 16 cm).

    About Realp

    Realp is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,546 m to 1,642 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gemsstock, 8.5 km to the east.

    Snow at Realp through a normal winter

    At about 1,594 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 December and lasts to 6 May: about 21 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm, with 67 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 44 cm to 107 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 44 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 80 cm, around 25 February.

    How Realp is laid out

    The vertical drop is 96 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,546 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Dammastock, 3,630 m high and 7.8 km north-west of the resort.
