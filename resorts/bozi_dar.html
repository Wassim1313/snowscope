    About Boží Dar

    With some 8.0 km of runs, Boží Dar belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 883 m and 1,130 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skiareál Klínovec, 0.9 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Boží Dar has 14 graded runs adding up to about 7.8 km. Most of the length is blue (56%, about 4.4 km). With 56% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Boží Dar is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 247 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 883 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 5 T-bars and 2 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,006 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 69 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 26 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 0 cm in a poor year to 44 cm in a big one.

    Boží Dar: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Boží Dar?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Boží Dar at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Boží Dar?

    The top is at 1,130 m and the base at 883 m.

    Can beginners ski at Boží Dar?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 4.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Boží Dar?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
