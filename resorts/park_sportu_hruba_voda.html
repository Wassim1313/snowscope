    About Park sportu Hrubá Voda

    Park sportu Hrubá Voda is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 146 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Its lifts climb from 340 m to 553 m. Hlubočky is the next resort, 4.8 km away to the south-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Park sportu Hrubá Voda gives 213 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 340 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 3 button lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
