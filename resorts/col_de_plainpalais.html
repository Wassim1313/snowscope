    About Col de Plainpalais

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Col de Plainpalais sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,171 m up to 1,450 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Aillon-Margériaz, 2.2 km to the east.

    The pistes of Col de Plainpalais

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 2.9 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.6 km (56%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,310 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 123 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 44 cm to 181 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 72 cm (lean years 15 cm, snowy years 157 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 12 March, with about 151 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Col de Plainpalais?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Col de Plainpalais have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 72 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Col de Plainpalais?

    The top is at 1,450 m and the base at 1,171 m.

    Is Col de Plainpalais good for beginners?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Col de Plainpalais?

    Aillon-Margériaz, 2.2 km to the east, with about 22 km of pistes.
