    About skiareál U Čápa

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, skiareál U Čápa sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 689 m and 795 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Skiareál Rejdice (1.2 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    skiareál U Čápa has 7 graded runs adding up to about 2.8 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 2.8 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at skiareál U Čápa through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 742 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 January and lasts to 5 March: about 6 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm; a snowy December gave 58 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 4 cm to 71 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 40 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    106 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 689 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 3 T-bars and 1 button lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    skiareál U Čápa: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at skiareál U Čápa?

    Aim for late January and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at skiareál U Čápa at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at skiareál U Čápa?

    The top is at 795 m and the base at 689 m.

    Is skiareál U Čápa suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does skiareál U Čápa have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
