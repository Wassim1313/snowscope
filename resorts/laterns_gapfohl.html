    About Laterns-Gapfohl

    Laterns-Gapfohl is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 24 km of pistes put it at number 257 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,141 m and reach 1,770 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Gröllerkopf-Übersaxen, 5.4 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Laterns-Gapfohl

    Laterns-Gapfohl has 38 graded runs adding up to about 24 km. Most of the length is black (43%, about 10 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 50% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 10 km of black or expert runs. 1.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How much snow Laterns-Gapfohl usually gets

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,456 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-May. By mid-April the typical depth is 63 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 76 cm. Most winters land between 45 cm and 104 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 40 cm; a snowy December gave 62 cm, a dry one 15 cm.

    The mountain at Laterns-Gapfohl

    From top to bottom that makes 629 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 8 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Sünser Spitze at 2,061 m, standing 291 m above the top lift.

    Laterns-Gapfohl: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Laterns-Gapfohl?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Laterns-Gapfohl at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 40 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Laterns-Gapfohl at?

    Between 1,141 m and 1,770 m, so 629 m of vertical drop.

    Does Laterns-Gapfohl have steep runs?

    Yes: about 12 km of its graded pistes (50%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Laterns-Gapfohl?

    Damüls-Mellau-Faschina, 10 km to the east, with about 101 km of pistes.
