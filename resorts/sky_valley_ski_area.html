    About Sky Valley

    Sky Valley is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 220 of 385 in Japan by length. Skiing here happens between 536 m and 992 m above sea level. Hyper Bowl Tohachi is the next resort, 0.8 km away to the south-west.

    How Sky Valley is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 456 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 536 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 2.1 km north-west to Hachi Kita, with about 8.0 km of runs. Hachi Kita reaches 1,217 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Quick answers

    How high is Sky Valley?

    Its highest lift reaches 992 m; the lowest starts at 536 m (456 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Sky Valley?

    Hachi Kita, 2.1 km to the north-west, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Sky Valley?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
