    About Grindelwald First

    With some 39 km of runs, Grindelwald First belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,061 m up to 2,497 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Axalp, 6.8 km to the north.

    The pistes of Grindelwald First

    Grindelwald First has 47 graded runs adding up to about 39 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 17 km, or 44%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Experts can count on some 7.2 km of difficult runs. 0.8 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Grindelwald First

    Skied top to bottom, Grindelwald First gives 1,436 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 3 gondolas, 3 chairlifts and 2 T-bars, 8 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Nässihorn (3,741 m), 7.8 km to the south-east, about 1,244 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Grindelwald First usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,779 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 1 December and lasts to 27 May: about 26 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 115 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 72 cm and 141 cm by mid-February. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 118 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 60 cm, from 30 cm in a poor year to 78 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When does Grindelwald First have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Grindelwald First at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 60 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Grindelwald First?

    Between 1,061 m and 2,497 m, so 1,436 m of vertical drop.

    Is Grindelwald First good for beginners?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Grindelwald First?

    Grindelwald, 9.7 km to the south-west, with about 88 km of pistes.
