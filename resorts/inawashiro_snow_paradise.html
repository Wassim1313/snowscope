    About Inawashiro Snow Paradise

    Inawashiro Snow Paradise is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 14 km of pistes put it at number 33 of 385. Skiing here happens between 698 m and 1,249 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Family Snow Park Bandai X2 (2.4 km, north-east).
