    About Rubeshibe-cho Happodai

    Rubeshibe-cho Happodai is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 240 m up to 335 m. Oketocho Minamigaoka is the next resort, 10 km away to the south.

    The mountain at Rubeshibe-cho Happodai

    The vertical drop is 95 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 240 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The only lift is a chairlift.
