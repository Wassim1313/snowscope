    About Watles

    Watles is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 502 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,725 m to 2,495 m. Its nearest neighbour is Watles, 0.4 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Watles has 18 graded runs adding up to about 9.5 km. The largest share is red, with 4.8 km (51%). Intermediate runs make up 51% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. 0.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Watles is laid out

    The vertical drop is 770 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,725 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Piz Sesvenna, 3,204 m high and 7.0 km west of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Nauders Bergkastel (18 km north) has about 49 km of pistes against 10 km here. For more reliable snow, Minschuns usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 28 weeks, against 22 here. The highest nearby is Nauders Bergkastel, topping out at 2,735 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,110 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 14 December and keeps it until about 16 May, roughly 22 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 41 cm and 83 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 62 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is usually deepest around 27 March, with about 68 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 37 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 58 cm, a dry one 21 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Watles?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Watles at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 37 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Watles at?

    Between 1,725 m and 2,495 m, so 770 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Watles best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 51% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Watles?

    Nauders Bergkastel, 18 km to the north, with about 49 km of pistes.
