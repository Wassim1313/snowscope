    About Area Sciistica Campigna

    Compact and local, Area Sciistica Campigna has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,479 m and reach 1,630 m at the top. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Area sciistica Pian del Falco, 2.1 km to the south-east.

    The pistes of Area Sciistica Campigna

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.5 km to ski. Most of the length is red (57%, about 0.9 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    How Area Sciistica Campigna is laid out

    The vertical drop is 151 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,479 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a button lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Monte Falco (1,657 m), Monte Falterona (1,654 m) and Poggio Piancancelli (1,575 m).

    Snow at Area Sciistica Campigna in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at about 1,554 m, the middle of the ski area stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.
