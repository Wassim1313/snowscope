    About Harghita Ski

    Harghita Ski is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 5.0 km of pistes put it at number 47 of 312. Skiing here happens between 1,237 m and 1,373 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Madarasi Hargita, 8.0 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Harghita Ski

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 4.8 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.9 km, or 59%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Harghita Ski is laid out

    136 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 3 lifts here are T-bars.
