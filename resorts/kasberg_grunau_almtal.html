    About Kasberg Grünau

    Kasberg Grünau is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 25 km of pistes put it at number 245 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 618 m to 1,598 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Feuerkogel, 20 km to the west.

    The pistes of Kasberg Grünau

    Kasberg Grünau has 63 graded runs adding up to about 24 km. Most of the length is blue (63%, about 15 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Kasberg Grünau is laid out

    The vertical drop is 980 m: enough height for proper long runs. Because the lifts start as low as 618 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 7 T-bars and 3 moving carpets, 13 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Kasberg, 1,747 m high and 2.7 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 10 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,108 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm; a snowy December gave 45 cm, a dry one 4 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 22 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 42 cm, around 26 February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 18 cm to 65 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Hochberghaus (mountain hut), Sporthotel Wimmergreuth (hotel), Sepp Huber Hütte (mountain hut) and Alpenpension Birkenhof (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Kasberg Grünau: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Kasberg Grünau?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    Can you ski at Kasberg Grünau at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Kasberg Grünau?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,598 m; the lowest starts at 618 m (980 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Kasberg Grünau?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    How big is Kasberg Grünau?

    About 25 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
