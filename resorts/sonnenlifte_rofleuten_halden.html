    About Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden

    Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 904 m and 980 m above sea level. Skizentrum Pfronten is the next resort, 2.8 km away to the south-east.

    The pistes of Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden

    Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden has 8 graded runs adding up to about 1.3 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.7 km (56%). With 56% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden gives 76 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,104 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Füssener Jöchle-Grän, reach 1,869 m. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 drag lift and 3 rope tows. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The skyline is dominated by Brentenjoch, 2,000 m high and 6.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow at Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 942 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 6 cm to 45 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 8 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. The snow is usually deepest around 12 February, with about 23 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 27 cm in the best years.
