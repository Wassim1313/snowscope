    About Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden

    Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 904 m and 980 m above sea level. Skizentrum Pfronten is the next resort, 2.8 km away to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Sonnenlifte Röfleuten/Halden gives 76 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,104 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Füssener Jöchle-Grän, reach 1,869 m.
