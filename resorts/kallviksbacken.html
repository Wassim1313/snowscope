    About Källviksbacken

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Källviksbacken sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 130 m and 226 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Nybroberget, 20 km south-west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 24 km south-west to Romme Alpin, with about 28 km of runs. Kungsberget has the longer snow season: around 4 weeks of solid cover, compared with 1 at Källviksbacken.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Källviksbacken today: about 16 cm up high. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 9 cm by Tuesday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 5 cm at mid-mountain.

    How Källviksbacken is laid out

    96 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mjölnarboberget at 334 m, standing 108 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 178 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 9 February and lasts to 16 February: about 1 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 31 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 34 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 9 cm and 49 cm.

    Questions about Källviksbacken

    When is the best time to ski at Källviksbacken?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-February.

    Can you ski at Källviksbacken at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    How high is Källviksbacken?

    Between 130 m and 226 m, so 96 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Källviksbacken?

    Romme Alpin, 24 km to the south-west, with about 28 km of pistes.

    How big is Källviksbacken?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
