    About Kolbensattel (Oberammergau)

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Kolbensattel (Oberammergau) one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 856 m and reach 909 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Steckenberg, 2.5 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Hörnle: about 5.0 km of pistes, 6.8 km to the north. For more reliable snow, Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 19 weeks, against 0 here. For higher slopes, Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau climbs to 1,671 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    53 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 856 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 3 rope tows, 9 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Kienjoch at 1,953 m, standing 1,044 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Kolbensattel (Oberammergau) usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 882 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 12 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 28 cm in a big one. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 43 cm. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 14 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Kolbensattel (Oberammergau)?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    How much snow does Kolbensattel (Oberammergau) have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).

    How high is Kolbensattel (Oberammergau)?

    Its highest lift reaches 909 m; the lowest starts at 856 m (53 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Kolbensattel (Oberammergau)?

    Hörnle, 6.8 km to the north, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Kolbensattel (Oberammergau) have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
