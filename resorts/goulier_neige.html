    About Goulier-Neige

    Compact and local, Goulier-Neige has about 5.0 km of runs, small for the Pyrenees. Its lifts climb from 1,483 m to 1,825 m. Ordino-Arcalis is the next resort, 12 km away to the south.

    Goulier-Neige and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Ax 3 Domaines: about 51 km of pistes, 24 km to the east. For more reliable snow, Ordino-Arcalis usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 20 weeks, against 8 here. For higher slopes, Ordino-Arcalis climbs to 2,626 m.

    What there is to ski at Goulier-Neige

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 4.5 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (54%, about 2.4 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Goulier-Neige gives 342 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,483 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Ordino-Arcalis, reach 2,626 m. Getting up the mountain: 3 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Pic de l'Aspre (2,744 m), 7.2 km to the south, about 919 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Goulier-Neige usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,654 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to mid-March, about 8 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 15 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 49 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 1 February, with about 45 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 85 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Gîte (guesthouse), Gîte Retrouvance de Bertasque (mountain hut), Auberge du Montcalm (hotel) and Eau berges (guesthouse).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Goulier-Neige?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Goulier-Neige at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in 8 winters out of ten, about 15 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Goulier-Neige?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,825 m; the lowest starts at 1,483 m (342 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Goulier-Neige?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 2.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Goulier-Neige?

    Ax 3 Domaines, 24 km to the east, with about 51 km of pistes.
