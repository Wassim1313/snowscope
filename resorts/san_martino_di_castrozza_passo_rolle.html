    About San Martino di Castrozza

    With some 43 km of runs, San Martino di Castrozza belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,402 m and 2,351 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being San Martino di Castrozza, 1.4 km to the south.

    The pistes of San Martino di Castrozza

    San Martino di Castrozza has 63 graded runs adding up to about 42 km. The largest share is blue, with 20 km (48%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 2.9 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, San Martino di Castrozza gives 949 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,402 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 4 gondolas, 12 chairlifts, 4 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 22 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Cima della Vezzana at 3,192 m, standing 841 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,876 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 16 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 29 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 51 cm, around 2 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 31 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 29 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 4 cm in a poor year to 55 cm in a big one.

    Questions about San Martino di Castrozza

    When does San Martino di Castrozza have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at San Martino di Castrozza at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 29 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    When does the ski season start at San Martino di Castrozza?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is San Martino di Castrozza at?

    The top is at 2,351 m and the base at 1,402 m.

    Is San Martino di Castrozza good for beginners?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 20 km, are easy.
