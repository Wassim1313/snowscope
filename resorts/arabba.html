    About Arabba

    Arabba is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 45 km of pistes put it at number 130 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,609 m up to 2,485 m. Belvedere is the next resort, 6.1 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 67 runs and around 33 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 14 km (42%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 5.9 km of difficult runs.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,047 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 68 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 62 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 40 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 76 cm, a dry one 14 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 42 cm and 102 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 876 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,609 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 3 gondolas, 9 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets, 16 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Punta Penia, 3,343 m high and 6.4 km south of the resort.

    Arabba: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Arabba?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Arabba at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 40 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Arabba?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 18 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Arabba?

    Between 1,609 m and 2,485 m, so 876 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Arabba best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 42% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
