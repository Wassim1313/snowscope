    About Gohrersberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gohrersberg offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 748 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 809 m and 946 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wengen, 5.6 km to the south-east.

    How Gohrersberg is laid out

    The vertical drop is 137 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,009 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.

    How Gohrersberg compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Schwärzenlifte Eschach: about 3.0 km of pistes, 5.9 km to the east.
