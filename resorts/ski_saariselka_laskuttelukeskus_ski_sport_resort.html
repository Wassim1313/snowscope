    What 46 winters say about Ski Saariselkä

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Ski Saariselkä (354 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 39 cm, with a normal range of 32 cm–48 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 62 cm; the middle half of winters range from 52 cm to 66 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 63 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 15 % of winters, and by 1 January in 28 %.

    Best winter on record: 1997-98, peaking at 108 cm. Worst: 1987-88, which never passed 56 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 160 days.

    So far this winter: 18 cm on 28 November (usual: 25 cm).

    Snow at Ski Saariselkä through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 354 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 4 December and keeps it until about 11 May, roughly 23 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 39 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 54 cm, a dry one 27 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 59 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 63 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 48 cm to 73 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Saariselkä gives 171 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar, 2 lifts in all. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Iisakkipää (454 m), Vahtamapää (452 m) and Kaunispää (438 m).
