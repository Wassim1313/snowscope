    About Kühtai

    Kühtai is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 35 km of pistes put it at number 178 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,939 m up to 2,507 m. Hochoetz-Kühtai is the next resort, 5.2 km away to the west.

    Snow at Kühtai through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,223 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 166 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 153 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Year to year the snow is steady, with 105 cm to 196 cm in mid-February in most winters. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 82 cm (lean years 48 cm, snowy years 118 cm).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Kühtai has 81 graded runs adding up to about 33 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 23 km, or 69%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 5.0 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    568 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 1,939 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Its top at 2,507 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 1 gondola, 6 chairlifts, 3 T-bars and 3 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Zwieselbacher Rosskogel Südgipfel, 3,080 m high and 5.9 km south of the resort.

    Questions about Kühtai

    When does Kühtai have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Kühtai at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 82 cm deep.

    What altitude is Kühtai at?

    Between 1,939 m and 2,507 m, so 568 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Kühtai best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Kühtai have?

    About 35 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
