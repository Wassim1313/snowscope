    About Shizukuishi

    Shizukuishi is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 18 km of pistes put it at number 22 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 423 m up to 1,133 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Amihari Onsen, 5.2 km to the north.

    How Shizukuishi is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Shizukuishi gives 710 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Because the lifts start as low as 423 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 34 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Shizukuishi. 22 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 15 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Shizukuishi: common questions

    What altitude is Shizukuishi at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,133 m; the lowest starts at 423 m (710 m of vertical).

    How big is Shizukuishi?

    About 18 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
