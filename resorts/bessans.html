    About Bessans

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Bessans one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,706 m up to 1,842 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bonneval-sur-Arc, 6.2 km to the north-east.

    Bessans and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 15 km north to Tignes, with about 211 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Tignes does better, with about 29 weeks of a good base instead of 17. For higher slopes, Tignes climbs to 3,447 m.

    What there is to ski at Bessans

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 1.0 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.0 km, or 100%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Bessans today: about 22 cm up high. 23 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 44 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 14 cm).

    How Bessans is laid out

    136 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,706 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Tignes reaches 3,447 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pointe de Charbonnel at 3,752 m, standing 1,910 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Bessans through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,774 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 December and keeps it until about 12 April, roughly 17 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 36 cm and 88 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 65 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 26 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 32 cm; a snowy December gave 64 cm, a dry one 16 cm.

    Questions about Bessans

    When does Bessans have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Bessans at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 32 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Bessans?

    The top is at 1,842 m and the base at 1,706 m.

    Can beginners ski at Bessans?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bessans?

    Tignes, 15 km to the north, with about 211 km of pistes.
