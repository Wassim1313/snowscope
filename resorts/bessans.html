    The snow at Bessans, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Bessans (about 1,774 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 32 cm; half of the winters sit between 23 cm and 46 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 61 cm; the middle half of winters range from 47 cm to 77 cm.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 65 cm.

    13 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 17 % by 1 January.

    2017-18 was the big one (141 cm); 1988-89 the lean one (38 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-December until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 121 days.

    Bessans: quick answers

    Is there snow in Bessans at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 32 cm, and only 17 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 60 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Bessans?

    February: the snow peaks at about 65 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to mid-April.

    How deep does the snow get at Bessans?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 65 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 141 cm (2017-18), the thinnest 38 cm (1988-89).

    How high do the lifts go at Bessans?

    The top is at 1,842 m and the base at 1,706 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bessans?

    Tignes, 15 km to the north, with about 211 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Bessans have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.

    About Bessans

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Bessans one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,706 m up to 1,842 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bonneval-sur-Arc, 6.2 km to the north-east.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Bessans today: about 60 cm up high. 28 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 19 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    How Bessans is laid out

    136 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,706 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Tignes reaches 3,447 m.
