    About Familienabfahrt

    Familienabfahrt is a ski area. The closest other ski area is Rahmensteinhang (0.1 km, south-west).

    How much snow Familienabfahrt usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 590 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 1 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 12 cm). Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 18 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 5 cm, around 26 January. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January.
