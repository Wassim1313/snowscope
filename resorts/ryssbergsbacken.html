    About Ryssbergsbacken

    Ryssbergsbacken is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 2 m and reach 51 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Kila Valley, 16 km north, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 26 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late February. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 27 January. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 27 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; a snowy December gave 17 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    The mountain at Ryssbergsbacken

    The vertical drop is 49 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The only lift is a T-bar.

    Ryssbergsbacken: common questions

    Aim for mid-January and late February.

    Can you ski at Ryssbergsbacken at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Ryssbergsbacken?

    The top is at 51 m and the base at 2 m.
