    About Aizu Kogen Daikura

    Aizu Kogen Daikura is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 9.0 km of pistes put it at number 84 of 385. Its lifts climb from 836 m to 1,219 m. The closest other ski area is Aizu Kogen Takatsue (7.0 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Aizu Kogen Daikura

    The piste map lists 15 graded runs, about 8.8 km in total. The largest share is red, with 4.0 km (45%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.0 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 383 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The base at 836 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Aizu Kogen Daikura has roughly 13 cm of snow on the ground today. 11 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Friday.
