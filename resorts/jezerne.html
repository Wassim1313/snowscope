    About Jezerné

    Jezerné is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 557 m up to 616 m. U Bambuchů is the next resort, 2.4 km away to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Jezerné gives 59 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 557 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Soláň, reach 856 m.
