    About Mellau

    Mellau is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 24 km of pistes put it at number 260 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 689 m up to 1,920 m. The closest other ski area is Damüls-Mellau (3.9 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Mellau

    Mellau has 50 graded runs adding up to about 24 km. The largest share is black, with 8.2 km (35%). Steep ground is the speciality: 41% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 9.8 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Mellau

    Skied top to bottom, Mellau gives 1,231 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 689 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Glatthorn, 2,133 m high and 7.9 km south of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Damüls-Mellau-Faschina: about 101 km of pistes, 3.9 km to the south. For more reliable snow, Fontanella-Faschina usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 25 weeks, against 19 here.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Mellau hold around 8 cm of snow today. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 35 cm by Saturday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (12 cm).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,304 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 37 cm and 101 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm, from 12 cm in a poor year to 55 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 68 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 40 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Quick answers

    When does Mellau have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Mellau at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 34 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Mellau?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,920 m; the lowest starts at 689 m (1,231 m of vertical).

    Is Mellau good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 9.8 km of its graded pistes (41%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Mellau?

    Damüls-Mellau-Faschina, 3.9 km to the south, with about 101 km of pistes.
