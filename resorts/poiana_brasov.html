    About Poiana Brașov

    Only 3 of the 312 ski areas of the Carpathians are larger than Poiana Brașov, which has some 20 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 974 m up to 1,772 m. Its nearest neighbour is Bunloc, 8.7 km east, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Poiana Brașov

    Counting every graded piste, there are 34 runs and around 18 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (39%, about 7.1 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 38% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.9 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    798 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 974 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 2 cable cars, 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts and 3 button lifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    Poiana Brașov: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Poiana Brașov?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,772 m; the lowest starts at 974 m (798 m of vertical).

    Is Poiana Brașov good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 6.9 km of its graded pistes (38%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Poiana Brașov have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
