    About Hallingskarvet skisenter

    Hallingskarvet skisenter has about 0.3 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 748 m to 878 m. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hallingskarvet, 0.1 km to the north-east.

    Hallingskarvet skisenter and the resorts around it

    For more reliable snow, Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 30 weeks, against 24 here. For higher slopes, Hemsedal skisenter climbs to 1,439 m.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Hallingskarvet skisenter today: about 10 cm up high. The last seven days brought 14 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 22 cm at mid-mountain).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Hallingskarvet skisenter gives 130 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hemsedal skisenter reaches 1,439 m. Above the ski area rises Miljonuten (1,851 m), 7.2 km to the south, about 973 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Hallingskarvet skisenter through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 813 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 4 December and keeps it until about 19 May, roughly 24 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 88 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 31 March, with about 92 cm. Most winters land between 53 cm and 113 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 46 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 21 cm, snowy years 83 cm). For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Hallingskarvet skisenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hallingskarvet skisenter?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Hallingskarvet skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 46 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Hallingskarvet skisenter?

    Between 748 m and 878 m, so 130 m of vertical drop.
