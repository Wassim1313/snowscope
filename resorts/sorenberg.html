    About Sörenberg

    With some 38 km of runs, Sörenberg belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,119 m to 2,257 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mörlialp, 6.3 km to the east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,138 m: enough height for proper long runs. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
