    About Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses

    Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses is a mid-sized ski area with about 11 km of pistes, number 474 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,198 m and 1,558 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Sainte-Croix/Les Rasses, 0.4 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,378 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 January and lasts to 9 March: about 8 weeks. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 148 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 19 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 89 cm, a dry one 0 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 62 cm.

    How Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 360 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars and 4 button lifts, 6 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Le Chasseron (1,607 m), Petites Roches (1,583 m) and Aiguilles de Baulmes (1,560 m).

    Questions about Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses

    When does Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    Can you ski at Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 19 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in 8 winters out of ten.

    What altitude is Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses at?

    The top is at 1,558 m and the base at 1,198 m.

    Is Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses suitable for families?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 5.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
