    About Peuramaa

    Neither huge nor tiny, Peuramaa offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 164 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 5 m up to 54 m. Granibacken is the next resort, 19 km away to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Peuramaa has 13 graded runs adding up to about 2.5 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 59%. With 59% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Peuramaa is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 49 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Alhovuori, for one, climbs to 147 m.     The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 30 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 17 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 5 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 15 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 42 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.
