    Mid-February typically sees 70 cm, between 55 cm and 91 cm in a normal winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 20 % of winters, and by 1 January in 37 %.

    Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø: quick answers

    Can you ski Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 42 cm, and only 37 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 21 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 80 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø get?

    About 80 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 59 cm in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 218 cm in the biggest (1996-97).

    How high is Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø?

    Its highest lift reaches 179 m; the lowest starts at 109 m (70 m of vertical).

    How much snow Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø usually gets

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 144 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. Year to year the snow is steady, with 42 cm to 119 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm, from 19 cm in a poor year to 73 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 80 cm, around 1 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 68 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March.
