    About Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø

    Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 109 m and 179 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg (33 km, south-east).

    Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø and the resorts around it

    Holtanlia has the longer snow season: around 29 weeks of solid cover, compared with 24 at Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø. The highest nearby is Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg, topping out at 486 m.

    How much snow Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø usually gets

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 144 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. Year to year the snow is steady, with 42 cm to 119 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm, from 19 cm in a poor year to 73 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 80 cm, around 1 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 68 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 70 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Skaugtuva (626 m), 6.2 km to the north-east, about 447 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 42 cm deep.

    How high is Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø?

    Its highest lift reaches 179 m; the lowest starts at 109 m (70 m of vertical).
