    About Niederalpl

    Neither huge nor tiny, Niederalpl offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 588 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,054 m up to 1,418 m. Brunnalm is the next resort, 6.0 km away to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Niederalpl has 13 graded runs adding up to about 7.3 km. The largest share is red, with 4.0 km (54%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    How much snow Niederalpl usually gets

    Count on about 14 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,236 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 27 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 56 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 23 cm and 83 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 55 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 17 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Niederalpl gives 364 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Hohe Veitsch, 1,981 m high and 4.1 km south-east of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 mountain huts: Wetterinalm Sennhütte, Sohlenalm, Weißalm and Halterhütte Ochsenboden. Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Niederalpl

    When is the best time to ski at Niederalpl?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Is there snow at Niederalpl at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 27 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Niederalpl?

    The top is at 1,418 m and the base at 1,054 m.

    Is Niederalpl suitable for families?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 3.3 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Niederalpl?

    Aflenzer Bürgeralm, 18 km to the south-west, with about 18 km of pistes.
