    About Nollen

    Nollen is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 707 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 766 m and 1,046 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Raten, 8.6 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 3.8 km. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 2.0 km). With 53% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Nollen hold around 17 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 32 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 9 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    280 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 766 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Rigi-Scheidegg, for one, climbs to 1,647 m. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Wildspitz at 1,580 m, standing 534 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Nollen through a normal winter

    Count on about 3 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 906 m) in an ordinary winter, from early February to late February. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 11 cm to 50 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 16 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 27 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 11 February, with about 31 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 16 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Nollen?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    Can you ski at Nollen at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Nollen?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,046 m; the lowest starts at 766 m (280 m of vertical).

    Is Nollen suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Nollen?

    Sattel-Hochstuckli, 10 km to the south-east, with about 10 km of pistes.
