    About Skizentrum Tännicht Sohland

    Skizentrum Tännicht Sohland has about 0.3 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 381 m and 440 m above sea level. Sebnitz is the next resort, 11 km away to the south-west.

    Skizentrum Tännicht Sohland and the resorts around it

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 410 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; a snowy December gave 16 cm, a dry one 0 cm. For the best snow, aim for late December and early January. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 22 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 26 January, with about 6 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    How Skizentrum Tännicht Sohland is laid out

    59 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 381 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Zadní Telnice, reach 795 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Skizentrum Tännicht Sohland: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Skizentrum Tännicht Sohland?

    Aim for late December and early January.

    Can you ski at Skizentrum Tännicht Sohland at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Skizentrum Tännicht Sohland?

    Between 381 m and 440 m, so 59 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Skizentrum Tännicht Sohland have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
