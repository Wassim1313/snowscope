    About Kope

    Kope is a mid-sized ski area with about 16 km of pistes, number 365 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 705 m and 1,535 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Rogla, 12 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Kope

    Kope has 16 graded runs adding up to about 15 km. Most of the length is blue (57%, about 8.7 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    830 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 705 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 11 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts and 7 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Črni vrh (1,543 m), Jezerski vrh (1,537 m) and Mali Črni vrh (1,536 m).

    Snow at Kope through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,120 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 56 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 9 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 34 cm). A normal season tops out near 14 cm, around 19 January.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Apartma Pungart (chalet), Grmovškov dom pod Veliko Kopo (mountain hut), Počitniški dom Partizanka (guesthouse) and Planinska koča na Pesniku (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Kope?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Kope at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 9 cm).

    What altitude is Kope at?

    Between 705 m and 1,535 m, so 830 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kope suitable for families?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 8.7 km, are easy.

    How big is Kope?

    About 16 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
