    About Estación de Esquí de Manzaneda

    Only 3 of the 11 ski areas of the Cantabrian Mountains are larger than Estación de Esquí de Manzaneda, which has some 13 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,503 m up to 1,762 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Estación de Esquí de Manzaneda has 27 graded runs adding up to about 13 km. Most of the length is blue (76%, about 9.8 km). With 76% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Estación de Esquí de Manzaneda

    The vertical drop is 259 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,503 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 6 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 4 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Estación de Esquí de Manzaneda?

    The top is at 1,762 m and the base at 1,503 m.

    Is Estación de Esquí de Manzaneda good for beginners?

    Yes: 76% of the graded pistes, about 9.8 km, are easy.

    How big is Estación de Esquí de Manzaneda?

    About 13 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
