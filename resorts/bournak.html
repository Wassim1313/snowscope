    About Bouřňák

    Bouřňák is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 7.0 km of pistes put it at number 34 of 257. The slopes start at 587 m and reach 870 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Winterwelt Rehefeld (4.3 km, north).

    What there is to ski at Bouřňák

    The piste map lists 18 graded runs, about 6.7 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 3.0 km, or 45%. No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    283 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 587 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 870 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts and 1 rope tow, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Snow at Bouřňák through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 728 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 34 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm, with 25 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 12 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Bouřňák?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    Is there snow at Bouřňák at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Bouřňák?

    Its highest lift reaches 870 m; the lowest starts at 587 m (283 m of vertical).

    Is Bouřňák good for beginners?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 2.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Bouřňák?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
