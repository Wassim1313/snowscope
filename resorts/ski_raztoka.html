    About Ski Ráztoka

    Compact and local, Ski Ráztoka has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 627 m up to 830 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Podjavorník, 3.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Ski Ráztoka

    203 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 627 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.
