    What there is to ski at Ski Alpin Ovivat

    Ski Alpin Ovivat has 4 graded runs adding up to about 1.5 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.6 km (41%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 85 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 521 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 3 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 564 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and late December. At Christmas there was snow here in 4 winters out of ten, typically 0 cm, with 12 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 3 cm, around 24 January. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 19 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Questions about Ski Alpin Ovivat

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Alpin Ovivat?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    Is there snow at Ski Alpin Ovivat at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 4 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 0 cm).

    What altitude is Ski Alpin Ovivat at?

    Between 521 m and 606 m, so 85 m of vertical drop.

    Is Ski Alpin Ovivat suitable for families?
