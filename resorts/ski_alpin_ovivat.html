    What there is to ski at Ski Alpin Ovivat

    Ski Alpin Ovivat has 4 graded runs adding up to about 1.5 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.6 km (41%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 85 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 521 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 3 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
