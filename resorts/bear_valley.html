    About Bear Valley

    Bear Valley is a mid-sized ski area with about 36 km of pistes, number 63 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 2,024 m and 2,586 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Kirkwood (22 km, north).

    This week's snow

    Up high, Bear Valley has roughly 65 cm of snow on the ground today. 15 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 64 cm by Saturday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 14 cm).

    What there is to ski at Bear Valley

    Bear Valley has 59 graded runs adding up to about 35 km. Most of the length is blue square (46%, about 16 km). Intermediate runs make up 46% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 15 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Bear Valley through a normal winter

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,305 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 57 cm to 231 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 69 cm, from 7 cm in a poor year to 151 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 89 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 159 cm, around 11 March.

    Lifts and terrain

    562 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 2,024 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Its top at 2,586 m is the third highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. Its 9 lifts break down into 7 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Mokelumne Peak (2,837 m), 7.3 km to the north-west, about 251 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 3 hotels: Bear Valley Day Lodge, Bear Valley Lodge and Tamarack Lodge.

    Bear Valley: common questions

    When does Bear Valley have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Bear Valley have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 69 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Bear Valley?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,586 m; the lowest starts at 2,024 m (562 m of vertical).

    Does Bear Valley have steep runs?

    Yes: about 15 km of its graded pistes (44%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Bear Valley have?

    About 36 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
