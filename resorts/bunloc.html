    About Bunloc

    Bunloc is a ski area. The slopes start at 674 m and reach 1,175 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Poiana Brașov, 8.7 km west, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Bunloc gives 501 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 674 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Sinaia reaches 2,085 m.

    Quick answers

    How high is Bunloc?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,175 m; the lowest starts at 674 m (501 m of vertical).
