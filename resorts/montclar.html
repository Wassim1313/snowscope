    About Montclar

    Around 24 km of marked runs place Montclar among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 261 by length. The slopes start at 1,334 m and reach 2,465 m at the top. Chabanon is the next resort, 9.5 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 42 graded runs, about 24 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 46%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow Montclar usually gets

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,900 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 64 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 124 cm. For about 8 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Most winters land between 54 cm and 169 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm (lean years 14 cm, snowy years 112 cm).

    How Montclar is laid out

    1,131 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,334 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 8 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 2 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Sommet de Dormillouse (2,510 m), Neillière (2,459 m) and Pic de Bernardez (2,430 m).

    How Montclar compares nearby

    For more terrain, Espace Lumière (18 km south-east) has about 185 km of pistes against 24 km here. If snow is the priority, Les Orres does better, with about 25 weeks of a good base instead of 22. Les Orres reaches 2,700 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Montclar?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Montclar at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 54 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Montclar?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,465 m; the lowest starts at 1,334 m (1,131 m of vertical).

    Is Montclar suitable for families?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Montclar?

    Espace Lumière, 18 km to the south-east, with about 185 km of pistes.
