    About Muntele Mic

    About 13 km of pistes make Muntele Mic one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Carpathians (number 8). Skiing here happens between 1,415 m and 1,793 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Rausor, 29 km east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 24 marked runs cover roughly 13 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 8.9 km, or 67%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The mountain at Muntele Mic

    From top to bottom that makes 378 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,415 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,793 m is the highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet, 8 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
