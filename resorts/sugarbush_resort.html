    About Sugarbush Resort

    Only 2 of the 93 ski areas of US Northeast are larger than Sugarbush Resort, which has some 80 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 461 m up to 1,238 m. The closest other ski area is Mad River Glen (5.7 km, north).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 140 runs and around 80 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 35 km (44%). With 40% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 32 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    777 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 461 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,238 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 18 lifts break down into 13 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Ellen (1,234 m), Mount Abraham (1,223 m) and Lincoln Peak (1,212 m).

    How much snow Sugarbush Resort usually gets

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 850 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to late March. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 53 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 23 cm and 84 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 23 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 6 cm in a poor year to 49 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Sugarbush Resort?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Can you ski at Sugarbush Resort at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 23 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Sugarbush Resort?

    The announced opening day is 21 November 2026.

    What altitude is Sugarbush Resort at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,238 m; the lowest starts at 461 m (777 m of vertical).

    Does Sugarbush Resort have steep runs?

    Yes: about 32 km of its graded pistes (40%) are difficult.
