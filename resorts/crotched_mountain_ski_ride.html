    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 268 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 316 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
