    About Schilifte Kleinlobming

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Schilifte Kleinlobming one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 748 m to 892 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gaberl, 7.9 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 144 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 748 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Klippitztörl, for one, climbs to 1,817 m. To get up there are 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Rappoldkogel at 1,928 m, standing 1,036 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Schilifte Kleinlobming through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 820 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 1 cm in a poor year to 29 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in early January at roughly 12 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 26 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Schilifte Kleinlobming: common questions

    When does Schilifte Kleinlobming have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Is there snow at Schilifte Kleinlobming at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 9 cm).

    How high is Schilifte Kleinlobming?

    Between 748 m and 892 m, so 144 m of vertical drop.

    Is there a larger resort near Schilifte Kleinlobming?

    Salzstiegl, 10 km to the south, with about 10 km of pistes.

    How big is Schilifte Kleinlobming?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
