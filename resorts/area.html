    About Золотая Долина

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Золотая Долина sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 20 m up to 114 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero), 1.7 km to the north-west.

    Snow at Золотая Долина through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 67 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 January and lasts to 2 April: about 12 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 3 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 42 cm, around 4 February. Most winters land between 21 cm and 63 cm by mid-February.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 94 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 9 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Золотая Долина: common questions

    When does Золотая Долина have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    How much snow does Золотая Долина have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Золотая Долина?

    Its highest lift reaches 114 m; the lowest starts at 20 m (94 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Золотая Долина best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 47% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Золотая Долина have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
