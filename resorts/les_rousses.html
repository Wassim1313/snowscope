    About Les Rousses

    Around 32 km of marked runs place Les Rousses among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 193 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,060 m and 1,663 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Les Rousses (4.3 km, south).

    The pistes of Les Rousses

    Les Rousses has 92 graded runs adding up to about 32 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 14 km, or 43%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow Les Rousses usually gets

    Count on about 8 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,362 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to mid-March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 91 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 21 cm, with 78 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 50 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 603 m: runs of a moderate length. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 20 lifts: 1 cable car, 4 chairlifts, 7 button lifts, 4 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include La Dôle (1,678 m), Pointe de Poêle Chaud (1,628 m) and Le Noirmont (1,567 m).

    Questions about Les Rousses

    When does Les Rousses have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Les Rousses at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 21 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Rousses?

    The top is at 1,663 m and the base at 1,060 m.

    Is Les Rousses suitable for families?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 24 km, are easy.

    How big is Les Rousses?

    About 32 km of pistes and 20 lifts.
