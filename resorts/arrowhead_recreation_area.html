    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Arrowhead gives 42 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 183 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Okemo Mountain, for one, climbs to 1,012 m. To get up there are 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Craigue Hill at 1,040 m, standing 815 m above the top lift.

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 204 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.
