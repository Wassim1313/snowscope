    How Estació d'esquí Port del Comte is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Estació d'esquí Port del Comte gives 378 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,744 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; GrandValira reaches 2,619 m. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 6 button lifts and 4 moving carpets, 13 lifts in all. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Pedró dels Quatre Batlles, 2,386 m high and 2.4 km north-west of the resort.
