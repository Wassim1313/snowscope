    About Estació d'esquí Port del Comte

    Estació d'esquí Port del Comte is a mid-sized ski area with about 27 km of pistes, number 22 of 49 in the Pyrenees by length. Skiing here happens between 1,744 m and 2,122 m above sea level. Rasos de Peguera is the next resort, 18 km away to the east.

    The pistes of Estació d'esquí Port del Comte

    Counting every graded piste, there are 37 runs and around 26 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 16 km (59%). With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How Estació d'esquí Port del Comte is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Estació d'esquí Port del Comte gives 378 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,744 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; GrandValira reaches 2,619 m. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 6 button lifts and 4 moving carpets, 13 lifts in all. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Pedró dels Quatre Batlles, 2,386 m high and 2.4 km north-west of the resort.

    Snow at Estació d'esquí Port del Comte through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,933 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and late January. A normal season tops out near 16 cm, around 3 February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 34 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; a snowy December gave 21 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Questions about Estació d'esquí Port del Comte

    When does Estació d'esquí Port del Comte have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Is there snow at Estació d'esquí Port del Comte at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 4 cm).

    How high is Estació d'esquí Port del Comte?

    Between 1,744 m and 2,122 m, so 378 m of vertical drop.

    Is Estació d'esquí Port del Comte suitable for families?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Estació d'esquí Port del Comte?

    GrandValira, 43 km to the north, with about 136 km of pistes.
