    Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise (2,127 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 63 cm; half of the winters sit between 44 cm and 82 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 111 cm; the middle half of winters range from 88 cm to 126 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 116 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 50 % of winters, and by 1 January in 78 %.

    Best winter on record: 2019-20, peaking at 186 cm. Worst: 1991-92, which never passed 85 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-May, about 170 days.

    This winter so far: 47 cm on 23 November, against a usual 28 cm for the date.

    About Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise

    Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 208 km of pistes put it at number 11 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,306 m to 2,948 m. The closest other ski area is Val Cenis (3.3 km, north-east).

    The pistes of Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise

    The piste map lists 349 graded runs, about 206 km in total. Most of the length is blue (42%, about 86 km). With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. 1.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise gives 1,642 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,306 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,948 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 4 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 28 chairlifts, 22 button lifts, 11 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 9 moving carpets, 77 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 9 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Dent Parrachée at 3,697 m, standing 749 m above the top lift.
