    About Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise

    Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 208 km of pistes put it at number 11 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,306 m to 2,948 m. The closest other ski area is Val Cenis (3.3 km, north-east).

    The pistes of Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise

    The piste map lists 349 graded runs, about 206 km in total. Most of the length is blue (42%, about 86 km). With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. 1.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Espace Haute Maurienne Vanoise gives 1,642 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,306 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,948 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 4 gondolas, 29 chairlifts, 22 button lifts, 11 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 9 moving carpets, 77 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 9 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Dent Parrachée at 3,697 m, standing 749 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 2,127 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 1 December and lasts to 15 May: about 24 weeks. A normal season tops out near 116 cm, around 5 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 63 cm (lean years 33 cm, snowy years 93 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 7 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 63 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Most winters land between 78 cm and 137 cm by mid-February.
