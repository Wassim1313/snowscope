    The mountain at Lifjell

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Øysteinnatten (1,174 m), Gleksefjell (1,121 m) and Økteren (1,040 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 488 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 40 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 18 cm in a poor year to 71 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 11 March, with about 85 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 69 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 52 cm and 123 cm by mid-February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Lifjell?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Lifjell at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 40 cm deep.
