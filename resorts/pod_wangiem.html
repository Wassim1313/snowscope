    How much snow Pod Wangiem usually gets

    At about 802 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 February and lasts to 1 March: about 3 weeks. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 58 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 35 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 44 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Questions about Pod Wangiem

    When does Pod Wangiem have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    How much snow does Pod Wangiem have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm; the higher slopes do better.
