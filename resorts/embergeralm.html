    About Embergeralm

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Embergeralm sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,659 m and 2,110 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Gitschtal (13 km, south-east).

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,884 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 December and lasts to 17 May: about 24 weeks. A normal season tops out near 83 cm, around 28 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 78 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 48 cm and 105 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 45 cm; a snowy December gave 72 cm, a dry one 21 cm.

    The mountain at Embergeralm

    Skied top to bottom, Embergeralm gives 451 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,659 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 3 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Hochkreuz, 2,709 m high and 7.4 km north-west of the resort.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 16 marked runs cover roughly 4.8 km. Most of the length is red (66%, about 3.1 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Embergeralm?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Embergeralm have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Embergeralm?

    Between 1,659 m and 2,110 m, so 451 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Embergeralm best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Embergeralm?

    Nassfeld, 24 km to the south, with about 70 km of pistes.
