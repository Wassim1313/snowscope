    About Hachi Kita

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Hachi Kita sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Skiing here happens between 562 m and 1,217 m above sea level. Mikata Snow Park is the next resort, 1.5 km away to the north-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 655 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 562 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 1,217 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
