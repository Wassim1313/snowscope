    About Izumigatake

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Izumigatake sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Its lifts climb from 546 m to 764 m. The closest other ski area is Spring Valley Izumi Kogen (2.9 km, north).

    The pistes of Izumigatake

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 2.1 km to ski. Most of the length is red (66%, about 1.4 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Izumigatake gives 218 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 546 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
