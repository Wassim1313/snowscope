    Охта-Парк (Okhta Park): quick answers

    Can you ski Охта-Парк (Okhta Park) at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 16 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 4 % of 46 winters. Right now: 5 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Охта-Парк (Okhta Park)?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 32 cm). For a sure base, mid-January to late March is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Охта-Парк (Okhta Park) get?

    About 32 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 8 cm in the leanest winter (2019-20) and 82 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    How high is Охта-Парк (Okhta Park)?

    Between 31 m and 80 m, so 49 m of vertical drop.

    Does Охта-Парк (Okhta Park) have steep runs?

    About Охта-Парк (Okhta Park)

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Охта-Парк (Okhta Park) one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 31 m up to 80 m. Its nearest neighbour is Спортивный комплекс "Юкки" ГАУ Л, 7.4 km west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Охта-Парк (Okhta Park)

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 2.2 km. Most of the length is black (51%, about 1.1 km). With 51% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 1.1 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 49 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 6 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 3 moving carpets, 11 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include "104,8" (104 m), "101,2" (101 m) and "82,5" (82 m).

    The snow right now

    Up high, Охта-Парк (Okhta Park) has roughly 5 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 7 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 6 cm at mid-mountain).

    Snow at Охта-Парк (Okhta Park) through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 56 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 31 January and keeps it until about 12 March, roughly 5 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 33 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 9 March, with about 32 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 11 cm and 55 cm.
