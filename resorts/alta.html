    About Alta

    Around 77 km of marked runs place Alta among the larger resorts of US West, number 24 by length. Its lifts climb from 2,602 m to 3,217 m. The closest other ski area is Snowbird (0.5 km, south-west).

    What there is to ski at Alta

    In all, 125 marked runs cover roughly 71 km. The largest share is black diamond, with 37 km (52%). Good skiers will like it here; 56% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 40 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 2,910 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May, about 28 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 214 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Year to year the snow is steady, with 138 cm to 298 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 3 April, with about 243 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 22 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 128 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 71 cm, snowy years 235 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Alta gives 615 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,602 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its top at 3,217 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 7 chairlifts, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets, 11 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Twin Peaks (west), 3,502 m high and 3.0 km south-west of the resort.

    Alta: common questions

    When does Alta have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Alta have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 128 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Alta?

    The announced opening day is 20 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Alta?

    The top is at 3,217 m and the base at 2,602 m.

    Does Alta have steep runs?

    Yes: about 40 km of its graded pistes (56%) are difficult.
