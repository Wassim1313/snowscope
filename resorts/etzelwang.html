    About Etzelwang

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Etzelwang sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 415 m and 513 m above sea level. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Sandskigebiet Monte Kaolino, 28 km to the east.

    What there is to ski at Etzelwang

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.7 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 1.1 km (67%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Snow at Etzelwang through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 464 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 7 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late December and late January. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 14 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 19 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Etzelwang gives 98 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 415 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a T-bar.

    Etzelwang: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Etzelwang?

    Aim for late December and late January.

    How much snow does Etzelwang have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    How high is Etzelwang?

    Its highest lift reaches 513 m; the lowest starts at 415 m (98 m of vertical).

    Who is Etzelwang best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Etzelwang have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
