    About Goule

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Goule one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 884 m and 968 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is La Combe Saint-Pierre (7.5 km, south).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Bugnenets-Savagnières (22 km south-east) has about 16 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. For more reliable snow, La Corbatière usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 4 weeks, against 0 here. For higher slopes, Bugnenets-Savagnières climbs to 1,448 m.

    How much snow Goule usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 926 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 14 cm, around 26 January. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 31 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 50 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early February.

    How Goule is laid out

    84 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,084 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Bugnenets-Savagnières, for one, climbs to 1,448 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Le Peu (1,046 m), Le Faux Verger (985 m) and Mont Miroir (982 m).

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Gîte d'accueil (chalet), Hôtel de France (hotel), Le Bellevue (hotel) and Gîte d'étape (hostel).

    Questions about Goule

    When does Goule have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Is there snow at Goule at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 7 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Goule?

    Its highest lift reaches 968 m; the lowest starts at 884 m (84 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Goule?

    Bugnenets-Savagnières, 22 km to the south-east, with about 16 km of pistes.

    How big is Goule?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
