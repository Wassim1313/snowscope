    The snow at Lagazuoi, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Lagazuoi (2,226 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 43 cm; half of the winters sit between 28 cm and 62 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 62 cm; the middle half of winters range from 52 cm to 85 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in April: about 79 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 30 % of winters, and by 1 January in 48 %.

    The deepest winter was 1990-91 (188 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2016-17 (48 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 183 days.

    Skiing Lagazuoi: the practical side

    Lifts: 8. Pistes: 34 km. Heights: 1,725 m at the bottom, 2,727 m at the top (1,002 m of vertical).

    Common questions

    Can you ski Lagazuoi at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 43 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 48 % of 46 winters. Right now: 37 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Lagazuoi?

    April: the snow peaks at about 79 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Lagazuoi get?

    About 79 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-April); between 48 cm in the leanest winter (2016-17) and 188 cm in the biggest (1990-91).

    What altitude is Lagazuoi at?

    Between 1,725 m and 2,727 m, so 1,002 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Lagazuoi?

    Dolomiti Superski, 9.2 km to the west, with about 1,100 km of pistes.

    How big is Lagazuoi?

    About 34 km of pistes and 8 lifts.

    About Lagazuoi

    Lagazuoi is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 34 km of pistes put it at number 182 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,725 m and 2,727 m above sea level. Cortina Tofane is the next resort, 7.2 km away to the east.

    The mountain at Lagazuoi

    1,002 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,725 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Lagazuoi today: about 37 cm up high. The last seven days brought 10 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rifugio Lagazuoi (mountain hut), Hotel al Sasso di Stria (hotel), Punta Sant'anna (hotel) and Rifugio Col Gallina (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
