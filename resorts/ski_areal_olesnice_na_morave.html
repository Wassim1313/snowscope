    About Ski areál Olešnice na Moravě

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Ski areál Olešnice na Moravě one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. The slopes start at 560 m and reach 651 m at the top. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bystré, 9.8 km to the north.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 91 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 560 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
