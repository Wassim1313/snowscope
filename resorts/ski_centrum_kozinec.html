    About Ski centrum Kozinec

    Ski centrum Kozinec is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 4.0 km of pistes put it at number 73 of 312. Its lifts climb from 704 m to 995 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Zábava Hruštín, 4.9 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 3.2 km to ski. Most of the length is red (45%, about 1.4 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    How Ski centrum Kozinec is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 291 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 704 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 2 button lifts, 3 lifts in all.
