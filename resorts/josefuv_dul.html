    About Josefův Důl

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Josefův Důl sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The slopes start at 567 m and reach 729 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Bukovka, 0.2 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.4 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 1.4 km (100%). Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How much snow Josefův Důl usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 648 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 43 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm; a snowy December gave 37 cm, a dry one 0 cm. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 19 February.

    The mountain at Josefův Důl

    Skied top to bottom, Josefův Důl gives 162 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 567 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 4 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
