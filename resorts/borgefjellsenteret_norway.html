    About Borgefjellsenteret

    Borgefjellsenteret is a ski area. Børgefjellsenteret is the next resort, 0.1 km away to the south-east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 566 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 29 weeks of good cover. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early April and mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 94 cm; a snowy December gave 123 cm, a dry one 55 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 107 cm and 209 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 188 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 189 cm, around 14 April.

    Questions about Borgefjellsenteret

    When does Borgefjellsenteret have the most snow?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Borgefjellsenteret at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 94 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
