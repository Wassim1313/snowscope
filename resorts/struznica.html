    The snow right now

    How much snow Strużnica usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 627 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 34 cm in a big one. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 48 cm. A normal season tops out near 22 cm, around 22 February.

    Questions about Strużnica

    When does Strużnica have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Can you ski at Strużnica at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.
