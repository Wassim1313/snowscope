    About Benecko

    Benecko has about 0.2 km of pistes. The slopes start at 878 m and reach 930 m at the top. Skiareál Benecko is the next resort, 0.6 km away to the south-west.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 904 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to early March, about 8 weeks of good cover. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 7 cm to 71 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 44 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 15 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 58 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    The mountain at Benecko

    Skied top to bottom, Benecko gives 52 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 878 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 button lift and 1 rope tow.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Benecko?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    How much snow does Benecko have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 15 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Benecko?

    Between 878 m and 930 m, so 52 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Benecko?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
