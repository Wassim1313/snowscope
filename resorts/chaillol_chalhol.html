    About Chaillol

    Neither huge nor tiny, Chaillol offers about 11 km of pistes (number 456 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,457 m and 1,929 m above sea level. Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes is the next resort, 6.7 km away to the south-east.

    The pistes of Chaillol

    Counting every graded piste, there are 22 runs and around 11 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.5 km, or 41%. With 59% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Chaillol compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 13 km east to Orcières Merlette, with about 49 km of runs. Orcières Merlette has the longer snow season: around 26 weeks of solid cover, compared with 19 at Chaillol. The highest nearby is Orcières Merlette, topping out at 2,731 m.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Chaillol has roughly 10 cm of snow on the ground today. 14 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 42 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 10 cm now.

    How Chaillol is laid out

    The vertical drop is 472 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,457 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 6 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 9 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Vieux Chaillol (3,163 m), 5.6 km to the north-east, about 1,234 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,693 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 35 cm; a snowy December gave 53 cm, a dry one 8 cm. A normal season tops out near 74 cm, around 6 March. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 44 cm to 89 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 52 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Where to stay

    Questions about Chaillol

    When does Chaillol have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Chaillol have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 35 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Chaillol at?

    Between 1,457 m and 1,929 m, so 472 m of vertical drop.

    Is Chaillol good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 6.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Chaillol?

    Orcières Merlette, 13 km to the east, with about 49 km of pistes.
