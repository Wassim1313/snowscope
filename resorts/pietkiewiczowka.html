    About Pietkiewiczówka

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pietkiewiczówka offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 118 of 257 in Central Europe). Its lifts climb from 643 m to 721 m. The closest other ski area is Szrenica (1.5 km, south-west).

    Pietkiewiczówka and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 1.5 km south-west to Szrenica, with about 9.0 km of runs. The highest nearby is Skiareál Horní Domky, topping out at 1,308 m.

    The mountain at Pietkiewiczówka

    Skied top to bottom, Pietkiewiczówka gives 78 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 643 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Skiareál Horní Domky reaches 1,308 m.
