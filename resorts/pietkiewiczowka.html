    About Pietkiewiczówka

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pietkiewiczówka offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 118 of 257 in Central Europe). Its lifts climb from 643 m to 721 m. The closest other ski area is Szrenica (1.5 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Pietkiewiczówka has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.8 km. Most of the length is blue (72%, about 1.3 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Pietkiewiczówka

    Skied top to bottom, Pietkiewiczówka gives 78 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 643 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Skiareál Horní Domky reaches 1,308 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 682 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 49 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 9 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 41 cm). Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 28 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Pietkiewiczówka?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    How much snow does Pietkiewiczówka have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Pietkiewiczówka?

    Between 643 m and 721 m, so 78 m of vertical drop.

    Is Pietkiewiczówka good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Pietkiewiczówka?

    Szrenica, 1.5 km to the south-west, with about 9.0 km of pistes.
