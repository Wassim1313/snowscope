    About Kristberg-Silbertal

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Kristberg-Silbertal sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,430 m up to 1,595 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sonnenkopf, 5.9 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Kristberg-Silbertal and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Lech/Zürs (14 km north-east) has about 120 km of pistes against 4.0 km here. The highest nearby is Lech/Zürs, topping out at 2,436 m.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Kristberg-Silbertal has roughly 48 cm of snow on the ground today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 6 cm by Friday.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 165 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,430 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Ski Arlberg, for one, climbs to 2,642 m.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 hotels: Wohlfühl-Pension Faneskla, Panoramagasthof Kristberg, Hotel Bergkristall and Hotel Silbertal.
