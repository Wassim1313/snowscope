    About Kristberg-Silbertal

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Kristberg-Silbertal sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,430 m up to 1,595 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sonnenkopf, 5.9 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,512 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 59 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 85 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 78 cm to 160 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 125 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 94 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 9 weeks.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 165 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,430 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Ski Arlberg, for one, climbs to 2,642 m. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 2 T-bars. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Above the ski area rises Westliche Eisentalerspitze (2,710 m), 7.4 km to the east, about 1,115 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Kristberg-Silbertal?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Kristberg-Silbertal have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 59 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Kristberg-Silbertal at?

    The top is at 1,595 m and the base at 1,430 m.

    Is Kristberg-Silbertal suitable for families?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Kristberg-Silbertal?

    Lech/Zürs, 14 km to the north-east, with about 120 km of pistes.
