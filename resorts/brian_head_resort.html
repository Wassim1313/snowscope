    About Brian Head Resort

    Brian Head Resort is a mid-sized ski area with about 38 km of pistes, number 54 of 175 in US West by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,929 m up to 3,325 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 69 graded runs, about 38 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 39%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 11 km.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Brian Head Resort hold around 114 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 10 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (20 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 396 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,929 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Getting up the mountain: 8 chairlifts, 2 rope tows and 3 moving carpets, 13 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Brian Head, 3,443 m high and 1.6 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Brian Head Resort usually gets

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 3,127 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 58 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 148 cm in the best years. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 14 cm to 206 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 107 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 140 cm, around 12 March.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Brian Head Resort?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Brian Head Resort at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 58 cm deep.

    What altitude is Brian Head Resort at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,325 m; the lowest starts at 2,929 m (396 m of vertical).

    Does Brian Head Resort have steep runs?

    Yes: about 11 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.

    How big is Brian Head Resort?

    About 38 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
