    About Arieşeni

    Neither huge nor tiny, Arieşeni offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 117 of 312 in the Carpathians). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,185 m up to 1,441 m. Izvorul Minunilor is the next resort, 20 km away to the north.

    What there is to ski at Arieşeni

    Arieşeni has 2 graded runs adding up to about 1.7 km. The largest share is red, with 1.3 km (77%). Intermediate runs make up 77% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The mountain at Arieşeni

    The vertical drop is 256 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,441 m is the third highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 2 drag lifts.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Pârtiile Mărișel: about 3.0 km of pistes, 35 km to the north-east. For higher slopes, Buscat climbs to 1,671 m.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Arieşeni?

    Between 1,185 m and 1,441 m, so 256 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Arieşeni best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 77% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Arieşeni?

    Pârtiile Mărișel, 35 km to the north-east, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Arieşeni have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
