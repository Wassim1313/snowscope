    About Conca dell'Alben

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Conca dell'Alben one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,345 m up to 1,419 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zambla Alta, 2.9 km to the north-east.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,382 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to early April, about 11 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 41 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 11 cm to 72 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 22 cm; a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 4 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 22 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Foppolo-Carona: about 16 km of pistes, 18 km to the north. If snow is the priority, Foppolo-Carona does better, with about 18 weeks of a good base instead of 11. Foppolo-Carona reaches 2,149 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Conca dell'Alben today and the next days

    Up high, Conca dell'Alben has roughly 16 cm of snow on the ground today. 30 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (8 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 74 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,345 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Pizzo Arera (2,512 m), 7.0 km to the north-east, about 1,093 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Conca dell'Alben include Albergo Luisella (hotel), Hotel Carrara (hotel), Baita Carlo Nembrini m.1771 (mountain hut) and Rifugio Monte Alben (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Conca dell'Alben

    What is the best month to ski at Conca dell'Alben?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late January to early April.

    Is there snow at Conca dell'Alben at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Conca dell'Alben at?

    Between 1,345 m and 1,419 m, so 74 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Conca dell'Alben?

    Foppolo-Carona, 18 km to the north, with about 16 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Conca dell'Alben have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
