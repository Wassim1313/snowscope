    About Litmanová

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Litmanová sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 673 m and reach 912 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Arena narciarska Jaworki Homole (5.2 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Litmanová

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 2.4 km. Most of the length is red (83%, about 2.0 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The mountain at Litmanová

    Skied top to bottom, Litmanová gives 239 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 673 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 912 m is the second highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km. All 5 lifts here are button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    Litmanová: common questions

    What altitude is Litmanová at?

    Its highest lift reaches 912 m; the lowest starts at 673 m (239 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Litmanová best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 83% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Litmanová have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
