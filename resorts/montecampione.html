    About Montecampione

    Neither huge nor tiny, Montecampione offers about 20 km of pistes (number 315 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,145 m up to 1,831 m. Val Palot is the next resort, 5.5 km away to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Montecampione

    The piste map lists 29 graded runs, about 20 km in total. Most of the length is red (63%, about 13 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.1 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,488 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 6 January and lasts to 25 April: about 16 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 62 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 23 cm to 101 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 28 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 63 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 42 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    The mountain at Montecampione

    The vertical drop is 686 m: runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts and 1 button lift, 4 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Above the ski area rises Monte Crestoso (2,208 m), 7.8 km to the east, about 377 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Montecampione

    When does Montecampione have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    Is there snow at Montecampione at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 28 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Montecampione?

    The top is at 1,831 m and the base at 1,145 m.

    Who is Montecampione best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 63% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Montecampione have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
