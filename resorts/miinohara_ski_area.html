    About Miinohara

    Miinohara is a ski area. The slopes start at 725 m and reach 783 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Kenmin no Mori (4.4 km, south-west).

    How Miinohara is laid out

    58 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 725 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.

    Questions about Miinohara

    What altitude is Miinohara at?

    Its highest lift reaches 783 m; the lowest starts at 725 m (58 m of vertical).
