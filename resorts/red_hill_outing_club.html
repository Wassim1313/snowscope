    About Red Hill Outing Club

    Compact and local, Red Hill Outing Club has about 1.0 km of runs, small for US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 190 m to 254 m. The closest other ski area is Campton Mountain (20 km, north-west).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 222 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 8 January and keeps it until about 5 April, roughly 13 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 22 cm; a snowy December gave 36 cm, a dry one 5 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 17 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. A normal season tops out near 56 cm, around 17 March. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. Most winters land between 25 cm and 81 cm by mid-February.

    How Red Hill Outing Club compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Waterville Valley Resort: about 31 km of pistes, 26 km to the north. Waterville Valley Resort has the longer snow season: around 17 weeks of solid cover, compared with 13 at Red Hill Outing Club. For higher slopes, Waterville Valley Resort climbs to 1,163 m.

    How Red Hill Outing Club is laid out

    64 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 190 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The only lift is a rope tow. Above the ski area rises Mount Roberts (782 m), 7.4 km to the east, about 528 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 2 hotels: Center Harbor Inn and Sutton House Bed & Breakfast.

    Quick answers

    When does Red Hill Outing Club have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Can you ski at Red Hill Outing Club at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high is Red Hill Outing Club?

    Between 190 m and 254 m, so 64 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Red Hill Outing Club?

    Waterville Valley Resort, 26 km to the north, with about 31 km of pistes.

    How big is Red Hill Outing Club?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
