    What 46 winters say about Pomerelle

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Pomerelle (about 8,189 ft (2,496 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 29 in (74 cm), with a normal range of 15 in (38 cm)–52 in (132 cm).

    Mid-February typically sees 55 in (140 cm), between 31 in (80 cm) and 72 in (182 cm) in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 58 in (148 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 48 % of winters, and by 1 January in 72 %.

    2023-24 was the big one (197 in (500 cm)); 2025-26 the lean one (15 in (39 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs late November to early April (130 days).
