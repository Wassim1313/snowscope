    About Pomerelle

    Pomerelle is a mid-sized ski area with about 21 km of pistes, number 99 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 2,352 m and 2,640 m above sea level.

    What there is to ski at Pomerelle

    In all, 46 marked runs cover roughly 21 km. Most of the length is blue square (52%, about 11 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 5.0 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Pomerelle

    From top to bottom that makes 288 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,352 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Mount Harrison, 2,824 m high and 4.0 km west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,496 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to early April. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 148 cm. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 11 cm and 224 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 74 cm (lean years 15 cm, snowy years 174 cm). By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Pomerelle: common questions

    When does Pomerelle have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from early December to early April.

    How much snow does Pomerelle have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 74 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Pomerelle at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,640 m; the lowest starts at 2,352 m (288 m of vertical).

    Who is Pomerelle best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Pomerelle have?

    About 21 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
