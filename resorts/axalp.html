    About Axalp

    Axalp is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 425 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,423 m and reach 1,937 m at the top. Grindelwald First is the next resort, 6.8 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Axalp

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 12 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 8.1 km, or 65%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 1.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How much snow Axalp usually gets

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,680 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. For about 8 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is usually deepest around 20 March, with about 111 cm. Most winters land between 66 cm and 128 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 49 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 66 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 106 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Axalp today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Axalp hold around 20 cm of snow today. 21 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 37 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 21 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Axalp gives 514 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,423 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 3 T-bars. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Schwarzhoren at 2,927 m, standing 990 m above the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Axalp include Hotel Chemihüttli (hotel), Bärghuus Axalp (hotel), Grandhotel Giessbach (hotel) and Neurüti (mountain hut).

    Axalp: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Axalp?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Axalp have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 49 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Axalp?

    Between 1,423 m and 1,937 m, so 514 m of vertical drop.

    Is Axalp good for beginners?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 8.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Axalp?

    Grindelwald, 14 km to the south-west, with about 88 km of pistes.
