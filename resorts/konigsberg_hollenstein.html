    About Königsberg

    Königsberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 549 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 682 m and reach 1,056 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Hochkar, 12 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Königsberg

    In all, 16 marked runs cover roughly 7.6 km. Most of the length is blue (58%, about 4.4 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Hochkar: about 19 km of pistes, 12 km to the south-east. Hochkar has the longer snow season: around 21 weeks of solid cover, compared with 8 at Königsberg. For higher slopes, Hochkar climbs to 1,788 m.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Königsberg hold around 21 cm of snow today. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 10 cm at mid-mountain).

    The mountain at Königsberg

    From top to bottom that makes 374 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. At 682 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 4 T-bars and 1 button lift, 5 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Hochkogel (1,774 m), 6.7 km to the south, about 718 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Königsberg usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 869 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 January and lasts to 20 March: about 8 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 9 cm and 70 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 40 cm, around 16 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 8 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 53 cm).

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Ziegelau Bio- und Kinderbauernhof (guesthouse), Gästehaus Obstgarten (guesthouse), Kitzhütte (mountain hut) and Siebenhütte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Königsberg have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Königsberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    How high is Königsberg?

    The top is at 1,056 m and the base at 682 m.

    Is Königsberg good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 4.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Königsberg?

    Hochkar, 12 km to the south-east, with about 19 km of pistes.
