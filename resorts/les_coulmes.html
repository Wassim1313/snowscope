    About Les Coulmes

    Compact and local, Les Coulmes has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,064 m to 1,226 m. Méaudre is the next resort, 2.6 km away to the south-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 2.5 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.0 km, or 41%. With 80% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Les Coulmes and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Montagnes de Lans: about 23 km of pistes, 11 km to the east. Autrans has the longer snow season: around 15 weeks of solid cover, compared with 5 at Les Coulmes. For higher slopes, Montagnes de Lans climbs to 1,789 m.

    The mountain at Les Coulmes

    From top to bottom that makes 162 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Roche de Méaudre (1,628 m), 0.7 km to the east, about 402 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Les Coulmes through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,145 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 January and keeps it until about 3 March, roughly 5 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 1 February, with about 33 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 15 cm; a snowy December gave 75 cm, a dry one 0 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 107 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Les Coulmes?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Les Coulmes at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in 8 winters out of ten, about 15 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Coulmes?

    Between 1,064 m and 1,226 m, so 162 m of vertical drop.

    Is Les Coulmes good for beginners?

    Yes: 80% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Les Coulmes?

    Montagnes de Lans, 11 km to the east, with about 23 km of pistes.
