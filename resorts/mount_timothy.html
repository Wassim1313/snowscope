    About Mount Timothy

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mount Timothy offers about 26 km of pistes (number 23 of 53 in Canada West). The slopes start at 1,374 m and reach 1,650 m at the top.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 36 graded runs, about 26 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 14 km, or 54%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 5.5 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,512 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 November and keeps it until about 7 May, roughly 24 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 80 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 31 March, with about 98 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 58 cm to 122 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 64 cm, from 36 cm in a poor year to 97 cm in a big one.

    The mountain at Mount Timothy

    Skied top to bottom, Mount Timothy gives 276 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,374 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    Mount Timothy: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Mount Timothy?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    How much snow does Mount Timothy have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 64 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Mount Timothy?

    The top is at 1,650 m and the base at 1,374 m.

    Who is Mount Timothy best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 54% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Mount Timothy?

    About 26 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
