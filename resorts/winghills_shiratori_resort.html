    About WINGHILLS Shiratori Resort

    WINGHILLS Shiratori Resort is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 11 km of pistes put it at number 67 of 385. Its lifts climb from 875 m to 1,339 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Snow Wave Park Shiratori Kogen, 2.1 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 11 km to ski. The largest share is green, with 3.8 km (35%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.3 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 464 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,075 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola and 3 chairlifts, 4 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns.
