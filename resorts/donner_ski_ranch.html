    About Donner Ski Ranch

    Neither huge nor tiny, Donner Ski Ranch offers about 18 km of pistes (number 104 of 175 in US West). The ski area spans the mountain from 2,145 m up to 2,364 m. Its nearest neighbour is Boreal, 1.5 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 65 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Donner Ski Ranch. 20 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 59 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 15 cm).

    Runs by difficulty

    Donner Ski Ranch has 47 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. The largest share is blue square, with 7.2 km (40%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.5 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,254 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March. Most winters land between 68 cm and 284 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 117 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 194 cm. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 81 cm (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 174 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Donner Ski Ranch gives 219 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,145 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. To get up there are 6 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Castle Peak at 2,773 m, standing 409 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Donner Ski Ranch

    When does Donner Ski Ranch have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Donner Ski Ranch have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 81 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Donner Ski Ranch?

    The top is at 2,364 m and the base at 2,145 m.

    Does Donner Ski Ranch have steep runs?

    Yes: about 4.5 km of its graded pistes (25%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Donner Ski Ranch have?

    About 18 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
