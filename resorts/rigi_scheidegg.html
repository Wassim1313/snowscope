    About Rigi-Scheidegg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Rigi-Scheidegg offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 669 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,014 m and reach 1,647 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Rigi, 4.4 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,330 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 14 December and keeps it until about 22 May, roughly 23 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 80 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 43 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 56 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 68 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 98 cm.

    The mountain at Rigi-Scheidegg

    The vertical drop is 633 m: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,647 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 cable cars and 1 T-bar. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Rigi (1,798 m), 4.9 km to the north-west, about 151 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Rigi-Scheidegg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late May.

    Is there snow at Rigi-Scheidegg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 43 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Rigi-Scheidegg at?

    Between 1,014 m and 1,647 m, so 633 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Rigi-Scheidegg?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rigi-Scheidegg?

    Stoos, 12 km to the south-east, with about 19 km of pistes.
