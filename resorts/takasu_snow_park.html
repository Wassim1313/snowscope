    About Takasu Snow Park

    With some 17 km of runs, Takasu Snow Park belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 966 m up to 1,520 m. The closest other ski area is Dynaland (1.0 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Takasu Snow Park

    The piste map lists 17 graded runs, about 16 km in total. Green runs make up the biggest part: 7.8 km, or 47%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Experts can count on some 3.8 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 554 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The base at 966 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,520 m. To get up there are 1 gondola and 3 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.
