    About Niederthai

    Compact and local, Niederthai has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,528 m and reach 1,667 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Gries, 7.6 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Niederthai and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Hochoetz-Kühtai (9.6 km north) has about 54 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. For more reliable snow, Hochoetz-Kühtai usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 28 weeks, against 14 here. For higher slopes, Hochoetz-Kühtai climbs to 2,507 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 1.5 km in total. Most of the length is green (beginner) (50%, about 0.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Niederthai hold around 14 cm of snow today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 16 cm by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (12 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    139 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,528 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. All 2 lifts here are button lifts. Above the ski area rises Strahlkogel (3,288 m), 4.4 km to the south-east, about 1,621 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 14 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,598 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to late April. Most winters land between 30 cm and 60 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 24 cm, with 36 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 46 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 33 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Niederthai: common questions

    When does Niederthai have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late April.

    Is there snow at Niederthai at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Niederthai?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,667 m; the lowest starts at 1,528 m (139 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Niederthai?

    Yes: 76% of the graded pistes, about 1.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Niederthai?

    Hochoetz-Kühtai, 9.6 km to the north, with about 54 km of pistes.
