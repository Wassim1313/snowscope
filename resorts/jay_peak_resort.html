    About Jay Peak Resort

    With some 41 km of runs, Jay Peak Resort belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of US Northeast. The ski area spans the mountain from 560 m up to 1,166 m. Its nearest neighbour is Mont Sutton, 20 km north, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Jay Peak Resort

    The piste map lists 79 graded runs, about 41 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 21 km, or 50%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 8.9 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 606 m: runs of a moderate length. At 560 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,166 m. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 1 cable car, 6 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Jay Peak (1,177 m), Big Jay (1,115 m) and North Jay Peak (1,048 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 16 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 863 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to early April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 34 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 9 cm, snowy years 59 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 34 cm and 97 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 74 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 6 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height.

    Questions about Jay Peak Resort

    When is the best time to ski at Jay Peak Resort?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    How much snow does Jay Peak Resort have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    What altitude is Jay Peak Resort at?

    The top is at 1,166 m and the base at 560 m.

    What level of skier is Jay Peak Resort best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Jay Peak Resort have?

    About 41 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
