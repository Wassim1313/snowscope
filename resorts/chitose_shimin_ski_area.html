    About Chitose Shimin

    Chitose Shimin has about 0.3 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 27 m and 57 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Eniwa City, 11 km to the north-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 30 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 27 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Fu's snow area reaches 544 m. The only lift is a chairlift.
