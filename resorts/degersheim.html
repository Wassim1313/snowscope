    About Degersheim

    Neither huge nor tiny, Degersheim offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 648 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 808 m up to 1,051 m. Schwellbrunn is the next resort, 2.9 km away to the south-east.

    The pistes of Degersheim

    Degersheim has 10 graded runs adding up to about 5.3 km. Most of the length is blue (60%, about 3.2 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 243 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 808 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Hochhamm, 1,274 m high and 6.2 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 930 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the typical depth is 8 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 17 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 5 cm to 34 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 24 cm).

    Degersheim: common questions

    When does Degersheim have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    Is there snow at Degersheim at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Degersheim?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,051 m; the lowest starts at 808 m (243 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Degersheim?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Degersheim?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
