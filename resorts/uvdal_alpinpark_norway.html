    About Uvdal Alpinpark

    Uvdal Alpinpark is a ski area. The closest other ski area is Uvdal Alpinpark (0.1 km, south-west).

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,086 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 November and lasts to 25 May: about 28 weeks. For about 6 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 100 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 60 cm (lean years 38 cm, snowy years 90 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 72 cm to 123 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 25 March, with about 107 cm.

    Uvdal Alpinpark: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Uvdal Alpinpark?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Uvdal Alpinpark at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 60 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
