    About Hickory

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hickory offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 59 of 93 in US Northeast). The ski area spans the mountain from 209 m up to 540 m. The closest other ski area is Double H Ranch (Hidden Valley) (11 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Hickory

    Hickory has 14 graded runs adding up to about 5.2 km. Most of the length is black diamond (33%, about 1.7 km). Good skiers will like it here; 33% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 1.7 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Hickory

    The vertical drop is 331 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 209 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 540 m is the third highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Black Spruce Mountain (716 m), 7.1 km to the south-east, about 176 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 374 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to mid-March, about 6 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 37 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 14 cm and 70 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 4 cm in a poor year to 37 cm in a big one.

    Hickory: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hickory?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Hickory at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 16 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Hickory?

    The top is at 540 m and the base at 209 m.

    Does Hickory have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.7 km of its graded pistes (33%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Hickory have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
