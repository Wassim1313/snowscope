    About Superstacja Czarnów-Ski Pisarzow

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Superstacja Czarnów-Ski Pisarzow sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 572 m up to 712 m. Strużnica is the next resort, 5.8 km away to the north-west.

    The pistes of Superstacja Czarnów-Ski Pisarzow

    The piste map lists 3 graded runs, about 2.2 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.5 km, or 69%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 140 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 572 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow, 3 lifts in all.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 642 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 49 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 23 cm, around 22 February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 8 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 34 cm in a big one.
