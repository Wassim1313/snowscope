    About Ski Geilo

    Around 35 km of marked runs place Ski Geilo among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 13 by length. Its lifts climb from 779 m to 1,163 m. The closest other ski area is Geilo (1.9 km, south).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 70 runs and around 35 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 13 km (37%). With 62% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 971 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 November and keeps it until about 28 May, roughly 28 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 104 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 110 cm, around 21 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 76 cm and 128 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 64 cm, with 92 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 384 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,163 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 20 lifts: 4 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 9 button lifts and 4 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Oddnakk (1,192 m), Havsdalshovda (1,173 m) and Budalshøgdin (1,129 m).

    Questions about Ski Geilo

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Geilo?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Ski Geilo at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 64 cm deep.

    What altitude is Ski Geilo at?

    Between 779 m and 1,163 m, so 384 m of vertical drop.

    Is Ski Geilo suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 22 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ski Geilo have?

    About 35 km of pistes and 20 lifts.
