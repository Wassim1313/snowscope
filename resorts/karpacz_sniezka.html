    When the snow is good

    Count on about 3 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 787 m) in an ordinary winter, from early February to early March. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 34 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and late February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 57 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm, with 43 cm in the best years.

    How Karpacz / Śnieżka is laid out

    The vertical drop is 4 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 785 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Karpacz / Śnieżka: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Karpacz / Śnieżka?

    Aim for late January and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Can you ski at Karpacz / Śnieżka at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    What altitude is Karpacz / Śnieżka at?

    Between 785 m and 789 m, so 4 m of vertical drop.
