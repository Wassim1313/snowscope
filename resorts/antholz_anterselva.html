    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 358 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Brunnalm / St. Jakob reaches 2,682 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hochgall - Monte Collalto at 3,436 m, standing 1,835 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,422 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 11 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 14 cm to 59 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 19 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 42 cm). A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 16 February.

    Antholz: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Antholz?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Is there snow at Antholz at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 19 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Antholz at?

    Between 1,243 m and 1,601 m, so 358 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Antholz best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Antholz?

    Klausberg, 18 km to the north-west, with about 60 km of pistes.
