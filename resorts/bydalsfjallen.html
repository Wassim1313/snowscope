    About Bydalsfjällen

    About 48 km of pistes make Bydalsfjällen one of the ten biggest ski areas of Scandinavia (number 5). Its lifts climb from 583 m to 1,002 m. The closest other ski area is Gräftåvallen (13 km, south-east).

    What there is to ski at Bydalsfjällen

    The piste map lists 51 graded runs, about 48 km in total. Most of the length is red (46%, about 22 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 2.9 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Bydalsfjällen gives 419 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,002 m. Its 13 lifts break down into 2 button lifts and 11 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Österfjället (1,211 m), 8.0 km to the south-east, about 209 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 792 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to early May. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 76 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 73 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 51 cm and 94 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 44 cm (lean years 30 cm, snowy years 69 cm).

    Bydalsfjällen: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Bydalsfjällen?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Can you ski at Bydalsfjällen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 44 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Bydalsfjällen at?

    Between 583 m and 1,002 m, so 419 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Bydalsfjällen?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 23 km, are easy.

    How big is Bydalsfjällen?

    About 48 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
