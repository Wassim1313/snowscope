    About Koziniec SKI

    Neither huge nor tiny, Koziniec SKI offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 95 of 312 in the Carpathians). Its lifts climb from 771 m to 896 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Grapa SKI Czarna Góra, 2.3 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Koziniec SKI

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 2.6 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.0 km (78%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Koziniec SKI

    Skied top to bottom, Koziniec SKI gives 125 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 771 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
