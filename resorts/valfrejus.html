    About Valfréjus

    With some 36 km of runs, Valfréjus belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,552 m and 2,724 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is La Norma, 5.4 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 41 graded runs, about 36 km in total. Most of the length is blue (54%, about 20 km). With 56% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. 0.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Valfréjus

    From top to bottom that makes 1,172 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,552 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 9 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Rocca Bernauda / Roche Bernaude, 3,222 m high and 6.9 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,138 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 65 cm (lean years 35 cm, snowy years 97 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 120 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 96 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 80 cm and 140 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Valfréjus: common questions

    When does Valfréjus have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Valfréjus have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 65 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Valfréjus?

    The top is at 2,724 m and the base at 1,552 m.

    Is Valfréjus suitable for families?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 20 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Valfréjus have?

    About 36 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
