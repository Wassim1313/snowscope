    The snow at Valfréjus, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Valfréjus (2,138 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 65 cm; half of the winters sit between 46 cm and 84 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 114 cm; the middle half of winters range from 89 cm to 130 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 120 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 50 % of winters, and by 1 January in 74 %.

    The deepest winter was 2017-18 (188 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2004-05 (92 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late May, about 178 days.

    Questions about Valfréjus

    Can you ski Valfréjus at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 65 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 74 % of 46 winters. Right now: 119 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Valfréjus?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 120 cm). For a sure base, late November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Valfréjus?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 120 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 188 cm (2017-18), the thinnest 92 cm (2004-05).

    How high do the lifts go at Valfréjus?

    The top is at 2,724 m and the base at 1,552 m.

    How many km of pistes does Valfréjus have?

    About 36 km of pistes and 9 lifts.

    About Valfréjus

    With some 36 km of runs, Valfréjus belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,552 m and 2,724 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is La Norma, 5.4 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.
