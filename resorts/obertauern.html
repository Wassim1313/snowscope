    About Obertauern

    With some 87 km of runs, Obertauern belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,628 m to 2,297 m. Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl is the next resort, 9.8 km away to the north-west.

    Snow at Obertauern through a normal winter

    At about 1,962 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 29 November and lasts to 18 May: about 25 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 55 cm; a snowy December gave 92 cm, a dry one 23 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 120 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 70 cm to 140 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 8 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 96 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    How Obertauern is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Obertauern gives 669 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,628 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 2 chair-gondolas, 17 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Gödernierkarkopf (2,595 m), 7.9 km to the south-west, about 298 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Obertauern?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Obertauern at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 55 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Obertauern?

    The announced opening day is 20 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 2 May 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Obertauern?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,297 m; the lowest starts at 1,628 m (669 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Obertauern?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 40 km, are easy.
