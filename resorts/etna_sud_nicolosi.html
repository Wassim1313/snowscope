    About Etna Sud/Nicolosi

    Neither huge nor tiny, Etna Sud/Nicolosi offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 19 of 40 in the Apennines). Skiing here happens between 1,928 m and 2,597 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Etna Nord, 8.9 km north, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Etna Sud/Nicolosi

    Skied top to bottom, Etna Sud/Nicolosi gives 669 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 1,928 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 3 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Etna Sud/Nicolosi has 8 graded runs adding up to about 6.1 km. The largest share is red, with 5.0 km (82%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.
