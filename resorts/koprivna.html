    About Kopřivná

    Kopřivná is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 135 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Its lifts climb from 695 m to 879 m. The closest other ski area is Skicentrum Pawlin (2.1 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 1.9 km in total. Most of the length is blue (69%, about 1.3 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Kopřivná today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Kopřivná hold around 22 cm of snow today. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 184 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 695 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    Quick answers

    How high is Kopřivná?

    Its highest lift reaches 879 m; the lowest starts at 695 m (184 m of vertical).

    Is Kopřivná suitable for families?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Kopřivná?

    Skicentrum Pawlin, 2.1 km to the south-west, with about 4.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Kopřivná have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
