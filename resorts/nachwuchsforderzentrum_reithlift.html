    How Nachwuchsförderzentrum "Reithlif is laid out

    119 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 832 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Hahnenkamm, for one, climbs to 1,879 m.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Villa Ludwig (hotel), Martha-Maria-Hotel Hohenschwangau (hotel), Alpe Reith (mountain hut) and Hotel Weinbauer (hotel).
