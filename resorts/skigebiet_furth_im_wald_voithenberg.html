    About Furth im Wald

    Furth im Wald is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 92 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Skiing here happens between 616 m and 890 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Freizeitzentrum Althütte, 2.1 km to the west.

    What there is to ski at Furth im Wald

    Furth im Wald has 3 graded runs adding up to about 2.7 km. Black runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 45%. With 45% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.2 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 274 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 616 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a T-bar.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 753 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and late January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 8 cm, with 34 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 19 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 51 cm.

    Furth im Wald: common questions

    When does Furth im Wald have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and late January.

    Is there snow at Furth im Wald at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 8 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Furth im Wald?

    The top is at 890 m and the base at 616 m.

    Does Furth im Wald have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.2 km of its graded pistes (45%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Furth im Wald?

    Großer Arber, 34 km to the south-east, with about 11 km of pistes.
