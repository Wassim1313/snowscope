    About Ovindoli

    Only 3 of the 40 ski areas of the Apennines are larger than Ovindoli, which has some 18 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 1,426 m and 2,054 m above sea level. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Campo Felice, 6.7 km to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Ovindoli

    Counting every graded piste, there are 38 runs and around 18 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 8.4 km (46%). Intermediate runs make up 46% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 5.8 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Ovindoli

    628 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,426 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 12 lifts: 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts and 4 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts.
