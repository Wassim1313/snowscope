    About Plateau de Retord

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Plateau de Retord sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 996 m up to 1,228 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Terre Ronde, 12 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 7.1 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.9 km, or 69%. With 84% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Plateau de Retord

    Skied top to bottom, Plateau de Retord gives 232 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 996 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 9 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Crêt du Nû at 1,351 m, standing 123 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Plateau de Retord through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,112 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 27 cm, around 21 January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 65 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 107 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Plateau de Retord: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Plateau de Retord?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Is there snow at Plateau de Retord at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Plateau de Retord?

    Between 996 m and 1,228 m, so 232 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Plateau de Retord?

    Yes: 84% of the graded pistes, about 6.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Plateau de Retord have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
