    About Gemsstock

    With roughly 18 km of runs, Gemsstock sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,437 m and 2,958 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Andermatt-Sedrun, 4.5 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,521 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,437 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
