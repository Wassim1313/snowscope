    About Gemsstock

    With roughly 18 km of runs, Gemsstock sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,437 m and 2,958 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Andermatt-Sedrun, 4.5 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,521 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,437 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 2 cable cars, 1 chairlift and 5 T-bars. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The skyline is dominated by Müeterlishorn, 3,066 m high and 7.6 km west of the resort.

    Snow at Gemsstock through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,198 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 66 cm, with 94 cm in the best years. For about 13 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is usually deepest around 6 April, with about 133 cm. Most winters land between 75 cm and 147 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 127 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    What there is to ski at Gemsstock

    Gemsstock has 35 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. The largest share is red, with 9.9 km (54%). Intermediate runs make up 54% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.6 km.

    Questions about Gemsstock

    What is the best month to ski at Gemsstock?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Gemsstock have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 66 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Gemsstock?

    Between 1,437 m and 2,958 m, so 1,521 m of vertical drop.

    Is Gemsstock good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 6.6 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How big is Gemsstock?

    About 18 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
