    About Le Reposoir

    Le Reposoir is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 978 m and 1,081 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf, 2.3 km to the west.

    Le Reposoir and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Le Grand Massif (12 km east) has about 174 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Le Grand Massif does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 12. Le Grand Massif reaches 2,484 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How Le Reposoir is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Le Reposoir gives 103 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 978 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Le Grand Massif reaches 2,484 m. All 3 lifts here are button lifts. Above the ski area rises Pointe Percée (2,750 m), 6.1 km to the south, about 1,669 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,030 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to late March, about 12 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 23 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 3 cm in a poor year to 43 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 20 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. A normal season tops out near 47 cm, around 18 February. Most winters land between 20 cm and 70 cm by mid-February.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Le Reposoir include La Chartreuse (hotel), Le Chalet "Saint-Joseph, La Balmette" (chalet), Gîte du Passant (chalet) and Gite de Séjour - Les Cyclamens (chalet).

    Questions about Le Reposoir

    When is the best time to ski at Le Reposoir?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Can you ski at Le Reposoir at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 23 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Le Reposoir at?

    Between 978 m and 1,081 m, so 103 m of vertical drop.

    Is there a larger resort near Le Reposoir?

    Le Grand Massif, 12 km to the east, with about 174 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Le Reposoir have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
