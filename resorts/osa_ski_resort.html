    About Osa

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Osa sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 705 m up to 934 m. Its nearest neighbour is Geihoku Kokusai, 4.4 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    How Osa is laid out

    229 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 705 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Osorakan Snow Park, for one, climbs to 1,274 m. All 5 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    How high is Osa?

    The top is at 934 m and the base at 705 m.

    Is Osa suitable for families?

    Is there a larger resort near Osa?

    How many km of pistes does Osa have?
