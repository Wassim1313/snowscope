    About Storklinten

    Storklinten is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 82 m and 267 m above sea level.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 174 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 52 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 66 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 46 cm to 82 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 38 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 56 cm in the best years.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. Most of the length is red (100%, about 1.2 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Storklinten

    Skied top to bottom, Storklinten gives 185 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Storklinten today: about 32 cm up high. The last seven days brought 6 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 23 cm at mid-mountain).

    Questions about Storklinten

    What is the best month to ski at Storklinten?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Storklinten at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 38 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Storklinten?

    The top is at 267 m and the base at 82 m.

    What level of skier is Storklinten best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Storklinten?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
