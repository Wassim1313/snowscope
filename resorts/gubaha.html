    About Губаха (Gubaha)

    Губаха (Gubaha) is one of the bigger ski areas of Russia: about 8.0 km of pistes put it at number 18 of 86. Skiing here happens between 181 m and 446 m above sea level.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 8.4 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.6 km, or 55%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Губаха (Gubaha) gives 265 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 8 drag lifts, 9 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    How high do the lifts go at Губаха (Gubaha)?
