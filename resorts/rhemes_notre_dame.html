    About Rhemes

    Neither huge nor tiny, Rhemes offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 630 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,687 m and 2,093 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Valgrisenche Ski (7.2 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Rhemes

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 6.2 km in total. Most of the length is blue (39%, about 2.4 km). Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 1.9 km of difficult runs.

    Rhemes and the resorts around it

    This week's snow

    Up high, Rhemes has roughly 12 cm of snow on the ground today. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 64 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 19 cm).

    The mountain at Rhemes

    406 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,687 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Tignes, reach 3,447 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grande Rousse Nord at 3,607 m, standing 1,514 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,890 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 22 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 40 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 44 cm, with 66 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 78 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 54 cm to 95 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Rhemes: common questions

    When does Rhemes have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Rhemes at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 44 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Rhemes?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,093 m; the lowest starts at 1,687 m (406 m of vertical).

    Is Rhemes good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.9 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rhemes?

    Tignes, 20 km to the south-west, with about 211 km of pistes.
