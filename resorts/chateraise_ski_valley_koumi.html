    About Chateraise Ski Valley Koumi

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Chateraise Ski Valley Koumi sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Skiing here happens between 1,408 m and 1,770 m above sea level. Yachiho Kōgen is the next resort, 3.2 km away to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Chateraise Ski Valley Koumi

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 5.4 km in total. The largest share is red, with 1.9 km (34%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Chateraise Ski Valley Koumi gives 362 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,408 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. All 5 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
