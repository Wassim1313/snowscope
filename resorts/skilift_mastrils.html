    About Skilift Mastrils

    Compact and local, Skilift Mastrils has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 719 m to 948 m. St. Margrethenberg is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the north-west.

    How Skilift Mastrils compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 7.7 km west to Pizol, with about 40 km of runs. For higher slopes, Hochwang climbs to 2,282 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 229 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 719 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hochwang reaches 2,282 m.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Skilift Mastrils include Zur Tanne (hotel), Schweizerhof (hotel), Naturfreunde Haus (mountain hut) and Grand Hotel Quellenhof (hotel).
