    About Skilift Mastrils

    Compact and local, Skilift Mastrils has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 719 m to 948 m. St. Margrethenberg is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.9 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.0 km, or 53%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 229 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 719 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hochwang reaches 2,282 m. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Muntaluna, 2,422 m high and 7.2 km west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 834 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 47 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 15 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 28 cm, a dry one 4 cm. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 17 February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 18 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Skilift Mastrils: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Skilift Mastrils?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    How much snow does Skilift Mastrils have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 15 cm deep.

    How high is Skilift Mastrils?

    Its highest lift reaches 948 m; the lowest starts at 719 m (229 m of vertical).

    Is Skilift Mastrils suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilift Mastrils?

    Pizol, 7.7 km to the west, with about 40 km of pistes.
