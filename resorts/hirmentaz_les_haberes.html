    The snow at Hirmentaz, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Hirmentaz (about 1,272 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 44 cm; half of the winters sit between 23 cm and 71 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 76 cm (52 cm–97 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 80 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 35 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 48 %.

    The deepest winter was 1983-84 (203 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1989-90 (48 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to mid-May, about 159 days.

    About Hirmentaz

    With some 26 km of runs, Hirmentaz belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 940 m up to 1,604 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Massif des Brasses, 5.9 km to the south-west.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Les Gets-Morzine (16 km south-east) has about 95 km of pistes against 26 km here. For higher slopes, Les Gets-Morzine climbs to 1,985 m.
