    About Uonuma City Ohara

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Uonuma City Ohara one of the smaller places to ski in Japan. Its lifts climb from 389 m to 644 m. Suhara is the next resort, 11 km away to the west.

    How Uonuma City Ohara is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Uonuma City Ohara gives 255 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 389 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.
