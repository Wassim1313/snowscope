    About Uonuma City Ohara

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Uonuma City Ohara one of the smaller places to ski in Japan. Its lifts climb from 389 m to 644 m. Suhara is the next resort, 11 km away to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.8 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 64%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Uonuma City Ohara is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Uonuma City Ohara gives 255 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 389 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Questions about Uonuma City Ohara

    What altitude is Uonuma City Ohara at?

    Is Uonuma City Ohara good for beginners?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Uonuma City Ohara?

    Suhara, 11 km to the west, with about 6.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Uonuma City Ohara have?
