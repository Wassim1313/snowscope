    About Lauenen

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Lauenen one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,239 m up to 1,405 m. Its nearest neighbour is Wispile, 4.1 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Lauenen

    Lauenen has 2 graded runs adding up to about 2.0 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.0 km, or 100%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Up high, Lauenen has roughly 2 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 16 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 51 cm by Friday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 6 cm).

    How Lauenen is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Lauenen gives 166 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Glacier 3000, for one, climbs to 2,931 m. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Wildhorn, 3,250 m high and 7.7 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 10 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,322 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to late March. The snow is usually deepest around 18 February, with about 45 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 22 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Most winters land between 25 cm and 64 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 37 cm).

    Lauenen: common questions

    When does Lauenen have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    How much snow does Lauenen have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    What altitude is Lauenen at?

    Between 1,239 m and 1,405 m, so 166 m of vertical drop.

    Is Lauenen suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Lauenen?

    Schönried, 11 km to the north, with about 59 km of pistes.
