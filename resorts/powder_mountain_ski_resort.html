    The snow at Powder Mountain, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Powder Mountain (7,822 ft (2,384 m)), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 31 in (78 cm); half of the winters sit between 21 in (54 cm) and 46 in (116 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 56 in (143 cm) (43 in (110 cm)–75 in (191 cm)).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 63 in (160 cm).

    59 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 83 % by 1 January.

    2010-11 was the big one (161 in (408 cm)); 2025-26 the lean one (31 in (80 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-May, about 177 days.

    Today's 15 in (39 cm) compares with a usual 14 in (36 cm) for 28 November.

    Powder Mountain: quick answers

    Can you ski Powder Mountain at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 31 in (78 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 83 % of 46 winters. Right now: 22 in (56 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Powder Mountain?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 63 in (160 cm)). For a sure base, mid-November to mid-May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Powder Mountain?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 63 in (160 cm) in mid-March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 161 in (408 cm) (2010-11), the thinnest 31 in (80 cm) (2025-26).

    When does the ski season start at Powder Mountain?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026.

    What altitude is Powder Mountain at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,745 m; the lowest starts at 2,023 m (722 m of vertical).

    Is Powder Mountain good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 50 km of its graded pistes (38%) are difficult.

    The mountain at Powder Mountain

    Skied top to bottom, Powder Mountain gives 722 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,023 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its top at 2,745 m is the third highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 11 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 rope tows, 14 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The skyline is dominated by James Peak, 2,869 m high and 3.1 km north-west of the resort.
