    About Powder Mountain

    Only 5 of the 175 ski areas of US West are larger than Powder Mountain, which has some 130 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 2,023 m to 2,745 m. The closest other ski area is Nordic Valley Resort (12 km, south-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 154 runs and around 130 km to ski. The largest share is black diamond, with 50 km (38%). With 38% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 50 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Powder Mountain through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,384 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 25 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 123 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 68 cm and 207 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 160 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 78 cm; a snowy December gave 170 cm, a dry one 32 cm. For about 17 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    The mountain at Powder Mountain

    Skied top to bottom, Powder Mountain gives 722 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,023 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its top at 2,745 m is the third highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 11 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 rope tows, 14 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The skyline is dominated by James Peak, 2,869 m high and 3.1 km north-west of the resort.

    Questions about Powder Mountain

    When is the best time to ski at Powder Mountain?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Powder Mountain have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 78 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Powder Mountain?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026.

    What altitude is Powder Mountain at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,745 m; the lowest starts at 2,023 m (722 m of vertical).

    Is Powder Mountain good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 50 km of its graded pistes (38%) are difficult.
