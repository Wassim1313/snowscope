    About Wujiashan

    Compact and local, Wujiashan has about 1.0 km of runs, small for China. The ski area spans the mountain from 337 m up to 493 m. The closest other ski area is Songhua Lake (11 km, south-west).

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Beidahu: about 54 km of pistes, 38 km to the south. The highest nearby is Beidahu, topping out at 1,395 m.

    The mountain at Wujiashan

    Skied top to bottom, Wujiashan gives 156 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 337 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.
