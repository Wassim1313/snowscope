    About Lopušná Dolina

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Lopušná Dolina sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 770 m and reach 924 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Skitatry Zadná Lopušná dolina (0.7 km, south-west).

    What there is to ski at Lopušná Dolina

    Lopušná Dolina has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.5 km. Most of the length is red (100%, about 1.5 km). Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How Lopušná Dolina is laid out

    The vertical drop is 154 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 770 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 4 lifts here are button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    What level of skier is Lopušná Dolina best for?
