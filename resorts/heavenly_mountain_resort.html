    What 46 winters say about Heavenly

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Heavenly (about 8,304 ft (2,531 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 25 in (64 cm); half of the winters sit between 12 in (30 cm) and 39 in (99 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 47 in (119 cm) (34 in (86 cm)–63 in (159 cm)).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 53 in (135 cm).

    By 15 December 48 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 65 %.

    Best winter on record: 2022-23, peaking at 120 in (305 cm). Worst: 2014-15, which never passed 24 in (61 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs late November to mid-May (167 days).

    Today's 24 in (61 cm) compares with a usual 3.5 in (9 cm) for 22 November.

    About Heavenly

    Heavenly is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 85 km of pistes put it at number 19 of 175. Its lifts climb from 2,025 m to 3,037 m. The closest other ski area is Sierra-at-Tahoe (21 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 138 graded runs, about 85 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (64%, about 55 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 23 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,012 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,025 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 3,037 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 3 funiculars, 1 gondola, 18 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets, 27 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Monument Peak (3,068 m), Trimmer Peak (3,016 m) and East Peak (2,899 m).

    Snow at Heavenly through a normal winter

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,531 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 64 cm, from 9 cm in a poor year to 129 cm in a big one. For about 10 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 135 cm, around 15 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 83 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 66 cm and 204 cm.
