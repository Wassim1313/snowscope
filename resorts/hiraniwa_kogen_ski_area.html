    About Hiraniwa Kogen

    Hiraniwa Kogen is a ski area. The slopes start at 664 m and reach 911 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Sonei Kunohe, 15 km north-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Hiraniwa Kogen is laid out

    The vertical drop is 247 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 664 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.
