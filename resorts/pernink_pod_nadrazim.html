    About Pernink

    Pernink has about 0.3 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 850 m and 900 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Pernink (0.6 km, south-east).

    How Pernink is laid out

    50 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 850 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The only lift is a button lift.
