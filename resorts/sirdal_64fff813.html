    Snow at Sirdal through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 772 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 5 December and lasts to 25 May: about 25 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 94 cm and 217 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 160 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 65 cm; a snowy December gave 111 cm, a dry one 24 cm. A normal season tops out near 177 cm, around 15 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March.

    Quick answers

    When does Sirdal have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Sirdal have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 65 cm deep.
