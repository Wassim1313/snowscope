    About Miyagi Zao Eboshi Resort

    Miyagi Zao Eboshi Resort is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 13 km of pistes put it at number 45 of 385. Its lifts climb from 687 m to 1,361 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Miyagi Zao Sumikawasu Snow Park, 2.4 km to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Miyagi Zao Eboshi Resort has 23 graded runs adding up to about 13 km. The largest share is beginner, with 8.2 km (62%). With 84% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Miyagi Zao Eboshi Resort gives 674 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 687 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. All 8 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Miyagi Zao Eboshi Resort?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,361 m; the lowest starts at 687 m (674 m of vertical).

    Is Miyagi Zao Eboshi Resort good for beginners?

    Yes: 84% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How big is Miyagi Zao Eboshi Resort?

    About 13 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
