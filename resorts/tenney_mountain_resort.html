    About Tenney

    Neither huge nor tiny, Tenney offers about 20 km of pistes (number 33 of 93 in US Northeast). Its lifts climb from 225 m to 653 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Campton Mountain, 19 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Tenney gives 428 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 225 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
