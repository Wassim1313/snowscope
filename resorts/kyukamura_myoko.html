    About Kyukamura Myoko

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kyukamura Myoko offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 252 of 385 in Japan). The slopes start at 722 m and reach 878 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Seki Onsen, 1.3 km to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Kyukamura Myoko has 4 graded runs adding up to about 2.6 km. Most of the length is green (82%, about 2.1 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Kyukamura Myoko is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 156 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 922 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Myoko Suginohara, for one, climbs to 1,857 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
