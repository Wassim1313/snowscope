    About Forni di Sopra

    With roughly 13 km of runs, Forni di Sopra sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 882 m to 2,068 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sella Ciampigotto, 5.3 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Forni di Sopra

    1,186 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,082 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 2,068 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.

    Forni di Sopra today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Forni di Sopra hold around 8 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 10 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Quick answers

    How high is Forni di Sopra?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,068 m; the lowest starts at 882 m (1,186 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Forni di Sopra have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
