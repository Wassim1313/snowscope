    Taos Ski Valley in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Taos Ski Valley (10,584 ft (3,226 m)), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 21 in (54 cm), with a normal range of 9.1 in (23 cm)–39 in (99 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 46 in (117 cm) (19 in (49 cm)–77 in (195 cm)).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 55 in (140 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 39 % of winters, and by 1 January in 59 %.

    The deepest winter was 1982-83 (174 in (442 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (16 in (41 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to early May, about 168 days.

    Today's 6.7 in (17 cm) compares with a usual 8.7 in (22 cm) for 26 November.

    About Taos Ski Valley

    Taos Ski Valley is a mid-sized ski area with about 43 km of pistes, number 45 of 175 in US West by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,850 m up to 3,603 m. The closest other ski area is Red River (14 km, north).

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 3,226 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 December and lasts to 3 May: about 20 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early January and late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 20 cm to 243 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the typical depth is 79 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For about 6 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 140 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 54 cm (lean years 9 cm, snowy years 141 cm).

    The pistes of Taos Ski Valley

    In all, 100 marked runs cover roughly 42 km. Most of the length is green circle (40%, about 17 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 35% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 15 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Taos Ski Valley gives 753 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,850 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its top at 3,603 m is the highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 gondola, 9 chairlifts and 4 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Wheeler Peak (4,011 m), 3.8 km to the south-east, about 408 m higher than the top lift.
