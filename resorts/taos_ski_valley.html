    Taos Ski Valley in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Taos Ski Valley (10,584 ft (3,226 m)), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 21 in (54 cm), with a normal range of 9.1 in (23 cm)–39 in (99 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 46 in (117 cm) (19 in (49 cm)–77 in (195 cm)).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 55 in (140 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 39 % of winters, and by 1 January in 59 %.

    The deepest winter was 1982-83 (174 in (442 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (16 in (41 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to early May, about 168 days.

    Common questions

    Can you ski Taos Ski Valley at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 21 in (54 cm), and only 59 % of the past 46 winters had 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 17 in (43 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Taos Ski Valley?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 55 in (140 cm)). For a sure base, mid-November to early May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How snowy is Taos Ski Valley?

    About 55 in (140 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 16 in (41 cm) in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 174 in (442 cm) in the biggest (1982-83).

    What altitude is Taos Ski Valley at?

    Between 2,850 m and 3,603 m, so 753 m of vertical drop.

    About Taos Ski Valley

    Taos Ski Valley is a mid-sized ski area with about 43 km of pistes, number 45 of 175 in US West by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,850 m up to 3,603 m. The closest other ski area is Red River (14 km, north).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Taos Ski Valley gives 753 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,850 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its top at 3,603 m is the highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km.
