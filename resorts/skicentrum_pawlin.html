    About Skicentrum Pawlin

    Neither huge nor tiny, Skicentrum Pawlin offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 79 of 312 in the Carpathians). The slopes start at 685 m and reach 845 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Karlov (0.8 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 4.3 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 3.9 km (89%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    160 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 685 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Figura Praděd, reach 1,441 m. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 5 button lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
