    About Antelope Butte

    Antelope Butte is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 6.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,566 m up to 2,855 m.

    The pistes of Antelope Butte

    The piste map lists 16 graded runs, about 5.4 km in total. The largest share is black diamond, with 3.1 km (57%). With 57% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 3.1 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Antelope Butte gives 289 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,566 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Bruce Mountain, 3,148 m high and 5.8 km east of the resort.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Antelope Butte hold around 20 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 14 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (24 cm).

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,710 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 December and keeps it until about 15 May, roughly 24 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. Most winters land between 42 cm and 107 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 90 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 46 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 82 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 80 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Questions about Antelope Butte

    What is the best month to ski at Antelope Butte?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Antelope Butte have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 46 cm deep.

    How high is Antelope Butte?

    Between 2,566 m and 2,855 m, so 289 m of vertical drop.

    Does Antelope Butte have steep runs?

    Yes: about 3.1 km of its graded pistes (57%) are difficult.

    How big is Antelope Butte?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
