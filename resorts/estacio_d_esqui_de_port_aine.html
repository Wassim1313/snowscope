    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Estació d'esquí de Port-Ainé (2,052 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 31 cm, with a normal range of 16 cm–46 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 60 cm, between 42 cm and 91 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 65 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    About Estació d'esquí de Port-Ainé

    Neither huge nor tiny, Estació d'esquí de Port-Ainé offers about 22 km of pistes (number 29 of 49 in the Pyrenees). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,680 m up to 2,424 m. Espot is the next resort, 18 km away to the north-west.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,052 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 December and keeps it until about 15 April, roughly 16 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 31 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 64 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 17 cm and 108 cm. A normal season tops out near 65 cm, around 5 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 30 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    What there is to ski at Estació d'esquí de Port-Ainé

    Estació d'esquí de Port-Ainé has 39 graded runs adding up to about 22 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 8.2 km (37%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at Estació d'esquí de Port-Ainé

    From top to bottom that makes 744 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,680 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 8 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Torreta de l'Orri (2,438 m), Roca de Coma-sarrera (2,374 m) and Bony de la Socarrada (2,275 m).
