    About Hochoetz

    Hochoetz is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 288 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,408 m and 2,268 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Hochoetz-Kühtai (2.1 km, east).

    Snow at Hochoetz through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,838 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 November and lasts to 29 May: about 26 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 49 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 71 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 97 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 60 cm to 113 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 88 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 860 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,408 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 13 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 6 chairlifts, 4 button lifts and 1 rope tow. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The skyline is dominated by Sulzkogel, 3,016 m high and 7.2 km south-east of the resort.

    Questions about Hochoetz

    When is the best time to ski at Hochoetz?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Hochoetz have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 49 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Hochoetz?

    The top is at 2,268 m and the base at 1,408 m.

    Is Hochoetz suitable for families?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Hochoetz?

    Hochoetz-Kühtai, 2.1 km to the east, with about 54 km of pistes.
