    About La Chia

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, La Chia sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,001 m up to 1,308 m. Moléson is the next resort, 3.6 km away to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 2.6 km in total. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 2.6 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Charmey (14 km east) has about 12 km of pistes against 3.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Moléson does better, with about 25 weeks of a good base instead of 15. Moléson reaches 1,975 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How La Chia is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, La Chia gives 307 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Moléson reaches 1,975 m. The skyline is dominated by Le Moléson, 2,002 m high and 4.6 km south of the resort.

    How much snow La Chia usually gets

    Count on about 15 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,154 m) in an ordinary winter, from late December to early April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 50 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 26 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Most winters land between 16 cm and 80 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 30 cm, with 59 cm in the best years.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Chalet Le Pliano (chalet), Hôtel-Restaurant de la Croix Blanche (hotel), Cabane des Clés (mountain hut) and Cabane des Portes (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    La Chia: common questions

    When does La Chia have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Can you ski at La Chia at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 30 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at La Chia?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,308 m; the lowest starts at 1,001 m (307 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at La Chia?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near La Chia?

    Charmey, 14 km to the east, with about 12 km of pistes.
