    About Bellwald

    With roughly 16 km of runs, Bellwald sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,622 m up to 2,558 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gluringen, 5.5 km to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Bellwald has 56 graded runs adding up to about 16 km. Most of the length is blue (56%, about 9.0 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,090 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May or later, about 26 weeks of good cover. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. Most winters land between 84 cm and 162 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 67 cm, with 103 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 139 cm, around 28 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 129 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    How Bellwald is laid out

    936 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,622 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 2 chairlifts and 2 T-bars. Above the ski area rises Grosses Wannenhorn (3,906 m), 7.9 km to the north-west, about 1,348 m higher than the top lift.

    Bellwald: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Bellwald?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Bellwald at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 67 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Bellwald at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,558 m; the lowest starts at 1,622 m (936 m of vertical).

    Is Bellwald suitable for families?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 9.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Bellwald have?

    About 16 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
