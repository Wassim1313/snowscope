    About Vormaine

    Compact and local, Vormaine has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,483 m and 1,611 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Balme, 2.2 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Vormaine and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.8 km south-west to Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix), with about 41 km of runs. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 3 of 7). For higher slopes, Les Grands Montets climbs to 2,768 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,547 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 November and lasts to 24 May: about 26 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 91 cm to 189 cm in mid-February in most winters. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 15 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 146 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 118 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 76 cm; a snowy December gave 121 cm, a dry one 34 cm.

    What there is to ski at Vormaine

    The piste map lists 6 graded runs, about 2.6 km in total. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 62%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 128 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,483 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. All 4 lifts here are button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aiguille Verte at 4,121 m, standing 2,510 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find L'Olympique (hotel), Hameau de Bec Rouge (hotel), Chalet alpin du Tour (mountain hut) and Chalet Pierre Semard (hotel).

    Vormaine: common questions

    When does Vormaine have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Vormaine at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 76 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Vormaine?

    Between 1,483 m and 1,611 m, so 128 m of vertical drop.

    Is Vormaine suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Vormaine?

    Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix), 8.8 km to the south-west, with about 41 km of pistes.
