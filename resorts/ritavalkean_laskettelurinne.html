    About Ritavalkean laskettelurinne

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ritavalkean laskettelurinne offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 165 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 190 m to 305 m. The closest other ski area is Ritavalkea (0.0 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Ritavalkean laskettelurinne

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 3.0 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 1.5 km (52%). Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The mountain at Ritavalkean laskettelurinne

    Skied top to bottom, Ritavalkean laskettelurinne gives 115 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 305 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Makkaravaara (239 m), Jai-Paljukka (209 m) and Iso Perävaara (183 m).

    How much snow Ritavalkean laskettelurinne usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 248 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 55 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 65 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 39 cm; a snowy December gave 53 cm, a dry one 26 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 77 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Ritavalkean laskettelurinne have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Ritavalkean laskettelurinne have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 39 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Ritavalkean laskettelurinne?

    Its highest lift reaches 305 m; the lowest starts at 190 m (115 m of vertical).

    Is Ritavalkean laskettelurinne suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.
