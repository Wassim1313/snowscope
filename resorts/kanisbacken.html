    About Kanisbacken

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Kanisbacken sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The slopes start at 73 m and reach 225 m at the top.

    The pistes of Kanisbacken

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 2.2 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.2 km (55%). With 79% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 16 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Kanisbacken. The last seven days brought 11 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 13 cm by Friday. An ordinary winter would have around 16 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    152 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Stor-Kanisberget (310 m), Rackberget (254 m) and Kvällsberget (240 m).

    How much snow Kanisbacken usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 149 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 39 cm and 82 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 33 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 20 cm in a poor year to 58 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 61 cm, around 7 February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 48 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Kanisbacken include Hotel Villa Sparta (hotel), Guesthouse (Arctic-Mountain-Team) (guesthouse) and Wärdshuset Bergsparken (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When does Kanisbacken have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Kanisbacken at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 33 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Kanisbacken?

    Its highest lift reaches 225 m; the lowest starts at 73 m (152 m of vertical).

    Is Kanisbacken suitable for families?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 1.7 km, are easy.

    How big is Kanisbacken?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
