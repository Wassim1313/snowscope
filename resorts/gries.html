    About Gries

    Gries has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,589 m to 1,650 m. The closest other ski area is Niederthai (7.6 km, north-west).

    The mountain at Gries

    The vertical drop is 61 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,589 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sölden, for one, climbs to 3,247 m. To get up there are 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Schrankogel, 3,497 m high and 6.1 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Gries usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,620 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 21 December and keeps it until about 23 April, roughly 18 weeks. Most winters land between 39 cm and 76 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 33 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 31 cm; a snowy December gave 46 cm, a dry one 16 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 59 cm, around 24 February.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Gries include Schöpf (hotel), first mountain (hotel), Bergkristall (guesthouse) and Tiroler Hütte (chalet).

    Gries: common questions

    When does Gries have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Gries at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 31 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Gries?

    The top is at 1,650 m and the base at 1,589 m.

    How big is Gries?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
