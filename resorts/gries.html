    What 46 winters say about Gries

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Gries (1,620 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 31 cm of cover (22 cm to 38 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 56 cm, between 46 cm and 67 cm in a normal winter.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 59 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 4 % of winters, and by 1 January in 4 %.

    1998-99 was the big one (105 cm); 2016-17 the lean one (34 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April, about 137 days.

    Gries: quick answers

    Is there snow in Gries at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 31 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 4 % of 46 winters. Right now: 18 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Gries?

    February: the snow peaks at about 59 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Gries get?

    About 59 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late February); between 34 cm in the leanest winter (2016-17) and 105 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    When does Gries have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How high do the lifts go at Gries?

    The top is at 1,650 m and the base at 1,589 m.

    How big is Gries?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 2 lifts.

    Gries today and the next days

    Today there are about 18 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Gries. The last seven days brought 29 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 21 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The mountain at Gries

    The vertical drop is 61 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,589 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sölden, for one, climbs to 3,247 m. To get up there are 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Schrankogel, 3,497 m high and 6.1 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Gries usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,620 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 21 December and keeps it until about 23 April, roughly 18 weeks. Most winters land between 39 cm and 76 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 33 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 31 cm; a snowy December gave 46 cm, a dry one 16 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 59 cm, around 24 February.
