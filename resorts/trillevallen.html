    About Trillevallen

    With some 16 km of runs, Trillevallen belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. The slopes start at 663 m and reach 994 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Edsåsdalen (7.3 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 40 marked runs cover roughly 16 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 10 km, or 64%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 1.4 km of difficult runs.

    When the snow is good

    At about 828 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 November and lasts to 20 May: about 26 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 92 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 52 cm; a snowy December gave 74 cm, a dry one 37 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 97 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 59 cm and 106 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 331 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 9 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 4 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Välliste (1,025 m), Grofjället (959 m) and Hållfjället (906 m).

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Trillevallens Högfjällshotell (hotel), Ottsjö Fjällhotell (hotel), Köjagården (hotel) and Offsjökojan (chalet).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Trillevallen?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Trillevallen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 52 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Trillevallen at?

    The top is at 994 m and the base at 663 m.

    Who is Trillevallen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 64% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Trillevallen have?

    About 16 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
