    About Gače

    Gače is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 615 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 679 m up to 964 m. The closest other ski area is Izver (36 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 5.8 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.1 km, or 72%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Gače gives 285 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 679 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 964 m is the third highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 4 drag lifts, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Veliki Rog at 1,099 m, standing 135 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 822 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; a snowy December gave 31 cm, a dry one 0 cm. A normal season tops out near 12 cm, around 16 February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 68 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Gače: common questions

    When does Gače have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Is there snow at Gače at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 5 cm).

    What altitude is Gače at?

    Between 679 m and 964 m, so 285 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Gače best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 72% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Gače?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
