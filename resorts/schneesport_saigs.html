    About Schneesport-Saigs

    Compact and local, Schneesport-Saigs has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 922 m up to 994 m. The closest other ski area is Skilift Fischbach (5.2 km, south).

    How much snow Schneesport-Saigs usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 958 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 9 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 30 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late December and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 21 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    The mountain at Schneesport-Saigs

    Skied top to bottom, Schneesport-Saigs gives 72 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,122 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Feldberg reaches 1,446 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Windeck (1,209 m), 7.2 km to the west, about 215 m higher than the top lift.
