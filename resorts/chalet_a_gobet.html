    About Chalet a Gobet

    Compact and local, Chalet a Gobet has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 846 m to 863 m. The closest other ski area is Froideville (3.6 km, north).

    Chalet a Gobet and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Thollon-les-Mémises: about 19 km of pistes, 21 km to the south. For more reliable snow, Thollon-les-Mémises usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 24 weeks, against 4 here. The highest nearby is Thollon-les-Mémises, topping out at 1,873 m.

    The snow right now

    There is no snow on the ground at Chalet a Gobet yet. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (7 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 17 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 846 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Thollon-les-Mémises, for one, climbs to 1,873 m. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Tour de Gourze (927 m) and Signal de Grandvaux (806 m).

    How much snow Chalet a Gobet usually gets

    Count on about 4 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 854 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 54 cm in the best years. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 8 cm to 78 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 11 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 34 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Chalet a Gobet?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Chalet a Gobet at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Chalet a Gobet?

    Its highest lift reaches 863 m; the lowest starts at 846 m (17 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Chalet a Gobet?

    Thollon-les-Mémises, 21 km to the south, with about 19 km of pistes.
