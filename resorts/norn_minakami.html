    About Norn Minakami

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Norn Minakami sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Its lifts climb from 823 m to 1,214 m. Its nearest neighbour is White Valley, 6.6 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Norn Minakami

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 5.5 km in total. Most of the length is red (57%, about 3.2 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How Norn Minakami is laid out

    391 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,023 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    Norn Minakami: common questions

    How high is Norn Minakami?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,214 m; the lowest starts at 823 m (391 m of vertical).

    Is Norn Minakami good for beginners?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 2.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Norn Minakami?

    How big is Norn Minakami?
