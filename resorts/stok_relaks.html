    About Stok Relaks

    Stok Relaks is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 610 m to 650 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Karpatka Panorama, 1.5 km to the north-west.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Stok Relaks has roughly 5 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 6 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 40 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 610 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Karpacz, for one, climbs to 1,350 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 630 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 48 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 24 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 34 cm in a big one.
