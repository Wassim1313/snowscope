    About Lajoux

    Lajoux has about 0.5 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,174 m and 1,233 m above sea level. Les Hautes Combes is the next resort, 2.5 km away to the south-west.

    Lajoux and the resorts around it

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,204 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 January and keeps it until about 9 March, roughly 5 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 35 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 78 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 17 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 78 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 14 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Lajoux. The last seven days brought 39 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 10 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 5 cm at mid-mountain).

    The mountain at Lajoux

    The vertical drop is 59 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Colomby de Gex, 1,689 m high and 6.2 km south of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Chalet Jean d'Auferville (chalet), Chalet la Baumette (chalet), L'étincelle du Manon (chalet) and Gabelou Hôtel (hotel).

    Lajoux: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Lajoux?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Lajoux have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 17 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in 8 winters out of ten.

    How high do the lifts go at Lajoux?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,233 m; the lowest starts at 1,174 m (59 m of vertical).

    How big is Lajoux?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 1 lift.
