    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Drei Zinnen (about 1,678 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 33 cm; half of the winters sit between 20 cm and 49 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 53 cm, between 44 cm and 67 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 57 cm.

    15 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 26 % by 1 January.

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 164 days.

    Drei Zinnen: quick answers

    Can you ski Drei Zinnen at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 33 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 26 % of 46 winters. Right now: 29 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Drei Zinnen?

    March: the snow peaks at about 57 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to early May.

    How snowy is Drei Zinnen?

    About 57 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 42 cm in the leanest winter (2016-17) and 143 cm in the biggest (2013-14).

    When does Drei Zinnen open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Drei Zinnen?

    The top is at 2,221 m and the base at 1,135 m.

    Is Drei Zinnen good for beginners?

    About Drei Zinnen

    Drei Zinnen is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 101 km of pistes put it at number 48 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,135 m to 2,221 m. 3 Zinnen Dolomites is the next resort, 1.4 km away to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 191 runs and around 84 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 45 km, or 54%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,678 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 20 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 47 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 35 cm and 90 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 28 March, with about 57 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 33 cm (lean years 8 cm, snowy years 64 cm).

    The mountain at Drei Zinnen

    1,086 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The lift system counts 36 lifts: 7 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 14 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 6 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 6 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Dreischusterspitze - Punta dei Tre Scarperi (3,152 m), 3.5 km to the south-west, about 931 m higher than the top lift.
