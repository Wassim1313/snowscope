    About Skiareál Rejdice

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Skiareál Rejdice sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The slopes start at 666 m and reach 828 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skiareál Rejdice, 0.2 km to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    Skiareál Rejdice has 4 graded runs adding up to about 2.2 km. Most of the length is blue (64%, about 1.4 km). With 64% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Skiareál Rejdice is laid out

    The vertical drop is 162 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 666 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 drag lift, 2 lifts in all.
