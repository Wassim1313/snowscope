    About Alpe Lusia

    With some 28 km of runs, Alpe Lusia belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,371 m up to 2,339 m. Its nearest neighbour is Passo Rolle, 8.9 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 48 graded runs, about 28 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 11 km (40%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 5.8 km of black or expert runs.

    How Alpe Lusia is laid out

    The vertical drop is 968 m: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,371 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 4 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 9 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Cima Bocche - Cimon de Boce, 2,745 m high and 4.8 km east of the resort.

    How much snow Alpe Lusia usually gets

    At about 1,855 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 December and lasts to 17 April: about 16 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 30 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 26 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 54 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 26 cm to 72 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 47 cm.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Rifugio Passo Lusia (mountain hut), Rifugio la Rezila (mountain hut), Devil's bite (mountain hut) and Malga Passerella (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Alpe Lusia?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Alpe Lusia at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 26 cm deep.

    What altitude is Alpe Lusia at?

    The top is at 2,339 m and the base at 1,371 m.

    Can beginners ski at Alpe Lusia?

    Yes: 40% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Alpe Lusia?

    San Pellegrino, 9.8 km to the east, with about 53 km of pistes.
