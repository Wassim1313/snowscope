    About Engelberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Engelberg offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 572 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,013 m and reach 2,058 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Bannalp, 4.7 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Engelberg

    In all, 12 marked runs cover roughly 7.0 km. Freeride runs make up the biggest part: 1.8 km, or 25%. Steep ground is the speciality: 37% of the length is graded difficult. 1.8 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Engelberg is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Engelberg gives 1,045 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 2 cable cars, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Titlis (3,238 m), 7.4 km to the south, about 1,180 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Engelberg through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,536 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 22 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 62 cm and 124 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 49 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 49 cm; a snowy December gave 66 cm, a dry one 24 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 28 February, with about 96 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Engelberg?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Engelberg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 49 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Engelberg?

    Between 1,013 m and 2,058 m, so 1,045 m of vertical drop.

    Does Engelberg have steep runs?

    Yes: about 2.6 km of its graded pistes (37%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Engelberg?

    Engelberg Titlis, 5.0 km to the south, with about 42 km of pistes.
