    About Yad Moss

    Yad Moss is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 597 m to 719 m. Allenheads is the next resort, 12 km away to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Yad Moss gives 122 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 597 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The only lift is a button lift.
