    About Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets

    With some 61 km of runs, Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,127 m to 2,097 m. La Forclaz VD is the next resort, 5.3 km away to the north-west.

    The pistes of Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets

    Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets has 106 graded runs adding up to about 61 km. The largest share is blue, with 39 km (64%). With 66% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How much snow Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,612 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 6 December and keeps it until about 18 May, roughly 24 weeks. Most winters land between 75 cm and 154 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 121 cm, around 5 March. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 55 cm, with 87 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 102 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 970 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 3 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 7 button lifts, 3 rope tows and 4 moving carpets, 30 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Sommet des Diablerets (3,210 m), 5.3 km to the south-east, about 1,113 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets

    When does Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 55 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026.

    What altitude is Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets at?

    Between 1,127 m and 2,097 m, so 970 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Villars/Gryon/Les Diablerets?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 41 km, are easy.
