    About Rausor

    Compact and local, Rausor has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Carpathians. The slopes start at 1,249 m and reach 1,448 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Muntele Mic (29 km, west).

    Rausor and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Muntele Mic (29 km west) has about 13 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. For higher slopes, Șureanu climbs to 1,968 m.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 31 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Rausor. 10 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Sunday.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Rausor gives 199 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Straja, for one, climbs to 1,865 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 2 button lifts, 3 lifts in all.
