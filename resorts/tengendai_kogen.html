    About Tengendai Kogen

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Tengendai Kogen sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The slopes start at 927 m and reach 1,815 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Grandeco Snow Resort (6.9 km, south).

    How Tengendai Kogen is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 888 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,127 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,815 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car and 3 chairlifts, 4 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Tengendai Kogen has 10 graded runs adding up to about 7.3 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.3 km, or 72%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 1.3 km of difficult runs.

    Tengendai Kogen: common questions

    What altitude is Tengendai Kogen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,815 m; the lowest starts at 927 m (888 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Tengendai Kogen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 72% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Tengendai Kogen?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
