    About Venet

    With roughly 17 km of runs, Venet sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 776 m and reach 2,205 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Patschiedlift (4.6 km, south-west).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,429 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 976 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.

    How Venet compares nearby

    Of the 7 ski areas compared here, Venet is the third biggest by piste length. For higher slopes, Serfaus Fiss Ladis climbs to 2,817 m.
