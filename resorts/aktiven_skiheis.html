    About Aktiven skiheis

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Aktiven skiheis one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. The slopes start at 362 m and reach 528 m at the top. Furedalen alpin is the next resort, 1.8 km away to the west.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Aktiven skiheis has roughly 9 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 12 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 166 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 4 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Voss Resort Fjellheisar reaches 948 m.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Nordnestunet (guesthouse), Kvamseter Lodge (guesthouse) and Thon Hotel Sandven (hotel).

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Aktiven skiheis?

    Its highest lift reaches 528 m; the lowest starts at 362 m (166 m of vertical).
