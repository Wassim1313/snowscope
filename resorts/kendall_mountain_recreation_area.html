    About Kendall Mountain

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Kendall Mountain one of the smaller places to ski in US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,835 m up to 2,904 m. Silverton Mountain is the next resort, 7.3 km away to the north.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,870 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to early May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 67 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 58 cm to 154 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 71 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 120 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 122 cm. For about 7 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March.

    The pistes of Kendall Mountain

    The piste map lists 10 graded runs, about 2.0 km in total. The largest share is green circle, with 0.8 km (42%). Good skiers will like it here; 42% of the runs are steep.

    How Kendall Mountain is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Kendall Mountain gives 69 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,835 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. The only lift is a chairlift. The skyline is dominated by Tower Mountain, 4,119 m high and 5.9 km north-east of the resort.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Kendall Mountain?

    Aim for early January and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    How much snow does Kendall Mountain have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 71 cm deep.

    What altitude is Kendall Mountain at?

    Between 2,835 m and 2,904 m, so 69 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kendall Mountain good for beginners?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Kendall Mountain?

    Telluride, 20 km to the north-west, with about 101 km of pistes.
