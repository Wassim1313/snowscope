    About Gujo Vacances Mura

    Gujo Vacances Mura is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,040 m and 1,113 m above sea level. WhitePIA Takasu is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the south.

    How Gujo Vacances Mura compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Takasu Snow Park: about 17 km of pistes, 7.1 km to the west. For higher slopes, Meiho climbs to 1,577 m.
