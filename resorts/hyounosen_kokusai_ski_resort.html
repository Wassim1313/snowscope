    About Hyounosen Kokusai

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hyounosen Kokusai offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 244 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 606 m and 943 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hachikogen, 2.6 km to the north.

    How Hyounosen Kokusai compares nearby

    For more terrain, Hachi Kita (4.7 km north) has about 8.0 km of pistes against 3.0 km here. The highest nearby is Hachi Kita, topping out at 1,217 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 2.8 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.3 km, or 83%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Hyounosen Kokusai gives 337 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 606 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Hyounosen Kokusai hold around 8 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 16 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.
