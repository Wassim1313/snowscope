    About Skilift Eberschwang

    Compact and local, Skilift Eberschwang has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 620 m and reach 742 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skilift Kronberg, 27 km to the south.

    Skilift Eberschwang and the resorts around it

    The mountain at Skilift Eberschwang

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Eberschwang gives 122 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 620 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Göblberg (801 m), Sulzberg (771 m) and Lederer Kopf (760 m).

    How much snow Skilift Eberschwang usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 681 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 13 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 24 cm). For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 30 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Skilift Eberschwang have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and mid-January.

    How much snow does Skilift Eberschwang have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 8 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilift Eberschwang?

    Its highest lift reaches 742 m; the lowest starts at 620 m (122 m of vertical).

    Who is Skilift Eberschwang best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 60% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilift Eberschwang?

    Kasberg Grünau, 46 km to the south-east, with about 25 km of pistes.
