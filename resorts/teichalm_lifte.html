    About Teichalm Lifte

    Teichalm Lifte is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 726 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,175 m and reach 1,415 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Strallegg, 21 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Teichalm Lifte

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 4.1 km to ski. Most of the length is red (51%, about 2.1 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Teichalm Lifte gives 240 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 2 T-bars and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Hochlantsch, 1,720 m high and 3.1 km north-west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,295 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 27 cm, around 14 February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 57 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 11 cm; a snowy December gave 32 cm, a dry one 2 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Teichalm Lifte?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Teichalm Lifte at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Teichalm Lifte?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,415 m; the lowest starts at 1,175 m (240 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Teichalm Lifte?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 1.7 km, are easy.

    How big is Teichalm Lifte?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
