    Snow at De Uithof in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at mid-mountain stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    How De Uithof is laid out

    Among the 4 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Outdoorski Bergschenhoek reaches 25 m. Its 4 lifts break down into 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.
