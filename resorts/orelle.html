    About Orelle

    Neither huge nor tiny, Orelle offers about 23 km of pistes (number 270 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 2,338 m up to 3,221 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Val Thorens, 3.4 km to the north.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Orelle today: about 103 cm up high. 32 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 103 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    The mountain at Orelle

    From top to bottom that makes 883 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. At 2,338 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Its top at 3,221 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
