    About Ricken

    Compact and local, Ricken has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 807 m up to 861 m. The closest other ski area is Atzmännig (4.4 km, north-west).

    Other resorts close by

    The mountain at Ricken

    From top to bottom that makes 54 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,007 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Wolzenalp, reach 1,461 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Tanzboden at 1,443 m, standing 582 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Ricken through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 834 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Year to year the snow is steady, with 9 cm to 46 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 28 cm, around 9 February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 15 cm, from 3 cm in a poor year to 27 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 13 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.
