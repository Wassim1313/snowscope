    About Chikusa Highland

    Neither huge nor tiny, Chikusa Highland offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 193 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 907 m and 1,125 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Banshu Tokura Snow Park, 16 km to the north-east.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Hachikogen (23 km north-east) has about 7.0 km of pistes against 4.0 km here.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 218 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 907 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.
