    How Taivalvaara is laid out

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Suvantovaara (318 m) and Repovaara (259 m).

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 232 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 57 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 67 cm. Most winters land between 49 cm and 76 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 41 cm (lean years 26 cm, snowy years 53 cm).

    Questions about Taivalvaara

    When does Taivalvaara have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Taivalvaara have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 41 cm deep.
