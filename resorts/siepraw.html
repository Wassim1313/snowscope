    About Siepraw

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Siepraw one of the smaller places to ski in the Carpathians. Skiing here happens between 274 m and 334 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sport Arena Myślenice, 12 km to the south.

    How Siepraw compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 33 km south-east to Stacja Narciarska Laskowa-Kamion, with about 4.0 km of runs. The highest nearby is Kasina SKI, topping out at 863 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 60 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 274 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Tobołów, reach 965 m. To get up there are 5 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
