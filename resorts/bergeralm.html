    About Bergeralm

    Bergeralm is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 345 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,066 m and 2,175 m above sea level. Serlesbahnen is the next resort, 9.0 km away to the north-west.

    Snow at Bergeralm through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,620 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 November and keeps it until about 17 May, roughly 24 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm, with 80 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 67 cm to 131 cm in mid-February in most winters. For about 7 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 109 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 85 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Runs by difficulty

    Bergeralm has 30 graded runs adding up to about 16 km. Most of the length is red (46%, about 7.6 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.9 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,109 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,175 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 gondola, 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Kesselspitze at 2,727 m, standing 552 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Bergeralm have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Bergeralm at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 54 cm deep.

    What altitude is Bergeralm at?

    Between 1,066 m and 2,175 m, so 1,109 m of vertical drop.

    Is Bergeralm suitable for families?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 7.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Bergeralm have?

    About 17 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
