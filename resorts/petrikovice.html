    About Petříkovice

    Neither huge nor tiny, Petříkovice offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 117 of 257 in Central Europe). Its lifts climb from 437 m to 576 m. Its nearest neighbour is Radvanice, 4.9 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Petříkovice

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.7 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 100%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Petříkovice

    Skied top to bottom, Petříkovice gives 139 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 437 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Žacléř, for one, climbs to 810 m. All 2 lifts here are T-bars.
