    About Mezihoří

    Mezihoří is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 663 m up to 748 m. Svahová is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the north-east.

    The mountain at Mezihoří

    Skied top to bottom, Mezihoří gives 85 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 663 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 706 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 31 January, with about 12 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), with 23 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 31 cm.

    Questions about Mezihoří

    What is the best month to ski at Mezihoří?

    Aim for early February and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Mezihoří at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 3 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Mezihoří?

    Between 663 m and 748 m, so 85 m of vertical drop.
