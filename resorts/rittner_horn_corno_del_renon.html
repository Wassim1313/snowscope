    About Rittner Horn

    Neither huge nor tiny, Rittner Horn offers about 12 km of pistes (number 445 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,533 m to 2,259 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Reinswald, 12 km to the north.

    How much snow Rittner Horn usually gets

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,896 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early January to early May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 27 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 51 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 47 cm. Most winters land between 27 cm and 68 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 39 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Runs by difficulty

    Rittner Horn has 22 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. Most of the length is blue (80%, about 9.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 726 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,533 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Sarner Scharte - Monte Forcella Sarentina, 2,458 m high and 7.5 km north-west of the resort.

    How Rittner Horn compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 16 km east to Seiser Alm, with about 60 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Meran 2000 does better, with about 26 weeks of a good base instead of 18.

    Rittner Horn: common questions

    When does Rittner Horn have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to early May.

    Can you ski at Rittner Horn at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 27 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Rittner Horn?

    Between 1,533 m and 2,259 m, so 726 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Rittner Horn?

    Yes: 80% of the graded pistes, about 9.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Rittner Horn?

    Seiser Alm, 16 km to the east, with about 60 km of pistes.
