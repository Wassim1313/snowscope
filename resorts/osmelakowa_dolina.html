    About Osmelakowa Dolina

    Osmelakowa Dolina has about 0.3 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 740 m and 796 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Orlické Záhoří (6.7 km, west).

    Snow at Osmelakowa Dolina through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 768 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 26 January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 46 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and early February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 8 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 32 cm in a big one.

    How Osmelakowa Dolina is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 56 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 740 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Orlické Záhoří, for one, climbs to 1,022 m. The only lift is a button lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Osmelakowa Dolina?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    How much snow does Osmelakowa Dolina have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Osmelakowa Dolina?

    Its highest lift reaches 796 m; the lowest starts at 740 m (56 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Osmelakowa Dolina have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
