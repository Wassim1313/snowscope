    About Røldal Skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Røldal Skisenter offers about 11 km of pistes (number 69 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 690 m to 1,269 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sauda skisenter, 36 km to the south-west.

    The pistes of Røldal Skisenter

    The piste map lists 22 graded runs, about 11 km in total. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 5.6 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Røldal Skisenter is laid out

    The vertical drop is 579 m: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,269 m is the highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 3 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Tindane at 1,527 m, standing 258 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Røldal Skisenter through a normal winter

    At about 980 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 5 November and lasts to the end of May: about 30 weeks. Most winters land between 176 cm and 354 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 289 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 146 cm (lean years 94 cm, snowy years 210 cm). For about 25 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. The snow is usually deepest around 26 March, with about 299 cm.

    Questions about Røldal Skisenter

    What is the best month to ski at Røldal Skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Røldal Skisenter have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 146 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Røldal Skisenter at?

    The top is at 1,269 m and the base at 690 m.

    Can beginners ski at Røldal Skisenter?

    Yes: 73% of the graded pistes, about 7.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Røldal Skisenter have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
