    About Serre Eyraud

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Serre Eyraud sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,462 m to 1,990 m. Its nearest neighbour is Orcières Merlette, 5.5 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Serre Eyraud today: about 13 cm up high. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 41 cm by Sunday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 13 cm).

    How Serre Eyraud is laid out

    The vertical drop is 528 m: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,462 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. All 3 lifts here are button lifts. The skyline is dominated by Pointe de la Diablée, 2,928 m high and 7.1 km east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,726 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 18 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 105 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 83 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 41 cm; a snowy December gave 65 cm, a dry one 14 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 44 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Questions about Serre Eyraud

    What is the best month to ski at Serre Eyraud?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Serre Eyraud have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 41 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Serre Eyraud?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,990 m; the lowest starts at 1,462 m (528 m of vertical).

    Who is Serre Eyraud best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 49% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Serre Eyraud?

    Orcières Merlette, 5.5 km to the north-east, with about 49 km of pistes.
