    About Maiszinken

    Maiszinken is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 661 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 759 m and reach 1,059 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Lackenhof, 6.7 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Maiszinken

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 4.5 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 2.4 km (53%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Maiszinken hold around 24 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 9 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 10 cm at mid-mountain.

    How Maiszinken is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Maiszinken gives 300 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 759 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Hochkar, for one, climbs to 1,788 m. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Springkogel at 1,678 m, standing 619 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 909 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 February and lasts to 19 February: about 1 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 30 cm, around 13 February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 5 cm to 54 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 17 cm, from 3 cm in a poor year to 45 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Questions about Maiszinken

    When does Maiszinken have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to mid-February.

    Can you ski at Maiszinken at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Maiszinken at?

    The top is at 1,059 m and the base at 759 m.

    Is Maiszinken suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 2.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Maiszinken?

    Hochkar, 20 km to the south-west, with about 19 km of pistes.
