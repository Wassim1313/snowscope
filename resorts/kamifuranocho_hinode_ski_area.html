    About Kamifuranocho Hinode

    Kamifuranocho Hinode has about 0.2 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 227 m to 262 m. Its nearest neighbour is Nakafurano Hokusei, 7.6 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 2 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Kamifuranocho Hinode. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 35 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 227 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Asahidake, reach 1,589 m. The only lift is a rope tow.
