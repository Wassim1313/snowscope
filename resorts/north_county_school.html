    About North County School

    North County School is a ski area. Whiteface Mountain is the next resort, 17 km away to the north.

    How much snow North County School usually gets

    At about 826 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 December and lasts to 11 April: about 16 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 30 cm, with 50 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 33 cm to 86 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 21 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 63 cm.

    Questions about North County School

    What is the best month to ski at North County School?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at North County School at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 30 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
