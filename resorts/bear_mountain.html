    About Bear Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Bear Mountain offers about 13 km of pistes (number 118 of 175 in US West). The ski area spans the mountain from 2,177 m up to 2,680 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Snow Summit, 2.9 km to the west.

    The pistes of Bear Mountain

    Counting every graded piste, there are 26 runs and around 13 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 5.8 km, or 43%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 3.6 km of difficult runs.

    How Bear Mountain is laid out

    503 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,177 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 7 chairlifts and 4 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Sugarloaf Mountain (3,029 m), 4.8 km to the south-east, about 349 m higher than the top lift.

In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 5 February, with about 6 cm.
