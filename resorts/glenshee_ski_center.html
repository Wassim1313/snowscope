    About Glenshee

    Glenshee has about 22 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 635 m up to 1,028 m. The closest other ski area is Cairngorm Mountain (31 km, north-west).

    The mountain at Glenshee

    Skied top to bottom, Glenshee gives 393 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,028 m. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 15 button lifts and 1 rope tow, 22 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    The pistes of Glenshee

    Counting every graded piste, there are 40 runs and around 22 km to ski. Most of the length is green (beginner) (34%, about 7.6 km). With 67% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.
