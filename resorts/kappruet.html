    About Kappruet

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kappruet offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 113 of 262 in Scandinavia). The slopes start at 692 m and reach 854 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Funäsdalen (18 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Kappruet

    Kappruet has 7 graded runs adding up to about 5.6 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.3 km, or 77%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How much snow Kappruet usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 773 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 November and lasts to 11 May: about 23 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 69 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 44 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 63 cm, a dry one 27 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 76 cm. Most winters land between 56 cm and 89 cm by mid-February.

    The mountain at Kappruet

    The vertical drop is 162 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Tänndalen, reach 1,020 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Blåstöten at 1,332 m, standing 478 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Kappruet?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Kappruet at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 44 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Kappruet at?

    Its highest lift reaches 854 m; the lowest starts at 692 m (162 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Kappruet?

    Yes: 77% of the graded pistes, about 4.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Kappruet?

    Tänndalen, 30 km to the south-west, with about 26 km of pistes.
