    About Kappruet

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kappruet offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 113 of 262 in Scandinavia). The slopes start at 692 m and reach 854 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Funäsdalen (18 km, south-west).

    Kappruet and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Tänndalen: about 26 km of pistes, 30 km to the south-west.

    Kappruet today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Kappruet hold around 10 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    The mountain at Kappruet

    The vertical drop is 162 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Tänndalen, reach 1,020 m.

    What altitude is Kappruet at?

    Its highest lift reaches 854 m; the lowest starts at 692 m (162 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Kappruet?
