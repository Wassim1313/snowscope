    About Verena 2000

    Neither huge nor tiny, Verena 2000 offers about 11 km of pistes (number 477 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,640 m up to 1,997 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Val Formica, 5.5 km to the north.

    Snow at Verena 2000 through a normal winter

    Count on about 10 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,818 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to mid-March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 7 cm to 102 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 19 February, with about 41 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 9 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 67 cm).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 16 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.6 km, or 43%. Good skiers will like it here; 35% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 3.7 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 357 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,640 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 1,997 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 5 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Cima Portule at 2,310 m, standing 313 m above the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Folgaria (16 km west) has about 46 km of pistes against 11 km here. No resort in the list keeps good snow longer: about 10 weeks of solid cover in an ordinary winter.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Verena 2000?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Verena 2000 at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Verena 2000?

    The top is at 1,997 m and the base at 1,640 m.

    Is Verena 2000 good for beginners?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 4.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Verena 2000?

    Folgaria, 16 km to the west, with about 46 km of pistes.
