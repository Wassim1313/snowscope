    About Kirkwood

    With roughly 41 km of runs, Kirkwood sits in the middle of the size table of US West. Its lifts climb from 2,382 m to 2,884 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sierra-at-Tahoe, 13 km north, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Kirkwood

    Kirkwood has 58 graded runs adding up to about 41 km. Most of the length is black diamond (47%, about 19 km). With 47% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 19 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,633 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. By mid-April the typical depth is 158 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 221 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 109 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 16 cm, snowy years 201 cm). Most winters land between 101 cm and 323 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 19 weeks.

    How Kirkwood is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 502 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,382 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 10 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 moving carpets, 13 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Round Top (3,164 m), 6.0 km to the east, about 280 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Kirkwood

    When is the best time to ski at Kirkwood?

    Aim for early January and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Kirkwood at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 109 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Kirkwood?

    Between 2,382 m and 2,884 m, so 502 m of vertical drop.

    Does Kirkwood have steep runs?

    Yes: about 19 km of its graded pistes (47%) are difficult.

    How big is Kirkwood?

    About 41 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
