    About Muroran-shi

    Muroran-shi has about 0.5 km of pistes. The slopes start at 411 m and reach 472 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Muroran Kogen Danpara, 0.6 km south, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow right now

    There is no snow on the ground at Muroran-shi yet. The last seven days brought 13 cm of new snow.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 61 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 411 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Muroran-shi?

    Its highest lift reaches 472 m; the lowest starts at 411 m (61 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Muroran-shi have?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 1 lift.
