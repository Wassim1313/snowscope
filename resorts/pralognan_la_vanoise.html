    About Pralognan-la-Vanoise

    With some 25 km of runs, Pralognan-la-Vanoise belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,406 m to 2,336 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Courchevel, 8.2 km to the west.

    What there is to ski at Pralognan-la-Vanoise

    The piste map lists 41 graded runs, about 25 km in total. The largest share is green (beginner), with 7.5 km (30%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,871 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 November and lasts to 21 May: about 26 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 70 cm (lean years 37 cm, snowy years 109 cm). For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 96 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 84 cm to 165 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 142 cm, around 8 March.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 930 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,406 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Les Trois Vallées, reach 3,221 m. To get up there are 1 cable car, 4 chairlifts and 7 drag lifts. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. Above the ski area rises La Grande Casse (3,855 m), 7.7 km to the east, about 1,519 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Hôtel Le Grand Bec, Hôtel Edelweiss, Petit Mont-Blanc and Igesa.

    Pralognan-la-Vanoise: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Pralognan-la-Vanoise?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Pralognan-la-Vanoise at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 70 cm deep.

    How high is Pralognan-la-Vanoise?

    Between 1,406 m and 2,336 m, so 930 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Pralognan-la-Vanoise?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Pralognan-la-Vanoise?

    Les Trois Vallées, 13 km to the west, with about 542 km of pistes.
