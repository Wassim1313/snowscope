    About Pralognan-la-Vanoise

    With some 25 km of runs, Pralognan-la-Vanoise belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,406 m to 2,336 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Courchevel, 8.2 km to the west.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 94 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Pralognan-la-Vanoise. 45 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 89 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 930 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,406 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Les Trois Vallées, reach 3,221 m.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Hôtel Le Grand Bec, Hôtel Edelweiss, Petit Mont-Blanc and Igesa.
