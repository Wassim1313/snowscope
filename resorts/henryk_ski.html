    About Henryk Ski

    Henryk Ski is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 127 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Skiing here happens between 600 m and 811 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Góra Parkowa, 0.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Henryk Ski

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 2.0 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.0 km, or 100%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Henryk Ski gives 211 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 600 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 4 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Henryk Ski: common questions

    How high is Henryk Ski?

    Its highest lift reaches 811 m; the lowest starts at 600 m (211 m of vertical).

    Is Henryk Ski good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Henryk Ski?

    Słotwiny Arena, 1.6 km to the north-west, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Henryk Ski have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
