    About Unterberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Unterberg offers about 12 km of pistes (number 451 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 917 m to 1,321 m. The closest other ski area is Rohr im Gebirge (5.4 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Unterberg

    In all, 16 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Most of the length is blue (75%, about 8.5 km). With 75% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Unterberg gives 404 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 917 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 4 T-bars and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Unterberg (1,342 m), Trafelberg (1,146 m) and Sonnstein (1,124 m).

    How much snow Unterberg usually gets

    Count on about 2 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,119 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early February to late February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 35 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm, from 3 cm in a poor year to 41 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 6 cm and 61 cm by mid-February.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Unterberg Schutzhaus (mountain hut), Schneidlifthütte (mountain hut), Enzianhütte (mountain hut) and Fanni (chalet). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Unterberg

    When is the best time to ski at Unterberg?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    Is there snow at Unterberg at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    How high is Unterberg?

    Between 917 m and 1,321 m, so 404 m of vertical drop.

    Is Unterberg good for beginners?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 8.5 km, are easy.

    How big is Unterberg?

    About 12 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
