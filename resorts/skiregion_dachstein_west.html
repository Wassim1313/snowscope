    About Dachstein West

    Dachstein West is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 39 km of pistes put it at number 157 of 1,056. The slopes start at 745 m and reach 1,616 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lammertallift, 9.4 km to the south-west.

    The pistes of Dachstein West

    In all, 66 marked runs cover roughly 38 km. The largest share is red, with 28 km (73%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.4 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Dachstein West usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,180 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 19 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm, with 64 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 39 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Year to year the snow is steady, with 36 cm to 86 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 62 cm, around 19 February.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 871 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 745 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 25 lifts: 1 cable car, 3 gondolas, 9 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 7 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 7 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Große Bischofsmütze at 2,458 m, standing 842 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Dachstein West

    When is the best time to ski at Dachstein West?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Dachstein West at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Dachstein West?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Dachstein West?

    The top is at 1,616 m and the base at 745 m.

    What level of skier is Dachstein West best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
