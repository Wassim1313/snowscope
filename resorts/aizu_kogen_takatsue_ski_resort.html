    About Aizu Kogen Takatsue

    Aizu Kogen Takatsue is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 10 km of pistes put it at number 68 of 385. The slopes start at 960 m and reach 1,628 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Aizu Kogen Daikura, 7.0 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Aizu Kogen Takatsue

    Aizu Kogen Takatsue has 15 graded runs adding up to about 10 km. The largest share is green, with 6.2 km (59%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Aizu Kogen Takatsue

    Skied top to bottom, Aizu Kogen Takatsue gives 668 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,160 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,628 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 8 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Aizu Kogen Takatsue?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,628 m; the lowest starts at 960 m (668 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Aizu Kogen Takatsue?

    Yes: 78% of the graded pistes, about 8.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Aizu Kogen Takatsue have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
