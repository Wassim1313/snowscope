    About Aizu Kogen Takatsue

    Aizu Kogen Takatsue is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 10 km of pistes put it at number 68 of 385. The slopes start at 960 m and reach 1,628 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Aizu Kogen Daikura, 7.0 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Aizu Kogen Takatsue

    Skied top to bottom, Aizu Kogen Takatsue gives 668 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,160 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,628 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 8 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Aizu Kogen Takatsue. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 24 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.

    How high do the lifts go at Aizu Kogen Takatsue?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,628 m; the lowest starts at 960 m (668 m of vertical).
