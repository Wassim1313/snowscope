    About Pukkivuori

    Pukkivuori is a ski area. The closest other ski area is Pukkivuori (0.0 km, south-east).

    How much snow Pukkivuori usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 125 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early April, about 13 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February. Most winters land between 20 cm and 56 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 22 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 41 cm). A normal season tops out near 44 cm, around 7 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 22 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Pukkivuori?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Is there snow at Pukkivuori at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 22 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
