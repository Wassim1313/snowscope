    About Estación de Esquí y Montaña Alto

    Estación de Esquí y Montaña Alto tops the size table of the Cantabrian Mountains with roughly 20 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 1,648 m to 2,118 m. Its nearest neighbour is Alto Campoo, 3.1 km east, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Estación de Esquí y Montaña Alto

    Estación de Esquí y Montaña Alto has 30 graded runs adding up to about 19 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 9.1 km, or 47%. With 58% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Estación de Esquí y Montaña Alto

    The vertical drop is 470 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,648 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 4 chairlifts, 4 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Estación de Esquí y Montaña Alto at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,118 m; the lowest starts at 1,648 m (470 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Estación de Esquí y Montaña Alto?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Estación de Esquí y Montaña Alto have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
