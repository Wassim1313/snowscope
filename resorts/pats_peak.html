    About Pats Peak

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pats Peak offers about 14 km of pistes (number 43 of 93 in US Northeast). The ski area spans the mountain from 180 m up to 409 m. Its nearest neighbour is Crotched Mountain Ski & Ride, 18 km south-west, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Pats Peak has 39 graded runs adding up to about 14 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 8.8 km, or 61%. With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 229 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 180 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 6 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Craney Hill (420 m), Mount Hunger (405 m) and Bear Hill (402 m).

    How much snow Pats Peak usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 294 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 5 cm to 55 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 27 cm, around 4 March. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 11 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 31 cm).

    Pats Peak: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Pats Peak?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Is there snow at Pats Peak at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Pats Peak?

    The top is at 409 m and the base at 180 m.

    Can beginners ski at Pats Peak?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 8.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Pats Peak have?

    About 14 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
