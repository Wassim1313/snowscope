    About Klewenalp

    With roughly 18 km of runs, Klewenalp sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,199 m up to 1,934 m. The closest other ski area is Gitschenen (3.8 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Klewenalp

    In all, 71 marked runs cover roughly 18 km. Most of the length is blue (70%, about 12 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 735 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 20 lifts: 5 cable cars, 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 3 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Gross Rimistock (2,582 m), 7.7 km to the south, about 648 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Klewenalp through a normal winter

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,566 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 111 cm, around 5 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 54 cm, from 24 cm in a poor year to 68 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 67 cm and 133 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 92 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 4 weeks.

    Questions about Klewenalp

    What is the best month to ski at Klewenalp?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Klewenalp at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 54 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Klewenalp?

    Between 1,199 m and 1,934 m, so 735 m of vertical drop.

    Is Klewenalp good for beginners?

    Yes: 73% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How big is Klewenalp?

    About 18 km of pistes and 20 lifts.
