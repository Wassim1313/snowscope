    About Le Frenz

    Compact and local, Le Frenz has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 762 m up to 790 m. The closest other ski area is Le Markstein (5.8 km, east).

    The mountain at Le Frenz

    Skied top to bottom, Le Frenz gives 28 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 962 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: La Bresse, for one, climbs to 1,347 m.
