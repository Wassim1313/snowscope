    What 46 winters say about Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero)

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero) (about 68 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 20 cm, with a normal range of 12 cm–32 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 41 cm, between 28 cm and 52 cm in a normal winter.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 42 cm.

    By 15 December 2 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 7 %.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 86 cm. Worst: 2019-20, which never passed 11 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late December until early April in a normal winter, roughly 91 days.

    Questions about Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero)

    Is there snow in Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero) at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 20 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 7 % of 46 winters. Right now: 11 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero)?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 42 cm). For a sure base, late December to early April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero)?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 42 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 86 cm (1981-82), the thinnest 11 cm (2019-20).
