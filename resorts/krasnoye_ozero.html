    About Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero)

    Neither huge nor tiny, Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero) offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 114 of 262 in Scandinavia). Skiing here happens between 21 m and 116 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Золотая Долина (1.7 km, south-east).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 68 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 12 January and keeps it until about 1 April, roughly 12 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 42 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 5 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 20 cm to 63 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 36 cm).

    How Красное озеро (Krasnoye Ozero) is laid out

    95 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
