    About Hautacam

    Hautacam is a small ski area by the standards of the Pyrenees, with about 10 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,499 m and 1,726 m above sea level. Luz-Ardiden is the next resort, 11 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 26 runs and around 9.5 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 3.4 km, or 36%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 13 km south-east to Grand Tourmalet, with about 54 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Luz-Ardiden usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 19 weeks, against 13 here. For higher slopes, Piau-Engaly climbs to 2,527 m.

    How Hautacam is laid out

    227 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,499 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Piau-Engaly reaches 2,527 m. Getting up the mountain: 5 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Pène Det Pouri (2,587 m), 7.8 km to the south-east, about 861 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Hautacam through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,612 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 11 January and keeps it until about 9 April, roughly 13 weeks. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 47 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 22 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 21 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 50 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 84 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Questions about Hautacam

    When is the best time to ski at Hautacam?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    Can you ski at Hautacam at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 21 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Hautacam?

    The top is at 1,726 m and the base at 1,499 m.

    Is Hautacam good for beginners?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 6.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Hautacam?

    Grand Tourmalet, 13 km to the south-east, with about 54 km of pistes.
