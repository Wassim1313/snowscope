    About Hautacam

    Hautacam is a small ski area by the standards of the Pyrenees, with about 10 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,499 m and 1,726 m above sea level. Luz-Ardiden is the next resort, 11 km away to the south-west.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 13 km south-east to Grand Tourmalet, with about 54 km of runs. For higher slopes, Piau-Engaly climbs to 2,527 m.

    How Hautacam is laid out

    227 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,499 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Piau-Engaly reaches 2,527 m.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Gite La Hount, Grand Hotel de France, Hôtel Restaurant de la Gare and Hôtel des Touristes.
