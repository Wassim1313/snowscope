    About La Colmiane

    La Colmiane is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 399 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,431 m to 1,787 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Colmiane, 0.4 km to the north.

    What there is to ski at La Colmiane

    La Colmiane has 19 graded runs adding up to about 14 km. Most of the length is blue (32%, about 4.5 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 356 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,431 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Isola 2000, for one, climbs to 2,601 m. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 4 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Mont Pépoiri (2,674 m), 5.5 km to the north, about 887 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,609 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 60 cm. A normal season tops out near 19 cm, around 3 February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 7 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 37 cm).

    Questions about La Colmiane

    What is the best month to ski at La Colmiane?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does La Colmiane have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 6 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at La Colmiane?

    The top is at 1,787 m and the base at 1,431 m.

    Is La Colmiane suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 8.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near La Colmiane?

    Auron, 30 km to the north-west, with about 62 km of pistes.
