    About Rusutsu Resort

    Rusutsu Resort ranks number 6 of 385 ski areas in Japan by piste length, with about 31 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 422 m to 988 m. Kyogoku Chomin is the next resort, 14 km away to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 27 graded runs, about 31 km in total. Most of the length is red (56%, about 17 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 3.6 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Rusutsu Resort

    The vertical drop is 566 m: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 422 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 4 gondolas and 14 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.

    Quick answers

    How high is Rusutsu Resort?

    Its highest lift reaches 988 m; the lowest starts at 422 m (566 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Rusutsu Resort best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 56% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Rusutsu Resort?

    About 31 km of pistes and 18 lifts.
