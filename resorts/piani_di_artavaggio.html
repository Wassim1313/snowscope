    About Piani di Artavaggio

    Compact and local, Piani di Artavaggio has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,610 m up to 1,648 m. Piani di Bobbio is the next resort, 4.4 km away to the north-west.

    How much snow Piani di Artavaggio usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,629 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to mid-April, about 13 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 26 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 22 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 63 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 15 cm to 106 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 12 March, with about 61 cm.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 1.3 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.3 km (100%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 38 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,610 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 1 cable car and 4 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Zuccone Campelli, 2,161 m high and 2.3 km north-west of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 4 mountain huts: Rifugio Angelo Casari, Rifugio Sassi Castelli, Baita della Luna and Rifugio Nicola. Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Piani di Artavaggio

    When does Piani di Artavaggio have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Piani di Artavaggio at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Piani di Artavaggio at?

    Between 1,610 m and 1,648 m, so 38 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Piani di Artavaggio?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Piani di Artavaggio?

    Piani di Bobbio, 4.4 km to the north-west, with about 18 km of pistes.
