    About Schneiderkopflifte

    Schneiderkopflifte is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 704 m and 871 m above sea level. Alberschwende is the next resort, 4.0 km away to the south-east.

    Schneiderkopflifte and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Bödele: about 21 km of pistes, 7.6 km to the south. Bödele has the longer snow season: around 19 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Schneiderkopflifte. For higher slopes, Bödele climbs to 1,439 m.

    The mountain at Schneiderkopflifte

    From top to bottom that makes 167 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 904 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Bödele reaches 1,439 m. To get up there are 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Geißkopf (1,198 m), 5.7 km to the south, about 327 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 788 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 7 February. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 11 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 6 cm to 38 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 29 cm).

    Schneiderkopflifte: common questions

    When does Schneiderkopflifte have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Schneiderkopflifte at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Schneiderkopflifte?

    The top is at 871 m and the base at 704 m.

    Is Schneiderkopflifte good for beginners?

    Yes: 45% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Schneiderkopflifte?

    Bödele, 7.6 km to the south, with about 21 km of pistes.
