    The snow at Schmittenhöhe Zell am See, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Schmittenhöhe Zell am See (about 1,360 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 31 cm; half of the winters sit between 20 cm and 42 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 57 cm; the middle half of winters range from 43 cm to 68 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 62 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    4 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 13 % by 1 January.

    1981-82 was the big one (97 cm); 2006-07 the lean one (40 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early December to early May (157 days).

    Schmittenhöhe Zell am See: quick answers

    Is there snow in Schmittenhöhe Zell am See at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 31 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 13 % of 46 winters. Right now: 19 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Schmittenhöhe Zell am See?

    February: the snow peaks at about 62 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to early May.

    How snowy is Schmittenhöhe Zell am See?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 62 cm in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 97 cm (1981-82), the thinnest 40 cm (2006-07).

    How high do the lifts go at Schmittenhöhe Zell am See?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,963 m; the lowest starts at 758 m (1,205 m of vertical).

    About 59 km of pistes and 27 lifts.

    About Schmittenhöhe Zell am See

    Schmittenhöhe Zell am See is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 59 km of pistes put it at number 97 of 1,056. The slopes start at 758 m and reach 1,963 m at the top. Lechnerberglifte is the next resort, 6.5 km away to the south.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,205 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 758 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.
