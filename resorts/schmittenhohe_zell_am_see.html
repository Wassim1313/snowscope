    About Schmittenhöhe Zell am See

    Schmittenhöhe Zell am See is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 59 km of pistes put it at number 97 of 1,056. The slopes start at 758 m and reach 1,963 m at the top. Lechnerberglifte is the next resort, 6.5 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Schmittenhöhe Zell am See

    The piste map lists 129 graded runs, about 59 km in total. Most of the length is blue (45%, about 27 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 11 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,360 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to early May. A normal season tops out near 62 cm, around 27 February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm; a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 12 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 33 cm to 74 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 37 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,205 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 758 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 2 cable cars, 6 gondolas, 10 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 3 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets, 27 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Oberer Gernkogel, 2,175 m high and 6.9 km west of the resort.

    When does Schmittenhöhe Zell am See have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    In general yes, with around 31 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Schmittenhöhe Zell am See?
