    About Ski areál Šukačka

    Ski areál Šukačka has about 0.4 km of pistes. The slopes start at 883 m and reach 952 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Javorná, 2.2 km to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 69 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 883 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a button lift.

    How much snow Ski areál Šukačka usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 918 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 24 cm, around 11 February. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and mid-February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 11 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 37 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 58 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Ski areál Šukačka: common questions

    When does Ski areál Šukačka have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Ski areál Šukačka at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).

    What altitude is Ski areál Šukačka at?

    Between 883 m and 952 m, so 69 m of vertical drop.
