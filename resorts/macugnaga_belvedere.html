    About Macugnaga

    Macugnaga is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 583 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,373 m up to 1,906 m. The closest other ski area is Macugnaga (4.1 km, north-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 6.6 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 2.5 km (39%). With 63% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 1 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Macugnaga. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 17 cm by Friday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (9 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 533 m: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,373 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Dufourspitze, 4,634 m high and 6.2 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 13 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,640 m in an ordinary winter, from late January to late April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 19 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 36 cm, a dry one 11 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 22 cm to 59 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 32 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 39 cm.

    Macugnaga: common questions

    When does Macugnaga have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late April.

    Can you ski at Macugnaga at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 19 cm deep.

    How high is Macugnaga?

    The top is at 1,906 m and the base at 1,373 m.

    Is Macugnaga good for beginners?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Macugnaga?

    Zermatt, 18 km to the west, with about 208 km of pistes.
