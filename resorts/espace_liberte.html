    About Espace Liberté

    Espace Liberté is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 103 km of pistes put it at number 46 of 1,056. The slopes start at 994 m and reach 2,094 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is La Chapelle d'Abondance, 2.7 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 218 runs and around 101 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 42 km, or 42%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 0.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,544 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 November and keeps it until about 22 May, roughly 25 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 62 cm; a snowy December gave 104 cm, a dry one 18 cm. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 93 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 135 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 74 cm and 174 cm by mid-February.

    How Espace Liberté is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Espace Liberté gives 1,100 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 994 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 53 lifts: 2 gondolas, 18 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 15 button lifts, 12 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Mont de Grange, 2,432 m high and 2.5 km west of the resort.

    Questions about Espace Liberté

    When does Espace Liberté have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Espace Liberté at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 62 cm deep.

    When does Espace Liberté open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 18 April 2027.

    What altitude is Espace Liberté at?

    The top is at 2,094 m and the base at 994 m.

    Can beginners ski at Espace Liberté?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 52 km, are easy.
