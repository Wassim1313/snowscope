    About Predaia

    Compact and local, Predaia has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,260 m to 1,392 m. The closest other ski area is Mendel (9.6 km, north-east).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,326 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 December and keeps it until about 29 April, roughly 19 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 17 March, with about 48 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 23 cm to 77 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 43 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 9 cm in a poor year to 64 cm in a big one.

    What there is to ski at Predaia

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.9 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.9 km, or 100%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Predaia and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 24 km west to Folgarida-Marilleva, with about 41 km of runs. For snow it is second in the group, behind Jochgrimm (20 weeks of good cover, 19 here). For higher slopes, Jochgrimm climbs to 2,227 m.

    How Predaia is laid out

    The vertical drop is 132 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta, for one, climbs to 2,511 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 2 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Roen (2,116 m), 5.4 km to the north-east, about 724 m higher than the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rifugio Predaia «ai Todes ci» (mountain hut), Rifugio Sores (mountain hut), Hotel Enrosadira (hotel) and Malga Nuova Coredo (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Predaia?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Predaia at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 32 cm deep.

    How high is Predaia?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,392 m; the lowest starts at 1,260 m (132 m of vertical).

    Is Predaia suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Predaia?

    Folgarida-Marilleva, 24 km to the west, with about 41 km of pistes.
