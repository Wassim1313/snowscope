    About Innerkrems

    Innerkrems is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 289 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,465 m and reach 2,167 m at the top. Schönfeld is the next resort, 2.3 km away to the north-east.

    How much snow Innerkrems usually gets

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,816 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 54 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 66 cm, around 26 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 34 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 57 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 37 cm to 87 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    What there is to ski at Innerkrems

    Innerkrems has 22 graded runs adding up to about 22 km. The largest share is red, with 12 km (52%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 2.3 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 702 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,465 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 9 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 1 drag lift and 2 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Kilnprein, 2,408 m high and 7.1 km east of the resort.

    Questions about Innerkrems

    When does Innerkrems have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Innerkrems at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Innerkrems?

    Between 1,465 m and 2,167 m, so 702 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Innerkrems best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Innerkrems have?

    About 22 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
