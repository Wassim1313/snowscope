    About Ski centar Savin kuk

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Ski centar Savin kuk sits in the middle of the size table of the Balkans. The slopes start at 1,514 m and reach 2,175 m at the top.

    The mountain at Ski centar Savin kuk

    The vertical drop is 661 m: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,514 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 5 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 3 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 4.2 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.6 km (62%). With 62% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Ski centar Savin kuk?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,175 m; the lowest starts at 1,514 m (661 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Ski centar Savin kuk?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 2.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ski centar Savin kuk?

    Kolašin 1600, 55 km to the south-east, with about 21 km of pistes.

    How big is Ski centar Savin kuk?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
