    About Sachrang

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Sachrang one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 731 m and reach 825 m at the top. Zahmer Kaiser is the next resort, 6.2 km away to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 1.4 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 100%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Sachrang today and the next days

    Snow on the ground at Sachrang today: about 11 cm up high. The last seven days brought 14 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (7 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 94 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 731 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Wendelstein reaches 1,718 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 5 rope tows, 6 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Geigelstein (1,808 m), 6.4 km to the east, about 983 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Sachrang through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 778 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 12 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 30 cm in the best years. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 5 cm to 44 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 23 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February.

    Questions about Sachrang

    When is the best time to ski at Sachrang?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    How much snow does Sachrang have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).

    What altitude is Sachrang at?

    Between 731 m and 825 m, so 94 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Sachrang?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sachrang?

    Sudelfeld, 16 km to the west, with about 26 km of pistes.
