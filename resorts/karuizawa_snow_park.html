    About Karuizawa Snow Park

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Karuizawa Snow Park sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Its lifts climb from 1,271 m to 1,402 m. Prince Hotels and Resorts Karuiz is the next resort, 9.5 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Karuizawa Snow Park

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 2.3 km in total. Green runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 73%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.
