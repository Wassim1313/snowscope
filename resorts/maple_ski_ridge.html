    About Maple Ski Ridge

    Maple Ski Ridge is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 66 of 93 in US Northeast by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 163 m up to 247 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ridge Runner, 12 km north, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 205 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 7 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 22 cm). Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 47 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 11 February, with about 17 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    84 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 163 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 4 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 2 rope tows.

    Questions about Maple Ski Ridge

    When is the best time to ski at Maple Ski Ridge?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Maple Ski Ridge at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 7 cm).

    How high is Maple Ski Ridge?

    Between 163 m and 247 m, so 84 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Maple Ski Ridge best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 61% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Maple Ski Ridge?

    West Mountain, 58 km to the north-east, with about 9.0 km of pistes.
