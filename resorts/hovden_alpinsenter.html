    About Hovden Alpinsenter

    Hovden Alpinsenter is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 32 km of pistes put it at number 16 of 262. The slopes start at 762 m and reach 1,183 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Hallbjønnsekken skisenter (33 km, south-east).

    The pistes of Hovden Alpinsenter

    Hovden Alpinsenter has 42 graded runs adding up to about 31 km. Most of the length is red (45%, about 14 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 2.8 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Hovden Alpinsenter usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 972 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early November to late May or later, about 30 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 29 March, with about 220 cm. For about 24 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 213 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 145 cm and 252 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 119 cm, from 75 cm in a poor year to 156 cm in a big one.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 421 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 3 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 9 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Veggine, 1,341 m high and 5.1 km north-west of the resort.

    Hovden Alpinsenter: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Hovden Alpinsenter?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Hovden Alpinsenter have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 119 cm deep.

    How high is Hovden Alpinsenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,183 m; the lowest starts at 762 m (421 m of vertical).

    Is Hovden Alpinsenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Hovden Alpinsenter have?

    About 32 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
