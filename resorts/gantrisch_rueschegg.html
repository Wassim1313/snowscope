    About Gantrisch

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gantrisch offers about 11 km of pistes (number 463 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,051 m up to 1,554 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skilift Gurnigel-Bad, 2.0 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 11 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 7.3 km (65%). With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Gantrisch through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,302 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to mid-April, about 15 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 27 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 46 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 56 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 30 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Year to year the snow is steady, with 22 cm to 83 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    The mountain at Gantrisch

    503 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. Above the ski area rises Ochsen (2,188 m), 5.4 km to the south, about 634 m higher than the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Gantrisch has roughly 4 cm of snow on the ground today. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 41 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 7 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Gantrisch include Bergheim STB (chalet), Stockhütte (mountain hut), Chrigelerhütte (chalet) and Schwefelberg Bad (hotel).

    Questions about Gantrisch

    When is the best time to ski at Gantrisch?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Gantrisch at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 27 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Gantrisch at?

    The top is at 1,554 m and the base at 1,051 m.

    Can beginners ski at Gantrisch?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 7.3 km, are easy.

    How big is Gantrisch?

    About 11 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
