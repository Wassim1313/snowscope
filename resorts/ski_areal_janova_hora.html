    About Ski areál Janova hora

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Ski areál Janova hora one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 633 m to 775 m. The closest other ski area is Aldrov (1.8 km, south-west).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 142 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 633 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Špindlerův Mlýn, reach 1,035 m. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow.
