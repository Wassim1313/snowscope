    About Sarves Alta

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sarves Alta offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 103 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 64 m to 269 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 19 marked runs cover roughly 6.2 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 2.3 km, or 37%. With 69% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Sarves Alta hold around 14 cm of snow today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (29 cm).

    When the snow is good

    At about 166 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 November and lasts to 24 May: about 26 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 82 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 56 cm and 96 cm. A normal season tops out near 87 cm, around 31 March. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 50 cm, with 70 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 205 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Ulvehenget, 412 m high and 3.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Questions about Sarves Alta

    What is the best month to ski at Sarves Alta?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Sarves Alta at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 50 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Sarves Alta?

    Between 64 m and 269 m, so 205 m of vertical drop.

    Is Sarves Alta suitable for families?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Sarves Alta have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
