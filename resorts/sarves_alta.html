    About Sarves Alta

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sarves Alta offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 103 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 64 m to 269 m.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Sarves Alta hold around 14 cm of snow today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Sunday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts
