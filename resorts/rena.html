    About Rena

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Rena sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 497 m up to 712 m. Its nearest neighbour is Budor skitrekk, 25 km south, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Rena through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 604 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 November and keeps it until about 7 May, roughly 23 weeks. Most winters land between 65 cm and 117 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 77 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 20 March, with about 90 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 52 cm, from 27 cm in a poor year to 81 cm in a big one.

    Other resorts close by

    The mountain at Rena

    The vertical drop is 215 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. The skyline is dominated by Hemmelfjellet, 885 m high and 6.8 km west of the resort.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Rena hold around 11 cm of snow today. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 35 cm by now.

    Quick answers

    When does Rena have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Can you ski at Rena at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 52 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Rena?

    Its highest lift reaches 712 m; the lowest starts at 497 m (215 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Rena?

    Hafjell, 45 km to the west, with about 39 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Rena have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
