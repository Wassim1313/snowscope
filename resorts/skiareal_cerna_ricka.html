    About Skiareál Černá Říčka

    Skiareál Černá Říčka is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 681 m up to 781 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skiareál Jizerský kopec, 2.5 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 4.3 km south-west to Tanvaldský Špičák, with about 6.0 km of runs. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 1.5 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 62%. With 62% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skiareál Černá Říčka gives 100 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 681 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 731 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 50 cm. A normal season tops out near 28 cm, around 21 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 9 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 44 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early January and late January.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Skiareál Černá Říčka?

    Aim for early January and late January.

    Is there snow at Skiareál Černá Říčka at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Skiareál Černá Říčka at?

    Its highest lift reaches 781 m; the lowest starts at 681 m (100 m of vertical).

    Is Skiareál Černá Říčka good for beginners?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skiareál Černá Říčka?

    Tanvaldský Špičák, 4.3 km to the south-west, with about 6.0 km of pistes.
