    About Sugadaira Kogen Davos Hill

    Around 13 km of marked runs place Sugadaira Kogen Davos Hill among the larger resorts of Japan, number 46 by length. The slopes start at 1,325 m and reach 1,598 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Sugadaira Taro Area (1.8 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Sugadaira Kogen Davos Hill

    In all, 23 marked runs cover roughly 12 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 7.7 km, or 62%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Sugadaira Kogen Davos Hill is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Sugadaira Kogen Davos Hill gives 273 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,325 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Sugadaira Kogen Davos Hill?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,598 m; the lowest starts at 1,325 m (273 m of vertical).

    Is Sugadaira Kogen Davos Hill suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 7.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Sugadaira Kogen Davos Hill have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
