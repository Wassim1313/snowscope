    About Buchserberg

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Buchserberg one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 1,377 m and reach 1,483 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Wildhaus (7.6 km, north-west).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Flumserberg (14 km south-west) has about 59 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Malbun does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 16. The highest nearby is Chäserrugg-Alt St. Johann, topping out at 2,260 m.

    The pistes of Buchserberg

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 2.0 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 2.0 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Buchserberg today and the next days

    Snow on the ground at Buchserberg today: about 7 cm up high. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 38 cm by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 12 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    106 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,377 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Gamsberg (2,385 m), 4.5 km to the west, about 902 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,430 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to late April, about 16 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 26 cm (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 38 cm). Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 57 cm. Most winters land between 33 cm and 70 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 41 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Buchser Hof (hotel), Alp Wisliboda (mountain hut), Schihus Wisliboda (mountain hut) and Boarding House BoHo (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Buchserberg

    When is the best time to ski at Buchserberg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    How much snow does Buchserberg have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 26 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Buchserberg at?

    Between 1,377 m and 1,483 m, so 106 m of vertical drop.

    Is Buchserberg suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Buchserberg?

    Flumserberg, 14 km to the south-west, with about 59 km of pistes.
