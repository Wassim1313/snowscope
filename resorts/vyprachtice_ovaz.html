    About Výprachtice

    Výprachtice has about 0.4 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 698 m and 767 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Čenkovice (1.9 km, north-east).

    The mountain at Výprachtice

    Skied top to bottom, Výprachtice gives 69 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 698 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.
