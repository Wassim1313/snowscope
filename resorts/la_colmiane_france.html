    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,739 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 26 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 10 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 42 cm). Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 69 cm.
