    About Oberholz

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Oberholz sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 851 m and reach 1,167 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Fischenthal, 4.0 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Oberholz usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,009 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 4 January and keeps it until about 1 May, roughly 17 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm; a snowy December gave 43 cm, a dry one 8 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 52 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 22 cm to 82 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 54 cm.

    What there is to ski at Oberholz

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 4.2 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 3.2 km (78%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Oberholz

    The vertical drop is 316 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 851 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow, 3 lifts in all. Above the ski area rises Tweralpspitz (1,332 m), 6.0 km to the east, about 165 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Oberholz

    When is the best time to ski at Oberholz?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early January to early May.

    Is there snow at Oberholz at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Oberholz?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,167 m; the lowest starts at 851 m (316 m of vertical).

    Is Oberholz suitable for families?

    Yes: 78% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Oberholz have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
