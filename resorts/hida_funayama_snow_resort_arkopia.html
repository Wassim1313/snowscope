    About Hida Funayama Snow Resort Arkopi

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Hida Funayama Snow Resort Arkopi sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Skiing here happens between 857 m and 1,259 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mont Deus Hida-kuraiyama Snow Pa, 4.3 km to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Hida Funayama Snow Resort Arkopi gives 402 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,057 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
