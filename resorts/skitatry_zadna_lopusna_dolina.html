    About Skitatry Zadná Lopušná dolina

    Compact and local, Skitatry Zadná Lopušná dolina has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Carpathians. Its lifts climb from 780 m to 1,016 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lopušná Dolina, 0.7 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 236 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 780 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 10 km south-west to Liptovská Teplička, with about 5.0 km of runs. For higher slopes, Liptovská Teplička climbs to 1,255 m.
