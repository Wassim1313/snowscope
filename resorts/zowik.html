    About Żółwik

    Żółwik has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 776 m up to 810 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pod Wangiem, 0.2 km to the north-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    34 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 976 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 810 m is the second highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.
