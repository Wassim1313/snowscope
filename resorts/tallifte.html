    About Tallifte

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Tallifte one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 1,128 m and reach 1,253 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Heuberg Arena/Walmendingerhorn, 1.5 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Tallifte compares nearby

    For more terrain, Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf (5.0 km north-east) has about 108 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. For more reliable snow, Warth-Schröcken usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 27 weeks, against 9 here. The highest nearby is Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf, topping out at 2,213 m.

    The pistes of Tallifte

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.9 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 1.3 km (70%). With 70% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Tallifte is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 125 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf, for one, climbs to 2,213 m. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Biberkopf, 2,599 m high and 8.0 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,190 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to mid-March, about 9 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 22 cm, from 3 cm in a poor year to 40 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 50 cm. Most winters land between 25 cm and 80 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 25 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Questions about Tallifte

    What is the best month to ski at Tallifte?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Tallifte have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Tallifte?

    Between 1,128 m and 1,253 m, so 125 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Tallifte?

    Yes: 70% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Tallifte?

    Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf, 5.0 km to the north-east, with about 108 km of pistes.
