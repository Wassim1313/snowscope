    About Rokkosan Snow Park

    Rokkosan Snow Park has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 813 m and 840 m above sea level.

    The snow right now

    Rokkosan Snow Park has no snow cover yet today.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 27 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 813 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 cable car and 2 chairlifts. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.
