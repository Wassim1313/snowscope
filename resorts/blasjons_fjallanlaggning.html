    About Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning

    Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 83 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 473 m up to 923 m. The closest other ski area is Borgefjellsenteret (28 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 8.9 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.4 km, or 49%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning gives 450 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 button lift and 3 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mesklumpen (924 m), Halvvägestklumpen (874 m) and Skacken (841 m).

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 698 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 16 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 84 cm, with 106 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 158 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 96 cm and 180 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 18 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 162 cm, around 3 April.

    Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 84 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning?

    Between 473 m and 923 m, so 450 m of vertical drop.

    Is Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning good for beginners?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 6.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
