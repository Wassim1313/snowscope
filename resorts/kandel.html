    About Kandellifte

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Kandellifte sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,039 m and reach 1,201 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Bregtallift Furtwangen (12 km, east).

    The pistes of Kandellifte

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 2.5 km to ski. Most of the length is red (45%, about 1.1 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 162 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 2 drag lifts and 2 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Kandel (1,242 m), Sattelhöhe (1,216 m) and Hornkopf (1,121 m).

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,120 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 11 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; a snowy December gave 23 cm, a dry one 0 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 38 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Kandellifte: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Kandellifte?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Can you ski at Kandellifte at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 5 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Kandellifte?

    The top is at 1,201 m and the base at 1,039 m.

    Who is Kandellifte best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 45% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Kandellifte?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
