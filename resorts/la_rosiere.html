    About La Rosière

    With some 68 km of runs, La Rosière belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,192 m to 2,799 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Thuile ski, 7.7 km to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 104 graded runs, about 68 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 29 km, or 43%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 2.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at La Rosière

    From top to bottom that makes 1,607 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. To get up there are 10 chairlifts, 9 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 9 moving carpets. First-timers get 9 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Aiguille de l'Ermite / Aiguille dell'Hermite (2,998 m), 6.8 km to the north, about 199 m higher than the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Paradiski: about 398 km of pistes, 15 km to the south-west. Paradiski has the longer snow season: around 29 weeks of solid cover, compared with 26 at La Rosière. For higher slopes, Les Arcs climbs to 3,205 m.

    How much snow La Rosière usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,996 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 November and lasts to 25 May: about 26 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 95 cm to 189 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 128 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 15 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 160 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 82 cm; a snowy December gave 127 cm, a dry one 42 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at La Rosière?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at La Rosière at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 82 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does La Rosière open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 17 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at La Rosière?

    Between 1,192 m and 2,799 m, so 1,607 m of vertical drop.

    Is La Rosière good for beginners?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 36 km, are easy.
