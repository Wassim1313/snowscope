    The snow at La Rosière, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at La Rosière (1,996 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 82 cm, with a normal range of 55 cm–107 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 152 cm, between 124 cm and 173 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 160 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 65 % of winters, and by 1 January in 96 %.

    The deepest winter was 1994-95 (262 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2010-11 (125 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to late May, about 194 days.

    La Rosière: quick answers

    Is there snow in La Rosière at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 82 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 96 % of 46 winters. Right now: 138 cm.

    What is the best time to ski La Rosière?

    March: the snow peaks at about 160 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to late May.

    How snowy is La Rosière?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 160 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 262 cm (1994-95), the thinnest 125 cm (2010-11).

    How high do the lifts go at La Rosière?

    Between 1,192 m and 2,799 m, so 1,607 m of vertical drop.

    Is there a larger resort near La Rosière?

    Paradiski, 15 km to the south-west, with about 398 km of pistes.

    How big is La Rosière?

    About 68 km of pistes and 29 lifts.
