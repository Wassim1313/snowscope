    About Schlick 2000

    With roughly 21 km of runs, Schlick 2000 sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,007 m and 2,235 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being 11er Neustift/Neder, 5.3 km to the south.
