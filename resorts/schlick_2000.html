    About Schlick 2000

    With roughly 21 km of runs, Schlick 2000 sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,007 m and 2,235 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being 11er Neustift/Neder, 5.3 km to the south.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,621 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 88 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 37 cm; a snowy December gave 54 cm, a dry one 20 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 57 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 71 cm.

    How Schlick 2000 is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,228 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 1 button lift, 8 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Schwarzhorn (2,812 m), 5.9 km to the west, about 577 m higher than the top lift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Schlick 2000 has 48 graded runs adding up to about 21 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 14 km, or 68%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes. 0.9 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Schlick 2000: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Schlick 2000?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Schlick 2000 at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 37 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Schlick 2000?

    The top is at 2,235 m and the base at 1,007 m.

    Is Schlick 2000 suitable for families?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How big is Schlick 2000?

    About 21 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
