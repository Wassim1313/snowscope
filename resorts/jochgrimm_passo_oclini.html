    About Jochgrimm

    Neither huge nor tiny, Jochgrimm offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 620 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,843 m and 2,227 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Passo Lavazè, 3.0 km to the east.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,035 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. Most winters land between 25 cm and 85 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 35 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 70 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 29 March, with about 61 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 47 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    What there is to ski at Jochgrimm

    Jochgrimm has 13 graded runs adding up to about 5.9 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 3.5 km, or 59%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Jochgrimm

    Skied top to bottom, Jochgrimm gives 384 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,843 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Pala di Santa - Pala de Zanca (2,493 m), 4.9 km to the east, about 266 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Jochgrimm

    What is the best month to ski at Jochgrimm?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Jochgrimm have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 35 cm deep.

    How high is Jochgrimm?

    The top is at 2,227 m and the base at 1,843 m.

    Is Jochgrimm suitable for families?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 2.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Jochgrimm?

    Latemar Dolomites, 7.7 km to the east, with about 40 km of pistes.
