    About Ski Králičák

    With some 8.0 km of runs, Ski Králičák belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The slopes start at 622 m and reach 863 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Skiareál Kunčice, 2.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Ski Králičák

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 8.4 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.3 km, or 51%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Ski Králičák is laid out

    The vertical drop is 241 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 622 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets, 6 lifts in all. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 742 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 49 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 12 cm, with 38 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. A normal season tops out near 28 cm, around 26 February.

    Questions about Ski Králičák

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Králičák?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Ski Králičák at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 12 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Králičák?

    Its highest lift reaches 863 m; the lowest starts at 622 m (241 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Ski Králičák?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 4.3 km, are easy.

    How big is Ski Králičák?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
