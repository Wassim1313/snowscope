    About Králíky

    Neither huge nor tiny, Králíky offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 137 of 312 in the Carpathians). Its lifts climb from 804 m to 927 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skalka, 1.9 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Králíky

    Králíky has 4 graded runs adding up to about 1.8 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Králíky

    Skied top to bottom, Králíky gives 123 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 804 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 1 drag lift.

    Questions about Králíky

    What altitude is Králíky at?

    The top is at 927 m and the base at 804 m.

    Can beginners ski at Králíky?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Králíky?

    Šachtičky, 12 km to the north-east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Králíky?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
