    About Dongbeiya

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Dongbeiya sits in the middle of the size table of China. Its lifts climb from 153 m to 346 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Guaipo Ski Field, 6.5 km to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 10 graded runs, about 3.9 km in total. Most of the length is black diamond (54%, about 2.1 km). Good skiers will like it here; 54% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 2.1 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Dongbeiya gives 193 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 153 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 346 m. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 1 button lift and 4 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    Is Dongbeiya good for advanced skiers?
