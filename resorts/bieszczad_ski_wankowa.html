    About Bieszczad Ski Wańkowa

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Bieszczad Ski Wańkowa sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 408 m and reach 617 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Czerwony szlak, 10 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Bieszczad Ski Wańkowa

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 2.5 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.5 km (100%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 209 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 408 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 3 moving carpets. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts.
