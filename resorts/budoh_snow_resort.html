    About Budoh Snow Resort

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Budoh Snow Resort sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The slopes start at 153 m and reach 657 m at the top. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hachimoriyama, 34 km to the north.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Budoh Snow Resort gives 504 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 153 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Budoh Snow Resort today: about 13 cm up high. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 50 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.
