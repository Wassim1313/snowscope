    About Väsjöbacken

    Väsjöbacken is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 24 m and 98 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Skistar Hammarbybacken, 19 km south-east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.
