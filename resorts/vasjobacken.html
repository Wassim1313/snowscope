    About Väsjöbacken

    Väsjöbacken is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 24 m and 98 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Skistar Hammarbybacken, 19 km south-east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Väsjöbacken

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 1.4 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.0 km, or 75%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 74 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 6 lifts break down into 3 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 drag lift. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Väsjöbacken (95 m), Tunberget (62 m) and Erikslundstippen (59 m).

    How much snow Väsjöbacken usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 61 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm, with 20 cm in the best years. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 33 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 10 cm, around 17 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Questions about Väsjöbacken

    When does Väsjöbacken have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Is there snow at Väsjöbacken at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Väsjöbacken?

    The top is at 98 m and the base at 24 m.

    What level of skier is Väsjöbacken best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 75% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Väsjöbacken have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
