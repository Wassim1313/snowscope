    About Bregtallift Furtwangen

    Bregtallift Furtwangen has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 907 m and reach 969 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Kalte Herberge (5.0 km, south).

    How much snow Bregtallift Furtwangen usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 938 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 31 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 9 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; a snowy December gave 22 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and mid-January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Bregtallift Furtwangen gives 62 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 907 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Rosseck (1,155 m), 6.6 km to the north-west, about 186 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Bregtallift Furtwangen have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and mid-January.

    Can you ski at Bregtallift Furtwangen at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Bregtallift Furtwangen at?

    The top is at 969 m and the base at 907 m.

    How big is Bregtallift Furtwangen?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
