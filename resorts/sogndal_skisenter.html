    About Sogndal skisenter

    Sogndal skisenter is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 12 km of pistes put it at number 60 of 262. The slopes start at 487 m and reach 938 m at the top. Sogn skisenter is the next resort, 13 km away to the east.

    What there is to ski at Sogndal skisenter

    Sogndal skisenter has 13 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.9 km, or 40%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 712 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late October to late May or later, about 31 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 146 cm and 329 cm by mid-February. For about 26 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 255 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 279 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 154 cm, from 96 cm in a poor year to 213 cm in a big one.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 451 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Synnevaskjer (1,543 m), 6.6 km to the west, about 605 m higher than the top lift.

    Sogndal skisenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Sogndal skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    How much snow does Sogndal skisenter have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 154 cm deep.

    How high is Sogndal skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 938 m; the lowest starts at 487 m (451 m of vertical).

    Is Sogndal skisenter good for beginners?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 9.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Sogndal skisenter?

    About 12 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
