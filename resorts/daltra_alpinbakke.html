    Snow at Daltrøa alpinbakke through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 219 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 December and keeps it until about 28 April, roughly 19 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 39 cm, with 61 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 60 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 82 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 42 cm and 103 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Daltrøa alpinbakke

    What is the best month to ski at Daltrøa alpinbakke?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Daltrøa alpinbakke at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
