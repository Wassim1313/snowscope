    About Proctor

    Compact and local, Proctor has about 2.0 km of runs, small for US Northeast. Skiing here happens between 190 m and 315 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ragged, 5.7 km north, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Proctor and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Mount Sunapee: about 31 km of pistes, 23 km to the south-west. Tenney has the longer snow season: around 9 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Proctor. Mount Sunapee reaches 827 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The pistes of Proctor

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.5 km to ski. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 1.5 km, or 100%. Steep ground is the speciality: 100% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.5 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Proctor gives 125 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 190 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Mount Kearsarge (891 m), 5.7 km to the south, about 576 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 252 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; a snowy December gave 30 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 28 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 60 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Proctor have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Can you ski at Proctor at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 12 cm).

    What altitude is Proctor at?

    Between 190 m and 315 m, so 125 m of vertical drop.

    Does Proctor have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.5 km of its graded pistes (100%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Proctor?

    Mount Sunapee, 23 km to the south-west, with about 31 km of pistes.
