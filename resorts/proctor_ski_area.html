    About Proctor

    Compact and local, Proctor has about 2.0 km of runs, small for US Northeast. Skiing here happens between 190 m and 315 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ragged, 5.7 km north, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Proctor and the resorts around it

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Proctor gives 125 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 190 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Highland Lake Inn (guesthouse).
