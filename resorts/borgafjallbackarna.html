    About Borgafjällbackarna

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Borgafjällbackarna sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The slopes start at 503 m and reach 792 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Klimpfjällsbackarna, 29 km to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Borgafjällbackarna

    The piste map lists 14 graded runs, about 10 km in total. Most of the length is blue (58%, about 5.9 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at Borgafjällbackarna

    289 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Jiengejehtseme, 1,477 m high and 7.6 km north-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 648 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 88 cm and 136 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 72 cm; a snowy December gave 98 cm, a dry one 49 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 25 March, with about 124 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 120 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Questions about Borgafjällbackarna

    When does Borgafjällbackarna have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Borgafjällbackarna at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 72 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Borgafjällbackarna?

    The top is at 792 m and the base at 503 m.

    Is Borgafjällbackarna good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 5.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Borgafjällbackarna?

    About 10 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
