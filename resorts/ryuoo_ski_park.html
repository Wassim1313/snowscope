    About Ryuoo Ski Park

    Ryuoo Ski Park is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 12 km of pistes put it at number 50 of 385. Its lifts climb from 867 m to 1,922 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kitashiga Komaruyama, 1.8 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Ryuoo Ski Park

    Ryuoo Ski Park has 23 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. Most of the length is black (37%, about 4.5 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 37% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 4.5 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,055 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 867 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The lift system counts 12 lifts: 1 cable car and 11 chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    Ryuoo Ski Park: common questions

    What altitude is Ryuoo Ski Park at?

    Between 867 m and 1,922 m, so 1,055 m of vertical drop.

    Does Ryuoo Ski Park have steep runs?

    Yes: about 4.5 km of its graded pistes (37%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Ryuoo Ski Park have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
