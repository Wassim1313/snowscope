    About Velká Úpa

    Velká Úpa is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 140 of 257 in Central Europe by length. The slopes start at 706 m and reach 1,071 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is U Karla, 2.0 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Velká Úpa

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 2.1 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.1 km, or 100%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How much snow Velká Úpa usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 888 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 January and keeps it until about 9 March, roughly 7 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 5 cm to 70 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 43 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 56 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 365 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 906 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Questions about Velká Úpa

    When is the best time to ski at Velká Úpa?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    How much snow does Velká Úpa have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Velká Úpa?

    Between 706 m and 1,071 m, so 365 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Velká Úpa best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Velká Úpa?

    Černá Hora-Pec, 3.7 km to the south-west, with about 44 km of pistes.
