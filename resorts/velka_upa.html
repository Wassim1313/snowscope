    About Velká Úpa

    Velká Úpa is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 140 of 257 in Central Europe by length. The slopes start at 706 m and reach 1,071 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is U Karla, 2.0 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Velká Úpa compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 3.7 km south-west to Černá Hora-Pec, with about 44 km of runs. For higher slopes, Černá Hora-Pec climbs to 1,280 m.
