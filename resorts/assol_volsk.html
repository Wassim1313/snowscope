    About Ассоль (Assol volsk)

    Ассоль (Assol volsk) is a ski area. The slopes start at 62 m and reach 214 m at the top.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    152 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 62 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. All 2 lifts here are rope tows.
