    About Beaver Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Beaver Mountain offers about 31 km of pistes (number 76 of 175 in US West). Skiing here happens between 2,181 m and 2,670 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Cherry Peak, 18 km west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Beaver Mountain today: about 50 cm up high. The last seven days brought 57 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Lifts and terrain
