    About Les Houches

    Les Houches is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 36 km of pistes put it at number 172 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 978 m to 1,887 m. Le Tourchet (Chamonix) is the next resort, 4.3 km away to the east.

    The pistes of Les Houches

    Les Houches has 69 graded runs adding up to about 36 km. Most of the length is blue (48%, about 17 km). No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. Experts can count on some 4.6 km of difficult runs.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,432 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 5 December and keeps it until about 17 May, roughly 24 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 118 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 58 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 25 cm, snowy years 96 cm). Most winters land between 71 cm and 152 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 77 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For about 8 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    The mountain at Les Houches

    909 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,178 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 12 mountain railways, 1 gondola, 8 chairlifts, 3 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 27 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aiguille de Bionnassay at 4,052 m, standing 2,165 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Les Houches

    When does Les Houches have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Les Houches at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 58 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Les Houches?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Houches?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,887 m; the lowest starts at 978 m (909 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Les Houches?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 18 km, are easy.
