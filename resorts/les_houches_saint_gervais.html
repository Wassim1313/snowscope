    The snow at Les Houches, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Les Houches (about 1,432 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 58 cm of cover (38 cm to 82 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 114 cm; the middle half of winters range from 97 cm to 133 cm.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 118 cm.

    43 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 74 % by 1 January.

    1998-99 was the big one (191 cm); 2010-11 the lean one (88 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to mid-May, about 167 days.

    Skiing Les Houches: the practical side

    The pistes run from 978 m to 1,887 m (909 m of vertical), with 36 km of marked runs and 27 lifts.

    Les Houches: quick answers

    Can you ski Les Houches at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 58 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 74 % of 46 winters. Right now: 115 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Les Houches?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 118 cm). For a sure base, early December to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Les Houches?

    About 118 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 88 cm in the leanest winter (2010-11) and 191 cm in the biggest (1998-99).

    How high do the lifts go at Les Houches?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,887 m; the lowest starts at 978 m (909 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Les Houches?

    Evasion Mont Blanc, 9.7 km to the south-west, with about 293 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Les Houches have?

    About 36 km of pistes and 27 lifts.

    About Les Houches

    Les Houches is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 36 km of pistes put it at number 172 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 978 m to 1,887 m. Le Tourchet (Chamonix) is the next resort, 4.3 km away to the east.

    The mountain at Les Houches
