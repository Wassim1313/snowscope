    About Zermatt

    With some 208 km of runs, Zermatt belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,559 m up to 3,872 m. Valtournenche is the next resort, 7.7 km away to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 2,313 m of vertical, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Starting at 1,559 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 3,872 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 63 lifts break down into 8 cable cars, 3 funiculars, 12 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 22 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 11 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 11 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Matterhorn (4,478 m), 3.9 km to the north-west, about 606 m higher than the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Zermatt

    The piste map lists 296 graded runs, about 208 km in total. Most of the length is red (71%, about 147 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 16 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Zermatt usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,716 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 68 cm to 125 cm in mid-February in most winters. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 19 April, with about 123 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 117 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 64 cm; a snowy December gave 94 cm, a dry one 37 cm.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Gandegghütte (mountain hut), Rifugio Teodulo (mountain hut), Hotel Lo Stambecco (hotel) and Rifugio Guide del Cervino (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Zermatt

    When is the best time to ski at Zermatt?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Zermatt have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 64 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Zermatt open for the 2026/27 season?

    It opened on 24 October 2026; the last ski day is planned for 2 May 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Zermatt?

    The top is at 3,872 m and the base at 1,559 m.

    What level of skier is Zermatt best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 71% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
