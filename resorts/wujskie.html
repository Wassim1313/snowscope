    About Wujskie

    Wujskie is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 417 m and 488 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Lesko-Ski, 13 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Wujskie

    The vertical drop is 71 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 417 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Laworta reaches 741 m.
