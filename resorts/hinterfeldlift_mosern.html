    About Hinterfeldlift Mösern

    Hinterfeldlift Mösern has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,235 m and 1,266 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Seefeld, 1.9 km to the north-east.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Hinterfeldlift Mösern today: about 5 cm up high. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Friday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 10 cm at mid-mountain).

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,250 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 January and lasts to 20 April: about 15 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 32 cm and 67 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 31 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The snow is usually deepest around 27 February, with about 52 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 26 cm; a snowy December gave 42 cm, a dry one 11 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    31 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Hohe Munde Westgipfel (2,662 m), 7.2 km to the north-west, about 1,396 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Vitalhotel Kaiserhof, Hotel Lärchenhof Natur, NIDUM Hotel and Hotel Olympia Tirol.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Hinterfeldlift Mösern?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Hinterfeldlift Mösern at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 26 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Hinterfeldlift Mösern at?

    Between 1,235 m and 1,266 m, so 31 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Hinterfeldlift Mösern?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
