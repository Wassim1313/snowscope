    What there is to ski at Javalambre

    The piste map lists 18 graded runs, about 8.4 km in total. Most of the length is blue (72%, about 6.0 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Javalambre gives 331 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,673 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 3 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.
