    About Černá Hora-Pec

    About 44 km of pistes make Černá Hora-Pec one of the ten biggest ski areas of Central Europe (number 3). Skiing here happens between 563 m and 1,280 m above sea level. vlek Kladenka is the next resort, 1.3 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Černá Hora-Pec

    In all, 103 marked runs cover roughly 44 km. The largest share is red, with 22 km (49%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.2 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    717 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. At 563 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 58 lifts: 3 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 15 T-bars, 25 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 6 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 6 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
