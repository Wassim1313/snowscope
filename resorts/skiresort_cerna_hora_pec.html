    About Černá Hora-Pec

    About 44 km of pistes make Černá Hora-Pec one of the ten biggest ski areas of Central Europe (number 3). Skiing here happens between 563 m and 1,280 m above sea level. vlek Kladenka is the next resort, 1.3 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Černá Hora-Pec

    In all, 103 marked runs cover roughly 44 km. The largest share is red, with 22 km (49%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.2 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    717 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. At 563 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 58 lifts: 3 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 15 T-bars, 25 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 6 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 6 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 922 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 January and keeps it until about 10 March, roughly 7 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 56 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 43 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 7 cm and 69 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Černá Hora-Pec?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    How much snow does Černá Hora-Pec have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Černá Hora-Pec?

    Between 563 m and 1,280 m, so 717 m of vertical drop.

    Is Černá Hora-Pec good for beginners?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 21 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Černá Hora-Pec have?

    About 44 km of pistes and 58 lifts.
