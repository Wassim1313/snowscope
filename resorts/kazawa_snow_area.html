    About Kazawa Snow Area

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Kazawa Snow Area sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Skiing here happens between 1,350 m and 1,666 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Yunomaru, 4.4 km to the south.

    The pistes of Kazawa Snow Area

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 5.9 km to ski. Most of the length is green (47%, about 2.8 km). With 44% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.6 km.

    How Kazawa Snow Area is laid out

    316 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,350 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. All 8 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.
