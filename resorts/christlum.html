    About Christlum

    Neither huge nor tiny, Christlum offers about 15 km of pistes (number 381 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 942 m to 1,746 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Planberg- & Wiesenlifte Pertisau, 8.3 km to the south.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,344 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 December and lasts to 29 April: about 18 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 33 cm and 76 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 30 cm; a snowy December gave 50 cm, a dry one 12 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 57 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 45 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    What there is to ski at Christlum

    In all, 32 marked runs cover roughly 15 km. Most of the length is red (56%, about 8.4 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.9 km.

    The mountain at Christlum

    The vertical drop is 804 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 942 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 4 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Stuhlböcklkopf at 2,169 m, standing 423 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Christlum?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Christlum at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 30 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Christlum?

    The top is at 1,746 m and the base at 942 m.

    What level of skier is Christlum best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 56% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Christlum have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
