    About Feldis

    Neither huge nor tiny, Feldis offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 542 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,480 m up to 1,974 m. Brambrüesch is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of Feldis

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 7.8 km. Most of the length is red (87%, about 6.8 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How much snow Feldis usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,727 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 55 cm, from 30 cm in a poor year to 78 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 93 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 4 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 64 cm to 125 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 103 cm, around 27 February.

    Feldis today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Feldis hold around 34 cm of snow today. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (30 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Feldis gives 494 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,480 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 1 chairlift and 2 rope tows. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Stätzer Horn, 2,574 m high and 6.5 km south-east of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Berghotel Sterna (hotel), Feldiser Skihütte (hotel), Hotel Restaurant Feldiserhof (hotel) and Emser Skihütte (mountain hut).

    Feldis: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Feldis?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Feldis have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 55 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Feldis at?

    Between 1,480 m and 1,974 m, so 494 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Feldis best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 87% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Feldis?

    Arosa Lenzerheide, 10 km to the south-east, with about 164 km of pistes.
