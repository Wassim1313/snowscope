    About Zwardoń Ski

    With some 5.0 km of runs, Zwardoń Ski belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. Skiing here happens between 689 m and 953 m above sea level. Zwardoń is the next resort, 1.6 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of Zwardoń Ski

    Zwardoń Ski has 6 graded runs adding up to about 4.5 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.6 km (56%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Zwardoń Ski

    From top to bottom that makes 264 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 689 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 953 m. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow.
