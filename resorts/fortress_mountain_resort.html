    About Fortress

    Fortress is a mid-sized ski area with about 31 km of pistes, number 19 of 53 in Canada West by length. The slopes start at 1,982 m and reach 2,315 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Nakiska, 15 km north, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,148 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 58 cm (lean years 41 cm, snowy years 93 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 92 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Year to year the snow is steady, with 60 cm to 105 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 25 April, with about 96 cm.

    What there is to ski at Fortress

    Fortress has 51 graded runs adding up to about 31 km. Most of the length is black diamond (47%, about 14 km). Good skiers will like it here; 47% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 14 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    333 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 1,982 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mount Galatea at 3,185 m, standing 870 m above the top lift.

    Fortress: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Fortress?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Fortress have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 58 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Fortress?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,315 m; the lowest starts at 1,982 m (333 m of vertical).

    Does Fortress have steep runs?

    Yes: about 14 km of its graded pistes (47%) are difficult.
