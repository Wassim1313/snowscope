    About Brattleboro Ski Hill

    Compact and local, Brattleboro Ski Hill has about 1.0 km of runs, small for US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 130 m to 179 m. The closest other ski area is The Hermitage Club at Haystack M (27 km, west).

    How Brattleboro Ski Hill is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Brattleboro Ski Hill gives 49 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 130 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Stratton, for one, climbs to 1,183 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Governors Mountain, 497 m high and 7.1 km west of the resort.

    How much snow Brattleboro Ski Hill usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 154 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 50 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 14 February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 7 cm, with 26 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March.
