    About Val Formica

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Val Formica one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,641 m up to 1,763 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Verena 2000, 5.5 km to the south.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,702 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 February and lasts to 12 April: about 9 weeks. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 10 cm to 67 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm; a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 3 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 29 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 31 March, with about 39 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 122 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,641 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Cima Dodici (Ferozzo) (2,337 m), 5.5 km to the north-east, about 574 m higher than the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 5 runs and around 2.3 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (75%, about 1.7 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Val Formica include Rifugio Val Formica (mountain hut), Rifugio Larici da Alessio (mountain hut), Malga Costa di sotto (chalet) and Malga Pusterle (guesthouse). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Val Formica: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Val Formica?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-April.

    How much snow does Val Formica have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Val Formica?

    The top is at 1,763 m and the base at 1,641 m.

    Is Val Formica good for beginners?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 1.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Val Formica?

    Folgaria, 17 km to the south-west, with about 46 km of pistes.
