    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 474 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Year to year the snow is steady, with 0 cm to 13 cm in mid-February in most winters. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-January and late January. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 4 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 10 cm).

    The mountain at Heumöderntal

    The vertical drop is 79 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 434 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Questions about Heumöderntal

    What is the best month to ski at Heumöderntal?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Can you ski at Heumöderntal at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 1 cm).

    How high is Heumöderntal?

    Its highest lift reaches 513 m; the lowest starts at 434 m (79 m of vertical).
