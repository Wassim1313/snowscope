    About Artesina

    Artesina is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 39 km of pistes put it at number 152 of 1,056. The slopes start at 801 m and reach 2,091 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Prato Nevoso (2.9 km, east).

    The pistes of Artesina

    The piste map lists 46 graded runs, about 34 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 19 km, or 56%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.5 km.

    How Artesina is laid out

    1,290 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 801 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 8 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 4 moving carpets, 13 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Cima delle Saline at 2,612 m, standing 521 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Artesina through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,446 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 56 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm, with 39 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 20 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Artesina include Rifugio FIE Artesina (mountain hut), Rifugio Mettolo Castellino (mountain hut), Hotel Galassia (hotel) and Rifugio Balma (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Artesina have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Artesina at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 6 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Artesina?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,091 m; the lowest starts at 801 m (1,290 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Artesina best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 56% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Artesina?

    About 39 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
