    About Artesina

    Artesina is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 39 km of pistes put it at number 152 of 1,056. The slopes start at 801 m and reach 2,091 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Prato Nevoso (2.9 km, east).

    How Artesina is laid out

    1,290 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 801 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Artesina include Rifugio FIE Artesina (mountain hut), Rifugio Mettolo Castellino (mountain hut), Hotel Galassia (hotel) and Rifugio Balma (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    How high is Artesina?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,091 m; the lowest starts at 801 m (1,290 m of vertical).

    How big is Artesina?

    About 39 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
