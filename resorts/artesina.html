    What 46 winters say about Artesina

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Artesina (about 1,446 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 6 cm of cover (1 cm to 21 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 17 cm; the middle half of winters range from 2 cm to 37 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 20 cm.

    2 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 4 % by 1 January.

    1995-96 was the big one (119 cm); 2024-25 the lean one (12 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early January until mid-March in a normal winter, roughly 57 days.

    Artesina: quick answers

    Does Artesina have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 6 cm, and only 4 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is no snow.

    What is the best time to ski Artesina?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 20 cm). For a sure base, early January to mid-March is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Artesina?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 20 cm in early February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 119 cm (1995-96), the thinnest 12 cm (2024-25).

    How high is Artesina?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,091 m; the lowest starts at 801 m (1,290 m of vertical).

    How big is Artesina?

    About 39 km of pistes and 13 lifts.

    About Artesina

    Artesina is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 39 km of pistes put it at number 152 of 1,056. The slopes start at 801 m and reach 2,091 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Prato Nevoso (2.9 km, east).

    How Artesina is laid out

    1,290 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 801 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
