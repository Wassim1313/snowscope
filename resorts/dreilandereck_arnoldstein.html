    About Dreiländereck

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Dreiländereck sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 691 m and 1,538 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Macesnovc Rateče, 4.7 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Dreiländereck

    The piste map lists 23 graded runs, about 11 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.1 km, or 38%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 2.0 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Dreiländereck gives 847 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. At 691 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 drag lift, 7 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The skyline is dominated by Ponza Grande / Visoka Ponca, 2,274 m high and 7.8 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,114 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 43 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 42 cm. For the best snow, aim for early April and mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 20 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 5 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Dreiländereck?

    Aim for early April and mid-April.

    Is there snow at Dreiländereck at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Dreiländereck?

    The top is at 1,538 m and the base at 691 m.

    Is Dreiländereck good for beginners?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 4.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Dreiländereck?
