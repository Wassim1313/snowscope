    About Jerikobakken

    Jerikobakken has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 160 m to 194 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being SNØ Oslo, 4.2 km to the north-east.

    Other resorts close by

    How much snow Jerikobakken usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 177 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 January and keeps it until about 16 March, roughly 8 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 39 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 6 cm to 63 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 36 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 34 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Oslo Skisenter, for one, climbs to 370 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Slengfehøgda, 420 m high and 7.0 km north of the resort.

    Jerikobakken: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Jerikobakken?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Jerikobakken at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Jerikobakken?

    The top is at 194 m and the base at 160 m.

    How big is Jerikobakken?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
