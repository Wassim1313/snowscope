    About Jerikobakken

    Jerikobakken is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 160 m to 194 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being SNØ Oslo, 4.2 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 34 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Oslo Skisenter, for one, climbs to 370 m.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Jerikobakken hold around 29 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.
