    About Borsec

    Neither huge nor tiny, Borsec offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 120 of 312 in the Carpathians). The slopes start at 936 m and reach 1,074 m at the top. Borsec is the next resort, 5.2 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Borsec

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 2.1 km. The largest share is blue, with 0.8 km (39%). Good skiers will like it here; 34% of the runs are steep.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 7 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Borsec. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    The mountain at Borsec

    The vertical drop is 138 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,136 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Havas Bucsin, for one, climbs to 1,494 m. The only lift is a button lift.

    Borsec: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Borsec?

    The top is at 1,074 m and the base at 936 m.

    Is Borsec good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 0.7 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Borsec?

    Havas Bucsin, 43 km to the south-west, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Borsec?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
