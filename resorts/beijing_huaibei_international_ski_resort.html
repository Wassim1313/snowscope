    About Beijing Huaibei International

    Beijing Huaibei International is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 32 of 92 in China by length. The slopes start at 136 m and reach 291 m at the top. Yunfoshan is the next resort, 19 km away to the east.

    The pistes of Beijing Huaibei International

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 4.9 km. The largest share is blue square, with 3.0 km (61%). Intermediate runs make up 61% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 1.2 km of black or expert runs.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    155 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 136 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 291 m is the second highest of the 7 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 3 chairlifts and 5 moving carpets. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
