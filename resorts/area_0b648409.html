    About Спортивный курорт Чекерил

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Спортивный курорт Чекерил sits in the middle of the size table of Russia. Skiing here happens between 125 m and 234 m above sea level.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Спортивный курорт Чекерил has 8 graded runs adding up to about 4.5 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.5 km, or 55%. Intermediate runs make up 55% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The mountain at Спортивный курорт Чекерил

    From top to bottom that makes 109 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 2 rope tows, 6 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
