    About Idre Himmelfjäll

    With some 27 km of runs, Idre Himmelfjäll belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 590 m and 870 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Idre Fjäll, 4.1 km east, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 32 graded runs, about 27 km in total. Most of the length is green (beginner) (46%, about 12 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow Idre Himmelfjäll usually gets

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 730 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 56 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 65 cm, around 22 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 84 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 39 cm, from 19 cm in a poor year to 60 cm in a big one.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    280 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Lofsdalen, for one, climbs to 1,106 m. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 4 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Städjan (1,131 m), 6.2 km to the east, about 261 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Idre Himmelfjäll have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Idre Himmelfjäll have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 39 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Idre Himmelfjäll at?

    The top is at 870 m and the base at 590 m.

    Can beginners ski at Idre Himmelfjäll?

    Yes: 82% of the graded pistes, about 22 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Idre Himmelfjäll have?

    About 27 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
