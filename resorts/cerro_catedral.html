    About Cerro Catedral

    Only 1 of the 30 ski areas of Andes is larger than Cerro Catedral, which has some 57 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 1,038 m and 2,092 m above sea level. Complejo Turístico Teleférico Ce is the next resort, 7.4 km away to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 80 marked runs cover roughly 54 km. The largest share is red, with 25 km (46%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 10 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,054 m: enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,092 m is the highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 33 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 21 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 5 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
