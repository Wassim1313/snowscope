    About Flachau

    With some 44 km of runs, Flachau belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 882 m and reach 1,977 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski amadé, 2.5 km to the south.

    The pistes of Flachau

    Counting every graded piste, there are 73 runs and around 44 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 32 km (74%). Intermediate runs make up 74% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.7 km.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,430 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 38 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 66 cm, a dry one 10 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 87 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 52 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 43 cm and 103 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,095 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 882 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 1 cable car, 5 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 4 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 5 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Lackenkogel (2,051 m), Saukarfunktel (2,024 m) and Saukarkopf (2,014 m).

    Questions about Flachau

    When does Flachau have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Flachau at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 38 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Flachau?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Flachau at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,977 m; the lowest starts at 882 m (1,095 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Flachau best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 74% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
