    About Salamandra Resort

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Salamandra Resort sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. Skiing here happens between 579 m and 850 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Blanc Ostrý Grúň, 18 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Salamandra Resort

    The piste map lists 1 graded run, about 1.4 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 100%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Salamandra Resort

    From top to bottom that makes 271 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 579 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 1 button lift.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Salamandra Resort hold around 8 cm of snow today. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Salamandra Resort at?

    Its highest lift reaches 850 m; the lowest starts at 579 m (271 m of vertical).

    Who is Salamandra Resort best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Salamandra Resort?

    Ski centrum Drozdovo, 23 km to the west, with about 2.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Salamandra Resort?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
