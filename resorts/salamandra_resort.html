    About Salamandra Resort

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Salamandra Resort sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. Skiing here happens between 579 m and 850 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Blanc Ostrý Grúň, 18 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Ski centrum Drozdovo: about 2.0 km of pistes, 23 km to the west. Skalka reaches 1,228 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The mountain at Salamandra Resort

    From top to bottom that makes 271 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 579 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Salamandra Resort at?

    Its highest lift reaches 850 m; the lowest starts at 579 m (271 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Salamandra Resort?

    Ski centrum Drozdovo, 23 km to the west, with about 2.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Salamandra Resort?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
