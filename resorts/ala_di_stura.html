    About Ala di Stura

    Ala di Stura is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 639 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,018 m and reach 1,727 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Pian Benot, 11 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Ala di Stura and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Bonneval-sur-Arc (21 km west) has about 20 km of pistes against 5.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Bonneval-sur-Arc does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 7. For higher slopes, Bonneval-sur-Arc climbs to 2,948 m.

    What there is to ski at Ala di Stura

    The piste map lists 11 graded runs, about 5.3 km in total. Most of the length is freeride (28%, about 1.5 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 49% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 1.1 km of difficult runs. 1.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Ala di Stura

    Skied top to bottom, Ala di Stura gives 709 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Tignes, for one, climbs to 3,447 m. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Uia di Mondrone, 2,964 m high and 7.5 km west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 1,372 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 January and lasts to 12 March: about 7 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 12 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. Most winters land between 16 cm and 56 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 18 cm; a snowy December gave 36 cm, a dry one 5 cm. A normal season tops out near 38 cm, around 19 February.

    Quick answers

    When does Ala di Stura have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Ala di Stura at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 18 cm deep.

    How high is Ala di Stura?

    The top is at 1,727 m and the base at 1,018 m.

    Is Ala di Stura good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 2.6 km of its graded pistes (49%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ala di Stura?

    Bonneval-sur-Arc, 21 km to the west, with about 20 km of pistes.
