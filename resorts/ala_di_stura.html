    About Ala di Stura

    Ala di Stura is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 639 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,018 m and reach 1,727 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Pian Benot, 11 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Ala di Stura and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Bonneval-sur-Arc (21 km west) has about 20 km of pistes against 5.0 km here. For higher slopes, Bonneval-sur-Arc climbs to 2,948 m.

    The mountain at Ala di Stura

    Skied top to bottom, Ala di Stura gives 709 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Tignes, for one, climbs to 3,447 m.
