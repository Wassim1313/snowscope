    Snowhaven in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Snowhaven (5,466 ft (1,666 m)), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 31 in (80 cm), with a normal range of 22 in (57 cm)–40 in (101 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 51 in (130 cm) (40 in (102 cm)–62 in (158 cm)).

    April is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 112 in (285 cm).

    76 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 89 % by 1 January.

    1981-82 was the big one (220 in (558 cm)); 2014-15 the lean one (43 in (110 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs late November to mid-May (176 days).

    Snowhaven: quick answers

    Is there snow in Snowhaven at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 31 in (80 cm) at Christmas, and 89 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 7.5 in (19 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Snowhaven?

    The deepest snow comes in April (about 112 in (285 cm)). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How snowy is Snowhaven?

    About 112 in (285 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 43 in (110 cm) in the leanest winter (2014-15) and 220 in (558 cm) in the biggest (1981-82).

    How high is Snowhaven?

    Between 1,609 m and 1,722 m, so 113 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Snowhaven?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.

    About Snowhaven

    Snowhaven is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 4.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,609 m and 1,722 m above sea level. Cottonwood Butte Ski Hill is the next resort, 36 km away to the north-west.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 19 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Snowhaven. The last seven days brought 12 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 13 cm by Saturday.

    The mountain at Snowhaven

    Skied top to bottom, Snowhaven gives 113 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,609 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
