    About Snowhaven

    Snowhaven is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 4.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,609 m and 1,722 m above sea level. Cottonwood Butte Ski Hill is the next resort, 36 km away to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Snowhaven has 9 graded runs adding up to about 3.8 km. Most of the length is blue square (65%, about 2.5 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,666 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 November and keeps it until about 5 May, roughly 23 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 255 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Most winters land between 68 cm and 184 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 285 cm, around 29 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 80 cm, with 133 cm in the best years. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    The mountain at Snowhaven

    Skied top to bottom, Snowhaven gives 113 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,609 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows. The skyline is dominated by High Camp, 1,866 m high and 2.8 km west of the resort.

    Questions about Snowhaven

    What is the best month to ski at Snowhaven?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Is there snow at Snowhaven at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 80 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Snowhaven?

    Between 1,609 m and 1,722 m, so 113 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Snowhaven best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 65% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Snowhaven?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
