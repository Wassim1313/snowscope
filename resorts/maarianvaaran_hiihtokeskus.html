    About Maarianvaaran hiihtokeskus

    Neither huge nor tiny, Maarianvaaran hiihtokeskus offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 143 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 108 m up to 239 m.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 174 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 18 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm; a snowy December gave 50 cm, a dry one 13 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 50 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 80 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 64 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Questions about Maarianvaaran hiihtokeskus

    When is the best time to ski at Maarianvaaran hiihtokeskus?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Maarianvaaran hiihtokeskus at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Maarianvaaran hiihtokeskus?

    Its highest lift reaches 239 m; the lowest starts at 108 m (131 m of vertical).

    How big is Maarianvaaran hiihtokeskus?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
