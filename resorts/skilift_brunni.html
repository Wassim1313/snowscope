    About Skilift Brunni

    Compact and local, Skilift Brunni has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,101 m to 1,196 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Brunni-Haggenegg, 1.1 km to the north-west.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue: about 27 km of pistes, 1.7 km to the south-west. The highest nearby is Ibergeregg-Handgruobi, topping out at 1,591 m.

    Skilift Brunni today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Skilift Brunni hold around 6 cm of snow today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 22 cm by Wednesday.

    The mountain at Skilift Brunni

    95 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Hoch-Ybrig, reach 1,831 m.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Brunni (hotel), Berggasthaus Holzegg (hotel), Berggasthof Haggenegg (guesthouse) and Hotel Passhöhe (hotel).
