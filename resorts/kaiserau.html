    About Kaiserau

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Kaiserau sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,133 m up to 1,369 m. The closest other ski area is Hohentauern (11 km, south).

    Kaiserau and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Hinterstoder: about 35 km of pistes, 29 km to the north-west. Hinterstoder reaches 1,987 m, the most of the resorts listed.
