    About Kaiserau

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Kaiserau sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,133 m up to 1,369 m. The closest other ski area is Hohentauern (11 km, south).

    Kaiserau and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Hinterstoder: about 35 km of pistes, 29 km to the north-west. If snow is the priority, Planneralm does better, with about 23 weeks of a good base instead of 18. Hinterstoder reaches 1,987 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    What there is to ski at Kaiserau

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 3.3 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (72%, about 2.4 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,251 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 December and keeps it until about 23 April, roughly 18 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 32 cm to 90 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 67 cm, around 3 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 32 cm; a snowy December gave 57 cm, a dry one 6 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 43 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 236 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Admonter Reichenstein, 2,251 m high and 5.0 km north-east of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Kaiserau include Atelier Bergblick (guesthouse), Oberst Klinke Hütte (mountain hut), Gästehaus Ornig (guesthouse) and Gästehaus Huber (guesthouse).

    Kaiserau: common questions

    When does Kaiserau have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Kaiserau at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 32 cm deep.

    How high is Kaiserau?

    Between 1,133 m and 1,369 m, so 236 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kaiserau suitable for families?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 2.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Kaiserau?

    Hinterstoder, 29 km to the north-west, with about 35 km of pistes.
