    About Ascou

    Ascou is a small ski area by the standards of the Pyrenees, with about 12 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,496 m up to 2,064 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mijanès-Donezan, 3.8 km to the east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Ax 3 Domaines: about 51 km of pistes, 14 km to the south-west. For higher slopes, Les Angles climbs to 2,358 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 568 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,496 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.

    Ascou today and the next days

    Up high, Ascou has roughly 13 cm of snow on the ground today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Wednesday.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Centre de Vacance Le Saint-Bernard (hostel), Cabane du col du Pradel (mountain hut), Refuge (chalet) and Relais de Pailheres (hotel).
