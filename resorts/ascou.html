    About Ascou

    Ascou is a small ski area by the standards of the Pyrenees, with about 12 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,496 m up to 2,064 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mijanès-Donezan, 3.8 km to the east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Ax 3 Domaines: about 51 km of pistes, 14 km to the south-west. For more reliable snow, Formiguères usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 7 weeks, against 0 here. For higher slopes, Les Angles climbs to 2,358 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 568 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,496 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 5 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pic de Baxouillade at 2,546 m, standing 482 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,780 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 62 cm. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 31 January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 7 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 33 cm, a dry one 0 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Centre de Vacance Le Saint-Bernard (hostel), Cabane du col du Pradel (mountain hut), Refuge (chalet) and Relais de Pailheres (hotel).

    Questions about Ascou

    When does Ascou have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Is there snow at Ascou at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Ascou at?

    The top is at 2,064 m and the base at 1,496 m.

    Is Ascou suitable for families?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 7.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Ascou?

    Ax 3 Domaines, 14 km to the south-west, with about 51 km of pistes.
