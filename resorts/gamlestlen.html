    About Gamlestølen

    Gamlestølen is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 872 m to 975 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Spatind, 15 km to the north-east.

    When the snow is good

    At about 924 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 November and lasts to 20 May: about 26 weeks. A normal season tops out near 97 cm, around 20 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 61 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 95 cm, a dry one 37 cm. Most winters land between 69 cm and 118 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 92 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    The mountain at Gamlestølen

    The vertical drop is 103 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Spåtind, 1,414 m high and 7.3 km north-east of the resort.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Gamlestølen today: about 17 cm up high. More is on the way: about 5 cm of new snow is expected by Monday. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 51 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Quick answers

    When does Gamlestølen have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Gamlestølen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 61 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Gamlestølen?

    Between 872 m and 975 m, so 103 m of vertical drop.
