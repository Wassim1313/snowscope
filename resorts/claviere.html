    About Claviere

    Claviere is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 41 km of pistes put it at number 143 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,536 m and reach 2,399 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Montgenèvre, 3.6 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Claviere today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Claviere hold around 77 cm of snow today. 38 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 26 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Montgenèvre (3.6 km west) has about 62 km of pistes against 41 km here. The highest nearby is Sestriere, topping out at 2,786 m.

    The mountain at Claviere

    The vertical drop is 863 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,536 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 hotels: Baita della Luna, Hotel Miramonti, Hotel Bes and Residence Hotel Passero Pellegrino.
