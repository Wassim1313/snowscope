    About Claviere

    Claviere is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 41 km of pistes put it at number 143 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,536 m and reach 2,399 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Montgenèvre, 3.6 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Claviere

    The piste map lists 79 graded runs, about 41 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 19 km, or 48%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 1.9 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Claviere

    The vertical drop is 863 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,536 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 9 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Punta Muta (3,171 m), 8.0 km to the south-east, about 772 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,968 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. A normal season tops out near 103 cm, around 6 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 57 cm to 123 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 53 cm; a snowy December gave 94 cm, a dry one 27 cm. For about 2 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 64 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Claviere?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Claviere at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 53 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Claviere?

    Between 1,536 m and 2,399 m, so 863 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Claviere?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 19 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Claviere?

    Montgenèvre, 3.6 km to the west, with about 62 km of pistes.
