    About Geißkopf

    With some 8.0 km of runs, Geißkopf belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The slopes start at 834 m and reach 1,108 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Silberberg (16 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Geißkopf

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 7.9 km. Most of the length is red (72%, about 5.7 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The mountain at Geißkopf

    Skied top to bottom, Geißkopf gives 274 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 834 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 8 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 4 T-bars, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.
