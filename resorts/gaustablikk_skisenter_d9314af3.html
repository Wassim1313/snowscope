    Snow at Gaustablikk Skisenter through a normal winter

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 892 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 121 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 76 cm to 153 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 111 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 68 cm (lean years 41 cm, snowy years 103 cm). For about 14 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.
