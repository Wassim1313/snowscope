    About Grebenzen

    Grebenzen is a mid-sized ski area with about 13 km of pistes, number 410 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,004 m and 1,869 m above sea level. Kreischberg is the next resort, 20 km away to the west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Grebenzen gives 865 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Grebenzen hold around 6 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 12 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 10 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Auszeit (hotel), Grebenzen Lodge Hirsch (chalet), Grebenzenhaus (mountain hut) and Grebenzen Lodges (chalet).
