    About Grebenzen

    Grebenzen is a mid-sized ski area with about 13 km of pistes, number 410 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,004 m and 1,869 m above sea level. Kreischberg is the next resort, 20 km away to the west.

    How much snow Grebenzen usually gets

    At about 1,436 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 December and lasts to 26 April: about 19 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 56 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 35 cm (lean years 8 cm, snowy years 59 cm). For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 49 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Year to year the snow is steady, with 27 cm to 76 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Grebenzen gives 865 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Dritte Grebenzenhöhe (1,892 m), Zweite Grebenzenhöhe (1,874 m) and Grebenzen (1,870 m).

    Questions about Grebenzen

    What is the best month to ski at Grebenzen?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Grebenzen have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 35 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Grebenzen at?

    Between 1,004 m and 1,869 m, so 865 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Grebenzen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 68% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Grebenzen have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
