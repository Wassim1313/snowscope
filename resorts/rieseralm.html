    About Rieseralm

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Rieseralm sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,321 m and 1,606 m above sea level. Schilift Obdach is the next resort, 7.5 km away to the south-east.

    Snow at Rieseralm through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,464 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 December and lasts to 1 April: about 12 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 23 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 29 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 58 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 16 cm to 60 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 43 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    What there is to ski at Rieseralm

    Counting every graded piste, there are 19 runs and around 8.7 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 4.2 km (49%). Intermediate runs make up 49% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.3 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    285 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,321 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 4 T-bars and 1 button lift, 5 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Zirbitzkogel (2,396 m), 4.4 km to the south-west, about 790 m higher than the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 17 km south to Klippitztörl, with about 13 km of runs. Grebenzen has the longer snow season: around 19 weeks of solid cover, compared with 12 at Rieseralm. Grebenzen reaches 1,869 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Rieseralm: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Rieseralm?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Is there snow at Rieseralm at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 29 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Rieseralm?

    Between 1,321 m and 1,606 m, so 285 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Rieseralm best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 49% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Rieseralm?

    Klippitztörl, 17 km to the south, with about 13 km of pistes.
