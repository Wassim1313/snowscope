    About Rieseralm

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Rieseralm sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,321 m and 1,606 m above sea level. Schilift Obdach is the next resort, 7.5 km away to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    285 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,321 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 17 km south to Klippitztörl, with about 13 km of runs. Grebenzen reaches 1,869 m, the most of the resorts listed.
