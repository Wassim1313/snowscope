    About Pylypets

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Pylypets sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 703 m up to 1,165 m. The closest other ski area is Izki (4.6 km, south-east).

    What there is to ski at Pylypets

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 2.3 km. The largest share is red, with 1.6 km (70%). Intermediate runs make up 70% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Pylypets gives 462 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 703 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,165 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
