    About Hlubočky

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hlubočky offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 210 of 312 in the Carpathians). The ski area spans the mountain from 294 m up to 380 m. Park sportu Hrubá Voda is the next resort, 4.8 km away to the north-east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 4.8 km north-east to Park sportu Hrubá Voda, with about 2.0 km of runs. The highest nearby is Skiareál Čerťák, topping out at 818 m.

    The mountain at Hlubočky

    The vertical drop is 86 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 294 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Karlov, for one, climbs to 849 m. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 2 button lifts.
