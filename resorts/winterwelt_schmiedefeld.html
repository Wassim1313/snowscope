    About Winterwelt Schmiedefeld

    Winterwelt Schmiedefeld is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 143 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Its lifts climb from 741 m to 878 m. Wintersportzentrum Oberhof is the next resort, 13 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Winterwelt Schmiedefeld has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.7 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.0 km (58%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 137 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 741 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 878 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
