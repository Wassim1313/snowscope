    About Radstadt

    Neither huge nor tiny, Radstadt offers about 12 km of pistes (number 444 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 854 m and 1,565 m above sea level. Monte Popolo is the next resort, 5.5 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 18 runs and around 12 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 6.1 km (50%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Radstadt has roughly 17 cm of snow on the ground today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 7 cm by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (10 cm).

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,210 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 31 cm to 74 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 34 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 30 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 53 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 59 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 711 m: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,054 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Ski amadé reaches 2,642 m. Its 7 lifts break down into 3 gondolas, 2 chairlifts and 2 button lifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Strimskogel at 2,139 m, standing 574 m above the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Hacklbauer, Hotel Scheffer, Hotel Stegerbräu and Appartement Königslehen.

    Radstadt: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Radstadt?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Radstadt have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 30 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Radstadt?

    Between 854 m and 1,565 m, so 711 m of vertical drop.

    Is Radstadt good for beginners?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 6.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Radstadt?

    Ski amadé, 8.5 km to the south-west, with about 605 km of pistes.
