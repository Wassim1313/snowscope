    How much snow Kostelec usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 678 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 15 cm, around 25 January. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 0 cm in a poor year to 25 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 36 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Kostelec?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Can you ski at Kostelec at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 5 cm, so check before you go.
