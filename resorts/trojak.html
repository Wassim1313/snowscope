    About Troják

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Troják one of the smaller places to ski in the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 500 m up to 595 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Trnava-Luhy, 5.1 km to the south-east.

    Troják and the resorts around it

    How Troják is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Troják gives 95 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 500 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
