    About Buron

    Buron is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 608 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 895 m and 1,434 m above sea level. Ellegglifte is the next resort, 3.2 km away to the north.

    How much snow Buron usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,164 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 January and lasts to 18 April: about 11 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 34 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Year to year the snow is steady, with 19 cm to 63 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 37 cm, around 15 February. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 22 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 37 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Buron gives 539 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 895 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,434 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Wertacher Hörnle (1,695 m), 7.8 km to the south-west, about 261 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Buron?

    Aim for early March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Buron at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Buron?

    The top is at 1,434 m and the base at 895 m.

    What level of skier is Buron best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 91% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Buron?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
