    About Hochwang

    Hochwang is a mid-sized ski area with about 18 km of pistes, number 339 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,544 m and 2,282 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Fideriser Heuberge (5.9 km, east).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 17 km. Most of the length is red (53%, about 9.2 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.8 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 1,913 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 1 December and lasts to 28 May: about 26 weeks. For about 10 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 104 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm (lean years 29 cm, snowy years 73 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 110 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 71 cm to 132 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    The mountain at Hochwang

    From top to bottom that makes 738 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,544 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows, 4 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Weisshorn at 2,653 m, standing 371 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Hochwang?

    Aim for late February and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Hochwang at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 54 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Hochwang?

    Between 1,544 m and 2,282 m, so 738 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Hochwang best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 53% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Hochwang have?

    About 18 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
