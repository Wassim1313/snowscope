    About Chapelco

    Only 5 of the 30 ski areas of Andes are larger than Chapelco, which has some 30 km of runs. The slopes start at 1,259 m and reach 1,971 m at the top. C4 Parque de Nieve is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the west.

    The pistes of Chapelco

    Counting every graded piste, there are 41 runs and around 30 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 19 km, or 65%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 2.2 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Chapelco

    712 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,971 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 4 rope tows and 2 moving carpets, 16 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.
