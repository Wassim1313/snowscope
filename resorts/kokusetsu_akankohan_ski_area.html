    About Kokusetsu Akankohan

    Kokusetsu Akankohan is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 485 m up to 758 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tsubetsu Townsmen, 31 km to the north.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    273 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 485 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 758 m.
