    About Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter

    Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 894 m and 1,072 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ål skisenter, 13 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.
