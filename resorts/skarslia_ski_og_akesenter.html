    About Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter

    Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 894 m and 1,072 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ål skisenter, 13 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter compares nearby

    For more terrain, Hemsedal skisenter (22 km north-east) has about 48 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. For snow it is second in the group, behind Hemsedal skisenter (30 weeks of good cover, 30 here). Hemsedal skisenter reaches 1,439 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    How much snow Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 983 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 7 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 30 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 122 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 125 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 77 cm, from 52 cm in a poor year to 108 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. Most winters land between 87 cm and 145 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks.

    What there is to ski at Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter

    The piste map lists 10 graded runs, about 2.2 km in total. Black runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 54%. With 54% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 1.2 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter gives 178 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Brenthovd at 1,243 m, standing 171 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Can you ski at Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 77 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter at?

    The top is at 1,072 m and the base at 894 m.

    Is Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.2 km of its graded pistes (54%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Skarslia Ski- og Akesenter?

    Hemsedal skisenter, 22 km to the north-east, with about 48 km of pistes.
