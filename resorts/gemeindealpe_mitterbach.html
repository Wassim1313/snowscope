    About Gemeindealpe Mitterbach

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Gemeindealpe Mitterbach sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 804 m to 1,622 m. Its nearest neighbour is Mariazell, 5.6 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 10.0 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 5.9 km (59%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,213 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early April, about 13 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 27 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 60 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 17 cm and 74 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 48 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 20 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    How Gemeindealpe Mitterbach is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 818 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,004 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Ötscher (1,893 m), 7.2 km to the north-west, about 271 m higher than the top lift.

    Gemeindealpe Mitterbach: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Gemeindealpe Mitterbach?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Can you ski at Gemeindealpe Mitterbach at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 27 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Gemeindealpe Mitterbach?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,622 m; the lowest starts at 804 m (818 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Gemeindealpe Mitterbach?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 5.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Gemeindealpe Mitterbach?

    Lackenhof, 8.9 km to the north-west, with about 15 km of pistes.
