    About Ritavalkea

    Ritavalkea is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 194 m and 210 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ritavalkean laskettelurinne, 0.0 km north, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    16 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Ritavalkea has roughly 10 cm of snow on the ground today. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    Staying nearby
