    About Ritavalkea

    Ritavalkea has about 0.1 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 194 m and 210 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ritavalkean laskettelurinne, 0.0 km north, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 202 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 12 December and keeps it until about 3 May, roughly 21 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 39 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 26 cm, snowy years 53 cm). A normal season tops out near 65 cm, around 26 February. By mid-April the typical depth is 55 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 49 cm to 77 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Lifts and terrain

    16 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Makkaravaara (239 m), Jai-Paljukka (209 m) and Iso Perävaara (183 m).

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Pellon Helmi (chalet), Hotelli Pellonhovi (hotel) and Pello Basecamp (guesthouse).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Ritavalkea?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Ritavalkea have at Christmas?

    How high do the lifts go at Ritavalkea?
