    Klövsjö / Storhogna in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Klövsjö / Storhogna (about 666 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 38 cm of cover (28 cm to 47 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 59 cm, between 51 cm and 71 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 63 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 11 % of winters, and by 1 January in 39 %.

    The deepest winter was 1987-88 (120 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1995-96 (40 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 145 days.

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Klövsjö / Storhogna at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 38 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 39 % of 46 winters. Right now: 22 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Klövsjö / Storhogna?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 63 cm). For a sure base, early December to early May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Klövsjö / Storhogna get?

    About 63 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 40 cm in the leanest winter (1995-96) and 120 cm in the biggest (1987-88).

    How high do the lifts go at Klövsjö / Storhogna?

    The top is at 876 m and the base at 456 m.

    How big is Klövsjö / Storhogna?

    About 20 km of pistes and 17 lifts.
