    About Col de Porte

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Col de Porte one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,226 m up to 1,352 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Col de Porte, 0.4 km to the south.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse: about 7.0 km of pistes, 8.5 km to the north-east. The highest nearby is Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse, topping out at 1,743 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    126 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Col de Porte has roughly 1 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 17 cm by Wednesday.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Le Cartusia (hotel), Chalet Jaune Chartreuse (chalet), Domaine de Rozan (chalet) and Gîte Les Clarines (chalet).

    Questions about Col de Porte

    How high do the lifts go at Col de Porte?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,352 m; the lowest starts at 1,226 m (126 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Col de Porte?

    Saint-Pierre-de-Chartreuse, 8.5 km to the north-east, with about 7.0 km of pistes.
