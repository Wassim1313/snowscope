    About Koralpe

    Koralpe is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 433 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,626 m and reach 2,065 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Weinebene, 5.7 km to the north-east.

    How much snow Koralpe usually gets

    At about 1,846 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 January and lasts to 29 March: about 10 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 23 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 57 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 98 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 72 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    439 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,626 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 3 T-bars and 1 drag lift. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Großer Speikkogel (2,140 m), Kleiner Speikkogel (2,116 m) and Krakaberg (2,070 m).

    Koralpe: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Koralpe?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Can you ski at Koralpe at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 23 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Koralpe?

    Between 1,626 m and 2,065 m, so 439 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Koralpe best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 60% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Koralpe?

    About 12 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
