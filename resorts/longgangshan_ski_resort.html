    About Longgangshan

    About 28 km of pistes make Longgangshan one of the ten biggest ski areas of China (number 8). Its lifts climb from 546 m to 1,334 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Longgangshan gives 788 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 546 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. To get up there are 1 gondola and 3 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.
