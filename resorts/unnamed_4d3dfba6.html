    About Skilift Zuflucht

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skilift Zuflucht one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 907 m and 962 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Skilift Lamm (4.8 km, south-east).

    Skilift Zuflucht and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 44 km south to Skilift Schlossberg Oberkirnach, with about 2.0 km of runs. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    How much snow Skilift Zuflucht usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 934 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 23 January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 18 cm). By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 26 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late December and early February.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Zuflucht gives 55 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 907 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Lettstädter Höhe (966 m), Rossbühl (963 m) and Sandkopf (954 m).

    Skilift Zuflucht: common questions

    When does Skilift Zuflucht have the most snow?

    Aim for late December and early February.

    Can you ski at Skilift Zuflucht at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilift Zuflucht?

    Between 907 m and 962 m, so 55 m of vertical drop.

    Is Skilift Zuflucht suitable for families?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Skilift Zuflucht?

    Skilift Schlossberg Oberkirnach, 44 km to the south, with about 2.0 km of pistes.
