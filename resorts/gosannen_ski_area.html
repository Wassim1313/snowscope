    About Gosannen

    Gosannen has about 0.5 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 56 m up to 82 m. Its nearest neighbour is Omagari Family, 14 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Gosannen hold around 9 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 38 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.

    How Gosannen is laid out

    26 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 56 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Jeunesse Kurikoma, reach 771 m. All 3 lifts here are rope tows.
