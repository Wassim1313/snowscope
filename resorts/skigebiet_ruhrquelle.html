    About Ruhrquelle

    Ruhrquelle has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 627 m and 664 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skiliftkarussell Winterberg, 2.5 km to the south-west.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 646 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 29 January, with about 8 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm, with 19 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 23 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 37 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 627 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Familienskigebiet Sahnehang reaches 825 m. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Ruhrquelle?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Ruhrquelle at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Ruhrquelle?

    The top is at 664 m and the base at 627 m.

    How many km of pistes does Ruhrquelle have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
