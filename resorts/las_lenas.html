    About Las Leñas

    About 27 km of pistes make Las Leñas one of the ten biggest ski areas of Andes (number 7). Its lifts climb from 2,215 m to 3,416 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Planchón, 33 km to the west.

    The mountain at Las Leñas

    From top to bottom that makes 1,201 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. A high base at 2,215 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. To get up there are 7 chairlifts, 5 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    What there is to ski at Las Leñas

    The piste map lists 33 graded runs, about 27 km in total. The largest share is red, with 16 km (60%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 3.1 km of black or expert runs.
