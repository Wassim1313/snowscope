    About ErlebnisBocksBerg Hahnenklee

    ErlebnisBocksBerg Hahnenklee is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 67 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Its lifts climb from 553 m to 725 m. The closest other ski area is Ski-Alpinum Schulenberg (5.3 km, east).

    The pistes of ErlebnisBocksBerg Hahnenklee

    ErlebnisBocksBerg Hahnenklee has 6 graded runs adding up to about 3.4 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.9 km, or 58%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    172 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 553 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. To get up there are 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.
