    About Zöblen-Schattwald

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Zöblen-Schattwald sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,075 m and reach 1,564 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Hindelang Oberjoch (3.1 km, west).

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,320 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late April, about 18 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 54 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 13 cm, snowy years 51 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 39 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Year to year the snow is steady, with 31 cm to 82 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    The pistes of Zöblen-Schattwald

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 9.8 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (62%, about 6.1 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Zöblen-Schattwald gives 489 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 4 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gaishorn at 2,247 m, standing 683 m above the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    Of the 7 ski areas compared here, Zöblen-Schattwald is the second biggest by piste length. If snow is the priority, Füssener Jöchle-Grän does better, with about 24 weeks of a good base instead of 18. The highest nearby is Füssener Jöchle-Grän, topping out at 1,869 m.

    Questions about Zöblen-Schattwald

    When is the best time to ski at Zöblen-Schattwald?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Zöblen-Schattwald at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 31 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Zöblen-Schattwald?

    The top is at 1,564 m and the base at 1,075 m.

    Is Zöblen-Schattwald suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 6.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Zöblen-Schattwald have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
