    About Mont des Brumes Piste de Ski

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Mont des Brumes Piste de Ski sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 383 m up to 523 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Val de Wanne, 8.1 km south, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    140 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 383 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.
