    About Gräftåvallen

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Gräftåvallen sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 657 m to 765 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Bydalsfjällen, 13 km to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 4.1 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.3 km, or 79%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Gräftåvallen has roughly 27 cm of snow on the ground today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 6 cm by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (24 cm).

    The mountain at Gräftåvallen

    108 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 drag lift, 2 lifts in all. Above the ski area rises Storfjället (1,260 m), 7.2 km to the west, about 495 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Gräftåvallen through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 711 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 8 December and keeps it until about 4 May, roughly 21 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 60 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 68 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 39 cm, with 62 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 45 cm and 89 cm by mid-February.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Gräftåvallen?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Gräftåvallen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Gräftåvallen?

    Its highest lift reaches 765 m; the lowest starts at 657 m (108 m of vertical).

    Is Gräftåvallen suitable for families?

    Yes: 92% of the graded pistes, about 3.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Gräftåvallen?

    Bydalsfjällen, 13 km to the north-west, with about 48 km of pistes.
