    The snow at Gräftåvallen, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Gräftåvallen (about 711 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 39 cm, with a normal range of 31 cm–48 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 65 cm (53 cm–79 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 68 cm.

    By 15 December 17 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 41 %.

    Best winter on record: 1987-88, peaking at 129 cm. Worst: 1995-96, which never passed 46 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 149 days.

    Getting to Gräftåvallen and what it costs

    Questions about Gräftåvallen

    Can you ski Gräftåvallen at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 39 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 41 % of 46 winters. Right now: 31 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Gräftåvallen?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 68 cm). For a sure base, early December to early May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Gräftåvallen get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 68 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 129 cm (1987-88), the thinnest 46 cm (1995-96).

    How high do the lifts go at Gräftåvallen?

    Its highest lift reaches 765 m; the lowest starts at 657 m (108 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Gräftåvallen?

    Bydalsfjällen, 13 km to the north-west, with about 48 km of pistes.

    How big is Gräftåvallen?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
