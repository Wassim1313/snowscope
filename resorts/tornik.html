    About Tornik

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Tornik sits in the middle of the size table of the Balkans. Skiing here happens between 1,108 m and 1,486 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Obudojevica (7.6 km, north-east).

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 378 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,486 m.
