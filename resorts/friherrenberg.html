    About Skilift Friherrenberg

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skilift Friherrenberg one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 924 m up to 1,110 m. The closest other ski area is Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln (2.5 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.5 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.5 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Skilift Friherrenberg is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 186 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,124 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Chli Aubrig at 1,642 m, standing 532 m above the top lift.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Skilift Friherrenberg hold around 18 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 46 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 11 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Snow at Skilift Friherrenberg through a normal winter

    Count on about 8 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,017 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 23 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 56 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 20 cm; a snowy December gave 33 cm, a dry one 5 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 36 cm.

    Skilift Friherrenberg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Skilift Friherrenberg?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Skilift Friherrenberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilift Friherrenberg?

    Between 924 m and 1,110 m, so 186 m of vertical drop.

    Is Skilift Friherrenberg suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilift Friherrenberg?

    Biberegg, 7.4 km to the south-west, with about 5.0 km of pistes.
