    About Antagnod

    Neither huge nor tiny, Antagnod offers about 11 km of pistes (number 454 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,727 m and 2,305 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Chamois, 5.0 km to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Antagnod

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Most of the length is blue (63%, about 6.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,016 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 44 cm, with 69 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 76 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 83 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 52 cm to 101 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    How Antagnod is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Antagnod gives 578 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,727 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Grand-Tournalin - Punta Nord, 3,379 m high and 5.5 km north of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Hotel Le Clocher, Hotel l'Espoir, RTA Trompe l'Oeil and Residence RTA Lo Pejo.

    Quick answers

    When does Antagnod have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Antagnod at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 44 cm deep.

    What altitude is Antagnod at?

    The top is at 2,305 m and the base at 1,727 m.

    Can beginners ski at Antagnod?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 6.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Antagnod have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
