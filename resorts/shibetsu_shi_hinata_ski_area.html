    About Shibetsu-shi Hinata

    Shibetsu-shi Hinata is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 181 m up to 402 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Furen, 3.5 km to the north.

    The mountain at Shibetsu-shi Hinata

    221 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 181 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 402 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 20 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Shibetsu-shi Hinata. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 5 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Shibetsu-shi Hinata: common questions

    What altitude is Shibetsu-shi Hinata at?

    Its highest lift reaches 402 m; the lowest starts at 181 m (221 m of vertical).
