    How Jauerling is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 88 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 854 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Jauerling (961 m), Burgstock (960 m) and Dürreck (948 m).

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 898 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow.     Questions about Jauerling

    When is the best time to ski at Jauerling?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Is there snow at Jauerling at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 3 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Jauerling?

    Its highest lift reaches 942 m; the lowest starts at 854 m (88 m of vertical).

    How big is Jauerling?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
