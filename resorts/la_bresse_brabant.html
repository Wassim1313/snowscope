    About La Bresse Brabant

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, La Bresse Brabant sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 869 m up to 982 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ventron, 7.1 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 2.7 km to ski. The largest share is black, with 1.2 km (43%). Steep ground is the speciality: 43% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 1.2 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow La Bresse Brabant usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 926 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 46 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 3 cm; a snowy December gave 31 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 9 cm.

    How La Bresse Brabant is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, La Bresse Brabant gives 113 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 869 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Le Grand Ventron, 1,204 m high and 5.4 km south-east of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at La Bresse Brabant?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    How much snow does La Bresse Brabant have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 3 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is La Bresse Brabant?

    The top is at 982 m and the base at 869 m.

    Is La Bresse Brabant good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.2 km of its graded pistes (43%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near La Bresse Brabant?

    Gérardmer, 7.9 km to the north, with about 23 km of pistes.
