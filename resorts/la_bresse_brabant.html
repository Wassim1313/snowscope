    About La Bresse Brabant

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, La Bresse Brabant sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 869 m up to 982 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ventron, 7.1 km to the south.

    How La Bresse Brabant is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, La Bresse Brabant gives 113 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 869 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Auberge du Brabant (hotel), Les Chatelminés (hotel), Le Gîte de la Chèvrerie du Brabant (chalet) and Vieille Ferme Du Brabant (chalet).
