    About Jižní svahy

    Neither huge nor tiny, Jižní svahy offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 213 of 312 in the Carpathians). Its lifts climb from 987 m to 1,089 m. Severní svahy is the next resort, 0.6 km away to the south-east.

    Other resorts close by

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 1.1 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.1 km (100%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 102 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 987 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. All 2 lifts here are T-bars.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Jižní svahy hold around 7 cm of snow today. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 5 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.

    Is Jižní svahy good for beginners?

    How many km of pistes does Jižní svahy have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
