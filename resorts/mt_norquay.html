    About Mt Norquay

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mt Norquay offers about 25 km of pistes (number 24 of 53 in Canada West). Its lifts climb from 1,632 m to 2,093 m. Banff Sunshine Village is the next resort, 17 km away to the south-west.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 47 km north-west to Lake Louise, with about 81 km of runs. For higher slopes, Banff Sunshine Village climbs to 2,686 m.

    How Mt Norquay is laid out

    The vertical drop is 461 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,632 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Banff Sunshine Village reaches 2,686 m.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Mt Norquay has roughly 39 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Mt Norquay include 4 hotels: Juniper Hotel, Bow View Lodge, Banff Ptarmigan Inn and The Kenrick Hotel.

    Questions about Mt Norquay

    How high do the lifts go at Mt Norquay?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,093 m; the lowest starts at 1,632 m (461 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Mt Norquay?

    Lake Louise, 47 km to the north-west, with about 81 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Mt Norquay have?

    About 25 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
