    About Mt Norquay

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mt Norquay offers about 25 km of pistes (number 24 of 53 in Canada West). Its lifts climb from 1,632 m to 2,093 m. Banff Sunshine Village is the next resort, 17 km away to the south-west.

    The pistes of Mt Norquay

    Counting every graded piste, there are 66 runs and around 25 km to ski. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 44%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.7 km.

    Snow at Mt Norquay through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,862 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 21 November and keeps it until about 26 May, roughly 27 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 52 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 31 cm in a poor year to 85 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. A normal season tops out near 81 cm, around 14 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 52 cm to 94 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 76 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    How Mt Norquay is laid out

    The vertical drop is 461 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,632 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Banff Sunshine Village reaches 2,686 m. Its 7 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Cascade Mountain, 3,006 m high and 4.2 km north-east of the resort.

    Questions about Mt Norquay

    When does Mt Norquay have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Mt Norquay at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 52 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Mt Norquay open for the 2026/27 season?

    It opened on 6 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Mt Norquay?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,093 m; the lowest starts at 1,632 m (461 m of vertical).

    Is Mt Norquay suitable for families?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.
