    About Les Planards (Chamonix)

    Compact and local, Les Planards (Chamonix) has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,053 m and reach 1,242 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix), 2.8 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 1.9 km in total. Most of the length is blue (49%, about 0.9 km). With 83% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Les Planards (Chamonix) is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Les Planards (Chamonix) gives 189 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Les Grands Montets, reach 2,768 m. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mont Blanc du Tacul at 4,248 m, standing 3,006 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,148 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 December and lasts to 26 April: about 19 weeks. A normal season tops out near 81 cm, around 19 February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 44 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 42 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 70 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 50 cm to 107 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Les Planards (Chamonix): common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Les Planards (Chamonix)?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Les Planards (Chamonix) at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 42 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Planards (Chamonix)?

    Between 1,053 m and 1,242 m, so 189 m of vertical drop.

    Is Les Planards (Chamonix) good for beginners?

    Yes: 83% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Les Planards (Chamonix)?

    Brévent/Flégère (Chamonix), 2.8 km to the north, with about 41 km of pistes.
