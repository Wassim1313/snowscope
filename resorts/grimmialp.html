    About Grimmialp

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Grimmialp sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,229 m and reach 1,828 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Wiriehorn, 6.1 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Grimmialp

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 8.6 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.6 km, or 54%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 0.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How much snow Grimmialp usually gets

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,528 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 112 cm. For about 5 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 84 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 51 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 82 cm, a dry one 18 cm. Most winters land between 66 cm and 152 cm by mid-February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 599 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Albristhorn, 2,762 m high and 6.8 km south of the resort.

    Snow this week

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Spillgerten (hotel), Hotel Kurhaus Grimmialp (hotel), Bergheim Wildgrimmi (mountain hut) and Stierenberg (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Grimmialp

    What is the best month to ski at Grimmialp?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Grimmialp at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 51 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Grimmialp?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,828 m; the lowest starts at 1,229 m (599 m of vertical).

    Is Grimmialp suitable for families?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 4.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Grimmialp have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
