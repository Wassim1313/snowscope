    What 46 winters say about Mölltaler Gletscher

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Mölltaler Gletscher (about 2,594 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 131 cm of cover (100 cm to 164 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 230 cm, between 186 cm and 257 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 263 cm.

    91 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 98 % by 1 January.

    2000-01 was the big one (427 cm); 1995-96 the lean one (202 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early October to late June (265 days).

    About Mölltaler Gletscher

    With roughly 21 km of runs, Mölltaler Gletscher sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 2,103 m and 3,084 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Sportgastein, 5.5 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 981 m: enough height for proper long runs. At 2,103 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
