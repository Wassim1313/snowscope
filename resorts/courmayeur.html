    Christmas week typically brings 80 cm of cover (53 cm to 109 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 162 cm, between 140 cm and 190 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 174 cm.

    63 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 91 % by 1 January.

    About Courmayeur

    Around 43 km of marked runs place Courmayeur among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 136 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,204 m and 2,749 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is La Thuile ski (12 km, south).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,545 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 24 lifts break down into 6 cable cars, 3 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 5 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 5 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Mont Blanc / Monte Bianco (4,807 m), 6.7 km to the north-west, about 2,058 m higher than the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Courmayeur

    In all, 91 marked runs cover roughly 42 km. Most of the length is red (50%, about 21 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 4.8 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,976 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 80 cm, from 41 cm in a poor year to 133 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 174 cm, around 2 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. Most winters land between 102 cm and 214 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 96 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.
