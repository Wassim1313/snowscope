    About Dynaland

    With some 13 km of runs, Dynaland belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. The slopes start at 984 m and reach 1,425 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Takasu Snow Park, 1.0 km to the north-east.

    How Dynaland is laid out

    441 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The base at 984 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,425 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.

    Dynaland: common questions

    What altitude is Dynaland at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,425 m; the lowest starts at 984 m (441 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Dynaland have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
