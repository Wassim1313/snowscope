    Not every year: about 14 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 0 % of 46 winters. Right now: 14 cm.

    About Romme Alpin

    With some 28 km of runs, Romme Alpin belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. The slopes start at 141 m and reach 406 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Nybroberget, 4.5 km north-east, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Romme Alpin gives 265 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 406 m is the highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km.

    Snow this week
