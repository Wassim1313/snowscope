    The snow at Gourette, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Gourette (1,903 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 42 cm of cover (17 cm to 62 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 93 cm; the middle half of winters range from 59 cm to 122 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 102 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 28 % of winters, and by 1 January in 50 %.

    2012-13 was the big one (231 cm); 1989-90 the lean one (53 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to late April, about 150 days.

    Questions about Gourette

    Does Gourette have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 42 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 50 % of 46 winters. Right now: 16 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Gourette?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 102 cm). For a sure base, early December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Gourette?

    About 102 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 53 cm in the leanest winter (1989-90) and 231 cm in the biggest (2012-13).

    How high do the lifts go at Gourette?

    Between 1,353 m and 2,453 m, so 1,100 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Gourette have?

    About 30 km of pistes and 12 lifts.

    About Gourette

    With roughly 30 km of runs, Gourette sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,353 m up to 2,453 m. Its nearest neighbour is Artouste, 6.8 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,100 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,353 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
