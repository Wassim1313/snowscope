    About Gourette

    With roughly 30 km of runs, Gourette sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,353 m up to 2,453 m. Its nearest neighbour is Artouste, 6.8 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 64 graded runs, about 30 km in total. Most of the length is red (43%, about 13 km). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.3 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,100 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,353 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 3 gondolas, 5 chairlifts, 1 drag lift and 3 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pic des Tourettes at 2,771 m, standing 318 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Gourette through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,903 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 December and keeps it until about 26 April, roughly 19 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 42 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 87 cm, a dry one 6 cm. Most winters land between 37 cm and 154 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 102 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 56 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Quick answers

    When does Gourette have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Gourette at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 42 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Gourette open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 29 March 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Gourette?

    Between 1,353 m and 2,453 m, so 1,100 m of vertical drop.

    Is Gourette good for beginners?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.
