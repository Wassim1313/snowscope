    About Tisovec

    Tisovec is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 611 m and 812 m above sea level. Ski Zbojská is the next resort, 4.5 km away to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    201 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 611 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a button lift.
