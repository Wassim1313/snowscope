    About Sapporo Teine

    With some 14 km of runs, Sapporo Teine belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Skiing here happens between 340 m and 986 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Sapporo Bankei (8.8 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Sapporo Teine has 19 graded runs adding up to about 14 km. Most of the length is green (60%, about 8.4 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 646 m: runs of a moderate length. At 340 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 986 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 gondola, 10 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Questions about Sapporo Teine

    How high do the lifts go at Sapporo Teine?

    Its highest lift reaches 986 m; the lowest starts at 340 m (646 m of vertical).

    Is Sapporo Teine good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 8.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Sapporo Teine?

    About 14 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
