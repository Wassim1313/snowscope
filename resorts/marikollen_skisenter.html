    About Marikollen skisenter

    Marikollen skisenter is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 198 m and 342 m above sea level. SNØ Oslo is the next resort, 6.1 km away to the north-west.

    How Marikollen skisenter is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 144 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 342 m is the highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Barlindåsen (398 m), Bjønnåsen (396 m) and Ramstadslottet (394 m).

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 270 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 January and lasts to 16 March: about 8 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 33 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 19 February, with about 36 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 59 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Marikollen skisenter?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Marikollen skisenter have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    How high is Marikollen skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 342 m; the lowest starts at 198 m (144 m of vertical).
