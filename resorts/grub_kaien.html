    About Grub-Kaien

    Grub-Kaien is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 751 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 849 m and 1,068 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Spitze, 1.8 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Grub-Kaien

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 2.8 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 60%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes. 1.1 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Grub-Kaien

    The vertical drop is 219 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 849 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Gäbris, 1,250 m high and 7.1 km south of the resort.

    Snow at Grub-Kaien through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 958 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 February and keeps it until about 28 February, roughly 1 weeks. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 10 cm to 58 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 31 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 24 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 18 cm; a snowy December gave 33 cm, a dry one 3 cm.

    Questions about Grub-Kaien

    What is the best month to ski at Grub-Kaien?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    Is there snow at Grub-Kaien at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Grub-Kaien?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,068 m; the lowest starts at 849 m (219 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Grub-Kaien?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Grub-Kaien have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
