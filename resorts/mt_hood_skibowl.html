    About Mt. Hood Skibowl

    Mt. Hood Skibowl is a mid-sized ski area with about 26 km of pistes, number 85 of 175 in US West by length. Skiing here happens between 1,113 m and 1,528 m above sea level. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Timberline Lodge, 5.3 km to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Mt. Hood Skibowl

    Counting every graded piste, there are 62 runs and around 26 km to ski. Most of the length is black diamond (49%, about 13 km). Good skiers will like it here; 49% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 13 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 415 m: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,528 m is the third highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 4 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 3 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Tom Dick and Harry Mountain (1,544 m), Zigzag Mountain (1,526 m) and Veda Butte (1,518 m).

    Snow at Mt. Hood Skibowl through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,320 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 9 December and lasts to 27 April: about 20 weeks. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 72 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 68 cm, from 9 cm in a poor year to 133 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 17 cm and 203 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 135 cm, around 25 February.

    Mt. Hood Skibowl: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Mt. Hood Skibowl?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    Is there snow at Mt. Hood Skibowl at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 68 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Mt. Hood Skibowl at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,528 m; the lowest starts at 1,113 m (415 m of vertical).

    Is Mt. Hood Skibowl good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 13 km of its graded pistes (49%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Mt. Hood Skibowl have?

    About 26 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
