    About Ski&Bike Špičák

    Ski&Bike Špičák is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 7.0 km of pistes put it at number 38 of 257. Skiing here happens between 842 m and 1,200 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Za Nádražím, 0.7 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 16 runs and around 7.4 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (51%, about 3.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Ski&Bike Špičák gives 358 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 842 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,200 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 3 drag lifts, 7 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Snow at Ski&Bike Špičák through a normal winter

    Count on about 1 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,021 m) in an ordinary winter, from early February to mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 45 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 30 cm, around 10 February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 5 cm to 73 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Ski&Bike Špičák: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Ski&Bike Špičák?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-February.

    Can you ski at Ski&Bike Špičák at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Ski&Bike Špičák?

    Between 842 m and 1,200 m, so 358 m of vertical drop.

    Is Ski&Bike Špičák good for beginners?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 3.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ski&Bike Špičák have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
