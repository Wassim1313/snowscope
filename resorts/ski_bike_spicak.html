    About Ski&Bike Špičák

    Ski&Bike Špičák is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 7.0 km of pistes put it at number 38 of 257. Skiing here happens between 842 m and 1,200 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Za Nádražím, 0.7 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 16 runs and around 7.4 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (51%, about 3.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Ski&Bike Špičák gives 358 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 842 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,200 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 3 drag lifts, 7 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
