    About Howelson Hill

    Howelson Hill is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 7.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 2,048 m and 2,186 m above sea level. Bowes Ski Hill is the next resort, 2.4 km away to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 19 graded runs, about 6.6 km in total. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 4.6 km, or 69%. With 69% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Howelson Hill

    From top to bottom that makes 138 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,048 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Emerald Mountain (2,515 m), 2.6 km to the south, about 329 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Howelson Hill through a normal winter

    At about 2,117 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 December and lasts to 21 April: about 20 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 50 cm; a snowy December gave 71 cm, a dry one 21 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 44 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 87 cm, around 27 February. Most winters land between 60 cm and 111 cm by mid-February.

    Howelson Hill: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Howelson Hill?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    Can you ski at Howelson Hill at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 50 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Howelson Hill?

    The top is at 2,186 m and the base at 2,048 m.

    Can beginners ski at Howelson Hill?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 4.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Howelson Hill have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
