    About Howelson Hill

    Howelson Hill is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 7.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 2,048 m and 2,186 m above sea level. Bowes Ski Hill is the next resort, 2.4 km away to the north-east.

    The mountain at Howelson Hill

    From top to bottom that makes 138 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,048 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most.
