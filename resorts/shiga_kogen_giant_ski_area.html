    About Shiga Kogen Giant

    Compact and local, Shiga Kogen Giant has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,336 m up to 1,596 m. The closest other ski area is Shiga Kogen Hasuike (0.4 km, south-west).

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 1.5 km north-east to Shiga Kogen Nishidateyama, with about 5.0 km of runs. Shiga Kogen Higashidateyama reaches 1,985 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 18 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Shiga Kogen Giant. 13 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Shiga Kogen Giant gives 260 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,336 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    Quick answers

    How high is Shiga Kogen Giant?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,596 m; the lowest starts at 1,336 m (260 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Shiga Kogen Giant?

    Shiga Kogen Nishidateyama, 1.5 km to the north-east, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Shiga Kogen Giant have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
