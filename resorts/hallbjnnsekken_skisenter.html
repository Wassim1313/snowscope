    About Hallbjønnsekken skisenter

    Hallbjønnsekken skisenter is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 140 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The slopes start at 778 m and reach 1,057 m at the top. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hovden Alpinsenter, 33 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Hallbjønnsekken skisenter

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 3.5 km. Most of the length is red (60%, about 2.1 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How much snow Hallbjønnsekken skisenter usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 918 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. Most winters land between 102 cm and 184 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 151 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 140 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 19 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 76 cm, with 114 cm in the best years.

    The mountain at Hallbjønnsekken skisenter

    From top to bottom that makes 279 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Napuren (1,284 m), 4.3 km to the south-west, about 227 m higher than the top lift.

    Hallbjønnsekken skisenter: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Hallbjønnsekken skisenter?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Hallbjønnsekken skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 76 cm deep.

    How high is Hallbjønnsekken skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,057 m; the lowest starts at 778 m (279 m of vertical).

    Who is Hallbjønnsekken skisenter best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 60% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Hallbjønnsekken skisenter?

    Hovden Alpinsenter, 33 km to the north-west, with about 32 km of pistes.
