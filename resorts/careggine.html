    About Careggine

    Compact and local, Careggine has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,103 m and 1,208 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Area Sciistica Sant'Annapelago (20 km, north-east).

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Area Sciistica Abetone (27 km east) has about 39 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. Area Sciisitica Febbio reaches 2,060 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Careggine has 4 graded runs adding up to about 1.6 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 100%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at Careggine in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,156 m) stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Careggine gives 105 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Area Sciisitica Febbio reaches 2,060 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Monte Tambura, 1,890 m high and 6.7 km west of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Careggine include Genzianella (hotel), Hotel delle Alpi (hotel), Il Lago (hotel) and Il Vitollo (hostel).

    How high is Careggine?

    Between 1,103 m and 1,208 m, so 105 m of vertical drop.

    Is Careggine suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Careggine?

    Area Sciistica Abetone, 27 km to the east, with about 39 km of pistes.

    How big is Careggine?
