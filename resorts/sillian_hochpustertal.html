    About Sillian

    Around 24 km of marked runs place Sillian among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 262 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,080 m and 2,399 m above sea level. Kartitsch / St. Oswald is the next resort, 7.1 km away to the south-east.

    The mountain at Sillian

    From top to bottom that makes 1,319 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 2 gondolas, 3 chairlifts and 2 T-bars. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Hohes Haus (2,784 m), 7.4 km to the north, about 385 m higher than the top lift.

    The pistes of Sillian

    In all, 29 marked runs cover roughly 23 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 58%. Intermediate runs make up 58% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.4 km.

    How much snow Sillian usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,740 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 38 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 73 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 55 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 37 cm and 97 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 62 cm, around 27 February.

    Sillian: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Sillian?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Sillian at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Sillian?

    The top is at 2,399 m and the base at 1,080 m.

    Who is Sillian best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 58% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Sillian have?

    About 24 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
