    About Karkogel

    Neither huge nor tiny, Karkogel offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 515 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 713 m to 1,191 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lammertallift, 6.3 km to the south.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 478 m: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 713 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.
