    About Karkogel

    Neither huge nor tiny, Karkogel offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 515 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 713 m to 1,191 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lammertallift, 6.3 km to the south.

    What there is to ski at Karkogel

    The piste map lists 17 graded runs, about 8.7 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 5.9 km (68%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 478 m: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 713 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chair-gondola, 1 T-bar and 3 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Bleikogel (2,411 m), 6.0 km to the south-west, about 1,220 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Karkogel usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 952 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 8 January and keeps it until about 4 April, roughly 13 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 49 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 26 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 25 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 51 cm). Most winters land between 22 cm and 66 cm by mid-February.

    Karkogel: common questions

    When does Karkogel have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Is there snow at Karkogel at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Karkogel?

    Between 713 m and 1,191 m, so 478 m of vertical drop.

    Is Karkogel suitable for families?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 5.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Karkogel?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
