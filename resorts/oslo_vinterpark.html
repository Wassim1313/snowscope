    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 258 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early April, about 13 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 12 cm and 74 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 43 cm). A normal season tops out near 52 cm, around 8 February.

    Oslo Vinterpark: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Oslo Vinterpark?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    How much snow does Oslo Vinterpark have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
