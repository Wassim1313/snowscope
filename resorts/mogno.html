    About Mogno

    Mogno is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,156 m to 1,249 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mogno, 0.1 km to the south.

    How Mogno compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 9.9 km north-west to Airolo Pesciüm, with about 21 km of runs. For higher slopes, Cari climbs to 2,280 m.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Mogno has roughly 3 cm of snow on the ground today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 14 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 93 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Bosco Gurin, reach 2,326 m.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Colonia Pazzalino (chalet), Albergo Pineta (hotel), Antica Osteria Dazio (hotel) and Hotel Fusio (hotel).

    Questions about Mogno

    What altitude is Mogno at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,249 m; the lowest starts at 1,156 m (93 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Mogno?

    Airolo Pesciüm, 9.9 km to the north-west, with about 21 km of pistes.

    How big is Mogno?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
