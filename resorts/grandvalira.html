    GrandValira in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of GrandValira (about 2,146 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 33 cm, with a normal range of 18 cm–53 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 75 cm; the middle half of winters range from 52 cm to 103 cm.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 78 cm.

    22 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 39 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 152 cm. Worst: 1989-90, which never passed 26 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-December until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 125 days.

    Questions about GrandValira

    Does GrandValira have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 33 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 39 % of 46 winters. Right now: 3 cm.

    What is the best time to ski GrandValira?

    February: the snow peaks at about 78 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to mid-April.

    How much snow does GrandValira get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 78 cm in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 152 cm (1981-82), the thinnest 26 cm (1989-90).

    How high do the lifts go at GrandValira?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,619 m; the lowest starts at 1,674 m (945 m of vertical).

    How big is GrandValira?

    About 136 km of pistes and 53 lifts.

    About GrandValira

    About 136 km of pistes make GrandValira one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Pyrenees (number 2). The slopes start at 1,674 m and reach 2,619 m at the top. Canaro is the next resort, 3.4 km away to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, GrandValira gives 945 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,674 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
