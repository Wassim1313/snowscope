    About Shiga Kogen Shibutoge

    Compact and local, Shiga Kogen Shibutoge has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,156 m up to 2,291 m. The closest other ski area is Shiga Kogen Yokoteyama (1.7 km, north-west).

    How Shiga Kogen Shibutoge is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Shiga Kogen Shibutoge gives 135 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,156 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Shiga Kogen Shibutoge has 2 graded runs adding up to about 1.4 km. The largest share is red, with 1.4 km (100%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Shiga Kogen Shibutoge: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Shiga Kogen Shibutoge?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,291 m; the lowest starts at 2,156 m (135 m of vertical).

    Who is Shiga Kogen Shibutoge best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Shiga Kogen Shibutoge?

    Shiga Kogen Yokoteyama, 1.7 km to the north-west, with about 14 km of pistes.

    How big is Shiga Kogen Shibutoge?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
