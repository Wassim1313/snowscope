    About Lavarone

    With roughly 13 km of runs, Lavarone sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,181 m and 1,549 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Val Formica, 8.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Lavarone

    The piste map lists 19 graded runs, about 13 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 9.0 km (71%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Lavarone is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 368 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 6 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 7 moving carpets, 16 lifts in all. For small children there are 7 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Becco di Filadonna at 2,150 m, standing 601 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Lavarone usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,365 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to early March, about 2 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 13 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 32 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 75 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 51 cm, a dry one 2 cm.

    Questions about Lavarone

    When is the best time to ski at Lavarone?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Is there snow at Lavarone at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    What altitude is Lavarone at?

    Between 1,181 m and 1,549 m, so 368 m of vertical drop.

    Is Lavarone good for beginners?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 9.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Lavarone?

    About 13 km of pistes and 16 lifts.
