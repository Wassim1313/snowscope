    About Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn

    Only 4 of the 257 ski areas of Central Europe are larger than Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn, which has some 20 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 693 m up to 1,233 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Špindlerův Mlýn, 1.7 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 43 graded runs, about 16 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 9.1 km, or 56%. With 56% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn gives 540 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 693 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,233 m. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 4 button lifts, 9 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Snow at Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 963 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to mid-March, about 8 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 8 cm and 76 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 61 cm in a big one. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 47 cm.

    Questions about Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn

    What is the best month to ski at Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,233 m; the lowest starts at 693 m (540 m of vertical).

    Is Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn good for beginners?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 9.1 km, are easy.

    How big is Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn?

    About 20 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
