    About Mt Baker

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mt Baker offers about 23 km of pistes (number 92 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 1,106 m and reach 1,556 m at the top.

    How much snow Mt Baker usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,331 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 307 cm, around 30 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 289 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For about 25 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 157 cm and 332 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 144 cm, from 89 cm in a poor year to 213 cm in a big one.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 47 graded runs, about 23 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (57%, about 13 km). Intermediate runs make up 57% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 5.1 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Mt Baker gives 450 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Its 10 lifts break down into 8 chairlifts and 2 rope tows. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The skyline is dominated by Mount Shuksan, 2,784 m high and 5.4 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow right now

    Mt Baker: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Mt Baker?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Mt Baker at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 144 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Mt Baker?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,556 m; the lowest starts at 1,106 m (450 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Mt Baker best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 57% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Mt Baker have?

    About 23 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
