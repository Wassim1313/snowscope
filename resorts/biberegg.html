    About Biberegg

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Biberegg sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 954 m up to 1,321 m. Sattel-Hochstuckli is the next resort, 3.3 km away to the south-west.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue (6.9 km south) has about 27 km of pistes against 5.0 km here. For more reliable snow, Brunni-Haggenegg usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 20 weeks, against 16 here. The highest nearby is Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue, topping out at 1,568 m.

    The pistes of Biberegg

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 5.0 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (44%, about 2.2 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Experts can count on some 1.4 km of difficult runs.

    Snow at Biberegg through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,138 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 16 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 32 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 19 cm and 69 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 27 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 42 cm, a dry one 9 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 46 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 367 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,154 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Grosser Mythen, 1,898 m high and 6.5 km south of the resort.

    Questions about Biberegg

    What is the best month to ski at Biberegg?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Biberegg at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 27 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Biberegg?

    The top is at 1,321 m and the base at 954 m.

    Can beginners ski at Biberegg?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Biberegg?

    Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue, 6.9 km to the south, with about 27 km of pistes.
