    About Klausberg

    Around 60 km of marked runs place Klausberg among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 93 by length. Skiing here happens between 938 m and 2,473 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Speikboden, 3.6 km to the south-west.

    How Klausberg is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,535 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,138 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 2,473 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 20 lifts: 6 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 4 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Schwarzenstein - Sasso Nero (3,369 m), 7.9 km to the north-west, about 896 m higher than the top lift.

    The pistes of Klausberg

    The piste map lists 83 graded runs, about 60 km in total. Most of the length is blue (50%, about 30 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,706 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 17 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 19 February, with about 62 cm. Most winters land between 38 cm and 81 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 33 cm, from 15 cm in a poor year to 53 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 36 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    Questions about Klausberg

    When does Klausberg have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Klausberg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 33 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Klausberg open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Klausberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,473 m; the lowest starts at 938 m (1,535 m of vertical).

    Is Klausberg good for beginners?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 30 km, are easy.
