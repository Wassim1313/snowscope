    What 46 winters say about Klausberg

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Klausberg (about 1,706 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 33 cm, with a normal range of 23 cm–43 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 60 cm, between 47 cm and 71 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 62 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 9 % of winters, and by 1 January in 17 %.

    The deepest winter was 2013-14 (98 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2016-17 (33 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April, about 139 days.

    How much snow does Klausberg get?
