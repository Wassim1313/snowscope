    About Bödele

    With roughly 21 km of runs, Bödele sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 814 m to 1,439 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Alberschwende, 4.4 km to the north.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,126 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 December and lasts to 3 May: about 19 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 25 cm to 87 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 55 cm, around 16 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 43 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 30 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 9 cm in a poor year to 50 cm in a big one.

    How Bödele is laid out

    The vertical drop is 625 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 814 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 12 lifts: 1 chairlift, 4 T-bars, 6 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Leuenkopf, 1,831 m high and 6.8 km south of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When does Bödele have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    How much snow does Bödele have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 30 cm deep.

    How high is Bödele?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,439 m; the lowest starts at 814 m (625 m of vertical).

    Is Bödele good for beginners?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Bödele have?

    About 21 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
