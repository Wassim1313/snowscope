    About Skilift Maiergschwendt

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Skilift Maiergschwendt one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 703 m and 740 m above sea level. Westernberg is the next resort, 1.2 km away to the north-east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Maiergschwendt gives 37 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 703 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, reach 1,853 m. The only lift is a rope tow. Above the ski area rises Gurnwandkopf (1,691 m), 6.7 km to the south-west, about 951 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 722 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 23 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 12 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 27 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.
