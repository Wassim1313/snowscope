    About Sanlaiva

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sanlaiva offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 182 of 385 in Japan). Its lifts climb from 356 m to 720 m. Orofure is the next resort, 10.0 km away to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 12 marked runs cover roughly 5.3 km. Most of the length is red (35%, about 1.9 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Sanlaiva

    The vertical drop is 364 m: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 356 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Sanlaiva today: about 1 cm up high. The last seven days brought 15 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Monday.

    Sanlaiva: common questions

    How high is Sanlaiva?

    Between 356 m and 720 m, so 364 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Sanlaiva?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 2.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Sanlaiva have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
