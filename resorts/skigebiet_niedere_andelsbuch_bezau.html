    The snow at Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau), winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau) (about 1,452 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 42 cm, with a normal range of 25 cm–54 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 78 cm (61 cm–97 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 84 cm.

    17 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 39 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 176 cm. Worst: 2006-07, which never passed 55 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to mid-May, about 171 days.

    Today's 3 cm compares with a usual 17 cm for 25 November.

    Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau): quick answers

    Can you ski Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau) at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 42 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 39 % of 46 winters. Right now: 35 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau)?

    February: the snow peaks at about 84 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to mid-May.

    How snowy is Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau)?

    About 84 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 55 cm in the leanest winter (2006-07) and 176 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    How high is Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau)?

    Between 1,200 m and 1,704 m, so 504 m of vertical drop.

    Is Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau) good for beginners?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 3.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau)?

    Mellau, 7.4 km to the south-west, with about 24 km of pistes.

    About Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau)

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau) sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,200 m to 1,704 m. Schetteregg is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the north-east.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,452 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 16 December and keeps it until about 19 May, roughly 22 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 42 cm (lean years 15 cm, snowy years 69 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 63 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 84 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 44 cm to 121 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    The pistes of Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau)

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 6.2 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.3 km, or 54%. With 54% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Niedere (Andelsbuch-Bezau) is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 504 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 2 T-bars and 1 button lift, 4 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Holenke (2,044 m), 7.0 km to the south, about 340 m higher than the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Mellau (7.4 km south-west) has about 24 km of pistes against 6.0 km here. Its snow is the most reliable of the group: a 30 cm base lasts about 22 weeks in a typical winter, longer than at any neighbour listed. For higher slopes, Mellau climbs to 1,920 m.
