    The snow at Terenten, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Terenten (about 1,308 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 21 cm; half of the winters sit between 12 cm and 31 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 34 cm; the middle half of winters range from 25 cm to 45 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 35 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 4 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 7 %.

    The deepest winter was 2019-20 (83 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2016-17 (12 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late December to late March (99 days).

    This winter so far: 6 cm on 26 November, against a usual 6 cm for the date.

    About Terenten

    Terenten is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,235 m and reach 1,381 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Gitschberg Jochtal, 10.0 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Kronplatz: about 76 km of pistes, 17 km to the south-east. If snow is the priority, Gitschberg Jochtal does better, with about 25 weeks of a good base instead of 8. The highest nearby is Brixen Plose, topping out at 2,502 m.

    How Terenten is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 146 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Brixen Plose reaches 2,502 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Graunock - Monte Grigio at 2,827 m, standing 1,446 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Terenten usually gets

    Count on about 8 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,308 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 21 cm; a snowy December gave 41 cm, a dry one 4 cm. A normal season tops out near 35 cm, around 21 February. By mid-April the typical depth is 16 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 18 cm and 58 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February.
