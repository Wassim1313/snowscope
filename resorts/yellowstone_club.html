    About Yellowstone Club

    Yellowstone Club is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 103 km of pistes put it at number 13 of 175. Skiing here happens between 2,190 m and 3,001 m above sea level. Big Sky Resort is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Yellowstone Club

    In all, 98 marked runs cover roughly 103 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 46 km, or 45%. No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. The steepest pistes add up to about 31 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,596 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 14 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 82 cm (lean years 60 cm, snowy years 117 cm). For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 139 cm, around 16 April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 138 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 94 cm to 155 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March.

    How Yellowstone Club is laid out

    The vertical drop is 811 m: enough height for proper long runs. At 2,190 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. The lift system counts 17 lifts: 1 funicular, 3 gondolas, 12 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Lone Mountain, 3,397 m high and 4.5 km north of the resort.

    Questions about Yellowstone Club

    What is the best month to ski at Yellowstone Club?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Yellowstone Club have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 82 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Yellowstone Club at?

    The top is at 3,001 m and the base at 2,190 m.

    Is Yellowstone Club good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 31 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.

    How big is Yellowstone Club?

    About 103 km of pistes and 17 lifts.
