    About Pokljuka

    Pokljuka is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,262 m up to 1,564 m. The closest other ski area is Zatrnik (7.7 km, east).

    How Pokljuka is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Pokljuka gives 302 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,564 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 6 lifts break down into 2 T-bars, 2 drag lifts and 2 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mali Triglav at 2,738 m, standing 1,174 m above the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Vogel (13 km south-west) has about 16 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Soriška planina does better, with about 10 weeks of a good base instead of 2.

    The pistes of Pokljuka

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 1.9 km. Most of the length is blue (76%, about 1.4 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Pokljuka usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,413 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 February and keeps it until about 3 March, roughly 2 weeks. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 58 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 18 cm, with 50 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. A normal season tops out near 31 cm, around 21 February.

    Questions about Pokljuka

    When is the best time to ski at Pokljuka?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-February to early March.

    How much snow does Pokljuka have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 18 cm deep.

    How high is Pokljuka?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,564 m; the lowest starts at 1,262 m (302 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Pokljuka?

    Yes: 76% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Pokljuka?

    Vogel, 13 km to the south-west, with about 16 km of pistes.
