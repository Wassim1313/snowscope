    About Resort Levočská dolina

    Neither huge nor tiny, Resort Levočská dolina offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 157 of 312 in the Carpathians). Its lifts climb from 635 m to 875 m. The closest other ski area is Poráč Park (23 km, south-east).

    How Resort Levočská dolina is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Resort Levočská dolina gives 240 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 635 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 button lifts and 1 drag lift.
