    About Oberegg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Oberegg offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 775 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 848 m up to 1,081 m. Heiden is the next resort, 2.3 km away to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 2.5 km. Most of the length is blue (55%, about 1.4 km). With 55% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Oberegg usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 964 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 February and lasts to 4 March: about 3 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 18 cm, from 3 cm in a poor year to 33 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 10 cm to 59 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 32 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 24 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    How Oberegg is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 233 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 848 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Gäbris (1,250 m), 7.1 km to the south-west, about 169 m higher than the top lift.

    Oberegg: common questions

    When does Oberegg have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to early March.

    Is there snow at Oberegg at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 18 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Oberegg at?

    The top is at 1,081 m and the base at 848 m.

    Is Oberegg suitable for families?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Oberegg have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
