    The snow at Stöten i Sälen, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Stöten i Sälen (about 637 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 37 cm; half of the winters sit between 26 cm and 49 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 66 cm, between 52 cm and 77 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 68 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 17 % of winters, and by 1 January in 39 %.

    Best winter on record: 1987-88, peaking at 125 cm. Worst: 1995-96, which never passed 42 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April, about 141 days.

    This winter so far: 7 cm on 27 November, against a usual 16 cm for the date.

    Questions about Stöten i Sälen

    Is there snow in Stöten i Sälen at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 37 cm, and only 39 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 19 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Stöten i Sälen?

    March: the snow peaks at about 68 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Stöten i Sälen get?

    About 68 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 42 cm in the leanest winter (1995-96) and 125 cm in the biggest (1987-88).

    When does Stöten i Sälen have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    When does the ski season start at Stöten i Sälen?

    The announced opening day is 10 December 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Stöten i Sälen?

    The top is at 835 m and the base at 439 m.

    About Stöten i Sälen

    Around 36 km of marked runs place Stöten i Sälen among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 11 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 439 m up to 835 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hundfjället, 11 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Stöten i Sälen

    The piste map lists 59 graded runs, about 36 km in total. Most of the length is green (beginner) (56%, about 20 km). With 84% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Snow at Stöten i Sälen through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 637 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 December and keeps it until about 29 April, roughly 20 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 88 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 68 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 53 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 37 cm, from 13 cm in a poor year to 63 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 396 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 26 lifts: 3 chairlifts, 11 T-bars, 9 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Östra Granfjället at 981 m, standing 146 m above the top lift.
