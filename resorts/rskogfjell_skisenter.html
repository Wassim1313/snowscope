    About Ørskogfjell skisenter

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Ørskogfjell skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 318 m up to 320 m. Orskogfjell is the next resort, 0.1 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Ørskogfjell skisenter

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 2.5 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.5 km, or 100%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 28 km south to Strandafjellet, with about 20 km of runs. Strandafjellet has the longer snow season: around 31 weeks of solid cover, compared with 21 at Ørskogfjell skisenter. Strandafjellet reaches 1,139 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Ørskogfjell skisenter today and the next days

    Today there are about 30 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Ørskogfjell skisenter. 11 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 14 cm at mid-mountain).

    The mountain at Ørskogfjell skisenter

    Skied top to bottom, Ørskogfjell skisenter gives 2 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Strandafjellet, reach 1,139 m. The only lift is a moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Sprovstind (1,194 m), 2.9 km to the north-east, about 874 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 319 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 84 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 98 cm, around 21 March. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 42 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 77 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 38 cm to 128 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Ørskogfjell skisenter?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Ørskogfjell skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 42 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Ørskogfjell skisenter?

    The top is at 320 m and the base at 318 m.

    Can beginners ski at Ørskogfjell skisenter?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ørskogfjell skisenter?

    Strandafjellet, 28 km to the south, with about 20 km of pistes.
