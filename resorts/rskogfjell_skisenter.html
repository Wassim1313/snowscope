    About Ørskogfjell skisenter

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Ørskogfjell skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 318 m up to 320 m. Orskogfjell is the next resort, 0.1 km away to the north-east.

    The mountain at Ørskogfjell skisenter

    Skied top to bottom, Ørskogfjell skisenter gives 2 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Strandafjellet, reach 1,139 m.
