    About Boí Taüll

    With roughly 35 km of runs, Boí Taüll sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. Its lifts climb from 2,039 m to 2,747 m. Espot is the next resort, 19 km away to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 49 runs and around 35 km to ski. Most of the length is red (43%, about 15 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 9.2 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Boí Taüll through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,393 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 16 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 27 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 16 cm and 138 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm; a snowy December gave 80 cm, a dry one 6 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 84 cm.

    The mountain at Boí Taüll

    The vertical drop is 708 m: enough height for proper long runs. At 2,039 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,747 m. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 6 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gran Pic del Pessó at 2,894 m, standing 147 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Boí Taüll

    What is the best month to ski at Boí Taüll?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Boí Taüll at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Boí Taüll at?

    Between 2,039 m and 2,747 m, so 708 m of vertical drop.

    Is Boí Taüll good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 9.2 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    How big is Boí Taüll?

    About 35 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
