    The snow at Boí Taüll, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Boí Taüll (about 2,393 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 34 cm; half of the winters sit between 15 cm and 54 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 84 cm; the middle half of winters range from 38 cm to 114 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 84 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 24 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 39 %.

    Best winter on record: 2013-14, peaking at 196 cm. Worst: 2024-25, which never passed 30 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-December until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 125 days.

    Skiing Boí Taüll: the practical side

    Lifts: 9. Pistes: 35 km. Heights: 2,039 m at the bottom, 2,747 m at the top (708 m of vertical).

    Questions about Boí Taüll

    Can you ski Boí Taüll at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 34 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 39 % of 46 winters. Right now: 8 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Boí Taüll?

    February: the snow peaks at about 84 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to mid-April.

    How deep does the snow get at Boí Taüll?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 84 cm in mid-February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 196 cm (2013-14), the thinnest 30 cm (2024-25).

    What altitude is Boí Taüll at?

    Between 2,039 m and 2,747 m, so 708 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Boí Taüll?

    About 35 km of pistes and 9 lifts.

    About Boí Taüll

    With roughly 35 km of runs, Boí Taüll sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. Its lifts climb from 2,039 m to 2,747 m. Espot is the next resort, 19 km away to the north-east.

    The mountain at Boí Taüll

    The vertical drop is 708 m: enough height for proper long runs. At 2,039 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,747 m.
