    About Matsudai Family

    Matsudai Family is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 197 m and 386 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Matsunoyama Onsen, 8.3 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Matsudai Family and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Joetsu International (19 km south-east) has about 23 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. For higher slopes, Joetsu International climbs to 931 m.

    What there is to ski at Matsudai Family

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 2.1 km to ski. The largest share is green, with 1.3 km (62%). With 62% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Matsudai Family

    The vertical drop is 189 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 197 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; New Greenpia Tsunan reaches 963 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Questions about Matsudai Family

    What altitude is Matsudai Family at?

    The top is at 386 m and the base at 197 m.

    Is Matsudai Family good for beginners?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Matsudai Family?

    Joetsu International, 19 km to the south-east, with about 23 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Matsudai Family have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
