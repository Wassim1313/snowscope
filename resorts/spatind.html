    About Spatind

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Spatind one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 798 m up to 888 m. Gamlestølen is the next resort, 15 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 5 runs and around 2.2 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 62%. Intermediate runs make up 62% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 843 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 November and keeps it until about 18 May, roughly 26 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 89 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 70 cm and 122 cm. A normal season tops out near 97 cm, around 21 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 60 cm, from 36 cm in a poor year to 95 cm in a big one.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    90 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Skeikampen, reach 1,114 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Spåtind, 1,414 m high and 7.4 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Spatind today: about 15 cm up high. The coming days should add about 6 cm by Monday. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 49 cm by now.

    Spatind: common questions

    When does Spatind have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Spatind have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 60 cm deep.

    How high is Spatind?

    Its highest lift reaches 888 m; the lowest starts at 798 m (90 m of vertical).

    Who is Spatind best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 62% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Spatind?

    Skeikampen Alpinsenter, 27 km to the north-east, with about 15 km of pistes.
