    About Mad River Glen

    With roughly 27 km of runs, Mad River Glen sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. The ski area spans the mountain from 502 m up to 1,106 m. The closest other ski area is Sugarbush Resort (5.7 km, south).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 69 graded runs, about 26 km in total. The largest share is black diamond, with 10 km (39%). With 39% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 10 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Mad River Glen

    From top to bottom that makes 604 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. At 502 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 5 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts and 1 rope tow. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Mount Ellen, 1,234 m high and 4.3 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 804 m) in an ordinary winter, from early January to late March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 25 cm and 87 cm by mid-February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 55 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 25 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 51 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Mad River Glen?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Is there snow at Mad River Glen at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 25 cm deep.

    How high is Mad River Glen?

    Between 502 m and 1,106 m, so 604 m of vertical drop.

    Is Mad River Glen good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 10 km of its graded pistes (39%) are difficult.

    How big is Mad River Glen?

    About 27 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
