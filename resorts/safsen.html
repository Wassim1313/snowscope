    About Säfsen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Säfsen offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 93 of 262 in Scandinavia). The slopes start at 317 m and reach 469 m at the top.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 8.3 km to ski. The largest share is green (beginner), with 3.7 km (44%). With 75% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 8 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 393 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 37 cm, around 28 February. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 12 cm to 56 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    152 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Österhöjden (525 m), Råberget (475 m) and Solberget (472 m).

    Questions about Säfsen

    When is the best time to ski at Säfsen?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Säfsen have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 18 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Säfsen at?

    The top is at 469 m and the base at 317 m.

    Is Säfsen suitable for families?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 6.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Säfsen?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
