    About Regensberg

    Regensberg has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 585 m and reach 629 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Röhrenmoos, 9.0 km south-west, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Regensberg in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at about 607 m, the middle of the ski area stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Snow this week

    Regensberg has no snow cover yet today. The last seven days brought 2 cm of new snow.

    How Regensberg is laid out

    44 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 585 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Nollen, reach 1,046 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Lägern (866 m), 2.8 km to the west, about 237 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Bienengarten, Krone, Löwen and Zur Au.
