    About White World Oze Iwakura

    White World Oze Iwakura is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 11 km of pistes put it at number 66 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,043 m up to 1,673 m. The closest other ski area is Katashina Kogen (1.5 km, south-east).

    What there is to ski at White World Oze Iwakura

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. The largest share is red, with 7.3 km (69%). Intermediate runs make up 69% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How White World Oze Iwakura is laid out

    The vertical drop is 630 m: runs of a moderate length. Its 11 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 10 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.
