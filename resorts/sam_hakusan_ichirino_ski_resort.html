    About SAM Hakusan Ichirino

    SAM Hakusan Ichirino is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 216 of 385 in Japan by length. The slopes start at 547 m and reach 1,031 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour, 9.3 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 4.3 km to ski. Green runs make up the biggest part: 2.1 km, or 49%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, SAM Hakusan Ichirino gives 484 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 547 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 1,031 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 gondola and 3 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.
