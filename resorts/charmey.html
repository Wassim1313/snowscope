    About Charmey

    Neither huge nor tiny, Charmey offers about 12 km of pistes (number 427 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 871 m to 1,618 m. Its nearest neighbour is La Berra, 6.4 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Charmey

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 12 km. The largest share is blue, with 6.5 km (52%). With 52% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. 1.0 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Charmey

    From top to bottom that makes 747 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 871 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 4 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Above the ski area rises Hochmatt (2,151 m), 5.8 km to the south, about 533 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Charmey usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,244 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to mid-April, about 16 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 27 cm; a snowy December gave 47 cm, a dry one 5 cm. Most winters land between 22 cm and 78 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 34 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 53 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Charmey have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Charmey have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 27 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Charmey?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,618 m; the lowest starts at 871 m (747 m of vertical).

    Is Charmey suitable for families?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 6.5 km, are easy.

    How big is Charmey?

    About 12 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
