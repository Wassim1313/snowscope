    About Halde

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Halde one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. The slopes start at 776 m and reach 822 m at the top. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skilift Halde, 2.6 km to the north.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Halde gives 46 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 976 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.3 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 1.3 km (100%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 799 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for mid-December and late December. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 8 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 18 cm). Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 28 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Quick answers

    When does Halde have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    How much snow does Halde have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).

    How high is Halde?

    Its highest lift reaches 822 m; the lowest starts at 776 m (46 m of vertical).

    Is Halde good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Halde?

    Bläsiberg Wiesensteig, 5.9 km to the north, with about 4.0 km of pistes.
