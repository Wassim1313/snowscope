    The snow at Les Gets-Morzine, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Les Gets-Morzine (1,484 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 41 cm, with a normal range of 23 cm–58 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 87 cm (65 cm–105 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 91 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 22 % of winters, and by 1 January in 41 %.

    1981-82 was the big one (161 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (52 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to early May (148 days).

    Today's 30 cm compares with a usual 13 cm for 27 November.

    About Les Gets-Morzine

    Les Gets-Morzine is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 95 km of pistes put it at number 53 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 984 m to 1,985 m. The closest other ski area is Praz de Lys Sommand (8.8 km, west).

    Runs by difficulty

    The mountain at Les Gets-Morzine

    Skied top to bottom, Les Gets-Morzine gives 1,001 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 984 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 1 cable car, 3 gondolas, 24 chairlifts, 4 button lifts, 7 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 5 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Les Hauts Forts, 2,466 m high and 7.2 km east of the resort.

    Snow at Les Gets-Morzine through a normal winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,484 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 91 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 62 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 41 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 10 cm in a poor year to 71 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 120 cm.
