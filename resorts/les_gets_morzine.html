    About Les Gets-Morzine

    Les Gets-Morzine is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 95 km of pistes put it at number 53 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 984 m to 1,985 m. The closest other ski area is Praz de Lys Sommand (8.8 km, west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Les Gets-Morzine has 191 graded runs adding up to about 95 km. The largest share is blue, with 47 km (50%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at Les Gets-Morzine

    Skied top to bottom, Les Gets-Morzine gives 1,001 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 984 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 1 cable car, 3 gondolas, 23 chairlifts, 4 button lifts, 7 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 5 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Les Hauts Forts, 2,466 m high and 7.2 km east of the resort.

    Snow at Les Gets-Morzine through a normal winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,484 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 91 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 62 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 41 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 10 cm in a poor year to 71 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 49 cm and 120 cm.

    Les Gets-Morzine: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Les Gets-Morzine?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Les Gets-Morzine at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 41 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Les Gets-Morzine?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    What altitude is Les Gets-Morzine at?

    The top is at 1,985 m and the base at 984 m.

    Is Les Gets-Morzine suitable for families?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 55 km, are easy.
