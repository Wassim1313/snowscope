    What 46 winters say about Auron

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Auron (about 2,021 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 50 cm of cover (22 cm to 72 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 91 cm, between 62 cm and 120 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 92 cm.

    By 15 December 33 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 52 %.

    2013-14 was the big one (212 cm); 2006-07 the lean one (29 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to early May, about 153 days.

    Auron: quick answers

    Is there snow in Auron at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 50 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 52 % of 46 winters. Right now: 29 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Auron?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 92 cm). For a sure base, early December to early May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Auron?

    About 92 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-February); between 29 cm in the leanest winter (2006-07) and 212 cm in the biggest (2013-14).

    About Auron

    Around 62 km of marked runs place Auron among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 90 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,597 m up to 2,445 m. The closest other ski area is Valberg (16 km, south).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Auron gives 848 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,597 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,445 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
