    About Auron

    Around 62 km of marked runs place Auron among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 90 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,597 m up to 2,445 m. The closest other ski area is Valberg (16 km, south).

    The pistes of Auron

    Counting every graded piste, there are 64 runs and around 62 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (52%, about 32 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow Auron usually gets

    At about 2,021 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 December and lasts to 29 April: about 21 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 5 February, with about 92 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 50 cm (lean years 12 cm, snowy years 110 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 33 cm and 144 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 45 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Auron gives 848 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,597 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,445 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 2 cable cars, 1 gondola, 9 chairlifts, 3 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Mont Ténibre / Monte Ténibres (3,031 m), 7.9 km to the north-east, about 586 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Auron?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    Is there snow at Auron at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 50 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Auron open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 12 April 2027.

    How high is Auron?

    The top is at 2,445 m and the base at 1,597 m.

    Can beginners ski at Auron?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 34 km, are easy.
