    About Brot-Dessus

    Brot-Dessus has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,016 m up to 1,059 m. Sommartel / Le Locle is the next resort, 6.6 km away to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Brot-Dessus gives 43 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses, for one, climbs to 1,558 m. The only lift is a rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Le Soliat, 1,465 m high and 4.9 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,038 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 19 cm, around 16 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 51 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 8 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 39 cm).

    Quick answers

    When does Brot-Dessus have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Is there snow at Brot-Dessus at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Brot-Dessus?

    The top is at 1,059 m and the base at 1,016 m.

    How big is Brot-Dessus?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
