    About Zuoz

    With roughly 14 km of runs, Zuoz sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,711 m and 2,447 m above sea level. Müsella is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 28 marked runs cover roughly 14 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 6.8 km, or 49%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 2.8 km of black or expert runs. 0.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow at Zuoz through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,079 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 November and lasts to 26 May: about 26 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 50 cm (lean years 30 cm, snowy years 74 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 84 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 94 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 58 cm and 111 cm.

    The mountain at Zuoz

    Skied top to bottom, Zuoz gives 736 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,711 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Piz Kesch (3,418 m), 5.9 km to the west, about 971 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Zuoz

    What is the best month to ski at Zuoz?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Zuoz at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 50 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Zuoz at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,447 m; the lowest starts at 1,711 m (736 m of vertical).

    Is Zuoz good for beginners?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 6.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Zuoz?

    Livigno, 16 km to the south-east, with about 83 km of pistes.
