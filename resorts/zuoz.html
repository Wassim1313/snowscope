    About Zuoz

    With roughly 14 km of runs, Zuoz sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,711 m and 2,447 m above sea level. Müsella is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the south.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The mountain at Zuoz

    Skied top to bottom, Zuoz gives 736 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,711 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
