    About Skipark Frymburk

    Skipark Frymburk is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 752 m to 833 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ski areál Lipno-Kramolín, 4.6 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Skipark Frymburk through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 792 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 17 cm, around 25 January. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 8 cm; a snowy December gave 27 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 45 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early March.

    How Skipark Frymburk is laid out

    81 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 752 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Hochficht-Böhmerwald, reach 1,332 m. Its 4 lifts break down into 2 drag lifts and 2 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Questions about Skipark Frymburk

    When is the best time to ski at Skipark Frymburk?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Is there snow at Skipark Frymburk at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 8 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Skipark Frymburk?

    Its highest lift reaches 833 m; the lowest starts at 752 m (81 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Skipark Frymburk?

    Hochficht-Böhmerwald, 23 km to the west, with about 16 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Skipark Frymburk have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
