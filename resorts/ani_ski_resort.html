    About Ani

    Ani is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 104 of 385 in Japan by length. Skiing here happens between 542 m and 1,202 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ani Yuguchinai, 11 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Ani

    The vertical drop is 660 m: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 542 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,202 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
