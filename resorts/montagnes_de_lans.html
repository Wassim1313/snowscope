    About Montagnes de Lans

    Neither huge nor tiny, Montagnes de Lans offers about 23 km of pistes (number 269 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,374 m to 1,789 m. Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte is the next resort, 6.4 km away to the north.

    The pistes of Montagnes de Lans

    The piste map lists 44 graded runs, about 23 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 12 km (54%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,582 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to early April, about 14 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 29 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 136 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 13 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 145 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 70 cm, around 17 February.

    How Montagnes de Lans is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Montagnes de Lans gives 415 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,374 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 1,789 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 12 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Roc Cornafion (2,049 m), 5.6 km to the south, about 260 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Montagnes de Lans

    When does Montagnes de Lans have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    How much snow does Montagnes de Lans have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 29 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Montagnes de Lans?

    The top is at 1,789 m and the base at 1,374 m.

    Is Montagnes de Lans suitable for families?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Montagnes de Lans have?

    About 23 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
