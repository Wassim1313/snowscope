    About Montagnes de Lans

    Neither huge nor tiny, Montagnes de Lans offers about 23 km of pistes (number 269 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,374 m to 1,789 m. Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte is the next resort, 6.4 km away to the north.

    How Montagnes de Lans is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Montagnes de Lans gives 415 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,374 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 1,789 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
