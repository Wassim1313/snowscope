    About Debrník

    Neither huge nor tiny, Debrník offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 157 of 257 in Central Europe). Its lifts climb from 804 m to 895 m. Samoty is the next resort, 0.6 km away to the north.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 1.7 km north to Nad Nádražím, with about 4.0 km of runs. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 850 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 65 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 41 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 11 February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Debrník gives 91 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 804 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a button lift.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Debrník hold around 7 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (5 cm).

    Debrník: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Debrník?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Debrník at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Debrník?

    The top is at 895 m and the base at 804 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Debrník?

    Nad Nádražím, 1.7 km to the north, with about 4.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Debrník?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
