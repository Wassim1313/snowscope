    About Klimpfjällsbackarna

    Around 12 km of marked runs place Klimpfjällsbackarna among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 59 by length. Skiing here happens between 591 m and 808 m above sea level. Borgafjällbackarna is the next resort, 29 km away to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 12 km. The largest share is blue, with 4.0 km (33%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 700 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 10 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 79 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 95 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for early April and mid-April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 93 cm and 159 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 138 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 141 cm.

    How Klimpfjällsbackarna is laid out

    The vertical drop is 217 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 button lift and 2 drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Durrenpiken (1,286 m), 7.4 km to the north-west, about 478 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Klimpfjällsbackarna

    When is the best time to ski at Klimpfjällsbackarna?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at Klimpfjällsbackarna at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 79 cm deep.

    What altitude is Klimpfjällsbackarna at?

    Between 591 m and 808 m, so 217 m of vertical drop.

    Is Klimpfjällsbackarna good for beginners?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 8.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Klimpfjällsbackarna?

    About 12 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
