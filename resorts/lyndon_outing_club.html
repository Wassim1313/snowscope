    About Lyndon Outing Club

    Lyndon Outing Club is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 61 of 93 in US Northeast by length. Its lifts climb from 258 m to 379 m. Its nearest neighbour is Burke, 8.3 km north-east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Lyndon Outing Club

    121 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 258 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Cannon Mountain, for one, climbs to 1,231 m. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Kirby Mountain at 833 m, standing 454 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Lyndon Outing Club through a normal winter

    Count on about 9 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 318 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 39 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 17 cm to 66 cm in mid-February in most winters. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 44 cm.

    Lyndon Outing Club: common questions

    When does Lyndon Outing Club have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    How much snow does Lyndon Outing Club have at Christmas?

    What altitude is Lyndon Outing Club at?

    Its highest lift reaches 379 m; the lowest starts at 258 m (121 m of vertical).
