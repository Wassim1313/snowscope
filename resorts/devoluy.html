    The snow at Dévoluy, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Dévoluy (1,976 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 64 cm; half of the winters sit between 39 cm and 82 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 124 cm, between 94 cm and 140 cm in a normal winter.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 126 cm.

    46 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 72 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 2017-18, peaking at 217 cm. Worst: 1992-93, which never passed 59 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early December to mid-May (167 days).

    Common questions

    Can you ski Dévoluy at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 64 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 72 % of 46 winters. Right now: 38 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Dévoluy?

    February: the snow peaks at about 126 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to mid-May.

    How deep does the snow get at Dévoluy?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 126 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 217 cm (2017-18), the thinnest 59 cm (1992-93).

    How high do the lifts go at Dévoluy?

    The top is at 2,495 m and the base at 1,458 m.

    How big is Dévoluy?

    About 70 km of pistes and 23 lifts.

    About Dévoluy

    Dévoluy is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 70 km of pistes put it at number 79 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,458 m to 2,495 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lus la Jarjatte, 11 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Dévoluy

    From top to bottom that makes 1,037 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,458 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,495 m.
