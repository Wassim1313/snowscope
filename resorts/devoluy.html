    About Dévoluy

    Dévoluy is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 70 km of pistes put it at number 79 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,458 m to 2,495 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lus la Jarjatte, 11 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 101 runs and around 70 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 42 km (61%). With 64% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. 2.8 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Dévoluy

    From top to bottom that makes 1,037 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,458 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,495 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chair-gondola, 5 chairlifts, 13 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 3 moving carpets, 23 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pic de Bure at 2,709 m, standing 214 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Dévoluy usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,976 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 4 December and keeps it until about 14 May, roughly 23 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 64 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 100 cm, a dry one 17 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 9 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 65 cm and 161 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 126 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 84 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Dévoluy: common questions

    When does Dévoluy have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Dévoluy at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 64 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Dévoluy open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Dévoluy?

    The top is at 2,495 m and the base at 1,458 m.

    Is Dévoluy suitable for families?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 45 km, are easy.
