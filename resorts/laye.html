    About Laye

    Neither huge nor tiny, Laye offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 518 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,301 m and 1,831 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Chaillol, 9.9 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Laye

    Laye has 15 graded runs adding up to about 8.6 km. Most of the length is red (75%, about 6.4 km). Intermediate runs make up 75% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Snow at Laye through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,566 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 11 December and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 22 weeks. A normal season tops out near 93 cm, around 13 February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 42 cm and 130 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 81 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 50 cm (lean years 19 cm, snowy years 86 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Laye has roughly 3 cm of snow on the ground today. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 18 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 17 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Laye gives 530 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,301 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Orcières Merlette reaches 2,731 m. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 T-bar, 4 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Sommet de Raz de Bec, 2,385 m high and 6.6 km north-west of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Centre Oxygénation (hotel), Gîte forestier de Chaudun (chalet), Gîte de Chauvet (chalet) and Le Connétable (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Laye?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Laye at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 50 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Laye?

    Between 1,301 m and 1,831 m, so 530 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Laye best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 75% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Laye?

    Dévoluy, 13 km to the west, with about 70 km of pistes.
