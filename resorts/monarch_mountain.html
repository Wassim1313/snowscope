    About Monarch Mountain

    With roughly 37 km of runs, Monarch Mountain sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The slopes start at 3,260 m and reach 3,598 m at the top.

    The pistes of Monarch Mountain

    In all, 76 marked runs cover roughly 37 km. Most of the length is blue square (45%, about 16 km). With 35% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 13 km of black or expert runs.

    How Monarch Mountain is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Monarch Mountain gives 338 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 3,260 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Getting up the mountain: 6 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets, 9 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Mount Aetna (4,160 m), 8.0 km to the north, about 562 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Monarch Mountain usually gets

    At about 3,429 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 November and lasts to 22 May: about 25 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 61 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 20 cm, snowy years 117 cm). For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 134 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 43 cm and 171 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 115 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 12 weeks.

    Monarch Mountain: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Monarch Mountain?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Monarch Mountain have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 61 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Monarch Mountain?

    Between 3,260 m and 3,598 m, so 338 m of vertical drop.

    Is Monarch Mountain good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 13 km of its graded pistes (35%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Monarch Mountain have?

    About 37 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
