    About Ski alpin am Inselsberg

    Ski alpin am Inselsberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 122 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Its lifts climb from 719 m to 895 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skilift am Seimberg, 4.4 km south, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Ski alpin am Inselsberg has 5 graded runs adding up to about 2.2 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.4 km (63%). With 63% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Ski alpin am Inselsberg

    Skied top to bottom, Ski alpin am Inselsberg gives 176 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 919 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 807 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 24 cm. A normal season tops out near 11 cm, around 29 January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, with 24 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-December and early January.

    Questions about Ski alpin am Inselsberg

    What is the best month to ski at Ski alpin am Inselsberg?

    Aim for mid-December and early January.

    How much snow does Ski alpin am Inselsberg have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Ski alpin am Inselsberg?

    The top is at 895 m and the base at 719 m.

    Can beginners ski at Ski alpin am Inselsberg?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ski alpin am Inselsberg have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
