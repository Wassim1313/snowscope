    About Aillon-le-Jeune

    Compact and local, Aillon-le-Jeune has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 972 m and 1,153 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Aillon-Margériaz (6.2 km, north-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Aillon-le-Jeune has 2 graded runs adding up to about 1.1 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.1 km (100%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,062 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 December and lasts to 6 May: about 21 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 85 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 61 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 43 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 8 cm in a poor year to 99 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 26 cm and 130 cm.

    The mountain at Aillon-le-Jeune

    From top to bottom that makes 181 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,172 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Le Collet d'Allevard, reach 2,084 m. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pointe des Arlicots at 2,060 m, standing 907 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find D'Aillon et d'ailleurs (hotel), Centre de Vacances "Les Nivéoles" (hotel), Ferme de la Mense (chalet) and Hôtel du Soleil (hotel).

    Aillon-le-Jeune: common questions

    When does Aillon-le-Jeune have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Aillon-le-Jeune at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 43 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Aillon-le-Jeune?

    The top is at 1,153 m and the base at 972 m.

    Is Aillon-le-Jeune suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Aillon-le-Jeune?

    Aillon-Margériaz, 6.2 km to the north-west, with about 22 km of pistes.
