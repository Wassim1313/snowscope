    About Riihivuori Resort

    Riihivuori Resort is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 128 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 81 m to 197 m. The closest other ski area is Luoteis-Himos (31 km, south-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 13 graded runs, about 5.1 km in total. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 1.9 km, or 37%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    Snow at Riihivuori Resort through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 139 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 January and lasts to 18 April: about 15 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 24 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 37 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 27 cm to 58 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 47 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 33 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    The mountain at Riihivuori Resort

    Skied top to bottom, Riihivuori Resort gives 116 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Hiihtokeskus Himosvuori, for one, climbs to 230 m. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Nurmistonmäki (229 m), Heinoskorkee (228 m) and Sääksvuori (225 m).

    Questions about Riihivuori Resort

    What is the best month to ski at Riihivuori Resort?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Riihivuori Resort at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Riihivuori Resort at?

    The top is at 197 m and the base at 81 m.

    Can beginners ski at Riihivuori Resort?

    Yes: 45% of the graded pistes, about 2.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Riihivuori Resort have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
