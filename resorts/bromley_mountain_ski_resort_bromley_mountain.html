    About Bromley Mountain ;Bromley Mounta

    With roughly 23 km of runs, Bromley Mountain ;Bromley Mounta sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. The slopes start at 604 m and reach 997 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Stratton (13 km, south).

    How Bromley Mountain ;Bromley Mounta is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bromley Mountain ;Bromley Mounta gives 393 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 604 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 6 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet, 8 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Styles Peak (1,028 m), Bromley Mountain (1,000 m) and Mount Tabor (933 m).

    Snow at Bromley Mountain ;Bromley Mounta through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 800 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to late March, about 9 weeks of good cover. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 19 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 4 cm in a poor year to 40 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 14 cm and 77 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 45 cm.
