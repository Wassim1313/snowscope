    About Shibetsu Choei Kanayama

    Compact and local, Shibetsu Choei Kanayama has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 175 m up to 352 m. Unabetsu is the next resort, 22 km away to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 177 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 175 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 352 m is the highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
