    About Hörnle

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hörnle offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 675 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 911 m and reach 1,397 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Steckenberg, 5.4 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Hörnle through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,154 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 9 February and keeps it until about 21 April, roughly 8 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 32 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Year to year the snow is steady, with 17 cm to 58 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. A normal season tops out near 34 cm, around 31 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 22 cm, with 41 cm in the best years.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Hörnle gives 486 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 911 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar. Above the ski area rises Am Zahn (1,615 m), 7.9 km to the south, about 218 m higher than the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    What there is to ski at Hörnle

    Hörnle has 9 graded runs adding up to about 5.0 km. The largest share is blue, with 4.2 km (83%). With 83% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Quick answers

    When does Hörnle have the most snow?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early February to late April.

    Is there snow at Hörnle at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Hörnle?

    Between 911 m and 1,397 m, so 486 m of vertical drop.

    Is Hörnle good for beginners?

    Yes: 83% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    How big is Hörnle?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
