    About / Rodelgebiet Laubendorf

    / Rodelgebiet Laubendorf is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 334 m up to 363 m. Skilift Burgbernheim is the next resort, 32 km away to the west.

    Snow at / Rodelgebiet Laubendorf through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 348 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 3 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 0 cm and 11 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 5 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 0 cm, with 7 cm in the best years.

    The mountain at / Rodelgebiet Laubendorf

    Skied top to bottom, / Rodelgebiet Laubendorf gives 29 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 334 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a T-bar.
