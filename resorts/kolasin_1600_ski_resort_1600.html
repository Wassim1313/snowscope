    About Kolašin 1600

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Kolašin 1600 sits in the middle of the size table of the Balkans. Its lifts climb from 1,585 m to 2,028 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kolašin 1600, 0.3 km south-east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 7.2 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.3 km, or 60%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Kolašin 1600

    Skied top to bottom, Kolašin 1600 gives 443 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,585 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,028 m is the second highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a chairlift.
