    About Hochhäderich

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Hochhäderich sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,216 m and reach 1,499 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Hochlitten-Riefensberg (2.5 km, north).

    How much snow Hochhäderich usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,358 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 December and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 20 weeks. A normal season tops out near 60 cm, around 3 March. Most winters land between 30 cm and 90 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 30 cm; a snowy December gave 51 cm, a dry one 10 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 51 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    What there is to ski at Hochhäderich

    Counting every graded piste, there are 24 runs and around 9.1 km to ski. Most of the length is red (66%, about 6.0 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 2.0 km of difficult runs.

    How Hochhäderich is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Hochhäderich gives 283 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 7 lifts: 1 chairlift, 4 T-bars and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Hochgrat, 1,834 m high and 6.9 km east of the resort.

    How Hochhäderich compares nearby

    In this group of 7, Hochhäderich is number 3 for piste length. Its snow is the most reliable of the group: a 30 cm base lasts about 20 weeks in a typical winter, longer than at any neighbour listed. The highest nearby is Steibis, topping out at 1,704 m.

    Hochhäderich: common questions

    When does Hochhäderich have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Is there snow at Hochhäderich at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 30 cm deep.

    What altitude is Hochhäderich at?

    The top is at 1,499 m and the base at 1,216 m.

    What level of skier is Hochhäderich best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Hochhäderich?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
