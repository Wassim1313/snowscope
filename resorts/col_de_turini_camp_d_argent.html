    About Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent

    Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,741 m and 1,922 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is La Colmiane (17 km, north-west).

    How Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 30 km north-west to Isola 2000, with about 55 km of runs. Roubion-Les-Buisses has the longer snow season: around 16 weeks of solid cover, compared with 6 at Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent. Isola 2000 reaches 2,601 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The pistes of Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 1.4 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 63%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,832 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 January and keeps it until about 12 March, roughly 6 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 44 cm). Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 35 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 88 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 181 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,741 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. All 4 lifts here are button lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Cime du Diable, 2,685 m high and 6.8 km north of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Le Yéti (hotel), Gîte d'étape L'Estive du Mercantour (guesthouse), Les Trois Vallées (hotel) and Les Chamois (hotel).

    Questions about Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent

    What is the best month to ski at Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent?

    Between 1,741 m and 1,922 m, so 181 m of vertical drop.

    Is Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent good for beginners?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Col de Turini/​Camp d'Argent?

    Isola 2000, 30 km to the north-west, with about 55 km of pistes.
