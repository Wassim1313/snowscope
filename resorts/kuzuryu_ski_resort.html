    About Kuzuryu

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kuzuryu offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 204 of 385 in Japan). Skiing here happens between 420 m and 675 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Fukui Izumi (5.2 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Kuzuryu

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 3.9 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.7 km, or 69%. Intermediate runs make up 69% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Kuzuryu today: about 17 cm up high. The last seven days brought 37 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Sunday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 255 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 420 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Takasu Snow Park, for one, climbs to 1,520 m. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.

    Quick answers

    How high is Kuzuryu?

    The top is at 675 m and the base at 420 m.

    Who is Kuzuryu best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Kuzuryu?

    Takasu Snow Park, 22 km to the north-east, with about 17 km of pistes.

    How big is Kuzuryu?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
