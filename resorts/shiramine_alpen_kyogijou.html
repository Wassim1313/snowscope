    About Shiramine Alpen Kyogijou

    Shiramine Alpen Kyogijou is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 481 m up to 854 m. Its nearest neighbour is SAM Hakusan Ichirino, 11 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Shiramine Alpen Kyogijou is laid out

    373 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 481 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Takasu Snow Park reaches 1,520 m.
