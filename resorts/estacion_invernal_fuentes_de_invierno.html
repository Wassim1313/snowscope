    About Estación Invernal Fuentes de Inv

    About 9.0 km of pistes make Estación Invernal Fuentes de Inv one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Cantabrian Mountains (number 6). The slopes start at 1,493 m and reach 1,949 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Estación Invernal y de Montaña S (1.4 km, south).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 16 runs and around 9.2 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 3.6 km, or 39%. No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.4 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 456 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,493 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 1,949 m is the second highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Estación Invernal Fuentes de Inv: common questions

    What altitude is Estación Invernal Fuentes de Inv at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,949 m; the lowest starts at 1,493 m (456 m of vertical).

    Is Estación Invernal Fuentes de Inv suitable for families?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 4.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Estación Invernal Fuentes de Inv have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
