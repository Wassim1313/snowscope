    About Zinkenlifte

    Zinkenlifte is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 682 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 833 m and 1,315 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Rossfeld, 2.3 km to the south.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,074 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 25 December and lasts to 4 May: about 19 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 30 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 58 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 41 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 62 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 33 cm to 88 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 4.8 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.5 km (53%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Zinkenlifte is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 482 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The base at 833 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 2 T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Hoher Göll, 2,522 m high and 7.2 km south of the resort.

    Zinkenlifte: common questions

    When does Zinkenlifte have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Is there snow at Zinkenlifte at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 30 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Zinkenlifte?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,315 m; the lowest starts at 833 m (482 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Zinkenlifte?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    How big is Zinkenlifte?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
