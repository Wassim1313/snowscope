    About Salzstiegl

    Neither huge nor tiny, Salzstiegl offers about 10 km of pistes (number 498 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,293 m up to 1,705 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gaberl, 6.1 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Salzstiegl

    The piste map lists 17 graded runs, about 9.6 km in total. Most of the length is blue (50%, about 4.7 km). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Advanced skiers have about 1.3 km of black or expert runs.

    How Salzstiegl is laid out

    The vertical drop is 412 m: runs of a moderate length. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 6 lifts break down into 4 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Ameringkogel at 2,187 m, standing 482 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,499 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 January and lasts to 5 March: about 4 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 4 cm in a poor year to 46 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 5 cm to 51 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 35 cm.

    Salzstiegl: common questions

    When does Salzstiegl have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does Salzstiegl have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 18 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Salzstiegl?

    Between 1,293 m and 1,705 m, so 412 m of vertical drop.

    Is Salzstiegl suitable for families?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 4.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Salzstiegl have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
