    About Beaver Creek Resort (Arrowhead M

    Beaver Creek Resort ranks number 5 of 175 ski areas in US West by piste length, with about 167 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,259 m up to 3,484 m. The closest other ski area is Vail (14 km, east).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,225 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,259 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among the 6 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Breckenridge reaches 3,913 m.
