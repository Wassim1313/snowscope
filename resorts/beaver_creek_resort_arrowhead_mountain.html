    About Beaver Creek Resort (Arrowhead M

    Beaver Creek Resort ranks number 5 of 175 ski areas in US West by piste length, with about 167 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,259 m up to 3,484 m. The closest other ski area is Vail (14 km, east).

    The pistes of Beaver Creek Resort

    Counting every graded piste, there are 246 runs and around 167 km to ski. Most of the length is green circle (53%, about 88 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,225 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,259 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among the 6 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Breckenridge reaches 3,913 m. Its 25 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 15 chairlifts and 7 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 7 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Bachelor Gulch Mountain (2,914 m), Arrowhead Mountain (2,802 m) and Davos Peak (2,717 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,872 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 104 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 21 March, with about 131 cm. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 87 cm to 159 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 72 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 110 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    Beaver Creek Resort: common questions

    When does Beaver Creek Resort have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Beaver Creek Resort have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 72 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Beaver Creek Resort?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,484 m; the lowest starts at 2,259 m (1,225 m of vertical).

    Is Beaver Creek Resort good for beginners?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 88 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Beaver Creek Resort have?

    About 167 km of pistes and 25 lifts.
