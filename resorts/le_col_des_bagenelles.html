    About Le Col des Bagenelles

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Le Col des Bagenelles sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 874 m and reach 1,074 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Lac Blanc, 7.0 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 3.2 km to ski. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 53%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 974 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and early February. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 16 cm in a big one. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 22 cm. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 4 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Le Col des Bagenelles gives 200 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,074 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; La Bresse reaches 1,347 m. Above the ski area rises Grand Brézouard (1,229 m), 2.9 km to the east, about 155 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Le Col des Bagenelles include Auberge de la Canardière (hotel), Renaud Rausch (guesthouse), La Tête des Faux (hotel) and Hôtel de la Poste (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When does Le Col des Bagenelles have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and early February.

    Is there snow at Le Col des Bagenelles at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Le Col des Bagenelles?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,074 m; the lowest starts at 874 m (200 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Le Col des Bagenelles?

    Yes: 83% of the graded pistes, about 2.7 km, are easy.
