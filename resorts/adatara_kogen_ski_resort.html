    About Adatara Kogen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Adatara Kogen offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 230 of 385 in Japan). Its lifts climb from 950 m to 1,129 m. Its nearest neighbour is Nihonmatsu Shiozawa, 2.2 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 3.3 km in total. Beginner runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 50%. With 87% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    179 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,150 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Questions about Adatara Kogen

    How high do the lifts go at Adatara Kogen?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,129 m; the lowest starts at 950 m (179 m of vertical).

    Is Adatara Kogen good for beginners?

    Yes: 87% of the graded pistes, about 2.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Adatara Kogen?

    Numajiri Ski, 7.0 km to the west, with about 9.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Adatara Kogen?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
