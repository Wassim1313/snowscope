    About Tonami Yumenotaira Ski Ground

    Tonami Yumenotaira Ski Ground is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 310 m and reach 503 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ushidake Onsen, 3.8 km to the south-east.

    Snow this week

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Tonami Yumenotaira Ski Ground gives 193 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 310 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hida Nagareha Resort reaches 1,398 m.
