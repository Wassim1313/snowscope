    About Stade des Neiges du Barioz

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Stade des Neiges du Barioz one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,366 m to 1,693 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les 7 Laux, 8.2 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.9 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 0.8 km, or 43%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts.

    How Stade des Neiges du Barioz is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 327 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,366 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Rocher d'Arguille (2,885 m), 7.9 km to the south-east, about 1,192 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,530 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 20 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 67 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 48 cm to 120 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 42 cm; a snowy December gave 71 cm, a dry one 11 cm. A normal season tops out near 90 cm, around 27 February.
