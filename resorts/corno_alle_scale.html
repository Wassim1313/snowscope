    About Corno alle Scale

    Neither huge nor tiny, Corno alle Scale offers about 12 km of pistes (number 429 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,421 m up to 1,940 m. Its nearest neighbour is Doganaccia, 3.6 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Corno alle Scale has 20 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. The largest share is red, with 5.6 km (47%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.9 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Corno alle Scale gives 519 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,421 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 6 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Corno alle Scale (1,944 m), Punta Sofia (1,939 m) and Punta Giorgina (1,927 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,680 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 61 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 1 cm (snow lay here in 5 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 19 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 5 cm.

    Questions about Corno alle Scale

    When is the best time to ski at Corno alle Scale?

    Aim for mid-February and early March.

    Is there snow at Corno alle Scale at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 5 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Corno alle Scale at?

    Between 1,421 m and 1,940 m, so 519 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Corno alle Scale best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 47% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Corno alle Scale have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
