    About Corno alle Scale

    Neither huge nor tiny, Corno alle Scale offers about 12 km of pistes (number 429 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,421 m up to 1,940 m. Its nearest neighbour is Doganaccia, 3.6 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Corno alle Scale gives 519 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,421 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
