    About Skiareál Strážné

    Compact and local, Skiareál Strážné has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 675 m up to 779 m. The closest other ski area is Lyžařský vlek Popelka (0.1 km, north-west).

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Herlíkovice: about 10 km of pistes, 1.3 km to the south-west. Lyžařský vlek Popelka has the longer snow season: around 6 weeks of solid cover, compared with 3 at Skiareál Strážné. For higher slopes, Herlíkovice climbs to 1,005 m.

    The pistes of Skiareál Strážné

    The piste map lists 6 graded runs, about 1.3 km in total. Black runs make up the biggest part: 1.3 km, or 100%. With 100% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 1.3 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skiareál Strážné gives 104 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 675 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The only lift is a T-bar.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 727 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early February to late February, about 3 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 33 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 6 cm to 55 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 45 cm in the best years.

    Skiareál Strážné: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Skiareál Strážné?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    Can you ski at Skiareál Strážné at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Skiareál Strážné?

    Its highest lift reaches 779 m; the lowest starts at 675 m (104 m of vertical).

    Is Skiareál Strážné good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.3 km of its graded pistes (100%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skiareál Strážné?

    Herlíkovice, 1.3 km to the south-west, with about 10 km of pistes.
