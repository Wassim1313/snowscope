    What 46 winters say about Valberg

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Valberg (about 1,713 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 40 cm; half of the winters sit between 15 cm and 65 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 82 cm; the middle half of winters range from 40 cm to 113 cm.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 89 cm.

    By 15 December 30 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 41 %.

    2012-13 was the big one (236 cm); 2006-07 the lean one (25 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until late April in a normal winter, roughly 143 days.

    Questions about Valberg

    Does Valberg have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 40 cm, and only 41 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is no snow.

    Which month has the most snow at Valberg?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 89 cm). For a sure base, early December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Valberg?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 89 cm in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 236 cm (2012-13), the thinnest 25 cm (2006-07).

    How high do the lifts go at Valberg?

    The top is at 2,012 m and the base at 1,414 m.

    How big is Valberg?

    About 54 km of pistes and 25 lifts.
