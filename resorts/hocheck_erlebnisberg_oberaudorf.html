    Snow this week

    Up high, Hocheck Erlebnisberg Oberaudorf has roughly 2 cm of snow on the ground today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 6 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    358 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 491 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 4 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Steilnerjoch at 1,747 m, standing 898 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 670 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 31 cm). A normal season tops out near 27 cm, around 16 February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 7 cm to 49 cm.

    Questions about Hocheck Erlebnisberg Oberaudorf

    What is the best month to ski at Hocheck Erlebnisberg Oberaudorf?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Hocheck Erlebnisberg Oberaudorf at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Hocheck Erlebnisberg Oberaudorf?

    Its highest lift reaches 849 m; the lowest starts at 491 m (358 m of vertical).

    Who is Hocheck Erlebnisberg Oberaudorf best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 80% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Hocheck Erlebnisberg Oberaudorf have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
