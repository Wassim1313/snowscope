    About Grindelwald

    Grindelwald is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 88 km of pistes put it at number 59 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 938 m to 2,394 m. Grindelwald is the next resort, 2.2 km away to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 103 marked runs cover roughly 88 km. Most of the length is red (44%, about 39 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 12 km of difficult runs. 2.1 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Grindelwald is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,456 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,138 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 27 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 8 mountain railways, 3 gondolas, 9 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 3 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Jungfrau, 4,158 m high and 6.8 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,666 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 December and lasts to 12 May: about 21 weeks. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 66 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 47 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 42 cm and 86 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm; a snowy December gave 46 cm, a dry one 16 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Bellevue (hotel), Desalpes (hotel), Berggasthaus Stöcki Grindelwaldblick (mountain hut) and Beausite Park (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Grindelwald?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Grindelwald at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Grindelwald open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 21 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 25 April 2027.

    How high is Grindelwald?

    The top is at 2,394 m and the base at 938 m.

    Who is Grindelwald best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 44% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
