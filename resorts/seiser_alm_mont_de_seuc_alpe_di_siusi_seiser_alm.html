    The snow at Seiser Alm, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Seiser Alm (1,721 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 17 cm, with a normal range of 9 cm–27 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 28 cm, between 21 cm and 38 cm in a normal winter.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 29 cm.

    4 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 2 % by 1 January.

    2013-14 was the big one (90 cm); 2016-17 the lean one (14 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to mid-April, about 117 days.

    Seiser Alm: quick answers

    Can you ski Seiser Alm at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 17 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 2 % of 46 winters. Right now: 35 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Seiser Alm?

    February: the snow peaks at about 29 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to mid-April.

    How snowy is Seiser Alm?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 29 cm in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 90 cm (2013-14), the thinnest 14 cm (2016-17).

    What altitude is Seiser Alm at?

    The top is at 2,221 m and the base at 1,221 m.

    How many km of pistes does Seiser Alm have?

    About 60 km of pistes and 27 lifts.
