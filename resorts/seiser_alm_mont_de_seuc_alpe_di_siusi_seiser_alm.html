    About Seiser Alm

    With some 60 km of runs, Seiser Alm belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,221 m and 2,221 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Val Gardena, 8.1 km to the east.

    The pistes of Seiser Alm

    Seiser Alm has 90 graded runs adding up to about 60 km. Most of the length is red (60%, about 36 km). Intermediate runs make up 60% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.1 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,000 m: enough height for proper long runs. Its 27 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 15 chairlifts, 5 button lifts and 4 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Saslonch - Langkofel - Sassolungo at 3,181 m, standing 960 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,721 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 29 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 17 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Most winters land between 13 cm and 50 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 17 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 36 cm). For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Quick answers

    When does Seiser Alm have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Seiser Alm at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 17 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Seiser Alm?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Seiser Alm at?

    The top is at 2,221 m and the base at 1,221 m.

    Who is Seiser Alm best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 60% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
