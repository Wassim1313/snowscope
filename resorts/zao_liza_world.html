    About Zao Liza World

    Neither huge nor tiny, Zao Liza World offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 229 of 385 in Japan). The slopes start at 1,071 m and reach 1,446 m at the top. Zao Sarukura is the next resort, 2.9 km away to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 3.6 km to ski. Green runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 47%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Zao Liza World gives 375 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.

    How Zao Liza World compares nearby

    For more terrain, Zao Onsen (4.0 km north) has about 39 km of pistes against 4.0 km here. For higher slopes, Zao Onsen climbs to 1,661 m.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Zao Liza World at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,446 m; the lowest starts at 1,071 m (375 m of vertical).

    Is Zao Liza World good for beginners?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 2.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Zao Liza World?

    Zao Onsen, 4.0 km to the north, with about 39 km of pistes.

    How big is Zao Liza World?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
