    About Slavske

    With some 5.0 km of runs, Slavske belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 769 m and reach 1,082 m at the top. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Slavske, 2.1 km to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 13 graded runs, about 4.7 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 3.1 km (67%). With 67% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Slavske is laid out

    The vertical drop is 313 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 769 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift.
