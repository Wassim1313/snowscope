    How Planberg- & Wiesenlifte Pertisau is laid out

    The vertical drop is 73 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 942 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Spieljoch reaches 2,049 m. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Sonnjoch (2,458 m), 7.6 km to the west, about 1,443 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 978 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 14 cm and 44 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 17 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 16 cm, with 32 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 28 cm.
