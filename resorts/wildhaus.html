    About Wildhaus

    With some 31 km of runs, Wildhaus belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,016 m to 2,070 m. Chäserrugg-Alt St. Johann is the next resort, 3.1 km away to the west.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,543 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 41 cm; a snowy December gave 61 cm, a dry one 14 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 75 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 50 cm and 112 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 87 cm, around 6 March. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    What there is to ski at Wildhaus

    The piste map lists 33 graded runs, about 31 km in total. The largest share is red, with 13 km (42%). Steep ground is the speciality: 43% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.0 km. 10 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Wildhaus is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Wildhaus gives 1,054 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,070 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Säntis, 2,502 m high and 7.4 km north of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    Wildhaus: common questions

    When does Wildhaus have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late May.

    How much snow does Wildhaus have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 41 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Wildhaus?

    The top is at 2,070 m and the base at 1,016 m.

    Is Wildhaus good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 13 km of its graded pistes (43%) are difficult.

    How big is Wildhaus?

    About 31 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
