    About Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine

    Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 163 km of pistes put it at number 23 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,124 m and 3,306 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Les Deux Alpes, 13 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 2,182 m of vertical, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.

    Up high, Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine has roughly 86 cm of snow on the ground today. 22 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 84 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.
