    About Sonnenkopf

    Sonnenkopf is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 31 km of pistes put it at number 199 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 998 m to 2,287 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kristberg-Silbertal, 5.9 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Sonnenkopf

    Sonnenkopf has 50 graded runs adding up to about 31 km. Most of the length is red (58%, about 18 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Sonnenkopf

    Skied top to bottom, Sonnenkopf gives 1,289 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 998 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Pflunspitzen (2,912 m), 6.6 km to the south-east, about 625 m higher than the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Ski Arlberg: about 272 km of pistes, 11 km to the north-east. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 4 of 7). The highest nearby is Ski Arlberg, topping out at 2,642 m.

    Snow at Sonnenkopf through a normal winter

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,642 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 147 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 92 cm and 187 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 117 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For about 13 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 70 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 100 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March.

    Sonnenkopf: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Sonnenkopf?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Sonnenkopf at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 70 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Sonnenkopf?

    The top is at 2,287 m and the base at 998 m.

    Is Sonnenkopf good for beginners?

    Yes: 40% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sonnenkopf?

    Ski Arlberg, 11 km to the north-east, with about 272 km of pistes.
