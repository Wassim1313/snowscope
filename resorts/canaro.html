    Canaro in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Canaro (about 2,108 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 34 cm of cover (21 cm to 55 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 73 cm; the middle half of winters range from 52 cm to 97 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 78 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    24 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 37 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 2004-05, peaking at 156 cm. Worst: 1989-90, which never passed 33 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to early May (140 days).

    Today's 7 cm compares with a usual 10 cm for 27 November.

    About Canaro

    Canaro is a mid-sized ski area with about 27 km of pistes, number 21 of 49 in the Pyrenees by length. Its lifts climb from 1,716 m to 2,500 m. Its nearest neighbour is GrandValira, 3.4 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,108 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. Most winters land between 28 cm and 116 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 78 cm, around 23 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm, from 9 cm in a poor year to 72 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 53 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    The mountain at Canaro

    784 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,716 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 24 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 5 drag lifts, 5 rope tows and 7 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 7 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pic de l'Estanyó at 2,915 m, standing 415 m above the top lift.
