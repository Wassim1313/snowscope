    About Hakuba Norikura Onsen

    With some 11 km of runs, Hakuba Norikura Onsen belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. The slopes start at 809 m and reach 1,278 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hakuba Cortina, 1.9 km to the north-east.

    Hakuba Norikura Onsen today and the next days

    Today there are about 34 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Hakuba Norikura Onsen. The last seven days brought 64 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 17 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Hakuba Norikura Onsen gives 469 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 809 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.
