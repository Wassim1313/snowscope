    How much snow Mahdlift Neuleutasch usually gets

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,250 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. A normal season tops out near 52 cm, around 28 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 32 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Most winters land between 30 cm and 71 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 27 cm, with 46 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February.

    The mountain at Mahdlift Neuleutasch

    55 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Leutascher Dreitorspitze at 2,682 m, standing 1,405 m above the top lift.

    Mahdlift Neuleutasch: common questions

    When does Mahdlift Neuleutasch have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Mahdlift Neuleutasch at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 27 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Mahdlift Neuleutasch?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,277 m; the lowest starts at 1,222 m (55 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Mahdlift Neuleutasch have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
