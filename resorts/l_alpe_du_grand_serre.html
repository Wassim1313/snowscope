    L'Alpe du Grand Serre in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of L'Alpe du Grand Serre (about 1,764 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 80 cm, with a normal range of 47 cm–102 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 146 cm; the middle half of winters range from 112 cm to 164 cm.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 151 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 57 % of winters, and by 1 January in 76 %.

    Best winter on record: 1985-86, peaking at 271 cm. Worst: 1992-93, which never passed 68 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 168 days.

    Common questions

    Can you ski L'Alpe du Grand Serre at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 80 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 76 % of 46 winters. Right now: 35 cm.

    When is the best month to ski L'Alpe du Grand Serre?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 151 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at L'Alpe du Grand Serre?

    About 151 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 68 cm in the leanest winter (1992-93) and 271 cm in the biggest (1985-86).

    What altitude is L'Alpe du Grand Serre at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,166 m; the lowest starts at 1,363 m (803 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does L'Alpe du Grand Serre have?

    About 37 km of pistes and 14 lifts.

    About L'Alpe du Grand Serre

    With some 37 km of runs, L'Alpe du Grand Serre belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,363 m to 2,166 m. The closest other ski area is Domaine Alpin du Col d'Ornon (10 km, east).

    Lifts and terrain

    803 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,363 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
