    About L'Alpe du Grand Serre

    With some 37 km of runs, L'Alpe du Grand Serre belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,363 m to 2,166 m. The closest other ski area is Domaine Alpin du Col d'Ornon (10 km, east).

    Lifts and terrain

    803 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,363 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 14 cm of snow on the upper slopes of L'Alpe du Grand Serre. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 45 cm by Saturday.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Gîte du Chardon Bleu (chalet), Grizzli III (guesthouse), Gite de l'Alpe du Grand Serre (mountain hut) and Le Mas (chalet).

    Quick answers

    What altitude is L'Alpe du Grand Serre at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,166 m; the lowest starts at 1,363 m (803 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does L'Alpe du Grand Serre have?

    About 37 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
