    About L'Alpe du Grand Serre

    With some 37 km of runs, L'Alpe du Grand Serre belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,363 m to 2,166 m. The closest other ski area is Domaine Alpin du Col d'Ornon (10 km, east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 52 graded runs, about 37 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 20 km, or 55%. With 62% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Snow at L'Alpe du Grand Serre through a normal winter

    At about 1,764 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 3 December and lasts to 16 May: about 24 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 151 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 15 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 80 cm to 187 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 80 cm, from 19 cm in a poor year to 117 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 106 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Lifts and terrain

    803 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,363 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 14 lifts: 3 chairlifts and 11 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Le Taillefer at 2,857 m, standing 691 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Gîte du Chardon Bleu (chalet), Grizzli III (guesthouse), Gite de l'Alpe du Grand Serre (mountain hut) and Le Mas (chalet).

    Quick answers

    When does L'Alpe du Grand Serre have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at L'Alpe du Grand Serre at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 80 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is L'Alpe du Grand Serre at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,166 m; the lowest starts at 1,363 m (803 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at L'Alpe du Grand Serre?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 23 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does L'Alpe du Grand Serre have?

    About 37 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
