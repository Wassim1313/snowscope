    What there is to ski at Kinderskiparadies La Nars

    Kinderskiparadies La Nars has 8 graded runs adding up to about 4.2 km. Most of the length is blue (90%, about 3.8 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Kinderskiparadies La Nars is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Kinderskiparadies La Nars gives 740 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 rope tows and 4 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Piz Arblatsch, 3,203 m high and 6.4 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,541 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 December and lasts to 22 April: about 17 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 47 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 55 cm, around 12 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 32 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 34 cm and 70 cm.

    Kinderskiparadies La Nars: common questions

    When does Kinderskiparadies La Nars have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Kinderskiparadies La Nars have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 31 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Kinderskiparadies La Nars at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,911 m; the lowest starts at 1,171 m (740 m of vertical).

    Is Kinderskiparadies La Nars suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Kinderskiparadies La Nars have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
