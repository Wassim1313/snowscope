    About Kimberley Alpine Resort

    Kimberley Alpine Resort is one of the bigger ski areas of Canada West: about 60 km of pistes put it at number 12 of 53. Its lifts climb from 1,235 m to 1,904 m.

    The pistes of Kimberley Alpine Resort

    The piste map lists 70 graded runs, about 60 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (36%, about 22 km). Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 19 km of difficult runs.

    Snow at Kimberley Alpine Resort through a normal winter

    At about 1,570 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 November and lasts to 13 May: about 25 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 62 cm to 114 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 76 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 59 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 90 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 9 March, with about 89 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    669 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Its 5 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Sullivan Hill (1,893 m) and North Star Hill (1,787 m).

    Kimberley Alpine Resort: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Kimberley Alpine Resort?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Kimberley Alpine Resort at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 59 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Kimberley Alpine Resort open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026.

    How high is Kimberley Alpine Resort?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,904 m; the lowest starts at 1,235 m (669 m of vertical).

    Is Kimberley Alpine Resort good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 19 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.
