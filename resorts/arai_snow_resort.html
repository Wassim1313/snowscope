    About ARAI SNOW RESORT

    ARAI SNOW RESORT is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 13 km of pistes put it at number 38 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 369 m up to 1,278 m. Charmant Hiuchi Snow Resort is the next resort, 7.3 km away to the west.

    The pistes of ARAI SNOW RESORT

    In all, 12 marked runs cover roughly 13 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 6.8 km, or 53%. With 53% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How ARAI SNOW RESORT is laid out

    The vertical drop is 909 m: enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 369 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 4 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.
