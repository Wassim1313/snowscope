    About ARAI SNOW RESORT

    ARAI SNOW RESORT is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 13 km of pistes put it at number 38 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 369 m up to 1,278 m. Charmant Hiuchi Snow Resort is the next resort, 7.3 km away to the west.

    How ARAI SNOW RESORT is laid out

    The vertical drop is 909 m: enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 369 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.
