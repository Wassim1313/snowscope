    About Hakuba Happo One

    Only 6 of the 385 ski areas of Japan are larger than Hakuba Happo One, which has some 30 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 751 m to 1,827 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hakuba Iwatake, 2.9 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Hakuba Happo One

    The piste map lists 43 graded runs, about 30 km in total. Green runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 49%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 5.6 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Hakuba Happo One

    1,076 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 751 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 22 lifts: 1 gondola and 21 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    Questions about Hakuba Happo One

    How high do the lifts go at Hakuba Happo One?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,827 m; the lowest starts at 751 m (1,076 m of vertical).

    Is Hakuba Happo One suitable for families?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    How big is Hakuba Happo One?

    About 30 km of pistes and 22 lifts.
