    Les Portes du Mont-Blanc in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Les Portes du Mont-Blanc (about 1,478 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 64 cm of cover (42 cm to 91 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 122 cm (105 cm–144 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 123 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 50 % of winters, and by 1 January in 78 %.

    1998-99 was the big one (204 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (97 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late May, about 179 days.

    Les Portes du Mont-Blanc: quick answers

    Can you ski Les Portes du Mont-Blanc at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 64 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 78 % of 46 winters. Right now: 57 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Les Portes du Mont-Blanc?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 123 cm). For a sure base, late November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Les Portes du Mont-Blanc?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 123 cm in mid-February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 204 cm (1998-99), the thinnest 97 cm (2022-23).

    What altitude is Les Portes du Mont-Blanc at?

    The top is at 1,925 m and the base at 1,031 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Les Portes du Mont-Blanc?

    Evasion Mont Blanc, 7.1 km to the south-east, with about 293 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Les Portes du Mont-Blanc have?

    About 86 km of pistes and 24 lifts.

    About Les Portes du Mont-Blanc

    Les Portes du Mont-Blanc is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 86 km of pistes put it at number 62 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,031 m and reach 1,925 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Megève, 7.0 km to the south-east.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Les Portes du Mont-Blanc has roughly 57 cm of snow on the ground today. 10 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 73 cm by Tuesday.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Evasion Mont Blanc (7.1 km south-east) has about 293 km of pistes against 86 km here. For higher slopes, La Clusaz climbs to 2,476 m.
