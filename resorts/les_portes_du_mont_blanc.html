    Les Portes du Mont-Blanc: quick answers

    Most years, yes: around 64 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 78 % of 46 winters. Right now: 32 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Les Portes du Mont-Blanc?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 123 cm). For a sure base, late November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Les Portes du Mont-Blanc?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 123 cm in mid-February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 204 cm (1998-99), the thinnest 97 cm (2022-23).

    When does the ski season start at Les Portes du Mont-Blanc?

    What altitude is Les Portes du Mont-Blanc at?

    The top is at 1,925 m and the base at 1,031 m.

    Is Les Portes du Mont-Blanc good for beginners?

    About Les Portes du Mont-Blanc

    Les Portes du Mont-Blanc is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 86 km of pistes put it at number 62 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,031 m and reach 1,925 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Megève, 7.0 km to the south-east.

    The pistes of Les Portes du Mont-Blanc

    In all, 145 marked runs cover roughly 85 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 35 km, or 42%. No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. Advanced skiers have about 8.6 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Les Portes du Mont-Blanc usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,478 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 123 cm. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 64 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 24 cm, snowy years 105 cm). Most winters land between 76 cm and 162 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 94 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Les Portes du Mont-Blanc has roughly 32 cm of snow on the ground today. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 65 cm by Tuesday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 26 cm at mid-mountain). The 2026/27 season at Les Portes du Mont-Blanc is announced to start on 19 December 2026 and to run until 4 April 2027.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    894 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 6 button lifts, 7 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is La Grande Balmaz at 2,616 m, standing 691 m above the top lift.
