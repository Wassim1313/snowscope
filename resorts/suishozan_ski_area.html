    About Suishozan

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Suishozan one of the smaller places to ski in Japan. Skiing here happens between 268 m and 423 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Hanawa, 5.7 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Suishozan compares nearby

    The mountain at Suishozan

    The vertical drop is 155 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 268 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Appi Kogen, for one, climbs to 1,269 m. The only lift is a chairlift.
