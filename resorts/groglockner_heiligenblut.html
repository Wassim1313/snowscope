    About Großglockner Heiligenblut

    Around 24 km of marked runs place Großglockner Heiligenblut among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 255 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,235 m up to 2,905 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mölltaler Gletscher, 12 km to the east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Großglockner Heiligenblut gives 1,670 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,905 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
