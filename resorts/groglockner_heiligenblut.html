    About Großglockner Heiligenblut

    Around 24 km of marked runs place Großglockner Heiligenblut among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 254 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,235 m up to 2,905 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mölltaler Gletscher, 12 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Großglockner Heiligenblut has 32 graded runs adding up to about 24 km. Most of the length is red (58%, about 14 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Großglockner Heiligenblut gives 1,670 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,905 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 3 cable cars, 3 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Hocharn (3,254 m), 6.4 km to the east, about 349 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Großglockner Heiligenblut today: about 65 cm up high. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 54 cm by now.

    Snow at Großglockner Heiligenblut through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,070 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 11 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 78 cm; a snowy December gave 113 cm, a dry one 42 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 25 March, with about 155 cm. Most winters land between 92 cm and 179 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 137 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Großglockner Heiligenblut: common questions

    When does Großglockner Heiligenblut have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Großglockner Heiligenblut at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 78 cm deep.

    How high is Großglockner Heiligenblut?

    The top is at 2,905 m and the base at 1,235 m.

    Is Großglockner Heiligenblut good for beginners?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 10.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Großglockner Heiligenblut have?

    About 24 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
