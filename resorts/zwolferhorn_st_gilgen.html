    About Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen)

    Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen) is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 556 m to 1,470 m. The closest other ski area is Schmiedhornlift (8.8 km, west).

    From top to bottom that makes 914 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. At 556 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
