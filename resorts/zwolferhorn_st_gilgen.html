    About Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen)

    Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen) is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 556 m to 1,470 m. The closest other ski area is Schmiedhornlift (8.8 km, west).

    The mountain at Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen)

    From top to bottom that makes 914 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. At 556 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The only lift is a gondola. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Above the ski area rises Schafberg (1,783 m), 5.4 km to the east, about 313 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen) through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,013 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 27 January and keeps it until about 2 April, roughly 10 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 44 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 19 cm to 57 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 29 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 3 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one.

    Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen): common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen)?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to early April.

    Is there snow at Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen) at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen)?

    Between 556 m and 1,470 m, so 914 m of vertical drop.
