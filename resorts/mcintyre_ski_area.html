    About McIntyre

    McIntyre is a small ski area by the standards of US Northeast, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 107 m to 156 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pats Peak, 34 km to the north-west.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Pats Peak (34 km north-west) has about 14 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. The highest nearby is Crotched Mountain Ski & Ride, topping out at 584 m.

    What there is to ski at McIntyre

    McIntyre has 13 graded runs adding up to about 1.8 km. The largest share is blue square, with 1.0 km (55%). Intermediate runs make up 55% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 132 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 9 February, with about 15 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 6 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 23 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 50 cm.

    The mountain at McIntyre

    49 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 107 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Tower Hill (209 m), Mount Miner (171 m) and Campbell Hill (169 m).

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of McIntyre include Blue Door Bungalow (hotel), Ash Street Inn (guesthouse), Tru (hotel) and Fairfield Inn & Suites Hooksett (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at McIntyre?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Can you ski at McIntyre at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 6 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at McIntyre?

    Its highest lift reaches 156 m; the lowest starts at 107 m (49 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at McIntyre?

    Yes: 45% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near McIntyre?

    Pats Peak, 34 km to the north-west, with about 14 km of pistes.
