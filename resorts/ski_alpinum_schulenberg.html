    The mountain at Ski-Alpinum Schulenberg

    152 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 478 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Wintersportgebiet Wurmberg reaches 968 m.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Ski-Alpinum Schulenberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 630 m; the lowest starts at 478 m (152 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Ski-Alpinum Schulenberg?

    Matthias-Schmidt-Berg, 17 km to the south-east, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Ski-Alpinum Schulenberg?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
