    About Kolåsen

    Kolåsen has about 0.2 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 471 m up to 511 m. The closest other ski area is Huså (30 km, south).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 491 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 November and lasts to 19 May: about 25 weeks. For about 4 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 62 cm to 119 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 97 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 28 March, with about 101 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 53 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 75 cm in the best years.

    This week's snow

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 40 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 4 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Åre reaches 1,318 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gudmundtjärnhöjden at 633 m, standing 122 m above the top lift.

    Kolåsen: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Kolåsen?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Kolåsen at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 53 cm deep.

    What altitude is Kolåsen at?

    Between 471 m and 511 m, so 40 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Kolåsen?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
