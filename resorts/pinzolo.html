    About Pinzolo

    With roughly 21 km of runs, Pinzolo sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 850 m to 2,097 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta, 8.0 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 32 graded runs, about 20 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 10 km, or 52%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 4.8 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Pinzolo

    1,247 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,050 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 13 lifts break down into 5 gondolas, 6 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Cima Tosa at 3,136 m, standing 1,039 m above the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta: about 57 km of pistes, 8.0 km to the north-east. For more reliable snow, Ponte di Legno usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 23 weeks, against 7 here. The highest nearby is Ponte di Legno, topping out at 2,993 m.

    The snow right now

    Snow at Pinzolo through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,474 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to mid-March, about 7 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 19 cm to 57 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 22 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 42 cm). Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 36 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 20 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Pinzolo?

    Aim for early January and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Pinzolo at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Pinzolo?

    Between 850 m and 2,097 m, so 1,247 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Pinzolo best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Pinzolo?

    Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta, 8.0 km to the north-east, with about 57 km of pistes.
