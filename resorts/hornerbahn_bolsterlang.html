    About Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang

    Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 490 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 886 m and reach 1,662 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Grasgehren, 3.7 km to the south-west.

    Snow at Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,274 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 December and keeps it until about 7 May, roughly 20 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 69 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 35 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 58 cm, a dry one 12 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 46 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 36 cm and 104 cm.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 27 runs and around 9.5 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (68%, about 6.5 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang

    Skied top to bottom, Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang gives 776 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 886 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,662 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 6 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Riedberger Horn (1,787 m), 4.5 km to the west, about 125 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang

    When does Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 35 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang at?

    The top is at 1,662 m and the base at 886 m.

    Is Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang good for beginners?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 6.5 km, are easy.

    Can Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang make its own snow?

    Yes, on about 6.4 km of its pistes (67%).
