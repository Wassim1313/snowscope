    About Ushidake Onsen

    Ushidake Onsen is a ski area. The slopes start at 334 m and reach 651 m at the top. Tonami Yumenotaira Ski Ground is the next resort, 3.8 km away to the north-west.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Ushidake Onsen hold around 11 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 34 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 11 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.

    Lifts and terrain

    317 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 334 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.
