    About Großeck-Speiereck

    Around 28 km of marked runs place Großeck-Speiereck among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 214 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,080 m up to 2,338 m. Petersbründllift St. Michael is the next resort, 4.5 km away to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,258 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,338 m. Its 11 lifts break down into 2 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Schareck, 2,466 m high and 5.8 km north-west of the resort.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 52 runs and around 28 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (69%, about 19 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Großeck-Speiereck through a normal winter

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,709 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. A normal season tops out near 66 cm, around 27 February. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 53 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Most winters land between 37 cm and 81 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 34 cm (lean years 14 cm, snowy years 57 cm).

    Quick answers

    When does Großeck-Speiereck have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Großeck-Speiereck have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 34 cm deep.

    How high is Großeck-Speiereck?

    The top is at 2,338 m and the base at 1,080 m.

    Can beginners ski at Großeck-Speiereck?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 19 km, are easy.

    How big is Großeck-Speiereck?

    About 28 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
