    About Adlafquén

    Adlafquén is a ski area. The slopes start at 1,146 m and reach 1,345 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Parque de Nieve Batea Mahuida (6.8 km, east).

    The mountain at Adlafquén

    Skied top to bottom, Adlafquén gives 199 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Las Araucarias (Formerly Llaima), for one, climbs to 1,910 m.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Adlafquén?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,345 m; the lowest starts at 1,146 m (199 m of vertical).
