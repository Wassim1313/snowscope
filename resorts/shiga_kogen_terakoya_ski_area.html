    About Shiga Kogen Terakoya

    Shiga Kogen Terakoya is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,908 m up to 2,060 m. Shiga Kogen Higashidateyama is the next resort, 1.1 km away to the south-west.

    How Shiga Kogen Terakoya is laid out

    The vertical drop is 152 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,908 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 1.8 km north-west to Shiga Kogen Ichinose Diamond, with about 11 km of runs.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Shiga Kogen Terakoya at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,060 m; the lowest starts at 1,908 m (152 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Shiga Kogen Terakoya have?
