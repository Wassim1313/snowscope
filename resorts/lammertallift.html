    About Lammertallift

    Compact and local, Lammertallift has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 904 m and 1,073 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is St. Martin im Tennengebirge, 3.7 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Dachstein West: about 39 km of pistes, 9.4 km to the north-east. Bergbahnen Werfenweng reaches 1,828 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Lammertallift hold around 15 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 5 cm of new snow is expected by Friday.

    Lifts and terrain

    169 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,104 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Flachau, for one, climbs to 1,977 m.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Berghotel Lämmerhof (hotel), Ferienhaus Lämmerhof (chalet), Lammertaler Hof (hostel) and Spießalm (mountain hut).
