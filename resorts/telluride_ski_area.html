    About Telluride

    Telluride is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 101 km of pistes put it at number 14 of 175. The slopes start at 2,662 m and reach 3,812 m at the top. Silverton Mountain is the next resort, 16 km away to the east.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,150 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 2,662 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 20 lifts break down into 1 funicular, 3 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 11 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Dallas Peak, 4,168 m high and 7.7 km north of the resort.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 160 runs and around 101 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 42 km, or 42%. Good skiers will like it here; 32% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 33 km.

    How much snow Telluride usually gets

    At about 3,237 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 November and lasts to 13 May: about 25 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 117 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 147 cm. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 79 cm and 180 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 84 cm, with 133 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March.

    Questions about Telluride

    What is the best month to ski at Telluride?

    Aim for early January and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Telluride at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 84 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Telluride?

    The announced opening day is 26 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Telluride at?

    Between 2,662 m and 3,812 m, so 1,150 m of vertical drop.

    Does Telluride have steep runs?

    Yes: about 33 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.
