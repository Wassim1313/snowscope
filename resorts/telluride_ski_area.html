    Telluride in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Telluride (about 10,620 ft (3,237 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 33 in (84 cm), with a normal range of 21 in (53 cm)–45 in (114 cm).

    Mid-February typically sees 49 in (125 cm), between 36 in (92 cm) and 60 in (153 cm) in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 58 in (147 cm).

    72 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 80 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1983-84, peaking at 101 in (256 cm). Worst: 2025-26, which never passed 30 in (76 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs mid-November to mid-May (189 days).

    Telluride: quick answers

    Is there snow in Telluride at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 33 in (84 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 80 % of 46 winters. Right now: 44 in (112 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Telluride?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 58 in (147 cm)). For a sure base, mid-November to mid-May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Telluride?

    About 58 in (147 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early April); between 30 in (76 cm) in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 101 in (256 cm) in the biggest (1983-84).

    What altitude is Telluride at?

    Between 2,662 m and 3,812 m, so 1,150 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Telluride?

    About 101 km of pistes and 20 lifts.

    About Telluride

    Telluride is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 101 km of pistes put it at number 14 of 175. The slopes start at 2,662 m and reach 3,812 m at the top. Silverton Mountain is the next resort, 16 km away to the east.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,150 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 2,662 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Telluride has roughly 112 cm of snow on the ground today. 16 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.
