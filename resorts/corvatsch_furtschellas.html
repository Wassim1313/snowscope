    The snow at Corvatsch-Furtschellas, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Corvatsch-Furtschellas (2,548 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 64 cm, with a normal range of 42 cm–87 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 102 cm (78 cm–122 cm).

    The snowiest month is April, when the pack peaks at about 120 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 52 % of winters, and by 1 January in 70 %.

    Best winter on record: 2000-01, peaking at 233 cm. Worst: 2004-05, which never passed 79 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to late May (192 days).

    So far this winter: 18 cm on 19 November (usual: 27 cm).

    About Corvatsch-Furtschellas

    Around 40 km of marked runs place Corvatsch-Furtschellas among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 147 by length. The slopes start at 1,799 m and reach 3,296 m at the top. Corviglia is the next resort, 8.3 km away to the north.

    How Corvatsch-Furtschellas is laid out

    1,497 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Starting at 1,799 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 3 cable cars, 6 chairlifts, 3 T-bars and 3 button lifts, 15 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Piz Morteratsch at 3,751 m, standing 455 m above the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Corvatsch-Furtschellas

    Counting every graded piste, there are 70 runs and around 40 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 29 km, or 72%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 5.4 km of difficult runs.

    Snow at Corvatsch-Furtschellas through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,548 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 November and keeps it until about 30 May, roughly 27 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 70 cm and 135 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 120 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in mid-April at roughly 120 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 64 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 33 cm, snowy years 99 cm). For the best snow, aim for early March and early April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks.
