    About Owani Onsen International Area

    With some 10 km of runs, Owani Onsen International Area belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 101 m up to 550 m. Soma Romantopia is the next resort, 17 km away to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 449 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 101 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.
