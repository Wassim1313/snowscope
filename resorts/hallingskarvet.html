    About Hallingskarvet

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hallingskarvet offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 96 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 741 m to 1,225 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hallingskarvet skisenter, 0.1 km south-west, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Hallingskarvet

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 7.4 km to ski. Most of the length is red (56%, about 4.1 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 2.4 km of black or expert runs.

    How Hallingskarvet is laid out

    The vertical drop is 484 m: runs of a moderate length. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Miljonuten (1,851 m), 7.2 km to the south, about 626 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Hallingskarvet through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 983 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 November and lasts to 30 May: about 29 weeks. A normal season tops out near 126 cm, around 27 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 16 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 74 cm (lean years 47 cm, snowy years 100 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 123 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. Most winters land between 83 cm and 146 cm by mid-February.

    Quick answers

    When does Hallingskarvet have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Hallingskarvet at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 74 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Hallingskarvet?

    The top is at 1,225 m and the base at 741 m.

    Does Hallingskarvet have steep runs?

    Yes: about 2.4 km of its graded pistes (33%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hallingskarvet?

    Hemsedal skisenter, 35 km to the north-east, with about 48 km of pistes.
