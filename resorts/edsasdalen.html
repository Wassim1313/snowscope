    About Edsåsdalen

    Edsåsdalen is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 73 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 478 m up to 961 m. The closest other ski area is Trillevallen (7.3 km, south-east).

    Edsåsdalen and the resorts around it
