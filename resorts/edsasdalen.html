    About Edsåsdalen

    Edsåsdalen is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 73 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 478 m up to 961 m. The closest other ski area is Trillevallen (7.3 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 9.6 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 7.4 km (77%). With 93% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Edsåsdalen

    From top to bottom that makes 483 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. All 5 lifts here are T-bars. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Välliste (1,025 m), Renfjället (986 m) and Grofjället (959 m).

    How much snow Edsåsdalen usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 720 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 26 November and keeps it until about 15 May, roughly 25 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 86 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 48 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 69 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 53 cm and 101 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 90 cm, around 31 March. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April.

    Edsåsdalen: common questions

    When does Edsåsdalen have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Edsåsdalen at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 48 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Edsåsdalen?

    Its highest lift reaches 961 m; the lowest starts at 478 m (483 m of vertical).

    Is Edsåsdalen good for beginners?

    Yes: 93% of the graded pistes, about 8.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Edsåsdalen?

    Åre, 10 km to the north, with about 116 km of pistes.
