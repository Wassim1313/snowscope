    About Nara

    Neither huge nor tiny, Nara offers about 13 km of pistes (number 414 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 925 m and reach 2,114 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cari, 6.6 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Nara

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 13 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 7.7 km (61%). Intermediate runs make up 61% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. 2.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,189 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 925 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 2 rope tows, 7 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Cima di Gana Bianca (2,842 m), 8.0 km to the east, about 728 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Nara usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,520 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 12 January and keeps it until about 16 April, roughly 14 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 30 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 42 cm). For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 42 cm, around 18 February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 25 cm to 58 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Nara: common questions

    When does Nara have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Nara have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    What altitude is Nara at?

    Between 925 m and 2,114 m, so 1,189 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Nara best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 61% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Nara have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
