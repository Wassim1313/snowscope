    About Vimka

    Vimka has about 0.4 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 17 m and 51 m above sea level. Nõmme Lumepark is the next resort, 17 km away to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    34 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    How much snow Vimka usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 34 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 16 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 3 February, with about 12 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 30 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February.

    Vimka: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Vimka?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Can you ski at Vimka at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 5 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Vimka at?

    Between 17 m and 51 m, so 34 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Vimka?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
