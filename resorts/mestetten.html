    About Meßstetten

    Meßstetten has about 0.4 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 836 m to 932 m. The closest other ski area is Ebingen (4.9 km, north-east).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Meßstetten gives 96 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 836 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Weichenwang (988 m), Oberer Berg (982 m) and Sickersberg (981 m).

    Snow at Meßstetten through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 884 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 7 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 24 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 3 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 14 cm in a big one. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Questions about Meßstetten

    When does Meßstetten have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early March.

    Is there snow at Meßstetten at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 3 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Meßstetten?

    The top is at 932 m and the base at 836 m.

    How many km of pistes does Meßstetten have?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
