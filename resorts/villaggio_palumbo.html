    About Villaggio Palumbo

    Villaggio Palumbo is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 18 of 40 in the Apennines by length. The slopes start at 1,338 m and reach 1,651 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Pagliara, 19 km to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Villaggio Palumbo

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 7.2 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (50%, about 3.6 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 313 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,338 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Villaggio Palumbo?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,651 m; the lowest starts at 1,338 m (313 m of vertical).

    Is Villaggio Palumbo good for beginners?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 4.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Villaggio Palumbo have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
