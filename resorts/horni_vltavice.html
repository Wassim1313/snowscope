    About Horní Vltavice

    Horní Vltavice has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 802 m and reach 879 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kubova Huť, 3.1 km to the north.

    Horní Vltavice and the resorts around it

    If snow is the priority, Nové Hutě does better, with about 5 weeks of a good base instead of 0. For higher slopes, Nové Hutě climbs to 1,146 m.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 5 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Horní Vltavice. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 6 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    How Horní Vltavice is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 77 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 802 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Nové Hutě reaches 1,146 m. The only lift is a drag lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 840 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 44 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 8 cm, with 30 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 16 cm.

    Questions about Horní Vltavice

    When does Horní Vltavice have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Is there snow at Horní Vltavice at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 8 cm).

    What altitude is Horní Vltavice at?

    The top is at 879 m and the base at 802 m.

    How many km of pistes does Horní Vltavice have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
