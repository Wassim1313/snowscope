    About Atzmännig

    Neither huge nor tiny, Atzmännig offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 539 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 825 m up to 1,193 m. Its nearest neighbour is Atzmännig, 0.0 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Atzmännig usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,009 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early May, about 17 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 42 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 23 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 6 cm in a poor year to 43 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 20 cm to 77 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 46 cm.

    How Atzmännig is laid out

    368 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,025 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Tweralpspitz (1,332 m), 2.1 km to the north-east, about 139 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Atzmännig

    What is the best month to ski at Atzmännig?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early May.

    Can you ski at Atzmännig at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 23 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Atzmännig?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,193 m; the lowest starts at 825 m (368 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Atzmännig best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 64% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Atzmännig?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
