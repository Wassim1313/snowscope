    About Kuma Ski Land

    Kuma Ski Land is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 895 m to 995 m. The closest other ski area is SOL-FA Oda ski slopes (20 km, south).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 100 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 895 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
