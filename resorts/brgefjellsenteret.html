    About Børgefjellsenteret

    Neither huge nor tiny, Børgefjellsenteret offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 178 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 461 m up to 577 m. The closest other ski area is Borgefjellsenteret (0.1 km, north-east).

    Snow at Børgefjellsenteret through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 519 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 13 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early April and mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 14 April, with about 189 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 94 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 55 cm, snowy years 123 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 188 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 107 cm and 209 cm.

    The mountain at Børgefjellsenteret

    From top to bottom that makes 116 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Blåsjöns Fjällanläggning, reach 923 m.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Børgefjellsenteret has roughly 17 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 56 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Børgefjellsenteret?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Børgefjellsenteret at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 94 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Børgefjellsenteret at?

    The top is at 577 m and the base at 461 m.
