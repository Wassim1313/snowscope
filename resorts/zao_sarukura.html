    About Zao Sarukura

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Zao Sarukura one of the smaller places to ski in Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 682 m up to 803 m. Zao Liza World is the next resort, 2.9 km away to the east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Zao Onsen (4.6 km north-east) has about 39 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. The highest nearby is Zao Onsen, topping out at 1,661 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Zao Sarukura has 6 graded runs adding up to about 1.5 km. Most of the length is beginner (60%, about 0.9 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Zao Sarukura is laid out

    The vertical drop is 121 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 682 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Zao Onsen, reach 1,661 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Questions about Zao Sarukura

    What altitude is Zao Sarukura at?

    Its highest lift reaches 803 m; the lowest starts at 682 m (121 m of vertical).

    Is Zao Sarukura good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Zao Sarukura?

    Zao Onsen, 4.6 km to the north-east, with about 39 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Zao Sarukura have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
