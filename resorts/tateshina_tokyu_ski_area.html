    About Tateshina Tokyu

    Tateshina Tokyu is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,337 m and reach 1,412 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Shirakaba 2 in 1, 2.8 km to the north.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Shirakaba Kogen Kokusai Ski: about 11 km of pistes, 6.7 km to the north. The highest nearby is Pilatus Tateshina Snow Resort, topping out at 2,222 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 75 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,337 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Pilatus Tateshina Snow Resort, for one, climbs to 2,222 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet, 2 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
