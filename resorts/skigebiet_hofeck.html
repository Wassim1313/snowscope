    About Hofeck

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Hofeck sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 954 m up to 1,126 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Spitzenberg und Köpfle, 2.7 km to the south-east.

    Hofeck and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Feldberg: about 22 km of pistes, 3.8 km to the north. For higher slopes, Feldberg climbs to 1,446 m.

    The mountain at Hofeck

    172 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 954 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Feldberg, reach 1,446 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Landgasthof Bergblick (hotel), Pension Hofeck (guesthouse), Kirchenrain Hof (chalet) and Krunkelbachhütte (guesthouse).

    Questions about Hofeck

    How high do the lifts go at Hofeck?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,126 m; the lowest starts at 954 m (172 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Hofeck?

    Feldberg, 3.8 km to the north, with about 22 km of pistes.

    How big is Hofeck?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
