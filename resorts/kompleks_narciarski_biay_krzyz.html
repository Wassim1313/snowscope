    About Kompleks Narciarski Biały Krzyż

    Kompleks Narciarski Biały Krzyż is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 892 m up to 929 m. Its nearest neighbour is Szczyrk, 3.7 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 37 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 892 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 929 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
