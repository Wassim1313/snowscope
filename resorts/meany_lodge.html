    About Meany Lodge

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Meany Lodge one of the smaller places to ski in US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 891 m up to 1,029 m. The closest other ski area is Three Summits (15 km, north-west).

    How Meany Lodge compares nearby

    For more terrain, Three Summits (15 km north-west) has about 43 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Stevens Pass has the longer snow season: around 29 weeks of solid cover, compared with 23 at Meany Lodge. The highest nearby is Crystal Mountain, topping out at 2,072 m.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 960 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 December and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 23 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 58 cm, with 89 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 88 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For about 7 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 59 cm and 148 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 10 March, with about 116 cm.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Meany Lodge has 5 graded runs adding up to about 1.1 km. Most of the length is green circle (72%, about 0.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    The mountain at Meany Lodge

    138 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 891 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 6 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Crystal Mountain reaches 2,072 m. All 3 lifts here are rope tows. The skyline is dominated by Keechelus Ridge, 1,570 m high and 7.3 km north of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Meany Lodge (mountain hut).

    Questions about Meany Lodge

    What is the best month to ski at Meany Lodge?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Meany Lodge have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 58 cm deep.

    What altitude is Meany Lodge at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,029 m; the lowest starts at 891 m (138 m of vertical).

    Is Meany Lodge good for beginners?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Meany Lodge?

    Three Summits, 15 km to the north-west, with about 43 km of pistes.
