    About Ski Zábava Hruštín

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski Zábava Hruštín offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 171 of 312 in the Carpathians). Its lifts climb from 927 m to 1,141 m. Kubínská hola is the next resort, 3.3 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Ski Zábava Hruštín

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.9 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.6 km, or 83%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 214 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 927 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,141 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 T-bar and 2 button lifts.

    Ski Zábava Hruštín and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Vrátna: about 12 km of pistes, 17 km to the south-west. For higher slopes, Vrátna climbs to 1,529 m.

    Questions about Ski Zábava Hruštín

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Zábava Hruštín?

    The top is at 1,141 m and the base at 927 m.

    What level of skier is Ski Zábava Hruštín best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 83% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Ski Zábava Hruštín?

    How many km of pistes does Ski Zábava Hruštín have?
