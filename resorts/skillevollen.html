    About Skillevollen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Skillevollen offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 170 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 45 m to 302 m.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 30 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 174 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early November to late May or later. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 119 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 70 cm, snowy years 163 cm). Most winters land between 133 cm and 253 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. For about 24 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 234 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is usually deepest around 14 April, with about 236 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 257 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Innerstøtten at 746 m, standing 444 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Skillevollen

    When is the best time to ski at Skillevollen?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Can you ski at Skillevollen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 119 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Skillevollen?

    The top is at 302 m and the base at 45 m.

    How big is Skillevollen?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
