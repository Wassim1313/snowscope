    What 46 winters say about Lindvallen

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Lindvallen (711 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 33 cm, with a normal range of 22 cm–45 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 60 cm (47 cm–70 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 62 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 13 % of winters, and by 1 January in 28 %.

    The deepest winter was 1987-88 (118 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1995-96 (36 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-December until late April in a normal winter, roughly 133 days.

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Lindvallen at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 33 cm, and only 28 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 29 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Lindvallen?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 62 cm). For a sure base, mid-December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Lindvallen?

    About 62 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 36 cm in the leanest winter (1995-96) and 118 cm in the biggest (1987-88).

    About Lindvallen

    With some 35 km of runs, Lindvallen belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 568 m and 854 m above sea level. Högfjället is the next resort, 3.2 km away to the west.
