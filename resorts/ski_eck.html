    About Ski Eck

    Ski Eck is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 87 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Skiing here happens between 841 m and 1,057 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Hohenbogen (8.6 km, north).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 2.9 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 50%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    The mountain at Ski Eck

    216 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,041 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,057 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.

    This week's snow

    Snow at Ski Eck through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 949 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 78 cm. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 11 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 46 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February.

    Ski Eck: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Eck?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Ski Eck at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    What altitude is Ski Eck at?

    Between 841 m and 1,057 m, so 216 m of vertical drop.

    Is Ski Eck good for beginners?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Ski Eck?

    Großer Arber, 12 km to the south-east, with about 11 km of pistes.
