    About Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf

    With some 108 km of runs, Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 823 m and 2,213 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Fellhorn/Kanzelwand, 1.8 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf

    Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf has 211 graded runs adding up to about 108 km. The largest share is blue, with 48 km (44%). No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. Advanced skiers have about 14 km of black or expert runs. 0.6 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf

    From top to bottom that makes 1,390 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 823 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 4 cable cars, 2 funiculars, 9 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 12 chairlifts, 8 T-bars, 3 button lifts, 8 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 4 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Elfer at 2,387 m, standing 174 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,518 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 45 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 19 cm, snowy years 69 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 54 cm and 115 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 28 February, with about 89 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 63 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Questions about Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf

    When does Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf?

    Between 823 m and 2,213 m, so 1,390 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kleinwalsertal-Oberstdorf good for beginners?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 50 km, are easy.
