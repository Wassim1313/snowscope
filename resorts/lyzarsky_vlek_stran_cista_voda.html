    How Lyžařský vlek Stráň Čistá Voda is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 129 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 653 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Špindlerův Mlýn, reach 1,035 m. The only lift is a button lift.
