    About Utah Olympic Park

    Utah Olympic Park is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 5.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,098 m up to 2,464 m. Woodward is the next resort, 4.9 km away to the north-west.

    The mountain at Utah Olympic Park

    The vertical drop is 366 m: runs of a moderate length. At 2,098 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Snowbird, reach 3,340 m.
