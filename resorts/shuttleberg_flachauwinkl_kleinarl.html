    About Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar

    With some 28 km of runs, Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 989 m to 1,921 m. Ski amadé is the next resort, 3.5 km away to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 41 marked runs cover roughly 26 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 14 km, or 55%. With 55% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How much snow Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar usually gets

    At about 1,455 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 December and lasts to 30 April: about 18 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 30 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 53 cm, a dry one 10 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 40 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 62 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 30 cm and 73 cm.

    The mountain at Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar

    The vertical drop is 932 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 989 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 7 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Kraxnkogel at 2,436 m, standing 515 m above the top lift.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Ski amadé: about 605 km of pistes, 3.5 km to the north. If snow is the priority, Ski amadé does better, with about 23 weeks of a good base instead of 18. For higher slopes, Ski amadé climbs to 2,642 m.

    Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 30 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar at?

    Between 989 m and 1,921 m, so 932 m of vertical drop.

    Is Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar suitable for families?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl-Kleinar?

    Ski amadé, 3.5 km to the north, with about 605 km of pistes.
