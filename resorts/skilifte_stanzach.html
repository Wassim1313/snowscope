    About Skilifte Stanzach

    Skilifte Stanzach is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 940 m and reach 1,051 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Tannheim (13 km, north).

    The pistes of Skilifte Stanzach

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.5 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 56%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes. 0.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skilifte Stanzach gives 111 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 940 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Hoch-Imst, for one, climbs to 2,022 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows, 3 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Wasserfallkarspitze, 2,557 m high and 6.9 km south-west of the resort.

    How much snow Skilifte Stanzach usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 996 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 10 cm and 50 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 29 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 27 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Skilifte Stanzach?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Is there snow at Skilifte Stanzach at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilifte Stanzach?

    The top is at 1,051 m and the base at 940 m.

    Is Skilifte Stanzach suitable for families?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Skilifte Stanzach?

    Berwang, 15 km to the east, with about 28 km of pistes.
