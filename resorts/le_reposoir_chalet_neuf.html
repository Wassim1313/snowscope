    About Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,323 m up to 1,620 m. Its nearest neighbour is Le Reposoir, 2.3 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf

    The piste map lists 6 graded runs, about 3.6 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.4 km, or 67%. With 67% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,472 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 59 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 18 cm in a poor year to 96 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and mid-March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 8 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 88 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 69 cm and 153 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 116 cm, around 21 February.

    How Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf gives 297 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,323 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pointe Percée at 2,750 m, standing 1,130 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf include Gite de Séjour - Les Cyclamens (chalet), Gîte du Passant (chalet), Le Chalet "Saint-Joseph, La Balmette" (chalet) and La Chartreuse (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf?

    Aim for mid-February and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 59 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf at?

    Between 1,323 m and 1,620 m, so 297 m of vertical drop.

    Is Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf good for beginners?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 2.4 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf?

    Le Grand Massif, 15 km to the east, with about 174 km of pistes.
