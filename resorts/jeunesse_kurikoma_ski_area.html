    About Jeunesse Kurikoma

    Jeunesse Kurikoma is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 10 km of pistes put it at number 74 of 385. Its lifts climb from 390 m to 771 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tengamori, 9.9 km to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 9.6 km. The largest share is green, with 6.1 km (63%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Jeunesse Kurikoma is laid out

    381 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 390 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 771 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    Jeunesse Kurikoma: common questions

    How high is Jeunesse Kurikoma?

    Its highest lift reaches 771 m; the lowest starts at 390 m (381 m of vertical).

    Is Jeunesse Kurikoma suitable for families?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 6.1 km, are easy.

    How big is Jeunesse Kurikoma?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
