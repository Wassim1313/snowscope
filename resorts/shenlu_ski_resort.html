    About Shenlu

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Shenlu one of the smaller places to ski in China. The slopes start at 260 m and reach 358 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Xudong Ski Playground (5.8 km, south-east).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    98 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 260 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 gondola and 3 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.
