    About Eschenlohe auf der Rieder

    Compact and local, Eschenlohe auf der Rieder has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 643 m and reach 698 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Oberau (5.7 km, south-west).

    How Eschenlohe auf der Rieder compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.4 km south-west to Farchant, with about 1.0 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee) does better, with about 23 weeks of a good base instead of 0. For higher slopes, Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau climbs to 1,671 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 55 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 643 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau, reach 1,671 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Krottenkopf, 2,086 m high and 4.3 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 670 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 32 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 23 cm, a dry one 0 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 13 cm.

    Eschenlohe auf der Rieder: common questions

    When does Eschenlohe auf der Rieder have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Eschenlohe auf der Rieder at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 8 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Eschenlohe auf der Rieder at?

    Its highest lift reaches 698 m; the lowest starts at 643 m (55 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Eschenlohe auf der Rieder?

    Farchant, 8.4 km to the south-west, with about 1.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Eschenlohe auf der Rieder?
