    About Monte Fumaiolo

    Compact and local, Monte Fumaiolo has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,269 m to 1,371 m. Its nearest neighbour is Monte Carpegna, 21 km east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Monte Fumaiolo and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Monte Nerone: about 3.0 km of pistes, 43 km to the south-east. The highest nearby is Area Sciistica Campigna, topping out at 1,630 m.

    What there is to ski at Monte Fumaiolo

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.5 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 61%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Snow at Monte Fumaiolo in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,320 m stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    How Monte Fumaiolo is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 102 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Area Sciistica Campigna, reach 1,630 m. The only lift is a drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Monte Fumaiolo (1,406 m), Monte Comero (1,371 m) and Monte Aquilone (1,354 m).

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Fumaiolo Paradise Hotel (hotel), Hotel Monte Fumaiolo (hotel), Rifugio Biancaneve (mountain hut) and Albergo Bellavista (hotel).

    How high do the lifts go at Monte Fumaiolo?

    Who is Monte Fumaiolo best for?

    How big is Monte Fumaiolo?
