    About Duolemeidi

    Neither huge nor tiny, Duolemeidi offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 27 of 92 in China). The slopes start at 1,659 m and reach 1,972 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Thaiwoo, 8.0 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Duolemeidi has 9 graded runs adding up to about 7.5 km. Most of the length is blue square (78%, about 5.8 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    313 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,659 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.
