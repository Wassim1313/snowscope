    About Lélex

    Neither huge nor tiny, Lélex offers about 21 km of pistes (number 295 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 866 m and 1,659 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Les Moussières, 4.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 40 graded runs, about 21 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 7.3 km (35%). With 66% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The mountain at Lélex

    The vertical drop is 793 m: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 866 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,659 m. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 2 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift, 5 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include J1 (1,721 m), Crêt de la Neige (1,718 m) and Le Reculet (1,717 m).

    How much snow Lélex usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,262 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to early March, about 8 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 144 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 49 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 93 cm in the best years.

    Questions about Lélex

    When does Lélex have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    Is there snow at Lélex at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in 8 winters out of ten.

    What altitude is Lélex at?

    Between 866 m and 1,659 m, so 793 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Lélex?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Lélex have?

    About 21 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
