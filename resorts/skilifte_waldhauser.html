    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 61 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 971 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.
