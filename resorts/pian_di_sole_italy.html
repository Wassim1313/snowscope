    How much snow Pian di Sole usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 732 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 10 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm, with 19 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 28 cm.
