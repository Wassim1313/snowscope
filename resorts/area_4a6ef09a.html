    The mountain at Экстремальный парк "Фристайл"

    From top to bottom that makes 37 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Горнолыжный комплекс "Лисья гора, for one, climbs to 196 m. All 4 lifts here are rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
