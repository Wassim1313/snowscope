    About Sattel-Hochstuckli

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sattel-Hochstuckli offers about 10 km of pistes (number 499 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 775 m and reach 1,231 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Biberegg (3.3 km, north-east).

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 5 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Sattel-Hochstuckli. The last seven days brought 4 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 12 cm by Saturday.

    How Sattel-Hochstuckli is laid out

    The vertical drop is 456 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 775 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Sattel-Hochstuckli include Berggasthaus Herrenboden (guesthouse), Berggasthaus Mostelberg (guesthouse), SAC Clubhaus Bärenfang (mountain hut) and Burg (hotel).

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Sattel-Hochstuckli?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,231 m; the lowest starts at 775 m (456 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Sattel-Hochstuckli have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
