    About Sattel-Hochstuckli

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sattel-Hochstuckli offers about 10 km of pistes (number 499 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 775 m and reach 1,231 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Biberegg (3.3 km, north-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 19 marked runs cover roughly 10 km. The largest share is blue, with 6.8 km (67%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Sattel-Hochstuckli is laid out

    The vertical drop is 456 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 775 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 gondola, 1 T-bar, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Grosser Mythen, 1,898 m high and 4.6 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow at Sattel-Hochstuckli through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,003 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 10 January and lasts to 28 March: about 11 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 62 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 39 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 23 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 23 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 37 cm).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Sattel-Hochstuckli?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Can you ski at Sattel-Hochstuckli at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 23 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Sattel-Hochstuckli?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,231 m; the lowest starts at 775 m (456 m of vertical).

    Is Sattel-Hochstuckli good for beginners?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 6.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Sattel-Hochstuckli have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
