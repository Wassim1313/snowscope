    About Prat-Peyrot

    With some 9.0 km of runs, Prat-Peyrot belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,286 m up to 1,468 m. The closest other ski area is Bleymard Mont Lozère (40 km, north-east).

    The pistes of Prat-Peyrot

    In all, 16 marked runs cover roughly 8.6 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.6 km, or 42%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Prat-Peyrot

    182 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 12 lifts here are button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Questions about Prat-Peyrot

    How high is Prat-Peyrot?

    Between 1,286 m and 1,468 m, so 182 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Prat-Peyrot?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 6.1 km, are easy.

    How big is Prat-Peyrot?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
