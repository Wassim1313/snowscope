    About Rabbit Hill Snow Resort

    About 3.0 km of pistes make Rabbit Hill Snow Resort one of the smaller places to ski in Canada West. The slopes start at 630 m and reach 702 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Snow Valley Ski Club, 14 km to the north-east.

    The mountain at Rabbit Hill Snow Resort

    72 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 630 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 702 m is the highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km.

    What altitude is Rabbit Hill Snow Resort at?
