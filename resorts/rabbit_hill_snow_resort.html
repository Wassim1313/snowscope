    About Rabbit Hill Snow Resort

    About 3.0 km of pistes make Rabbit Hill Snow Resort one of the smaller places to ski in Canada West. The slopes start at 630 m and reach 702 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Snow Valley Ski Club, 14 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Rabbit Hill Snow Resort has 6 graded runs adding up to about 1.9 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 1.0 km, or 56%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Rabbit Hill Snow Resort usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 666 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Most winters land between 8 cm and 45 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 24 cm, around 25 January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 15 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 36 cm in a big one.

    The mountain at Rabbit Hill Snow Resort

    72 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 630 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 702 m is the highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 3 rope tows and 2 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Rabbit Hill Snow Resort: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Rabbit Hill Snow Resort?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at Rabbit Hill Snow Resort at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 15 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Rabbit Hill Snow Resort at?

    Its highest lift reaches 702 m; the lowest starts at 630 m (72 m of vertical).

    Is Rabbit Hill Snow Resort good for beginners?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Rabbit Hill Snow Resort have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
