    About Visperterminen

    Visperterminen is a mid-sized ski area with about 16 km of pistes, number 376 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,379 m up to 2,302 m. The closest other ski area is Gspon (3.3 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Visperterminen

    Visperterminen has 24 graded runs adding up to about 16 km. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 8.7 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow Visperterminen usually gets

    At about 1,840 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 November and lasts to 27 May: about 26 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 64 cm, with 91 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 10 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 102 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and early April. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 116 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 74 cm and 136 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 923 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,379 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 3 T-bars. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Böshorn (3,269 m), 7.7 km to the south-east, about 967 m higher than the top lift.

    Visperterminen: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Visperterminen?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Visperterminen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 64 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Visperterminen?

    Between 1,379 m and 2,302 m, so 923 m of vertical drop.

    Is Visperterminen suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 8.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Visperterminen?

    Unterbäch, 12 km to the west, with about 37 km of pistes.
