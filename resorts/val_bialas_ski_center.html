    About Val Bialas

    Neither huge nor tiny, Val Bialas offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 69 of 93 in US Northeast). The ski area spans the mountain from 172 m up to 248 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Woods Valley, 26 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Val Bialas

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 2.5 km in total. The largest share is green circle, with 1.3 km (53%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    76 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 172 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 2 rope tows. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Harts Hill (210 m) and Burrstone Hill (183 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 210 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 11 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 29 cm in a big one. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 7 cm to 57 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 27 cm, around 12 February.

    Val Bialas: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Val Bialas?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Val Bialas at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Val Bialas?

    Between 172 m and 248 m, so 76 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Val Bialas?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    How big is Val Bialas?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
