    About Rogla

    Rogla is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 446 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,066 m and reach 1,511 m at the top. Trije Kralji is the next resort, 8.7 km away to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 32 graded runs, about 12 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 5.9 km (47%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The mountain at Rogla

    Skied top to bottom, Rogla gives 445 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Its 13 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 5 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Jezerski vrh (1,537 m), Mulejev vrh (1,533 m) and Rogla (1,517 m).

    How much snow Rogla usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,288 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 21 cm, around 31 January. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 93 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 46 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Rogla?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Can you ski at Rogla at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Rogla?

    Between 1,066 m and 1,511 m, so 445 m of vertical drop.

    Is Rogla suitable for families?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 6.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Rogla have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
