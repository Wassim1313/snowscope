    About Sun Valley (Сонячна Долина)

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Sun Valley (Сонячна Долина) gives 27 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 184 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.
