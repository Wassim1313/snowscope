    About Gråkallparken

    Gråkallparken has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 389 m and reach 437 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Vintervasskleiva, 0.9 km south-west, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 413 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 6 December and lasts to 7 May: about 22 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 83 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 45 cm, with 73 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 46 cm to 116 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 93 cm, around 3 April.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 48 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 5 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Storheia (565 m), 4.0 km to the south-west, about 128 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Gråkallparken

    When is the best time to ski at Gråkallparken?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Gråkallparken have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 45 cm deep.

    How high is Gråkallparken?

    Between 389 m and 437 m, so 48 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Gråkallparken?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
