    About Rahmensteinhang

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Rahmensteinhang one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 550 m to 605 m. There are 5 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Familienabfahrt, 0.1 km to the north-east.

    Other resorts close by

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Rahmensteinhang gives 55 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 550 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a drag lift.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Rahmensteinhang?

    Its highest lift reaches 605 m; the lowest starts at 550 m (55 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Rahmensteinhang?

    Hirtenteich, 20 km to the north-west, with about 4.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Rahmensteinhang have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
