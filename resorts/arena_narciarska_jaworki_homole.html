    About Arena narciarska Jaworki Homole

    Arena narciarska Jaworki Homole is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 596 m up to 711 m. Pod Durbaszką is the next resort, 2.0 km away to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Arena narciarska Jaworki Homole gives 115 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 596 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Pod Durbaszką, for one, climbs to 925 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 button lift.
