    About Faltschen

    Faltschen is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 651 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,030 m and 1,339 m above sea level. Aeschiallmend is the next resort, 2.2 km away to the north.

    What there is to ski at Faltschen

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 4.8 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 2.7 km (57%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Faltschen has roughly 20 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 54 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (18 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 309 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Mürren/Schilthorn reaches 2,949 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Glütschhöreli, 2,521 m high and 7.3 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Faltschen usually gets

    At about 1,184 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 December and lasts to 27 April: about 18 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 48 cm, a dry one 12 cm. Most winters land between 35 cm and 80 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 39 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 59 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    Faltschen: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Faltschen?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Faltschen at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 31 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Faltschen?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,339 m; the lowest starts at 1,030 m (309 m of vertical).

    Is Faltschen good for beginners?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 2.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Faltschen?

    Sigriswill-Schwanden, 11 km to the north, with about 12 km of pistes.
