    About Brixen Plose

    Brixen Plose is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 279 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,038 m up to 2,502 m. Its nearest neighbour is Villnöß, 5.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Brixen Plose

    From top to bottom that makes 1,464 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,502 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 4 chairlifts and 1 button lift. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Tullen, 2,655 m high and 6.3 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow at Brixen Plose through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,770 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 6 January and keeps it until about 29 April, roughly 16 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 41 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm, with 46 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 27 cm and 61 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 34 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Brixen Plose?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    Is there snow at Brixen Plose at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 24 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Brixen Plose?

    The top is at 2,502 m and the base at 1,038 m.

    Is Brixen Plose suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Brixen Plose?

    Val Gardena, 14 km to the south, with about 118 km of pistes.
