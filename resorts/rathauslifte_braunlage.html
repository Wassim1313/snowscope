    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Rathauslifte Braunlage gives 249 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 568 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
