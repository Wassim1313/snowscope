    About Les Bossons

    Les Bossons is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,040 m up to 1,419 m. Its nearest neighbour is Le Tourchet (Chamonix), 3.3 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 8 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Les Bossons. 36 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 119 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 13 cm now.

    The mountain at Les Bossons

    From top to bottom that makes 379 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The only lift is a chairlift. Above the ski area rises Mont Blanc / Monte Bianco (4,807 m), 7.4 km to the south, about 3,388 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,230 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 58 cm and 129 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 96 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 43 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 45 cm, from 14 cm in a poor year to 77 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Les Bossons?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Les Bossons have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Les Bossons?

    The top is at 1,419 m and the base at 1,040 m.
