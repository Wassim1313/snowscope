    About Kohútka

    Around 6.0 km of marked runs place Kohútka among the larger resorts of the Carpathians, number 40 by length. Its lifts climb from 701 m to 939 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Čertov Javorníky, 2.3 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 238 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 701 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 939 m. Its 8 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 6 button lifts and 1 rope tow. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
