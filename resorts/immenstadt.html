    About Immenstadt

    Immenstadt is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 753 m up to 1,412 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried, 4.7 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,082 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 January and lasts to 6 April: about 13 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 25 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 46 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 25 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 43 cm, a dry one 5 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 21 cm to 76 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 659 m: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 753 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,412 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts. Above the ski area rises Buralpkopf (1,772 m), 7.7 km to the south-west, about 360 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Immenstadt has roughly 24 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 18 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 14 cm at mid-mountain).

    Questions about Immenstadt

    What is the best month to ski at Immenstadt?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    How much snow does Immenstadt have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 25 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Immenstadt at?

    The top is at 1,412 m and the base at 753 m.
