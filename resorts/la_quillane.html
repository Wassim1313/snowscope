    About La Quillane

    About 3.0 km of pistes make La Quillane one of the smaller places to ski in the Pyrenees. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,709 m up to 1,806 m. Les Angles is the next resort, 5.0 km away to the north-west.

    How La Quillane compares nearby

    For more terrain, Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 (6.0 km west) has about 36 km of pistes against 3.0 km here. Espace Cambre d'Aze reaches 2,377 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The mountain at La Quillane

    The vertical drop is 97 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,709 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt reaches 2,495 m.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of La Quillane include Hôtel Corrieu (hostel), Le Clos Cerdan (hotel), Chalet Ma Néou (hotel) and Ancienne Auberge (hotel).
