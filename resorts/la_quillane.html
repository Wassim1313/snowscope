    About La Quillane

    About 3.0 km of pistes make La Quillane one of the smaller places to ski in the Pyrenees. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,709 m up to 1,806 m. Les Angles is the next resort, 5.0 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 3.0 km in total. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 56%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at La Quillane

    The vertical drop is 97 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,709 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Estació d'esquí i muntanya Vallt reaches 2,495 m. To get up there are 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mont Llaret at 2,376 m, standing 570 m above the top lift.

    How much snow La Quillane usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,758 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 18 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 51 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm, with 31 cm in the best years.

    Quick answers

    When does La Quillane have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at La Quillane at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is La Quillane at?

    The top is at 1,806 m and the base at 1,709 m.

    Can beginners ski at La Quillane?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near La Quillane?

    Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000, 6.0 km to the west, with about 36 km of pistes.
