    About Le Grand Puy

    Le Grand Puy is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,349 m up to 1,602 m. Chabanon is the next resort, 7.7 km away to the west.

    How Le Grand Puy is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Le Grand Puy gives 253 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,349 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Les Orres reaches 2,700 m.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Gîte de Rocheclose (guesthouse), Hôtel Le Vieux Tilleul** (hotel), Gîte des Silves (chalet) and La Chaumiere (hotel).
