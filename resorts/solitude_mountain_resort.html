    About Solitude

    Solitude is a mid-sized ski area with about 37 km of pistes, number 61 of 175 in US West by length. The slopes start at 2,439 m and reach 3,051 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Brighton Resort (2.7 km, south-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 61 graded runs, about 37 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 25 km (67%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 9.2 km.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 86 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Solitude. The last seven days brought 38 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 56 cm at mid-mountain).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,745 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 155 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For about 19 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 188 cm. Most winters land between 113 cm and 247 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 103 cm, with 190 cm in the best years.

    Lifts and terrain

    612 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 2,439 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Its 9 lifts break down into 8 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Monte Cristo (3,393 m), 6.5 km to the south-west, about 342 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Solitude include Silver Fork Lodge (hotel), Alpine Lodge (guesthouse), Brighton Lodge (hotel) and Snowpine Lodge (hotel).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Solitude?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Solitude at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 103 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Solitude?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,051 m; the lowest starts at 2,439 m (612 m of vertical).

    Does Solitude have steep runs?

    Yes: about 9.2 km of its graded pistes (25%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Solitude?

    Park City, 7.1 km to the north-east, with about 256 km of pistes.
