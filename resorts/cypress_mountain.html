    About Cypress Mountain

    With roughly 27 km of runs, Cypress Mountain sits in the middle of the size table of Canada West. Its lifts climb from 824 m to 1,426 m. Grouse Mountain is the next resort, 8.3 km away to the east.

    What there is to ski at Cypress Mountain

    Counting every graded piste, there are 67 runs and around 26 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 12 km (48%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 7.0 km.

    How much snow Cypress Mountain usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,125 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 300 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. For about 25 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 130 cm and 312 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 289 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 141 cm; a snowy December gave 221 cm, a dry one 74 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    602 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 824 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 6 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is The West Lion at 1,654 m, standing 228 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Cypress Mountain?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Cypress Mountain have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 141 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Cypress Mountain?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026.

    How high is Cypress Mountain?

    The top is at 1,426 m and the base at 824 m.

    Does Cypress Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 7.0 km of its graded pistes (27%) are difficult.
