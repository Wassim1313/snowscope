    About Pilatus Tateshina Snow Resort

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Pilatus Tateshina Snow Resort sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,751 m up to 2,222 m. Its nearest neighbour is Tateshina Tokyu, 4.7 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Pilatus Tateshina Snow Resort has 8 graded runs adding up to about 5.4 km. The largest share is red, with 3.2 km (59%). Intermediate runs make up 59% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.1 km.

    The mountain at Pilatus Tateshina Snow Resort

    The vertical drop is 471 m: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,751 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,222 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 cable car and 2 chairlifts. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.
