    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 594 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 26 January, with about 6 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 1 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 15 cm). Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 20 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Willingen: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Willingen?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Willingen at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.
