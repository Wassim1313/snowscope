    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Bitz gives 31 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 863 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Burg (976 m), Braunhardsberg (969 m) and Kreuzbühl (960 m).

    How much snow Bitz usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 878 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 23 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 23 January. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 12 cm).

    Bitz: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Bitz?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Bitz at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Bitz at?

    Its highest lift reaches 894 m; the lowest starts at 863 m (31 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Bitz have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
