    About Madarao Kogen

    Madarao Kogen is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 17 km of pistes put it at number 23 of 385. Skiing here happens between 918 m and 1,339 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Tangram Madarao Ski Circus (1.1 km, north-west).

    What there is to ski at Madarao Kogen

    The piste map lists 43 graded runs, about 17 km in total. The largest share is green, with 9.1 km (53%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The mountain at Madarao Kogen

    Skied top to bottom, Madarao Kogen gives 421 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 918 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,339 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 9 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.

    The snow right now
