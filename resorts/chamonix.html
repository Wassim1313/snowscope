    About Les Grands Montets

    Les Grands Montets is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 26 km of pistes put it at number 235 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,234 m and 2,768 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Les Chosalets (Chamonix), 2.1 km to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,534 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,768 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 3 gondolas, 3 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 1 drag lift, 9 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. Above the ski area rises Aiguille Verte (4,121 m), 3.6 km to the south-east, about 1,353 m higher than the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 38 graded runs, about 26 km in total. Most of the length is red (33%, about 8.3 km). With 36% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 7.1 km. 2.1 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Les Grands Montets has roughly 53 cm of snow on the ground today. 44 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 98 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (30 cm).

    Snow at Les Grands Montets through a normal winter

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,001 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 83 cm, from 43 cm in a poor year to 137 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 176 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 114 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Most winters land between 104 cm and 219 cm by mid-February. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    Questions about Les Grands Montets

    When is the best time to ski at Les Grands Montets?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Les Grands Montets have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 83 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Grands Montets?

    Between 1,234 m and 2,768 m, so 1,534 m of vertical drop.

    Does Les Grands Montets have steep runs?

    Yes: about 9.2 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How big is Les Grands Montets?

    About 26 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
