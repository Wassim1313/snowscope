    About Réallon

    With roughly 13 km of runs, Réallon sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,553 m and reach 2,111 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ancelle (11 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Réallon

    The piste map lists 16 graded runs, about 12 km in total. Most of the length is blue (37%, about 4.6 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How much snow Réallon usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,832 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 2 December and lasts to 15 May: about 24 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 62 cm, from 25 cm in a poor year to 96 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 122 cm. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 82 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 73 cm and 147 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Réallon hold around 14 cm of snow today. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 44 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 23 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Réallon gives 558 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,553 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Mourre-Froid, 2,993 m high and 6.9 km north-east of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hôtel des 4 Saisons (hotel), Fondbriand (chalet), Gîte des 3 Cols (mountain hut) and Les Sittelles (chalet).

    Réallon: common questions

    When does Réallon have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Réallon at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 62 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Réallon?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,111 m; the lowest starts at 1,553 m (558 m of vertical).

    Is Réallon good for beginners?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 6.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Réallon?

    Les Orres, 20 km to the south-east, with about 54 km of pistes.
