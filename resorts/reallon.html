    About Réallon

    With roughly 13 km of runs, Réallon sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,553 m and reach 2,111 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ancelle (11 km, west).

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Les Orres (20 km south-east) has about 54 km of pistes against 13 km here. The highest nearby is Orcières Merlette, topping out at 2,731 m.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Réallon hold around 22 cm of snow today. 12 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 19 cm of new snow is expected by Monday.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Réallon gives 558 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,553 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hôtel des 4 Saisons (hotel), Fondbriand (chalet), Gîte des 3 Cols (mountain hut) and Les Sittelles (chalet).

    Réallon: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Réallon?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,111 m; the lowest starts at 1,553 m (558 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Réallon?

    Les Orres, 20 km to the south-east, with about 54 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Réallon have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
