    About Skilift Solla

    Compact and local, Skilift Solla has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Central Europe. The slopes start at 671 m and reach 756 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Skiabfahrt Grainet, 8.0 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Mitterdorf: about 5.0 km of pistes, 13 km to the north-east. Skilifte Waldhäuser has the longer snow season: around 3 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Skilift Solla. For higher slopes, Mitterdorf climbs to 1,142 m.

    How much snow Skilift Solla usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 714 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 23 January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 11 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 37 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 60 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 85 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 671 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Mitterdorf, for one, climbs to 1,142 m. The only lift is a T-bar.

    Quick answers

    When does Skilift Solla have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Skilift Solla at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilift Solla?

    Its highest lift reaches 756 m; the lowest starts at 671 m (85 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Skilift Solla?

    Mitterdorf, 13 km to the north-east, with about 5.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Skilift Solla have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
