    About Ounasvaara

    Compact and local, Ounasvaara has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 94 m to 203 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ounasvaaran laskettelukeskus, 0.3 km west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    In this group of 3, Ounasvaara is number 3 for piste length. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell.

    What there is to ski at Ounasvaara

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.9 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (68%, about 1.3 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Ounasvaara today: about 14 cm up high. The last seven days brought 16 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (18 cm).

    The mountain at Ounasvaara

    Skied top to bottom, Ounasvaara gives 109 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 button lift, 2 lifts in all. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Santavaara (215 m), Korkalovaara (213 m) and Ounasvaara (203 m).

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 148 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 20 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 47 cm and 73 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm, with 50 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 62 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 53 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Ounasvaara: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ounasvaara?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Ounasvaara have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 36 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Ounasvaara?

    Its highest lift reaches 203 m; the lowest starts at 94 m (109 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Ounasvaara?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ounasvaara have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
