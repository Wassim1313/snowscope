    About TJ Čenkovice

    About 1.0 km of pistes make TJ Čenkovice one of the smaller places to ski in Central Europe. Skiing here happens between 777 m and 877 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Čenkovice, 1.0 km to the south-west.

    Snow at TJ Čenkovice through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 827 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to late February, about 4 weeks of good cover. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 67 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm, with 47 cm in the best years. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 34 cm, around 24 February.

    The mountain at TJ Čenkovice

    From top to bottom that makes 100 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 977 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    TJ Čenkovice: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at TJ Čenkovice?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to late February.

    Can you ski at TJ Čenkovice at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is TJ Čenkovice at?

    Between 777 m and 877 m, so 100 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near TJ Čenkovice?

    Horský resort Buková hora, 1.0 km to the south-east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How big is TJ Čenkovice?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
