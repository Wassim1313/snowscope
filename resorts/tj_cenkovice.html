    The mountain at TJ Čenkovice

    From top to bottom that makes 100 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 977 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
