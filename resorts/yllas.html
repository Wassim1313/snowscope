    About Ylläs

    Ylläs is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 18 km of pistes put it at number 37 of 262. Skiing here happens between 273 m and 717 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ylläs, 0.5 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Ylläs

    The piste map lists 47 graded runs, about 18 km in total. Most of the length is blue (75%, about 13 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 444 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 10 T-bars, 8 button lifts and 1 rope tow, 21 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Yllästunturi (719 m), Keskinenlaki (615 m) and Lainiotunturi (613 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 495 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-May. By mid-April the typical depth is 64 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. A normal season tops out near 70 cm, around 7 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 45 cm, with 59 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 55 cm and 84 cm.

    Ylläs: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Ylläs?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Ylläs have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Ylläs?

    Its highest lift reaches 717 m; the lowest starts at 273 m (444 m of vertical).

    Is Ylläs good for beginners?

    Yes: 85% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    How big is Ylläs?

    About 18 km of pistes and 21 lifts.
