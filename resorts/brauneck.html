    About Brauneck

    With some 23 km of runs, Brauneck belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 715 m and 1,594 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Reiserlift, 6.7 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Brauneck through a normal winter

    Count on about 13 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,154 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to late April. The snow is usually deepest around 9 April, with about 38 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 16 cm to 59 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm, from 7 cm in a poor year to 46 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 37 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    How Brauneck is laid out

    The vertical drop is 879 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 715 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 gondola, 5 chairlifts, 9 T-bars, 1 button lift and 2 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Benediktenwand at 1,800 m, standing 206 m above the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Brauneck-Gipfelhaus (mountain hut), Florianshütte (mountain hut), Ferienhaus Streidl (chalet) and Stie-Alm (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Brauneck

    When does Brauneck have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late January to late April.

    Can you ski at Brauneck at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 24 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Brauneck at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,594 m; the lowest starts at 715 m (879 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Brauneck best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 63% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Brauneck have?

    About 23 km of pistes and 18 lifts.
