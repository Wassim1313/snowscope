    Orcières Merlette in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Orcières Merlette (2,278 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 84 cm of cover (52 cm to 99 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 144 cm; the middle half of winters range from 114 cm to 164 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 152 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 54 % of winters, and by 1 January in 85 %.

    Best winter on record: 2019-20, peaking at 244 cm. Worst: 2004-05, which never passed 95 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until late May in a normal winter, roughly 182 days.

    Questions about Orcières Merlette

    Does Orcières Merlette have snow over Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 84 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 85 % of 46 winters. Right now: 83 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Orcières Merlette?

    March: the snow peaks at about 152 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Orcières Merlette get?

    About 152 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 95 cm in the leanest winter (2004-05) and 244 cm in the biggest (2019-20).

    What altitude is Orcières Merlette at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,731 m; the lowest starts at 1,826 m (905 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Orcières Merlette have?

    About 49 km of pistes and 26 lifts.

    About Orcières Merlette

    With some 49 km of runs, Orcières Merlette belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,826 m and 2,731 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Serre Eyraud, 5.5 km to the south-west.

    The mountain at Orcières Merlette

    905 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. At 1,826 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,731 m.

    Orcières Merlette today and the next days
