    About Schneeberglifte Thiersee

    Schneeberglifte Thiersee is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 794 m and reach 858 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Tirolina, 2.1 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Schneeberglifte Thiersee compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Sudelfeld: about 26 km of pistes, 10 km to the north. For more reliable snow, Sudelfeld usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 14 weeks, against 9 here. The highest nearby is Sudelfeld, topping out at 1,507 m.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 826 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 21 January and keeps it until about 22 March, roughly 9 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 14 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Most winters land between 16 cm and 72 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 45 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 41 cm.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 4 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Schneeberglifte Thiersee. The last seven days brought 2 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 12 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 6 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Schneeberglifte Thiersee gives 64 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 794 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 2 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Großer Traithen, 1,852 m high and 7.9 km north-west of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Hagerhof (hotel), Ferienhütte Schneeberg (chalet), Hotel Frohnatur (hotel) and Alpengasthof Schneeberg (guesthouse).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Schneeberglifte Thiersee?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does Schneeberglifte Thiersee have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    What altitude is Schneeberglifte Thiersee at?

    The top is at 858 m and the base at 794 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Schneeberglifte Thiersee?

    Sudelfeld, 10 km to the north, with about 26 km of pistes.

    How big is Schneeberglifte Thiersee?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
