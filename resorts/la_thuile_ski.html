    About La Thuile ski

    With some 79 km of runs, La Thuile ski belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,468 m up to 2,608 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Rosière, 7.6 km to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, La Thuile ski gives 1,140 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,468 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Hotel du Glacier, Residence Planibel, Hotel Chateau Blanc and Hotel La Thuile & SPA.

    Questions about La Thuile ski

    What altitude is La Thuile ski at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,608 m; the lowest starts at 1,468 m (1,140 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does La Thuile ski have?

    About 79 km of pistes and 17 lifts.
