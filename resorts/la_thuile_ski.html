    About La Thuile ski

    With some 79 km of runs, La Thuile ski belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,468 m up to 2,608 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Rosière, 7.6 km to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at La Thuile ski

    In all, 131 marked runs cover roughly 78 km. The largest share is red, with 42 km (54%). Intermediate runs make up 54% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 11 km.

    How much snow La Thuile ski usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,038 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 27 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 138 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 106 cm and 220 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 88 cm, from 47 cm in a poor year to 140 cm in a big one. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 178 cm, around 5 March.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, La Thuile ski gives 1,140 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,468 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 10 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 4 moving carpets, 17 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Mont Berrio Blanc, 3,252 m high and 6.8 km north of the resort.

    Questions about La Thuile ski

    When is the best time to ski at La Thuile ski?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at La Thuile ski at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 88 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at La Thuile ski?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    What altitude is La Thuile ski at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,608 m; the lowest starts at 1,468 m (1,140 m of vertical).

    Who is La Thuile ski best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 54% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
