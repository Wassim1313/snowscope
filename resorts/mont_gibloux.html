    About Mont-Gibloux

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Mont-Gibloux one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 961 m to 1,145 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Berra, 10 km to the east.

    The pistes of Mont-Gibloux

    Mont-Gibloux has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.4 km. Most of the length is red (100%, about 1.4 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Snow at Mont-Gibloux through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,053 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 December and lasts to 24 April: about 18 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 37 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 19 cm and 86 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 50 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 33 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 66 cm).

    Mont-Gibloux and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Charmey (15 km south-east) has about 12 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Moléson does better, with about 25 weeks of a good base instead of 18. The highest nearby is Moléson, topping out at 1,975 m.

    The mountain at Mont-Gibloux

    184 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,161 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Moléson, reach 1,975 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet, 2 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    Questions about Mont-Gibloux

    When is the best time to ski at Mont-Gibloux?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Mont-Gibloux at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 33 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Mont-Gibloux?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,145 m; the lowest starts at 961 m (184 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Mont-Gibloux best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Mont-Gibloux?

    Charmey, 15 km to the south-east, with about 12 km of pistes.
