    About Aspen Snowmass

    About 120 km of pistes make Aspen Snowmass one of the ten biggest ski areas of US West (number 7). The ski area spans the mountain from 2,474 m up to 3,815 m. Buttermilk is the next resort, 6.9 km away to the east.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,341 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,474 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 3,815 m.
