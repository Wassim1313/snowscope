    About Gilja Alpin

    Gilja Alpin is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 187 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 460 m up to 637 m. The closest other ski area is Gilja Alpin (0.1 km, south-east).
