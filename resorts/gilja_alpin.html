    About Gilja Alpin

    Gilja Alpin is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 187 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 460 m up to 637 m. The closest other ski area is Gilja Alpin (0.1 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.8 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 51%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    The mountain at Gilja Alpin

    The vertical drop is 177 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Vinjakula (907 m), 7.0 km to the south, about 270 m higher than the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 3 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Gilja Alpin. The last seven days brought 7 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 5 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 548 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to late April, about 17 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 81 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 52 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 19 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 54 cm). Most winters land between 28 cm and 108 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Gilja Alpin

    What is the best month to ski at Gilja Alpin?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    How much snow does Gilja Alpin have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 19 cm deep.

    What altitude is Gilja Alpin at?

    The top is at 637 m and the base at 460 m.

    Is Gilja Alpin suitable for families?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Gilja Alpin?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
