    About Casa Baraj

    Casa Baraj is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 196 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Skiing here happens between 609 m and 726 m above sea level. Semenic is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the south-east.

    How Casa Baraj is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Casa Baraj gives 117 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 609 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Muntele Mic, reach 1,793 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar.
