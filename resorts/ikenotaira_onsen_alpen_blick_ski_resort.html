    About Ikenotaira Onsen ALPEN BLICK

    With some 14 km of runs, Ikenotaira Onsen ALPEN BLICK belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Skiing here happens between 760 m and 1,408 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Akakura Kanko (2.1 km, north).

    What there is to ski at Ikenotaira Onsen ALPEN BLICK

    Counting every graded piste, there are 18 runs and around 14 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.0 km, or 52%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    How Ikenotaira Onsen ALPEN BLICK is laid out

    The vertical drop is 648 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 760 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
