    How much snow Sno-Shoe Hollow usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,283 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 25 November and keeps it until about 12 May, roughly 24 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 68 cm, with 104 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 31 March, with about 127 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 99 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Year to year the snow is steady, with 81 cm to 152 cm in mid-February in most winters.
