    About Migovo

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Migovo sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 502 m and reach 709 m at the top.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.4 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 0.9 km, or 67%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How Migovo is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 207 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 502 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 2 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Questions about Migovo

    What altitude is Migovo at?

    Its highest lift reaches 709 m; the lowest starts at 502 m (207 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Migovo best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Migovo?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
