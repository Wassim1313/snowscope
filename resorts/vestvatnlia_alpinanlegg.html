    About Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg

    Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 134 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 176 m to 486 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skarmoen Alpinpark Bodø, 33 km north-west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg

    Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg has 6 graded runs adding up to about 5.3 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (50%, about 2.6 km). With 81% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How much snow Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg usually gets

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 331 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. A normal season tops out near 160 cm, around 12 April. Most winters land between 95 cm and 175 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 160 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for early April and mid-April. For about 19 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 76 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 48 cm, snowy years 119 cm).

    How Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg is laid out

    310 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 486 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is St. Olav at 707 m, standing 221 m above the top lift.

    Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 76 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg?

    Is Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg suitable for families?

    Yes: 81% of the graded pistes, about 4.3 km, are easy.
