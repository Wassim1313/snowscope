    About Rosskopf

    With roughly 15 km of runs, Rosskopf sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 961 m up to 2,117 m. The closest other ski area is Ladurns (5.5 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 15 runs and around 15 km to ski. Most of the length is red (69%, about 10 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,156 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 961 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 1 chair-gondola, 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Amthorspitze - Cima Gallina (2,748 m), 7.9 km to the north-east, about 631 m higher than the top lift.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Rosskopf hold around 24 cm of snow today. 16 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 16 cm by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 18 cm at mid-mountain.

    Snow at Rosskopf through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,539 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 29 December and lasts to 18 April: about 16 weeks. Most winters land between 28 cm and 65 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 31 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 45 cm, around 17 February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 28 cm (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 48 cm).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Rosskopf?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Rosskopf have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 28 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Rosskopf at?

    The top is at 2,117 m and the base at 961 m.

    Who is Rosskopf best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Rosskopf?

    Gitschberg Jochtal, 19 km to the east, with about 27 km of pistes.
