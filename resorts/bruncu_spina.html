    About Bruncu Spina

    Compact and local, Bruncu Spina has about 3.0 km of runs, small for the Apennines. The slopes start at 1,560 m and reach 1,822 m at the top.

    The pistes of Bruncu Spina

    Bruncu Spina has 6 graded runs adding up to about 3.4 km. The largest share is red, with 1.8 km (52%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 1.2 km of difficult runs.

    How Bruncu Spina is laid out

    The vertical drop is 262 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,560 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all.

    Bruncu Spina: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Bruncu Spina?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,822 m; the lowest starts at 1,560 m (262 m of vertical).

    Does Bruncu Spina have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.2 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Bruncu Spina have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
