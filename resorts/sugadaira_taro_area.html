    About Sugadaira Taro Area

    With some 10 km of runs, Sugadaira Taro Area belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Its lifts climb from 1,267 m to 1,444 m. Sugadaira Kogen Davos Hill is the next resort, 1.8 km away to the east.
