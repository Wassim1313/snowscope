    About Copper Mountain

    About 115 km of pistes make Copper Mountain one of the ten biggest ski areas of US West (number 10). Its lifts climb from 2,959 m to 3,756 m. The closest other ski area is Breckenridge (8.2 km, east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 153 graded runs, about 115 km in total. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 37 km, or 32%. Good skiers will like it here; 41% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 48 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Copper Mountain usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 3,358 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 14 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 136 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 153 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 95 cm to 169 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 80 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 118 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    797 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 2,959 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. To get up there are 1 chair-gondola, 15 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 4 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Crystal Peak at 4,213 m, standing 457 m above the top lift.

    Copper Mountain: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Copper Mountain?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Copper Mountain at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 80 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Copper Mountain open for the 2026/27 season?

    It opened on 6 November 2026.

    What altitude is Copper Mountain at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,756 m; the lowest starts at 2,959 m (797 m of vertical).

    Does Copper Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 48 km of its graded pistes (41%) are difficult.
