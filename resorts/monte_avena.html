    About Monte Avena

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Monte Avena sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,217 m up to 1,456 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lagorai, 15 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Monte Avena has 11 graded runs adding up to about 8.3 km. Most of the length is blue (60%, about 5.0 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Monte Avena and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is San Martino di Castrozza: about 43 km of pistes, 27 km to the north. Lagorai has the longer snow season: around 20 weeks of solid cover, compared with 10 at Monte Avena. For higher slopes, San Martino di Castrozza climbs to 2,351 m.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,336 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 21 January and keeps it until about 2 April, roughly 10 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 22 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 16 cm to 72 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 20 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 45 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 37 cm, around 8 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 239 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 3 lifts here are button lifts. The skyline is dominated by Monte Pavione, 2,334 m high and 7.6 km north of the resort.

    Questions about Monte Avena

    What is the best month to ski at Monte Avena?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to early April.

    Can you ski at Monte Avena at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Monte Avena?

    Between 1,217 m and 1,456 m, so 239 m of vertical drop.

    Is Monte Avena good for beginners?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 5.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Monte Avena?

    San Martino di Castrozza, 27 km to the north, with about 43 km of pistes.
