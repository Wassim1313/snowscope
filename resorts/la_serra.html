    About La Serra

    La Serra is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 658 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,176 m and 1,462 m above sea level. Lajoux is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 5.1 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 3.0 km (60%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow La Serra usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,319 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to mid-March, about 8 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 22 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 96 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 45 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 93 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    How La Serra is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 286 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,462 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 drag lift and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises La Dôle (1,678 m), 7.8 km to the east, about 216 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of La Serra hold around 12 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 63 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Friday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (6 cm).

    Questions about La Serra

    What is the best month to ski at La Serra?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at La Serra at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 22 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at La Serra?

    Between 1,176 m and 1,462 m, so 286 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at La Serra?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 3.0 km, are easy.

    How big is La Serra?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
