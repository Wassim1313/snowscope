    About Rotarun

    About 3.0 km of pistes make Rotarun one of the smaller places to ski in US West. The slopes start at 1,670 m and reach 1,790 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Sun Valley (19 km, north).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 19 km north to Sun Valley, with about 79 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Sun Valley does better, with about 24 weeks of a good base instead of 15. For higher slopes, Sun Valley climbs to 2,780 m.

    The pistes of Rotarun

    Rotarun has 11 graded runs adding up to about 2.7 km. Most of the length is black diamond (48%, about 1.3 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 48% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.3 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    120 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,670 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Sun Valley, reach 2,780 m. To get up there are 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Prospects Hill (2,187 m), 5.1 km to the south-east, about 397 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Rotarun through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,730 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 21 December and keeps it until about 3 April, roughly 15 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Most winters land between 31 cm and 108 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 73 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm; a snowy December gave 72 cm, a dry one 6 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Rotarun?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Can you ski at Rotarun at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 36 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Rotarun at?

    The top is at 1,790 m and the base at 1,670 m.

    Does Rotarun have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.3 km of its graded pistes (48%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Rotarun?

    Sun Valley, 19 km to the north, with about 79 km of pistes.
