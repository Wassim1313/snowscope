    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Woodward gives 108 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 1,964 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Snowbird reaches 3,340 m.
