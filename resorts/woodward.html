    About Woodward

    About 4.0 km of pistes make Woodward one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Skiing here happens between 1,964 m and 2,072 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Utah Olympic Park, 4.9 km south-east, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Woodward today: about 15 cm up high. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 14 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 17 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Woodward gives 108 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 1,964 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Snowbird reaches 3,340 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 3 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Murdock Peak at 2,922 m, standing 850 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 18 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,018 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 40 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 16 cm in a poor year to 80 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 75 cm. Most winters land between 37 cm and 110 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 37 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Questions about Woodward

    What is the best month to ski at Woodward?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Woodward at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 40 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Woodward?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,072 m; the lowest starts at 1,964 m (108 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Woodward?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Woodward?

    Park City, 11 km to the south, with about 256 km of pistes.
