    About Aflenzer Bürgeralm

    With roughly 18 km of runs, Aflenzer Bürgeralm sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 858 m and 1,798 m above sea level. Seeberg / Seewiesen is the next resort, 8.0 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Aflenzer Bürgeralm

    In all, 34 marked runs cover roughly 18 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 82%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,328 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. By mid-April the typical depth is 44 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Most winters land between 42 cm and 127 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 90 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 43 cm, with 83 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    940 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 858 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,798 m. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars and 3 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Karlhochkogel at 2,096 m, standing 298 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Gasthof Schönleitenblick (mountain hut), Hotel Post Karlon (hotel), Almrauschhütte (mountain hut) and Schönleitenhaus (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Aflenzer Bürgeralm have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Aflenzer Bürgeralm at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 43 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Aflenzer Bürgeralm?

    Between 858 m and 1,798 m, so 940 m of vertical drop.

    Is Aflenzer Bürgeralm good for beginners?

    Yes: 82% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    How big is Aflenzer Bürgeralm?

    About 18 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
