    Snow at Eikedalen through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 589 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 November and lasts to the end of May: about 27 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 200 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 93 cm (lean years 33 cm, snowy years 147 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 20 weeks. A normal season tops out near 222 cm, around 23 March. Most winters land between 99 cm and 267 cm by mid-February.

    Eikedalen: common questions

    When does Eikedalen have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Eikedalen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 93 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
