    About Tärmaby

    Tärmaby is one of the bigger ski areas of Scandinavia: about 16 km of pistes put it at number 41 of 262. Skiing here happens between 446 m and 813 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Hemavan, 14 km north-west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 26 marked runs cover roughly 16 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 8.3 km, or 53%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    367 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 2 drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Sarviestjåhke, 1,148 m high and 7.1 km north of the resort.

    Snow at Tärmaby through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 630 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 10 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. For about 17 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 74 cm (lean years 58 cm, snowy years 98 cm). A normal season tops out near 132 cm, around 27 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 130 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 91 cm and 156 cm.

    Tärmaby: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Tärmaby?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Can you ski at Tärmaby at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 74 cm deep.

    How high is Tärmaby?

    Between 446 m and 813 m, so 367 m of vertical drop.

    Is Tärmaby good for beginners?

    Yes: 71% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How big is Tärmaby?

    About 16 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
