    About Breimsbygda Skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Breimsbygda Skisenter offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 156 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 635 m to 845 m. Its nearest neighbour is Stryn Vinterski, 20 km north-east, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Breimsbygda Skisenter usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 740 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 October and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 32 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 290 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 315 cm, around 24 March. Most winters land between 170 cm and 362 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 168 cm, from 111 cm in a poor year to 237 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 27 weeks.

    What there is to ski at Breimsbygda Skisenter

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 2.7 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.8 km (68%). With 68% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Breimsbygda Skisenter

    210 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 T-bar and 2 button lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Dunheia at 1,165 m, standing 320 m above the top lift.

    Breimsbygda Skisenter: common questions

    When does Breimsbygda Skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    Is there snow at Breimsbygda Skisenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 168 cm deep.

    What altitude is Breimsbygda Skisenter at?

    Between 635 m and 845 m, so 210 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Breimsbygda Skisenter?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Breimsbygda Skisenter?

    Harpefossen skisenter, 38 km to the north-west, with about 10 km of pistes.
