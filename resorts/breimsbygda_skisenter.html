    About Breimsbygda Skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Breimsbygda Skisenter offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 156 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 635 m to 845 m. Its nearest neighbour is Stryn Vinterski, 20 km north-east, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Breimsbygda Skisenter

    210 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
