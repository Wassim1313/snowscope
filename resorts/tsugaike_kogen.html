    About Tsugaike Kogen

    About 34 km of pistes make Tsugaike Kogen one of the ten biggest ski areas of Japan (number 4). The ski area spans the mountain from 803 m up to 1,707 m. Hakuba Norikura Onsen is the next resort, 2.0 km away to the north-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 31 runs and around 33 km to ski. Green runs make up the biggest part: 20 km, or 60%. With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 904 m: enough height for proper long runs. The base at 803 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,707 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 1 gondola and 13 chairlifts, 15 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too.

    Questions about Tsugaike Kogen

    How high is Tsugaike Kogen?

    The top is at 1,707 m and the base at 803 m.

    Is Tsugaike Kogen good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 20 km, are easy.
