    About Wexl Arena

    Neither huge nor tiny, Wexl Arena offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 730 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 842 m and 1,193 m above sea level. Mönichkirchen is the next resort, 6.6 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Wexl Arena

    The piste map lists 14 graded runs, about 3.9 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.8 km (46%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 351 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,042 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 8 lifts break down into 1 button lift, 4 drag lifts and 3 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Arabichl at 1,595 m, standing 402 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,018 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 7 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 22 cm). A normal season tops out near 12 cm, around 9 February. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 31 cm.

    Wexl Arena: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Wexl Arena?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Wexl Arena at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 7 cm).

    What altitude is Wexl Arena at?

    The top is at 1,193 m and the base at 842 m.

    Can beginners ski at Wexl Arena?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 2.1 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Wexl Arena?

    Stuhleck, 19 km to the west, with about 22 km of pistes.
