    Isny / Felderhalde in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Isny / Felderhalde (about 744 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 7 cm of cover (1 cm to 15 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 8 cm (1 cm–19 cm).

    The snowiest month is January, when the pack peaks at about 9 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 0 % of winters, and by 1 January in 0 %.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 55 cm. Worst: 2019-20, which never passed 6 cm.

    A 30 cm base is rare here: most winters stay below it.

    About Isny / Felderhalde

    Isny / Felderhalde is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 718 m up to 771 m. Its nearest neighbour is Iberg, 5.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Isny / Felderhalde

    53 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 718 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Thaler Höhe, for one, climbs to 1,163 m.
