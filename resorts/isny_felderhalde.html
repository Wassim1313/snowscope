    The mountain at Isny / Felderhalde

    53 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 718 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Thaler Höhe, for one, climbs to 1,163 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Schwarzer Grat, 1,119 m high and 7.1 km east of the resort.
