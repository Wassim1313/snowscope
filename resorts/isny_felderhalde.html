    About Isny / Felderhalde

    Isny / Felderhalde is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 718 m up to 771 m. Its nearest neighbour is Iberg, 5.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Isny / Felderhalde

    The mountain at Isny / Felderhalde

    53 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 718 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Thaler Höhe, for one, climbs to 1,163 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Schwarzer Grat, 1,119 m high and 7.1 km east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 744 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 9 cm, around 30 December. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and late February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 28 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 7 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 22 cm).

    Isny / Felderhalde: common questions

    When does Isny / Felderhalde have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and late February.

    Can you ski at Isny / Felderhalde at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Isny / Felderhalde?

    Its highest lift reaches 771 m; the lowest starts at 718 m (53 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Isny / Felderhalde?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Isny / Felderhalde?

    Iberg, 5.5 km to the south, with about 3.0 km of pistes.
