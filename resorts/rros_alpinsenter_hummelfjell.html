    About Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The slopes start at 672 m and reach 861 m at the top. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hummelfjell, 0.1 km to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell has 13 graded runs adding up to about 6.3 km. The largest share is blue, with 3.5 km (56%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell gives 189 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gråhøgda at 1,543 m, standing 682 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 766 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 84 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 56 cm and 95 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 48 cm, from 31 cm in a poor year to 69 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 78 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Quick answers

    When does Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 48 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell?

    Between 672 m and 861 m, so 189 m of vertical drop.

    Is Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 3.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Røros Alpinsenter Hummelfjell have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
