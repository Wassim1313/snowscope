    What 46 winters say about Cortina Tofane

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Cortina Tofane (about 1,976 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 39 cm, with a normal range of 25 cm–56 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 57 cm; the middle half of winters range from 48 cm to 76 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 67 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 24 % of winters, and by 1 January in 41 %.

    1990-91 was the big one (168 cm); 2001-02 the lean one (40 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 180 days.

    Questions about Cortina Tofane

    Is there snow in Cortina Tofane at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 39 cm, and only 41 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 19 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Cortina Tofane?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 67 cm). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Cortina Tofane?

    About 67 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 40 cm in the leanest winter (2001-02) and 168 cm in the biggest (1990-91).

    What altitude is Cortina Tofane at?

    Between 1,212 m and 2,739 m, so 1,527 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Cortina Tofane have?

    About 39 km of pistes and 13 lifts.

    About Cortina Tofane

    Cortina Tofane is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 39 km of pistes put it at number 153 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,212 m and 2,739 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cortina d'Ampezzo, 2.5 km to the east.

    How Cortina Tofane is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,527 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,739 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
