    About Nálepkovo

    Nálepkovo is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 533 m to 631 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski centrum Mraznica, 4.9 km to the north-west.

    The mountain at Nálepkovo

    Skied top to bottom, Nálepkovo gives 98 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 533 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Ski Gugel, reach 991 m. The only lift is a button lift.
