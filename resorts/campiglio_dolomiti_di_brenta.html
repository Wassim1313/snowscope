    About Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta

    Around 57 km of marked runs place Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 101 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,513 m up to 2,511 m. Folgarida-Marilleva is the next resort, 6.7 km away to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 88 runs and around 56 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (50%, about 28 km). With 50% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,012 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 58 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Year to year the snow is steady, with 40 cm to 98 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 39 cm, from 13 cm in a poor year to 70 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 64 cm.

    The mountain at Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta

    998 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,513 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 8 gondolas, 10 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 20 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Cima Occidentale di Brenta, 3,107 m high and 7.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 39 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta?

    The top is at 2,511 m and the base at 1,513 m.

    Can beginners ski at Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 28 km, are easy.
