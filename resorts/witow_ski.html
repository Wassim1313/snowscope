    About Witów-Ski

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Witów-Ski one of the smaller places to ski in the Carpathians. The slopes start at 833 m and reach 972 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is UBOC, 1.4 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Witów-Ski

    Skied top to bottom, Witów-Ski gives 139 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 833 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Witów-Ski today: about 9 cm up high. The last seven days brought 13 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.
