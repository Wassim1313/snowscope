    About Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 88 m and 162 m above sea level. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kalpalinna, 33 km to the north.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 125 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 29 January and lasts to 26 March: about 8 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 17 cm; a snowy December gave 29 cm, a dry one 1 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 36 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 13 cm to 55 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 74 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 5 lifts break down into 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    Is there snow at Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 17 cm deep.

    What altitude is Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus at?

    Its highest lift reaches 162 m; the lowest starts at 88 m (74 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 44% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
