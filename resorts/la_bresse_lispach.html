    About La Bresse

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, La Bresse sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 909 m to 1,116 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gérardmer, 3.1 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at La Bresse

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 3.2 km in total. Most of the length is blue (60%, about 1.9 km). With 80% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,012 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 42 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 13 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 64 cm.

    How La Bresse is laid out

    The vertical drop is 207 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 909 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 5 lifts here are button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hohneck at 1,363 m, standing 247 m above the top lift.

    La Bresse: common questions

    When does La Bresse have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Is there snow at La Bresse at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 4 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at La Bresse?

    The top is at 1,116 m and the base at 909 m.

    Can beginners ski at La Bresse?

    Yes: 80% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near La Bresse?

    La Bresse, 3.4 km to the south-east, with about 24 km of pistes.
