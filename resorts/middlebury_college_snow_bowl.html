    About Middlebury College Snow Bowl

    Middlebury College Snow Bowl is a mid-sized ski area with about 16 km of pistes, number 41 of 93 in US Northeast by length. Its lifts climb from 521 m to 811 m. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Sugarbush Resort, 24 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 24 graded runs, about 16 km in total. The largest share is black diamond, with 6.1 km (38%). Good skiers will like it here; 38% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.1 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 290 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 521 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 3 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Bread Loaf Mountain, 1,165 m high and 7.9 km north of the resort.

    Snow at Middlebury College Snow Bowl through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 666 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to late March, about 9 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 19 cm, from 5 cm in a poor year to 44 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 44 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 18 cm and 71 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.     Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Middlebury College Snow Bowl?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does Middlebury College Snow Bowl have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 19 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Middlebury College Snow Bowl?

    Between 521 m and 811 m, so 290 m of vertical drop.

    Does Middlebury College Snow Bowl have steep runs?

    Yes: about 6.1 km of its graded pistes (38%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Middlebury College Snow Bowl?

    Killington Resort, 38 km to the south, with about 107 km of pistes.
