    About Le Champ du Feu

    Le Champ du Feu is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 582 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 894 m up to 1,066 m. Its nearest neighbour is Le Col des Bagenelles, 26 km south-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 6.8 km. The largest share is red, with 3.0 km (43%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Le Champ du Feu usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 980 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 19 January, with about 4 cm. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 1 cm (snow lay here in 5 winters out of ten), with 18 cm in the best years. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 27 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Le Champ du Feu gives 172 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,094 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: La Bresse, for one, climbs to 1,347 m. Getting up the mountain: 9 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 10 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Champ du Feu (1,099 m), Kreuzberg (1,019 m) and Le Haut des Monts (994 m).

    Le Champ du Feu: common questions

    When does Le Champ du Feu have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Le Champ du Feu at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 5 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Le Champ du Feu?

    The top is at 1,066 m and the base at 894 m.

    Can beginners ski at Le Champ du Feu?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 3.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Le Champ du Feu?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
