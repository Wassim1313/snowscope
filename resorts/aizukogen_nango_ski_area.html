    About Aizukogen Nango

    Aizukogen Nango is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 133 of 385 in Japan by length. The slopes start at 519 m and reach 997 m at the top. Aizu Kogen Daikura is the next resort, 11 km away to the south-east.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 17 km south-east to Aizu Kogen Takatsue, with about 10 km of runs. For higher slopes, Aizu Kogen Takatsue climbs to 1,628 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 478 m: runs of a moderate length. At 519 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Aizukogen Nango today: about 25 cm up high. 22 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Monday.

    Questions about Aizukogen Nango

    What altitude is Aizukogen Nango at?

    Its highest lift reaches 997 m; the lowest starts at 519 m (478 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Aizukogen Nango?

    Aizu Kogen Takatsue, 17 km to the south-east, with about 10 km of pistes.

    How big is Aizukogen Nango?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
