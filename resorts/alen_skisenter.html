    About Ålen skisenter

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Ålen skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 398 m to 724 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hummelfjell, 39 km south, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Ålen skisenter

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 5.4 km to ski. The largest share is green (beginner), with 4.2 km (76%). With 76% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at Ålen skisenter through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 561 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 26 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 30 March, with about 120 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 68 cm and 135 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 63 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 36 cm in a poor year to 93 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 116 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    How Ålen skisenter is laid out

    326 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Storvollhøgda, 1,015 m high and 7.4 km south of the resort.

    Ålen skisenter: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ålen skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Ålen skisenter have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 63 cm deep.

    How high is Ålen skisenter?

    Between 398 m and 724 m, so 326 m of vertical drop.

    Is Ålen skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 76% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ålen skisenter have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
