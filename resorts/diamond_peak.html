    About Diamond Peak

    Diamond Peak is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 112 of 175 in US West by length. Its lifts climb from 2,052 m to 2,598 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mount Rose Ski Tahoe, 8.0 km to the north.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Diamond Peak today: about 75 cm up high. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Monday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (13 cm).

    Diamond Peak and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Northstar at Tahoe Resort (19 km west) has about 83 km of pistes against 15 km here. If snow is the priority, Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley does better, with about 23 weeks of a good base instead of 19. The highest nearby is Mount Rose Ski Tahoe, topping out at 2,916 m.

    The piste map lists 31 graded runs, about 15 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (39%, about 5.6 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 46% of the length is graded difficult. Advanced skiers have about 6.7 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    546 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 2,052 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The skyline is dominated by Relay Peak, 3,135 m high and 7.3 km north of the resort.

    How much snow Diamond Peak usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,325 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 December and lasts to 23 April: about 19 weeks. A normal season tops out near 105 cm, around 22 February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 28 cm to 189 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 46 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For about 4 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 48 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 125 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February.

    Quick answers

    When does Diamond Peak have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Diamond Peak at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 48 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Diamond Peak?

    Between 2,052 m and 2,598 m, so 546 m of vertical drop.

    Does Diamond Peak have steep runs?

    Yes: about 6.7 km of its graded pistes (46%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Diamond Peak?

    Northstar at Tahoe Resort, 19 km to the west, with about 83 km of pistes.
