    About Biel

    Biel is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 568 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,623 m and reach 1,940 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Ratzi, 2.6 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Biel is laid out

    317 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,623 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,940 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Restaurant Skihaus Eldelweiss (hotel), Alphotel Biel-Kinzig (hotel), Naturfreundehaus Rietlig (mountain hut) and Alp Spilau (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
