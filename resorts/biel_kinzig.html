    About Biel

    Biel is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 568 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,623 m and reach 1,940 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Ratzi, 2.6 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,782 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 November and lasts to the end of May: about 27 weeks. For about 17 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 163 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 80 cm (lean years 41 cm, snowy years 99 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and mid-April. Most winters land between 98 cm and 189 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 13 April, with about 163 cm.

    How Biel is laid out

    317 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,623 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,940 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 9 lifts break down into 7 cable cars, 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The skyline is dominated by Schächentaler Windgällen, 2,764 m high and 6.4 km east of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Restaurant Skihaus Eldelweiss (hotel), Alphotel Biel-Kinzig (hotel), Naturfreundehaus Rietlig (mountain hut) and Alp Spilau (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Biel: common questions

    When does Biel have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Biel at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 80 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Biel?

    The top is at 1,940 m and the base at 1,623 m.

    Who is Biel best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Biel?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
