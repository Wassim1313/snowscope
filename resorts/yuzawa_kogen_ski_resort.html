    About Yuzawa Kogen

    Yuzawa Kogen is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 132 of 385 in Japan by length. Skiing here happens between 348 m and 1,141 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Ippon Sugi (1.2 km, east).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Yuzawa Kogen gives 793 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 348 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.
