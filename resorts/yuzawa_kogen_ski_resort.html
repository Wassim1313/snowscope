    About Yuzawa Kogen

    Yuzawa Kogen is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 132 of 385 in Japan by length. Skiing here happens between 348 m and 1,141 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Ippon Sugi (1.2 km, east).

    The pistes of Yuzawa Kogen

    Yuzawa Kogen has 13 graded runs adding up to about 7.3 km. The largest share is green, with 4.7 km (65%). With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Yuzawa Kogen gives 793 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 348 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 cable car and 6 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns.
