    The snow at Zugspitze, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Zugspitze (1,811 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 68 cm of cover (54 cm to 83 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 135 cm, between 111 cm and 157 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 148 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 57 % of winters, and by 1 January in 89 %.

    The deepest winter was 1998-99 (232 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2024-25 (115 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until late May in a normal winter, roughly 189 days.

    So far this winter: 33 cm on 26 November (usual: 28 cm).

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 68 cm at Christmas, and 89 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 74 cm.

    How deep does the snow get at Zugspitze?

    About Zugspitze

    Neither huge nor tiny, Zugspitze offers about 16 km of pistes (number 364 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 995 m and reach 2,627 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Ehrwalder Alm, 3.3 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Zugspitze

    The vertical drop is 1,632 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 995 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,627 m. The lift system counts 40 lifts: 4 cable cars, 32 mountain railways, 2 chairlifts and 2 T-bars. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Zugspitze at 2,962 m, standing 335 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Zugspitze usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,811 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 November and lasts to 28 May: about 26 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 148 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 125 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 68 cm; a snowy December gave 103 cm, a dry one 38 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 98 cm and 175 cm.
