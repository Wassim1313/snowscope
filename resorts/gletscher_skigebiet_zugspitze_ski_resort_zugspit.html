    About Zugspitze

    Neither huge nor tiny, Zugspitze offers about 16 km of pistes (number 364 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 995 m and reach 2,627 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Ehrwalder Alm, 3.3 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Zugspitze

    The vertical drop is 1,632 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 995 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,627 m. The lift system counts 38 lifts: 4 cable cars, 30 mountain railways, 2 chairlifts and 2 T-bars. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Zugspitze at 2,962 m, standing 335 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Zugspitze usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,811 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 November and lasts to 28 May: about 26 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 148 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 125 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 68 cm; a snowy December gave 103 cm, a dry one 38 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 98 cm and 175 cm.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Zugspitze include Iglu-Dorf Zugspitze (hotel), Münchner Haus (mountain hut), Wiener-Neustädter-Hütte (mountain hut) and Knorrhütte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Zugspitze

    What is the best month to ski at Zugspitze?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Zugspitze at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 68 cm deep.

    What altitude is Zugspitze at?

    Between 995 m and 2,627 m, so 1,632 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Zugspitze best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 75% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Zugspitze?

    Garmisch-Classic, 8.3 km to the north-east, with about 38 km of pistes.
