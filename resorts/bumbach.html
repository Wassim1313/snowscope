    About Bumbach

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Bumbach sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 916 m and reach 1,355 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Marbachegg, 4.1 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 5 runs and around 5.0 km to ski. Most of the length is red (56%, about 2.8 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Bumbach today and the next days

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Bumbach gives 439 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 916 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Sörenberg, reach 2,257 m. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. The highest named summit within 8 km is Furggenguetsch at 2,197 m, standing 842 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 9 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,136 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to mid-March. Most winters land between 17 cm and 54 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 26 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 21 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 33 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 37 cm, around 18 February.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Bumbach?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Bumbach have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 21 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Bumbach at?

    Between 916 m and 1,355 m, so 439 m of vertical drop.

    Is Bumbach good for beginners?

    Yes: 44% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bumbach?

    Sörenberg, 11 km to the east, with about 38 km of pistes.
