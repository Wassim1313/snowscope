    About Mosorny Groń

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Mosorny Groń one of the smaller places to ski in the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 713 m up to 1,044 m. Its nearest neighbour is Wyciągi Baca, 3.2 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 331 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 713 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 1,044 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.4 km to ski. Most of the length is red (100%, about 1.4 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.
