    The snow at Le Grand-Bornand, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Le Grand-Bornand (1,479 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 61 cm of cover (37 cm to 82 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 118 cm, between 95 cm and 139 cm in a normal winter.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 119 cm.

    By 15 December 43 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 74 %.

    The deepest winter was 1983-84 (200 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2022-23 (74 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to late May (175 days).

    So far this winter: 34 cm on 23 November (usual: 20 cm).

    Questions about Le Grand-Bornand

    Can you ski Le Grand-Bornand at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 61 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 74 % of 46 winters. Right now: 56 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Le Grand-Bornand?

    February: the snow peaks at about 119 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to late May.

    How snowy is Le Grand-Bornand?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 119 cm in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 200 cm (1983-84), the thinnest 74 cm (2022-23).

    When does the ski season start at Le Grand-Bornand?

    How high is Le Grand-Bornand?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,027 m; the lowest starts at 931 m (1,096 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Le Grand-Bornand?

    About Le Grand-Bornand

    Le Grand-Bornand is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 59 km of pistes put it at number 96 of 1,056. The slopes start at 931 m and reach 2,027 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Le Reposoir Chalet Neuf, 5.3 km to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Le Grand-Bornand

    Counting every graded piste, there are 114 runs and around 56 km to ski. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 20 km, or 35%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow Le Grand-Bornand usually gets

    At about 1,479 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 1 December and lasts to 23 May: about 25 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 92 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 119 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 61 cm, with 99 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and mid-March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 10 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 71 cm and 157 cm.

    How Le Grand-Bornand is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,096 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 931 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 10 chairlifts, 7 button lifts, 3 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Pointe Percée (2,750 m), 6.6 km to the east, about 723 m higher than the top lift.
