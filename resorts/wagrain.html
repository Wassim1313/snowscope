    Wagrain in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Wagrain (1,270 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 27 cm; half of the winters sit between 16 cm and 35 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 48 cm (37 cm–62 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 54 cm.

    By 15 December 2 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 7 %.

    The deepest winter was 2018-19 (91 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (31 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to late April (135 days).

    So far this winter: 4 cm on 22 November (usual: 8 cm).

    Questions about Wagrain

    Does Wagrain have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 27 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 7 % of 46 winters. Right now: 15 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Wagrain?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 54 cm). For a sure base, mid-December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Wagrain?

    About 54 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late February); between 31 cm in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 91 cm in the biggest (2018-19).

    When does Wagrain have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    When does Wagrain open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    About Wagrain

    With some 39 km of runs, Wagrain belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 735 m to 1,804 m. Its nearest neighbour is Snow Space Salzburg, 2.7 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 109 marked runs cover roughly 38 km. The largest share is blue, with 22 km (58%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Snow at Wagrain through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,270 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late April, about 17 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 27 cm and 68 cm. A normal season tops out near 54 cm, around 28 February. By mid-April the typical depth is 35 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 27 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 7 cm in a poor year to 50 cm in a big one.

    The mountain at Wagrain

    Skied top to bottom, Wagrain gives 1,069 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 735 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 18 lifts: 4 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Sandkogel at 2,249 m, standing 445 m above the top lift.
