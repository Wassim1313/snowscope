    Wagrain in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Wagrain (1,270 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 27 cm; half of the winters sit between 16 cm and 35 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 48 cm (37 cm–62 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 54 cm.

    By 15 December 2 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 7 %.

    The deepest winter was 2018-19 (91 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (31 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to late April (135 days).

    Questions about Wagrain

    Does Wagrain have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 27 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 7 % of 46 winters. Right now: 20 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Wagrain?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 54 cm). For a sure base, mid-December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Wagrain?

    About 54 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late February); between 31 cm in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 91 cm in the biggest (2018-19).

    How high do the lifts go at Wagrain?

    Between 735 m and 1,804 m, so 1,069 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Wagrain?

    Ski amadé, 7.9 km to the east, with about 605 km of pistes.

    How big is Wagrain?

    About 39 km of pistes and 18 lifts.

    About Wagrain

    With some 39 km of runs, Wagrain belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 735 m to 1,804 m. Its nearest neighbour is Snow Space Salzburg, 2.7 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Wagrain

    Skied top to bottom, Wagrain gives 1,069 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 735 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Wagrain hold around 20 cm of snow today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.
