    About L'Abbaye

    L'Abbaye is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 580 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,012 m and reach 1,435 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Dent de Vaulion (5.4 km, north-east).

    The pistes of L'Abbaye

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 6.8 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 4.8 km (70%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Snow at L'Abbaye through a normal winter

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,224 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 86 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 14 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 64 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 37 cm.

    The mountain at L'Abbaye

    The vertical drop is 423 m: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,435 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 4 lifts here are button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Mont Tendre, 1,679 m high and 5.1 km south of the resort.

    L'Abbaye: common questions

    When does L'Abbaye have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    How much snow does L'Abbaye have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    What altitude is L'Abbaye at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,435 m; the lowest starts at 1,012 m (423 m of vertical).

    Is L'Abbaye good for beginners?

    Yes: 70% of the graded pistes, about 4.8 km, are easy.

    How big is L'Abbaye?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
