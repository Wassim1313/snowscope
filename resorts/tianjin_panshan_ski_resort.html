    About Tianjin Panshan

    Tianjin Panshan is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 67 of 92 in China by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 45 m up to 96 m. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Jizhou, 9.4 km to the north-east.

    How Tianjin Panshan is laid out

    51 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 45 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Beijing Yuyang International, for one, climbs to 319 m.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Tianjin Panshan?

    Between 45 m and 96 m, so 51 m of vertical drop.
