    About Hochkar

    Hochkar is a ski area. Its nearest neighbour is Hochkar, 1.6 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,019 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 January and keeps it until about 2 April, roughly 13 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 27 cm, with 59 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 56 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 20 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 23 cm and 90 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Hochkar

    When is the best time to ski at Hochkar?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Is there snow at Hochkar at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 27 cm deep.
