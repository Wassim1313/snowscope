    About Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour

    Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 9.0 km of pistes put it at number 88 of 385. Its lifts climb from 305 m to 1,020 m. The closest other ski area is SAM Hakusan Ichirino (9.3 km, south).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour has 11 graded runs adding up to about 8.9 km. The largest share is green, with 4.1 km (46%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 1.9 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 715 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 305 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.

    Questions about Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour

    How high is Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,020 m; the lowest starts at 305 m (715 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 4.1 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Hakusan Senjo Onsen Seymour have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
