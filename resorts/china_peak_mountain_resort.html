    About China Peak

    With roughly 35 km of runs, China Peak sits in the middle of the size table of US West. Skiing here happens between 2,143 m and 2,630 m above sea level.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of China Peak hold around 74 cm of snow today. 63 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 7 cm by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 6 cm at mid-mountain.

    The pistes of China Peak

    The piste map lists 104 graded runs, about 35 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 18 km, or 51%. Intermediate runs make up 51% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 9.2 km. 0.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    487 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,143 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 6 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 4 moving carpets. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Kaiser Peak, 3,142 m high and 7.9 km north of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,386 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 108 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 27 cm to 181 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the typical depth is 59 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 36 cm, with 104 cm in the best years. For about 4 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Questions about China Peak

    When is the best time to ski at China Peak?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at China Peak at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 36 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is China Peak at?

    Between 2,143 m and 2,630 m, so 487 m of vertical drop.

    Does China Peak have steep runs?

    Yes: about 9.9 km of its graded pistes (28%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does China Peak have?

    About 35 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
