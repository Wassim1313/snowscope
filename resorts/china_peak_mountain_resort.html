    What 46 winters say about China Peak

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of China Peak (about 7,828 ft (2,386 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 14 in (36 cm), with a normal range of 4.3 in (11 cm)–30 in (76 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 36 in (92 cm); the middle half of winters range from 22 in (56 cm) to 51 in (130 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 43 in (108 cm).

    By 15 December 26 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 52 %.

    The deepest winter was 2022-23 (134 in (341 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2013-14 (14 in (36 cm)).

    Expect 12 in (30 cm) or more from mid-December until late April in a normal winter, roughly 139 days.

    So far this winter: 23 in (59 cm) on 19 November (usual: 2.4 in (6 cm)).

    China Peak: quick answers

    Can you ski China Peak at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 14 in (36 cm) at Christmas on average, with a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 52 % of 46 winters. Right now: 29 in (74 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at China Peak?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 43 in (108 cm)). For a sure base, mid-December to late April is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How snowy is China Peak?

    About 43 in (108 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (mid-February); between 14 in (36 cm) in the leanest winter (2013-14) and 134 in (341 cm) in the biggest (2022-23).

    What altitude is China Peak at?

    Between 2,143 m and 2,630 m, so 487 m of vertical drop.

    Does China Peak have steep runs?

    Yes: about 9.9 km of its graded pistes (28%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does China Peak have?

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    487 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,143 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 6 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 4 moving carpets. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Kaiser Peak, 3,142 m high and 7.9 km north of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,386 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to late April. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 108 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 27 cm to 181 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the typical depth is 59 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 36 cm, with 104 cm in the best years. For about 4 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.
