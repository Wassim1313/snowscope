    About Semmering Hirschenkogel

    Neither huge nor tiny, Semmering Hirschenkogel offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 560 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 990 m to 1,328 m. Happylift is the next resort, 0.8 km away to the north.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,159 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 7 January and keeps it until about 5 March, roughly 8 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 7 cm to 71 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 21 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 4 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 37 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    338 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 990 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 gondola and 1 chairlift. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Stuhleck (1,782 m), 6.7 km to the south-west, about 454 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Semmering Hirschenkogel?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to early March.

    Is there snow at Semmering Hirschenkogel at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 21 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Semmering Hirschenkogel at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,328 m; the lowest starts at 990 m (338 m of vertical).

    Is Semmering Hirschenkogel good for beginners?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Semmering Hirschenkogel have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
