    About Misurina

    Misurina is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 662 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,752 m up to 2,110 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cristallo, 7.2 km to the south-west.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Cortina d'Ampezzo: about 113 km of pistes, 11 km to the south-west. The highest nearby is Cortina d'Ampezzo, topping out at 2,739 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    358 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,752 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
