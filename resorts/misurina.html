    About Misurina

    Misurina is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 662 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,752 m up to 2,110 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cristallo, 7.2 km to the south-west.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,931 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-May. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 89 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 85 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 48 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 20 cm, snowy years 90 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 58 cm and 126 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    358 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,752 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 button lift, 2 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Cristallo at 3,221 m, standing 1,111 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Misurina?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Misurina at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 48 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Misurina at?

    The top is at 2,110 m and the base at 1,752 m.

    Who is Misurina best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 70% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Misurina?

    Cortina d'Ampezzo, 11 km to the south-west, with about 113 km of pistes.
