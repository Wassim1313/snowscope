    About Vail

    Only 2 of the 175 ski areas of US West are larger than Vail, which has some 215 km of runs. The slopes start at 2,452 m and reach 3,526 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Beaver Creek Resort (14 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Vail

    In all, 322 marked runs cover roughly 215 km. The largest share is black diamond, with 82 km (38%). Good skiers will like it here; 39% of the runs are steep. Experts can count on some 83 km of difficult runs.

    How Vail is laid out

    1,074 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,452 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. The lift system counts 32 lifts: 2 gondolas, 20 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts and 6 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 6 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Bald Mountain, 3,698 m high and 6.8 km north-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,989 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 November and lasts to 21 May: about 26 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 75 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 35 cm in a poor year to 114 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 91 cm and 166 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 117 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 143 cm.

    Questions about Vail

    When does Vail have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Vail at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 75 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Vail?

    It opened on 13 November 2026.

    What altitude is Vail at?

    Between 2,452 m and 3,526 m, so 1,074 m of vertical drop.

    Does Vail have steep runs?

    Yes: about 83 km of its graded pistes (39%) are difficult.
