    About Sunlight

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sunlight offers about 37 km of pistes (number 62 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 2,480 m to 3,002 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Stout, 16 km north-west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Sunlight

    The piste map lists 67 graded runs, about 37 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 14 km (37%). With 34% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Experts can count on some 13 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,741 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 November and lasts to 22 May: about 26 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 80 cm; a snowy December gave 127 cm, a dry one 41 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 149 cm. For about 13 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Most winters land between 92 cm and 184 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 93 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 522 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,480 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Aspen Snowmass, reach 3,815 m. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts. Above the ski area rises Sunlight Peak (3,223 m), 5.0 km to the north-west, about 221 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Sunlight?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Sunlight have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 80 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Sunlight?

    Between 2,480 m and 3,002 m, so 522 m of vertical drop.

    Does Sunlight have steep runs?

    Yes: about 13 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Sunlight?

    Aspen Snowmass, 40 km to the south-east, with about 120 km of pistes.
