    What 46 winters say about Sulitjelma Fjellandsby

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Sulitjelma Fjellandsby (465 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 93 cm; half of the winters sit between 79 cm and 113 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 146 cm (128 cm–170 cm).

    April is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 168 cm.

    By 15 December 93 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 98 %.

    Best winter on record: 1996-97, peaking at 267 cm. Worst: 2000-01, which never passed 125 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from early November until mid-June in a normal winter, roughly 230 days.

    This winter so far: 47 cm on 27 November, against a usual 61 cm for the date.

    About Sulitjelma Fjellandsby

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Sulitjelma Fjellandsby one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 388 m up to 542 m. The closest other ski area is Holtanlia (34 km, north-west).

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 30 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 465 m in an ordinary winter, from early November to late May or later. Most winters land between 108 cm and 187 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 93 cm, with 142 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. Depth normally peaks in mid-April at roughly 168 cm. For about 22 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. By mid-April the typical depth is 167 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    How Sulitjelma Fjellandsby is laid out

    The vertical drop is 154 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Ávvilumtjåhkkå at 1,050 m, standing 508 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Sulitjelma Fjellandsby include Jakobsbakken fjellsenter (mountain hut) and Sulitjelma hotell (hotel).
