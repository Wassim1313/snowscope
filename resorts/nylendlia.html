    The mountain at Nylendlia

    Skied top to bottom, Nylendlia gives 63 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Marikollen skisenter, reach 342 m. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Røverkollen (404 m), 6.9 km to the west, about 231 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Nylendlia through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 142 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 26 January and keeps it until about 4 March, roughly 5 weeks. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 35 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 10 cm; a snowy December gave 33 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 59 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Nylendlia?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Is there snow at Nylendlia at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 10 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Nylendlia?

    Its highest lift reaches 173 m; the lowest starts at 110 m (63 m of vertical).

    How big is Nylendlia?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
