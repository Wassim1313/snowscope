    About Schauinsland

    Neither huge nor tiny, Schauinsland offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 714 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 984 m and 1,205 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Haldenköpfle (2.6 km, south).

    The pistes of Schauinsland

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 3.4 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.8 km, or 52%. Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    How much snow Schauinsland usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,094 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 41 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 11 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; a snowy December gave 27 cm, a dry one 0 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 221 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 984 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Feldberg, for one, climbs to 1,446 m. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 4 drag lifts and 1 rope tow. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The highest named summit within 8 km is Stübenwasen at 1,389 m, standing 184 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Schauinsland have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Can you ski at Schauinsland at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Schauinsland at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,205 m; the lowest starts at 984 m (221 m of vertical).

    Is Schauinsland suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Schauinsland?

    Todtnauberg, 7.0 km to the south-east, with about 17 km of pistes.
