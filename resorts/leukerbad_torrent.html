    About Leukerbad (Torrent)

    Around 24 km of marked runs place Leukerbad (Torrent) among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 259 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,387 m and 2,596 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Leukerbad, 4.1 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 42 marked runs cover roughly 24 km. Most of the length is red (57%, about 14 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 7.8 km.

    The mountain at Leukerbad (Torrent)

    The vertical drop is 1,209 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,387 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 1 cable car, 2 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Balmhorn (3,697 m), 7.1 km to the north-east, about 1,101 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,992 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 58 cm; a snowy December gave 85 cm, a dry one 32 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 3 April, with about 115 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 112 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 69 cm to 141 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. For about 10 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Questions about Leukerbad (Torrent)

    When is the best time to ski at Leukerbad (Torrent)?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Leukerbad (Torrent) at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 58 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Leukerbad (Torrent)?

    Between 1,387 m and 2,596 m, so 1,209 m of vertical drop.

    Does Leukerbad (Torrent) have steep runs?

    Yes: about 7.8 km of its graded pistes (33%) are difficult.

    How big is Leukerbad (Torrent)?

    About 24 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
