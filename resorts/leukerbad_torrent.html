    About Leukerbad (Torrent)

    Around 24 km of marked runs place Leukerbad (Torrent) among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 259 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,387 m and 2,596 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Leukerbad, 4.1 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Leukerbad (Torrent) today and the next days

    Up high, Leukerbad (Torrent) has roughly 117 cm of snow on the ground today. 33 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 38 cm by Wednesday.

    The mountain at Leukerbad (Torrent)

    The vertical drop is 1,209 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,387 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.
