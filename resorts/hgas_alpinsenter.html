    About Høgås alpinsenter

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Høgås alpinsenter one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. The slopes start at 100 m and reach 235 m at the top. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Haukåsløypa Drammen, 24 km to the north.

    How Høgås alpinsenter compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Kongsberg: about 10 km of pistes, 38 km to the north-west. If snow is the priority, Kongsberg does better, with about 20 weeks of a good base instead of 11. The highest nearby is Kongsberg, topping out at 567 m.

    How much snow Høgås alpinsenter usually gets

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 168 m) in an ordinary winter, from early January to late March. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 5 cm to 81 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 18 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 47 cm in a big one. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 52 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    135 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 235 m is the second highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Hvittingen (404 m), 4.0 km to the north-west, about 169 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Høgås alpinsenter?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Is there snow at Høgås alpinsenter at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 18 cm deep.

    What altitude is Høgås alpinsenter at?

    The top is at 235 m and the base at 100 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Høgås alpinsenter?

    Kongsberg, 38 km to the north-west, with about 10 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Høgås alpinsenter have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
