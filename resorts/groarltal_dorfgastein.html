    The snow at Großarltal-Dorfgastein, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Großarltal-Dorfgastein (about 1,437 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 36 cm of cover (25 cm to 49 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 70 cm (54 cm–84 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 80 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 17 % of winters, and by 1 January in 26 %.

    Best winter on record: 1985-86, peaking at 112 cm. Worst: 2016-17, which never passed 50 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until early May in a normal winter, roughly 167 days.

    This winter so far: 15 cm on 27 November, against a usual 15 cm for the date.

    About Großarltal-Dorfgastein

    Around 52 km of marked runs place Großarltal-Dorfgastein among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 115 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 847 m up to 2,027 m. The closest other ski area is Goldegger Buchberg (10 km, north-west).

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,437 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 20 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 36 cm, with 62 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 41 cm to 94 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 47 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 80 cm, around 29 February.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Großarltal-Dorfgastein gives 1,180 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,047 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 2,027 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 20 lifts break down into 6 gondolas, 7 chairlifts, 5 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Frauenkogel (2,424 m), 7.7 km to the south, about 397 m higher than the top lift.
