    The snow at Großarltal-Dorfgastein, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Großarltal-Dorfgastein (about 1,437 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 36 cm of cover (25 cm to 49 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 70 cm (54 cm–84 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 80 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 17 % of winters, and by 1 January in 26 %.

    Best winter on record: 1985-86, peaking at 112 cm. Worst: 2016-17, which never passed 50 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until early May in a normal winter, roughly 167 days.

    Questions about Großarltal-Dorfgastein

    Is there snow in Großarltal-Dorfgastein at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 36 cm, and only 26 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 21 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Großarltal-Dorfgastein?

    February: the snow peaks at about 80 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to early May.

    How deep does the snow get at Großarltal-Dorfgastein?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 80 cm in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 112 cm (1985-86), the thinnest 50 cm (2016-17).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Großarltal-Dorfgastein gives 1,180 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,047 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 2,027 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
