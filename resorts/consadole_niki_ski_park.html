    About Consadole Niki Ski Park

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Consadole Niki Ski Park sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The slopes start at 36 m and reach 189 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Otaru Tenguyama, 20 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Consadole Niki Ski Park has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.8 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 0.7 km, or 40%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 153 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 36 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kiroro Resort reaches 1,180 m. The only lift is a chairlift.
