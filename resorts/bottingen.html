    About Böttingen

    Compact and local, Böttingen has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 900 m and 964 m above sea level. Aggershausen is the next resort, 2.3 km away to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.1 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 0.4 km (40%). With 31% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs.

    The mountain at Böttingen

    Skied top to bottom, Böttingen gives 64 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,100 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 964 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Lemberg (1,016 m), Hochberg (1,009 m) and Wandbühl (1,007 m).

    Snow at Böttingen through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 932 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm; a snowy December gave 12 cm, a dry one 0 cm. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. The snow is usually deepest around 22 January, with about 5 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 19 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Böttingen?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    How much snow does Böttingen have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Böttingen?

    The top is at 964 m and the base at 900 m.

    Is Böttingen good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 0.3 km of its graded pistes (31%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Böttingen?

    Tieringen, 14 km to the north, with about 2.0 km of pistes.
