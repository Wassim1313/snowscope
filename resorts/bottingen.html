    About Böttingen

    Compact and local, Böttingen has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 900 m and 964 m above sea level. Aggershausen is the next resort, 2.3 km away to the south.

    The mountain at Böttingen

    Skied top to bottom, Böttingen gives 64 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,100 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 964 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
