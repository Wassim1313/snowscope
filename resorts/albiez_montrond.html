    About Albiez-Montrond

    Albiez-Montrond is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 305 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,490 m up to 2,012 m. Les Karellis is the next resort, 4.0 km away to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 23 runs and around 20 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 11 km (55%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Albiez-Montrond today: about 28 cm up high. 34 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 77 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 28 cm now.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    522 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,490 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Orelle reaches 3,221 m. Its 7 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 4 T-bars and 1 button lift. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Pointe de Pierre Fendue (3,037 m), 7.6 km to the south, about 1,025 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Albiez-Montrond through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,751 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 91 cm to 177 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 76 cm (lean years 38 cm, snowy years 113 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 111 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 145 cm.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hôtel Restaurant La Rua (hotel), Le Talapet (guesthouse), Gite de la Villette (guesthouse) and Cabane des chasseurs (mountain hut).

    Albiez-Montrond: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Albiez-Montrond?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Albiez-Montrond at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 76 cm deep.

    What altitude is Albiez-Montrond at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,012 m; the lowest starts at 1,490 m (522 m of vertical).

    Is Albiez-Montrond suitable for families?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Albiez-Montrond?

    Les Sybelles, 9.2 km to the west, with about 153 km of pistes.
