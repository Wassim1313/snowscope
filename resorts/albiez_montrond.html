    About Albiez-Montrond

    Albiez-Montrond is a mid-sized ski area with about 20 km of pistes, number 305 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,490 m up to 2,012 m. Les Karellis is the next resort, 4.0 km away to the east.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Albiez-Montrond today: about 97 cm up high. 11 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 27 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    522 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,490 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Orelle reaches 3,221 m.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hôtel Restaurant La Rua (hotel), Le Talapet (guesthouse), Gite de la Villette (guesthouse) and Cabane des chasseurs (mountain hut).
