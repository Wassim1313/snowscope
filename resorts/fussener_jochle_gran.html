    About Füssener Jöchle-Grän

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Füssener Jöchle-Grän sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,155 m to 1,869 m. Tannheim is the next resort, 3.9 km away to the south-west.

    How much snow Füssener Jöchle-Grän usually gets

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,512 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 63 cm and 137 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 79 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 102 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 51 cm; a snowy December gave 82 cm, a dry one 24 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 2 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Füssener Jöchle-Grän gives 714 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Kellenspitze, 2,238 m high and 3.8 km east of the resort.
