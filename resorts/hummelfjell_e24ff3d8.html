    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Gråhøgda (1,543 m), Elvhøgda (1,326 m) and Skarvhøgda (1,306 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 810 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 2 December and lasts to 13 May: about 23 weeks. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 85 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 49 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 69 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 78 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 57 cm and 96 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find 4 chalets: Volltjønnvollen, Henningsmoen, Bråkåten and Øvensenget.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Hummelfjell?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Hummelfjell at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 49 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
