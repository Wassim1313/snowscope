    About Nollen

    Nollen has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 690 m up to 729 m. Its nearest neighbour is Oberwangen, 13 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Nollen through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 710 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm, with 21 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for late December and mid-January. A normal season tops out near 10 cm, around 19 January. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 26 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    How Nollen is laid out

    The vertical drop is 39 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 690 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Schwellbrunn, for one, climbs to 1,083 m. The only lift is a rope tow. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Homberg (764 m), Uerentaler Berg (741 m) and Nollen (734 m).

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Nollen (hotel), Thurgauer Tiny House (chalet), Hof Wunderli (guesthouse) and Grünegg (hotel).

    Questions about Nollen

    When is the best time to ski at Nollen?

    Aim for late December and mid-January.

    Is there snow at Nollen at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Nollen?

    Its highest lift reaches 729 m; the lowest starts at 690 m (39 m of vertical).

    How big is Nollen?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
