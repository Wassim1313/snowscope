    About Pukkivuori

    Pukkivuori is a ski area. The closest other ski area is Pukkivuori (0.0 km, north-west).

    How much snow Pukkivuori usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 125 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early April, about 13 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 22 cm, with 41 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. A normal season tops out near 44 cm, around 7 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 20 cm and 56 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 22 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Pukkivuori?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    Is there snow at Pukkivuori at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 22 cm deep.
