    About Skilifte Sinswang

    Skilifte Sinswang is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 808 m and 880 m above sea level. Hündle-Thalkirchdorf is the next resort, 5.2 km away to the south-east.

    Skilifte Sinswang and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.2 km south-east to Hündle-Thalkirchdorf, with about 18 km of runs. Steibis has the longer snow season: around 8 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at Skilifte Sinswang. The highest nearby is Steibis, topping out at 1,354 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Skilifte Sinswang has 4 graded runs adding up to about 1.2 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.2 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Skilifte Sinswang

    72 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,008 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Steibis reaches 1,704 m. To get up there are 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 2 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The highest named summit within 8 km is Vorderer Prodel at 1,470 m, standing 590 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Skilifte Sinswang through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 844 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 37 cm. A normal season tops out near 18 cm, around 11 February. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 28 cm). By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Questions about Skilifte Sinswang

    What is the best month to ski at Skilifte Sinswang?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    Is there snow at Skilifte Sinswang at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Skilifte Sinswang at?

    Its highest lift reaches 880 m; the lowest starts at 808 m (72 m of vertical).

    Is Skilifte Sinswang suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilifte Sinswang?

    Hündle-Thalkirchdorf, 5.2 km to the south-east, with about 18 km of pistes.
