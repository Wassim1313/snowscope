    About Arêches Beaufort

    With some 38 km of runs, Arêches Beaufort belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,022 m up to 2,319 m. The closest other ski area is Espace Diamant (13 km, north).

    The mountain at Arêches Beaufort

    Skied top to bottom, Arêches Beaufort gives 1,297 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Paradiski reaches 3,205 m.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 21 km south-east to Paradiski, with about 398 km of runs. For higher slopes, Paradiski climbs to 3,205 m.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are L'Abade (chalet), Le Chornais (hostel), Le Nid d'Aigle (chalet) and Refuge les Arolles (mountain hut).

    Quick answers

    How high is Arêches Beaufort?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,319 m; the lowest starts at 1,022 m (1,297 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Arêches Beaufort?

    Paradiski, 21 km to the south-east, with about 398 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Arêches Beaufort have?

    About 38 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
