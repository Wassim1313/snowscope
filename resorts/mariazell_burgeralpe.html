    About Mariazell

    Mariazell is a mid-sized ski area with about 11 km of pistes, number 467 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 870 m and reach 1,256 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Gemeindealpe Mitterbach (5.6 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 25 runs and around 8.3 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (60%, about 5.0 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 386 m: runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,070 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Vorderer Zeller Hut (1,629 m), 7.6 km to the south-west, about 373 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,063 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 3 February and lasts to 14 March: about 6 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 8 cm to 60 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 12 February. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 21 cm; a snowy December gave 51 cm, a dry one 3 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 5 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then.

    Quick answers

    When does Mariazell have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Mariazell at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 21 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Mariazell at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,256 m; the lowest starts at 870 m (386 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Mariazell?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 5.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Mariazell have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
