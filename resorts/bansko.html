    About Bansko

    Only 3 of the 51 ski areas of the Balkans are larger than Bansko, which has some 34 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 993 m and 2,516 m above sea level. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Dobrinishte, 8.5 km to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Bansko

    Counting every graded piste, there are 40 runs and around 30 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 50%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Bansko is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bansko gives 1,523 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,193 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,516 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 3 drag lifts, 4 rope tows and 1 moving carpet, 19 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Bansko: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Bansko?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,516 m; the lowest starts at 993 m (1,523 m of vertical).

    Is Bansko good for beginners?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 19 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Bansko have?

    About 34 km of pistes and 19 lifts.
