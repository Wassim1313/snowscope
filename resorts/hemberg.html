    About Hemberg

    Compact and local, Hemberg has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,068 m and reach 1,128 m at the top. Hörnli is the next resort, 4.4 km away to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Hemberg has 6 graded runs adding up to about 2.0 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 2.0 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Hemberg

    60 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Stockberg, 1,782 m high and 7.9 km south-east of the resort.

    How Hemberg compares nearby

    By piste length Hemberg comes third among these 7 resorts. If snow is the priority, Tanzboden does better, with about 16 weeks of a good base instead of 2. The highest nearby is Wolzenalp, topping out at 1,461 m.

    Snow this week

    How much snow Hemberg usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,098 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 February and keeps it until about 29 February, roughly 2 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 15 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 15 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 30 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 31 cm. Most winters land between 10 cm and 56 cm by mid-February.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Sonnehöckli (chalet), Pension Missionshaus Alpenblick (hotel), Swiss Views (hotel) and Gasthaus Löwen (hotel).

    Questions about Hemberg

    What is the best month to ski at Hemberg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    Can you ski at Hemberg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 15 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Hemberg?

    Between 1,068 m and 1,128 m, so 60 m of vertical drop.

    Is Hemberg suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Hemberg have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
