    About Luoteis-Himos

    Luoteis-Himos is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 91 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Skiing here happens between 84 m and 222 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Hiihtokeskus Himosvuori, 1.3 km south-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 18 marked runs cover roughly 7.7 km. The largest share is red, with 4.8 km (63%). Intermediate runs make up 63% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 1.7 km of difficult runs.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 153 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to early April, about 14 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 23 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 47 cm. Most winters land between 23 cm and 59 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 22 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 37 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    138 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 4 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Himosvuori (228 m), Kankaanmäki (215 m) and Pyykinvuori (205 m).

    Luoteis-Himos: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Luoteis-Himos?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    How much snow does Luoteis-Himos have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 22 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Luoteis-Himos?

    The top is at 222 m and the base at 84 m.

    What level of skier is Luoteis-Himos best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 63% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Luoteis-Himos have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
