    About Tianshan Tianchi

    Neither huge nor tiny, Tianshan Tianchi offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 47 of 92 in China). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,252 m up to 1,449 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being XueLianShan, 32 km to the south-west.

    The pistes of Tianshan Tianchi

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 3.3 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 2.4 km (74%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    How Tianshan Tianchi is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Tianshan Tianchi gives 197 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 2 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    Questions about Tianshan Tianchi

    How high is Tianshan Tianchi?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,449 m; the lowest starts at 1,252 m (197 m of vertical).

    Is Tianshan Tianchi good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 0.8 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    How big is Tianshan Tianchi?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
