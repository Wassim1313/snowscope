    About Aggershausen

    Aggershausen is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 849 m to 897 m. The closest other ski area is Böttingen (2.3 km, north).

    The pistes of Aggershausen

    Aggershausen has 5 graded runs adding up to about 1.4 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.4 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    48 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 849 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hummelsberg at 1,002 m, standing 105 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 873 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 5 cm, around 23 January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 19 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 11 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Aggershausen: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Aggershausen?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Aggershausen at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Aggershausen?

    Its highest lift reaches 897 m; the lowest starts at 849 m (48 m of vertical).

    Is Aggershausen suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Aggershausen?

    Tieringen, 16 km to the north, with about 2.0 km of pistes.
