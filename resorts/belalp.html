    What 46 winters say about Belalp

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Belalp (about 2,216 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 63 cm, with a normal range of 44 cm–79 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 102 cm; the middle half of winters range from 86 cm to 118 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 111 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    50 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 76 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 2023-24 (174 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2022-23 (85 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until early June in a normal winter, roughly 193 days.

    Belalp: quick answers

    Is there snow in Belalp at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 63 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 76 % of 46 winters. Right now: 84 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Belalp?

    March: the snow peaks at about 111 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to early June.

    How snowy is Belalp?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 111 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 174 cm (2023-24), the thinnest 85 cm (2022-23).

    About Belalp

    Around 40 km of marked runs place Belalp among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 145 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,332 m to 3,101 m. Aletsch Arena is the next resort, 8.0 km away to the east.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,769 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Starting at 1,332 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 3,101 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
