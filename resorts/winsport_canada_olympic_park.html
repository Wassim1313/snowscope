    About WinSport (Canada Olympic Park)

    About 3.0 km of pistes make WinSport (Canada Olympic Park) one of the smaller places to ski in Canada West. The slopes start at 1,126 m and reach 1,267 m at the top.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 10 marked runs cover roughly 2.5 km. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 1.7 km, or 69%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at WinSport (Canada Olympic Park) today: about 5 cm up high. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (9 cm).

    The mountain at WinSport (Canada Olympic Park)

    Skied top to bottom, WinSport (Canada Olympic Park) gives 141 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 5 moving carpets, 7 lifts in all. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,196 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 18 cm, around 8 January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 26 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 36 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Questions about WinSport (Canada Olympic Park)

    What is the best month to ski at WinSport (Canada Olympic Park)?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    How much snow does WinSport (Canada Olympic Park) have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at WinSport (Canada Olympic Park)?

    Between 1,126 m and 1,267 m, so 141 m of vertical drop.

    Is WinSport (Canada Olympic Park) good for beginners?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 1.7 km, are easy.
