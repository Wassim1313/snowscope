    About Ohana Resort

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ohana Resort offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 257 of 385 in Japan). The slopes start at 382 m and reach 588 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Makino Kogen, 7.0 km south-west, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Ohana Resort has roughly 4 cm of snow on the ground today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Friday.

    Lifts and terrain

    206 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 382 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.
