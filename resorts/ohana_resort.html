    About Ohana Resort

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ohana Resort offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 257 of 385 in Japan). The slopes start at 382 m and reach 588 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Makino Kogen, 7.0 km south-west, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Ohana Resort

    Ohana Resort has 6 graded runs adding up to about 3.1 km. The largest share is green, with 1.7 km (55%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    206 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 382 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
