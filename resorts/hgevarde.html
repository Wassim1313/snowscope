    About Høgevarde

    Around 18 km of marked runs place Høgevarde among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 35 by length. The slopes start at 192 m and reach 1,172 m at the top. Norefjell is the next resort, 0.2 km away to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Høgevarde

    Counting every graded piste, there are 32 runs and around 18 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (54%, about 9.8 km). With 79% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 980 m: enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 1,172 m is the highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 3 drag lifts and 1 rope tow, 10 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Agunshaug (1,342 m), 5.6 km to the north-west, about 170 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 682 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 25 weeks of good cover. For about 4 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 104 cm, around 11 March. Most winters land between 70 cm and 136 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 92 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 59 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 95 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    Questions about Høgevarde

    When does Høgevarde have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Høgevarde at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 59 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Høgevarde?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,172 m; the lowest starts at 192 m (980 m of vertical).

    Is Høgevarde good for beginners?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How big is Høgevarde?

    About 18 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
