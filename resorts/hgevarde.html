    About Høgevarde

    Around 18 km of marked runs place Høgevarde among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 35 by length. The slopes start at 192 m and reach 1,172 m at the top. Norefjell is the next resort, 0.2 km away to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 980 m: enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 1,172 m is the highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km.
