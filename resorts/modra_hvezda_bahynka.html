    About Modrá hvězda

    Neither huge nor tiny, Modrá hvězda offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 112 of 257 in Central Europe). The ski area spans the mountain from 609 m up to 727 m. The closest other ski area is Ski areál Sachrovka (0.3 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Modrá hvězda

    The piste map lists 4 graded runs, about 1.9 km in total. Most of the length is blue (83%, about 1.5 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 2.2 km north-east to Skiareál Horní Domky, with about 9.0 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Skiareál Horní Domky does better, with about 9 weeks of a good base instead of 4. The highest nearby is Skiareál Horní Domky, topping out at 1,308 m.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Modrá hvězda today: about 3 cm up high. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 8 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 118 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 609 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 2 button lifts and 2 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 668 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 3 February and lasts to 29 February: about 4 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 34 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 5 cm to 57 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 48 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Modrá hvězda?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    Is there snow at Modrá hvězda at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Modrá hvězda?

    Between 609 m and 727 m, so 118 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Modrá hvězda?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Modrá hvězda?

    Skiareál Horní Domky, 2.2 km to the north-east, with about 9.0 km of pistes.
