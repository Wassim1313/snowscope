    About Pajarito Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pajarito Mountain offers about 34 km of pistes (number 70 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 2,736 m to 3,179 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 48 graded runs, about 31 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (41%, about 13 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 8.5 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Pajarito Mountain through a normal winter

    Count on about 12 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,958 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early January to late March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm; a snowy December gave 75 cm, a dry one 2 cm. A normal season tops out near 66 cm, around 18 February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 4 cm to 163 cm.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Pajarito Mountain has roughly 9 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 16 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 7 cm at mid-mountain.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    443 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 2,736 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. To get up there are 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Caballo Mountain (3,200 m), Pajarito Mountain (3,176 m) and Cerro Rubio (3,169 m).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Pajarito Mountain?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    How much snow does Pajarito Mountain have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 20 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Pajarito Mountain?

    Between 2,736 m and 3,179 m, so 443 m of vertical drop.

    Is Pajarito Mountain good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 8.5 km of its graded pistes (27%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Pajarito Mountain have?

    About 34 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
