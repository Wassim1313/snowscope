    About Zdobnice

    Compact and local, Zdobnice has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Central Europe. The slopes start at 607 m and reach 726 m at the top. Kostelec is the next resort, 1.8 km away to the north.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Deštné v Orlických horách: about 6.0 km of pistes, 8.0 km to the north-west. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. For higher slopes, Orlické Záhoří climbs to 1,022 m.

    Snow at Zdobnice through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 666 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 28 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 37 cm. For the best snow, aim for late December and mid-January. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 16 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    119 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 607 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 3 button lifts and 2 drag lifts, 6 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
