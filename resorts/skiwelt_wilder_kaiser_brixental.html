    About SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser

    SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser ranks number 9 of 1,056 ski areas in the Alps by piste length, with about 238 km of runs. The slopes start at 609 m and reach 1,865 m at the top. Hochfeld is the next resort, 11 km away to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,256 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 609 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 16 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 32 chairlifts, 8 T-bars, 8 button lifts, 2 drag lifts, 5 rope tows and 8 moving carpets, 80 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 8 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Gampenkogel (1,957 m), Fleiding (1,892 m) and Nachtsöllberg (1,886 m).

    How much snow SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,237 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 21 December and keeps it until about 10 May, roughly 20 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 40 cm and 96 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 61 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 33 cm, with 58 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 72 cm.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 434 runs and around 238 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 112 km, or 47%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. The steepest pistes add up to about 8.4 km. 11 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Questions about SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser

    When is the best time to ski at SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Is there snow at SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 33 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026.

    What altitude is SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser at?

    Between 609 m and 1,865 m, so 1,256 m of vertical drop.

    Is SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser good for beginners?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 112 km, are easy.
