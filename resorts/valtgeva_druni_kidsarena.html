    About Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena)

    Neither huge nor tiny, Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena) offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 603 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,449 m up to 2,196 m. The closest other ski area is Disentis/Mustér (3.7 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena)

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 6.8 km. The largest share is blue, with 6.0 km (88%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow at Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena) through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,822 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 December and lasts to 22 May: about 23 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 44 cm (lean years 23 cm, snowy years 56 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 52 cm to 100 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 86 cm, around 24 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 78 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    How Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena) is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 747 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,449 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 cable car, 2 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Oberalpstock, 3,328 m high and 6.6 km north of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Andermatt-Sedrun: about 45 km of pistes, 10.0 km to the west. Gemsstock has the longer snow season: around 28 weeks of solid cover, compared with 23 at Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena). Gemsstock reaches 2,958 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena)?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late May.

    Is there snow at Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena) at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 44 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena)?

    The top is at 2,196 m and the base at 1,449 m.

    Is Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena) suitable for families?

    Yes: 88% of the graded pistes, about 6.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena)?

    Andermatt-Sedrun, 10.0 km to the west, with about 45 km of pistes.
