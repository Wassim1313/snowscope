    About Hurdal Skisenter

    Hurdal Skisenter is a small ski area by the standards of Scandinavia, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 321 m to 523 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hurdal Skisenter, 0.1 km south-west, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 422 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 24 December and keeps it until about 20 April, roughly 17 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 42 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm; a snowy December gave 58 cm, a dry one 11 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 32 cm to 84 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 62 cm, around 29 February. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    The mountain at Hurdal Skisenter

    Skied top to bottom, Hurdal Skisenter gives 202 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 523 m is the second highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Fjellsjøkampen at 812 m, standing 289 m above the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 1.4 km in total. The largest share is red, with 0.6 km (41%). Good skiers will like it here; 31% of the runs are steep.

    Questions about Hurdal Skisenter

    What is the best month to ski at Hurdal Skisenter?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Hurdal Skisenter have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 31 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Hurdal Skisenter at?

    The top is at 523 m and the base at 321 m.

    Does Hurdal Skisenter have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.5 km of its graded pistes (31%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Hurdal Skisenter have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
