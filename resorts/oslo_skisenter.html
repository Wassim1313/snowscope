    About Oslo Skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Oslo Skisenter offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 163 of 262 in Scandinavia). The slopes start at 213 m and reach 370 m at the top. Jerikobakken is the next resort, 5.1 km away to the south-east.

    Snow at Oslo Skisenter through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 292 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 5 January and lasts to 4 April: about 13 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 12 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Most winters land between 16 cm and 75 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. A normal season tops out near 54 cm, around 14 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 22 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 44 cm).

    The pistes of Oslo Skisenter

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 2.8 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 49%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes.

    The mountain at Oslo Skisenter

    Skied top to bottom, Oslo Skisenter gives 157 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The skyline is dominated by Frønsvollsåsen, 475 m high and 7.7 km north-west of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Oslo Skisenter?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    How much snow does Oslo Skisenter have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high is Oslo Skisenter?

    Between 213 m and 370 m, so 157 m of vertical drop.

    Is Oslo Skisenter good for beginners?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Oslo Skisenter have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
