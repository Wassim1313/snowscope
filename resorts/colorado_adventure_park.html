    About Colorado Adventure Park

    Colorado Adventure Park is a ski area. The slopes start at 2,622 m and reach 2,656 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Winter Park Resort (8.6 km, south).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 34 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 2,622 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Loveland Basin, for one, climbs to 3,866 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Morse Mountain at 3,155 m, standing 499 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,639 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to mid-April, about 18 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 87 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm, from 16 cm in a poor year to 57 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 70 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 36 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Colorado Adventure Park?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Colorado Adventure Park have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 36 cm deep.

    How high is Colorado Adventure Park?

    The top is at 2,656 m and the base at 2,622 m.
