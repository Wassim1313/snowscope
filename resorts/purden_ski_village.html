    About Purden Ski Village

    Neither huge nor tiny, Purden Ski Village offers about 20 km of pistes (number 26 of 53 in Canada West). Skiing here happens between 1,042 m and 1,365 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Tabor Mountain, 38 km west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Purden Ski Village

    Counting every graded piste, there are 23 runs and around 20 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 10 km (52%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 4.2 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Purden Ski Village usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,204 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to early May, about 24 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 84 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is usually deepest around 24 March, with about 101 cm. Most winters land between 61 cm and 131 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 68 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 44 cm, snowy years 99 cm).

    The mountain at Purden Ski Village

    From top to bottom that makes 323 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 1 T-bar.

    Quick answers

    When does Purden Ski Village have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to early May.

    Is there snow at Purden Ski Village at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 68 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Purden Ski Village?

    Between 1,042 m and 1,365 m, so 323 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Purden Ski Village best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Purden Ski Village?

    About 20 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
