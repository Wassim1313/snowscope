    About Snowbird

    Around 97 km of marked runs place Snowbird among the larger resorts of US West, number 15 by length. Skiing here happens between 2,365 m and 3,340 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Alta (0.5 km, north-east).

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Snowbird hold around 64 cm of snow today. 20 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 46 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    How Snowbird is laid out

    The vertical drop is 975 m: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,365 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
