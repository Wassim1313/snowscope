    About Snowbird

    Around 97 km of marked runs place Snowbird among the larger resorts of US West, number 15 by length. Skiing here happens between 2,365 m and 3,340 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Alta (0.5 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Snowbird

    Snowbird has 199 graded runs adding up to about 95 km. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 40 km, or 42%. Good skiers will like it here; 61% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 58 km of black or expert runs.

    How Snowbird is laid out

    The vertical drop is 975 m: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,365 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 14 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 10 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Twin Peaks (west) (3,502 m), 2.5 km to the south-west, about 162 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Snowbird usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 2,852 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 November and lasts to 26 May: about 28 weeks. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 114 cm, with 211 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 187 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 127 cm and 274 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 218 cm.

    Questions about Snowbird

    When does Snowbird have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Snowbird at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 114 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Snowbird?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Snowbird?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,340 m; the lowest starts at 2,365 m (975 m of vertical).

    Does Snowbird have steep runs?

    Yes: about 58 km of its graded pistes (61%) are difficult.
