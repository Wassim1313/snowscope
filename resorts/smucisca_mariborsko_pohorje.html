    About Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje

    With some 35 km of runs, Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 327 m up to 1,317 m. Its nearest neighbour is Trije Kralji, 11 km south-west, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje is laid out

    The vertical drop is 990 m: enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 327 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.
