    About Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje

    With some 35 km of runs, Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 327 m up to 1,317 m. Its nearest neighbour is Trije Kralji, 11 km south-west, and 5 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje has 49 graded runs adding up to about 35 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 20 km, or 57%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje is laid out

    The vertical drop is 990 m: enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 327 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 16 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 6 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 6 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Žigartov vrh (1,346 m), Peršetov vrh (1,245 m) and Sedovec (1,231 m).

    How much snow Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 822 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 24 January. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 29 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 4 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), with 19 cm in the best years.

    Questions about Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje

    When does Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,317 m; the lowest starts at 327 m (990 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 21 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Smučišča Mariborsko Pohorje have?

    About 35 km of pistes and 16 lifts.
