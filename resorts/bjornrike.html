    About Björnrike

    Around 22 km of marked runs place Björnrike among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 27 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 480 m up to 948 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Vemdalsskalet, 7.4 km to the north.

    The pistes of Björnrike

    Counting every graded piste, there are 36 runs and around 22 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 7.1 km (33%). With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How Björnrike is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Björnrike gives 468 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 11 drag lifts and 3 moving carpets, 15 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Skorvdalsfjället (1,009 m), Varggranshågna (966 m) and Gråhågna (950 m).

    Snow this week

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 714 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 12 December and keeps it until about 2 May, roughly 20 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 39 cm, with 62 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 52 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 63 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 42 cm to 84 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Björnrike?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Björnrike at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Björnrike?

    The top is at 948 m and the base at 480 m.

    Can beginners ski at Björnrike?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Björnrike have?

    About 22 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
