    About Mt. Baldy Family

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mt. Baldy Family offers about 27 km of pistes (number 21 of 53 in Canada West). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,732 m up to 2,119 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Mt. Baldy Family has 37 graded runs adding up to about 27 km. Most of the length is green circle (40%, about 11 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.6 km.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    How Mt. Baldy Family is laid out

    387 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,732 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Baldy Mountain, 2,310 m high and 2.0 km north-west of the resort.

    How much snow Mt. Baldy Family usually gets

    At about 1,926 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 November and lasts to 16 May: about 25 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 55 cm to 104 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 7 April, with about 97 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 59 cm, with 82 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 91 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Mt. Baldy Family: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Mt. Baldy Family?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Mt. Baldy Family have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 59 cm deep.

    When does Mt. Baldy Family open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Mt. Baldy Family?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,119 m; the lowest starts at 1,732 m (387 m of vertical).

    How big is Mt. Baldy Family?

    About 27 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
