    About Schroon Lake

    Schroon Lake is a ski area. Dynamite Hill is the next resort, 19 km away to the south.

    How much snow Schroon Lake usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 393 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to late March, about 11 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 22 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 42 cm). By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Year to year the snow is steady, with 22 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 48 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    Questions about Schroon Lake

    When does Schroon Lake have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Can you ski at Schroon Lake at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 22 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
