    About Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe)

    Neither huge nor tiny, Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe) offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 75 of 257 in Central Europe). Skiing here happens between 694 m and 811 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski- und Rodelarena Wasserkuppe, 1.7 km north, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe)

    Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe) has 8 graded runs adding up to about 3.7 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 2.4 km, or 65%. Intermediate runs make up 65% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Snow at Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe) through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 752 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 24 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 20 cm). A normal season tops out near 10 cm, around 30 January. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-December and late December. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    117 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 694 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 6 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Ski- und Rodelarena Wasserkuppe reaches 926 m. All 2 lifts here are T-bars.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe)?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    Is there snow at Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe) at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 2 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe) at?

    Between 694 m and 811 m, so 117 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe) best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 65% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Zuckerfeld (Wasserkuppe)?

    Arnsberglifte, 9.8 km to the south, with about 14 km of pistes.
