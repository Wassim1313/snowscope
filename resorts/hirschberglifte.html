    About Hirschberglifte

    Compact and local, Hirschberglifte has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 778 m to 1,187 m. Kirchberglifte is the next resort, 1.5 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Hirschberglifte

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 2.1 km in total. Most of the length is black (33%, about 0.7 km). Good skiers will like it here; 33% of the runs are steep.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 409 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The base at 778 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 3 rope tows, 4 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Risserkogel (1,826 m), 5.7 km to the south-east, about 639 m higher than the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 13 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Hirschberglifte. The last seven days brought 13 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 9 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 982 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 30 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 8 cm and 46 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 9 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 16 February. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February.

    Hirschberglifte: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hirschberglifte?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Can you ski at Hirschberglifte at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    How high is Hirschberglifte?

    Between 778 m and 1,187 m, so 409 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Hirschberglifte?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Hirschberglifte have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
