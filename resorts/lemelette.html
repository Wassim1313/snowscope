    About leMelette

    Neither huge nor tiny, leMelette offers about 12 km of pistes (number 437 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,422 m and 1,731 m above sea level. Monte Valbella (Asiago) is the next resort, 6.0 km away to the south.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 309 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,422 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    The snow right now

    Up high, leMelette has roughly 1 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 9 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Relax&Gourmet (mountain hut), Rifugio Campomulo (mountain hut), Albergo Ristorante Valbella (hotel) and La Lepre Bianca (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
