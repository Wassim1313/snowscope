    About leMelette

    Neither huge nor tiny, leMelette offers about 12 km of pistes (number 437 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,422 m and 1,731 m above sea level. Monte Valbella (Asiago) is the next resort, 6.0 km away to the south.

    The pistes of leMelette

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 12 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 8.2 km, or 71%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.6 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 309 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,422 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 4 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Corno di Campo Bianco (2,044 m), 7.6 km to the north-west, about 313 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,576 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 January and lasts to 14 March: about 6 weeks. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 7 cm to 84 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 10 March, with about 41 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 56 cm, a dry one 1 cm.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Relax&Gourmet (mountain hut), Rifugio Campomulo (mountain hut), Albergo Ristorante Valbella (hotel) and La Lepre Bianca (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does leMelette have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at leMelette at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at leMelette?

    Between 1,422 m and 1,731 m, so 309 m of vertical drop.

    Who is leMelette best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 71% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does leMelette have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
