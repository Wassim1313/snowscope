    About Orlické Záhoří

    Neither huge nor tiny, Orlické Záhoří offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 116 of 257 in Central Europe). Its lifts climb from 740 m to 1,022 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kostelec, 4.5 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Orlické Záhoří gives 282 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 740 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,022 m. The only lift is a chairlift.
