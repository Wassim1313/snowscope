    About Pian delle Betulle

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Pian delle Betulle sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,475 m and reach 1,721 m at the top. Alpe Cainallo is the next resort, 5.7 km away to the south-west.

    How much snow Pian delle Betulle usually gets

    Count on about 15 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,598 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to early April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 17 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 58 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 26 cm, with 73 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 18 cm to 116 cm.

    The pistes of Pian delle Betulle

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 3.1 km to ski. Most of the length is red (90%, about 2.8 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    How Pian delle Betulle is laid out

    246 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,475 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 1 chairlift and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Monte Legnone, 2,609 m high and 7.4 km north of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When does Pian delle Betulle have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    How much snow does Pian delle Betulle have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 26 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Pian delle Betulle?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,721 m; the lowest starts at 1,475 m (246 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Pian delle Betulle best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 90% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Pian delle Betulle?

    Piani di Bobbio, 9.7 km to the south-east, with about 18 km of pistes.
