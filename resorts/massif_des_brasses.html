    About Massif des Brasses

    With roughly 16 km of runs, Massif des Brasses sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,099 m to 1,491 m. The closest other ski area is Hirmentaz (5.9 km, north-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Massif des Brasses has 31 graded runs adding up to about 15 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 6.7 km, or 44%. The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Advanced skiers have about 1.7 km of black or expert runs. 0.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,295 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 12 December and lasts to 14 May: about 22 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 22 February, with about 83 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 75 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 45 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 89 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 35 cm to 118 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 392 m: runs of a moderate length. Its 10 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 9 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Haute-Pointe at 1,958 m, standing 467 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Le Jorat (hotel), Chalet de la Vuagère (mountain hut), Aux Touristes (hotel) and Les Chavannes (hostel).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Massif des Brasses?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Massif des Brasses at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Massif des Brasses?

    Between 1,099 m and 1,491 m, so 392 m of vertical drop.

    Is Massif des Brasses suitable for families?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 7.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Massif des Brasses?

    Les Gets-Morzine, 17 km to the east, with about 95 km of pistes.
