    About Jizerky

    Around 10 km of marked runs place Jizerky among the larger resorts of Central Europe, number 20 by length. The slopes start at 539 m and reach 807 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tanvaldský Špičák, 0.6 km to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Jizerky

    In all, 29 marked runs cover roughly 10 km. The largest share is blue, with 7.0 km (68%). With 68% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Jizerky gives 268 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 539 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 18 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 14 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 673 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 25 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 8 cm, with 41 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 46 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Questions about Jizerky

    What is the best month to ski at Jizerky?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Jizerky at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 8 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Jizerky at?

    Its highest lift reaches 807 m; the lowest starts at 539 m (268 m of vertical).

    Is Jizerky good for beginners?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 7.0 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Jizerky have?

    About 10 km of pistes and 18 lifts.
