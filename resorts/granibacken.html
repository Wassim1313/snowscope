    About Granibacken

    Granibacken has about 0.5 km of pistes. The slopes start at 27 m and reach 71 m at the top. Serena Ski is the next resort, 12 km away to the north.

    How Granibacken is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 44 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus, for one, climbs to 162 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Tränukättbacket (63 m), Härklammenkallio (60 m) and Pöllökallio (60 m).

    Snow at Granibacken through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 49 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 7 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 21 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 48 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 23 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for early January and late February.

    Granibacken: common questions

    When does Granibacken have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and late February.

    Is there snow at Granibacken at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 7 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Granibacken?

    Its highest lift reaches 71 m; the lowest starts at 27 m (44 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Granibacken have?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
