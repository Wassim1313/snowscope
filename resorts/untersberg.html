    About Untersberg

    Untersberg is a ski area. Zinkenlifte is the next resort, 9.4 km away to the south-east.

    How Untersberg is laid out

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Berchtesgadener Hochthron (1,972 m), Hochalpkopf (1,886 m) and Salzburger Hochthron (1,853 m).

    Untersberg today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Untersberg hold around 19 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 26 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (9 cm).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 779 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 January and lasts to 9 March: about 6 weeks. Most winters land between 16 cm and 58 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 34 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), with 38 cm in the best years.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Toni-Lenz-Hütte (mountain hut), Zeppezauerhaus (mountain hut), Hotel Untersberg (hotel) and Landgasthof Fürstenbrunn (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Untersberg

    What is the best month to ski at Untersberg?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Untersberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 18 cm deep.
