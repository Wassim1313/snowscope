    Mammoth Mountain in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Mammoth Mountain (about 9,541 ft (2,908 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 37 in (93 cm); half of the winters sit between 17 in (42 cm) and 55 in (140 cm).

    Mid-February typically sees 64 in (163 cm), between 52 in (131 cm) and 88 in (223 cm) in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 79 in (201 cm).

    63 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 76 % by 1 January.

    2022-23 was the big one (215 in (547 cm)); 2014-15 the lean one (34 in (87 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs late November to late May (189 days).

    So far this winter: 13 in (33 cm) on 27 November (usual: 11 in (27 cm)).

    About Mammoth Mountain

    Around 86 km of marked runs place Mammoth Mountain among the larger resorts of US West, number 17 by length. Skiing here happens between 2,457 m and 3,360 m above sea level. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being June Mountain, 14 km to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Mammoth Mountain

    Counting every graded piste, there are 133 runs and around 82 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 52 km (64%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 13 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    903 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 2,457 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. To get up there are 3 gondolas, 22 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mammoth Mountain (3,355 m), Crystal Crag (3,141 m) and Lincoln Mountain (3,072 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 2,908 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 November and lasts to 27 May: about 26 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 156 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Year to year the snow is steady, with 84 cm to 284 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 93 cm; a snowy December gave 189 cm, a dry one 16 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 18 weeks. A normal season tops out near 201 cm, around 10 March.
