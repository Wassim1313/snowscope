    Mammoth Mountain in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Mammoth Mountain (about 9,541 ft (2,908 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 37 in (93 cm); half of the winters sit between 17 in (42 cm) and 55 in (140 cm).

    Mid-February typically sees 64 in (163 cm), between 52 in (131 cm) and 88 in (223 cm) in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 79 in (201 cm).

    63 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 76 % by 1 January.

    2022-23 was the big one (215 in (547 cm)); 2014-15 the lean one (34 in (87 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs late November to late May (189 days).

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Mammoth Mountain at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 37 in (93 cm) on 25 December, and a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 76 % of 46 winters. Right now: 35 in (88 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Mammoth Mountain?

    March: the snow peaks at about 79 in (201 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to late May.

    How snowy is Mammoth Mountain?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 79 in (201 cm) in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 215 in (547 cm) (2022-23), the thinnest 34 in (87 cm) (2014-15).

    About Mammoth Mountain

    Around 86 km of marked runs place Mammoth Mountain among the larger resorts of US West, number 17 by length. Skiing here happens between 2,457 m and 3,360 m above sea level. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being June Mountain, 14 km to the north-west.
