    About Lyžařský vlek Malý Šišák

    Lyžařský vlek Malý Šišák has about 0.3 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,109 m and 1,153 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn, 4.3 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,131 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 January and lasts to 11 March: about 8 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 51 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 6 cm to 87 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 64 cm, a dry one 1 cm.

    The mountain at Lyžařský vlek Malý Šišák

    44 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift.

    Questions about Lyžařský vlek Malý Šišák

    What is the best month to ski at Lyžařský vlek Malý Šišák?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    How much snow does Lyžařský vlek Malý Šišák have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Lyžařský vlek Malý Šišák?

    The top is at 1,153 m and the base at 1,109 m.

    How many km of pistes does Lyžařský vlek Malý Šišák have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
