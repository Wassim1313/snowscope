    About Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf

    With some 35 km of runs, Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 921 m and reach 1,964 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Spechtenseelift (7.9 km, east).

    What there is to ski at Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf

    Counting every graded piste, there are 94 runs and around 35 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 19 km, or 54%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The mountain at Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf

    1,043 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 921 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 5 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 2 moving carpets, 18 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Grimming, 2,351 m high and 7.3 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,442 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to early May, about 19 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 34 cm; a snowy December gave 60 cm, a dry one 9 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 45 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 36 cm and 84 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 72 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Is there snow at Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf?

    The top is at 1,964 m and the base at 921 m.

    Is Tauplitz / Bad Mitterndorf good for beginners?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 19 km, are easy.
