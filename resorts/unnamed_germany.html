    About Spießhorn Menzenschwand

    Spießhorn Menzenschwand is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 602 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 849 m to 1,202 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Spitzenberg und Köpfle, 3.3 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 7.4 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.0 km, or 53%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,026 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 30 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for late December and late January. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm, with 22 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 9 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 353 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The base at 849 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Feldberg (1,494 m), 6.7 km to the north-west, about 292 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Spießhorn Menzenschwand have the most snow?

    Aim for late December and late January.

    Can you ski at Spießhorn Menzenschwand at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Spießhorn Menzenschwand?

    The top is at 1,202 m and the base at 849 m.

    Is Spießhorn Menzenschwand good for beginners?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Spießhorn Menzenschwand?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
