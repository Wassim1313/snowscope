    The snow at Ax 3 Domaines, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Ax 3 Domaines (1,860 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 18 cm; half of the winters sit between 10 cm and 39 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 42 cm, between 18 cm and 67 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 46 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 13 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 11 %.

    The deepest winter was 1980-81 (151 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1989-90 (19 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late December until early April in a normal winter, roughly 110 days.

    So far this winter: 0 cm on 23 November (usual: 5 cm).

    About Ax 3 Domaines

    Only 7 of the 49 ski areas of the Pyrenees are larger than Ax 3 Domaines, which has some 51 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,376 m up to 2,345 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ax 3 Domaines, 5.7 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Ax 3 Domaines

    In all, 72 marked runs cover roughly 51 km. The largest share is blue, with 21 km (40%). With 69% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 969 m: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,376 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 5 button lifts, 3 drag lifts, 3 rope tows and 3 moving carpets, 24 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Pic de Rulhe, 2,785 m high and 7.6 km south-west of the resort.

    How much snow Ax 3 Domaines usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,860 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 January and lasts to 9 March: about 7 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 16 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 8 cm to 94 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 18 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 51 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 46 cm.
