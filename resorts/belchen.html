    About Belchen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Belchen offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 642 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,092 m to 1,355 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Münstertal-Wieden, 2.9 km to the north-east.

    The pistes of Belchen

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 5.1 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 3.8 km (74%). With 74% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 263 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The only lift is a gondola. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Belchen (1,414 m), Trubelsmattkopf (1,281 m) and Heidstein (1,274 m).

    Snow at Belchen through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,224 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 26 cm in a big one. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 49 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 30 January, with about 13 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Belchen?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Can you ski at Belchen at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Belchen?

    The top is at 1,355 m and the base at 1,092 m.

    Is Belchen good for beginners?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 3.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Belchen?

    Todtnauberg, 9.2 km to the north-east, with about 17 km of pistes.
