    How much snow Pian di Sole usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 732 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 28 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 30 January, with about 10 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, with 19 cm in the best years.

    Questions about Pian di Sole

    When is the best time to ski at Pian di Sole?

    Can you ski at Pian di Sole at Christmas?
