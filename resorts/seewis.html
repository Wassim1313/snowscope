    About Seewis

    Seewis is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,012 m to 1,182 m. Its nearest neighbour is Grüsch-Danusa, 3.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Seewis and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 3.5 km south to Grüsch-Danusa, with about 22 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Malbun does better, with about 27 weeks of a good base instead of 7. For higher slopes, Malbun climbs to 1,991 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Seewis has 3 graded runs adding up to about 1.7 km. Most of the length is blue (62%, about 1.0 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Seewis

    170 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Hochwang, reach 2,282 m. The only lift is a T-bar. The skyline is dominated by Vorder Grauspitz, 2,599 m high and 7.8 km north-west of the resort.

    Snow at Seewis through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,097 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 February and lasts to 1 April: about 7 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 26 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Year to year the snow is steady, with 20 cm to 47 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 32 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 20 cm, with 31 cm in the best years.

    Seewis: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Seewis?

    Aim for late February and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to early April.

    Can you ski at Seewis at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Seewis?

    Between 1,012 m and 1,182 m, so 170 m of vertical drop.

    Is Seewis good for beginners?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Seewis?

    Grüsch-Danusa, 3.5 km to the south, with about 22 km of pistes.
