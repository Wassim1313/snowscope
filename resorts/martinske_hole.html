    About Martinské Hole

    Martinské Hole is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 8.0 km of pistes put it at number 28 of 312. The slopes start at 1,149 m and reach 1,452 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Martinské hole (0.0 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Martinské Hole

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 7.5 km. Most of the length is red (60%, about 4.5 km). Intermediate runs make up 60% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The mountain at Martinské Hole

    303 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,452 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 3 button lifts and 2 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.

    Martinské Hole: common questions

    What altitude is Martinské Hole at?

    Between 1,149 m and 1,452 m, so 303 m of vertical drop.

    Is Martinské Hole good for beginners?

    Yes: 40% of the graded pistes, about 3.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Martinské Hole?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
