    About Unterbäch

    Unterbäch is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 37 km of pistes put it at number 171 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,232 m and reach 2,432 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Bürchen (3.9 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 68 graded runs, about 37 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 21 km (56%). With 56% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Snow cannons cover about 10 km of the runs (27%), mostly the busiest runs and the way back to the bottom.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,200 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its 11 lifts break down into 2 cable cars, 3 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Schwarzhorn at 3,201 m, standing 769 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,832 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 54 cm and 100 cm. A normal season tops out near 88 cm, around 6 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 46 cm (lean years 26 cm, snowy years 67 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 78 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Quick answers

    When does Unterbäch have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Unterbäch at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 46 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Unterbäch?

    Between 1,232 m and 2,432 m, so 1,200 m of vertical drop.

    Is Unterbäch suitable for families?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 21 km, are easy.

    Does Unterbäch have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 10 km of its pistes (27%).
