    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 36 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 836 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Wendelstein reaches 1,718 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Rotwand, 1,884 m high and 7.7 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 854 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 3 cm to 41 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 11 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 28 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 15 February.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Pfannilift am Schliersee?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Pfannilift am Schliersee at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).
