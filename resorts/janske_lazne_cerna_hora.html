    About Janské Lázně

    Janské Lázně skis between 630 m and 1,264 m: 634 m of vertical. There are some 18 km of marked runs and 17 lifts. By piste length it is one of the ten biggest ski areas in Central Europe. Its top is higher than most of its neighbours (888 m on average for the closest resorts), so the snow usually lasts longer up there.
