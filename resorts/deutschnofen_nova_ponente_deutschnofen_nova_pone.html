    About Deutschnofen

    Compact and local, Deutschnofen has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,399 m and reach 1,524 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Petersberg, 4.3 km to the west.

    How Deutschnofen compares nearby

    For more terrain, Latemar Dolomites (10.0 km south-east) has about 40 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Jochgrimm does better, with about 20 weeks of a good base instead of 8. The highest nearby is Latemar Dolomites, topping out at 2,423 m.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 5 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Deutschnofen. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (5 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 125 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,399 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Latemar Dolomites, for one, climbs to 2,423 m. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Schwarzhorn - Corno Nero (2,439 m), 7.7 km to the south, about 915 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,462 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 February and keeps it until about 18 April, roughly 8 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 39 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 32 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 23 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 3 cm in a poor year to 60 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 11 cm to 60 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Deutschnofen?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Deutschnofen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 23 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Deutschnofen?

    Between 1,399 m and 1,524 m, so 125 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near Deutschnofen?

    Latemar Dolomites, 10.0 km to the south-east, with about 40 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Deutschnofen have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
