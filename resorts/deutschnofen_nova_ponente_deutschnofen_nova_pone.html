    About Deutschnofen

    Compact and local, Deutschnofen has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,399 m and reach 1,524 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Petersberg, 4.3 km to the west.

    How Deutschnofen compares nearby

    For more terrain, Latemar Dolomites (10.0 km south-east) has about 40 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. The highest nearby is Latemar Dolomites, topping out at 2,423 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 125 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,399 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Latemar Dolomites, for one, climbs to 2,423 m.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Pfösl (hotel), Hotel Regglbergerhof (hotel), Erica (hotel) and Gasthof Stern (guesthouse).
