    About Wapiti

    Wapiti is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 38 of 53 in Canada West by length. The slopes start at 1,359 m and reach 1,650 m at the top.

    How much snow Wapiti usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,504 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 November and keeps it until about 22 May, roughly 27 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 60 cm to 103 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 92 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 58 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 43 cm, snowy years 89 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-March and early April. The snow is usually deepest around 1 April, with about 95 cm.

    The pistes of Wapiti

    Counting every graded piste, there are 21 runs and around 8.7 km to ski. Most of the length is green circle (77%, about 6.7 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Wapiti today and the next days

    How Wapiti is laid out

    291 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,359 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Mount Hadiken (2,886 m), 7.1 km to the west, about 1,236 m higher than the top lift.

    Wapiti: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Wapiti?

    Aim for mid-March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Wapiti at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 58 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Wapiti?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,650 m; the lowest starts at 1,359 m (291 m of vertical).

    Is Wapiti suitable for families?

    Yes: 77% of the graded pistes, about 6.7 km, are easy.

    How big is Wapiti?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
