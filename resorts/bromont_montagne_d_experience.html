    About Bromont Montagne d'Expérience

    Bromont Montagne d'Expérience is one of the bigger ski areas of US Northeast: about 46 km of pistes put it at number 11 of 93. The slopes start at 183 m and reach 547 m at the top. Hors-Piste is the next resort, 9.4 km away to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    How much snow Bromont Montagne d'Expérience usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 365 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to late March, about 12 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 31 cm and 77 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 57 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 26 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 46 cm, a dry one 7 cm.

    The mountain at Bromont Montagne d'Expérience

    The vertical drop is 364 m: runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 183 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Jay Peak Resort, reach 1,166 m. The lift system counts 11 lifts: 1 chair-gondola, 7 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mont Brome (554 m), Mont Shefford (526 m) and Pic du Chevreuil (510 m).

    Bromont Montagne d'Expérience: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Bromont Montagne d'Expérience?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Is there snow at Bromont Montagne d'Expérience at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 26 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Bromont Montagne d'Expérience open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026.

    What altitude is Bromont Montagne d'Expérience at?

    Its highest lift reaches 547 m; the lowest starts at 183 m (364 m of vertical).

    Is Bromont Montagne d'Expérience good for beginners?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 19 km, are easy.
