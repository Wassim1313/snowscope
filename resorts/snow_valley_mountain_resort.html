    About Snow Valley

    With roughly 13 km of runs, Snow Valley sits in the middle of the size table of US West. Its lifts climb from 2,050 m to 2,388 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Snow Summit, 13 km to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 39 graded runs, about 13 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 6.8 km (52%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 2.6 km of difficult runs. 1.1 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Snow Valley

    From top to bottom that makes 338 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,050 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its 13 lifts break down into 12 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Butler Peak, 2,571 m high and 5.4 km north-east of the resort.

    How much snow Snow Valley usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,219 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for late January and late February. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 5 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 4 winters out of ten, typically 0 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 15 cm in a big one. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 37 cm.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Dovid Oved Retreat Center (hotel), River Rock Lodge (mountain hut), Sugar Pine Lodge (mountain hut) and Meadow Lark (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Snow Valley

    When is the best time to ski at Snow Valley?

    Aim for late January and late February.

    How much snow does Snow Valley have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 4 winters out of ten, usually about 0 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Snow Valley?

    Between 2,050 m and 2,388 m, so 338 m of vertical drop.

    Is Snow Valley good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 3.8 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Snow Valley?

    Snow Summit, 13 km to the east, with about 20 km of pistes.
