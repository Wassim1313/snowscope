    About Mount Pisgah

    Mount Pisgah is a small ski area by the standards of US Northeast, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 540 m and 624 m above sea level. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Whiteface Mountain, 20 km to the east.

    Mount Pisgah and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Whiteface Mountain (20 km east) has about 37 km of pistes against 2.0 km here. Whiteface Mountain reaches 1,333 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 84 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 540 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 624 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km.
