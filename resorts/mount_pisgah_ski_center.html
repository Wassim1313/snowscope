    About Mount Pisgah

    Mount Pisgah is a small ski area by the standards of US Northeast, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 540 m and 624 m above sea level. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Whiteface Mountain, 20 km to the east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 582 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to early April, about 15 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 10 cm in a poor year to 48 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 84 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 61 cm, around 5 March. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    The pistes of Mount Pisgah

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 1.9 km to ski. Most of the length is black diamond (44%, about 0.8 km). Good skiers will like it here; 44% of the runs are steep.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 84 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 540 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its top at 624 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises McKenzie Mountain (1,165 m), 7.5 km to the east, about 541 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Mount Pisgah

    When does Mount Pisgah have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    How much snow does Mount Pisgah have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 31 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Mount Pisgah?

    The top is at 624 m and the base at 540 m.

    Is Mount Pisgah good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 0.8 km of its graded pistes (44%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Mount Pisgah?

    Whiteface Mountain, 20 km to the east, with about 37 km of pistes.
