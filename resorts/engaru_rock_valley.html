    About Engaru Rock Valley

    Neither huge nor tiny, Engaru Rock Valley offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 136 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 150 m up to 416 m. Gokazan is the next resort, 20 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of Engaru Rock Valley

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 6.3 km in total. Most of the length is green (55%, about 3.5 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Engaru Rock Valley is laid out

    The vertical drop is 266 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 150 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Engaru Rock Valley: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Engaru Rock Valley?

    Its highest lift reaches 416 m; the lowest starts at 150 m (266 m of vertical).

    Is Engaru Rock Valley good for beginners?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 4.3 km, are easy.

    How big is Engaru Rock Valley?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
