    About Pian Neiretto

    Neither huge nor tiny, Pian Neiretto offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 780 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,233 m up to 1,521 m. Its nearest neighbour is Pian del Frais, 19 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Pian Neiretto has 4 graded runs adding up to about 2.8 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.5 km, or 53%. Intermediate runs make up 53% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 1.0 km of difficult runs.

    Pian Neiretto and the resorts around it

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 8 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Pian Neiretto. The last seven days brought 28 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (10 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 288 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Sestriere, reach 2,786 m. To get up there are 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Punta Cristalliera at 2,801 m, standing 1,280 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,377 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 11 cm and 48 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 15 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 39 cm). A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 17 February. By mid-April the typical depth is 17 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 mountain huts: Palazzina Sertorio, Casa Alpina Rifugio Evelina Ostorero, Rifugio Balma and Rifugio Fontana Mura. Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Pian Neiretto

    When does Pian Neiretto have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Can you ski at Pian Neiretto at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 15 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Pian Neiretto?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,521 m; the lowest starts at 1,233 m (288 m of vertical).

    Is Pian Neiretto good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.0 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Pian Neiretto?

    Sestriere, 26 km to the west, with about 58 km of pistes.
