    About Shiga Kogen Nishidateyama

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Shiga Kogen Nishidateyama sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. The slopes start at 1,380 m and reach 1,739 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Shiga Kogen Hoppo Bunadaira (0.9 km, south).

    What there is to ski at Shiga Kogen Nishidateyama

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 4.8 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 2.6 km, or 54%. With 54% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Shiga Kogen Nishidateyama

    From top to bottom that makes 359 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,380 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.
