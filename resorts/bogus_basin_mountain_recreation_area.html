    Bogus Basin Mountain in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Bogus Basin Mountain (6,663 ft (2,031 m)), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 45 in (114 cm); half of the winters sit between 28 in (71 cm) and 69 in (176 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 73 in (186 cm); the middle half of winters range from 52 in (131 cm) to 90 in (229 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 120 in (305 cm).

    By 15 December 76 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 85 %.

    1981-82 was the big one (338 in (858 cm)); 2025-26 the lean one (22 in (57 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to mid-April, about 147 days.

    So far this winter: 10 in (26 cm) on 19 November (usual: 5.9 in (15 cm)).

    Questions about Bogus Basin Mountain

    Is there snow in Bogus Basin Mountain at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 45 in (114 cm) at Christmas, and 85 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 16 in (41 cm).

    What is the best time to ski Bogus Basin Mountain?

    March: the snow peaks at about 120 in (305 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to mid-April.

    How snowy is Bogus Basin Mountain?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 120 in (305 cm) in late March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 338 in (858 cm) (1981-82), the thinnest 22 in (57 cm) (2025-26).

    When does Bogus Basin Mountain have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early February; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-April.

    How high is Bogus Basin Mountain?

    The top is at 2,304 m and the base at 1,758 m.

    Is Bogus Basin Mountain good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 16 km of its graded pistes (27%) are difficult.

    About Bogus Basin Mountain

    Bogus Basin Mountain is one of the bigger ski areas of US West: about 59 km of pistes put it at number 29 of 175. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,758 m up to 2,304 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gateway Parks, 26 km west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Bogus Basin Mountain has 117 graded runs adding up to about 59 km. Most of the length is blue square (60%, about 36 km). Intermediate runs make up 60% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 16 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,031 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 25 November and keeps it until about 18 April, roughly 21 weeks. Most winters land between 58 cm and 258 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 114 cm; a snowy December gave 237 cm, a dry one 33 cm. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 305 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 144 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    How Bogus Basin Mountain is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bogus Basin Mountain gives 546 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,758 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 7 chairlifts and 4 moving carpets, 11 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Shafer Butte (2,308 m), Mores Mountain (2,202 m) and Doe Point (2,151 m).
