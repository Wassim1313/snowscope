    Wendelstein in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Wendelstein (1,253 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 31 cm of cover (14 cm to 48 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 61 cm (41 cm–83 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 69 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 17 % of winters, and by 1 January in 24 %.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 234 cm. Worst: 2006-07, which never passed 24 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until late April in a normal winter, roughly 131 days.

    So far this winter: 4 cm on 22 November (usual: 5 cm).

    Wendelstein: quick answers

    Does Wendelstein have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 31 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 24 % of 46 winters. Right now: 26 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Wendelstein?

    February: the snow peaks at about 69 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to late April.

    How deep does the snow get at Wendelstein?

    About 69 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 24 cm in the leanest winter (2006-07) and 234 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    How high do the lifts go at Wendelstein?

    Between 788 m and 1,718 m, so 930 m of vertical drop.

    Does Wendelstein have steep runs?

    Yes: about 3.4 km of its graded pistes (39%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Wendelstein have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 51 lifts.

    About Wendelstein

    Neither huge nor tiny, Wendelstein offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 535 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 788 m and reach 1,718 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Sudelfeld (3.5 km, south-east).

    Snow at Wendelstein through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,253 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 December and lasts to 23 April: about 18 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 66 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 38 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 26 cm and 106 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 69 cm.

    The mountain at Wendelstein

    930 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 788 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 cable car, 48 mountain railways and 2 drag lifts. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Hochmiesing (1,883 m), 7.1 km to the south-west, about 165 m higher than the top lift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 13 runs and around 8.8 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 5.4 km (61%). Intermediate runs make up 61% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.4 km.
