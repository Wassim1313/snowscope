    About Les Prévondes

    Les Prévondes has about 0.4 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 983 m up to 1,070 m. Les Pléiades is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the north-west.

    Other resorts close by

    If snow is the priority, Rochers-de-Naye does better, with about 26 weeks of a good base instead of 17. For higher slopes, Leysin climbs to 2,192 m.

    How Les Prévondes is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 87 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,183 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Leysin, for one, climbs to 2,192 m. The lift system counts 11 lifts: 10 funiculars and 1 drag lift. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Rochers de Naye (2,042 m), 4.4 km to the south-east, about 972 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Les Prévondes usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,026 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 December and lasts to 18 April: about 17 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 50 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 17 cm to 81 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 30 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 32 cm, with 67 cm in the best years.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Les Prévondes include Grand Hôtel Suisse Majestic (hotel), Hôtel Tralala (hotel), Le Coucou (hotel) and En Baye (chalet).

    Quick answers

    When does Les Prévondes have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Les Prévondes at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 32 cm deep.

    What altitude is Les Prévondes at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,070 m; the lowest starts at 983 m (87 m of vertical).

    How big is Les Prévondes?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
