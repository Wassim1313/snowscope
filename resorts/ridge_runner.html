    How much snow Ridge Runner usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 185 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 7 February, with about 17 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 7 cm, with 23 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 48 cm.
