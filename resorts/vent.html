    About Vent

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Vent sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,883 m up to 2,644 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pitztaler Gletscher, 7.1 km to the north.

    How Vent compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km north-east to Sölden, with about 107 km of runs. For higher slopes, Pitztaler Gletscher climbs to 3,424 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Vent gives 761 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 1,883 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Pitztaler Gletscher, reach 3,424 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Hotel Vent, Hotel Bergwelt, Natur- und Alpinhotel Post and Berghotel Gasthof Gstrein.
