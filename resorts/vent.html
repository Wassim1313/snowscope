    About Vent

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Vent sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,883 m up to 2,644 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pitztaler Gletscher, 7.1 km to the north.

    What there is to ski at Vent

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 9.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 5.0 km (52%). With 52% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Vent compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km north-east to Sölden, with about 107 km of runs. Kaunertaler Gletscher has the longer snow season: around 35 weeks of solid cover, compared with 22 at Vent. For higher slopes, Pitztaler Gletscher climbs to 3,424 m.

    Vent today and the next days

    Up high, Vent has roughly 31 cm of snow on the ground today. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 11 cm by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (26 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Vent gives 761 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 1,883 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Pitztaler Gletscher, reach 3,424 m. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 2 T-bars. Above the ski area rises Wildspitze (3,768 m), 4.1 km to the north-west, about 1,124 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Vent through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,264 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 November and keeps it until about 4 May, roughly 22 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 93 cm, around 15 February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 40 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 51 cm (lean years 30 cm, snowy years 74 cm). Most winters land between 60 cm and 116 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Vent

    When does Vent have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Can you ski at Vent at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 51 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Vent?

    The top is at 2,644 m and the base at 1,883 m.

    Is Vent good for beginners?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 4.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Vent?

    Sölden, 11 km to the north-east, with about 107 km of pistes.
