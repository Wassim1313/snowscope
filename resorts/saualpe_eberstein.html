    About Saualpe Eberstein

    Compact and local, Saualpe Eberstein has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,577 m to 1,717 m. Its nearest neighbour is Klippitztörl, 14 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Saualpe Eberstein through a normal winter

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,647 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early January to late March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 74 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 22 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 57 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 8 cm and 100 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    How Saualpe Eberstein is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Saualpe Eberstein gives 140 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,577 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Ladinger Spitz (2,079 m), 2.8 km to the north-east, about 362 m higher than the top lift.

    Saualpe Eberstein and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Klippitztörl: about 13 km of pistes, 14 km to the north. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 3 of 7). Koralpe reaches 2,065 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Questions about Saualpe Eberstein

    When is the best time to ski at Saualpe Eberstein?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Can you ski at Saualpe Eberstein at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    What altitude is Saualpe Eberstein at?

    Between 1,577 m and 1,717 m, so 140 m of vertical drop.

    Is there a larger resort near Saualpe Eberstein?

    Klippitztörl, 14 km to the north, with about 13 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Saualpe Eberstein have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
