    About Chamrousse

    Chamrousse is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 50 km of pistes put it at number 120 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,411 m up to 2,246 m. Its nearest neighbour is Chamrousse, 0.6 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine (16 km east) has about 163 km of pistes against 50 km here. The highest nearby is Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine, topping out at 3,306 m.

    How Chamrousse is laid out

    835 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,411 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.
