    About Chamrousse

    Chamrousse is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 50 km of pistes put it at number 120 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,411 m up to 2,246 m. Its nearest neighbour is Chamrousse, 0.6 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 120 marked runs cover roughly 50 km. The largest share is blue, with 22 km (45%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,828 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 24 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 74 cm and 161 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 93 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 65 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 22 cm in a poor year to 95 cm in a big one. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 138 cm, around 23 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March.

    How Chamrousse is laid out

    835 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,411 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its 19 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts, 8 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Grande Lauzière (2,741 m), 7.3 km to the north-east, about 495 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Chamrousse?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Chamrousse have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 65 cm deep.

    When does Chamrousse open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    What altitude is Chamrousse at?

    The top is at 2,246 m and the base at 1,411 m.

    Can beginners ski at Chamrousse?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 27 km, are easy.
