    About IIZUNA HIGASHI KOGEN RESORT

    Neither huge nor tiny, IIZUNA HIGASHI KOGEN RESORT offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 109 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 924 m up to 1,470 m. Its nearest neighbour is Iizuna Kogen, 2.7 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.
