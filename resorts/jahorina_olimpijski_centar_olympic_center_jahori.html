    About Jahorina olimpijski centar

    About 35 km of pistes make Jahorina olimpijski centar one of the ten biggest ski areas of the Balkans (number 3). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,305 m up to 1,886 m. Ski centar "Ravna Planina" is the next resort, 6.6 km away to the north.

    What there is to ski at Jahorina olimpijski centar

    In all, 101 marked runs cover roughly 34 km. The largest share is blue, with 17 km (50%). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 2.7 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Jahorina olimpijski centar gives 581 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,305 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,886 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 5 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 7 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 7 conveyor-belt lifts.

    Questions about Jahorina olimpijski centar

    How high do the lifts go at Jahorina olimpijski centar?

    The top is at 1,886 m and the base at 1,305 m.

    Can beginners ski at Jahorina olimpijski centar?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Jahorina olimpijski centar have?

    About 35 km of pistes and 22 lifts.
