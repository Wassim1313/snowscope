    About Vorhegg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Vorhegg offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 681 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 700 m to 1,201 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Embergeralm, 16 km to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Vorhegg

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 4.5 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.5 km, or 100%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 501 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 700 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. The skyline is dominated by Ht. Mooskofel, 2,506 m high and 8.0 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 950 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early February. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 11 cm, with 27 cm in the best years. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 5 cm to 46 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 21 cm.

    Vorhegg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Vorhegg?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Can you ski at Vorhegg at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).

    How high is Vorhegg?

    Between 700 m and 1,201 m, so 501 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Vorhegg best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Vorhegg?

    Nassfeld, 24 km to the south-east, with about 70 km of pistes.
