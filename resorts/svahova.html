    About Svahová

    Svahová has about 0.3 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 697 m to 768 m. Skiareál Pyšná is the next resort, 1.5 km away to the east.

    The mountain at Svahová

    The vertical drop is 71 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 697 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a button lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 732 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 34 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm; a snowy December gave 26 cm, a dry one 0 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 26 January, with about 12 cm.

    Svahová: common questions

    When does Svahová have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Svahová at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Svahová?

    The top is at 768 m and the base at 697 m.

    How many km of pistes does Svahová have?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
