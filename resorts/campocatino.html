    About Campocatino

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Campocatino sits in the middle of the size table of the Apennines. The slopes start at 1,619 m and reach 1,936 m at the top. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Campo Staffi, 9.7 km to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 317 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,619 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 1,936 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Campocatino has roughly 3 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.
