    About Shiga Kogen Ichinose Family

    Neither huge nor tiny, Shiga Kogen Ichinose Family offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 151 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,625 m up to 1,952 m. Its nearest neighbour is Shiga Kogen Tannenomori Okojo, 0.6 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Shiga Kogen Ichinose Family has 9 graded runs adding up to about 5.7 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 3.2 km, or 56%. With 56% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How Shiga Kogen Ichinose Family is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 327 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,625 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.
