    About Whitefish

    Around 84 km of marked runs place Whitefish among the larger resorts of US West, number 20 by length. The slopes start at 1,364 m and reach 2,063 m at the top.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 137 runs and around 84 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 45 km (53%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 23 km.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,714 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May, about 27 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 196 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For about 21 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 214 cm, around 31 March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 115 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 149 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 135 cm and 243 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Whitefish gives 699 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,364 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 16 lifts: 12 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.

    Questions about Whitefish

    When is the best time to ski at Whitefish?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Whitefish at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 115 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Whitefish?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Whitefish?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,063 m; the lowest starts at 1,364 m (699 m of vertical).

    Is Whitefish good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 23 km of its graded pistes (28%) are difficult.
