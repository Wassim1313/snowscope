    What 46 winters say about Whitefish

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Whitefish (about 5,623 ft (1,714 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 45 in (115 cm), with a normal range of 36 in (92 cm)–52 in (133 cm).

    Mid-February typically sees 70 in (177 cm), between 57 in (146 cm) and 82 in (208 cm) in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 84 in (214 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 98 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 100 %.

    Best winter on record: 2010-11, peaking at 156 in (397 cm). Worst: 2014-15, which never passed 70 in (179 cm).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to late May, about 192 days.
