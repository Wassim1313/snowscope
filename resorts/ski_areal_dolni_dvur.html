    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 71 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 561 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Friesovy boudy, reach 1,229 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 1 rope tow, 4 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Snow at Ski areál Dolní Dvůr through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 596 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. A normal season tops out near 23 cm, around 23 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 9 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 32 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 42 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.
