    About Bania Ski

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, Bania Ski sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 707 m and reach 761 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Stacja Narciarska Kotelnica Biał (1.1 km, south-west).

    How Bania Ski is laid out

    54 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 907 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Rusiń-Ski reaches 944 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts.
