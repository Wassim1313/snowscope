    About Lärchenhof

    Lärchenhof is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 687 m up to 824 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hausberglift, 4.5 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Steinplatte Winklmoosalm (7.4 km north-east) has about 37 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Steinplatte Winklmoosalm does better, with about 21 weeks of a good base instead of 0. The highest nearby is Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, topping out at 1,853 m.

    How Lärchenhof is laid out

    137 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 687 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, reach 1,853 m. The only lift is a button lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Zwölferhörndl at 2,105 m, standing 1,281 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 756 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and mid-January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 14 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 34 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 23 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 7 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 43 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Lärchenhof?

    Aim for early January and mid-January.

    Is there snow at Lärchenhof at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 14 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Lärchenhof?

    The top is at 824 m and the base at 687 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Lärchenhof?

    Steinplatte Winklmoosalm, 7.4 km to the north-east, with about 37 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Lärchenhof have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
