    About Gore Mountain

    Only 7 of the 93 ski areas of US Northeast are larger than Gore Mountain, which has some 61 km of runs. The slopes start at 321 m and reach 1,092 m at the top. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Dynamite Hill, 18 km to the east.

    The pistes of Gore Mountain

    Gore Mountain has 103 graded runs adding up to about 61 km. Most of the length is blue square (50%, about 31 km). Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 22 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Gore Mountain gives 771 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. At 321 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 1 gondola, 10 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Gore Mountain (1,086 m), Pete Gay Mountain (944 m) and Height of Land Mountain (897 m).

    Snow at Gore Mountain through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 706 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 6 January and lasts to 30 March: about 12 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 26 cm; a snowy December gave 47 cm, a dry one 8 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 55 cm, around 3 March. Most winters land between 27 cm and 83 cm by mid-February.

    Gore Mountain: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Gore Mountain?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Is there snow at Gore Mountain at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 26 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Gore Mountain?

    The top is at 1,092 m and the base at 321 m.

    Does Gore Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 22 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Gore Mountain have?

    About 61 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
