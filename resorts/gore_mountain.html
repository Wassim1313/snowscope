    Gore Mountain in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Gore Mountain (2,316 ft (706 m)), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 10 in (26 cm) of cover (5.5 in (14 cm) to 14 in (36 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 20 in (51 cm) (16 in (40 cm)–27 in (68 cm)).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 22 in (55 cm).

    4 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 15 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1992-93, peaking at 45 in (114 cm). Worst: 2015-16, which never passed 7.1 in (18 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs late December to late March (98 days).

    Questions about Gore Mountain

    Is there snow in Gore Mountain at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 10 in (26 cm) at Christmas on average, with a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 15 % of 46 winters. Right now: 7.1 in (18 cm).

    What is the best time to ski Gore Mountain?

    February: the snow peaks at about 22 in (55 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late December to late March.

    How deep does the snow get at Gore Mountain?

    About 22 in (55 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 7.1 in (18 cm) in the leanest winter (2015-16) and 45 in (114 cm) in the biggest (1992-93).

    How high do the lifts go at Gore Mountain?

    The top is at 1,092 m and the base at 321 m.

    About 61 km of pistes and 14 lifts.

    About Gore Mountain

    Only 7 of the 93 ski areas of US Northeast are larger than Gore Mountain, which has some 61 km of runs. The slopes start at 321 m and reach 1,092 m at the top. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Dynamite Hill, 18 km to the east.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Gore Mountain gives 771 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. At 321 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
