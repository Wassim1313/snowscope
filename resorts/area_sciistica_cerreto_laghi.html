    About Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi

    Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 538 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,350 m up to 1,844 m. Area Sciistica Ventasso Laghi is the next resort, 10 km away to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 12 marked runs cover roughly 8.2 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.6 km, or 93%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The mountain at Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi

    Skied top to bottom, Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi gives 494 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,350 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Alpe di Succiso at 2,016 m, standing 172 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,597 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 4 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 64 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 5 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 1 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 21 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Questions about Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi

    When does Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    How much snow does Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 5 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,844 m; the lowest starts at 1,350 m (494 m of vertical).

    Who is Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 93% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Area Sciistica Cerreto Laghi have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
