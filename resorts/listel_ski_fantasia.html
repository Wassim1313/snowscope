    About Listel Ski Fantasia

    Listel Ski Fantasia is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 253 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 597 m up to 856 m. Family Snow Park Bandai X2 is the next resort, 6.3 km away to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Listel Ski Fantasia

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 2.9 km to ski. Most of the length is green (72%, about 2.1 km). With 72% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 13 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Listel Ski Fantasia. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 5 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    The mountain at Listel Ski Fantasia

    259 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 597 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Minowa, reach 1,472 m. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
