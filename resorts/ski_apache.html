    About Ski Apache

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski Apache offers about 31 km of pistes (number 77 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 2,943 m and reach 3,490 m at the top.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 45 runs and around 31 km to ski. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 38%. Good skiers will like it here; 38% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 12 km.

    The mountain at Ski Apache

    From top to bottom that makes 547 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,943 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its 9 lifts break down into 1 gondola and 8 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Sierra Blanca Peak at 3,620 m, standing 130 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Ski Apache through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 3,216 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 36 cm). In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and mid-January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 35 cm. Depth normally peaks in early January at roughly 8 cm.

    Ski Apache: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Apache?

    Aim for early January and mid-January.

    Can you ski at Ski Apache at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Ski Apache at?

    The top is at 3,490 m and the base at 2,943 m.

    Is Ski Apache good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 12 km of its graded pistes (38%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Ski Apache have?

    About 31 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
