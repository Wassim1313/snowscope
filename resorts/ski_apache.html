    What 46 winters say about Ski Apache

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Ski Apache (10,551 ft (3,216 m)), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 1.6 in (4 cm) of cover (0 in (0 cm) to 6.7 in (17 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 2 in (5 cm) (0 in (0 cm)–6.7 in (17 cm)).

    The snowiest month is January, when the pack peaks at about 3.1 in (8 cm).

    4 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 7 % by 1 January.

    2009-10 was the big one (48 in (123 cm)); 2017-18 the lean one (2.8 in (7 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs early January to late February (39 days).

    Ski Apache: quick answers

    Does Ski Apache have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 1.6 in (4 cm), and only 7 % of the past 46 winters had 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 6.3 in (16 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Ski Apache?

    January: the snow peaks at about 3.1 in (8 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from early January to late February.

    How snowy is Ski Apache?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 3.1 in (8 cm) in early January. The deepest of 46 winters reached 48 in (123 cm) (2009-10), the thinnest 2.8 in (7 cm) (2017-18).

    What altitude is Ski Apache at?

    The top is at 3,490 m and the base at 2,943 m.

    How many km of pistes does Ski Apache have?

    About 31 km of pistes and 9 lifts.

    About Ski Apache

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski Apache offers about 31 km of pistes (number 77 of 175 in US West). The slopes start at 2,943 m and reach 3,490 m at the top.

    The mountain at Ski Apache

    From top to bottom that makes 547 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 2,943 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter.
