    About Spiazzi di Gromo

    Neither huge nor tiny, Spiazzi di Gromo offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 561 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,159 m and 1,691 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Nuova Lizzola, 6.2 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 8.2 km. The largest share is red, with 6.2 km (76%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 2.0 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Spiazzi di Gromo

    Skied top to bottom, Spiazzi di Gromo gives 532 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 4 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Presolana Occidentale (2,521 m), 7.2 km to the east, about 830 m higher than the top lift.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Monte Pora (13 km south-east) has about 17 km of pistes against 8.0 km here. Monte Pora has the longer snow season: around 20 weeks of solid cover, compared with 6 at Spiazzi di Gromo. Colere reaches 2,174 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 22 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Spiazzi di Gromo. 28 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (8 cm).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,425 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early February to mid-March, about 6 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 17 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 38 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 41 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 14 cm and 62 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 20 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rifugio Vodala (mountain hut), Locanda del Cacciatore (hotel), Cà Rosei (guesthouse) and Baita Valle Azzurra (hostel).

    Questions about Spiazzi di Gromo

    When does Spiazzi di Gromo have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and late February; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    How much snow does Spiazzi di Gromo have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Spiazzi di Gromo?

    The top is at 1,691 m and the base at 1,159 m.

    Who is Spiazzi di Gromo best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 76% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Spiazzi di Gromo?

    Monte Pora, 13 km to the south-east, with about 17 km of pistes.
