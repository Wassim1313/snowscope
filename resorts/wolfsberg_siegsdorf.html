    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 806 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 37 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 17 cm, around 14 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 12 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 29 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.
