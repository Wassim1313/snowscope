    About Monte Bondone

    Neither huge nor tiny, Monte Bondone offers about 12 km of pistes (number 440 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,184 m and 2,087 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Paganella (13 km, north).

    How much snow Monte Bondone usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,636 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 19 December and keeps it until about 23 April, roughly 18 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 67 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 28 cm to 79 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 30 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 58 cm, a dry one 8 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 55 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March.

    The mountain at Monte Bondone

    From top to bottom that makes 903 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Monte Cornetto (2,178 m), Dos d'Abramo (2,140 m) and Cima Verde (2,102 m).

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Folgaria: about 46 km of pistes, 20 km to the south-east. No resort in the list keeps good snow longer: about 18 weeks of solid cover in an ordinary winter.

    Questions about Monte Bondone

    When is the best time to ski at Monte Bondone?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Monte Bondone at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 30 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Monte Bondone?

    The top is at 2,087 m and the base at 1,184 m.

    Who is Monte Bondone best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 68% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Monte Bondone?

    Folgaria, 20 km to the south-east, with about 46 km of pistes.
