    About Virgen

    Virgen is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,109 m and reach 1,179 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Skilifte Prägraten am Großvenedi, 6.3 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Virgen and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Kals-Matrei (12 km east) has about 50 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. Weißsee Gletscherwelt has the longer snow season: around 28 weeks of solid cover, compared with 4 at Virgen. Brunnalm / St. Jakob reaches 2,682 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 70 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Brunnalm / St. Jakob reaches 2,682 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Hoher Eichham (3,371 m), 6.9 km to the north-west, about 2,192 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,144 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 February and keeps it until about 2 March, roughly 4 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 8 February, with about 33 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 22 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 19 cm, with 37 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 20 cm to 52 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Virgen: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Virgen?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    How much snow does Virgen have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 19 cm deep.

    What altitude is Virgen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,179 m; the lowest starts at 1,109 m (70 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Virgen?

    Kals-Matrei, 12 km to the east, with about 50 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Virgen have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
