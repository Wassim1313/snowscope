    About Tarvisio

    With some 27 km of runs, Tarvisio belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 749 m to 1,773 m. Sella Nevea is the next resort, 14 km away to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 80 marked runs cover roughly 26 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 9.3 km, or 35%. With 32% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 8.6 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,024 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 749 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 15 lifts: 1 gondola, 8 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 5 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Jôf Fuart (2,666 m), 6.4 km to the south-west, about 893 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,261 m) in an ordinary winter, from early February to early March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 6 cm to 63 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 16 cm; a snowy December gave 47 cm, a dry one 3 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 34 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 15 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Tarvisio?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Is there snow at Tarvisio at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Tarvisio at?

    The top is at 1,773 m and the base at 749 m.

    Does Tarvisio have steep runs?

    Yes: about 8.6 km of its graded pistes (32%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Tarvisio have?

    About 27 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
