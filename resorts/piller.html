    About Piller

    Piller is a ski area. Venet is the next resort, 5.6 km away to the west.

    Snow at Piller through a normal winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,744 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 40 cm (lean years 21 cm, snowy years 58 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 68 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 78 cm. Most winters land between 51 cm and 97 cm by mid-February.

    Piller today and the next days

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Gasthof Hirschen (guesthouse), Pension Neuner (guesthouse), Neu Amerika (guesthouse) and Naturchalet Pitztal (chalet).

    Questions about Piller

    What is the best month to ski at Piller?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Piller at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 40 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
