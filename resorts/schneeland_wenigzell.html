    About Schneeland Wenigzell

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Schneeland Wenigzell one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 948 m and 1,121 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Strallegg (4.1 km, west).

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Stuhleck (18 km north) has about 22 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. For higher slopes, Stuhleck climbs to 1,767 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 173 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 948 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Stuhleck, reach 1,767 m.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Joglland Hotel Prettenhofer (hotel), Hotel-Gasthof Fast (hotel), Fastenhaus Dunst (hotel) and Gh. Almblick (guesthouse).
