    How Skilift Rabenstein is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Rabenstein gives 41 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 923 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Großer Arber, for one, climbs to 1,437 m. The only lift is a drag lift.
