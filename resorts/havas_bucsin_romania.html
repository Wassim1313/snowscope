    How Havas Bucsin is laid out

    The vertical drop is 135 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,359 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,494 m. The only lift is a button lift.
