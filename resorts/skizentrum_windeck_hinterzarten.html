    About Skizentrum Windeck Hinterzarten

    Skizentrum Windeck Hinterzarten is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 676 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 971 m up to 1,184 m. The closest other ski area is Schwarzenbachlift (5.9 km, south).

    The pistes of Skizentrum Windeck Hinterzarten

    In all, 12 marked runs cover roughly 4.6 km. Most of the length is blue (76%, about 3.5 km). With 76% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Skizentrum Windeck Hinterzarten gives 213 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 971 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 3 lifts here are T-bars. The highest named summit within 8 km is Feldberg at 1,494 m, standing 310 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Skizentrum Windeck Hinterzarten through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,078 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. A normal season tops out near 11 cm, around 21 January. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 26 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 39 cm.
