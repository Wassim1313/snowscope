    About Beckenhalde

    Beckenhalde is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 760 m and 819 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Speicher, 4.0 km to the east.

    What there is to ski at Beckenhalde

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.0 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (67%, about 0.7 km). With 67% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes. 0.3 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 790 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late December and mid-March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 30 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-January at roughly 13 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm, with 23 cm in the best years.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    59 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 760 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Appenzell, for one, climbs to 1,164 m. To get up there are 2 funiculars and 1 button lift. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Gäbris (1,250 m), 7.4 km to the south-east, about 431 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Beckenhalde

    When does Beckenhalde have the most snow?

    Aim for late December and mid-March.

    Is there snow at Beckenhalde at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 8 cm).

    What altitude is Beckenhalde at?

    The top is at 819 m and the base at 760 m.

    Is Beckenhalde suitable for families?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Beckenhalde?

    Appenzell, 10 km to the south, with about 6.0 km of pistes.
