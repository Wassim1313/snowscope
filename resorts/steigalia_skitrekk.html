    About Steigalia skitrekk

    Steigalia skitrekk is a ski area. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Knyken Skisenter, 19 km to the north.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 381 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. For about 7 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 60 cm and 131 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 54 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 30 cm, snowy years 88 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 100 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 107 cm, around 2 March.

    Questions about Steigalia skitrekk

    When does Steigalia skitrekk have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Steigalia skitrekk at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 54 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
