    About Miyagi Zao Shiroishi

    Neither huge nor tiny, Miyagi Zao Shiroishi offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 175 of 385 in Japan). The ski area spans the mountain from 861 m up to 1,131 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Miyagi Zao Eboshi Resort, 6.4 km to the north.

    What there is to ski at Miyagi Zao Shiroishi

    The piste map lists 13 graded runs, about 5.5 km in total. Beginner runs make up the biggest part: 2.1 km, or 39%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Miyagi Zao Shiroishi gives 270 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 861 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.
