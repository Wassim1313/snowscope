    About Estación de Esquí y Montaña de S

    Estación de Esquí y Montaña de S has about 106 km of pistes. The slopes start at 2,138 m and reach 3,300 m at the top.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,162 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,138 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 14 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 5 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts.

    The pistes of Estación de Esquí y Montaña de S

    Estación de Esquí y Montaña de S: common questions

    How high is Estación de Esquí y Montaña de S?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,300 m; the lowest starts at 2,138 m (1,162 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Estación de Esquí y Montaña de S?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 52 km, are easy.

    How big is Estación de Esquí y Montaña de S?

    About 106 km of pistes and 22 lifts.
