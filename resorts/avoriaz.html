    The snow at Avoriaz, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Avoriaz (about 1,703 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 68 cm of cover (43 cm to 95 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 139 cm (109 cm–162 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 146 cm.

    48 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 80 % by 1 January.

    1981-82 was the big one (254 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (96 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to mid-May (172 days).

    This winter so far: 37 cm on 19 November, against a usual 16 cm for the date.

    Questions about Avoriaz

    Is there snow in Avoriaz at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 68 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 80 % of 46 winters. Right now: 35 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Avoriaz?

    March: the snow peaks at about 146 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How snowy is Avoriaz?

    About 146 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 96 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 254 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    When does Avoriaz open for the 2026/27 season?

    How high is Avoriaz?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,250 m; the lowest starts at 1,156 m (1,094 m of vertical).

    Is Avoriaz suitable for families?

    Yes: 81% of the graded pistes, about 63 km, are easy.

    About Avoriaz

    With some 81 km of runs, Avoriaz belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,156 m up to 2,250 m. Its nearest neighbour is Champéry, 3.2 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Avoriaz has 184 graded runs adding up to about 78 km. Most of the length is blue (67%, about 52 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The mountain at Avoriaz

    The vertical drop is 1,094 m: enough height for proper long runs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 40 lifts: 3 gondolas, 19 chairlifts, 11 button lifts and 7 drag lifts. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The skyline is dominated by Dent de Barme, 2,756 m high and 7.1 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Avoriaz usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,703 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 25 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 122 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 68 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 113 cm, a dry one 24 cm. Most winters land between 89 cm and 185 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 146 cm.
