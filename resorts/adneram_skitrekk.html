    About Ådneram Skitrekk

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Ådneram Skitrekk sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 574 m up to 824 m. Fidjeland Skitrekk is the next resort, 7.0 km away to the south.

    What there is to ski at Ådneram Skitrekk

    Ådneram Skitrekk has 29 graded runs adding up to about 6.4 km. Most of the length is blue (62%, about 4.0 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 699 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 24 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 57 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 17 cm, snowy years 106 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. Most winters land between 89 cm and 206 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 16 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 153 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 172 cm.

    How Ådneram Skitrekk is laid out

    250 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 5 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hilleknuten at 1,209 m, standing 385 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Ådneram Skitrekk?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Ådneram Skitrekk have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 57 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Ådneram Skitrekk?

    Its highest lift reaches 824 m; the lowest starts at 574 m (250 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Ådneram Skitrekk?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Ådneram Skitrekk?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
