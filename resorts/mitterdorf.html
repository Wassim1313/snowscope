    About Mitterdorf

    Mitterdorf is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 5.0 km of pistes put it at number 54 of 257. The ski area spans the mountain from 844 m up to 1,142 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skilift Herzogsreut, 5.3 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 5.2 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 2.5 km (48%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 298 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 844 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 3 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts.

    How much snow Mitterdorf usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 993 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 21 February and keeps it until about 26 February, roughly 1 weeks. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 5 cm to 68 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 42 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 30 cm, around 21 February.

    Mitterdorf: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Mitterdorf?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late February to late February.

    Can you ski at Mitterdorf at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Mitterdorf?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,142 m; the lowest starts at 844 m (298 m of vertical).

    Is Mitterdorf suitable for families?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 2.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Mitterdorf?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
