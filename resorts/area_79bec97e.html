    About Гора

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Гора sits in the middle of the size table of Russia. The ski area spans the mountain from 125 m up to 205 m. The closest other ski area is Глушата (0.4 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Гора

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 1.7 km. The largest share is red, with 0.7 km (41%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Гора

    From top to bottom that makes 80 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Иван-гора, for one, climbs to 318 m. The only lift is a T-bar.
