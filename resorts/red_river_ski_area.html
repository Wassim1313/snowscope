    Red River in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Red River (9,462 ft (2,884 m)), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 7.5 in (19 cm), with a normal range of 3.1 in (8 cm)–15 in (39 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 16 in (40 cm); the middle half of winters range from 7.1 in (18 cm) to 27 in (69 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 18 in (45 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 4 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 22 %.

    The deepest winter was 1992-93 (53 in (135 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (7.1 in (18 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late December to mid-April, about 98 days.

    So far this winter: 2.4 in (6 cm) on 27 November (usual: 3.5 in (9 cm)).

    Red River: quick answers

    Is there snow in Red River at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 7.5 in (19 cm) at Christmas on average, with a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 22 % of 46 winters. Right now: 5.9 in (15 cm).

    What is the best time to ski Red River?

    February: the snow peaks at about 18 in (45 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late December to mid-April.

    How snowy is Red River?

    About 18 in (45 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 7.1 in (18 cm) in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 53 in (135 cm) in the biggest (1992-93).

    When does Red River have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    What altitude is Red River at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,132 m; the lowest starts at 2,635 m (497 m of vertical).

    Does Red River have steep runs?

    Yes: about 8.2 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.

    About Red River

    Neither huge nor tiny, Red River offers about 29 km of pistes (number 81 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 2,635 m to 3,132 m. The closest other ski area is Taos Ski Valley (14 km, south).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 82 runs and around 28 km to ski. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 10 km, or 37%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 8.2 km of difficult runs.

    How much snow Red River usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,884 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 January and keeps it until about 1 April, roughly 10 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 45 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 19 cm, with 51 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for early February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 21 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 6 cm to 83 cm.

    The mountain at Red River

    Skied top to bottom, Red River gives 497 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. At 2,635 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. To get up there are 5 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Gold Hill (3,872 m), 7.7 km to the south-west, about 740 m higher than the top lift.
