    About Skeikampen

    Skeikampen is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 149 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 783 m to 1,114 m. Skeikampen Alpinsenter is the next resort, 1.2 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Skeikampen has 6 graded runs adding up to about 4.3 km. The largest share is red, with 3.0 km (69%). Intermediate runs make up 69% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 331 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets, 6 lifts in all. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Prestkampen at 1,244 m, standing 130 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 948 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 18 November and keeps it until about 20 May, roughly 26 weeks. Most winters land between 68 cm and 120 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 88 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 61 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 93 cm, a dry one 37 cm. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 95 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March.

    Skeikampen: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Skeikampen?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Skeikampen have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 61 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Skeikampen?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,114 m; the lowest starts at 783 m (331 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Skeikampen best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Skeikampen?

    Hafjell, 23 km to the south-east, with about 39 km of pistes.
