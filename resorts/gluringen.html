    About Gluringen

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Gluringen one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,329 m to 1,449 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Münster, 4.4 km to the north-east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Aletsch Arena (14 km south-west) has about 90 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Bellwald does better, with about 26 weeks of a good base instead of 14. Aletsch Arena reaches 2,869 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Gluringen hold around 16 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 31 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 11 cm now.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 120 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,329 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Jungfraujoch Snow Fun Park, reach 3,461 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Vorderes Galmihorn (3,507 m), 5.8 km to the north-west, about 2,058 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Gluringen through a normal winter

    At about 1,389 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 January and lasts to 13 April: about 14 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 28 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 25 cm, with 37 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 51 cm. Most winters land between 32 cm and 62 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    Questions about Gluringen

    When does Gluringen have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Gluringen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Gluringen?

    The top is at 1,449 m and the base at 1,329 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Gluringen?

    Aletsch Arena, 14 km to the south-west, with about 90 km of pistes.

    How big is Gluringen?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
