    About Aillon-Margériaz

    Neither huge nor tiny, Aillon-Margériaz offers about 22 km of pistes (number 276 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,386 m to 1,830 m. Its nearest neighbour is Col de Plainpalais, 2.2 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Aillon-Margériaz

    The piste map lists 42 graded runs, about 22 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 71%. With 77% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,608 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 25 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 233 cm, around 11 April. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 224 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. For about 20 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 99 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 184 cm, a dry one 26 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 105 cm and 294 cm.

    How Aillon-Margériaz is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Aillon-Margériaz gives 444 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,386 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,830 m. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 6 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Colombier at 2,045 m, standing 215 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Aillon-Margériaz

    What is the best month to ski at Aillon-Margériaz?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Aillon-Margériaz at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 99 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Aillon-Margériaz?

    Between 1,386 m and 1,830 m, so 444 m of vertical drop.

    Is Aillon-Margériaz suitable for families?

    Yes: 77% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.

    How big is Aillon-Margériaz?

    About 22 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
