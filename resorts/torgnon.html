    About Torgnon

    With roughly 13 km of runs, Torgnon sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,542 m and 2,258 m above sea level. La Magdeleine is the next resort, 5.2 km away to the east.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Torgnon has roughly 18 cm of snow on the ground today. 15 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 39 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    How Torgnon is laid out

    The vertical drop is 716 m: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,542 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 hotels: Hotel Ogli-go, Caprice des neiges, Hotel Ristorante Baita Cian and Panoramique.

    Questions about Torgnon

    How high do the lifts go at Torgnon?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,258 m; the lowest starts at 1,542 m (716 m of vertical).

    How big is Torgnon?
