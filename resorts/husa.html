    About Huså

    Around 13 km of marked runs place Huså among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 50 by length. Its lifts climb from 419 m to 677 m. The closest other ski area is Åre (9.5 km, south).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 13 km. Black runs make up the biggest part: 9.2 km, or 70%. Good skiers will like it here; 70% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 9.2 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Huså hold around 36 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 26 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Snow at Huså through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 548 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 90 cm, around 12 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 49 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 72 cm, a dry one 32 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 86 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 57 cm and 106 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 258 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Åre, for one, climbs to 1,318 m. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Åreskutan, 1,420 m high and 6.2 km south of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 chalets: Torpet, Sportstugan, Lillstugan and Stora Härbret.

    Quick answers

    When does Huså have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Huså at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 49 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Huså?

    Between 419 m and 677 m, so 258 m of vertical drop.

    Does Huså have steep runs?

    Yes: about 9.2 km of its graded pistes (70%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Huså have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 1 lift.
