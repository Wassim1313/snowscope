    About Kaunertaler Gletscher

    Around 31 km of marked runs place Kaunertaler Gletscher among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 198 by length. Its lifts climb from 2,175 m to 3,107 m. The closest other ski area is Schnalstal (12 km, south-east).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,641 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 1 October and lasts to the end of May: about 35 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 35 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 1158 cm and 1213 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 20 March, with about 1220 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 1073 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 937 cm, from 914 cm in a poor year to 955 cm in a big one.

    What there is to ski at Kaunertaler Gletscher

    Counting every graded piste, there are 80 runs and around 31 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 14 km (45%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 5.5 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Kaunertaler Gletscher gives 932 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,175 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet, 9 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Weißkugel - Palla Bianca at 3,738 m, standing 631 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Kaunertaler Gletscher include Langtauferer Hof (hotel), Alpenjuwel (hotel), Rauhekopfhütte (mountain hut) and Gepatsch Alm (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Kaunertaler Gletscher

    When is the best time to ski at Kaunertaler Gletscher?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early October to late May.

    How much snow does Kaunertaler Gletscher have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 937 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Kaunertaler Gletscher at?

    Between 2,175 m and 3,107 m, so 932 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kaunertaler Gletscher suitable for families?

    Yes: 45% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Kaunertaler Gletscher?

    Nauders Bergkastel, 13 km to the west, with about 49 km of pistes.
