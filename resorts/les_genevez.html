    About Les Genevez

    Compact and local, Les Genevez has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,050 m up to 1,171 m. Its nearest neighbour is Tramelan, 5.8 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Bugnenets-Savagnières: about 16 km of pistes, 16 km to the south-west. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell. For higher slopes, Bugnenets-Savagnières climbs to 1,448 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 5 runs and around 1.9 km to ski. Most of the length is red (63%, about 1.2 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Snow at Les Genevez through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,110 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 21 cm). Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 7 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 30 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 121 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Montbautier (1,160 m), Les Craux (1,124 m) and Le Coteniat (900 m).

    Questions about Les Genevez

    When is the best time to ski at Les Genevez?

    Aim for mid-February and early March.

    How much snow does Les Genevez have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Genevez?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,171 m; the lowest starts at 1,050 m (121 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Les Genevez best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 63% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Les Genevez?

    Bugnenets-Savagnières, 16 km to the south-west, with about 16 km of pistes.
