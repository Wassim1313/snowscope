    About Nihonmatsu Shiozawa

    Compact and local, Nihonmatsu Shiozawa has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Japan. The slopes start at 792 m and reach 973 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Adatara Kogen (2.2 km, south-west).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 19 km north-west to Grandeco Snow Resort, with about 13 km of runs. Grandeco Snow Resort reaches 1,581 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    181 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 992 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
