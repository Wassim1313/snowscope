    About La Féclaz

    Neither huge nor tiny, La Féclaz offers about 12 km of pistes (number 434 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,296 m up to 1,529 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Col de Plainpalais, 2.9 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 18 runs and around 12 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 5.3 km, or 43%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,412 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. The snow is usually deepest around 29 March, with about 176 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 50 cm and 200 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 144 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For about 14 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 81 cm (lean years 17 cm, snowy years 173 cm).

    How La Féclaz is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, La Féclaz gives 233 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 chairlift, 3 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mont Margeriaz at 1,845 m, standing 316 m above the top lift.

    Questions about La Féclaz

    What is the best month to ski at La Féclaz?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does La Féclaz have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 81 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is La Féclaz?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,529 m; the lowest starts at 1,296 m (233 m of vertical).

    Is La Féclaz good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 7.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does La Féclaz have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
