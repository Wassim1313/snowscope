    About Edmonton Ski Club

    Edmonton Ski Club is a small ski area by the standards of Canada West, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 625 m and 661 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Sunridge, 7.7 km north-east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Edmonton Ski Club usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 643 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 February and lasts to 20 February: about 2 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 17 cm (lean years 6 cm, snowy years 37 cm). Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 30 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 8 cm to 49 cm in mid-February in most winters. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 36 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 625 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Rabbit Hill Snow Resort, for one, climbs to 702 m. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 T-bar, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Bellamy Hill (686 m), Grierson Hill (678 m) and McDougall Hill (670 m).
