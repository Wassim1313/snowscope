    About Golm

    Golm is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 28 km of pistes put it at number 213 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 650 m to 2,103 m. Its nearest neighbour is Silvretta Montafon, 9.2 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Golm

    The piste map lists 45 graded runs, about 28 km in total. Most of the length is blue (59%, about 16 km). With 59% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How Golm is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Golm gives 1,453 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 650 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 3 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Großer Turm at 2,830 m, standing 727 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,376 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 40 cm (lean years 16 cm, snowy years 58 cm). Most winters land between 48 cm and 103 cm by mid-February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 79 cm, around 28 February. By mid-April the typical depth is 53 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Questions about Golm

    When is the best time to ski at Golm?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Golm have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 40 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Golm at?

    The top is at 2,103 m and the base at 650 m.

    Is Golm good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 16 km, are easy.

    How big is Golm?

    About 28 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
