    About Golm

    Golm is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 28 km of pistes put it at number 213 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 650 m to 2,103 m. Its nearest neighbour is Silvretta Montafon, 9.2 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Golm is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Golm gives 1,453 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 650 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
