    The mountain at Skilift Beuerberg

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Beuerberg gives 38 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 621 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Herzogstandbahn (Walchensee) reaches 1,621 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Fürst Tegernberg (718 m), Hennenbühl (651 m) and Kirnberg (622 m).

    Snow at Skilift Beuerberg through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 640 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 16 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 15 December, with about 6 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-December and early February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 19 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.
