    Lifts and terrain

    98 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 143 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Waterville Valley Resort, for one, climbs to 1,163 m.
