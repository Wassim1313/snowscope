    Snow at Górnik (Rzeczka) through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 504 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 18 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 21 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 20 January, with about 6 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-March.

    When is the best time to ski at Górnik (Rzeczka)?

    Can you ski at Górnik (Rzeczka) at Christmas?
