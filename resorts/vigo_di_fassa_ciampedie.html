    About Vigo di Fassa

    Neither huge nor tiny, Vigo di Fassa offers about 13 km of pistes (number 420 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,331 m and reach 2,095 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Val di Fassa, 5.5 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Val di Fassa (5.5 km north-east) has about 78 km of pistes against 13 km here. The highest nearby is Val di Fassa, topping out at 2,435 m.

    The mountain at Vigo di Fassa

    The vertical drop is 764 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,331 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: San Pellegrino, for one, climbs to 2,513 m.
