    About Vigo di Fassa

    Neither huge nor tiny, Vigo di Fassa offers about 13 km of pistes (number 420 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,331 m and reach 2,095 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Val di Fassa, 5.5 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 13 km. The largest share is blue, with 7.5 km (59%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Vigo di Fassa through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,713 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 30 December and lasts to 3 April: about 13 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 23 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 51 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 19 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 46 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 22 cm and 74 cm.

    The mountain at Vigo di Fassa

    The vertical drop is 764 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,331 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: San Pellegrino, for one, climbs to 2,513 m. All 5 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Above the ski area rises Kesselkogel - Catinaccio d'Antermoia (3,004 m), 4.8 km to the north, about 909 m higher than the top lift.

    Vigo di Fassa: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Vigo di Fassa?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Can you ski at Vigo di Fassa at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 23 cm deep.

    How high is Vigo di Fassa?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,095 m; the lowest starts at 1,331 m (764 m of vertical).

    Is Vigo di Fassa suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 7.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Vigo di Fassa?

    Val di Fassa, 5.5 km to the north-east, with about 78 km of pistes.
