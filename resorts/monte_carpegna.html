    About Monte Carpegna

    Compact and local, Monte Carpegna has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,234 m and 1,390 m above sea level. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Monte Fumaiolo, 21 km to the west.

    Snow at Monte Carpegna in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,312 m stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Monte Nerone (31 km south-east) has about 3.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. The highest nearby is Area Sciistica Campigna, topping out at 1,630 m.

    The mountain at Monte Carpegna

    Skied top to bottom, Monte Carpegna gives 156 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 chairlift, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Monte Carpegna (1,415 m), Testa del Monte (1,406 m) and Monte Carpegna Est (1,406 m).

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Monte Carpegna include Rifugio dell'Eremo (mountain hut), Hotel Ulisse (hotel), Rifugio del Lago di Monte Pietracandella (mountain hut) and Rifugio Fontanelle (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
