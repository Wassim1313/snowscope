    About Parra Snowpark

    Parra Snowpark is a ski area. The slopes start at 109 m and reach 167 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Sotkanrinteet (21 km, east).

    How Parra Snowpark is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Parra Snowpark gives 58 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are T-bars.

    Snow this week

    There is no snow on the ground at Parra Snowpark yet. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 9 cm by Sunday.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 138 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 6 February and lasts to 20 March: about 6 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 32 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 14 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 30 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 12 cm and 49 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 7 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. For the best snow, aim for early January and early February.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Parra Snowpark include 4 chalets: Karhula, Rivitalo, Marjamaa and Yrittäjien mökki.

    Questions about Parra Snowpark

    When does Parra Snowpark have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early February; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Parra Snowpark at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in every winter, typically 14 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Parra Snowpark?

    Between 109 m and 167 m, so 58 m of vertical drop.
