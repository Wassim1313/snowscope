    About Mt. Spokane

    Mt. Spokane is a mid-sized ski area with about 36 km of pistes, number 65 of 175 in US West by length. Its lifts climb from 1,181 m to 1,783 m.

    The pistes of Mt. Spokane

    Counting every graded piste, there are 61 runs and around 36 km to ski. The largest share is black diamond, with 13 km (36%). Good skiers will like it here; 36% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 13 km of black or expert runs.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 602 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 6 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Spokane (1,784 m), Mount Kit Carson (1,600 m) and Beauty Mountain (1,587 m).

    Mt. Spokane today and the next days

The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 13 cm by Friday. An ordinary winter would have around 17 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,482 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 11 December and keeps it until about 20 March, roughly 14 weeks. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 339 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. The snow is usually deepest around 18 February, with about 162 cm. For about 7 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 51 cm, from 12 cm in a poor year to 166 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Mt. Spokane

    When does Mt. Spokane have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Mt. Spokane at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 51 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Mt. Spokane?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,783 m; the lowest starts at 1,181 m (602 m of vertical).

    Is Mt. Spokane good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 13 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Mt. Spokane have?

    About 36 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
