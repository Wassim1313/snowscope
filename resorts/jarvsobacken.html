    About Järvsöbacken

    Neither huge nor tiny, Järvsöbacken offers about 11 km of pistes (number 66 of 262 in Scandinavia). Skiing here happens between 178 m and 370 m above sea level.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 25 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Most of the length is blue (64%, about 6.9 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow Järvsöbacken usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 274 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 January and lasts to 28 March: about 12 weeks. Most winters land between 15 cm and 64 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 7 February, with about 41 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 10 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 22 cm (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 43 cm). For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 17 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Järvsöbacken. The last seven days brought 10 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 10 cm by Friday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 6 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Järvsöbacken gives 192 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 13 lifts break down into 3 chairlifts, 2 button lifts, 4 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Järvsöklacken (390 m), Öjeberget (370 m) and Konnberget (362 m).

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are JBP hotell (hotel), Bergshotellet (hotel), Top Resort Järvsö (hotel) and Järvsö crêperie & nattlogi (chalet).

    Järvsöbacken: common questions

    When does Järvsöbacken have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    Is there snow at Järvsöbacken at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Järvsöbacken?

    Its highest lift reaches 370 m; the lowest starts at 178 m (192 m of vertical).

    Is Järvsöbacken good for beginners?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 7.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Järvsöbacken have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
