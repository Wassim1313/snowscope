    Järvsöbacken in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Järvsöbacken (274 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 22 cm; half of the winters sit between 11 cm and 31 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 40 cm (25 cm–53 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 41 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 2 % of winters, and by 1 January in 7 %.

    The deepest winter was 1981-82 (90 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1991-92 (19 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late December to late March (103 days).

    Questions about Järvsöbacken

    Is there snow in Järvsöbacken at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 22 cm, and only 7 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 29 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Järvsöbacken?

    February: the snow peaks at about 41 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to late March.

    How much snow does Järvsöbacken get?

    About 41 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early February); between 19 cm in the leanest winter (1991-92) and 90 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    How high do the lifts go at Järvsöbacken?

    Its highest lift reaches 370 m; the lowest starts at 178 m (192 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Järvsöbacken have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 13 lifts.

    About Järvsöbacken

    Neither huge nor tiny, Järvsöbacken offers about 11 km of pistes (number 66 of 262 in Scandinavia). Skiing here happens between 178 m and 370 m above sea level.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 29 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Järvsöbacken. The last seven days brought 17 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Järvsöbacken gives 192 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning.
