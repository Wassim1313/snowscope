    About Holtanlia

    Holtanlia is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 52 m up to 388 m. Its nearest neighbour is Vestvatnlia alpinanlegg, 33 km south-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 220 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 9 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 94 cm (lean years 57 cm, snowy years 136 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 183 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 8 April, with about 183 cm. For about 21 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 109 cm and 200 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 336 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 388 m is the third highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a T-bar.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Holtanlia has roughly 17 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 13 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 5 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 51 cm by now.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Holtanlia include Scandic Fauske (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Holtanlia?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Can you ski at Holtanlia at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 94 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Holtanlia?

    Its highest lift reaches 388 m; the lowest starts at 52 m (336 m of vertical).
