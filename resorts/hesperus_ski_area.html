    About Hesperus

    Compact and local, Hesperus has about 8.0 km of runs, small for US West. The slopes start at 2,523 m and reach 2,713 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Chapman Hill, 17 km to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 39 marked runs cover roughly 7.9 km. The largest share is green circle, with 3.8 km (48%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.8 km.

    How Hesperus is laid out

    The vertical drop is 190 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,523 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. The only lift is a chairlift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Baldy Peak at 3,307 m, standing 594 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,618 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 40 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 106 cm). For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 20 cm to 157 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 21 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is usually deepest around 24 February, with about 95 cm.

    Hesperus: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hesperus?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Hesperus at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 40 cm deep.

    How high is Hesperus?

    Between 2,523 m and 2,713 m, so 190 m of vertical drop.

    Is Hesperus suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 3.8 km, are easy.

    How big is Hesperus?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
