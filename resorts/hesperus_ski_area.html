    Hesperus in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Hesperus (8,589 ft (2,618 m)), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 16 in (40 cm); half of the winters sit between 7.5 in (19 cm) and 33 in (83 cm).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 34 in (87 cm) (20 in (50 cm)–54 in (136 cm)).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 37 in (95 cm).

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 30 % of winters, and by 1 January in 54 %.

    Best winter on record: 1992-93, peaking at 98 in (249 cm). Worst: 2024-25, which never passed 13 in (34 cm).

    Expect 12 in (30 cm) or more from early December until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 124 days.

    Hesperus: quick answers

    Is there snow in Hesperus at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 16 in (40 cm) at Christmas on average, with a 20 in (50 cm) base by New Year in 54 % of 46 winters. Right now: 0.4 in (1 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Hesperus?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 37 in (95 cm)). For a sure base, early December to mid-April is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How much snow does Hesperus get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 37 in (95 cm) in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 98 in (249 cm) (1992-93), the thinnest 13 in (34 cm) (2024-25).

    How high is Hesperus?

    Between 2,523 m and 2,713 m, so 190 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Hesperus?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
