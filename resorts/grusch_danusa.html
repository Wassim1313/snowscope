    About Grüsch-Danusa

    Grüsch-Danusa is a mid-sized ski area with about 22 km of pistes, number 285 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 597 m and 1,776 m above sea level. Seewis is the next resort, 3.5 km away to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 22 graded runs, about 22 km in total. The largest share is red, with 16 km (75%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 3.3 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Grüsch-Danusa

    The vertical drop is 1,179 m: enough height for proper long runs. The lowest slopes are at only 597 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars and 3 moving carpets, 9 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Vilan (2,377 m), 6.4 km to the north, about 601 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 15 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Grüsch-Danusa. The last seven days brought 48 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 13 cm at mid-mountain.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 1,186 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 January and lasts to 17 April: about 13 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 24 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 9 cm in a poor year to 35 cm in a big one. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 26 cm and 56 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 32 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is usually deepest around 19 February, with about 39 cm.

    Grüsch-Danusa: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Grüsch-Danusa?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Grüsch-Danusa at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Grüsch-Danusa?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,776 m; the lowest starts at 597 m (1,179 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Grüsch-Danusa best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 75% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Grüsch-Danusa?

    About 22 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
