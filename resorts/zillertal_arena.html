    About Zillertal Arena

    With some 123 km of runs, Zillertal Arena belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 572 m and 2,501 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gerlosstein / Hainzenberg, 11 km to the west.

    How Zillertal Arena is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Zillertal Arena gives 1,929 m of descent: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. At 572 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Its 54 lifts break down into 14 gondolas, 17 chairlifts, 7 T-bars, 3 button lifts, 3 rope tows and 10 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 10 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Ankenkopf, 2,700 m high and 7.7 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,536 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 9 December and keeps it until about 6 May, roughly 21 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 93 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 40 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 68 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 54 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 50 cm and 110 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Zillertal Arena

    When does Zillertal Arena have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Zillertal Arena have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 40 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Zillertal Arena open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high is Zillertal Arena?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,501 m; the lowest starts at 572 m (1,929 m of vertical).

    Who is Zillertal Arena best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 55% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
