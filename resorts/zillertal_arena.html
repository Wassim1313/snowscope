    What 46 winters say about Zillertal Arena

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Zillertal Arena (about 1,536 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 40 cm, with a normal range of 30 cm–56 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 82 cm (64 cm–95 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 93 cm.

    22 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 39 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1999-00, peaking at 143 cm. Worst: 2024-25, which never passed 62 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to early May (170 days).

    Common questions

    Does Zillertal Arena have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 40 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 39 % of 46 winters. Right now: 36 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Zillertal Arena?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 93 cm). For a sure base, late November to early May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Zillertal Arena get?

    About 93 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 62 cm in the leanest winter (2024-25) and 143 cm in the biggest (1999-00).

    How high is Zillertal Arena?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,501 m; the lowest starts at 572 m (1,929 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Zillertal Arena have?

    About 123 km of pistes and 54 lifts.

    About Zillertal Arena

    With some 123 km of runs, Zillertal Arena belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 572 m and 2,501 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gerlosstein / Hainzenberg, 11 km to the west.

    How Zillertal Arena is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Zillertal Arena gives 1,929 m of descent: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. At 572 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point.
