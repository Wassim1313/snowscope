    About Saku Ski Garden Parada

    Saku Ski Garden Parada is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 181 of 385 in Japan by length. The slopes start at 758 m and reach 1,047 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Prince Hotels and Resorts Karuiz (13 km, north-east).

    The pistes of Saku Ski Garden Parada

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 4.6 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 3.3 km, or 72%. With 72% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Saku Ski Garden Parada is laid out

    The vertical drop is 289 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 958 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Takamine Mountain Park reaches 2,046 m. All 8 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.
