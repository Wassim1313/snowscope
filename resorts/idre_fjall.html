    About Idre Fjäll

    Around 34 km of marked runs place Idre Fjäll among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 14 by length. Its lifts climb from 579 m to 890 m. Idre Himmelfjäll is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 55 marked runs cover roughly 34 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 18 km, or 52%. With 72% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Lifts and terrain

    311 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 14 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 3 moving carpets, 24 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Städjan (1,131 m), 4.0 km to the north-east, about 241 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Idre Fjäll usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 734 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 21 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 84 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 56 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 39 cm (lean years 20 cm, snowy years 60 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 21 March, with about 65 cm.

    Questions about Idre Fjäll

    What is the best month to ski at Idre Fjäll?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Idre Fjäll at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Idre Fjäll?

    Between 579 m and 890 m, so 311 m of vertical drop.

    Is Idre Fjäll suitable for families?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 25 km, are easy.

    How big is Idre Fjäll?

    About 34 km of pistes and 24 lifts.
