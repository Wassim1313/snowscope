    The snow at Idre Fjäll, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Idre Fjäll (about 734 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 39 cm; half of the winters sit between 30 cm and 47 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 62 cm; the middle half of winters range from 54 cm to 75 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 65 cm.

    By 15 December 15 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 39 %.

    The deepest winter was 1987-88 (119 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2024-25 (40 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 150 days.

    Skiing Idre Fjäll: the practical side

    The pistes run from 579 m to 890 m (311 m of vertical), with 34 km of marked runs and 24 lifts.

    Questions about Idre Fjäll

    Can you ski Idre Fjäll at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 39 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 39 % of 46 winters. Right now: 6 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Idre Fjäll?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 65 cm). For a sure base, early December to early May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Idre Fjäll get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 65 cm in late March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 119 cm (1987-88), the thinnest 40 cm (2024-25).
