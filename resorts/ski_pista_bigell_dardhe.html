    Heights, vertical and lifts

    86 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,592 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
