    About Ankogel Mallnitz

    Ankogel Mallnitz is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 422 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,271 m and reach 2,630 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Graukogel, 9.8 km to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,359 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Getting up the mountain: 2 cable cars, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet, 6 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Ankogel (3,249 m), 4.5 km to the north-east, about 619 m higher than the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 21 runs and around 12 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 7.4 km, or 62%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.1 km.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,950 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 30 November and keeps it until about 15 May, roughly 24 weeks. For about 4 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 104 cm, around 18 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 96 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 53 cm, with 86 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 58 cm and 125 cm.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hannoverhaus (mountain hut), Jausenstation Raineralm (mountain hut), Schwussnerhütte (mountain hut) and Eggerhof (hotel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Ankogel Mallnitz?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Ankogel Mallnitz have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 53 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Ankogel Mallnitz?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,630 m; the lowest starts at 1,271 m (1,359 m of vertical).

    Is Ankogel Mallnitz good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 3.1 km of its graded pistes (27%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Ankogel Mallnitz?

    Ski Gastein, 15 km to the north-west, with about 74 km of pistes.
