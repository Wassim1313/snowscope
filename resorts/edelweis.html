    About Эдельвейс (Edelweis)

    With some 7.0 km of runs, Эдельвейс (Edelweis) belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Russia. The ski area spans the mountain from 41 m up to 382 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Красная Сопка (Krasnaya Sopka), 3.3 km to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 7.2 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.1 km, or 71%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.1 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 341 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 41 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
