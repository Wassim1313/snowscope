    The mountain at Ski- & Rodelarena Hoherodskopf

    Skied top to bottom, Ski- & Rodelarena Hoherodskopf gives 150 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 597 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.

    How much snow Ski- & Rodelarena Hoherodskopf usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 672 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 5 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and late December. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 5 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 1 cm; a snowy December gave 15 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 18 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Ski- & Rodelarena Hoherodskopf?
