    About Cuchara

    Cuchara is a mid-sized ski area with about 16 km of pistes, number 110 of 175 in US West by length. Its lifts climb from 2,823 m to 2,903 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 31 graded runs, about 16 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 7.7 km (48%). Intermediate runs make up 48% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 2.0 km of difficult runs.

    How Cuchara is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Cuchara gives 80 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 2,823 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. The only lift is a chairlift. Above the ski area rises Trinchera Peak (4,118 m), 6.9 km to the south-west, about 1,215 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,863 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 27 February and lasts to 27 February: about 0 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 15 cm (lean years 3 cm, snowy years 39 cm). Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 6 cm to 51 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 27 February, with about 30 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 12 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Questions about Cuchara

    What is the best month to ski at Cuchara?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from late February to late February.

    Is there snow at Cuchara at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 15 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Cuchara?

    Between 2,823 m and 2,903 m, so 80 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Cuchara best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 48% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Cuchara have?

    About 16 km of pistes and 1 lift.
