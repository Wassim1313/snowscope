    About Diavolezza-Lagalb

    With roughly 19 km of runs, Diavolezza-Lagalb sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 2,084 m to 3,003 m. Its nearest neighbour is Pontresina, 8.8 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,544 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 9 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. A normal season tops out near 168 cm, around 11 April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 94 cm; a snowy December gave 144 cm, a dry one 50 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 168 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early April and mid-April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 98 cm to 202 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 19 graded runs, about 19 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 78%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 4.2 km of black or expert runs.

    How Diavolezza-Lagalb is laid out

    The vertical drop is 919 m: enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,084 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 7 lifts break down into 2 cable cars, 1 chairlift, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Piz Bernina (4,049 m), 7.3 km to the south-west, about 1,046 m higher than the top lift.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, Livigno (16 km north-east) has about 83 km of pistes against 19 km here. No resort in the list keeps good snow longer: about 29 weeks of solid cover in an ordinary winter. For higher slopes, Corvatsch-Furtschellas climbs to 3,296 m.

    Diavolezza-Lagalb: common questions

    When does Diavolezza-Lagalb have the most snow?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at Diavolezza-Lagalb at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 94 cm deep.

    What altitude is Diavolezza-Lagalb at?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,003 m; the lowest starts at 2,084 m (919 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Diavolezza-Lagalb best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 78% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Diavolezza-Lagalb?

    Livigno, 16 km to the north-east, with about 83 km of pistes.
