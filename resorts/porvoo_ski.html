    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 30 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 11 cm; a snowy December gave 25 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 53 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 26 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Porvoo Ski: common questions

    When does Porvoo Ski have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at Porvoo Ski at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 11 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Porvoo Ski?

    Between 2 m and 57 m, so 55 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Porvoo Ski best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 44% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Porvoo Ski have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
