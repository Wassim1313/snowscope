    About Oak Mountain

    Neither huge nor tiny, Oak Mountain offers about 10 km of pistes (number 46 of 93 in US Northeast). Skiing here happens between 558 m and 737 m above sea level. Gore Mountain is the next resort, 33 km away to the north-east.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 648 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to early April, about 15 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 65 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 92 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 12 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 10 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one.

    What there is to ski at Oak Mountain

    The piste map lists 21 graded runs, about 9.7 km in total. Green circle runs make up the biggest part: 5.1 km, or 53%. With 53% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 179 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 558 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Dug Mountain at 980 m, standing 243 m above the top lift.

    Oak Mountain: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Oak Mountain?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    How much snow does Oak Mountain have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 32 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Oak Mountain at?

    The top is at 737 m and the base at 558 m.

    Is Oak Mountain suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 5.1 km, are easy.

    How big is Oak Mountain?

    About 10 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
