    About Bílá

    With some 5.0 km of runs, Bílá belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 536 m up to 767 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mezivodí, 4.4 km to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Bílá

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 4.3 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 2.2 km (51%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    231 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 536 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Bílá has roughly 19 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 10 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Bílá: common questions

    How high do the lifts go at Bílá?

    Between 536 m and 767 m, so 231 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Bílá?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 2.1 km, are easy.

    How big is Bílá?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
