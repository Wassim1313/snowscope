    About San Bernadino

    Neither huge nor tiny, San Bernadino offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 713 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,620 m up to 1,783 m. The closest other ski area is Confin / San Bernardino (2.6 km, west).

    San Bernadino and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci (14 km east) has about 35 km of pistes against 4.0 km here. Vals reaches 2,849 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of San Bernadino hold around 11 cm of snow today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 7 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 163 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,620 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Vals, reach 2,849 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of San Bernadino include 4 hotels: Lodge Brocco e Posta, Hotel Brocco & Posta, Bellevue and Aparthotel.
