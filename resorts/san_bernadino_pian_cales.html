    About San Bernadino

    Neither huge nor tiny, San Bernadino offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 713 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,620 m up to 1,783 m. The closest other ski area is Confin / San Bernardino (2.6 km, west).

    The pistes of San Bernadino

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 3.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.2 km (60%). With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of San Bernadino hold around 12 cm of snow today. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (18 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 163 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,620 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Vals, reach 2,849 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Pizzo Tambo (3,280 m), 7.6 km to the north-east, about 1,497 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow San Bernadino usually gets

    At about 1,702 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 December and lasts to 27 April: about 17 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 34 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 61 cm, a dry one 14 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 65 cm. Most winters land between 39 cm and 87 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about San Bernadino

    When does San Bernadino have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    How much snow does San Bernadino have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 32 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at San Bernadino?

    The top is at 1,783 m and the base at 1,620 m.

    Can beginners ski at San Bernadino?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near San Bernadino?

    Valchiavenna Madesimo/Campodolci, 14 km to the east, with about 35 km of pistes.
