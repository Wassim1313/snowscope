    The mountain at Sonnenbergbahn

    The vertical drop is 78 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Stubai, reach 3,208 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Habicht, 3,277 m high and 6.1 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,063 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm, from 6 cm in a poor year to 25 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 28 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 15 cm and 35 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 24 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.
