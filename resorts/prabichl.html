    About Präbichl

    Neither huge nor tiny, Präbichl offers about 14 km of pistes (number 402 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,073 m to 1,608 m. Aflenzer Bürgeralm is the next resort, 21 km away to the east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,340 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 December and lasts to 25 April: about 20 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 53 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 40 cm and 124 cm. A normal season tops out near 92 cm, around 5 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm (lean years 9 cm, snowy years 82 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    The pistes of Präbichl

    In all, 24 marked runs cover roughly 14 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 10 km, or 74%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 535 m: runs of a moderate length. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 3 T-bars and 2 moving carpets, 8 lifts in all. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Eisenerzer Reichenstein, 2,165 m high and 2.5 km south-west of the resort.

    Präbichl: common questions

    When does Präbichl have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Präbichl at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 42 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Präbichl at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,608 m; the lowest starts at 1,073 m (535 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Präbichl best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 74% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Präbichl?

    About 14 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
