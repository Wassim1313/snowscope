    About Brunnalm

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Brunnalm sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,059 m and reach 1,420 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Niederalpl, 6.0 km to the north-west.

    How much snow Brunnalm usually gets

    At about 1,240 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 December and lasts to 10 April: about 17 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 34 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 70 cm, a dry one 7 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 26 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 6 March, with about 63 cm. Most winters land between 25 cm and 91 cm by mid-February.

    What there is to ski at Brunnalm

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 6.7 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 3.7 km (55%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Brunnalm

    361 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hohe Veitsch at 1,981 m, standing 561 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Alpengasthof Scheikl (guesthouse), Schalleralm (mountain hut), Graf Meran Haus (mountain hut) and Seebodenhütte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When does Brunnalm have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early April.

    Can you ski at Brunnalm at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Brunnalm?

    The top is at 1,420 m and the base at 1,059 m.

    Is Brunnalm good for beginners?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 3.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Brunnalm?

    Aflenzer Bürgeralm, 16 km to the south-west, with about 18 km of pistes.
