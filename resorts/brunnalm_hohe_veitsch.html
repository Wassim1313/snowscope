    About Brunnalm

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Brunnalm sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,059 m and reach 1,420 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Niederalpl, 6.0 km to the north-west.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 16 km south-west to Aflenzer Bürgeralm, with about 18 km of runs. Aflenzer Bürgeralm reaches 1,798 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The mountain at Brunnalm

    361 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Brunnalm today: about 4 cm up high. The last seven days brought 3 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Saturday.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Alpengasthof Scheikl (guesthouse), Schalleralm (mountain hut), Graf Meran Haus (mountain hut) and Seebodenhütte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
