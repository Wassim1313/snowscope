    About Eggberge

    Eggberge is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 437 m and 1,747 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Eggberge, 0.7 km to the east.

    The mountain at Eggberge

    1,310 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The lowest slopes are at only 437 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. To get up there are 1 cable car and 1 T-bar. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Rot Gitschen, 2,673 m high and 7.8 km south-west of the resort.

    This week's snow

    Today there are about 13 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Eggberge. 10 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 36 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 12 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Snow at Eggberge through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,092 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 28 December and keeps it until about 5 May, roughly 19 weeks. A normal season tops out near 48 cm, around 4 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 27 cm to 60 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 45 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 29 cm, from 11 cm in a poor year to 39 cm in a big one.

    Eggberge: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Eggberge?

    Aim for early March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Is there snow at Eggberge at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 29 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Eggberge at?

    Between 437 m and 1,747 m, so 1,310 m of vertical drop.
