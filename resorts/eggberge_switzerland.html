    About Eggberge

    Eggberge is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 437 m and 1,747 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Eggberge, 0.7 km to the east.

    The mountain at Eggberge

    1,310 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The lowest slopes are at only 437 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.
