    About Sternenberg

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Sternenberg one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 835 m to 879 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Steg, 5.4 km to the south.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 44 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 835 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 3 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Sternenberg. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Sunnebad (hotel), Sternwarte Sternenberg (hotel), HörnliHaus (chalet) and Haus Storchenegg (chalet).
