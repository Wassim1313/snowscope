    About Sternenberg

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Sternenberg one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 835 m to 879 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Steg, 5.4 km to the south.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 44 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 835 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Warten, 1,206 m high and 7.3 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 4 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Sternenberg. The last seven days brought 16 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (6 cm).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 857 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 11 cm; a snowy December gave 25 cm, a dry one 1 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 18 February, with about 19 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early February. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 5 cm to 36 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Sternenberg?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    How much snow does Sternenberg have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Sternenberg at?

    The top is at 879 m and the base at 835 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Sternenberg?

    Steg, 5.4 km to the south, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Sternenberg have?
