    About Sandia Peak

    With roughly 25 km of runs, Sandia Peak sits in the middle of the size table of US West. Skiing here happens between 2,646 m and 3,133 m above sea level.

    The mountain at Sandia Peak

    Skied top to bottom, Sandia Peak gives 487 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,646 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most.
