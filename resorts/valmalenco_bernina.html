    About Valmalenco Bernina

    Valmalenco Bernina is a mid-sized ski area with about 23 km of pistes, number 274 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,525 m up to 2,350 m. Its nearest neighbour is Corvatsch-Furtschellas, 16 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 42 graded runs, about 23 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 8.4 km (37%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 3.7 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Valmalenco Bernina gives 825 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,525 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 1 funicular, 6 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Piz Glüschaint, 3,594 m high and 7.7 km north of the resort.

    How much snow Valmalenco Bernina usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,938 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 9 January and keeps it until about 28 April, roughly 16 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 39 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 45 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 28 cm and 59 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 24 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 44 cm, a dry one 10 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Valmalenco Bernina?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    How much snow does Valmalenco Bernina have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Valmalenco Bernina at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,350 m; the lowest starts at 1,525 m (825 m of vertical).

    Is Valmalenco Bernina good for beginners?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 10 km, are easy.

    How big is Valmalenco Bernina?

    About 23 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
