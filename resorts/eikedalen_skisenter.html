    About Eikedalen skisenter

    Around 13 km of marked runs place Eikedalen skisenter among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 48 by length. The slopes start at 454 m and reach 908 m at the top. Eikedalen is the next resort, 0.0 km away to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Eikedalen skisenter

    In all, 15 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. Most of the length is blue (64%, about 6.7 km). With 84% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Eikedalen skisenter is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Eikedalen skisenter gives 454 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Its 9 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 4 drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Above the ski area rises Fotasperrnuten (1,194 m), 6.7 km to the east, about 286 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 681 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. For about 22 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 106 cm (lean years 38 cm, snowy years 168 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 25 March, with about 245 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 225 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 110 cm and 295 cm.

    Eikedalen skisenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Eikedalen skisenter?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Eikedalen skisenter have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 106 cm deep.

    How high is Eikedalen skisenter?

    The top is at 908 m and the base at 454 m.

    Is Eikedalen skisenter good for beginners?

    Yes: 84% of the graded pistes, about 8.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Eikedalen skisenter have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
