    About Роза Хутор

    Роза Хутор tops the size table of Russia with roughly 90 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 938 m and 2,453 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Альпика-Сервис, 3.9 km north-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Роза Хутор

    The piste map lists 123 graded runs, about 85 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 39 km, or 45%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. 7.2 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Роза Хутор

    Skied top to bottom, Роза Хутор gives 1,515 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,138 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,453 m. Its 26 lifts break down into 7 gondolas, 10 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift, 2 rope tows and 5 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 5 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.
