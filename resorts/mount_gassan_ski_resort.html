    About Mount Gassan

    Mount Gassan is a ski area. The slopes start at 1,228 m and reach 1,502 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is 湯殿山スキー場, 6.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Mount Gassan is laid out

    274 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,502 m.
