    About Willard Mountain

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Willard Mountain sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 285 m to 420 m. The closest other ski area is West Mountain (34 km, north-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 14 marked runs cover roughly 3.6 km. Most of the length is black diamond (39%, about 1.4 km). With 39% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 1.4 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 135 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 285 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include The Cobble (443 m), Willard Mountain (423 m) and Herrington Hill (369 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 352 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 8 cm (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 26 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 56 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 20 cm.

    Questions about Willard Mountain

    When does Willard Mountain have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    Can you ski at Willard Mountain at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 8 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Willard Mountain?

    Its highest lift reaches 420 m; the lowest starts at 285 m (135 m of vertical).

    Does Willard Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.4 km of its graded pistes (39%) are difficult.

    How big is Willard Mountain?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
