    Willard Mountain in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Willard Mountain (1,155 ft (352 m)), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 3.1 in (8 cm); half of the winters sit between 1.6 in (4 cm) and 6.7 in (17 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 7.5 in (19 cm); the middle half of winters range from 3.1 in (8 cm) to 13 in (34 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 7.9 in (20 cm).

    1992-93 was the big one (33 in (84 cm)); 2015-16 the lean one (3.1 in (8 cm)).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-January to early March, about 43 days.

    Willard Mountain: quick answers

    Is there snow in Willard Mountain at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 3.1 in (8 cm), and only 0 % of the past 46 winters had 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is no snow.

    When is the best month to ski Willard Mountain?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 7.9 in (20 cm)). For a sure base, mid-January to early March is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Willard Mountain?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 7.9 in (20 cm) in mid-February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 33 in (84 cm) (1992-93), the thinnest 3.1 in (8 cm) (2015-16).

    How high do the lifts go at Willard Mountain?

    Its highest lift reaches 420 m; the lowest starts at 285 m (135 m of vertical).

    How big is Willard Mountain?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
