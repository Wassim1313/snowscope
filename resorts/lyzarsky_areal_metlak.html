    About Lyžařský areál Metlák

    Lyžařský areál Metlák is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 181 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Skiing here happens between 549 m and 648 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Šachty, 0.5 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lyžařský areál Metlák and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Paseky nad Jizerou: about 7.0 km of pistes, 2.6 km to the north. Paseky nad Jizerou has the longer snow season: around 7 weeks of solid cover, compared with 4 at Lyžařský areál Metlák. For higher slopes, Paseky nad Jizerou climbs to 859 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    99 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 549 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Paseky nad Jizerou, for one, climbs to 859 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Lyžařský areál Metlák has roughly 3 cm of snow on the ground today. 2 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 7 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Snow at Lyžařský areál Metlák through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 598 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 1 February and lasts to 28 February: about 4 weeks. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 4 cm to 56 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. A normal season tops out near 32 cm, around 11 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 48 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Lyžařský areál Metlák: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Lyžařský areál Metlák?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    Can you ski at Lyžařský areál Metlák at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 13 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Lyžařský areál Metlák?

    Its highest lift reaches 648 m; the lowest starts at 549 m (99 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Lyžařský areál Metlák?

    Paseky nad Jizerou, 2.6 km to the north, with about 7.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Lyžařský areál Metlák?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
