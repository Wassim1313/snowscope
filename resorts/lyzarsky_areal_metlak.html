    About Lyžařský areál Metlák

    Lyžařský areál Metlák is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 181 of 257 in Central Europe by length. Skiing here happens between 549 m and 648 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski Šachty, 0.5 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lyžařský areál Metlák and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Paseky nad Jizerou: about 7.0 km of pistes, 2.6 km to the north. For higher slopes, Paseky nad Jizerou climbs to 859 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    99 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 549 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Paseky nad Jizerou, for one, climbs to 859 m.
