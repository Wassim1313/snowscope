    About Sappee

    Sappee is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 169 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 100 m to 208 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 5 runs and around 2.5 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 1.9 km (78%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Sappee gives 108 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 4 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets, 8 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 154 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to late March, about 10 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 18 cm; a snowy December gave 32 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Most winters land between 17 cm and 53 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 40 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 12 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Questions about Sappee

    What is the best month to ski at Sappee?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    How much snow does Sappee have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Sappee at?

    The top is at 208 m and the base at 100 m.

    What level of skier is Sappee best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 78% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Sappee have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
