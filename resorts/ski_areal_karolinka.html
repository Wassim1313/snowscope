    About Ski areál Karolinka

    Neither huge nor tiny, Ski areál Karolinka offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 162 of 312 in the Carpathians). Skiing here happens between 492 m and 700 m above sea level. Ski areál Kyčerka is the next resort, 2.6 km away to the east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 5.6 km south to Kohútka, with about 6.0 km of runs. For higher slopes, Kohútka climbs to 939 m.

    How Ski areál Karolinka is laid out

    208 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 492 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
