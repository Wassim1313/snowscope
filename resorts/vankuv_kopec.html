    About Vaňkův kopec

    Compact and local, Vaňkův kopec has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Carpathians. Its lifts climb from 316 m to 409 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ski aerál Skalka, 7.1 km south-east, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Vaňkův kopec

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 0.8 km, or 64%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    How Vaňkův kopec is laid out

    The vertical drop is 93 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 316 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Ondrášova dolina reaches 768 m. To get up there are 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts and 2 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
