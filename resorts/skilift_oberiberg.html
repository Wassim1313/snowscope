    About Skilift Oberiberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Skilift Oberiberg offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 719 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,081 m up to 1,332 m. The closest other ski area is Skilifte Ibergeregg (3.1 km, west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 3.9 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 3.9 km (100%). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,206 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 December and lasts to 24 April: about 18 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 49 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 62 cm, around 3 March. Most winters land between 34 cm and 90 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 38 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it.

    The mountain at Skilift Oberiberg

    From top to bottom that makes 251 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 2 moving carpets. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Druesberg, 2,282 m high and 5.3 km south-east of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Roggenstock Lodge (hotel), Hotel Sonne (hotel), Sagentobel (guesthouse) and Rösslipost (hotel).

    Questions about Skilift Oberiberg

    When does Skilift Oberiberg have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Skilift Oberiberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 32 cm deep.

    How high is Skilift Oberiberg?

    Between 1,081 m and 1,332 m, so 251 m of vertical drop.

    Is Skilift Oberiberg suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilift Oberiberg?

    Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue, 6.7 km to the west, with about 27 km of pistes.
