    About Glencoe

    Glencoe has about 13 km of pistes. The slopes start at 374 m and reach 1,079 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Nevis Range, 25 km north, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    The mountain at Glencoe

    The vertical drop is 705 m: enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 3 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 9 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    The pistes of Glencoe

    The piste map lists 28 graded runs, about 13 km in total. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 6.7 km). With 53% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.
