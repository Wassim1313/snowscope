    About Thanner Hubel

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Thanner Hubel one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,004 m and 1,160 m above sea level. Le Schlumpf is the next resort, 9.2 km away to the south-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    156 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The only lift is a button lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Rimbachkopf (1,195 m), Rossberg (1,191 m) and Thanner Hubel (1,183 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,082 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm; a snowy December gave 55 cm, a dry one 0 cm. A normal season tops out near 13 cm, around 28 January. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 75 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Thanner Hubel?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    How much snow does Thanner Hubel have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 6 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Thanner Hubel?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,160 m; the lowest starts at 1,004 m (156 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Thanner Hubel?

    Le Markstein, 12 km to the north, with about 9.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Thanner Hubel?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
