    Axamer Lizum in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Axamer Lizum (about 1,613 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 34 cm; half of the winters sit between 25 cm and 41 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 60 cm, between 45 cm and 69 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 62 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    4 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 13 % by 1 January.

    2000-01 was the big one (101 cm); 2024-25 the lean one (43 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early December to mid-May (166 days).

    Questions about Axamer Lizum

    Is there snow in Axamer Lizum at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 34 cm, and only 13 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 37 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Axamer Lizum?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 62 cm). For a sure base, early December to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Axamer Lizum get?

    About 62 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 43 cm in the leanest winter (2024-25) and 101 cm in the biggest (2000-01).

    About Axamer Lizum

    Axamer Lizum is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 38 km of pistes put it at number 162 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 892 m to 2,334 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sonnenlift Axams, 4.2 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,442 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 892 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 2,334 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
