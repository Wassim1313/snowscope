    About Domobianca

    Domobianca is a mid-sized ski area with about 11 km of pistes, number 459 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,090 m up to 1,843 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cheggio, 9.3 km to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Domobianca has 27 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 9.2 km, or 81%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.3 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Domobianca through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,466 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 2 January and keeps it until about 2 May, roughly 17 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 59 cm, around 2 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 44 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 75 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 26 cm (lean years 16 cm, snowy years 49 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Domobianca gives 753 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Its 10 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts, 3 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 2 moving carpets. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Pizzo del Fornalino at 2,562 m, standing 719 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Corona (hotel), Agriturismo La Tensa (guesthouse), Hotel Ristorante Eurossola (hotel) and Albergo Emiliana (hotel).

    Domobianca: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Domobianca?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early May.

    Is there snow at Domobianca at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 26 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Domobianca?

    Between 1,090 m and 1,843 m, so 753 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Domobianca best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 81% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Domobianca have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
