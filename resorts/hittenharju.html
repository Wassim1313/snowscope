    About Hittenharju

    Hittenharju is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 38 m and 73 m above sea level. Salomonkallio is the next resort, 14 km away to the north.

    Lifts and terrain

    35 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 56 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 6 cm and 36 cm. A normal season tops out near 22 cm, around 12 February. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 20 cm). In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.
