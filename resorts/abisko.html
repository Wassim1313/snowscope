    Abisko in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Abisko (about 652 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 48 cm of cover (40 cm to 55 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 69 cm (58 cm–79 cm).

    The snowiest month is April, when the pack peaks at about 78 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 24 % of winters, and by 1 January in 54 %.

    2024-25 was the big one (124 cm); 2017-18 the lean one (54 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until mid-June in a normal winter, roughly 211 days.

    Abisko: quick answers

    Can you ski Abisko at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 48 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 54 % of 46 winters. Right now: 137 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Abisko?

    April: the snow peaks at about 78 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-June.

    How much snow does Abisko get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 78 cm in early April. The deepest of 46 winters reached 124 cm (2024-25), the thinnest 54 cm (2017-18).

    How high do the lifts go at Abisko?

    The top is at 902 m and the base at 401 m.

    Which bigger ski area is near Abisko?

    Riksgränsen, 27 km to the west, with about 13 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Abisko have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.

    About Abisko

    Neither huge nor tiny, Abisko offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 175 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 401 m to 902 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Björkliden, 5.0 km to the north-west.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 137 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Abisko. The last seven days brought 6 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Abisko gives 501 m of descent: runs of a moderate length.
