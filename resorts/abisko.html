    About Abisko

    Neither huge nor tiny, Abisko offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 175 of 262 in Scandinavia). Its lifts climb from 401 m to 902 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Björkliden, 5.0 km to the north-west.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 139 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Abisko. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 31 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 1 marked runs cover roughly 2.1 km. The largest share is red, with 2.1 km (100%). Intermediate runs make up 100% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Abisko gives 501 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The only lift is a chairlift. The skyline is dominated by Slåttatjåkka, 1,186 m high and 3.1 km west of the resort.

    Abisko and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 27 km west to Riksgränsen, with about 13 km of runs. Riksgränsen has the longer snow season: around 32 weeks of solid cover, compared with 27 at Abisko.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 652 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May or later, about 27 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 54 cm and 89 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 77 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 78 cm, around 4 April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 48 cm; a snowy December gave 64 cm, a dry one 33 cm.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Abisko include STF Abisko Turiststation (hotel), Abiskojåkka Fjällstuga (mountain hut), Vandrarhem Keron (hostel) and Abisko Mountain Lodge (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When does Abisko have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Abisko have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 48 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Abisko?

    The top is at 902 m and the base at 401 m.

    Who is Abisko best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Abisko?

    Riksgränsen, 27 km to the west, with about 13 km of pistes.
