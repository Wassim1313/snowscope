    About Sami Swoi

    Sami Swoi is a small ski area by the standards of the Carpathians, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 781 m to 914 m. Its nearest neighbour is U Steni, 3.0 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Sami Swoi gives 133 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 781 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a drag lift.
