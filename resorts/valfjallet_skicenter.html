    About Valfjället

    Valfjället is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 107 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 97 m to 310 m. Bjørkelangen skisenter is the next resort, 40 km away to the west.

    The pistes of Valfjället

    Counting every graded piste, there are 34 runs and around 6.7 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.6 km, or 54%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    213 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 2 drag lifts, 4 lifts in all. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Valfjället (311 m) and Hugnsåsen (252 m).

    How much snow Valfjället usually gets

    At about 204 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 9 February and lasts to 10 February: about 0 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm; a snowy December gave 30 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The snow is usually deepest around 9 February, with about 30 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 4 cm to 49 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Valfjället: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Valfjället?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early February to early February.

    Is there snow at Valfjället at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 9 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Valfjället at?

    The top is at 310 m and the base at 97 m.

    Is Valfjället suitable for families?

    Yes: 72% of the graded pistes, about 4.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Valfjället have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
