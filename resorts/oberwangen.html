    About Oberwangen

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Oberwangen one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 624 m and reach 759 m at the top. Gähwil is the next resort, 3.3 km away to the south.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 9.7 km south-west to Steg, with about 3.0 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Ghöch does better, with about 15 weeks of a good base instead of 0. Fischenthal reaches 1,049 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    What there is to ski at Oberwangen

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.3 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (60%, about 0.8 km). With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Oberwangen today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Oberwangen hold around 2 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 11 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Tuesday.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 135 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 624 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Fischenthal, for one, climbs to 1,049 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Hörnli, 1,133 m high and 6.4 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 692 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 25 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 28 December. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), with 20 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and mid-January.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Oberwangen?

    Aim for early January and mid-January.

    Is there snow at Oberwangen at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Oberwangen?

    Between 624 m and 759 m, so 135 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Oberwangen?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Oberwangen?

    Steg, 9.7 km to the south-west, with about 3.0 km of pistes.
