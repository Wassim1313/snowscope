    About Elsigenalp

    With some 28 km of runs, Elsigenalp belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,323 m to 2,283 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Adelboden, 5.9 km to the west.

    What there is to ski at Elsigenalp

    Counting every graded piste, there are 35 runs and around 28 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 53%. Intermediate runs make up 53% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 2.6 km of difficult runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    960 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,323 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 5 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 3 rope tows, 11 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Vordere Loner, 3,049 m high and 6.6 km south of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,803 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 24 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 67 cm to 142 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 109 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 95 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 7 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 54 cm, with 81 cm in the best years.

    Elsigenalp: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Elsigenalp?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Elsigenalp at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 54 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Elsigenalp at?

    Between 1,323 m and 2,283 m, so 960 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Elsigenalp best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 53% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Elsigenalp?

    About 28 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
