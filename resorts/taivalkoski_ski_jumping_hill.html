    About Taivalkoski ski jumping hill

    Taivalkoski ski jumping hill is a ski area. Its nearest neighbour is Taivalvaaran Hiihtokeskus, 0.1 km south, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Suvantovaara (318 m), Kivivaara (261 m) and Repovaara (259 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 232 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 11 December and keeps it until about 4 May, roughly 21 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 57 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 41 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 53 cm, a dry one 26 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 67 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 49 cm to 76 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Taivalkoski ski jumping hill: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Taivalkoski ski jumping hill?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Taivalkoski ski jumping hill at Christmas?
