    About Szrenica

    Around 9.0 km of marked runs place Szrenica among the larger resorts of Central Europe, number 26 by length. Its lifts climb from 713 m to 1,306 m. Pietkiewiczówka is the next resort, 1.5 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Szrenica

    Counting every graded piste, there are 7 runs and around 8.0 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 6.2 km (78%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.4 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    593 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The base at 713 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,306 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 3 chairlifts and 4 T-bars, 7 lifts in all.
