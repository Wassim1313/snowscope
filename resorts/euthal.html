    About Euthal

    Euthal has about 0.5 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 896 m and 1,039 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Sattelegg (4.2 km, north-east).

    The snow right now

    Up high, Euthal has roughly 15 cm of snow on the ground today. 30 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 14 cm by Thursday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 8 cm at mid-mountain.

    How Euthal is laid out

    The vertical drop is 143 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 896 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Hoch-Ybrig, reach 1,831 m. The skyline is dominated by Diethelm, 2,099 m high and 5.7 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Euthal usually gets

    At about 968 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 February and lasts to 8 March: about 4 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 19 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 18 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 30 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 13 cm and 50 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 32 cm.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Euthal include Post (hotel), Rösslipost (hotel), Sankt Joseph (hotel) and Haus 2 (chalet).

    Questions about Euthal

    What is the best month to ski at Euthal?

    Aim for early January and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Can you ski at Euthal at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Euthal at?

    Between 896 m and 1,039 m, so 143 m of vertical drop.
