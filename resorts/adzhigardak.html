    About Аджигардак (Adzhigardak)

    Around 11 km of marked runs place Аджигардак (Adzhigardak) among the larger resorts of Russia, number 13 by length. Skiing here happens between 276 m and 658 m above sea level. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Миньяр (Minyar), 18 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Аджигардак (Adzhigardak) has 13 graded runs adding up to about 11 km. Most of the length is red (56%, about 6.2 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 2.7 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Аджигардак (Adzhigardak)

    382 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 276 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 2 T-bars and 6 button lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
