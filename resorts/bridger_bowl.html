    About Bridger Bowl

    With roughly 42 km of runs, Bridger Bowl sits in the middle of the size table of US West. Skiing here happens between 1,865 m and 2,457 m above sea level. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Bear Canyon, 24 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 63 marked runs cover roughly 42 km. Most of the length is blue square (53%, about 23 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 8.7 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Bridger Bowl through a normal winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,161 m in an ordinary winter, from late November to early May. Most winters land between 73 cm and 150 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 58 cm, with 107 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 124 cm, around 31 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 104 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    How Bridger Bowl is laid out

    The vertical drop is 592 m: runs of a moderate length. A high base at 1,865 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. To get up there are 8 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 3 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Saddle Peak (2,784 m), 3.3 km to the south-west, about 327 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 2 hotels: Bridger Vista Lodge and Artful Lodger Bed & Breakfast.

    Bridger Bowl: common questions

    When does Bridger Bowl have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Is there snow at Bridger Bowl at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 58 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Bridger Bowl?

    The top is at 2,457 m and the base at 1,865 m.

    Who is Bridger Bowl best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 53% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Bridger Bowl have?

    About 42 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
