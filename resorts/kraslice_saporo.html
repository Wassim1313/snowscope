    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 660 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 36 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The snow is usually deepest around 1 February, with about 15 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 26 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    The mountain at Kraslice

    From top to bottom that makes 157 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 582 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Am Hirschkopf, for one, climbs to 941 m. The only lift is a button lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Kraslice?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Can you ski at Kraslice at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 5 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Kraslice?

    Its highest lift reaches 739 m; the lowest starts at 582 m (157 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Kraslice?

    Bublava, 5.9 km to the north, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Kraslice?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
