    About Góra Żar

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Góra Żar sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 456 m and reach 757 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Kocierz (6.8 km, east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.9 km. Most of the length is red (84%, about 1.6 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 301 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 456 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 3 funiculars, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 6 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
