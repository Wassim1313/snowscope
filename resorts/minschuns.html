    About Minschuns

    With roughly 16 km of runs, Minschuns sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 2,127 m and reach 2,652 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Watles, 15 km to the north-east.

    How much snow Minschuns usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,390 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 1 April, with about 125 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 121 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 73 cm to 155 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 68 cm, from 39 cm in a poor year to 107 cm in a big one. For about 14 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    How Minschuns is laid out

    The vertical drop is 525 m: runs of a moderate length. At 2,127 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 rope tow, 4 lifts in all. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Piz Tavrü (3,168 m), 4.8 km to the north-west, about 516 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Minschuns?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Minschuns at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 68 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Minschuns?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,652 m; the lowest starts at 2,127 m (525 m of vertical).

    Is Minschuns suitable for families?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 7.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Minschuns?

    About 16 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
