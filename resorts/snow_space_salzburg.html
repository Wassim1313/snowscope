    About Snow Space Salzburg

    With some 85 km of runs, Snow Space Salzburg belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 667 m to 1,977 m. Wagrain is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Snow Space Salzburg has 187 graded runs adding up to about 84 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 47 km, or 56%. Intermediate runs make up 56% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 5.4 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Snow Space Salzburg gives 1,310 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 667 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. To get up there are 1 cable car, 9 gondolas, 15 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 8 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 6 rope tows and 3 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Gabel (2,037 m), Saukarfunktel (2,024 m) and Saukarkopf (2,014 m).

    How much snow Snow Space Salzburg usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,322 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 28 December and keeps it until about 27 April, roughly 17 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 36 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 28 cm (lean years 7 cm, snowy years 51 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 28 cm and 69 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 56 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March.

    Questions about Snow Space Salzburg

    When does Snow Space Salzburg have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Snow Space Salzburg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 28 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Snow Space Salzburg?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Snow Space Salzburg at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,977 m; the lowest starts at 667 m (1,310 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Snow Space Salzburg best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 56% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
