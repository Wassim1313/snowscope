    What 46 winters say about Snow Space Salzburg

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Snow Space Salzburg (1,322 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 28 cm of cover (17 cm to 37 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 50 cm, between 37 cm and 63 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 56 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 2 % of winters, and by 1 January in 9 %.

    2018-19 was the big one (94 cm); 2025-26 the lean one (32 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to late April, about 140 days.

    Questions about Snow Space Salzburg

    Does Snow Space Salzburg have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 28 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 9 % of 46 winters. Right now: 13 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Snow Space Salzburg?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 56 cm). For a sure base, mid-December to late April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Snow Space Salzburg get?

    About 56 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 32 cm in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 94 cm in the biggest (2018-19).

    What altitude is Snow Space Salzburg at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,977 m; the lowest starts at 667 m (1,310 m of vertical).

    How big is Snow Space Salzburg?

    About 85 km of pistes and 47 lifts.

    About Snow Space Salzburg

    With some 85 km of runs, Snow Space Salzburg belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 667 m to 1,977 m. Wagrain is the next resort, 2.7 km away to the south-west.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Snow Space Salzburg gives 1,310 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 667 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
