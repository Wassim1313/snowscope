    About Passo Rolle

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Passo Rolle sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,875 m to 2,216 m. Its nearest neighbour is San Martino di Castrozza, 2.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Passo Rolle

    Passo Rolle has 20 graded runs adding up to about 9.7 km. Most of the length is blue (48%, about 4.7 km). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. Experts can count on some 1.4 km of difficult runs.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,046 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 December and lasts to 27 April: about 19 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 37 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. A normal season tops out near 58 cm, around 3 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 35 cm to 88 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 33 cm (lean years 5 cm, snowy years 62 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    341 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 1,875 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its 6 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Cima della Vezzana, 3,192 m high and 3.4 km east of the resort.

    Passo Rolle: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Passo Rolle?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Passo Rolle at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 33 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Passo Rolle?

    Between 1,875 m and 2,216 m, so 341 m of vertical drop.

    Is Passo Rolle suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 4.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Passo Rolle?

    San Pellegrino, 7.3 km to the north, with about 53 km of pistes.
