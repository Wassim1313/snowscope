    About Passo Rolle

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Passo Rolle sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,875 m to 2,216 m. Its nearest neighbour is San Martino di Castrozza, 2.5 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    For more terrain, San Pellegrino (7.3 km north) has about 53 km of pistes against 10 km here. For higher slopes, San Pellegrino climbs to 2,513 m.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 27 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Passo Rolle. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 14 cm by Wednesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    341 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. A high base at 1,875 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hotel Venezia (hotel), Hotel Alpenrose (hotel), Albergo Vezzana - Bar/Ristorante (hotel) and Capanna Cervino (mountain hut).
