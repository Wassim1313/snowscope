    About Bennau /Einsiedeln

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Bennau /Einsiedeln one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 882 m to 1,052 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Schnabelsberg / Einsiedeln, 0.5 km to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Bennau /Einsiedeln

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 100%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Bennau /Einsiedeln today: about 13 cm up high. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 10 cm at mid-mountain.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Bennau /Einsiedeln gives 170 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 882 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow. The skyline is dominated by Spital, 1,574 m high and 7.5 km south of the resort.

    Snow at Bennau /Einsiedeln through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 967 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 January and lasts to 26 March: about 11 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Most winters land between 14 cm and 60 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 22 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 36 cm). Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 38 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 21 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Bennau /Einsiedeln: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Bennau /Einsiedeln?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Is there snow at Bennau /Einsiedeln at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 22 cm deep.

    How high is Bennau /Einsiedeln?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,052 m; the lowest starts at 882 m (170 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Bennau /Einsiedeln?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Bennau /Einsiedeln?

    Biberegg, 7.1 km to the south-west, with about 5.0 km of pistes.
