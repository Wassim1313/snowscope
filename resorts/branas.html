    About Branäs

    Around 32 km of marked runs place Branäs among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 15 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 150 m up to 568 m. The closest other ski area is Hovfjället (39 km, south).

    The pistes of Branäs

    Branäs has 83 graded runs adding up to about 32 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 17 km, or 54%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Branäs today: about 4 cm up high. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Tuesday. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 13 cm at mid-mountain by this date. The 2026/27 season at Branäs is announced to start on 11 December 2026 and to run until 4 April 2027.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    418 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. To get up there are 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 22 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 4 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Branäsberget (567 m), Rönnknölen (524 m) and Möresberget (506 m).

    Snow at Branäs through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 359 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 January and keeps it until about 8 April, roughly 14 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 26 cm, with 45 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 51 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Most winters land between 23 cm and 68 cm by mid-February.
