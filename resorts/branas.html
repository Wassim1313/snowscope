    About Branäs

    Around 32 km of marked runs place Branäs among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 15 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 150 m up to 568 m. The closest other ski area is Hovfjället (39 km, south).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    By piste length Branäs comes third among these 7 resorts. For more reliable snow, Högfjället usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 19 weeks, against 14 here. For higher slopes, Högfjället climbs to 886 m.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Branäs today: about 7 cm up high. The last seven days brought 11 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 7 cm at mid-mountain). The 2026/27 season at Branäs is announced to start on 11 December 2026 and to run until 4 April 2027.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    418 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Branäsberget (567 m), Rönnknölen (524 m) and Möresberget (506 m).

    Snow at Branäs through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 359 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 January and keeps it until about 8 April, roughly 14 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 26 cm, with 45 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 51 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 16 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Most winters land between 23 cm and 68 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Branäs

    When is the best time to ski at Branäs?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to early April.

    How much snow does Branäs have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 26 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Branäs?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Branäs at?

    Its highest lift reaches 568 m; the lowest starts at 150 m (418 m of vertical).

    How big is Branäs?

    About 32 km of pistes and 33 lifts.
