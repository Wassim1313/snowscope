    About Gunstock

    Gunstock is a mid-sized ski area with about 26 km of pistes, number 26 of 93 in US Northeast by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 287 m up to 684 m. The closest other ski area is Abenaki (14 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 61 graded runs, about 25 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (59%, about 15 km). Intermediate runs make up 59% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 7.7 km of difficult runs.

    How Gunstock is laid out

    397 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 287 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 684 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 6 lifts break down into 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Belknap Mountain (726 m), Gunstock Mountain (674 m) and Piper Mountain (617 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 486 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 February and lasts to 23 February: about 1 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and early March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 9 cm to 61 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 28 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 31 cm.

    Gunstock: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Gunstock?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    How much snow does Gunstock have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Gunstock at?

    The top is at 684 m and the base at 287 m.

    Does Gunstock have steep runs?

    Yes: about 7.7 km of its graded pistes (31%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Gunstock have?

    About 26 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
