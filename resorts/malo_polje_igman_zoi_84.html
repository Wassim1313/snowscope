    About Malo polje Igman (ZOI '84)

    Malo polje Igman (ZOI '84) is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 37 of 51 in the Balkans by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,153 m up to 1,490 m. The closest other ski area is OC Bjelašnica ZOI '84 (6.6 km, south).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 26 km east to Jahorina olimpijski centar, with about 35 km of runs. The highest nearby is OC Bjelašnica ZOI '84, topping out at 2,056 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    337 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 chairlift and 3 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up.
