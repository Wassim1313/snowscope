    About MAJAsport

    With roughly 1.0 km of runs, MAJAsport sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 711 m to 794 m. The closest other ski area is Špindlerův Mlýn (0.8 km, north).

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at MAJAsport today: about 3 cm up high. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Saturday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (7 cm).

    Snow at MAJAsport through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 752 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 February and keeps it until about 3 March, roughly 5 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 35 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm, with 48 cm in the best years. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 6 cm and 57 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 83 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 711 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a button lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at MAJAsport?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Is there snow at MAJAsport at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    What altitude is MAJAsport at?

    Between 711 m and 794 m, so 83 m of vertical drop.

    Which bigger ski area is near MAJAsport?

    Skiareál Špindlerův Mlýn, 2.4 km to the north, with about 20 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does MAJAsport have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
