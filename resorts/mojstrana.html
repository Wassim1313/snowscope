    About Mojstrana

    Mojstrana has about 0.5 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 651 m and 761 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Španov vrh, 8.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Mojstrana usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 706 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 11 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 33 cm). In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and early April. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 28 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 3 February, with about 12 cm.

    How Mojstrana is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 110 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 651 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kranjska Gora reaches 1,568 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Škrnatarica (2,463 m), 7.5 km to the west, about 1,702 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find River House Mojstrana (chalet), Lukn'a hostel (hostel), Pr' Jozlnu (hostel) and Gostilna Veranda (guesthouse).

    Mojstrana: common questions

    When does Mojstrana have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-December and early April.

    Is there snow at Mojstrana at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).

    How high is Mojstrana?

    Its highest lift reaches 761 m; the lowest starts at 651 m (110 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Mojstrana have?

    About 0.5 km of pistes and 1 lift.
