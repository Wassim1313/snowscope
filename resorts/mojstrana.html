    About Mojstrana

    Mojstrana is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 651 m and 761 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Španov vrh, 8.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Mojstrana is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 110 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 651 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kranjska Gora reaches 1,568 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find River House Mojstrana (chalet), Lukn'a hostel (hostel), Pr' Jozlnu (hostel) and Gostilna Veranda (guesthouse).

    How high is Mojstrana?
