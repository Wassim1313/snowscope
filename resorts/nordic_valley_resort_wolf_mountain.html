    About Nordic Valley Resort (Wolf Mount

    Neither huge nor tiny, Nordic Valley Resort offers about 23 km of pistes (number 93 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 1,632 m to 2,150 m. The closest other ski area is Powder Mountain (12 km, north-east).

    How Nordic Valley Resort compares nearby

    For more terrain, Powder Mountain (12 km north-east) has about 130 km of pistes against 23 km here. Wasatch Peaks Ranch reaches 2,843 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Nordic Valley Resort today and the next days

    Snow on the ground at Nordic Valley Resort today: about 44 cm up high. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 38 cm by Tuesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 518 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,632 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Wasatch Peaks Ranch reaches 2,843 m.

    Staying nearby
