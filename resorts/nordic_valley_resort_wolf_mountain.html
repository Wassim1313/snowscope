    About Nordic Valley Resort (Wolf Mount

    Neither huge nor tiny, Nordic Valley Resort offers about 23 km of pistes (number 93 of 175 in US West). Its lifts climb from 1,632 m to 2,150 m. The closest other ski area is Powder Mountain (12 km, north-east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Nordic Valley Resort has 40 graded runs adding up to about 22 km. Most of the length is green circle (39%, about 8.8 km). Steep ground is the speciality: 30% of the length is graded difficult. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.8 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 518 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,632 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Wasatch Peaks Ranch reaches 2,843 m. To get up there are 4 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Chilly Peak (2,606 m), 7.1 km to the north-west, about 456 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,891 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to mid-April, about 17 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 21 cm and 132 cm. A normal season tops out near 88 cm, around 11 February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 45 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 11 cm, snowy years 94 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 26 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Quick answers

    When does Nordic Valley Resort have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Nordic Valley Resort have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 45 cm deep.

    What altitude is Nordic Valley Resort at?

    The top is at 2,150 m and the base at 1,632 m.

    Is Nordic Valley Resort good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 6.8 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.

    Which bigger ski area is near Nordic Valley Resort?

    Powder Mountain, 12 km to the north-east, with about 130 km of pistes.
