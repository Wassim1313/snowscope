    About Sigriswill-Schwanden

    Sigriswill-Schwanden is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 448 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,075 m and reach 1,460 m at the top. Niederhorn is the next resort, 3.8 km away to the south-east.

    How much snow Sigriswill-Schwanden usually gets

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,268 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-May. By mid-April the typical depth is 56 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 68 cm, around 17 February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 89 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 38 cm, from 18 cm in a poor year to 54 cm in a big one.

    The pistes of Sigriswill-Schwanden

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 12 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 6.6 km, or 56%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Sigriswill-Schwanden is laid out

    The vertical drop is 385 m: runs of a moderate length. Its 3 lifts break down into 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. The skyline is dominated by Burgfeldstand, 2,063 m high and 4.1 km east of the resort.

    Sigriswill-Schwanden: common questions

    When does Sigriswill-Schwanden have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Sigriswill-Schwanden at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Sigriswill-Schwanden?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,460 m; the lowest starts at 1,075 m (385 m of vertical).

    Is Sigriswill-Schwanden suitable for families?

    Yes: 56% of the graded pistes, about 6.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Sigriswill-Schwanden have?

    About 12 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
