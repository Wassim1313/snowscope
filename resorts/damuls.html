    About Damüls

    Damüls is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 64 km of pistes put it at number 88 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,376 m to 2,021 m. Damüls-Mellau is the next resort, 0.8 km away to the north.

    What there is to ski at Damüls

    Damüls has 124 graded runs adding up to about 60 km. Most of the length is red (38%, about 23 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 12 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,698 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. The snow is usually deepest around 3 March, with about 142 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 84 cm to 179 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 60 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 92 cm, a dry one 23 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 109 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    How Damüls is laid out

    645 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,376 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 10 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 5 moving carpets, 16 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Zitterklapfen at 2,403 m, standing 382 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Damüls

    When does Damüls have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Damüls have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 60 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Damüls open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Damüls?

    The top is at 2,021 m and the base at 1,376 m.

    Is Damüls suitable for families?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 25 km, are easy.
