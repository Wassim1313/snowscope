    What 46 winters say about Damüls

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Damüls (about 1,698 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 60 cm of cover (40 cm to 73 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 126 cm, between 100 cm and 151 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 142 cm.

    41 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 70 % by 1 January.

    1998-99 was the big one (265 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (92 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early December to late May (175 days).

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Damüls at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 60 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 70 % of 46 winters. Right now: 36 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Damüls?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 142 cm). For a sure base, early December to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Damüls?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 142 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 265 cm (1998-99), the thinnest 92 cm (2022-23).

    How high do the lifts go at Damüls?

    The top is at 2,021 m and the base at 1,376 m.

    About Damüls

    Damüls is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 64 km of pistes put it at number 88 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,376 m to 2,021 m. Damüls-Mellau is the next resort, 0.8 km away to the north.

    How Damüls is laid out

    645 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,376 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
