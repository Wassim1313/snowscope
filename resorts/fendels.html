    About Fendels

    Fendels is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 486 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,340 m and 2,248 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kaunertal, 5.0 km to the east.

    Snow at Fendels through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,794 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 November and keeps it until about 27 May, roughly 26 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 84 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 58 cm (lean years 32 cm, snowy years 82 cm). For about 8 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 76 cm and 143 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 114 cm.

    How Fendels is laid out

    908 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,340 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. To get up there are 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars and 1 button lift. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Pfroslkopf, 3,148 m high and 7.8 km south of the resort.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Fendels?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Fendels at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 58 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Fendels at?

    The top is at 2,248 m and the base at 1,340 m.

    Is Fendels good for beginners?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 5.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Fendels?

    Serfaus Fiss Ladis, 9.1 km to the west, with about 169 km of pistes.
