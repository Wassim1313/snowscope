    About Říčky v Orlických horách

    With some 5.0 km of runs, Říčky v Orlických horách belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 741 m to 986 m. The closest other ski area is Zdobnice (4.0 km, west).

    The pistes of Říčky v Orlických horách

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 4.5 km. Most of the length is blue (53%, about 2.4 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Říčky v Orlických horách is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Říčky v Orlických horách gives 245 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 941 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 986 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 864 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 10 cm, with 42 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 58 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 28 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Questions about Říčky v Orlických horách

    What is the best month to ski at Říčky v Orlických horách?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Říčky v Orlických horách at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    What altitude is Říčky v Orlických horách at?

    Between 741 m and 986 m, so 245 m of vertical drop.

    Is Říčky v Orlických horách suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 2.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Říčky v Orlických horách have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
