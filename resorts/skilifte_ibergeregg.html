    About Skilifte Ibergeregg

    Skilifte Ibergeregg is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 720 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,356 m to 1,581 m. Ibergeregg-Handgruobi is the next resort, 2.6 km away to the south-west.

    Snow at Skilifte Ibergeregg through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,468 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 14 December and keeps it until about 15 May, roughly 22 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 79 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 55 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 95 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 41 cm; a snowy December gave 56 cm, a dry one 18 cm.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 10 runs and around 3.8 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.0 km, or 53%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Skilifte Ibergeregg is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Skilifte Ibergeregg gives 225 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,356 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are T-bars. Above the ski area rises Druesberg (2,282 m), 7.1 km to the east, about 701 m higher than the top lift.

    Skilifte Ibergeregg and the resorts around it

    Skilifte Ibergeregg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Skilifte Ibergeregg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Skilifte Ibergeregg have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 41 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Skilifte Ibergeregg?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,581 m; the lowest starts at 1,356 m (225 m of vertical).

    Is Skilifte Ibergeregg suitable for families?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 2.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skilifte Ibergeregg?

    Brunni-Holzegg-Rotenflue, 3.8 km to the west, with about 27 km of pistes.
