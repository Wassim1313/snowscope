    About Engstligenalp

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Engstligenalp sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,946 m to 2,354 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Leukerbad, 4.7 km to the south-east.

    How Engstligenalp is laid out

    The vertical drop is 408 m: runs of a moderate length. A high base at 1,946 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its top at 2,354 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Adelboden (5.3 km north-west) has about 83 km of pistes against 11 km here. Leukerbad (Torrent) reaches 2,596 m, the most of the resorts listed.
