    About Engstligenalp

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Engstligenalp sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,946 m to 2,354 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Leukerbad, 4.7 km to the south-east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 11 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 5.3 km (46%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.9 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,150 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 3 December and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 26 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 62 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 91 cm, a dry one 35 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. For about 13 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 74 cm to 148 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 120 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A normal season tops out near 124 cm, around 2 April.

    How Engstligenalp is laid out

    The vertical drop is 408 m: runs of a moderate length. A high base at 1,946 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Its top at 2,354 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 8 lifts break down into 2 cable cars, 3 T-bars and 3 rope tows. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Above the ski area rises Rinderhorn (3,449 m), 6.7 km to the east, about 1,095 m higher than the top lift.

    Engstligenalp: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Engstligenalp?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Engstligenalp at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 62 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Engstligenalp?

    Between 1,946 m and 2,354 m, so 408 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Engstligenalp?

    Yes: 46% of the graded pistes, about 5.3 km, are easy.

    Does Engstligenalp have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 1.8 km of its pistes (16%).
