    About Muggenbrunn

    Neither huge nor tiny, Muggenbrunn offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 586 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 967 m to 1,235 m. The closest other ski area is Haldenköpfle (2.5 km, north-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 6.9 km. Most of the length is blue (61%, about 4.2 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Muggenbrunn through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,101 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; a snowy December gave 27 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 42 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 21 February, with about 11 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 268 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,167 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. To get up there are 5 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Feldberg at 1,494 m, standing 259 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Muggenbrunn

    When does Muggenbrunn have the most snow?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    Is there snow at Muggenbrunn at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Muggenbrunn?

    The top is at 1,235 m and the base at 967 m.

    Is Muggenbrunn suitable for families?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 4.2 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Muggenbrunn?

    Todtnauberg, 3.1 km to the east, with about 17 km of pistes.
