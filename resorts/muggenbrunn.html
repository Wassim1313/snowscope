    About Muggenbrunn

    Neither huge nor tiny, Muggenbrunn offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 586 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 967 m to 1,235 m. The closest other ski area is Haldenköpfle (2.5 km, north-west).

    Muggenbrunn and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 3.1 km east to Todtnauberg, with about 17 km of runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 268 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,167 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.
