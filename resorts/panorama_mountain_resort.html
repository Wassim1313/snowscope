    What 46 winters say about Panorama

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Panorama (1,760 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 64 cm; half of the winters sit between 54 cm and 86 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 84 cm; the middle half of winters range from 76 cm to 102 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 97 cm.

    72 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 89 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 2011-12 (163 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1983-84 (78 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-November until late May in a normal winter, roughly 190 days.

    Today's 13 cm compares with a usual 29 cm for 19 November.

    About Panorama

    Panorama ranks number 9 of 53 ski areas in Canada West by piste length, with about 75 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,152 m up to 2,368 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Fairmont Hot Springs, 31 km to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,216 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 6 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Mount Brewer (2,797 m), 7.3 km to the south, about 429 m higher than the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Panorama

    In all, 114 marked runs cover roughly 73 km. The largest share is blue square, with 47 km (63%). Intermediate runs make up 63% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 18 km.

    How much snow Panorama usually gets

    At about 1,760 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 November and lasts to 26 May: about 27 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 65 cm to 120 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 94 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 64 cm, with 100 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 10 March, with about 97 cm.
