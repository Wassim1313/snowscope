    About Hohe Winde

    Hohe Winde is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 619 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 766 m and 1,062 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Schwengimatt (8.8 km, south-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 4.3 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.9 km (69%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Hohe Winde

    296 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 766 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hornegg at 1,225 m, standing 163 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Hohe Winde through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 914 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and late January. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 21 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 6 cm, around 25 January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 2 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 15 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Quick answers

    When does Hohe Winde have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Is there snow at Hohe Winde at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 2 cm).

    What altitude is Hohe Winde at?

    The top is at 1,062 m and the base at 766 m.

    Is Hohe Winde good for beginners?

    Yes: 69% of the graded pistes, about 2.9 km, are easy.

    How big is Hohe Winde?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
