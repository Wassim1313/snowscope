    About Gantrisch

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gantrisch offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 616 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,339 m up to 1,575 m. Gantrisch is the next resort, 3.3 km away to the north-west.

    How much snow Gantrisch usually gets

    At about 1,457 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 16 December and lasts to 17 May: about 22 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 41 cm and 104 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 41 cm, from 15 cm in a poor year to 65 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 58 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 76 cm, around 19 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Runs by difficulty

    Gantrisch has 8 graded runs adding up to about 5.9 km. Most of the length is blue (80%, about 4.7 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Gantrisch is laid out

    236 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,339 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift. The skyline is dominated by Stockhorn, 2,190 m high and 7.3 km south-east of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Bergheim STB (chalet), Untere Gantrischhütte (mountain hut), Schwefelberg Bad (hotel) and Alp Oberwirtneren (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Gantrisch: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Gantrisch?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Gantrisch at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 41 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Gantrisch at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,575 m; the lowest starts at 1,339 m (236 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Gantrisch?

    Yes: 80% of the graded pistes, about 4.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Gantrisch have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
