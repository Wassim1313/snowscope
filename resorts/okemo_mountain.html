    About Okemo Mountain

    About 70 km of pistes make Okemo Mountain one of the ten biggest ski areas of US Northeast (number 6). Skiing here happens between 353 m and 1,012 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Plymouth Notch (13 km, north).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 114 marked runs cover roughly 69 km. The largest share is green circle, with 25 km (36%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 19 km of black or expert runs. 1.2 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    659 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The lowest slopes are at only 353 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 1,012 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 13 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 7 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. 7 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Ludlow Mountain (1,012 m), South Mountain (968 m) and Sawyer Rocks (717 m).

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 682 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to late March, about 10 weeks of good cover. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Year to year the snow is steady, with 19 cm to 79 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 21 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 44 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 47 cm, around 2 March.

    Questions about Okemo Mountain

    When does Okemo Mountain have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    Can you ski at Okemo Mountain at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 21 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Okemo Mountain open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 21 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Okemo Mountain?

    Between 353 m and 1,012 m, so 659 m of vertical drop.

    Is Okemo Mountain good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 21 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.
