    What 46 winters say about Les Contamines

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Les Contamines (about 1,801 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 69 cm; half of the winters sit between 47 cm and 96 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 140 cm (119 cm–164 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 153 cm.

    By 15 December 54 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 83 %.

    Best winter on record: 1994-95, peaking at 260 cm. Worst: 2010-11, which never passed 108 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late November to late May (186 days).

    So far this winter: 91 cm on 23 November (usual: 26 cm).

    About Les Contamines

    Around 58 km of marked runs place Les Contamines among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 99 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,158 m and 2,444 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Evasion Mont Blanc, 5.6 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    1,286 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,444 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 25 lifts: 4 gondolas, 9 chairlifts, 8 button lifts and 4 drag lifts. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The skyline is dominated by Aiguille de la Bérangère, 3,425 m high and 7.3 km east of the resort.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 65 marked runs cover roughly 58 km. The largest share is red, with 23 km (40%). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. The steepest pistes add up to about 11 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,801 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 November and lasts to 25 May: about 26 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 69 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 34 cm in a poor year to 116 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 153 cm, around 5 March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 119 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 88 cm to 189 cm in mid-February in most winters.
