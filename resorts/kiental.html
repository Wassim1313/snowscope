    About Kiental

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, Kiental sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 925 m to 1,457 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Faltschen, 5.1 km to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    Kiental has 11 graded runs adding up to about 7.0 km. The largest share is red, with 4.5 km (64%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 1.4 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Kiental has roughly 2 cm of snow on the ground today. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 35 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 11 cm at mid-mountain).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 1,191 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 December and lasts to 28 April: about 19 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 40 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 62 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 37 cm to 85 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 50 cm in the best years.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Kiental gives 532 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 925 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar. Above the ski area rises Dündenhorn (2,862 m), 7.1 km to the south, about 1,405 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Kiental include Hotel- Restaurant Bären Kiental (hotel), Alpenruh (hotel), Grathütte (mountain hut) and Rustica (hotel).

    Questions about Kiental

    When does Kiental have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Kiental at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 32 cm deep.

    What altitude is Kiental at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,457 m; the lowest starts at 925 m (532 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Kiental best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 64% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Kiental?

    Mürren/Schilthorn, 13 km to the east, with about 39 km of pistes.
