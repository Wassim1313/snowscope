    About Meran 2000

    With roughly 16 km of runs, Meran 2000 sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,617 m and 2,295 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being San Vigilio, 13 km to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Meran 2000

    The piste map lists 19 graded runs, about 16 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 9.7 km, or 59%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,956 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to late May, about 26 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 96 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 65 cm to 122 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 95 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 54 cm (lean years 26 cm, snowy years 88 cm).

    How Meran 2000 is laid out

    The vertical drop is 678 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,617 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 1 cable car, 3 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Hirzer - Punta Cervina, 2,781 m high and 6.1 km north of the resort.

    Meran 2000: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Meran 2000?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Meran 2000 at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 54 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Meran 2000?

    Between 1,617 m and 2,295 m, so 678 m of vertical drop.

    Is Meran 2000 good for beginners?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 9.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Meran 2000 have?

    About 16 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
