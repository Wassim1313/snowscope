    About Melchsee-Frutt

    Around 34 km of marked runs place Melchsee-Frutt among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 183 by length. Skiing here happens between 1,080 m and 2,232 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Meiringen, 4.8 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Melchsee-Frutt

    Melchsee-Frutt has 72 graded runs adding up to about 34 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 20 km, or 58%. With 60% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How Melchsee-Frutt is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Melchsee-Frutt gives 1,152 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 4 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Pfaffenhuet (3,009 m), 7.7 km to the east, about 777 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Melchsee-Frutt usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,656 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 3 December and keeps it until about 25 May, roughly 25 weeks. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 127 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 60 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 78 cm, a dry one 28 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 117 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 78 cm and 153 cm by mid-February.

    Quick answers

    When does Melchsee-Frutt have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Melchsee-Frutt have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 60 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Melchsee-Frutt?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,232 m; the lowest starts at 1,080 m (1,152 m of vertical).

    Is Melchsee-Frutt good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 20 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Melchsee-Frutt have?

    About 34 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
