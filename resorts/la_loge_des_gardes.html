    About La Loge des Gardes

    La Loge des Gardes is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 110 of 257 in Central Europe by length. The slopes start at 1,052 m and reach 1,159 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Chalmazel, 37 km to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 9 marked runs cover roughly 2.2 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 54%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at La Loge des Gardes through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,106 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 22 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. At Christmas there was snow here in 5 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 12 cm). The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. A normal season tops out near 4 cm, around 26 January. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Lifts and terrain

    107 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts.

    Quick answers

    When does La Loge des Gardes have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at La Loge des Gardes at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 5 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 1 cm).

    What altitude is La Loge des Gardes at?

    The top is at 1,159 m and the base at 1,052 m.

    Can beginners ski at La Loge des Gardes?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does La Loge des Gardes have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
