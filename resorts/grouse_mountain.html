    Snow at Grouse Mountain through a normal winter

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,050 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 215 cm, around 3 April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 108 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 184 cm, a dry one 59 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 95 cm and 241 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 211 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For about 24 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    How Grouse Mountain is laid out

    The vertical drop is 356 m: runs of a moderate length. The base at 872 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Crown Mountain, 1,504 m high and 3.5 km north of the resort.
