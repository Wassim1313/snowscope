    Badger Pass in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Badger Pass (about 7,513 ft (2,290 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 26 in (65 cm) of cover (12 in (30 cm) to 51 in (130 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 50 in (127 cm) (30 in (76 cm)–68 in (173 cm)).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 56 in (141 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 50 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 61 %.

    The deepest winter was 2022-23 (170 in (431 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2013-14 (22 in (57 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs late November to mid-May (161 days).

    Badger Pass: quick answers

    Is there snow in Badger Pass at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 26 in (65 cm) at Christmas, and 61 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 26 in (66 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Badger Pass?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 56 in (141 cm)). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How snowy is Badger Pass?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 56 in (141 cm) in mid-February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 170 in (431 cm) (2022-23), the thinnest 22 in (57 cm) (2013-14).
