    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 26 in (65 cm) at Christmas, and 61 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 22 in (57 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Badger Pass?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 56 in (141 cm)). For a sure base, late November to mid-May is the usual window with 12 in (30 cm) or more.

    How snowy is Badger Pass?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 56 in (141 cm) in mid-February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 170 in (431 cm) (2022-23), the thinnest 22 in (57 cm) (2013-14).

    About Badger Pass

    Compact and local, Badger Pass has about 6.0 km of runs, small for US West. The slopes start at 2,203 m and reach 2,376 m at the top.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Badger Pass has roughly 57 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 9 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 23 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    How much snow Badger Pass usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,290 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 65 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 169 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 9 weeks. A normal season tops out near 141 cm, around 20 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 73 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 43 cm to 250 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 173 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 2,203 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. To get up there are 4 chairlifts and 1 drag lift. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Above the ski area rises Ostrander Rocks (2,482 m), 6.9 km to the north-east, about 106 m higher than the top lift.
