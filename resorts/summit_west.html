    About Summit West

    About 7.0 km of pistes make Summit West one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Skiing here happens between 917 m and 1,150 m above sea level. Sahalie Ski Club is the next resort, 1.2 km away to the north.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Three Summits: about 43 km of pistes, 2.3 km to the south. For more reliable snow, Stevens Pass usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 29 weeks, against 23 here. For higher slopes, Crystal Mountain climbs to 2,072 m.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,034 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 3 December and keeps it until about 9 May, roughly 23 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 61 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 27 cm in a poor year to 96 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For about 11 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 46 cm to 180 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 92 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is usually deepest around 25 February, with about 123 cm.

    What there is to ski at Summit West

    The piste map lists 19 graded runs, about 7.0 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (66%, about 4.6 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.5 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 233 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 917 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 5 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Mount Thomson, 1,998 m high and 7.3 km north-east of the resort.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Summit House (hotel), Slide-In Lodge (mountain hut), Alpenhaus Lodge (mountain hut) and Guye Cabin (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Summit West: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Summit West?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Summit West have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 61 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Summit West at?

    The top is at 1,150 m and the base at 917 m.

    What level of skier is Summit West best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Summit West?

    Three Summits, 2.3 km to the south, with about 43 km of pistes.
