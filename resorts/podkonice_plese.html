    About Podkonice

    Podkonice is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 5.0 km of pistes put it at number 50 of 312. Its lifts climb from 808 m to 976 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Selce, 5.0 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 4.7 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.4 km (51%). With 51% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 168 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 808 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a button lift.

    Questions about Podkonice

    How high is Podkonice?

    Is Podkonice good for beginners?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 2.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Podkonice?
