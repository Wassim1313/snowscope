    About Åre

    Åre tops the size table of Scandinavia with roughly 116 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 389 m to 1,318 m. There are 5 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Huså, 9.5 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Åre has 218 graded runs adding up to about 110 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 57 km, or 52%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Åre is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 929 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,318 m. The lift system counts 48 lifts: 1 cable car, 2 funiculars, 1 gondola, 8 chairlifts, 23 T-bars, 10 button lifts and 3 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Åreskutan at 1,420 m, standing 102 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 854 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 85 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 47 cm, with 72 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 51 cm and 102 cm. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 89 cm.

    Questions about Åre

    When is the best time to ski at Åre?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Åre at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 47 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Åre?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026.

    What altitude is Åre at?

    Between 389 m and 1,318 m, so 929 m of vertical drop.

    Is Åre suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 68 km, are easy.
