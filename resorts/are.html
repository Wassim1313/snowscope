    What 46 winters say about Åre

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Åre (about 854 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 47 cm; half of the winters sit between 40 cm and 56 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 81 cm, between 66 cm and 93 cm in a normal winter.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 89 cm.

    By 15 December 30 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 59 %.

    Best winter on record: 1987-88, peaking at 149 cm. Worst: 1990-91, which never passed 63 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 178 days.

    So far this winter: 20 cm on 26 November (usual: 29 cm).

    Getting to Åre and what it costs

    Lifts: 48. Pistes: 116 km. Heights: 389 m at the bottom, 1,318 m at the top (929 m of vertical).

    About Åre

    Åre tops the size table of Scandinavia with roughly 116 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 389 m to 1,318 m. There are 5 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Huså, 9.5 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Åre has 211 graded runs adding up to about 111 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 58 km, or 52%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How Åre is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 929 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,318 m. The lift system counts 48 lifts: 1 cable car, 2 funiculars, 1 gondola, 8 chairlifts, 23 T-bars, 10 button lifts and 3 moving carpets. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Åreskutan at 1,420 m, standing 102 m above the top lift.

For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 85 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 47 cm, with 72 cm in the best years.
