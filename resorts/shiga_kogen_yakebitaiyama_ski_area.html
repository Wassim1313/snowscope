    About Shiga Kogen Yakebitaiyama

    With some 17 km of runs, Shiga Kogen Yakebitaiyama belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Its lifts climb from 1,548 m to 1,998 m. The closest other ski area is Shiga Kogen Ichinose Yamanokami (1.0 km, south-west).

    The mountain at Shiga Kogen Yakebitaiyama

    From top to bottom that makes 450 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,548 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,998 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
