    About Shiga Kogen Yakebitaiyama

    With some 17 km of runs, Shiga Kogen Yakebitaiyama belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Its lifts climb from 1,548 m to 1,998 m. The closest other ski area is Shiga Kogen Ichinose Yamanokami (1.0 km, south-west).

    What there is to ski at Shiga Kogen Yakebitaiyama

    The piste map lists 22 graded runs, about 16 km in total. The largest share is red, with 9.2 km (56%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 2.4 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Shiga Kogen Yakebitaiyama

    From top to bottom that makes 450 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,548 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 1,998 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 2 gondolas and 3 chairlifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up.

    Shiga Kogen Yakebitaiyama: common questions
