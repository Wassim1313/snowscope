    About Zieleniec

    Zieleniec is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 144 of 257 in Central Europe by length. The slopes start at 1,011 m and reach 1,016 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zieleniec, 0.1 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Zieleniec

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.6 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.6 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 5 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,016 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.

    How much snow Zieleniec usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,014 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 60 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 10 cm, with 37 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 24 cm, around 1 February.

    Zieleniec: common questions

    When does Zieleniec have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Zieleniec at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Zieleniec at?

    Between 1,011 m and 1,016 m, so 5 m of vertical drop.

    Is Zieleniec suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    How big is Zieleniec?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
