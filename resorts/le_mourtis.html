    About Le Mourtis

    About 13 km of pistes make Le Mourtis one of the smaller places to ski in the Pyrenees. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,386 m up to 1,795 m. There are 7 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Superbagnères, 23 km to the south-west.

    Le Mourtis and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 28 km south-east to Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret, with about 154 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Saint Lary-Soulan does better, with about 15 weeks of a good base instead of 5. Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret reaches 2,600 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    What there is to ski at Le Mourtis

    In all, 36 marked runs cover roughly 13 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 4.7 km, or 36%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 409 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,386 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Aramón Cerler, for one, climbs to 2,618 m. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts, 6 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Cap de Gauch (2,148 m), 6.7 km to the south-east, about 353 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Le Mourtis usually gets

    Count on about 5 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,590 m in an ordinary winter, from late January to early March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 42 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 76 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 1 February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Le Mourtis: common questions

    When does Le Mourtis have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to early March.

    Can you ski at Le Mourtis at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 13 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Le Mourtis?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,795 m; the lowest starts at 1,386 m (409 m of vertical).

    Is Le Mourtis good for beginners?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 8.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Le Mourtis?

    Estació d'Esquí Baqueira-Beret, 28 km to the south-east, with about 154 km of pistes.
