    About Kaberlaba

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kaberlaba offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 698 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 997 m to 1,153 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Monte Valbella (Asiago), 6.8 km to the east.

    How Kaberlaba compares nearby

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 156 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,197 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Verena 2000, reach 1,997 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find 4 hotels: Hotel da Barba, Albergo Vescovi, Meltar Boutique Hotel and La Locanda.
