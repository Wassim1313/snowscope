    About Kaberlaba

    Neither huge nor tiny, Kaberlaba offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 698 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 997 m to 1,153 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Monte Valbella (Asiago), 6.8 km to the east.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 6 marked runs cover roughly 3.9 km. Most of the length is blue (67%, about 2.6 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Up high, Kaberlaba has roughly 28 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 18 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 156 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,197 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Verena 2000, reach 1,997 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 4 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets, 7 lifts in all. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Monte Erio (1,627 m), 7.4 km to the north-west, about 474 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,075 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in 7 winters out of ten), with 25 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for early January and early March. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 36 cm. A normal season tops out near 9 cm, around 21 January. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Kaberlaba: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Kaberlaba?

    Aim for early January and early March.

    How much snow does Kaberlaba have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 5 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Kaberlaba at?

    The top is at 1,153 m and the base at 997 m.

    Is Kaberlaba good for beginners?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 2.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Kaberlaba?

    Lavarone, 20 km to the north-west, with about 13 km of pistes.
