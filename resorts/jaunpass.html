    Jaunpass in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Jaunpass (about 1,506 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 45 cm; half of the winters sit between 26 cm and 62 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 88 cm (70 cm–104 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 90 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 30 % of winters, and by 1 January in 43 %.

    1998-99 was the big one (166 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (52 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-December until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 152 days.

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Jaunpass at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 45 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 43 % of 46 winters. Right now: 32 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Jaunpass?

    February: the snow peaks at about 90 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to mid-May.

    How snowy is Jaunpass?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 90 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 166 cm (1998-99), the thinnest 52 cm (2022-23).

    How high is Jaunpass?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,606 m; the lowest starts at 1,405 m (201 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Jaunpass?

    Schönried, 9.4 km to the south, with about 59 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Jaunpass have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.

    About Jaunpass

    Neither huge nor tiny, Jaunpass offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 654 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,405 m and reach 1,606 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen, 4.2 km to the west.

    Other resorts close by
