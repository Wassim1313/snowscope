    About Aizu Kogen Takahata

    Neither huge nor tiny, Aizu Kogen Takahata offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 102 of 385 in Japan). The slopes start at 725 m and reach 1,249 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Oze Hinoemata Onsen (8.6 km, south-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 10 graded runs, about 7.6 km in total. Most of the length is green (41%, about 3.1 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Aizu Kogen Takahata

    Skied top to bottom, Aizu Kogen Takahata gives 524 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Below about 925 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 4 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.
