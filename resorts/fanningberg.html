    About Fanningberg

    Fanningberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 23 km of pistes, number 265 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,494 m to 2,090 m. Its nearest neighbour is Großeck-Speiereck, 4.6 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 35 runs and around 23 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 15 km, or 68%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow Fanningberg usually gets

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,792 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 42 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 18 cm in a poor year to 70 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 53 cm to 109 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 65 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 27 February, with about 90 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 596 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,494 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 2 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Above the ski area rises Großes Gurpitscheck (2,526 m), 7.1 km to the north-west, about 436 m higher than the top lift.

    How Fanningberg compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Obertauern: about 87 km of pistes, 14 km to the north-west. If snow is the priority, Obertauern does better, with about 25 weeks of a good base instead of 22. Großeck-Speiereck reaches 2,338 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Fanningberg: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Fanningberg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Fanningberg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 42 cm deep.

    How high is Fanningberg?

    The top is at 2,090 m and the base at 1,494 m.

    Is Fanningberg good for beginners?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 15 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Fanningberg?

    Obertauern, 14 km to the north-west, with about 87 km of pistes.
