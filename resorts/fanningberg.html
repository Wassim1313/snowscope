    About Fanningberg

    Fanningberg is a mid-sized ski area with about 23 km of pistes, number 265 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,494 m to 2,090 m. Its nearest neighbour is Großeck-Speiereck, 4.6 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 596 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,494 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
