    About Betelberg

    Around 24 km of marked runs place Betelberg among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 258 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,089 m to 1,999 m. Gstaad is the next resort, 8.0 km away to the west.

    The pistes of Betelberg

    In all, 30 marked runs cover roughly 24 km. The largest share is red, with 12 km (50%). Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 910 m: enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Wysshore (2,947 m), 7.7 km to the south-east, about 948 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 34 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Betelberg. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (18 cm).

    Snow at Betelberg through a normal winter

    At about 1,544 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 23 December and lasts to 8 May: about 20 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 67 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 32 cm; a snowy December gave 48 cm, a dry one 13 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 40 cm to 85 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 48 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Betelberg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Is there snow at Betelberg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 32 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Betelberg at?

    Between 1,089 m and 1,999 m, so 910 m of vertical drop.

    Is Betelberg suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How big is Betelberg?

    About 24 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
