    About Super Besse

    No ski area of Central Europe has more pistes than Super Besse, with about 58 km. Its lifts climb from 1,225 m to 1,831 m. The closest other ski area is Chastreix-Sancy (4.9 km, west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 118 marked runs cover roughly 58 km. The largest share is blue, with 22 km (38%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 606 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 37 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts, 20 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 6 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 6 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Super Besse: common questions

    What altitude is Super Besse at?

    The top is at 1,831 m and the base at 1,225 m.

    Is Super Besse suitable for families?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 37 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Super Besse have?

    About 58 km of pistes and 37 lifts.
