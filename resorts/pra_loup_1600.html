    About Pra-Loup 1600

    Pra-Loup 1600 is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 89 km of pistes put it at number 57 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,495 m and reach 2,497 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Espace Lumière, 2.4 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,002 m: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,495 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
