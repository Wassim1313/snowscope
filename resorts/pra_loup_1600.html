    What 46 winters say about Pra-Loup 1600

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Pra-Loup 1600 (about 1,996 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 37 cm; half of the winters sit between 28 cm and 55 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 78 cm, between 58 cm and 93 cm in a normal winter.

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 79 cm.

    By 15 December 22 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 35 %.

    2000-01 was the big one (149 cm); 1992-93 the lean one (34 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-December to late April (147 days).

    About Pra-Loup 1600

    Pra-Loup 1600 is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 89 km of pistes put it at number 57 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,495 m and reach 2,497 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Espace Lumière, 2.4 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Pra-Loup 1600 today: about 64 cm up high. The last seven days brought 73 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 41 cm by Thursday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,002 m: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,495 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
