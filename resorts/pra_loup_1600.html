    About Pra-Loup 1600

    Pra-Loup 1600 is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 89 km of pistes put it at number 57 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,495 m and reach 2,497 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Espace Lumière, 2.4 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Pra-Loup 1600

    Pra-Loup 1600 has 245 graded runs adding up to about 89 km. The largest share is red, with 34 km (38%). With 30% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 5.8 km. 21 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,996 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 20 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 37 cm, with 80 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 43 cm and 108 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 79 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 49 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,002 m: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,495 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 4 chairlifts, 9 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 4 moving carpets, 21 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Grande Séolane, 2,911 m high and 3.6 km south-west of the resort.

    Pra-Loup 1600: common questions

    When does Pra-Loup 1600 have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Pra-Loup 1600 have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 37 cm deep.

    When does Pra-Loup 1600 open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Pra-Loup 1600 at?

    Between 1,495 m and 2,497 m, so 1,002 m of vertical drop.

    Is Pra-Loup 1600 good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 27 km of its graded pistes (30%) are difficult.
