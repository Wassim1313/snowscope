    About Nannestad Skisenter

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Nannestad Skisenter one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 208 m to 352 m. The closest other ski area is Rambydalen (19 km, south-east).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 280 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 December and lasts to 9 April: about 15 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 25 cm to 78 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 58 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 21 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 28 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 50 cm, a dry one 6 cm.

    What there is to ski at Nannestad Skisenter

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 1.2 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 0.8 km (70%). With 70% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Nannestad Skisenter

    The vertical drop is 144 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Marifjell (720 m), 7.9 km to the north-west, about 368 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Nannestad Skisenter today: about 7 cm up high. The last seven days brought 1 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 6 cm at mid-mountain.

    Nannestad Skisenter: common questions

    When does Nannestad Skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Can you ski at Nannestad Skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 28 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high is Nannestad Skisenter?

    The top is at 352 m and the base at 208 m.

    Is Nannestad Skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 70% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    How big is Nannestad Skisenter?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
