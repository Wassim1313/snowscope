    About Vassfjellet skisenter

    Around 13 km of marked runs place Vassfjellet skisenter among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 56 by length. Its lifts climb from 198 m to 656 m. Stokkan Alpinbakke is the next resort, 16 km away to the north.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    458 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 656 m.
