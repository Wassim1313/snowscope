    About Vassfjellet skisenter

    Around 13 km of marked runs place Vassfjellet skisenter among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 56 by length. Its lifts climb from 198 m to 656 m. Stokkan Alpinbakke is the next resort, 16 km away to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 16 runs and around 13 km to ski. Most of the length is red (46%, about 5.9 km). With 54% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How much snow Vassfjellet skisenter usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 427 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 23 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 1 April, with about 96 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 45 cm, with 74 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 50 cm and 116 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 86 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    458 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 656 m. The lift system counts 9 lifts: 1 chairlift, 4 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Vassfjellet (710 m), Tortberga (631 m) and Litlfjellet (605 m).

    Questions about Vassfjellet skisenter

    When does Vassfjellet skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Vassfjellet skisenter have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Vassfjellet skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 656 m; the lowest starts at 198 m (458 m of vertical).

    Is Vassfjellet skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 6.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Vassfjellet skisenter have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
