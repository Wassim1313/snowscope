    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Lyžařský areál Kobyla sits in the middle of the size table of Central Europe. The ski area spans the mountain from 952 m up to 1,104 m. Its nearest neighbour is Churáňov, 0.9 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Lyžařský areál Kobyla is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Lyžařský areál Kobyla gives 152 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,152 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.
