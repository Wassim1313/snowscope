    About Skiareál Kunčice

    Neither huge nor tiny, Skiareál Kunčice offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 73 of 257 in Central Europe). Skiing here happens between 571 m and 750 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Králičák, 2.8 km to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Skiareál Kunčice

    Skiareál Kunčice has 9 graded runs adding up to about 3.8 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.5 km, or 65%. With 65% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Skiareál Kunčice

    179 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 571 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. To get up there are 1 chairlift and 2 button lifts.

    How much snow Skiareál Kunčice usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 660 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 24 cm. For the best snow, aim for early January and late February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 35 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 44 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Skiareál Kunčice?

    Aim for early January and late February.

    Is there snow at Skiareál Kunčice at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 10 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Skiareál Kunčice?

    Between 571 m and 750 m, so 179 m of vertical drop.

    Is Skiareál Kunčice suitable for families?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Skiareál Kunčice?

    Czarna Góra, 12 km to the north-west, with about 10 km of pistes.
