    About Dwie Doliny Muszyna-Wierchomla

    With some 10 km of runs, Dwie Doliny Muszyna-Wierchomla belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. Its lifts climb from 592 m to 987 m. The closest other ski area is Jaworzyna Krynicka (5.6 km, east).

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 24 marked runs cover roughly 9.5 km. The largest share is blue, with 5.6 km (59%). With 70% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Dwie Doliny Muszyna-Wierchomla is laid out

    395 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. At 592 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 987 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 10 lifts break down into 2 chairlifts and 8 button lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Dwie Doliny Muszyna-Wierchomla at?

    The top is at 987 m and the base at 592 m.

    Is Dwie Doliny Muszyna-Wierchomla good for beginners?

    Yes: 70% of the graded pistes, about 6.7 km, are easy.

    How big is Dwie Doliny Muszyna-Wierchomla?

    About 10 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
