    About Spitzingsee

    With roughly 16 km of runs, Spitzingsee sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 981 m and 1,569 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Oberer Maxlraineralm, 1.4 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Spitzingsee usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,275 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 3 January and lasts to 20 April: about 16 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 28 cm, with 59 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 59 cm, around 2 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 34 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 24 cm and 88 cm.

    What there is to ski at Spitzingsee

    The piste map lists 35 graded runs, about 16 km in total. Most of the length is red (58%, about 9.3 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 2.8 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Spitzingsee

    588 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The base at 981 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 11 lifts: 3 chairlifts, 5 T-bars and 3 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Hinteres Sonnwendjoch, 1,986 m high and 7.3 km south-east of the resort.

    Questions about Spitzingsee

    When is the best time to ski at Spitzingsee?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Spitzingsee have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 28 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Spitzingsee?

    The top is at 1,569 m and the base at 981 m.

    Who is Spitzingsee best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 58% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Spitzingsee?

    About 16 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
