    About Kvitfjell

    Only 8 of the 262 ski areas of Scandinavia are larger than Kvitfjell, which has some 38 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 184 m up to 1,038 m. There are 6 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skeikampen Alpinsenter, 12 km to the south.

    What there is to ski at Kvitfjell

    Kvitfjell has 68 graded runs adding up to about 37 km. Green (beginner) runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 35%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The mountain at Kvitfjell

    Skied top to bottom, Kvitfjell gives 854 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 4 drag lifts and 3 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Prestkampen, 1,244 m high and 8.0 km south of the resort.

    How much snow Kvitfjell usually gets

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 611 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late November to mid-May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 56 cm (lean years 35 cm, snowy years 85 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 64 cm to 109 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 77 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The snow is usually deepest around 19 March, with about 86 cm.

    Kvitfjell: common questions

    When does Kvitfjell have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Kvitfjell have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 56 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Kvitfjell?

    Between 184 m and 1,038 m, so 854 m of vertical drop.

    Is Kvitfjell good for beginners?

    Yes: 67% of the graded pistes, about 25 km, are easy.

    How big is Kvitfjell?

    About 38 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
