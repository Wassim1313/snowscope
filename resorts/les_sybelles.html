    About Les Sybelles

    Les Sybelles is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 153 km of pistes put it at number 26 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,083 m and 2,587 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Albiez-Montrond, 9.2 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Les Sybelles has 243 graded runs adding up to about 153 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 77 km, or 51%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The mountain at Les Sybelles

    1,504 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 1 gondola, 28 chairlifts, 12 button lifts, 19 drag lifts and 13 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 13 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Aiguille Saint-Phalle, 2,897 m high and 7.9 km west of the resort.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Les Sybelles has roughly 34 cm of snow on the ground today. 36 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 74 cm by Sunday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 22 cm now. The 2026/27 season at Les Sybelles is announced to start on 12 December 2026 and to run until 9 April 2027.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,835 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 5 March, with about 130 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 81 cm to 161 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 92 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 67 cm, with 97 cm in the best years.

    Quick answers

    When does Les Sybelles have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Les Sybelles have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 67 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Les Sybelles open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 9 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Sybelles?

    The top is at 2,587 m and the base at 1,083 m.

    Can beginners ski at Les Sybelles?

    Yes: 64% of the graded pistes, about 98 km, are easy.
