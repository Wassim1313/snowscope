    About Changchun Lianhuashan

    Changchun Lianhuashan is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 29 of 92 in China by length. Skiing here happens between 253 m and 380 m above sea level. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tiandingshan, 11 km to the west.

    What there is to ski at Changchun Lianhuashan

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 1.7 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (100%, about 1.7 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    127 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 253 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 2 gondolas and 4 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas.

    Quick answers

    How high is Changchun Lianhuashan?

    Its highest lift reaches 380 m; the lowest starts at 253 m (127 m of vertical).

    Who is Changchun Lianhuashan best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 100% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Changchun Lianhuashan have?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
