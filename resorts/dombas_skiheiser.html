    What 46 winters say about Dombas Skiheiser

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Dombas Skiheiser (about 911 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 75 cm; half of the winters sit between 59 cm and 83 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 100 cm, between 88 cm and 118 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 109 cm.

    83 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 98 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1989-90 (164 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2025-26 (71 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to late May, about 199 days.

    This winter so far: 8 cm on 24 November, against a usual 44 cm for the date.

    Dombas Skiheiser: quick answers

    Does Dombas Skiheiser have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 75 cm at Christmas, and 98 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 13 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Dombas Skiheiser?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 109 cm). For a sure base, mid-November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Dombas Skiheiser get?

    About 109 cm at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 71 cm in the leanest winter (2025-26) and 164 cm in the biggest (1989-90).

    How high is Dombas Skiheiser?

    The top is at 1,070 m and the base at 752 m.

    About Dombas Skiheiser

    Dombas Skiheiser is a ski area. The slopes start at 752 m and reach 1,070 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Fjellsyn Skisenter (35 km, east).

    When the snow is good

    At about 911 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 12 November and lasts to 26 May: about 28 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 75 cm (lean years 52 cm, snowy years 94 cm). For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 21 March, with about 109 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 78 cm to 128 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 105 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 318 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grønsæterhøe at 1,417 m, standing 347 m above the top lift.

    Can you ski at Dombas Skiheiser at Christmas?
