    About Val-Louron

    Neither huge nor tiny, Val-Louron offers about 16 km of pistes (number 35 of 49 in the Pyrenees). The slopes start at 1,442 m and reach 2,071 m at the top. Peyragudes is the next resort, 5.9 km away to the east.

    Val-Louron and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Saint-Lary (12 km north-west) has about 72 km of pistes against 16 km here. Piau-Engaly reaches 2,527 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The snow right now

    Snow on the ground at Val-Louron today: about 12 cm up high. The last seven days brought 8 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 25 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.

    The mountain at Val-Louron

    Skied top to bottom, Val-Louron gives 629 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,442 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Piau-Engaly, reach 2,527 m.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 hotels: Le Nabias, Le Lustou, Lou Lapadé and Gourgs Blancs.
