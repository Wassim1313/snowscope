    About Münstertal-Wieden

    Neither huge nor tiny, Münstertal-Wieden offers about 15 km of pistes (number 386 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 865 m and 1,225 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Belchen, 2.9 km to the south-west.

    What there is to ski at Münstertal-Wieden

    Counting every graded piste, there are 23 runs and around 15 km to ski. Most of the length is red (46%, about 6.8 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.9 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,045 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 32 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and late January. The snow is usually deepest around 17 January, with about 8 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 24 cm). In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    The mountain at Münstertal-Wieden

    360 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Below about 1,065 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 6 lifts here are T-bars. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Belchen (1,414 m), 3.1 km to the south-west, about 189 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Münstertal-Wieden

    When is the best time to ski at Münstertal-Wieden?

    Aim for early January and late January.

    Can you ski at Münstertal-Wieden at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 4 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Münstertal-Wieden?

    Between 865 m and 1,225 m, so 360 m of vertical drop.

    Is Münstertal-Wieden suitable for families?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 6.1 km, are easy.
