    About Crested Butte

    Around 56 km of marked runs place Crested Butte among the larger resorts of US West, number 32 by length. The slopes start at 2,783 m and reach 3,488 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Irwin Lodge Snowcat (14 km, west).

    What there is to ski at Crested Butte

    Counting every graded piste, there are 93 runs and around 56 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 29 km, or 52%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 9.3 km of difficult runs.

    How Crested Butte is laid out

    705 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,783 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its top at 3,488 m is the third highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 15 lifts: 10 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 3 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Crested Butte at 3,704 m, standing 216 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 3,136 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 16 November and keeps it until about 13 May, roughly 26 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 74 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 120 cm, a dry one 37 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 118 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 14 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. A normal season tops out near 151 cm, around 30 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 88 cm and 182 cm.

    Crested Butte: common questions

    When does Crested Butte have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Crested Butte at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 74 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Crested Butte?

    The announced opening day is 25 November 2026.

    What altitude is Crested Butte at?

    The top is at 3,488 m and the base at 2,783 m.

    What level of skier is Crested Butte best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
