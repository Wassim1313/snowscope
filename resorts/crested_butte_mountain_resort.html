    About Crested Butte

    Around 56 km of marked runs place Crested Butte among the larger resorts of US West, number 32 by length. The slopes start at 2,783 m and reach 3,488 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Irwin Lodge Snowcat (14 km, west).

    How Crested Butte is laid out

    705 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,783 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its top at 3,488 m is the third highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Crested Butte has roughly 36 cm of snow on the ground today. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 15 cm by Saturday.
