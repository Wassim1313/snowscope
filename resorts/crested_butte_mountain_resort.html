    What 46 winters say about Crested Butte

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Crested Butte (10,289 ft (3,136 m)), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 29 in (74 cm); half of the winters sit between 22 in (57 cm) and 38 in (96 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 49 in (124 cm); the middle half of winters range from 40 in (101 cm) to 61 in (154 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 59 in (151 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 72 % of winters had reached 20 in (50 cm); by New Year it was 87 %.

    The deepest winter was 1984-85 (97 in (246 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 1980-81 (41 in (104 cm)).

    The skiable season with at least 12 in (30 cm) typically runs mid-November to mid-May (181 days).

    Skiing Crested Butte: the practical side

    Crested Butte skis between 9,131 ft (2,783 m) and 11,444 ft (3,488 m): 2,313 ft (705 m) of vertical, 35 mi of pistes, 15 lifts.

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Crested Butte at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 29 in (74 cm) at Christmas, and 87 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 27 in (69 cm).

    When is the best month to ski Crested Butte?

    March: the snow peaks at about 59 in (151 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to mid-May.

    How snowy is Crested Butte?

    About 59 in (151 cm) at the peak of a normal winter (late March); between 41 in (104 cm) in the leanest winter (1980-81) and 97 in (246 cm) in the biggest (1984-85).

    What altitude is Crested Butte at?

    The top is at 3,488 m and the base at 2,783 m.

    How big is Crested Butte?

    About 56 km of pistes and 15 lifts.

    How Crested Butte is laid out

    705 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Even the lowest lift starts at 2,783 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its top at 3,488 m is the third highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km.
