    What 46 winters say about Tromsø Alpinpark

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Tromsø Alpinpark (272 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 83 cm, with a normal range of 68 cm–104 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 140 cm (119 cm–153 cm).

    April is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 174 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 87 % of winters, and by 1 January in 100 %.

    1992-93 was the big one (252 cm); 1984-85 the lean one (137 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from early November until early June in a normal winter, roughly 215 days.

    This winter so far: 23 cm on 21 November, against a usual 46 cm for the date.

    Questions about Tromsø Alpinpark

    Does Tromsø Alpinpark have snow over Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 83 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 100 % of 46 winters. Right now: 63 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Tromsø Alpinpark?

    The deepest snow comes in April (about 174 cm). For a sure base, early November to early June is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Tromsø Alpinpark get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 174 cm in early April. The deepest of 46 winters reached 252 cm (1992-93), the thinnest 137 cm (1984-85).

    What altitude is Tromsø Alpinpark at?

    The top is at 499 m and the base at 45 m.

    Is Tromsø Alpinpark suitable for families?

    Yes: 63% of the graded pistes, about 3.5 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Tromsø Alpinpark have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.

    About Tromsø Alpinpark

    Tromsø Alpinpark is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 132 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 45 m to 499 m. Tromsø Alpinpark is the next resort, 0.4 km away to the north.

    Tromsø Alpinpark today and the next days

    Up high, Tromsø Alpinpark has roughly 63 cm of snow on the ground today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 13 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 52 cm by now.

    What there is to ski at Tromsø Alpinpark

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 5.5 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 2.8 km (51%). With 63% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 30 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 272 m) in an ordinary winter, from early November to late May or later. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 83 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 122 cm in the best years. Year to year the snow is steady, with 107 cm to 176 cm in mid-February in most winters. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 19 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 9 April, with about 174 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 170 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    The mountain at Tromsø Alpinpark

    The vertical drop is 454 m: runs of a moderate length. All 3 lifts here are T-bars. The skyline is dominated by Tromsdalstinden - Sálašoaivi, 1,238 m high and 7.7 km south of the resort.
