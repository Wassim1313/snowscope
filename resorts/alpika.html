    About Альпика-Сервис

    Альпика-Сервис is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 30 of 86 in Russia by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 577 m up to 2,162 m. Its nearest neighbour is Курорт Красная Поляна, 2.6 km west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Альпика-Сервис

    In all, 8 marked runs cover roughly 5.2 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (60%, about 3.1 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Альпика-Сервис

    Skied top to bottom, Альпика-Сервис gives 1,585 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. At 577 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Роза Хутор, for one, climbs to 2,453 m. To get up there are 1 gondola, 5 chair-gondolas, 1 chairlift, 1 drag lift, 3 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    How Альпика-Сервис compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Роза Хутор: about 90 km of pistes, 3.9 km to the south-east. For higher slopes, Роза Хутор climbs to 2,453 m.

    Quick answers

    How high is Альпика-Сервис?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,162 m; the lowest starts at 577 m (1,585 m of vertical).

    Is Альпика-Сервис good for beginners?

    Yes: 77% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Альпика-Сервис?

    Роза Хутор, 3.9 km to the south-east, with about 90 km of pistes.

    How big is Альпика-Сервис?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
