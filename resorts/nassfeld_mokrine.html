    About Nassfeld

    Around 70 km of marked runs place Nassfeld among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 80 by length. Its lifts climb from 612 m to 1,996 m. Gitschtal is the next resort, 12 km away to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 141 marked runs cover roughly 70 km. Most of the length is red (70%, about 49 km). Intermediate runs make up 70% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 1.9 km of black or expert runs.

    How Nassfeld is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,384 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Because the lifts start as low as 612 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its 29 lifts break down into 6 gondolas, 8 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 7 button lifts and 6 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 6 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Creta di Aip / Trogkofel (2,279 m), 4.0 km to the west, about 283 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 18 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Nassfeld. 22 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 8 cm by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 7 cm at mid-mountain. The 2026/27 season at Nassfeld is announced to start on 4 December 2026.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 8 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,304 m) in an ordinary winter, from late January to mid-March. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 18 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Most winters land between 17 cm and 59 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 26 February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 18 cm, with 38 cm in the best years.

    Quick answers

    When does Nassfeld have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Nassfeld at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 18 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Nassfeld?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026.

    What altitude is Nassfeld at?

    Between 612 m and 1,996 m, so 1,384 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Nassfeld best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 70% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
