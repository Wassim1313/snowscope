    Nassfeld in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Nassfeld (1,304 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 18 cm; half of the winters sit between 8 cm and 29 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 32 cm (25 cm–38 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 36 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 4 % of winters, and by 1 January in 4 %.

    Best winter on record: 1991-92, peaking at 110 cm. Worst: 2001-02, which never passed 16 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late December until mid-April in a normal winter, roughly 109 days.

    Nassfeld: quick answers

    Is there snow in Nassfeld at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 18 cm, and only 4 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 21 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Nassfeld?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 36 cm). For a sure base, late December to mid-April is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Nassfeld?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 36 cm in late February. The deepest of 46 winters reached 110 cm (1991-92), the thinnest 16 cm (2001-02).

    What altitude is Nassfeld at?

    Between 612 m and 1,996 m, so 1,384 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Nassfeld?

    About 70 km of pistes and 29 lifts.
