    About Charmant Hiuchi Snow Resort

    Around 9.0 km of marked runs place Charmant Hiuchi Snow Resort among the larger resorts of Japan, number 86 by length. Skiing here happens between 518 m and 1,009 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being ARAI SNOW RESORT, 7.3 km to the east.

    Charmant Hiuchi Snow Resort today and the next days

    Up high, Charmant Hiuchi Snow Resort has roughly 49 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 69 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 17 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.

    How Charmant Hiuchi Snow Resort is laid out

    491 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 518 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.
