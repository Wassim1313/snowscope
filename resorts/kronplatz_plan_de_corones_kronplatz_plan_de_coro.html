    Questions about Kronplatz

    Is there snow in Kronplatz at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 31 cm, and only 26 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 24 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Kronplatz?

    February: the snow peaks at about 58 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to late April.

    How much snow does Kronplatz get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 58 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 129 cm (2013-14), the thinnest 35 cm (2016-17).

    About Kronplatz

    Around 76 km of marked runs place Kronplatz among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 73 by length. Its lifts climb from 925 m to 2,273 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Antermoia, 8.6 km to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 134 marked runs cover roughly 82 km. Most of the length is blue (47%, about 39 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 15 km.

    Snow at Kronplatz through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,599 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 December and lasts to 28 April: about 19 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 58 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 62 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 34 cm and 89 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 44 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February.

    How Kronplatz is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,348 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 925 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 27 lifts break down into 21 gondolas, 5 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Muntejela de Ciastlins, 2,673 m high and 7.7 km south-east of the resort.
