    About Kronplatz

    Around 76 km of marked runs place Kronplatz among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 73 by length. Its lifts climb from 925 m to 2,273 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Antermoia, 8.6 km to the south-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 125 marked runs cover roughly 76 km. Most of the length is blue (47%, about 36 km). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. The steepest pistes add up to about 14 km.

    Snow at Kronplatz through a normal winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,599 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 December and lasts to 28 April: about 19 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 58 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 31 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 62 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 34 cm and 89 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 44 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February.

    How Kronplatz is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 1,348 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With the bottom at 925 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 26 lifts break down into 21 gondolas, 4 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Muntejela de Ciastlins, 2,673 m high and 7.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Kronplatz?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Kronplatz have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 31 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Kronplatz?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 11 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Kronplatz?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,273 m; the lowest starts at 925 m (1,348 m of vertical).

    Is Kronplatz good for beginners?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 36 km, are easy.
