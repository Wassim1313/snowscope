    About Fageralm

    Neither huge nor tiny, Fageralm offers about 20 km of pistes (number 311 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 935 m and reach 1,854 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Reiteralm (4.8 km, east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 27 runs and around 19 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 61%. With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl: about 38 km of pistes, 11 km to the south-west. For more reliable snow, Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 22 weeks, against 19 here. The highest nearby is Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl, topping out at 2,109 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 919 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,135 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 gondola, 4 T-bars and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. Above the ski area rises Hinterer Geißstein (2,190 m), 5.8 km to the south, about 336 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,394 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to early May, about 19 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 42 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 35 cm and 81 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 33 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 10 cm in a poor year to 56 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 69 cm.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Unterbergalm (mountain hut), Lechneralm (mountain hut), Hotel Innviertler (hotel) and Premhütte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Fageralm: common questions

    When does Fageralm have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    How much snow does Fageralm have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 33 cm deep.

    What altitude is Fageralm at?

    The top is at 1,854 m and the base at 935 m.

    Is Fageralm good for beginners?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Fageralm?

    Zauchensee-Flachauwinkl, 11 km to the south-west, with about 38 km of pistes.
