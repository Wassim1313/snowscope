    About Schöneben

    With some 27 km of runs, Schöneben belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,458 m and reach 2,381 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Nauders Bergkastel, 8.4 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Schöneben has 49 graded runs adding up to about 26 km. The largest share is blue, with 11 km (43%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.0 km.

    The mountain at Schöneben

    Skied top to bottom, Schöneben gives 923 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,458 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 14 lifts: 4 gondolas, 4 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 5 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 5 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Piz S-chalambert Dadaint at 3,031 m, standing 650 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Schöneben through a normal winter

    At about 1,920 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 7 December and lasts to 16 May: about 23 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 45 cm, with 64 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 73 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 83 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Most winters land between 52 cm and 100 cm by mid-February.

    Schöneben: common questions

    When does Schöneben have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Schöneben at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 45 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Schöneben?

    Between 1,458 m and 2,381 m, so 923 m of vertical drop.

    Is Schöneben good for beginners?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Schöneben?

    Nauders Bergkastel, 8.4 km to the north, with about 49 km of pistes.
