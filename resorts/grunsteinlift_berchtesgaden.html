    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Grünsteinlift (Berchtesgaden) gives 109 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 642 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Almenwelt Lofer reaches 1,674 m.
