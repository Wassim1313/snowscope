    About Hochfelln

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Hochfelln sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,060 m to 1,638 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skilift Maiergschwendt, 4.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Hochfelln through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,349 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 December and keeps it until about 11 May, roughly 21 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late April at roughly 112 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 35 cm, with 72 cm in the best years. Most winters land between 31 cm and 106 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 92 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March.

    The mountain at Hochfelln

    578 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,638 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 5 lifts break down into 2 cable cars, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Hochgern (1,748 m), 4.1 km to the south-west, about 110 m higher than the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 3.1 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (58%, about 1.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Hochfelln: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hochfelln?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Hochfelln at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 35 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Hochfelln?

    Between 1,060 m and 1,638 m, so 578 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Hochfelln?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Hochfelln have?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
