    About Sun Meadows Kiyosato

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sun Meadows Kiyosato offers about 6.0 km of pistes (number 157 of 385 in Japan). Its lifts climb from 1,611 m to 1,880 m. Its nearest neighbour is Chateraise Ski Valley Nobeyama, 8.5 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 11 marked runs cover roughly 5.4 km. Most of the length is red (43%, about 2.3 km). No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them.

    How Sun Meadows Kiyosato is laid out

    The vertical drop is 269 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,611 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 1,880 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 3 chairlifts and 2 button lifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level.
