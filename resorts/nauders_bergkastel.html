    About Nauders Bergkastel

    Nauders Bergkastel is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 49 km of pistes put it at number 123 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,342 m to 2,735 m. The closest other ski area is Schöneben (8.4 km, south).

    The mountain at Nauders Bergkastel

    Skied top to bottom, Nauders Bergkastel gives 1,393 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,342 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.

    How Nauders Bergkastel compares nearby

    Of the 7 ski areas compared here, Nauders Bergkastel is the second biggest by piste length. The highest nearby is Kaunertaler Gletscher, topping out at 3,107 m.
