    About Nauders Bergkastel

    Nauders Bergkastel is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 49 km of pistes put it at number 123 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,342 m to 2,735 m. The closest other ski area is Schöneben (8.4 km, south).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 104 runs and around 49 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 25 km (52%). Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 5.8 km of difficult runs. 5.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    The mountain at Nauders Bergkastel

    Skied top to bottom, Nauders Bergkastel gives 1,393 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,342 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 7 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift and 9 moving carpets, 21 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 9 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Mittlerer Seekarkopf (3,063 m), 6.7 km to the east, about 328 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 2,038 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 65 cm (lean years 35 cm, snowy years 92 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 110 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 12 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 29 March, with about 120 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 77 cm and 147 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Nauders Bergkastel?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    How much snow does Nauders Bergkastel have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 65 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Nauders Bergkastel open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Nauders Bergkastel?

    The top is at 2,735 m and the base at 1,342 m.

    Who is Nauders Bergkastel best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 52% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
