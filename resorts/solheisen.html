    About Solheisen

    Neither huge nor tiny, Solheisen offers about 4.0 km of pistes (number 150 of 262 in Scandinavia). The ski area spans the mountain from 730 m up to 1,050 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Hemsedal skisenter, 8.6 km to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Solheisen

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 3.7 km in total. Most of the length is green (beginner) (58%, about 2.1 km). With 87% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 890 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May, about 28 weeks of good cover. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 3 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 103 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 62 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 40 cm in a poor year to 101 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 66 cm and 123 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 97 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    The mountain at Solheisen

    From top to bottom that makes 320 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. Above the ski area rises Hydalsberget (1,740 m), 7.9 km to the north-east, about 690 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Solheisen hold around 33 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 16 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (39 cm).

    Quick answers

    When does Solheisen have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Solheisen have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 62 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Solheisen?

    Between 730 m and 1,050 m, so 320 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Solheisen?

    Yes: 87% of the graded pistes, about 3.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Solheisen?

    Hemsedal skisenter, 8.6 km to the south-east, with about 48 km of pistes.
