    About Les Breuleux

    Neither huge nor tiny, Les Breuleux offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 660 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,069 m to 1,239 m. Tramelan is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the east.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 7.6 km south to Bugnenets-Savagnières, with about 16 km of runs. Its snow season is the shortest of the group, so it is best on a cold, snowy spell. Bugnenets-Savagnières reaches 1,448 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    What there is to ski at Les Breuleux

    In all, 5 marked runs cover roughly 4.5 km. The largest share is blue, with 2.6 km (57%). With 57% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Les Breuleux gives 170 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,239 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows. The highest named summit within 8 km is Chasseral at 1,607 m, standing 368 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Les Breuleux usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,154 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 18 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 8 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 40 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 59 cm.

    Questions about Les Breuleux

    When does Les Breuleux have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Les Breuleux at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 8 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Les Breuleux?

    Between 1,069 m and 1,239 m, so 170 m of vertical drop.

    Is Les Breuleux suitable for families?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 2.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Les Breuleux?

    Bugnenets-Savagnières, 7.6 km to the south, with about 16 km of pistes.
