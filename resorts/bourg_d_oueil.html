    About Bourg d'Oueil

    Bourg d'Oueil is a small ski area by the standards of the Pyrenees, with about 2.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,321 m up to 1,424 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Peyragudes, 9.2 km to the south.

    What there is to ski at Bourg d'Oueil

    Bourg d'Oueil has 10 graded runs adding up to about 2.1 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.2 km, or 55%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 12 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Bourg d'Oueil. The last seven days brought 18 cm of new snow. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 5 cm by Tuesday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 7 cm at mid-mountain).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Bourg d'Oueil gives 103 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,321 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Piau-Engaly reaches 2,527 m. All 3 lifts here are button lifts. Above the ski area rises Pic de Montious (2,173 m), 4.4 km to the west, about 749 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Bourg d'Oueil usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,372 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 25 cm, around 31 January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 65 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 9 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 36 cm).

    Bourg d'Oueil: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Bourg d'Oueil?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Bourg d'Oueil at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 9 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Bourg d'Oueil?

    Between 1,321 m and 1,424 m, so 103 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Bourg d'Oueil?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Bourg d'Oueil?

    Saint-Lary, 20 km to the west, with about 72 km of pistes.
