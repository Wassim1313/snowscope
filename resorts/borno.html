    About Borno

    With roughly 12 km of runs, Borno sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 950 m to 1,686 m. Presolana is the next resort, 6.7 km away to the west.

    The pistes of Borno

    Borno has 11 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.9 km, or 51%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.7 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,318 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 January and lasts to 7 April: about 12 weeks. A normal season tops out near 45 cm, around 5 March. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Most winters land between 14 cm and 70 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 23 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm; a snowy December gave 51 cm, a dry one 4 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    736 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 950 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 3 chairlifts and 1 button lift. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Pizzo Camino, 2,491 m high and 6.5 km north of the resort.

    Borno and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Montecampione (11 km south) has about 20 km of pistes against 12 km here. If snow is the priority, Monte Pora does better, with about 20 weeks of a good base instead of 12. For higher slopes, Colere climbs to 2,174 m.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Borno include Hotel Valle d'Oro (hotel), Capanna Plai (mountain hut), Hotel San Martino (hotel) and Hotel Aprica (hotel).

    Quick answers

    When does Borno have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early April.

    Can you ski at Borno at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 24 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Borno?

    The top is at 1,686 m and the base at 950 m.

    What level of skier is Borno best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 51% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Borno?

    Montecampione, 11 km to the south, with about 20 km of pistes.
