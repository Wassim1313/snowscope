    About Kaprun

    Kaprun is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 46 km of pistes put it at number 129 of 1,056. The slopes start at 765 m and reach 3,024 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lechnerberglifte, 7.4 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    2,259 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. With the bottom at 765 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 3,024 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 22 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 funicular, 6 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 6 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Hoher Tenn (3,368 m), 6.4 km to the south-east, about 344 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,894 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. The snow is usually deepest around 26 March, with about 95 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 86 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 59 cm and 110 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 47 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 26 cm in a poor year to 73 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 79 marked runs cover roughly 45 km. Most of the length is blue (36%, about 16 km). With 31% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.5 km. 7.4 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Kaprun include 4 mountain huts: Häuslalm, Krefelder Hütte, Salzburger Hütte and Kesselfall Alpenhaus. Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Kaprun?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Kaprun at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 47 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Kaprun?

    It opened on 10 October 2026; the last ski day is planned for 17 May 2027.

    How high is Kaprun?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,024 m; the lowest starts at 765 m (2,259 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Kaprun?

    Yes: 40% of the graded pistes, about 18 km, are easy.
