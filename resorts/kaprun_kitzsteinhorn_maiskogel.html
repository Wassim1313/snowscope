    About Kaprun

    Kaprun is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 46 km of pistes put it at number 129 of 1,056. The slopes start at 765 m and reach 3,024 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Lechnerberglifte, 7.4 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    2,259 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. With the bottom at 765 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its top at 3,024 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Kaprun include 4 mountain huts: Häuslalm, Krefelder Hütte, Salzburger Hütte and Kesselfall Alpenhaus. Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
