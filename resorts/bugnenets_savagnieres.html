    About Bugnenets-Savagnières

    Bugnenets-Savagnières is a mid-sized ski area with about 16 km of pistes, number 361 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,079 m and reach 1,448 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Crêt-du-Puy, 3.9 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 16 km. Most of the length is red (54%, about 8.6 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.1 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 369 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Its top at 1,448 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 10 lifts break down into 8 T-bars and 2 moving carpets. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Chasseral (1,607 m), 3.4 km to the east, about 159 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Bugnenets-Savagnières usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,264 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-February to late February, about 1 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 35 cm, around 16 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 11 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 59 cm). By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 110 cm.

    Questions about Bugnenets-Savagnières

    When is the best time to ski at Bugnenets-Savagnières?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    How much snow does Bugnenets-Savagnières have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 11 cm).

    How high is Bugnenets-Savagnières?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,448 m; the lowest starts at 1,079 m (369 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Bugnenets-Savagnières best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 54% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Bugnenets-Savagnières have?

    About 16 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
