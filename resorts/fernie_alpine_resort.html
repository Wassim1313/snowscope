    About Fernie Alpine Resort

    Fernie Alpine Resort ranks number 6 of 53 ski areas in Canada West by piste length, with about 82 km of runs. The slopes start at 1,059 m and reach 2,097 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Island Lake Cat Skiing (8.2 km, north-west).

    What there is to ski at Fernie Alpine Resort

    Counting every graded piste, there are 118 runs and around 82 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 39 km, or 48%. Intermediate runs make up 48% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 23 km of black or expert runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,578 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to mid-May, about 26 weeks of good cover. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 81 cm and 135 cm. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 100 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 72 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 51 cm in a poor year to 102 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 112 cm, around 1 March.

    How Fernie Alpine Resort is laid out

    The vertical drop is 1,038 m: enough height for proper long runs. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 7 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mount Fernie at 2,210 m, standing 113 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Fernie Alpine Resort?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Fernie Alpine Resort at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 72 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Fernie Alpine Resort?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026.

    What altitude is Fernie Alpine Resort at?

    Between 1,059 m and 2,097 m, so 1,038 m of vertical drop.

    Does Fernie Alpine Resort have steep runs?

    Yes: about 23 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.
