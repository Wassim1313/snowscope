    About Newcomb Ski Slope

    Compact and local, Newcomb Ski Slope has about 2.0 km of runs, small for US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 513 m to 556 m. Gore Mountain is the next resort, 34 km away to the south.

    How much snow Newcomb Ski Slope usually gets

    At about 534 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 28 December and lasts to 2 April: about 14 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 57 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 29 cm (lean years 10 cm, snowy years 48 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 9 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. Most winters land between 32 cm and 83 cm by mid-February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 43 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 513 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Baldwin Mountain at 863 m, standing 307 m above the top lift.

    Newcomb Ski Slope: common questions

    When does Newcomb Ski Slope have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to early April.

    Can you ski at Newcomb Ski Slope at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 29 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Newcomb Ski Slope?

    Its highest lift reaches 556 m; the lowest starts at 513 m (43 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Newcomb Ski Slope have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
