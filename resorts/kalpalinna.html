    About Kalpalinna

    Kalpalinna is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 190 of 262 in Scandinavia by length. Its lifts climb from 88 m to 159 m. Sveitsin Hiihtokeskus is the next resort, 33 km away to the south.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 124 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to late March, about 9 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 36 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 5 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 14 cm and 52 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 17 cm, with 30 cm in the best years.

    What there is to ski at Kalpalinna

    Kalpalinna has 11 graded runs adding up to about 2.1 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.8 km (85%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Kalpalinna

    Skied top to bottom, Kalpalinna gives 71 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 7 lifts break down into 6 button lifts and 1 drag lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Kilpivuori (162 m), Inkolinna (160 m) and Kappolanvuori (149 m).

    Questions about Kalpalinna

    When does Kalpalinna have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to late March.

    Is there snow at Kalpalinna at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Kalpalinna?

    Between 88 m and 159 m, so 71 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Kalpalinna?

    Yes: 85% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Kalpalinna?

    Messilän Lumikeskus, 53 km to the east, with about 6.0 km of pistes.
