    About Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp

    Around 31 km of marked runs place Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 197 by length. Skiing here happens between 599 m and 2,072 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Ramsau am Dachstein (12 km, south-west).

    The pistes of Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp

    In all, 36 marked runs cover roughly 24 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 18 km, or 78%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 1.3 km of difficult runs.

    How Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp gives 1,473 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Because the lifts start as low as 599 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,072 m. To get up there are 3 cable cars, 1 chairlift, 2 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Großer Koppenkarstein at 2,863 m, standing 791 m above the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 16 km south to Schladming Dachstein, with about 179 km of runs. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 4 of 7).

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,336 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 21 December and lasts to 2 May: about 19 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 32 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 58 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 26 February, with about 64 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 40 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 34 cm and 78 cm.

    Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Can you ski at Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 32 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Freesports Arena Dachstein Kripp?
