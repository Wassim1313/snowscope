    About Damüls-Mellau-Faschina

    Around 101 km of marked runs place Damüls-Mellau-Faschina among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 49 by length. The slopes start at 689 m and reach 2,021 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Damüls-Mellau, 0.4 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Damüls-Mellau-Faschina

    Counting every graded piste, there are 211 runs and around 97 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (36%, about 35 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 27 km.

    The mountain at Damüls-Mellau-Faschina

    1,332 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Because the lifts start as low as 689 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 31 lifts: 2 gondolas, 15 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 9 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 9 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Zitterklapfen (2,403 m), 7.6 km to the south-east, about 382 m higher than the top lift.
