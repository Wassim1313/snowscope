    About Damüls-Mellau-Faschina

    Around 101 km of marked runs place Damüls-Mellau-Faschina among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 49 by length. The slopes start at 689 m and reach 2,021 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Damüls-Mellau, 0.4 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Damüls-Mellau-Faschina

    Counting every graded piste, there are 211 runs and around 97 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (36%, about 35 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. The steepest pistes add up to about 27 km.

    The mountain at Damüls-Mellau-Faschina

    1,332 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Because the lifts start as low as 689 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 31 lifts: 2 gondolas, 15 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 2 button lifts, 1 drag lift, 1 rope tow and 9 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 9 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Zitterklapfen (2,403 m), 7.6 km to the south-east, about 382 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Damüls-Mellau-Faschina usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,355 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 73 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 43 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm, with 58 cm in the best years. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 40 cm and 104 cm.

    Damüls-Mellau-Faschina: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Damüls-Mellau-Faschina?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Damüls-Mellau-Faschina at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 36 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Damüls-Mellau-Faschina?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Damüls-Mellau-Faschina?

    Between 689 m and 2,021 m, so 1,332 m of vertical drop.

    Is Damüls-Mellau-Faschina suitable for families?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 40 km, are easy.
