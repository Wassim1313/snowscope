    About Three Summits

    With roughly 43 km of runs, Three Summits sits in the middle of the size table of US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 800 m up to 1,183 m. Its nearest neighbour is Summit West, 2.3 km north, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Three Summits

    In all, 103 marked runs cover roughly 43 km. Most of the length is blue square (58%, about 25 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 9.3 km of difficult runs.

    The snow right now

    How much snow Three Summits usually gets

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 992 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 61 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 45 cm; a snowy December gave 72 cm, a dry one 18 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 90 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 27 cm to 136 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    383 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The base at 800 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. To get up there are 15 chairlifts and 5 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. 5 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Snoqualmie Mountain East (1,915 m), 6.4 km to the north, about 732 m higher than the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Hyak Chalet (hotel), Summit House (hotel), Alpenhaus Lodge (mountain hut) and Slide-In Lodge (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Questions about Three Summits

    What is the best month to ski at Three Summits?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Three Summits have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 45 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Three Summits?

    The top is at 1,183 m and the base at 800 m.

    What level of skier is Three Summits best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 58% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Three Summits have?

    About 43 km of pistes and 20 lifts.
