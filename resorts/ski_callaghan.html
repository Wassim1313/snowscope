    About Ski Callaghan

    Ski Callaghan is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 848 m up to 998 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Whistler Blackcomb, 18 km to the south-east.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 923 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 5 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 30 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 311 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 191 cm (lean years 123 cm, snowy years 256 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 305 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 187 cm to 332 cm in mid-February in most winters. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 27 weeks.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Ski Callaghan today: about 18 cm up high. The last seven days brought 11 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 101 cm by now.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 150 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 848 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The only lift is a chairlift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hidden Peak at 2,280 m, standing 1,282 m above the top lift.

    Ski Callaghan: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Ski Callaghan?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Ski Callaghan have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 191 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Ski Callaghan at?

    Between 848 m and 998 m, so 150 m of vertical drop.
