    About Ski Callaghan

    Ski Callaghan is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 848 m up to 998 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Whistler Blackcomb, 18 km to the south-east.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Ski Callaghan today: about 11 cm up high. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 22 cm by Thursday.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 150 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 848 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.
