    About Kokava

    Kokava is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 220 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. Skiing here happens between 757 m and 814 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Látky, 5.4 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Kokava compares nearby

    For more terrain, Skorušiná (8.0 km north) has about 6.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. The highest nearby is Látky, topping out at 1,035 m.

    How Kokava is laid out

    The vertical drop is 57 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 757 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. To get up there are 4 button lifts and 1 rope tow. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
