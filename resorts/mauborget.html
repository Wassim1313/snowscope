    About Mauborget

    Mauborget has about 0.4 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,122 m and 1,190 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Robella, 4.8 km to the west.

    Snow this week

    Today there are about 2 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Mauborget. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 17 cm by Wednesday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 68 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses reaches 1,558 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Le Chasseron (1,607 m), 5.2 km to the west, about 417 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,156 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 14 February and lasts to 21 February: about 1 weeks. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 1 cm to 85 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 14 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 60 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 33 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Mauborget include Maison des Amis de la Nature (chalet), Les Tempêteries (chalet), La Grandsonnaz (chalet) and Les Illars (mountain hut).

    Quick answers

    When does Mauborget have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-February to late February.

    Is there snow at Mauborget at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    How high do the lifts go at Mauborget?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,190 m; the lowest starts at 1,122 m (68 m of vertical).

    How big is Mauborget?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
