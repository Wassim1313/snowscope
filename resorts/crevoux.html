    About Crévoux

    Neither huge nor tiny, Crévoux offers about 20 km of pistes (number 310 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,615 m and 2,537 m above sea level. Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul is the next resort, 6.1 km away to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 29 marked runs cover roughly 20 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 8.6 km, or 43%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 1.0 km of difficult runs. 4.2 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Snow at Crévoux through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,076 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 133 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 93 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 73 cm and 167 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 64 cm, with 128 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 11 weeks.

    The mountain at Crévoux

    The vertical drop is 922 m: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,615 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Le Grand Parpaillon at 2,990 m, standing 453 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Crévoux

    When does Crévoux have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Crévoux at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 64 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Crévoux?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,537 m; the lowest starts at 1,615 m (922 m of vertical).

    Is Crévoux suitable for families?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 9.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Crévoux?

    Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul, 6.1 km to the north-east, with about 131 km of pistes.
