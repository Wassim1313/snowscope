    About Nax

    Nax is a mid-sized ski area with about 19 km of pistes, number 324 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,508 m to 2,638 m. Vercorin is the next resort, 5.2 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Nax

    Counting every graded piste, there are 22 runs and around 19 km to ski. Most of the length is red (67%, about 13 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Nax today: about 50 cm up high. The last seven days brought 77 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (32 cm).

    The mountain at Nax

    The vertical drop is 1,130 m: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,508 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 3 chairlifts and 2 button lifts. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Above the ski area rises Becs de Bosson (3,143 m), 6.6 km to the south-east, about 505 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Nax usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,073 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 90 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 55 cm, with 79 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 102 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 62 cm to 119 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Nax?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Nax have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 55 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Nax at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,638 m; the lowest starts at 1,508 m (1,130 m of vertical).

    Who is Nax best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 67% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Does Nax have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 0.7 km of its pistes (4%).
