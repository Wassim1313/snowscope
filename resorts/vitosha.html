    About Vitosha

    Neither huge nor tiny, Vitosha offers about 15 km of pistes (number 13 of 51 in the Balkans). The slopes start at 1,519 m and reach 2,245 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Panichishte, 38 km south, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Vitosha gives 726 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,519 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,245 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km.
