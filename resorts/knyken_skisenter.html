    About Knyken Skisenter

    Neither huge nor tiny, Knyken Skisenter offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 194 of 262 in Scandinavia). The slopes start at 245 m and reach 383 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Steigalia skitrekk (19 km, south).

    How Knyken Skisenter compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 33 km east to Vassfjellet skisenter, with about 13 km of runs. Nerskogen skisenter has the longer snow season: around 30 weeks of solid cover, compared with 22 at Knyken Skisenter. For higher slopes, Nerskogen skisenter climbs to 1,060 m.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Knyken Skisenter has roughly 15 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 10 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 19 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Knyken Skisenter gives 138 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Vassfjellet skisenter, for one, climbs to 656 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 314 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 6 December and lasts to 4 May: about 22 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 48 cm and 119 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 44 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 73 cm, a dry one 24 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 91 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 77 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Knyken Skisenter: common questions

    When does Knyken Skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Knyken Skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 44 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Knyken Skisenter?

    The top is at 383 m and the base at 245 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Knyken Skisenter?

    Vassfjellet skisenter, 33 km to the east, with about 13 km of pistes.

    How big is Knyken Skisenter?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
