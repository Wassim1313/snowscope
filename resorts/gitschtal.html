    About Gitschtal

    Gitschtal is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 747 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 818 m to 1,136 m. The closest other ski area is Bergbahn Weissensee (5.4 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Gitschtal has 6 graded runs adding up to about 2.9 km. Most of the length is red (73%, about 2.1 km). Intermediate runs make up 73% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Gitschtal hold around 19 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 27 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (5 cm).

    How Gitschtal is laid out

    The vertical drop is 318 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,018 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Goldeck, reach 2,117 m. Its 4 lifts break down into 2 button lifts and 2 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Reißkofel, 2,371 m high and 7.2 km west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 977 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 7 cm to 49 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early February. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 11 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 27 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 24 cm, around 19 February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Gitschtal?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    How much snow does Gitschtal have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 11 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Gitschtal?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,136 m; the lowest starts at 818 m (318 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Gitschtal best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 73% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Gitschtal?

    Nassfeld, 12 km to the south, with about 70 km of pistes.
