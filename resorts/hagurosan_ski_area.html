    About Hagurosan

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Hagurosan sits in the middle of the size table of Japan. Its lifts climb from 240 m to 380 m. The closest other ski area is たらのきだいスキー場 (8.1 km, south-west).

    How Hagurosan is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 140 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 240 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 380 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    How high do the lifts go at Hagurosan?

    The top is at 380 m and the base at 240 m.
