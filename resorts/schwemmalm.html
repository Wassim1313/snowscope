    About Schwemmalm

    Neither huge nor tiny, Schwemmalm offers about 19 km of pistes (number 328 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,512 m and 2,625 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is San Vigilio, 17 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Schwemmalm

    Schwemmalm has 26 graded runs adding up to about 19 km. Most of the length is blue (49%, about 9.4 km). The grades are spread fairly evenly, so most levels find something to ski. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.6 km.

    The mountain at Schwemmalm

    From top to bottom that makes 1,113 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,512 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Hasenöhrl - L'Orecchia di Lepre (3,257 m), 5.2 km to the west, about 632 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 23 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,068 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to mid-May. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 74 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 45 cm to 101 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm, with 76 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 70 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Schwemmalm: common questions

    When does Schwemmalm have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Schwemmalm at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 42 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Schwemmalm?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,625 m; the lowest starts at 1,512 m (1,113 m of vertical).

    Is Schwemmalm suitable for families?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 9.4 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Schwemmalm have?

    About 19 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
