    About Trije Kralji

    Trije Kralji is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 1,153 m and 1,337 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Rogla (8.7 km, west).

    How Trije Kralji is laid out

    The vertical drop is 184 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 chairlift and 1 drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Žigartov vrh (1,346 m), Veliki vrh (1,344 m) and Mizni vrh (1,340 m).

    Snow at Trije Kralji through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,245 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 71 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 31 January, with about 15 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 32 cm, a dry one 0 cm.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Jakec (hotel), Štuhecov dom (mountain hut), Apartment Zeleni Dragulj (guesthouse) and Dom na Osankarici (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Trije Kralji?

    Aim for early February and mid-February.

    Can you ski at Trije Kralji at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 8 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 8 cm).

    What altitude is Trije Kralji at?

    The top is at 1,337 m and the base at 1,153 m.
