    About Trije Kralji

    Trije Kralji is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 1,153 m and 1,337 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Rogla (8.7 km, west).

    How Trije Kralji is laid out

    The vertical drop is 184 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Jakec (hotel), Štuhecov dom (mountain hut), Apartment Zeleni Dragulj (guesthouse) and Dom na Osankarici (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
