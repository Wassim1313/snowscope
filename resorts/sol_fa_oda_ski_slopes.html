    About SOL-FA Oda ski slopes

    Compact and local, SOL-FA Oda ski slopes has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Japan. Its lifts climb from 994 m to 1,206 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tengukogen, 18 km to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at SOL-FA Oda ski slopes

    SOL-FA Oda ski slopes has 3 graded runs adding up to about 2.0 km. Most of the length is green (49%, about 1.0 km). With 71% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How SOL-FA Oda ski slopes is laid out

    212 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 994 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.
