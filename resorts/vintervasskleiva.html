    About Vintervasskleiva

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Vintervasskleiva one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. The slopes start at 390 m and reach 538 m at the top. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gråkallparken, 0.9 km to the north-east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 464 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to early May, about 23 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 92 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Most winters land between 50 cm and 122 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 49 cm; a snowy December gave 81 cm, a dry one 24 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 2 April, with about 99 cm.

    How Vintervasskleiva is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Vintervasskleiva gives 148 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 538 m is the second highest of the 6 ski areas within 50 km. The only lift is a drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Storheia (565 m), Gråkallen (554 m) and Bosbergheia (537 m).

    Vintervasskleiva: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Vintervasskleiva?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Vintervasskleiva at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 49 cm deep.

    What altitude is Vintervasskleiva at?

    Between 390 m and 538 m, so 148 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Vintervasskleiva?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
