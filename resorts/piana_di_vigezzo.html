    About Piana di Vigezzo

    Neither huge nor tiny, Piana di Vigezzo offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 527 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,254 m and reach 2,053 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Malesco, 5.2 km to the south.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,654 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 December and lasts to 20 May: about 24 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 79 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 44 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 23 cm in a poor year to 84 cm in a big one. Most winters land between 54 cm and 129 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 24 February, with about 98 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 13 marked runs cover roughly 9.3 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.0 km, or 43%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. 1.5 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Piana di Vigezzo is laid out

    The vertical drop is 799 m: enough height for proper long runs. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 gondola, 2 chairlifts and 1 button lift. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. The skyline is dominated by Pizzo di Madèi, 2,551 m high and 7.5 km north of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Piana di Vigezzo?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Piana di Vigezzo at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 44 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Piana di Vigezzo?

    Between 1,254 m and 2,053 m, so 799 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Piana di Vigezzo?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 4.0 km, are easy.

    How big is Piana di Vigezzo?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
