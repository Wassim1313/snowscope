    About Adelboden

    Adelboden is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 564 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 1,401 m and 1,956 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Adelboden (4.8 km, south-west).

    What there is to ski at Adelboden

    Adelboden has 6 graded runs adding up to about 6.5 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.8 km, or 74%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Snow at Adelboden through a normal winter

    At about 1,678 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 12 December and lasts to 24 May: about 24 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and early March. Most winters land between 61 cm and 131 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 49 cm; a snowy December gave 75 cm, a dry one 22 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 84 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 101 cm.

    The mountain at Adelboden

    From top to bottom that makes 555 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With its base at 1,401 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Vordere Loner, 3,049 m high and 5.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Quick answers

    When does Adelboden have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late May.

    How much snow does Adelboden have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 49 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Adelboden at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,956 m; the lowest starts at 1,401 m (555 m of vertical).

    Who is Adelboden best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 74% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Adelboden?

    Adelboden, 4.8 km to the south-west, with about 83 km of pistes.
