    About Ormbergsbacken

    Ormbergsbacken has about 0.2 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 30 m to 68 m.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Ormbergsbacken today: about 16 cm up high. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 6 cm by Sunday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 6 cm at mid-mountain).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 38 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Hertsöberget (57 m), Svartöberget (25 m) and Mjölkuddsberget (25 m).

    How much snow Ormbergsbacken usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow at about 49 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 22 January and keeps it until about 16 April, roughly 12 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 16 cm; a snowy December gave 40 cm, a dry one 5 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 23 cm to 64 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 45 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 31 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Ormbergsbacken?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Ormbergsbacken at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 16 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Ormbergsbacken?

    Between 30 m and 68 m, so 38 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Ormbergsbacken have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
