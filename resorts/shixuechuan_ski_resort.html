    About Shixuechuan

    Shixuechuan is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 40 of 92 in China by length. Skiing here happens between 1,343 m and 1,477 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Fulong, 1.5 km to the west.

    What there is to ski at Shixuechuan

    The piste map lists 9 graded runs, about 4.6 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 1.9 km, or 41%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 1.1 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Shixuechuan

    The vertical drop is 134 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,343 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Thaiwoo reaches 2,152 m. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
