    About Peyragudes

    Only 9 of the 49 ski areas of the Pyrenees are larger than Peyragudes, which has some 44 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 1,389 m and 2,360 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Val-Louron, 5.9 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Peyragudes

    Counting every graded piste, there are 82 runs and around 43 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 17 km, or 40%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Advanced skiers have about 6.5 km of black or expert runs.

    How Peyragudes is laid out

    The vertical drop is 971 m: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,389 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. The lift system counts 23 lifts: 1 gondola, 9 chairlifts, 5 button lifts, 1 drag lift and 7 moving carpets. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there are 7 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Pico Belloc, 3,008 m high and 7.6 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 1,874 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 13 January and lasts to 18 March: about 9 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 8 cm: spring snow is mostly gone at this height. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and early March. A normal season tops out near 59 cm, around 31 January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 17 cm, from 2 cm in a poor year to 60 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 5 cm to 104 cm.

    Peyragudes: common questions

    When does Peyragudes have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Peyragudes at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 17 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Peyragudes?

    The announced opening day is 5 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 29 March 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Peyragudes?

    The top is at 2,360 m and the base at 1,389 m.

    Can beginners ski at Peyragudes?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 20 km, are easy.
