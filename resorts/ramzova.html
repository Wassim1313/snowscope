    About Ramzová

    With some 7.0 km of runs, Ramzová belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 764 m up to 1,328 m. Skiaréna R3 is the next resort, 1.1 km away to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Ramzová

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 6.8 km in total. The largest share is red, with 4.4 km (65%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 1.0 km of difficult runs.

    How Ramzová is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Ramzová gives 564 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 764 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 1 button lift.
