    About Hohwald Beatenberg

    Neither huge nor tiny, Hohwald Beatenberg offers about 7.0 km of pistes (number 577 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,290 m up to 1,604 m. Its nearest neighbour is Habkern, 3.5 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How much snow Hohwald Beatenberg usually gets

    Count on about 22 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,447 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-May. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 52 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 38 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 55 cm, a dry one 19 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Most winters land between 44 cm and 90 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 71 cm, around 28 February.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 6.5 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.4 km, or 68%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Hohwald Beatenberg gives 314 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 drag lift and 2 rope tows. The highest named summit within 8 km is Burgfeldstand at 2,063 m, standing 459 m above the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Sigriswill-Schwanden: about 12 km of pistes, 6.5 km to the west. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 3 of 7). Niederhorn reaches 1,938 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Hohwald Beatenberg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Hohwald Beatenberg have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 38 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Hohwald Beatenberg?

    The top is at 1,604 m and the base at 1,290 m.

    Is Hohwald Beatenberg good for beginners?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 4.4 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Hohwald Beatenberg?

    Sigriswill-Schwanden, 6.5 km to the west, with about 12 km of pistes.
