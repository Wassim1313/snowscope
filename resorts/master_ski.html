    About Master-Ski

    With some 7.0 km of runs, Master-Ski belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 566 m up to 685 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Master Ski, 0.5 km to the north-west.

    The pistes of Master-Ski

    The piste map lists 21 graded runs, about 7.4 km in total. Most of the length is blue (79%, about 5.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Master-Ski

    Skied top to bottom, Master-Ski gives 119 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 566 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Jaworzyna Krynicka reaches 1,108 m. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Master-Ski: common questions

    What altitude is Master-Ski at?

    Its highest lift reaches 685 m; the lowest starts at 566 m (119 m of vertical).

    Is Master-Ski suitable for families?

    Yes: 79% of the graded pistes, about 5.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Master-Ski have?

    About 7.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
