    About Mosty u Jablunkova

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Mosty u Jablunkova sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The ski area spans the mountain from 529 m up to 676 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skiareál Severka, 3.8 km to the west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    147 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 529 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.
