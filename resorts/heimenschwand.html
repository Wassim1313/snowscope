    About Heimenschwand

    Compact and local, Heimenschwand has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,002 m and reach 1,176 m at the top. Schindelberg Linden is the next resort, 1.3 km away to the north.

    How Heimenschwand compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Sigriswill-Schwanden: about 12 km of pistes, 12 km to the south-east. If snow is the priority, Sigriswill-Schwanden does better, with about 22 weeks of a good base instead of 11. For higher slopes, Sigriswill-Schwanden climbs to 1,460 m.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,089 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to mid-April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 25 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 40 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 17 cm to 63 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 30 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 43 cm.

    The mountain at Heimenschwand

    174 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Bichsel (1,208 m), Roteberg (1,201 m) and Egg (1,172 m).

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Alpenblick (chalet), Motorrad Team Konolfingen (chalet), Bären (hotel) and Parkhotel Schloss Hünigen (hotel).

    Questions about Heimenschwand

    When does Heimenschwand have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-January to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Heimenschwand at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 25 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Heimenschwand?

    The top is at 1,176 m and the base at 1,002 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Heimenschwand?

    Sigriswill-Schwanden, 12 km to the south-east, with about 12 km of pistes.

    How big is Heimenschwand?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
