    About Hoshino Resorts Nekoma Mountain

    Hoshino Resorts Nekoma Mountain is one of the bigger ski areas of Japan: about 23 km of pistes put it at number 11 of 385. The ski area spans the mountain from 736 m up to 1,335 m. Its nearest neighbour is Grand Sunpia Inawashiro Resort, 3.2 km south-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Hoshino Resorts Nekoma Mountain

    Hoshino Resorts Nekoma Mountain has 36 graded runs adding up to about 23 km. Green runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 55%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    599 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The base at 736 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. All 13 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.
