    About Scuol Motta Naluns

    Scuol Motta Naluns is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 43 km of pistes put it at number 138 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,294 m to 2,792 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tarasp, 4.8 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 56 graded runs, about 43 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 22 km, or 50%. Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 3.3 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,498 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its top at 2,792 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 1 gondola, 6 chairlifts, 2 T-bars and 4 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Nördliche Augstenspitze (3,228 m), 7.1 km to the north-west, about 436 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 2,043 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 7 December and keeps it until about 21 May, roughly 24 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 70 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 81 cm, around 27 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 44 cm (lean years 24 cm, snowy years 62 cm). The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 52 cm and 98 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Scuol Motta Naluns?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Scuol Motta Naluns at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 44 cm deep.

    When does Scuol Motta Naluns open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026.

    How high is Scuol Motta Naluns?

    Between 1,294 m and 2,792 m, so 1,498 m of vertical drop.

    Is Scuol Motta Naluns suitable for families?

    Yes: 42% of the graded pistes, about 18 km, are easy.
