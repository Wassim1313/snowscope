    About Schafmatt

    Compact and local, Schafmatt has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 745 m and reach 863 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Langenbruck (17 km, south-west).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 28 km west to Hohe Winde, with about 6.0 km of runs. For higher slopes, Schwengimatt climbs to 1,099 m.

    Snow at Schafmatt in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at about 804 m, the middle of the ski area stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    118 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 745 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Wisenberg (1,001 m), 5.7 km to the west, about 138 m higher than the top lift.
