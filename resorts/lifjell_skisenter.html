    About Lifjell Skisenter

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Lifjell Skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 445 m up to 652 m. Its nearest neighbour is Lifjell, 0.2 km west, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Lifjell Skisenter

    Counting every graded piste, there are 8 runs and around 4.4 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 4.4 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Lifjell Skisenter gives 207 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Øysteinnatten at 1,174 m, standing 522 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 21 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 548 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to early May. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 89 cm. Most winters land between 57 cm and 126 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 43 cm (lean years 23 cm, snowy years 74 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 73 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    Questions about Lifjell Skisenter

    When is the best time to ski at Lifjell Skisenter?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Is there snow at Lifjell Skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 43 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Lifjell Skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 652 m; the lowest starts at 445 m (207 m of vertical).

    Is Lifjell Skisenter good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 4.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Lifjell Skisenter?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
