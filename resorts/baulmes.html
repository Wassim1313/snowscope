    About Baulmes

    Neither huge nor tiny, Baulmes offers about 3.0 km of pistes (number 733 of 1,056 in the Alps). Skiing here happens between 1,210 m and 1,424 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Station d'Entre-les-Fourgs, 5.2 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,317 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to early March, about 8 weeks of good cover. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 140 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 57 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in 8 winters out of ten, typically 19 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 84 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 2.7 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.3 km, or 50%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    214 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Le Suchet (1,588 m), 1.1 km to the south-west, about 164 m higher than the top lift.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km south-west to Metabief, with about 26 km of runs. For snow it is second in the group, behind Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses (8 weeks of good cover, 8 here).

    Baulmes: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Baulmes?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    Is there snow at Baulmes at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 19 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Baulmes at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,424 m; the lowest starts at 1,210 m (214 m of vertical).

    Is Baulmes suitable for families?

    Yes: 50% of the graded pistes, about 1.3 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Baulmes?

    Metabief, 11 km to the south-west, with about 26 km of pistes.
