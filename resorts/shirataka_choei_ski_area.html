    About Shirataka Choei

    Shirataka Choei is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 521 m up to 619 m. The closest other ski area is Asahi Shizenkan Snow Park (12 km, north-west).

    Shirataka Choei today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Shirataka Choei hold around 33 cm of snow today. 4 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 15 cm of new snow is expected by Wednesday.

    How Shirataka Choei is laid out

    The vertical drop is 98 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 521 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.

    Shirataka Choei: common questions

    How high is Shirataka Choei?

    Its highest lift reaches 619 m; the lowest starts at 521 m (98 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Shirataka Choei?

    Zao Onsen, 24 km to the east, with about 39 km of pistes.
