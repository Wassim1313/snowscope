    About Pirika

    Pirika is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 180 of 385 in Japan by length. Its lifts climb from 127 m to 405 m. The closest other ski area is Oshamambe Choei (15 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 11 runs and around 4.7 km to ski. The largest share is green, with 2.2 km (47%). Good skiers will like it here; 33% of the runs are steep. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.6 km.

    How Pirika is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Pirika gives 278 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 127 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. The only lift is a chairlift.

    What altitude is Pirika at?

    Between 127 m and 405 m, so 278 m of vertical drop.

    Is Pirika suitable for families?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 2.2 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Pirika have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
