    About Winterberglifte

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Winterberglifte one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. The slopes start at 882 m and reach 947 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Skilift Weißenbach (4.4 km, south).

    Winterberglifte and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Kandellifte: about 3.0 km of pistes, 15 km to the south-west. For higher slopes, Kandellifte climbs to 1,201 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    65 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 882 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Kandellifte reaches 1,201 m.

    There is no snow on the ground at Winterberglifte yet. The coming days should add about 6 cm by Tuesday.

    Quick answers

    What altitude is Winterberglifte at?

    Its highest lift reaches 947 m; the lowest starts at 882 m (65 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Winterberglifte?

    Kandellifte, 15 km to the south-west, with about 3.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Winterberglifte?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
