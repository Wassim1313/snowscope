    About Skilift Lahm

    Skilift Lahm has about 0.2 km of pistes. The slopes start at 480 m and reach 526 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Skilift Romansthal, 7.5 km west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 503 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 28 January, with about 6 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 16 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 13 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and late December.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Skilift Lahm gives 46 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 480 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Ochsenkopf reaches 1,012 m. The only lift is a rope tow.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Skilift Lahm?

    Aim for mid-December and late December.

    Is there snow at Skilift Lahm at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 6 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 1 cm).

    How high is Skilift Lahm?

    Its highest lift reaches 526 m; the lowest starts at 480 m (46 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Skilift Lahm have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
