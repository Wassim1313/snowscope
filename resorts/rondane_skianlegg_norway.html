    When the snow is good

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,018 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May. Depth normally peaks in mid-March at roughly 102 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 96 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 78 cm to 120 cm in mid-February in most winters. For about 3 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 69 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 91 cm in the best years.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Rondane Høyfjellshotell (hotel), Mysusæter fjell-losji (hotel), Rondane Fjellstue (guesthouse) and Veslestua (chalet).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Rondane Skianlegg?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Rondane Skianlegg at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 69 cm deep.
