    About Skilift Riedlberg

    Skilift Riedlberg is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 709 m and 840 m above sea level. Großer Arber is the next resort, 5.5 km away to the north-east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    131 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 709 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Großer Arber reaches 1,437 m.

    Questions about Skilift Riedlberg

    What altitude is Skilift Riedlberg at?

    Its highest lift reaches 840 m; the lowest starts at 709 m (131 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Skilift Riedlberg?

    Großer Arber, 5.5 km to the north-east, with about 11 km of pistes.

    How big is Skilift Riedlberg?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
