    About Stubaier Gletscher

    Stubaier Gletscher is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 49 km of pistes put it at number 125 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,692 m and 3,208 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gries, 11 km to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,516 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,692 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 5 gondolas, 6 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 5 moving carpets, 23 lifts in all. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. For small children there are 5 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Zuckerhütl (3,508 m), 3.8 km to the south-east, about 300 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,450 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 11 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 28 March, with about 195 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 183 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 120 cm to 222 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 99 cm (lean years 62 cm, snowy years 137 cm).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 101 graded runs, about 49 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 23 km, or 46%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.6 km.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Stubaier Gletscher include Dresdner Hütte (mountain hut), Alpensporthotel Mutterberg (hotel), Hildesheimer Hütte (mountain hut) and Sulzenauhütte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Stubaier Gletscher?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Stubaier Gletscher have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 99 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Stubaier Gletscher open for the 2026/27 season?

    It opened on 9 October 2026.

    How high is Stubaier Gletscher?

    The top is at 3,208 m and the base at 1,692 m.

    Can beginners ski at Stubaier Gletscher?

    Yes: 45% of the graded pistes, about 22 km, are easy.
