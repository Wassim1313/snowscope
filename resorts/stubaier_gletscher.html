    About Stubaier Gletscher

    Stubaier Gletscher is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 49 km of pistes put it at number 125 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,692 m and 3,208 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gries, 11 km to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,516 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,692 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Stubaier Gletscher include Dresdner Hütte (mountain hut), Alpensporthotel Mutterberg (hotel), Hildesheimer Hütte (mountain hut) and Sulzenauhütte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
