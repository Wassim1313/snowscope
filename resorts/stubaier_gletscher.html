    The snow at Stubaier Gletscher, winter after winter

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Stubaier Gletscher (2,450 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 99 cm, with a normal range of 74 cm–122 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 178 cm (141 cm–204 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 195 cm.

    By 15 December 83 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 96 %.

    2000-01 was the big one (303 cm); 2024-25 the lean one (142 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late October to mid-June (236 days).

    Stubaier Gletscher: quick answers

    Is there snow in Stubaier Gletscher at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 99 cm at Christmas, and 96 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 52 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Stubaier Gletscher?

    March: the snow peaks at about 195 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late October to mid-June.

    How much snow does Stubaier Gletscher get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 195 cm in late March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 303 cm (2000-01), the thinnest 142 cm (2024-25).

    How high is Stubaier Gletscher?

    The top is at 3,208 m and the base at 1,692 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Stubaier Gletscher?

    Sölden, 12 km to the west, with about 107 km of pistes.

    How big is Stubaier Gletscher?

    About 49 km of pistes and 23 lifts.

    About Stubaier Gletscher

    Stubaier Gletscher is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 49 km of pistes put it at number 125 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,692 m and 3,208 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gries, 11 km to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,516 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The bottom station at 1,692 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Stubaier Gletscher today: about 52 cm up high. The last seven days brought 38 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 20 cm by Friday.
