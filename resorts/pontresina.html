    About Pontresina

    Pontresina has about 0.3 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,841 m up to 1,925 m. Its nearest neighbour is Corviglia, 7.1 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 84 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,841 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Corvatsch-Furtschellas, for one, climbs to 3,296 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar, 2 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Piz Languard at 3,261 m, standing 1,336 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Pontresina usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,883 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 December and lasts to 23 April: about 18 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 33 cm, from 18 cm in a poor year to 58 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 31 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is usually deepest around 16 February, with about 61 cm. Most winters land between 39 cm and 80 cm by mid-February. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    Pontresina: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Pontresina?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Pontresina have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 33 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Pontresina?

    Between 1,841 m and 1,925 m, so 84 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Pontresina?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
