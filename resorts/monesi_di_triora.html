    About Monesi di Triora

    With roughly 11 km of runs, Monesi di Triora sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,371 m to 2,157 m. The closest other ski area is Riserva Bianca (17 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Monesi di Triora

    The piste map lists 14 graded runs, about 11 km in total. Freeride runs make up the biggest part: 11 km, or 97%. Steep ground is the speciality: 97% of the length is graded difficult. 11 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Monesi di Triora gives 786 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,371 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 2,157 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mont Bertrand / Monte Bertrand at 2,482 m, standing 325 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,764 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 6 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 44 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 70 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 2 March.

    Staying nearby

    Near the lifts you will find Rifugio La Terza (mountain hut), Hotel San Bernardo (hotel), Agriturismo il Castagno (guesthouse) and Rifugio Realdo (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Monesi di Triora: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Monesi di Triora?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Monesi di Triora have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 6 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Monesi di Triora at?

    The top is at 2,157 m and the base at 1,371 m.

    Is Monesi di Triora good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 11 km of its graded pistes (97%) are difficult.

    Is there a larger resort near Monesi di Triora?

    Riserva Bianca, 17 km to the north-west, with about 42 km of pistes.
