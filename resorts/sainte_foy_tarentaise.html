    About Sainte-Foy Tarentaise

    Around 27 km of marked runs place Sainte-Foy Tarentaise among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 228 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,509 m to 2,620 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Rosière, 7.7 km to the north-west.

    How much snow Sainte-Foy Tarentaise usually gets

    At about 2,064 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 November and lasts to 27 May: about 27 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 161 cm. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Most winters land between 97 cm and 189 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 134 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 83 cm; a snowy December gave 128 cm, a dry one 43 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,111 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,509 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets, 6 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Mont Pourri, 3,779 m high and 7.0 km south-west of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Sainte-Foy Tarentaise include Black Diamond Lodge (hotel), Résidence Club MMV L’Étoile des Cimes (hotel), chalet du Praz (chalet) and Le Rocher (chalet).

    Sainte-Foy Tarentaise: common questions

    When does Sainte-Foy Tarentaise have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Sainte-Foy Tarentaise at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 83 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Sainte-Foy Tarentaise?

    The top is at 2,620 m and the base at 1,509 m.

    Is Sainte-Foy Tarentaise good for beginners?

    Yes: 48% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Sainte-Foy Tarentaise?

    Paradiski, 14 km to the south-west, with about 398 km of pistes.
