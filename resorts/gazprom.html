    About Газпром

    About 37 km of pistes make Газпром one of the ten biggest ski areas of Russia (number 4). The ski area spans the mountain from 780 m up to 2,253 m. There are 3 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Альпика-Сервис, 3.2 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 36 graded runs, about 31 km in total. Most of the length is red (51%, about 16 km). Intermediate runs make up 51% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 3.3 km.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 1,473 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 980 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 5 gondolas, 5 chair-gondolas, 6 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 2 moving carpets, 21 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Quick answers

    How high is Газпром?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,253 m; the lowest starts at 780 m (1,473 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Газпром best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 51% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Газпром?

    About 37 km of pistes and 21 lifts.
