    About Stacja Narciarska Kaniówka

    Stacja Narciarska Kaniówka is a small ski area by the standards of the Carpathians, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 776 m and 874 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Stacja narciarska Horników wierc (0.1 km, north-west).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 98 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 976 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: U Steni, for one, climbs to 989 m. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chairlift, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    How Stacja Narciarska Kaniówka compares nearby

    Skiers after mileage can drive 1.6 km north-west to Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia, with about 17 km of runs.
