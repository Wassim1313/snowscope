    About Slavske

    Slavske is one of the bigger ski areas of the Carpathians: about 7.0 km of pistes put it at number 37 of 312. Its lifts climb from 678 m to 1,213 m. Slavske is the next resort, 6.9 km away to the north.

    What there is to ski at Slavske

    The piste map lists 12 graded runs, about 6.5 km in total. Most of the length is blue (78%, about 5.1 km). With 78% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Slavske

    535 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 678 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 3 T-bars and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work.
