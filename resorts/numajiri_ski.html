    About Numajiri Ski

    Numajiri Ski is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 97 of 385 in Japan by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 865 m up to 1,213 m. Its nearest neighbour is Minowa, 3.8 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Numajiri Ski

    The piste map lists 18 graded runs, about 9.1 km in total. The largest share is beginner, with 4.9 km (55%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Numajiri Ski is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 348 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 865 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle.

    Questions about Numajiri Ski

    How high is Numajiri Ski?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,213 m; the lowest starts at 865 m (348 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Numajiri Ski?

    Yes: 84% of the graded pistes, about 7.6 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Numajiri Ski have?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
