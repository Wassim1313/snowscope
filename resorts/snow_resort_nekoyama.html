    About Snow Resort Nekoyama

    Snow Resort Nekoyama is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 263 of 385 in Japan by length. Skiing here happens between 697 m and 1,000 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Dogoyama-Kougen (2.7 km, north).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 303 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 697 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw.
