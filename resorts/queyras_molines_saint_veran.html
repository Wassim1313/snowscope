    About Queyras

    Queyras is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 30 km of pistes put it at number 205 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,739 m to 2,805 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Queyras, 7.1 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Queyras has 44 graded runs adding up to about 30 km. The largest share is red, with 12 km (41%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    The mountain at Queyras

    From top to bottom that makes 1,066 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,739 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,805 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 6 button lifts, 4 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Tête de Longet, 3,146 m high and 6.7 km south-east of the resort.

    Snow at Queyras through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,272 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 6 December and keeps it until about 6 May, roughly 22 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 48 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 94 cm in the best years. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 62 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 47 cm and 123 cm. A normal season tops out near 99 cm, around 7 March. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Queyras?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Queyras at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 48 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Queyras?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,805 m; the lowest starts at 1,739 m (1,066 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Queyras?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 16 km, are easy.

    How big is Queyras?

    About 30 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
