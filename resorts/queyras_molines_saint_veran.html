    About Queyras

    Queyras is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 30 km of pistes put it at number 205 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,739 m to 2,805 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Queyras, 7.1 km to the north.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Queyras has roughly 58 cm of snow on the ground today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 34 cm of new snow is expected by Tuesday.

    The mountain at Queyras

    From top to bottom that makes 1,066 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,739 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,805 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
