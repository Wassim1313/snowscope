    About Zásada

    Zásada is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 521 m up to 612 m. The closest other ski area is Lyžařský vlek Filip (3.4 km, north).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    91 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 521 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Tanvaldský Špičák, reach 807 m. The only lift is a T-bar.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 566 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 12 February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 3 cm to 53 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually late January and early February. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 12 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 46 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Zásada

    When is the best time to ski at Zásada?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Zásada at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Zásada?

    The top is at 612 m and the base at 521 m.
