    About Sattelegg

    Sattelegg is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,179 m and 1,219 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Wägital, 2.8 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Sattelegg through a normal winter

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,199 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 48 cm. Most winters land between 22 cm and 70 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 34 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 27 cm, from 10 cm in a poor year to 42 cm in a big one.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    40 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a T-bar. The highest named summit within 8 km is Diethelm at 2,099 m, standing 880 m above the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 11 km south-west to Skilift Oberiberg, with about 4.0 km of runs. Only Skilift Oberiberg keeps a solid base longer: about 18 weeks against 17 here.

    Quick answers

    When does Sattelegg have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Sattelegg at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 27 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Sattelegg at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,219 m; the lowest starts at 1,179 m (40 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Sattelegg?

    Skilift Oberiberg, 11 km to the south-west, with about 4.0 km of pistes.

    How big is Sattelegg?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
