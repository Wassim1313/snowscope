    About New Greenpia Tsunan

    New Greenpia Tsunan is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 145 of 385 in Japan by length. The slopes start at 657 m and reach 963 m at the top. Nakasato Kiyotsu is the next resort, 8.4 km away to the north-east.

    New Greenpia Tsunan and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.4 km east to Gala Yuzawa Snow Resort, with about 19 km of runs. For higher slopes, Kagura Area climbs to 1,838 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, New Greenpia Tsunan gives 306 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 657 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.

    Snow this week
