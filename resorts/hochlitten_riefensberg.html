    About Hochlitten-Riefensberg

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Hochlitten-Riefensberg sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 970 m up to 1,154 m. Steibis is the next resort, 2.4 km away to the east.

    How Hochlitten-Riefensberg compares nearby

    Of the 7 ski areas compared here, Hochlitten-Riefensberg is the third biggest by piste length. For more reliable snow, Hochhäderich usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 20 weeks, against 5 here. The highest nearby is Steibis, topping out at 1,704 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 184 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 970 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its 4 lifts break down into 3 T-bars and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Hochgrat (1,834 m), 7.3 km to the east, about 680 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,062 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 February and lasts to 8 March: about 5 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 11 February, with about 32 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 21 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 18 cm (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 33 cm). Year to year the snow is steady, with 14 cm to 55 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Hochlitten-Riefensberg

    When is the best time to ski at Hochlitten-Riefensberg?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early February to early March.

    Can you ski at Hochlitten-Riefensberg at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 18 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Hochlitten-Riefensberg at?

    Between 970 m and 1,154 m, so 184 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Hochlitten-Riefensberg best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 63% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Hochlitten-Riefensberg?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
