    About Saint François Longchamp

    Saint François Longchamp is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 405 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,761 m to 2,516 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Le Grand Domaine, 2.2 km to the north-east.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Saint François Longchamp hold around 61 cm of snow today. 25 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 89 cm of new snow is expected by Saturday.

    Other resorts close by

    Skiers after mileage can drive 18 km south-east to Les Trois Vallées, with about 542 km of runs. The highest nearby is Les Trois Vallées, topping out at 3,221 m.

    How Saint François Longchamp is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Saint François Longchamp gives 755 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,761 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are La Perelle (hotel), Hôtel Le Lac Bleu (hotel), Goélia Les Quatre Vallées (chalet) and Parc Madeleine (chalet).
