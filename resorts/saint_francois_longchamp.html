    About Saint François Longchamp

    Saint François Longchamp is a mid-sized ski area with about 14 km of pistes, number 405 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,761 m to 2,516 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Le Grand Domaine, 2.2 km to the north-east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 16 marked runs cover roughly 14 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 7.9 km, or 58%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 28 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,138 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May or later. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 123 cm and 226 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 101 cm, from 53 cm in a poor year to 146 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 186 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 194 cm, around 2 March. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Saint François Longchamp hold around 31 cm of snow today. 19 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 108 cm of new snow is expected by Friday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 39 cm).

    How Saint François Longchamp is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Saint François Longchamp gives 755 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,761 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 2 chairlifts and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Le Cheval Noir (2,832 m), 2.7 km to the east, about 316 m higher than the top lift.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are La Perelle (hotel), Hôtel Le Lac Bleu (hotel), Goélia Les Quatre Vallées (chalet) and Parc Madeleine (chalet).

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Saint François Longchamp?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Saint François Longchamp at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 101 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Saint François Longchamp?

    The top is at 2,516 m and the base at 1,761 m.

    Is Saint François Longchamp good for beginners?

    Yes: 58% of the graded pistes, about 7.9 km, are easy.

    Can Saint François Longchamp make its own snow?

    Yes, on about 2.8 km of its pistes (20%).
