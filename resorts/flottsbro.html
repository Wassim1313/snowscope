    About Flottsbro

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Flottsbro sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 4 m up to 102 m. Its nearest neighbour is Ekebyhovsbacken, 6.3 km north-west, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 1.7 km to ski. Most of the length is red (47%, about 0.8 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    98 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 102 m. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 chairlift, 1 button lift and 1 drag lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Flottsbrobacken (103 m), Masmoberget (94 m) and Vårbergstoppen (90 m).

    Snow at Flottsbro through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 53 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 3 cm; a snowy December gave 20 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 33 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 5 February, with about 9 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    Flottsbro: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Flottsbro?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    How much snow does Flottsbro have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only 7 winters out of ten had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 3 cm).

    What altitude is Flottsbro at?

    Between 4 m and 102 m, so 98 m of vertical drop.

    Is Flottsbro good for beginners?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 0.9 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Flottsbro have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
