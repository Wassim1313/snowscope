    About Asahigaoka

    Asahigaoka is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 196 m up to 289 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Niseko United, 6.2 km to the south-west.

    The mountain at Asahigaoka

    From top to bottom that makes 93 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 196 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. The only lift is a chairlift.

    Asahigaoka: common questions

    How high is Asahigaoka?

    Its highest lift reaches 289 m; the lowest starts at 196 m (93 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Asahigaoka?

    Niseko United, 6.2 km to the south-west, with about 59 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Asahigaoka have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
