    About Saint-Lary

    Only 3 of the 49 ski areas of the Pyrenees are larger than Saint-Lary, which has some 72 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 1,595 m to 2,462 m. The closest other ski area is Saint Lary-Soulan (5.9 km, east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Saint-Lary has 113 graded runs adding up to about 71 km. Most of the length is blue (57%, about 40 km). With 62% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How much snow Saint-Lary usually gets

    At about 2,028 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 26 December and lasts to 13 April: about 16 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 29 cm; a snowy December gave 74 cm, a dry one 3 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 80 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 11 cm to 119 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 25 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 867 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,595 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 2 gondolas, 10 chairlifts, 10 button lifts and 4 moving carpets, 27 lifts in all. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts. Above the ski area rises Pic de Bugatet (2,877 m), 7.0 km to the west, about 415 m higher than the top lift.

That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 8 cm at mid-mountain). The 2026/27 season at Saint-Lary is announced to start on 4 December 2026 and to run until 29 March 2027.

    Questions about Saint-Lary

    What is the best month to ski at Saint-Lary?

    Aim for early February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Saint-Lary have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 29 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Saint-Lary?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 29 March 2027.

    What altitude is Saint-Lary at?

    Between 1,595 m and 2,462 m, so 867 m of vertical drop.

    Is Saint-Lary suitable for families?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 44 km, are easy.
