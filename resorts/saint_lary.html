    About Saint-Lary

    Only 3 of the 49 ski areas of the Pyrenees are larger than Saint-Lary, which has some 72 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 1,595 m to 2,462 m. The closest other ski area is Saint Lary-Soulan (5.9 km, east).

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 867 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,595 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 7 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.
