    About Mont-Bellevue (Sherbrooke)

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Mont-Bellevue (Sherbrooke) sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. The ski area spans the mountain from 248 m up to 325 m. Its nearest neighbour is Parc du Mont Hatley, 9.2 km south, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Mont-Bellevue (Sherbrooke) has 10 graded runs adding up to about 3.5 km. Most of the length is green circle (71%, about 2.5 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 77 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 248 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Mont Orford, for one, climbs to 837 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift and 1 drag lift, 2 lifts in all. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mont John-S. Bourque (365 m), Mont Bellevue (333 m) and Colline de Beauvoir (313 m).

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 10 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 286 m in an ordinary winter, from mid-January to late March. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 46 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 20 cm, with 42 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 22 cm to 65 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Mont-Bellevue (Sherbrooke)

    When is the best time to ski at Mont-Bellevue (Sherbrooke)?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to late March.

    How much snow does Mont-Bellevue (Sherbrooke) have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 20 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Mont-Bellevue (Sherbrooke)?

    The top is at 325 m and the base at 248 m.

    Is Mont-Bellevue (Sherbrooke) good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 3.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Mont-Bellevue (Sherbrooke)?

    Bromont Montagne d'Expérience, 58 km to the west, with about 46 km of pistes.
