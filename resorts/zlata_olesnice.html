    About Zlatá Olešnice

    Compact and local, Zlatá Olešnice has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Central Europe. Its lifts climb from 513 m to 652 m. The closest other ski area is Skiareál Rejdice (2.6 km, north).

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, Paseky nad Jizerou (2.8 km north-east) has about 7.0 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. If snow is the priority, Skiareál Rejdice does better, with about 7 weeks of a good base instead of 3. For higher slopes, Paseky nad Jizerou climbs to 859 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.2 km. Most of the length is blue (60%, about 0.7 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Zlatá Olešnice is laid out

    The vertical drop is 139 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 513 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. The only lift is a button lift.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Zlatá Olešnice hold around 3 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 5 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (6 cm).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 3 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 582 m) in an ordinary winter, from early February to late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 54 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 12 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 47 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 32 cm.

    Zlatá Olešnice: common questions

    When does Zlatá Olešnice have the most snow?

    Aim for late January and early February; the cover is usually good from early February to late February.

    Can you ski at Zlatá Olešnice at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 12 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Zlatá Olešnice at?

    The top is at 652 m and the base at 513 m.

    Is Zlatá Olešnice good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Zlatá Olešnice?

    Paseky nad Jizerou, 2.8 km to the north-east, with about 7.0 km of pistes.
