    Heights, vertical and lifts

    799 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 22 lifts: 5 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 8 button lifts and 5 moving carpets. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Skurvefjellet at 1,741 m, standing 302 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,040 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 7 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 30 weeks. The snow is usually deepest around 15 March, with about 122 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 79 cm, with 114 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 87 cm and 144 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 118 cm, plenty for sunny spring days.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Hemsedal skisenter?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at Hemsedal skisenter at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 79 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Hemsedal skisenter open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Hemsedal skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,439 m; the lowest starts at 640 m (799 m of vertical).

    Is Hemsedal skisenter good for beginners?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 35 km, are easy.
