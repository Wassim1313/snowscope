    About Saint-Hilaire-du-Touvet

    Saint-Hilaire-du-Touvet is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 672 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 974 m and reach 1,318 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Col de Marcieu, 4.9 km to the north-east.

    The mountain at Saint-Hilaire-du-Touvet

    Skied top to bottom, Saint-Hilaire-du-Touvet gives 344 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,174 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Saint-Hilaire-du-Touvet include 4 chalets: Gîte Pierre Dorée et Bois Bossu, Chalet Nurit, Gîte des Petites Roches and L'air Libre.
