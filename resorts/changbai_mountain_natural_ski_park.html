    About Changbai Mountain Natural Ski Pa

    Changbai Mountain Natural Ski Pa is a ski area. The slopes start at 1,767 m and reach 2,092 m at the top. Hongsongwang is the next resort, 21 km away to the north-east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 325 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,767 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,092 m.
