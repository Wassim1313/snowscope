    Mt. Bachelor in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Mt. Bachelor (about 7,320 ft (2,231 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 63 in (159 cm) of cover (41 in (105 cm) to 91 in (230 cm) in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 124 in (315 cm); the middle half of winters range from 94 in (238 cm) to 154 in (391 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 152 in (385 cm) at the peak of a typical winter.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 83 % of winters, and by 1 January in 91 %.

    The deepest winter was 1998-99 (283 in (720 cm) at its peak); the thinnest was 2014-15 (46 in (118 cm)).

    Expect 12 in (30 cm) or more from mid-November until early June in a normal winter, roughly 204 days.

    Questions about Mt. Bachelor

    Does Mt. Bachelor have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 63 in (159 cm) at Christmas, and 91 % of the past 46 winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 1 January. Today there is 5.9 in (15 cm).

    Which month has the most snow at Mt. Bachelor?

    March: the snow peaks at about 152 in (385 cm) then. A base of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from mid-November to early June.

    How snowy is Mt. Bachelor?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 152 in (385 cm) in late March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 283 in (720 cm) (1998-99), the thinnest 46 in (118 cm) (2014-15).

    How high is Mt. Bachelor?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,724 m; the lowest starts at 1,738 m (986 m of vertical).

    How big is Mt. Bachelor?

    About 95 km of pistes and 15 lifts.
