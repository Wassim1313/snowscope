    About Mt. Bachelor

    With some 95 km of runs, Mt. Bachelor belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of US West. Skiing here happens between 1,738 m and 2,724 m above sea level.

    Runs by difficulty

    Mt. Bachelor has 128 graded runs adding up to about 95 km. The largest share is blue square, with 52 km (55%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Experts can count on some 28 km of difficult runs.

    How much snow Mt. Bachelor usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,231 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 146 cm and 422 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 159 cm, with 271 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 331 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 25 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and early February. The snow is usually deepest around 31 March, with about 385 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 986 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,738 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its 15 lifts break down into 12 chairlifts and 3 moving carpets. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Bachelor (2,764 m), Tumalo Mountain (2,370 m) and Tot Mountain (2,309 m).

    Mt. Bachelor: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Mt. Bachelor?

    Aim for early January and early February; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Mt. Bachelor at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 159 cm deep.

    When does Mt. Bachelor open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026.

    How high is Mt. Bachelor?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,724 m; the lowest starts at 1,738 m (986 m of vertical).

    Does Mt. Bachelor have steep runs?

    Yes: about 28 km of its graded pistes (29%) are difficult.
