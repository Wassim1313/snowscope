    About Granite Gorge

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Granite Gorge sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. Its lifts climb from 248 m to 400 m. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Bellows Falls Ski Tow, 26 km to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 12 runs and around 4.3 km to ski. Most of the length is blue square (43%, about 1.9 km). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 152 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 248 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets, 4 lifts in all. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Boynton Hill (529 m), 7.6 km to the north, about 129 m higher than the top lift.

    How much snow Granite Gorge usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 324 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 28 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 22 cm. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 4 cm to 51 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March.

    Granite Gorge: common questions

    When does Granite Gorge have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March.

    Is there snow at Granite Gorge at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 9 cm).

    What altitude is Granite Gorge at?

    Between 248 m and 400 m, so 152 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Granite Gorge best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 43% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Granite Gorge?

    Mount Sunapee, 42 km to the north, with about 31 km of pistes.
