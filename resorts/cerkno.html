    What 46 winters say about Cerkno

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Cerkno (1,118 m), from 46 past winters.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 13 cm, with a normal range of 6 cm–32 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 28 cm (14 cm–56 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 29 cm.

    By 15 December 9 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 15 %.

    Best winter on record: 2005-06, peaking at 141 cm. Worst: 2001-02, which never passed 14 cm.

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early January to late March, about 83 days.

    This winter so far: 16 cm on 27 November, against a usual 3 cm for the date.

    About Cerkno

    Cerkno is a mid-sized ski area with about 9.0 km of pistes, number 507 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 946 m up to 1,290 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Soriška planina, 8.6 km to the north-west.

    What there is to ski at Cerkno

    In all, 21 marked runs cover roughly 9.3 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 4.3 km, or 46%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Lifts and terrain

    344 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 946 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 button lift, 6 lifts in all. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. The skyline is dominated by Porezen, 1,632 m high and 6.5 km west of the resort.

    How much snow Cerkno usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,118 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 13 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 55 cm, a dry one 2 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 8 February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 2 cm to 73 cm.
