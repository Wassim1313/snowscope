    About Steinplatte Winklmoosalm

    Steinplatte Winklmoosalm is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 37 km of pistes put it at number 170 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 768 m to 1,853 m. Hausberglift is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the south.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,310 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 14 December and keeps it until about 7 May, roughly 21 weeks. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 38 cm and 90 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 33 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 9 cm, snowy years 64 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 57 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 70 cm.

    How Steinplatte Winklmoosalm is laid out

    1,085 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Below about 968 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 1,853 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 10 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 16 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Steinplatte (1,869 m), Steinplattengipfel (1,848 m) and Dürrnbachhorn (1,778 m).
