    About Val Comelico

    Neither huge nor tiny, Val Comelico offers about 23 km of pistes (number 273 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,211 m and reach 1,941 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Auronzo (8.5 km, south-west).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 39 runs and around 21 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (63%, about 13 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    How much snow Val Comelico usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,576 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to late April, about 18 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 34 cm and 82 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 29 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 11 cm in a poor year to 57 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 41 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 51 cm.

    Val Comelico today and the next days

    Up high, Val Comelico has roughly 24 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 27 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 18 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Lifts and terrain

    730 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts and 3 moving carpets. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Croda dei Toni - Zwölferkofel at 3,094 m, standing 1,153 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Comelico (hotel), Agriturismo Moiè (guesthouse), Sport Hotel (hotel) and Active Hotel La Torre (hotel).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Val Comelico?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Val Comelico at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 29 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Val Comelico?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,941 m; the lowest starts at 1,211 m (730 m of vertical).

    Is Val Comelico good for beginners?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Val Comelico?

    Drei Zinnen, 9.8 km to the north-west, with about 101 km of pistes.
