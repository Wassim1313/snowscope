    About Mehliskopf

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mehliskopf offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 133 of 257 in Central Europe). Its lifts climb from 825 m to 996 m. Its nearest neighbour is Hundseck, 0.8 km south-west, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Mehliskopf has 6 graded runs adding up to about 2.1 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 1.8 km, or 85%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    171 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 825 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 4 T-bars and 1 rope tow. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 910 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-December and early January. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 4 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 26 cm). Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 36 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 24 January, with about 9 cm.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Mehliskopf?

    Aim for mid-December and early January.

    Can you ski at Mehliskopf at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 6 winters out of ten, usually about 4 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Mehliskopf?

    The top is at 996 m and the base at 825 m.

    Can beginners ski at Mehliskopf?

    Yes: 85% of the graded pistes, about 1.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Mehliskopf have?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
