    About Bedřichov

    Bedřichov is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 732 m to 786 m. Bedřichov is the next resort, 0.6 km away to the north-west.

    How Bedřichov is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bedřichov gives 54 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 932 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.
