    About Folgaria

    Folgaria is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 46 km of pistes put it at number 128 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,182 m and reach 1,811 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Lavarone, 9.4 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of Folgaria

    Counting every graded piste, there are 71 runs and around 46 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 25 km (55%). With 55% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    The mountain at Folgaria

    The vertical drop is 629 m: runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 32 lifts: 1 gondola, 17 chairlifts, 3 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 9 moving carpets. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 9 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Becco di Filadonna at 2,150 m, standing 339 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Folgaria through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,496 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter); a snowy December gave 57 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 104 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 29 cm.

    Folgaria: common questions

    When does Folgaria have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    Can you ski at Folgaria at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    When does Folgaria open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Folgaria?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,811 m; the lowest starts at 1,182 m (629 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Folgaria?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 25 km, are easy.
