    The snow at Folgaria, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Folgaria (about 1,496 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 9 cm, with a normal range of 3 cm–31 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 26 cm (7 cm–63 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 29 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    9 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 15 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1984-85, peaking at 281 cm. Worst: 2011-12, which never passed 17 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late December to late March (92 days).

    Folgaria: quick answers

    Can you ski Folgaria at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 9 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 15 % of 46 winters. Right now: 22 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Folgaria?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 29 cm). For a sure base, late December to late March is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Folgaria get?

    About 29 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 17 cm in the leanest winter (2011-12) and 281 cm in the biggest (1984-85).

    How high do the lifts go at Folgaria?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,811 m; the lowest starts at 1,182 m (629 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Folgaria have?

    About 46 km of pistes and 32 lifts.
