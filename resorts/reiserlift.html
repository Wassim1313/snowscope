    How Reiserlift is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Reiserlift gives 136 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 721 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Wallberg reaches 1,616 m. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Auerkamp, 1,607 m high and 7.0 km south-east of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 789 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and mid-February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 31 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 8 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 9 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 24 cm). Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 13 cm, around 27 January.
