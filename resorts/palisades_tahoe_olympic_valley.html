    About Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley

    With some 70 km of runs, Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of US West. Skiing here happens between 1,890 m and 2,665 m above sea level. Palisades Tahoe Alpine Meadows is the next resort, 4.4 km away to the south.

    The pistes of Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley

    Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley has 120 graded runs adding up to about 70 km. Most of the length is black diamond (48%, about 33 km). With 48% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 33 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley gives 775 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. At 1,890 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 2,665 m. The lift system counts 27 lifts: 1 cable car, 20 chairlifts and 6 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 6 conveyor-belt lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Granite Chief (2,749 m), Lyon Peak (2,713 m) and Tinker Knob (2,713 m).

    How much snow Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 2,278 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 100 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 68 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 12 cm in a poor year to 138 cm in a big one. A normal season tops out near 168 cm, around 16 March. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 68 cm to 248 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 68 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley?

    The announced opening day is 25 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley?

    Between 1,890 m and 2,665 m, so 775 m of vertical drop.

    Is Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 33 km of its graded pistes (48%) are difficult.
