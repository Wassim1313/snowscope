    Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Palisades Tahoe Olympic Valley (about 7,474 ft (2,278 m)), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 27 in (68 cm); half of the winters sit between 13 in (33 cm) and 44 in (111 cm).

    On 15 February expect around 55 in (139 cm); the middle half of winters range from 39 in (100 cm) to 74 in (189 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 66 in (168 cm).

    52 % of the winters had at least 20 in (50 cm) by 15 December, and 72 % by 1 January.

    Best winter on record: 1982-83, peaking at 136 in (346 cm). Worst: 2014-15, which never passed 25 in (64 cm).

    A cover of 12 in (30 cm) or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May, about 167 days.
