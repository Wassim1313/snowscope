    About Seefeld

    Neither huge nor tiny, Seefeld offers about 9.0 km of pistes (number 531 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,181 m to 1,497 m. Its nearest neighbour is Seefeld / Birkenlift & Geigenbüh, 1.8 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 23 marked runs cover roughly 8.4 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 8.4 km, or 100%. With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Snow at Seefeld through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,339 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 December and keeps it until about 24 April, roughly 17 weeks. Most winters land between 34 cm and 70 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 34 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 55 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 28 cm, from 14 cm in a poor year to 45 cm in a big one. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    The mountain at Seefeld

    Skied top to bottom, Seefeld gives 316 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 1,497 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 13 lifts: 3 funiculars, 1 chairlift, 5 T-bars, 1 button lift, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Hohe Munde Ostgipfel at 2,592 m, standing 1,095 m above the top lift.

    Seefeld: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Seefeld?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Is there snow at Seefeld at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 28 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Seefeld?

    Between 1,181 m and 1,497 m, so 316 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Seefeld?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 8.4 km, are easy.

    How big is Seefeld?

    About 9.0 km of pistes and 13 lifts.
