    About Nowa Morawa

    Nowa Morawa has about 0.4 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 630 m and 715 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Kamienica/Bolesławów, 1.5 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Other resorts close by

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Nowa Morawa hold around 5 cm of snow today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 6 cm at mid-mountain).

    The mountain at Nowa Morawa

    85 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 630 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.

    How much snow Nowa Morawa usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 672 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 10 cm; a snowy December gave 35 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early January and late February. A normal season tops out near 24 cm, around 1 February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 45 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Nowa Morawa?

    Aim for early January and late February.

    How much snow does Nowa Morawa have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    What altitude is Nowa Morawa at?

    Its highest lift reaches 715 m; the lowest starts at 630 m (85 m of vertical).
