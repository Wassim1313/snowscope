    About Paavonrinteet

    Compact and local, Paavonrinteet has about 2.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 124 m up to 194 m. The closest other ski area is Äänemäki (25 km, east).

    How much snow Paavonrinteet usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 159 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 16 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 29 cm to 64 cm in mid-February in most winters. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 28 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 43 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 35 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 50 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    70 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Karjomäki (181 m) and Roninmäki (175 m).

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Paavonrinteet include Hotelli Kaiho (hotel) and Ruustinna (guesthouse).

    Paavonrinteet: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Paavonrinteet?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    Is there snow at Paavonrinteet at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 28 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Paavonrinteet at?

    The top is at 194 m and the base at 124 m.
