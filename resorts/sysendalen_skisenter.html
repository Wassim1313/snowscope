    About Sysendalen Skisenter

    Sysendalen Skisenter is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 779 m and 985 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Haugsåsen (Granvin Alpin), 35 km north-west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 31 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 882 m) in an ordinary winter, from late October to late May or later. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 24 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 117 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 163 cm, a dry one 75 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 209 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 216 cm, around 26 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 130 cm to 261 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April.

    How Sysendalen Skisenter is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 206 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hallingskarvet reaches 1,225 m. The only lift is a drag lift. Above the ski area rises Fagernuten (1,436 m), 7.0 km to the south, about 451 m higher than the top lift.

    This week's snow

    Questions about Sysendalen Skisenter

    When is the best time to ski at Sysendalen Skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    Is there snow at Sysendalen Skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 117 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Sysendalen Skisenter?

    The top is at 985 m and the base at 779 m.
