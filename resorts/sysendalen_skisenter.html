    About Sysendalen Skisenter

    Sysendalen Skisenter is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 779 m and 985 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Haugsåsen (Granvin Alpin), 35 km north-west, and 1 ski area lie within 40 km in all.

    How Sysendalen Skisenter is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 206 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hallingskarvet reaches 1,225 m.
