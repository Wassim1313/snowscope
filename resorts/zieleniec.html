    About Zieleniec

    Only 5 of the 257 ski areas of Central Europe are larger than Zieleniec, which has some 20 km of runs. The slopes start at 739 m and reach 1,015 m at the top. Zieleniec is the next resort, 0.1 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Zieleniec has 77 graded runs adding up to about 20 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 68%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Zieleniec

    From top to bottom that makes 276 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 739 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,015 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 chair-gondola, 6 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 16 button lifts and 5 moving carpets, 30 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
