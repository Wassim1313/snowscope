    About Zieleniec

    Only 5 of the 257 ski areas of Central Europe are larger than Zieleniec, which has some 20 km of runs. The slopes start at 739 m and reach 1,015 m at the top. Zieleniec is the next resort, 0.1 km away to the north-west.

    The mountain at Zieleniec

    From top to bottom that makes 276 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 739 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,015 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km.
