    About Zieleniec

    Only 5 of the 257 ski areas of Central Europe are larger than Zieleniec, which has some 20 km of runs. The slopes start at 739 m and reach 1,015 m at the top. Zieleniec is the next resort, 0.1 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Zieleniec has 77 graded runs adding up to about 20 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 68%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The mountain at Zieleniec

    From top to bottom that makes 276 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 739 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,015 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 1 chair-gondola, 6 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 16 button lifts and 5 moving carpets, 30 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 5 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    How much snow Zieleniec usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 877 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 8 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 0 cm in a poor year to 33 cm in a big one. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 49 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late January and early February. A normal season tops out near 20 cm, around 25 January.

    Questions about Zieleniec

    When is the best time to ski at Zieleniec?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Can you ski at Zieleniec at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 8 cm).

    What altitude is Zieleniec at?

    Between 739 m and 1,015 m, so 276 m of vertical drop.

    Is Zieleniec suitable for families?

    Yes: 68% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Zieleniec have?

    About 20 km of pistes and 30 lifts.
