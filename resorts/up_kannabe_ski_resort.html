    About UP Kannabe

    UP Kannabe is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 283 m and reach 399 m at the top. Manba is the next resort, 2.5 km away to the south-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    116 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 283 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Hachi Kita reaches 1,217 m.
