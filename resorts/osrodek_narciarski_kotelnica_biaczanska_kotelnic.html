    About Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia

    Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia ranks number 6 of 312 ski areas in the Carpathians by piste length, with about 17 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 705 m up to 942 m. The closest other ski area is Stacja Narciarska Kotelnica Biał (0.3 km, north-west).

    What there is to ski at Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia

    Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia has 38 graded runs adding up to about 18 km. The largest share is blue, with 13 km (76%). With 81% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia

    From top to bottom that makes 237 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 705 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. The lift system counts 22 lifts: 9 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 3 drag lifts and 4 moving carpets. For small children there are 4 conveyor-belt lifts.

    Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia: common questions

    How high is Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia?

    Its highest lift reaches 942 m; the lowest starts at 705 m (237 m of vertical).

    Is Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia suitable for families?

    Yes: 81% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ośrodek Narciarski Kotelnica Bia have?

    About 17 km of pistes and 22 lifts.
