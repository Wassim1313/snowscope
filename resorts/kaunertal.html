    About Kaunertal

    Kaunertal is a ski area. Fendels is the next resort, 5.0 km away to the west.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 2,107 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May or later. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 108 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 124 cm, around 24 March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 81 cm and 150 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 62 cm; a snowy December gave 89 cm, a dry one 36 cm. For about 12 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for late February and late March.

    Kaunertal: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Kaunertal?

    Aim for late February and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Kaunertal at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 62 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
