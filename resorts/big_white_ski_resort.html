    About Big White

    Big White ranks number 4 of 53 ski areas in Canada West by piste length, with about 96 km of runs. The slopes start at 1,512 m and reach 2,271 m at the top.

    The pistes of Big White

    In all, 112 marked runs cover roughly 96 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 40 km, or 42%. Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Experts can count on some 26 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Big White

    Skied top to bottom, Big White gives 759 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,512 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 10 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets, 16 lifts in all. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 3 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.

    Snow at Big White through a normal winter

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,892 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-November to late May. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 140 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 90 cm to 141 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 81 cm, with 108 cm in the best years. Depth normally peaks in mid-April at roughly 141 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Big White?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Big White at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 81 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Big White open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 26 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Big White?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,271 m; the lowest starts at 1,512 m (759 m of vertical).

    Is Big White good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 26 km of its graded pistes (28%) are difficult.
