    About Lyžařský areál Lužná

    Lyžařský areál Lužná is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 232 of 312 in the Carpathians by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 455 m up to 655 m. Skiareál Zděchov is the next resort, 5.0 km away to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Lyžařský areál Lužná

    Lyžařský areál Lužná has 4 graded runs adding up to about 1.2 km. Most of the length is red (53%, about 0.6 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Lifts and terrain

    200 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 455 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 2 lifts here are button lifts.
