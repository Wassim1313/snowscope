    About Brambrüesch

    Neither huge nor tiny, Brambrüesch offers about 10 km of pistes (number 482 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,184 m up to 2,171 m. The closest other ski area is Pradaschier (4.4 km, south).

    The pistes of Brambrüesch

    The piste map lists 7 graded runs, about 9.9 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 5.2 km, or 53%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.4 km.

    Brambrüesch and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.9 km south-east to Arosa Lenzerheide, with about 164 km of runs. Hochwang has the longer snow season: around 26 weeks of solid cover, compared with 23 at Brambrüesch. For higher slopes, Arosa Lenzerheide climbs to 2,854 m.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,678 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 December and lasts to 16 May: about 23 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 78 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. A normal season tops out near 90 cm, around 23 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 55 cm to 106 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 46 cm (lean years 25 cm, snowy years 65 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Brambrüesch gives 987 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 1 gondola, 1 chairlift and 1 T-bar, 4 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Felsberger Calanda (2,697 m), 7.9 km to the north-west, about 526 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Brambrüesch

    When does Brambrüesch have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Brambrüesch at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 46 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Brambrüesch?

    The top is at 2,171 m and the base at 1,184 m.

    What level of skier is Brambrüesch best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 53% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Brambrüesch?

    Arosa Lenzerheide, 8.9 km to the south-east, with about 164 km of pistes.
