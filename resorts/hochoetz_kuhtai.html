    About Hochoetz-Kühtai

    Hochoetz-Kühtai is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 54 km of pistes put it at number 110 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,408 m to 2,507 m. The closest other ski area is Hochoetz (2.1 km, west).

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,958 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 20 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 70 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 43 cm in a poor year to 103 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 20 March, with about 146 cm. For about 14 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 131 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Most winters land between 95 cm and 176 cm by mid-February.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 132 marked runs cover roughly 52 km. Most of the length is red (61%, about 32 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 6.2 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 1,099 m: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,408 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 2,507 m is the second highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 26 lifts break down into 3 gondolas, 12 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 4 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 3 moving carpets. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Sulzkogel at 3,016 m, standing 509 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When does Hochoetz-Kühtai have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Hochoetz-Kühtai have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 70 cm deep.

    When does Hochoetz-Kühtai open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026.

    What altitude is Hochoetz-Kühtai at?

    Between 1,408 m and 2,507 m, so 1,099 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Hochoetz-Kühtai best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 61% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
