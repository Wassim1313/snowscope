    About Bjørnestad Skisenter

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Bjørnestad Skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 388 m to 585 m. Its nearest neighbour is Gilja Alpin, 24 km north-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Bjørnestad Skisenter and the resorts around it

    In this group of 7, Bjørnestad Skisenter is number 3 for piste length. If snow is the priority, Sirdal does better, with about 25 weeks of a good base instead of 16. For higher slopes, Sirdal Skisenter climbs to 804 m.

    What there is to ski at Bjørnestad Skisenter

    In all, 2 marked runs cover roughly 1.3 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (55%, about 0.7 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Bjørnestad Skisenter is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 197 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sirdal Skisenter, for one, climbs to 804 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Brendamønet (852 m), 2.8 km to the north-west, about 267 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 486 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 January and lasts to 23 April: about 16 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 52 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 16 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 54 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 20 March, with about 89 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 19 cm and 112 cm.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Bjørnestad Skisenter?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to late April.

    How much snow does Bjørnestad Skisenter have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 16 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Bjørnestad Skisenter?

    Between 388 m and 585 m, so 197 m of vertical drop.

    Is Bjørnestad Skisenter suitable for families?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    How big is Bjørnestad Skisenter?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
