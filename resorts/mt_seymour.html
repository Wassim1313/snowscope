    About Mt Seymour

    Neither huge nor tiny, Mt Seymour offers about 17 km of pistes (number 28 of 53 in Canada West). Skiing here happens between 934 m and 1,255 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Grouse Mountain, 9.5 km west, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Mt Seymour has 66 graded runs adding up to about 17 km. Most of the length is blue square (50%, about 8.5 km). Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 3.9 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,094 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 252 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 25 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 27 March, with about 269 cm. Most winters land between 138 cm and 307 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 140 cm, from 76 cm in a poor year to 231 cm in a big one.

    The mountain at Mt Seymour

    321 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 934 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. To get up there are 3 chairlifts, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mount Bishop at 1,509 m, standing 254 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Mt Seymour

    When is the best time to ski at Mt Seymour?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Mt Seymour have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 140 cm deep.

    When does Mt Seymour open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 11 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Mt Seymour?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,255 m; the lowest starts at 934 m (321 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Mt Seymour best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 50% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
