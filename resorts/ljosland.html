    About Ljosland

    Ljosland is a ski area. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ljosland, 0.1 km to the west.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 856 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to late May, about 25 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 134 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 83 cm to 177 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 149 cm, around 10 March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 61 cm; a snowy December gave 98 cm, a dry one 26 cm. For about 15 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more.

    Ljosland: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ljosland?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    How much snow does Ljosland have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 61 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
