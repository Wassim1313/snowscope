    The snow at Masella, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Masella (about 2,062 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 22 cm, with a normal range of 10 cm–32 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 42 cm (22 cm–60 cm).

    The snowiest month is February, when the pack peaks at about 47 cm.

    By 15 December 7 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 9 %.

    Best winter on record: 1981-82, peaking at 124 cm. Worst: 1989-90, which never passed 16 cm.

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs late December to mid-April (113 days).

    Today's 0 cm compares with a usual 3 cm for 22 November.

    Masella: quick answers

    Can you ski Masella at Christmas?

    Not every year: about 22 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 9 % of 46 winters. Right now: 3 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Masella?

    February: the snow peaks at about 47 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late December to mid-April.

    How snowy is Masella?

    About 47 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early February); between 16 cm in the leanest winter (1989-90) and 124 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    How high do the lifts go at Masella?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,509 m; the lowest starts at 1,615 m (894 m of vertical).

    Is Masella suitable for families?

    Yes: 49% of the graded pistes, about 25 km, are easy.

    About Masella

    Masella ranks number 7 of 49 ski areas in the Pyrenees by piste length, with about 52 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,615 m up to 2,509 m. La Molina is the next resort, 3.2 km away to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Masella

    Masella has 98 graded runs adding up to about 52 km. The largest share is blue, with 24 km (46%). No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. The steepest pistes add up to about 4.1 km.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 2,062 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 January and lasts to 20 March: about 9 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 7 cm to 72 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 22 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 46 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 12 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. A normal season tops out near 47 cm, around 10 February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 894 m: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,615 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 18 lifts: 5 chairlifts, 2 button lifts, 5 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 4 moving carpets. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include la Tosa (2,536 m), Mirador de la Tosa (2,526 m) and Puig de Comabella (2,482 m).
