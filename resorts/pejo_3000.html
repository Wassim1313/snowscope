    About Pejo 3000

    Pejo 3000 is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 353 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,406 m up to 2,982 m. Its nearest neighbour is Santa Caterina, 13 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 21 marked runs cover roughly 17 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 69%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 2.1 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,576 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,406 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 1 cable car, 1 gondola and 4 chairlifts. Most of the lifts are chairs or cabins, so you sit on the way up and beginners rarely struggle. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. The highest named summit within 8 km is Palon de la Mare at 3,703 m, standing 721 m above the top lift.

    How Pejo 3000 compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta: about 57 km of pistes, 21 km to the south-east. Passo Stelvio has the longer snow season: around 30 weeks of solid cover, compared with 19 at Pejo 3000. The highest nearby is Passo Stelvio, topping out at 3,355 m.

    How much snow Pejo 3000 usually gets

    Count on about 19 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 2,194 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to early May. A normal season tops out near 53 cm, around 3 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 46 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 29 cm, from 12 cm in a poor year to 52 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 32 cm to 72 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Questions about Pejo 3000

    What is the best month to ski at Pejo 3000?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    How much snow does Pejo 3000 have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 29 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Pejo 3000?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,982 m; the lowest starts at 1,406 m (1,576 m of vertical).

    Who is Pejo 3000 best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 69% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Pejo 3000?

    Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta, 21 km to the south-east, with about 57 km of pistes.
