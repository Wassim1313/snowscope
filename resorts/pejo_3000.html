    About Pejo 3000

    Pejo 3000 is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 353 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,406 m up to 2,982 m. Its nearest neighbour is Santa Caterina, 13 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,576 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,406 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March.

    How Pejo 3000 compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta: about 57 km of pistes, 21 km to the south-east. The highest nearby is Passo Stelvio, topping out at 3,355 m.
