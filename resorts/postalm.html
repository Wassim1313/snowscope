    About Postalm

    Neither huge nor tiny, Postalm offers about 8.0 km of pistes (number 557 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,162 m up to 1,454 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zwölferhorn (St Gilgen), 12 km to the north.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 292 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Postalm include Alpenrose (hotel), Huber Hütte (mountain hut), Welser Hütte (mountain hut) and Historische Postalmhütte (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
