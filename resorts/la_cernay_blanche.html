    About La Cernay Blanche

    Compact and local, La Cernay Blanche has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 883 m to 977 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Site du Meix Musy, 18 km to the east.

    La Cernay Blanche and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is La Robella: about 11 km of pistes, 18 km to the south-east. Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses has the longer snow season: around 8 weeks of solid cover, compared with 0 at La Cernay Blanche. For higher slopes, Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses climbs to 1,558 m.

    The mountain at La Cernay Blanche

    From top to bottom that makes 94 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 883 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Sainte-Croix / Les Rasses, for one, climbs to 1,558 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Le Crêt Monniot, 1,141 m high and 2.7 km north-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 930 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 15 February, with about 17 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 50 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm, with 37 cm in the best years.

    Questions about La Cernay Blanche

    When does La Cernay Blanche have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does La Cernay Blanche have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 7 winters out of ten, typically 8 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is La Cernay Blanche at?

    Between 883 m and 977 m, so 94 m of vertical drop.

    Is La Cernay Blanche good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near La Cernay Blanche?

    La Robella, 18 km to the south-east, with about 11 km of pistes.
