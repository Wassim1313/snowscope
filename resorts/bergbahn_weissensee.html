    About Bergbahn Weissensee

    Bergbahn Weissensee is a mid-sized ski area with about 5.0 km of pistes, number 643 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 932 m up to 1,365 m. The closest other ski area is Gitschtal (5.4 km, south-west).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Bergbahn Weissensee has 9 graded runs adding up to about 5.2 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 3.9 km, or 75%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Bergbahn Weissensee is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 433 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 932 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 3 button lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises Golz (2,004 m), 6.2 km to the south-east, about 639 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,148 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 17 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 4 cm in a poor year to 31 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 27 cm. Most winters land between 16 cm and 47 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 14 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-April and late April.

    Quick answers

    When does Bergbahn Weissensee have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-April and late April.

    Is there snow at Bergbahn Weissensee at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 17 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Bergbahn Weissensee?

    The top is at 1,365 m and the base at 932 m.

    Is Bergbahn Weissensee suitable for families?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 3.9 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Bergbahn Weissensee?

    Nassfeld, 15 km to the south, with about 70 km of pistes.
