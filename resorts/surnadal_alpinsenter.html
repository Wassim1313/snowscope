    About Surnadal alpinsenter

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Surnadal alpinsenter one of the smaller places to ski in Scandinavia. The slopes start at 105 m and reach 404 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Surnadal, 0.7 km north-east, and 2 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 21 km south to Sunndal alpinsenter, with about 5.0 km of runs. Nerskogen skisenter has the longer snow season: around 30 weeks of solid cover, compared with 23 at Surnadal alpinsenter. The highest nearby is Nerskogen skisenter, topping out at 1,060 m.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Surnadal alpinsenter has roughly 24 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 20 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (30 cm).

    The mountain at Surnadal alpinsenter

    299 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its top at 404 m is the third highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. The highest named summit within 8 km is Honnstadknyken at 1,071 m, standing 667 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Surnadal alpinsenter through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 254 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 56 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 88 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 101 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 66 cm and 140 cm. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. A normal season tops out near 118 cm, around 23 March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March.

    Surnadal alpinsenter: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Surnadal alpinsenter?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Surnadal alpinsenter have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 56 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Surnadal alpinsenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 404 m; the lowest starts at 105 m (299 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Surnadal alpinsenter?

    Sunndal alpinsenter, 21 km to the south, with about 5.0 km of pistes.
