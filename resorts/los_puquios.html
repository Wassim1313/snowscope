    About Los Puquios

    About 5.0 km of pistes make Los Puquios one of the smaller places to ski in Andes. Skiing here happens between 2,679 m and 2,809 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Los Penitentes, 5.3 km east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Los Puquios

    Los Puquios has 14 graded runs adding up to about 5.3 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 1.6 km (30%). Mixed groups do well here, with gentle slopes for learners and steeper pistes for the experts. Advanced skiers have about 1.3 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 130 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 2,679 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Its top at 2,809 m is the third highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. All 6 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.
