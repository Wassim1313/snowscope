    About Ontake

    With some 18 km of runs, Ontake belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Its lifts climb from 1,684 m to 2,212 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mia, 6.9 km to the north.

    The pistes of Ontake

    Counting every graded piste, there are 15 runs and around 17 km to ski. Most of the length is green (61%, about 11 km). With 61% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How Ontake is laid out

    The vertical drop is 528 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,684 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 21 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Ontake. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 26 cm of new snow is expected by Thursday.

    Questions about Ontake

    How high is Ontake?

    The top is at 2,212 m and the base at 1,684 m.

    Is Ontake suitable for families?

    Yes: 61% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Ontake have?

    About 18 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
