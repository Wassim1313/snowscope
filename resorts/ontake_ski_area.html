    About Ontake

    With some 18 km of runs, Ontake belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Its lifts climb from 1,684 m to 2,212 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Mia, 6.9 km to the north.

    How Ontake is laid out

    The vertical drop is 528 m: runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,684 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 19 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Ontake. 17 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 29 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.
