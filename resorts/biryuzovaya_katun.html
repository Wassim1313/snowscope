    About Бирюзовая Катунь (Biryuzovaya Ka

    Бирюзовая Катунь is a ski area. The slopes start at 465 m and reach 732 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Манжерок (Manzherok), 6.4 km to the east.

    How Бирюзовая Катунь is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 267 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 465 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
