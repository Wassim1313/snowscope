    About Ramudla

    Ramudla has about 0.4 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,787 m up to 1,868 m. Schnalstal is the next resort, 9.1 km away to the north-east.

    Ramudla and the resorts around it

    If snow is the priority, Kaunertaler Gletscher does better, with about 35 weeks of a good base instead of 23. The highest nearby is Schnalstal, topping out at 3,200 m.

    This week's snow

    Up high, Ramudla has roughly 18 cm of snow on the ground today. 17 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 30 cm at mid-mountain.

    The mountain at Ramudla

    Skied top to bottom, Ramudla gives 81 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,787 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Schnalstal, for one, climbs to 3,200 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Schwemser Spitze - Punta di Oberettes at 3,456 m, standing 1,588 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,828 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 23 weeks of good cover. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 65 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 48 cm, with 76 cm in the best years. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 55 cm to 110 cm in mid-February in most winters. A normal season tops out near 87 cm, around 6 March.

    Where to stay

    Ramudla: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Ramudla?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Ramudla at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 48 cm deep.

    How high is Ramudla?

    Between 1,787 m and 1,868 m, so 81 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Ramudla?

    About 0.4 km of pistes and 1 lift.
