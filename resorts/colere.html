    About Colere

    With roughly 12 km of runs, Colere sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,058 m and 2,174 m above sea level. Nuova Lizzola is the next resort, 4.9 km away to the north-west.

    Runs by difficulty

    Colere has 17 graded runs adding up to about 12 km. Most of the length is red (63%, about 7.7 km). Intermediate runs make up 63% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 4.6 km of difficult runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 1,116 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 3 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet, 5 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Pizzo Tornone, 2,577 m high and 7.7 km north-east of the resort.

    How much snow Colere usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 1,616 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 6 January and lasts to 17 April: about 15 weeks. A normal season tops out near 59 cm, around 4 March. Most winters land between 22 cm and 87 cm by mid-February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 24 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 4 cm, snowy years 52 cm). Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. By mid-April the typical depth is 31 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Beds near the slopes

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Chalet dell'Aquila (mountain hut), Rifugio Luigi Albani (mountain hut), Albergo Brescia (hotel) and Ostello Quattro Matte (hostel). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Colere?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Colere at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 24 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Colere?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,174 m; the lowest starts at 1,058 m (1,116 m of vertical).

    Is Colere good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 4.6 km of its graded pistes (37%) are difficult.

    How big is Colere?

    About 12 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
