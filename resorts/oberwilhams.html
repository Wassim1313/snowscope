    About Liftanlagen Oberwilhams

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Liftanlagen Oberwilhams sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 879 m to 1,025 m. Its nearest neighbour is Skilift Weitnau, 2.6 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Liftanlagen Oberwilhams has 8 graded runs adding up to about 2.5 km. Most of the length is blue (62%, about 1.6 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Liftanlagen Oberwilhams is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Liftanlagen Oberwilhams gives 146 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,079 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Getting up the mountain: 1 drag lift and 2 rope tows, 3 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gschwender Horn at 1,450 m, standing 425 m above the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 952 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 49 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 16 cm (lean years 2 cm, snowy years 36 cm). Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 26 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 10 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April.
