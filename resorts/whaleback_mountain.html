    About Whaleback Mountain

    With roughly 8.0 km of runs, Whaleback Mountain sits in the middle of the size table of US Northeast. The ski area spans the mountain from 348 m up to 545 m. The closest other ski area is Storrs Hill (6.8 km, north-west).

    The pistes of Whaleback Mountain

    The mountain at Whaleback Mountain

    The vertical drop is 197 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 348 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its top at 545 m is the second highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 5 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Prospect Hill (641 m), Snow Mountain (570 m) and Methodist Hill (552 m).

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 446 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 10 February and lasts to 15 March: about 5 weeks. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 10 cm to 65 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 36 cm, around 25 February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 15 cm, from 4 cm in a poor year to 35 cm in a big one.

    Quick answers

    When does Whaleback Mountain have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    How much snow does Whaleback Mountain have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 15 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Whaleback Mountain at?

    The top is at 545 m and the base at 348 m.

    Is Whaleback Mountain suitable for families?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 3.7 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Whaleback Mountain have?

    About 8.0 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
