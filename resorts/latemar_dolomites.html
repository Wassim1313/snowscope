    What 46 winters say about Latemar Dolomites

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Latemar Dolomites (about 1,981 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 32 cm, with a normal range of 14 cm–50 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 48 cm (36 cm–67 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 54 cm.

    A 50 cm base was there by 15 December in 15 % of winters, and by 1 January in 22 %.

    2000-01 was the big one (165 cm); 2016-17 the lean one (30 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to early May, about 141 days.

    Today's 26 cm compares with a usual 10 cm for 27 November.

    Latemar Dolomites: quick answers

    Can you ski Latemar Dolomites at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 32 cm, and only 22 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 24 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Latemar Dolomites?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 54 cm). For a sure base, early December to early May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Latemar Dolomites?

    About 54 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 30 cm in the leanest winter (2016-17) and 165 cm in the biggest (2000-01).

    When does the ski season start at Latemar Dolomites?

    The announced opening day is 27 November 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Latemar Dolomites?

    Between 1,539 m and 2,423 m, so 884 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Latemar Dolomites best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 66% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    About Latemar Dolomites

    Around 40 km of marked runs place Latemar Dolomites among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 149 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,539 m to 2,423 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Passo Lavazè, 4.8 km to the west.

    Snow at Latemar Dolomites through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,981 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 20 weeks of good cover. Most winters land between 28 cm and 79 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 54 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 42 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 32 cm; a snowy December gave 66 cm, a dry one 5 cm.

    The mountain at Latemar Dolomites

    From top to bottom that makes 884 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,539 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 3 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 12 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets, 20 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Cimon del Latemar - Diamantiditurm, 2,842 m high and 3.8 km north-east of the resort.
