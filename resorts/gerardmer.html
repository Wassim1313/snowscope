    About Gérardmer

    Neither huge nor tiny, Gérardmer offers about 23 km of pistes (number 266 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 776 m up to 1,138 m. The closest other ski area is Xonrupt-Longemer (2.4 km, north).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 40 runs and around 22 km to ski. The largest share is green (beginner), with 9.8 km (44%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Snow at Gérardmer through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 957 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 20 February, with about 8 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 3 cm (snow lay here in 6 winters out of ten), with 29 cm in the best years. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 0 cm to 47 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and early February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Gérardmer gives 362 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Below about 976 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The lift system counts 18 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 15 drag lifts and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. The highest named summit within 8 km is Haut de Falimont at 1,306 m, standing 168 m above the top lift.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Gérardmer?

    Aim for early January and early February.

    Is there snow at Gérardmer at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 3 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Gérardmer?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,138 m; the lowest starts at 776 m (362 m of vertical).

    Is Gérardmer suitable for families?

    Yes: 59% of the graded pistes, about 13 km, are easy.

    How big is Gérardmer?

    About 23 km of pistes and 18 lifts.
