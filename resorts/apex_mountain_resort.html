    About Apex

    Neither huge nor tiny, Apex offers about 48 km of pistes (number 15 of 53 in Canada West). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,606 m up to 2,173 m.

    What there is to ski at Apex

    In all, 72 marked runs cover roughly 47 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 17 km, or 37%. With 34% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 16 km.

    How Apex is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Apex gives 567 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,606 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet, 4 lifts in all. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Beaconsfield Mountain (2,247 m), Apex Mountain (2,247 m) and Green Mountain (2,106 m).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,890 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 24 November and lasts to 21 May: about 26 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 103 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 67 cm, with 98 cm in the best years. For about 5 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 74 cm to 121 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 106 cm.

    Questions about Apex

    When does Apex have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Apex at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 67 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does the ski season start at Apex?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Apex?

    Between 1,606 m and 2,173 m, so 567 m of vertical drop.

    Is Apex good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 16 km of its graded pistes (34%) are difficult.
