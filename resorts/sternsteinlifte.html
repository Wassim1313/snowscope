    About Sternsteinlifte

    Sternsteinlifte is one of the bigger ski areas of Central Europe: about 5.0 km of pistes put it at number 63 of 257. Skiing here happens between 813 m and 1,100 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Ski areál Lipno-Kramolín, 11 km north, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Sternsteinlifte has 5 graded runs adding up to about 4.6 km. Most of the length is red (61%, about 2.8 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 287 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 813 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,100 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 chair-gondola, 1 drag lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 956 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter) (lean years 1 cm, snowy years 28 cm). For the best snow, aim for early January and mid-January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 1 cm to 51 cm. Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 18 cm.

    Questions about Sternsteinlifte

    When is the best time to ski at Sternsteinlifte?

    Aim for early January and mid-January.

    How much snow does Sternsteinlifte have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 9 cm).

    How high is Sternsteinlifte?

    The top is at 1,100 m and the base at 813 m.

    Who is Sternsteinlifte best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 61% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Sternsteinlifte?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
