    About Arolla

    With roughly 16 km of runs, Arolla sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,982 m and 2,880 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Forclaz, 8.6 km to the north-east.

    What there is to ski at Arolla

    Arolla has 20 graded runs adding up to about 16 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 77%. Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Around 26% of the pistes, some 4.1 km, can be covered with machine-made snow, mostly the busiest runs and the way back to the bottom.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Arolla has roughly 54 cm of snow on the ground today. 15 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 18 cm by Thursday. At mid-mountain that is more snow than in nine winters out of ten for the date (usually about 29 cm).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 2,431 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May or later. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 4 March, with about 104 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 53 cm; a snowy December gave 78 cm, a dry one 29 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 100 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For about 4 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 62 cm to 117 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    898 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. A high base at 1,982 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. The lift system counts 6 lifts: 5 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Above the ski area rises La Ruinette (3,875 m), 7.4 km to the south-west, about 995 m higher than the top lift.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Arolla?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Arolla at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 53 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Arolla at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,880 m; the lowest starts at 1,982 m (898 m of vertical).

    Is Arolla good for beginners?

    Yes: 77% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    Does Arolla have snowmaking?

    Yes, on about 4.1 km of its pistes (26%).
