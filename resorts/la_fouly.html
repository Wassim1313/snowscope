    About La Fouly

    With roughly 7.0 km of runs, La Fouly sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,607 m to 2,159 m. The closest other ski area is Vichères-Bavon (7.0 km, north-east).

    La Fouly today and the next days

    Up high, La Fouly has roughly 52 cm of snow on the ground today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 22 cm now.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 30 runs and around 7.4 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 4.1 km, or 55%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 552 m: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,607 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its 4 lifts break down into 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Aiguille d'Argentière, 3,901 m high and 7.3 km north-west of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,883 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 12 December and lasts to 19 May: about 23 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 86 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 51 cm and 108 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 64 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 44 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 22 cm, snowy years 69 cm).

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Auberge des Glaciers (hotel), Gîte de la Fouly (hotel), Edelweiss (hotel) and Le Dolent (chalet).

    Questions about La Fouly

    When is the best time to ski at La Fouly?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How much snow does La Fouly have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 44 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is La Fouly at?

    The top is at 2,159 m and the base at 1,607 m.

    Is La Fouly suitable for families?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 4.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near La Fouly?

    Les Grands Montets, 13 km to the west, with about 26 km of pistes.
