    About Müsella

    Müsella is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 1,698 m and 1,781 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Zuoz, 4.1 km to the north.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Müsella has roughly 12 cm of snow on the ground today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 14 cm by Monday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 83 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,698 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Corvatsch-Furtschellas, for one, climbs to 3,296 m.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Müsella include B & B Chesa d'Arch (hotel), Krone Säumerei am Inn (hotel), La Mora (hotel) and Ferienhaus Rudigier (chalet).
