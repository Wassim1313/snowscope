    About skijalište Vučići

    skijalište Vučići has about 0.3 km of pistes. The slopes start at 332 m and reach 402 m at the top.

    How much snow skijalište Vučići usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 367 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 39 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. At Christmas there was snow here in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 20 cm). Depth normally peaks in late January at roughly 6 cm.

    How skijalište Vučići is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 70 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 332 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 2 lifts here are rope tows. The skyline is dominated by Klek, 1,181 m high and 5.6 km west of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Frankopan (hotel), Lovačka kuća St. Hubert (guesthouse), Klek (hotel) and Planinarski dom Klek (mountain hut).

    Questions about skijalište Vučići

    What is the best month to ski at skijalište Vučići?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Is there snow at skijalište Vučići at Christmas?

    How high is skijalište Vučići?
