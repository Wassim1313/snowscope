    About Ski Lysá

    Around 10 km of marked runs place Ski Lysá among the larger resorts of the Carpathians, number 19 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 628 m up to 1,068 m. Its nearest neighbour is Fričkovce, 8.0 km east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 37 runs and around 10 km to ski. Most of the length is red (57%, about 6.0 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 2.8 km of black or expert runs.

    The mountain at Ski Lysá

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Lysá gives 440 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Because the lifts start as low as 628 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,068 m. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chairlift, 3 button lifts and 1 drag lift. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Questions about Ski Lysá

    How high do the lifts go at Ski Lysá?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,068 m; the lowest starts at 628 m (440 m of vertical).

    Is Ski Lysá good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 2.8 km of its graded pistes (27%) are difficult.

    How big is Ski Lysá?

    About 10 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
