    What 46 winters say about Kiririnteet

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Kiririnteet (about 332 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 42 cm, with a normal range of 36 cm–48 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 65 cm; the middle half of winters range from 58 cm to 71 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 68 cm.

    By 15 December 7 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 43 %.

    1997-98 was the big one (107 cm); 1987-88 the lean one (54 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early December to mid-May (162 days).

    This winter so far: 22 cm on 26 November, against a usual 23 cm for the date.

    Questions about Kiririnteet

    Does Kiririnteet have snow over Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 42 cm, and only 43 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 23 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Kiririnteet?

    March: the snow peaks at about 68 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from early December to mid-May.

    How deep does the snow get at Kiririnteet?

    About 68 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-March); between 54 cm in the leanest winter (1987-88) and 107 cm in the biggest (1997-98).

    When does Kiririnteet have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    What altitude is Kiririnteet at?

    The top is at 385 m and the base at 279 m.

    Can beginners ski at Kiririnteet?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.4 km, are easy.

    About Kiririnteet

    Compact and local, Kiririnteet has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The slopes start at 279 m and reach 385 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Rukatunturi (40 km, east).

    Kiririnteet and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 40 km east to Rukatunturi, with about 25 km of runs. No resort in the list keeps good snow longer: about 23 weeks of solid cover in an ordinary winter.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 332 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 6 December and keeps it until about 11 May, roughly 23 weeks. Most winters land between 51 cm and 75 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 63 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 42 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 27 cm. A normal season tops out near 68 cm, around 13 March.

    The pistes of Kiririnteet

    The piste map lists 2 graded runs, about 1.4 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 1.4 km (100%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 106 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 2 lifts break down into 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Iso Karitunturi (417 m) and Vesakko-Karitunturi (380 m).
