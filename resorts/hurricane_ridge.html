    About Hurricane Ridge

    About 2.0 km of pistes make Hurricane Ridge one of the smaller places to ski in US West. Skiing here happens between 1,485 m and 1,660 m above sea level.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 175 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,485 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 1 button lift and 2 rope tows. The highest named summit within 8 km is Mount Angeles at 1,968 m, standing 308 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 26 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,572 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 135 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 20 cm, snowy years 238 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 400 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 22 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late April at roughly 468 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 41 cm to 354 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and early April.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 7 marked runs cover roughly 1.7 km. Most of the length is blue square (69%, about 1.2 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    Questions about Hurricane Ridge

    When is the best time to ski at Hurricane Ridge?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Hurricane Ridge at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 135 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Hurricane Ridge at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,660 m; the lowest starts at 1,485 m (175 m of vertical).

    Does Hurricane Ridge have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.5 km of its graded pistes (31%) are difficult.

    How big is Hurricane Ridge?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
