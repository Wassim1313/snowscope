    About Corviglia

    Corviglia is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 68 km of pistes put it at number 84 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,733 m up to 3,021 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pontresina, 7.1 km to the east.

    The mountain at Corviglia

    Skied top to bottom, Corviglia gives 1,288 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,733 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. To get up there are 2 cable cars, 24 funiculars, 1 gondola, 12 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 2 rope tows and 4 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. 4 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Piz Güglia - Piz Julier (3,380 m), 4.8 km to the west, about 359 m higher than the top lift.

    What there is to ski at Corviglia

    The piste map lists 108 graded runs, about 66 km in total. The largest share is red, with 31 km (47%). Intermediate runs make up 47% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Experts can count on some 10 km of difficult runs.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,377 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 20 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 28 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. By mid-April the typical depth is 113 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 69 cm and 138 cm by mid-February. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 10 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 63 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 98 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 13 April, with about 114 cm.

    Questions about Corviglia

    When does Corviglia have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Corviglia at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 63 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Corviglia?

    The announced opening day is 28 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Corviglia?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,021 m; the lowest starts at 1,733 m (1,288 m of vertical).

    What level of skier is Corviglia best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 47% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.
