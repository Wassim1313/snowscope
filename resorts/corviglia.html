    Corviglia in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Corviglia (about 2,377 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 63 cm, with a normal range of 45 cm–85 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 100 cm (76 cm–118 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in April: about 114 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 52 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 70 %.

    The deepest winter was 2000-01 (207 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2004-05 (79 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to early June, about 200 days.

    Corviglia: quick answers

    Does Corviglia have snow over Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 63 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 70 % of 46 winters. Right now: 41 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Corviglia?

    April: the snow peaks at about 114 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to early June.

    How snowy is Corviglia?

    About 114 cm at the peak of a normal winter (mid-April); between 79 cm in the leanest winter (2004-05) and 207 cm in the biggest (2000-01).

    How high do the lifts go at Corviglia?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,021 m; the lowest starts at 1,733 m (1,288 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Corviglia have?

    About 68 km of pistes and 49 lifts.

    About Corviglia

    Corviglia is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 68 km of pistes put it at number 84 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,733 m up to 3,021 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Pontresina, 7.1 km to the east.

    The mountain at Corviglia

    Skied top to bottom, Corviglia gives 1,288 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,733 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher.
