    How Sankt Englmar (Grün-Pröller) is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 172 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,048 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 6 lifts break down into 5 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.

    Snow at Sankt Englmar (Grün-Pröller) through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 934 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 82 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 54 cm in a big one. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 12 February. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February.
