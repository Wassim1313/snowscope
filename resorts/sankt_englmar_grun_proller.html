    How Sankt Englmar (Grün-Pröller) is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 172 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,048 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its 6 lifts break down into 5 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas.
