    About Cimoncino

    Neither huge nor tiny, Cimoncino offers about 5.0 km of pistes (number 647 of 1,056 in the Alps). The slopes start at 1,403 m and reach 1,441 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Cimone, 1.7 km to the north-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 8 graded runs, about 4.7 km in total. Most of the length is red (84%, about 3.9 km). Intermediate runs make up 84% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Snow at Cimoncino through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,422 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 56 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 1 cm (snow lay here in 5 winters out of ten), from 0 cm in a poor year to 18 cm in a big one. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. The snow is usually deepest around 22 January, with about 4 cm.

    Skiers after mileage can drive 9.1 km south-west to Area Sciistica Abetone, with about 39 km of runs. Of these resorts it loses its snow first in spring; go when it is cold. The highest nearby is Corno alle Scale, topping out at 1,940 m.

    The mountain at Cimoncino

    From top to bottom that makes 38 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,403 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Area Sciisitica Febbio reaches 2,060 m. All 3 lifts here are moving carpets. 3 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Monte Cimone at 2,165 m, standing 724 m above the top lift.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Hotel Buona Stella (hotel), Rifugio Zambelli (mountain hut), Albergo Edelweiss (hotel) and Albergo Passo del Lupo (hotel).

    Questions about Cimoncino

    When does Cimoncino have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and early March.

    How much snow does Cimoncino have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 5 winters out of ten, typically 1 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Cimoncino?

    The top is at 1,441 m and the base at 1,403 m.

    Who is Cimoncino best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 84% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Which bigger ski area is near Cimoncino?

    Area Sciistica Abetone, 9.1 km to the south-west, with about 39 km of pistes.
