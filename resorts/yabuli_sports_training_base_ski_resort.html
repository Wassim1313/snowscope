    About Yabuli Sports Training Base

    Compact and local, Yabuli Sports Training Base has about 1.0 km of runs, small for China. The ski area spans the mountain from 479 m up to 600 m. Yabuli Sun Mountain is the next resort, 1.5 km away to the north-west.

    Yabuli Sports Training Base and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Yabuli Sun Mountain (1.5 km north-west) has about 16 km of pistes against 1.0 km here. The highest nearby is Yabuli Sports Training Base, topping out at 1,367 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 1 runs and around 1.2 km to ski. Most of the length is green circle (100%, about 1.2 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    The mountain at Yabuli Sports Training Base

    The vertical drop is 121 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 479 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. All 2 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Quick answers

    How high is Yabuli Sports Training Base?

    Its highest lift reaches 600 m; the lowest starts at 479 m (121 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Yabuli Sports Training Base?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Yabuli Sports Training Base?

    Yabuli Sun Mountain, 1.5 km to the north-west, with about 16 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Yabuli Sports Training Base have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
