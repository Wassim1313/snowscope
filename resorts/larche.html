    About Larche

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Larche one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,685 m to 1,854 m. Sainte-Anne la Condamine is the next resort, 11 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 2 runs and around 1.2 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 1.2 km (100%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Larche has roughly 3 cm of snow on the ground today. 7 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 21 cm by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (15 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    169 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,685 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul, reach 2,722 m. All 2 lifts here are button lifts. The skyline is dominated by Parias Coupà / Parrias Coupa, 3,248 m high and 7.7 km north of the resort.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,770 m) in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to mid-April. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 84 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 41 cm (lean years 14 cm, snowy years 84 cm). In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 25 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 34 cm and 121 cm.

    Larche: common questions

    When does Larche have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-April.

    Can you ski at Larche at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 41 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Larche at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,854 m; the lowest starts at 1,685 m (169 m of vertical).

    Is Larche good for beginners?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.2 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Larche?

    Forêt Blanche : Vars/Risoul, 22 km to the north-west, with about 131 km of pistes.
