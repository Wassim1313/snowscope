    About Sollifjellet alpinsenter

    Sollifjellet alpinsenter is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 218 m and 531 m above sea level.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Sollifjellet alpinsenter hold around 19 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 17 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 38 cm at mid-mountain).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 313 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a drag lift. The highest named summit within 8 km is Storhornet at 722 m, standing 191 m above the top lift.

    Snow at Sollifjellet alpinsenter through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 374 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 25 November and keeps it until about 16 May, roughly 25 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 60 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 32 cm in a poor year to 101 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 146 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 148 cm, around 27 March. For about 16 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 81 cm to 160 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April.

    Questions about Sollifjellet alpinsenter

    What is the best month to ski at Sollifjellet alpinsenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Sollifjellet alpinsenter have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 60 cm deep.

    How high is Sollifjellet alpinsenter?

    Between 218 m and 531 m, so 313 m of vertical drop.
