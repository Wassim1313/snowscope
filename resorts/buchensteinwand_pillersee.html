    About Buchensteinwand (Pillersee)

    Buchensteinwand (Pillersee) is a mid-sized ski area with about 19 km of pistes, number 319 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Skiing here happens between 854 m and 1,452 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Skicircus Saalbach-Hinterglemm L, 9.2 km to the south-east.

    The pistes of Buchensteinwand (Pillersee)

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Buchensteinwand (Pillersee) gives 598 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 854 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Großes Ochsenhorn, 2,513 m high and 7.9 km north-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 1,153 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late January to early April, about 10 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 21 cm; a snowy December gave 40 cm, a dry one 5 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 28 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 22 cm and 58 cm. A normal season tops out near 39 cm, around 20 February.
