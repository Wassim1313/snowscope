    About Nebelhorn

    Neither huge nor tiny, Nebelhorn offers about 12 km of pistes (number 450 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 823 m up to 2,213 m. The closest other ski area is Stinesser Lifte (6.8 km, north-west).

    The mountain at Nebelhorn

    From top to bottom that makes 1,390 m of vertical, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 823 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 2 gondolas, 2 chairlifts, 1 T-bar and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Großer Wilder, 2,379 m high and 6.5 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,518 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 15 December and keeps it until about 12 May, roughly 21 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 40 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 17 cm, snowy years 62 cm). For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 49 cm to 103 cm in mid-February in most winters. The snow is usually deepest around 2 March, with about 83 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 58 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    What there is to ski at Nebelhorn

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 12 km to ski. Most of the length is red (41%, about 4.8 km). Good skiers will like it here; 37% of the runs are steep. Advanced skiers have about 4.3 km of black or expert runs.

    Questions about Nebelhorn

    When does Nebelhorn have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Nebelhorn at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 40 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Nebelhorn at?

    Between 823 m and 2,213 m, so 1,390 m of vertical drop.

    Is Nebelhorn good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 4.3 km of its graded pistes (37%) are difficult.

    How big is Nebelhorn?

    About 12 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
