    About Ski Kamenec

    Ski Kamenec is a small ski area by the standards of Central Europe, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 502 m and reach 600 m at the top. Radvanice is the next resort, 8.6 km away to the west.

    Snow at Ski Kamenec through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 551 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the best snow, aim for late January and early February. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 4 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten) (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 25 cm). A normal season tops out near 11 cm, around 24 January. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 0 cm to 30 cm.

    The mountain at Ski Kamenec

    The vertical drop is 98 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 502 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. The only lift is a button lift.

    Ski Kamenec: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Ski Kamenec?

    Aim for late January and early February.

    Is there snow at Ski Kamenec at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 4 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Ski Kamenec?

    Its highest lift reaches 600 m; the lowest starts at 502 m (98 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Ski Kamenec have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
