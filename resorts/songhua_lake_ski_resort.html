    About Songhua Lake

    Only 2 of the 92 ski areas of China are larger than Songhua Lake, which has some 37 km of runs. The slopes start at 320 m and reach 910 m at the top. Wujiashan is the next resort, 11 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of Songhua Lake

    The piste map lists 46 graded runs, about 35 km in total. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 18 km, or 50%. Intermediate runs make up 50% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 7.1 km of black or expert runs.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 590 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. At 320 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 gondolas, 5 chairlifts and 8 moving carpets, 15 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 8 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
