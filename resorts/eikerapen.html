    About Eikerapen

    Eikerapen is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 377 m up to 646 m. The closest other ski area is Knaben (23 km, north-west).

    The snow right now

    Up high, Eikerapen has roughly 51 cm of snow on the ground today. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 22 cm by Saturday.

    Skied top to bottom, Eikerapen gives 269 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 5 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Bortelid Skisenter reaches 805 m.
