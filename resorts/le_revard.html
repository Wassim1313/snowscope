    About Le Revard

    With roughly 9.0 km of runs, Le Revard sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,275 m to 1,548 m. Its nearest neighbour is Col de Plainpalais, 4.2 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Le Revard

    Le Revard has 16 graded runs adding up to about 8.6 km. The largest share is blue, with 4.2 km (49%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How Le Revard is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 273 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 7 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 5 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 button lifts and 2 moving carpets. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The skyline is dominated by Mont Margeriaz, 1,845 m high and 4.9 km south of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,412 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early April, about 17 weeks of good cover. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 57 cm, with 137 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 102 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 26 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. Most winters land between 20 cm and 158 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Le Revard

    When does Le Revard have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early April.

    Is there snow at Le Revard at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 57 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Le Revard?

    Between 1,275 m and 1,548 m, so 273 m of vertical drop.

    Is Le Revard good for beginners?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 4.9 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Le Revard?

    Aillon-Margériaz, 5.5 km to the south-east, with about 22 km of pistes.
