    About Raggal

    Raggal is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 627 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 962 m and reach 1,236 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Sonntag Stein (7.6 km, east).

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 15 runs and around 5.8 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 2.5 km, or 43%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 1.3 km of difficult runs.

    Other resorts close by

    Snow this week

    Up high, Raggal has roughly 7 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 6 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 17 cm by Friday. That is close to a normal winter for the date (about 8 cm at mid-mountain).

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 274 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 962 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Damüls, reach 2,021 m. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Gamsfreiheit (2,213 m), 6.2 km to the south-east, about 977 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Raggal through a normal winter

    Count on about 11 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,099 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early January to late March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 22 cm and 61 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 24 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is usually deepest around 1 March, with about 41 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 24 cm, from 8 cm in a poor year to 41 cm in a big one.

    Raggal: common questions

    When does Raggal have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from early January to late March.

    How much snow does Raggal have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 24 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Raggal?

    The top is at 1,236 m and the base at 962 m.

    Can beginners ski at Raggal?

    Yes: 43% of the graded pistes, about 2.5 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Raggal?

    Damüls-Mellau-Faschina, 11 km to the north, with about 101 km of pistes.
