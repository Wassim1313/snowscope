    About Raggal

    Raggal is a mid-sized ski area with about 6.0 km of pistes, number 627 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 962 m and reach 1,236 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Sonntag Stein (7.6 km, east).

    Other resorts close by

    The big name nearby is Damüls-Mellau-Faschina: about 101 km of pistes, 11 km to the north. Damüls reaches 2,021 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Raggal has roughly 11 cm of snow on the ground today. 9 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 17 cm by Tuesday.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 274 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 962 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Damüls, reach 2,021 m.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Rössle Raggal (hotel), Fairrooms (self-check-in) (hotel), Propstei Sankt Gerold (hotel) and Haus Burgl - Enzian (chalet).
