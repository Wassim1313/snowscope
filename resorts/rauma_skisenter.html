    About Rauma skisenter

    With roughly 4.0 km of runs, Rauma skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 319 m and 619 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Tusten Skiheiser, 28 km north-west, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Rauma skisenter

    Rauma skisenter has 5 graded runs adding up to about 3.6 km. The largest share is red, with 1.5 km (41%). The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 469 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 28 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for late March and early April. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 99 cm (lean years 63 cm, snowy years 148 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 178 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 13 April, with about 179 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 105 cm and 212 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks.

    The mountain at Rauma skisenter

    300 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 7 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Bjorli Skisenter reaches 1,213 m. The only lift is a drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Varden, 1,238 m high and 7.0 km south of the resort.

    The snow right now

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Rauma skisenter?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Can you ski at Rauma skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 99 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Rauma skisenter at?

    Between 319 m and 619 m, so 300 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Rauma skisenter best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 41% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Rauma skisenter?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
