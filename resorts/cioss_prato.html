    About Cioss Prato

    Compact and local, Cioss Prato has about 2.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 1,542 m and reach 1,598 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Airolo Pesciüm, 10 km to the east.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 20 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,570 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from mid-December to early May. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 36 cm, from 17 cm in a poor year to 66 cm in a big one. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 105 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 78 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 42 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    What there is to ski at Cioss Prato

    The piste map lists 6 graded runs, about 1.6 km in total. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 1.6 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Up high, Cioss Prato has roughly 14 cm of snow on the ground today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 19 cm by Sunday. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 14 cm now.

    The mountain at Cioss Prato

    Skied top to bottom, Cioss Prato gives 56 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,542 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Getting up the mountain: 1 button lift and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Pizzo Rotondo, 3,192 m high and 3.2 km north-west of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Ristorante All'Acqua (hotel), Capanna Piansecco (mountain hut), Locanda Orelli (hotel) and Capanna Cristallina (mountain hut). Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Cioss Prato?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Cioss Prato have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 36 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Cioss Prato at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,598 m; the lowest starts at 1,542 m (56 m of vertical).

    Is Cioss Prato suitable for families?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Cioss Prato?

    Airolo Pesciüm, 10 km to the east, with about 21 km of pistes.
