    About Beartooth Basin Summer

    Compact and local, Beartooth Basin Summer has about 2.0 km of runs, small for US West. The slopes start at 3,163 m and reach 3,329 m at the top. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being Red Lodge Mountain, 24 km to the north.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Beartooth Basin Summer hold around 55 cm of snow today. 3 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 23 cm of new snow is expected by Monday. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 78 cm by now.

    How Beartooth Basin Summer is laid out

    The vertical drop is 166 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 3,163 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. All 2 lifts here are button lifts. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Wymont (3,344 m), Tibbs Butte (3,248 m) and Tolman Mountain (3,171 m).

    How much snow Beartooth Basin Summer usually gets

    Count on about 30 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 3,246 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from early November to late May or later. By mid-April the typical depth is 191 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 197 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 100 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 66 cm, snowy years 152 cm). A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 22 weeks. Most winters land between 100 cm and 205 cm by mid-February.

    The pistes of Beartooth Basin Summer

    Counting every graded piste, there are 9 runs and around 1.8 km to ski. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 1.1 km, or 60%. Steep ground is the speciality: 60% of the length is graded difficult. Experts can count on some 1.1 km of difficult runs.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Beartooth Basin Summer?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Beartooth Basin Summer have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 100 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Beartooth Basin Summer?

    Between 3,163 m and 3,329 m, so 166 m of vertical drop.

    Is Beartooth Basin Summer good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 1.1 km of its graded pistes (60%) are difficult.

    How big is Beartooth Basin Summer?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
