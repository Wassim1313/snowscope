    The snow at Alta Badia, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Alta Badia (about 1,934 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 33 cm, with a normal range of 22 cm–48 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 56 cm; the middle half of winters range from 44 cm to 70 cm.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 59 cm.

    By 15 December 15 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 30 %.

    Best winter on record: 2013-14, peaking at 129 cm. Worst: 2016-17, which never passed 37 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from early December until early May in a normal winter, roughly 158 days.

    Alta Badia: quick answers

    Can you ski Alta Badia at Christmas?

    It depends on the winter. The usual Christmas depth is 33 cm, and only 30 % of the past 46 winters had 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 44 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Alta Badia?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 59 cm). For a sure base, early December to early May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Alta Badia?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 59 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 129 cm (2013-14), the thinnest 37 cm (2016-17).

    What altitude is Alta Badia at?

    Between 1,341 m and 2,526 m, so 1,185 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Alta Badia have?

    About 127 km of pistes and 59 lifts.

    About Alta Badia

    Alta Badia is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 127 km of pistes put it at number 34 of 1,056. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,341 m up to 2,526 m. Its nearest neighbour is Dolomiti Superski, 2.4 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Alta Badia gives 1,185 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,341 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months.
