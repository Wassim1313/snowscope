    About Patscherkofel Ski

    Patscherkofel Ski is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 442 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 890 m up to 1,961 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Glungezer, 7.3 km to the north-east.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,426 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 17 December and keeps it until about 6 May, roughly 20 weeks. Most winters land between 37 cm and 75 cm by mid-February. A normal season tops out near 60 cm, around 28 February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 35 cm (lean years 15 cm, snowy years 56 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 41 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    The pistes of Patscherkofel Ski

    The piste map lists 28 graded runs, about 12 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 6.6 km, or 55%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 2.4 km of black or expert runs.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,071 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 890 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet, 6 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Rosenjoch, 2,796 m high and 7.9 km south-east of the resort.

    Patscherkofel Ski: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Patscherkofel Ski?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Can you ski at Patscherkofel Ski at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 35 cm deep.

    What altitude is Patscherkofel Ski at?

    The top is at 1,961 m and the base at 890 m.

    Who is Patscherkofel Ski best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 55% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Patscherkofel Ski?

    Axamer Lizum, 11 km to the west, with about 38 km of pistes.
