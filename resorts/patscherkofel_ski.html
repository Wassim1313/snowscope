    About Patscherkofel Ski

    Patscherkofel Ski is a mid-sized ski area with about 12 km of pistes, number 442 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 890 m up to 1,961 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Glungezer, 7.3 km to the north-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 1,071 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. With the bottom at 890 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Patscherkofel Ski include Bärenwirth (hotel), Patscherkofel Schutzhaus (mountain hut), Grünwalderhof (hotel) and Römerhof (hotel).
