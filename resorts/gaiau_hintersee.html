    About Gaißau -Hintersee

    Gaißau -Hintersee is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 1,196 m and 1,362 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Kesselmannlifte, 7.1 km to the north.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,279 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to mid-May, about 21 weeks of good cover. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 58 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 36 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 69 cm, a dry one 9 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February. Most winters land between 43 cm and 109 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 24 February, with about 74 cm.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    The vertical drop is 166 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The highest named summit within 8 km is Gennerhorn at 1,734 m, standing 372 m above the top lift.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 1.3 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (54%, about 0.7 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Gaißau -Hintersee: common questions

    When does Gaißau -Hintersee have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Gaißau -Hintersee have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 36 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Gaißau -Hintersee?

    Between 1,196 m and 1,362 m, so 166 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Gaißau -Hintersee?

    Yes: 54% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Gaißau -Hintersee?

    Rossfeld, 14 km to the south-west, with about 10 km of pistes.
