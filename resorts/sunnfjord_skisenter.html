    About Sunnfjord skisenter

    Sunnfjord skisenter is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 344 m to 595 m. The closest other ski area is Jølster Skisenter (19 km, east).

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Sunnfjord skisenter hold around 29 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 45 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 42 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At about 470 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 107 cm to 283 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and mid-April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 228 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 246 cm, around 22 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 104 cm, from 55 cm in a poor year to 158 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 22 weeks.

    The mountain at Sunnfjord skisenter

    Skied top to bottom, Sunnfjord skisenter gives 251 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 3 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Litlehesten, 912 m high and 7.7 km south-west of the resort.

    Beds near the slopes

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Sunnfjord skisenter include Birdbox Fauske (mountain hut), Thon Hotel Førde (hotel), Førde Pensjonat (guesthouse) and Scandic Sunnfjord Hotel & Spa (hotel).

    Sunnfjord skisenter: common questions

    When does Sunnfjord skisenter have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and mid-April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Sunnfjord skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 104 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Sunnfjord skisenter?

    Its highest lift reaches 595 m; the lowest starts at 344 m (251 m of vertical).
