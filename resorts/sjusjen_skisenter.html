    About Sjusjøen Skisenter

    With roughly 5.0 km of runs, Sjusjøen Skisenter sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 737 m and 950 m above sea level. Its nearest neighbour is Lysgårdsbakkene hoppanlegg, 14 km west, and 6 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Sjusjøen Skisenter

    The piste map lists 10 graded runs, about 5.0 km in total. Most of the length is blue (39%, about 1.9 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 844 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 26 weeks of good cover. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 7 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-March and late March. A normal season tops out near 105 cm, around 24 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 62 cm (lean years 34 cm, snowy years 95 cm). Most winters land between 73 cm and 135 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 102 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    213 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Getting up the mountain: 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 3 lifts in all. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Lunkefjell (1,012 m), Sjusjøfjellet (995 m) and Snørvillen (995 m).

    Questions about Sjusjøen Skisenter

    When is the best time to ski at Sjusjøen Skisenter?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to mid-May.

    Is there snow at Sjusjøen Skisenter at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 62 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Sjusjøen Skisenter?

    Between 737 m and 950 m, so 213 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Sjusjøen Skisenter?

    Yes: 55% of the graded pistes, about 2.7 km, are easy.

    How big is Sjusjøen Skisenter?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
