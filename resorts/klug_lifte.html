    About Klug Lifte

    Klug Lifte is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 2.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 1,322 m to 1,458 m. Modriach-Winkel Hoislifte is the next resort, 5.4 km away to the north-east.

    The pistes of Klug Lifte

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 2.1 km in total. Most of the length is blue (75%, about 1.6 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Klug Lifte

    The vertical drop is 136 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,322 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. Above the ski area rises Handalm (1,853 m), 6.9 km to the south, about 395 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Klug Lifte through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 1,390 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 28 cm. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 2 cm to 55 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 14 cm; a snowy December gave 35 cm, a dry one 2 cm. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any.

    Klug Lifte: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Klug Lifte?

    Aim for early March and mid-March.

    How much snow does Klug Lifte have at Christmas?

    It is a gamble: only nearly every winter had snow at mid-mountain on Christmas Day (typically 14 cm).

    How high is Klug Lifte?

    The top is at 1,458 m and the base at 1,322 m.

    Can beginners ski at Klug Lifte?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Klug Lifte?

    Weinebene, 8.5 km to the south, with about 13 km of pistes.
