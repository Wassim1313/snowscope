    About Chäserrugg-Alt St. Johann

    Around 33 km of marked runs place Chäserrugg-Alt St. Johann among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 188 by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 891 m up to 2,260 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Wildhaus, 3.1 km to the east.

    The mountain at Chäserrugg-Alt St. Johann

    The vertical drop is 1,369 m: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Below about 1,091 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. Its top at 2,260 m is the highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 23 lifts: 1 cable car, 15 funiculars, 3 gondolas, 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. Above the ski area rises Gamsberg (2,385 m), 7.1 km to the south-east, about 125 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    At mid-mountain (about 1,576 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 12 December and lasts to 25 May: about 24 weeks. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. By mid-April the typical depth is 79 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 52 cm and 115 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 44 cm (snow lay here in every winter) (lean years 15 cm, snowy years 64 cm). Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 90 cm.
