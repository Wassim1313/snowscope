    About Madarasi Hargita

    With roughly 3.0 km of runs, Madarasi Hargita sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. The slopes start at 1,484 m and reach 1,676 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Harghita Ski, 8.0 km to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Madarasi Hargita

    Madarasi Hargita has 8 graded runs adding up to about 3.0 km. Most of the length is blue (42%, about 1.3 km). With 52% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Madarasi Hargita gives 192 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,484 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. No other ski area within 50 km reaches as high as its 1,676 m. All 4 lifts here are drag lifts. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Madarasi Hargita: common questions

    What altitude is Madarasi Hargita at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,676 m; the lowest starts at 1,484 m (192 m of vertical).

    Is Madarasi Hargita good for beginners?

    Yes: 52% of the graded pistes, about 1.6 km, are easy.

    How big is Madarasi Hargita?

    About 3.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
