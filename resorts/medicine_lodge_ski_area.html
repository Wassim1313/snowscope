    About Medicine Lodge

    Compact and local, Medicine Lodge has about 5.0 km of runs, small for Canada West. Its lifts climb from 1,004 m to 1,116 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    112 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 T-bar and 3 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 17 runs and around 4.8 km to ski. Most of the length is blue square (45%, about 2.2 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Advanced skiers have about 1.7 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Medicine Lodge usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,060 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-January and late January. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 15 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 31 cm in the best years. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 6 cm to 40 cm. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. A normal season tops out near 21 cm, around 10 January.

    Quick answers

    When does Medicine Lodge have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and late January.

    Is there snow at Medicine Lodge at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 15 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Medicine Lodge?

    Between 1,004 m and 1,116 m, so 112 m of vertical drop.

    Does Medicine Lodge have steep runs?

    Yes: about 1.7 km of its graded pistes (36%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Medicine Lodge have?

    About 5.0 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
