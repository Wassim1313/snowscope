    The snow at Ramundberget, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Ramundberget (about 831 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 51 cm of cover (42 cm to 62 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 83 cm (72 cm–91 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 92 cm.

    By 15 December 43 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 70 %.

    The deepest winter was 1987-88 (136 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1995-96 (61 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 179 days.

    Ramundberget: quick answers

    Does Ramundberget have snow over Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 51 cm at Christmas, and 70 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 19 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Ramundberget?

    March: the snow peaks at about 92 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from late November to mid-May.

    How much snow does Ramundberget get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 92 cm in late March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 136 cm (1987-88), the thinnest 61 cm (1995-96).

    How high do the lifts go at Ramundberget?

    Its highest lift reaches 961 m; the lowest starts at 701 m (260 m of vertical).

    How big is Ramundberget?

    About 22 km of pistes and 10 lifts.

    About Ramundberget

    With some 22 km of runs, Ramundberget belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 701 m to 961 m. Its nearest neighbour is Tänndalen, 17 km south-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    How Ramundberget is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Ramundberget gives 260 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.
