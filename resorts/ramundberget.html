    The snow at Ramundberget, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Ramundberget (about 831 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 51 cm of cover (42 cm to 62 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 83 cm (72 cm–91 cm).

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 92 cm.

    By 15 December 43 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 70 %.

    The deepest winter was 1987-88 (136 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 1995-96 (61 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until mid-May in a normal winter, roughly 179 days.

    Today's 10 cm compares with a usual 32 cm for 28 November.

    How much snow does Ramundberget get?

    About Ramundberget

    With some 22 km of runs, Ramundberget belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Scandinavia. Its lifts climb from 701 m to 961 m. Its nearest neighbour is Tänndalen, 17 km south-west, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 47 marked runs cover roughly 22 km. Most of the length is red (36%, about 8.2 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Experts can count on some 5.8 km of difficult runs.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 831 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to mid-May, about 25 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 29 March, with about 92 cm. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late March and early April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 64 cm and 100 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 51 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 71 cm in the best years. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 87 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered.

    How Ramundberget is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Ramundberget gives 260 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among its 3 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 7 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet, 10 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. The skyline is dominated by Mittåkläppen, 1,212 m high and 5.5 km north-east of the resort.

    The snow right now

    Up high, Ramundberget has roughly 20 cm of snow on the ground today. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 40 cm by now.
