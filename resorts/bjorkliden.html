    About Björkliden

    Around 11 km of marked runs place Björkliden among the larger resorts of Scandinavia, number 63 by length. Its lifts climb from 424 m to 933 m. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Abisko, 5.0 km to the south-east.

    The pistes of Björkliden

    In all, 27 marked runs cover roughly 11 km. The largest share is blue, with 7.4 km (68%). With 76% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Björkliden gives 509 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Its top at 933 m is the second highest of the 4 ski areas within 50 km. All 5 lifts here are drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Latnjačorru at 1,446 m, standing 513 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Björkliden usually gets

    At mid-mountain (about 678 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 2 November and lasts to the end of May: about 30 weeks. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 76 cm, with 105 cm in the best years. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. A normal season tops out near 129 cm, around 8 April. Year to year the snow is steady, with 86 cm to 146 cm in mid-February in most winters. The best weeks for snow are usually early April and mid-April. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 126 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Björkliden include Hotell Fjället (hotel), Gammelgården (hostel), STF Abisko Turiststation (hotel) and Abiskojåkka Fjällstuga (mountain hut).

    Björkliden: common questions

    When does Björkliden have the most snow?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    How much snow does Björkliden have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 76 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Björkliden at?

    The top is at 933 m and the base at 424 m.

    Is Björkliden suitable for families?

    Yes: 76% of the graded pistes, about 8.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Björkliden have?

    About 11 km of pistes and 5 lifts.
