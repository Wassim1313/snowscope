    The mountain at Morgedal

    Skied top to bottom, Morgedal gives 79 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Rauland skisenter, for one, climbs to 1,123 m. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Tulla, 1,224 m high and 7.5 km north-east of the resort.

    Snow at Morgedal through a normal winter

    At about 480 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 December and lasts to 7 May: about 22 weeks. Most winters land between 60 cm and 130 cm by mid-February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 45 cm, with 77 cm in the best years. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. The snow is usually deepest around 9 March, with about 96 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 82 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Morgedal: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Morgedal?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    How much snow does Morgedal have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 45 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Morgedal at?

    Its highest lift reaches 519 m; the lowest starts at 440 m (79 m of vertical).

    How big is Morgedal?

    About 0.3 km of pistes and 1 lift.
