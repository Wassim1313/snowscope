    Le Grand Domaine in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Le Grand Domaine (1,868 m), from 46 past winters.

    Christmas week typically brings 84 cm of cover (54 cm to 110 cm in the middle half of winters).

    Mid-February typically sees 160 cm, between 130 cm and 181 cm in a normal winter.

    The deepest snow comes in March: about 169 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    61 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 93 % by 1 January.

    2019-20 was the big one (273 cm); 2010-11 the lean one (136 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to early June, about 190 days.

    Skiing Le Grand Domaine: the practical side

    Lifts: 53. Pistes: 125 km. Heights: 1,219 m at the bottom, 2,516 m at the top (1,297 m of vertical).

    Le Grand Domaine: quick answers

    Can you ski Le Grand Domaine at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 84 cm at Christmas, and 93 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 119 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Le Grand Domaine?

    March: the snow peaks at about 169 cm then. A base of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-November to early June.

    How snowy is Le Grand Domaine?

    About 169 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 136 cm in the leanest winter (2010-11) and 273 cm in the biggest (2019-20).

    What altitude is Le Grand Domaine at?

    Between 1,219 m and 2,516 m, so 1,297 m of vertical drop.

    How big is Le Grand Domaine?

    About 125 km of pistes and 53 lifts.

    About Le Grand Domaine

    With some 125 km of runs, Le Grand Domaine belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,219 m and reach 2,516 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Valmorel (2.1 km, north-east).

    Le Grand Domaine today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Le Grand Domaine hold around 119 cm of snow today. 47 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 102 cm by Monday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,297 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Les Trois Vallées, reach 3,221 m.
