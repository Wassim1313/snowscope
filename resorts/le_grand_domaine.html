    About Le Grand Domaine

    With some 125 km of runs, Le Grand Domaine belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,219 m and reach 2,516 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Valmorel (2.1 km, north-east).

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 213 runs and around 125 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 65 km, or 52%. With 74% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Snow cannons cover about 29 km of the runs (23%), mostly the busiest runs and the way back to the bottom.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,297 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Les Trois Vallées, reach 3,221 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 cable car, 4 gondolas, 13 chairlifts, 17 button lifts, 4 drag lifts, 2 rope tows and 12 moving carpets, 53 lifts in all. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. First-timers get 12 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Le Cheval Noir (2,832 m), 2.1 km to the south, about 316 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Le Grand Domaine through a normal winter

    Count on about 27 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,868 m) in an ordinary winter, from late November to late May or later. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 105 cm and 205 cm. A normal season tops out near 169 cm, around 5 March. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 149 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 84 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 128 cm, a dry one 39 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Le Grand Domaine?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Can you ski at Le Grand Domaine at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 84 cm deep.

    When does Le Grand Domaine open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 9 April 2027.

    What altitude is Le Grand Domaine at?

    Between 1,219 m and 2,516 m, so 1,297 m of vertical drop.

    Is Le Grand Domaine suitable for families?

    Yes: 74% of the graded pistes, about 92 km, are easy.
