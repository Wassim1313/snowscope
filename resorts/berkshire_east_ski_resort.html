    About Berkshire East

    Neither huge nor tiny, Berkshire East offers about 19 km of pistes (number 34 of 93 in US Northeast). Its lifts climb from 180 m to 466 m. The closest other ski area is Easton (24 km, east).

    What there is to ski at Berkshire East

    The piste map lists 39 graded runs, about 19 km in total. Most of the length is green circle (38%, about 7.3 km). There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. Advanced skiers have about 4.7 km of black or expert runs.

    How Berkshire East is laid out

    The vertical drop is 286 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 180 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Its 6 lifts break down into 4 chairlifts and 2 moving carpets. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. 2 moving carpets serve the beginner and children's areas. The highest named summit within 8 km is Parker Hill at 617 m, standing 151 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Berkshire East usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 323 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 25 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 4 cm to 58 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 8 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 28 cm in a big one.

    Questions about Berkshire East

    When does Berkshire East have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    Can you ski at Berkshire East at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 8 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Berkshire East?
