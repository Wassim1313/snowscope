    About Berkshire East

    Neither huge nor tiny, Berkshire East offers about 19 km of pistes (number 34 of 93 in US Northeast). Its lifts climb from 180 m to 466 m. The closest other ski area is Easton (24 km, east).

    How Berkshire East is laid out

    The vertical drop is 286 m: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 180 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.

    Staying nearby

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Bird's Nest (guesthouse), Stump Sprouts Lodge (hostel) and Stump Sprouts Farmhouse (hostel).
