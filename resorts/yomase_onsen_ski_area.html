    About Yomase Onsen

    Yomase Onsen is a mid-sized ski area with about 8.0 km of pistes, number 119 of 385 in Japan by length. Skiing here happens between 728 m and 1,178 m above sea level. X-JAM Takaifuji is the next resort, 1.4 km away to the north.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    The big name nearby is Ryuoo Ski Park: about 12 km of pistes, 5.0 km to the east. For higher slopes, Ryuoo Ski Park climbs to 1,922 m.

    Snow this week

    The mountain at Yomase Onsen

    Skied top to bottom, Yomase Onsen gives 450 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. With the bottom at 728 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry.
