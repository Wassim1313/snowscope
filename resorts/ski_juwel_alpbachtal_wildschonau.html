    About Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau

    Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 69 km of pistes put it at number 81 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 820 m to 2,026 m. Ski Juwel is the next resort, 6.6 km away to the north-west.

    How much snow Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau usually gets

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,423 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 28 cm and 66 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 26 cm, from 11 cm in a poor year to 44 cm in a big one. By mid-April the typical depth is 42 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 52 cm, around 26 February.

    How Ski Juwel Alpbachtal-Wildschönau is laid out

    1,206 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The base at 820 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. The lift system counts 22 lifts: 7 gondolas, 6 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 5 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Großer Galtenberg at 2,424 m, standing 398 m above the top lift.
