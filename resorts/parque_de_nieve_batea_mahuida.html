    About Parque de Nieve Batea Mahuida

    Parque de Nieve Batea Mahuida is a small ski area by the standards of Andes, with about 3.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 1,542 m and reach 1,622 m at the top. Adlafquén is the next resort, 6.8 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 4 marked runs cover roughly 2.9 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (100%, about 2.9 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Parque de Nieve Batea Mahuida gives 80 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,542 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its top at 1,622 m is the second highest of the 5 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 button lift.
