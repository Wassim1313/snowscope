    About Lech/Zürs

    With some 120 km of runs, Lech/Zürs belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,430 m and reach 2,436 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ski Arlberg, 3.8 km to the east.

    The pistes of Lech/Zürs

    Counting every graded piste, there are 179 runs and around 119 km to ski. Most of the length is red (45%, about 54 km). Intermediate runs make up 45% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. The steepest pistes add up to about 17 km.

    Snow at Lech/Zürs through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,933 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 22 November and lasts to the end of May: about 27 weeks. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 183 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 86 cm; a snowy December gave 119 cm, a dry one 39 cm. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and late March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 114 cm and 220 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 168 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Lech/Zürs gives 1,006 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,430 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,436 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. To get up there are 3 cable cars, 4 gondolas, 1 chair-gondola, 14 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 3 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 4 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 4 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Valluga, 2,809 m high and 7.9 km south-east of the resort.

    Lech/Zürs: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Lech/Zürs?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from late November to late May.

    Is there snow at Lech/Zürs at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 86 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Lech/Zürs open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 2 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 18 April 2027.

    How high do the lifts go at Lech/Zürs?

    Between 1,430 m and 2,436 m, so 1,006 m of vertical drop.

    Is Lech/Zürs suitable for families?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 49 km, are easy.
