    About Sappada

    Sappada is a mid-sized ski area with about 15 km of pistes, number 392 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,197 m to 2,007 m. Its nearest neighbour is Sauris, 12 km south, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Sappada

    Sappada has 29 graded runs adding up to about 15 km. Most of the length is red (65%, about 9.6 km). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 1.1 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,602 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 19 December and lasts to 1 May: about 19 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 43 cm to 108 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 34 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 11 cm in a poor year to 66 cm in a big one. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 71 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 54 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February.

    The mountain at Sappada

    810 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Its top at 2,007 m is the third highest of the 8 ski areas within 50 km. Its 9 lifts break down into 5 chairlifts, 3 button lifts and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. The skyline is dominated by Monte Peralba, 2,694 m high and 7.1 km north of the resort.

    Other resorts close by

    Sappada: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Sappada?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    How much snow does Sappada have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Sappada at?

    The top is at 2,007 m and the base at 1,197 m.

    What level of skier is Sappada best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 65% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How many km of pistes does Sappada have?

    About 15 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
