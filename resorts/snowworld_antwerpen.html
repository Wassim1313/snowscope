    About SnowWorld Antwerpen

    SnowWorld Antwerpen has about 0.2 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 18 m to 20 m. There are 1 other ski area within 40 km, the closest being SnowWorld Terneuzen, 40 km to the north-west.

    Snow at SnowWorld Antwerpen in a normal winter

    In most past winters the snow at mid-mountain (about 19 m) stayed thin, rarely more than a few centimetres for long. The upper runs and any machine-made snow carry the season here, so check today's numbers before you travel.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 2 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 18 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. All 2 lifts here are moving carpets. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes.
