    About Sternrodt

    Neither huge nor tiny, Sternrodt offers about 2.0 km of pistes (number 134 of 257 in Central Europe). The ski area spans the mountain from 583 m up to 782 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Willingen, 3.9 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 1.4 km. The largest share is blue, with 1.4 km (100%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Sternrodt is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Sternrodt gives 199 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 583 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Of the 6 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Getting up the mountain: 2 T-bars and 1 rope tow, 3 lifts in all.
