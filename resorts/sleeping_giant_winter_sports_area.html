    About Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are

    Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are is a small ski area by the standards of US West, with about 6.0 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 2,022 m up to 2,263 m.

    Runs by difficulty

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 5.4 km. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 2.4 km, or 43%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.1 km.

    The mountain at Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are

    241 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 2,022 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. The lift system counts 3 lifts: 2 chairlifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Cody Peak, 3,129 m high and 6.8 km west of the resort.

    How much snow Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,142 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 3 December and keeps it until about 26 April, roughly 21 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 48 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. For the best snow, aim for early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 77 cm, around 18 March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm; a snowy December gave 62 cm, a dry one 27 cm. Most winters land between 53 cm and 96 cm by mid-February.

    Questions about Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are

    What is the best month to ski at Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    Is there snow at Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 42 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are?

    Between 2,022 m and 2,263 m, so 241 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 43% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Sleeping Giant Winter Sports Are?

    About 6.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
