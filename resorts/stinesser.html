    About Stinesser Lifte

    Compact and local, Stinesser Lifte has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 760 m to 839 m. The closest other ski area is Hörnerbahn Bolsterlang (3.8 km, west).

    Stinesser Lifte and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 6.2 km north-west to Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried, with about 15 km of runs. For more reliable snow, Grasgehren usually keeps a 30 cm base for about 23 weeks, against 0 here. For higher slopes, Nebelhorn climbs to 2,213 m.

    How Stinesser Lifte is laid out

    79 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 760 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Nebelhorn, reach 2,213 m. The lift system counts 2 lifts: 1 T-bar and 1 drag lift. The skyline is dominated by Westlicher Wengenkopf, 2,235 m high and 7.3 km south-east of the resort.

    How much snow Stinesser Lifte usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 800 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 10 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten), with 24 cm in the best years. The snow is usually deepest around 13 February, with about 17 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 2 cm to 35 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. For the best snow, aim for mid-January and early February.

    Quick answers

    When does Stinesser Lifte have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-January and early February.

    How much snow does Stinesser Lifte have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 8 winters out of ten, usually about 10 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Stinesser Lifte?

    Its highest lift reaches 839 m; the lowest starts at 760 m (79 m of vertical).

    Is Stinesser Lifte good for beginners?

    Yes: 66% of the graded pistes, about 0.7 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Stinesser Lifte?

    Ofterschwang/​Gunzesried, 6.2 km to the north-west, with about 15 km of pistes.
