    About Oshamambe Choei

    Oshamambe Choei has about 0.2 km of pistes. Skiing here happens between 8 m and 38 m above sea level. Pirika is the next resort, 15 km away to the south-west.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Oshamambe Choei has roughly 2 cm of snow on the ground today. 5 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry, and this thin early cover is expected to melt away by Friday.

    The mountain at Oshamambe Choei

    30 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 8 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Niseko Moiwa, reach 789 m. The only lift is a rope tow.
