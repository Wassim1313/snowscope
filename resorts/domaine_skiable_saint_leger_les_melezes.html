    About Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes

    Neither huge nor tiny, Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes offers about 15 km of pistes (number 382 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,263 m to 1,983 m. The closest other ski area is Ancelle (2.8 km, south).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 20 graded runs, about 15 km in total. The largest share is green (beginner), with 9.2 km (64%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,623 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 8 December and lasts to 9 May: about 22 weeks. Most winters land between 52 cm and 129 cm by mid-February. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 74 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 98 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 50 cm; a snowy December gave 82 cm, a dry one 18 cm.

    The mountain at Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes

    Skied top to bottom, Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes gives 720 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 2 chairlifts, 6 button lifts and 2 moving carpets, 10 lifts in all. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Grande Autane (2,782 m), 5.5 km to the east, about 799 m higher than the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes include Les Fauvettes (hotel), Le Pascarel (hotel), Le Grillon (hotel) and La Coustille (guesthouse).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from early December to early May.

    Can you ski at Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 50 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,983 m; the lowest starts at 1,263 m (720 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes?

    Yes: 84% of the graded pistes, about 12 km, are easy.

    How big is Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes?

    About 15 km of pistes and 10 lifts.
