    About Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes

    Neither huge nor tiny, Saint-Léger-Les-Mélèzes offers about 15 km of pistes (number 382 of 1,056 in the Alps). Its lifts climb from 1,263 m to 1,983 m. The closest other ski area is Ancelle (2.8 km, south).
