    About Lubomierz

    Neither huge nor tiny, Lubomierz offers about 1.0 km of pistes (number 231 of 312 in the Carpathians). The slopes start at 819 m and reach 834 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Polanki (5.6 km, east).

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Lubomierz gives 15 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 819 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. The only lift is a button lift.
