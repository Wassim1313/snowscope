    About Hapfere

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Hapfere one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 864 m and 932 m above sea level. Gantrisch is the next resort, 6.4 km away to the east.

    Hapfere and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Schwarzsee: about 12 km of pistes, 8.2 km to the south. If snow is the priority, Gantrisch does better, with about 22 weeks of a good base instead of 0. For higher slopes, Schwarzsee climbs to 1,748 m.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 68 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 864 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Schwarzsee, for one, climbs to 1,748 m. The only lift is a button lift. The skyline is dominated by Pfyffe, 1,665 m high and 7.1 km east of the resort.

    How much snow Hapfere usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 898 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in 6 winters out of ten; the usual depth was 2 cm, from 0 cm in a poor year to 16 cm in a big one. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 22 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. A normal season tops out near 10 cm, around 10 February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February.

    Questions about Hapfere

    When does Hapfere have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Hapfere at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 6 winters out of ten, typically 2 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    What altitude is Hapfere at?

    Between 864 m and 932 m, so 68 m of vertical drop.

    Can beginners ski at Hapfere?

    Yes: 100% of the graded pistes, about 1.0 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near Hapfere?

    Schwarzsee, 8.2 km to the south, with about 12 km of pistes.
