    About Marmolada

    Neither huge nor tiny, Marmolada offers about 20 km of pistes (number 314 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,455 m up to 3,235 m. Arabba is the next resort, 6.3 km away to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Marmolada gives 1,780 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,455 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point. To get up there are 3 cable cars, 1 chairlift and 1 button lift. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up. The skyline is dominated by Punta Penia, 3,343 m high and 3.2 km west of the resort.

    What there is to ski at Marmolada

    Marmolada has 20 graded runs adding up to about 13 km. Red runs make up the biggest part: 8.0 km, or 60%. Intermediate runs make up 60% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers. Advanced skiers have about 1.5 km of black or expert runs.

    How much snow Marmolada usually gets

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,345 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 1 December and keeps it until about 16 May, roughly 24 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is usually deepest around 28 March, with about 81 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 48 cm to 122 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 76 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 48 cm, from 14 cm in a poor year to 90 cm in a big one.

    Marmolada: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Marmolada?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    How much snow does Marmolada have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 48 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    What altitude is Marmolada at?

    Between 1,455 m and 3,235 m, so 1,780 m of vertical drop.

    What level of skier is Marmolada best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 60% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Marmolada?

    Dolomiti Superski, 11 km to the north, with about 1,100 km of pistes.
