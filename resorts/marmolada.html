    About Marmolada

    Neither huge nor tiny, Marmolada offers about 20 km of pistes (number 314 of 1,056 in the Alps). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,455 m up to 3,235 m. Arabba is the next resort, 6.3 km away to the north-west.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Marmolada gives 1,780 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. With its base at 1,455 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the second highest lift-served point.
