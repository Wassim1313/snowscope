    About Strallegg

    Compact and local, Strallegg has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 688 m and reach 989 m at the top. Schneeland Wenigzell is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Strallegg gives 301 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 688 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Stuhleck, reach 1,767 m.

    Beds near the slopes

    Near the lifts you will find Gh. Almblick (guesthouse), Die Jagamühl (chalet), Ferienhaus Wetzelhütter (chalet) and Chalet Teufelsteinblick (chalet).
