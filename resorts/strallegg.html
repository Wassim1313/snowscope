    About Strallegg

    Compact and local, Strallegg has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 688 m and reach 989 m at the top. Schneeland Wenigzell is the next resort, 4.1 km away to the east.

    What there is to ski at Strallegg

    Strallegg has 2 graded runs adding up to about 1.5 km. The largest share is red, with 1.1 km (72%). Intermediate runs make up 72% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Strallegg gives 301 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 688 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Stuhleck, reach 1,767 m. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Teufelstein, 1,498 m high and 7.0 km north-west of the resort.

    How much snow Strallegg usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 838 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. The snow is usually deepest around 7 January, with about 8 cm. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 29 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 6 cm; a snowy December gave 20 cm, a dry one 1 cm. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow.

    Quick answers

    When does Strallegg have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    Can you ski at Strallegg at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 6 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Strallegg?

    Between 688 m and 989 m, so 301 m of vertical drop.

    Who is Strallegg best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 72% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    Is there a larger resort near Strallegg?

    Stuhleck, 18 km to the north, with about 22 km of pistes.
