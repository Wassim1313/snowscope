    About Ovronnaz

    Ovronnaz is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 351 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,340 m up to 2,456 m. La Martinaux is the next resort, 7.3 km away to the west.

    Ovronnaz today and the next days

    The upper slopes of Ovronnaz hold around 77 cm of snow today. 36 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 83 cm by Saturday.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,116 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,340 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,456 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Ovronnaz include Hôtel des Bains d'Ovronnaz (hotel), Lui d'Aout (mountain hut), Hôtel des Muverans (hotel) and Hostellerie de l'Ardève (hotel).

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Ovronnaz?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,456 m; the lowest starts at 1,340 m (1,116 m of vertical).

    How big is Ovronnaz?

    About 17 km of pistes and 7 lifts.
