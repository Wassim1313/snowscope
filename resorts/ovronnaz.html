    The snow at Ovronnaz, winter after winter

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Ovronnaz (about 1,898 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 62 cm, with a normal range of 41 cm–81 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 122 cm; the middle half of winters range from 103 cm to 141 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 130 cm.

    48 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 80 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1994-95 (214 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2010-11 (99 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until early June in a normal winter, roughly 193 days.

    So far this winter: 73 cm on 28 November (usual: 24 cm).

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Ovronnaz at Christmas?

    Most years, yes: around 62 cm on 25 December, and a 50 cm base by New Year in 80 % of 46 winters. Right now: 66 cm.

    When is the best month to ski Ovronnaz?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 130 cm). For a sure base, late November to early June is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How snowy is Ovronnaz?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 130 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 214 cm (1994-95), the thinnest 99 cm (2010-11).

    How high do the lifts go at Ovronnaz?

    What level of skier is Ovronnaz best for?

    About Ovronnaz

    Ovronnaz is a mid-sized ski area with about 17 km of pistes, number 351 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,340 m up to 2,456 m. La Martinaux is the next resort, 7.3 km away to the west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 38 runs and around 17 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 12 km, or 72%. Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety. Advanced skiers have about 3.1 km of black or expert runs.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,116 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,340 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Its top at 2,456 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. Getting up the mountain: 4 chairlifts, 2 button lifts and 1 moving carpet, 7 lifts in all. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Grand Muveran (3,051 m), 4.0 km to the north, about 595 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,898 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 1 December and lasts to the end of May: about 26 weeks. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 62 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 31 cm in a poor year to 98 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 13 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Most winters land between 81 cm and 160 cm by mid-February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 114 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. A normal season tops out near 130 cm, around 3 March.
