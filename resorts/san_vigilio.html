    About San Vigilio

    With roughly 6.0 km of runs, San Vigilio sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The slopes start at 1,497 m and reach 1,835 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Meran 2000, 13 km to the north-east.

    San Vigilio and the resorts around it

    For more terrain, Schwemmalm (17 km south-west) has about 19 km of pistes against 6.0 km here. For higher slopes, Schwemmalm climbs to 2,625 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 338 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,497 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Obergurgl-Hochgurgl, for one, climbs to 3,024 m.

    Where to stay

    Near the lifts you will find vigilius mountain resort (hotel), Gasthof Jocher - Albergo Giogo (guesthouse), Familienresidence Grafenstein (hotel) and Stuberhof (guesthouse).
