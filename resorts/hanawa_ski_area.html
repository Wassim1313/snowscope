    About Hanawa

    Hanawa is a mid-sized ski area with about 4.0 km of pistes, number 198 of 385 in Japan by length. Its lifts climb from 256 m to 744 m. Its nearest neighbour is Suishozan, 5.7 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 4 runs and around 3.6 km to ski. Red runs make up the biggest part: 1.4 km, or 38%. There is a bit of everything, from nursery slopes to proper steep pistes.

    The mountain at Hanawa

    From top to bottom that makes 488 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. At 256 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. All 3 lifts here are chairlifts.

    Hanawa: common questions

    How high is Hanawa?

    Its highest lift reaches 744 m; the lowest starts at 256 m (488 m of vertical).

    Does Hanawa have steep runs?

    Yes: about 0.9 km of its graded pistes (26%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Hanawa have?

    About 4.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
