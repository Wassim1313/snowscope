    About Porté-Puymorens

    With roughly 26 km of runs, Porté-Puymorens sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. Its lifts climb from 1,621 m to 2,474 m. GrandValira is the next resort, 11 km away to the west.

    How much snow Porté-Puymorens usually gets

    Halfway up the slopes, around 2,048 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 17 December and lasts to 24 April: about 19 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 25 cm to 117 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 48 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. A normal season tops out near 80 cm, around 24 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 34 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 8 cm in a poor year to 71 cm in a big one.

    Runs by difficulty

    Porté-Puymorens has 31 graded runs adding up to about 26 km. Most of the length is green (beginner) (36%, about 9.6 km). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    853 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. The bottom station at 1,621 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Among its 6 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. To get up there are 4 chairlifts, 4 button lifts and 1 rope tow. Above the ski area rises Puigpedrós (2,915 m), 7.7 km to the south-west, about 441 m higher than the top lift.

    Other resorts close by

    For more terrain, GrandValira (11 km west) has about 136 km of pistes against 26 km here. Only Canaro keeps a solid base longer: about 20 weeks against 19 here.

    Porté-Puymorens: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Porté-Puymorens?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    How much snow does Porté-Puymorens have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 34 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    When does Porté-Puymorens open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 28 March 2027.

    What altitude is Porté-Puymorens at?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,474 m; the lowest starts at 1,621 m (853 m of vertical).

    Is Porté-Puymorens good for beginners?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 17 km, are easy.
