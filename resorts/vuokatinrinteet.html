    About Vuokatinrinteet

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Vuokatinrinteet sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 149 m and 323 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Vuokatti (0.2 km, north-east).

    What there is to ski at Vuokatinrinteet

    Counting every graded piste, there are 21 runs and around 9.7 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 5.2 km, or 54%. With 57% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    How much snow Vuokatinrinteet usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 236 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 19 weeks of good cover. Year to year the snow is steady, with 47 cm to 76 cm in mid-February in most winters. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 57 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 35 cm, with 49 cm in the best years. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and mid-March. A normal season tops out near 64 cm, around 27 February.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Vuokatinrinteet gives 174 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lift system counts 8 lifts: 2 chairlifts, 4 T-bars, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Porttivaara (345 m), Vuokatti (326 m) and Iso-Pölly (326 m).

    Questions about Vuokatinrinteet

    When is the best time to ski at Vuokatinrinteet?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Can you ski at Vuokatinrinteet at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 35 cm deep.

    How high is Vuokatinrinteet?

    Between 149 m and 323 m, so 174 m of vertical drop.

    Is Vuokatinrinteet suitable for families?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 5.5 km, are easy.

    How big is Vuokatinrinteet?

    About 10 km of pistes and 8 lifts.
