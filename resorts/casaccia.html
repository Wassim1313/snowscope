    About Casaccia

    Compact and local, Casaccia has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,475 m to 1,599 m. The closest other ski area is Maloja (3.1 km, east).

    Casaccia and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 17 km north-east to Corviglia, with about 68 km of runs. Bivio has the longer snow season: around 28 weeks of solid cover, compared with 7 at Casaccia. Corvatsch-Furtschellas reaches 3,296 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    The upper slopes of Casaccia hold around 4 cm of snow today. 6 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 6 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 5 cm at mid-mountain.

    How Casaccia is laid out

    124 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The bottom station at 1,475 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Corvatsch-Furtschellas, for one, climbs to 3,296 m. The only lift is a T-bar. Above the ski area rises Piz Bacun (3,244 m), 5.8 km to the south, about 1,645 m higher than the top lift.

    Snow at Casaccia through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,537 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-January to early March, about 7 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 20 cm; a snowy December gave 45 cm, a dry one 4 cm. A normal season tops out near 43 cm, around 22 February. The best weeks for snow are usually early February and mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 14 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. Year to year the snow is steady, with 25 cm to 69 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Casaccia: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Casaccia?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-January to early March.

    Can you ski at Casaccia at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 20 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in nearly every winter.

    What altitude is Casaccia at?

    The top is at 1,599 m and the base at 1,475 m.

    Is there a larger resort near Casaccia?

    Corviglia, 17 km to the north-east, with about 68 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Casaccia have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 1 lift.
