    About Ski Strelníky

    Ski Strelníky is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 689 m up to 762 m. Hronec (vlek Majer) is the next resort, 13 km away to the north-east.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Ski Strelníky gives 73 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 689 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better.

    This week's snow

    There is no snow on the ground at Ski Strelníky yet. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. More is on the way: about 8 cm of new snow is expected by Friday.
