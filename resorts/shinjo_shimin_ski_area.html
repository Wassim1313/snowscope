    About Shinjo Shimin

    Shinjo Shimin is a small ski area by the standards of Japan, with about 1.0 km of pistes. Its lifts climb from 228 m to 338 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Akiyama, 13 km to the north-west.

    How Shinjo Shimin is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 110 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 228 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow.
