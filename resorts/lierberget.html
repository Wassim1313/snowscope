    About Lierberget

    Lierberget is a ski area. The slopes start at 280 m and reach 375 m at the top. Budor skitrekk is the next resort, 12 km away to the north-east.

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Sjusjøen Skisenter has the longer snow season: around 26 weeks of solid cover, compared with 17 at Lierberget. The highest nearby is Sjusjøen Skisenter, topping out at 950 m.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Lierberget today: about 4 cm up high. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 15 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Lierberget gives 95 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Sjusjøen Skisenter reaches 950 m. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Yksenåsen (695 m), 6.7 km to the north-east, about 320 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 328 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 17 weeks of good cover. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 30 cm; a snowy December gave 58 cm, a dry one 12 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 63 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 38 cm and 82 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 35 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy.

    Questions about Lierberget

    When does Lierberget have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Lierberget have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 30 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Lierberget?

    Its highest lift reaches 375 m; the lowest starts at 280 m (95 m of vertical).
