    About Czerwony szlak

    Czerwony szlak is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 498 m and 527 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Małego Króla (0.3 km, east).

    How Czerwony szlak is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 29 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 498 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Laworta, for one, climbs to 741 m. The only lift is a button lift.
