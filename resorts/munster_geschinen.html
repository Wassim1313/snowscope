    About Münster

    Münster is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 772 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,362 m and reach 1,588 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gluringen, 4.4 km to the south-west.

    How Münster compares nearby

    The big name nearby is Aletsch Arena: about 90 km of pistes, 18 km to the south-west. For higher slopes, Aletsch Arena climbs to 2,869 m.

    Snow this week

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Münster gives 226 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,362 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter.
