    What 46 winters say about Münster

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Münster (1,475 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 28 cm; half of the winters sit between 20 cm and 36 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 53 cm (46 cm–65 cm).

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 56 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    By 15 December 0 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 2 %.

    2000-01 was the big one (84 cm); 2022-23 the lean one (30 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from late December until late April in a normal winter, roughly 120 days.

    Today's 40 cm compares with a usual 11 cm for 27 November.

    About Münster

    Münster is a mid-sized ski area with about 3.0 km of pistes, number 772 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 1,362 m and reach 1,588 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gluringen, 4.4 km to the south-west.

    The pistes of Münster

    In all, 3 marked runs cover roughly 2.6 km. Most of the length is blue (100%, about 2.6 km). With 100% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    Snow this week

    The upper slopes of Münster hold around 24 cm of snow today. The last seven days brought 43 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. For the time of year this is an unusually snowy start: mid-mountain normally has about 12 cm now.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Münster gives 226 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Starting at 1,362 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. To get up there are 2 T-bars and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Above the ski area rises Vorderes Galmihorn (3,507 m), 6.5 km to the west, about 1,919 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 16 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,475 m) in an ordinary winter, from early January to late April. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 28 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 14 cm in a poor year to 41 cm in a big one. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 31 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. A normal season tops out near 56 cm, around 19 February. Year to year the snow is steady, with 36 cm to 69 cm in mid-February in most winters.
