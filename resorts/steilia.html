    About Steilia

    Steilia is a ski area.

    The snow right now

    The upper slopes of Steilia hold around 59 cm of snow today. 1 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. The season is behind a normal winter, which would have about 63 cm at mid-mountain by this date.

    Snow at Steilia through a normal winter

    Count on about 31 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 455 m) in an ordinary winter, from late October to late May or later. Depth normally peaks in early April at roughly 189 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 119 cm to 207 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 101 cm, from 66 cm in a poor year to 144 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 23 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 188 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early April and mid-April.

    Questions about Steilia

    When is the best time to ski at Steilia?

    Aim for early April and mid-April; the cover is usually good from late October to late May.

    Is there snow at Steilia at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 101 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
