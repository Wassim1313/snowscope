    Snow this week

    Up high, Lachtal has roughly 7 cm of snow on the ground today. 8 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. An ordinary winter would have around 7 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 6 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,191 m) in an ordinary winter, from early February to mid-March. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 23 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 45 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 35 cm, around 13 February. Most winters land between 13 cm and 51 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 6 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Lachtal?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Lachtal at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 23 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
