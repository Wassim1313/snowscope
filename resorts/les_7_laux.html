    About Les 7 Laux

    Les 7 Laux is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 55 km of pistes put it at number 108 of 1,056. The slopes start at 1,333 m and reach 2,372 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Stade des Neiges du Barioz, 8.2 km north, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 103 graded runs, about 55 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 18 km (33%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    How much snow Les 7 Laux usually gets

    Count on about 25 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,852 m in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. The snow is usually deepest around 8 March, with about 116 cm. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 58 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 19 cm in a poor year to 87 cm in a big one. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 64 cm and 145 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 96 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    How Les 7 Laux is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Les 7 Laux gives 1,039 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Starting at 1,333 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 23 lifts break down into 1 chair-gondola, 8 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 5 button lifts, 6 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Le Rocher Blanc (2,927 m), 7.0 km to the east, about 555 m higher than the top lift.

    Questions about Les 7 Laux

    When does Les 7 Laux have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and mid-March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Les 7 Laux at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 58 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does Les 7 Laux open for the 2026/27 season?

    The announced opening day is 19 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 29 March 2027.

    What altitude is Les 7 Laux at?

    Between 1,333 m and 2,372 m, so 1,039 m of vertical drop.

    Is Les 7 Laux good for beginners?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 28 km, are easy.
