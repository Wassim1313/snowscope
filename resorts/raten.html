    About Raten

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Raten one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,081 m and 1,119 m above sea level. Bennau /Einsiedeln is the next resort, 4.9 km away to the east.

    How much snow Raten usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,100 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from early January to mid-April, about 15 weeks of good cover. A normal season tops out near 43 cm, around 7 February. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. Most winters land between 18 cm and 67 cm by mid-February. By mid-April the typical depth is 29 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 26 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 8 cm in a poor year to 40 cm in a big one.

    Raten and the resorts around it

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.4 km south to Sattel-Hochstuckli, with about 10 km of runs. Only Biberegg keeps a solid base longer: about 16 weeks against 15 here. The highest nearby is Biberegg, topping out at 1,321 m.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Raten gives 38 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. All 2 lifts here are rope tows. The highest named summit within 8 km is Höch Tändli at 1,523 m, standing 404 m above the top lift.

    The snow right now

    Today there are about 16 cm of snow on the upper slopes of Raten. The last seven days brought 36 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. So far the season is running close to normal, which is about 13 cm at mid-mountain.

    Quick answers

    When does Raten have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from early January to mid-April.

    How much snow does Raten have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 26 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Raten?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,119 m; the lowest starts at 1,081 m (38 m of vertical).

    Which bigger ski area is near Raten?

    Sattel-Hochstuckli, 8.4 km to the south, with about 10 km of pistes.

    How many km of pistes does Raten have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
