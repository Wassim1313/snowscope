    About Harper Mountain

    With roughly 13 km of runs, Harper Mountain sits in the middle of the size table of Canada West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,083 m up to 1,483 m. Sun Peaks Resort is the next resort, 25 km away to the north-east.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Harper Mountain has roughly 65 cm of snow on the ground today. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. That is less than usual: mid-mountain normally has about 19 cm by now.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 19 graded runs, about 13 km in total. Most of the length is blue square (56%, about 7.2 km). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. Experts can count on some 5.1 km of difficult runs.

    The mountain at Harper Mountain

    Skied top to bottom, Harper Mountain gives 400 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Getting up the mountain: 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar and 2 rope tows, 4 lifts in all. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Mount Fleet (1,451 m), Mount Harper (1,416 m) and Rockface Mountain (1,337 m).

    Snow at Harper Mountain through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 1,283 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 10 December and keeps it until about 21 April, roughly 19 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 35 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. For the best snow, aim for late January and late February. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 39 cm and 82 cm. A normal season tops out near 62 cm, around 1 March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 41 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 61 cm, a dry one 27 cm.

    Harper Mountain: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Harper Mountain?

    Aim for late January and late February; the cover is usually good from early December to late April.

    How much snow does Harper Mountain have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 41 cm deep.

    How high do the lifts go at Harper Mountain?

    Between 1,083 m and 1,483 m, so 400 m of vertical drop.

    Does Harper Mountain have steep runs?

    Yes: about 5.1 km of its graded pistes (40%) are difficult.

    How big is Harper Mountain?

    About 13 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
