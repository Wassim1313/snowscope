    About Bäretswil

    Bäretswil is a small ski area by the standards of the Alps, with about 1.0 km of pistes. The slopes start at 712 m and reach 842 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Ghöch (2.8 km, east).

    Nearby alternatives compared

    Skiers after mileage can drive 8.6 km south-east to Oberholz, with about 4.0 km of runs. If snow is the priority, Oberholz does better, with about 17 weeks of a good base instead of 0. Oberholz reaches 1,167 m, the most of the resorts listed.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 1.2 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 0.8 km, or 65%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Bäretswil is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Bäretswil gives 130 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The base at 712 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Atzmännig, reach 1,194 m. Getting up the mountain: 1 button lift and 2 rope tows, 3 lifts in all. The skyline is dominated by Brandegg, 1,243 m high and 6.8 km south-east of the resort.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 777 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 12 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Year to year the snow is steady, with 6 cm to 36 cm in mid-February in most winters. At Christmas there was snow here in nearly every winter, typically 11 cm; a snowy December gave 23 cm, a dry one 2 cm. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. The snow is usually deepest around 14 February, with about 19 cm.

    Questions about Bäretswil

    When does Bäretswil have the most snow?

    Aim for mid-February and late February.

    How much snow does Bäretswil have at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in nearly every winter, usually about 11 cm, so check before you go.

    How high is Bäretswil?

    The top is at 842 m and the base at 712 m.

    Is Bäretswil suitable for families?

    Yes: 65% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bäretswil?

    Oberholz, 8.6 km to the south-east, with about 4.0 km of pistes.
