    Valmorel in past winters

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Valmorel (about 1,809 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 111 cm of cover (73 cm to 147 cm in the middle half of winters).

    On 15 February expect around 207 cm; the middle half of winters range from 172 cm to 234 cm.

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 215 cm.

    78 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 98 % by 1 January.

    2019-20 was the big one (352 cm); 2010-11 the lean one (176 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-November until early June in a normal winter, roughly 198 days.

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Valmorel at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 111 cm at Christmas, and 98 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 121 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Valmorel?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 215 cm). For a sure base, mid-November to early June is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Valmorel get?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 215 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 352 cm (2019-20), the thinnest 176 cm (2010-11).

    How high is Valmorel?

    Valmorel is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 74 km of pistes put it at number 76 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,219 m to 2,399 m. Le Grand Domaine is the next resort, 2.1 km away to the south-west.
