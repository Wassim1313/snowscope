    About Valmorel

    Valmorel is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 74 km of pistes put it at number 76 of 1,056. Its lifts climb from 1,219 m to 2,399 m. Le Grand Domaine is the next resort, 2.1 km away to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 134 marked runs cover roughly 73 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 44 km, or 60%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,809 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 November and lasts to the end of May: about 28 weeks. By mid-April the typical depth is 194 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 138 cm and 266 cm. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 215 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 111 cm (lean years 51 cm, snowy years 171 cm).

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    1,180 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Les Trois Vallées, reach 3,221 m. Its 33 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 4 gondolas, 6 chairlifts, 13 button lifts, 2 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 6 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 6 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Le Cheval Noir at 2,832 m, standing 433 m above the top lift.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are 4 hotels: Villages Clubs du Soleil, ClubMed, Résidence Anitéa and Club Med Valmorel.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Valmorel?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Valmorel have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 111 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Valmorel?

    The announced opening day is 18 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 10 April 2027.

    How high is Valmorel?

    Between 1,219 m and 2,399 m, so 1,180 m of vertical drop.

    Is Valmorel good for beginners?

    Yes: 75% of the graded pistes, about 55 km, are easy.
