    About Steibis

    Steibis is a mid-sized ski area with about 16 km of pistes, number 373 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. The slopes start at 870 m and reach 1,354 m at the top. The closest other ski area is Hochlitten-Riefensberg (2.4 km, west).

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 36 graded runs, about 16 km in total. Most of the length is blue (60%, about 9.8 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    Snow at Steibis through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,112 m) reaches a solid 30 cm base around 23 January and keeps it until about 19 March, roughly 8 weeks. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. Most winters land between 16 cm and 62 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 21 cm; a snowy December gave 37 cm, a dry one 4 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 26 cm, enough on top while the bottom gets patchy. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 37 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Steibis gives 484 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. The base at 870 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Its top at 1,354 m is the third highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 14 lifts: 1 gondola, 1 chairlift, 4 T-bars, 4 button lifts and 4 drag lifts. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. Above the ski area rises Hochgrat (1,834 m), 5.5 km to the south-east, about 480 m higher than the top lift.

    Steibis: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Steibis?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Can you ski at Steibis at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 21 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Steibis?

    The top is at 1,354 m and the base at 870 m.

    Is Steibis good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 9.8 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Steibis have?

    About 16 km of pistes and 14 lifts.
