    About Kagura Area

    With some 9.0 km of runs, Kagura Area belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. The slopes start at 1,322 m and reach 1,838 m at the top. Tashiro Area is the next resort, 2.6 km away to the south-east.

    How Kagura Area is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 516 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,322 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. Its top at 1,838 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
