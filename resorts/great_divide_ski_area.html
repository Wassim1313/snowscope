    About Great Divide

    Around 45 km of marked runs place Great Divide among the larger resorts of US West, number 42 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,769 m to 2,194 m.

    The pistes of Great Divide

    Counting every graded piste, there are 109 runs and around 45 km to ski. Blue square runs make up the biggest part: 31 km, or 68%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 12 km.

    Snow this week

    Up high, Great Divide has roughly 11 cm of snow on the ground today. The last seven days brought 13 cm of new snow. The coming days should add about 5 cm by Sunday. An ordinary winter would have around 24 cm at mid-mountain by now, so this one is on track.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    Skied top to bottom, Great Divide gives 425 m of descent: runs of a moderate length. Starting at 1,769 m, the resort is high enough for skiing to the bottom most of the winter. Getting up the mountain: 5 chairlifts and 1 rope tow, 6 lifts in all. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Greenhorn Mountain (2,289 m), Mount Belmont (2,232 m) and Bald Butte (2,132 m).

    Snow at Great Divide through a normal winter

    At about 1,982 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 29 November and lasts to 28 April: about 22 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 57 cm; a snowy December gave 96 cm, a dry one 24 cm. The snow is usually deepest around 2 April, with about 138 cm. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 76 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For about 9 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 62 cm to 165 cm in mid-February in most winters. For the best snow, aim for early February and mid-February.

    Questions about Great Divide

    What is the best month to ski at Great Divide?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late November to late April.

    How much snow does Great Divide have at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 57 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Great Divide?

    The top is at 2,194 m and the base at 1,769 m.

    Is Great Divide good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 12 km of its graded pistes (27%) are difficult.

    How many km of pistes does Great Divide have?

    About 45 km of pistes and 6 lifts.
