    About Les Monts d'Olmes

    Neither huge nor tiny, Les Monts d'Olmes offers about 16 km of pistes (number 34 of 49 in the Pyrenees). The ski area spans the mountain from 1,405 m up to 1,947 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Ax 3 Domaines, 15 km to the south-east.

    What there is to ski at Les Monts d'Olmes

    The piste map lists 25 graded runs, about 16 km in total. Red runs make up the biggest part: 9.2 km, or 59%. This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Count on about 8 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at about 1,676 m, the middle of the ski area in an ordinary winter, from late January to mid-March. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 11 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. The snow is usually deepest around 30 January, with about 40 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 19 cm; a snowy December gave 46 cm, a dry one 0 cm. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 3 cm to 76 cm.

    How Les Monts d'Olmes is laid out

    542 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, runs of a moderate length. The bottom station at 1,405 m sits at a comfortable height for snow through the main winter months. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 1 T-bar, 8 button lifts and 2 rope tows. With so many tows and T-bars, the lifts are simple and quick, but tiring for first-time snowboarders. The skyline is dominated by Pic de Soularac, 2,368 m high and 3.6 km south-east of the resort.

    Where to stay

    Among the closest beds to the pistes are Les Airelles (hotel), Les Marmottes (hotel), Cabane de La Taula (mountain hut) and Complexe des Oustalous (chalet).

    Questions about Les Monts d'Olmes

    When does Les Monts d'Olmes have the most snow?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from late January to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Les Monts d'Olmes at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 19 cm deep.

    What altitude is Les Monts d'Olmes at?

    The top is at 1,947 m and the base at 1,405 m.

    Who is Les Monts d'Olmes best for?

    Mostly intermediates: 59% of the graded pistes are of medium difficulty.

    How big is Les Monts d'Olmes?

    About 16 km of pistes and 12 lifts.
