    About Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau

    Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau is a ski area. Its lifts climb from 899 m to 1,671 m. Its nearest neighbour is Kolbensattel (Oberammergau), 3.8 km west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 1,285 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to early May, about 19 weeks of good cover. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 32 cm, from 14 cm in a poor year to 55 cm in a big one. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 60 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. A normal season tops out near 62 cm, around 5 March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 34 cm to 91 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 772 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. The base at 899 m is fairly low, so expect the best snow on the upper half of the mountain. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the highest lift-served point. The only lift is a cable car. Enclosed cabins mean non-skiers can ride up too. The highest named summit within 8 km is Notkarspitze at 1,888 m, standing 217 m above the top lift.

    Questions about Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau

    What is the best month to ski at Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to early May.

    Is there snow at Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 32 cm deep.

    What altitude is Laber-Bergbahn Oberammergau at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,671 m; the lowest starts at 899 m (772 m of vertical).
