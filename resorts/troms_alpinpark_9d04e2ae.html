    About Tromsø Alpinpark

    Tromsø Alpinpark is a ski area. There are 2 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Tromsø Alpinpark, 0.3 km to the south-east.

    Lifts and terrain

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Skarsfjellet - Stuoračazačohkka (843 m), Blåfjellet - Davit Geađgečohkka (778 m) and Blåmannen - Lulit Geađgečohkka (766 m).

    When the snow is good

    At mid-mountain (about 222 m), a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 10 November and lasts to the end of May: about 29 weeks. Most winters land between 95 cm and 162 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 152 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 73 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 47 cm in a poor year to 112 cm in a big one. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 17 weeks. The best weeks for snow are usually late March and early April. A normal season tops out near 156 cm, around 8 April.

    Questions about Tromsø Alpinpark

    When does Tromsø Alpinpark have the most snow?

    Aim for late March and early April; the cover is usually good from early November to late May.

    Is there snow at Tromsø Alpinpark at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 73 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.
