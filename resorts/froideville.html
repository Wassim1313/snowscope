    About Froideville

    Froideville has about 0.2 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 790 m up to 835 m. Chalet a Gobet is the next resort, 3.6 km away to the south.

    Froideville and the resorts around it

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 45 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With the bottom at 790 m, the lower runs can turn soft or bare in mild weather while the top stays wintry. Among the 8 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Thollon-les-Mémises reaches 1,873 m. The only lift is a rope tow.

    Snow at Froideville through a normal winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 812 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Expect big differences between winters; the usual mid-February range runs from 6 cm to 63 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 15 cm, with 48 cm in the best years. By mid-April the typical depth is 8 cm, so the season is over at mid-mountain by then. A normal season tops out near 26 cm, around 16 February. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and late February.

    Staying nearby

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Froideville include Centre scout cantonal (chalet), Ben Ouais (guesthouse), Chalet Scout (mountain hut) and Aquatis Hotel (hotel).

    Froideville: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Froideville?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    How much snow does Froideville have at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in nearly every winter, about 15 cm deep.

    How high is Froideville?

    The top is at 835 m and the base at 790 m.

    How many km of pistes does Froideville have?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
