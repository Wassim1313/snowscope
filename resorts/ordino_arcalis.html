    About Ordino-Arcalis

    With roughly 27 km of runs, Ordino-Arcalis sits in the middle of the size table of the Pyrenees. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,943 m up to 2,626 m. The closest other ski area is Pal-Arinsal (9.6 km, south).

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,284 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to early May, about 20 weeks of good cover. Snow conditions have usually peaked in mid-February and early March. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 42 cm (lean years 14 cm, snowy years 86 cm). Most winters land between 38 cm and 137 cm by mid-February. The snow is usually deepest around 23 February, with about 96 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 68 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 29 runs and around 27 km to ski. The largest share is red, with 13 km (49%). This is intermediate country: long, flowing runs for skiers who can link their turns. The steepest pistes add up to about 1.1 km. 1.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 683 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Even the lowest lift starts at 1,943 m, high enough for the bottom of the resort to keep cold snow longer than most. Its top at 2,626 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km. The lift system counts 11 lifts: 1 gondola, 4 chairlifts, 3 button lifts, 2 drag lifts and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Pic de Canalbona, 2,965 m high and 7.5 km north-west of the resort.

    How Ordino-Arcalis compares nearby

    For more terrain, GrandValira (17 km south-east) has about 136 km of pistes against 27 km here. Only Canaro keeps a solid base longer: about 20 weeks against 20 here.

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Ordino-Arcalis?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early May.

    Is there snow at Ordino-Arcalis at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 42 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Ordino-Arcalis?

    The announced opening day is 4 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    How high is Ordino-Arcalis?

    The top is at 2,626 m and the base at 1,943 m.

    Can beginners ski at Ordino-Arcalis?

    Yes: 41% of the graded pistes, about 11 km, are easy.
