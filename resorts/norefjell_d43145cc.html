    The mountain at Norefjell

    Named peaks within a few kilometres include Agunshaug (1,342 m), Grindefjellhaugen (1,284 m) and Anfinnskutan (1,280 m).

    How much snow Norefjell usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 446 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from late November to early May, about 23 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 11 March, with about 91 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 49 cm; a snowy December gave 83 cm, a dry one 28 cm. For the best snow, aim for late February and early March. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 56 cm and 124 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 73 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    Quick answers

    When does Norefjell have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from late November to early May.

    Is there snow at Norefjell at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 49 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
