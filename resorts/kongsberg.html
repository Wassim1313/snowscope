    About Kongsberg

    With roughly 10 km of runs, Kongsberg sits in the middle of the size table of Scandinavia. Skiing here happens between 241 m and 567 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Storeskar skisenter, 23 km to the south-west.

    Pistes and who they suit

    In all, 17 marked runs cover roughly 9.7 km. The largest share is green (beginner), with 3.3 km (34%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 404 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-December to late April, about 20 weeks of good cover. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. Depth normally peaks in early February at roughly 79 cm. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 39 cm; a snowy December gave 74 cm, a dry one 15 cm. Most winters land between 39 cm and 115 cm by mid-February. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 56 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well.

    The mountain at Kongsberg

    326 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. To get up there are 1 chairlift, 5 T-bars, 1 button lift and 2 moving carpets. Most of them are surface lifts that pull you up on your skis, which beginner snowboarders find hard work. For small children there are 2 conveyor-belt lifts. The highest named summit within 8 km is Jonsknuten at 904 m, standing 337 m above the top lift.

    Kongsberg: common questions

    When is the best time to ski at Kongsberg?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to late April.

    Is there snow at Kongsberg at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 39 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Kongsberg?

    Its highest lift reaches 567 m; the lowest starts at 241 m (326 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Kongsberg?

    Yes: 53% of the graded pistes, about 5.1 km, are easy.

    How big is Kongsberg?

    About 10 km of pistes and 9 lifts.
