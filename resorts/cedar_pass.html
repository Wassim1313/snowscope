    About Cedar Pass

    Compact and local, Cedar Pass has about 2.0 km of runs, small for US West. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,836 m up to 1,954 m.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 6 runs and around 2.4 km to ski. The largest share is blue square, with 1.6 km (67%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Cedar Pass today: about 21 cm up high. The last seven days brought 22 cm of new snow. Little new snow is expected over the next 3 days. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (17 cm).

    Lifts and terrain

    118 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 1,836 m, the base is high: snow down to the bottom station is the norm in midwinter. Getting up the mountain: 1 T-bar and 1 rope tow, 2 lifts in all. The highest named summit within 8 km is Bald Mountain at 2,518 m, standing 564 m above the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Halfway up the slopes, around 1,895 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 15 December and lasts to 6 April: about 16 weeks. A normal season tops out near 74 cm, around 22 February. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 14 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early February and mid-February. Snow here is hit and miss from year to year: mid-February has seen anything from 4 cm to 132 cm. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 42 cm; a snowy December gave 101 cm, a dry one 4 cm.

    Cedar Pass: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Cedar Pass?

    Aim for early February and mid-February; the cover is usually good from mid-December to early April.

    How much snow does Cedar Pass have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 42 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high is Cedar Pass?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,954 m; the lowest starts at 1,836 m (118 m of vertical).

    Is Cedar Pass good for advanced skiers?

    Yes: about 0.8 km of its graded pistes (33%) are difficult.

    How big is Cedar Pass?

    About 2.0 km of pistes and 2 lifts.
