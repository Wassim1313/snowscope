    What 46 winters say about Aussois

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Aussois (about 2,106 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 66 cm, with a normal range of 45 cm–85 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 113 cm, between 88 cm and 128 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 119 cm.

    By 15 December 50 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 76 %.

    Best winter on record: 2017-18, peaking at 186 cm. Worst: 2004-05, which never passed 91 cm.

    Expect 30 cm or more from late November until late May in a normal winter, roughly 179 days.

    Today's 23 cm compares with a usual 24 cm for 19 November.

    Skiing Aussois: the practical side

    Lifts: 10. Pistes: 30 km. Heights: 1,532 m at the bottom, 2,680 m at the top (1,148 m of vertical).

    Count on 38 € for a day pass (212 € for 6 days) and from 40 € a night per person.

    Nearest airports: Turin (TRN, 72 km), Grenoble-Alpes-Isere (GNB, 111 km), Geneva (GVA, 121 km).

    About Aussois

    Around 30 km of marked runs place Aussois among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 202 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,532 m to 2,680 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being La Norma, 6.7 km to the south.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 54 graded runs, about 29 km in total. Most of the length is red (31%, about 9.2 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    In a typical winter the snow at about 2,106 m, the middle of the ski area reaches a solid 30 cm base around 29 November and keeps it until about 24 May, roughly 25 weeks. Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 96 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 66 cm, with 98 cm in the best years. A normal season tops out near 119 cm, around 8 March. For about 10 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early March and mid-March. Year to year the snow is steady, with 78 cm to 139 cm in mid-February in most winters.

    How Aussois is laid out

    1,148 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. With its base at 1,532 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Its summit is modest for the area; the highest lifts nearby, at Orelle, reach 3,221 m. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 4 chairlifts, 1 button lift, 3 drag lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. First-timers get 1 moving carpet on the gentle nursery slopes. Above the ski area rises Dent Parrachée (3,697 m), 4.9 km to the north, about 1,017 m higher than the top lift.
