    About Skilift Zuflucht

    Compact and local, Skilift Zuflucht has about 1.0 km of runs, small for the Alps. The slopes start at 907 m and reach 962 m at the top. Skilift Lamm is the next resort, 4.8 km away to the south-east.

    How Skilift Zuflucht is laid out

    55 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Below about 1,107 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet.
