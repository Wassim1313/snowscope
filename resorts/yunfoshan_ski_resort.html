    About Yunfoshan

    Yunfoshan is a mid-sized ski area with about 2.0 km of pistes, number 69 of 92 in China by length. Its lifts climb from 122 m to 247 m. There are 4 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Nanshan, 16 km to the south.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 3 graded runs, about 1.2 km in total. The largest share is blue square, with 0.6 km (52%). Intermediate runs make up 52% of the length, the sweet spot for most holiday skiers.

    Run colours follow the North American system: green circles are easiest, blue squares intermediate, black diamonds difficult.

    Lifts and terrain

    125 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 122 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. To get up there are 2 gondolas and 5 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. On stormy days the enclosed cabins are the warmest way up.
