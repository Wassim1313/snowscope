    About Winiarska

    Winiarska is a ski area. The slopes start at 452 m and reach 497 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Chyrowa, 13 km to the west.

    How Winiarska is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Winiarska gives 45 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 452 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Karlików, for one, climbs to 678 m. The only lift is a button lift.
