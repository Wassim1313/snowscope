    What 46 winters say about Kaltbrunn

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Kaltbrunn (about 1,192 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    Christmas week typically brings 38 cm of cover (25 cm to 52 cm in the middle half of winters).

    By mid-February the usual depth is 68 cm (54 cm–85 cm).

    February is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 70 cm.

    By 15 December 17 % of winters had reached 50 cm; by New Year it was 33 %.

    The deepest winter was 1981-82 (165 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2022-23 (37 cm).

    A cover of 30 cm or more usually lasts from mid-December to mid-May, about 150 days.

    So far this winter: 29 cm on 28 November (usual: 16 cm).

    Questions about Kaltbrunn

    Does Kaltbrunn have snow over Christmas?

    Not every year: about 38 cm at Christmas on average, with a 50 cm base by New Year in 33 % of 46 winters. Right now: 19 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Kaltbrunn?

    The deepest snow comes in February (about 70 cm). For a sure base, mid-December to mid-May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How much snow does Kaltbrunn get?

    About 70 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 37 cm in the leanest winter (2022-23) and 165 cm in the biggest (1981-82).

    When does Kaltbrunn have the most snow?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-December to mid-May.

    How high is Kaltbrunn?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,232 m; the lowest starts at 1,153 m (79 m of vertical).

    Is there a larger resort near Kaltbrunn?

    Wolzenalp, 5.2 km to the north-east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    About Kaltbrunn

    About 1.0 km of pistes make Kaltbrunn one of the smaller places to ski in the Alps. Its lifts climb from 1,153 m to 1,232 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Amden Mattstock, 4.7 km to the south-east.

    When the snow is good

    At about 1,192 m, the middle of the ski area, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 18 December and lasts to 11 May: about 21 weeks. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 56 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 38 cm (snow lay here in every winter); a snowy December gave 60 cm, a dry one 13 cm. Most winters land between 35 cm and 104 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 70 cm.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 79 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Speer (1,950 m), 1.5 km to the south-east, about 718 m higher than the top lift.

    Near the lifts you will find 4 mountain huts: Obere Bogmen, Alp Oberchäseren, Tanzbodenhütte and Federihütte. Several of them are mountain huts, reached on skis or on foot.
