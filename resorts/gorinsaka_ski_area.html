    About Gorinsaka

    Gorinsaka has about 0.2 km of pistes. The ski area spans the mountain from 74 m up to 97 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Gionyama, 12 km to the south-west.

    This week's snow

    Snow on the ground at Gorinsaka today: about 18 cm up high. 4 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The next days look mild and dry; the cover is expected to settle to about 12 cm by Friday.

    How Gorinsaka is laid out

    23 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The lowest slopes are at only 74 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Jeunesse Kurikoma reaches 771 m. The only lift is a rope tow.

    Quick answers

    How high do the lifts go at Gorinsaka?

    Its highest lift reaches 97 m; the lowest starts at 74 m (23 m of vertical).

    How big is Gorinsaka?

    About 0.2 km of pistes and 1 lift.
