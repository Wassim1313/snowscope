    What 46 winters say about Les Trois Vallées

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Les Trois Vallées (about 2,166 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 92 cm; half of the winters sit between 64 cm and 120 cm.

    By mid-February the usual depth is 166 cm (136 cm–192 cm).

    March is the deepest month, with the cover peaking near 182 cm.

    74 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 98 % by 1 January.

    1994-95 was the big one (283 cm); 2004-05 the lean one (150 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs mid-November to late May (195 days).

    Common questions

    Can you ski Les Trois Vallées at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 92 cm at Christmas, and 98 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 118 cm.

    Which month has the most snow at Les Trois Vallées?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 182 cm). For a sure base, mid-November to late May is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Les Trois Vallées?

    In a typical winter the upper-mountain snow peaks at about 182 cm in early March. The deepest of 46 winters reached 283 cm (1994-95), the thinnest 150 cm (2004-05).

    How high do the lifts go at Les Trois Vallées?

    The top is at 3,221 m and the base at 1,110 m.
