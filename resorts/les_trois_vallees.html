    About Les Trois Vallées

    Only 2 of the 1,056 ski areas of the Alps are larger than Les Trois Vallées, which has some 542 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 1,110 m to 3,221 m. The closest other ski area is Méribel (3.4 km, north).

    Runs by difficulty

    Les Trois Vallées has 869 graded runs adding up to about 539 km. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 249 km, or 46%. It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 17 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    How Les Trois Vallées is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 2,111 m of vertical, one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Getting up the mountain: 6 cable cars, 1 funicular, 37 gondolas, 55 chairlifts, 33 button lifts, 2 drag lifts, 4 rope tows and 31 moving carpets, 169 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. First-timers get 31 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Aiguille de Péclet at 3,561 m, standing 340 m above the top lift.

    How much snow Les Trois Vallées usually gets

    Past winters give a clear pattern at about 2,166 m, the middle of the ski area: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May, about 27 weeks of good cover. For about 18 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. Year to year the snow is steady, with 116 cm to 207 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 92 cm (lean years 51 cm, snowy years 138 cm). By mid-April the typical depth is 159 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 182 cm.

    Les Trois Vallées: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Les Trois Vallées?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    How much snow does Les Trois Vallées have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 92 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Les Trois Vallées?

    The announced opening day is 21 November 2026.

    How high do the lifts go at Les Trois Vallées?

    The top is at 3,221 m and the base at 1,110 m.

    Can beginners ski at Les Trois Vallées?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 323 km, are easy.
