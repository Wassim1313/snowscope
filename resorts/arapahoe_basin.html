    About Arapahoe Basin

    Around 49 km of marked runs place Arapahoe Basin among the larger resorts of US West, number 38 by length. The slopes start at 3,289 m and reach 3,803 m at the top. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Loveland Valley, 5.6 km to the north.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 102 runs and around 49 km to ski. Black diamond runs make up the biggest part: 23 km, or 47%. With 57% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. Advanced skiers have about 28 km of black or expert runs.

    Snow at Arapahoe Basin through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern halfway up the slopes, around 3,546 m: a 30 cm base is usually there from mid-November to late May or later, about 29 weeks of good cover. By mid-April the typical depth is 166 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. Most winters land between 78 cm and 176 cm by mid-February. Depth normally peaks in late April at roughly 175 cm. For about 19 weeks of a typical winter there is a metre of snow or more. The best weeks for snow are usually early January and mid-March. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 81 cm (snow lay here in every winter), with 128 cm in the best years.

    Lifts and terrain

    From top to bottom that makes 514 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. A high base at 3,289 m means the lowest runs rarely turn to slush in midwinter. Among its 8 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point. Its 9 lifts break down into 6 chairlifts, 1 rope tow and 2 moving carpets. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Grays Peak at 4,352 m, standing 549 m above the top lift.

    Arapahoe Basin: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Arapahoe Basin?

    Aim for early January and mid-March; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Arapahoe Basin at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 81 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high do the lifts go at Arapahoe Basin?

    Between 3,289 m and 3,803 m, so 514 m of vertical drop.

    Does Arapahoe Basin have steep runs?

    Yes: about 28 km of its graded pistes (57%) are difficult.

    Can Arapahoe Basin make its own snow?

    Yes, on about 5.9 km of its pistes (12%).
