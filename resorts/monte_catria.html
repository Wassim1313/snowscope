    About Monte Catria

    Monte Catria ranks number 10 of 40 ski areas in the Apennines by piste length, with about 10 km of runs. Skiing here happens between 577 m and 1,482 m above sea level.

    What there is to ski at Monte Catria

    The piste map lists 17 graded runs, about 9.5 km in total. The largest share is red, with 7.4 km (78%). Its heart is intermediate terrain, ideal for skiers who are confident on most slopes and want variety.

    Lifts and terrain

    905 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, enough height for proper long runs. At 577 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Getting up the mountain: 1 gondola and 2 chairlifts, 3 lifts in all. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. Named peaks within a few kilometres include La Rocchetta (1,163 m), Monte Petrano (1,106 m) and La Roccaccia (1,062 m).
