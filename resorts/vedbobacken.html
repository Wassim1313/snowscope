    About Vedbobacken

    Compact and local, Vedbobacken has about 1.0 km of runs, small for Scandinavia. The ski area spans the mountain from 25 m up to 70 m. Skidbacke Karlbergsskogen is the next resort, 29 km away to the south-west.

    How much snow Vedbobacken usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at about 48 m, the middle of the ski area rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 5 cm (snow lay here in 8 winters out of ten); a snowy December gave 22 cm, a dry one 0 cm. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. A normal season tops out near 10 cm, around 4 February. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 1 cm to 32 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Lifts and terrain

    45 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 3 lifts break down into 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 1 moving carpet. For small children there is 1 conveyor-belt lift. Named peaks within a few kilometres include Vedbobacken (70 m) and Åkestaåsen (65 m).

    Questions about Vedbobacken

    When is the best time to ski at Vedbobacken?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    How much snow does Vedbobacken have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in 8 winters out of ten, typically 5 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high is Vedbobacken?

    Between 25 m and 70 m, so 45 m of vertical drop.

    How many km of pistes does Vedbobacken have?

    About 1.0 km of pistes and 3 lifts.
