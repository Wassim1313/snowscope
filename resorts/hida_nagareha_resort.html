    About Hida Nagareha Resort

    With some 12 km of runs, Hida Nagareha Resort belongs to the largest quarter of the ski areas of Japan. Skiing here happens between 745 m and 1,398 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Hida Kawai (17 km, south-west).

    What there is to ski at Hida Nagareha Resort

    The piste map lists 14 graded runs, about 12 km in total. Black runs make up the biggest part: 6.3 km, or 55%. With 55% of the pistes graded difficult, it suits strong skiers looking for steep runs. The steepest pistes add up to about 6.3 km.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    From top to bottom that makes 653 m of vertical, runs of a moderate length. Below about 945 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. All 6 lifts here are chairlifts. You sit down on most of the lifts here, which suits children and snowboarders.

    Snow this week

    Snow on the ground at Hida Nagareha Resort today: about 28 cm up high. The last seven days brought 44 cm of new snow. More is on the way: about 27 cm of new snow is expected by Sunday.
