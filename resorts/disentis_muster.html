    About Disentis/Mustér

    Around 29 km of marked runs place Disentis/Mustér among the larger resorts of the Alps, number 208 by length. Its lifts climb from 1,223 m to 2,770 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Valtgeva (Druni KidsArena), 3.7 km to the south-west.

    How Disentis/Mustér is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Disentis/Mustér gives 1,547 m of descent: a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. Of the 9 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. The lift system counts 11 lifts: 1 cable car, 3 chairlifts, 3 T-bars, 2 button lifts, 1 rope tow and 1 moving carpet. The cabin lifts are sheltered on cold or windy days and also take walkers and sightseers up. 1 moving carpet serves the beginner and children's areas. Above the ski area rises Oberalpstock (3,328 m), 5.2 km to the north-west, about 558 m higher than the top lift.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 52 graded runs, about 29 km in total. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 13 km, or 44%. The mix is balanced: easy slopes to learn on and enough steeper runs to keep good skiers busy. Experts can count on some 3.9 km of difficult runs. 3.7 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 24 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) at mid-mountain (about 1,996 m) in an ordinary winter, from early December to late May. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 46 cm (lean years 25 cm, snowy years 62 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 25 March, with about 95 cm. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 55 cm and 107 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 90 cm, so spring skiing usually holds up well. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March.

    Quick answers

    When is the best time to ski at Disentis/Mustér?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Is there snow at Disentis/Mustér at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 46 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    How high is Disentis/Mustér?

    Its highest lift reaches 2,770 m; the lowest starts at 1,223 m (1,547 m of vertical).

    Is Disentis/Mustér good for beginners?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 14 km, are easy.

    How big is Disentis/Mustér?

    About 29 km of pistes and 11 lifts.
