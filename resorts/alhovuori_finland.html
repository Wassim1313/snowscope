    About Alhovuori

    Alhovuori is a ski area. Its nearest neighbour is Alhovuori, 0.1 km east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Snow at Alhovuori through a normal winter

    Halfway up the slopes, around 88 m, a base of at least 30 cm usually builds by 4 February and lasts to 16 March: about 6 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 13 cm, from 1 cm in a poor year to 26 cm in a big one. The snow is usually deepest around 7 March, with about 33 cm. Most winters land between 9 cm and 53 cm by mid-February. Spring comes early here: by mid-April the middle of the ski area is usually free of snow. The best weeks for snow are usually late February and early March.

    Alhovuori: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Alhovuori?

    Aim for late February and early March; the cover is usually good from early February to mid-March.

    Is there snow at Alhovuori at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 13 cm; the higher slopes do better.
