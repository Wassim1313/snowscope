    About Hurdal

    Hurdal is a ski area. The closest other ski area is Hurdal Skisenter (0.6 km, east).

    Snow at Hurdal through a normal winter

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 410 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from late December to mid-April, about 17 weeks of good cover. The snow is usually deepest around 29 February, with about 62 cm. By mid-April the typical depth is 41 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and early March. Most winters land between 32 cm and 84 cm by mid-February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 31 cm, from 11 cm in a poor year to 58 cm in a big one.

    Hurdal: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Hurdal?

    Aim for mid-February and early March; the cover is usually good from late December to mid-April.

    How much snow does Hurdal have at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 31 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.
