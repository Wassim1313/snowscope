    About Estación Invernal y de Montaña S

    Estación Invernal y de Montaña S ranks number 9 of 11 ski areas in the Cantabrian Mountains by piste length, with about 5.0 km of runs. Its lifts climb from 1,660 m to 1,944 m. Its nearest neighbour is Estación Invernal y de Montaña S, 1.0 km south-east, and 4 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    284 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. With its base at 1,660 m, the lower slopes usually hold their snow from midwinter into March. Among its 4 neighbours within 50 km, it has the third highest lift-served point.
