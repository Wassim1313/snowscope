    About Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen

    With roughly 13 km of runs, Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. Skiing here happens between 1,021 m and 1,570 m above sea level. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Jaunpass, 4.2 km to the east.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 25 runs and around 13 km to ski. Blue runs make up the biggest part: 6.8 km, or 53%. With 57% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    When the snow is good

    Count on about 17 weeks of solid cover (30 cm or more) halfway up the slopes, around 1,296 m in an ordinary winter, from late December to late April. The snow is fairly dependable: in mid-February most winters had between 27 cm and 84 cm. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 38 cm; by then it pays to ski early, before the sun softens it. For the best snow, aim for mid-February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in every winter, typically 30 cm; a snowy December gave 52 cm, a dry one 6 cm. Depth normally peaks in mid-February at roughly 58 cm.

    The mountain at Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen

    The vertical drop is 549 m: runs of a moderate length. The lift system counts 4 lifts: 1 chairlift, 2 T-bars and 1 button lift. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The skyline is dominated by Dent de Savigny, 2,252 m high and 7.5 km south-west of the resort.

    The snow right now

    Where to stay

    Places to stay close to the slopes of Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen include Chalet Frohsinn (guesthouse), Hotel de la Cascade (hotel), Ferienhaus Höfli (chalet) and Bärghus Gastlosen (mountain hut).

    Quick answers

    What is the best month to ski at Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen?

    Aim for mid-February and late February; the cover is usually good from late December to late April.

    Can you ski at Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen at Christmas?

    In general yes, with around 30 cm at mid-mountain on Christmas Day in a typical year.

    What altitude is Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen at?

    Its highest lift reaches 1,570 m; the lowest starts at 1,021 m (549 m of vertical).

    Is Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen good for beginners?

    Yes: 57% of the graded pistes, about 7.3 km, are easy.

    How many km of pistes does Jaun-Gastlosen Bergbahnen have?

    About 13 km of pistes and 4 lifts.
