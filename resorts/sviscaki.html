    About Sviščaki

    Sviščaki is a ski area. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,213 m up to 1,305 m. Its nearest neighbour is Rudnik Tršće, 17 km east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    Skied top to bottom, Sviščaki gives 92 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Of the 5 ski areas within 50 km, only 1 reaches higher. All 2 lifts here are drag lifts. The skyline is dominated by Veliki Snežnik, 1,796 m high and 3.8 km east of the resort.

    How much snow Sviščaki usually gets

    Even in a typical winter the snow at mid-mountain (about 1,259 m) rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. By mid-April the snow at mid-mountain has usually melted; only the top keeps any. For the best snow, aim for early February and late February. At Christmas there was snow here in 7 winters out of ten, typically 6 cm (lean years 0 cm, snowy years 36 cm). The snow is usually deepest around 17 February, with about 16 cm. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 120 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking.

    Questions about Sviščaki

    What is the best month to ski at Sviščaki?

    Aim for early February and late February.

    Can you ski at Sviščaki at Christmas?

    Sometimes. Christmas snow at mid-mountain came in 7 winters out of ten, usually about 6 cm, so check before you go.

    How high do the lifts go at Sviščaki?

    Between 1,213 m and 1,305 m, so 92 m of vertical drop.
