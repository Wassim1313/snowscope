    About Mogno

    Mogno is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 1,156 m and 1,261 m above sea level. The closest other ski area is Mogno (0.1 km, north).

    The mountain at Mogno

    From top to bottom that makes 105 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. The only lift is a button lift. Above the ski area rises Pizzo Campo Tencia (3,071 m), 4.9 km to the east, about 1,810 m higher than the top lift.

    The snow season, winter by winter

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,208 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. Depth normally peaks in late February at roughly 21 cm. Year to year the snow is steady, with 10 cm to 31 cm in mid-February in most winters. In mid-April a normal winter still leaves 11 cm, and the lower runs are often thin by then. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 9 cm (snow lay here in nearly every winter), from 2 cm in a poor year to 22 cm in a big one. Snow conditions have usually peaked in late February and early March.

    Mogno: common questions

    What is the best month to ski at Mogno?

    Aim for late February and early March.

    How much snow does Mogno have at Christmas?

    Not reliably. On 25 December there was snow at mid-mountain in nearly every winter, typically 9 cm; the higher slopes do better.

    How high do the lifts go at Mogno?

    Between 1,156 m and 1,261 m, so 105 m of vertical drop.
