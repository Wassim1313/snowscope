    About Obersaxen

    Obersaxen is one of the bigger ski areas of the Alps: about 92 km of pistes put it at number 55 of 1,056. Skiing here happens between 1,245 m and 2,269 m above sea level. Brigels-Waltensburg-Andiast is the next resort, 7.5 km away to the north-west.

    The pistes of Obersaxen

    Counting every graded piste, there are 133 runs and around 91 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (51%, about 47 km). With 51% of the length graded easy, learners and families are well served.

    Snow at Obersaxen through a normal winter

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,757 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 4 December and keeps it until about 23 May, roughly 25 weeks. Depth normally peaks in early March at roughly 106 cm. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 7 weeks. Year to year the snow is steady, with 65 cm to 130 cm in mid-February in most winters. By mid-April the typical depth is 97 cm: Easter holidays are usually still well covered. For the holidays at the end of December, expect around 55 cm (snow lay here in every winter), from 30 cm in a poor year to 77 cm in a big one. For the best snow, aim for early March and late March.

    The mountain at Obersaxen

    From top to bottom that makes 1,024 m of vertical, enough height for proper long runs. Getting up the mountain: 9 chairlifts, 2 T-bars, 1 button lift, 1 drag lift, 4 rope tows and 2 moving carpets, 19 lifts in all. First-timers get 2 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The skyline is dominated by Lappi, 2,472 m high and 7.1 km south-west of the resort.

    Obersaxen: common questions

    When does Obersaxen have the most snow?

    Aim for early March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to late May.

    Can you ski at Obersaxen at Christmas?

    Most years, yes. Snow lay at mid-mountain at Christmas in every winter, about 55 cm deep.

    When does the ski season start at Obersaxen?

    The announced opening day is 18 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 4 April 2027.

    What altitude is Obersaxen at?

    The top is at 2,269 m and the base at 1,245 m.

    Is Obersaxen suitable for families?

    Yes: 51% of the graded pistes, about 47 km, are easy.
