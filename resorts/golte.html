    What 46 winters say about Golte

    46 winters of snow depth on the upper mountain of Golte (about 1,424 m), so you can plan with real odds rather than hope.

    On 25 December the usual depth is 10 cm, with a normal range of 4 cm–26 cm.

    On 15 February expect around 20 cm; the middle half of winters range from 5 cm to 67 cm.

    The deepest snow comes in February: about 29 cm at the peak of a typical winter.

    2 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 7 % by 1 January.

    2005-06 was the big one (232 cm); 1988-89 the lean one (13 cm).

    Expect 30 cm or more from mid-January until mid-March in a normal winter, roughly 62 days.

    Today's 13 cm compares with a usual 1 cm for 26 November.

    About Golte

    Golte is a mid-sized ski area with about 10 km of pistes, number 488 of 1,056 in the Alps by length. Its lifts climb from 1,279 m to 1,570 m. Its nearest neighbour is Petzen, 19 km north-west, and 7 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    Counting every graded piste, there are 22 runs and around 9.8 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 5.6 km (57%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    How Golte is laid out

    Skied top to bottom, Golte gives 291 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Its 6 lifts break down into 1 cable car, 4 chairlifts and 1 drag lift. Chairs and cabins make up most of the network: a comfortable ride for every level. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. Above the ski area rises Komen (1,684 m), 6.0 km to the north-west, about 114 m higher than the top lift.

    When the snow is good

    Even in a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 1,424 m rarely reaches a 30 cm base; the season relies on cold spells, the upper slopes and machine-made snow. In a normal winter mid-mountain is bare again by mid-April. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in nearly every winter; the usual depth was 10 cm, with 49 cm in the best years. Winters vary a lot: in mid-February the depth has ranged from 0 cm to 79 cm in most years, so look at the snow before booking. The best weeks for snow are usually mid-February and late February. A normal season tops out near 29 cm, around 31 January.
