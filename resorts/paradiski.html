    About Paradiski

    Only 4 of the 1,056 ski areas of the Alps are larger than Paradiski, which has some 398 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,199 m up to 3,205 m. Its nearest neighbour is La Plagne, 4.9 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Pistes and who they suit

    Counting every graded piste, there are 807 runs and around 392 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (60%, about 234 km). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. 2.1 km of marked freeride routes are not groomed and need off-piste skills.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 2,006 m: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 164 lifts break down into 6 cable cars, 3 funiculars, 21 gondolas, 53 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 19 button lifts, 14 drag lifts, 14 rope tows and 33 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. First-timers get 33 moving carpets on the gentle nursery slopes. The highest named summit within 8 km is Sommet de Bellecôte at 3,417 m, standing 212 m above the top lift.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Paradiski hold around 62 cm of snow today. 39 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 83 cm by Sunday. Mid-mountain is about where a typical winter would be now (45 cm). The 2026/27 season at Paradiski is announced to start on 12 December 2026 and to run until 17 April 2027.

    When the snow is good

    In a typical winter the snow halfway up the slopes, around 2,202 m reaches a solid 30 cm base around 13 November and keeps it until about the end of May, roughly 29 weeks. A normal season tops out near 186 cm, around 6 March. By mid-April the typical depth is 178 cm, which keeps the runs open into spring. Year to year the snow is steady, with 121 cm to 219 cm in mid-February in most winters. Snow conditions have usually peaked in early March and early April. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 21 weeks. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 99 cm; a snowy December gave 152 cm, a dry one 54 cm.

    Questions about Paradiski

    When is the best time to ski at Paradiski?

    Aim for early March and early April; the cover is usually good from mid-November to late May.

    Is there snow at Paradiski at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 99 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    When does the ski season start at Paradiski?

    The announced opening day is 12 December 2026; the last ski day is planned for 17 April 2027.

    How high is Paradiski?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,205 m; the lowest starts at 1,199 m (2,006 m of vertical).

    Can beginners ski at Paradiski?

    Yes: 62% of the graded pistes, about 243 km, are easy.
