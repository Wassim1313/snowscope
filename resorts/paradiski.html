    Paradiski in past winters

    Here is how the snow usually builds up at Paradiski (2,202 m), from 46 past winters.

    At Christmas the upper slopes usually hold 99 cm; half of the winters sit between 75 cm and 132 cm.

    Mid-February typically sees 175 cm, between 144 cm and 202 cm in a normal winter.

    The snowiest month is March, when the pack peaks at about 186 cm.

    78 % of the winters had at least 50 cm by 15 December, and 98 % by 1 January.

    The deepest winter was 1994-95 (300 cm at its peak); the thinnest was 2004-05 (156 cm).

    The skiable season with at least 30 cm typically runs early November to early June (211 days).

    Common questions

    Is there snow in Paradiski at Christmas?

    Usually yes. The upper slopes hold about 99 cm at Christmas, and 98 % of the past 46 winters had at least 50 cm by 1 January. Today there is 135 cm.

    What is the best time to ski Paradiski?

    The deepest snow comes in March (about 186 cm). For a sure base, early November to early June is the usual window with 30 cm or more.

    How deep does the snow get at Paradiski?

    About 186 cm at the peak of a normal winter (early March); between 156 cm in the leanest winter (2004-05) and 300 cm in the biggest (1994-95).

    How high is Paradiski?

    Its highest lift reaches 3,205 m; the lowest starts at 1,199 m (2,006 m of vertical).

    How many km of pistes does Paradiski have?

    About 398 km of pistes and 164 lifts.

    About Paradiski

    Only 4 of the 1,056 ski areas of the Alps are larger than Paradiski, which has some 398 km of runs. The ski area spans the mountain from 1,199 m up to 3,205 m. Its nearest neighbour is La Plagne, 4.9 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Lifts and terrain

    The vertical drop is 2,006 m: one of the longest descents you can ski in one go. Of the 8 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher.

    This week's snow

    The upper slopes of Paradiski hold around 135 cm of snow today. 49 cm of fresh snow fell in the last 24 hours. The coming days should add about 75 cm by Monday.
