    About Bernex

    With roughly 16 km of runs, Bernex sits in the middle of the size table of the Alps. The ski area spans the mountain from 995 m up to 1,870 m. Its nearest neighbour is Thollon-les-Mémises, 3.6 km north-east, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    What there is to ski at Bernex

    Counting every graded piste, there are 41 runs and around 15 km to ski. Most of the length is blue (47%, about 7.1 km). No single level dominates; beginners and good skiers both find runs to suit them. Advanced skiers have about 1.4 km of black or expert runs.

    When the snow is good

    Past winters give a clear pattern at mid-mountain (about 1,432 m): a 30 cm base is usually there from early December to mid-May, about 24 weeks of good cover. A metre-deep snowpack is normal for around 12 weeks. For the best snow, aim for mid-March and late March. Christmas week brought snow on the ground in every winter; the usual depth was 80 cm (lean years 21 cm, snowy years 133 cm). Spring skiers should know that mid-April usually has 123 cm, plenty for sunny spring days. Year to year the snow is steady, with 60 cm to 145 cm in mid-February in most winters. Depth normally peaks in late March at roughly 140 cm.

    The mountain at Bernex

    Skied top to bottom, Bernex gives 875 m of descent: enough height for proper long runs. Below about 1,195 m the snow can be patchy in a mild spell; the higher lifts are the safer bet. The lift system counts 10 lifts: 3 chairlifts, 1 T-bar, 1 button lift and 5 drag lifts. Drag lifts dominate, so snowboarders new to the sport should expect some practice on the way up. The highest named summit within 8 km is Les Cornettes de Bise at 2,432 m, standing 562 m above the top lift.

    Bernex and the resorts around it

    The big name nearby is Espace Liberté: about 103 km of pistes, 12 km to the south-east. Its snow reliability sits in the middle of the group (number 4 of 7). For higher slopes, Espace Liberté climbs to 2,094 m.

    Questions about Bernex

    What is the best month to ski at Bernex?

    Aim for mid-March and late March; the cover is usually good from early December to mid-May.

    Can you ski at Bernex at Christmas?

    Usually: past winters had about 80 cm at mid-mountain on 25 December, with snow in every winter.

    How high do the lifts go at Bernex?

    The top is at 1,870 m and the base at 995 m.

    Is Bernex suitable for families?

    Yes: 47% of the graded pistes, about 7.1 km, are easy.

    Which bigger ski area is near Bernex?

    Espace Liberté, 12 km to the south-east, with about 103 km of pistes.
