    About Polana Sosny

    With roughly 2.0 km of runs, Polana Sosny sits in the middle of the size table of the Carpathians. Its lifts climb from 499 m to 592 m. There are 8 other ski areas within 40 km, the closest being Czorsztyn-Ski Kluszkowce, 6.0 km to the north.

    Pistes and who they suit

    The piste map lists 5 graded runs, about 1.7 km in total. The largest share is blue, with 0.8 km (44%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. Experts may find it tame: there are hardly any steep pistes.

    The mountain at Polana Sosny

    Skied top to bottom, Polana Sosny gives 93 m of descent: short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 499 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. It tops out lower than almost all of its neighbours: Bachledova dolina, for one, climbs to 1,151 m. To get up there are 2 button lifts and 1 drag lift.
