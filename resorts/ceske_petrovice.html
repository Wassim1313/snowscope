    About České Petrovice

    České Petrovice is a mid-sized ski area with about 1.0 km of pistes, number 155 of 257 in Central Europe by length. The slopes start at 637 m and reach 731 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Umbule, 12 km south-west, and 8 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    The pistes of České Petrovice

    Counting every graded piste, there are 3 runs and around 1.3 km to ski. The largest share is blue, with 0.8 km (60%). It is a resort for beginners, families and relaxed cruising: more than half the pistes are easy. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    Heights, vertical and lifts

    94 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. At 637 m the base is low, and the bottom runs depend on cold weather or machine-made snow. Among the 9 ski areas within 50 km it is one of the lowest; Dolní Morava sport & relax resor reaches 1,232 m. All 3 lifts here are button lifts.

    Questions about České Petrovice

    What altitude is České Petrovice at?

    Its highest lift reaches 731 m; the lowest starts at 637 m (94 m of vertical).

    Is České Petrovice good for beginners?

    Yes: 60% of the graded pistes, about 0.8 km, are easy.

    Is there a larger resort near České Petrovice?

    Horský resort Buková hora, 14 km to the south-east, with about 8.0 km of pistes.

    How big is České Petrovice?
