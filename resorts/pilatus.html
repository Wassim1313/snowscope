    About Pilatus

    Pilatus is a ski area. Skiing here happens between 513 m and 2,063 m above sea level. Hergiswil is the next resort, 2.9 km away to the south-east.

    How Pilatus is laid out

    1,550 m separate the highest and lowest lifts, a long top-to-bottom run that tests the legs. The lowest slopes are at only 513 m, so snow down to the bottom needs a cold spell; the upper runs hold it far better. Its top at 2,063 m is the highest of the 9 ski areas within 50 km.
