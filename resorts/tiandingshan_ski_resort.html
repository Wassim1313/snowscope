    About Tiandingshan

    Tiandingshan is a mid-sized ski area with about 7.0 km of pistes, number 28 of 92 in China by length. The slopes start at 271 m and reach 362 m at the top. Its nearest neighbour is Changchun Lianhuashan, 11 km east, and 3 ski areas lie within 40 km in all.

    Runs by difficulty

    The piste map lists 13 graded runs, about 6.7 km in total. The largest share is green circle, with 3.5 km (53%). Easy slopes dominate, which makes it a good choice for a first ski holiday or for young children. There is little here for expert skiers looking for steep slopes.

    How Tiandingshan is laid out

    From top to bottom that makes 91 m of vertical, short runs, good for quick laps and for learning. Because the lifts start as low as 271 m, the valley runs are the first to suffer in a thaw. Of the 4 ski areas within 50 km, only 2 reach higher. Its 5 lifts break down into 2 gondolas and 3 moving carpets. Cabins keep the ride up dry and warm when the weather turns. For small children there are 3 conveyor-belt lifts.
